The Bible Digas that “the man deu names a todos the tame animals, a the birds em the sky e a todos the wild animals. Mas Adam did not find a helper that was right para ele” (Gênesis 2:20, ICB). Adam must have wondered: Por que isn’t there someone just like me?

Deus had the Responda alLeiay planned. O que was Deus’s surprise, e como did isso come a be? **Leia Gênesis 2:21, 22.**

Once again, Deus used Seu own hands a criar someone em Seu image (recite seu Versículo para Decorar).

Primeiro, Ele let Adam fall into a peaceful sleep. Then Ele carefully opened up Adam’s side, took out a rib, e gently molded isso into a female. She was like Adam, mas different. Deus had designed eles a fit together always.

Deus took Eve a Adam, woke ele up, then stood back a watch. amor must have filled Adam’s heart as ele looked into her smiling eyes. O que a lindo surprise Deus had made, just para ele. Ele welcomed her a the world as seu very own especial friend—someone quem could talk com ele, laugh com ele, e understand seu heart.

Adam couldn’t talk com the animals, mas ele could always talk com Eve. Words are one de Deus’s gifts a nos porque nós are made em Seu image. Talking com people helps nos get a know, understand, e amor eles. Kind words de a friend can drive away loneliness e ajude nos feel close, just as Adam e Eve felt. O que Façaes the Bible Diga sobre kind words? **Leia Provérbios 16:24.** The next time você eat honey ou something sweet, Pense sobre como kind words can Faça nos happy e healthy.
