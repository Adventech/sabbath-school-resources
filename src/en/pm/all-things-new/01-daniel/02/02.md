[[Daniel 2:1-4]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- From here through the end of chapter 7 the original language of the book of Daniel switches from Hebrew to Aramaic.
- This change is significant. Aramaic was the language of the nations, of trade and commerce, as well as of the common person. This means that, while the entire book is important, what happens in chapters 2 through 7 is crucial for every person. The events concern the fate of all humanity.

[[Daniel 2:5-11]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- A profound clash of worldviews is about to occur! The king knew the dream meant something important. But he feared that the interpretation his current court would bring would be manipulative or simply false. Only a truly divine source could both tell him what the dream was _and_ accurately interpret it.

[[Daniel 2:12-38]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- With verse 38 a specific and historically accurate progression begins. Babylon is the first empire represented in this dream, symbolized by gold—a fitting symbol, given the immense amount of gold that Babylon had in its possession. The kingdoms that progressively appear in the remainder of the dream are represented by other materials, materials that reflect the characteristics of those kingdoms’ respective reigns.

[[Daniel 2:39-49]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The chest and arms of silver represent the Medo-Persian Empire, which overthrew the Babylonians in 539 B.C. (see Daniel 5:31).
- The belly and thighs of bronze represent the Greek Empire, which overthrew the Medo-Persians in 331 B.C. under the leadership of Alexander the Great (see Daniel 8:20, 21).
- The legs of iron symbolize the Roman Empire, which defeated the Greek Empire in 168 B.C. The Romans indeed were strong like iron for centuries.
- The iron mixed with clay in the feet and toes is significant, for Rome did not fall to one particular empire, but rather gradually crumbled through repeated attacks by the peoples of Western Europe during the fifth century A.D.
- Just as iron does not mix with clay, so Western Europe has remained divided into different nations for centuries, despite such leaders as Charlemagne, Napoleon, and Hitler attempting to unite them. Despite the efforts of kings, conquerors, and currency, Europe has never been truly united since the fall of Rome. History confirms all these facts. This is powerful evidence of God’s ability to accurately reveal the future.
- God’s kingdom, symbolized by the stone, will soon overthrow all earthly kingdoms and be established forever. Just as surely as each previous kingdom was succeeded by another as predicted, so shall this final kingdom in our day.
- God is looking for people of justice, goodness, and love to join Him in that final kingdom! Are you willing to become that kind of person?
