As Jesus began teaching from the Scriptures in the synagogue, He noticed a man who couldn’t use one of his hands at all. It would have been very hard for him to be a farmer, a carpenter, or a fisherman. These jobs needed _two_ strong hands to dig, saw, and lift. Jesus wanted to help him.

What did Jesus say? **Read Luke 6:8.**

The Jewish leaders watched as the man came forward. They whispered angrily to each other. _Surely Jesus wouldn’t dare break their laws and heal someone on Sabbath!_

Jesus answered their thoughts, “I ask you, which is it right to do on the Sabbath day: to do good or to do evil? Is it right to save a life or to destroy one?” (Luke 6:9, ICB).

Everyone became quiet. Of course, it was better to help someone on Sabbath! Jesus turned back to the man and kindly said, “Stretch out your hand” (Matthew 12:13). As he “stretched out his hand . . . it was completely healed!” (Luke 6:10, ICB).

The man looked down in amazement to see straight bones, strong muscles, and happy wriggling fingers. His face shone with delight. He must have given Jesus an extra big squeeze with his new healed hand.

The Jewish leaders looked on with grumpy faces. They didn’t like Jesus healing people on Sabbath.

But Jesus is “Lord of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:28). He wanted the people to see that Sabbath is a day to show God’s love, and to help people. He wants us to do the same.
