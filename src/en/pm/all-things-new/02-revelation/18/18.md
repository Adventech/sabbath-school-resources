[[Revelation 18:1-3]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Verses 1-3 symbolize the great final movement of Jesus’ true followers to share globally the good news of salvation through Him. They will again point out the fallen nature of Babylon, and that Jesus is the only way to salvation.

[[Revelation 18:4-8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- God’s call in verse 4 to “come out of her, my people” makes it clear that just prior to the very last events on earth, many of God’s true followers will be _inside_ Babylon. They have been deceived and are unaware of the fallen nature of the religious organizations they are in.
- But when they hear the final proclamation of the good news about Jesus Christ, they will come out of spiritual Babylon and join God’s true remnant church.
- Are you currently in part of spiritual Babylon? Have you been deceived by her teachings, her false claims regarding salvation and goodness?
- If so, God is calling you today: “Come out of her, my people”!

[[Revelation 18:9-19]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The unholy trinity and Babylon together will be a global religious-political confederacy such as the world has never seen.
- Many of those who receive the mark of the beast will quickly gain incredible financial prosperity from trade and economic agreements made on an international scale.
- But this economic and political success will even more quickly come crashing down as Babylon is utterly destroyed, both by the hand of God and from within.

[[Revelation 18:20-24]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Babylon can promise only short-term—and yet fatal—success. A truly valuable life is far more than mere worldly wealth and power. Jesus is the infinitely better choice!
