**Focus:** The human eye is an amazing example of God’s intricate creation.

- **Have you ever wondered what it would be like to be blind?**
- **How do you think your eyes work to be able to see? Let’s find out!**

The incredible human eye weighs less than one ounce (28 grams), yet it is one of the most complex organs in the human body—only the brain is more complex. The eye allows us to see by catching light rays passing through the air. These light rays are turned into electrical impulses, or messages, as they hit the back of the eye, also called the retina. The messages travel to the brain through a special pathway called the optic nerve. Then the brain changes the messages into pictures that allow us to see and experience the world around us.

Even though the eye has only six muscles, these muscles are the most active in the human body. They work with nearly 2 million different parts to give us sight. The busy muscles in our eyes are always adjusting to light, distance, and speed, all without us having to think about it. The very detailed design of the eye allows humans to be able to tell the difference between 10 million different colors!

God created our eyes to see and enjoy the incredible variety of designs, colors, and details in the beautiful world around us. He wanted us to see how much He loves us with our own eyes. But more important than just appreciating God’s beautiful world, we must remember that God created each of us in His own image.

- **How do you feel, knowing that God created such an incredible thing as the human eye?**
- **God created you in His image. What would He like to see in you when He returns to take His people to heaven?**
