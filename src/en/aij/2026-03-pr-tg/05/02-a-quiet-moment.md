**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It is a miracle to see a young life grow. Their fresh, tender eyes take in all they see. Their sensitive ears absorb all they hear. And as they develop, we come to see that they are remarkable imitators—so much so that sometimes it’s confronting. Their movements, their words, their gestures, and their habits so often mirror those living closest to them.

As we seek to guide those in our care, how wonderful to know that there is Someone to whom we can look to as our example. This Someone, the One “altogether lovely” (Song of Solomon 5:16), can directly shape our children too, as we invite them to be His friend.

As a child, Jesus grew “in the sunlight of His Father’s countenance” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 68). In such closeness with God, He grew to imitate Him.

“As a child, Jesus manifested a peculiar loveliness of disposition. His willing hands were ever ready to serve others. He manifested a patience that nothing could disturb, and a truthfulness that would never sacrifice integrity. In principle firm as a rock, His life revealed the grace of unselfish courtesy” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 68, 69).

What an inspiration He is.

First Peter 2:21 tells us that Jesus is our example in all things, and we are to follow in His steps. We should imitate and learn from Him because Jesus alone is the true and perfect example. The secret to this is found in living close to Him. It is His work in and through us.

As you and the children explore the story of Jesus’ childhood years, may you be drawn to His example and loveliness, knowing that by spending time close to Him, we all can grow to be more like Him each day.
