**Big Idea:**\
God is loving. I will obey Him.

_^[“If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (John 14:23)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Obey”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To obey means to follow a command. When we drive in a car, we obey the road rules so we can stay safe. To obey God is to always follow God’s way, not my way.

The First Family

Ten little fingers, 10 little toes, two sleepy eyes, and a cute button nose. Eve wrapped her teeny baby boy in a cloth and stroked his head until his eyes grew heavy and he fell fast sleep.

Adam and Eve were so happy. God wanted them to have a loving family and had blessed them with such a sweet little baby. They named him Cain. Every day Cain grew bigger. He grew and grew, until he was a little boy who could run, jump, talk, and play.

One exciting day Cain became a big brother! What was his baby brother’s name? Read the first part of Genesis 4:2.

Adam and Eve loved their boys with all their hearts. They laughed and played with them, and told them wonderful stories. Adam and Eve especially loved telling Cain and Abel about the beautiful Garden of Eden. And how they had walked and talked with God face to face.

Then with sadness in their voices, they told their boys how they had disobeyed God’s wise rules. They had eaten the fruit from the tree God had told them not to touch. Sin had changed their world. It had caused sadness, pain, and death. How they wished they had chosen to obey God instead of listening to the serpent in the tree.

Adam and Eve hugged Cain and Abel tightly. Their story would have a happy ending. God had promised to save them from the mess of sin! Bowing together, they thanked God for His plan to come and die so that they could be saved.
