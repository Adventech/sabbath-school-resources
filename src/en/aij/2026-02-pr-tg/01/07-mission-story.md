**Focus:** We are never too young to follow God’s leading.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Bolivia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

One Sabbath 12-year-old Ariel heard a sermon in a home church in Bolivia that really made him think. The pastor said that everyone should be involved in missionary work. Ariel liked the idea of being a missionary, but what could a 12-year-old boy do?

Ariel began to pray. “Dear God, what do I do now? Help me to be a real missionary for You.”

He began to look for people who might be interested in learning about Jesus. As he looked, he kept asking God to lead him. “Dear God,” he prayed, “help me to be a real missionary for You.”

One day he joined his parents and sister in visiting another family. While there, he saw that one of them, a girl named Irene, was interested in the Bible. He offered to give her Bible studies, and she agreed.

Ariel had never given Bible studies before, and he wasn’t really sure what to do. But he found a set of Bible lessons and studied them with Irene. He prayed a lot that God would lead and help him. And God did!

Irene looked forward to each new Bible lesson. When they finished the lessons, she decided to give her heart to Jesus. She was baptized.

Ariel was so happy. God had led him to be a real missionary for Him.

What do I do now? he thought. He remembered his prayer and decided to keep praying that God would lead him, every day, to be a real missionary.

After Irene got baptized, her father also decided to give his heart to Jesus. Then her mother too. The whole family ended up being baptized!

Ariel learned that no one is ever too young to be a missionary.

- **What can you do to help guide you to others who need to know about Jesus?**
