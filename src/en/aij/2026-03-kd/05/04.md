Mary, Joseph, and Jesus could finally see Jerusalem. It was beautiful! They were so happy to come and worship God.

Long ago King David had written a song for travelers to sing when they came to worship God. Like a grand choir, the traveling crowd with Jesus sang joyfully:

“Let’s go to the Temple of the Lord.\
“Jerusalem, we are standing at your gates.\
“Jerusalem is built as a city where friends can come together” (Psalm 122:1-3, ICB).

The Passover celebrations had begun!

What a special time Jesus had. For the first time He saw the temple and for the first time He saw the priests give the sin offering to God. Jesus watched very closely. As He looked at everything that happened in the temple, He realized it was all about Him. He would be the offering, the one who “takes away the sin of the world!” (John 1:29).

Jesus found the teachers and Pharisees in the synagogue and sat quietly at their feet. He listened to their words and then began asking questions. He asked deep and difficult questions about the coming Savior.

The teachers and Pharisees looked at Him and were puzzled. Who was this boy? He wasn’t one of their students. Where had He learned so much about the Scriptures? He seemed so wise.

If only these teachers and Pharisees had realized that sitting at their feet was God’s own Son, the promised Savior! If only they had had open hearts to learn from Jesus.
