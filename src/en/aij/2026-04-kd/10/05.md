The crowd hushed and watched closely as Bartimaeus stood in front of Jesus. Everyone waited to see what Jesus would do next.

Jesus looked with deep care at Bartimaeus. Then He asked, “What do you want Me to do for you?” (Luke 18:41).

With a quiet calmness, Bartimaeus answered, “I want to see again” (Luke 18:41, ICB).

What did Jesus say next? **Read Luke 18:42.**

As soon as Jesus spoke these few words, bright light flashed into Bartimaeus’s eyes! He instantly squinted in the dazzling sunshine. Blinking and rubbing his eyes, he looked around. For the first time he saw the sky, the trees, and the faces of the crowd. These faces smiled as they saw Bartimaeus looking around in wonder. He could see!

Then Bartimaeus’s eyes rested on the lovely face of Jesus. Jesus, who had listened to his shouts for help. Jesus, who had healed his eyes, and saved him from his life of begging. Once again, Bartimaeus began to shout—this time, with joy!

His thankfulness was contagious! “And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God.” (Luke 18:43). Jesus had shown them that He loves to listen, help, and save us. The Bible says, “And it shall come to pass that whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved” (Joel 2:32). Bartimaeus decided to stay close to Jesus all the way down the road. He couldn’t imagine a life of darkness anymore, and he wanted to follow Jesus wherever He went.
