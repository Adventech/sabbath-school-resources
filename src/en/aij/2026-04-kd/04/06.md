As Jesus stepped into the boat with Peter, the storm completely stopped. The wild wind disappeared. The water became flat and calm.

Peter sat quietly. He didn’t feel like boasting about walking on the water because he knew that his fear and pride had made him sink.

The only thing he could boast about was Jesus. It was Jesus who had helped him walk on the water. It was the powerful, loving, and forgiving Jesus who had saved Him from drowning.

The disciples in the boat were quiet too. They rubbed their eyes, and looked out at the still night air and the gentle water. Wow, Jesus was amazing.

They thought about their day with Jesus, and all of the people He had healed. He had made a little lunch grow so big that it had fed more than 5,000 people!

Then, Jesus had helped them. He had walked on top of the water in the middle of a storm to save them! He had stopped the storm.

But now, the disciples couldn’t be quiet anymore. They were so full of love for Jesus, they knelt before Him and worshiped Him.

“Truly You are the Son of God,” they said (Matthew 14:33).

With hearts full of love for Jesus, we can worship Him too. Jesus loves, helps, teaches, and saves us. He is truly an amazing God! And He truly wants to be your best Friend.
