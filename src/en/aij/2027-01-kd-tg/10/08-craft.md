**What you need:**

Week 10 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, glue stick.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/10a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2027-01-kd-craft-10.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color page 1 of the craft template and cut around the outside.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/10b.jpg)

2\. Color page 2 of the craft template and cut out the window flaps.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/10c.jpg)

3\. Take window flap 1 and fold along the dotted line. Glue the edge of the window flap so it covers commandment I, on page 1 of the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/10d.jpg)

4\. Continue gluing the window flaps over the rest of the commandments. See if you can guess what the commandment is by looking at the numbers then open the windows to check the answer!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/10e.jpg)
