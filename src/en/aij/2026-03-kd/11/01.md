**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God. He wants to teach me.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“More than that, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Luke 11:28).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Listen”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To listen is to pay attention to sound. When we pay attention to the words someone is saying, we are listening. Jesus asks us to listen to His wise words, and then do what He says.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Matthew 5:14-16; Matthew 13:31-34; Luke 6:43-45; Luke 6:46-49; Matthew 6:9-13; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 298-314.

Jesus the Storyteller

The little boy leaned forward and lifted his chin. He sat on his hands and made his body very still. Carefully He listened to Jesus.

A girl beside him squeezed a little closer. She bumped into him, but the boy didn’t notice. He kept looking right at Jesus. The Teacher’s kind eyes shone as He told another story. One was funny. Another, exciting! One made him look at the pretty trees and flowers around him and sigh with happiness. Even though he was young, he could understand all the stories. Jesus was the best storyteller of all.

Jesus told stories to small groups in small houses. And big groups, under big blue skies. He spoke in temples, from boats, and while He walked along dusty roads.

How often did Jesus tell stories? **Read Matthew 13:34.** Jesus talked about flitting birds, colorful flowers, and fields of wheat. Boys and girls could look and see these things as they quietly listened. He told stories about naughty sheep, silly builders, and busy farmers. Oh, everyone knew what they were like.

Whether gray-haired and wrinkly or young and wiggly, everyone loved to listen and learn from Jesus. People even traveled from far, far away to hear His wise words.

Jesus is still our best storyteller. If we listen closely, His stories can help us grow closer to Him. The Bible says, “More than that, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!” (Luke 11:28).
