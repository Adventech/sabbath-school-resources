**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children to play a game of **“Toss the Beanbag.”** Use a strip of masking tape to mark the starting line for the children to stand on when it is their turn. Place a bucket far enough away from the starting line that it’s a challenge for your older children but not impossible for the younger ones. Have each child take turns standing at the starting line and tossing the two beanbags into the bucket. The first time, they should look at the bucket. The second time, they should close their eyes. Allow each child to have a turn tossing the two beanbags.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What did you learn from this challenge?**
- **What helped you to do better?**

**Teaching Point:** Keeping our eyes on the goal helped us to do better in this game.

- **What other things do you give your focus, your full attention, to?** (Prompt them if they have hobbies, learn music, or play sports, etc.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** This week’s story takes us back to the Sea of Galilee and another storm. The disciples had to choose what to focus on. Let’s discover what happened. Share the **Big Idea: Jesus helps me do the impossible when I keep my eyes on Him.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask any children who experimented with the objects that floated or sunk in the Early Arrival activity to share their findings with the rest of the group. **Were there any surprises?**

Then show the children an orange or mandarin with the peel on.

- **Do you think the orange will float or sink?** Place it in the bowl of water. It will float.

Then remove the peel from around it and place it in the water a second time.

- **Now what do you think will happen?** It will sink.

Explain that an orange has tiny air pockets in the peel that protect the orange and also make it float. When the peel is removed, the orange gets heavy and sinks.

**Teaching Point:** We’re a little like this orange. Life can feel heavy sometimes. We might have worries or fears (as Peter did when he looked at the waves around him). Satan might tempt us to focus on ourselves or other things that distract us from Jesus. But when we focus on Jesus, we won’t sink under all the hard things or temptations of life.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}}) How can we “keep our eyes on Jesus” when we can’t really see Him, as Peter could?** (We can read stories about Him in our Bibles, remember that He’s with us all through our day, pray to Him any time of the day, and include Him in our thoughts and decisions.)

Say the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus helps us do the impossible when we keep our eyes on Him, just as He helped Peter.**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/assets/4-isaiah-45-22.mp3] “Isaiah 45:22” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **Who is talking in this verse?**
- **Who is He talking to? Who are the “ends of the earth”?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Take your globe or world map and invite someone to place a sticker on a country. Identify the name of the country, then say, “God says, ‘Look to Me and be saved, people in (Taiwan).’” “God says, ‘Look to Me and be saved, people in (Romania).’” “God says, ‘Look to Me and be saved, people in (Peru).’” Proceed through a range of countries.

Play **Ball Toss:** Children will form pairs and throw a soft ball to each other. As they catch the ball they will say the next word in the memory verse.

Affirm children who are “focused” on the ball.

- **Who do we need to focus on each day?** (Jesus.)
- **Why do we need to focus on Jesus?** (He is loving, He is just, He is kind, He helps, He listens, He heals, He encourages and guides, He forgives, He saves. He can do the impossible.)

**Teaching Point:** Sometimes we are like Peter. When problems or temptations come, we focus on them instead of keeping our eyes on our wonderful Jesus. We can trust Jesus because He will always help us—even in situations that seem impossible! We can also focus on Jesus because He is truly awesome, and we want to become more like Him.
