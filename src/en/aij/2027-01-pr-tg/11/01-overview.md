**Lesson Summary**

Moses spent 40 days with God on Mount Sinai while Aaron was in charge of the people. Their faith waned as they waited, and they looked backward to Egypt instead of upward to God. They reverted to false worship when they demanded Aaron make a god they could see. Moses returned with the Ten Commandments tablets and broke them because the people had broken God’s law. The repentant were forgiven, and God rewrote His law on stone. The people remained committed to God while Moses was up on the mountain for another 40 days. They saw the new Ten Commandments and accepted God’s invitation for a loving, committed friendship with Him.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is committed to His people and that they can have a loving and respectful relationship with Him.
- **Feel** a desire to commit to a friendship with God.
- **Respond** by asking God to help them grow a committed friendship with Him.
