“Moses grew” (Exodus 2:11, ICB), and grew, and grew. He wasn’t a little boy anymore. He had become a big strong man with a caring heart.

One day Prince Moses left the royal palace and went to visit his people, the Israelites. They worked in the hot sun as slaves to the people of Egypt. He noticed the slaves were made to work very hard. Life for his people was so tiring and so unfair!

As Moses watched them work, he saw an Egyptian soldier hitting an Israelite slave. He was so upset! “So he looked this way and that way, and when he saw no one, he killed the Egyptian” (Exodus 2:12).

Oh, no! Moses had let his anger turn into angry actions. He had done something terrible. What does the Bible tell us about anger? **Read James 1:20.**

The news of what Moses did spread quickly. Soon Pharaoh heard. He was furious! Moses knew it wasn’t safe to stay in Egypt any longer, so he quickly ran away.

Even though Moses had done a terrible wrong and acted in anger, God still loved him. God stayed by his side and guided him to a place far from Egypt.

God is sad when we use angry, hurtful words or actions. But He still loves us and wants to change us. He wants to help us stay calm and in control, even when we feel angry. We can pray, “Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me” (Psalm 51:10).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What do you think Moses should have done when he saw the soldier hurting the slave?
- Have you ever been so angry that you hurt someone? What is something you could do the next time you are angry?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to help you stay calm the next time you are angry.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Instead of letting your anger turn into angry actions, let it out in a safe, active way. Try one of these ways now: pray, count from 1 to 10, jump or hop 10 times, or sing or repeat a Bible promise while you skip.
