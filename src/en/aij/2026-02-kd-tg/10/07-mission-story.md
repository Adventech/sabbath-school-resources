**Focus:** God sends a miracle to give Grandmother Brigitte what she needs.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Germany** on your **globe** or **world map**.

^[Prop: water spray bottle. (To engage the children in the story, lightly mist the air with the water spray bottle for rain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

_Drip, drip, drip_ went the droplets of rain on Grandmother Brigitte’s floor. Oh, no! It had been a very hot summer in Germany, and her roof had cracked. Now, when it rained, water leaked into her home.

Grandmother Brigitte didn’t have enough money for a new roof, so she began to save money.

But then at church she heard about a wonderful mission project to teach people about Jesus in another country. She decided that was more important than a new roof! She gave all her money to the mission project. She decided to trust God to supply her with a new roof.

Just a few days after she gave the money to the mission project, she received a surprise letter with enough money to pay for the new roof. It was a miracle from God!

The old leaky roof came off the house. The new roof, however, wouldn’t be ready for four weeks.

Grandmother Brigitte was worried. All her things were still in the house with no roof. If it rained, everything would get wet and ruined!

“God,” Grandmother Brigitte prayed, “please don’t send rain.” There was no rain that day.

Grandmother Brigitte prayed the same prayer every day, and there was no rain. Three weeks passed, and every day she prayed, no rain fell.

But then one day big black clouds filled the sky. Grandmother Brigitte prayed, “God, please don’t send rain.”

Then she saw rain starting to fall on the windows of the neighbor’s house: first a few drops and then more ^[(spray water bottle to the side)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Soon rain was pouring down on her neighbor’s house. Grandmother Brigitte looked at the other neighbors’ houses. Rain was pouring down on all their houses too.

For 30 minutes rain fell in sheets on the right, and on the left, and across the street. But no rain fell on Grandmother Brigitte’s house without a roof.

“Thank You, dear Lord, thank You,” she prayed.

A week later the house was covered with a new roof. Just as the roof was finished, rain started to fall. But Grandmother Brigitte didn’t mind. Everything in the house was safe.

- **What did God supply for Grandmother Brigitte?**

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is with me. He gives me everything I need.**
