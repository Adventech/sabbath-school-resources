Have you ever wanted to walk on water, just like Peter? What an incredible, miraculous treat that would be! God delights in creating the fun, the fabulous, and the totally unexpected. In fact, He has given two creatures in our amazing world the super-cool ability to walk on water.

**The northern water shrew** may be small, but it’s BIG on wow factor! The world’s smallest mammal can scoot across the water for five feet (1.5 meters) or more. It has stiff hairs on the edges of its feet that spread out when it swims and when it runs across the water. Some scientists believe air bubbles get trapped in the hairs, helping the shrew to stay afloat and run on the surface of the water. Look it up online with your family to see it in action.

The shrew runs lightly across the water to escape from its enemies. What can you do to escape from your enemy Satan?

**The banded basilisk lizard** takes water walking to the next level! This large lizard lives in the jungles of Central and South America. Unlike the shrew, it is not tiny or light. But even though it grows to three feet long (just under a meter), it thinks nothing of dashing across the surface of a river or lake for up to a quarter of a mile (400 meters). Run, lizard, run!

God has given the banded basilisk lizard special toes on its back feet. These long toes have extra bits of skin like scales that spread out on the water. As they run, they slap their feet hard against the surface. This makes tiny air pockets that help them stay afloat—as long as they pump their legs very fast. Their long tail also helps them stay afloat while dashing across the top of the water.

The banded basilisk lizard works hard to run on water. Could you also run on water if you worked very hard at it? Why?

Science can explain why the shrew and banded basilisk can move on the water’s surface. But there is no way science can explain how Jesus and Peter did. It is absolute proof that God can do impossible things to build our faith. “With God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26).
