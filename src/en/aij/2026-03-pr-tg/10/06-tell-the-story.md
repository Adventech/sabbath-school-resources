**Big Idea:** Jesus feeds me with His words and with food.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The disciples dragged their feet. Some sighed. Others yawned. They had been traveling as preachers, and they were so tired.

Jesus saw how weary they were. He longed to be with them—He knew they needed to be with Him too.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did He invite them to do? Read Mark 6:31.** (To rest awhile by themselves in a deserted place.)

Jesus knew about their physical needs: they were tired and hungry. He also knew about their spiritual needs: they needed time with God. He knew just the place where He could meet both needs!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a gentle splash they pushed their boat out into the lake and set off across the Sea of Galilee to a lonely, peaceful spot on the other side. As the boat sailed across the water, they spent time together. Jesus encouraged His friends. They rested, they focused on positive things, and they prayed. And after a while, their energy began to return.

Meanwhile, the crowds who had been with Jesus missed Him. Some had seen Him sail away with His friends, and word quickly spread. Eager people hurried around the lake on foot. They ran so quickly that they arrived before Jesus and the disciples!

Many travelers were on their way to Jerusalem to celebrate the Passover, so they joined the crowd too. Soon 5,000 men had gathered, as well as women and children.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus felt sorry for the people. They were really hungry to hear His words, because the priests and rulers didn’t feed them much spiritual food—just rules and laws instead. So even though He really needed rest, Jesus began to teach the people about God’s love.

Jesus spoke of a kind and forgiving God who cared. He shared the Scriptures with the people and taught them how to be saved. He healed the sick. He encouraged the lonely.

The hours quickly passed by as the people listened carefully to Jesus. They had such a wonderful day; they hadn’t stopped to eat.

When the sun was low in the western sky, the disciples told Jesus how hungry they were, and that the people must be hungry too. They asked Jesus to send the people away so they could buy food for themselves from the villages.

Jesus replied, “You give them food to eat” (Mark 6:37, ICB).

The disciples looked at Jesus in surprise. They didn’t have any food! They said, “We can’t buy enough bread to feed all these people! We would all have to work a month to earn enough money to buy that much bread!” (Mark 6:37, ICB).

Jesus smiled. “How many loaves of bread do you have now? Go and see” (Mark 6:38, ICB).

Andrew found a little boy who hadn’t eaten his picnic lunch: five little barley loaves and two small fish. ^[(Show a little basket with paper loaves and fish.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He brought it to Jesus. It wasn’t much. But Jesus knew exactly what He could do with it!

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did Jesus do next? Read Mark 6:41** (He looked to heaven and blessed the food and broke it. He gave it to the disciples who went and gave it to the people.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was a miracle! The people scratched their heads and blinked their eyes in surprise. _How could thousands of barley loaves come from just five?_ ^[(Hold up the five paper loaves.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _How could thousands of fish come from just two?_ ^[(Hold up the two paper fish.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

All the men, women, and children in that happy crowd ate and ate until they were full. After the crowd had finished eating, the disciples collected up to 12 big baskets of leftovers! ^[(Show 12 paper baskets.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Another day, Jesus was again teaching a great crowd of people. This time He was on a different side of the Sea of Galilee. The crowd was different too. They were mostly Gentiles—non-Jewish people. They didn’t know very much about God.

Full of hope, the crowds brought all their sick and hurting friends and family to Jesus, “and He healed them” (Matthew 15:30). The people were amazed and praised God!

For three whole days Jesus stayed with them, healing, helping, and loving them. By the third day the people’s food had run out, and they were hungry. Jesus didn’t want to send them away with empty stomachs.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did Jesus do? Read Matthew 15:32-36.** (He took the seven loaves the disciples gave Him, gave thanks, broke them, and gave them to His disciples to give to the people.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Once again Jesus used what they had—a tiny bit of food—and multiplied it to meet a great big need. This time 4,000 men and their families were blessed by God’s power.
