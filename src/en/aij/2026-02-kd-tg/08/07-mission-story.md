**Focus:** We can make good choices to show others God’s love.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Australia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Arlo ran to the window and waited. Every morning, just after the sun came up, a car would whiz up his steep street. The car would slow down—but not very much—as the driver threw a newspaper out the window toward the house on the opposite side of the street.

The newspaper would end up in the funniest places! Little 6-year-old Arlo loved to see where it would land. Then he’d watch as Tony, his elderly neighbor, walked down his steep driveway to try to find it.

As Arlo watched, he noticed that Tony wasn’t walking too well. He called his mommy to come see. Mommy went and talked to Tony. She came back looking sad. Tony had blood cancer and was sick.

What could Arlo do? He chose to be a helper. Mommy liked his plan a lot! Every morning Arlo collected the newspaper and took it up the very steep driveway to Tony’s door.

Sometimes he put sticky notes on the newspaper. Mommy would write on it too, telling Tony that their family was praying for him. Tony was so happy! He told all his family and friends about his friend Arlo, who loved God and chose to be kind. His grown-up children left thank-you presents for Arlo in the mailbox.

Sometimes Arlo really wanted to sleep in. But he knew that Tony would be waiting for his newspaper, so he chose to get up early every day—even on the weekends. Then he started to wheel Tony’s empty trash bins from the road to the back of Tony’s house every week.

After a year of Arlo helping and encouraging him, Tony got better! But Arlo kept picking up his newspaper and taking in the trash bins. It made them both very happy.

- **What do you think of Arlo’s choices?**
- **What do you think Tony and his family thought about God because of Arlo’s kindness?**
- **What might you do to show God’s love to someone this week?**

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He helps me make good choices.**
