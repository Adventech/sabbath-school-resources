**Opening Activities**

Source a **large piece of cardstock** and draw on a tree or large bush, or source a **potted plant** to be your burning bush this week. Several activities will utilize this prop: early arrivals; story; **Secret Surprise Box**; and **Story Explorer Box**.

Source items for early arrivals: print a **sheep template for each child**, cut out, and source **crayons/pencils, cotton balls** or **crepe paper**, and **glue** for the children to decorate their sheep. Use **tape** to stick the sheep to the drawn tree or wall, or place near the potted plant.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: create “flames” out of items available to you: **red and orange paper, cellophane, crepe paper,** or **orange/flowers or leaves**. Source **tape or sticky tac** so children can attach to your drawn tree/plant.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **storytelling props**: “Prince Moses” props: **crown** and **colored cloak**. “Shepherd Moses” props: **head covering, plain cloak**, and **shepherd’s staff** (long stick). Use the sheep made before Sabbath School. A **toy snake (or piece of string)**. Use the tree/plant from earlier activity.

Source **peg people** or a **felt set**, and **^[week 2 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

After the story, have a helper move the tree/plant off to the side, so the children can follow you to reach it during the Story Explorer segment. Have the helper hide or stick on lots of little strips of paper with the following Bible promise written on it: “I will be with you” (Exodus 3:12, ICB).

Source a Bible to place inside the **Story Explorer Box**.
