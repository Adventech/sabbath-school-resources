**Focus:** Jesus can save us when we are helpless.

_If possible, source a toy dog to use while telling the story._

**Location:** Help a child locate **Australia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

It was a quiet Sunday morning at Lara and Jack’s home in Cooranbong, Australia. After breakfast the two children said goodbye to their new puppy, Milly. (Bring out toy dog and invite children to wave goodbye to it. Place dog out of sight.) Then they got into the car, and their dad drove them to a school playground where they played soccer and basketball for an hour.

On the way home they noticed something strange. Smoke was filling the sky. It looked as though it was coming from their house!

As they rounded the corner, they realized it was their house. Lara and Jack jumped out of the car and raced to the back door. It was locked. “Where’s Milly?” Lara cried. “Hurry! Open the door!”

Father opened the door. Thick black smoke filled the room. Lara frantically called for Milly, but the puppy didn’t come. “Where is she?” Laura asked. “Milly! Milly!”

Father tried to enter the house to look for the puppy, but it was too smoky. Crying, the children ran to the front lawn. Lara said to Jack, “We can’t do anything about the fire. Let’s just kneel down and pray.”

“Dear God,” Lara said. “You know what is happening, and You know how much we love little Milly. You said that You look after the sparrows, so please look after Milly. Please help the firefighters find Milly.”

Soon eight fire trucks, lights flashing and sirens wailing, raced to the house. As the firefighters unwound their hoses, Father pleaded for them to look for Milly. The firefighters couldn’t find the puppy. Lara and Jack, still crying, crossed the street to the neighbor’s house to wait.

After a short time Father’s voice rang out. “Lara, Jack, come out!” he shouted. Lara and Jack ran out of the house. Father held Milly in his arms. (Show toy dog again.) Her white fur was black with soot, but her tail was wagging happily. Sad tears turned to happy tears, and Lara and Jack threw their arms around the puppy and each other. “Thank You, God! Thank You, God!” the children exclaimed.

Firefighters called Milly a miracle. After putting out the fire, they had found Milly hiding under a bed. Somehow a pocket of air had formed under the bed, and Milly had been able to breathe the air instead of the poisonous smoke.

Lara and Jack couldn’t save Milly. Even the firefighters couldn’t save Milly. But Jesus could. Today Milly is back to her normal happy self, except that she has a new nickname. Lara calls her “Miracle Milly.” (Show children the picture of Lara, Jack, and Milly below.)
