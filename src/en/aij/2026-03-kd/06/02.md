The dusty road was packed with excited travelers. The priests in long robes and important Jewish teachers came first. Tax collectors hurried along behind them. Fishermen and farmers came next, laughing as they walked. And behind them, families crowded the road—from old grandfather to new baby brother. Everyone was on their way to see the new preacher.

The Bible talks about a voice calling out loudly in the desert, “Prepare the way of the Lord” (Isaiah 40:3). And that’s just where all the people were going—the desert!

John was now a special messenger for God. He was called John the Baptist.

The people all rushed to the desert to hear him speak. What a surprise they got! He didn’t look or act like anyone they had ever seen before.

What did John wear and eat? **Read Matthew 3:4.**

John was different from other preachers, and his message was too. God wanted it that way. It was time for a big change!

The people didn’t need to change their shoes. They didn’t need to change their head scarves. John said, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2).

Jesus wants to make a big change in your heart too! Why not come to Him today and ask, “Create in me a clean heart, O God” (Psalm 51:10)?
