**Big Idea:** Jesus heals me when I reach out to Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use your facial expressions and body language to express the range of emotions experienced by the woman OR draw each emotion as a face on **paper plates** or **circular pieces of cardstock**.
- Use a piece of **cloth** as a prop, **peg people**, or **felt set** to help tell the story.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The woman stopped beside the busy road and caught her breath. She watched the crowd of people as they hurried by. Everyone was going to see Jesus! She was going to see Jesus too. I hope that He can heal me, she thought, as she slowly set off again.

For 12 long years she had suffered from constant bleeding. She had seen every doctor and tried every treatment. It was painful and expensive, and nothing had worked! “She had spent all that she had and was no better, but rather grew worse” (Mark 5:26). Hearing about Jesus, she hoped for a miracle.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What had Jesus come to earth to do? Read John 10:10.** (“I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly.”) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

With a tired sigh she finally reached the place where Jesus was teaching and healing. The crowd was huge! It was impossible to push her way through.

When Jesus left, the crowd followed, and the woman followed along too. But again she was unable to get to Jesus. Would she ever reach Him?

Just as she began to lose hope, Jesus turned and headed in her direction. This was it—her moment to meet Jesus!

Fearful of losing her only chance, the tired woman pushed forward. With every step she said to herself, “If only I may touch His clothes, I shall be made well” (Mark 5:28) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

As Jesus was passing she stretched out her arm. With all her strength she reached for Jesus. Her fingertips just touched the hem of His clothes. ^[(Hold up the cloth.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Then what happened? Read Mark 5:29.** (Immediately she stopped bleeding and was healed.)

Immediately Jesus healed her! No more pain or bleeding, no more tiredness or worry. It felt so good to be well! With a thankful heart she turned to quietly go. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Suddenly Jesus stopped. The jostling people stopped with Him. With a loud voice He said, “Who touched My clothes?” (Mark 5:30).

The people were pressed together tightly—pushing, bumping, shoving. Everyone was surprised by His question!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Peter say to Jesus? Read Luke 8:45.** (“Master, the multitudes throng and press You, and You say, ‘Who touched Me?’ ”)

Jesus replied, “Someone did touch me! I felt power go out from me” (Luke 8:46, ICB).

Jesus knew who had touched Him, and He knew it was a touch of faith and trust. But He asked the question so that the woman would have the opportunity to tell others. Everyone needed to hear about the beautiful miracle that happened when she reached out to Jesus. It would encourage them to reach out to Jesus too.

The grateful woman had tried to blend into the crowd, unnoticed. But when Jesus looked toward her, she realized she couldn’t hide any longer. She slowly made her way toward Jesus. This time the crowd parted. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Trembling, she fell down at His feet.

Through tears the woman began to tell her story. She told Jesus how the long, hopeless years of suffering had left her tired. She told Him how she had heard about Jesus and was filled with hope. She had desperately reached out to touch His clothes and instantly, miraculously, she had been healed!

Jesus smiled as He gently said, “Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well. Go in peace” (Luke 8:48).

Jesus had called her “Daughter . . .” There was so much meaning in that one word. She was loved. She belonged. She was part of God’s big family. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

With a smile the woman headed home. There was no shuffling, no bleeding, no weakness. She had been fully healed.
