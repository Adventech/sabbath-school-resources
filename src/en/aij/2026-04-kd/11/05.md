The crowd looked at Jesus, their eyes wide. What would He do for the man who had shown so much trust in Him?

Jesus said, “Go your way; and as you have believed, so let it be done for you” (Matthew 8:13).

What happened when Jesus spoke these words? **Read Luke 7:10.**

What a wonderful Savior! Jesus loved to help the Roman soldier who had trusted His power. And Jesus loves, and will save, everyone who trusts Him today too!

That evening, as the sun began to disappear in the sky, the centurion may have gathered his family and servants together for evening worship. Imagine how thankful they were. Jesus had shown them His power. Jesus had shown them His love. They were invited to be part of God’s family. They could always trust in Him!

You can trust Him too. “And those who know Your name will put their trust in You; For You, Lord, have not forsaken those who seek You” (Psalm 9:10).
