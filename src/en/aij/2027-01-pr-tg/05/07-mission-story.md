**Focus:** God protects a mother and her daughter during a frightening storm.

**What’s one of your favorite thank-You or worship songs about God? Why?**

**Location:** Help a child to locate **Myanmar** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Mother shook 7-year-old Kiim. “Wake up,” she said. “It’s raining really, really hard. Let’s go up to the second floor.” Kiim opened her eyes and saw water swirling around on the dirt floor of the house. Rain pelted down, and lightning flashed. Kiim obediently got up and followed Mother up the bamboo ladder leading to the second floor. Mother worked as a housekeeper for the family who lived there: a father, mother, and their two small daughters.

As the rain poured down outside, the two little girls cried. They were frightened. The rickety bamboo house shook as if it might fall at any moment. Bang! Suddenly the plastic windows on the second floor blew away in a gust of wind. Crash! A mighty wind tore off the roof. The pounding rain immediately soaked Kiim. She saw that the floodwaters were rising rapidly and had nearly reached the second floor of the house. She could not swim. None of them knew how to swim! They were frightened. What would happen next?

Mother prayed. “Dear God, if You save my daughter and me from this storm, I will give my daughter to You and believe that You will take us to a better place,” she said. Mother repeated the prayer many times. Kiim also prayed. The man and woman prayed. In that frightening situation they relied on God completely and trusted Him as their leader. After a while the rain stopped. But the two families were stranded on the second floor as they waited for the water to go away. They had no food or electricity. It was impossible to build a fire.

They waited seven long days. Finally they were able to leave the house. Somehow they had survived. Mother thanked God for answering their prayers.

Three years later God answered Mother’s prayers in a special way when she and Kiim were able to move to the United States as refugees. Kiim was 10, and she had never attended school. Mother kept praying, and Kiim ended up studying at a Seventh-day Adventist school in the U.S. state of Georgia.
