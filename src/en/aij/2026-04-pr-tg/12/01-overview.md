**Word of the Week**

**Sympathy:** Sympathy is having a kind understanding and concern for others when they are sad, suffering, or having a difficult time.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

When Jesus and His disciples approached the small town of Nain, they encountered a funeral procession for the only son of a widow who lived in the town. Jesus felt sympathy for the woman and told her not to cry. He then went to the stretcher, touched it, and told the lifeless young man to get up. Immediately the young man heard, sat up, and began to talk. Jesus then gave the young man to his mother, and there was a joyous reunion. Those who saw it were amazed and called Jesus a prophet sent from God to help His people.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus comforts us when we are sad, but also gives us hope and joy.
- **Feel** grateful that Jesus cares about our every thought and feeling.
- **Respond** by sharing the hope that Jesus gives us with others.
