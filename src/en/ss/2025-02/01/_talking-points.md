### ^[Lesson Study Intro]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

“We are going to study this quarter the theme of how to interpret Bible prophecy, specifically some of the allusions, stories, images, and metaphors that unlock prophetic truth and final events.” Qtly, Teacher’s Ed, p. 3

### ^[1. Prophecy is intended to be understood (Sun, Mon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- Spiritual things can only be understood spiritually (1 Cor. 2:14)
	- God’s thoughts are infinite (Ps. 147:5; Is. 55:9)
		- He urges us to forsake our thoughts in order to receive His (Is. 55:7), and to become spiritual (1 Cor. 2:15, 16).
- Truth, therefore, is a matter of revelation (Dt. 29:29).
	- You could observe a person’s behaviors and draw some conclusions about them; you could take them into a lab and run tests; but the only way you can really know them is if they reveal themselves to you. Reason can never replace revelation.
		- See Qtly, Mon, par 4
- God didn’t give prophecy to confuse, but to enlighten (Mt. 24:15; Rev. 1:3).
	- “Without being surrendered to the Lord, and without a heart open to learning the truth, those who read the Bible will likely come away not only missing its message but misunderstanding the loving and holy character of the God revealed in its pages.” Qtly, Sun, par 4
	- “Though we will never understand everything, we can understand what is necessary for our salvation…. Indeed, in the end, Bible prophecy must ultimately, in one way or another, lead us to Jesus and the promise of salvation that He offers to all humanity.” Qtly, Mon, par 4

### ^[2. The Advent Movement is a Movement of Prophecy (Tue)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- Daniel’s prophecy was not for himself, but for us (Dan. 12:4, 9-10; 1 Pet. 1:10-12; Rev. 10:1-11).
	- The prophecy of Daniel 12 is “unsealed” in the prophecy of Revelation 10 and fulfilled in the rise of the Advent Movement.
		- “Understanding of Daniel increased rapidly… after the end of the 1,260–year prophecy, which ended in 1798, when multiple expositors around the globe started concluding that something spectacular was going to happen around 1843…. The birth of our global movement, in other words, is a fulfillment of Daniel’s prediction that “knowledge shall increase” at “the time of the end.” Qtly, Tue, par 4; see also Loughborough, The Great Second Advent Movement, pp. 84-86

### ^[3. Right methods lead to right conclusions (Wed, Thu)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- There is a right way and wrong way to study the Bible (2 Tim. 2:15; Lk. 10:25-28).
	- Ellen White had encouraged believers to follow “Father Miller’s” “simple but intelligent and important rules for Bible study and interpretation” –
		- “1. Every word must have its proper bearing on the subject presented in the Bible; 2. All Scripture is necessary, and may be understood by diligent application and study; 3. Nothing revealed in Scripture can or will be hid from those who ask in faith, not wavering; 4. To understand doctrine, bring all the scriptures together on the subject you wish to know, then let every word have its proper influence; and if you can form your theory without a contradiction, you cannot be in error; 5. Scripture must be its own expositor, since it is a rule of itself.” A portion of Miller’s rules shared by Ellen White in the Review and Herald, Nov. 25, 1884
		- “By following the simple rule that the Bible must be allowed to define its own terms, most of the mystery behind prophetic symbolism simply disappears.” Qtly, Thu, par 2
	- ^[The language of the Bible should be explained according to its obvious meaning, unless a symbol or figure is employed. GC 599.1]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})
