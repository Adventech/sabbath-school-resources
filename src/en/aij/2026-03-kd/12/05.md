“Zacchaeus, . . . come down, for today I must stay at your house,” Jesus said, smiling up into the tree (Luke 19:5).

Zacchaeus looked down. _Was he dreaming? Was Jesus really talking to him?_

It seemed so hard to believe.

Coming down from the tree, Zacchaeus gazed into the loving face of Jesus. He didn’t quite know what to say. He didn’t deserve this kindness from Jesus.

_Jesus knew his name! Jesus loved and cared for him and wanted to visit his house!_

The love and forgiveness of Jesus was changing the old lying Zacchaeus. What can Jesus change us into? **Read 2 Corinthians 5:17.**

That’s just what Zacchaeus was now! Looking up at Jesus, he said, “I will give half of my money to the poor. If I have cheated anyone, I will pay that person back four times more!” (Luke 19:8, ICB).

Right away, immediately, this new, loving Zacchaeus began to make things right. He didn’t want to just give back the money he had taken. He wanted to give the poor people much more money than what he had stolen from them, to say he was sorry for hurting them.

When we let Jesus change us, we will want to make our wrongs right too. We will want to be as loving and generous as He.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of a time you did something wrong? How did you make it right?
- Is there anything you would like to ask God to forgive you for?
- Ask Jesus to help you make any wrongs right with others.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Find six items of the same thing: bottle tops, pencils, toys. Make two piles: a pile for a worker, with two items; a pile for Zacchaeus, with four items. Pretend Zacchaeus took one item. How many would the worker have left? When Jesus changed Zacchaeus, he didn’t just give back the one item. He gave back four! How many items would the worker have now?
