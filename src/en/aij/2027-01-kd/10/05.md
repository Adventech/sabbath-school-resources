^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever told a lie? How did it make you and others feel?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you speak only honest words.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask a parent or caregiver to pour honey into your hand. Try to “hide” the honey in your hand. How does it feel? This is just like lying. At first it might seem like a “sweet” idea to lie, but as time goes on, it gets more uncomfortable. Eventually we just want to be clean. Jesus is the only one who can clean our hearts when we ask for forgiveness and own up to our lies.
