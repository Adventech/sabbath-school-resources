---
title: Some Be’s to Remember
---

- Be early. You should be the first ones to Sabbath School if your work is to be successful.
- Be pleasant and cheerful. “A true Christian constantly acknowledges Christ. He is always cheerful, always ready to speak words of hope and comfort to the suffering.”<sup>5</sup>
- Be courteous. “A kind, courteous Christian is the most powerful argument that can be produced in favor of Christianity.”<sup>6</sup>
- Be an example. “There is nothing that the Saviour desires so much as agents who will represent to the world His Spirit and His character.”<sup>7</sup>
- Be reverent. A cheerful, reverent dignity bears a strong influ ence. True reverence is not cold and cheerless. We influence others more by what we are and do than by what we say. Christ’s follow ers are admonished to “guard against the tendency to lose the spirit of reverence and godly fear.”<sup>8</sup>
- Be alert. Your work will present many opportunities. Watch for them. “We are to cultivate tact and sharp discernment, to be quick to see opportunities to do good, and to seize these opportunities and make the most of them.”<sup>9</sup>
- Be diligent. “Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might” (Eccles. 9:10). “Whatever you do, do all to the glory of God” (1 Cor. 10:31).

A valuable publication on this topic is _How to Say Hello Without Saying Goodbye_, by James Zackrison, available from AdventSource.
