Sing

Sing the story to the tune of “Row, Row Your Boat.”

Float, float, went the ark,\
High upon the sea.\
Flying, flying, went the dove,\
Looking for a tree.

Float, float, went the ark,\
High upon the sea.\
Back came the flying dove\
With an olive leaf!

Explore

Go for a little hike/walk and see if you can find a bird’s nest up in the trees. You might like to also make your own nest using bendy twigs, vines, bark, and/or dry grass. Simply bend the twigs and loop them around as though you were tying a knot. Continue winding twigs around until you get a nice nest shape, then fill with bark or grass. Talk about how Noah’s dove was able to find all these things to build her new home when the floodwaters went down.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#35b668"}}}
Create

Make a flying dove puppet. Fold a letter-size [A4] piece of thin white cardboard vertically in half. From the fold line, draw and cut a semicircle for the body and a wing shape for the wings. Then draw and cut a circle for the head, a small triangle for the beak and a tail. Glue the pieces together to make the dove. Then stick a bamboo skewer in the middle of the two semicircle flaps of the body so it is secure. Using a paper straw or thinly rolled piece of cardboard, cut two slits about a third of the way down to make two flaps. Slide it on to the skewer, fold out the flaps and glue them to the underside of each wing. You might like to glue a green olive twig to the beak too. Holding the bamboo stick, slide the paper straw up and down to make the dove fly!

Pray

Share how God was faithfully watching over the ark. He knew what was best. He would keep everyone safe in the ark until it was time to make a new home. He even cared about making a home for the little birds! He cares about you. Name each person in the family and thank God for caring for each of you. “Thank You, God, for caring for Daddy . . .”
