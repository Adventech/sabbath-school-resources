**Word of the Week**

**Grow:** To grow is to get bigger and stronger. When you are a child, your body grows a little taller each day. People can also grow to be more loving and kind. When Jesus lives in your heart, you can grow to be more like Him.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus grew up with His family in Nazareth. He learned how to help Joseph in his carpenter’s shop, and He learned how to help Mary in the home. He went to the temple for a big feast and learned what it meant to be the Messiah. He always chose to be loving, cheerful, and obedient so that He could show others how much God loved them too.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus grew up to be kind and loving and always did His best.
- **Feel** happy that they can also choose to be kind and loving like Jesus, with His help.
- **Respond** by wanting to be kind, loving, and obedient, like Jesus.
