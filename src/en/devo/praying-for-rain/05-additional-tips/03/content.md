---
title: Let the Holy Spirit Lead
---

The prayer leader naturally feels the responsibility to lead and keep things moving. However, as already stated, each prayer session is a group conversation with God, and as such, we must expect the occasional lull in conversation. These periods of silence may feel awkward, but they are not to be feared.

The Holy Spirit uses these times to speak to hearts. Also, it may be during these moments that the more timid find the opportunity to pray. As a leader, resist the urge to dominate by jumping in immediately to keep the prayer time moving. Allow the Holy Spirit to work and dictate the pace.
