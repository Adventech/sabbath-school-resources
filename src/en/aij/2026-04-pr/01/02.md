The afternoon sun began to fade as the day by the lake neared its end. Jesus was exhausted. He’d hardly had a moment to rest or to eat. He knew the listening crowds weren’t going to go away, so He needed to go away instead. Where did Jesus decide to go? **Read Luke 8:22.**

It was peaceful on the other side of the lake, and there wouldn’t be jostling crowds waiting for Jesus. The towns along the eastern shore were quiet and small, making it the perfect place to rest and pray.

Jesus said goodbye to the crowds, stepped into the boat with His disciples, and quickly set off. Others climbed into their fishing boats and followed Him.

The sun set and the stars began to twinkle overhead. The tired Teacher sat in the back of the little boat as it rocked gently on the water. He rested His head, closed His eyes, and fell fast asleep.

Before we close our eyes and fall asleep each night, we can pray one of God’s beautiful promises, Psalm 4:8. “I will both lie down in peace, and sleep; for You alone, O Lord, make me dwell in safety.” Whether we’re in a comfy bed, a tent, a car, a hammock, or a bobbing boat, we can always ask God for a peaceful sleep.

As Jesus continued to sleep peacefully in the back of the boat, the disciples enjoyed their evening away from the crowds. Jesus and His disciples had done the work God had called them to do, and now they were enjoying the peaceful rest God had given them.

Jesus understands our tiredness after times of busyness and serving others. He knows that we need peaceful times away from lots of people so that we will have the energy we need to be able to help others again. He says, “Come to me, . . . and I will give you rest” (Matthew 11:28).

^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Your parents sometimes get very tired at the end of a busy day. Next time you notice how tired they are, offer to help them with a job (or do it for them) so that they can have some time of peaceful rest. Pray that God will fill them up with new energy!

- Where do you feel at peace after a busy time?
