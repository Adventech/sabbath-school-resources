Joseph could hardly believe his ears. Pharaoh, King of Egypt, wanted to see him!

His mind swirled with questions as the soldiers rushed him from the prison toward the king’s palace. What a surprising day! A servant gave him a haircut and some new clean clothes. Then as quick as a wink, Joseph, the slave and prisoner, was standing before the most important man in Egypt.

The king looked worried. He’d had two very strange dreams and no one could tell him what they meant. Could his butler’s friend from prison help? What did Pharaoh say to Joseph? **Read Genesis 41:15.**

Joseph quickly shook his head. He said to the king, “I am not able to explain the meaning of dreams. God will do this for the king” (Genesis 41:16, ICB).

Joseph wanted everyone, even Pharaoh, to know his amazing God. He believed what it says in Psalm 86:10, “For You are great, and do wondrous things; You alone are God.”

Joseph listened quietly as Pharaoh told him his dreams. Then God gave him the meaning. Joseph said, “Both of these dreams mean the same thing. . . . God is showing the king what He is about to do. You will have seven years of good crops and plenty to eat in all the land of Egypt. But after those seven years, there will come seven years of hunger” (Genesis 41:25, 28-30, ICB).

God had helped Joseph explain the dream. What an amazing God He is!
