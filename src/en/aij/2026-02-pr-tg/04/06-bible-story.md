**Big Idea:** Because God forgives me, I can learn to forgive others.

**TELL THE STORY**

Jacob’s face lit up with a smile as he heard the shepherd’s words. He was in Haran! After a long, long walk he’d finally made it! Jacob quickly asked about his Uncle Laban—did the shepherds know him? Yes! The shepherds pointed to a young woman on her way to the well. “Look, his daughter Rachel is coming now with his sheep,” they said (Genesis 29:6, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob rushed to the well and kindly fetched water for all Laban’s sheep. He hugged Rachel and cried happy tears. He was safe. God had led him to his family!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Laban welcomed Jacob into his home with a big hug too. Right away Jacob started helping Laban with his sheep. Laban soon noticed that his nephew was a hard worker, and he asked Jacob to become one of his paid workers.

“What would you like me to pay you?” he asked Jacob (Genesis 29:15, ICB).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Jacob didn’t want money—what did he want? Read Genesis 29:18.** ^[(To work for seven years to marry Rachel.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#000000"}}}) ^[(Hold up paper heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Laban nodded. What a good deal! Jacob worked very hard and was happy to do his best.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally the seven years were over, and it was the night of the wedding. Jacob was so excited to finally marry Rachel! Late in the evening Laban brought the bride to Jacob. She was wearing a heavy veil over her face ^[(cloth over face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). A happy Jacob led her through the darkness to his tent.

In the morning Jacob opened his eyes and gasped in surprise. It wasn’t Rachel lying beside him—it was her older sister, Leah! Laban had tricked him into marrying the wrong woman! ^[(Tear heart in two.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jacob was so upset. Laban didn’t even say he was sorry! Laban laid out a new mean plan: he would let Jacob also marry Rachel a week later, if he would agree to work for free for another seven years. It wasn’t fair, but Jacob agreed.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**PAUSE: Had Jacob ever done anything this sneaky and deceitful?** (Yes!) **What?** (He pretended to be his brother to get the birthright blessing from his father.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How do you think he felt about his birthright trick now?** (Very, very sorry!)

Even though he had been lied to and tricked, Jacob began working hard and well for Laban for another seven years. Sadly Jacob’s life at home wasn’t as happy. His two wives, Rachel and Leah, jealously fought for his love. In the Garden of Eden, God had given Adam only one wife, and He never intended for men to ever have more than one.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Do you think he had changed since he tricked his family?** (Yes.) **What do you think changed him?** (God’s forgiveness. Clue: ladder dream.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had great plans for Jacob. He blessed him with a big family, just as He’d promised. But after 14 years of working for sneaky Laban, Jacob was ready to go home. Laban didn’t want to lose his best worker, so he offered to pay Jacob whatever he liked.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What wages did Jacob ask for? Read Genesis 30:31-33.** (All the speckled and spotted sheep and goats, and all the brown lambs.) ^[(Show craft sample.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Laban smiled and agreed. Then he snuck off and took most of the speckled and spotted animals from his flock and hid them.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But Laban was no match for God. God helped Jacob’s flock of speckled and spotted animals grow bigger and stronger. Laban changed the rules of their agreement 10 times to try to cheat Jacob, but God continued to give Jacob good things.

After 20 years God told Jacob to go back to the land of his family. “I will be with you,” God promised (Genesis 31:3, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob and his family quickly packed up everything and left for Canaan while Laban was away.

Three days later Laban found out, and he was furious! He, his sons, and a band of fighting men went on the chase to force them to return. But God spoke to Laban in a dream. He said “Be careful that you speak to Jacob neither good nor bad” (Genesis 31:24). God was with Jacob, keeping him safe.

When Laban caught up with Jacob, he kept his red-hot anger from bubbling over. He told him about the dream from God and suggested they make an agreement together, then go their different ways. They promised to always have peace between their families.
