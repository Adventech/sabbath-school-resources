**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Jesus understands every emotion we have ever felt. He was separated from heaven and rejected by His own creation, feeling suffering and pain more deeply than we’ll ever know.

Jesus was a stranger on our earth, with very few possessions and no place to rest His head, so He knows what it’s like to feel alone.

Jesus is part of the Godhead and knows the joy of deep connection with both His Father and the Holy Spirit, but also with the angels and His creation. He understands joy, peace, and love to a degree that we can only imagine.

It’s so comforting to know that Jesus understands exactly what we feel, and so much more. He understood the sadness in the mother’s heart in this week’s story, and He loved to give her joy. He is intensely interested in the details of our lives and notices both the big and small moments in our days. No matter how we feel or what we experience, He wants to be part of our days. He says to us, “Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. . . . you will find rest for your souls” (Matthew 11:28, 29).

“His heart of love is touched by our sorrows and even by our utterances of them. . . . Nothing that in any way concerns our peace is too small for Him to notice. . . . No calamity can befall the least of His children . . . of which our heavenly Father is unobservant, or in which He takes no immediate interest” (Ellen G. White, _Steps to Christ_, p. 100).

It’s so comforting to know that Jesus cares and understands and that He loves to give us joy. May you and the children know this personally as you explore this story together this week.
