**Focus:** God cared for Julius as a sick baby and boy, and He cares for us too.

**Location:** Help a child locate **New Caledonia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

- **What do you do in the morning to get ready for the day?**

Mommy woke up her son Julius very early each morning. “Julius, you have to go to school,” she said. “Get ready.” Julius got up, but then went outside to play with the dogs. “Julius, come back in! Did you forget you have school today?” Mommy called.

Julius felt bad for not listening. He put a pot of water on the outdoor fire to heat up for his bath. Then he quickly washed and dressed for school. Mommy reminded him to put on his glasses, to place hearing aids in his ears, and take his school bag. Then Mommy reminded him to eat his yummy porridge breakfast.

Mommy always prayed for Julius and his little brother before school. She prayed an extra-special prayer for Julius, because at school some of the other children were mean to him. Usually he played alone.

Mommy knew that God cared very much for Julius. When he was born, he didn’t breathe, and his heart didn’t beat. Doctors somehow started his heart, but he was still very sick for two and a half weeks. The doctors didn’t think he would get better, but he did! Today Julius is happy and healthy. He just needs constant reminders.

Mommy noticed that often Julius didn’t think about helping others. He was selfish. Mommy told him that Jesus always shares with us. She started praying about this too. “Dear Jesus,” she prayed, “please help Julius to have a sharing heart.”

A few weeks passed. One morning Mommy heard Julius call out to his little brother. “Come take a bath,” he said. “I have heated a pot of water for you.” When Mommy reminded Julius about breakfast, he obeyed. He also washed his little brother’s cup and filled it with yummy porridge too! Mommy was amazed! God had answered her prayer. He had given Julius a beautiful, sharing heart!

Julius now wants to share Jesus with everyone. He wants to be a preacher.

- **Why do you think Julius changed from the beginning of the story to the end?**
- **Who cared for Julius? How do you know?** (God did, and his mommy did. She prayed, and God answered.)

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is with me. He cares for me.**
