---
title: Cliff-Hanger
date: 24/10/2026
---

If you had to hang from a rope over a frighteningly high cliff and were given a choice between two ropes—a thin, frayed one or a scientifically engineered, strong one—which rope would you place more faith in? It’s scary either way! But I think you would choose the strong rope.

And would you rather step off the cliff with a lot of faith in the thin rope or a little faith in the strong rope?

Ultimately, it wouldn’t matter how much faith you had because your safety doesn’t rely on your level of faith; it relies on the strength of the rope. Faith is only as good as the object or person that we have faith in.

God gives us skills and talents and encourages us to use them wisely, but never to hang our whole weight on them or even have faith in them.

Our well-honed skills, proven strategies, time-tested programs, and maybe even our track record of successes are a great blessing, but God calls us to have faith in Him, not our abilities. Compared to God’s strength and support, even at their best, our skills and talents are just a thin little rope.

What sort of rope have you been placing your faith in this week?

### Appeal

It’s now time to return God’s tithe and give Him our regular and systematic Promise offerings. What a privilege it is to know our awesome God and believe that whatever our situation is when we have faith in Him, He will save us and hold us tight.
