Soldiers raced through the town of Bethlehem on their galloping horses. They banged on every door, searching, searching everywhere. King Herod was furious! He had ordered his soldiers to find the baby King. The soldiers looked in every home, searching for Jesus, who would be “two years old and under” (Matthew 2:16).

But Baby Jesus had already left Bethlehem. He was safely on the road with his parents, traveling to faraway Egypt. Isn’t it amazing that God knew Herod’s evil plan even before he did it? That is because God knew Herod’s thoughts. God sees and knows everything.

Jesus wasn’t alone on the trip. He had His parents and someone even stronger with Him. Who else was there? **Read Psalm 34:7.**

God was doing everything needed to save Jesus’ life so that one day Jesus could grow up to save you!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of something that God knows and that you don’t know?
- How is the verse in today’s lesson (Psalm 34:7) like your memory verse?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that because He knows everything, He can keep us safe from danger.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw a picture of something on a piece of paper while your family closes their eyes. Fold the picture up and ask each family member to guess what is drawn. We don’t know everything, but God does! God knew exactly what you were going to draw even before you drew it.
