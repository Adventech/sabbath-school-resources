The butler stood perfectly still and waited. Finally Pharaoh cleared his throat. Then looking at the butler, he gave a tiny nod. Quickly and carefully the butler carried the king’s cup and jug to his side. Then he poured the drink without spilling a drop. He was so happy to be back in the palace, serving the king!

Joseph’s God had told him the meaning of his dream. And it had come true! The butler had bowed and thanked Joseph, over and over.

Before he left the prison, Joseph had said to him, “When you are free, remember me. Be kind to me. Tell the king about me so that I can get out of this prison. . . . I have done nothing here to deserve being put in prison” (Genesis 40:14,15, ICB).

Joseph waited for good news from the butler—surely he would be free soon too! He waited . . . and waited . . . and waited. For two whole years Joseph waited. What a long time to be waiting!

What had happened to the butler? Read Genesis 40:23.

Back in the beautiful palace, the butler had forgotten him. He had forgotten that Joseph had helped him and was still stuck in prison.

But God hadn’t forgotten Joseph!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Joseph had to learn patience in prison. How patient are you?
- Who can help you become more patient?
