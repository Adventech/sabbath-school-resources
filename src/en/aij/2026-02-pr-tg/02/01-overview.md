**Word of the Week**

**Truthful:** Telling the truth and being honest in the way we speak and act.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Isaac’s family lied and deceived others to gain what they wanted instead of trusting in God. In his old age, Isaac decided to give his older son, Esau, the birthright blessing, despite God’s promise that it would be for Jacob. Rebekah and Jacob lied with their words and actions and tricked Isaac into giving the blessing to Jacob instead. Their lies hurt themselves and each other, as well as their family members.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God wants His children to be truthful.
- **Feel** a desire to always be truthful.
- **Respond** by being honest and truthful.
