**Focus:** God gives us courage and helps us to be faithful to Him.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Kyrgyzstan** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Kamila loved God and loved spending time with Him, especially on His holy Sabbath. But she had a big problem when she started eighth grade in Bishkek, the capital of Kyrgyzstan. The public school required that all eighth graders study six days a week—from Monday through Saturday.

Kamila and her mother went to the pastor of their Seventh-day Adventist church to ask for help. The pastor prayed about it. Then he wrote a letter to Kamila’s school, asking that she be excused on Saturdays.

Kamila and her mother took the letter to the principal. “Other Christians go to church on Sunday, and your daughter can go to church on Sunday too,” she said to Mother. “Then she can study on Saturday. We cannot excuse her.”

Kamila didn’t go to school the next Sabbath. Instead, she went to church with Mother. She had decided to be faithful to God no matter what. At church Mother saw the principal of the Seventh-day Adventist boarding school in Tokmok, located about two hours away by car.

Mother explained the school situation to the principal and asked whether Kamila could go to the Adventist school. The principal prayed for Kamila. She asked God to find someone to help pay Kamila’s school fees—it was too much for Mother.

“I’ll tell you next Sabbath whether I’ve found someone,” the principal said.

All week Kamila prayed for God to find someone to help.

The next Sabbath at church, the principal greeted her with a big smile. “God has blessed you!” she said. “We have found someone.”

“Thank You so much!” Kamila prayed to her faithful Friend.
