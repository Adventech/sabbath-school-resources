Hundreds of eager people headed toward the centurion’s house with Jesus. Such a big crowd couldn’t move very fast, so news that Jesus was on His way reached the Roman officer long before the Savior did.

The centurion didn’t think he was worthy to have Jesus come to his house and sent friends to Jesus to tell Him so. But he did not need to send friends. Jesus loved him. Who does the Bible tell us we are when we love God? **Read Luke 7:6.**

Jesus heard the message, but He kept walking anyway. When the centurion realized that Jesus was getting closer and closer, he finally found the courage to leave his house and ran to meet Him on the road.

The humble Roman officer greeted Jesus respectfully and said, “Lord, I am not worthy that You should come under my roof. But only speak a word, and my servant will be healed. For I also am a man under authority, having soldiers under me. And I say to this one, ‘Go,’ and he goes; and to another, ‘Come,’ and he comes; and to my servant, ‘Do this,’ and he does it.” (Matthew 8:9).

Just as the Roman government had authority over him and he had authority over his soldiers, he believed God had authority over Jesus, and Jesus had authority over things on earth.

When Jesus heard those words, He was amazed. Here was a Roman who believed that Jesus’ words had so much power that He didn’t even need to see or touch a person to heal them!

Now, that was great faith—faith that warmed the heart of Jesus.
