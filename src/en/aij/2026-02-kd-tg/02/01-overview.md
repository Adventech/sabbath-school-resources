**Word of the Week**

**Honest:** Being honest means telling the truth. God is always honest; He never lies. When God lives in your heart, you can speak honest words and not lie or cheat.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

In his old age Isaac prepared to give the birthright blessing to his older son, Esau. God had told Rebekah that Jacob, the younger, would be the leader. But Isaac chose to do things his own way. Rebekah heard Isaac’s plan, and also decided to take things into her own hands. She developed a sneaky plan to trick her blind husband into giving the blessing to Jacob instead. Jacob pretended to be Esau and lied through his words and actions. He deceived Isaac and received the blessing. Esau was devastated and planned to kill his brother. As a result Jacob had to leave the broken family and run away to live with Rebekah’s family.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that telling lies causes sadness and hurting hearts.
- **Feel** a desire to ask God to help them speak honest words and not lie.
- **Respond** by asking God to live in their hearts.
