Our heavenly Father works in wonderful ways to open doors to spread His message of love and faith around the world, and our offerings are shared to meet the needs of many new ministries.

But have you ever wondered what happens when carefully planned evangelistic meetings are successful way beyond expectations? Suddenly, existing churches overflow, and new churches must be built, rented, or purchased—fast. But that costs money beyond the planned amount the church had set aside before the evangelist meetings began. How can the church provide the local church buildings, Bibles, leadership training, and more so that new believers can grow spiritually?

God has impressed the church to make a way to prepare for the unexpected by establishing the Unusual Opportunities Fund, whose resources may be used when such unexpected blessings come to God’s people. If your territory has adopted the Combined Offering Plan, a portion of all your regular and systematic unassigned offerings will be allocated to the World Mission Unusual Opportunities Fund. But if, in addition to your tithes and Promise offerings, you want to do more for the mission of the church, you can give directly to this fund so that the church is ready when God says yes to our prayers. Together, we can make giant leaps for mission.

### Appeal

The Seventh-day Adventist Church’s system of tithes and offerings is a powerful way for us to unite in God’s worldwide mission. Alone, our offerings don’t go far, but together, we are changing the lives of millions of people. As you return your tithe and Promise offerings, let us put our desires last and God first.
