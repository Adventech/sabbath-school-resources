**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Beginnings are important. They say much about our identity. The beginning of our world tells us how precious we are to God—for all of creation was a vast, generous act of love. The whole of the Godhead planned the intricate details of our world with much delight, thought, and care. All was given in love, because of love.

This lesson begins to paint a picture of the startling details within this gift. We see color-filled light; clustered cloud droplets; vast open spaces bending in breezy waves; grand, soaring forests and deep, sparkling oceans; iridescent feathers; pulsing, living tissue; and rippling sunlight on water—the variety was astounding and the intricacy awe-inspiring. Not only was this to be for God’s “crowning work” of humanity (Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 44), but the earth itself and all that was created in those first days responded with joy in their existence. “In the work of Creation, when the dawn of the first day broke, and the heavens and the earth, by the call of infinite power, came out of darkness, responsive to the rising light, ‘the morning stars sang together, and all the sons of God shouted for joy’ ” (Ellen G. White, in _Signs of the Times_, January 8, 1880). This world was breathtaking and alive with praise. Even “the angelic host viewed the scene with delight, and rejoiced at the wonderful works of God” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 44).

Though now marred and flawed, nature still sings praises to God.

This week, may you join in praise to your God as you delight in His creation.
