**Word of the Week**

**Compassion:** Compassion is noticing when people need help or are hurt and then wanting to do something to make things better for them.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus knew that when people were treated with compassion or saw compassion in action, they were likely to become more compassionate people themselves. By healing the man with leprosy, the hard-of-hearing and speech-impaired man, and the blind man, Jesus showed He noticed the needs of individuals and His love and acceptance of everyone. He also showed compassion by gently teaching those who didn’t understand God’s love or the truth about sin with love and kindness.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God cares and has compassion for each of us.
- **Feel** loved and accepted by God.
- **Respond** by showing compassion to others who need help or are hurt.
