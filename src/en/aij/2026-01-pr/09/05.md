God longed for the tower builders to stop building and turn to Him, but some now believed they were more powerful than God. Others believed that He didn’t even exist! God knew that these sad lies would spread, and people’s hearts would become as hard as stone.

Out of love, God stepped in to stop the building Himself. He chose an interesting way to do it! What did God do? Read Genesis 11:7.

God and His team of angels went swiftly about their work to create total confusion. When someone asked for bricks, they may have been handed sticky tar. Another builder asking for a hammer might have been offered a shovel!

Everything was mixed up and messed up! Soon there was confusion and shouting, and angry fights up and down the tower. Some builders clenched their fists and kept on babbling, while others walked off the job. Just like that, work on the tower stopped completely.

Because of what happened, the city was called “Babel” (Genesis 11:9),
which sounds like the Hebrew word for “confusion” (balal), so they would always remember how God confused the languages there.

The Bible tells us in Proverbs 16:9, “A man’s heart plans his way, but the Lord directs his steps.” What the proud city dwellers thought was a clever plan was really very foolish. God needed to direct their steps and challenge their dependence on themselves before wickedness grew bigger than their tower.
