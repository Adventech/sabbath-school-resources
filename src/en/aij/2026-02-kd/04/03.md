As Jacob shut the gate to the sheep pen, his smile stretched wide. His seven years of working for Uncle Laban were finished! Running to see his uncle, he said excitedly, “Give me Rachel so that I may marry her. The time I promised to work for you is over” (Genesis 29:21, ICB).

Uncle Laban agreed. What did he do next? **Read Genesis 29:22.**

Jacob’s bride was brought to him wearing a beautiful long dress and a veil that covered her face completely. Jacob was so happy. Finally Rachel would be his wife!

As the sun rose the next morning, a sleepy Jacob slowly opened his eyes. He looked at his new wife to see her for the first time without her veil. His eyes opened wide in shock!

_How could this be? This isn’t Rachel! It’s her older sister Leah!_

Uncle Laban had tricked him. He had given Leah as the bride instead of Rachel. Jacob had married the wrong sister!

Jacob jumped out of bed and raced to Uncle Laban’s tent. Angrily he said, “What have you done to me? I worked hard for you so that I could marry Rachel! Why did you trick me?” (Genesis 29:25, ICB).

Poor Jacob! He now knew what it felt like to be lied to and tricked. It wasn’t a good feeling at all!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been tricked? How did you feel?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you to treat people how you would like to be treated.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Play dress up with your family or friends. Do your best to look completely different. Then tell everyone who you really are. Think about how important it is to be honest with our words and actions.
