Oh, how the angels could sing! Nothing brought the angels more joy than being before God’s throne, singing sweet songs to their Creator and King.

Even Lucifer would forget about himself for a while, swept up in joyful singing. But as soon as the music hushed, his selfish thoughts would return.

When they worshipped God’s Son, Lucifer became jealous. He thought to himself, _Why should God’s Son be more important than me?_

He was not content anymore. From his place beside God’s throne, Lucifer’s jealousy of God’s Son grew stronger and stronger. He thought: _Why can’t I sit on the throne with God? Why wasn’t I asked to help make decisions? I am smart and handsome. I am the leader of the angels. God isn’t being fair to me!_ What else did he think? **Read Isaiah 14:13, 14.**

If only Lucifer had talked to God about his bad thoughts and feelings. God always “takes care of all he has made” (Psalm 145:17, ICB). God would have happily and lovingly talked to Lucifer to change his doubts.

But instead Lucifer began to talk to the other angels. He even began to tell lies about God and His law. Oh, no! What bad, bad choices Lucifer was making.

God had always shown Lucifer lots of love. But now Lucifer thought only about himself. Lucifer had changed so much, but God stayed the same: always loving, always true.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you ever want something that someone else has? What can you do when you feel that way?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you talk to Him when you feel sad or angry and to help you to make good choices.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw a circle on a deflated balloon. Ask someone to slowly blow up the balloon. What happened to the size of the circle? How was Lucifer’s pride like the circle, and the balloon?
