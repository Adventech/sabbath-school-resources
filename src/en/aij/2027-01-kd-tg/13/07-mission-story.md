**Focus:** We can remember God’s good works by saying thank You.

**Location:** Invite a child to help you find the **Marshall Islands**, northeast of Papua New Guinea, on your **globe** or **world map**; also find **Texas, U.S.A.**

**What’s the first thing you want to do when you open a gift? Play with it? Put it somewhere special?**

The first thing Jaira does is write a thank-you letter. She started writing thank-you letters a few years ago, when a surprise box came in the mail. Her mother, father, and two older brothers were Filipino missionaries living in the Marshall Islands.

The box came from a kind man named Bob, who lived in the state of Texas in America. Bob had read about their family’s mission work and wanted to help. He always gave to the mission offerings during Sabbath School, but he wanted to do more. So he sent a box to the missionary family.

Inside the box were new toys! A toy car for each of the boys, and a little doll, just for Jaira. The children wanted to play with them right away, but their father stopped them.

“We thank the Lord for these gifts, but at the same time we remember that the Lord gave them to us through this kind man,” he said. “You must first write a thank-you note to Bob.”

Jaira frowned. She was only 6 years old and didn’t know how to write yet. Then suddenly she had an idea. “I can draw a picture!” she said cheerfully. Jaira got a piece of paper and drew a big red heart. Then she drew smaller hearts around it. At the top she wrote her name. Now Bob would know that she was very thankful for the doll.

A month later Jaira was thrilled to find another box in the mail. Bob, who became known as Grandpa Bob by the family, sent Jaira’s family a box every month, year after year. Every time Jaira and her brothers opened a new box, they would first write a thank-you letter to Grandpa Bob.

God gives us so many blessings every month, year after year. Like Jaira, we can remember to thank God first, by saying a simple prayer or drawing a picture.
