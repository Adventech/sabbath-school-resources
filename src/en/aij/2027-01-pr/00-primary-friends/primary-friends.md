This quarter you’ll learn about many more exciting Bible stories!

Did you know that the same God who did these great miracles is the same God you love and pray to, today? You may not see great miracles every day, but God is still just as powerful. He also still speaks to His people—you!—although it’s usually through your Bible rather than face-to-face as He spoke to Moses. And God still promises to be with you and lead you, as He led His Israelite people all those years ago.

Yes, God is with you today, and He promises to be with you tomorrow and forever as you put your hand in His. He is such a good, kind, faithful God, and He loves you more than you may realize.

I hope you’ll see Him clearly this quarter and love Him back even more than you do today.
