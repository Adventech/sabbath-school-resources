**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**.

- **Who likes gifts?**
- **When do you sometimes give or receive gifts?** (Birthdays, Christmas, weddings, or other occasions in your culture when gifts are given.)

**Share:** Our Secret Surprise Box is hiding somewhere in our room, and today it looks like a gift. Let’s all look for it.

Give children a few minutes to find the **Secret Surprise Box**.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **I wonder what kind of gift do you think is inside? Let me give you a clue: It’s for a wedding.**
- **What kinds of gifts do we give people at a wedding?** (Something for their new home.)

Invite the children to open the gift to reveal some **paper or plastic cups** (enough for each child).

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In today’s story Jesus went to a wedding, and there was a big problem. Jesus gave the couple a very special gift, and it solved the problem. These cups are a clue to help us find out more about the special gift from Jesus.

Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He helps me with any problems.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story, **“The Wedding Surprise,”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the storytelling suggestions and/or the Program PowerPoint.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a set of blocks.

**Share:** In our story today, the people at the wedding had a big problem that seemed impossible.

- **What are some problems we face that seem hard or sometimes even impossible?** (Cleaning up a big mess, doing my chores cheerfully, sharing with siblings, etc.)

Begin to build a small tower with the blocks. As each problem is mentioned, add another block to the tower.

**Share:** This tower we’ve built is a small one. Sometimes our problems might be small, but other times our problems might feel very big—like an enormous tower (stretch up tall with arms high). They can seem too hard for us to solve. But no problem is too big or too small for Jesus. He will always find a way to knock down the problems to help us!

Ask a volunteer to come and knock down the tower.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He helps me with any problems.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})**

- **What kind of fruit reminds you of our story today?** (Grapes.)

Hold up **Nature Card 35: Grapes** and share the following facts:

- Grapes are a type of fruit that grow in clusters of 15 to 300.
- They can be red, black, dark blue, yellow, green—or even orange or pink!
- They are juicy and sweet. They are made up of 81 percent water.
- Some kinds are eaten raw. Other kinds are used for making grape juice.
- Grapes are mentioned many times in the Bible, in both the Old Testament and the New Testament.
- Can you think of other stories in the Bible that mention grapes? (For example, the spies coming back from Canaan, the Last Supper.)

Give each child **Nature Card 35: Grapes** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
