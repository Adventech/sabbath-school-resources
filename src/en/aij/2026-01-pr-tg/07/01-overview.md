**Word of the Week**

**Faithful:** Being committed and loyal to someone, no matter what.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores faithfulness: God’s faithfulness; and the faithful response of Enoch and Noah. Spending time with God taught Enoch and Noah to trust Him. God called Noah to build an ark to save people from a coming worldwide flood. Noah faithfully obeyed, built, and preached. The flood came as promised. God provided safety for people, but only Noah and his family went inside—and a parade of animals, as promised.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** God loves the world and its people.
- **Feel** grateful that God is faithful and always makes a way for us to be saved.
- **Respond** by choosing to spend time with God and follow His voice.
