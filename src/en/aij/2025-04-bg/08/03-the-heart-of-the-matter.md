God has given you the most important role there is—that of a God-loving parent. It can be easy to lose sight of that amidst the chaos, chatter, and delightful mess of parenting. But it is an opportunity not to miss. He wants you to serve Him faithfully by bringing up your children to love and honor Him. Consider:

- Investing your time and love to your children through play, reading, and talking
- Showing them God’s unconditional love, even when they are naughty
- Being intentional in developing Christlike qualities in your children
- Sharing God’s Word with your child
- Showing patience in chaotic moments
- Discipling your child in a firm, consistent, kind way
- Modeling active service to others, and involving the family in helping others
- Bringing things of God into daily conversations

Slow down. Forget the jobs and the to do lists. Focus on the importance of serving God faithfully in this wonderful job He has given you. As you slow down, you will become more aware of the needs of others. Pause throughout the day and listen to God’s gentle promptings. Bask in the blessings He has given you. Serving God is such an honor. “Everyone who accepts Christ as his personal Saviour will long for the privilege of serving God” (Ellen G. White, _The Ministry of Healing_, p. 502). As you attend to these things, you will be focusing on eternity.

What a privilege to serve God by bringing up our children to know and follow Him. Like Joshua, we can claim, “But as for me and my house, we will serve the Lord” (Joshua 24:15).
