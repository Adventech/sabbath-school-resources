**Option 2: Craft Stick Ark**

**What you need:**

A piece of thick cardboard, 50-60 craft sticks, craft glue, scissors.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/07/7b1.png)

**Instructions:**

- Glue one craft stick onto the cardboard as shown.
- Arrange the next two craft sticks (overlapping each other) and glue.
- Continue until all six are in place. This will form the base of the boat.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/07/7b2.png)

- Dot each end of the craft sticks with glue and carefully layer with more craft sticks.
- Build your ark 10-15 craft sticks high, making sure to overlap each time, so there is a slight gap between each craft stick above and below.
- Once dry, cut the excess cardboard off around the base and enjoy playing with your ark!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/07/7b3.png)
