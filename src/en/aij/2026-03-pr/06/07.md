Celebrate Sabbath in a special way to suit your family and climate this week.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#534195", "size": "xl"}}}
Walk

If you live near a body of water, go for a walk there. Sit down to rest and imagine Jesus getting baptized by John in the Jordan River. Thank God for preparing your heart to be baptized.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#534195", "size": "xl"}}}
Craft Activity: Make a Dove Mobile

1. Draw a simple dove shape on a piece of cardstock and cut it out.
2. Trace around it 11 times and cut out the doves. (You will have 12 doves in all.)
3. Write “[Your name] is My beloved [son/daughter]; in You I am well pleased” (Luke 3:22).
4. Attach the doves to strings and tie the strings to a coat hanger.
5. Hang the mobile in your room to remind you of this beautiful truth.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#534195", "size": "xl"}}}
Object Lesson: Jesus Forgives Our Sins When We Repent

You will need: a pen or water-soluble marker; a permanent marker.

Do: Write several mistakes you have recently made on a piece of paper using the water-soluble markers. Put the paper into some water and watch the words disappear. Think about how Jesus will forgive your mistakes whenever you ask Him.

Use a permanent marker to write “Jesus loves me” on another piece of paper. Immerse it in the water. Think about how Jesus’ love for you can never be washed away.
