Out in the field, nobody saw the terrible thing Cain had done to his brother, but God knew. How did God know? Read Psalm 139:1-3.

God had seen what was in Cain’s heart, and He saw what Cain had done. But God’s love could easily grow in Cain’s heart—if only he would let it. So God gave Cain a chance.

God’s kind voice rang out to Cain, “Where is Abel your brother?”

Cain angrily replied, “I do not know. Am I my brother’s keeper?” (Genesis 4:9).

God was sadly disappointed. Cain had now added lying to his string of bad choices. Cain didn’t want a new heart; he’d chosen to keep his selfish one instead.

Because Cain wouldn’t admit what he’d done and say he was sorry, there would now be very sad consequences. First, God told Cain he would have to work much harder to grow food in the ground, because he had killed his brother there.

But even sadder, God had to send him away from his home and family to wander around the earth. Adam and Eve wept as they watched their son walk away. No Abel, and now no Cain. Their hearts and their home felt so empty. Like a dark cave filled with gloom.

Only God’s love could fill the gap their precious sons had left.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _If you could have comforted Adam and Eve, what would you have said and done?_
- _How does it make you feel to know that God knows everything?_

Because God sees and knows everything, He knows how to help and encourage us to choose Him and His way, even after we have done something wrong.
