Lucifer had a very important job to do. He was one of the cherubim angels that stood next to God and covered Him with his wings. Not only did he stand right next to God the Father, but the Bible says that he was the leader of all the other angels! (Ezekiel 28:14).

He was beautiful too. Lucifer means “shining one.” The Bible says Lucifer shone as if covered by all kinds of precious stones, such as diamonds, emeralds, and sapphires. He sparkled as he stood next to God, with the light from God’s throne shining on him. What else was Lucifer like? Read Ezekiel 28:12.

Lucifer was also the leader of the heavenly choir. Every day his beautiful voice led the worship songs, praising God. The angels gathered close as they listened to Lucifer sing with such loveliness. They burst into songs of praise to God and His Son too. How beautiful their songs must have sounded. They sang out loud words such as: “Give thanks to the God of heaven. His love continues forever” (Psalm 136:26, ICB). Nothing brings greater contentment than having God’s joy in your heart!

God the Father, His Son, and the Holy Spirit all loved the angels. In fact, they had so much love to give that they decided to make a new world. They talked and planned all the things they could create. It would be amazing!
