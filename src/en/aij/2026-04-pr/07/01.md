Jesus, a Friend to All

The busy town of Capernaum buzzed with excitement! Someone had spotted a fishing boat making its way across the Sea of Galilee. Not just any fishing boat; this one had Jesus on board! The last time Jesus was here, He had healed many people, including Peter’s mother-in-law. Here He was again, returning to Capernaum from the other side of the lake.

As the wonderful news traveled up and down the streets, people stopped what they were doing and rushed down to the shore to welcome Jesus. When the little fishing boat landed on the shore, a huge crowd greeted Him with joy. Jesus stayed there on the shore for a while, healing and teaching as He always loved to do. With kindness He encouraged the people to believe in God’s love.

Then Jesus made His way to Matthew’s house, for He had been invited to a feast with the town’s tax collectors. Matthew had been a tax collector before Jesus had said to him, “Follow me” (Matthew 9:9). The Pharisees were disgusted that Jesus would eat with tax collectors because they were considered thieving sinners (Matthew 9:11). What did Jesus think about sinners? **Read Luke 19:10.**

Jesus made the most of the moment to show the tax collectors God’s love. He wanted to encourage Matthew’s tax collector friends so that maybe they would choose to follow Him too.

No matter where Jesus was—in a boat, by the sea, in a town or in a person’s home, He always tried to encourage people to follow Him and believe in His Father, God. Jesus loves to encourage us, too, through His words in the Bible. The Lord says, “I have called you by name, and you are mine. . . . You are precious to me. I give you honor, and I love you” (Isaiah 43:1-4, ICB).
