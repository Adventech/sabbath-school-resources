**Location:** Help a child locate **southern India** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Seven-year-old Lakshmi [LAHK-shmee] and her mommy were searching in the forest near their home for firewood to sell in the marketplace. As her mommy stooped to pick up a piece of wood, she saw an anthill. She knew it was the home of a king cobra snake, a deadly snake who she worshipped as a god. Mommy began praying softly to the snake. While she was still praying, a king cobra slithered out of the anthill and bit her leg hard. Strong poison went into her leg. Mommy screamed.

Lakshmi came running and saw the snake on her mother’s leg. She didn’t know what to do, but she remembered that her teachers at her Christian school encouraged the children to pray to Jesus whenever they needed help. “Mommy!” Lakshmi cried. “Pray! Say, ‘Jesus, save me!’”

Mommy stopped screaming and said, “Jesus, save me! Jesus, save me!” The snake disappeared into the anthill. Mother tore a strip of cloth off her sari [dress] and tied it around her leg to help stop the poison from spreading. Then they both hurried home.

Mommy sat down and looked at her leg. She was so surprised that the bite mark on her leg wasn’t red or swollen. She had never heard of someone living after a king cobra’s bite before. It was impossible! Washing her wound, she sat down to rest.

“How did you know to ask Jesus to save me?” she asked Lakshmi.

“He is the God of the universe,” Lakshmi said, excitedly. “It’s good to worship God, who created our world, rather than created beings who live in this world.” Mommy nodded slowly. Lakshmi shared everything she could remember about Jesus as the two rested in the shade.

People soon found out about Lakshmi’s mommy’s snakebite. They were amazed that she wasn’t hurt. The teeth marks on Mommy’s leg were proof that her life had been saved by a God who was more powerful than any of the gods they had worshipped before.

Lakshmi taught her mommy to pray and read the Bible. Today she is God’s good friend.

- **What did Lakshmi’s mommy think was impossible?** (That she would live after a snakebite.)
- **Who made the impossible happen?** (God!)

Repeat **Big Idea: God is loving. He is always faithful.**
