**What you need:**

Week 4 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, glue, seven craft sticks, craft glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/4a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/2027-01-pr-craft-04.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color the craft template. Use red to color the top and sides of the door frame. Cut out the house template, including three sides of the door so it opens.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/4b.jpg)

2\. Fold along the lines and glue the tabs to each side to make a house.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/4c.jpg)

3\. Use craft glue to stick a row of craft sticks onto the roof of the house. Show your craft to a neighbor and tell them about the meaning of Passover. (4, 5)
