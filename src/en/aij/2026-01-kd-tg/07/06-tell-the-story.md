**Big Idea:** God is loving. I can be faithful to Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a group of sad and happy peg people in a sandbox.
- Use wooden blocks as cities and for boat building, toy boat.
- Shine a light on peg “Enoch.”
- Children use toy tools (such as hammer, saw, screwdriver, etc.) to help you build the “ark.”
- Use a peg “Noah” for his part in the story.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (week 7 Program PowerPoint).
- Use a felt set, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Seth and his children loved God. They were His faithful followers ^[(happy peg people in sandbox).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Seth’s family began mixing with Cain’s family, who were selfish. They stopped following God. Sin had spread everywhere—oh, no! ^[(Unhappy peg people in the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Enoch was God’s faithful friend. He spent lots of time with God and showed others God’s love ^[(shine a flashlight onto “Enoch” peg person).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God took Enoch to heaven! ^[(Take “Enoch” out of the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Noah was also God’s faithful friend. ^[(Use a peg “Noah” to tell the story.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God would send a flood to clean the earth from sin. God told Noah to build an ark to save everyone!
- Noah and his sons began to build God’s huge boat—with places for people and animals. ^[(Invite children to help make the “ark” using the toy tools and wooden blocks. Have them follow your instructions.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Noah told the people to get ready for the flood, but they didn’t believe him! ^[(Peg people, sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Noah faithfully built the ark and preached for 120 years. But nobody believed Noah. They laughed at him ^[(peg “Noah”).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The ark was finished! Only Noah and his family went into the ark. God would save Noah and his family, just as He had promised ^[(toy boat, sand box).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam and Eve’s son Seth and his children loved God. They were His faithful followers. ^[(Place the happy peg people into one side of a sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But not everyone loved and followed God’s ways. Cain’s family lived far away and had become very selfish. ^[(Place the sad peg people into the sandbox on the opposite side.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They stopped following God and instead did things their own way.

But one day Seth’s family began mixing with Cain’s family. ^[(Invite the children to start grouping together the peg people in the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After a while they stopped living God’s way. Sin had now spread everywhere! Oh, no!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But there was a faithful man named Enoch, who was best friends with God. He loved to spend quiet time alone with God. ^[(Invite children to close eyes and have a silent moment with God.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Everyone could tell he had been with God. ^[(Shine a light onto one of the peg people.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was so kind and loving.

For more than 300 years “Enoch walked with God” (Genesis 5:24, ICB). Then one day “Enoch was taken to heaven . . . because God had taken him away” (Hebrews 11:5, ICB). ^[(Take “Enoch” out of the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God was glad that He still had a faithful friend in the world. His name was Noah. ^[(Use a peg “Noah” to tell the story.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Like Enoch, Noah was best friends with God, and faithful to Him.

God told Noah about His plan to rescue the world from sin and make it clean and new. “I will bring a flood of water on the earth. I will destroy all living things” (Genesis 6:17, ICB). God asked His friend Noah to make a big boat called an ark! Even though the people in the world didn’t love God, God still loved them. He was faithful. He would invite everyone into the ark to be safe from the coming flood.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Bang, bang, bang! Saw, saw, saw!_ ^[(Invite children to help make the “ark” using the toy tools and wooden blocks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah and his three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, began to build God’s huge boat. Every day, except for Sabbath, Noah and his sons faithfully chopped, measured, sawed, and hammered. It was a big job! The ark had to be just the right length, three stories high, and have one door. Inside the boat there were to be lots of little rooms to carry lots of people and lots of animals. “Noah did everything that God commanded him” (Genesis 6:22, ICB). ^[(Tell children to hammer here or there and see if the children follow your instructions.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah, the faithful boat builder, also told the people that God was going to send a flood. _Water covering all the land in the whole world? No way!_ The people shook their heads and laughed at Noah.

For 120 years Noah and his family faithfully built the ark. For 120 years Noah faithfully preached to the people about the coming flood and about the safe place inside the boat. The people didn’t want to believe Noah.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally, the ark was finished. ^[(Place a toy or homemade boat inside the sand box.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One last time Noah begged the people to come inside the ark and be safe. But the people didn’t love God. They refused to listen. Instead, they just teased Noah and joked about the flood. They had made their choice.

The door of the ark was wide open. God called Noah and His family to come inside. God would save Noah and his family, just as He had promised. And God has also promised to save us from this sad, evil world. Soon Jesus will come back to take His faithful followers to heaven with Him. We can trust His promise!
