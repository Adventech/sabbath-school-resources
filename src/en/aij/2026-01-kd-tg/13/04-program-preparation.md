**Opening Activities**

Make simple lines in different directions on a table using **colored tape**.

Provide **straws** and **lightweight balls** (table tennis balls or pom-poms).

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: two balloons, thick sticky tape, and a safety pin.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: gray-colored fabric, walking stick, sheet to create a tent, small flashlights, bowl, picture of a starry sky, towel, cup, toy fruit, baby doll, peg people (optional).

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Bible marked at 2 Peter 3:13 with a **Promise Picture Card**. Write these three discussion questions on three separate pieces of paper:

- How did God help Abraham and Sarah wait patiently?
- How do you act when you have to wait patiently for something?
- How did God show that He was faithful in the story?

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
