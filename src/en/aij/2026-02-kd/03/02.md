Jacob dragged his sore, tired feet down the lonely road. His family was far, far behind him now. But his Uncle Laban’s tents, in Haran, were still a long way ahead.

As he walked, he felt worried. His father had given him the blessing from God, but did God still love him after everything he’d done? He’d lied straight to his father’s face! God had seen it all. He had heard it all. _Surely, God wouldn’t want me as His leader,_ he thought.

Even though he was scared to talk to God, Jacob stopped to pray. He cried as he said he was sorry to God for all his lies. He asked God to forgive him for the wrong things he’d done.

The Bible says, “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins” (1 John 1:9). That means if you are sorry for your mistakes and ask God to forgive you, He will!

As darkness fell, after a long day of walking, what did Jacob do? **Read Genesis 28:11.**

Imagine trying to sleep on that pillow! It wouldn’t be very comfortable. But Jacob was so tired, he didn’t care. Closing his eyes, he fell asleep.

God spoke to Jacob as he went to sleep on the cold, hard “pillow.” God had watched over him every step of the way.

He accepted Jacob’s prayer for forgiveness. God loved him very much, just as God loves and forgives you! And God planned to show Jacob something very special to prove it—that very night!

- What good things about God can you think of when you are sad?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that we can always ask Him to forgive us—and He always will!

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go outside with some chalk. On a rock draw a mark for every bad thing Jacob had done. Wash the rock with water or a damp cloth. Just as the rock is clean, God forgives us.
