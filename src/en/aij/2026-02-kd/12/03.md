Naomi smiled and hummed as she kneaded bread by the fire. She still missed her husband, but she knew God was with her. He had filled her home with laughter again. There were now five happy people sharing her home. Naomi’s two sons had married lovely young women. One son married Ruth and the other Orpah.

Ruth pushed the hair from her eyes as she crushed barley on the grinding stone. Orpah was not far away sweeping and shaking the dusty mats. The women smiled as they listened to Naomi singing Scripture songs as she made the bread. Their home was always full of peace and love.

Every day Naomi showed her family God’s goodness. Her friendly smiles, her gentle words, and her thoughtful actions showed them that “God is love” (1 John 4:16, ICB).

Naomi told wonderful stories too. True stories! She told stories about Creation, Noah and the Flood, Abraham, Joseph, and Moses. The Bible says we should also tell each other stories of God’s love. When should we tell each other? **Read Deuteronomy 11:19.**

Naomi also loved to pray. The women had never heard such peaceful, thankful prayers. This wasn’t how the Moabite people prayed to idols. No! Naomi talked to her God as though He was her Best Friend. In this home they found they belonged. How nice it was to be part of this happy family who loved God.

Ruth and Orpah just loved being part of this happy family who let their love shine for God.

- Which stories from the Bible are your favorite ones?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
