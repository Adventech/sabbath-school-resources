What would Ruth and Orpah do? They looked at the road in front of them and then behind them. Would they turn around and go back

to their families in Moab? Or would they go with Naomi to a new faraway land?

“The women cried together again. Then Orpah kissed Naomi good-bye” (Ruth 1:14, ICB). What did Ruth do? **Read the rest of Ruth 1:14.**

Orpah turned to leave but Ruth still held onto Naomi. “Go back with her,” Naomi kindly said to Ruth (Ruth 1:15, ICB). She still wanted the very best for her.

Ruth looked into Naomi’s eyes and said, “Every place you go, I will go. Every place you live, I will live. Your people will be my people. Your God will be my God” (Ruth 1:16, ICB).

Wow! Ruth had seen God’s goodness shining so brightly through Naomi’s life that now her heart also glowed with God’s love. She wanted to stay close to Naomi and close to God forever.

Our memory verse says, “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven” (Matthew 5:16). Ask Him to help you shine brightly today.
