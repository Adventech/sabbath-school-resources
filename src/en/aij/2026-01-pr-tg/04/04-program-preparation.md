Opening Activities

Write the following **Bible verses** on **strips of paper**: Ezekiel 28:17; Exodus 20:17; John 5:30; Isaiah 14:12. Give one verse to the first five children as they arrive. Help them find the verse and **bookmark** it for use during the storytelling section.

Print an **^[Angel outline]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** for each child. Source **glitter, markers**, or other resources of your choice, and **pushpins**. (Angels will be used during the story.)

Print an **^[extra angel]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** from **^[Angel outline to be Lucifer]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**. Decorate with extra sparkle before class.

Object lesson: source **hard and soft items** in the **shape of a heart** to show contrast. You may choose soft and hard bread (fresh and stale/overtoasted), or soft play-dough (from last week) to contrast with a stone or old play-dough. Both will be used throughout the program.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source **^[Picture PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.

Source **felt set** and **felt board** or **peg people**. Create a scene in **heaven** with as many angels as you can source.

Choose three **^[Strength Cards]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** that represent the abilities of specific children in your class. Source a see-through drinking **glass, dirt**, and a **weed** with a substantial tap root or root system. Clean dirt off, store in **plastic bag**.

Source **Hard and soft hearts** to unpack the story.
