Krishna looked at the rice paddy and smiled. As of today the field that would be flooded with water to grow rice was his! But Krishna had decided it wouldn’t be just his. This would be God’s rice paddy.

Krishna hurried off to find a farmer in his village in Nepal to plant the rice for him. Nepal is a beautiful country that lies between India and China. The majestic Himalayan Mountains run through the north, containing eight out of 10 of the highest mountains in the world. Cut into mountainsides, in lush valleys, and on the plains are many farms, used to grow food. Growing food is the most common job in Nepal, followed by working with textiles, then tourism.

The farmer Krishna found soon planted the field. Krishna watched and waited. He loved God, and knew that God would help show His power to the people in his village who didn’t know about Him. Most Nepalese people are Hindu (81 percent), and only a very few (1.4 percent) are Christians.

When the planting was finished, Krishna asked the farmer to walk around the paddy with him while he prayed. “God, I bought this land in this place where Your name is not known. Please use this field to tell the people in this area that You are the living God.”

When the field was ripe, the farmer harvested more than twice as much as the farmers of other fields of the same size! The neighbors were amazed and wanted to know why, so the farmer told them that Krishna, the owner, is a Christian. “He asked His God to bless the crop, and this is the result,” the man said.

Once again, Krishna prayed over the field, and again the yield was twice what the neighboring rice paddies produced.

The neighbors asked Krishna what he was doing to grow so much rice. This was Krishna’s opportunity to share Jesus with the people who did not know Him. He explained that God loves us and wants to bless us when we follow Him.

Many of the villagers went on to learn about the living God, including the farmer who saw God’s hand bless Krishna’s field. Because of his faith, God grew a miracle.
