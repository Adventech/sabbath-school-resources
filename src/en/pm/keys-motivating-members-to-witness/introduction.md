### Motivating Members to Witness

Do you want to motivate your members for outreach? This book presents field-tested strategies that work! It includes 21 secrets for motivation, such as:

- Stress “privilege” first and “responsibility” second
- Try to fit activities to abilities, but be careful – don’t limit God
- Recognize that many would be willing to work if they were taught how to begin
- Engage participants in planning, goal setting and evaluation
- Work together toward target dates and provide rest periods
