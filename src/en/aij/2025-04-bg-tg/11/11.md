**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: I can be God’s helper.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- Welcome children and parents by smiling. Tell the children you are happy to see them today!
- **^[“Christmas Star.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **star on a stick** and talk about the angel star.
- **^[“Riding on a Camel.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Walk around the room as children imagine they are riding a camel across the desert as they follow the star. Alternatively, use toy camels or camels on sticks.
- **^[“I’ll Share My Home.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **toy cow**. Talk about what it would have been like for Mary and Joseph in the stable.
- **^[“It Was a Happy Day.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give the children **an angel, a sheep, a wrapped gift** as you sing each verse. Use your classroom props and decorations to tell the story of Baby Jesus.
- **^[“Baby Jesus.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **small doll** to hold, wash, wrap, and rock.
- **^[“Who Is Jesus?”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **picture of the grown man, Jesus**.

**WEEK 11: BORN IN A STABLE**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag:** Baby doll, cloth to wrap (or use finger puppets), hay, shoe box, toy animals

Ask the children to lie down as though sleeping. Say: **All was quiet in Bethlehem. Little children, big sisters and brothers, and even mommies and daddies were asleep. Inside the stable, old cow, woolly sheep, and little donkey were sleeping too. All was quiet and still. Until . . . What’s that sound?** (Wah, wah!) **It’s a baby! Who is it?**

**Mary smiled as she held her new Baby close. Oh how she loved Him! She loved His little nose.** Ask the children to touch their nose. **She loved His little toes.** Ask the children to touch their toes. **The Baby was perfect. Joseph smiled at his new Son and gently whispered His name. It was the most special name ever . . .** Whisper: **Jesus!**

Open your Bible, point, and say: **The Bible tells us that Mary wrapped little Baby Jesus in “cloths.”** Wrap the baby doll. **He needed a bed. Where would Baby Jesus, God’s Son, sleep? Joseph found a manger, the animals’ feeding tray. And Mary “laid Him in a manger”** (Luke 2:7). Place straw in a shoe box and invite a child to place Baby Jesus into the manger.

Give the children some toy farm animals each, inviting them to make the animal sound. Talk about what it might have been like in the stable. Invite them to then place the animals around Baby Jesus lying in the manger.

**Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. Angels are watching over you. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. God the Father is always with You. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. Mary and Joseph will love and care for You. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. We will love You too!**

Say together: **Thank You, God, for sending Baby Jesus.**

```=Craft

Precut a yellow triangle, a colored oval, and a small brown circle shape. Adults can help children glue four craft sticks to make a “stable” shape onto dark blue or black cardstock. At the bottom of a yellow paper triangle, cut vertical slits and glue point-side down in the center of the stable, ruffling the strips of paper so they stick out. Underneath, glue two halves of a craft stick so they overlap to complete the “manger.” Draw a face onto the brown paper circle and glue it onto a colored oval shape. Slide “Baby Jesus” into the top of the manger. Stick a star above the stable and dot the rest of the sky with glitter glue.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** dark blue or black cardstock, yellow triangle, colored oval, brown paper circle, 5 craft sticks, glue, glitter, black marker.
```
