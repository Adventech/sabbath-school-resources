**Focus:** A grumbling dog may be cute, but a grumbling human isn’t!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](nature-lesson.jpg)

Fox terriers are active, smart, and loyal dogs. Elaine had a fox terrier named Zannie. White, with a rusty splotch across his back and head, he always had a puzzled look on his face.

It seemed as if Zannie could figure out what Elaine wanted him to do almost before she told him. He could leap as high as her head to take sticks from her hand. He could also dig like a gopher. And he loved to go shopping, proudly carrying her package home!

But Zannie had one failing. He was a grumbler! When Elaine asked him to do something he didn’t want to do, he’d reluctantly obey. Then as soon as she turned her back, the dog would make grumbling sounds under his breath. If she glanced back at him while he griped, he’d stop—until she quit looking. Somehow he always had the last word!

Zannie’s behavior made Elaine laugh. But in humans, grumbling isn’t funny! The Bible describes ungodly people as “grumblers” and “complainers” (Jude 16). They concentrate on every negative thing they can find instead of looking for the positive.

Next time you want to complain, ask yourself why. Is it for a valid reason or just a selfish one? Is there a better way to respond?

- **What kind of people do you enjoy being around?** (Happy, positive, kind people.)
- **Who can help you become that kind of person?** (God.)
