### Prepare the Soil of the Heart

The first phase in the disciple-making process is to ^[PREPARE]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) the soil of the heart. When we come close to people in friendship
and service, we show that we care and gain their confidence. Ministering to people’s practical needs with kindness and
compassion prepares their hearts to receive the truth of the gospel.

### ^[ESTABLISH HEALTH AND FRIENDSHIP-BUILDING MINISTRIES:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})

- ^[ELECT]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) health ministry and community service leaders (Church Manual, pp. 100, 107).
- ^[BUDGET]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) annually for need-based ministries and events.
- ^[ASSESS]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) local community needs (consider contacting civic leaders) along with church personnel and resources that could be used in health and friendship-building ministries and events.
- ^[PLAN]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) ways to inspire, equip, and involve members in making friends and doing kind deeds, while also scheduling events<sup>*</sup> to build relationships and meet needs in the community.
- ^[PROVIDE]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) leadership support—prayer, promotion, financial support, and participation.

<sup>*</sup> Local church examples of health and friendship-building events include:

- Health Expos
- Cooking Schools
- Centers of Influence
- Free Dental Clinics
- Food and Clothing Ministries
- Disaster Response
- Vacation Bible Schools
- Music Concerts
- Community Gardens
- Family Seminars
- Finance Seminars
- After School Programs
