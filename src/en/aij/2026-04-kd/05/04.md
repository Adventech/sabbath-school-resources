One warm Sabbath afternoon, Jesus and His disciples walked through Jerusalem. They had just spent the morning worshipping in the temple (church).

Jesus noticed a man sitting on the side of the street, begging. Jesus always notices the people who need kindness and love. This man had been blind since he was born. This means he’d never seen his mom or dad’s face. He had never seen trees swaying or clouds floating. He had never seen the colors of a rainbow.

Close your eyes for a minute. What can you see? That’s right. Nothing. Just darkness. That’s what this man saw.

Jesus began talking to the disciples, saying, “I am the light of the world” (John 9:5).

Jesus is like the sunshine that brings warmth and helps us grow. He turns our dark world of sin and sadness into bright joy and happiness.

As Jesus talked, He crouched down in front of the man and spat on the ground. He then used His hands to mix His spit with the dirt to make some mud.

_What is He doing?_ The disciples must have wondered. _How strange!_

Then Jesus began painting the mud on the man’s eyes.

What did Jesus tell the man? **Read the first half of John 9:7.**

With muddy eyelids, the man was led to the pool of Siloam to wash his face. Cool water splashed on his face, and his eyes flickered open. There was a bright burst of light. He squinted and looked around for the first time ever. He could see! The man shouted for joy. Jesus had healed him!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What would be the first thing you would want to see if you had been healed from blindness?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go outside and make two eyes using mud and rocks. Talk about the story. If you’re inside, cover your eyes and count slowly to ten, then open them wide! Imagine what the man in this story felt and saw. (Don’t put mud in your eyes, though!)

^[Mission Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Set up a Sabbath School at home for your toys. What would you sing, say, and share if you were the teacher? Practice this week, then later in the week invite a young family member or friend to join you. Also invite them to your Sabbath School on Sabbath.
