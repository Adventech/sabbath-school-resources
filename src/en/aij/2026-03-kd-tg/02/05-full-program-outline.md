**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a baby doll, some hay** (to be placed in the manger), and **a cloth/baby wrap** (to symbolize what Jesus was wrapped in). Show each of the items to the children.

- **What do you think it would be like to be wrapped in strips of cloth or to sleep on hay?** (Accept children’s answers and discuss together.)

Let the children help you **wrap the baby doll** and lay it on the hay. Let the children wrap one of their hands in the swaddling cloth and place it on top of the hay to feel what it might be like to sleep on rough hay.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Do you think a king’s baby today would be wrapped in cloth and left to sleep on hay in an animal feeding trough? Why?**
- **How do you think a king’s baby would be dressed, and what would their bed look and feel like?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain that Jesus is the King of kings, yet He was born to parents who didn’t have a lot of money or a fancy house. Jesus understands what it’s like to not have very much. He understands and loves everyone who has ever lived on our earth.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** revealing a **ball of thick, woolly yarn**.

- **Where does wool/yarn come from?** (Sheep.)

Stand in a circle. Hold the end of the yarn and say thank You to God for one of His gifts to you. Keep hold of the end of the yarn and throw the ball of yarn to a child, inviting them to name a gift from God. They hold the yarn and throw the ball to another child to make a web. Continue until everyone has held and thrown the yarn, and everyone who wants to has thanked God for one of His good gifts.

**Teaching Point:** God has given us so many wonderful things because He loves us. But Jesus is the greatest gift ever! We can share His love with others, because everyone needs Jesus’ love.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He is the greatest gift ever.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})**

Hold up **Nature Card 28: Sheep** and share the following facts:

- Sheep remind us to be humble.
- They humbly follow their leader.
- They are gentle, hardworking, and peaceful.
- They keep making woolly yarn for us all throughout their lives. Even after they are shorn, they grow another coat for us to have next year!
- They eat simple food, such as grass and root vegetables.

Give each child **Nature Card 28: Sheep** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

Share the Mission Story, **“The Missionary’s Gift.”**
