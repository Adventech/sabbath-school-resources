**Word of the Week**

**Thankful:** When we are thankful, we are happy or pleased because of something. We feel and want to show our thanks. We can be thankful for water to drink and food to eat. We can be thankful for God, who gives us all things because He loves us.

**Lesson Summary**

The Israelites praised God after their Red Sea experience. But after three days in the desert, they became very thirsty. When a spring of water proved too bitter to drink, their attitude quickly changed from thankfulness to complaining. God performed a miracle, and the water became sweet. Soon after, the Israelites again found themselves without water, and again, they complained. This time God instructed Moses to strike a rock with his staff, and miraculously, water gushed out of the rock. Despite the Israelites’ lack of faith and gratitude, God provided for them every time.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** the difference between complaining and thankfulness.
- **Feel** thankful to God for looking after their needs.
- **Respond** by thanking God for the good things He gives every day.
