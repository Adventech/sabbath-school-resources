**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Fill one cup with water. Gently place a paper heart shape on the surface. It may float briefly, but will then sink. Talk about how sadness can sometimes weigh our hearts down.

- **What are some things that might make our hearts feel heavy?** (If people around us argue or are mean to us; if we do badly at something, make mistakes, etc.)

Then fill the second cup with water halfway. Add two tablespoons of oil and gently place the second paper heart on the oil. It will float.

- **How does Jesus help you when you’re sad?** (He encourages us through other people, through promises in the Bible, and helps us to be thankful—which makes us feel happier.)

Look at the floating heart and talk about how Jesus surrounds and supports us in sad times and lifts us up.

Invite one child to read **Psalm 55:22**: “Cast your burden on the Lord, and He shall sustain you.”

Let’s discover how Jesus responded to someone on what was probably the hardest day of their life.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus can comfort us when we are sad, to give us hope and joy.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, and ask God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Hold your Bible and briefly share why the promises in it encourage you personally and give you joy. Share that the Bible is full of hope for us all—but we need to search for the promises and believe them!

Tell the children that you have hidden some tiny pieces of paper (promises) around the room that they can find. Then they can come and sit close to you on the floor. Invite each child to read the promise they found. Together, talk about the hope that each one gives us.

Then read one last promise (1 Thessalonians 4:16, 17). Explain that unlike the mother in today’s Bible story, we won’t experience the resurrection of our loved ones until the Second Coming. But knowing the promise of Jesus’ return, and the new earth He will make for us, gives us hope and joy!

- **Imagine if Jesus hadn’t promised to come back to raise those who love him. How would that change things for us now?** (We wouldn’t have hope to see them again. That would be very sad!)
- **What will the new earth that Jesus has promised to make be like?** (There won’t be any death, sorrow, or pain there, as mentioned in the promise of Revelation 21:4, 5.)

Encourage the children to give their tiny promise to someone who needs it.

**^[MISSION FOCUS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite a missionary from your church (or surrounding area, or online) to **share with the children what it’s like to be a missionary**. They can encourage the children to think about being missionaries themselves as they grow older. Allow the children to ask some of the questions they have thought of during the past week. If you run out of time, allow for a few more questions after Sabbath School.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to discuss and **plan with your family how you can help the missionaries in an area you have learned about this quarter**.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sit in a circle. Place small pieces of paper (with an affirming message on each one) in the middle. Invite each person to choose a message to give to the person on their right.

- **How did it make you feel to hear those encouraging words?** (If needed, break into pairs for this activity.)

Share that Jesus was like this. He encouraged others with His words and actions. He wanted everyone to be His friend, and He wants us to be His friends too. Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus can comfort us when we are sad, and give us hope and joy.**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing **“God Be with You.”** Then pray, thanking God for Jesus, who comforts us when we are sad and gives us hope and joy.
