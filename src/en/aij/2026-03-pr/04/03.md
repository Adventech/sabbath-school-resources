The night was dark and still. The hour was late. Joseph’s eyes opened, and he was instantly wide awake. Yes, he was sure of the next steps he must take. In a flash he woke Mary, and together they quickly loaded their little donkey. Silently they hurried away into the night, with God’s precious Son held tightly in Mary’s arms.

God could see that there was terrible danger ahead for His precious Son. Satan was determined to destroy Jesus, using King Herod. But God was watching over His beloved Son. What message had He sent Joseph in his dream? **Read Matthew 2:13.**

Joseph praised God for providing a safe hiding place in Egypt. As they traveled the lonely path to a foreign land, Joseph may also have praised God for the Wise Men’s expensive gifts, for they would pay for the journey and the things they would need while living in Egypt.

God was an amazing guide in Joseph and Mary’s lives. He had thought of and provided for their needs: direction, protection, money to live, and a safe hiding place. They were truly blessed by trusting Him.

Just as He guided Joseph to protect Jesus, our caring God will guide your parents, grandparents, and other adults in your life to protect you as you grow up. There may be some adults who do not want to keep you safe, but there will always be many that follow God’s guidance. You can trust their guidance and protection and thank God for them.
