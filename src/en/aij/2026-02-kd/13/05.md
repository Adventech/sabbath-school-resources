One day Naomi called Ruth to her side. She smiled as she told Ruth it was time for her to get married again. She knew the perfect man for her—kind Boaz! Boaz had shown them that He loved God.

They must find out if he would take care of Ruth. Naomi knew just what to do. Ruth listened carefully to her plan. Yes, she would do everything Naomi said.

That evening Ruth made her way to the place where Boaz worked. He lay under a blanket on the work floor, fast asleep. Ruth tip-toed over to where he slept. She lay down on the ground near his feet. Then she gently pulled a little bit of his blanket off his feet and placed it over her. Boaz kept sleeping.

What happened next? **Read Ruth 3:8.**

What a surprise! “Who are you?” he asked.

“I am Ruth . . . ” she said. “Spread your cover over me because you are the one who is to take care of me” (Ruth 3:9, ICB).

By lying at his feet, Ruth was asking Boaz, “Will you marry me?”

Boaz was amazed and happy. Yes, he would marry her! “The Lord bless you . . . I will do everything you ask,” he said (Ruth 3:10, 11, ICB).

Early the next morning, Boaz filled Ruth’s coat full of barley to take home to Naomi. Ruth raced home, smiling. She couldn’t wait to give Naomi the kind gift and tell her the exciting news. She would marry Boaz! She would be part of his family.
