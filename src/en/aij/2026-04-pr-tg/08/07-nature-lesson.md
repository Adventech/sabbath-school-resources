**Focus:** God gave Sabbath to us as a gift, not a burden.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/nature-lesson.png)

Have you ever tasted a fig? A fig is an edible fruit that grows on a small deciduous tree (a tree that loses its leaves in winter) or large shrub. But did you know that figs are technically not a fruit? They are actually an inside-out flower! Unlike an apple tree, which flowers and then turns into the fruit, a fig tree’s flowers bloom inside the pear-shaped pod. They then grow into the fruit we eat.

Figs are also one of the oldest known fruits in the world. They are mentioned in the Bible many times. In fact, the Pharisees had made up a Sabbath rule that said people couldn’t carry anything that weighed more than two dried figs. That’s not very much, is it?

The Jewish leaders made lots more rules too! They had many different types of work that were forbidden on the Sabbath.

Jesus showed the Jewish leaders—and all people—that Sabbath was created for people to enjoy. When we spend it close to Jesus and allow His love to change us, Sabbath can be like a sweet fig, blooming from a flower!

Have you ever tasted a fig before?

- **What do you think of the Pharisees’ rules about carrying a burden?**
- **What’s something new you could do to keep the Sabbath as Jesus did?**
