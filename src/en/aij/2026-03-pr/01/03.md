Mary swept the floors, careful to catch the dust that hugged the corners. It was an ordinary day. She didn’t know something extraordinary was about to happen.

Suddenly the room was filled with beautiful light. Mary was stunned! A dazzling angel, Gabriel, stood before her. “Rejoice, highly favored one, the Lord is with you; blessed are you among women!” said Gabriel (Luke 1:28). Mary could hardly believe her eyes. What was happening?

“Don’t be afraid, Mary,” said the angel. He had the best news ever! What did he say would happen to her? **Read Luke 1:31-33.**

“How can this be?” asked Mary (Luke 1:34). She wasn’t married to Joseph yet—only engaged. Gabriel kindly told her that the Holy Spirit would cause Mary to become pregnant and that the baby would be called the Son of God. Wow!

Mary stood in awe before the angel. Many mothers in Israel had longed to be the mother of the promised Messiah, yet God had chosen her. God had looked for a gentle, loving, humble woman to be the mother of His dear Son, Jesus. And He had found Mary. What a wonderful privilege!

In quiet surrender, Mary said, “I am the servant girl of the Lord. Let this happen to me as you say!” (Luke 1:38, ICB).

Mary shared the news with Joseph. He felt worried about her being judged and shamed for being pregnant before she was married. So God sent an angel to him in a dream. What did the angel say? **Read Matthew 1:20, 21.** Joseph quietly surrendered to God’s plan too. What wonderful parents Mary and Joseph would be.

God has great plans for each of us. He calls us to surrender to Him, and then He gives us the privilege of having a special part in His big story, just like Mary.
