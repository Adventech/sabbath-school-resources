**Big Idea:** Godly friends bring me to Jesus, who has the power to heal my body and forgive my sins.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use your facial expressions and body language.
- Use a **Lego man and four Lego “friends”** and carry the man in a little cloth. You may like to make a simple **Lego building** and take off tiles to then drop the Lego man through the roof. Alternatively, you may like to use **peg people** and **a cardboard box** as the house.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He couldn’t move his arms; he couldn’t move his legs. The man from Capernaum had paralysis. He could only lie still on his mat as time slowly passed by. He was sorry that he was in pain and couldn’t move. But he was even more sorry for the sinful, unhealthy choices he’d made that had led him to get sick ^[(Lego or peg man)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

He’d asked the doctors and the priests to help him, but they’d ignored him. They wrongly believed his suffering was God’s punishment for his sins. That’s not like God at all!

One day the man heard the best news! Jesus was back in town! Everyone knew what that meant for the sick and suffering townspeople: miracles, wonderful stories of God’s kingdom, comfort, and hope.

The man’s hopes rose . . . then sank again. He remembered the bad choices he’d made that had led to his condition. _What if Jesus doesn’t want to be near me? What if He treats me the way the priests and doctors have? What if He sends me away?_

The man had four good friends ^[(four peg/Lego people)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They encouraged him to see Jesus. They would take him to the great Healer themselves! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Gladly they picked up the man’s bed mat and carried him toward Peter’s house, where Jesus was.

Crowds of people pressed into every square inch of Peter’s home. Many more lined the open doors and windows. It seemed as though everyone wanted to be near Jesus!

The man and his four friends tried to get through the crowd, but it was no use. The crowd wouldn’t let them inside! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Then the man had a bright idea. His friends smiled and quickly carried him to the roof. ^[(Put figures on the roof of Lego brick house or box.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What were they doing on this roof? Read Luke 5:19.** (Making a hole in the roof so they could let their friend down to see Jesus.) ^[(Make a hole in your structure.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The surprised crowds below looked up to see . . . sunshine . . . four smiling faces . . . then a bed mat with a man on it being slowly lowered from the roof!

Down, down it came, until the man was lying at Jesus’ feet. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “When Jesus saw their faith, He said to the man with paralysis, “Son, your sins are forgiven you” (Mark 2:5).

Jesus had just spoken the beautiful words the man longed to hear: “Your sins are forgiven” (Matthew 9:2). Instantly he was changed. His guilt, regret, and hopelessness were gone. In their place was peaceful joy.

The people looked at Jesus in awe. They didn’t fully understand it, but Jesus had just shown them that His forgiveness is the only thing that can heal the hurting heart of a sinner. But not everyone in the crowd was impressed. A few scowled and muttered under their breath.

Jesus could read their faces and their hearts. He knew they didn’t want to believe that He was the Son of God, who loves to forgive. Jesus said, “Why are you thinking evil thoughts?” (Matthew 9:4, ICB). The scribes and Pharisees didn’t give an answer.

Jesus looked right at them and asked another question.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He ask? Read Mark 2:9.** (“Which is easier, to say to the paralytic, ‘Your sins are forgiven you,’ or to say, ‘Arise, take up your bed and walk’?”)

Not one of the Pharisees could answer. Jesus continued speaking: “ ‘But that you may know that the Son of Man has power on earth to forgive sins’—He said to the paralytic, ‘I say to you, arise, take up your bed, and go to your house’ ” (Mark 2:10, 11). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Instantly the man sprang to his feet. ^[(Put peg or Lego man on his feet.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was healed all over! With his bed tucked under one arm, he strode toward the door, and this time the crowd let him through. They praised God and said, “We never saw anything like this!” (Mark 2:12). “Today we have seen amazing things!” (Luke 5:26, ICB).

The man burst through the door, still praising God with every step. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was forgiven and healed!
