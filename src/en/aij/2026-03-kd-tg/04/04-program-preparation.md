**Opening Activities**

Source a **tub of water; plastic animals, fish, birds, people; slotted spoons; large rock shape; soft white cloth or sponge; plastic garbage; “recycling bin.”**

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a **toy that can be guided**, such as a remote-controlled car, battery-operated train set, or a toy on wheels with a string to lead it. Also source a **piece of chalk** and **lengths of string or paper**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props:** angel sticks from week 1; a travel bag (or sheet or tablecloth) filled with a few household items that Mary and Joseph may have had, such as wooden bowls, pots, cloths, brushes, spoons, etc.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box: pictures of children** completing various everyday activities: crossing a street, playing with a ball, going shopping, riding in a car, etc.
