What runs but never walks? Water!

Where can you find an ocean with no water? On a map.

Did you know that more than half of your body is made of water? Jellyfish are nearly all water.

Did you know that there is more water in the sky above us than in all our rivers put together?

Did you know that water is the only thing in nature that can be hard, runny, or a floating gas?

Did you know that water is strong enough to slice through metal, using a waterjet?

You might live somewhere where there’s lots of water, or very little. But without any water at all, we would soon lose our life. There would be no tall trees, no green grass, or flowers. There would be no animals or people. Without water there would be no clouds floating in the sky, or snow falling in winter. The world would very quickly become a very hot, empty place.

Just like the Israelites, we can be thankful to God for the miracle of water. And for all the daily miracles He does to give us what we need. The Bible says, “Praise the Lord. . . . He made heaven and earth, the sea and everything in it. He remains loyal forever” (Psalm 146: 1-6, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What do you like to do the most with water?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for the miracle of water. Pray for those who have only a little water.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Take two cups and fill one with water, over a sink or bucket. Use the filled cup to slowly fill up the empty cup—just one small splash at a time. With each little pour, thank God for something good He has made.

```=Memory Verse

“Oh, give thanks to the Lord, for He is good! For His mercy endures forever” (Psalm 136:1).
```
