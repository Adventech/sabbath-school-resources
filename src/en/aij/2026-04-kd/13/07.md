Ellen and her sister Elizabeth chatted happily with a classmate as they walked down the road together. The sun shone like normal. Horses clip-clopped down the road like normal. But today would not be normal for Ellen. Today would be a day she would never forget.

As they walked along, a rock suddenly shot through the air. Thud! The rock had hit Ellen right in the face. Ellen fell to the ground.

Ellen couldn’t stand up. Her legs were weak and she felt dizzy. Together Elizabeth and their friend slowly carried Ellen home.

For many weeks, Ellen lay in her bed unable to do anything. When visitors came by, they took one look at Ellen and thought she would soon die. But the all-powerful God had other plans.

Slowly, slowly, Ellen began to improve. Then one day, her mother announced that Ellen could return to school. Ellen was so excited.

But her excitement didn’t last long. As she started her studies, Ellen tried to look at the words on the page but everything was blurry. She tried to write with a pen, but her hand just shook. The teacher decided Ellen would have to give up school.

It seemed impossible for Ellen to be able to read and write. But God is all-powerful. There is nothing He can’t do!

When Ellen White was older, God gave her important visions—true awake dreams—and asked her to write down everything she saw.

With her trembling hand, Ellen picked up a pen. She began to write. In an instant, something changed. God healed her. Ellen could now write smoothly and clearly.

In her lifetime, God gave Ellen the strength to write many books, articles, and letters. In total it amounts to about 100,000 pages for God. That is a lot of words. What a miracle!
