**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Living our lives can sometimes feel like doing a puzzle—with a blindfold on! Some pieces fit together, but others are a total mystery. We lose people—people we deeply care about. We move or go through difficult periods of change. And we all must deal with the puzzle of family life. Sometimes family members turn away from God, and hurt themselves or others. And sometimes they hurt us.

This was Jacob’s experience. This was Joseph’s experience. This may be your experience too.

The wonderful news is that God cares. He cares about us and for us. In response to the messy puzzle, He invites us to trust Him; to give our cares to Him. He sees the big picture—in fact, He designed it.

May you be reminded this week of God’s care for you and the big picture of your life. Give Him all that’s on your mind or seems hard to piece together. Our loving God, who is in control, cares deeply for you. Staying close to Him gives peace, purpose, and direction. “When we are in trouble and pressed down with anxieties, the Lord is near, and He bids us cast all our care upon Him, because He cares for us” (Ellen G. White, _Sons and Daughters of God_, p. 19).

What a comfort to know we are cherished by our heavenly Father. “For I know the thoughts that I think toward you, says the Lord, thoughts of peace and not of evil, to give you a future and a hope” (Jeremiah 29:11).
