^[Word of the Week—Peace:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#a47b9b", "size": "xl"}}})\
The feeling of quiet, calm, security, and contentment we have in our hearts, even when there is trouble around us. Peace is also a time without fighting, or jostling crowds, or busyness. We have true peace when we trust in God.

Teaching and Healing

Jesus smiled as the crowd pressed closer for yet another story. Day after day He had taught the many people who were by the shores of Lake Galilee. Farmers, noble rulers, mothers, and children all hung on His every word. He stopped to heal the sick people brought to Him in faith. Then He began another amazing story about God’s kingdom. The crowds listened to stories about baskets and light, farmers and seeds, hungry birds and rocky paths. They never seemed to get tired of listening, so Jesus told them one more story, and then another . . .

Jesus didn’t rest very often. He gave the weary people what they needed. He knew they needed to hear about God’s kingdom of love and peace. Their lives didn’t have much of either. They didn’t understand how much God loved them, and the Roman rulers hadn’t taught or shown them much about peace.

Jesus taught them that love would bring peace. Instead of starting a war with their Roman rulers, He suggested something totally different. “But I say to you, love your enemies, bless those who curse you, do good to those who hate you, and pray for those who spitefully use you and persecute you” (Matthew 5:44).

Little by little, Jesus taught them about the peace that God would give them. With peace in their hearts, they could face anything. The Bible tells us, “The God of love and peace will be with you” (2 Corinthians 13:11).

As the people heard Jesus teach about love and peace again and again, their hearts began to open up. They began to hope: Maybe our lives can be different . . . Maybe God is close to us after all . . . Maybe He really is a God of peace!

The Bible scrolls had promised the people many wonderful things about Jesus. **In Psalm 29:11 what does the Bible say God would do?**

As Jesus spoke, the promise was being fulfilled right before their very eyes!
