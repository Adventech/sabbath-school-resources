**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

How often do you stop and listen—really listen—to someone else? In our fast-paced world, we’re often too busy or easily distracted to take the time to listen well. Wonderfully, our Savior is never too busy, and He always listens when we call out to Him. His eye is always on us, and His ear is always attentive to our cries.

“The Saviour is in communication with every part of His vast dominion. He stoops from His throne to listen to the cries of His children. His heart of love is filled with pity and compassion for them” (Ellen G. White _manuscript 94_, 1904).

On earth Jesus was always surrounded by people, always on a mission. Yet He was never too busy to listen to someone who needed Him. Above the noise of an excited crowd one day, Jesus heard Bartimaeus’ cry, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:38), and the longing that lay behind those words. Jesus listened and responded in a miraculous way.

May we be encouraged and inspired by His example this week. May we cry out to Him, knowing He listens and cares. And may we extend the same respect and value to those around us—especially the little ones in our midst. This is the best preparation you can have for sharing this week’s story with the children in your care.

“But certainly God has heard me; He has attended to the voice of my prayer. Blessed be God” (Psalm 66:19, 20).
