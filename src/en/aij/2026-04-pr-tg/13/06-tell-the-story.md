**Big Idea:** Jesus has the power to resurrect those who have died.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Prepare the cardstock cut with a small hole, and have pictures from a book/magazine for the activity.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to John 11 and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As Jesus traveled from place to place, He knew He was always welcome at the home of His close friend Lazarus, who lived in Bethany. He often stayed with Lazarus and his two sisters, Mary and Martha. They loved Him and were like His own family. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

One day Lazarus suddenly became very sick. Mary and Martha tried to help him, but he didn’t get better. The sisters thought about their good Friend Jesus. He would be able to heal Lazarus! He was a two-day journey away, so they quickly sent Him a message.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What was the message? Read John 11:3.** (“Lord, behold, he whom You love is sick.”)

Jesus heard the message—but He kept on teaching and helping others. His disciples were surprised! Why didn’t He leave at once? He didn’t seem worried or sad.

The disciples didn’t understand the whole situation, but Jesus did. Here was an opportunity for Jesus to prove that He was truly God’s Son.

Lazarus grew weaker and weaker until he died. Poor Mary and Martha were so sad! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Two days later Jesus told His disciples that they would go to Bethany. As they walked and talked Jesus said, “Our friend Lazarus sleeps, but I go that I may wake him up” (John 11:11). The disciples thought Jesus meant He was sleeping and would soon get well. So Jesus told them plainly, “Lazarus is dead” (John 11:14). What a shock! But Jesus knew that death is like a sleep. Those who follow Him will rest in the grave until He returns to wake them up.

_(Note: take no longer than five minutes to do the following activity.)_

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DO:** Cut a small hole in a piece of thick paper or cardstock. Find a picture from a magazine or book. Place the cardstock over the image. Ask a child to guess the whole picture by seeing through the small hole. Allow them to move the hole around the picture and make guesses.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DISCUSS:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **When Jesus got the message that Lazarus was sick, what did He do?** (He waited two days before starting the trip to Bethany.)
- **What do you think Martha and Mary thought about Jesus not coming sooner?** (Perhaps they wondered if Jesus really cared.)
- **Why do you think Jesus delayed going to Bethany?** (He had a greater purpose in mind to give people another piece of evidence that He was God’s Son. He wanted them to believe in Him so that they might have everlasting life.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Talk about when we could see only a tiny part of the picture, we couldn’t work out what it was. The disciples, Mary, and Martha couldn’t see the whole picture, so they couldn’t understand why Jesus delayed. But God sees the whole picture. God always understands the whole situation and has a plan.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**TEACHING POINT:** When we don’t understand what is happening in our lives, we can trust that Jesus sees the whole picture and has a plan.

Let’s continue with the story. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

When Jesus finally arrived in the little town, Martha rushed down the road to meet Him.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**When she got to Jesus, what did she say? Read John 11:21, 22.** (“Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died. But even now I know that whatever You ask of God, God will give You.”)

Jesus said gently, “Your brother will rise again” (John 11:23). Then He encouraged her by adding, **“I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in Me, though he may die, he shall live”** (John 11:25).

Martha looked up into His loving eyes. “Yes, Lord,” she said. “I believe that you are the Christ, the Son of God” (John 11:27, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Mary also rushed to see Jesus. Falling at His feet, she cried the same words her sister had. “Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died” (John 11:32).

Jesus was so deeply sad to see their grief! He asked them softly, “Where have you laid Him?” (John 11:34).

Together they led Jesus toward the cave in a rock where Lazarus was buried. As He walked, “Jesus wept” (John 11:35).

Jesus looked at the big stone that covered the entrance to Lazarus’ grave and said, “Take away the stone” (John 11:39).

Four days was plenty of time for a body to begin decomposing. Lazarus’ body wouldn’t have looked or smelled good. The fact that Lazarus had been in the grave for four days proved He was definitely dead.

The stone was rolled away. Jesus lifted His eyes toward heaven and spoke.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jesus say? Read John 11:41, 42.** (“Father, I thank You that You have heard Me. And I know that You always hear Me, but because of the people who are standing by I said this, that they may believe that You sent Me.”)

Jesus cried out in a loud, clear voice, “Lazarus, come forth!” (John 11:43). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

With the graveclothes wrapped tightly around him, Lazarus shuffled out. The crowd stared in awe as Jesus said, “Take the cloth off of him and let him go” (John 11:44, ICB).

Lazarus stood healthy and strong! His eyes beamed with love for Jesus. He was alive! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus had done the impossible! While the sisters hugged Lazarus, the disciples praised God along with everyone else in the crowd that day. He had shown everyone that He truly is the Son of God and has the power to resurrect those who have died.
