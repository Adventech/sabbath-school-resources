**What you need:**

**Option 1:** white cardstock, green paper strips, colored fabrics, colored tissue paper, craft glue, and scissors.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3a.png)

**Option 2:** small glass jars, acrylic paints, paintbrush, water, and smocks (to cover clothing) (Option 2).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3a2.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out a vase shape in the fabric.
- Cut green strips of paper.

**Create it together:**

- Encourage children to create a special vase of flowers just for Sabbath.

**Option 1:**

- Glue vertical lines and stick sections of the green paper strips to make wavy stems, as shown.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3b.png)

- Glue fabric vase to page, so it overlaps the bottom of each stem.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3c.png)

- Create colorful flowers by gluing scrunched up pieces of colored tissue paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3d.png)

- Children can share their special vase picture with someone next week to open Sabbath.

**Option 2:**

- Alternatively, if you have access to small glass jars, children can paint the jars with acrylic paints to use as a Sabbath vase (Option 2).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/03/3b2.png)
