Aaron nodded at his brother, Moses. He and his sons were ready. Their beautiful new clothes were finished. Aaron’s holy vest, shining with jewels, had been carefully sewn together too.

Moses smiled. This was great news! God had chosen Aaron to be the high priest, and his sons to be priests in the sanctuary. God’s new home would be a Holy Place of worship. The priests would teach the people about God’s love and forgiveness. They would help the people come close to Him.

At the front of God’s home was a courtyard. It was a place for the people to bring their offering to God and ask for forgiveness. When they came with their lamb, which had been killed, and told God they were sorry, He promised to “remove the person’s sin” (Leviticus 1:4, ICB). The priests would then wash their hands and feet. This would show the people that God had made them clean.

Their small offerings in God’s home would remind them of God’s big promise. He had promised to send a Rescuer to take the place of the lamb offering. He would take away all sin. Who would it be? **Read John 1:29.**

Yes, Jesus! When we ask Jesus for forgiveness, He promises to take away our dirty sinfulness and wash us clean.
