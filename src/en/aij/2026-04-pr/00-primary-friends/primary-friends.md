What’s something you like doing? Do you run fast, or are you musical, or good at math? Are there other funny things you’d like to learn to do, such as wiggle your ears without touching them, or walk in a straight line with your eyes closed? There’s always more to learn! What’s something new you’ll learn today?

Jesus can do anything, but we know He never showed off or did things to draw attention to Himself. He was humble and kind and always thought of others. Jesus is loving and kind, and when He was on earth, sometimes He didn’t eat all day because He was caring for others. Jesus had a special message for everyone. He wanted the whole world to know how much God loves them.

Jesus loves you too—very, very much; more than anyone else in the whole world. No matter where you live or what you’re good at, Jesus sees you, and He wants you to remember that you are loved.
