Bartimaeus heard voices speaking to him, “Cheer up! Get to your feet. Jesus is calling you” (Mark 10:49, ICB). The excited man didn’t need to be told twice. Neither did he need anyone to help him. He leapt to his feet and threw his coat aside. He didn’t allow anything, not even his coat, to slow him down as he raced toward the voice of Jesus.

Standing before the great Teacher, Bartimaeus heard Jesus say, “What do you want Me to do for you?” (Mark 10:51). Jesus already knew the answer. But He asked the question so that Bartimaeus could share his faith with the crowd. Then they would be encouraged to believe and ask Jesus for a blessing too.

How did Bartimaeus answer Jesus’ question? **Read Luke 18:41.**

Jesus answered with the most amazing words Bartimaeus would ever hear. “Go your way; your faith has made you well” (Mark 10:52).

Suddenly Bartimaeus could see! Jesus had healed him! He could see the smile of Jesus and the happy crowd around him. He could see the blue sky and the road where he had sat begging for so long. He would sit there no longer. Jesus had said, “Go your way”!

How wonderful that Jesus, the Son of David, stopped when He heard the excited cries of Bartimaeus. Jesus healed him and showed him mercy.

Just as Bartimaeus left his coat behind, we might need to get rid of some things in our lives that could stop us from focusing on Jesus. These things are different for each of us, but might include unhealthy food, games, or media time. When we do get rid of some things, He will always give us something much better in return, such as peace and happiness. And best of all, He gives us a closer friendship with Himself.
