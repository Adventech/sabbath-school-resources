**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Give a warm welcome to each child as they arrive at Sabbath school. Talk about something you looked for and found this week. Then ask:

- **Did you look for something this week?**
- **Did you find what you were looking for? How did you feel when you found it?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite each child to **search for the Baby Jesus pictures that you have hidden in the room**. Tell them to first look for a star that will help lead them to Jesus. When they have each found the picture of Jesus, they can come and sit at the table to color it. Talk about some of the ways that they can look for Jesus and find Him, such as in Bible stories, prayer, worship, nature, and by serving or helping others.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a box filled with **sawdust**, **sand**, **salt**, or **fine grains**, in which there are **hidden gold “coins”** and **heart shapes**. Let each child search for one gold “coin” and one heart shape.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What did it feel like when you found a “treasure”** (coin) **and “love”** (heart) **in the Secret Surprise Box?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** We can find Jesus in all kinds of unexpected places when we ask Him to help us find Him! Whenever we learn something new about Jesus, it’s like discovering treasure. When we learn about His love and share it with others, we find Jesus and can also then help others find Him.

Say the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He wants me to seek Him and find Him.**

In our story today, we will learn about the people who looked for Baby Jesus because they wanted to find the Messiah. I wonder . . .

- **How did they search for Him?**
- **What did they do when they found Him?**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **paper footprints, a Bible, a worship songbook, a picture of praying hands, a bag of food** to give away, and **a beautiful item from nature** such as a flower in a pot or some fruits and vegetables.

Place the paper footprints in a line to form a path around the room, ending at a picture of Jesus or a 3-D Nativity scene. Along the path, place the other items from the box. Go for a walk with your class along the path. Talk about the different things that can help us to seek Jesus—the Bible, worship, prayer, kindness to others, and learning about God by studying His Creation. Ask:

- **What do you like doing in your family that helps you to seek Jesus and be close to Him?** (Allow time for children to respond and ask some adult helpers to share their thoughts. Then share how you, the teacher, love to be close to Jesus because He’s your best friend.)

**Teaching Point:** Share that the best way to seek Jesus is by reading His living Word, the Bible. When we read it with a seeking heart, we learn so much about Him. We can seek Jesus when we go to church, pray, sing, walk outside in nature, and when we help others to know Him too. Jesus loves to see you seek Him, and He’ll always let you find Him.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He wants me to seek Him and find Him.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 29: Indigo Bunting** and share the following facts:

- Indigo Buntings live in North America. They fly south in winter to stay warm.
- They look at the stars and work out the right direction to go south.
- They even hop in that direction before they set off, to practice the right way to go!
- They fly south at night so that they can follow the stars in the sky, just as the Wise Men in our story did.
- Give each child **Nature Card 29: Indigo Bunting** for them to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
