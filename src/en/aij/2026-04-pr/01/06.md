Only moments before, the disciples had been in a tossing boat in a raging storm—wet, tired, and helpless. Now they were sitting in a calm boat on the still waters, staring at Jesus, speechless. He hadn’t been afraid in the storm, not even a tiny bit. He had simply stood up and spoken those three little words, “Peace, be still” (Mark 4:39). Wow!

Jesus asked His disciples why they were afraid. Then He asked them sadly, “Where is your faith?” (Luke 8:25). The disciples couldn’t answer, for the miracle had left them speechless.

They would have plenty of time, in the coming hours and days, to think about Jesus’ question. He had shown His disciples how to trust God in a storm, and how to have peace through a storm. He had shown them that He could calm a storm, and that He answers cries for help. And most importantly, He had shown them that He could calm their fears and give them peace when they were scared. Their night on the lake had given their faith a wonderful opportunity to grow, and Jesus hoped and prayed it would. He wanted them to find peace while trusting Him. He wants the same for you! **Read Romans 15:13.**

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- If you had been on one of the other boats, what would you tell people about Jesus?
- What do you think this miracle might have done for the faith of those who saw it, and of those who heard about it from people they knew? Sometimes those around us are blessed when God answers our cry for help.
