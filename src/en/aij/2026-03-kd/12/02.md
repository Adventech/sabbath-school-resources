Zacchaeus sat at his tax table in busy Jericho and listened. He didn’t listen to the clink, clink of coins. He listened very closely to the buzzing crowds. It seemed everyone in Jericho was talking about Jesus.

Some told stories of His kindness. Others spoke of His wise words. Then there were the amazing stories about His mighty miracles. The more Zacchaeus heard, the more he wanted to meet Jesus.

Zacchaeus wasn’t very popular. Tax collectors were not well liked, even when they were honest. But Zacchaeus wasn’t honest. He was a cheat and a thief. Would Jesus want to meet him? **Read Luke 15:2.**

One day, as Zacchaeus sat at his table counting coins, he heard a shout. And then another. Jesus! Jesus was coming!

Mothers left their cooking. Fathers dropped their tools. Children stopped playing. Together they all raced down the road. “Jesus was going through the city of Jericho” (Luke 19:1, ICB).

When Zacchaeus heard the exciting news, his heart leaped with joy. Jesus, the one he had heard so much about, was right here in Jericho! He knew what he must do. He must meet Jesus!
