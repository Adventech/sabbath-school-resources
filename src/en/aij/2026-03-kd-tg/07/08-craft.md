**What you need:**

Week 7 craft template on red paper, scissors, hole punch, string or thin ribbon.


**Preparation instructions:**

- Cut out the heart shapes and hole punch around the edges.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/07/7b.png)

- Cut lengths of string for each child

**Create it together:**

1\. Place the half heart over the full heart. Thread half of the string, in and out, through one side of the hearts; starting from the bottom and finishing at the top middle point.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/07/7c.png)

2\. Repeat step one, threading the other side of the heart.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/07/7d.png)

3\. Tie a bow at the top with the remaining string and store your memory verses inside your “heart.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/07/7e.png)
