**Opening Activities**

Prepare music to be played for musical flashlight. **Mark out a short circular course** around some chairs or obstacles. Make **two signs** for either end that read: Moab; Bethlehem. Source a **flashlight** that will be easy for the children to hold and use.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: flashlight** and a **small, dark blanket**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: flashlight, backpack, toy spoons, pots, and a small blanket.

Source a **felt set** or **wooden peg people** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Cover your **Story Explorer Box** in **aluminum foil**, if available. Use something else that’s reflective, such as a mirror, if you don’t have foil. **Small birthday candles** (one for each child), matches. Source a small box of tightly packed **sand** or a loaf of bread to stick the candles into later.
