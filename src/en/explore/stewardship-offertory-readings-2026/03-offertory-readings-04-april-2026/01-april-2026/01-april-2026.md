---
title: Dying to Our Self
date: 04/04/2026
---

Six thousand years ago, Satan tempted Eve to think of herself before God. She did.

Four thousand years later, Satan tempted Jesus in the wilderness with the same offer on a different contract—look out for Yourself. He didn’t.

For three years, Satan tempted Jesus to put Himself first. Just once. But He never did. Not once.

Weeks passed. Prophecy’s unmovable deadline approached. Satan got wildly desperate. He had Jesus flogged, tortured, spat on, and forsaken by all, but Christ remained sinless and selfless.

Satan nailed Him to a cross and lifted Him high for the final attack. If he could plant a tiny seed of self-preservation in Christ’s torture-hazed mind, the victory would be his. Demons entered religious leaders, soldiers, and the criminal alongside Jesus as they hurled a hellfire of temptation at Him, “_If_ You are the Son of God, save Yourself.” He still didn’t.

“Jesus, defend Yourself! Prove Yourself!” It was the frantic heart cry of every disciple, of every weeping soul at the foot of the cross (and of Satan!).

Finally, the Jewish leaders unknowingly spoke a profound truth: “He cannot save Himself.”

Jesus could not save both Himself and us, so He chose us. Today, He invites us to find and crucify our self.

### Appeal

As we now return God’s tithe and give our regular and systematic Promise offerings, let’s each take a moment to ask God to remove any selfish desires in our hearts. Let’s ask Him for a new heart that is selfless and generous. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
