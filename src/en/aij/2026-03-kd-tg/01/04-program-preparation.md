**Opening Activities**

Source **small boxes with lids** or **paper lunch sacks; pictures of small toys** to stick on the containers; **glue** or **tape**; **small toys** that will fit into the boxes/bags; and **a few other objects** that are not so interesting to children.

- Glue pictures of small toys onto the lids or bags.
- Put the same toys as shown in the pictures into half of the containers. Leave a few containers empty.
- Place objects that children will not find interesting into the rest of the containers, such as cleaning brushes, clothespins, spoons, etc.

Familiarize yourself with this quarter’s mission project/offering destination to share with the children. Print out the **^[13 Mission Fast Facts]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for the quarter.

Print the **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and create a **colorful container** to collect the offering.

Source a **bell** or pretty **sound maker** to use to get the children’s attention before opening the Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box. For example, a bell, toy flute, castanet, sealed cardboard tube filled with dried beans.

Source **two boxes** (shoeboxes) to be used for Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: a baby doll, items used in caring for babies.**

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props:** a baby doll (from the Secret Surprise Box).

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a **box with a lid or gift bag** with **small gifts or treats** for the children.
