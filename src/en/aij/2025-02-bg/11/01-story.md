Father Jairus patted his little girl’s head (pat head). It was hot. He frowned. His little girl was very sick. She lay in bed sleeping (sleeping hands). She didn’t hear the birds singing outside. She didn’t see the light sparkle on the lake nearby. She didn’t run and play with her friends. She could only rest and sleep (sleeping hands). Father was sad. He didn’t know how to make her better. The doctors didn’t know how to make her better. But Jesus could help! Father ran to find Him. Father ran into town. Run, run, huff-puff. He found Jesus in the middle of a very big crowd. He bowed down at Jesus’ feet. Everyone stopped and looked. Then Father said to Jesus, “My little girl is dying [very sick]. Please come and put your hands on her. Then she will be healed and will live” (Mark 5:23, ICB). Father knew Jesus could help! Jesus nodded and smiled. Yes, He would love to make this little girl well.

Jesus and Father Jairus started walking home. Suddenly,  a man ran to Father to tell him something. His little girl had been too sick—she had died. Father began to cry (wipe tear). But Jesus told Father to believe. Jesus would help her! Father stopped crying. Could Jesus help his little girl? Yes! He trusted Jesus. He hurried to his house with Jesus by his side. At home, everyone was outside crying. But Jesus wasn’t sad. Father wasn’t sad. Jesus would help the little girl—just as He’d promised. Jesus went into her room. He gently took her hand (take child’s hand). With a kind voice Jesus said, “Little girl, I tell you to stand up!” (Mark 5:41, ICB).

The little girl opened her eyes and stood up. She began to walk around. She walked over to Father and smiled (smile). Then she turned to Mother for a hug (hug). Mother and Father were so happy! Their little girl was alive again! (Clap your hands.) Thank You, Jesus! It was a day they would never forget. God is so powerful. He loves to help us.\
(Say together) Thank You, Jesus, for helping me when I need You.
