---
title: The Purpose of Branch Sabbath Schools
---

A Branch Sabbath School is an evangelistic type of service conducted primarily for the benefit of non-Seventh-day Adventists. It often begins in a simple way and can easily be adapted to fit the particular interest and needs of the people. A Branch Sabbath School is conducted under the approval of the Sabbath School council or the conference Sabbath School director. Regardless of the day on which it is held, the term Branch Sabbath School is used to designate any regular weekly Sabbath School evangelistic endeavor in which Sabbath School materials are used.
