**What you need:**

Week 3 craft template, yarn, two straws, scissors, small bead, sticky tape, red and black markers, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3a.jpg)

**Preparation instructions:**

Cut 11.5-inch (30-centimeter) pieces of yarn.

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2027-01-kd-craft-03.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut straws into one-inch (2.5-centimeter) pieces (1 per child).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3b.jpg)

2\. Tie a bead to one end of the piece of string (adults).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3c.jpg)

3\. Wrap a piece of tape around the other end of the string. Children can thread the pieces of straw onto the string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3d.jpg)

4\. Cut out two snake heads and one tongue. Draw eyes and a nose using black marker. Color in the tongue with a red marker.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3e.jpg)

5\. Glue the tongue onto the snake head. Place the string between the snake heads and glue together. Children can reenact Moses’ first miracle by holding their uncut straw with one hand to represent Moses’ staff, then throwing down their straw snake with the other hand.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/3f.jpg)
