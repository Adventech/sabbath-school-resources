More than twenty-two million people from every walk of life call themselves Seventh-day Adventists. Standing side by side, with our arms outstretched and hands joined, we could form a globe-circling human chain reaching from Sydney to Auckland to Santiago to Buenos Aires to Johannesburg to Perth and right back to Sydney!

From the poorest of the poor to multibillionaires, from people on life-support machines to Olympic athletes, Adventists wear every skin color and worship God in more than 900 languages. We are as multicultural and diversely assorted as any group of people on earth, yet we are also one.

Our unity is found in Jesus Christ, the One who prayed in John chapter 17 that His followers would also be one. He wants us to be united in joy (verse 13), in mission (verse 18), in truth (verses 14, 17, 19), in witness (verse 20), in glory (verse 22), and in love (verse 26).

One way that we do this is by faithfully stewarding His global tithe and offering system. Together, we unite our gifts to God, and He uses them to support and grow His work across the planet.

### Appeal

Our tithe and offerings today, as always, are given to God through the church, not to the church to meet its needs. All our giving is a love response to God, and He asks us to show our love by giving to Him and His work through His church. Thank you for faithfully giving to God’s global mission through your tithe and offerings today.
