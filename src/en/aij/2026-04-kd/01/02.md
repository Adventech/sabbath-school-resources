The water lapped gently against the boat, as Jesus finished telling the people His very last story. The people smiled back at Jesus. They could see He loved them so, so much. It had been a very good day.

It had been a very long day too. The moon glowed softly in the darkening sky and Jesus stopped and turned to His disciples. What did He say? **Mark 4:35.**

Jesus looked tired. He sounded tired. The disciples thought that going across the lake was a good idea. It was quiet on the other side. Not as many people lived there. It would be a good place to stop, be alone, and rest.

Climbing into the boat with Jesus, the disciples pushed the boat out into the deep water. Some people from the crowd didn’t want Jesus to go, so they scrambled into the other boats and followed Him. The little group of sailing boats floated closely behind Jesus like a mother duck with her little ducklings.

The stars twinkled. There was a lovely quietness that put God’s peace into everyone’s tired hearts.

Jesus found a spot at the back of the boat and lay down, resting His head on a soft pillow. It wasn’t long before the gentle rocking of the boat made Jesus fall into a deep sleep.

After a busy day, your body needs to go to sleep too. When you say goodnight, and quietly lay your head on your pillow, you can thank Jesus for being with you as you sleep. The Bible says, “I go to bed and sleep in peace. Lord, only you keep me safe” (Psalm 4:8, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of a time when you were so tired you could barely keep your eyes open?
- How does it make you feel to know that Jesus is with us when we sleep?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Lie down on the floor and be peaceful and quiet. Can you hear your heartbeat? Can you hear yourself breathing? Quietly sing your favorite prayer song.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for sleep that helps your body grow strong and healthy.
