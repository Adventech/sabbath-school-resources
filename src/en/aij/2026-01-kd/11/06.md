Abram and Sarai made lots of friends in Canaan. They told all their friends about God and His love for everyone.

The people in Canaan noticed that Abram was different from other people. He was happy, friendly, and kind. He put others first, instead of himself. He spoke wise words about God. And he left “thank-You” altars to God everywhere he went.

Abram _was_ different—he had God’s love in his heart! It made him stand out like a bright light that shines in the darkness.

The Bible says, “Be a light for other people. Live so that they will see the good things you do. Live so that they will praise your Father in heaven” (Matthew 5:16, ICB).

With God’s love in your heart, you can be a bright light too. You can smile at others, say hello, speak caring words, show kindness, and tell others about your Best Friend Jesus. When you do these things, you are shining God’s love.

Who knows, maybe others will notice and want to make Jesus their best friend too!
