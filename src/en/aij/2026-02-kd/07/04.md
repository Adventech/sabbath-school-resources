Joseph sat at the bottom of the dark pit, bruised and sore. He cried. His sad feelings hurt even more than the scrapes on his body. How could his brothers be so unkind?

He could hear his brothers talking and laughing as they sat down to eat the food that he had brought for them. Joseph called out for help, but they just ignored him.

As they ate, the brothers noticed a long line of camels coming closer and closer. The smell of spices drifted on the breeze. The camels carried Ishmaelite traders traveling to Egypt to sell spices and other sweet-smelling things.

As they watched, brother Judah came up with an idea. Without hurting Joseph, they could still get rid of him for good. How? **Read Genesis 37:27.**

Big brother Reuben wasn’t with them. But the other brothers nodded. Yes! What a great idea. As the traders came closer the brothers waved them down and made a deal to sell their brother. In those days, there was always a wealthy family in Egypt who wanted a slave to work for them. A young, healthy slave would make the traders lots of money.

The brothers reached into the dark pit and pulled poor Joseph out. Then they sold him for 20 pieces of silver.

Joseph couldn’t believe it! He knew his life had just changed forever.
