**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **globe/world map**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **note cards** or make from cardstock or recycle some cardboard. Draw symbols that children in your culture will recognize. Heart (love); peace symbol; cross (Jesus/Christianity); thumbs-up (good/I like it); power button (power); monetary symbol; star; Wifi symbol; arrow (go this way); magnifying glass (search); signs for male and female; recycling sign; Olympic rings; pedestrian walking sign; music symbol; ampersand (and); thought bubble (thinking); eternity sign. Hide these around your room or area, or outside, before children arrive.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Create a dramatic rescue scene using **small toys** such as Lego figurines. Place them in **gelatin** before letting it set. **Alternatively, place them inside a container** and put in a **tub of either sensory items or toys**. Give the children tools to free them: **tongs, spoons, string, cloths**, etc.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Mark out the floor plan of the sanctuary with **masking tape or chalk**, big enough for the children to walk through (if possible). Mark each item of furniture with a piece of tape in its correct location.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: **white cloth, a toy lamb, candle, incense, Bible or picture of a Bible**.

Print **^[sanctuary symbol cards]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
