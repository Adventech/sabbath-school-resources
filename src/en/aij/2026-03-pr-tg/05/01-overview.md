**Word of the Week**

**Mature:** To mature is to grow up to become responsible and fully developed. People grow to be physically mature, as well as mature in their thinking, feelings, and how they speak and act. Some people mature more quickly than others. We can also say some children are mature for their age.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores how Jesus grew and matured, learning God’s ways from nature, His family, and the Holy Scriptures. He did not learn from the rabbis, taking little notice of their ceremonies and traditions. He chose instead to learn from the Word of God. He always did His best in everything and was the perfect example of how to relate to others, always helping others in any way He could.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they can grow to be like Jesus.
- **Feel** they can learn from Jesus, because He is our example.
- **Respond** by spending time with Jesus and learning more about His ways so that they can become more like Him each day with His help.
