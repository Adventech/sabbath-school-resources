Cain and Abel stood up tall beside Adam. They had grown so much! They weren’t little boys anymore.

They had lots of things to do on their own now. And lots of choices to make. Would they obey their mother when she asked them to collect a jar of water? Would they speak kindly to each other and use gentle hands? Would they tell their father the truth? Would they work hard? Every choice they made changed them little by little.

Big brother Cain chose not to listen to anyone else because he thought his way was best. He even began to think that he was good enough and strong enough to save himself. Adam tried to help Cain make better choices, but Cain wouldn’t listen.

As Abel grew bigger, he chose to learn about God and get to know Him. He chose to obey God and his parents. Following God’s rules of love made him happy.

Every day Abel grew more humble, gentle, and loving toward God and his family.

God is always there to help us make good choices. He says, “I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you” (Isaiah 41:10).

Choosing God’s way is always the best way. What does the Bible say about this? Read the first part of Joshua 24:15.

^[Parent Faith Talk]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Wrap sewing thread once around your child’s wrist. See if they can break it. Wrap another length around twice. Can they break it? Then wrap it 10-15 times and see if they can break it. Now it’s really strong! One bad choice like Cain’s would be easy to “break.” But repeating it can become a habit that is hard to break.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you think others in the family felt when Cain chose his own ways?
- How do you think others in the family felt when Abel chose to follow and trust God?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
