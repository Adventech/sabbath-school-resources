^[Genuine acts of kindness, compassion, and fellowship]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) can lead people to open their hearts to learning aboutGod or the Bible. Here are several ways to develop ^[friendship]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interests and to continue building a trusting relationship throughoutthe disciple-making journey:

- ^[Mingle and visit, strike up a conversation, and be a sympathizing listener.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Invite your friend home for a meal or to do a project or activity together.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Minister to a need (prepare a meal, babysit, etc.).]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Give a gift that would be meaningful.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Invite your friend to a cooking school or social event at your church.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
