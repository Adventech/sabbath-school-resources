{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God brought Jacob home to Haran and gave him 12 sons. Joseph loved and obeyed God. He bravely told the truth about his brothers’ bad behavior. Jacob made Joseph a coat of many colors as a gift to show his love. Jacob’s favoritism made his older sons jealous and hurtful toward Joseph. But God cared. He sent two dreams that showed His great plans for Joseph’s life.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God cares for them.
- **Feel** they are precious to God.
- **Respond** by choosing to trust God.
