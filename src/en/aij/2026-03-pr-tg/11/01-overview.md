**Nurture:** To nurture is to care for, protect, and encourage someone (or a living thing) while they are growing and developing.

**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the way Jesus interacted with His disciples and the people who came to listen to Him teach. He showed love and compassion and a desire to nurture their faith. He wanted His disciples to share God’s love with others, to help grow more disciples. Jesus showed through His actions how He wanted his disciples to interact with others. Through their mission of serving, loving, teaching, and giving, His disciples were asked to show what God’s kingdom is like.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that as a follower of Jesus, they are one of His disciples.
- **Feel** a desire to spend time with God, to learn more about Him so they can grow as His disciples.
- **Respond** by sharing what they have learned with others to help others become disciples.
