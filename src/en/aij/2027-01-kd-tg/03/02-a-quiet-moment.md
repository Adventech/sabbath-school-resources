**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

What a wonderful message for the world—our God is mighty to save. He sees us in our distress, He feels the impacts of the brokenness around us, and He responds. He doesn’t just empathize. God acts in powerful ways to liberate and save us.

God’s love and power are inextricably linked. Even as He brought devastating consequences upon Egypt for their oppression and cruelty, He warned and made provision for their protection should they choose to put their trust in Him. Over in Goshen, the land of the Israelites, His loving care was clearly on show. Here was a God that was not only powerful but also protective and loving of His own.

“The Lord your God in your midst, the Mighty One, will save. He will rejoice over you with gladness, He will quiet you with His love, He will rejoice over you with singing” (Zephaniah 3:17).

You are His chosen child. He has displayed His power at the cross and saved you by His grace. How will you respond this week?

Like Moses, may you grow in faith, choosing to trust and obey Him through the challenges. May the children in your care also come to know that He is their loving God who is mighty to save.
