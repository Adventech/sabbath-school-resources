**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The building of a huge wooden boat, the animals coming two by two, the angels’ protection, and of course the noisy floating zoo all bring wonder and amazement to a little child’s heart. Woven through it all is a story of God’s incredible protection. It’s a story of His desire for us to faithfully follow Him and trust Him wholeheartedly in all things.

We know this verse by memory, “Trust in the Lord with all your heart” (Proverbs 3:5), but how challenging it can be to fully trust God when times are tough and we cannot see the end of the story. Ever since the Fall the human heart has easily been influenced by pride, control, and self-sufficiency. This is the very opposite of what God wants for us! Noah was an incredible example of someone who trusted God at all times. When the world around him was steeped in sin, Noah trusted God. When God called Him to build a boat, Noah trusted God. When He was ridiculed, mocked, and made fun of, Noah trusted God. When He waited patiently for seven days for the rain to come, Noah trusted God. During the 40 stormy days and nights, Noah trusted God. And in the many, many days of waiting in the gentle, rocking boat, Noah trusted God.

As you teach the little hearts in your class about Noah’s trust in God, may you be inspired to grow closer to the God who cares about the big things and small things in your life. This is our God, a God who saves the animals and humanity on a rocking boat, decorates the sky with rainbows, and faithfully protects His children. This is your God, a God who provides for you, protects you, and loves you with His overwhelming, overflowing, eternity-stretching love. May you feel His presence close to you as you share His love for His little ones this quarter.
