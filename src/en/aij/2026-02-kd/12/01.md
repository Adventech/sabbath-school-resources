**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. I will let His love show.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Matthew 5:16)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Shine”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - The moon shines in the night sky. When something shines, it helps us to see the things around us. When we have God’s love in our hearts, we will want to show it—we’ll shine like bright lights. We can show God’s love to others by being kind, happy, and caring.

A Big Move

In a little town called Bethlehem, in Canaan, a hungry family busily packed their bags. They rolled up their clothes in blankets. They packed their sacks with their best tools, pots, bowls, and spoons. Then they packed the very last little bit of food they had.

It was many, many years after Joseph’s family had moved from Canaan to Egypt. And now there was another time of hunger for God’s people in Canaan. The farms were dry. The food was gone. Everyone was hungry!

Just like Jacob’s family, this little family decided to move away to find food. What were their names? **Read Ruth1:2.**

With a big wave goodbye to all their neighbors, the family set off. They had heard that there were fields full of food in a faraway country called Moab. It was a long, long walk to get there. But God had promised Jacob, “I am with you, and I will protect you everywhere you go” (Genesis 28:15, ICB). This promise was for them too.

As they walked they thought about the people of Moab. They didn’t believe in God, they worshipped idols. Elimelech and Naomi would show their new neighbors God’s love—and maybe they would learn to love God too! The Bible says, “Let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven” (Matthew 5:16).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you think Mahlon and Chilion felt as they packed their bags and began the long trip to another country?
- Have you ever moved? What was it like?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Pray for someone who is moving away or has recently moved to your town.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Plan something kind for someone who is new to the area where you live. Invite them to your house, make them a welcome card, or take them some food.
