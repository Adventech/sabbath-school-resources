**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He can keep me calm.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a tub of water as the lake with a toy boat, or plastic containers as boats, for Jesus and one or two others.
- Use Jesus figure or peg person/people or a felt set, if available.
- Flashlight
- Create storm sounds (or source online) and use a spray bottle of water. Jiggle tub for wild seas.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 1 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- A big crowd squished together beside the lake. They wanted to see and hear Jesus.
- Jesus had an idea. He got into a boat so they could all see. He told wonderful stories about God’s love. ^[(Place Jesus in “boat.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- It was time to rest. Jesus invited His disciples to hop into the boat and cross the lake. They nodded and climbed aboard. Other boats followed ^[(“boats” in tub of water)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- The stars twinkled above the lake. ^[(Turn flashlight on and off.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) All was calm and still. Jesus lay down in the boat and went to sleep.
- Suddenly a strong wind blew. Lightning flashed. Huge waves crashed. It was a very bad storm! ^[(Storm sounds, water spray.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The disciples tried to save their boat, but they weren’t strong enough. It was filling up with water!
- The disciples were scared. They forgot that Jesus was with them.
- They woke Jesus. Would He care? Would He help them? ^[(Yes!)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Jesus stood up and said, “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39). The storm stopped. All was calm and still ^[(no noise, calm water)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Everyone was amazed. Jesus had the power to stop the storm. He can do anything! We can trust Him to help us.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Stretching up tall on tiptoes, the children tried their very best to see Jesus. Elbows knocked as people squished together ^[(shuffle body)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Everyone wanted to be near Him.

Looking out at the big crowd beside the lake, Jesus had a wonderful idea. He walked over to a boat and climbed aboard. ^[(Place “Jesus” in the boat.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a loud, kind voice, He started to tell another story about God’s love. Now everyone could see Him and hear Him.

All day long the people by the lake listened closely to Jesus. He was the best storyteller! They didn’t want to go home—they wanted to hear just one more story . . .

Finally, as the sun was setting, Jesus looked at the lake and turning to His disciples, He said, “Let us cross over to the other side” (Mark 4:35).

The disciples nodded. It was quiet on the other side of the lake. After a long day, it would be a good place to rest and be alone.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Climbing into the boat with Jesus, the disciples pushed the boat out into the deep water. Some people from the crowd didn’t want Jesus to go, so they scrambled into the other boats and followed Him. ^[(Place more “boats” in tub of water.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The stars twinkled above the still lake. ^[(Turn flashlight on and off.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was quiet and peaceful out on the water. Jesus found a spot at the back of the boat and lay down, resting His head on a pillow. It wasn’t long before the gentle rocking of the boat made Jesus fall into a deep sleep. ^[(Lightly snore.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The moon shone a silver path across the peaceful Sea of Galilee. All was calm.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then suddenly a strong wind began to blow. Gentle waves grew into big waves and slapped against the boat. Dark clouds covered the stars. Crack! Jagged lightning lit up the sky as thunder rumbled. Huge waves crashed against the boat ^[(storm sounds, water spray)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The disciples became worried . . . then scared . . . then very, very frightened. This was a dangerous storm!

They used all their muscles to try to save themselves and their boat. But they weren’t strong enough. The storm was too big! “The waves began coming over the sides and into the boat. It was almost full of water” (Mark 4:37, ICB).

The disciples were scared. They had forgotten something very important. **What did they forget?** ^[(Pause for the children to respond.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus was with them in their boat. He wasn’t scared of the cloudy darkness or the noisy wind. He had made the wind and waves at Creation. In fact, Jesus was so peaceful, He was still fast asleep.

Holding on to the rocking boat, the disciples went to Jesus and woke Him. “Teacher, do you care about us? We will drown!” (Mark 4:38, ICB). **Did Jesus care? Do you think He would let them drown?**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus stood up and faced the angry storm. He lifted His strong hands above the crashing waves. And with a strong, calm voice He told the wind and the waves to stop.

“Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39).

The wind listened to Jesus’ voice. It stopped howling. The waves listened to Jesus’ voice. They grew still. ^[(Stop storm sounds, calm water.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Everything was calm and still.

The dark clouds rolled away, and the twinkling stars came out again in the clear, night sky. ^[(Turn flashlight on and off.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The disciples looked at Jesus with big, wondering eyes. Jesus had stopped the storm with just His words! Everyone was amazed. Jesus, the kind teacher and healer, also had power to tell nature what to do.

In the stillness the disciples’ voices whispered, “Who can this be, that even the wind and sea obey Him!” (Mark 4:41).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) We know the answer! Jesus is God. He is all powerful. He can do anything! We can always trust Him, and His promises in the Bible, to help us. He makes our hearts peaceful.
