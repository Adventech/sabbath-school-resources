Spending time together as a family is a great way to strengthen relationships. When we make time for each other, we make time for love.

This Sabbath afternoon, do something your family enjoys, or choose one of the options below. Have fun and be blessed!

- Go bird-watching.
- Go for a walk in the forest, on the beach, or at a favorite hiking area.
- Visit a park or gardens. Look for something in nature for every color of the rainbow, or for as many letters of the alphabet as you can. Photograph the objects and view them together when you get home.
- Draw or paint self-portraits. Cut them out and glue them onto a background to create a family portrait. Frame the family portrait in some special way.

If you have time, wrap a small gift (preferably something the family can share) a number of times, placing a clue that identifies a particular family member between some of the layers. For example, “Makes us laugh” or “Gives the best hugs.” Play a game of “pass the parcel”: The family sits in a circle and passes the parcel around the circle, unwrapping a layer before passing the parcel on. When a family member sees a clue after unwrapping a layer, they read out the clue for others to guess. Continue until the gift is unwrapped. Enjoy it together.
