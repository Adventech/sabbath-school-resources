**Word of the Week**

**Courage:** Courage is being able to do something even when it seems scary or hard. To have courage is to be brave. When we have our big, strong God by our side, we can be filled with courage knowing He will help us.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jacob sent Joseph on a journey to check on his brothers who were caring for the sheep in a distant area. Joseph bravely set off to find them. Alone with Joseph, the jealous brothers decided to kill him. Reuben intervened and convinced them to throw him into a deep, dark pit, intending to rescue him later. While Reuben was tending the sheep, Judah and his brothers sold Joseph as a slave to traders who were traveling to Egypt. The brothers returned home and deceived their father into believing that Joseph had been killed by a wild animal. God was with Joseph through it all.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is with them when they go through hard times.
- **Feel** courageous, knowing that God will help them.
- **Respond** by thanking God that He is with them.
