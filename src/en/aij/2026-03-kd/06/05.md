People smiled and talked excitedly by the river. They had said they were sorry for their sins, and God had forgiven them. With clean hearts and happy faces they were baptized.

Arriving at the river, Jesus passed the happy crowds and went straight toward John the Baptist. As soon as John saw Jesus, he realized that Jesus was different from all the other people. Everything about Jesus was pure and good and holy.

Jesus asked John to baptize Him, just as he was baptizing the people.

Jesus didn’t need to be sorry and ask for forgiveness. His heart was already completely clean. But Jesus wanted to be our example. He wanted to teach us about God’s forgiveness and power to make our hearts clean.

John realized that Jesus was the promised Savior, because after Jesus was baptized, something amazing happened. A very bright light poured down from heaven, from God, straight onto Jesus. This was God’s chosen Son!

While the light shone, the Holy Spirit also came down and rested on Jesus. What did the Holy Spirit look like? **Read Matthew 3:16.**

Then God’s voice spoke from the sky, saying, “You are My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11).

God is very pleased when we are sorry and ask for His forgiveness. He loves you and promises to wash you clean.
