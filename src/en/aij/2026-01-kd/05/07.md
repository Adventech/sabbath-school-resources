Molly raced out to the yard with a happy shout. The 7-year-old, from Florida in the United States, loved playing outside.

As Molly started to play, her grandmother followed behind her. And so did Haus, her big German shepherd dog. Wherever Molly went, Haus went too. Haus loved Molly. He was always close behind her, keeping her safe.

Suddenly Haus heard a rattling noise. His ears pricked up as he noticed something slithering in the grass. It was a rattlesnake!

Faster than lightning, Haus ran toward the deadly snake. The brave dog stood between the snake and Molly. The snake bit Haus. But Haus would not back away. He would protect Molly no matter what!

Finally Molly’s furry friend won—he saved Molly! But he had been bitten three times by the deadly rattlesnake. Haus was very hurt. Quickly Molly’s family raced him to the vet.

The vet worked hard to save Haus’ life. The dog needed a lot of care, and it cost a lot of money. People from all over the country heard about Molly’s brave dog. Haus was a hero! They wanted to help—so together they paid the vet bills. Everyone was so happy to see how much this dog loved Molly.

Finally Haus came home. It was a miracle! Molly was so excited to welcome home her brave dog. He had been willing to die to save her.

_Jesus is a lot like Haus. His heart is full and overflowing with love for you. Jesus’ love is so big that He was willing to have a big battle with a serpent named Satan. He even died so you could live. What a wonderful, loving Savior!_
