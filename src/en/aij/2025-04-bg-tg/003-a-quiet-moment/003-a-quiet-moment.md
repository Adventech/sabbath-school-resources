**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

What is on your “to do” list this week? Perhaps a job, housework, caring for family members, exercise, eating well, and maybe even other church responsibilities. At times our lists can feel far too long, and sometimes we need to eliminate and concentrate on what is truly important.

When your spouse, friend, or child helps you with your tasks, it can lift your spirits. In our rush the kind help of a stranger can feel like the hand of God reaching down to remind us that He sees and He is near.

In the same way, when you help others, you reflect God’s care to them. “We are to look upon every duty, however humble, as sacred because it is a part of God’s service. Our daily prayer should be, ‘Lord, help me to do my best. Teach me how to do better work. Give me energy and cheerfulness. Help me to bring into my service the loving ministry of the Savior,’ ” (Ellen G. White, The Ministry of Healing, p. 474). Picture Jesus gently offering His strong, kind hand to you, speaking to your heart, and encouraging you that “whatever you do, do heartily, as to [Me], and not to men” (Colossians 3:23).

As you teach the children in your Sabbath School class to be God’s helpers this quarter, remind them that they can be a powerful witness, even at their tender age. As you dedicate your time in helping His work this quarter, ask God to shine His beautiful light into your willing heart so that they too will want to serve God wholeheartedly.
