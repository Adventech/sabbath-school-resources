**Opening Activities**

Source a list of props and jobs to mix up for “Guess My Job.” Choose whatever is handy for you or relates to your culture. Some examples include:

- A painter: paintbrush, smock
- Sportsperson: sporting equipment
- Cook: apron and wooden spoon
- Gardener: big hat, shovel, seeds
- Doctor/nurse: first-aid kit, bandages
- Builder: tool belt or tool kit

Write out the jobs so you can whisper them to the children. You may like to place the props together or in a bag, so you can quickly mismatch them to each child (a tennis racket to the painter).

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: two pieces of colorful paper** (letter or A4) and a **glue stick**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
