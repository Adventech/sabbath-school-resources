The fruit didn’t taste good for long. Tears stung their eyes as Adam and Eve sat with shoulders drooped. A deep sadness filled their hearts, along with guilt and shame.

Blinking back tears, they realized God’s soft covering of light had disappeared from around their bodies. They felt lonely, chilled, and naked! Fumbling with fig leaves, they tried hard to make themselves some clothes. Then what did Adam and Eve hear, and what did they do? Read Genesis 3:8.

Guilt, fear, and shame drove them to run and hide from God. Their choice to disobey Him was sin, and sin had changed them inside. They didn’t know how much God’s heart ached with sorrow and love for them.

God knew how Adam and Eve felt, and He knew where they were. Wanting them to choose to see Him, He gently called to Adam, “Where are you?” (Genesis 3:9).

Adam’s head popped up, and he wondered if his ears had heard right. Did God still want to be with them? In response to the kindness in God’s voice, they quit hiding in the shadows. With heads hung low, they came out to see their God. He looked at them with eyes of both love and sorrow and then began to speak.

God asked what had happened, and the blaming began. Adam blamed Eve. Eve blamed the serpent. But the serpent had no one to blame. They had all hurt God by not trusting Him. Adam and Eve had disobeyed, and terrible consequences would follow. But God hadn’t left them! And His presence calmed their hearts.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

_God makes a beautiful promise in Deuteronomy 31:6, ICB: “He will not leave you or forget you.”_

- _How might Adam and Eve have felt if they could have read this promise?_
- _How do you feel, knowing that God will never leave you, even when you make a bad choice?_
- _Why is it good to know that God is always willing to forgive?_
