His piercing cries echoed through the mud-brick house. Baby Moses was tired and hungry, and he needed his mommy now! Quickly Mother Jochebed rushed to his side. He must be quiet—soldiers were near! She prayed desperately as she soothed her baby boy. He had grown bigger and louder over the three months since his birth. It was getting too hard to hide him!

Pharaoh had ordered all the Hebrew baby boys to be thrown into the Nile River. No way would Jochebed let her baby be killed. Instead, God gave her a plan: she would hide him in the river, and pray that God would keep him safe.

Jochebed took some tall papyrus reeds that grew along the riverbank and wove a little basket boat. Then she covered it with pitch to make it waterproof. Baby Moses was tucked snuggly into the little basket boat and taken down to the river to rock gently on the water among the reeds.

Sister Miriam watched and waited “afar off, to know what would be done to him” (Exodus 2:4). She wasn’t the only one watching this special child. Unseen angels hovered above the little floating boat and kept the precious baby safe.

Mother Jochebed had prepared a safe little boat for Moses. Miriam watched over him, and the angels kept him safe. Through all of them, God was protecting and preparing Moses for the big job he would one day do for Him.

God protects and prepares us as we grow up too. He may have big or small jobs for us to do one day, but all are important and part of God’s plan—just like the jobs Mother Jochebed and Miriam did that day in Egypt.
