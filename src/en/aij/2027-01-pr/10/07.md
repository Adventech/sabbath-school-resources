Ellina [Eh-LEE-na] was nervous. Her family had moved to a new town in Kazakhstan, and she would attend a very big public school. It was so big that some students went to school in the morning, and the rest went to school in the afternoon. Everyone went to school six days a week, with only Sunday off.

The 13-year-old was happy to miss school on Saturday. She loved God with all her heart and wanted to respect His Sabbath. But she hoped and prayed that she wouldn’t be put into Friday afternoon classes and need to miss another day!

Sadly, she was assigned to the afternoon class schedule, and the principal refused to move her.

When the sun began setting too early for her to attend classes on Friday, Ellina missed classes on Fridays as well as on Sabbaths. She worked extra hard to keep up with her classmates, but she didn’t complain. She tried to show God’s love to both her teachers and classmates.

One day one of the boys in Ellina’s class misbehaved while the teacher was out. He took another child’s book bag, pulled out their gym clothes, and stomped on the bag. Then as the class watched, he threw the bag and its contents out a window.

The principal learned what had happened, but she didn’t know who had thrown the bag out the window. She called the students who had been in the classroom into her office. Ellina prayed as she walked down the hall.

In the office the principal asked the children who had thrown the bag out the window. No one wanted to answer, because they were afraid of the boy who had done the wrong thing. Then the principal looked at Ellina. “I know someone who doesn’t lie,” she said. “Ellina, can you tell me what happened in class today?”

Ellina was nervous, but she knew that God was with her. He would help her show respect and honesty. She stood before the principal and told her what had happened.

A few days later Ellina’s teacher called her to her desk and gave her a paper. “The principal is transferring you to the morning session,” the teacher said.

Ellina smiled and thanked the teacher. God had helped her to be honest and show His love and respect to others. And now He had made a way for her to attend school on Fridays as well!
