A captive in Mesopotamia spends the night in a den of ravenous lions yet emerges the next day in perfect health. Six centuries later another captive is exiled to an island in the Aegean Sea, where he claims to have seen the future in astonishing detail. And both men, though separated by vast amounts of time and space, end up writing prophetic books detailing world events literally hundreds of years in advance—and every prophecy they made has come to pass exactly as predicted.

These two ancient prophetic books, known respectively as “The Book of Daniel” and “The Book of Revelation,” are published here for the first time in this explanatory format. Together, they have guided heads of state, predicted accurately the rise and fall of global empires, and in so doing have inspired countless millions to take their message seriously. In fact, the predictions these books make have been amazingly fulfilled.

Furthermore, Daniel and Revelation predict things about our day soon to take place. The oppression we see all over the world— and the oppression that perhaps even you or those you care about are suffering from right now—is soon to cease in history’s grand climax. _Justice_ is coming; evil is about to end. Given these books’ track record of fulfilled predictions, the thoughtful reader must take them seriously.

But these two books speak of more than justice; they also specialize in _goodness_. Is not a lack of goodness one of the greatest weaknesses in our world today? Countries conquer one another for the sake of sheer ambition. Families are destroyed due to selfishness and greed. Personal relationships so often falter and crumble because of a lack of compassion and empathy. There is a profound lack of goodness on our planet!

The books of Daniel and Revelation provide the solution to this global problem. Its writers knew firsthand what it meant to live under the oppression of ego-driven societies. And they also experienced firsthand the only answer to selfishness that lasts. Their lives were personally transformed. They learned how they themselves—and anyone else who is willing—can finally become the kind of person the world desperately needs: a person of justice, mercy, honor, compassion, dignity, respect, power, and wisdom. In other words, they found the secret to being a _good_ person. And you can too, if you read and apply this book.

These two books also speak of a final theme, the greatest theme of all: _love_. Can you imagine how different the world would be if even half of our planet were populated by people who first and foremost were people of love? We of course are not speaking merely of romantic love, but rather the kind of love that consistently places others first; the love that sacrifices itself for the well-being of another; the love that tirelessly pursues the betterment of others before itself.

Such love, as good as it is, seems like a mere dream, doesn’t it? But in the books of Daniel and Revelation, not only is such love revealed, but its Source is revealed. And that Source is made available to you, if you are willing to accept it.

So keep reading and rest assured that the adventure of a lifetime awaits you in the following pages. Best of all, justice, goodness, and love can become yours along the way.

### How to Use This Book

Both Daniel and Revelation are organized by chapters and verses. Daniel has 12 chapters and Revelation 22 chapters. The numbers of the chapters are featured with a bigger font size while the verses that are part of the chapters are numbered with a smaller font size. “Daniel 8:14,” for instance, is a reference to the eighth chapter of Daniel, verse 14, and “Revelation 14:6” refers to the fourteenth chapter of Revelation, verse 6.

This book is designed to give you an overview of the powerful themes the books of Daniel and Revelation present. While most of the verses are self-explanatory, some of them require some clarification. Thus, you will find notes throughout this book illuminating the more difficult parts. They appear with a different font right below the verses. Since you may have some unanswered questions, opportunities for additional study are available through QR codes you can scan with any camera-enabled device.

History’s grand conclusion is coming soon! And these two books, read thoughtfully and thoroughly, can help us understand what is just ahead and become people of justice, goodness, and love who are ready for that time.
