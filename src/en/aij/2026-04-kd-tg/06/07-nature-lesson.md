**Focus:** The luna moth shows us how Jesus can change us when we trust in Him.

Ask: **I wonder what special changes happen to a luna moth in its lifetime?**

_(Use a sock and something to stuff inside it to illustrate each time the caterpillar grows.)_

Did you know that some animals change a lot? Some animals start out one way and end up looking very different! One of them starts out as a tiny little egg. This tiny egg is laid very carefully on top of an oak, hickory, walnut, or willow leaf in a tree. The mother Luna Moth carefully chooses this very special leaf so that when the little baby hatches, it will have something delicious to eat.

After some time—and a little wriggling, a little squirming—out comes a beautiful, bright-green caterpillar. _Munch, munch, munch._ Yummy leaves for its lunch! The caterpillar eats and eats from morning till night. Just as you outgrow your clothes, the luna moth caterpillar outgrows its skin, and a larger, brighter caterpillar appears! This happens four times (hold up four fingers). Each time, the caterpillar changes and grows bigger.

Eight weeks later the caterpillar now looks for a place to rest. It spins a silvery cocoon and wraps a leaf around it for protection. This little cocoon is going to be the caterpillar’s very cozy little home to be all snug inside for the winter.

Something amazing and wonderful happens while the caterpillar is inside. Change is happening! As the days become warmer, the luna moth wakes up and comes out of the cocoon. It’s not a fat caterpillar anymore. Little wrinkled wings start to unfold and dry. With a flitter and a flutter, out comes a beautiful green luna moth. God changed it from a fat, wriggly caterpillar into a graceful moth!

**Teaching Point:** Just as Jesus made the caterpillar change into a beautiful luna moth, Jesus can help us grow and change when we have faith in Him.
