Isso wasn’t long antes Lucifer whispered seu thoughts a other Anjos. Ele was very clever e made isso sound as se ele were just trying a ajude Deus com some new ideas. Ele told the Anjos that eles didn’t really need Deus’s laws, e that Céu would be a much better place se eles could just Faça whatever eles wanted.

No one had heard anything like this antes! Lucifer worked closely com Deus as the leading Anjo, e todos the Anjos respected ele. Mas now eles were confused.

Some Anjos thought ele must know a lot more than eles did, e eles started a believe seu gossip. Others were very upset a hear o que ele was Digaing sobre Deus.

Isso wasn’t long antes Lucifer became bolder, e ele gathered muitos Anjos around ele as ele whispered clever lies. O que Façaes the Bible Diga a whisperer will Faça? **Leia Provérbios 16:28.**

The Anjos had always been the best de friends, mas the whispered lies were damaging seu friendships.

The loyal Anjos were sad that seu happy home was becoming polluted. Eles loved Lucifer e tried a remind Ele that Deus was powerful, loving, e kind. Mas ele would not listen. Ele had become selfish e jealous. Ele didn’t care whom ele hurt, então long as ele got seu own way. Seu sneaky ways convinced a Terceiro de the Anjos a side com ele.
