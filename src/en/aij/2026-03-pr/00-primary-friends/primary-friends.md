Imagine living on the same street as Jesus when He was a boy. Maybe you would have played outside in the fields, or run to the stream, or searched for sticks to build a toy house together. Jesus would have been your very best friend—loyal, kind, thoughtful, and I know He would have shown you what God is like, because that’s one of the reasons He came to our earth. The Bible tells us that “The Son reflects the glory of God. He is an exact copy of God’s nature. He holds everything together with his powerful word.” (Hebrews 1:3, ICB).

If you’d lived in Bible times, I know Jesus would have been your best friend, ever.

It’s amazing to know that Jesus can be your best friend, even today. Although He’s not a boy living in your neighborhood, and you can’t play with Him outside, He’s still loyal, kind, and thoughtful, and so many other wonderful things that a best friend should be. And even though you can’t see Him, you can talk to Him when you pray, and He can talk to you when you hear His words in your Bible.

This quarter, you’ll learn about Jesus as a baby, a boy, and as a grown man. Like Jesus, you’ve grown from being a baby to a Primary kid, and one day you’ll be a grown-up too. All through these years of your life, Jesus will be your best friend. He loves you more than your mind will ever really understand, and He’ll always be there for you.

Jesus is our God but He’s also our best friend.

Let’s get to know Him more together,
