**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **beach ball** (deflated) and a large **bedsheet**. Blow up the beach ball. Have the children stand around the perimeter of the sheet, holding it about waist high. Invite them to imagine that the sheet is a lake of water and the ball is a boat. Tell them that all is calm. Have them make gentle “ripples” with the sheet to show calm water. Then tell them that the wind is starting to blow, and a storm is coming. Ask them to move the sheet higher and lower, faster and faster, to show this storm. Have them stop moving suddenly and hold the sheet still.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How would it feel to be in the boat on a calm day?**
- **How would you feel if you were in the boat during a wild storm?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s story is about Jesus’ friends who found themselves in a really big, scary storm. I wonder who might help them with their big feelings. Our **Big Idea** might give us a clue. Let’s say it together: **Jesus can do anything. He can keep me calm.**

Ask: **“What can Jesus do?”** (Anything!)

Invite the children to copy your actions as you say the **Big Idea** together again as an answer. **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He can keep me calm** [sit down with legs crossed]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** again and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal several small toy or handmade boats. Ask the children to share what they are afraid of. Label the “sails” on the boats with words or pictures to describe their responses. For example:

- The dark (use a dark-blue marker to color the sail)
- Thunder and lightning (draw a lightning bolt)
- Getting lost (draw a stick child by himself)
- Scary noises (draw an ear with lines projecting out from it)

Invite the children to gently place each boat into a container partly filled with water. Talk to the children about how the disciples felt when the wind began to blow. Invite a child to blow gently on the boats. Then have more children blow on the boats, creating a “storm.”

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})**

- **What happened to the boats when we blew on them?** (They rocked from side to side.)
- **Did they sink?** (No, they stayed afloat.)
- **Like these little boats, the disciples were safe in the scary storm. Why?** (Jesus was with them; Jesus helped them.)
- Show me what you can do when you feel afraid of the things we have written on our boats. (Have children mime praying to Jesus.)

**Teaching Point:** When we are scared of something, our hearts feel all stormy and upset. Jesus can make our hearts calm and peaceful, just as He did to the big storm on the water. We can call out to Jesus anytime, and He will always be there to help us.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He can keep me calm** [sit down with legs crossed]**.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 40: Florida Manatee.**

- **How would you describe this animal?**
- Florida manatees are big animals that live in the sea. They can weigh as much as six baby elephants!
- These gentle giants are also known as sea cows, as they like to eat sea grasses.
- They don’t hurt any other animals. They are friendly and even use hugging and kissing to greet each other.
- Manatees are very calm and don’t get upset easily.

Let’s ask Jesus to help us to be as calm as a Florida manatee this week.

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 40: Florida Manatee** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
