Create

Use paint or ink to stamp your child’s footprints on a blank piece of paper. Share how we can follow Jesus by doing the kinds of things He loved to do: being kind and caring to those around us, helping, showing love with our words and actions, and obeying. Write or draw pictures of some things you could do to be like Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Play

Use a magnet, a string, and a stick to make a simple fishing rod. Put paper clips on some cut-out paper fish. Take turns catching fish.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explore

If there is snow, sand, or dirt nearby, go for a walk with your child. Go ahead of them and make footprints, encouraging your child to follow you by stepping in your footprints. When we follow Jesus we do kind and loving things, just as He did.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Connect

Set up an obstacle course. Let your child watch you complete the course, and then say, “Follow me.” Complete the course together. Jesus is there to help us with every hard or happy thing we face.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

If you'd like to, you can ask God to help you and your family to follow His kind and loving ways.
