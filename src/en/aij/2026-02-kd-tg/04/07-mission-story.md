**Focus:** Brothers Lukas and Thomas did their best for God—you can too.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Kyrgyzstan** on your **globe** or **world map**.

- **Do you know what this is?** (Hold up a paper plane, and then make another one in front of the children.)

Lukas and Thomas, two brothers who lived in Kyrgyzstan in central Asia, just loved making paper airplanes! They liked to fold the paper in new ways. Then they’d throw them and watch to see which plane would fly the highest . . . the farthest . . . the fastest!

The young boys also loved Jesus. Their daddy was a missionary at a Seventh-day Adventist school in Kyrgyzstan. It was his job to try to get extra money so more children could study there and learn about God. When the boys heard him praying for the school, they decided to work hard to help too!

They looked at their planes and knew just what to do. They’d make lots of paper planes and sell them! The boys set up a table at the school. Then they started to visit all the people who came into the school to work or study.

Daddy was so surprised! He was so happy they were choosing to do their best for Jesus. “Papa, would you buy an airplane?” the boys asked.

“Sure, I’ll buy one,” Daddy said.

The boys had many more customers. All the paper planes were sold!

The boys wanted to make more money. They built a special cardboard money box together and placed it inside the school so that people could give money to the school when they visited. Whenever little Lukas and Thomas received money for their birthdays, they also put it in the box to help!

The boys made a lot of money—US$150. God must have been smiling brightly at them for using their hands with their might!

- **What are some things you could do—with all your might—for God?** Repeat your memory verse one more time.
