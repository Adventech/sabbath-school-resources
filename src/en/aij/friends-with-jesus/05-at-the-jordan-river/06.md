Nishan bit his lip and tried not to go red in the face. He felt all mixed up and messy on the inside.

Mother sighed deeply. “I just can’t understand it,” she said. “There was a big, ripe, juicy mango right here this morning. But now it’s gone. Are you sure you don’t know anything about it, Nishan?”

He shook his head and quickly looked at the ground. “Can I go out and play now?” he asked quickly. Mother nodded slowly. She looked sad.

Nishan kicked the dirt. He thought about the mango he had taken. It had looked so sweet and good. But now he felt terrible. He had lied to Mother. She had been going to use the mango to make a special treat for Aunty. Nishan thought of his funny, favorite aunt and began to cry.

“Nishan?” Mother said, coming up behind him. “Is there anything bothering you?”

“I’m sorry,” Nishan sobbed. “I took the mango and lied about it.”

Mother gave him a hug. “I’m sad you would do that, but I forgive you,” she said. “Let’s stop now and tell Jesus you’re sorry too.”

“God loves to forgive us,” added Mother. “He says, ‘I am the One who forgives all your sins. . . . I will not remember your sins’ ” (Isaiah 43:25, ICB). Isn’t that good news?

Nishan nodded. He didn’t want to feel all messy on the inside. He wanted Jesus to make him feel clean and new. He dried his eyes and prayed, “Dear Jesus, I’m sorry I did wrong. I hurt Mother and Aunty. Please forgive me. Amen.” He opened his eyes and smiled.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- When we do something wrong, whom should we say we’re sorry to?
- Does God keep a list of your wrongs after you’ve said you’re sorry? No. What does today’s verse (Isaiah 43:25) say He does with them?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
