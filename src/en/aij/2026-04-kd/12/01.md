A Trip to Nain

As the warm sun rose early one morning, Jesus, His disciples, and a crowd of followers, packed their bags, put on their sandals, and said goodbye to their friends at Capernaum. They headed off down a dusty road on a special trip.

Where were they headed? **Read Luke 7:11.**

Nain means pleasant or lovely. Maybe it was called “lovely” because this little village sat high on a mountainside looking down on green and golden fields that stretched far into the distance. Maybe it was called “lovely” because of the kind, happy people who lived in the town. Whatever the case, Jesus and His followers were headed to the lovely village of Nain.

The disciples were always happy to be with Jesus. As they walked, they enjoyed listening to Him talk and teach them about the birds, trees, and flowers. Jesus always had a way of turning everything into a lesson about their beautiful God in heaven. Along the road Jesus would stop to heal the sick or speak words of kindness and love.

Wherever Jesus went, He would fill people with joy. The Bible says, “How beautiful is the person who comes over the mountains to bring good news” (Isaiah 52:7, ICB). Wherever Jesus went, He told people the good news of God’s love.

When you make Jesus your best Friend, you will want to share the good news of God’s love with others too.

The happy crowd came closer to the little town of Nain. Together they began walking up the sloping road toward the gates of the village. It made them so happy to walk and talk with Jesus.
