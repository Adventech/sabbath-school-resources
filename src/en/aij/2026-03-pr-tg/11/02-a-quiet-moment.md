**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

A simple definition of a disciple of Jesus could be “knowing Jesus, and making Jesus known.” The time we spend with Jesus changes us to be like Him. As He reaches out in love to others, we will reach out. As He saves, we will invest all our efforts in connecting people to our Savior.

“All who are imbued with His Spirit will love as He loved. The very principle that actuated Christ will actuate them in all their dealing one with another. This love is the evidence of their discipleship. ‘By this shall all men know that ye are my disciples,’ said Jesus, ‘if ye have love one to another.’ When men are bound together, not by force or self-interest, but by love, they show the working of an influence that is above every human influence” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 678).

When we are disciples, we will follow our Master’s example of selfless love. With His help and power, our actions and interactions can be characterized by love. This is not something that happens immediately. Jesus nurtured His disciples to grow in love, grace, and mercy. We too, can rely on Jesus to nurture us, even as we seek to nurture the children in our sphere of influence. This affects all our actions and interactions.

As you prepare this week’s lesson, may your love for the children overflow, as we are reminded how love for each other is a demonstration of being Jesus’ disciples.
