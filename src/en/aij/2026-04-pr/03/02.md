Good news flew down the streets of Capernaum, whipping up excitement as it went. Jesus was back in town! Everyone knew what that meant: miracles, healing, wonderful stories of God’s kingdom, comfort, and hope.

This was great news for the man with paralysis! His four friends rushed to tell him all about it. They barely paused for breath as they told him how others had been healed by Jesus. Even people with leprosy had been made well! His four faith-filled friends believed in Jesus. They encouraged him to believe that the miracle-working Teacher could cure him too.

The man’s hopes rose . . . then sank again. He remembered his bad choices and felt ashamed. What if Jesus doesn’t want to be near me? What if He treats me the way the priests and doctors have? What if He sends me away?

Would God treat him, or any of His children, like that? **Read Isaiah 41:10 to hear God’s answer.**

Surrounded by his four encouraging friends, the man decided that it didn’t matter so much if he wasn’t healed. What his heart desperately wanted was forgiveness.

He must see Jesus!

His friends smiled down at him. Hope was already changing their friend. They couldn’t wait to see what Jess would do for him.

God’s Word says, “A friend loves at all times” (Proverbs 17:17). The man with paralysis was blessed to have good friends who loved God and cared for him. They didn’t judge him; they wanted the best for him. They knew what he needed and encouraged him to believe in Jesus.

We can choose good friends like that, and we can also choose to be a good friend to someone else. “Some friends may ruin you. But a real friend will be more loyal than a brother” (Proverbs 18:24, ICB).
