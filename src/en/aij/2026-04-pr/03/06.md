His strong legs carried him all the way home. The healed man burst through the door, still praising God with every step. He was forgiven, healed, and home!

His family scarcely dared to believe what they saw! _Was this really the same man who was carried away this morning?_ they must have wondered. The whole family gathered around him with tears of joy. Just a short time ago they had seen him lying on his mat with sad, hopeless eyes, unable to move. Now his body glowed with strength and his eyes sparkled with happiness as he sang God’s praises.

His family must have burst into praise too. Jesus had given them a healthy, happy man in place of the old. With grateful hearts, this man and his family were ready to lay down their lives for Jesus. Nothing could shake their faith; nothing could dim their loyalty to Jesus. He had brought joy to their sad home. He was truly the friend of sinners (Matthew 11:19).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- The Bible says, “Greater love has no one than this, than to lay down one’s life for his friends” (John 15:13). No one died in this week’s story, so what could it mean to “lay down one’s life”? As you think about the friends and family of the man with paralysis, what do you think this verse means?
