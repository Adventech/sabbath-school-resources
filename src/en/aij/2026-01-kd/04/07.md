God loves us so much that He sends His angels to care for us, protect us, and help us. The Bible says, “The angel of the Lord encamps all around those who fear Him, and delivers them” (Psalm 34:7).

In the space to the right or on another piece of paper, draw and color a picture of one of God’s angels. But first, read these Bible clues about God’s faithful, helping angels.

- **^[What do they look like?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994cc"}}})** In the Bible, angels are very bright! They look as bright as lightning and bright fire (Matthew 28:3; Ezekiel 1:13, 14).
- **^[What about their clothes?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994cc"}}})** Matthew saw an angel with “clothing as white as snow” (Matthew 28:3). The covering angel, which had been Lucifer, was colorful, like bright-blue sapphires, red jasper, yellow topaz, and green beryl (Ezekiel 28:13). How will you color your angel?
- **^[Angels are fast (Daniel 9:21) and have wings to fly (Exodus 25:20).]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994cc"}}})** The Bible describes one type of angel as having six wings (Isaiah 6:2). The Bible describes another angel as having a sword (1 Chronicles 21:27).
- **^[What do they do?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994cc"}}})** “All the angels . . . serve God and are sent to help those who will receive salvation” (Hebrews 1:14, ICB). The Bible tells us they obey God and do what He asks (Psalm 103:20). Thank God for His happy, helping angels (Matthew 18:10).
