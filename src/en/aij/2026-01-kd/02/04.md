What a special first day it was for Adam on our earth! He listened to different birds sing. He watched the animals play. He smelled the beautiful, sweet flowers. He tasted yummy fruit. Everything was perfect! And God had made it all. Everything around him was perfect. Adam’s heart filled with so much love for God!

God had a special gift for Adam—a beautiful home. “God planted a garden . . . in a place called Eden” (Genesis 2:8, ICB). This garden would be a special place for Adam to live. It was filled with fields, forests, and flowers. A river flowed through Eden to water the plants. Adam felt so happy to care for his new garden home.

God gave Adam everything he needed to keep him healthy--nutritious food, a beautiful nature home, and he would exercise as he cared for the garden. God wants us to keep our bodies healthy today too.

God was so loving to Adam. What a wonderful time they had, walking together in the garden—smiling, talking, and noticing the beautiful things God had made. Then God told Adam that He had something very special for him to do. What was it? **Read Genesis 2:19.**

What a fun job! Imagine Adam pointing to the different animals and saying these names: “Penguin.” “Horse.” “Dolphin.” “Koala.” “Parrot.” “Leopard.” “Spider.” “Sloth.” The Bible says that “whatever Adam called each living creature, that was its name” (Genesis 2:19).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- How can you show that you are happy to help with the jobs you are given in your home?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask for God to help you to obey your mommy or daddy happily when they ask you to do special jobs for them around the house.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
See how quickly you can guess the names of these animals:

- I have soft fur and I hop.
- I have a long nose and big ears.
- I have the longest neck of all the animals.
- I can purr.
- I can jump and kick.
