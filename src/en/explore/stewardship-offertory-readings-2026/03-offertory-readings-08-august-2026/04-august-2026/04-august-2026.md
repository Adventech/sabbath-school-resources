---
title: Giving While You're Living
date: 22/08/2026
---

Have you ever heard the wise saying, “Do your giving while you’re living, so you know where it’s going”?

It’s good to leave money and investments to your family and God’s work when you die, but God also calls us to give generously while we’re alive.

Maybe you have heard about contested wills, where greedy beneficiaries ignore the wishes of the deceased. Or stories of mission-minded Christians who die and their estates are squandered by beneficiaries who just wanted another luxury holiday, another rental property, or some other self-focused acquisition.

As followers of Jesus Christ, we must seriously consider what He would have us do with His assets that He has entrusted us to manage, in both life and death. Should we leave them to our children, the church, or another entity? Is there a right way to divide God’s assets?

And related to this, should we hold tightly to our assets until we die, when they’re completely useless to us anyway? What would Jesus do?

In the book Counsels on Stewardship, Ellen G. White wrote, “Dying legacies are a miserable substitute for living benevolence. The servants of God should be making their wills every day, in good works and liberal offerings to God” (p. 326).

So, let’s hear that wise saying again, “Do your giving while you’re living, so you know where it’s going.”

### Appeal

As we now return God’s tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s remember that God is the Owner of everything and that, each day we are living, He invites us to be faithful stewards of all His gifts. Let’s do our giving while we’re living, so we know where it’s going. Thank you for your faithfulness.
