**Opening Activities**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source concealed **bag of toy animals ^[(or animal photos)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** that live in various habitats.\
Create animal “habitats” from colored **paper or fabrics**. E.g., blue for water, brown over boxes for mountains, green for plains, yellow for desert, and light blue for sky.\
Familiarize yourself with this quarter’s mission project/offering destination to share with the children.\
Print the **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and create a colorful **container to collect the offering in**.\
Source a **bell** or pretty **sound maker** to use to get the children’s attention before opening the Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box. E.g., a bell, toy flute, castanet, sealed cardboard tube filled with dried beans.\
Source **two boxes** (shoeboxes) to be used for Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box. **Secret Surprise Box: line the box with black paper.**\
Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: container of water, flashlight, bubbles, flowers and branches (or pictures of them), green blanket and herbs and fresh/toy fruit, silver stars, black blanket, moon, feathers and bird sounds, blue fabric, cardboard sunshine, toy birds, and fish.\
Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.\
Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a large collection (objects, pictures, or representations) of things created on Days 1-5 of Creation.\
Write the numbers 1-5 on separate pieces of paper. These will be used as labels during a sorting activity.

**Nature/Mission**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Print **^[Nature Card 1: Sunset]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child. Purchase or make a **Nature Collector Bag** for each child that can be hung on a wall or placed in a corner of the room. Write each child’s name on their bag.
