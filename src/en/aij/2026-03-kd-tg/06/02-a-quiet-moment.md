**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Jesus loves to forgive. He doesn’t want us to carry a heavy load of sin and regret throughout our lives, so He offers us forgiveness. God desires you to live a life of freedom a life forgiven. He promises to bury our sins in the deepest ocean (Micah 7:19), to remove them from us, as far as the east is from the west (Psalm 103:12). Could there be a better fix to the pain of wrongdoing, guilt, and regret? But first we must come to Him and confess.

God invites us to be honest with Him and avoid minimizing or justifying our wrongs. It is only when we see the truth about ourselves that we truly appreciate our need for our Savior. “The humble and broken heart, subdued by genuine repentance, will appreciate something of the love of God and the cost of calvary; and as a son confesses to a loving father, so will the true penitent bring all his sins before God” (Ellen G. White, Steps to Christ, p.41).

Truly: “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all unrighteousness” (1 John 1:9). What wonderful news for each of us, and for the children in our care! We will stumble. But God’s promise is to always, always, forgive us when we come to Him with repentant hearts.

What might you need to confess to Him today? Come, just as you are. He is waiting for you, ready to forgive and cleanse you once again.

May God guide you in sharing this beautiful, freeing truth with the children today.
