Frank slid the last suitcase into the back of the car. His wife, Elizabeth, climbed into the front seat. With a wag of his tail, their dog Bobbie jumped into the back. It was time to go!

Frank and Elizabeth had decided to make a very long trip across the United States to visit their family. This was a long time ago, in 1923. It was a big journey over mountains, past fields, and through a hot desert.

Finally, after many days of travel, Frank and Elizabeth arrived on the other side of the country. When they stopped for a rest, Bobbie jumped out of the car and began looking and sniffing around.

Suddenly a pack of three dogs ran toward Bobbie and attacked him. Poor Bobbie ran away into the nearby forest.

Back at the car, Frank and Elizabeth waited for Bobbie to return. “Bobbie, time to go,” Frank called. But no Bobbie came. They waited a very long time. Eventually they had to leave. They hoped Bobbie would use his sniffing skills to find them at their relative’s house not too far away.

Many days passed. It was now time for Frank and Elizabeth to return home. With sad hearts they began their long journey without their dog Bobbie.

Out in the forest, Bobbie was busy searching and seeking to find his owners again.

For days, weeks, and months, Bobbie traveled toward home, alone. He walked through tall fields of prickly grass, a dusty desert, and ice-cold snow. He swam through deep rivers. He loved his family. He would keep seeking them. Bobbie would not give up.

Finally, after six long months, Bobbie made it back across the country to home! As soon as he saw his family, Bobbie raced up to them and licked their faces. He’d found them!

Jesus wants us to love Him and seek after Him too. He promises, “And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart” (Jeremiah 29:13). We don’t have to walk to the other side of the country to seek Jesus. He is right here. But we can get closer to Him by putting Him first in our lives. We can do that by talking to Him every day, reading our Bible, and making good decisions that will lead us closer to Him.
