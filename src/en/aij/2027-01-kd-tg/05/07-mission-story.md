**Focus:** God does the impossible to heal Ales.

**Location:** Invite a child to help you find **Belarus** on a **map** or **globe**.

Ales was born in Belarus (in Eastern Europe) a month early. He didn’t weigh as much as other babies. When he was 3 months old, Mother took him to a doctor for tests. During those tests the doctor found that Ales could hear with only one ear.

Mother was shocked. She thought that the doctor must have made a mistake. She worked with children who couldn’t hear, so she also knew how to run hearing tests. She ran a hearing test on little Ales. The tests showed that Ales really couldn’t hear with both ears.

But Mother still didn’t want to believe it. She took Ales to a special hospital for children with hearing problems. The doctor did more hearing tests. Mother watched the tests on a screen. She saw deep inside Ales’ ears. One ear looked normal, but the other one did not. Mother saw that nothing could be done to help. She began to cry.

The doctor, however, didn’t want to see her tears. “Why are you crying?” he said gruffly. “Your son can hear with one ear, and that’s enough.”

Mother knew it would be impossible for her son to hear with both ears. But she loved a God who could do the impossible! She cried as she asked God to please heal her son. Every day she would pray for his healing. “You can do anything, God,” she prayed. “You gave life to my son. You can heal him.”

Three months passed, and Father had a birthday. Father didn’t want a cake for his birthday. He didn’t want any presents. He only wanted Ales to hear with both ears.

“Our son will hear,” he said to Mother. “God will do this as a gift for my birthday.”

He and Mother prayed and took Ales to the hospital for another hearing test. Finally the doctor called them in. When he finished the test, he said, “Your son can hear perfectly with both ears.”

Mother was so happy. “This is a miracle from God!” she cried.

Yes, it was a miracle. Mother had seen inside Ales’ ears on the screen three months earlier in the previous test. She had seen that no doctor could help Ales. It was impossible. But not with God!
