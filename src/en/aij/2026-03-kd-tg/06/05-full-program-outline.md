**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal the **backpack** or **bag** and some **heavy stones**.

Put the backpack on a child’s back (or use a bag) and add one stone after another until it feels heavy to them. Let other children feel the weight of the backpack or bag. Then take the stones out and let them feel how light and comfortable it now is.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What was it like to carry the heavy stones all by yourself, and how did it feel when all those stones were taken away?**
- **What does it feel like in your heart when you have done something wrong?**
- **What does it feel like when someone tells you that they forgive you?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain that when we have done wrong things we can start to feel sad and heavy, as if we are carrying a heavy load. Jesus loves us and wants us to feel light and happy, so He made a way to forgive our sins. We first tell Him that we’re sorry and that we need His help to not do that sin anymore. Jesus then forgives us. It’s like He empties all the heavy stones out of our bag and takes them away from us!

**Teaching Point:** God wants us to feel happy and loved; He is always ready to forgive us when we say we are sorry.

Today’s story is about John. He helped people get ready for Jesus by teaching them to be sorry for their sins, accept God’s forgiveness, and be baptized.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal the bubbles.

Give each child a bottle of bubbles—or let children take turns with one large bottle.

Say: Think about something that you want to ask God to forgive, then dip your bubble wand and blow bubbles. Imagine that the bubbles are the things that you have done wrong. Close your eyes. Imagine you’re asking God to forgive you for those sins. Now open your eyes and look for the bubbles!

- **Where are the sin bubbles? What has happened to our sins?** (They are gone.)
- **Will we be able to find them again and put them back in the bottle?** (No!)

**Teaching Point:** Just as we can never find the bubbles and put them back into the bottle, the Bible tells us that when we ask for forgiveness, God wipes away all our sins forever, so that they can never be found again. God is both fair and kind.

Stand and say the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.**
