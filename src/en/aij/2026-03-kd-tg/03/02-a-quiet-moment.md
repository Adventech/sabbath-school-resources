**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God often “hides” Himself in plain sight. Unlike our children, who tuck themselves behind doors or curtains or squeeze under beds, He wants to be found! God gave lots of clues to prepare people for the Messiah. There were prophecies that said He would come out of Bethlehem. He sent angels with clues to help the shepherds find Baby Jesus in a manger, and He sent a star for the Wise Men to follow.

And we can still seek and find Jesus today. One family asks each other every day, “Where did you see pictures of Jesus today?” and even the little children have answers! “When I watched the beautiful butterfly!” “When my brother was kind and helped me!” “When Daddy helped Mommy tidy the yard!”

This week, look around you each day for pictures of Jesus, and then write down where you found glimpses of Him. How does it change the way you see the world when you are always looking for Jesus’ love? Maybe you could share some of your experiences with the children in your class this Sabbath.

“Seek God with all the heart. . . . With intense earnestness learn the trade of seeking the rich blessings that God has promised, and with persevering, determined effort you shall have His light and His truth and His rich grace” (Ellen G. White, _Our Father Cares_, p. 114).
