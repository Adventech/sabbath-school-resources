**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **globe/world map**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Source **nature items, paper**, and **crayons or colored pencils** to use to do rubbings of the items.

Source a collection of **objects that represent commitment (e.g., baptismal certificate, wedding certificate, picture of bride and groom, calendar, sports team jersey)**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **peg people** (Moses, Joshua, Aaron); make a mountain by laying **fabric** over **containers** to create different levels; **blue paper or fabric**; a **toy cow** as the idol or make one out of **aluminum foil**. Alternatively, use a **felt set**.

Source **two pairs of shoes with laces**.
