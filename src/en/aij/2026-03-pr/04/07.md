Guided by an Eagle

As the hot sun beat down, Cynthia’s family became even more worried. Where was Uncle? How could they find him?

Cynthia is an Aboriginal girl who lives in Australia. Aborigines are the first people to have lived there.

At her Adventist school Cynthia learned that she can always ask God for help and guidance. She looked at the sky again—she really needed God’s help! Her aging uncle had wandered away from home. The family searched for him all day, but they couldn’t find him anywhere. Cynthia prayed that God would help them find her uncle.

The next day the searchers saw an eagle up on top of a hill. It was looking down toward a dry riverbed. The searchers decided to follow the eagle’s eyes, and soon they found their missing uncle.

“I thank God for using an eagle to help us find my uncle,” Cynthia said happily.

We can trust God to guide us with the big and little things in our lives too.

Guided by God’s Furry Angel

Six-year-old Menka’s only friend was her relative’s dog, Rani. The little girl from India had been taken away from her mother. Missing her mother terribly, she wanted to go back home.

Menka asked God to help her get home to her mother. One day Menka heard Rani barking, as if to say, “Follow me.” Menka chased Rani to the train station, where a train had just arrived. Menka couldn’t see Rani and thought he might be on the train. Menka climbed aboard to find Rani, and suddenly the train began to move.

When the train stopped, Menka got off and asked a station worker what town she was in. When the man told her, Menka’s eyes lit up. “My mommy lives here!” She told the man her mother’s name, and he took her home.

Mother hugged Menka tightly. “How did you get home?” Mother asked.

“I followed Rani to the train,” Menka said. “I couldn’t find him, and then the train started moving. When it stopped, I was here.”

“Hmm,” Mother said. “Perhaps Rani wasn’t a dog at all, but an angel—like a furry angel!”

Mother and Menka thanked God for sending an angel to guide Menka safely home.
