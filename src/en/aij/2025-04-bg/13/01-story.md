Far, far from Bethlehem, lived some Wise Men. They looked up into the dark night sky. What did they see way up high? _(Point to star.)_ It was a new star! The star was big. The star was bright. The star flickered like a shining light. The Wise Men were so happy to see the new star. They knew that it was a special star from God. It showed that a king had been born. Oh, how they wanted to meet Him! They packed their camels with some special treasures for the King. Then they set off to follow the star.\
_Plod, plod, plod_, went the camels. _Bumpity, bump, bump_, went the Wise Men on top. It was nighttime, but the Wise Men weren’t sleeping. They slept in the daytime. At night, they followed the big, bright star in the sky. They followed the star over the mountains and through winding rivers. They followed the star across hot, sandy deserts and grassy fields. _Plod, plod, plod_, went the camels. _Bumpity, bump, bump_, went the Wise Men on top. Days went by. Weeks went by. Every night, when the sky was bright, the Wise Men followed the star.

Finally, the star led the Wise Men all the way to Bethlehem. It stopped over a little house. The Wise Men climbed down from their camels. They brushed the dust off their beautiful robes. Then quickly they unpacked their special treasures. They were ready to meet the King. They were so happy, they clapped for joy _(clap)_. With big smiles, they knocked on the door. _Knock, knock, knock_. The door opened. Inside was Mary with little Jesus on her lap. The Wise Men had found the new King—Jesus!\
The Wise Men bowed before Jesus. Here was the King! They had looked forward to this day for a very long time. They were so happy! They knelt down in the little house and gave all their love to Jesus. Then they each gave Jesus their special gifts. These gifts showed that Jesus was a royal king. There was a chest of gold, two jars of special perfume called frankincense, and myrrh. The Wise Men left with happy hearts. They had seen King Jesus!

The Wise Men carefully followed the big, bright star. It led them to Jesus. Jesus is like a lovely bright star in our lives too. He wants us to follow Him. He shows us the best way to live and helps us make the right choices. When we follow Him, we choose His way, and become more like Him.
_(Say together)_ Dear God, thank You for the Wise Men who followed the star. I want to be like them and follow Jesus. Amen.
