**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Julia found an old shoebox that a friend had wrapped in pretty paper and left on her doorstep. Inside were lots of little packages. Each one had a little tag, reminding her that she was loved. There were tissues to remind her that God comforted her when she was sad, and hand cream to remind her that God soothed her when life felt rough. Every tiny gift had been chosen to illustrate God’s love for her.

Sometimes our best gifts arrive in unexpected packages. Maybe they come in plain boxes, wrapped in scraps of paper, or inside torn envelopes. But when we open the gifts, they contain a wonderful and unexpected delight! Similarly, Jesus was a surprising gift! No one expected a poor baby, wrapped in strips of cloth and sleeping in a manger, to be a special gift from God!

Jesus was the ultimate gift of love from our Father in heaven to our broken and hurting world. Jesus blessed not only His family and friends but every person who has ever lived. Jesus is the Messiah who saves us. “For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life” (John 3:16).

Jesus is a gift packed with humility, compassion, forgiveness, healing, comfort, encouragement, joy, peace, wisdom, hope, and so much more. Which of these gifts do you most need today as you share God’s love with the children in your Sabbath School class?

“The heart of God yearns over His earthly children with a love stronger than death. In giving up His Son, He has poured out to us all heaven in one gift. Through that gift there comes to us day by day the unfailing flow of Jehovah’s goodness” (Ellen G. White, _The Faith I Live By_, p. 45).
