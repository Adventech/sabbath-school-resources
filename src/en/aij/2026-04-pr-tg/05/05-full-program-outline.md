**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What do you think the word “compassion” means?** (Compassion is feeling sympathy and concern for the pain, sadness, or difficulties of others, and wanting to do something to help them. God shows us compassion and invites us to pass His love and kindness on to others.)

In pairs, invite the children to discuss one question each:

- **Did someone show compassion to me this past week? How?**
- **Did I show compassion to someone this past week? How?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Game: **Pass It On**

Invite the children to sit in a circle. Tell them you have a gift you will pass around while the music plays. Let’s imagine that the gift is called “compassion.” When the music stops, the person holding the gift can unwrap one layer. They will read out their card silently and act out what it says while the rest of the children guess their action. All children will receive a heart from the center.

Explain that our story this week teaches us how Jesus showed compassion to those around Him. We can learn from Him and show compassion to others just as He did, because “God’s love is for . . . all people.”

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus’ compassion for all people shows they are loved and accepted.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask an older child to come to the front and turn around—they are to guess a word via touch. Ask another child to come and slowly trace **“Love”** on their back (show the word written on paper to the child and class). If the guesser gets stuck, switch to a symbol: **a heart**.

Invite another older child to the front to guess another word via touch. Have another child come up and slowly trace “Value.” (Show the word written on paper to the child and class.) Explain that it’s a harder word, so not to worry if they don’t get it; swap to a **smiley-face** symbol if needed.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Show the children your words and symbols.

- **What was that challenge like? Was it hard or easy to guess through touch?**
- **What does it mean to “value” someone?** (Appreciate, respect, include, accept, treat as special and important.)
- **How did the people in today’s story know they were both loved and valued by Jesus?** (He touched them when others didn’t; He was thoughtful, caring, and stood up for them; He healed them; He helped grow their faith in Him.)
- **How can we start to be as loving and compassionate as Jesus?** (Hold up your heart from Focus Activity as a prompt.) (Ask Jesus to change us; spend time with Him in prayer; learn from Bible stories.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus used His words and actions to show others that they were important, special, and of great value to Him. He wants us to follow His example. We can start by inviting Him to fill our hearts with His love and by spending time with Him each day.

Invite the children to pick up the **heart** they received earlier, read the memory verse again, and **pray a silent prayer to Jesus in response**.
