**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask the children to form pairs to play **Mirror Partners**. Instruct each pair to stand facing their partner. The aim is for one person to be the leader and the other to be a mirror. Give the leader a list of actions that they can choose from (e.g., lift their arm, place their hand on their head, smile, frown, turn head to the side, jump). Let the “leader” slowly start doing an action and the “mirror” will mimic the action simultaneously, as if the “leader” were looking in a mirror. Practice simple actions slowly to improve. Let the children exchange roles, and ask them to be respectful as they think about the reason for this activity.

**^[DISCUSS and EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What made the activity easy or difficult?**
- **How did you improve at being a good leader and follower?**
- **Why do we mirror (copy) others so easily?**
- **How can we be good examples for others so that they are inspired to mirror some good things they see in us?**

Share with the children how our story today is about Jesus teaching the disciples. While Jesus had 12 special disciples, other people followed Him too. This meant they had to watch, listen, learn, and choose to want to become like Jesus.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) Do you think we can also be disciples?**

To affirm this, point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **Jesus teaches me and nurtures my faith so that I can grow as His disciple.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible story.

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Our story shared how Jesus often used nature to teach important lessons.

- **What lessons from the Bible can you remember that use nature to teach us something important?**

Make these titles: **Bible Reference Cards** and **Nature Object Cards**. Give each child either a Bible reference card or a nature object card and have them find the correct match. Then mix up the cards and distribute again for children to match the pairs.

- They do not worry, because God feeds them (Matthew 6:26)—Birds
- It doesn’t fear drought or sun (Jeremiah 17:7,8)—Tree
- Hardworking and wise (Proverbs 6:6)—Ant
- They do not worry about what to wear (Matthew 6:28)—Flowers
- They tell of God’s work (Psalms 19:1)—Skies
- God knows the exact number (Luke 12:7)—Hairs on our heads

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 37: Jesus Teaching in Nature** to place in their storage container. Our **memory verse** is another verse that talks about our biggest job as a disciple. Say John 13:35 together, using the following actions:

“By this all (use arms to create an arc in the air)\
will know (point to head)\
that you (point outward)\
are My disciples, (point to self, meaning Jesus)\
if you have love (put both hands over heart)\
for one another” (point to others around the room)\
John 13:35 (open and shut hands like a Bible book).

Form a circle and place hands on each other’s shoulders (or create another culturally appropriate gesture of connection) then sing the **Scripture Song, “John 13:35,”** together.
