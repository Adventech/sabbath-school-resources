Simon Peter sat in the corner of the boat as Jesus preached. He sighed and hung his head. He had just been fishing all night and had caught nothing. Not one single fish!

Finally, Jesus finished preaching. He turned to Peter and said, “Take the boat into deep water. If you will put your nets in the water, you will catch some fish” (Luke 5:4, ICB).

Peter hadn’t caught any fish the whole night. But he chose to listen to Jesus and follow His instructions.

The nets splashed into the deep water and sunk out of sight. Peter began to pull the nets up—but something was wrong! Peter grunted and groaned. No matter how hard he pulled, he couldn’t lift the nets out of the water. They were so heavy they even started to rip.

Peter yelled to his friends in a nearby boat to please come help him. As they pulled the nets up together, the fishermen couldn’t believe their eyes. There were so many flipping, flapping fish!

How heavy were the fish? **Read Luke 5:7.**

Wow! That’s a lot of fish! Jesus wanted Peter to see that God could provide for his needs. Peter could trust Jesus to look after him every moment of every day.

Peter stared at the piles and piles of fish. Jesus had done this just for him! Yes, he could trust Jesus. Amazed and overwhelmed, Peter fell down at Jesus’ feet, knowing that Jesus wasn’t just an ordinary man. Then Jesus said to Peter, “Do not be afraid. From now on you will catch men” (Luke 5:10).

After pulling his boat onto the shore, Peter left it there and followed Jesus.
