**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** To demonstrate the meaning of dependence on God, engage several children in a **“trust fall.”** Invite a tall child to stand about a foot (1/3 meter) in front of your smallest child with their back to him/her. Instruct the tall child to fall backward into the arms of the small child. Comment that they don’t look too eager (if true). **Stop them before they carry out this action!** Invite them to fall backward into an adult’s arms instead of the small child’s arms.

**^[DEBRIEF:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **Why didn’t** (child’s name) **want to fall backward the first time?**
- **What made it easier for** (child’s name) **to depend on the person behind him/her the second time?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** We can choose to depend on God because we know He is strong and loving.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **God lets me choose to depend on Him or on myself.**

Let’s learn more about depending on God in today’s story about people who lived after the flood.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite your **foreign language speakers** to hold a **hammer** and repeat a “building” instruction in another language, becoming animated/frustrated.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How did you start to feel when you couldn’t understand?** (A bit frustrated, confused, etc.)
- **How would you feel if you couldn’t understand anyone around you in real life?** (Alone, anxious.)

**Teaching Point:** When God mixed up the languages, He knew the people would feel the same way you did. He
was getting their attention and giving them another opportunity to choose to depend on Him. God sometimes gets
our attention in interesting ways to help us depend on Him.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your completed **Tower of Babel craft** and share instructions for making it.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** While the children do their craft, invite your foreign language speakers from different generations (ages) to share how they have chosen to depend on God. They may like to share a specific experience, or the many things they depend on Him for each day—things we tend to take for granted such as shelter, food, and clean water. Encourage them to be brief and simple. Thank them together as a class.

**^[PEER SHARE and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** If time allows, play **music** and invite the children to **walk around**. When the music stops, they can find a partner nearby and **share** something they’ve learned or enjoyed in today’s lesson. Do this a couple of times. On the last stop, invite them to pray on their own, and commit to depending on God more in the coming week.
