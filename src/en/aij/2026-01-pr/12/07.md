- As a family, find a comfy spot to reread the story sections of the lesson to familiarize yourselves with the characters and events.
- Sit in a circle and choose one spot to be the “hot seat.”
- The person in the hot seat chooses to be a character from the story.
- The rest of the family asks the hot-seat person questions with a yes-or-no answer to find out which character they are. Be sure to use the words “wise,” “unwise,” “unselfish,” “kind,” etc.
- Invite family members to take turns being in the hot seat.
