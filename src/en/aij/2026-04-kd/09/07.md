Carine waved goodbye to her daddy. He smiled back as he left for work wearing his police uniform. Carine was 4-years old, and she lived in Salvador, Brazil. She loved her daddy and mommy very much. She looked forward to seeing her daddy at the end of the day.

But that night, Daddy didn’t come home. He had been hurt very badly while doing his job as a policeman. The doctors in the hospital didn’t think that he would get better.

“Don’t worry,” Mommy said to Carine. “Jesus does miracles, and He will do a miracle for Daddy.”

That night, Mommy opened the Bible and read Carine the story about Jesus giving sight to a blind man. And many other stories about Jesus making sick people better.

When Mommy closed the Bible, she still looked sad. Carine offered to sing a song. Mommy liked to listen to Carine’s clear, sweet voice, and she welcomed the song. It helped her have faith.

Finally, after a month, Daddy came home! He looked tired, and he moved slowly, but Carine ran into his arms. “I’m going to take care of you by singing to you,’” Carine announced. She sang her favorite song, “Hallelujah.” She sang to her father about her love for Jesus every day—until he was well enough to go back to work. Jesus had saved her daddy!

The Bible has lots of stories and songs about God’s love. Psalm 37:40 (ICB) says, “The Lord helps them and saves them.”

Carine’s mother was so grateful that her daughter had so much faith. Her mommy said, “She always believed that God would solve everything.”
