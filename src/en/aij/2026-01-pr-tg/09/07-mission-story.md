**Focus:** We can depend on God for help to learn something important, yet difficult.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Belgium** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Imagine not being able to understand a word your teacher says!

That’s what it was like for Linda when she started school in the city of Brussels in Belgium. Her mother enrolled her in a French-speaking school. Linda knew no French at all! She spoke Flemish, a local language, as well as Spanish.

A few weeks after school started, Linda’s teacher, Miss Cathy, told Linda’s mother that Linda was having trouble in school because she couldn’t understand French.

Mother offered to help Linda learn French at home. Every afternoon Mother and Linda asked God to help Linda learn French. Then they worked together, studying hard.

Within a week Miss Cathy noticed that Linda’s work was improving. When she gave the children a test in math, Linda received the highest score.

That afternoon Miss Cathy asked Linda’s mother how Linda had improved so much in just a week. “We did two things,” Mother said. “We prayed, and we worked together.”

A few weeks later, when the class took a test in French, Linda again scored the highest grade in class.

Again Miss Cathy asked her mother how Linda had improved so quickly. And again Linda’s mother answered, “We prayed, and we worked together.”

Linda and her mother were grateful that God was helping Linda learn French quickly when they depended on Him for help. They were also glad that they could share this with Miss Cathy. Maybe one day she would learn to love God and depend on Him too.

- **What could you depend on God to help you with this week?**
