^[Word of the Week—REPENT]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#865ba6", "size": "xl"}}})\
Repent means to be sorry for the wrong things you have done and seek forgiveness for your sins. To repent is to change directions, away from sin and toward God—like making a full U-turn on the road. Once a person has repented, they will live peacefully with God and other people.

A Tiny Baby, A Big Job

While Jesus was growing from baby to boyhood, from student to teacher, another special baby boy was also growing up to do incredible things for God.

Elizabeth cuddled baby John as she pondered the angel’s words to Zacharias: “And he will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God” (Luke 1:16). Such an important job lay ahead for this tiny baby boy. Mother Elizabeth knew she had a big job too. She cuddled John closer and quietly committed to sharing God’s love with him.

When John was big enough to listen to stories, Elizabeth and Zacharias told him about the miracle of his birth. He heard how the angel Gabriel had suddenly appeared to Father Zacharias, telling him they would have a son. The faithful parents also told John about the miracle birth of his cousin Jesus, around six months later, who was growing up in Nazareth. In the days when people traveled by foot, Nazareth, in Galilee, was far away from the village in Judea where John was born. Though he likely never met his Cousin growing up, John learned all about Him. His parents taught him that Jesus was the promised Messiah whom John was to tell everyone about.

John would have help to do this important job for God. The angel had told Zacharias that John would be “filled with the Holy Spirit” (Luke 1:15).

Just as He did for John, God fills our hearts with His love and gives us His Holy Spirit so that we too can do what He asks us to do. “The love of God has been poured out in our hearts by the Holy Spirit who was given to us” (Romans 5:5).
