**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **colorful play-dough** and **small twigs**.

Ask the children to make a little person out of their play-dough, using the twigs for the arms and legs. As they finish, invite them to place their play-dough person on a table so you can see them all lined up together. Comment on how each child has made something or “someone” unique and different.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Do you think that every person is special to God, or just some?** (Everyone!)
- **Who do you think is welcome into God’s family?** (Everyone!)

**Teaching Point:** It does not matter if we look different from each other. Whether we have different skin or eyes or hair color, we are all part of God’s loving family! Being in God’s family makes us feel happy, special, and loved.

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: God is with me. He welcomes everyone into His family.In our Bible story today, we will see how God welcomed someone special and how God’s friends were welcoming too.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **balloon**, a **permanent marker**, a piece of **cloth**, and some **confetti**.

Blow up the balloon in front of the children and tie it. Draw a smiley face on the balloon with the marker, then place a small pile of paper confetti onto a plate in front of you.

- **How can I make the paper confetti stick to the balloon?**

Rub the balloon with a piece of fabric, then gently lower it down just above the paper confetti. Children will see how the static electricity attracts it to stick to the balloon. Invite some of the children to take turns trying what you have just shown them. (Have more than one balloon available.)

Share that as the smiley balloon “welcomed,” God loves everyone and welcomes us into His family. When we have His love in our hearts, we will be kind and welcoming to others too.

- **Who made Naomi feel welcome when she came home?** (The women of the town rushed to say hello and talk with her.)
- **Who made Ruth feel welcome when she went to collect grain, and how?** (The servants, and the field’s owner, Boaz. He kindly gave her a drink, food, and extra grain.)
- **Think about what happened at the end of the story. How did God show Ruth and Naomi that they were part of His special family?** (God gave them a new family and a baby, and later Jesus came into their family line.)
- **What things might you be able to do this week that help others know that they are part of God’s big loving family?** (Pray for someone who feels alone, make a card for someone, invite someone to Sabbath School.)

Share that God loves to welcome everyone to His family. Whether you’ve known about God for a long time or whether you’re just meeting Him now, He loves you. We can show others how kind and loving God is by making them feel welcome and special too. If time, sing “God’s Big Family.”

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He welcomes everyone into His family.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD and NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 26: Eastern Box Turtle** and share the following facts:

- These little turtles have an amazing, patterned shell. They can pull their little feet and head inside it, clamping it shut completely!
- You can find them near ponds, fields, meadows, and woodlands in eastern North America.
- They love to eat berries, insects, flowers, and roots.

Share that God created every type of animal with a special ability. All are different, but each one is precious to Him.

Give each child **Nature Card 26: Eastern Box Turtle** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**Share the Nature Lesson:** “Backyard Box Turtle,” about a boy who finds a turtle in his backyard, and discuss the question.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Fingerprint Picture Frame**. Take the fingerprints of the people in your Sabbath School class and make them into people to show that we are all part of God’s family. Then build a simple frame from craft sticks.

As the children make their craft, you may like to play the Scripture Song playlist in the background.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Hand out little handfuls of the confetti you used for the earlier activity to throw in the air as they answer the question. Alternatively, invite the children to hold up their picture frames as they answer:

- **Whom does God welcome into His family?** (Everyone! Me.)

Share that as God took care of Naomi and Ruth, He cares for us and welcomes us into His family too.

Every person is different. This week, let’s ask God to help us treat everyone as an important member of His family.
