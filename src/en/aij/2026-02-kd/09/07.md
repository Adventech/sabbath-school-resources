Dr. Livingstone loved God. He loved people too. And when God showed him that he could be a doctor and a missionary, he was so excited! He could help people learn about Jesus and feel better.

He left his home in England and went to live in Africa. This was around 200 years ago. It took a very long time for him to get to Africa. And he couldn’t take many things with him. But he was so happy to be helping people.

Dr. Livingstone worked for many, many years. Sometimes he didn’t have enough medicine to help people get better when they were sick, but he kept doing his best—day after day.

For a time Dr. Livingstone lived in a little hut in Ujiji in the middle of the African jungle. One day he was feeling very low. He was very sick. He had no food, no more medicine, nor any money to get any.

Suddenly his servant Susi ran into his tent crying, “An Englishman is here, Master! An Englishman has come!”

God had sent a man to help him. His name was Mr. Stanley. He brought food and medicine to Dr. Livingstone and looked after him until he was better again.

Mr. Stanley said to Dr. Livingstone, “Dr. Livingstone, you are tired. Come with me back to England so you can have a good vacation. You have worked too hard for too long.”

“No,” Dr. Livingstone said, and shook his head. “My work here is not finished. I need to work here until the job is done.”

Dr. Livingstone didn’t give up. He was faithful. He wouldn’t give up until his work was finished.

Joseph really wanted to get out of prison, but like Dr. Livingstone, he did his best every day in the place where he was until his work was done.
