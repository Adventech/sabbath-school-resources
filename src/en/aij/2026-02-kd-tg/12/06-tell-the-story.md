**Big Idea:** God is with me. I will let His love show.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a **backpack** to put some **toy spoons**, **pots**, and a **blanket** in and invite children to help you pack and unpack.
- Flick on a **flashlight** each time you say the word “shine.”
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 12 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a little town called Bethlehem, in Canaan, a hungry family busily packed their bags. They rolled up their clothes in blankets. They packed their sacks with their best tools, pots, bowls, and spoons. Then they packed the very last little bit of food they had. ^[(Have the children help you pack the spoons, pots, and blankets into the backpack.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

It was many, many years after Joseph’s family had moved from Canaan to Egypt. And now there was another time of hunger for God’s people in Canaan. All the ground was dry. All the food was gone. And everyone was hungry!

Father Elimelech, Mother Naomi, and their two boys, Mahlon and Chilion, were hungry too. They had decided to move to Moab to find food. With a big wave goodbye to all their neighbors, the family set off. It was a long, long walk to get there. But God was with them.

The family arrived in Moab, unpacked, and made a new home ^[(unpack backpack)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). There was lots of food and lots of water. But not everything in Moab was good. The people didn’t believe in God; they worshipped idols.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Elimelech and Naomi chose to be different. They had God’s love in their hearts. It was like a bright light. They decided to let it shine brightly to everyone in Moab, so the Moabite people might know about God too! ^[(Flick flashlight on.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

One day something terrible happened. Elimelech died. Oh, no! Naomi and her sons cried and hugged each other. They were thankful they had each other. They were really, really thankful God was with them.

Time went by. Instead of tears, there was lots of laughter in Naomi’s house again. Naomi’s sons had married two lovely young women, Ruth and Orpah.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mother Naomi had welcomed them into her home. She told them wonderful stories about God as they made the bread. She sang songs about God’s love as they cleaned. And she showed them how to pray.

Ruth and Orpah just loved being part of this happy family who let their love shine for God.

For 10 years Naomi, Mahlon, Chilion, Ruth, and Orpah lived as a happy family. But one day another very sad thing happened. Mahlon and Chilion died. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Naomi, Ruth, and Orpah hugged each other and cried. Although they couldn’t see Him, God was right there comforting them.

Good news soon came to sad Naomi. She heard that God had taken care of His people back in Judah. Her home, Bethlehem, had a good harvest of food coming! In this very sad time God was helping Naomi.

It was time to go home! Ruth and Orpah loved Naomi too much to let her go alone. They would go with her. They bundled up all the things they could carry and set off on the long journey to Bethlehem ^[(pack backpack again)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As they walked slowly down the dusty road, Naomi looked at brave Ruth and Orpah. They were so kind to come with her. But it was such a big move for them to leave their home and their country.

Suddenly Naomi stopped. She turned to Ruth and Orpah and told them to go home to their families. The two women didn’t want to go, but Naomi had made up her mind.

“The women cried together again. Then Orpah kissed Naomi good-bye” (Ruth 1:14, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But Ruth wouldn’t leave! Ruth said, “Every place you go, I will go. Every place you live, I will live. Your people will be my people. Your God will be my God” (Ruth 1:16, ICB).

Wow! Ruth had seen God’s goodness shining so brightly through Naomi’s life that now her heart also glowed with God’s love. She wanted to stay close to Naomi and close to God _forever_.
