Opening Activities

Prepare for **^[Character Cards activity]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** by pulling out the containers/rings used to store cards.

Prepare for Focus Activities: write out the words included below to create a set of **Making the Right Choice cards** and attach them to the walls around your room prior to the lesson.

Update **Offering Chart.**

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
