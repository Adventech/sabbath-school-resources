It was Sunday morning, and Joanne looked into the empty cupboards with a sigh. They had no food left, and no money to buy any more. Father had left the family and moved away after Mother started going to a Seventh-day Adventist church in the U.S. state of Oregon. “If you choose God, let your God feed you,” their father had said. “Let your God clothe you.”

Mother didn’t have a job, and she cried and prayed in her bedroom that Sunday. When lunchtime came, Joanne’s younger sister complained, “I’m hungry.” Her older brother sat still, trying to be brave even though he had no way to get them some food.

Then Joanne remembered reading in _Uncle Arthur’s Bedtime Stories_ about children who were helped by angels when they prayed. “All we have to do is pray!” she said.

Joanne didn’t know how to pray, but she started anyway. “Hello, God. We are really hungry. Would You send us something to eat, please?”

The children waited. No food. Hours passed, and dinnertime came. The children grew more hungry. Mother continued praying in the bedroom.

Then Joanne said, “Oh, I know what we did wrong! God doesn’t think that we believe Him, because we didn’t set the table.” Quickly they set the table. Joanne prayed, “Sorry about that, God. Could You please send us some food now? The table is set, and we’re ready!”

But nothing came. The children climbed into bed, hungry and disappointed. Early in the morning they woke up even hungrier. The children opened the front door to go to school, but their path was blocked—by a huge box filled with food!

Excitedly the children called Mother to the door. Mother couldn’t believe her eyes. God had provided just what they needed! “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26).

That was the moment that Joanne knew that God lives and that He loves her.
