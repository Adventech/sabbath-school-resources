José<sup>*</sup> loved to make shoes. His shoes were beautiful, but times were tough in Spain many years ago, and he was very poor. He was so poor that he owned only one hammer. He also had a radio that he played while he worked.

One day over this radio came the sound of a quartet singing a worship song. Then a man talked about the love of God and the Bible.

“Good sermon; good singing,” José murmured to himself. “I must tune in again next week.” He listened to the program many more times, and the more he heard, the more he liked it.

Then one morning a man selling books entered his shop. “How interesting,” José exclaimed. “These books say the same things I hear on the radio every week.”

“You mean on the _Voice of Prophecy_ program?” the book salesman asked.

“The very one,” José replied.

The delighted Adventist book salesman offered to share more with José and met with him every evening for a week, studying the Bible.
José accepted all the Bible truths except for the Sabbath. He just couldn’t give up work on this busy day.

“If God were to send you a sign telling you He wants you to keep Saturday holy, would you keep it then?” asked the bookseller.

“Oh, yes, yes, of course,” José replied.

The very next Sabbath José went to work as usual, but he couldn’t find his hammer. He searched high and low, in the cupboards and under the workbench, but there was no hammer. And since that was the only hammer he had, he couldn’t work that day.

He went back Sunday morning—and there was the hammer
as large as life!

“A strange thing,” he whispered. “Is this the Lord’s sign? No, it couldn’t be.”

So he went to work the next Sabbath too. He had no trouble finding the hammer this time. He lined up a nail and struck the nail with the hammer. But as the hammer came down, its iron head broke off in two pieces!

“It is the sign of the Lord!” José cried. My hammer wouldn’t work last Sabbath, and it won’t work today. And if it won’t work on Sabbath, I won’t either. From now on I shall keep the Sabbath.”

As soon as possible, José was baptized. Afterward, the pastor handed him a gift: a new hammer.

_<sup>*</sup>Name has been changed._
