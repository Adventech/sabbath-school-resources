Busy Excitement

Excitement filled the air as busy workers scurried back and forth. There was so much to do. Weddings in Bible times lasted for several days, and they were huge, happy occasions with lots of feasting, music, and singing. One of Jesus’ relatives was getting married, so His mother, Mary, was helping with the wedding preparations.

It’s fun to look forward to a big celebration. Our caring God loves to see us enjoy happy times with family and friends. What does the Bible say about this? **Read Psalm 133:1.**

Mary was busy, but sometimes her thoughts were far away . . . Jesus was coming to the wedding, and she couldn’t wait to see Him! She had missed Him very much since He left home. News of His baptism and His time in the wilderness had reached Mary in Nazareth, and she was both happy and worried.

Mary remembered the angel Gabriel’s words: “He will be great, and will be called the Son of the Highest; and the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David” (Luke 1:32). Was it time? She was filled with fresh hope that soon everyone would know that her Son was God’s Son, the Savior, the promised Messiah!

Mary also knew His life wouldn’t be easy. The old prophet Simeon had warned Mary that something would happen to Jesus that would make her very sad (Luke 2:35).

While Mary may have wondered about the future, God was in control. He had shown that He was the God of miracles. As Gabriel had told her: “For with God nothing will be impossible” (Luke 1:37).

Pushing her thoughts aside, Mary smiled. There was so much to do! Jesus would soon be here. What a wonderful time they would have together. She just couldn’t wait!
