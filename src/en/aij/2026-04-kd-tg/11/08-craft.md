**What you need:**

Week 11 craft template on cardstock, metallic markers, light-gray crayon, scissors, red feathers, tape.

**Create it Together:**

1\. Use metallic markers and gray crayon to color in the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/11/11b.png)

2\. Cut out the pieces, then stick the ear flaps below each end of the helmet.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/11/11c.png)

3\. Tape a rectangle strip to each side of the helmet.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/11/11d.png)

4\. Flip over the helmet and tape the feathers onto the top.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/11/11e.png)

5\. An adult can measure the child’s head and tape the strips together so the helmet fits. Wear the helmet as you reenact the story!
