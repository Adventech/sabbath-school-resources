God is so mighty and powerful that our human minds will never understand everything there is to know about Him, even when we get to heaven.

When the Bible talks about God, sometimes it’s talking about God the Father, other times God the Son (Jesus), other times God the Holy Spirit, and still other times all three! Together they’re called the Godhead, or the Trinity. They are three persons, but one God. They all share the same attributes. They are all loving, and they always work together. They have always been and always will be, forever.

There are so many attributes that could describe God the Father, such as loving, holy, merciful, and fair. He has many different names that tell us about Him, such as Elohim, which means “Mighty One,” and Elyon, meaning “Most High.”

God the Father shows us what He is like through His Son, Jesus. Although Jesus is truly God, He became a human to give up His life to save us. He is called Jesus and many other names—Messiah, Son of Man, the Good Shepherd, and the Rock. He is also our Judge and our Coming King.

The Holy Spirit is as much a person as the Father and the Son. He inspired the writers of the Bible and filled Jesus’ life with power. The Father and the Son sent the Holy Spirit to always be with us, and He is also called “Helper” or “Comforter.” He reminds us to make good choices by speaking to our minds and helping us to know what is true and right.
