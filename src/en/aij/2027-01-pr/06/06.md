The water kept on gushing from the rock in Horeb. The children guzzled it down and splashed about as the water tickled their toes. Adults watered the cattle and did the washing as they marveled at the miracle. God is so good!

But would they marvel and have faith in God the next time trouble came their way? Would they be grateful and content, even when they couldn’t see how God could provide for their needs? Would we?

God has some wise advice. He says, “My brethren, count it all joy when you fall into various trials, knowing that the testing of your faith produces patience” (James 1:2, 3). These words remind us of Moses. He had patience with those grumbling, complaining Israelites. Each time a problem came along, Moses didn’t let it snatch away his joy. His faith was as solid as a rock, so he went straight to God for help. And God had a solution to the problem every time! The water was bitter? No problem—throw in a tree. There was no water? No problem—strike the rock, and God would make it flow.

Through all the troubles that the wandering Israelites faced, God was trying to grow in them a faith that Moses had. He wanted to help them skip the grumbling and complaining and go straight to Him in faith.

God wants us to come straight to Him, asking for help with any problem, big or small. While He sorts out the problem, we can show that we trust Him by staying content and grateful. He has provided for our needs before, so He can do it again!
