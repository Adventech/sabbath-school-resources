---
title: Cross-Focused Christians
date: 07/02/2026
---

Some passages in the Bible seem to be completely out of place. For example, why did Jesus explain in Matthew 6 that “the eye is the lamp of the body” (verse 22, NIV) right in the middle of some materialism-blasting verses like, “Do not store up for yourselves treasures on earth” and “You cannot serve both God and money” (verses 19, 24, NIV)?

What do our earthly treasures and Christ’s “You cannot serve both God and money” statements have to do with our eyes?

We know that looking at fancy advertisements with our eyes can directly impact how we use the material blessings God entrusts to us. But could it also be that Jesus is warning us against the foolishness of walking along His path with our right eye on heavenly treasures and our left eye on earthly treasures? When we do that, we become a “diverted-eye Christian”! We are then unable to focus clearly on the eternal things that matter.

Thank God that Jesus is not only our Way, but He’s also the Light for our path, and He even offers eye salve to help us see clearly as we follow Him.

Let’s be clear-sighted, focused Christians by keeping both of our eyes on the cross of Jesus.

### Appeal

Let’s joyfully lay up our treasures in heaven, where we will enjoy them for eternity. As we return God’s tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s do it with both eyes on our Savior, Jesus Christ, from whom all our blessings come. Thank you, Jesus.
