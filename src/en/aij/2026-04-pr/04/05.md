Peter stared at Jesus, his mouth open and heart racing with excitement. Jesus was walking on water! As if he could hardly believe it, he cried out, “Lord, if that is really you, then tell me to come to you on the water” (Matthew 14:28, ICB). Jesus simply answered, “Come” (Matthew 14:29).

Peter scrambled out of the boat and walked toward Jesus with his eyes locked on his Master. He didn’t sink; he didn’t swim. He just kept walking on the foamy waves straight toward Jesus.

Peter felt pretty pleased with himself. He glanced back to see if his friends were watching. As soon as he took his eyes off Jesus, something happened. A huge wave rolled between them, blocking Jesus from view.

Peter panicked, and losing faith, he began to sink. As the fierce waves sucked him downward, Peter shouted, “Lord, save me!” (Matthew 14:30).

What did Jesus do? **Read Matthew 14:31.**

Jesus didn’t hesitate. He will always answer a cry like that.

Side by side and hand in hand, Peter and Jesus walked to the boat and climbed in. The waves calmed down, and the wind grew silent. Peter was still and silent too. He realized his pride and lack of faith had nearly caused his death. He was grateful that “the Lord looks after those who fear him. He watches over those who put hope in his love” (Psalm 33:18, ICB).

Peter had been reminded to always keep his eyes on Jesus. Through storms on the sea and storms in his heart, he needed to focus on Jesus.

Jesus helps us do the impossible when we keep our eyes on Him, just as He helped Peter. We can trust His power to help us make good choices and do hard things.

- What does it mean for you to keep your eyes on Jesus? How can you do this when you cannot see Him? (Clue: Think about how to keep His words in your mind and heart.)
