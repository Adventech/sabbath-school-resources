How amazing is the love that sweeps over you as you hold your new baby for the first time! You gaze into those sleepy eyes and wrap their tiny hand around your finger, and you wonder how life can be any sweeter! Time seems to stand still, and the worries of the world fade away.

What an incredible God we serve to create the love between a parent and a child. With the joy and rush of love comes a fierce desire to keep our children safe and cared for. How do we do this in an unpredictable world? It can be overwhelming knowing that there will be hard things ahead for our precious little ones. Like Jochebed, who turned her worries into prayers, we too can ask God to help us as we parent. We can trust Him to give us creative ways to instruct, guide, and protect our children.

Our Father loves to parent us as we parent our children. Just as Jochebed wrapped up her sweet baby in faith and placed him in a basket, so God wraps us up in His love and places us in the safest place we could be—in the palm of His hands.

As you read this story to your little one this week, be on the lookout for the many ways God is wrapping you up in His love. May you be inspired by the faith of Jochebed and Amram as they took their problems to the God of the universe. May you see glimpses of the Father’s unending love in the way you love your own little one.

> <callout>1 John 3:1</callout>
> “Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called children of God!”
