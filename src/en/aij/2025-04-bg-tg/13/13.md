**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: I can be God’s helper.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- Welcome children and parents by smiling. Tell the children you are happy to see them today!
- **^[“Christmas Star.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **star on a stick** and talk about the angel star.
- **^[“Riding on a Camel.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Walk around the room as children imagine they are riding a camel across the desert as they follow the star. Alternatively, use toy camels or camels on sticks.
- **^[“I’ll Share My Home.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **toy cow**. Talk about what it would have been like for Mary and Joseph in the stable.
- **^[“It Was a Happy Day.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give the children **an angel, a sheep, a wrapped gift** as you sing each verse. Use your classroom props and decorations to tell the story of Baby Jesus.
- **^[“Baby Jesus.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **small doll** to hold, wash, wrap, and rock.
- **^[“Who Is Jesus?”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **picture of the grown man, Jesus**.

**WEEK 13: THE WISE MEN**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag:** Sandbox with sand, small cardboard houses or wooden blocks or pieces of wood, glittery cardboard star, peg people (Mary, Joseph, Jesus, Wise Men), camels on craft sticks (or finger puppets), small box wrapped with ribbon, spices and scents to smell, gold-colored coins

Show the children your sandbox with sand piled in hills. Place some cardboard houses or small timber squares at one end as Bethlehem. Hold a star above the sandbox.

Say: **Far, far from Bethlehem lived some Wise Men. What did they see in the dark sky? It was a new star! The Wise Men knew that it was a special star from God. It showed that a king had been born. Oh how they wanted to meet Him! They packed their camels with some special treasures for the King. Then they set off to follow the star.** Invite the children to move your Wise Men and camels slowly across the sandbox toward the star. Discuss the journey and pause to ask if they can see the star.

**_Plod, plod, plod_ went the camels. It was nighttime, but the Wise Men didn’t sleep. They slept in the daytime. At night they followed the big, bright star in the sky.** Move the star over the sandbox as the children move the Wise Men and camels.

**Finally the star led the Wise Men all the way to Bethlehem. It stopped over a little house. The Wise Men knocked on the door. _Knock, knock, knock._ The door opened. Inside was Mary with little Jesus on her lap. The Wise Men had found the new King—Jesus!**

**The Wise Men bowed before Jesus. They knelt down in the little house and gave all their love to Jesus. Then they each gave Jesus their special gifts.** Show the children a small brightly wrapped present. **These gifts showed that Jesus was a royal king. There was a chest of gold and two jars of perfume called frankincense and myrrh. The Wise Men left with happy hearts. They had seen King Jesus!** Invite the children to hold the items you have placed inside. Smell the spices and scents together and touch the “gold.”

Say together: **Jesus wants me to follow Him.**
