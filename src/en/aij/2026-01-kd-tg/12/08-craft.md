**What you need:**

Sheet of colored paper, stickers and colored shapes, markers, tape, scissors and ribbon or string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/12/12a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Pre-fold the paper so it’s ready to be made into an envelope.
- Cut lengths of ribbon or string.

**Create it together:**

- Fold the paper along the creases and tape the sides together, as shown.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/12/12b.png)

- Fold the top flap down and cut a V shape to complete the envelope.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/12/12c.png)

- Decorate the tithe envelope using stickers, colored shapes, and markers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/12/12d.png)

- Tape a ribbon to the top of the envelope so it can be hung in a special place.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/12/12e.png)

- While the children are making the envelopes, talk to them about why we give.
