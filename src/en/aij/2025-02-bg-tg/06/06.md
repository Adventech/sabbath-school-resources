**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

- _Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: Jesus cares for them.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:
- **^[“Shake a Little Hand and Say Good Morning.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Welcome children and parents by **smiling and shaking hands**. Tell the children you are so happy to see them today!
- Jesus liked to walk by the lake with His friends. What did He see near the lake? Yes, a boat!
- Sing **^[“See the Boat.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a small boat to place in a large plastic container filled with water.
- What kinds of things do we find in the ocean or in a lake? Fish!
- Sing **^[“Fishy, Fishy.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a paper fish on a rod or a small toy fish to hold.
- What else can we find in the ocean? Shells!
- Sing **^[“Seashells.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a shell to place on the sand (yellow fabric).
- The ocean is so big and deep, and there are so many different fish and shells that live in the ocean.
- Sing **^[“The Big Blue Ocean.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** and give each child a blue ribbon to wave. Talk about how much Jesus loves each child.
- God cares for us so much. Sing **^[“God Cares for Me.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a heart while they hug their parent/caregiver.
- One of the ways our loving God cares for us is through giving us food.
- Sing **^[“The Loaves and the Fishes.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a small piece of bread or a little basket.
- **^[“Sabbath Is a Happy Day.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a musical instrument or shaker to play as you sing. Talk about the different things you love about the Sabbath day and why it is so special.
- **^[“1 . . . 2 . . . 3, God Loves Me.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a mirror. Talk about how much Jesus loves each child.

**WEEK 6: ONE THANKFUL MAN**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: White sticker dots, bandages, cardboard love hearts, 10 paper people, optional: markers

Take the sticker dots from your story bag and place them over your arms and wrap a bandage around your leg or arm. Give some sticker dots to the children too.

Open your Bible, point, and say: **Our Bible story today is about some people who were very sick with a sickness called leprosy. No one wanted to go near them. They were very sad and lonely.**

Say: **The Bible tells us that Jesus came into a small town. There were 10 men with leprosy who called to Him, “Jesus! Master! Please help us!”** (Luke 17:11-13, ICB).

Together with the children, count the ten paper men. Give the children sticker dots to stick on the men or use a marker to draw spots on the men.

Say: **Jesus loved these sick men. He wanted to help them and make their sickness go away. So, He said to them to “Go and show yourselves to the priests”** (Luke 17:14, ICB). **Then what happened?**

Start removing the spots and bandages from your arms and legs. Invite the children to help you turn over the paper men one by one. Count them together. Talk about how these men now have no spots!

Say: **Jesus made the sickness go away! There was one man who went back to Jesus. His heart was so happy, and he wanted to say “thank You” to Jesus.**

Give each child a paper heart with a drawn smile. Encourage the children to say: **“thank you”** as you do so.

Point to your Bible and say, **The Bible tells us that the man “went back to Jesus. He praised God in a loud voice . . . and thanked Him”** (Luke 17:15, ICB).

Say: **It makes Jesus happy when we are thankful to Him.**
