Jesus told Sarah the most amazing, wonderful news: she would have her own baby very soon! Did God do what He promised? **Read Genesis 21:2, 3.**

How happy everyone was when little Isaac was born. With big smiles they watched baby Isaac sleep and suck his fist. Sarah now laughed happily as she thought about God’s kindness and love. Sarah said, “God has made me laugh. . . . No one thought that I would be able to have Abraham’s child. But I have given Abraham a son while he is old” (Genesis 21:6, 7, ICB).

In Sarah’s language the word for Isaac and “he laughed” sound the same. Isaac would always remind her of the happy laughter God gave her when she was older than a grandma.

Everyone knew that Isaac truly was a special gift from God. He was a miracle baby!

Is anything too hard for God? No. God can do ANYTHING! “Know that the Lord your God, He is God, the faithful God” (Deuteronomy 7:9). God had made the impossible possible! We can trust that whatever God says He’ll do, He always does.
