The people in the crowd were very tall, but Zacchaeus was very short. His shoulders slumped. It was no use—he could never get past this crowd to see Jesus.

Then a brilliant idea popped into his head. A tree! Yes, he could climb a tree. Then he would be high enough to see over the crowd.

Zacchaeus “ran ahead to a place where he knew Jesus would come” (Luke 19:4. ICB). He grinned as he spotted a large shady sycamore tree on the side of the road. Its branches stretched out over the path. _Yes! That would be perfect._

What did he do next? **Read Luke 19:4.**

Gripping the low branches with his hands and pressing his feet against the rough tree trunk, Zacchaeus scrambled up, up, up. Then carefully he shuffled out onto a thick branch and waited. His legs dangled, and his eyes peered down the road.

Jesus and the crowd came closer and closer. Soon the people were right underneath the tree. Zacchaeus’ heart pounded. Jesus was right below him! Then something surprising happened. Jesus stopped. Everyone stopped.

Jesus “looked up and saw him.” Jesus’ eyes sparkled with love and care. “Zacchaeus,” Jesus said (Luke 19:5).

Zacchaeus could barely believe what he was hearing. Jesus knew his name! Jesus cared about him!
