**Word of the Week**

**Holy:** God is holy, which means He is perfect and good. When God makes something holy, He makes it different from ordinary things.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After a week of creating all things perfect and good, God gave the Sabbath as the pinnacle of Creation week. It is a special day He set aside for love, relationship, and worship of God, the Giver of all. Sabbath is a time to delight in God’s company and to be filled with wonder at the world He so lovingly created.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that the Sabbath is a special gift from God.
- **Feel** the happiness that Sabbath brings to our lives.
- **Respond** by saying “Thank You” and choosing to cherish the gift of Sabbath.
