Every place, every sound, every beautiful living thing, was perfectly created by our loving God. When did this happen? Read Genesis 1:1 (memory verse).

God made all things in the whole universe and in our world too. Can you make up an action for each thing God made, as we list them off?

The Bible says that “every perfect gift is from God. These good gifts come down from the Creator of the sun, moon, and stars” (James 1:17, ICB). All that God made in these five days was to be a perfect gift for us, for God’s work of creating wasn’t finished yet. He already had YOU in mind. The sixth day was coming, and everything was ready.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you remember what God created each day?
- How can you look after all the precious gifts God gave us at Creation?
