**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Can you imagine the trauma of riding inside a rocking, tipping, tossing ark day in and day out for five months? Even though it would have been challenging for Noah and his family, it was the only place to be, the safest place to be, for God was there. This was His offered haven of safety.

Noah and his family endured with God by their side. They faced the discomfort with God by their side. God saw them through.

Then God hung a rainbow in the sky—a token of love in the new world. “What compassion for erring man, to place the beautiful, variegated rainbow in the clouds, a token of the covenant of the great God with man! . . . This symbol in the clouds was to confirm the belief of all, and establish their confidence in God; for it was a token of divine mercy and goodness to man” (Ellen, G. White, _The Spirit of Prophecy_, vol. 1, pp. 77, 78).

Noah was saved, and his family had been spared. God’s love was so vivid, so tangible, Noah could see it in living color in the sky. His heart bubbled over with gratitude. Before doing anything else, Noah built an altar to the Lord.

As you share this incredible story with the children this week, may they know and feel that God is always with them. When we know God is with us, we can endure difficulties that come our way. When we know God is with us, gratitude and praise can be on our lips.
