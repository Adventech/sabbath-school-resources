---
title: Treasures of the Heart
date: 27/06/2026
---

Remember when Satan tempted Jesus in the wilderness? Do you ever feel similar temptations? Have you ever felt that society and media lead you up to a high place and tempt you with all the riches and comforts of the world?

Sadly, we often respond to these temptations like little children, saying, “I want it. I want it. I want it now!” Many people have already stored up far more than they _need_, but they’re addicted to “having just a little bit more.”

Christ’s statement in Matthew 6:19, 21 was not just a suggestion but a command: “Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on earth, . . . for where your treasure is, there your heart will be also.” Jesus didn’t start this sentence with, “I think it would be best if you don’t . . .” or “Things might go better if you try not to . . . “ He said, “Do not lay up for yourselves treasures on earth.” And why not? Because where your treasure is, your heart will follow.

If your treasure is on earth, then so is your heart, and that’s not a good thing.

Praise God for the old hymn that says, “Turn your eyes upon Jesus, look full in His wonderful face, and the things of earth will grow strangely dim in the light of His glory and grace.”

### Appeal

As we now return God’s tithe and give the Promise offerings set aside as a percentage of our income, let’s ask Him to help us see the attractions and distractions of this world in the light of eternity. By God’s grace, may we turn our eyes upon Jesus and cheerfully give our treasure to the mission and ministries of His heart.
