The calf-worshipping people had been forgiven by their loving God. But the precious Ten Commandment tablets lay shattered on the ground. They were supposed to be God’s gift to His people. What did God do? **Read Exodus 34:1, 2.**

Moses got to work right away, and before long he was making the familiar journey up the mountain. Once again God etched His law into stone tablets with His finger. And once again Moses spent 40 wonderful days in the cloud with God.

This time nobody worried or grumbled while they waited for Moses. Nobody thought Moses had deserted them, and nobody wanted to make an idol to worship. The people were learning from their mistakes. God’s forgiveness had shown that He really truly was committed to growing a friendship with them. And their hearts stayed committed right back.

This time when the people saw Moses walking down the mountain, everyone was happy—so happy! Then suddenly the happy smiles vanished. Some people turned away. Others covered their eyes. Still others ran to their tents to hide. What was the trouble? **Read Exodus 34:29, 30.**

Imagine seeing Moses’ face glowing from the dazzling light of God! Guilt made them fearful of coming so close to their perfect God, but when Moses covered his face with a veil and they heard the love in his voice, they came close to see the Ten Commandments God had graciously written again.

God is so good. He patiently grows love in the hearts of His children so that they can enjoy a loving, respectful, committed friendship with Him.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Moses’ face shone from being with God. When we are close to God, others will notice too. Our behavior and character will shine. What did Jesus say we should do after spending time with God? **Read Matthew 5:16.**
- What will you do today to spend time with God?
