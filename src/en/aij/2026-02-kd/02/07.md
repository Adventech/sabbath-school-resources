It was a beautiful hairnet. The young woman looked at it longingly. Oh, how she would love to have it! Her hair clips were so plain. In these times, long ago, women loved to cover their hair with a hairnet, and this one was very special.

She looked around the empty room that belonged to her friend, Mrs. White. Then she looked back at the hairnet. She was staying in Mrs. White’s home with some friends. But right now she was the only one home.

_It’s not very big,_ she thought to herself. _Just a little hairnet. I don’t think Mrs. White will miss it at all._ She quickly picked it up and put it in her pocket. Then she hid it in her trunk, a large box where she stored her clothes.

Later Mrs. White needed to go out. She looked for her hairnet, but couldn’t find it anywhere!

In the evening Mrs. White asked, “Has anyone seen my hairnet? It was on my dresser, but now it’s gone.” There was silence. How strange!

The next day, when Mrs. White walked through the girl’s room, a voice said, “Open that trunk.” She recognized the voice as that of an angel. When she lifted the lid, there was her hairnet!

She left it in the trunk but asked everyone about it again that evening. She hoped the girl would be honest. But no one spoke.

More days passed. One morning Mrs. White was given a surprising vision from God, like an awake dream. In her vision she saw the hand of a girl burn her hairnet in a small flame.

Once more, with sadness, Mrs. White asked everyone about the hairnet. Still no one told the truth.

Finally she called the young woman to her room and told her about the angel’s voice, what she’d seen, and her vision. The young woman’s eyes filled with tears as she admitted she had taken it. “I am so sorry; please forgive me,” she cried. Mrs. White gladly forgave her. She asked God to forgive her too.

This woman had thought that taking the hairnet was just a _little_ thing, but she learned that little things are important because they grow into big things. Little good choices grow into big good choices. Little bad choices can grow into big bad choices. This was a lesson in honesty she would never forget!
