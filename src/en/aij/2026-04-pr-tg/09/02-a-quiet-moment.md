**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

For 38 years a man had lived with a terrible sickness. Unable to walk, he lay on his mat feeling lonely, miserable, and discouraged. He had no one to help him, and his hope of healing had faded away.

But one day everything changed when Jesus came walking by the Pool of Bethesda. “Do you want to be made well?” was His simple question (John 5:6). Jesus didn’t check to first see if this man acknowledged Him as the Messiah. The man did not even know who Jesus was. Yet Jesus loved him and wanted to help him. Jesus healed him and changed his life from that day on.

As He did with this man, Jesus comes to where we are to heal us, to love us, and to change our lives for the better. We do nothing to deserve it, yet He still comes close to make us whole. This striking truth is completely life-changing! His love is so deep and wide that we’ll never completely grasp it.

This Sabbath, may both you and the children in your Sabbath School class know that you are loved by an incredible, powerful, holy God.

“For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities nor powers, nor things present nor things to come, nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 8:38, 39).

May you and the children be blessed this week as you explore the deep lovingkindness of God—His love in action that is generous, steadfast, and compassionate for all.
