Create

Use play-dough/modeling clay to create a basket boat. Make a tiny baby Moses to place in the basket.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#84A268"}}}
Explore

Go on a nature walk and collect leaves and grasses, or use indoor plants to feel textures and imagine making a basket boat made of plants. Hide a doll in your home and pretend it is crying, and you need to keep it quiet. Talk about the ways Jochebed, Amram, Miriam, and Aaron would have helped to care for baby Moses.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#84A268"}}}
Connect

Together with your child, make a card/gift for a new baby or younger sibling to let them know that you love and care for them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#84A268"}}}
Make

Help your child make a special book that has drawings or photographs of family members. Write a simple message beside each picture. For example: _This is Mommy/Daddy. Mommy/Daddy loves Jesus. Mommy/Daddy loves me._ Cuddle up together and read the book often.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#84A268"}}}
Pray

Pray for a family member each day. Invite your child to choose which family member to pray for. Thank God for giving us families who love and care for us.
