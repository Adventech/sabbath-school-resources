God never breaks His promises. He wants us to be like Him and know His pure, true love. What did He say was commandment number seven? **Read Exodus 20:14.**

The bride gracefully floated down the church aisle in her soft, white wedding dress. She looked beautiful as she made her way to the front. The bride and groom smiled lovingly at each other. They held hands as the pastor began to speak.

“Welcome, everyone. We are here today, in the presence of God, family, and friends, to watch this man and woman make a ‘forever’ marriage promise.”

The service continued with a Bible reading and songs. Finally it was time for the couple to make their promises to each other. Everyone listened closely as they both promised to love each other for the rest of their lives.

The pastor then bowed his head and prayed for God to bless the couple. Opening his eyes, the pastor said, “I now pronounce you husband and wife. ‘God has joined the two people together. So no one should separate them’ [Mark 10:9, ICB].”

God wants every marriage to be filled with love and last forever. He says, “Love one another; as I have loved you” (John 13:34). He asked us to keep our marriage promises true too.

_(Parents: This may be a sensitive topic in some homes in which divorce, abuse, or separation have occurred. If necessary, please talk with your child about your individual situation and pray together for God’s love to always fill your home.)_
