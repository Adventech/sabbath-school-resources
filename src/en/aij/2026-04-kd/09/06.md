The large female humpback whale was floating on the top of the water, breathing heavy and barely moving. A fisherman out in his boat saw the poor whale all tangled in nets just past the Farallon Islands. He could see she was in trouble and radioed for help.

A few hours later, a diving rescue team arrived. The whale was wrapped in a spiderweb of heavy crab traps, nets, and lines. It was twisted and knotted around the whale’s mouth, head, back, fins, and tail. The whale floated, helplessly tangled up and completely exhausted.

The rescue divers could clearly see there was no way she could free herself. They decided to save her.

They put on their scuba gear and splashed into the water. Together they all began cutting the tightly wrapped ropes. As the nets were cut, they sunk deep into the ocean.

After many hours the rescuers finally cut the last rope, and the large whale suddenly disappeared. She was free! The divers celebrated together in the ocean.

All of a sudden, they looked down in the water. Coming straight up toward them was the gigantic whale! The divers didn’t know whether to be scared or excited.

The huge whale came right up close to one of the divers and stopped. She gently nudged him on the chest and stared at him. Then she swam up close to the next diver, and the next diver, spending time looking at each one.

That evening, as the divers finally headed home with the setting sun, it was clear to them that the whale was thankful they had saved her.
