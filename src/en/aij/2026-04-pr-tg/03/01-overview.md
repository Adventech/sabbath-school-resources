**Word of the Week**

**Friend:** A friend is a person we have gotten to know well and like to spend time with. Someone we trust to be helpful and loyal.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

A man with paralysis was unwell and felt burdened by his sins. Four kind friends encouraged the man and took him to see Jesus, the great healer. Jesus was in a home, surrounded by a huge crowd. Seeing that they could not break through the crowd, the four friends carried the man with paralysis to the roof, made a hole, and lowered him down to be at the feet of Jesus. Jesus forgave the man’s sins and healed his body, and the man’s life was forever changed.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God has the power to heal and forgive.
- **Feel** a desire to help bring others to Jesus.
- **Respond** by being willing to lead their friends to Jesus so that they too can experience His love.
