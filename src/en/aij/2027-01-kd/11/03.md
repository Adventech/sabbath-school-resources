At the very bottom of Mount Sinai many of the Israelites were starting to frown and sigh. _Why was Moses taking so long?_ they wondered. _He’s been gone for ages!_

A big crowd gathered around Aaron. “Moses led us out of Egypt,” they grumbled, “but we don’t know what has happened to him. So make us gods who will lead us” (Exodus 32:1, ICB).

They wanted Aaron to make an idol! God had only just told them not to make idols. How could they forget? They had heard Him with their own ears. They had seen Him with their own eyes in the cloud and fire. An idol could never take the place of the living, saving God.

Surely Aaron would tell them not to be so silly and forgetful. Aaron was sure to point to the mountain and tell them to pray to the one true God. What did Aaron choose to do? **Read Exodus 32:4.**

Oh, no! Aaron made them an idol in the shape of a golden calf. It was just like the idols from Egypt.

Aaron let the crowd pressure him into doing the wrong thing. This wasn’t God’s way. God can help us to obey and choose His way. We can pray, “Teach me to do your will, for you are my God” (Psalm 143:10).

God’s way is always best—it leads to life and joy. Aaron should have asked God for courage to do the right thing. God is always willing to help us stay strong and brave if we ask Him.
