**Opening Activities**

Source **Small pieces of paper and colored markers**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: fruit with a hole in the center. You may like to core an apple and create apple rings, or orange rings, melon rings, or local fruit from your culture that can be cut with a hole in the middle.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **storytelling props**: a long **stick** for Moses’ staff, **cup** for each child, **blue fabric** for water, **tree branch**. A **box** as Mount Sinai, with **blue fabric** hidden inside, that can be pulled out as “gushing water.”

Prepare a **bottle** of “sweet” water by mixing some **honey** with **water**.

Use peg people (Moses, families) or a felt set, if desired, and the **^[week 6 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: some **things you are thankful for**, such as a card from a loved one, a toothbrush, or warm socks. Place items that the children might be thankful for (or photos of things) around your Sabbath School room/area or outside. For example, food, flowers, clothing, books, pictures of people, animals, etc.
