Little Ellen Harmon (who would later become Ellen White) lived in a country home with her family. Her house sat on a quiet hill. Behind their home was a valley, some woods, and a little stream.

In Ellen’s home every child had jobs to do. One of Ellen’s jobs was to bring the family cow, Bossy, up to the shed every evening so she could be milked. Ellen always found Bossy easily, because the cow seemed to know when it was time for her to be milked.

But one evening Ellen couldn’t find Bossy anywhere! _That’s strange_, she thought. _Where’s Bossy?_

Ellen began to call, “Come, Bossy; come, Bossy!” Then she listened, but she didn’t hear a thing. She called louder. Still no answer. Ellen knew something must be wrong, because Bossy had always come.

Ellen looked everywhere. But still no Bossy. She walked into the woods, calling for Bossy as she went. Finally Ellen heard a faint, “Moo!” She followed the sound. And there, in the middle of the stream, was Bossy. She was stuck in mud!

How was Ellen going to get her out? She picked some nice tall grass and leaned across the water so the cow could reach it. Bossy happily munched it up.

Ellen found some more grass, but this time she waved it in front of Bossy’s mouth with one hand while she held Bossy’s horn with the other. Then giving a mighty pull, she said, “Come on, Bossy.”

The cow made an extra effort to pull herself up out of the mud to reach the delicious grass. Once Bossy was out of the mud, Ellen gave her the yummy treat. Little Ellen had saved her!

God saw that His beautiful people were stuck in sin, just as Bossy was stuck in the mud. He sent Jesus to save us. Our God is mighty to save. “The Lord your God in your midst, the Mighty One, will save” (Zephaniah 3:17).
