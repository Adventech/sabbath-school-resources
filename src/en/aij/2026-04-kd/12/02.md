Jesus and the crowd of followers carefully walked up the rocky road. Slowly, they came closer and closer to the gates of the little mountain village of Nain.

But as they drew near, sounds of sad wailing and crying began to echo down the mountainside. Coming out of the city gates was a very sorry sight. A young man who had died was being carried out in a stretcher followed by his mother. She had no husband to hold her hand, and no other children to cry with. This son had been the only family she had left and now he had died too. How very sad for the widow!

Who else followed along? **Read the last part of Luke 7:12.**

Together, the mother and villagers began the slow, heartbreaking journey out the gates toward the rocky tombs not far out of town.

Jesus watched this poor lady walking along the road with tears streaming down her face. He felt her sadness deep in His own heart. He felt very sorry for her.

Jesus is so sad for us when the people we love die. He wants to give us joy and comfort when we’re sad.

God has bigger, better plans for us. The Bible says that Jesus is coming to take us to heaven. Then, God will make our earth new and perfect again. “He will wipe away every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death, sadness, crying, or pain” (Revelation 21:4, ICB).

Jesus sadly watched the mother wipe away her tears. He wanted to do something! He wanted to bring comfort to this sad woman.
