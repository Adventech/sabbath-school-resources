God’s rich, deep voice continued to rumble down the mountain as He shared His ninth commandment. “You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor” (Exodus 20:16). The Bible also says the same thing another way: “Do not lie to one another” (Colossians 3:9).

It is easy to say or do something that isn’t true; it’s easier than we might think. As well as telling a lie, we can lie by exaggerating, or keeping silent when the truth should be told, or avoiding the truth by our actions. When we lie about something we have or haven’t done, other people will not trust us, so we are hurting ourselves and our relationships. When we lie about others, we are hurting them and their reputation (what others think of them). How much happier everyone would be if we always told the truth!

When God spoke His tenth commandment, what did He say? **Read Exodus 20:17.**

God knows how miserable people can make themselves by never being happy with what they have. “I wish I had his bike instead of mine.” “I want Kate’s new game so badly.” “It’s not fair; he got to be the leader when I wanted to.” Such thoughts are coveting something belonging to your neighbor.

Wanting other people’s things is not a happy way to live. It is much better to be glad when others have something better than you. Ecclesiastes 4:6 tells us, “I say it is better to be content with what little you have. Otherwise, you will always be struggling for more. That is like chasing the wind” (ICB). What a waste of time that is!
