Noah drew God’s plans for the ark, and then he started to work.

Strong workmen came to help cut down tall trees that were almost as hard as rock. Other men carefully lined up the wooden boards, and then sealed them with pitch so that the ark wouldn’t leak. Noah carefully followed everything God had told him. He built the boat exactly as God had said. How many decks did the ark have? Read Genesis 6:16.

Years went by, and all the workers could see the huge boat taking shape. It was long—longer than four football fields placed end to end and as tall as a five-story building! As the workers came, Noah told them—and anyone else who came to see the ark—about God’s plan to wash the world. Noah was faithful. For 120 years he worked and preached and built and obeyed. That’s a lot of birthdays! The Bible says that “Noah did everything that God commanded him” (Genesis 6:22, ICB). Walking with God every day helped him to be faithful.

Noah’s sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, and his grandfather, Methuselah, as well as many others, helped build the ark. Methuselah was the oldest man who ever lived on this earth, and he died when he was 969 years old, a little while before the rains came. As long as they lived, people who loved God warned others about the Flood.

God hoped that every single person would choose to love and follow Him. Every person had a chance to hear God’s message, make a choice, and be safe.

As the people worked, Noah’s family collected such food as apples and potatoes, rice and peas, raisins and carrots—all kinds of food! They carefully stored large amounts of food, seeds, and grains on the ark. They would need them when the rain came.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Do you think Noah liked all the jobs he had to do? What does Genesis 6:22 tell us about how he worked?_

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#eef0f1", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#56666f"}}}
^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Build a fort and pretend it’s an ark. Gather toys to put in your ark and imagine what you would need to prepare to “live” in your ark for more than a year. Then, in your fort, read the story from your Bible.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#fbf7ee", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#dbac51"}}}
^[FOR YOU, PARENTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Be encouraged that your work as a mother or father is of eternal significance, despite how busy your life may be or how tired you may feel right now. You may wish you had an army of workers to help you (as did Noah!), but have peace knowing that when you nurture your child’s walk with God, you are doing the most important work in the world. Have courage; God will be your help and strength (Isaiah 41:10).
