Spreading God’s Love

Jesus and His disciple friends talked together as they walked along the dusty road that led away from Capernaum. They were headed toward the little mountain village of Nain, and had a long trip ahead of them.

All along the way, people spilled out of their homes and villages to meet Jesus. They were hungry to hear His words of love and sympathy, and they brought their sick to be healed. They may not have heard Jesus say, “Come to me all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest” (Matthew 11:28). But they knew to come anyway. Jesus didn’t disappoint them—He paused to encourage and heal many.

Why do you think the people left what they were doing, and ran and met Jesus when He hadn’t invited them to? **Read the first half of John 6:44.**

After meeting Jesus, the villagers were far too grateful and excited to return to their houses and chores! Instead, they joined the ever-growing crowd that surrounded Jesus and tramped uphill on the winding path toward Nain. Their steps slowed and their breathing deepened as the way became steep and rocky. But still the happy crowd walked on towards the little mountain village with Jesus. They were glad to have met Him, and they knew the people of Nain needed to meet Him too. Everybody needed to meet Jesus and see how much He loved them!
