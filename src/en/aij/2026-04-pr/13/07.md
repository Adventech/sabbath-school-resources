The Impossible Egg-speriment

Your task is to fit a whole egg into a bottle—with an opening that’s smaller than the egg. Can the impossible become possible?

**You will need:**

- _a glass bottle, with the opening slightly smaller than the diameter of the egg_
- _a peeled hard-boiled egg_
- _kettle_
- _water_
- _oven mitt_

1. Fill your kettle with water and bring to a boil.
2. With help from a grown-up, fill your bottle with the boiled water. Let it sit for 20 seconds.
3. Using an oven mitt, pour out the boiling water.
4. Wait 15 seconds, then pop your egg on top of the bottle. Now wait. Be patient and have faith.

**What happened?**

An unseen power, air pressure, changes everything! When you heat the bottle, the glass remains hot. This heats up the air inside, and it rushes out the top. This leaves low air pressure in the bottle that’s trapped by the egg, and it gets lower as the bottle cools. Now there’s a big difference between outside air pressure and inside air pressure. God has designed nature to be in an even, balanced state. So the air pressure pushes the egg through the seemingly impossible small hole, and balance is restored.

With our powerful Creator God all things are possible! Sometimes we have to wait in faith for what is promised but can’t be seen. Remember the true stories from this quarter; be patient and believe. God is trying to teach you things every day so that you will know, love, and follow Him. And the best news is that He’s coming soon!
