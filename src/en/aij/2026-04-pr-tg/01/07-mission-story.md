**Focus:** God still calms storms in people’s lives and hearts.

**Location:** Help a child locate **New Caledonia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Rain was falling, and a big storm was on its way. Cyclone Erika was about to hit Annie’s country of New Caledonia and people had been told to stay indoors.

Annie and her two children were in the car, a long way from home. Annie knew the storm was coming, and she was very afraid. Her car ran out of gas and came to a stop near a tree. She didn’t know where she was. She hoped this would be a safe place in the terrible storm.

“God, if You exist, I want You to look after my children and me tonight,” she said. She leaned back in the driver’s seat and looked out the window as her children slept. She blinked hard and looked again. To her amazement, the clouds rolled back, and the heavens opened. She trembled as she saw thousands of angels walking back and forth in the heavens. Then the heavens closed, and a ladder stretched from the sky to the car. It looked like a giant rope ladder.

Three angels came down the ladder. They were tall, had wings, and shone brightly with a white light. One angel stood in front of the car and extended his giant wings, reaching to the back of the car. The two other angels stood on each side of the car, also covering it with their wings.

Then Annie felt the car gently being rocked like a mother rocks a baby cradle. A pleasant warmth filled the cold car.

Annie’s fear vanished, and she felt a sense of peace. Her eyes grew heavy, and she slipped into a deep sleep.

Annie awoke to the sound of birds chirping. The cyclone was over! Looking out the window, she saw the ladder descend from the sky again. The angels folded their wings and went up the ladder.

Her prayer had been answered! Annie immediately gave her heart to Jesus. She was later baptized.

- What does it tell you about God?
