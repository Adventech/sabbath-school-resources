**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. His words can heal and are always true.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use peg people or felts for Jesus, His disciples, the nobleman, and his son.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 2 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a felt set or peg people, if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The nobleman walked slowly to his son’s bedroom ^[(peg man or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was a rich and important man—he worked for the king. He usually stood tall. But today his shoulders sagged. ^[(Show sagging shoulders.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) His son was sick, and he was very worried ^[(peg child or felt boy in bed)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The nobleman stopped beside his son’s bed. His little boy was so sick and tired he could barely open his eyes. He was very close to dying.

The nobleman didn’t want his son to die! What could he do?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Just then he heard some exciting news. Jesus the great Healer and Teacher was close by in the town of Cana! He was just an afternoon’s walk from the nobleman’s home in Capernaum.

Jesus is so near, thought the nobleman. He would go to Jesus and ask Him to save his son! He believed that Jesus could.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The nobleman galloped down the road on his horse, leaving a dusty cloud behind. He must hurry!

Arriving at the gates of Cana, he quickly climbed off and was told where to find Jesus. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Big crowds surrounded Jesus ^[(Jesus peg person or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). But the nobleman pushed and squeezed his way toward Him.

Bowing low ^[(bow low)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), the nobleman “went to Jesus and begged him to come to Capernaum and heal his son” (John 4:47, ICB).

He said, “Sir, come down before my child dies” (John 4:49).

Jesus’ heart filled with compassion. He already knew about the pain and sadness the nobleman and his family had gone through. He knew the nobleman before they had even met. He wanted to help.

Jesus answered, “Go your way; your son lives” (John 4:50).

Suddenly all the worry in the nobleman’s mind went away. He was filled with peace. With a happy heart he thanked Jesus. Then with a big smile on his face he walked away, believing Jesus’ words were true. He didn’t even go straight home. He stayed the night in Cana. He believed that Jesus’ words could heal and were true.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Early the next morning the nobleman packed his things and climbed on his horse. With a song in his heart, he headed down the road toward home.

As the nobleman came closer to his home, his servants ran to meet him ^[(two peg men or felts)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Puffing and panting ^[(puff and pant)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), they had big news to share. The nobleman’s son was alive and well!

But the nobleman wasn’t surprised at all. “The man asked, ‘What time did my son begin to get well?’ They answered,

‘It was about one o’clock yesterday when the fever left him’ ” (John 4:52, ICB).

The nobleman’s smile grew bigger and bigger. The one o’clock miracle was the very hour that Jesus had said, “Go your way; your son lives” (John 4:50).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The nobleman raced home and hugged his healthy son ^[(peg or felt child)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then he thanked God again and again for his son’s miracle healing. The excited nobleman quickly told everyone in his house about how the words of Jesus had healed his son. The words of Jesus were powerful. The words of Jesus were true!

Everyone in the household looked at the boy with his rosy cheeks and big smile. The Bible says, “So the man and all the people of his house believed in Jesus” (John 4:53, ICB).
