**Big Idea:** God can guide my life.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- You may like to use the following props: toy figurines or peg people to represent the Wise Men, Baby Jesus, Mary, and Joseph; a bright star or a flashlight to guide the Wise Men; a toy angel, peg person, or flashlight for the angel’s appearance.
- Ask children to open their Bibles and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) King Herod stomped down the halls of his beautiful palace. The visit by the Wise Men from the East had left him in a terrible mood. His robes swirled around him as he began to plot a wicked scheme.

The king had sent the Wise Men happily on their way. He had said, “Go and search carefully for the young Child, and when you have found Him, bring back word to me, that I may come and worship Him also” (Matthew 2:8). But it was all a lie. Instead of worshipping Baby Jesus, King Herod secretly planned to kill Him!

King Herod was jealous; he wanted power all to himself. The more he thought about it, the angrier he became. He was following Satan’s guidance to try to harm Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Wise Men knew nothing of Herod’s evil plans. They were full of joy as they packed up their belongings and prepared to leave Bethlehem. They had found the Messiah and worshipped him! Now it was time to begin their long journey home. They planned to stop in Jerusalem and share their wonderful news with King Herod. Then he, and all the Hebrew people, could find Baby Jesus and worship Him too. Or so they thought.

But God had a different plan. He was watching over those Wise Men, and He knew that stopping in Jerusalem was a very, VERY dangerous idea. He needed to warn them right away! That night, He gave them a dream.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did God tell them to do, and how did they respond? Read Matthew 2:12.** (God warned them not to return to Herod, so they went home a different way.)

The Wise Men were truly wise to trust God. God had guided them to the Bible scrolls, to the stars, and to Jesus. He would guide them safely home.

God could see there was terrible danger ahead for Jesus. King Herod was determined to get rid of the Baby King. But God was watching over Him.

That night, as Joseph slept, God also sent him a dream.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he tell him to do? Read Matthew 2:13.** (To take Jesus and Mary and quickly flee to Egypt. They were to stay in Egypt until God told them it was safe to leave again.)

Joseph’s eyes opened and he was instantly wide awake. Yes, he was sure of the next steps he must take. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a flash he woke Mary, and together they quickly loaded their little donkey. Silently they hurried away into the night, with Jesus held tightly in Mary’s arms.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) King Herod sat on his throne, fuming. His servants trembled as he began to rage. The Wise Men had not come back, as promised. He’d been tricked!

The king called for his soldiers. In a fit of rage, he ordered that all baby boys two years old and younger in Bethlehem and the surrounding districts, were to be killed. What a sad day that was.

God guided Joseph, Mary, and Jesus safely to Egypt. Here, they made a happy home. Baby Jesus grew into little Boy Jesus.

One night, when Jesus was about three years old, God gave Joseph a third dream ^[(hold up the angel prop again.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The angel told Joseph it was safe to return to Israel—Herod was no longer on the throne.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Once more, Joseph and Mary loaded up the donkey. Then taking little Jesus in their arms, they set off for Bethlehem. But Herod’s meanest son was now the ruler there. It was not a safe place for Jesus. On the way, God gave Joseph a fourth dream, guiding him to Nazareth instead. What a loving Father, Guide, and Friend.
