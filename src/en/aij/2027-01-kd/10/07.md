Stars lit up the night sky as Kevin and Janine Golcheski hopped into their car. With big smiles, they waved goodbye to their friends. The two grandparents were on vacation. They’d had a wonderful evening visiting old friends.

The couple chatted together happily as they drove back to their hotel. They were so busy talking that they missed their turn. On and on they drove. The streets got quieter and darker. Soon, open fields and bushland surrounded them. Kevin shook his head. “Where on earth are we?”

Kevin had a map on his phone. But he hadn’t been able to get the map to work.

Finally he pulled the car to a stop by the side of the road. There were no houses. No cars. They were really, truly lost! Janine bowed her head and prayed that God would help them.

As Janine said “Amen,” a police car pulled up beside them.

“What are you two doing way out here?” the policeman asked.

“We’re lost!” they said.

Kevin told him where their hotel was. “That’s a long, long way from here,” the policeman said. He picked up Kevin’s phone and helped to get the map to work. Then he said, “Follow me! I’ll take you all the way.”

With their map working, the policeman led them right to their hotel, back to safety.

The Ten Commandments are a lot like a map. They keep us from getting lost and hurt by sin. God’s map leads us to love and respect Him, and others.

Sometimes we might forget to follow God’s Word, His map. We may make mistakes and get lost. But we can count on God to help us. Just like the policeman, He says, “Follow me!” He will lead us back to His commandments. The Bible says, “The Lord will always lead you” (Isaiah 58:11, ICB).
