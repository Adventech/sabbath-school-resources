**Lesson Summary**

After the flood, Noah’s descendants could choose to depend either on God or on themselves. Many chose to depend on themselves. They built the Tower of Babel to defy God, His plan, and promises. This wouldn’t make them happy or content. God intervened and mixed up their languages. As a result, the people spread out, and God’s rescue plan and promise went forward.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that when we choose to depend on God, we will be happy and content.
- **Feel** thankful for the things our loving God does to help us depend on Him.
- **Respond** by choosing to depend on God.
