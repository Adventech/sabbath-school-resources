**Word of the Week**

**Peace:** Peace is quiet and calm. God’s peace in our hearts gives us a safe and content feeling deep inside. Our peaceful words are soft, gentle, and forgiving. Peace means no fighting or war.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Following God’s guidance, Jacob and his household secretly left Laban’s home. A furious Laban chased after them. God gave Laban a dream instructing him to peacefully listen, and the family made peace. Jacob was scared about meeting his brother, Esau. God showed him that angels protected him. Jacob sent Esau a polite message, then many animal gifts. Esau didn’t answer, but advanced with an army of men. Afraid, Jacob prayed. God came close and wrestled with Jacob in the night. God gave him forgiveness, peace, and a blessing. Jacob and Esau were peacefully reunited.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God gives peace and is with them.
- **Feel** happy and safe because God protects them.
- **Respond** by asking God for peace to be in their hearts.
