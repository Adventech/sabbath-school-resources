**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever tried to drive somewhere unknown without directions? It can feel very frustrating, not knowing whether to turn right or left or continue straight ahead. If only you had a map or GPS to guide you!

Fortunately, we do. God sees the story of our lives from beginning to end, and He knows the best route for us to take. Our role is to ask Him for guidance and then trust His leading.

Joseph’s life story is full of unexpected twists and turns. At many points it would have been easy to wonder whether God was directing him through the challenges or whether he was wandering along by himself. Yet he continued to seek and follow God’s guidance each day. God did amazing things through Joseph and at last brought him full circle—back into his father’s arms.

God will do the same for you, friend. Our journey on this earth may be full of twists and turns and different situations that are difficult to bear and that cause us to wonder what God is doing. But when we simply put our hand in His, He will lead us safely through these times to a glorious future.

“God never leads His children otherwise than they would choose to be led, if they could see the end from the beginning, and discern the glory of the purpose which they are fulfilling as co-workers with Him” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 224, 225).
