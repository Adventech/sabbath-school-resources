**What you need:**

Week 2 craft template on cardstock; scissors; colored pencils; clear contact paper; green, yellow, orange, and red tissue paper or cellophane; glue; battery-operated tealight candle (optional).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-pr-craft-02.pdf)**

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out pieces of clear contact paper in the shape of a treetop, ensuring they are large enough to cover the tree branches (in the craft template).
- Cut small pieces of green, yellow, orange and red tissue paper or cellophane.

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the pop-up template and fold in half along the dotted line. Cut two slits along the vertical lines in the center.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2b.jpg)

2\. Open the paper and invert the middle section to create a pop-up.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2c.jpg)

3\. Color and cut out the pictures in the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2d.jpg)

4\. Peel the backing off the piece of contact paper and carefully stick the branches of the tree over the top—facing down.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2e.jpg)

5\. Stick pieces of tissue paper/cellophane to cover the contact paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2f.jpg)

6\. Glue the trunk of the tree to the pop-up section of the card. Glue “Moses” on the lower section of the card. Place a battery-operated tealight candle behind the tree to make it glow (optional).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2g.jpg)

7\. Put your craft on display at home and see the burning bush glow at night!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/2h.jpg)
