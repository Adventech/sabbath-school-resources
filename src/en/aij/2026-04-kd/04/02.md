The disciples walked out into the cool water and then climbed into a boat. They could hear the happy sounds of people whose tummies were full of bread and fish. The day had ended. Now the sky was bright with stars. Jesus had asked them to leave early to sail to the other side of the lake. He would stay behind and say goodbye to the crowd.

After saying goodbye to Jesus, the people on the hillside finally went home. What an exciting day it had been! Jesus waved goodbye, then walked up the hill.

There was something Jesus needed to do. What was it? **Read Matthew 14:23.**

Jesus needed to spend some time talking with God, His Father. He talked with God about the disciples. He talked with God about the people He had seen that day. He wanted them to understand why He was here on earth. He prayed for a long time about the things that worried Him or made Him happy.

God listened to every word. The Bible tells us, “The Lord is near to all who call upon Him, to all who call on Him in truth” (Psalm 145:18).

Jesus wanted His Father, God, to know and help Him with everything. Praying helped Jesus to be brave and strong. God would always help Him.

You can pray to God when you are happy or sad too. You can pray when you are thankful, and when you need help. God will help you to be brave and strong. God will always be there for you.
