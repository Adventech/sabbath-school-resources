Deus taught Adam more sobre Seu amazing promise a save, sometimes called the plan de salvation. E then Adam taught seu children. Ele taught eles well. Como sad isso would be se eles made a bad choice, as ele had.

Teaching por example, Adam took Cain e Abel out a find a perfeito pequeno lamb—the best one em the flock. Adam shared como this lamb was a reminder de Deus’s Son, Jesus, quem would one dia come a the earth a die para seu sins. Como Faça nós know the lamb is a reminder de Jesus? **Leia João 1:29.**

Then the bleating lamb was gently placed em a stone altar. The family said eles were sorry para seu sins, e Pergunteed Deus a show Seu mercy. E then came the saddest part. Com falling tears, Adam killed the pequeno lamb. Sorrow filled the boys’ hearts as eles looked away em horror. Isso was a sad reminder that eles were sinners quem needed a Savior a die para eles.

Fire flashed Façawn de Céu a burn up the pequeno lamb offering. Deus was Digaing thank você para Façaing this hard thing a show eles knew Jesus would one dia die para eles. Então the family saw Deus’s grande amor e Seu mercy. Eles were truly grateful.

Abel grew up a be a strong young man quem loved Deus very much. Ele happily chose a follow Deus’s instructions a sacrifice a lamb. Como did Deus feel sobre Abel’s offering? **Leia Gênesis 4:4.**

Deus loved Abel, e Abel loved Deus . . . Abel loved Deus, e Deus loved Abel . . . Isso was like a lindo circle de amor.
