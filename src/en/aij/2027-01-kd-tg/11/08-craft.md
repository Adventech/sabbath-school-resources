**What you need:**

Week 11 craft template, red and green crayons, scissors, glue, a piece of cardstock, cotton swab, tape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-kd-craft-11.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color the wheel with red and green crayons and cut out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11b.jpg)

2\. Cut out the pictures and teardrop-shaped pointer on page 2 of the craft template. Sort the pictures into “good” and “bad” choices and set aside the pointer until step 7.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11c.jpg)

3\. Glue the four “good” choices onto the green wheel segments. Glue the four “bad” choices onto the red segments.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11d.jpg)

4\. Fold a piece of cardstock into four even sections (horizontally).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11e.jpg)

5\. Glue the two side sections of the cardstock together, making a triangular prism to be used as a handle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11f.jpg)

6\. Cut off the end of a cotton swab.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11g.jpg)

7\. Position the pointer and wheel with centers together on the flat side of the handle, as shown.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11h.jpg)

8\. Adults, use a pen to poke a hole through the center of the pointer, the center of the wheel, and the flat side of the handle. Poke the cotton swab through the hole, connecting the pointer, wheel, and handle. Tape the bottom end of the cotton swab to the pointy side of the prism.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11h.jpg)

9\. Push the pointer up to the felt tip of the cotton swab so it is not touching the wheel. Children can play the “choice game” with a friend. Take turns spinning the wheel and see who can land on the most green “good” choices!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/11i.jpg)
