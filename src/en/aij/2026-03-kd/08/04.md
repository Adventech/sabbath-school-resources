Jesus now had three followers: Andrew, John, and Simon Peter. There would be other followers too—they just didn’t know it yet!

With God’s joy in His heart, Jesus set off for Galilee. There He found a man named Philip. “Follow me,” Jesus said to him with a smile (John 1:43).

Philip nodded his head with a big yes! When you become a follower of Jesus, it’s impossible to keep it a secret. Philip rushed to tell his good friend, Nathanael.

In a quiet place, under a shady fig tree, sat Nathanael. He was quietly praying. Suddenly Philip appeared. Excitedly he explained to Nathanael how he had found the promised Messiah. His name was Jesus, from Nazareth.

But Nathanael wasn’t so sure. “Come and see,” Philip replied with a sparkle in his eye (John 1:46).

Nathanael followed Philip to Jesus. Jesus smiled as soon as He saw Nathanael. “Here is truly a person of Israel. There is nothing false in him,” Jesus said (John 1:47, ICB).

Nathanael was shocked. He’d never met Jesus before! “How do You know me?” he asked Jesus.

“Before Philip called you, when you were under the fig tree, I saw you,” Jesus replied (John 1:48).

Nathanael was amazed! Jesus knew all about his prayers under the fig tree. He must be the Messiah! What did Nathanael say next? **Read John 1:49.**

Philip and Nathanael had joined the group of Jesus’ followers. If we study the Bible and pray, God will teach us His truth and show us how to follow Him too.
