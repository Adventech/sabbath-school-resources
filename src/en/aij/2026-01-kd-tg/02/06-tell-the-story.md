**Big Idea:** God is loving. He created me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **dirt in a container** and hide a **peg person** down inside it. Have another **peg person for Eve**.
- Play the “Animal Noises” song.
- Pull **toy animals** out of a bag for the children to make the sounds and “name” the animals.
- Pictures of **^[baby animals]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **flashlight** to shine “light” for clothing.
- Lay a **green blanket** down as a “garden” and invite the children to sit on it.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 2 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- On DAY 6 God spoke and created animals, big and small! ^[(Toy animals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God made a boy and girl of every animal so they could have babies and big families. ^[(Two toy animals that are the same. Show pictures of baby animals and comment on them. Which is your favorite? Thank God for the baby animals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God gave each animal a special sound to make. ^[(Play animal noises game. Listen to the Animal Noises song.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God shaped a man out of the dust—the very first human. God breathed into him, and he came alive. ^[(Bend down and breathe life into “Adam.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God named this man Adam. He gave him clothes of light ^[(shine a light on your “Adam”).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God gave Adam a beautiful garden home—Eden ^[(lay out a green blanket for children to sit on).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Then God gave Adam the fun job of naming all the animals! ^[(Children may name and make the sound of the toy animals as you pull them out of a bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God made Adam a special friend from Adam’s rib while he was sleeping—Eve. ^[(Peg man and peg woman. Find and feel own ribs.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Adam woke up to meet Eve. He loved her. God united them in marriage ^[(stand the two peg people together).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God’s creation work was now done. “Then God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good” (Genesis 1:31). Repeat **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He created me!**

**TELL THE STORY**

Five whole days had come and gone since God began creating the beautiful new world. God could see that it was good ^[(look through the binoculars)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), but He wasn’t finished yet!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God spoke, and His powerful voice made all kinds of different animals. ^[(Point out different features on your collection of animals. Do not name them yet.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Some animals had soft, silky fur. Others had tough, rough skin. Some had huge ears, and others had teeny, tiny tails. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God made a male and female ^[(boy and girl)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) of every kind of animal so they could have babies and big families: two barking dogs, two big elephants, two fast leopards, and two mooing cows. ^[(Two toy animals that are the same. Show pictures of baby animals and comment on how cute they are. Which is your favorite? Thank God for the baby animals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God gave each animal a special sound to make too! Roar went the lions. Buzz went the bees. Hee-haw went the donkey. Cock-a-doodle-doo went the rooster. God must have smiled on the sixth day as He looked at all the noisy and playful animals He had just made. ^[(Invite each child to move and sound like their favorite animal, while the other children guess which animal they are imitating. If time allows, listen to the Animal Noises song.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then God bent down and took dust from the ground and began to shape the dirt into two feet and two long legs. He made a chest, two arms, and 10 fingers. Shaping a head, God made two ears, two eyes, a nose, and a mouth ^[(Begin to show parts of a buried peg “Adam” in your container of dirt.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God was making the very first human ever!

This man, whom God made in His likeness, was perfect. God bent down and breathed into the nose of the man, and suddenly life went into him! ^[(Bend down and breathe life into “Adam.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The man started to breathe and move. His eyes blinked open, and he looked into God’s kind face.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God named this man Adam. He also gave him special clothes to wear, made out of light, like the angels wore! Wow! ^[(Shine a light on your “Adam.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God gave Adam a garden home called Eden ^[(lay out a green blanket and invite children to sit on it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was filled with fields, forests, and flowers. A river flowed through Eden to water the plants. Adam felt so happy to care for his new garden home.

Then God gave Adam the fun job of naming all the animals! Imagine Adam pointing to the different animals and thinking of names for them. ^[(Invite children to name and make the sound of the toy animals as you pull them out of a bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

All the animals had special friends ^[(place two animals together)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Adam needed a special friend too! “So the Lord God caused the man to sleep very deeply” (Genesis 2:21, ICB). ^[(Place your peg “Adam” down for a “sleep.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Do you know where your rib bones are? Can you feel them? ^[(Invite children to stand up and feel their ribs.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) While Adam slept, God took out one of Adam’s ribs and used it to create a beautiful woman for him. ^[(Show the children your peg “Eve” and place it beside “Adam.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam’s eyelids fluttered open. Who was this? His heart filled with joy. Oh! She’s my match. Adam loved her. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God made Eve from Adam’s side so that she could always stand next to him as his helper and friend. Eve couldn’t have been happier! Side by side they stood, ^[(stand the two peg people “Adam” and “Eve” together)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) as God, their Creator and loving Friend, married them.

All was perfect. All was peaceful. God was very happy too—His work was now done. “Then God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good” (Genesis 1:31).

- **Invite the children to stand up and repeat the Big Idea after you: God is loving. He created me!**
