**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Father, mother, son, daughter, brother, sister. These words might stir within us different emotions for various reasons.

From the beginning, God intended for the family to be a representation of His character. “The family tie is the closest, the most tender and sacred, of any on earth. It was designed to be a blessing to mankind” (Ellen G. White, _The Adventist Home_, p. 18). It’s within the family circle that we feel supported, that we develop our identity, and that we may begin to understand God’s love for us. It’s within our families that we (hopefully) first feel that we belong.

Yet, like everything good that God created, sin has left its mark on families. Disconnection, divorce, and abuse have wreaked havoc in today’s families so that sometimes it can be hard to remember God’s ideal. Instead of being a source of joy and security, family relationships are too often characterized by pain and brokenness.

Despite this, there is hope. God’s family, though composed of imperfect humans, is still a place of safety and belonging. When God looks at us, with all our different backgrounds and cultures, He sees His beloved children. He welcomes each of us with open arms. In His family we find our true identity and worth—-as children of the King of the universe. This is where we truly belong.

As you teach this lesson to your Kindergarten Sabbath School family this week, pray that the children will experience joy that comes from belonging to God’s loving family.
