**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

What kinds of things do you worry about? Perhaps you can relate to these thoughts:

- How can I afford to pay all my bills and still keep food on the table?
- How will I meet that work deadline with so many conflicting priorities?
- Are my children making good choices?

We worry about these things and many others. Yet we forget something important: “And my God shall supply all your need” (Philippians 4:19).

Throughout the Scriptures, God reminds us that He cares about our needs and will provide at just the right time, in just the right way. Consider these facts:

- Every day for 40 years God provided food from heaven to millions of His people in the desert.
- God made water come out of a rock to quench their thirst.
- God sent birds to deliver food every day for three years to his prophet Elijah, who was in hiding.
- God miraculously replenished a widow’s oil and flour every day, allowing three people to survive a famine.
- Jesus served thousands a filling meal (with leftovers!) from only a few loaves of bread and fish.
- And in today’s story, He used one man—Joseph—to provide for the needs of an entire country and beyond!

Clearly God is more than capable of providing for us. We need to worry less and trust Him more.

As you teach the lesson this week, may God grant you confidence in His provision, and may you inspire the children to place their trust in their caring, all-powerful God.

“Our heavenly Father has a thousand ways to provide for us, of which we know nothing. Those who accept the one principle of making the service and honor of God supreme will find perplexities vanish, and a plain path before their feet” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 330).
