Moses quickly tied his sandals, nibbled some fresh manna, and stepped out into the morning sun. Father Jethro hurried to catch up with him. There was no more time for family stories in the tent. Moses had a big job to do. He had to help the people solve their problems.

Jethro listened closely as Moses calmly and wisely helped two people who couldn’t agree. Before long, more and more people crowded around Moses with their problems.

The sun crept high in the sky, as the day went on and on, and then it slid down low and disappeared from sight. Moses was still surrounded by people who wanted his help and God’s wisdom. Jethro was still watching and listening.

Finally, Moses said goodbye to the last of the people. Jethro noticed his sagging shoulders and his tired eyes. Moses was completely exhausted. Jethro asked Moses, “What is this thing that you are doing for the people? Why do you alone sit, and all the people stand before you from morning until evening?” (Exodus 18:14).

Moses shrugged. He knew that God’s way was best. He had to help the people learn what that was. What did Jethro say? **Read Exodus 18:17, 18.**

Jethro showed Moses his love by paying attention to him. He noticed what he needed, and wanted to help. You can do that for your family too! The Bible says, “Let all that you do be done with love” (1 Corinthians 16:14).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- How did Jethro find out that Moses needed help?
- What might you do to find out if your family needs a helping hand?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to give you eyes that notice, ears that listen, and a heart that’s eager to help.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Play a game of “I spy” with your family, in the outdoors if possible. Only choose and guess things in nature that God has made. He pays attention to all that we need, and has created it for us in love.
