**What you need:**

Week 2 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, glue, pieces of felt.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/2a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/craft-template.png)

```

**Instructions:**

1\. Color in the craft template and cut out the pieces.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/2b.png)

2\. Fold along the line above the hands and glue them onto the boy. Then fold the boy in half and glue the lower half onto the bed.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/2c.png)

3\. Fold along the lines on the bed to make it stand. Cut out a “pillow” and a “blanket” from the pieces of felt. Glue the pillow and add the blanket to the bed.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/2d.png)

4\. Pull the hands to make the boy sit up, as though he has been healed.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/2e.png)
