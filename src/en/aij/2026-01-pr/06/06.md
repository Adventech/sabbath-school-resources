Even after what Cain had done, God did something wonderful for him. God listened quietly while Cain complained that He wasn’t being fair. Cain grumbled that he didn’t want to work harder in the field, or roam the earth, or be hidden from God’s face. But more than that, he really didn’t want someone to kill him. And he was very scared they would, for hadn’t he just killed his brother? He didn’t even realize God had sent him to roam the earth to help protect him!

Imagine complaining to God like that! God wants us to be honest when we talk to Him, but we also need to be respectful. How did Jesus teach His friends to start their prayer to God? Read Matthew 6:9.

Even after listening to all that grumbling, God replied with great kindness. He put a mark of protection on Cain so that he would not be attacked. If anyone did try to kill Cain, they would be punished.

How loving is our God! We call that special kind of love “mercy,” for God was kind and forgiving when Cain wasn’t sorry, or honest, or respectful. Cain surely didn’t deserve God’s mercy. But God gave it anyway.
