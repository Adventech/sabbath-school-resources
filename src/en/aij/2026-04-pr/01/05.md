While the storm continued to rage, Jesus lay sleeping. The scared disciples slid over to His side, holding tightly to the boat. Quickly, they woke Him up, crying, “Lord save us! We will drown!” (Matthew 8:25, ICB).

Jesus opened His eyes. In the lightning’s glare, the disciples saw His peaceful face and the tender love that shone from His eyes.

While Jesus rose, the disciples grasped their oars to make one last effort to keep their sinking boat afloat. The fury of the storm had driven all the other boats close together, and the frightened people in those boats tried to keep themselves afloat too.

Jesus stood calmly in the tossing boat while the waves broke and the lightning pierced the darkness. He lifted His hand and spoke fearlessly to the angry storm: “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39).

Suddenly the wind stopped howling, and the waves sank. The clouds rolled away, and the stars shone. The boat floated gently on the quiet sea while the disciples and all the people in the other boats stared at Jesus in amazement. What did they say? **Read Matthew 8:27.**

They had truly seen God’s power. What a story they had to tell!

God could have saved the disciples in the storm, but He chose to do the impossible and stop the storm to calm their fears. Jesus can calm our fears and give us peace when we are scared. We can trust Him to help us be peaceful no matter what, for the Bible says, “And let the peace of God rule in your hearts” (Colossians 3:15).
