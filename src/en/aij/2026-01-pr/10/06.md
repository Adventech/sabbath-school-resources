Headed south toward Canaan, the long caravan made the dangerous journey through the desert. Those who had learned of Abram’s God in Haran came too. They experienced God’s leading and protection as they traveled with Abram.

When the caravan finally entered Canaan, the Promised Land, Abram stopped in a wide grassy valley under a grove of spreading trees. As the weary travelers rested, Abram heard God’s familiar voice say, “To your descendants I will give this land” (Genesis 12:7). In awe Abram looked at the fields of grain, rows of grapevines, and olive groves that surrounded them. God was so good! How did Abram respond to God’s goodness? **Read Genesis 12:7** (second half).

Imagine the Canaanite people watching this scene. They remembered little of God and worshipped idols made of wood or stone. For these people the most important things in life were things that would give them pleasure, and they would give up anything to get what they wanted. Seeing all of Abram’s household bowed in reverent worship was a beautiful witness to the true God. The Canaanites were distant cousins, descendants of Noah’s son Ham. God’s promise to bless all the families of the earth was for them too, if they would choose to believe (Genesis 12:3). Abram hoped they would.

Gazing up at the stars in Canaan, Abram quietly thought about the descendants God had promised him. Each star shone brightly, reminding him that all those who would be God’s friend, right down through time, would shine as his descendants. He was a star. His wife, Sarai, was a star. His household members were stars. You are a star. How amazing to be a star descendant!
