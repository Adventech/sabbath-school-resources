**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. Ask for a few answers to the questions about the mural and affirm answers. Explain that this quarter we will discover what happened at the very beginning of our earth, which is found at the beginning of the Bible. We’ll learn about an amazing, loving Creator God.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“God Is Our Creator.”** Introduce the song **“Be Still, Be Silent”** and explain that you will sing it each week before you pray. Listen to it and follow along with the words (printed or displayed digitally).

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-is-our-creator.mp3] “God Is Our Creator”

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/be-still-be-silent.mp3] “Be Still, Be Silent”

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Ask God to be close to you and the children as you learn more about Him in Sabbath School this quarter.

**^[BIBLE FOCUS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Introduce and listen to **“The Books of the Bible”** song. Display a list of the books of the Bible as the children listen. This song will be sung each week this quarter. Explain that learning the books of the Bible in order helps us find Bible verses quickly when we use our Bibles. Share a **Bible Fast Fact**. The Bible has two parts: the Old Testament and the New Testament. “Testament” means agreement, or covenant. The Old Testament was written before Jesus came to earth, and the New Testament was written after Jesus went back to heaven. They show the agreement God has made with us, His people.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/books-of-the-bible.mp3] “The Books of the Bible”

**^[GIVE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Have a child collect the offering as you all sing **“Thank You, Jesus.”** Later, after the money has been counted by an adult, invite a child to color the next section of your **Offering Chart** to show how much has been collected this quarter. Then share this week’s **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission project. Say a short prayer for God to bless and take the offering to where it’s most needed.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/thank-you-jesus.mp3] “Thank You Jesus”

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Warmly welcome any visitor and sing the **“Visitor Song.”** Sing **“Happy Birthday”** to a child who has had a birthday this week, and pray for God to bless them.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a **piece of fruit** and ask them to describe its **attributes** (qualities or features: color, shape, size, etc.). Prompt if necessary.

**Cut the fruit** in half and describe more of its attributes (parts, texture, etc.). Cut it into small pieces. Have the children taste it and further describe its attributes (sweet, juicy, etc.).

**Teaching Point:** Like the piece of fruit, God has many different attributes that tell us what He is like.

**^[BRAINSTORM:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a quick **one-minute brainstorm** about God’s attributes.

- **What is God like? Let’s list as many of His attributes as we can in a minute!** (Loving, kind, wise, holy, faithful, etc.)

Set a timer and quickly write the attributes on the whiteboard/poster paper as children share. Count them at the end.

- **Are any of these attributes new to you, or different from what you’ve been told God is like?**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How do we know what God is like?** (From others who have taught us, Bible stories we’ve heard, reading the Bible, etc.)

**Teaching Point:** Highlight that the **Bible** is the main way people can know about God’s attributes and how truly wonderful He is. It is like a letter from God in which He tells us about Himself. Let’s bring and use our Bibles in Sabbath School every week so we can learn more about our loving God!

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **On our list of God’s attributes, which one stands out the most for you?** (Prompt to identify love.)

**Read 1 John 4:8, 16.**

**Teaching Point:** God doesn’t just love us—**God IS love!** Many of His other attributes tell us about what His amazing love is like. (Hold up your **Bible**.) When we read our Bibles from Genesis, the first book of the Bible, to Revelation, the last book, God’s love for sinful people is very clear. Let’s first learn about how God’s creative power shows that He is love.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard/poster paper: **Our loving God made earth to be our wonderful home.**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible story.

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[ACTIVITY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What would our world be like without color? What would be different in our lives if we lived in black and white?**

Light can be broken into seven different colors. When it passes through raindrops in the air, it makes a rainbow. Spin your **color wheel** to show how light is made of seven colors we can’t normally see.

- **Which of God’s attributes do light and a rainbow remind you of?** (Faithful, promise keeper, creative, powerful.)

Everything God created tells us of Him in some way.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 1: Creator God** to place in their container (or on their key ring). Explain that they will receive one card each week they come to Sabbath School. Discuss the picture and have the children recite the memory verse. Then sing the **Scripture Song: “Romans 1:20.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/1-romans-1-20.mp3] “Romans 1:20”

- **What does the memory verse tell you about God? What helps us to understand what He is like?**

**Teaching Point:** The memory verse tells us that there are things about God that are invisible to people, but the things He’s created help us to understand what He is like.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to share either of the following:

- One of God’s attributes they’d especially like to thank Him for.
- What they’d like to thank God for creating during the first five days of Creation.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read this week’s mission story, **“Our Living God,”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your **color wheel spinner**. Highlight the features of both sides and explain how to make it.

Quietly play **“When God Speaks”** while children complete their craft. Draw attention to the parts that relate to the creation story.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/when-god-speaks.mp3] “When God Speaks”

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring children back together and sing the closing song **“God Bless You.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-bless-you.mp3] “God Bless You”
