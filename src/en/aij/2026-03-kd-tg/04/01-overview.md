**Word of the Week**

**Protect:** Protect means to keep someone or something safe from danger. God wants to keep you safe! He has given His angels the very special job of protecting you every day.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

An angel warned the Wise Men in a dream not to tell Herod where they had found Jesus, so they went home a different way. An angel also told Joseph to leave Bethlehem and take Mary and Jesus to Egypt to be safe. After a few years the angel told Joseph that Herod was dead and that they could safely go back to their home in Nazareth. God had sent His angels to
protect them and lead them.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants to protect and guide us.
- **Feel** safe in God’s loving protection.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His protection and guidance.
