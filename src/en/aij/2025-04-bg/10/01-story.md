The Journey\
^[to Bethlehem]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2D2821"}}})
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/10/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#514946ad"}}}
Mary carefully rolled up their blankets. Joseph tied them onto the back of little donkey. It was time to go. Mary and Joseph were going on a trip to Bethlehem. Mary climbed onto little donkey’s back. She was happy that little donkey could carry her. Baby Jesus was getting big in her tummy. It was almost time for Him to be born.\
_Clip-clop, clip-clop_ went little donkey, down the road. The road was bumpy _(give your child a bumpy knee ride)_. Mary was tired. Joseph stopped in the shade to give Mary a drink. Little donkey was thirsty too. After a long time of walking, they s-l-o-w-l-y walked down the last hill. They could see the tiny lights of Bethlehem in the distance. Little donkey walked faster now. _Clip-clop, clip-clop, clip-clop._ Mary smiled at Joseph. Soon they would be able to rest.
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/10/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#977243ad"}}}
Joseph led little donkey and Mary right to the door of an inn—a place to stay. _Knock, knock, knock._ Joseph waited at the door. There were so many people in Bethlehem. Joseph knocked again— louder this time. _Knock, knock, knock._ The innkeeper answered the door. His inn was already full of visitors. He sadly shook his head _(shake head)_. “There was no room for them in the inn” (Luke 2:7).\
Joseph looked over at Mary. She was so tired. She really needed a bed! The innkeeper had an idea. He did have one little room . . . It wasn’t in a house. It didn’t even have a bed. But it would keep them safe and warm. He led Joseph to a stable where the animals lived. There was a gentle old cow who mooed softly _(moo)_, a few lambs _(baa)_, and some chickens _(cluck, cluck)_. There was a spot for little donkey, and lots of soft hay.
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/10/03.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#676263ad"}}}
Joseph led Mary and little donkey into the stable. He made a bed for her in the clean hay. Mary was so tired from the long trip she fell fast asleep! _(Pretend to sleep.)_ They didn’t have a fancy room or a big bed. But they had God. He had looked after them on the bumpy road to Bethlehem. He was taking care of them in the stable. Very soon, God’s very own Son would be born!\
_(Say together)_ Thank you, God, for looking after Mary, Joseph, and Baby Jesus. Thank you for caring for me too!
