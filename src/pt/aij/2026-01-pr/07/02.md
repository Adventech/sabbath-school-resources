Sin had spLeia like a bad sickness across the new earth, mas there were still some quem loved Deus. A especial man named Enoch was Seth’s grande-grande-grande-grandson. Enoch loved Deus então much e was kind e humble. Ele chose Deus’s way as seu way. The Bible Digas that “Enoch walked com Deus” (Gênesis 5:22). Deus helped Enoch a be kind e gentle, e deu ele a strong, wise, e growing mind.

Enoch começou preaching sobre Deus’s amor a anyone quem would listen. Ele also pleaded com eles a follow Deus e stop Façaing wrong. Sometimes ele would go away onde isso was quiet então that ele could Ore e be com Deus. This especial time com Deus deu ele strength, energy, e joy quando ele came back a preach again.

Depois Enoch had been a friend de Deus’s para 365 years, o que happened? **Leia Gênesis 5:24.** Yes, Deus took Enoch a Céu! Just imagine como Enoch felt quando ele saw seu Best Friend, Deus, face-a-face!

The people living em this time were very tall e strong like Enoch, mas most de eles were not bom e kind like ele. Eles were violent e selfish. Eles took o que Deus had given e used isso a Faça themselves look bom por taking the best things para themselves—gems e gold a become rich, e wood a build better houses. Eles made lindo gardens para seu ils e did whatever eles wanted a Faça sem thinking de como isso hurt Deus, themselves, ou other people.

O que Façaes the Bible Diga Deus saw quando Ele looked em the people?

**Leia Gênesis 6:5.** Deus decided that se Ele didn’t Faça something, there would be no one left através whom the Savior could be born.
