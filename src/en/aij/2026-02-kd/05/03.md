As Jacob sat staring at the crackling fire, he thought hard. How could he meet Esau in a _peaceful_ way? He had an idea.

He called over one of his servants and asked them to send a peaceful message. What was it? **Read Genesis 32:4, 5.**

Jacob had used his words carefully. He had said, “My Master, Esau,” and told him the message was from “Jacob, your servant.” He didn’t want Esau to think he wanted to be a boss over him. God helped Jacob to be wise, polite, and peaceful.

The messengers came racing back, puffing and panting. They told him, “He is coming to meet you. And he has 400 men with him” (Genesis 32:6, ICB).

Poor Jacob began to tremble with fear. Esau was coming with an army of men, ready to fight him! He looked at his wives and children. They weren’t soldiers! They couldn’t possibly fight against an army.

Then Jacob stopped. How could he forget so quickly? He did have an army with him. It was an invisible angel army! He knelt down and asked God for help.

“Lord, you told me to return to my country and my family. You said that you would do good to me” Jacob prayed. “Please save me from my brother Esau. I am afraid” (Genesis 32:9,11, ICB).

God says in John 14:27, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you” (John 14:27). Whenever you are afraid, you can trust God to give you His peace.
