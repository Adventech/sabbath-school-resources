**Focus:** A fawn trusts its mother in all things, at all times.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/02/nature-lesson.png)

In the quiet of the forest a newborn fawn takes his first shaky steps. His legs tremble beneath him, and the world feels big and unfamiliar. But beside him stands his mother—watchful, steady, and strong.

The mother deer knows the dangers of the wild: lurking predators, sudden storms, and the unknown. But the fawn does not fear, because he trusts his mother completely. When she moves, he follows. When she stops, he stands still. When danger approaches, he hides close to her side, knowing that she will always protect him.

Even when the mother leaps through thick brush or across rushing streams, the fawn does not hesitate—he obeys right away and follows, not because he understands the path, but because he trusts the one leading it.

Just as the nobleman was convinced about Jesus’ words and actions, the young fawn is convinced he can trust his mother’s voice and actions. He listens and follows—even when it doesn’t make sense; even when he can’t see how it will work.

Discuss:

- **What words would you use to describe the fawn?**
- **What words would you use to describe the nobleman?**
- **As you think about your friendship with Jesus, what words would you use to describe you?**
