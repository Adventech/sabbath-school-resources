Dishes needed washing, beds needed making, and the floor in Father Joseph’s carpenter shop needed sweeping. Jesus saw what needed doing, and He did it. Mary and Joseph smiled as they watched their Son mature into a willing and happy worker.

These beautiful character qualities made Jesus a joy to be around at home and in the streets of Nazareth. Always thinking of others, Jesus was likely quick to help someone, share, and spread His love for God through words and songs. “And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52). This little Boy was such a big blessing.

Those happy childhood years sped by, and Jesus was soon in His twelfth year. This brought great excitement! Why? **Read Luke 2:41, 42.**

Jesus had looked forward to this day for years. His eyes danced as He began the journey with all the other noisy Hebrew travelers. They clogged the dusty roads as they sang and chatted their way to the Passover celebrations in Jerusalem.

After several days of travel Jesus saw the temple. It sat high on the city skyline, the white stone walls gleaming in the sunshine. This was the building He had heard so much about! When He arrived at the temple gates, He no doubt reverently followed the instructions of Psalm 100:4: “Enter into His gates with thanksgiving, and into His courts with praise. Be thankful to Him, and bless His name.”

Jesus was eager to learn all He could on this trip, and the temple was a good place to start.

Our loving God lived in Jesus’ young heart. Whether He was sweeping floors, helping the neighbors, on a long journey, or entering the temple courts, Jesus was cheerful and enjoyed praising God. When we focus on inviting Jesus into our hearts and getting to know Him better, we will want to become more like young Jesus. He can help us to be more willing, loving, faithful, and cheerful.
