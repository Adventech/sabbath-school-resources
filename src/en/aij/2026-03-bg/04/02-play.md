Create

Make an ocean together by setting blue jelly (gelatin) in the fridge. Use a spoon to create a path for the Israelites. Find or make little people to walk through the sea. Add plastic animals if you have them. If you don’t have blue jelly, you could use sand or dirt.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#51B8D5"}}}
Learn

Find an ocean book at home, borrow one, or look for a video online. Cuddle up with your child to look at the pictures. Talk about some of the fish and sea creatures that the Israelites may have seen as they walked through the Red Sea.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#51B8D5"}}}
Explore

Find a body of water that’s nearby—a creek, lake, swimming pool, or tub filled with water. If practical, let your child put their toes in to feel the water. Share how the water would have split in two and imagine what it would have been like to walk through the water on dry land.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#51B8D5"}}}
Connect

Play follow the leader around your home. Take turns being the leader. Talk about how the Israelites followed their Leader (God) in the desert by following God’s cloud in the daytime and God’s fire at night.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#51B8D5"}}}
Prayer

Thank God for leading the Israelites through the Red Sea. Talk about some of the ways God can lead us today. Ask Him to lead you in whatever you do that day.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “You shall walk after the Lord your God” (Deuteronomy 13:4).
