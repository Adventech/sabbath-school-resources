**Word of the Week**

**Calm:** Being calm is being still, quiet, or peaceful. When you are calm, you feel safe and relaxed.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After a busy day of teaching, Jesus and the disciples set out for the other side of the lake. Jesus was tired and fell asleep. A severe storm hit the Sea of Galilee, and the disciples feared they would lose their lives. In desperation, they cried out to Jesus. Jesus stood up, stretched out His hand, and said, “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39). Immediately the storm stopped. The disciples were amazed that even nature obeys Jesus.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus has power over nature.
- **Feel** a desire to trust Jesus when they are afraid.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to help them when they are afraid.
