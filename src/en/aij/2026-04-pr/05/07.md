**^[Option One:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})**

Read and discuss this quote about compassion, then make it into a poster. Search in your Bible for as many Bible verses as you can find about giving to others and write them on your poster.

“No one has ever become poor by giving”—Anne Frank.

**^[Option One Suggestions:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}})**

- _Take a photo and make a digital poster._
- _Draw and color an arched rainbow on half a paper plate, and glue cotton balls in the middle as clouds. Then write the quote across the bottom, attach some string at the top, and hang it as a mobile._
- _Draw a person on top of a tall rock, looking down. On a separate piece of card, draw another person and cut him/her out. Attach this person to a short piece of string. Make a small hole through the hand of the person on the rock and thread the string through. Then pull the string from the back and pretend you are helping the person up._

**^[Option Two:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})**

{"style":{"block":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"},"rounded":false},"wrapper":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"}},"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr/05/image10.png)

Invite each family member to create “before” and “after” pictures for one of Jesus’ healing miracles that you have learned about so far. Choose from the following: Peter’s mother-in-law (last quarter), the man with leprosy, the deaf and speech-impaired man, the man born blind from birth, the man with paralysis and his four faith-filled friends, or the nobleman’s son.

**^[Option Two Suggestions:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}})**

- _Draw or paint your pictures._
- _Model the story character from playdough or modeling clay._
- _Create your pictures on either side of a piece of card. Flip it as you tell the story._
