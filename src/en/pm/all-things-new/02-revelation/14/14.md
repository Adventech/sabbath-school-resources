[[Revelation 14:1-5]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The followers of Jesus are shown, at some time in the future, having overcome, by His power, the evil in their lives. There is no sin standing between them and God. They are indeed people of justice, goodness, and love.
- It’s as though Jesus is saying that though the unholy trinity will do horrific deeds, evil will not triumph! Jesus will instead save His people. His victory will be complete.
- The final victory with God’s people in heaven, which is how the story ends, is shown here. Verses 6-12 explains how that happens.

[[Revelation 14:6-7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- We have now arrived at the heart of the reason that the book of Revelation was written.
- In verses 6-12 we find what are often referred to as the three angels’ messages. They are the core of God’s final message to our dying planet.
- Verse 6 makes clear they are for “every nation, tribe, tongue, and people.” No one is to be excluded from hearing them.
- The first angel is described as having “the everlasting gospel.” The word “gospel” literally means “good news,” and among first century A.D. followers of Christ it came to refer specifically to the good news about Jesus and the salvation He offers. It is “everlasting” because God’s plan of salvation, like God Himself, is unchanging.
- Then comes the first angel’s first words: “Fear God and give glory to Him, for the hour of His judgment has come.” How can any message that starts with the word “fear” and speaks of judgment also be good news?
- Just in this way: the warning that the fi nal judgment has begun is bad news for the oppressor, but exceedingly good news for the oppressed.
- So much of the world today is oppressed! Whether it is by other people or simply by Satan and sin, billions of people all over the earth are daily living lives with oppression—and the first angel proclaims that those days are now nearly over.
- “The hour of His judgment has come” is reference to the final judgment prophecies we studied in Daniel 7 and 8. It also points to the end of the 2,300-year prophecy of Daniel 8:14, which shows that the final judgment of all humanity began in the heavenly sanctuary in the year 1844.
- To learn more about this, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r14a/)**.
- Since 1844, Jesus, our high priest in the heavenly sanctuary, is working to save as many people as possible in at least two ways. First, His work in the sanctuary helps set us free from sin, both through forgiving our sins when we confess and forsake them, and in giving us power to no longer be slaves to sinful habits. And second, through the power of the Holy Spirit, Jesus, our high priest, is daily trying to influence oppressors to stop their oppressive ways. Jesus loves them and wants to save them, if possible. But if they will not stop oppressing others, He will have no choice but to bring their evil ways to an end—bad news for the oppressor, but very good news for the oppressed.
- If you are an oppressor of others today, please stop! Jesus will forgive and transform your life into one of generosity rather than selfishness. But if you choose to continue in your oppression of others, know that the hour of God’s final judgment has come. Justice is coming, and soon.
- This word “fear” in the first angel’s message can also mean “honor” and “respect.” In this sense, to “fear” God means to honor Him, to respect Him and His ways and His laws. It means to put Jesus first in your life, living for Him.
- Honoring and respecting Jesus gives Him full permission to care for you. For all who accept Jesus as the leader of their lives, they receive eternal life, strength to endure, and power to overcome all obstacles.
- The end of the first angel’s message is also profoundly important: “Worship Him who made heaven and earth, the sea and springs of water.”
- This is a clear reference to the seventh-day Sabbath commandment, the fourth commandment of God’s ten-commandment law, found in the Old Testament in Exodus 20:8-11.
- To find out crucial information about the seventh-day Sabbath commandment, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r14b/)**.
- The implications of the first angel mentioning the seventh-day Sabbath command are profound:
	- It means that at the end of time, the days we live in right now, God is specifically calling humanity to worship Him as the one Creator God.
	- To obey the weekly seventh-day Sabbath command is a key part of this worship.
- In the end-times, when Satan will try to force the world to worship him through the work of the unholy trinity, the first angel loudly proclaims that the Creator God is the only being to be worshipped.
- This is why to understand that God is our Creator is the most important thing we can know about Him. Nothing is greater than this. To understand and embrace this fact will affect every portion of our lives.
- This is also why the first angel is so insistent that we keep the seventh-day Sabbath holy! It is a weekly reminder of who God really is that keeps us focused on Him and His truth for these last days.
- With all of this in mind, a good way to sum up the first angel’s message is this: _Honor and worship the Creator, for justice is coming, and time is running out._
	- The final heavenly judgment has already begun! Justice will soon triumph over evil!
	- Let us honor and worship our Creator God, for He and He alone is worthy of our allegiance.
	- And as we worship Him, He will also give us the power to become people of justice, as He, as our high priest, is working for justice now.

[[Revelation 14:8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- As we saw in our study of Daniel, the Babylonian Empire was a formidable enemy of God’s people.
- But at the time Revelation was written, the historical city of Babylon was in ruins, having been destroyed centuries earlier. Clearly, then, the second angel is not referring to _literal_ Babylon, but rather to a _spiritual_ Babylon.
- Spiritual Babylon takes the dominant characteristics of the ancient historical city of Babylon and uses them as a foundation for a counterfeit system of spiritual beliefs in the time of the end. The unholy trinity, working through spiritual Babylon, will then try to achieve their evil global agenda.
- The Old and New Testaments make clear that there were at least two prominent characteristics that defined ancient Babylon: (1) _the false teaching that we must earn our own salvation (such as through obedi ence to God’s law or through the good things we do)_; and (2) _the false teaching that God’s law has been changed or is no longer important_.
	- For more background on this, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r14c/)**.
- “Wine” in the second angel’s message refers to the false teachings of spiritual Babylon.
- “Fornication” is also translated correctly as “adulteries.” In the second angel’s message this is not referring to literal, physical adultery, but rather to spiritual adultery.
- Spiritual adultery occurs when God’s people turn away from Him, their true husband, to someone or something else, in violation of His laws. For more on this, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r14d/)**.
- So bringing the various symbols of the second angel together, we find the following:
- _“Babylon is fallen, is fallen . . .”_ Satan is a master of making that which is evil look good! But God here makes it clear that end-time spiritual Babylon, no matter how impressive she may look outwardly, is profoundly morally bankrupt, so much so that the angel says twice: she “is fallen, is fallen.”
- _“. . . that great city . . .”_ The use of the word “city” here implies that many people are “living” in spiritual Babylon by agreeing with her false teachings, and that she is the sinful, global capital of organized evil.
- _“. . . because she has made all nations drink . . .”_ Babylon at the end of time attempts to force the nations of the world to obey its false teachings, particularly that we must earn our own salvation and that God’s law has been changed or is no longer important.
- _“. . . of the wine of the wrath of her fornication.”_ Babylon wants the world to come to her to supposedly earn their salvation, and Babylon wants the world to either ignore God’s law or to keep a version of it that she has altered. All who “drink” this “wine” will commit spiritual fornication/ adultery against God and be lost.
- With these things in mind, a good way to summarize the second angel’s message is this: _Trying to earn my salvation, or changing or setting aside God’s law, won’t work._
- In warning the world of Babylon’s false teachings, God through the second angel is calling the world back to the true teachings of Jesus.
- There is no goodness in Babylon’s false “gospel” of earning our salvation and of bypassing God’s true law. Instead, it is only through Jesus’ salvation and power that anyone can become a truly good person—that is, someone like Jesus.
- The first angel called us to Jesus, in part that we might become people of justice as He is a God of justice. The second angel calls us to Jesus, in part that we might become genuinely _good_ people in Him—not merely people that _look_ good on the outside, but that truly are good people on the inside; people who truly do obey God, and who love other people as Jesus loves them.
- Justice and goodness are two of the greatest gifts we receive as we learn to know Jesus! And through the first and second angels’ messages, Jesus is boldly offering these gifts to the world—including to you, right now.
- Will you accept Babylon’s false “gospel”? Or will you choose the real, everlasting gospel by accepting Jesus and His transforming gift of salvation?

[[Revelation 14:9-11]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- What is this “mark of the beast” that God is warning the world to reject? It may indeed involve a visible mark of some sort. But since Revelation is largely a symbolic book, the mark of the beast is not simply a physical sign of some sort being placed on our skin.
- Instead, the mark of the beast will be given to those who show loyalty to the beast by either embracing its beliefs and values or at least going along with them. This will be very visible.
- Specifically, their loyalty to the beast will most likely be shown by keeping as holy a counterfeit day of worship—Sunday—instead of the seventh-day Sabbath instituted by God at the creation of the world. Those who thus worship on Sunday at the very end of time will receive the mark of the beast.
- Conversely, those who follow Jesus will show their love and allegiance to Him by loving others and obeying His laws, including the command to worship on God’s true day of worship, the seventh-day Sabbath. They will receive the seal of God.
- To be clear, _no one has the mark of the beast today_, including those who currently worship on Sunday. But nonetheless, at the very end of time, Sunday will be claimed by the beast power, the papacy, as a mark of its authority, and the observance of that day will eventually cause one to receive the mark of the beast.
- To see how this is so, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r14e/)**.
- Our power of choice is sacred to God. He will not force us to follow Him, even at the end of time.
- But God also loves us! And because He loves us, in the third angel’s message He tells us the truth about what is coming at the end of time _before_ it happens, and in the strongest language possible. He risks using such language in the hope of getting our attention, of breaking the grip Satan and sin have on our hearts, and of turning us back to Him before it is too late.
- To review:
	- The essence of the first angel’s message was: _“Honor and worship the Creator, for justice is coming, and time is running out.”_
	- The essence of the second angel’s message was: _“Trying to earn my salvation, or changing or setting aside God’s law, won’t work.”_
- And a good way to summarize the third angel’s message is this: _“Destruction is coming to all who follow Satan. I can choose that destruction if I wish. But God in His love is doing all He possibly can to change my mind.”_
- The third angel’s message is the strongest verbal warning God has ever given in human history.
- But _God’s warning is so strong because His love for humanity is so great._
- In the face of this greatest threat the earth has ever faced, God refuses to sit idly by, waiting for enemies to harm those He loves. He instead shouts in a loud voice so clear that even the most evilhearted person might hear, turn from selfishness, confess their sins, and accept Jesus as their Lord and Savior before it is too late.
- Jesus died on the cross as a substitute for you dying for your own sins. He lives and reigns today in the heavenly sanctuary as your high priest and Savior, ready to offer forgiveness and power over sin to all who will accept Him as the leader of their lives. He does not want Satan to gain the victory over you! And in His great love, through the third angel’s message He tells us the truth about where following Satan inevitably leads.
- These messages of the three angels are indeed God’s last offer of salvation to the world. There will not be another. The final judgment of all humanity began in 1844 and will soon reach its conclusion. There will be no second chance after that judgment ends.
- The seal of God and the mark of the beast are the only two options to choose from at the end of time. And ultimately the question will come to every living person on the planet: Will you worship Jesus in His way, on His day? Or will you worship Satan in his way, on his day?
- God has sent these three angels to you because of His justice, goodness, and love. His deep hope is that you will accept His offer of mercy and allow Him to transform you into a person of justice, goodness, and love, as well.

[[Revelation 14:12-16]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The “harvest” here refers to Jesus’ coming back the second time to take His followers home to heaven. It follows the three angels’ messages going to the world, which “ripen” this harvest. Their proclamation will give ample opportunity for all final decisions for or against following Jesus to be made in a fair and informed way.

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Verses 17-20 refer to the “harvest” of the wicked.
- The references to “grapes,” wine, and “wrath” remind us of similar language used in the messages of the second and third angels. Their warnings here come true.
