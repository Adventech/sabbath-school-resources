---
title: Facing Fear
---

1. Secure in His love, you are delivered from fear (1 Jn. 4:18,19; Ps. 56:3; Provo 3:23-26).
2. It is Satan who inspires fear and God who delivers you from it (2 Tim. 1:7;Ps. 27:1).
3. As you cast your burden of fear upon the Lord, He will sustain you and give you rest (Ps. 55:22; Matt. 11:26-28).
4. His presence banishes fear (Isa. 41:10; Ps. 61:2).
5. Since your trust is in God, you need not fear what man can do to you or say about you (Ps.56:11).
6. Since He is in control of all circumstances affecting you, you can face them confidently in Him (Ps. 46:1,2; 91:1-5).
