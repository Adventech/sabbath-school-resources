Excited About Jesus

Excitement about Jesus swept through the land like a rushing wind. Stories about Him left many eager for more.

The Bible tells us, “The news about Jesus spread all over Syria, and people brought all the sick to him. . . . Jesus healed all of them. Many people followed him. They came from Galilee, the Ten Towns [Decapolis], Jerusalem, Judea, and the land across the Jordan River” (Matthew 4:24, 25, ICB).

Outside the city of Jericho, beside the dusty main road, sat a man with his hand outstretched, begging for money. His name was Bartimaeus and he was blind. For a blind man in Jesus’ day, begging was his only way to earn money.

Bartimaeus couldn’t see the coins the travelers threw his way, but he could hear them clink when they landed in front of him. He heard the clip-clopping donkeys, rumbling carts, and the people’s excited chatter.

That’s how he heard about Jesus. Everyone on the road seemed to have a story about the wonderful Teacher and His beautiful words about God. Bartimaeus heard of His miracles too! He heard how Jesus had healed people with leprosy and diseases, and given sight to blind people!

Wow, that snippet of news got Bartimaeus excited. If Jesus had healed people in other towns, maybe He would come to Jericho one day. And maybe, Bartimaeus thought excitedly, he would be healed too.

The more he thought about it, the more he wondered if Jesus could do the impossible—for him!

Jesus said, “All things are possible to him who believes” (Mark 9:23). Bartimaeus was beginning to learn the exciting truth of these words.

Jesus loves to see us get excited about what He can do for us too.
