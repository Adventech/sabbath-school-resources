**Word of the Week**

**Peace:** The feeling of quiet, calm, safety, and contentment in our hearts. God’s love, protection, and care can give us peace no matter what trouble we face. Peace is also a time without fighting.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

As Jacob traveled back to Canaan, he found peace with God and the brother he had wronged. Jacob tried to make peace on his own, but his message to his brother was met with a show of force. Jacob spent the night alone praying to God, then wrestled with a Stranger who he eventually realized was Jesus. Jacob refused to let go until God blessed and forgave him, which He did. Feeling at peace with God, Jacob sent presents to Esau as he approached, then bowed humbly before him. With God’s help, Esau forgave Jacob, and the brothers made peace.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that peace comes from God.
- **Feel** desire to make peace with those they have wronged.
- **Respond** by asking God for His peace.
