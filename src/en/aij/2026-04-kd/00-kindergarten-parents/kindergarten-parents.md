What is your personal friendship with Jesus like, right now? Perhaps you’re weary, burdened by the pressures of work, family commitments, health challenges, or other issues. Jesus invites you to spend time with Him, saying, “Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls” (Matthew 11:28-29).

It’s an incredible exchange—to swap your burdens for His peace; your stress for His gentleness.

I encourage you to come to Him; to spend time with Him; to know Him more over these next few weeks as you read these stories to your child. These messages are for you, too.

We’ve been praying for you and your child as we’ve created these lessons, and we continue to pray for you as you read them together.

May grace and peace be yours always,

^[Nina]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#447e98", "typeface": "Angello", "size": "xl"}}})
