The camel knelt down on the dirt as Jacob packed the last of their treasures. Done! With a snort and a wobble, the camel stood up. It was time to go. The family was on the move again!

God asked Jacob to go to Bethel—the place where God had given him his loving ladder dream. God wanted to remind Jacob of the special thing that had happened there.

Jacob set off with Rachel, Leah, and his sons, Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, Zebulun, and Joseph and his daughter, Dinah. All their families, servants, and animals came too.

In Bethel they built an altar and gave an offering to God. Then God spoke to Jacob again. What did He say? **Read Genesis 35:11.**

What a beautiful promise! God loved him. He would always care for him.

Later, as they left Bethel, Rachel gave birth to a baby boy. A new little brother had joined their family! He was named Benjamin. Baby Benjamin was the sweetest little thing. Now Jacob had 12 sons!

Sadly, after Benjamin was born, Rachel died. Jacob’s heart hurt. He would miss her a lot. But Jacob knew God cared and would comfort him. In the Bible, God tells us, “Even when you are old, I will take care of you. Even when your hair has turned gray, I will take care of you. I made you and will take care of you” (Isaiah 46:4, ICB).

^[Parent Faith Talk]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Tell a story about a time you were puzzled about something, but after a while, it all made sense and God showed you why it happened.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- When you are sad, what helps to make you feel happy again?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Pray for someone you know who is feeling sad.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw a picture of the things you are sad or worried about. Tape it onto a ball and take it outside. Say a prayer, giving those things to Jesus. Then “cast” or throw that ball as far and hard as you can. Have an adult pull off and destroy the “paper worries” and then throw the ball back empty.
