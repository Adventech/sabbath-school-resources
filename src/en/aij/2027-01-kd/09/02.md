What was the very first commandment God gave? **Read Exodus 20:3.**

This true story might help explain what that means.

The little duckling pushed his way out of his shell and gave a loud quack. “Welcome to the world, little one,” said Farmer Barrie Hayman. He named the duckling Star, and moved him into a box with his brothers and sisters. Instantly the ducklings began flapping, pecking, and cheeping loudly. Farmer Barrie watched as Star’s brothers and sisters began attacking little Star.

This won’t do, thought kind Farmer Barrie. With his strong, caring hands he picked Star up and slid him into his warm shirt pocket.

From then on, their friendship grew. Whenever Farmer Barrie went out to the field, Star did too. When Farmer Barrie went home, Star did too. When Farmer Barrie brushed his teeth at night, Star stood and watched—because ducks don’t have teeth to brush!

Soon Farmer Barrie’s pocket was much too small for Star. He began following Farmer Barrie around everywhere. Star would even waddle after him in the shops. All of the townspeople knew, without a doubt, that Star loved Farmer Barrie. He was the most important and loved person in Star’s life.

God has made you, saved you, and loves you. He deserves to be first in your life. “You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength” (Deuteronomy 6:5). As you put Him first, the Holy Spirit will help you to be filled up with His goodness and love.
