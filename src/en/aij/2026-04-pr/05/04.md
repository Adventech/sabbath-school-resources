It was Sabbath morning when Jesus noticed him sitting beside the street. Jesus saw his eyes that wouldn’t see, and He noticed the deeper needs of his heart.

Jesus’ disciples saw the man who was blind too. They asked Jesus a question that we would find rude and wrong today. What did they ask Jesus? **Read John 9:2.**

Satan had led the Jews to wrongly believe that any kind of disease or disability was sent from God as a punishment for their sin, or the sin of their parents.

Jesus answered, “Neither this man nor his parents sinned” (John 9:3). Jesus felt sad that even His disciples had been fooled by this lie and didn’t understand God’s true character of love. He realized they suffered from a type of blindness themselves—spiritual blindness.

But Jesus could cure their spiritual blindness by teaching them the truth. He might have to teach them again and again, but He would show them the truth. For “He is gracious, and full of compassion” (Psalm 112:4) toward those who believe a lie.

We live in a sin-broken world where many of us live with disability, illness, and problems with our health. That wasn’t God’s plan. God designed us to be whole and healthy. One day, He will make everyone whole and completely new and well when He returns at the Second Coming. In the meantime, He wants us to be able to see Satan’s lies. Know that: God is love, and He will always forgive our sins when we ask Him to (1 John 1:9). God doesn’t hurt His children with sickness, “For I am the Lord who heals you” (Exodus 15:26).
