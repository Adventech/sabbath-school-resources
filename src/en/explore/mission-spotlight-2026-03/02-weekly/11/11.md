What began as a simple dormitory became a place of transformation. In Cambodia, the Dorm of Hope is helping students receive an education, discover Jesus, and build a brighter future.

Global Mission pioneers are ordinary people, church members who answer God's call to go where the gospel is needed most. They build relationships, plant churches, and share Jesus in practical ways, often in areas that have never heard the good news. Their work is challenging, but through faith and perseverance, they bring hope to forgotten communities.

One such pioneer is Vann. In 2016, Vann became a Global Mission pioneer in the northeastern part of Cambodia.

His journey wasn't easy. As a young student seeking a better education, Vann attended English classes at Banlung City Church. After being baptized, he finished his studies, got married, and moved back to his village. Cut off from the church by distance and lack of transportation, his spiritual life declined as he focused on farm work.

It wasn't until 2016, when the church needed help due to the illness of a pioneer leader, that Vann was called. At first, he felt unqualified and tried to avoid the call. But with encouragement from fellow believers, he returned to a struggling church, where only three families remained. Seeing the need, Vann accepted God's call to serve.

Vann's mission was to reach a local tribal group of youth who lived up in the mountains, far from the city, but desperate for education. For them, education is often the only way to a brighter future.

As a Global Mission pioneer, Vann helped establish a dormitory near the church, a safe place for students to live, study, and worship together. They called it the Dorm of Hope. The dorm allowed them to attend school regularly at no cost, join church activities, and grow in faith.

"I have been staying here for two years. What I love most about this dormitory is, first, I get to learn about Jesus. Second, it helps me with the cost, because I can stay in the dorm for free. Lastly, we have church members, elders, and pastors who support me—not only in my studies, but also in my relationship with God." —Bunny

The dorm became a vibrant center for education, discipleship, and community. Students participated in daily worship, Bible studies, English classes, and outreach programs. Many were baptized, and the church grew stronger.

"The program that I like the most here at Banlung City Church is evangelism. While there are many activities I enjoy, evangelism is very special to me because it's the time when we reach out to our neighbors and people in the community, so they can learn more about Jesus." —Phoeurng

The Dorm of Hope is more than just a building. It's a place where lives are transformed.

"The main reason I came to this church is because my family is poor. We don't have much money, and when I needed to attend high school in the town, I needed a place to stay. This church offered me a dormitory, a safe place to live while I study. But it's more than just lodging; the church also helps me with my studies and even supports some of my school expenses." —Samorn

Please pray for Global Mission pioneers around the world, and for the work being done here in Cambodia. Pray that these students will be able to complete their studies and return to their villages, sharing how great and wonderful God is.
