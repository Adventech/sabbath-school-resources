- **How does it make you feel when you look at this brightly colored flower?**

God created so many wonderful flowers! The gerbera is a beautiful, bright flower that comes in yellow, red, orange, purple, green, and pink. Just looking at their bright and cheerful colors makes you smile! Let’s take a closer look:

- The gerbera flower has layers and layers of delicate petals.
- Its stem is tall and strong, and even though it does not have any leaves, it can still hold the flower’s head high.
- Like their sunflower friends, gerberas also like to stretch their happy petal faces toward the sun’s gentle rays and light.
- The gerbera is so lovely that it attracts lots of God’s other special creatures, such as bees, hummingbirds, and butterflies that come and visit and taste its yummy, sweet pollen.

**Teaching Point:** Even though we might feel sad sometimes, God created beautiful things in nature like the gerbera daisy to bring us joy. When we see a pretty flower like this, we can smile knowing He cares for the flowers, and He has special love and care for us.
