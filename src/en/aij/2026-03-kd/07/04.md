Jesus blinked. He was no longer in the hot, sandy desert. No! He was standing at the top of a high building. And not just any building. Satan had taken Jesus to the highest point of God’s temple in Jerusalem. As Jesus looked down, He noticed the ground far, far below. If someone fell from this spot, they would really hurt themselves.

Then Satan spoke to Jesus. He said, “If you are the Son of God, jump off” (Matthew 4:6, ICB). Satan wanted Jesus to test God—to do something silly and see if God would send His angels to rescue Him.

What Bible verse did Jesus say to Satan? **Read Matthew 4:7.**

Jesus knew He didn’t need to jump off a building to prove God cared about Him. No, He would not do something dangerous just to see if God’s angels would save Him.

Once again, Bible words had shown Jesus what was right. God’s Holy Spirit and the Bible can give you wisdom and strength too. It says in Psalm 119:11, “Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You.”
