The dark storm cloud grew bigger . . . and bigger. _Whoosh, whoosh!_ A strong wind blew the cloud closer . . . and closer to the ark. It blocked out the bright sunshine. It covered the blue sky. Noah and his family waited inside the ark. They looked up, and they listened _(cup ear)_. _(Make rain taps and claps.) Drip-drop, drip-drop,_ the rain began to fall! _Tip-tap, tip-tap_ on the roof of the ark. Then faster and faster it fell. _Pitter, patter, pit! Pitter, patter, pit!_ God had sent the storm, just as He had promised! The very big door was shut tight. And Noah, his family, and all the animals were safe inside.\
_Boom! Boom!_ Thunder rolled across the dark sky. The ark shook from side to side _(move child side to side)_, but Noah and his family were safe inside. _Thrum, thrum, thrum,_ the rain began to drum _(drum on knees). Thrum, thrum, thrum._ Harder and harder it fell from the sky, but Noah and all the animals were safe and dry. God’s promise was true. He kept them safe while the rain fell, and the storm winds blew.

Day after day the rain poured down from the sky _(finger rain)_. Water sprung up from deep inside the ground _(hand fountain)_. The rivers grew to be lakes. Then the lakes became as big as oceans! The water rose higher and higher. Water covered the fields of green grass. It covered the top of the tall, leafy trees. It covered the top of the highest hill. The rain poured down, and the water rose higher and higher!\
The great big boat began to rock. Up and down _(rock with child on your lap)_ . . . up and down, went the ark over the big waves. God’s angels were with the ark. They kept it floating through the big storm. Noah and his family were safe inside. The tigers and panda bears were as dry as could be. The rain kept falling all day and all night to wash away the evil things.

It rained and rained! It rained for 10 days _(open, close 10 fingers for number of days)_ . . . 20 days . . . 30 days. But still it did not stop! More and more water covered the earth. The ark rocked and rocked over the big, big sea. The angels cared for Noah, his family, and the animals every day and every night. It rained for 40 days and 40 nights. Then, one day . . . listen _(pause with hand to ear)_. There was no _thrum, thrum, thrum_ on the roof. No _pitter, patter, pit._ No _tip-tap, tip-tap._ No _drip-drop, drip-drop._ Not a sound. The rain had stopped! The dark clouds had gone away. The sun shone brightly. The sky was blue again. God had kept His promise! The rain had stopped.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for sending angels to care for everyone on the ark! Thank You for sending angels to care for me too!
