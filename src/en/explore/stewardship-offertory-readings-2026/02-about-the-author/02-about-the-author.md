title: About the Author
subtitle: Julian Archer
---

Julian retired from a fast-paced business career in his late thirties and founded the ministry Faith vs. Finance. He is the author of HELP! I’ve Been Blessed! He talks about how to stop God’s blessings from becoming curses, and he loves to share the testimony God has given him as a way of strengthening the faith of others. Today, as Stewardship Director for the South Pacific Division, Julian is a passionate believer that stewardship education should be more about faith-raising than fundraising, and he has also discovered that grateful living leads to cheerful giving. He is married to a Proverbs 31 wife, Melinda, and they’re blessed with two adult sons. They live in a small country town on Australia’s east coast.
