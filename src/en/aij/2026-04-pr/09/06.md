God loves to give His great love to us! His love is deep and wide and bigger than we can imagine. God knows we are all helpless and sinful, like the sick man at the Pool of Bethesda. But He loves us all anyway. How can we be sure of this? **Read Romans 5:8.**

Such amazing love! It makes us want to obey Jesus, just like the man who picked up his bedroll and walked when Jesus told him to.

As obedient followers of Jesus, we are called to love others with the same love that Jesus loved. That can be hard sometimes. It is easy to love others when they love us and treat us well, but to love others unconditionally means to keep on loving them even when they are not loving toward us. We can’t do that by ourselves; we need God’s power to help us. The Bible says, “for it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure” (Philippians 2:13). This means that it’s God who gives us power to help us do what pleases Him.

When God’s power is working in our lives, this is what God’s love in our lives might look like:

- Forgiving someone when they have been unkind to you.
- Being patient with an irritating little brother or sister.
- Respecting someone, even though they may not dress well or keep themselves clean.
- Being kind to someone when you don’t feel like it.
- Letting someone join your game, even though they don’t play very well.
- Being patient and forgiving when someone has made a mistake.
- Being compassionate when you see someone hurting, even if it’s their own fault.
