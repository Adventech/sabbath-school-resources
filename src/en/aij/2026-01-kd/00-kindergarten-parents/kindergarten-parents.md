You’re also in my prayers as you read these wonderful stories to your child. I hope you’ll prioritize this special time each day by giving just 10 minutes to sit with your child as you share the Bible story. Although our lives are busy, you won’t regret having this time together. Make it a daily habit and you’ll notice that God will shape your child’s character and their love for Him. It’s what naturally happens when we spend time with Him in His living Word.

On that note, have your Bible close by each day as you read these stories. You’ll see a daily question and prompt where you’ll look in your Bible for the answer. Show your child how to use the Bible (the book, chapter, verse), and remind them that these stories are true because they come from the Bible.

So in the rush of life, when the urgent pulls you away from the important, may your daily prayer be “Strengthen me according to Your word” (Psalm 119:28), as you ask God to help you prioritize the growing faith of your child.

I know He’ll give you strength,

^[Nina]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#9C84A0", "typeface": "Angello", "size": "xl"}}})\
