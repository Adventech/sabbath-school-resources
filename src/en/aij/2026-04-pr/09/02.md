Jesus’ heart must have ached as He walked beside the Pool of Bethesda. He wanted to heal every last person who was sick or in pain, or who couldn’t walk. But it was the Sabbath day, and many people were on their way to the temple to worship. If Jesus healed hundreds of people on the Sabbath in such a public place, there would be big, BIG trouble from the Pharisees and Jewish leaders. They already wanted to get rid of Him. Such a big miracle might stop His very important work on earth.

Jesus noticed the one who needed Him the most. He was a lonely, miserable man lying on his bed mat, unable to walk on his own. He had been unwell for 38 long years. Jesus knew his heart was sad. He had been lying there in that spot by the pool for a long time. Jesus couldn’t walk on by.

Smiling down at the discouraged man, Jesus said, “Do you want to be made well?” (John 5:6).

The man didn’t know who Jesus was. How did he answer Jesus? **Read John 5:7.**

Jesus understood how hard it had been for the man for all these long years. Jesus didn’t have a long discussion with him, but simply said, “Stand up. Pick up your mat and walk” (John 5:8, ICB).

What a beautiful picture of Jesus’ love! Jesus didn’t expect the man to clean up his life and know all about Jesus before he was healed.

The same is true for us today. Jesus doesn’t expect us to be good before we come to Him. He says, “Come as you are.” Jesus wants you to know how much He loves you, even if you don’t know what you need or how to ask for it. Jesus will help you and He will change everything.
