Create

Draw a chalk-outline of an angel. Have your child lay down on the footpath or a concrete area, with their arms by their sides. As you draw around their outline, have them slowly move their straight arms up, until pointing to the sky, creating the outline of “wings.” If you live where there is snow, make a snow angel by laying down in the snow and moving your straight arms from your sides until they are straight up. Repeat a few times for a good indent. Share how beautiful it would have been to see the light swirling around the angels, and how the music would have filled the air!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#B15E97"}}}
Play

Act out the story of the shepherds and the angels, together. Find props to help you (a doll for Baby Jesus, cotton wool for fluffy sheep, a flashlight for the light in the sky). You may also use items from last week’s lesson. Talk about what the shepherds’ faces would have looked like when they first saw Baby Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#B15E97"}}}
Learn

Jesus came to bring joy to everyone. Talk to your child about the meaning of “joy.” Joy is the biggest, best happiness. It fills our hearts. Jesus came to give us joy even when we are feeling sad, tired, or grumpy. Joy is knowing we are loved no matter what happens.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#B15E97"}}}
Give

How could you bring joy to someone this week? Your child might sing for someone, draw them a picture, or help them with something. Brainstorm an idea and follow through on it this week. Remind your child that Jesus came to give us joy.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#B15E97"}}}
Sing

Sing or listen to the song “Angels We Have Heard on High.” Make some ribbon streamers to wave in the air as you sing/ listen. Repeat the song each night for worship this week.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#B15E97"}}}
Pray

Thank God for sending Jesus and for giving us joy.
