**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Do you give your all, in all aspects of life?

It’s easy to do our best with the things we love; the things that make us feel important and bring us joy and fulfillment. But what about the things that are more mundane, such as washing the dishes or the car, doing the laundry, mowing the lawn, or taking out the trash?

Perhaps the key is how we view these tasks. Are they merely unpleasant intrusions into our lives, or do they fulfill a greater purpose? As followers of God, everything we do is really for Him­­—whether it seems big or small from a human perspective. When we recognize that in every task we are working for the Lord, every job can take on greater significance. “And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men” (Colossians 3:23).

As you go through this week, may you sense God’s presence and purpose for you in all that you do. And may you help the children see their duties as God-given responsibilities, so that, like Jacob, they can do their best.

“Whatever our duties in various lines of work may be, remember that God is the general over all. . . . It is not position or rank by which the Lord estimates. He looks to see how much of the spirit of the Master you cherish and how much of the likeness of Christ your work reveals” (Ellen G. White letter 49, 1897, in _Manuscript Releases_, vol. 21, pp. 337, 338).
