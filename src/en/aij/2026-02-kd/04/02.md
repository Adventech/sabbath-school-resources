Jacob looked around Uncle Laban’s tents and sighed happily. He had found a new home. Although his parents were far away, he was now with family.

Every day Jacob worked hard to thank his family. The Bible says, “Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might” (Ecclesiastes 9:10). And that is exactly what Jacob did.

Uncle Laban soon realized Jacob was a very good worker. After a month of seeing Jacob do his best, Uncle Laban came to him with a big question. What was it? **Read Genesis 29:15.**

Jacob smiled. Uncle Laban had two daughters, big sister Leah and younger sister Rachel. From the day he had met beautiful Rachel at the well, Jacob had fallen in love with her. “Let me marry your younger daughter Rachel. If you will, I will work seven years for you,” Jacob said (Genesis 29:18, ICB).

Uncle Laban thought this was a great idea! He needed some extra help, and seven years was a long time. He nodded.

Jacob was excited. He happily started working right away. His love for God and for Rachel helped him do his best. He didn’t mind waiting for Rachel for seven long years. The Bible tells us “love is patient” (1 Corinthians 13:4, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever had to wait for a long time for something good? What was that like?
