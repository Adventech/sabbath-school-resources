**Lovingkindness:** Love in action, and love that is genuine, steadfast, and compassionate toward others. We can’t earn God’s lovingkindness, but He offers it to us because His love is so great.”

**Lesson Summary**

Walking in Jerusalem, Jesus came to the Pool of Bethesda, where it was believed that the sick could be healed if they were first in the water when it stirred. Jesus saw a man who had been unable to walk for 38 years lying there on his mat. He had lost all hope, and Jesus’ heart was filled with love and compassion. He showed His lovingkindness by healing the man, telling him to pick up his bed and walk. Joyfully the man carried his mat to the temple, praising God, which upset the Jewish leaders. Jesus found the man again and encouraged him to stop sinning. The man told everyone he knew about the healing love of Jesus.
