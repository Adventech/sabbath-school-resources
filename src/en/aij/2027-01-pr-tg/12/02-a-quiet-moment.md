**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God longs to be close to us. He always has and always will.

The God who created mountains, oceans, and galaxies chose to live with His people in a tent. God chose to “move in” with His people, settling His glory in a tent in the middle of their camp that traveled wherever they went. He asked Moses to have them “make Me a sanctuary, that I will dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8). Their flaws, failures, and wanderings did not deter Him. His desire was for closeness.

This is our own story too. God is not waiting for us to polish ourselves up before He comes near. Through Jesus He has made His dwelling within us. We are now His tabernacle, His chosen home.

When life feels chaotic, when your service feels unseen, when your heart feels weary, remember this: the God of the universe wants to be close to you. Not just in moments of worship or ministry, but in your kitchen, your workplace, your commute, and in your quiet sighs of prayer.

Take comfort. The One who ordered every detail of His dwelling place is also present in every detail of your life. He is not a distant God. He is “Immanuel,” “God with us” (Matthew 1:23).

As you and the children immerse yourselves in the story of the building of God’s wilderness sanctuary, may your hearts be full to overflowing with the knowledge that God can and will come close to His people. He wants to dwell in your hearts and make His presence felt, today and always.
