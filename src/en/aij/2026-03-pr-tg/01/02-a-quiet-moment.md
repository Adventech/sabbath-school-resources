**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The story of God’s love for us, recorded in the Bible, is truly amazing. It begins with God, who created people out of love, and for love, granting them freedom and dominion over a glorious world that He created. God invited mankind to be part of the story. And it has been our story ever since.

Sadly, we know what happened next: Adam and Eve chose disobedience and sin. Yet God responded in love, promising to send His own Son to take sin’s consequence, to save His people. God again invited people to take His story forward.

Enter Zacharias and Elizabeth. Devoted to God, this faithful couple were waiting for God’s promise to be fulfilled. Though Zacharias doubted the angel’s message at first, God still included him. God was willing to risk all—yet again—to include those whose hearts were surrendered to Him.

Mary, favored and blessed, was also invited to play a leading role as mother of the Savior. Young, overwhelmed, and unable to fully comprehend the angel’s message, she still spoke those incredible words: “I am the servant girl of the Lord. Let this happen to me as you say!” (Luke 1:38, ICB).

God also wants to include you in His story—one that is still unfolding. May we also be willing to surrender. In offering to Him all that we are and all that we have, we declare our love to God.

As you and the children explore this week’s story, be reminded of God’s bigger story and how He has risked and given all, that you may be part of it. How will you respond to Him?
