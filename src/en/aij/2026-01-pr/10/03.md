God had washed wickedness from the world and then confused languages to try to help His children put their trust in Him as their loving friend. But like fog on a river, that same wickedness had crept back, until it was almost everywhere. Ever since Adam and Eve’s choice to disobey God, our world has been in this back-and-forth struggle between good and evil.

So God decided to do something big and loving to help.
He wouldn’t use water or languages or lightning this time. No, He would use a man. A very special man who was His faithful friend.
If you were God, whom would you use?

God’s faithful friend had grown from a boy to a man. And his friendship with God had become even stronger. Abram and God often talked together, trusting each other in beautiful ways.

One day Abram heard God say, “Get out of your country, from your family and from your father’s house, to a land that I will show you” (Genesis 12:1).

What a big thing to be asked to do! Abram was now a rich man with a wife, a comfortable home, and lots of servants. People respected him as a good and successful man who shared his love for God. But would they respect him when he said he was moving away from friends and family—especially when they didn’t even know where they were moving to?

People might have thought he was crazy and foolish, but Abram didn’t question God. Not even for a moment. He had never doubted God’s voice before, and he wouldn’t now. He would obey his God. Why wasn’t it hard for Abram to do what God asked him to do? Read John 15:14.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Has your family ever had to choose to do something you were unsure about? How did this make you feel?_
- _In what ways does God speak to us today?_

God lovingly speaks to us in different ways. He longs for us to be His friend and listen to Him.
