[[Revelation 16:1-2]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The first five plagues have some striking similarities to the plagues God sent on Egypt thousands of years ago as punishment for enslaving His people (see Exodus, chapters 7-11).
- Because the plagues on Egypt were literal, we can safely assume that the last plagues will be literal, as well, and not only symbolic.

[[Revelation 16:3-16]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The sixth and seventh plagues, while clearly having literal effects on earth, also contain symbolic elements that add greatly to their meaning.
- To find out more about the symbols involved in the sixth plague, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r16a/)**.
- Verse 13 shows the unholy trinity gathering together wicked rulers on a global scale for the purpose of mounting one final great battle against Jesus: the battle of Armageddon.
- This will be both a literal and a spiritual battle in which the unholy trinity will use spiritualism and demonic manifestations to persuade the rulers of the world to follow them.
- To learn more about this battle, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r16b/)**.
- For many, there remains great confusion regarding the battle of Armageddon. For example, some interpreters see it purely as a literal war to be fought between the literal nation of Israel and the forces of evil in the world.
- To find out why this is not an accurate interpretation of prophecy, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r16c/)**.
