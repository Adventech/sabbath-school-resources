**What you need:**

Week 6 craft template on cardstock, colored paper, black marker, scissors, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6a.png?updated)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/craft-template.png)

```

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out colored square of paper: 2 x 2 inches (5 x 5 centimeters).
- Cut two long strips of colored paper roughly 1 x 11.5 inches (2.5 x 29.5 centimeters).

**Instructions:**

1\. Cut out the pieces from the template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6b.png?updated)

2\. Use a marker to write “Reach out to Jesus” on the square piece of colored paper, and glue to the lid of the box template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6c.png?updated)

3\. Fold along the lines and glue the tabs to make a box.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6d.png)

4\. Glue the ends of two strips of paper together (at a right angle).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6e.png)

5\. Fold the bottom strip over the top strip.

6\. Continue folding the strips over the top of each other to make a “spring.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6f.png)

7\. Once finished, glue the end of the strips together and the hand onto the top.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6g.png)

8\. Glue the bottom of the “spring” inside the box. Close the lid, then open it to see the hand reach up to Jesus!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/6h.png)
