Jesus slumped to the ground, totally exhausted from the battle with Satan. Seeing Him there, God did the most beautiful thing. He sent angels with food to remind Jesus of His Father’s great love. The rest of the angels filled heaven with their celebrations. Jesus had won!

All of heaven celebrates when we rely on God and His power to resist temptations too. Ephesians 6:10 tells us to “be strong in the Lord and in [His] power.” We can’t be strong all by ourselves, but we can rely on the Holy Spirit’s power.

The Holy Spirit helps us stay close to God in all the ways we learned about on Sunday. He helps us to be on guard before temptation comes: “Watch and pray, lest you enter into temptation” (Mark 14:38). Then if we are tempted to do something we’re not supposed to do, the Holy Spirit helps us to pray and run away. After that, He helps us find something good to do instead: “Do not be overcome by evil, but overcome evil with good” (Romans 12:21). We could find a sibling or friend to hang out with, or do something fun we are allowed to do.

God the Father, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit are there to help you, always. The victory Jesus achieved in the wilderness, and then at the cross, is yours. Just ask Him! Then thank Him—it’s yours. “But thanks be to God, who gives us the victory through our Lord Jesus Christ” (1 Corinthians 15:57).
