Mother opened and shut each cupboard door in the kitchen with a loud bang. She couldn’t find a single match anywhere to light their stove. It was nearly Sabbath. And it was much too late for a trip to the faraway shops on their island home in Fiji.

Four-year-old John watched his mother closely. Finally he asked, “Can we pray about the matches, Mother?”

Mother was surprised, but answered, “Of course.” She knew John believed that no problem was too small for God. She also knew that “with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26).

John prayed, “Dear Jesus, thank You for Your love. Please help us to have a match so we can have hot milk with our breakfast tomorrow morning. Amen.” Mother smiled and gave John a hug.

When John woke up the next morning, he remembered his prayer and began to look around the house. Finally his eye caught sight of a little box stuck between the front door and the screen. John pulled it out and slid it open. His heart leaped with joy. Racing to his mother’s bedroom, he waved the little box in the air. “Look, Mother, it’s matches! I think God’s angel must have brought them during the night.”

Inside the box were little white matches with silvery white tips. Mother had never seen matches like those before.

Together they knelt down. They thanked God for hearing their prayer about something as simple as a match, and for answering in such a powerful way. Then with happy hearts they went to the kitchen to light the stove and have warm milk with their breakfast.
