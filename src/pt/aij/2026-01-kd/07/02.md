As the world became more and more evil, there was one man who stayed faithful to God. His name was Enoch. Enoch and God were best friends. Enoch loved God with all his heart, with all his soul, and with all his strength.

Enoch’s favorite time of the day was his quiet time alone with God. He would go somewhere peaceful and talk to God about everything.

When Enoch returned to town, his face would glow with a holy light. Others could see that Enoch had been with God. They felt his love and kindness. They heard him speak God’s words.

Every day Enoch kept close to God. He didn’t just talk to God in his quiet time—he talked to God all day! The Bible says, “Enoch walked with God” (Genesis 5:24, ICB).

Wherever Enoch was, God was in his thoughts. Enoch would talk to God in the quiet fields and at the noisy market. He thought of God as he worked. He thanked God as he sat down with his family at night.

Oh, how different Enoch was from the evil world around him! Enoch knew that God is loving, and he was faithful to Him.

How can we be faithful like Enoch? **Read Psalm 1:1, 2.**
