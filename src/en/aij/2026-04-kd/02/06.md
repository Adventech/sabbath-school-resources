The nobleman raced home. Bursting through the door he hugged his healthy son joyfully, thanking God again and again for the miraculous healing.

The excited nobleman quickly told everyone in his house about Jesus. He told his joyful family. He told the smiling cook. He told the gardener outside. The nobleman told everyone in his house about how the words of Jesus had healed his son. The words of Jesus were powerful. The words of Jesus were true!

Everyone in the household looked at the boy with his rosy cheeks and big smile. The Bible says, “So the man and all the people of his house believed in Jesus” (John 4:53, ICB).

They all saw the miracle and chose to believe Jesus was the Son of God and their Savior. Their home was filled with joyful praises to the Lord who had saved.

When we believe the true words of Jesus, we can be saved too. Our memory verse says, “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved, you and your household” (Acts 16:31). Thank You, Jesus!
