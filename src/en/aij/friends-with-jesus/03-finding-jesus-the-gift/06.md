The Wise Men traveled across the sand, over hills, and through distant lands. Every night they followed the star. They were seeking the promised King!

Finally, after a very long journey, the Wise Men saw Jerusalem up ahead. They watched as the star stopped still, right above the temple. They must be getting close!

Excitedly the men entered the city gates. They began asking people where to find their newborn King. The people looked confused—they didn’t know.

They went to the palace of King Herod. Maybe he would know? But no, Herod didn’t know. In fact, their question made him very jealous. He was the king—he didn’t want there to be a new one!

Leaving Jerusalem, the Wise Men didn’t know where to go next. But then they saw the special star moving again! This time it led them right to a humble little home in Bethlehem.

What did they see and do next? **Read Matthew 2:11.**

The Wise Men dropped to their knees before Jesus. They opened their treasure box and pulled out three gifts. They gave Jesus shiny gold, special frankincense, and a sweet-smelling oil called myrrh. These gifts were worth a lot of money! They were special gifts for a king.

The Wise Men were so happy. God’s word was true: “And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart” (Jeremiah 29:13).
