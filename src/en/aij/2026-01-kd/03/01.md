**Big Idea:**\
God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Exodus 20:8)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Holy”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - God is holy, which means He is perfect and good. When God makes something holy, He makes it different from ordinary things.

An Extra-Special Gift

Adam and Eve watched the golden sun begin to set. Their smiles grew big and bright. What a beautiful world! In six days God had created everything: from blue sky to pink rose, and golden lions to rainbow fish.

They thought about all the things they had seen in just one day. Those giraffes had such long necks! And what about those playful monkeys? They loved their new garden home. They loved each other, too. And most of all, they loved their Creator.

God loved them too. Because of His big love, He gave them something very special on the seventh day. The Bible says, “And on the seventh day God ended His work which He had done, and He rested on the seventh day” (Genesis 2:2).

God had given them a gift to celebrate Creation. It was the gift of time—precious time with Him.

On the seventh day, the Sabbath, God rested with joy. He wasn’t tired, but He stopped His work so He could spend a whole day with His precious children—Adam and Eve.

What does the Bible say about this special day? Read Exodus 20:11.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Adam and Eve’s first Sabbath with God would have been very special. What kinds of things might they have done together?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for loving you so much that He stops everything to be with you in a special way every Sabbath.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Start making a Sabbath Treasure Box. Find a cardboard box or an adult shoe box. Cover it in wrapping paper or paint it with your favorite colors.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Be extra loving to your brother, sister, or someone else who is close to you. Do something kind or make them a surprise. Then tell them you love them.
