God’s plan is always the best plan.

We know it to be true, and yet sometimes doubt and reasoning cloud our better judgment. Sometimes God’s plans seem absurd! Yet it’s in the absurdity that our faith has a chance to grow, and we learn to trust God—really trust Him.

God asked Noah to build a massive boat at a time when it had never rained before! To the onlooking world, he seemed to be just a “wild enthusiast” _(Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 338)_. Nevertheless, “Thus Noah did; according to all that God commanded him, so he did” _(Genesis 6:22)_.

We too can trust in our Creator and Savior. “God never leads His children otherwise than they would choose to be led, if they could see the end from the beginning and discern the glory of the purpose which they are fulfilling as co-workers with Him” _(Ellen G. White, The Ministry of Healing, p. 479)_.

Sometimes God asks us to do things that don’t make sense. But when we know Him as a loving Father who only has our best interest at heart, then we can trust Him no matter what.

When it comes to following God’s plan for your life, expect the unexpected. That’s when the miracles show up!

> <callout>Proverbs 3:5, 6</callout>
> “Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.”
