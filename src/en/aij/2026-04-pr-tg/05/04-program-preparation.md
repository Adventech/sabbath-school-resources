**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source **colored cardstock, colored pencils or markers**, and craft items to decorate cards, such as **stickers, ribbon** if available.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source items for Focus Activity: Create a “Pass the Parcel” game using **small paper hearts** with **multiple layers of newspaper wrapping** for children to unwrap. Make enough hearts for each child in the class. On each heart, write the memory verse: “The Lord is gracious and full of compassion, slow to anger and great in mercy” (Psalm 145:8). Wrap the hearts in the center of the parcel and include **“compassion cards”** between each layer of newspaper. Ideas for compassion cards include: sitting with a lonely classmate, forgiving someone who has done something wrong to you, listening to someone who needs to talk, sharing a toy, inviting someone who is often left out to join you in a game or activity, helping by running an errand, encouraging someone, giving a gift, praying for someone, forgiving someone, speaking kind words, visiting someone who is sick, making something for someone who is sick, donating clothes or toys to people in need.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: **white stickers/face paint/skin lotion, a small bowl of dirt, a small bowl of water**.

Source **four pieces of paper** and write/draw in large letters on each: Love; heart symbol; Value; smiley-face symbol. Keep them covered up.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 44: Jesus Heals]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.
