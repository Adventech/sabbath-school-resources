**Word of the Week**

**Persistent:** Continuing to do, or trying to do, something in a determined way is being persistent. A persistent person doesn’t give up or stop trying even when things are difficult or if they are pressured to stop. God is persistent in His love for us—He never stops loving us.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

A woman had been bleeding for 12 long years. Even though no doctor or treatment had been able to make her well, she had faith that Jesus could heal her. When she heard that Jesus was in her town, she moved through the crowd to try to see Him. She believed that if she just reached out to touch the edge of His hem, she would be healed, and she was.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God wants to heal our sickness, brokenness, and loneliness.
- **Feel** thankful that God hears our prayers and sees our faith.
- **Respond** by reaching out to Jesus so He can help us.
