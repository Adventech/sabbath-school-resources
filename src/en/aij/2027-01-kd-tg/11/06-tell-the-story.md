**Big Idea:** God’s way is best, and I can choose Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 11 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- **Suggested props**: scrunched **newspaper piled high** and covered with **brown fabric** for “mountain,” **toy cow** for idol, **small cardboard** Ten Commandment tablets.
- Use peg people (Moses, Aaron, Joshua, others) or a felt set if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God called from the top of Mount Sinai, “Come up to me” (Exodus 24:1, ICB). He invited Moses, Aaron, and Israel’s leaders up the mountain. ^[(Move peg Moses, Aaron, and others up mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

They climbed up, up, up the mountain. Then, they saw God’s holiness, bright with majesty. After seeing and worshipping God from afar, “they ate and drank” (Exodus 24:11).

Then God invited Moses to come farther up the mountain. Up, up, up he climbed into the thick cloud, where God was. ^[(Move peg Moses up the mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

For 40 days and 40 nights Moses and God talked together, although Moses never saw God’s face. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then God made something special. With His own finger, God wrote the Ten Commandments on two stone tablets and gave them to Moses. They would be a gift of love from God to everyone.

At the very bottom of Mount Sinai many of the Israelites were starting to feel restless. What has happened to Moses? they wondered. He’s been gone for ages!

The people all crowded around Aaron. ^[(Move peg people around Aaron.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They asked him to make them an idol to lead them instead. Oh, no! God had just told them not to make idols. How could they forget? An idol could never take the place of the living, saving God.

Surely Aaron would tell them not to be so silly and forgetful.

_Hmm._ Aaron thought. What would he choose to do?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Aaron made them an idol in the shape of a golden calf. ^[(Show toy cow.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was just like the idols from Egypt. Aaron let the people talk him into doing the wrong thing.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites got up early. They made a feast, and they sacrificed to their new idol. Then they began to sing and dance around the golden calf. They sinned against God, breaking their love commitment to Him!

God saw it all. He sent Moses down, down, down the mountain. ^[(Move peg Moses down the mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses carried the Ten Commandments ^[(cardboard tablets)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As they came near camp, Moses and Joshua stopped. There was the golden calf idol. And there were God’s people dancing around it.

Moses was filled with anger! He threw the commandments to the ground. Crash! They smashed into pieces.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses rushed toward the ugly golden idol. Filled with love for God, he picked it up and threw it into the fire. Whoosh! The hot flames wrapped around it, and the gold began to melt. Moses took the melted metal out of the fire and crushed it into dust. Carrying the golden dust over to the nearby stream, he tossed it in. Then he made the people drink it. He stormed over to his brother Aaron and asked, “What did this people do to you that you have brought so great a sin upon them?” (Exodus 32:21). Aaron lied and made up excuses. If Aaron had chosen to stand up for what was right, this sinful mess would never have happened.

The people cried out to God for forgiveness.

Meanwhile Moses began chiseling and hammering. He was making new flat stones. The next morning he took them back up the mountain. ^[(Move peg Moses up the mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Up high on the mountain, God came to Moses. Would God forgive His people? Yes! God said, “The Lord is a God who shows mercy and is kind. . . . The Lord forgives people for wrong and sin” (Exodus 34:6, 7, ICB).

For 40 days and nights God and Moses talked together again, and God wrote the Ten Commandments on the stone tablets.

When God was finished, Moses carefully made his way back down the mountain with new Ten Commandments stones. ^[(Move Moses and tablets down mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But Moses now looked different. His face glowed! Moses had been so close to God’s glowing glory that his face glowed too.
