**What you need:**

Two different colored pieces of paper, scissors, stapler, peg doll or disposable wooden spoon, black marker, piece of fabric, rubber band.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1a.jpg)

**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut 11 x 0.5 inch (28 x 1.5 centimeter) strips from two different colored pieces of paper. You will need 6 strips of each color per child.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1b.jpg)

**Create it together:**

1\. Vertically line up 6 paper strips of one color and place a weight (e.g. stapler) at the top to keep them in place. Weave a different colored strip of paper horizontally—over and under the vertical strips.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1c.jpg)

2\. Weave another strip of paper under and over, so it’s opposite to the one above.

3\. Continue weaving the rest of the strips over and under, etc.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1d.jpg)

4\. Have someone place their hand over the bottom of the strips to hold them in place while you gather the top ends and staple together.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1e.jpg)

5\. Gather the opposite ends and staple together. 

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1f.jpg)

Then do the same for the other two sides, stapling them together in the middle to form a cover over the basket.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1g.jpg)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1h.jpg)

6\. Draw a face on the peg doll or spoon. Wrap a piece of fabric around the middle and secure with a rubber band.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1i.jpg)

7\. Place the baby in the basket and reenact the story of baby Moses.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/1i.jpg)
