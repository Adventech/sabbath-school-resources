[[Revelation 9:1-4]]

- The seal of God here protects the followers of Jesus from the judgments on the wicked. Even in the midst of global disaster, Christ’s people will be preserved.

[[Revelation 9:5-21]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- It is astonishing that even after much of the earth is destroyed because of their wickedness, the followers of Satan do not turn from their evil ways.
- Once evil becomes a way of life, we humans often never return to justice, goodness, or love.
- Choosing Jesus today, before our hearts become hardened in wickedness, will be a profound blessing both now and at the end of time.
