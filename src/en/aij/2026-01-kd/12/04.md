Quickly and bravely Abram gathered his troops. Then he raced to find the enemy who had taken Lot as prisoner. The Bible says they “chased the enemy all the way to the town of Dan” (Genesis 14:14, ICB).

The soldiers in the enemy camp didn’t think anyone would chase after them. They didn’t think anyone cared about the people they had as prisoners. But loyal Abram cared. He was brave and strong, and God helped him.

Day turned to night. The enemy soldiers sat around their campfires, eating and laughing together. Their prisoners were all tied together nearby. They thought all was well. Then what happened? **Read Genesis 14:15.**

What a surprise those soldiers got! Abram and his men worked quickly and with lots of energy. All the prisoners were saved!

Sometimes we are faced with big, scary problems. God is so much bigger than any problem we can ever have, and He is always with us to help us. The Bible says, “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble” (Psalm 46:1).

- Do you think this will change Lot’s mind about living in Sodom?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that no problem is ever too big for Him to help us through.
