**Word of the Week**

**Surrender:** To surrender is to be willing to give up your own ideas, wants, or possessions. To allow or trust someone else to make decisions for you.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Faithful Zacharias and Elizabeth loved God and were waiting for His promised Savior. An angel appeared to Zacharias and told him his wife would give birth to a son whom they would call John. He would prepare the way for the coming Savior. Zacharias doubted at first, and was unable to speak until the baby was born. But he believed. In Nazareth an angel took exciting news to a young girl named Mary, then in a dream to her betrothed, Joseph. They would have a son, the Savior, Jesus. They surrendered to God’s will. God included these people, who loved Him, as part of His great story.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is in control of His big story.
- **Feel** they can choose to be part of God’s story.
- **Respond** by surrendering their lives to God and be willing to follow His leading.
