**Word of the Week**

**Unselfish:** Saving or showing more concern for other people than yourself.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Abram was faced with the problem of insufficient land and space for his growing household and that of his nephew Lot. Abram prayed about the situation, and when God advised that he and Lot should separate, Abram followed God’s direction and unselfishly let Lot choose first which land he would like.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that our friend God will help us put others first.
- **Feel** a desire to put others first.
- **Respond** by unselfishly allowing others to have the first choice.
