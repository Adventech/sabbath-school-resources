**Word of the Week**

**Believe:** When you believe someone, you know in your heart that what they say is true. You feel sure about it.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

A nobleman’s son was very sick. He was very excited to hear Jesus was in town. He traveled from Capernaum to Cana to ask Jesus to heal his son. Jesus told the nobleman to go home, for his son would live. The nobleman believed Jesus’ words. He went home the next morning, and on the way his servants met him with the news that his son had recovered, beginning at the exact hour that Jesus had spoken.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus has the power to heal.
- **Feel** a desire to always trust Jesus.
- **Respond** by sharing belief in Jesus’ healing power with others.
