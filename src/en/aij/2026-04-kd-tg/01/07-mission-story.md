**Focus:** Jesus is all-powerful and can help me when I’m afraid.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Buru Island, Indonesia**, on your **globe** or **world map**.

Armi was a young student missionary on Buru Island in Indonesia. No Seventh-day Adventists lived there. Armi couldn’t wait to tell people about Jesus. But he wasn’t sure how to start.

One evening two villagers invited him to go fishing. He said yes with a smile. They looked at the sky and saw that the weather was good. They hoped to catch many fish. The trio got into a small wooden boat and set sail.

When the boat was far from shore, the weather suddenly changed. A fierce wind began to blow. Rain started to fall.

Large waves crashed against the boat. Armi and his two friends bailed out water, and, when that didn’t help, they threw their fishing gear into the ocean to make the boat lighter. But nothing helped.

The three friends thought they were all going to die! Suddenly Armi sensed a small voice say, “Pray.”

What a great idea! Armi thought. Closing his eyes and taking a deep breath, he screamed, “Lord, help us! Save us because we know that You are able to save us!”

Yelling with all his might, he repeated the prayer again and again. Then he said, “Amen” and opened his eyes.

At that moment the storm stopped. The wind no longer blew, and the ocean was still. The dark clouds rolled away, and bright stars shone down.

Armi cried out, “Jesus, thank You so much!”

His two friends cried with joy as they steered the boat back to shore. On the way they saw the fishing net that they had thrown out during the storm and stopped to pick it up. The net was bursting with fish!

When the boat landed, villagers rushed to the shore. They had seen the men sail away and thought that the boat had sunk in the storm. Armi’s friends told them about the answered prayer. The villagers then wanted to buy the miracle fish. It was the most fish the fishermen had ever caught.

After that evening the people asked Armi many questions about Jesus, the one who had calmed the storm. Four people were baptized. Three years later the village had its own Adventist church!
