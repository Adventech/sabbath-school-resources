**Focus:** We can make friends with others and lead them to Jesus.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Portugal** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Christof and his parents and two brothers once lived in a crowded city. But they wanted to live in the countryside, so they moved to a tiny village in a mountain valley in central Portugal.

One day Christof saw his neighbor Antonio herding his goats nearby. The boy waved a greeting and ran to the man. He asked Antonio about his goats and how to care for them. Antonio was happy to answer all of his questions and showed Christof how to milk a goat.

That evening the boy asked his parents if he could use his allowance to buy some goats. His parents said yes! Antonio helped the boy buy good goats and told him more about how to care for them. Their friendship grew.

One day Christof asked Antonio if he believed in God. Antonio said he did, but there was no church in the village. As they talked, Christof realized Antonio couldn’t read. Christof offered to read his Bible to him. The man happily accepted.

Word spread through the tiny village that Christof was reading the Bible to Antonio. One of Antonio’s neighbors asked Christof to read the Bible to her as well. In fact, most of the old villagers wanted the boy to read the Bible to them.

Christof and his brothers began riding their bikes into the village to spend time with the elderly villagers. The villagers loved their visits, for the boys were the only children within miles. The boys offered to help work in people’s gardens, care for their animals, and do other chores the villagers couldn’t manage well anymore.

One day Christof’s family organized a weeklong series of Bible studies for the villagers. People in neighboring villages heard about the meetings, and they wanted to hear the message too.

Today three villages have Bible study groups, and some villagers worship on Sabbath. And it all started with a young boy and his friendship with a goat herder and some goats.
