**Big Idea:** When God is my friend, He will help me put others first.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When Abram and Sarai left Ur to follow God on His great adventure, they took Abram’s nephew Lot with them. Lot’s father, Haran, had died some time before. Abram’s kindness to Lot made the two great friends.

When they finally arrived in Canaan together, Lot chose to set up camp close to his uncle Abram. When their sheep and cattle had eaten well, Abram and Lot moved their traveling village to where there was more rich, green grass.

At each new camping spot Abram built an altar so that everyone could worship God. When they packed up and moved on to new grazing grounds, the altar was left behind as a reminder of Abram’s deep love for God.

Over time, altars dotted the land. Each one silently told other travelers that God had been worshipped there. Sometimes they would pause and worship at the altars too.

Abram soon became “very rich in livestock, in silver, and in gold” (Genesis 13:2). Now their households were huge! Life was good for everyone.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But after a while angry shouts erupted from the shepherds in the hills, and grumpy words were spoken around the evening campfires. **^[What was the problem? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Genesis 13:6.** ^[(There wasn’t enough land to support them all.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

There simply wasn’t enough grass to feed so many animals and support so many people. Tempers raged, and fights broke out.

Abram prayed about the problem, and God showed His good friend what to do.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram went to find Lot, and said, “We should separate. The whole land is there in front of you. If you go to the left, I will go to the right. If you go to the right, I will go to the left” (Genesis 13:9, ICB). He could choose first! What an unselfish thing for Uncle Abram to do.

Uncle Abram was very unselfish when he said Lot could choose the land he wanted. Lot should have let his uncle choose first! Abram was older and had been so kind to his nephew. But Lot didn’t think of that. Instead he smiled greedily and made his choice.

Lot looked at the lush, green Jordan Valley. Yes, his herds would grow fat there. He thought of the busy cities where he could sell his animals and goods. He would be a wealthy man! Repaying Abram’s unselfishness with selfishness, Lot moved with his household to the plain of Jordan.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram sadly waved goodbye. He would miss Lot. But he also felt at peace, knowing he had put Lot first and let him choose. Anywhere with God was the right place to live!

God loved Abram’s unselfish heart. **^[What did God say to Abram? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Genesis 13:14, 15.** ^[(All the wonderful land he could see in every direction was his.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

Then God reminded Abram of the many descendants he had been promised. God had already said they would be like the stars in the night sky. **^[What did God now say Abram’s descendants would be like? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Genesis 13:16.** ^[(Dust—unable to be counted.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Many, many people would know and love God because of Abram’s faithfulness.

Abram knew that his descendants would unselfishly shine for God like stars in the night sky. And like specks of dust dancing in the sunlight, their lives would show others what a happy friendship with God looks like.

So even though Abram didn’t have the best land in Canaan, he was content to have God as his friend and to carry God’s promise in his heart. He truly knew the blessing of unselfishly putting others first.

**REFLECT AND DISCUSS**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}
- **Why was Abram able to unselfishly let Lot choose the land he wanted?** (Abram was God’s friend; he knew that letting Lot choose was the good thing to do.)
- **What else did Abram do in this story that reminds you he was God’s friend?** (He prayed, loved, and looked after Lot and his people, built altars, and worshipped God.)
