**Focus:** I can trust God to forgive and change me.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Taiwan** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Yu-Ting, who lives in Taiwan, grew up without ever going to church with her parents. But then her parents sent her to live with her grandmother in a small village. Grandmother took her to church every Sabbath.

She made new friends in this new village. But they weren’t good friends. They taught her many bad things.Grandmother was worried. Mother and Father were worried too.

One day the pastor said that it was important for everyone to spend special time with God every day, reading His Word and praying. Yu-Ting decided to try.

She became good friends with God. One day she read something that was very hard to read. It was about sin. She didn’t want to read it, and slammed the book shut! It took her three days to have the courage to read it properly, for she knew in her heart that her sin was in the way of her friendship with her loving God.

Yu-Ting ran to her bedroom. Locking the door, she knelt down. “Please forgive me for my sins,” she prayed, sobbing. “Please forgive me for the bad things that I have done.”

She realized how foolish she had been to do things that hurt others and God, even though she had known better.

Yu-Ting didn’t know if God had forgiven her. She cried and prayed every night for a week that He would show her. On the last day of the week, she again said to God that she was sorry, then told Him she would trust Him to guide her from now on.

When she woke up in the morning, she felt different. Her heart was filled with joy and peace. She felt like a new person. “Thank You, Lord!” she exclaimed.

When she returned to live with her parents after three years, they were amazed at the change in her. They decided to go to church with her. Then they gave their hearts to Jesus too!

Yu-Ting was so happy! God had forgiven her sins and given her and her family a new life in Him.

- **How do you know that God loves and forgives you?** (He tells us in His Word, the Bible; He gives us peace.)
