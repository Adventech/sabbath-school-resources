**Big Idea:** Jesus loves to see me get excited about what He can do for me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Outside the city of Jericho ^[(show Jericho on the map)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), beside the dusty main road, sat a man with his hand outstretched. His name was Bartimaeus, and he was begging. For a blind man in Jesus’ day, begging was his only way to get money.

Bartimaeus smiled as he heard a coin fall into his cup with a clink. ^[(Throw some coins into a basket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He listened carefully to the people’s excited chatter as they traveled by, hoping for more.

That’s how Bartimaeus heard about Jesus. Everyone on the road had a story to tell! He heard about the wonderful Teacher and His words of love. He heard about His miracles too—how He had healed people with leprosy and given sight to blind people!

Wow, that news got Bartimaeus excited! If Jesus had healed people in other towns, maybe He would come to Jericho one day. Maybe Bartimaeus would be healed too!

**DISCUSS:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What is something you get really excited about?** (A birthday party, visiting a friend/relative, etc.) Note: You may like to show a prop of something that excites you personally.
- **What was Bartimaeus excited about?** (Knowing Jesus was passing by.)
- **Why do you think that excited him?** (Jesus had healed many people, and he knew Jesus could heal him too.)
- **Can you think of something you have learned about Jesus that excites you?**

**TEACHING POINT:** Knowing Jesus is exciting, and He loves to see us get excited about what He can do for us.

Let’s continue the story. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

One day as he sat beside the road, Bartimaeus heard a big, happy crowd getting closer. He tilted his head to one side. They were coming closer and closer! When the noisy crowd reached Bartimaeus, he asked what was going on.

What did the people tell him? Read Luke 18:37. (They told him that Jesus of Nazareth was passing by.)

Excitement bubbled up inside him as he thought about the great Teacher and Healer being so close. He had waited and longed for this moment.

Bartimaeus shouted at the top of his voice, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:38). His words were filled with hope, excitement, and desperation—all mixed up together.

His words also showed his faith. Bartimaeus believed the Bible promise that said the Messiah would belong to the family of David. He couldn’t see Jesus, but he knew He was the Son of David—the Messiah!

Nobody heard Bartimaeus the first time, so he shouted the words again. “Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:39). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Those standing close by were annoyed, and they shouted back at him to be quiet.

Be quiet? Never! He was far too excited to be silent. He had to get Jesus’ attention! This might be his only chance. He cried out again and again, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:39).

Suddenly the noisy crowd became silent. Jesus asked the people to bring the blind man to him. Jesus had heard his cry!

Bartimaeus heard voices speaking to him, “Cheer up! Get to your feet. Jesus is calling you” (Mark 10:49, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The excited man didn’t need to be told twice. He leaped to his feet and moved toward the voice of Jesus. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Standing before the great Teacher, Bartimaeus heard Jesus say, “What do you want Me to do for you?” (Mark 10:51).

Jesus already knew the answer, but He asked the question so that Bartimaeus could share his faith with the crowd.

“Teacher, I want to see again,” Bartimaeus said (Mark 10:51, ICB).

Then Jesus spoke the most amazing words Bartimaeus would ever hear.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What were those words? Read Mark 10:52.** (“Go your way; your faith has made you well.”) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Instantly Bartimaeus could see! Jesus had healed him! The first thing he could see was the smiling face of Jesus. He could see the cheering crowd around him. He could see the blue sky and the road where he had sat begging for such a long time.

All the people watching were just as excited! They began to praise God. What a wonderful miracle! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus had said to Bartimaeus, “Go your way” (Mark 10:52). But Bartimaeus was too excited to leave Jesus. He wanted to stay close to His Friend and Savior, so Bartimaeus “followed Jesus on the road” (Mark 10:52). His faith in Jesus had made him well!
