**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHOW/DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Reveal several objects or pictures one at a time from a bag (or from beneath a cloth) that relate to guidance. Objects could include: a compass (the craft), a Bible, a star (last week), flashlight, angel, a few cardboard stepping stones, guide dog picture. Talk about each item as you show it. At the end, bring an adult to join the collection.

Ask:

- **What is similar about all these items?**

**Teaching Point:** All these things guide us in some way. When we use them correctly, they guide us well and they protect us.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **God can guide my life.** In our story today, God guided the Wise Men, and Mary, Joseph, and little Jesus through trouble. We can trust Him to guide us too.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Blindfold Guiding: Invite the children to take turns guiding a partner. The guide will lead their partner around the room and back to their seat, giving instructions and helping them along so they are safe.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How did it feel to be led around the room without being able to see?**
- **Did you trust your guide? Why?**
- **Who in our story today trusted God to be their guide? How?**
- **Why do you think they made that choice?**
- **We can also choose whether we let God be our Guide. Why would you say yes to trusting Him?**

**Teaching Point:** In our lesson today, God guided Joseph and Mary to take Jesus and flee to Egypt to keep Him safe. They couldn’t see the future, but they were willing to listen and let God guide them. Though God might not guide us by dreams, He has given us His Word and the Holy Spirit to guide us.

- **Who else in our story thus far was willing to let God guide them?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Talk about a time in your life where you trusted God to guide you in a big or small decision. Tell the children how this impacted you and the lessons you learned.

**Teaching Point:** We trust people that we know will want the best for us and can help us. God is our very best guide. We can trust and obey His words. Just as He guided Mary and Joseph, and the Wise Men, He will protect and care for us too.

**^[BIG IDEA:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea: God can guide my life.**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite one child to pray for your group to ask God to guide each of your lives as you trust Him more as you grow older.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 30: God Guides the Way**. Discuss the picture, then place card in their storage container/bag.

One big way God guides us is through His words in the **Bible**. What a special **privilege it is to have these words to learn from!**

To say the **memory verse**, invite the children to line up in front of the memory verse stepping stones. Each stepping stone will have a word from your memory verse, leading from beginning to end. Have them say each word as they walk along the path. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Psalm 32:8.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/assets/4-psalm-32-8.mp3] “Psalm 32:8” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"David Williams"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation® of Seventh-day Adventists."}

- **Can you think of something that God has “taught” you?**

**Teaching Point:** God really wants to guide our steps, to show us and teach us the right way to go, just as these stepping stones show us where to go.

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, **“Dogs That Guide”** and engage the children in the discussion question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your simple **Paper Plate Compass craft** sample. Explain how God is like a compass. He guides us and shows us the right way we should go. This compass we make can remind us that we can trust God to do this. Share instructions for making it. As you make the compass, invite children to share something they’d like God to guide them with. (Are there choices with your friends, at school, or with family that you need help with? Begin this sharing time by sharing something from your life before they share.)

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring the children back together to sing the closing song, **“God Bless You.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/assets/god-bless-you.mp3] “God Bless You” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singers","value":"David Williams, TVAC Choir"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Violin","value":"Tahlia Atcheson"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Bass","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Clicks","value":"David Williams"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Then invite children to **share something they enjoyed today** and **something that’s new**. Affirm comments.

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God can guide my life.**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) As a group, who in our community could we connect with and thank with a gratitude gift?** (People who serve others.) Decide on a plan.

Let’s invite **God to lead us** right now in this. This week, pray about these community people and decide on a Bible promise or message to include with the gift.

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Conclude with a final prayer, thanking God for being such a trustworthy Guide, and for giving us the Bible, parents, teachers, and family who can also help to guide us in His ways.
