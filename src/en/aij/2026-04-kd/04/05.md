Peter couldn’t believe it. It was like he was in a dream! He looked at Jesus with excitement.

But then he had a thought . . . _I wonder what the disciples think of me, back in the boat? They must think I’m pretty good!_ Peter looked back at his friends who were watching with eyes wide open.

It was then that he saw something. What was it? **Read the first part of Matthew 14:30.**

The rolling, crashing waves looked even bigger and more frightening now that he was out of the boat. A gigantic wave suddenly rolled high, right in front of him. For a moment, he lost sight of Jesus. He felt all alone. He didn’t feel brave anymore! All he could see were lots of huge waves all around him.

Instantly, fear filled Peter’s heart and he began to sink. He looked around, searching for Jesus again. Sinking, he reached up his hand and cried out, “Lord, save me!” (Matthew 14:30).

Immediately, Jesus was right there. He held out His strong hand and grasped Peter’s hand tightly. Jesus pulled wet Peter out of the water to be by His side.

“Your faith is small. Why did you doubt?” (Matthew 14:31, ICB), Jesus asked him. Together, they walked side-by-side and hand-in-hand across the water, back to the boat.

Just like Peter, sometimes we can feel so worried that we forget that Jesus is beside us. The Bible says, “Let us look only to Jesus” (Hebrews 12:2, ICB). We can talk and learn about Jesus. We can pray and sing to Him and show Him our love. This is how we can keep looking at Jesus. He will make us brave.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why did Peter sink?
- Why did Jesus save Peter?
- What fears would you like to give to Jesus?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Play “Pass the smile” with your family or friends. Sit where you can see each other’s faces. The first person starts by smiling, and “passes it on.” The next person will copy their smile, then pass on a new expression—a frown, a wink, or a look of surprise. Keep copying and passing on a new look. You’ll need to focus and watch closely! Think about how important it is to focus on Jesus by reading His words in the Bible.
