A few simple words started their adventure: “Follow Me”; “Come and see.” Now Andrew, Peter, John, Philip, Nathanael, and the other disciples were walking and talking with the Messiah on their way to a wedding in Cana—the very same wedding Jesus’ mother was busy helping to prepare!

The disciples enjoyed walking and talking with their new friend Jesus as they strolled through Galilee toward the wedding. As they watched and listened to Jesus, their faith grew. They began to understand more about who He really was and what He had come to do. They were learning from Jesus by being with Jesus. Their friendship deepened as they spent time together as friends, getting to know each other better.

Jesus wants us to learn from Him this way too. As we “walk and talk” with Jesus, we will become close friends with Him and abide in Him. This word means to stay, live, and dwell with Jesus, fixed to Him. What does He say will happen to us when we abide with Him? **Read John 15:4, 5.**

Arriving at the wedding, Jesus quickly scanned the crowds, looking for His mother. There she was! With a big smile Jesus went quickly to her side. Mary was thrilled to see her Son again, and to meet His new followers. Her faith grew as they told her what they had seen and heard at Jesus’ baptism, and how they believed He was the Messiah. Happiness filled Mary’s heart—others now knew that her Son was God’s Son too.

The Bible invites us to “grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord Jesus Christ” (2 Peter 3:18). Spending time with Jesus and sharing stories with each other helped the disciples’ and Mary’s faith to grow.
