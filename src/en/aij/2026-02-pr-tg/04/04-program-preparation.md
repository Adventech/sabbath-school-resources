Opening Activities

Brainstorm a list of jobs, write each one on a **small piece of paper**, and put them in a **bag** for drawing. For example: builder; violinist; dentist; hairstylist; astronaut, veterinarian; photographer; businessperson. These will be guessed quickly, so think of lots!

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, and **cloth** for veil, **craft sample** of spotted animal, **paper heart** to later tear in two.

Source a **bag** and **heavy objects, such as rocks or books**. Label each one with a word related to unforgiveness: lying, cheating, unfairness, hurt, anger, revenge, bitterness. A simple game to play, like catch with a **ball**. **Crumpled-up paper** to pick up.

Source **small stones**.
