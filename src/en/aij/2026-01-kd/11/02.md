Abram held up his big walking stick and waved. The sheep in the nearby field raised their heads. The goats in the valley below chomped and chewed. But far in the distance another shepherd waved back. It was Lot, Abram’s nephew.

Abram was so glad to be back in Canaan with Lot. They had been many places together since they had left Ur. Abram and Sarai had cared for Lot and taught him to trust and obey God as they did.

Like Abram, Lot was rich with his own herds, flocks, and tents. God had blessed him too! But there was a problem on Abram’s farm. What was it? **Read Genesis 13:6.**

Abram and Lot’s workers began to argue and fight. There were no friendly waves to each other across the fields. Pointing their fingers angrily, they may have shouted:

“Keep your sheep away from the best grass on the hill—it’s our turn!”

“No, it’s not! You’re just greedy!”

Back and forth, day after day, they fought with each other.

Soon Abram heard about their arguments. It made him sad. He knew that unkind words hurt everyone. The Bible says, “A gentle answer will calm a person’s anger. But an unkind answer will cause more anger” (Proverbs 15:1, ICB).

Abram could also see that there wasn’t enough land for all the animals to be together. What could they do?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- What do you think Abram and Lot should do about their problem?
- Think of a time you were frustrated about sharing something. What did you do?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you use kind and peaceful words, even when you’re frustrated.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Choose a toy that is special to you. How can you be unselfish and share your toy? Look through your toys and take some to Adventist Community Services or a thrift store so they can go to children who don’t have many.
