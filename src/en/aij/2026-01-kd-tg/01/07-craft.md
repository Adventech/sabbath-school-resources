**What you need:**

Cardboard heart shape, string, a variety of nature items you have collected, tape, scissors, and craft glue (optional).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/01/1a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out a large heart shape from the cardboard.
- Collect nature items that are easy to glue or weave.

**Create it together:**

- Secure a string loop at the top of the heart and tape securely.
- Make small cuts around the heart shape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/01/1b.png)

- Thread the end of the string through one of the cuts and tape to the back.
- Continue to wind the string around the heart from one side to the other, then tape the other end of the string to the back.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/01/1c.png)

- Children will use the nature items you have collected to glue and/or weave onto their cardboard hearts.
- The nature heart can be hung up in their home as a reminder of their amazing Creator.
