**Word of the Week**

**Fulfill:** To do something that is expected or has been promised.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores how God repeated His promise to give Abraham many descendants so that he would wait patiently for its fulfillment. God and several angels also visited Abraham in disguise to share that his promised son would be born next year. God fulfilled His promise, and baby Isaac was born to Abraham and Sarah.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they can believe God’s promises because God is faithful.
- **Feel** reassured that God will do what He says.
- **Respond** by waiting patiently for promises to be fulfilled, especially the promise of Jesus’ second coming.
