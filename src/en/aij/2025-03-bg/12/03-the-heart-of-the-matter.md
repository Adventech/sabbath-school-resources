The battle of Jericho was indeed a test of faith for the Israelites. The Commander of the Lord’s army appeared to Joshua alone and told him the plan to take the city. It was up to the Israelites to fully trust and obey the commands given to Joshua by God Himself.

Imagine walking around the walls of the city of Jericho thinking, _How are we seriously going to take this city?_ They had to fully put their trust in God. The miracle wasn’t instant. It took a whole week! A whole week of walking, following, and obeying. A whole week of being ridiculed by the enemy. “But the very plan of continuing this ceremony through so long a time prior to the final overthrow of the walls afforded opportunity for the development of faith among the Israelites. It was to be impressed upon their minds that their strength was not in the wisdom of man, nor in his might, but only in the God of their salvation. They were thus to become accustomed to relying wholly upon their divine Leader” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets,_ p. 493).

God wants us to rely upon Him too. We can choose to put our trust in Him, and obey Him, even when it seems as if He isn’t working, or it seems as if it’s taking a long time for our prayers to be answered. We can trust that He is always at work, even when we can’t see it! This is how our faith grows. “It was by faith that the walls of Jericho fell. They fell after the people had marched around the walls of Jericho for seven days” (Hebrews 11:30).

Will you place your confidence in God today? Will you follow His lead?
