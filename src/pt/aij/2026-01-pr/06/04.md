O que an awful, sad dia! Out em the field onde nobody saw, Cain did this terrible thing a seu younger brother—seu loving, innocent brother.

Mas Deus didn’t leave Adam alone com seu grief e guilt, just as Ele never leaves any de Seu children. Cain’s angry choices caused então much hurt, mas Deus was there a comfort e heal. Ele loves e comforts nos quando nós are sad too.
