**Focus:** When a Global Mission pioneer spreads the gospel, Jesus’ healing power is demonstrated.

**Location:** Help a child locate **India** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Kamala is a Global Mission pioneer in New Delhi, India, one of the most populated cities in the world. Global Mission pioneers start new groups of believers in neighborhoods where there are no Adventists.

Over time Kamala has made friends in her community. God has been blessing, and people have been open to learning about Jesus. Every Sabbath she speaks to a growing group that meets in a small room.

One of the members is Karishma. She came to know Jesus because of Kamala’s visits.

Karishma’s mother had been sick in bed for 25 years. As she lay in bed she listened in to Kamala and Karishma talking about Jesus. She was afraid that Karishma would want to become a Christian, so she warned her not to trust Kamala.

Karishma didn’t want to upset her mother, but she truly believed all she was learning in the Bible. Against her mother’s advice she decided to be baptized. She prayed faithfully for her mother every day.

Kamala continued to visit, and began telling Karishma’s mother about Jesus’ healing power. Eventually Karishma’s mother accepted Jesus into her life as well. After 25 years of sickness Karishma’s mother was healed and able to walk again!

Jesus has the power to heal those who are sick! Let’s pray for people like Kamala who are spreading the good news of Jesus’ love and power.
