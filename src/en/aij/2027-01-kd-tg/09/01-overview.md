**Word of the Week**

**Love:** Love is tender, kind, and unselfish, even to the people we don’t like. It doesn’t get angry easily, and forgives quickly. Love puts the other person first and wants what is best for them. God is love. He wants us to love Him and become like Him.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Today’s lesson explores the first four commandments, which teach us how to show we love God by putting Him first. When we know how much God loves us, we will want to obey Him. God’s Spirit helps us to obey.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God gave the commandments because He loves us.
- **Feel** a desire to be close to God.
- **Respond** by making a choice to put God first.
