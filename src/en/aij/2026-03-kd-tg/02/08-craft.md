**What you need:**

Week 2 craft template, patterned paper, scissors, glue, markers, ribbon. Optional: stickers.

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/02/craft-template.png)

```

**Preparation instructions:**

Cut a 3-3/4-inch by 3/4-inch (9.5 centimeters by 2 centimeters) rectangle and a 3-1/2-inch by 3-1/2-inch (9 centimeters by 9 centimeters) square out of patterned paper to fit the gift card.

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the pieces. Fold the bottom square up and the top rectangle down over the top to make a “gift box” shape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/02/2b.png)

2\. Glue the picture of baby Jesus inside. An adult can help write the memory verse or “Jesus is the best gift of all” on the blank section inside. Children can also decorate with stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/02/2c.png)

3\. Glue patterned paper to the front of the gift box. An adult can help tie a ribbon around it.
