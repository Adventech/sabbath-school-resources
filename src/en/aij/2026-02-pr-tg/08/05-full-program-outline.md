**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to complete a Joseph word search or build a pyramid. They may like to rotate through both options before Sabbath School begins.

**Word search:** How many words can they find relating to the story?

**Pyramid building activity:** Using plastic/paper cups, show them how to build a pyramid shape. Talk about how Joseph was taken as a slave to Egypt, where the Egyptians built amazing pyramids.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a picture-word matching game. Place the printed pictures on the floor or use the digital slideshow. Invite a child **to select an Attribute of God Card** (which declares a little of what He is like). They should then match the attribute to one of the pictures. (There are no wrong answers.)

When the children have matched all the words and pictures, point out that God is mighty and deserves honor. **Praise Him with a prayer of thanks** for being so good, kind, powerful, and wonderful in every way.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Discuss with the children how they can honor God—by spending time with Him, reading the Bible, praying, obeying their parents, visiting the sick, giving offerings to help the needy, choosing to do the right thing even when others around them don’t, etc. Write these ideas on a whiteboard or poster to refer to later. Invite two children to act out one idea from the list and see if anyone can guess what it is.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **Others may see that God is with me when I live to honor Him.** Our story today continues with Joseph. He chose to honor God, even in difficult times.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}}) Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** (Play some quiet music while the children do this activity.) **Draw a large heart** on your whiteboard or poster. Explain that Joseph chose to trust God with his whole heart.

**Hand out sticky notes and pens.** Ask the children to write answers to this question:

- **Why could Joseph trust God?**

Invite them to run and stick their sticky notes inside the large heart on the whiteboard when finished. Read out the answers and affirm them.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How was God faithful to Joseph’s big family?** (Abraham, Isaac, Jacob?)
- **How had God looked after Joseph?** (Kept him from being hurt by his brothers; kept him from being hurt or killed by Potiphar.)

Hand out one more sticky note to each child and ask them to respond to the question below, and then stick it on the large heart:

- **How did Joseph show he honored God?**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Joseph couldn’t bring himself to dishonor His loving God, or his master, by doing something he knew was wrong.

- **When we are pressured to do something wrong, what should we do?** (Ask the children to share their thoughts with the person beside them.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Divide the children into four groups. Place the peer pressure scenario cards in a basket in front of the room. Invite a child from each group to come to the front to choose one card (without looking at it), then take it back to their group to then show and discuss.

Give each group one to two minutes to talk about their scenario and their response to the situation. An adult helper can guide each group. Then invite one child from each group to share their scenario and the group’s response. Affirm answers.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Joseph faced pressure from Potiphar’s wife to do the wrong thing. If he’d given in, no one might have known. But Joseph knew that God would know, and He chose to honor God.

**We honor God and those around us when we choose to do what’s right—even when no one else is watching us.**

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 21: Joseph in Jail** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: **“Revelation 4:11.”**

Discuss:

- **Why is Jesus worthy to receive glory, honor, and power? What might glory, honor, and power look like as a gift?**
- **What do you admire and love about Jesus? Why does He deserve to receive glory, honor, and power/worship?** (Encourage children to spontaneously share their responses.)

**Memory Verse Challenge:** If time allows, write the memory verse on sticky notes (one word per note). Place the notes on the children’s heads. Then see if they can line up in the correct order of the memory verse. They will need to work together, without taking the word off their heads. For large classes, you may want to do this more than once and time the children to see how quickly they can complete the task. How did you show honor to God as you worked together to do this task? (Comment on encouraging words you heard the children say.)
