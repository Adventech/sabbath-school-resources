**Opening Activities**

**Scrap paper** to be scrunched into balls. Write “death” on each piece before scrunching. Store the balls in a **bag or container** then transfer to **Story Explorer Box** after the program has started.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a bag of **objects and pictures of things that are living and not living** (seeds, plants, pictures of animals, people, kitchen things, toys, etc.).

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: a simple **musical instrument** for each child. These could be purchased instruments (recorders, maracas, tambourines, etc.) or handmade (shakers made from an empty cardboard roll with dry beans inside and ends taped shut).

Source **peg people** (Jesus and Jairus) or a **felt set**, and the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a **large shield** made from a cardboard box and a marker. You may rest the shield under the **Story Explorer Box**.
