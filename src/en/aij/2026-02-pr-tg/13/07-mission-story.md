**Focus:** God notices our needs and provides.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Vanuatu** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Lana lives on an island in the nation of Vanuatu. Lana loves to share God’s love with others. Every week she, her parents, and her sister and brothers go to her father’s home village to share God’s love.

There weren’t any Adventists in her father’s village, so the family decided to start a Sabbath School. Father started a class for the adults, and Mother and Lana led the children’s group. They sang songs and listened to Bible stories. At first Lana helped her mother by leading the singing. Sometimes she told a Bible story. On days Mother couldn’t go to the village with the family, Lana led the entire children’s program.

“When we arrive in the village, the children come running to our cousin’s house, where we meet,” Lana says. “I welcome them, and we start singing. The children like the songs with actions the best. They sing lots of songs while more children arrive.

“After prayer we sometimes have a mission story, then I tell the Bible story,” Lana explains. “We use the new Bible story flipchart that the past Thirteenth Sabbath Offering helped to buy. The children love the pictures, and the older ones can read the Bible verse printed at the bottom. Many of the children don’t have a Bible of their own, so we review the Bible verse several times to help them remember it.”

The family prayed for supplies to make the Sabbath School more fun for the children. Then Mother found some Bible felts at a yard sale. She was excited! When the woman learned that Mother wanted to use the felts to teach children about Jesus, she gave Mother lots of books, craft items, pictures, and Bible coloring books too.

God notices the needs of everyone. Isn’t it wonderful how He provides for His children? Lana has been leading the Sabbath School for a year now, and she enjoys it. “I miss going when we can’t be there,” she says. “I learn a lot by teaching the children.”

- **How does this story remind you of this week’s Big Idea: God notices my needs?**
- **How can you help other people and give them what they need?** (When we do this, we share Jesus’ love with them.)
