Opening Activities

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Use **^[Storytelling Cube]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** from last week’s lesson.\
Source **sticky notes.**\
Update **Offering Chart.**\
Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
On small pieces of paper, write one of the following **wrong choice scenarios**. Create one scenario for each child.\
1\. Lying to your parents about homework\
2\. Breaking someone’s toy on purpose\
3\. Yelling at your parents\
4\. Hitting someone at school\
5\. Sneaking out of the house when you should be doing your chores\
6\. Stealing food from a classmate’s lunch box

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source **^[Picture PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.\
Write the following **Bible references** on the whiteboard to be looked up during the story parts: John 1:29; Genesis 4:3; Genesis 4:8.
Source two wrapped **baby dolls**. Make two altars from upside-down **boxes**. Write one of the following on each side of one box: I am sorry for my sins; Jesus would one day die like the lamb for my sins; I follow God’s instructions; I love God.\
Source a **toy lamb, twigs, cellophane** to look like fire, **fruit**, and **vegetables**.\
Print one copy of the **^[Lamb Cards]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**. Cut up the 12 lamb cards. Hide these around your Sabbath School room.
Source a **small plate, water, dried herbs** or **pepper**, and **dishwashing liquid**.
