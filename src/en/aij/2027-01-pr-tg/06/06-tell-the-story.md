**Big Idea:** God provides for my needs, so I can be content and grateful.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use the following props: **water bag/water bottle; two jugs—one with bitter water and the other with sweetened water; cups for each child; staff/stick**.
- Use your facial expressions and body language dramatically to express the range of emotions experienced by Moses and the grumbling Israelites.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to Exodus and Philippians and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It had been such a wonderful miracle! God’s people had walked through the Red Sea on dry land. God had delivered and saved them! The people sang praises to God as though they were a big, happy choir. They were safe! They were free! God was so good!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With hearts full of gratitude, the Israelites followed God’s cloud away from the Red Sea and out into the Wilderness of Shur. It was hot, dry, and sandy. On and on they walked. The cloud gave them shade, but they began to worry. There was no water anywhere. They were beginning to get very thirsty. What would they do?

Moses wasn’t worried. He followed God’s cloud, trusting Him with every step.

The Israelites went to bed thirsty. They woke up thirsty. They had been walking for three days without finding even a drop of water.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly a shout rang out from way up in the front: “Water! Water!” Everyone rushed toward the pool of water and stooped down to drink. But . . . yuck! Immediately they spat the water out. It was bitter—too bitter to drink! ^[(Give children a taste of your “bitter water.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Disappointed and angry, the people turned on Moses. They accused him of leading them to the bad water, forgetting that God’s presence in the cloud had led them! Frustrated, bitter words tumbled from their mouths as they grumbled and complained.

Moses did what they had forgotten to do. He cried out to God for help, and God told him exactly how to solve the problem.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He do? Read Exodus 15:25.** ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) (Moses threw a branch into the bitter water.)

^[(Throw your staff/stick on the ground.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people took a sip of the water. It was sweet and pure. God had given them miracle water! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Every man, woman, child, and animal drank and drank and drank. ^[(Give children a taste of your “sweetened water.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The water bags were filled ^[(water bag/bottle)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and the children set off again. As the donkeys clip-clopped along, the water splish-splashed inside the water bags. But after a long walk through the wilderness the water bags were empty. The people made their camp. Again there was no water—what would they do?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people rushed up to Moses and complained bitterly. They demanded, “Give us water, that we may drink” (Exodus 17:2).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What else did they say? Read Exodus 17:3.** (Moses had brought them out of Egypt to kill them all, and their animals.)

What a terrible thing to accuse Moses of. They were so angry that they even wanted to stone Him! He had been God’s faithful and patient leader, but they were so ungrateful and discontented. Again!

Moses was saddened by their bad attitude, so He did what He always did. He took the problem to his Best Friend, God. He prayed, and once again God had a solution.

Our loving and patient God was still trying to teach the Israelites to be content and grateful. Even as the people grumbled, God was solving the problem.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Where does the strength to be content and grateful come from? Read Philippians 4:11-13.** (Jesus Christ.)

Moses prayed, and God told him what to do. Moses quickly obeyed. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He grabbed his shepherd’s staff, gathered together some of the elders of Israel, and walked with them toward a large rock, where God had promised to be.

Here he followed the rest of God’s instructions: “You shall strike the rock, and water will come out of it, that the people may drink” (Exodus 17:6). ^[(Strike your “rock” with your stick.)({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Just like that, sparkling fresh water burst out of the rock. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people guzzled it, sipping and slurping. Yum! There was more than enough for the huge crowd of people and their thirsty cattle. Thank You, God!
