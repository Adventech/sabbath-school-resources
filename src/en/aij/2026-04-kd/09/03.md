The man with the sickness sat by the pool and watched the water carefully. He hoped to see the slightest ripple.

Suddenly a caring face bent right over in front of him. It was Jesus. “Do you want to be made well?” (John 5:6), Jesus asked kindly.

A flutter of hope filled the man’s heart. Was this kind Man offering to be a friend? Would this Man help him?

The sick man began to tell Jesus all about how helpless he felt. There was no one to help him into the pool. Someone else always got to the water before him.

Jesus listened patiently. Then, with excitement in His eyes, Jesus spoke again.

What did He say? **Read John 5:8.**

Immediately the man obeyed! With faith in his heart, he sprung to his feet. His legs stretched out straight. His arms felt strong. His whole body felt the buzz of health and energy.

He was so happy! His helpless and hopeless thoughts were changed into joy. God did care about him!

God cares about you too! He sent Jesus to save you. “For by grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God” (Ephesians 2:8).

The man rolled up his rug and tucked it happily under his arm. He turned back to thank his Healer. But the healed man looked left. He looked right. Where had Jesus gone?

The healed man hadn’t even had a chance to say a proper thank You. He didn’t even know the kind Stranger’s name! How could he ever find Jesus to thank Him?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you know that Jesus cares for you?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask a parent or caregiver to gently tie your feet together. Try running across the room (don’t hop or shuffle). Was that hard or easy? Untie your feet. Now try! Jesus can free us from our helpless situation.
