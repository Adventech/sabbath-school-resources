**Opening Activities**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Create a trail around the room with a long piece of **string**. At the end, place a selection of **books, puzzles,** or **other resources** of your choice.\
Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: something you can use for a **blindfold**.\
Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: world map, colored blocks, suitcase or bag, clothes, pillow, blanket, containers, green and blue fabric, toy fruit, plush animals.\
Reuse cardboard box altar from week 6, or source stones to stack.\
Make destination signs: “Ur,” “Haran,” “Shechem,” “Bethel,” and “Egypt.” Put these up in different areas around your Sabbath School room.\
Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: paper clips, a piece of cardstock, and a strong magnet.\
Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
