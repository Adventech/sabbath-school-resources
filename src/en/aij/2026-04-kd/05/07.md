How wonderful it is that God has given us five senses to experience the beautiful things He has created for us to enjoy. We can see, taste, touch, hear, and smell so many delightful things in our world!

Here are some interesting facts about our senses.

**Hear:** The smallest bone (stapes) and one of the hardest bones (temporal) can both be found in your ear.

**See:** Your eye is the fastest muscle in the body. That’s why we say, “In the blink of an eye!”

**Smell:** Everyone has a unique smell—just like a fingerprint. No two people smell the same, except for identical twins.

**Touch:** Your lips, tongue, and fingers are the most sensitive to touch.

**Taste:** You have taste buds in other places besides your tongue. There are also spots in your throat and nose that you taste with too.
