---
title: How to Create a Disciple-Making Plan
---

Making disciples is our mission. It’s at the heart of everything we do. Therefore, every church should develop an active and ongoing disciple-making plan. This _Global TMI Church Planner_ will guide you through this process. It will not provide you with an actual plan, but with a framework to help you create your own comprehensive plan in harmony with Christ’s method of making disciples.

1. ^[Start with prayer]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Ask God to give you and your church wisdom to develop a plan that involves the whole church, reaches the lost, makes active disciples, and glorifies Him.
2. ^[Plan ahead]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Don’t wait until January to begin working on your disciple-making plans for the year. Start with enough time to have your plan for the upcoming year completed before the year begins, with time to spare.
3. ^[Establish essential ministries]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). If your church has not already done so, appoint one or more members to lead every essential ministry in the five phases of disciple-making (see p. 4). Most phases will require the essential ministry leader(s) to collaborate with the pastor and other local leaders. In smaller churches, the personal ministries leader may be asked to lead multiple areas. Each ministry should be given a budget and be asked to submit a plan each year.
4. ^[Work as a team]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). As you prepare to create your church’s plan, one option is to ask the ministries in each area of disciple-making to recommend their own plans and then consolidate them into one. Alternatively, you could work on the entire plan together as a small team, or with the entire church board, but be sure essential ministry leaders are involved. If everyone gives input and feels part of the process, it will ensure buy-in and support.
5. ^[Review the sample plan]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). On pages 20 and 21, you will find a sample Disciple-Making Evangelism Plan. Review this plan to understand how to fill out the form and to gain ideas, but don’t feel you need to copy the sample when creating your plan. Also note that this sample plan provides only a broad overview. Before implementation, detailed plans for each activity would need developed.
6. ^[Use the blank planning form]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Use the blank form on pages 22 and 23 to outline your plans for the coming year. For many churches, this form will not provide enough space. An electronic version with ample space may be downloaded at [globaltmi.org](https://globaltmi.org). Alternatively, you may create your own form and follow the same categories and process. The blank version in this book, along with the two blank pages for notes, are there for you to begin brainstorming your ideas.
	- Complete both sections in each column. For each area of disciple-making ministry, include (a) plans to equip and involve members in personal labor, and (b) a list of church programs and events. Remember, the “best help” that pastors and local leaders can give our members “is not sermonizing, but planning work for them” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 6, p. 49).
	- ^[Gray Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Getting Ready:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#6D6E71", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Write down how and when you will emphasize spiritual revival, compile and review your interest list, and provide personal evangelism training.
	- ^[Blue Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Prepare:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) This category is for activities with the primary purpose of building relationships, not sharing truth.
	- ^[Brown Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Plant:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) This category is for activities that invite people to consider spiritual truth. Non-spiritual invitations (such as to a church social or cooking school) are not planting, but preparing.
	- ^[Green Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Cultivate:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3C8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) This vital category is for Bible studies that share our full doctrinal and prophetic message. Other Bible studies, while beneficial, would be considered planting rather than cultivating.
	- ^[Gold Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Harvest:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#EFAF44", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) In this category, list your plans to gain decisions and prepare people for baptism. In addition to traditional evangelistic meetings, a series of Bible studies may be considered harvesting when the frequency is increased (more than once per week) and baptismal appeals are made.
	- ^[Red Column—]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})^[Preserve:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) In this category, include plans to assign mentors and the curricula being used for discipleship studies. Include how you will nurture new members in spiritual habits, integrate them into the life of the church, and train them to make disciples.
7. ^[Evaluate each phase]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). After listing your plans, evaluate each column and strengthen any weak areas by adding
ministries or activities that address that phase of disciple-making. Pay special attention to the ^[plant]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), ^[cultivate]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3C8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), and
^[preserve]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) columns, which are often weak because they require more personal labor by members.
8. ^[Vote to approve and provide a calendar]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). After completing your Disciple-Making Evangelism Plan, the church board and/or the church at a business meeting should vote to approve it. In addition to the Disciple-Making Evangelism Planning Form, you may want to put your plans in a calendar format also and share these with your church family.
