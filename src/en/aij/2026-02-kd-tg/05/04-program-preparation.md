**Opening Activities**

Fill a large, **low container with water** and place on a table. Collect **leaves** of different shapes and sizes for children to make into boats. Make one or two as models. Try pushing the stem of one leaf through the middle of another leaf to make a boat with a sail. You may like to include **small twigs** and encourage experimentation.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box:** a clear **container of water**, **dishwashing liquid**, a **straw** (or a straw for each child), and a **tray** to catch the overflow.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: stick for staff, peg people (Jacob, Laban, Esau), glasses (or sunglasses with no lenses), an angel on a craft stick to fly around the children, and toy animals.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: glitter **play-dough** and peg people from story. You can add glitter to homemade or store-bought play-dough or buy premade glitter play-dough.
