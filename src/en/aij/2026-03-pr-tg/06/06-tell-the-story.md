**Big Idea:** God prepares hearts to receive Jesus.

**TELL THE STORY**

The desert was a wild place filled with sandy hills and rocky caves. There were no farms or gardens, people or houses. But there were beautiful sunsets, soaring birds, and chirping insects. It was peaceful and quiet. And it was John’s home.

John was Jesus’ cousin. God had called John to live in this wilderness. It was the perfect quiet place to prepare him for a very important job.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Do you know what job God had given John?**

He was to be a prophet to prepare the people’s hearts to love Jesus. Before his miracle birth, an angel had told John’s father Zacharias, “And he will turn many of the children of Israel to the Lord their God” (Luke 1:16).

Though he likely never met his cousin Jesus while growing up, John learned all about Him. His parents taught him that Jesus was the promised Messiah. When the time was right, John was to tell everyone about Him ^[(show toy figurine)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In the peaceful desert John searched the Bible scrolls ^[(show rolled-up paper scrolls)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He learned all He could about the promised Messiah. He also had plenty of quiet time to pray and listen to God’s voice. He could do God’s work only with the help of the Holy Spirit.

Long before John was born, God had told prophet Isaiah about John.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he say John would do? Read Isaiah 40:3-5.** (He was to “prepare the way of the Lord.”)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DO:** Divide the children into two teams and give each a rectangular table to work on. Give straws to each team member. Two people on each team will be the “Voice” and all others on the team the “Path Clearers.” The goal of the Path Clearers is to blow away all the rubbish on the “path” (which is the table) so the Voice can blow a ping-pong ball smoothly from one end of the table to the other end without it falling off the sides. Before they start, scatter paper or trash on the table. See which team can get their ping-pong ball to the other end first.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Resources:** two ping-pong balls, straws for each participant, dry lightweight rubbish, two rectangular tables, or other flat surface for the race.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Ask the Path Clearers: **What was your job?**

Isaiah 40:3-5 described John’s mission as clearing a path for the Lord, like removing obstacles to make a road smooth. John understood that his calling was to prepare people’s hearts to hear, love, and obey Jesus.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What obstacles might the people have had in their hearts?** (Pride, selfishness, doubt, greed, jealousy, etc.) They needed the Holy Spirit to show them their need to say they’re sorry for their sins and want to change with God’s help.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the time came, John began preaching by the Jordan River. People rushed there to hear him speak. They were amazed by this man in the desert. He wore clothes made from camel hair ^[(show rough cloth)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and preached with power. With a mighty voice He cried out to people to change—the Messiah was coming!

“Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2).

The people were shocked by his message. Some wondered if he was a prophet, raised from the dead. Others thought he might be the Messiah. John told them, “He who is coming after me is mightier than I!” (Matthew 3:11).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Repent,” cried John. That word stuck in many people’s hearts like a sharp arrow in a target. Repent. The Holy Spirit touched their hearts, and many people realized John’s words were true. Their hearts were selfish, proud, and full of sin. They were sinners in need of a Savior.

Many, many people responded to God by saying they were sorry and letting Him take away their sins.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) John invited the people who were sorry to step into the river Jordan. There he baptized them.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What is baptism, and why do people do it?** (Full immersion beneath the water as a public demonstration of commitment to God.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Would you like to be baptized one day to choose God for yourself and to let others know that you’ve made this choice?**

When the people were baptized, it showed their need for God and their faith in His power to forgive them. One by one John placed the people under the water as their “old” selves and they came up spiritually “new” and “clean.

The people were so happy! They were ready for a better life with God. “What should we do?” they asked John (Luke 3:10).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did John, who was now called John the Baptist, say? Read Luke 3:11.** (“If you have two shirts, share with the person who does not have one. If you have food, share that too” [ICB]).

He told the tax collectors not to be greedy, the soldiers not to be violent, and the grumblers to be happy with their pay. The people nodded. They had been forgiven by their loving God! They wanted to live kind and good lives.

One day John looked up to see Jesus making His way toward the river. The Holy Spirit showed him that here was the Messiah He had been preaching about. Excitedly he cried out: “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” (John 1:29).

John wanted everyone to notice Jesus! Jesus asked John to baptize Him. John shook his head. Jesus had no sin—how could he, a sinner, baptize Jesus? But Jesus told him, “We should do all things that are right” (Matthew 3:15, ICB). Jesus would be our example. John baptized Jesus while everyone watched.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After His baptism Jesus stepped from the water and prayed. Suddenly God’s brilliant light surrounded Him. The Holy Spirit, in the form of a beautiful white dove, came down and rested on His shoulder.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Then God spoke from heaven. What did God say? Read Matthew 3:17.** (“This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased.”) **Invite the children to repeat these words with you in reverence:** Jesus was God’s beloved Son, and God was pleased with His baptism.
