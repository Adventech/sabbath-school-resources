Many people had been given the opportunity to believe in Jesus that day—all because a Roman soldier acted on his faith.

The centurion could have sat at home and done nothing. He could have told himself that God might not listen to his request because he was a foreigner. James 2:17 says, “Thus also faith by itself, if it does not have works, is dead.” The centurion’s faith was far from dead! He put his faith into action: he asked the Jewish elders to talk to Jesus about healing his servant; he sent his friends to talk to Jesus; and he asked Jesus himself.

Jesus praised and responded to the centurion’s faith because it was alive, not dead. Jesus did this many times, as He helped and healed the people who had put their faith in Him. When a faith-filled blind man cried out, Jesus said, “Your faith has made you well” (Mark 10:52). When a woman with a troubled past poured oil over His feet to anoint Him before He died, He forgave her sins and said, “Your faith has saved you. Go in peace” (Luke 7:50). We can be sure He is delighted by our growing faith and will respond to us, too.
