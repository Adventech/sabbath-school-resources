Joseph watched his brothers closely as they ate, laughed, and talked together. It really looked like they had changed. He would find out for sure.

Joseph whispered to his most trusted servant, “Fill the men’s sacks with as much grain as they can carry. And put each man’s money into his sack with the grain” (Genesis 44:1, ICB). Then he told the servant to put one more thing in Benjamin’s sack. What was it? **Read Genesis 44:2.**

As soon as the sun rose, the brothers set off for home with heavy sacks and happy hearts. But wait, what’s that noise? The brothers spun around to see the governor’s servant galloping fast towards them. Oh, no! What was wrong?

The servant frowned as he pulled his horse to a sudden stop. He accused them of stealing the governor’s silver cup.

The brothers shook their heads. They didn’t steal his cup! Quickly they let down their sacks to prove it. Starting with Reuben, the servant searched each sack until he came to Benjamin’s. There was the silver cup!

Shocked and scared, the brothers were led back to Joseph’s house. Bravely Judah stepped forward. He told Joseph all about his father’s love for Benjamin. This was a big mix-up! But he would take the blame. He would become a slave, so Benjamin could go free. What a loving thing to do!
