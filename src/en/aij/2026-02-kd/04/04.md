Jacob was mad! He stared angrily at Uncle Laban, waiting for his answer. Why had his uncle tricked him?

Uncle Laban looked calmly at Jacob. He said that it was right for the older sister, Leah, to get married before the younger sister, Rachel. Jacob blinked with surprise. He knew it wasn’t right to lie and trick!

What does the Bible say about lying? **Read Leviticus 19:11.**

Deep down, Laban knew it wasn’t right either. But he was greedy. He wanted to trap Jacob into working even longer, for free. He said, “I will give you Rachel to marry also. But you must serve me another seven years” (Genesis 29:27, ICB).

Another seven years? Could Jacob really do his best and work seven more years for a man who had lied and tricked him?

Jacob took a deep breath and nodded. Yes, he would marry Rachel, and then he would work for Uncle Laban for another seven years with no pay. He would do the right thing for Leah and Rachel, even though his uncle had chosen to do the wrong thing. Having two wives was not part of God’s plan.

One week later, Jacob was finally married to Rachel, the one he loved. Then back to work he went. Jacob knew God loved to see His children do their best, so he worked hard and with honesty. And God blessed Jacob for his honest work.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#", "size": "lg"}}})

- How should you act when things aren’t fair?
- What can you learn from Jacob?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you do the right thing even when things seem unfair.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#", "size": "lg"}}})\
Make yourself one “not-fair” challenge to do with a smile. It’s not easy, but you can do it! Choose to do a job when it’s not your turn or clean something up when you didn’t make the mess. Others may not notice, but God does, and He will be smiling with you.
