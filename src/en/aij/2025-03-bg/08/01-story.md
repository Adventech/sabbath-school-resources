God’s Home\
^[Is Finished]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#AD3B5E", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/08/01.png)

At last God’s home, the tabernacle, was finished! _(Clap hands, yay!)_ It was beautiful. All the gold had been polished. _Rub, rub, rub._ All the bronze and silver had been polished too. _Rub, rub, rub._ God’s home sparkled. God’s home shined. But best of all, God’s home was right in the middle of camp.\
Moses smiled. He looked at the pretty curtains gently blowing in the breeze. He checked the shiny altar _(point to altar)._ He saw the beautiful golden lampstand. He looked at the beautiful golden ark with the two angels on top. He saw all of the furniture and smiled again. Everything was just right. It was all just as God had said. Moses thanked the people. They had hammered and sewed. They had carried and helped. He was proud of them for working together for God. Now it was time for God to move in!
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/08/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#25408E"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#E4F5FCad"}}}
The people watched and waited. _Shh! What would God do?_ God’s special cloud began to move! It floated closer . . . closer . . . closer. Then the cloud moved right over and inside the tabernacle. God was in His new home! The people cried happy tears. Their hearts were so full of joy! They whispered prayers of thanks that God had decided to live so close to them.\
When the cloud lifted from God’s home, the people knew it was time to pack up and start walking. _Follow, follow, follow._ If the cloud was still, they stayed in their camp. The cloud floated above God’s home during the day. At night a fire glowed inside the cloud so that all of God’s people could see it. They never felt alone—they knew God was right there with them.

God loved His people so much that He wanted to live right in the middle of their camp. God wants to be close to you too. He wants to live in your heart and be with you all day—every day!\
Thank You, Jesus, for wanting to be close to me. _(Say together)_ Please make Your home in my heart!
