**Word of the Week**

**Shine:** The moon shines in the night sky. When something shines, it helps us to see the things around us. When we have God’s love in our hearts, we will want to show it—we’ll shine like bright lights. We can show God’s love to others by being kind, happy, and caring.

**Lesson Summary**

Naomi and her family moved from Bethlehem to Moab to escape a famine. In Moab the people worshipped idols, but the family stayed true to God. Naomi’s husband died, but God was with Naomi. Her two sons married local women, Ruth and Orpah, and the family was happy once more. Naomi showed her love for God to her family and was like a shining light for God. Sadly, Naomi’s sons died. God sent good news that the famine in her homeland had lifted. Naomi decided to return, and Ruth and Orpah decided to go with her. Naomi left with the young women, then thought it kinder to send them home to their families. Orpah returned home, but Ruth did not. Naomi had shown her God’s love. She chose to follow Naomi and her God always.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God’s love lives in their hearts and shines out to others.
- **Feel** that God is close to them in happy times and sad times.
- **Respond** by asking God to help them show His shining love to others.
