**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Welcome** each child by name as they arrive at Sabbath School.

- Ask: **“What kind things did you do for others this past week?”**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to play “Guess My Job.” Whisper a simple occupation to each child: a painter, sportsperson, cook, etc. Then give each child a random prop that relates to a different job. To the painter, give a tennis racket; to the cook, a paintbrush; to the sportsperson, an apron and wooden spoon. Ask one child at a time to act out the job you whispered to them (even though they have an incorrect prop), while the other children guess.

Share that in today’s story someone dressed up as someone they weren’t. It was a big lie that made everything mixed up and everyone sad. Today we’ll learn that God can help us to be honest with our words and actions.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box**, revealing **two colored pieces of paper** and a **glue stick**.

Hold up one of the colored pieces of paper, pointing out that it is smooth and has no tears or rips. Tear up the other piece of paper in front of the children and place all the pieces on the floor. Ask the children to help you glue the torn pieces back together so it looks like the untorn paper.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What did the paper look like when we tore it up into pieces?** (Messy.)
- **When we tried to put it back together, was it easy or hard?**
- **What do you think the word “honest” means?** (Telling the truth, saying right words, not telling a lie or cheating.)

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}}) Teaching Point:** When we don’t tell the truth, it hurts people and makes them feel sad or angry. Just like all the torn-up pieces of paper, when we lie everything becomes very messy and ruined. When we try to piece it back together and make it right, it can sometimes be really hard!

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He helps me to be honest.** Our story this week is about someone who lied with his words and actions. He wasn’t honest, he was dishonest, and it caused a lot of trouble for everyone.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **blue balloon** and a **yellow balloon** (or two different colors). Invite the children to sit with you on the floor.

Explain that when we’re sneaky and tell lies, it makes God feel very sad, and it also makes us feel horrible inside! The feelings inside get bigger and bigger! (Blow up the blue balloon in front of the children. Let the balloon go, so that it flies around the room, to illustrate what happens when we tell lies.) Things get out of control!

Hold up your Jacob and Esau peg people from the story. Ask:
- Who was hurt by lying? (Both of them! Their mommy and daddy were also hurt, because Esau was mad and Jacob had to run away. When we lie, it hurts God, us, and those around us.)

Blow up the yellow balloon. Carefully tie it and draw a heart on it. Explain that when God lives inside our hearts, He helps us to speak honest, true words. The Bible tells us that God cannot lie. The ninth commandment is “You must not tell lies” (Exodus 20:16, ICB), because telling the truth is so important to God. The Bible says that Satan is the big liar, and all lies come from him (father of lies). He wants us to tell lies because he knows it will make our lives sad and hard. But God is so kind. He forgives us when we say we are sorry, and He always help us when we ask Him to, because He wants us to tell the truth.
- How can we be like the yellow balloon this week? (Ask God to help us to always tell the truth. We can say honest words and be truthful with our actions.)

Repeat **Big Idea** together: God is with me. He helps me to be honest.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold Up **Nature Card 15: Horse** and share the following facts:

- Horses are big, strong animals with four legs, a long tail, and a mane.
- Because horses are gentle, strong, smart, and honest, they make great friends for people.
- Horses have different ways of taking steps. The slowest is a walk. As the steps get faster, the horse trots, then canters, then gallops.
- **Horses are honest with their actions.** They don’t pretend. They will show exactly how they feel about someone. When a horse likes you, it will lower its head, blow or snort softly, follow you, or nuzzle you.

Give each child **Nature Card 15: Horse** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}}) Share the mission story**: “The Honest Brothers.”
