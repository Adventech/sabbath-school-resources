Long ago, in 1845, a retired sea captain named Joseph Bates read about the seventh-day Sabbath. He had always worshipped on Sunday, for God’s truth about the Sabbath had been lost over time, just as it had in Jesus’ day. What Joseph read was new and surprising to him.

Captain Bates studied all the Bible texts he could find about the Sabbath and its importance. After studying on his own, he traveled from Massachusetts to New Hampshire in America to learn more about the Sabbath. He soon accepted this Bible truth.

Upon returning home, he was greeted by a friend as he crossed an old bridge. His friend asked, “What’s the news, Captain Bates?”

Bates answered, “The news is that the seventh day is the Sabbath!” Captain Bates shared more of the Bible truth about the Sabbath with his friend. It wasn’t long before his friend became a believer and kept the Sabbath as well.

The next year Captain Bates wrote a booklet about the Bible Sabbath. He didn’t stop there. He decided to keep sharing the truth about God’s love, and the Sabbath He gave to us in love. He wrote: “It is my earnest desire to spend the remainder of my days in the service of God.”

Like Captain Bates, we can’t keep God’s love to ourselves, just as we can’t keep the blessings of the Sabbath to ourselves. God’s love is a generous, giving, sharing love.
