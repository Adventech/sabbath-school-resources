Jacob’s head bent low as he prayed to His peace-giving God. The sun set, but Jacob kept on praying. As he spoke to God, a great idea came into his mind. He had sent a peaceful message to Esau, now he would send a peaceful gift. And not just one gift—lots and lots of gifts! He would send them one after another, like the longest line of birthday presents ever. Esau would get a present, then another present . . . and another present.

Esau stared in surprise as a gift of goats trotted toward his camp with a servant. Then came a herd of goats . . . then rams . . . then plodding camels . . . bulls . . . donkeys . . . and finally, foals! Esau must have laughed with delight!

Each time a servant brought a new animal gift to Esau they said, “These animals belong to your servant Jacob. He sent them as a gift to you my master, Esau. And Jacob also is coming behind us” (Genesis 32:18, ICB).

What did Jacob hope would happen? **Read Genesis 32:20.**

While his servants were giving the gifts, Jacob stayed in camp praying that God would fill Esau’s heart with peace and forgiveness. Praying for Esau was even more generous than giving him animals! The Bible says, “Give, and you will receive . . . The way you give to others is the way God will give to you” (Luke 6:38, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- When did you last give someone a gift? What did they say and do?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
