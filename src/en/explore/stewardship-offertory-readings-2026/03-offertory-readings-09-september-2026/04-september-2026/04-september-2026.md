---
title: Timeless Truths
date: 26/09/2026
---

An elderly man once said, “Most of the wisdom that people know when they’re 70 can’t actually be put into words.” One of the joys of aging is that, over the years, we can see a build up of evidence for certain timeless truths.

Truths such as:

- “Prevention is better than a cure.”
- “Money can’t buy happiness.”
- “Every meal either harms or heals you.”
- “God’s ways are the best ways always.”

We usually hear these truths in our youth, but they seem too unbelievable or countercultural to be accurate, so we decide to live in the opposite direction for a time, just to test the truths. After a time, we realize that they really are true and that living by the truth can set us free.

But sometimes, these timeless truths seem to go against all common sense. For example, “It is more blessed to give than to receive.” Really? Hadn’t the author ever sat around a sparkling Christmas tree waiting to receive an expensive gift? No, they hadn’t. It was actually the Christ of Christmas who said it in Acts 20:35.

After decades of observing life, older Christians will tell you that there is overwhelming evidence that it’s not what we hold but what we share that makes us rich.

It truly is “more blessed to give than to receive.”

### Appeal

As we return our tithe and offerings to God today, let’s thank Him for the timeless truths in the Bible and for His Holy Spirit of Truth, who guides us and provides for our needs.
