[[Revelation 20:1-3]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The “bottomless pit” (Greek abysson) is here a reference to the now-decimated earth (see Jeremiah 4:23-26, which foreshadows this time). The seven last plagues have done their work.
- Satan is now bound to the earth. He will have no humans to tempt (all are either dead or in heaven), and no ability to leave. He will have 1,000 years—a millennium—to consider the apocalyptically evil choices he has made.
- This millennium begins at the second coming of Christ, when He will take His faithful followers to be with Him in heaven.

[[Revelation 20:4]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- While Satan is confined to the earth, the followers of Christ will have “judgment . . . committed to them” in heaven. God will open the records in which all the events of earth’s history are recorded. He of course already knows who has been saved and who has been lost, and God’s people will not change those decisions. Rather, the “judgment” done by God’s followers during the millennium is simply reviewing God’s judgments, confirming to their own satisfaction that His decisions are just, good, and loving.

[[Revelation 20:5-6]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- There are two resurrections of the dead. The first is for Christ’s followers. They will be raised back to life at His second coming. The second resurrection is for the wicked, at the end of the millennium. They will be raised back to life briefly at the same time Satan is released, as the following verses describe.

[[Revelation 20:7-10]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Even after 1,000 years, Satan has not changed. He gathers the wicked for one last attack. But fire comes down and destroys them all—this time forever.
- Verse 10 states that the torment will last “day and night forever and ever.” But this does not mean “forever and ever” in the sense of being everlasting. The fire will eventually go out, and neither Satan nor his followers will be alive anymore in any sense of the word.
- To see why this is so, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r20/)**.

[[Revelation 20:11-15]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Verses 11-15 again take a step back in time and give an overview of the final judgment. John here reminds us that anyone whose name was not found in the book of life would be cast into the lake of fire.
- The question “What will you do with Jesus?” is therefore the most important question a person can ever answer. We encourage you to choose to follow Jesus while His offer of mercy is still so freely given!
- Chapter 20 shows the result of the great battle of Armageddon. The unholy trinity, spiritual Babylon, and all who followed them will be completely destroyed. Evil will not rise a second time.
- After this final judgment it is time for God’s new world to begin!
