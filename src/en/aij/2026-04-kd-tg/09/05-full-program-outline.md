**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a peg person (man) inside a plastic bottle. Explain that someone has pushed the peg person into the bottle and it’s stuck. Shake it and turn it upside down to try to get it out. It seems impossible.

- **What can we do to help this peg person?**

Take some scissors and carefully cut the top of the bottle. The peg person can now be lifted out. Invite the children to clap. Jesus loves to help us when we feel stuck. Jesus can save us—something we can’t do for ourselves. Share that in today’s story, Jesus met a man who really needed Him. Let’s find out what Jesus did.

Today’s story will teach us our **Big Idea: “Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He wants to save me** [clasp hands together]**.”**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Pick Up Your Mat and Walk!”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a stack of dish towels or rags. Hand them out to each child as a “mat,” like the man in our story. Invite the children to sit down in a circle on their “mat” and pretend they can’t move. Then, when you say, “Get up, pick up your mat, and walk!” have the children jump up, take their “mats” to a tub or bag, and return to their seats.

- **How do you think the man felt when no one would help him into the pool?** (Sad, helpless.)
- **Why do you think he obeyed Jesus and jumped up?** (Jesus cared for Him; he trusted Jesus.)
- **Do you think Jesus cares for you when you feel sad or hurt?** (Yes! He loves to help you.)
- **The man was so happy—He had been saved! He thanked Jesus. What do you want to thank Jesus for today?**

**Teaching Point:** Jesus cares for you, and you, and you (say the children’s names). He knows when we feel hurt or sad. Jesus wants to save us from sin, just as He saved this man. The man didn’t do anything—Jesus did it all. It’s the same with us too. Jesus is the only one who can save us and help us to live by His ways of love.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He wants to save me** [clasp hands together]**.”** Invite the children to place their hand on their heart as they say “Thank You, Jesus.”

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 48: Abyssal Octopus** and share the following facts:

- The abyssal octopus has a soft body with eight long squiggly arms called tentacles.
- It lives deep, deep down in the world’s oceans.
- It lays a big number of eggs once in its lifetime: around 80,000.
- While a mother octopus protects her eggs, she will hardly move and does not eat for four years.

**Teaching Point:** Just as the mother octopus will do everything she possibly can to save her babies from harm and give them the best life, Jesus has done everything to save us so we can be with Him forever.

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 48: Abyssal Octopus** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Walking Finger Puppet.** Invite the children to make a walking (puppet) man to remind them how Jesus helped the man in our lesson story. As the children finish making their craft, invite them to tell the story to a friend and make the man “walk.” When time is up, they can make their finger puppet man “leap” back to the seating area.

Invite the children to crouch down low, and if they choose to, jump up and repeat your callout: “I choose to let Jesus help me and save me.”
