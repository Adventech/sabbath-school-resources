What would you do if Jesus knocked on your front door today? Would you ask Him to come back later? Would you politely host Him as you would a stranger, or would you invite Him to help himself to the fridge and the comfy couch, as you would a dear, faithful friend?

There was no room for Jesus in Bethlehem. But He wants you to make room in your heart for Him.

If there are things that are stopping you from spending time with God, let them go. Create a new routine. Ask God to show you what you need to give up in order to be totally free for Him.

Find a time that works best to enjoy quiet time with Jesus. Commit to making this a habit every day. Pray for God to take center-stage in your life.

Jesus doesn’t want to only be included in our lives during our devotion time—He wants a place in our hearts all day, every day! When you are happy—praise Him! When you are worried or frustrated—come to Him with your burdens. Talk with Him throughout your day. Sing to Him in the car. Let your everyday life be an offering to Him.

Jesus says, “Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me” (Revelation 3:20).
