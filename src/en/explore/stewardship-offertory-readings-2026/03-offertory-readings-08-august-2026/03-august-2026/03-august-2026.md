---
title: Survivor's Guilt
date: 15/08/2026
---

Every tragedy has victims and survivors.

Maybe your taxi was late, causing you to miss the plane that crashed and killed everyone on board.

Maybe you live on a hill, safe from the flooding that destroyed all the homes that you can see, day after day, from your front yard.

Maybe a week after the storm, you fall to your knees in tears as you mow the lawn of your perfectly pristine home while viewing the rubble where your neighbor’s house once stood.

Maybe you moved your family to another country just before a war broke out in the country you moved from.

Surviving a tragedy or a traumatic event can cause us to believe that we’ve done something wrong because we were unharmed when others were hurt or killed. Survivor’s guilt is real. In a world where violence is common and natural and man-made disasters are so prevalent, what should the Christian survivor’s response be?

If you feel blessed, even unfairly blessed, I encourage you to praise God for your blessings and then pass them on. Feed, clothe, visit, shelter, and love the victims and their families. As Jesus said in Acts 20:35, “It is more blessed to give than to receive.”

You survived for a purpose. Go out in God’s strength and fulfill that purpose.

### Appeal

As you gratefully return God’s 10% tithe and give your offerings today, consider ways to minister to people who may not feel as blessed as you are. Find someone who has suffered from a great loss and give them the comfort that God gave you in your times of loss. Thank you for your generosity.
