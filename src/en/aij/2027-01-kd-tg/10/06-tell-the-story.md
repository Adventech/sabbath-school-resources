**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. He teaches me to love others.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Giant cardboard heart from last week with 1-4 stuck on it; large cardboard numbers 5-10; Happy Heart posters for each station (on floor or wall); sticky tac or similar.
- The following materials for each commandment activity:

**Station 5: Small pile of clothing to fold/toys/toy container**.\
**Station 6: Paper cards for words; precut hand shapes** (enough to cover words); **marker; tape or glue**.\
**Station 7:** Enough **paper hearts** cut in two, for each child, **tape on floor**.\
**Station 8: Toy, book, and ball**.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY and STORY EXPLORER**

The Israelites were very still at the base of Mount Sinai. They could see God’s mighty presence as it covered the mountain with a cloud and fire. They had listened to God’s powerful voice tell them how to show God they loved Him. Then He shared important commandments with them about how to love each other. These rules would keep them happy, safe, and well.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Hold the special class Bible. Turn to Exodus 20 and show the children exactly where these commandments are written. Read each commandment from the Bible and explain it using the **Teaching Point** below. Then hold up the commandment number and invite a child to place it on one of the six Happy Heart stations around your space, matching with the small numbers on posters.)

No. 5. “Honor your father and your mother, that your days may be long upon the land which the Lord your God is giving you” (Exodus 20:12).

God has given us parents to protect, love, and guide us. We should always be loving and respectful to our mommies and daddies. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 6. “You shall not murder” (NKJV), which means “Do not hurt others” (Exodus 20:13). God never wants us to kill, hate, or hurt others. God wants us to show His love and kindness with every word, thought, and action. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 7. God said that we must keep marriage pure and true (see Exodus 20:14). He wants every marriage to be filled with love, and He wants it to last forever. God never, ever breaks His promises. He wants us to keep our marriage promises true too. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 8. “You shall not steal” (Exodus 20:15). Stealing is taking things that don’t belong to the person who takes them. Some people steal because they want to impress others, or to try to find happiness from things. God gives us everything we have. He doesn’t want us to take other people’s things. He knows that stealing hurts others and won’t make you happy. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 9. “You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor” (Exodus 20:16). God wants His people to always love and respect each other by telling the truth. Lies will only grow into a big, hurtful mess. We should always tell the truth, no matter what. Don’t speak part of the truth, or twist the truth, even if you get into trouble. The truth is always best. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 10. “You shall not covet” (Exodus 20:17). To covet means to want something that belongs to someone else. God wants us to be thankful for the things He has given us. He knows we will have joy when we are content and thankful. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Explain to your class that they’re going to think about these commandments a little bit more. Lead the group to six Happy Heart stations. Have a helper ring your bell or sound maker when it’s time to move to the next one. Don’t spend more than two minutes at each.)

**Happy Heart Spot 5: Honor Your Parents**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Instruct children to demonstrate honoring parents by helping—picking up toys or folding a small towel and placing it in a basket. You may like to set a timer to see how quickly it can be done.)

**Teaching Point:** Love and obey your parents. Be kind and listen to the grown-ups who care for you.

**Happy Heart Spot 6: No Hurting Others**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Show a display of words: “Hit,” “Hate,” “Push,” “Yell,” “Hurt,” “Pinch.” Have children tape/stick precut hand shapes on top of these words. Share that God wants us to make good choices with our hands. We can use our actions to show love instead. Write on the hands: “Kind,” “Gentle,” “Loving,” “Forgiving,” “Patient,” “Peaceful.”)

**Teaching Point:** We shouldn’t have hateful thoughts in our hearts or minds. God said, “Do not kill.” We should help others to feel safe, loved, and cared for.

**Happy Heart Spot 7: Keep Marriage Special**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Put twin heart-puzzle pieces on the floor [separated] and have children find one, then find the matching half. When they have a whole heart, they can carefully walk across the room, walking carefully on a line on the floor to remind us of the care and faithfulness of marriage.)

**Teaching Point:** God made a mommy and a daddy to fit together in marriage. He wants us to be careful to keep our promises, just as He has. When we promise to love and be with our marriage partner forever, we should keep that promise.

**Happy Heart Spot 8: Don’t Steal**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite two children to come and hold a book, a ball, and a toy. Ask them to pretend to use it. Invite three other children to help role-play three situations. Whisper instructions to them. One adult can sneakily “steal” the ball. Another can nicely ask to borrow the toy, play with it, then return it. The third can ask to borrow the book, but then leave it somewhere else and not return it. Briefly talk about each situation.)

**Teaching Point:** Don’t steal, be honest and fair, and always return what isn’t yours.

**Happy Heart Spot 9: Always Tell the Truth**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Select from the following **scenarios** as time permits. Children will stand up if true, or sit down if a lie.)

- Amina said she brushed her teeth, but her toothbrush was dry.
- Yuki said his dog ate his homework, but he doesn’t have a dog.
- Leila broke her new school bag and told her mom right away.
- Mia told her dad she felt scared about her first day at school.
- Niko said he didn’t eat the cookies, but there were cookie crumbs on his face.
- Soren said he flew to the moon last night. (This is useful for teaching about imagination versus what is real.)

**Teaching Point:** Tell the truth. Use your words to build trust and show love.

**Happy Heart Spot 10: Be Thankful, Not Jealous**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Children to think of one thing that they are thankful for [e.g., family, food, toys, pets]. Invite them to find a partner and whisper this to each other. Children can then find another partner and whisper two things they are thankful for. Then they can find another partner and whisper three things they’re thankful for!)

**Teaching Point:** God gives us so many reasons to be thankful. We can be happy with what we have, and be happy for the good things that others have too. Thank God for your blessings instead of wishing for what others have.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Ask the children to return to their seats. Then invite six children to take the number from each station and stick it up on your giant heart at the front.)

**Teaching Point:** God was so kind to give us these six commandments to show us ways we can love others. When we are kind, respectful, and caring to each other, everyone will be happier. Invite the children to repeat your **Big Idea: God’s way is best. He teaches me to love others.**
