A Happy Home

On a quiet road in the town of Bethany sat a small, simple house. It was probably just like all the other homes nearby, with a flat roof. But this simple home was one of Jesus’ favorite places to visit. It was the home of His three friends: Mary, Martha, and Lazarus.

“Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus” (John 11:5). Their home was a restful, peaceful place for Jesus.

It’s always good to visit a happy home. The Bible says that a home that is good and pleasant, is a home where God’s people live together in . . . (find the missing word in Psalm 133:1).

Lazarus, Mary, and Martha were a peaceful family. But they weren’t perfect. Mary and Martha sometimes disagreed, like sisters do. But Jesus helped them to make peace. Being with Him had changed them.

They loved Jesus and treasured His words. Their home was filled with kind and caring words and actions. Proverbs 16:24 says that “Pleasant words are like a honeycomb. They make a person happy” (ICB).

It is no wonder Jesus enjoyed visiting His friends at their house—especially when He was extra tired.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Which friends do you like to visit?
- What can you do this week to help make your home a peaceful and happy place?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Rub your hands together. Did you feel them get hot? This is called friction. Unkind words and actions are like this—they create heat and anger. God wants us to be peacemakers in our home. Ask Him to help you choose kind words and be patient.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to make your home happy and peaceful.
