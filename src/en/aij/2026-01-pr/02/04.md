When “God created man in His own image” (Genesis 1:27), one of the special things He gave humans was an amazing mind that worked better than the mind of anything He had created so far. His plan was to place the image of a loving, giving God inside of all people, forever.

God had made many animals intelligent and able to communicate. Dolphins, for example, can work together to hunt and play. Some animals even use shells as tools. But our brains are much larger. We can think things through, and plan, create, imagine, share, and solve problems (such as tricky math puzzles). Our many languages are very different and beautiful. How many ways can you say hello?

Humans can also think about the long-term future (if you’ll travel, take up a hobby or sport, do a job, or have a family). No animal can plan for the long-term future like people can.

But what makes us unique is that we are made in God’s image. That’s why God gave Adam the job of naming all the animals and the even more important job of looking after creation. Humans can also pray to God, as well as worship and serve Him.

What can we say to God, knowing that we have amazing minds that can think and love in special ways? Read Psalm 139:14.

God loves us so much that He gave us a mind to think and a heart to love.
It’s important to remember that God gave us our amazing minds, and we should praise Him for it.
