Jacob had thought about getting the birthright for a very long time and now it was his! He thought he would feel so happy he’d be jumping up and down. But he didn’t. He just felt bad, sad, and sorry all at the same time.

Jacob hid in the shadows. He watched Esau shaking his fist in the air and shouting angry words. He shouldn’t have lied. He had hurt Esau. He had hurt his father, Isaac, and most importantly he had hurt God. What a mess!

Rebekah was sad and sorry too. Her lies had caused so much hurt in her family. If only she had trusted God to do things at the right time, in the right way!

What does the Bible say we need to do when we trust God? **Read Psalm 27:14.**

With a sorry heart, Rebekah asked God to forgive her. God always loves to forgive. But angry Esau was not ready to forgive.

Then one day, Rebekah heard some terrible news. Quickly she sent for Jacob. “Listen,” she whispered, “your brother Esau is . . . planning to kill you” (Genesis 27:42, ICB).

Oh, no! Mother and son hugged each other tightly as tears spilled down their cheeks. It was no longer safe for Jacob to live at home. He must run far away!
