**Focus:** God is powerful to protect and save.

**Have you ever been outside in a cloud you couldn’t see through? How did you feel?**

Let’s learn more about this type of cloud.

What am I? (Invite children to put their hand up when they know. Wait until the end to ask a few different children, then reveal the answer.)

I’m a cloud that either forms near the ground or drops down to touch the earth.

In order for me to form, there needs to be water in the air, a gentle breeze, and both warm and cold air. For instance, a cool breeze passing over the warm water of a swamp will create me, just as warm air over the cold sea will cause me to appear.

The breeze spreads me around.

When the sun comes out, I evaporate and disappear quickly.

In many places where there isn’t much rain, I help by bringing moisture to the plants.

I can make sound seem muffled and strange.

I can be beautiful as I creep up valleys or across the plains.

I can make a familiar place seem strange and hard to recognize.

I can be an inconvenience, because pilots can’t see to take off or land their planes in me.

It can also be dangerous for people to drive or go tramping in me.

What am I?

```=Answer

I am fog.

```

Fog is just one type of cloud that God created. He made clouds to protect our planet by regulating temperature, both warming and cooling. They cool the planet by reflecting the sun’s light and energy back into space. They also act like a warming blanket. It’s the perfect balance that protects us and all of life on our world. Clouds also carry the water we need to survive. In this way, they both protect and deliver!

In our next few lessons we will learn about a very special cloud God used to further protect and guide His people—in a miraculous way!
