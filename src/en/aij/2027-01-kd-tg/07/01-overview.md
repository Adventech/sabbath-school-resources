**Word of the Week**

**Provide:** To provide is to give something that is needed. It is God who provides the food you eat and the water you drink. God also provides love and forgiveness.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

As the Israelites kept journeying through the desert, they started to worry about having no food. Again, they complained to Moses. God provided manna. The Israelites were given instructions as to how to gather the manna and respect the Sabbath. What God provided was all that they needed; however, they begged Moses for meat. God was angry against the ungrateful Israelites, but He still sent a huge flock of quail to their camp. The people gorged themselves and were sick, and some died. Despite their grumbling and disobedience, God continued to provide the manna they needed for the 40 years of their
wilderness experience.
