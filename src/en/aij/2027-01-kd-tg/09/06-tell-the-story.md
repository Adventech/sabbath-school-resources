**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. I will put Him first.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Giant **cardboard heart** from Early Arrivals section (use also next week); **Happy Heart posters** for each station (on floor or wall); **large cardstock numbers**; **sticky tac** or similar.
- Objects for **Station 1: tub, small paper heart for each child, collection of toys and paper, picture of the Bible. Station 2:** collection of **cardboard boxes labeled with pictures, beanbag. Station 3: paper towel tubes** for each pair of children. **Station 4: nature items, Sabbath School lessons, Sabbath picture books, instruments**.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY and STORY EXPLORER**

All over camp the Israelites were busy washing and scrubbing their dusty clothes. They were getting ready for a very special meeting. God wanted to meet and speak with them!

The people got ready for two whole days; then, on the third day, Moses led them out of the camp. They stood together at the bottom of Mount Sinai. The Israelites could see God’s mighty presence as it covered the mountain with a cloud and bright fire.

God told the people that He had saved them and that He loved them. ^[(Show the children your giant cardboard heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then He began to tell them His 10 special rules to live well. God knew that these rules would bring them closer to Him and each other, and make them safe and happy.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Hold the special class Bible. Turn to Exodus 20 and show the children exactly where these commandments are written. Read each commandment from the Bible and explain it using the **Teaching Point**. Then hold up the commandment number and invite a child to place it on one of the four Happy Heart stations around your space, matching with the small numbers on poster.)

No. 1. “You must not have any other gods except me” (Exodus 20:3, ICB). God has made you, saved you, and loves you. He deserves to be first in your life. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 2. “You must not make for yourselves any idols. . . . You must not worship or serve any idol” (Exodus 20:4, 5, ICB).

An idol is a statue or object that people worship. An idol can be anything that we make more important than God. Money, TV, or fancy things can be idols. These things can’t love or help us. God made everything, and He loves you more than you could ever imagine. He wants us to love and worship only Him, for He loves us so very much. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 3. “You must not use the name of the Lord your God thoughtlessly” (Exodus 20:7, ICB). Sometimes people use God’s name without any care, or swear falsely using God’s name. They say God’s precious name carelessly, rudely, or dishonestly. God asks us to always only speak His name with thoughtfulness, kindness, and love, because He thinks about us with thoughtfulness, kindness, and love. He wants our words to show that we treasure and love Him as the holy King of the universe. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

No. 4. “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy” (Exodus 20:8). Every week God invites you to a very special celebration from sunset Friday night to sunset Saturday night. God made you, and He made the seventh-day Sabbath as a special time to be with you. He wants to spend time with us. He wants us to choose to spend time with Him by worshipping Him, thinking about Him, learning more about Him from the Bible, praying to Him, being with others at church, and spending time with our families. ^[(Invite a child to stick the number on a Happy Heart station.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Explain to your class that they’re going to think about these commandments a little bit more. Lead the group to four Happy Heart stations. Have a helper ring your **bell or sound maker** when it’s time to move to the next one. Don’t spend more than two minutes at each station.)

**Happy Heart 1: God First**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite the children to each take a heart from a sensory tub. On the heart will include pictures of many distractions, such as toys, blocks, etc. Invite the children to bring their hearts and place them together. Then invite one child to place a Bible on top of the hearts. Say a prayer together, asking God to help us put Him first.)

**Teaching Point:** Love God most of all. Put God first in your heart and life.

**Happy Heart 2: No Idols!**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(With a small beanbag, knock down fake “idol” towers made of boxes—labeled with pictures of a statue, money, toys, TV, etc. Quickly rebuild so each person can have a turn knocking them over.)

**Teaching Point:** Worship only God. Trust and love Him above everything else. We shouldn’t love anything more than God. He’s all we need.

**Happy Heart 3: Respect God’s Name**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite the children to join into pairs and use paper towel tubes, or rolled cardstock, as a “truth phone.” One can speak while the other listens; then they can swap. Encourage them to whisper something special about God, using His name with respect. E.g., “God is good,” “I love You, God.”)

**Teaching Point:** Use God’s name with love and respect. Always speak kindly when you talk about God, and never use His name roughly or as a swearword.

**Happy Heart 4: Spend Sabbath With God, His Holy Day**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite children to take a nature item, Sabbath picture book, instrument, or Bible from a basket. Ask them to sit down, close their eyes and pray, thanking God for the joy and rest of the Sabbath.)

**Teaching Point:** Take time to rest and spend with God. Keep the Sabbath holy, different from all the other days.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Return to your seats. Invite four children to take the number from each station and stick it up on your giant heart at the front.)

**Teaching Point:** These first four commandments help us show God how much we love Him. They keep our hearts happy and pure. God said, “You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength” (Deuteronomy 6:5). We can do this by obeying God’s Ten Commandments. (We’ll learn about the other six next week.)
