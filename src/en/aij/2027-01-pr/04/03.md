The time had come. The Israelites would be delivered this very night! Up and down the dusty streets of Goshen, families were busy doing the final things that God had instructed them to do. Mothers had already asked their Egyptian neighbors for gold and silver jewelery to take with them on their journey. Fathers had already asked their masters to give them gold and silver vessels too. Why did the Egyptians hand over their gold and silver? **Read Exodus 11:3.**

Fathers had sacrificed the little lamb, and used a hyssop branch to smear the blood on the doorposts of their homes. Mothers had baked unleavened bread without yeast and collected bitter herbs to eat with the roasted lamb for their last dinner in Egypt. Everything was packed. The animals had been herded close by. Everyone was dressed for travel, with sandals on their feet and a staff in their hands.

The only thing left to do was eat the special Passover meal of lamb and unleavened bread with bitter herbs. God had instructed them to eat their meal in a special way. How were they to eat it? **Read Exodus 12:11.**

God was teaching His people important things that night. He was helping them to understand that by obeying His instructions, they were expressing their faith and showing that they trusted their mighty God.

When God is our friend, we express our faith and love for Him by trusting and obeying too. The words of a well-known hymn, “Trust and Obey,” say:

When we walk with the Lord in the light of His word,\
What a glory He sheds on our way!\
When we do His good will, He abides with us still,\
And with all who will trust and obey.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- What does each line of the above hymn mean? Discuss the words and phrases: “light of His word,” “glory,” “good will,” “abides.” If you know this hymn, sing it together with your family: “Trust and Obey” (_The Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal_, no. 590).
- How might you have felt if you had been a firstborn Israelite child?

^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Make unleavened bread: Mix together 2 cups of whole-wheat flour, 1 tablespoon of oil, 1 cup of water. Roll the dough to approximately ¼ inch (½ centimeter) thick on a floured baking tray. Cut into 1-inch (4-centimeter) squares and bake for 10 minutes at 350°F (180°C). Why not wrap some and share them as a gift?
