I Can Help\
^[My Family]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff", "typeface": "Omnes-Bold"}}}) Man 
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/06/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#7E6A40"},"block":{"backgroundColor": "#E7C77Bad"}}}
When God made the world, He created many wonderful things! He made hedgehogs and hippos. He made monkeys and meerkats. He created Adam and Eve and made them a family. God put us in families so we would feel safe and loved _(hug child)_. God put us in families so we could love and help each other.
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/06/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}}}
God created animals that are very good at helping! Can you see the dolphins gliding through the water? God created them to swim with their big families called pods. They love to be together. The mommies help their babies by giving them their milk to drink. The baby dolphins swim in the middle of the pod so they stay safe. They also use their long noses to keep danger away. Dolphins help each other by working together to catch fish. _Thank You, God, for dolphins!_
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/06/03.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#895856"}}}
Do you know the sound a lion makes? _(Roar!)_ God made lions to live in big families called prides. They each have a special job to help their family. The mommy lions’ job is to look for food and look after the baby cubs. God made mommy lions to look after the baby cubs. Mommy lions are very fast, so they can catch their food. All the mommy lions work together to find food. The daddy lions’ job is important too. They help their family by protecting them from danger. They guard the pride and look after the cubs when the mommy lions are finding dinner. _Thank You, God, for lions!_
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/06/04.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}}}
God made penguins to help their families in special ways too! The mommy penguin lays an egg and then carefully gives it to the daddy penguin. The egg needs to stay warm in the snow, so the daddy puts the egg on his feet and covers it with his tummy. _(Can you see the daddy penguins with the eggs?)_ He looks after the egg for a long time while the mommy is away catching fish. All the daddy penguins help each other stay warm by huddling close together. When the chicks are born, the mommies come back from the sea and feed the chicks while the daddies go and eat. How kind and helpful they are to each other! _Thank You, God, for penguins!_
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/06/05.png)
God created so many amazing animal families that help each other. Your family loves to help you too. They want to protect you and make sure you are safe and loved. If someone in your family ever tries to hurt you, you should say, “Stop” or “No,” even if they are bigger than you.\
But when your mommy or daddy asks you kindly to help, you can smile and say ,“Yes, Mommy” or “Yes, Daddy.” Jesus loves to see our families helping each other with a smile.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for all the animals that help their families. Help me to think of ways I can help my family too!
