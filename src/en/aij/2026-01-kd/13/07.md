Lightning flashed and thunder crashed. The little seaplane wobbled in the strong winds. This was a terrible storm! The pilot looked over at Ejler Jensen and nodded at the missionary. There was no choice. They would have to land the plane on the sea.

Ejler was a missionary in the cold, icy land of Alaska. He would often travel around on fishing boats and little seaplanes so he could visit people in faraway towns and villages. Ejler wanted everyone to know about the love of Jesus.

As the wild winds blew, the pilot landed the boat on the cold water. Ice quickly covered the wings and propeller. The plane began to sink!

Ejler and the pilot jumped into the freezing water and swam to the nearest shore. As they sat wet and shivering in the cold snow, Ejler prayed.

Suddenly an old, friendly Eskimo appeared, dressed in white. He waved at them to follow him. The men plodded through the thick snow, behind the Eskimo. Eventually they arrived at a little village. The local people gave them shelter until the storm passed.

Ejler asked the villagers about the Eskimo, but they told him that there was no such person. In that moment Ejler realized that God had done an impossible miracle to save them. He had sent an angel to guide them to warmth and safety.
