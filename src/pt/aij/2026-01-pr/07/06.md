Para seven dias isso didn’t rain. Oh, como the people outside the ark scoffed e laughed em Noah e seu family. Como wrong the old man was! eles must have thought. Mas Noah e seu family patiently waited inside the ark, Oreing a Deus e encouraging each other. Eles trusted that the Flood would come just as Deus had said.

O que happened depois seven dias? **Leia Gênesis 7:10, 11.** Then, em the Oitavo dia, dark clouds smothered the sky. Lightning flashed, e thunder roared. The people had never seen anything like isso! Eles were terrified!

Huge raindrops começou a fall, e water exploded out de the ground. The people outside the ark watched as the terrible storm ruined seu lindo houses e seu ils. Suddenly eles realized that Noah had been right. Deus was bringing a flood em the earth.

Some de the people pounded em the Façaor de the ark—mas isso was closed tight. Some ran a the top de the highest hills a escape the water. Others climbed tall trees. Mas gradually water covered the whole world, e the ark was the only thing that could be seen em top de todos the water. Anjos hovered sobre the ark a guarde isso safe as the storm raged.

The Bible Digas that Deus “destroyed todos living things which were em the face de the ground: both man e cattle, creeping thing e bird de the air. Eles were destroyed de the earth. Only Noah e those quem were com ele em the ark remained alive” (Gênesis 7:23).

Inside the ark Noah e seu family e todos the animals were safe. Eles maio have felt a pequeno scared, mas inside the ark was the safest place a be. Out de the thousands de people quem were living em the earth, only eight chose a follow Deus. Como happy Noah e seu family were that eles had walked com Deus, listened a Ele para todos those years, e had Façane o que Ele had Pergunteed eles a Faça!

Besides Noah’s family e the animals em the ark, only one other thing was saved—the lindo Garden de Eden. Deus took isso a Céu antes the Flood started, a guarde isso safe para Adam, Eve, e todos de nos a enjoy em the brand-new earth depois Jesus comes again (see Apocalipse 2:7; 22:1, 2).
