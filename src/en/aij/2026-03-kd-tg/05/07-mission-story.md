**Focus:** Pedrito learns about Jesus as he grows and shares Jesus with his family.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Paraguay** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Pedrito lived in Paraguay in a big village. His grandfather was the chief of the village. Pedrito’s grandfather saw that his grandson was learning and growing well, so he decided to send him to the best school he could find to help him grow and learn even more! Perdito’s school was called Escula Adventista de Coaaguazu (Adventist School of Coaaguazu).

Grandfather took Pedrito to the school. He could see that it was a fine school, and the children were good and kind. He wanted Pedrito to be kind too. Pedrito learned so many good things as he grew up in the school. He learned to read, to write, and how to solve math problems. But best of all he learned that God loves him and all his family. He learned that all his food came from God, and he learned how to pray and thank God for his food.

Pedrito was excited when there was a short break from school, and he could go and see his family again. He was excited to tell them all the things he was learning. He was excited for them to see how much he had grown. He wanted to be kind and good at home to show everyone the difference Jesus was making in his heart!

But when Pedrito’s daddy saw him praying to thank God for his food, he was upset and told Pedrito to eat outside in the yard. When Grandfather saw him eating outside, he said, “Hey, Pedrito! How big you are growing! But why are you eating out here?”

“Grandfather! I am learning all about God in the good school. I just wanted to thank God for my lovely food!”

Grandfather talked to Pedrito’s daddy and told him that his son was growing to be good and kind and wise. Pedrito’s daddy started to understand. Grandfather was so pleased with the way that Pedrito was growing up to know God that he asked the school to send someone to the village to tell everyone about God!

The school sent a pastor to tell everyone in the village about God. He was a good and kind man too, and everyone loved him. Soon there was a big baptism, and many people from the village, including Pedrito’s family, were baptized. All because Grandfather sent Pedrito to school, and Pedrito grew up to know God.

- **How do you thank God for meals in your family?**
- **How are you growing to be more like Jesus? What have you done this week to show other people God’s love?**

**Teaching Point:** Pedrito learned about God and helped to tell others about His love, just as Jesus did!

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He is loving and perfect.**
