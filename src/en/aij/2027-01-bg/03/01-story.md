The green grass sparkled in the morning sun as Noah walked through the forest of tall, tall trees. Fluffy bunnies hopped in a field of tiny white daisies, while honeybees buzzed _(buzz like a bee)_ from flower to flower. Noah heard the happy quacks _(quack, quack)_ of the mama duck swimming in the cool clear stream, as 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 _(count with fingers)_ little ducklings swam in a row behind her. It was the best time of day for Noah. This was when he talked to God, his very best friend.\
Noah and God talked every day. In the morning as the sun peeked over the hills, Noah talked with God about the new day. Every night as the sun set low in the sky, Noah told God about all the things he saw and did. It made God happy _(smile)_ to spend time with Noah each day. Noah listened _(hand to ear)_ to all God had to say. And God listened to Noah. Sometimes Noah told God about the things that made him sad _(look sad)_. God was sad about those things too.

Noah was sad because people didn’t love God anymore. He felt sad that they forgot how God made them and the big, beautiful world _(open arms wide)_. God was sad too. The people didn’t care for the wonderful things He’d made for them. They didn’t talk to God anymore or tell their children about God’s love. They weren’t kind to others. They used mean words and hurtful hands. It was like the world was messy and dirty.\
But God had a plan, a great big plan! _(Arms out big.)_ God would make it rain, and rain, and rain. It would rain until all the rivers were full. It would rain until the lakes were like the ocean. The rain would fall until all of the hills were covered. The rain would fall until God’s big, beautiful world was all under water! The rain would make everything clean and good again.

God had a plan—a really big plan! _(Arms out big.)_ God told Noah to build a boat—a big, big boat called an ark. It would be so big that it would fit people and every kind of animal inside. God would take care of the people and the animals while the world was covered with water. God told Noah how to build the really big boat from the tall, strong trees. He would need to build rooms for people and a place for the animals. God loved all the people in the world. If the people trusted God and came into the ark, God would keep them safe.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for Your really big plan! Thank You for saving us too when we trust You. Thank You for taking care of me.
