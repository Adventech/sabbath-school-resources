**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal: some feathers, cotton balls, or lightweight paper scraps.

Invite a child to place these items on a table at the front of your class. Ask another child to come to the front to try to move the object without touching it, and only by speaking. (They’ll try, but it won’t move.) Then have them blow gently so that the items move.

**Teaching Point:** Just as your breath moved the items, Jesus’ words have real power!

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In today’s story Jesus used His all-powerful, amazing words to help someone who believed in Him. Let’s find out who—and how!

Invite the children to stand by their seats and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. His words can heal and are always true** [use hands as an open book]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **bottle of water**, a **Ziplock bag**, and one or two **sharpened pencils**.

Pour some water from the bottle into the Ziplock bag until it is about half full and seal it. Hold the bag of water in one hand and the pencil in the other hand. Tell the children you are going to poke the pencil through the bag of water, yet the water won’t spill.

- **Do you believe I can do this? Why?** (No, it seems impossible. You trust me to tell the truth.)

Using a smooth motion, poke the pencil through the bag and out the other side, keeping it stuck through the bag. You can add another pencil, if you like.

- **Who in the story believed in something amazing before He saw it happen?** (The nobleman.)
- **Whom did he tell about Jesus’ miracle?** (Everyone in his house.) Share that they all believed in Jesus too.

**Teaching Point:** Share that we can believe in everything Jesus tells us in His Word. Sometimes we won’t be able to see how Jesus will help us, but we can trust His promises. His words can heal and are always true. Let’s be like the nobleman and tell everyone about Jesus so they can believe too!
