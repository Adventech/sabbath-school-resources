_Saw, saw, saw._ Jesus’ hands pushed and pulled the saw as He happily crafted furniture alongside Joseph in the little carpenter’s shop in Nazareth. As He worked, Jesus was the perfect example of Colossians 3:23: “In all the work you are doing, work the best you can. Work as if you were working for the Lord, not for men” (ICB).

Jesus always served the Lord happily, not just when He was hammering and sawing. We know that Jesus is love (1 John 4:8). That means He noticed when others were hungry and shared His food. If others were lonely, Jesus would have shared His time. When others were spoken to harshly, Jesus would have shared kind words. His quiet, unselfish ways drew some people to Him. But His constant kindness and goodness irritated others.

Often people who are up to mischief don’t like it when others don’t want to join in. It makes their bad choices stand out. Jesus would have had to have put up with being teased, laughed at, or avoided, because of His good choices. We can be sure He replied only with kind words.

The other boys could see that Jesus studied the Scriptures and obeyed what He read. Jesus always made the Bible His go-to for all answers and help from God. **Read Psalm 119:1-3**, a scripture He shared as a grown-up.

Jesus knew that during this part of His mission on earth, He was to be God’s example, even when it was hard. What a treasure His example is. Colossians 2:3: “And in him [Jesus] all the treasures of wisdom and knowledge are safely kept” (ICB).
