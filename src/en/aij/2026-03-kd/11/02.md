The little flame flickered as it grew bigger and bigger. Its golden glow warmed the room. Carefully the lamp was lifted and placed on a shelf. Now all the family could see each other’s smiles. They could find the cooking pot, the needle and thread, and a cozy cushion to sit on.

At night, Jerusalem and other surrounding towns sparkled with hundreds of these little lights. Every family used small pottery lamps to light their home.

One day Jesus sat on the side of a mountain, talking to a big crowd. He reminded them of these night lights. He said, “You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden” (Matthew 5:14).

The people listened closely. They thought about their homes and towns that shone in the darkness.

Jesus continued: “People don’t hide a light under a bowl” (Matthew 5:15, ICB). People smiled. Some chuckled. What a silly idea! No one would ever light a lamp and hide it under a bowl. Lights were for shining, not hiding!

Jesus added, “They put the light on a lampstand. Then the light shines for all the people in the house” (Matthew 5:15, ICB). What did Jesus say next? **Read Matthew 5:16.**

Everyone nodded. Yes, what a good story! Everyone had a lamp, and everyone could understand. Jesus wants us to speak and do things that show His goodness. We can be like a bright light for all the world to see.
