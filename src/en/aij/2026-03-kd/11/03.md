The sun glistened on the still water. Jesus sat in a boat on the edge of a lake and looked out at the smiling crowd. They sat on the sandy beach, quietly waiting. They were ready to listen to another story.

“The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed,” Jesus said loudly, “which a man took and sowed in his field” (Matthew 13:31).

Everyone imagined the teeny, tiny mustard seed. They pictured a farmer popping it in the ground and covering it gently with dirt. Pat, pat, pat.

Jesus continued: “That seed is the smallest of all seeds. But when it grows, it is one of the largest garden plants. It becomes a tree, big enough for the wild birds to come and make nests in its branches” (Matthew 13:32, ICB).

The people looked from the lake to the fields. Large mustard plants waved their branches in the breeze. Birds flitted from twig to twig, whistling pretty songs.

Why did Jesus tell them this story? **Read Matthew 13:34.**

Jesus loved using nature to teach them about His love and power. He wanted them to know that He could work a miracle in their hearts too, just like that seed. All they needed was a little bit of trust in Him.

Jesus promised that He can work a miracle inside of you too. He will help you grow stronger and braver every day when He lives in your heart.
