**Word of the Week**

**Seek:** Seeking is searching for something. Jesus wants us to seek Him with all our hearts. Some things we can do to spend time with Jesus include praying, reading the Bible, going to Sabbath School, and singing worship songs.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

When Jesus was born, the angels were very happy! They found some shepherds and told them that the Messiah had been born. Very soon after, the shepherds ran to look for Him and worship Him. Simeon and Anna had been looking for the Messiah for many years too! They were happy to see Baby Jesus in the temple! And finally, Wise Men traveled a long distance to find Jesus and worship Him.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants us to seek Him, and He will help us to find Him.
- **Feel** excited about looking for Jesus in their life.
- **Respond** by helping others to seek Jesus too.
