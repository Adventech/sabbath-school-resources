The warm sun shone through the window. Mommy Jochebed and Daddy Amram cuddled little baby Moses _(cuddle up close to your child)._ He was beautiful! _(Point to features.)_ He had two little eyes . . . two little ears . . . two little hands . . . 10 little toes . . . and one little nose. Their eyes sparkled with happiness. They said “Thank You” to God for their brand-new baby boy!\
Big sister Miriam and big brother Aaron leaned in close. They looked into their new brother’s two bright eyes. They whispered in his two ears, “I love you, baby brother.” They held his two little hands and tickled his 10 tiny toes _(tickle your child’s toes)._ They even kissed his one little nose! Everyone loved beautiful baby Moses.

Mommy Jochebed and Daddy Amram cared for Moses. They gave him smiles and cuddles. They made sure he was fed. And they helped him go to sleep in a soft little bed. Moses’ family kept him safe and well.\
The pharaoh king where baby Moses lived was not a kind man. He wanted to take all the baby boys away. No! Mommy Jochebed was not going to let anyone take Moses away! She prayed and asked God to look after her precious baby.\
Mommy Jochebed hid baby Moses in the house for many days and nights. But it was hard to hide a baby! Babies cry when they are hungry. Shh! Babies cry when they are tired. Shh! Sometimes babies cry when they need a cuddle. Shh! It was very hard to hide baby Moses when he cried. So the whole family knelt down and prayed to God _(fold hands for prayer)_. Dear Jesus, please help us to keep baby Moses safe.

God gave Mommy Jochebed a good idea. Mommy and Miriam collected lots of tall grass from the river. Together they used the grass to make a basket. They sang songs as they worked _(hum, hum, hum)._ The basket would be a bed for baby Moses—a bed that could float on the water like a little boat. Moses would stay hidden and safe in his basket boat. God chose a special family to look after baby Moses.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for giving me a family who loves me too.
