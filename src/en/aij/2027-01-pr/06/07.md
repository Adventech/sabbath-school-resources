God gave Ellen White an urgent message for the believers in Portsmouth, New Hampshire, United States. The journey there required a train trip, but there was no money for tickets. Nevertheless, Ellen and her sister Sarah prepared to go, trusting that the Lord would provide what was needed.

They dressed for the journey and were about to leave the house to walk the short distance to the train station when Ellen looked out the window and saw a man whom she knew. He was riding very fast up to the gate. His horse was covered with sweat.

He reined his horse in to a quick stop, rushed into the house, and said, “Does anyone here need money? I was impressed that someone here needs money.”

The girls quickly told him that they were going to Portsmouth to deliver God’s message but had no money for tickets. He handed them money for the trip there and back.

“Take a seat in my wagon, and I’ll drive you to the station,” he said.

On their way to the station he told them that the horse had wanted to come the 12 miles from home so fast that it had been difficult to keep him from galloping all the way. Ellen and Sarah had no sooner taken their seats in the train than it started. They were on their way, grateful that God had provided for their needs.
