**Big Idea:** God looks for people who love Him to be part of His big story.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images as you tell the story.
- You may also like to use the following props: **peg people and angel**, **blocks** to use as extra peg people (John’s family and friends), **flashlight** to shine on angel, **felt set** if available, **broom, blanket and pillow, baby doll, paper (or writing tablet) and pen**. You may like to invite the children to kneel in a prayer posture every time you say “surrender.”
- Ask children to open their Bibles and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask them.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

Old Zacharias and Elizabeth closed their eyes and began to pray. They were praying and waiting, waiting and praying. They were waiting for God’s promised Savior.

The faithful couple knew they wouldn’t have to wait too long. God had given His prophets a message about the future—called a prophecy. God had told Isaiah that a child, God’s Son, would be born. God also told Daniel when the promised Savior would come. That time was now! They believed God’s prophecies.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day, as priest Zacharias was burning incense in the temple, a bright shining angel appeared beside the altar ^[(flashlight on angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did the angel tell him? Read Luke 1:13.** (Your prayers have been heard; your wife will have a son. You will call him John.)

John would prepare people for the coming Savior.

Zacharias was so surprised! For a moment he doubted the message. He and Elizabeth were old—too old to have a baby! The angel Gabriel told him that because he had doubted, he wouldn’t be able to talk until after the baby was born.

Zacharias believed. His face lit up with joy. God had chosen his family to be God’s special helpers! They would be part of God’s big story! He would surrender (give up) his doubts, and trust God.

In Nazareth there lived a young woman named Mary. She hummed as she swept the floors ^[(broom)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly the room filled with beautiful light ^[(flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Mary was stunned! A dazzling angel, Gabriel, stood before her. “Rejoice, highly-favored one, the Lord is with you; blessed are you among women!” he said (Luke 1:28).

Mary could hardly believe her eyes. What was happening?

“Don’t be afraid, Mary,” said the angel. He had the best news ever!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he say would happen to her? Read Luke 1:31-33.** (She would have a son, the Savior! And He would be called Jesus.)

“How can this be?” asked Mary (Luke 1:34). She wasn’t married to Joseph yet—only engaged. Gabriel told her that the Holy Spirit would cause Mary to become pregnant and that the baby would be called the Son of God. Wow!

Mary stood in awe before the angel. God had chosen her! She would surrender. “I am the servant girl of the Lord. Let this happen to me as you say!” she said (Luke 1:38, ICB).

When Mary shared the news with Joseph, he was worried. Could it be true? What would people say—they weren’t even married yet!

Joseph went to bed with worries on his mind ^[(blanket, pillow)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). As he slept, God sent an angel to him in a dream.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did the angel say? Read Matthew 1:20, 21.** (Not to be afraid to take Mary as his wife. By the Holy Spirit she would have a son named Jesus. He would save the people from their sins.)

Joseph woke up and believed. He would marry the mother of God’s promised Son! He quietly surrendered to God’s plan too.

Mary set off to share their happy news with her cousin Elizabeth. The angel Gabriel had said Elizabeth would have a special miracle baby too—in three months!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After a long trek Mary arrived at Zacharias’ home with a cheerful hello. As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, she was filled with the Holy Spirit. “God has blessed you more than any other woman. And God has blessed the baby which you will give birth to,” she said (Luke 1:42).

Elizabeth rested a hand on her tummy. “When I heard your voice, the baby inside me jumped with joy” (Luke 1:44, ICB).

Mary and Elizabeth had a wonderful visit together. They were so excited! God loved them. He had chosen them to be an important part of His big story!

A few months later a baby’s cry filled that same little home ^[(baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Friends and family celebrated the birth of Elizabeth’s newborn son. She called him John. This puzzled their neighbors and relatives. Sons were often named after relatives, but no one in his family was named John.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Zacharias reached for his writing tablet ^[(paper and pen)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He wrote, “His name is John” (Luke 1:63). Immediately Zacharias could speak! He explained how God Himself had named their little baby John, which means “Jehovah [God] is gracious.” What a miracle! The people were sure God had big plans for this baby. They spread the amazing news everywhere.

God did have very big plans for John! He was to prepare the people’s hearts to be clean and ready to accept Jesus.

Luke 1:17 ^[(hold Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) tells us, “He will bring those who are not obeying God back to the right way of thinking. He will make people ready for the coming of the Lord” (ICB).

Elizabeth held her miracle son close. Her prayers had been answered—in such an amazing way! God’s promised Savior was on His way. Jesus was coming! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) And Jesus would go on to prepare the way for people everywhere to see a clearer picture of God.

**^[Discuss:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **God had a plan for each person in our story. What was God’s plan for each person?**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**The Angel:** To tell Zacharias about Elizabeth having a son.\
**Zacharias:** To be the father of John; to teach him about God and His plan for him.\
**Elizabeth:** To be the mother of John and teach him about God’s ways.\
**John:** To prepare the way for Jesus—to tell others to get ready for Jesus and repent (say they were sorry) for their sins.\
**Joseph:** To be the father of Jesus, God’s promised Son.\
**Mary:** To be the mother of Jesus.
