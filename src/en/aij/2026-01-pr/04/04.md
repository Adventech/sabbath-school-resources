It wasn’t long before Lucifer whispered his thoughts to other angels. He was very clever and made it sound as if he were just trying to help God with some new ideas. He told the angels that they didn’t really need God’s laws, and that heaven would be a much better place if they could just do whatever they wanted.

No one had heard anything like this before! Lucifer worked closely with God as the leading angel, and all the angels respected him. But now they were confused.

Some angels thought he must know a lot more than they did, and they started to believe his gossip. Others were very upset to hear what he was saying about God.

It wasn’t long before Lucifer became bolder, and he gathered many angels around him as he whispered clever lies. What does the Bible say a whisperer will do? **Read Proverbs 16:28.**

The angels had always been the best of friends, but the whispered lies were damaging their friendships.

The loyal angels were sad that their happy home was becoming polluted. They loved Lucifer and tried to remind Him that God was powerful, loving, and kind. But he would not listen. He had become selfish and jealous. He didn’t care whom he hurt, so long as he got his own way. His sneaky ways convinced a third of the angels to side with him.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How do you think our loving God felt about Lucifer’s gossip and lies?_
- _What can you do when you hear someone saying nasty things about another person?_

Our loving God is very happy when we believe what He says. He knows how much gossip and whispered lies will hurt us and our friendships.
