Jesus had said to Bartimaeus, “Go your way” (Mark 10:52). But there was no way he wanted to go anywhere by himself! He wanted to be close to His new Friend and Savior, and follow Him. And that’s exactly what he did. Bartimaeus “followed Jesus on the road” (Mark 10:52). Before the healing miracle, he had sat by the road. Now he was on the road, following Jesus.

Jesus had many followers who continued to walk with Him. He taught as He walked. Soon, He would send out His disciples in pairs to teach others about God’s kingdom of love. As they had followed Him, their faith had grown, and they could now excitedly share with others. They would do many miracles in Jesus’ name and power as they faithfully followed Him.

Jesus calls you to follow Him too. Just like Bartimaeus, you can come close and learn from Him each day. You can follow Jesus by reading His Word and talking with Him in prayer, then putting His love into practice, just as His disciples did. What does Jesus promise that God will do when we choose to follow Him?

**Read John 12:26.**
