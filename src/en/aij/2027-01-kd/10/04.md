God continued to speak from the mountaintop. What was the next commandment God spoke? **Read Exodus 20:15.**

Deep in the Papua New Guinea rain forest lives the Bowerbird. This bird has dark, shiny black feathers, purple eyes, and a love for anything blue. In fact, the male bowerbird loves blue things so much that he is constantly on the lookout for anything blue to snatch away for himself. It seems that no matter how many blue things he finds, he always wants more!

You may wonder what he does with so many blue bottle tops, buttons, and beads. He is decorating his bower, a little stage with two walls of arched sticks. He makes it blue and beautiful to impress female bowerbirds.

If a female Bowerbird comes close, he begins dancing, singing, and showing off his stolen treasures to her.

Sometimes people act a bit like Bowerbirds. They take things that don’t belong to them, without asking. This is called stealing. Some people steal because they want to impress others, or to try to find happiness from things. God says, “You shall not steal” (Leviticus 19:11). Stealing leaves others sad and won’t make us happy.

God’s Spirit can help us to be respectful and honest with other people’s things. Jesus gives us true joy and happiness. He is the greatest treasure of all.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
