**What you need:**

Recycled cardboard, blue paper, craft sticks, paper cups, cardboard cylinders, masking tape, craft glue, scissors, toy car (optional). Or other building materials available to you.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5a.png)

**Instructions:**

As a group or pair, build a bridge to span a blue sheet of paper, using the materials provided. Then test each bridge with a toy car. Alternatively, you can follow the example below:

- Cut out a strip of cardboard and fold each end into the middle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5b.png)

- In the folds, tape two cardboard cylinders to form ramps, and flip over.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5c.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5d.png)

- Glue craft sticks vertically along the length of the cardboard strip.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5e.png)

- (Optional) Add a railing by gluing four stacks of craft sticks horizontally along each side.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5f.png)

- Stick three craft sticks together to form a triangle. Repeat, till you have four triangles.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5g.png)

- Glue these to the railings, with a final craft stick across the top of each side.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5h.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/05/5i.png)

- Test your bridge with a toy car.
