**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Every day the sun rises and sets. And every day this predictable rhythm reminds us of God’s fresh daily mercy for us. “Let the fresh glories of each new morning awaken praise in your hearts for these tokens of His loving care” (Ellen G. White, in _Review and Herald_, April 21, 1885). When we appreciate His limitless love, and live our lives as a thank-You to Him, others will notice.

Some days our reflection of God may seem a little dim, but even that will brighten a darkened room. The more time we spend abiding in His presence, the stronger His light becomes. With our hearts aglow, we will feel compelled to share the knowledge of being loved by a mighty God with our neighbors, friends, family, and work colleagues.

To some, “shining brightly” might mean doing something tremendous or grand, such as receiving a prestigious award or gaining more followers on social media. But as we look at Christ’s example, we get a clearer picture. Jesus modeled gentle compassion, loving actions, kind and plain words, and a humble nature.

There are always opportunities to reflect God’s character when we ask for His Spirit to fill us and guide our interactions. As you teach this lesson to His precious children this week, let your light shine so brightly that they may see and know that God is your best friend and is full of love for them too.

“You are the light of the world. A city that is set on a hill cannot be hidden. Nor do they light a lamp and put it under a basket, but on a lampstand, and it gives light to all who are in the house” (Matthew 5:14, 15).
