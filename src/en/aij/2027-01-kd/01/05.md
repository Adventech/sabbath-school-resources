Someone was coming! Hidden by the side of the river, near the reeds, Miriam listened and watched closely. There, walking straight toward the water’s edge, was Pharaoh’s daughter, the Egyptian princess.

Suddenly the princess noticed something unusual bobbing up and down among the reeds. _Was it a green frog? No, it was too big to be a frog. Was it a duck? No, it was made of reeds, not feathers. Was it a fishing boat? No, it was much too small to be a fishing boat._ The princess became more and more curious. Excitedly she asked one of her servant girls to walk into the water to get it.

The helper picked up the basket and brought it to Pharaoh’s daughter. The princess opened the lid and saw a little baby inside. He was crying. What a surprise! The princess “felt sorry for him” (Exodus 2:6, ICB). She wanted to protect him. Then Miriam bravely came forward. What did she ask the princess? **Read Exodus 2:7.**

“Yes, please” (Exodus 2:8, ICB), Pharaoh’s daughter replied.

Miriam knew just the woman to care for the baby—his own mother! Miriam raced home and brought her back to the river. The princess kindly said to Mother Jochebed, “Take this baby and nurse him for me” (Exodus 2:9, ICB). The princess explained that she wanted to adopt him as her son when he grew into a big boy, and that his name would be Moses.

Jochebed nodded, and smiled with joy as she cuddled her baby. God had protected her precious baby boy in such a surprising way!
