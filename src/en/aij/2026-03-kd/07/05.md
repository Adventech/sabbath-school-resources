Whoosh! Satan took Jesus up, up, to the very top of a tall mountain. Wow! What a beautiful view! Jesus could see sparkling cities, marble palaces, bright-green fields, and vineyards full of delicious fruit. Oh, how different it looked from the dry, dusty desert.

Satan looked out at the beautiful world from way up high. He had already tried to tempt Jesus twice. But Jesus had used God’s Word to stay strong and say no. But Satan wasn’t about to give up.

As Jesus looked at the view too, Satan said, “All these things I will give You if You will fall down and worship me” (Matthew 4:9).

What did Jesus say to Satan? **Read Matthew 4:10.**

Jesus knew the Bible so well. He knew that only God should be worshipped. He would bow down only to God. The Holy Spirit had made Jesus strong and wise. Jesus had used the words in the Bible like an invisible sword to fight against Satan’s lies.

Satan lost the battle and left. Jesus had won!

With God’s strength you can beat Satan’s tricks too. His words in the Bible can give you the power you need to say no to what’s wrong and yes to what’s right.
