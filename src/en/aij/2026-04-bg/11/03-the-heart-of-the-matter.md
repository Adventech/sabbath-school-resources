Our God wants to be known by us, and so He sent Jesus to reveal His loving, merciful, true, and just character to humanity, to restore us back into a close relationship with Him. Jesus came down to change the trajectory of our future. No matter what our circumstances or backgrounds, nothing should stop us from knowing Jesus personally. He didn’t come just for the rich or educated. He came for us all!

> <callout>Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 43</callout>
> “The King of glory stooped low to take humanity. . . . His glory was veiled, that the majesty of His outward form might not become an object of attraction. He shunned all outward display. Riches, worldly honor, and human greatness can never save a soul from death; Jesus purposed that no attraction of an earthly nature should call men to His side. Only the beauty of heavenly truth must draw those who would follow Him. The character of the Messiah had long been foretold in prophecy, and He desired men to accept Him upon the testimony of the word of God.”

How true that the beauty of Jesus’ heavenly truth drew men to Him. His kind eyes, warm smile, and gentle touch spoke of true love to so many. He really did come to serve and not be served (Mark 10:45). The evidence of His humble, servant heart is clear in that stable; for the God of all glory met with His created beings in the form of an innocent and helpless babe. What an amazing God!

> <callout>1 John 4:10, 11</callout>
> “In this is love, not that we loved God, but that He loved us and sent His Son to be the propitiation for our sins. Beloved, if God so loved us, we also ought to love one another.”
