A Little\
^[Boy’s Lunch]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#b34097", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/05/01.png)

The morning sun was bright and warm.  The grassy hillside was dotted with pretty flowers. Butterflies flitted about. Birds sang in the trees. Jesus and His friends sat in the shade under a tree. A big, big (stretch arms out) crowd of people sat before Him. There were too many people to count! The people watched (cup eyes) and listened (cup ear). A little boy stood on the hillside and listened too. He was so happy to be there. He smiled up at Jesus (big smile). Jesus smiled back at the little boy. He smiled at all the people in the big, big crowd (big smile). His heart was full of love for them. He was happy they had come. Jesus helped the people who had hurt arms and legs. He cared for everyone. He made people’s eyes to see again and the sick to be healthy. Jesus talked about God, His kind Father in heaven. The people loved being with Jesus (imagine sitting there on the hillside).
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/05/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"},"block":{"backgroundColor": "#5f5f5fad"}}}
The day would soon be over. The little boy’s tummy began to rumble (rub tummy). He was hungry! The people beside him were hungry too. They had been there all day without anything to eat! Jesus’ friends wanted to help them. But how? Jesus asked them to find out if anyone had something to eat. Mothers shook their heads . . . no (shake head). Fathers shook their heads . . . no (shake head). No one had any food! The little boy looked in his basket. He had some food! (Clap hands.) He would share it with Jesus. The little boy carried his basket to Jesus. He gave it to Jesus’ friends. Oh, how happy he was to share with Jesus! His friends quickly looked inside the basket. There were five loaves of bread and two little fish. It was a big lunch for a little boy. But it was a very small lunch for a very big, big crowd of people! How would Jesus feed so many with so little? Jesus knew. He prayed and thanked God for the food.

One by one, Jesus took one . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five loaves of bread from the basket and broke them. There became more and more bread! Enough bread for everyone! Jesus then took one . . . two fish from the basket. Would there be enough fish too? There was! Everyone had so much to eat. The little boy was amazed. Jesus had made his food grow and grow to feed everyone.\
(Say together) Thank You, Jesus, for the gifts You give me so that I can help care for others.
