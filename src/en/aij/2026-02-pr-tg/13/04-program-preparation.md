Opening Activities

Source a **variety of grains** (wheat, barley, rice, oats, corn, quinoa, etc.) for children to experience. Use **containers** to hold the different grains. Source **scoops and cups**. Make some **labels** with the different names of the grains and hide them within the grain containers.

Write these Bible verses (ICB) on **small pieces of paper** and hide within the containers for the children to “notice” and find:

- “God even knows how many hairs are on your head” (Matthew 10:30).
- “Before I made you in your mother’s womb, I chose you” (Jeremiah 1:5).
- “Lord . . . you know all about me” (Psalm 139:1).
- Lord, “you know my thoughts before I think them” (Psalm 139:2).
- Lord, “you have put your hand on me” (Psalm 139:5).
- “I am the good shepherd. I know my sheep, and my sheep know me” (John 10:14).
- “Your Father knows the things you need before you ask him” (Matthew 6:8).

Source a **tray** with a **variety of items** of your choice and a **tea towel** to cover the items.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}) and prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **pillowcase** or sack stuffed with **fabric** or **towels** (to use as a sack full of grain).

Write the following discussion questions on **six different pieces of paper**, fold, and place in discrete places around your Sabbath School room for the children to later find, then read and discuss. Questions:

1. What needs did God notice in this story?
2. What did Boaz notice in this story?
3. What did Naomi notice in this story?
4. How did God show His kindness and care to Ruth and Naomi?
5. What attributes of God did Boaz show
6. What kind of things might you do for others to show that you to others? notice their needs?

Find a variety of flowers to show the children.
