Opening Activities

Think of unusual animals to use as prompts in charades for early arrivals.

Source a **large adult T-shirt** that will fit two children inside snugly. Write **“Faithfulness T-shirt”** on the front and write **“Our God is Faithful”** on the back.

Source **objects** and/or **furniture** and set up a short obstacle course. Pairs of children will do this inside the T-shirt, so it needs to be challenging! E.g., going between the legs of a chair or stepping up and over a small stool while remaining in the T-shirt.

Update **Offering Chart.**

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
