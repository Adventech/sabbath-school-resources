**Lesson Summary**

Abram developed a beautiful friendship with God. Because he loved God, recognized His voice, and followed His instructions to leave Ur, others around Abram got to know and love his God by the way he lived and worshipped.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** what it looks like to be God’s friend.
- **Feel** a desire to be God’s friend and share Him with others.
- **Respond** by choosing to be God’s friend and living in a way that tells others about Him as Abram did.
