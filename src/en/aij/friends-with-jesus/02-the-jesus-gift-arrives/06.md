Hidden in a dark attic of a French family home was a colorful little vase. For years it lay unnoticed in an old, dusty shoebox. No one in the family cared about it. In fact, it had been completely forgotten.

The vase had been given as a gift long ago. But no one really liked the gift, so it had been put in a shoebox and stored away in the attic.

One day while the family was cleaning out the attic, the vase was found again.

“H’mm,” Father said, “maybe we should see how much this vase is worth.”

“Yes, let’s find out,” answered Mother. With the shoebox under their arm they climbed into the car and drove to ask an expert.

The expert carefully opened the shoebox and gasped. This was a very rare and very precious vase. The vase was eventually sold for $19 million. That is a very, very large amount of money!

Many years ago, in an old, dusty stable, with noisy animals, lay another treasure. This treasure was worth more than the entire universe. This treasure was Jesus. As He lay in the animal feeding trough, people passed by without caring. No one noticed that the greatest, most valuable treasure ever was right there with them!
