It had been more than 1,500 years since God had made our beautiful world. God tried to turn people’s hearts and minds back to Him again and again, but only a few had listened and responded to His voice (see Genesis 6:5, 11-13). How did God feel about what was happening? Read Genesis 6:6.

God realized that He needed to stop wickedness from spreading like a deadly sickness. He would send a flood to cover the whole world. Water would cover the houses, the land, and even the tallest mountains.

God loved all people, and He wanted to warn them about the Flood. He needed a faithful man to help Him, and He chose Noah, for Noah “was a just man,” and he “walked with God” (Genesis 6:9).

God told Noah about His plans, and Noah listened carefully. He told God that he would do whatever He asked. God told him to build an ark and explained exactly how to do it. He told him its size and shape, the type of wood to use, and how to put it together. It needed to be almost as strong as stone, because there would be a terrible storm. The ark would have one big door on one side, and rooms for both people and animals. Everyone who chose to go through that door and into the ark would be safe from the Flood.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How did you listen to or talk to God today?_

God likes to talk to grown-ups, but He also loves to talk to children like you! God knows your heart, and He sees your hurts and joys—everything that’s on your mind! God loves it when you talk to Him—He loves to listen to you! Talking with God as you would talk to a friend is what prayer really is.
