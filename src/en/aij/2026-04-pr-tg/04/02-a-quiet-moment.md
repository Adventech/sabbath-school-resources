**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever felt as though you were sinking under life’s challenges?

Peter knew this feeling well as the wind howled around him and the waves began to roll toward him. It seemed too much, and he looked around at the storm, taking his eyes off Jesus. The dark water beneath his feet seemed deep, and perhaps he wondered why or how he was walking toward Jesus in the first place.

Perhaps you’re facing difficulties that seem to be crashing upon you today, like the storm Peter was in. Perhaps you’re wondering:

How am I going to pay the rent this week? How am I going to get through this health crisis? How am I going to help my child with their challenges?

As Peter started to sink, he remembered to look up at Jesus. Suddenly the storm and the waves were put into perspective, because Peter was with the One who made all of creation. Whether you’re focused more on the storm or on Jesus, remember that He is there, within arm’s reach from you today. He will help you, comfort you, and do the impossible when you keep your eyes on Him.
