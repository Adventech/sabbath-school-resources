---
title: Organization and Program for Family Groups
---

Where a family conducts its own Sabbath School, a simple program should be followed. At least two officers and a teacher will be required, although because of circumstances one person may be compelled to be both superintendent and teacher. The two officers basically required are a superintendent and a secretary. If there are several adults and a number of children, two teachers will be neces sary; one for adults and one for the children.

A simple Sabbath School program for the extension division that includes a whole family could be as follows:

| Opening Song | 4 min. |
| Prayer | 2 min. |
| Secretary’s Report | 3 min. |
| World Mission Report | 12 min. |
| Mission Offering | 3 min. |
| Records | 2 min. |
| Lesson Study | 30 min. |
| Closing Song | 3 min. |
| Dismissal Prayer | 1 min. |

If the conference isolated group attains to a membership of from ten to fifteen, it may be organized in counsel with the conference or mission Sabbath School director into a regular Sabbath School. If the majority of its members are non-Adventists, it may become a conference Branch Sabbath School and would report as such. Otherwise, all members of the small family Sabbath School group should report as extension division members.

The extension division member is expected to hold Sabbath School at some regular hour, carry out the simple program outlined on the back of the extension division envelope, and send this envelope filled out with the record (using a supplementary card if there are more than one in the school) to the Sabbath School extension division leader at the end of the quarter.

The extension division should never be made an excuse for absence from a regular Sabbath School. If one is able to attend he or she should meet with his or her brethren and sisters for fellowship in study and service.

The financial goal of the extension division member is the same as for every other Sabbath School member: “As God hath pros pered him or her.” The amount we give will be measured by what God has bestowed on us, not by the small sum we have left after we have spent most of His gifts on ourselves. Extension division members should be encouraged to give expense offerings, as well as mission offerings. Such faithful encouragement in support of the work of God at home and abroad has substantially increased mission funds and has often resulted in covering the cost of supplies.
