For 120 years Noah faithfully worked, building the ark according to God’s plan. Yet even his best efforts were never meant to replace his total dependence upon God. Only God can do the work of salvation. “All that man could do was done to render the work perfect, yet the ark could not of itself have withstood the storm which was to come upon the earth. God alone could preserve His servants upon the tempestuous waters” _(Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 95)_.

As a Christian parent, you might feel the pressure to raise your children well, and to secure their salvation. That is a good and noble aim. However, only God can preserve your child through the tempestuous waters of life.

As imperfect human beings in a fallen world, there is no such thing as a perfect parent or a perfect child. We all make mistakes. We all get things wrong. And we cannot predict or control the choices our child will make. At the end of the day, we must surrender these precious little ones into God’s hands, trusting that He will make up for our lack and do what we cannot do.

Be encouraged by this quote, “Parents, when you have faithfully done your duty, to the extent of your ability, you may then in faith ask the Lord to do that for your children which you cannot do” _(Ellen G. White, Child Guidance, p. 256)_. In other words—do your best, and let God do the rest!

> <callout>Philippians 1:6</callout>
> “Being confident of this very thing, that He who has begun a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ.”
