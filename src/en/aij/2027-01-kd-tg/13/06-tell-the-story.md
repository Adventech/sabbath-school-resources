**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. I will remember all His good works.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 13 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Suggested Props: Show some cotton wool or red tissue paper to represent the cloud, **Memory Box** (craft sample), **cards** to place in box (pillar of cloud and fire; bush; painted doorpost; Red Sea; water/manna), **tambourine**, cup of **water, bread**.
- Use peg people or a felt set if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites crowded around God’s new home. It was so beautiful! Every wall, curtain, and piece of furniture had been done exactly the way God had asked.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly, up rose God’s tall cloud ^[(cotton wool or red tissue paper)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It floated toward the sanctuary and came down over His new home. God’s holy sanctuary glowed with God’s brightness and goodness. God had chosen to live with them, just as He’d promised!

Sometimes God’s cloud would lift high above the sanctuary and start to move ^[(cotton wool or red tissue paper)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The people would pack up and quickly follow. They could remember His promise to lead them to Canaan.

God’s cloud was a reminder that He was faithfully leading them ^[(cloud card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses could remember the first time He had seen God’s brightness. Moses had been right here, near Mount Sinai.

Moses had seen a bush. ^[**Do you remember what was happening to the bush?** (God was in the burning bush. He promised to lead His people out of Egypt to this place to worship.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was burning, but it wasn’t burning up! A voice had called out to him from the burning bush, “Moses, Moses!” (Exodus 3:4). God was in the fire!

“I will be with you,” God had said to Moses. “You will lead the people out of Egypt. Then all of you will worship me on this mountain” (Exodus 3:12, ICB).

That’s just what happened! Here they were, worshipping God in His new sanctuary at Mount Sinai.

They could remember the burning bush and God keeping His promise ^[(bush card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The children raced through the camp with their friends. Others ran from their tents to join in. But just a few months before, back in Egypt, there had been no playtime. All day long the children had worked, worked, worked. They had been slaves, just as their parents had been slaves.

God had seen and saved them! God showed His power with 10 plagues and set them free. **^[What plagues do you remember?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The children could remember the plagues and Passover. They could remember that God is mighty to save ^[(painted doorpost card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Miriam heard the jingling of her tambourine as she brushed past it. ^[(Ring tambourine.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She began to hum. There was a song that all of Israel had sung together. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She could remember it well. It told the story of God’s saving them. It would help them always remember God’s miracle.

**^[What had God done for His people at the Red Sea?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})** ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had opened up a dry path right through the middle of the Red Sea. He had saved them from their enemies.

They could remember that God was their protector ^[(Red Sea card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Father passed a cup of water to those in his family. ^[(Show cup.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Sip, slurp._ All the people of Israel had water in this dry desert.

**^[How had God given them miracle water to drink?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})** ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When they’d found bitter water, God had made it sweet. When they’d found no water, God had made it pour from a rock.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mother passed around sweet manna cakes to eat. ^[(Show bread.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Mmm, yum!_ Every morning, except for Sabbath, God had sent bread from heaven. Manna was all they needed.

The Israelites could remember and have thankful hearts to God. They could remember that God had provided for them ^[(water and manna card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God wanted the Israelites to always remember what He’d done for them so that they could be happy and thankful. He knew this would help them to keep trusting Him. He wants us to do this too.
