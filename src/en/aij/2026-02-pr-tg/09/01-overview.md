**Word of the Week**

**Character:** All the qualities that make a person who they are. A person’s character will be shown by how they act, think, and feel. A person with a good character will act, think, and feel in good ways, such as being honest, respectful, responsible, caring, kind, and fair.

**Lesson Summary**

In jail Joseph displayed a godly character by looking for ways he could be a blessing to those around him. One day God enabled Joseph to interpret the dreams of two prisoners, a baker and a butler, and his interpretations came true. The baker lost his life; the butler returned to work for Pharaoh. Two years later Pharaoh had a dream and was anxious to know the meaning. The butler remembered Joseph and told Pharaoh about him. As a result, Joseph was brought out of jail to interpret Pharaoh’s dream and gave him wise counsel. Pharaoh saw that God was with Joseph, and put him second in command over Egypt.
