**Big Idea:** Jesus is God’s precious gift to me.

**TELL THE STORY**

Mary’s pregnant tummy grew bigger and bigger. And so did her excitement! God’s promised Savior God was growing inside her ^[(lay gold crepe paper or fabric across a female leader’s tummy)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and He would soon be born!

Mary patted her belly in wonder. The angel had told her, “That Holy One who is to be born will be called the Son of God” (Luke 1:35). God’s promised gift would soon arrive, and it would be the very best gift of all.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Why was God giving this gift? Read John 3:16** (your memory verse). (Because He loved the world so much.)

One day messengers rushed into town. Trumpets blew ^[(trumpet noise/action—blow two leaves together)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They had important news from Caesar Augustus, the ruler of Rome. Everyone across the empire was to be counted, and so they were ordered to go to the town their family had come from. They would be put on a tax list.

This was big news for Mary and Joseph! With a lot of other people they would need to leave Nazareth and travel a long way to Bethlehem. ^[(Pour out round dried beans on a baking tray to represent the crowd. Add the small block of wood as Mary and Joseph’s donkey.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They both came from the family of King David, who had been born there.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With Joseph’s help, Mary climbed on their little donkey and tried to get comfortable. Off they went. _Clip-clop. Bump-bump. Clip-clop. Bump-bump._ It was a tiring, bumpy journey for pregnant Mary.

God had told Micah the prophet that His Son would be born in Bethlehem. They were headed to that very town. It was just as God had said! ^[(Tilt the tray to make them “move” in one direction towards the other end of the tray, marked “Bethlehem.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After four or five days of dusty, bumpy travel, they finally arrived in Bethlehem. _Phew!_ ^[(Place the sticks in circle for the wall of a town and small houses within. Keep an opening for the “gate.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph and Mary walked wearily through the gates. With a sigh ^[(sigh)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), they joined the crowds of travelers looking for a place to stay. ^[(Gather the “crowd” dried beans inside the town walls.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[(Hold two beans and the “donkey” wood block.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They tried the inn ^[(shake head)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). There was no room in the inn. No room in a cozy home, no room in a rooftop shelter—no room anywhere!

In the town of King David, a baby King was about to be born. He would be the most important King ever, for He is called “Lord of lords and King of kings” (Revelation 17:14). But the people in Bethlehem didn’t even know it.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At last, Mary and Joseph found a place to sleep—a stable. (Identify “stable” with stick surrounds.) A cow mooed and a sheep bleated as Joseph opened the creaky door and shone a lantern inside. They quietly made a bed of clean straw among the animals. Then they smiled a tired smile as they finally lay down. They were content, because they trusted God.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) That night, in the quiet of the stable, God’s most precious gift was born. What a strange, messy place for the King of kings to arrive in! But Mary and Joseph just looked in wonder at the tiny Baby as they laid Him in a manger. (Place small bean inside “stable” area.) This was God’s Son! There were no words to catch their wonder. Mary softly spoke His name: “Jesus.” ^[(Drape gold crepe paper/fabric around the “stable.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Sing a verse of “Silent Night” together.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What does Jesus’ name mean? Read Matthew 1:21 together.** (“He will save His people from their sins.”)

The meaning of a name was very important in Bible times. Whenever Jesus’ name was spoken, people would know He was their Savior. What a special thought to keep saying out loud.

Jesus would be called by other names, too. Isaiah 7:14 calls Him “Immanuel,” which means “God is with us.” Jesus was also known as “Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace” (Isaiah 9:6). Jesus was just too amazing to have only one name!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary wrapped Jesus gently, cuddled Him, and fed Him. Such a tiny body for God to fit into! But this little baby would do big, BIG things. Jesus would live and die to show the whole world the most beautiful picture of God’s love anyone could ever show.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Sing a verse of **“Away in a Manger”** together. Invite children to use their hands to bring the words to life as they sing.

- **What surprises you in this story?** (That God’s Son, the King of kings, was born in a humble stable, surrounded by animals. That He slept in a manger. That the people of Bethlehem didn’t welcome Him.)
- **What amazing things would Jesus do when He grew up to be a man?** (Jesus died on the cross for my sins so that I can choose to go to heaven to live with Him forever, healed sick people, performed miracles, preached to many people, spent time with a close circle of friends.)
- **If you had been there in that stable, how might you have shown Baby Jesus what He means to you, knowing He will change your future and give you life forever in heaven?**
