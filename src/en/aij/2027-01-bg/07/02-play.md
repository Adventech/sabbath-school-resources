Learn

Teach your child how God created different foods to help different parts of our body. Slice a carrot and show how it looks like an eye and is good for our eyesight. Talk about how the walnut looks like a little brain and is good for our brain. Cut open a tomato and explain how it’s red and has four chambers, just like our heart. Show them a stalk of celery and tell them how it looks like a bone and helps to make our bones strong. Let them feel each food and taste them, reminding them that God made vegetables, fruits, and nuts to help our bodies be strong and healthy.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#70a041"}}}
Care

Visit a local farm or petting zoo where your child can hand-feed different animals. Talk about what it would have been like for Noah and his family to feed all the animals on the ark each day. Look at books or animal videos online as an alternative.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#70a041"}}}
Explore

Visit a local vegetable garden or farm where your child can see how different foods grow. Talk about how God gives us sunshine and rain to make foods grow and how He always provides what we need.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#70a041"}}}
Create

Make animal shapes using fruits and vegetables. You might like to use dried fruit and nut butter or nuts and hummus to stick on eyes and spots. Arrange them on the plate and have your child guess what the animal is. Let them have a turn making their own animal too, then enjoy eating the healthy snacks together! _(See examples above for inspiration.)_

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#70a041"}}}
Sing and Pray

Invite your child to make up a song about the foods God has given them. Sing it together and thank God for good food.
