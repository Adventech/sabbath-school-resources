**Have you ever seen a capybara before?**

These very friendly creatures are found in South America. They live near water areas, and other animals really like being friends with them! What else can we learn about the capybara?

- They have a little nose, short legs, brown hair, and almost no tail.
- Their long teeth never stop growing.
- They eat grass and water plants.
- Their ears, eyes, and nostrils are on top of their heads so they can stay just above the water.
- They have webbed feet, which means they are very good swimmers!

**Why do other animals like being friends with them?**

- If a small animal is on its own and doesn’t have a family, a capybara will often adopt them.
- They allow smaller animals to sit on them or beside them for a cuddle or ride. Birds will rest on their backs, helping to groom them; small monkeys will play or take a ride on their backs. Turtles and ducks just enjoy sitting beside them for happy company. Even though these smaller animals are different from them, it does not seem to matter to the friendly capybara. What a friendly, kind, and gentle creature God made!
- **How can you be like the capybara this week?**
- **Can you think of someone who is different from you? What can you do to be their friend and show kindness to them?**

Repeat **Big Idea: God is with me. He helps me to be kind.**
