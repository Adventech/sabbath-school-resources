```=Week 1: Hannah Prays for a Baby

Cut a circle piece of fabric with a diameter of 6 inches (15 centimeters). Trace around a cardboard cylinder in the center of the fabric and cut out the circle, so you end up with a doughnut-shaped “skirt.” Print week 1 printable from AIJ website and cut out the figure of Hannah. Children can then color and glue the printable onto a cardboard cylinder. Then glue around the middle of the cardboard cylinder to stick the fabric skirt. Retell the story while your child flips Hannah from sad to happy, showing how God answered her prayer.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** week 1 printable, cardboard cylinder, colored pencils, scissors, glue, circle piece of fabric.
```

```=Week 2: A Little Helper

Cut out a paper circle with a diameter of roughly 4 inches (10 centimeters). Fold the circle in half, and then half again so you end up with a quadrant. Cut slits around the bottom edge of the quadrant and ruffle them. Cut across the top of the quadrant and glue a craft stick in between the folds. Glue a strip of colored paper horizontally across the top. Children can pretend to sweep with their mini brooms, just like Samuel.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** paper circle 4 inches (10 cm diameter), craft stick, small paper rectangle, scissors, glue
```

```=Week 3: Samuel Listens to God

On a white piece of cardstock, write the child’s name in large letters using a white crayon. Explain how there are lots of people who don’t know us. The child can then paint with watercolors, revealing the letters of their name. Share how even if other people don’t know us, God knows our name and everything about us!

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** white piece of cardstock, white crayon, paint brush, watercolor paints, water.
```

```=Week 4: Ruth and Naomi

Cut out and glue two oval pieces of brown paper onto two colored semicircles. At the top of each face, glue pieces of colored yarn for hair. Draw or glue on the eyes, then draw the rest of the face. Stick a craft stick to the back of each face. Explain how Ruth and Naomi would always “stick” together because they cared about each other. Children can use the stick puppets to show how Ruth and Naomi journeyed together.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** 2 brown paper ovals, 2 colored paper semi-circles, pieces of yarn, 4 googly eyes, 2 craft sticks, black pen/marker, glue.
```

```=Week 5: Kind Boaz

Draw a horizontal line in the center of two paper plates. Cut out the top half of both paper plates along the inner circle, so you have two semicircle-shaped holes. On the “handle,” write the words: “God takes care of me.” Children can dip a disposable fork into orange or brown paint and make a basket pattern on the outside of the paper plates. Once dry, adults can assist with stapling or gluing the paper plates together to make a basket for children to store pretend grain stalks, like Ruth.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** 2 paper plates, scissors, plastic fork, orange/brown paint, stapler, grass stalks.
```

```=Week 6: I Can Help My Family

Cut out a beak and two feet from orange paper, an oval shape from white paper, and two oval wing shapes from black paper. Glue a black strip of paper around a cardboard cylinder. Alternatively, you can glue a black semicircle onto an upside-down paper cup. Glue a smaller semicircle out of white paper onto the black. Then glue on a beak and feet out of orange paper. Last, stick on googly eyes and two wings out of black paper. Provide a cotton ball as a pretend egg. Children can show how daddy penguins keep the baby egg warm by resting it on his feet and covering it with his tummy (placing the penguin over the top so the cotton ball is hidden).

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** cardboard cylinder (or paper cup); black, white and orange paper; googly eyes; pencil; scissors; glue; cotton ball.
```

```=Week 7: I Can Help Others

Cut out a nose from black paper and ears from white felt. On the back of a paper plate, trace around a glue stick to draw two circles. Below the circles, glue a black “nose” in the center. Then draw two curved lines from the bottom of the nose to the sides of the paper plate. Cut along these lines, then glue two pieces of felt to the top corners for ears. Next, tape a craft stick to the back. (Adults will need to cut out the eye circles.) Children can use their puppy mask and pretend to help like the guide dogs in today’s story.

[If measurements are helpful, the middle of the eyes should be roughly 2.33 inches (6 centimeters) from the top of the plate and .75 inches (2 centimeters) apart. The middle of the nose should be roughly 3.5 inches (9 centimeters) from the top.]

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** paper plate, pencil, two pieces of felt, black circle for nose, craft stick, scissors, glue, sticky tape.
```

```=Week 8: I Can Help God

Decorate a paper cup, using markers and stickers. Poke holes in the bottom of the cup, using a pen. Fill the cup with soil. Poke a hole in the soil with your finger and drop in a seed. Lightly cover it with soil, give it a little water, and place it in the sun. Share with the children how they can help flowers and plants grow just like the bees and squirrels in today’s story.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** paper cup, colored markers, stickers, pen, flower seeds, soil.
```

```=Week 9: The Shining Angel

Adults can assist with gluing the ends together to make a cone. Draw a face onto small circle and glue it to the top of the cone. Place glue along the center of the paper wings and stick to the back of the cone. Last, children can decorate with glitter glue and leave it to dry. At bedtime they can place their paper angel over a battery-operated candle to make their own “shining angel” nightlight.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** week 9 printable, scissors, glue, glitter glue, star stickers, battery-operated candle.
```

```=Week 10: The Journey to Bethlehem

Draw or print a donkey’s body onto cardstock. Glue pieces of black yarn for the tail and mane. Then glue on a googly eye and a small piece of fabric for the saddle. Attach two wooden clothespins for the legs. Use a brown marker to color “hoofs” onto the bottom of the clothespins. Children can have fun making their donkey clip-clop, like Mary’s donkey on the journey to Bethlehem.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** week 10 printable, cardboard, pencil, black yarn, googly eye, piece of fabric, 2 wooden clothespins, glue, black and brown markers.
```

```=Week 11: Born in a Stable

Precut a yellow triangle, a colored oval, and a small brown circle shape. Adults can help children glue four craft sticks to make a “stable” shape onto dark blue or black cardstock. At the bottom of a yellow paper triangle, cut vertical slits and glue point-side down in the center of the stable, ruffling the strips of paper so they stick out. Underneath, glue two halves of a craft stick so they overlap to complete the “manger.” Draw a face onto the brown paper circle and glue it onto a colored oval shape. Slide “Baby Jesus” into the top of the manger. Stick a star above the stable and dot the rest of the sky with glitter glue.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** dark blue or black cardstock, yellow triangle, colored oval, brown paper circle, 5 craft sticks, glue, glitter, black marker.
```

```=Week 12: The Shepherds

Precut a small angel shape out of thick cardstock, as well as hills out of green paper. Children can glue the green hills onto the bottom third of a black piece of cardstock. Then glue pieces of cotton balls on the green paper for sheep. On the black section, adults can assist children in tracing around the angel stencil with white chalk several times. They can then smudge the chalk with their fingers and spread glitter glue to make the angels look like they’re shining above the fields of Bethlehem.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** small cardboard angel shape, black piece of cardstock, green paper, cotton balls, white chalk, glue stick, glitter glue.

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg-tg/assets/12a.jpg)
```

```=Week 13: The Wise Men

Print out star and Wise Man shapes onto cardstock. Children can decorate the shapes with crayons and glitter glue. On the back of each Wise Man, adults can help them tape the end of a piece of yarn or string, tying the top of each string to a plastic coat hanger. Children can stick the star onto the coat hanger with double-sided or folded tape and hang it up in their room. The mobile can remind them how they can follow God, like the Wise Men who followed the star.

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** week 13 printable, scissors, crayons, pieces of yarn/string, sticky-tape, glitter glue, plastic coat hanger.
```
