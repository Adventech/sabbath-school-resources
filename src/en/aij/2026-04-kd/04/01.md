The End of a Wonderful Day

_(Remind your child that they learned about the feeding of the 5,000 last quarter.)_

The sun began to set as the big crowd of people munched and crunched on their miracle lunch. It was late in the day to eat lunch. But no one complained. It had been the best meal ever. Jesus had fed over 5,000 people!

The great big crowd of people sat on the mountainside, full of food, and full of joy. They talked happily to each other about all they had seen Jesus do that day.

His words had taught them. His hands had healed the sick. And then, to finish off the day, Jesus had turned a small lunch basket of barley loaves and fish into many, many baskets of food. He had fed everyone’s empty tummies!

“All the people ate and were satisfied” (Matthew 14:20, ICB).

The day was quickly slipping away. Time went quickly when the people were with Jesus. But the people didn’t want to go home. If only they could be with Jesus forever. . . .

Wouldn’t it be wonderful to be with Jesus forever? We can! Because Jesus died and rose again, we can live with Him forever. **Read Jesus’ words in John 10:28.**

The people made themselves comfortable on the hillside. They leaned in even closer to Jesus. They would keep on listening and talking with Him . . . for just a little bit longer.

- When did you have such a good day that you didn’t want it to end?
- What do you want to do with Jesus when you get to heaven?
