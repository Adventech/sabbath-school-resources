**Focus:** We put God first by keeping His commandments.

**Location:** Invite a child to help you find **Serbia** on a **map** or **globe**.

Marta loved playing the violin. She had played ever since she was a little girl. Now that she was 14, she dreamed of going to a special music school in the city. To get into the school, Marta needed to pass a test by playing her violin. But there was one problem. The test was on Saturday, the Sabbath.

What should she do? Marta knew the fourth commandment, which says, “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. Six days you shall labor and do all your work, but the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord your God. In it you shall do no work” (Exodus 20:8-10). Marta knew that if she wanted to keep the Sabbath holy, she should not do the test.

Marta was sad about missing out, but she trusted God. Marta, her parents, and church friends prayed about this.

The music school invited Marta’s parents to a meeting. Marta’s father explained the Sabbath commandment and asked if Marta could take the test another day. The principal said, “That’s not possible. The date of the test has never been changed before.” Marta’s father asked if she could take the test on Saturday evening after sunset. The principal said it would be too late, but promised she would try to help.

Marta waited. She kept praying and practicing her violin. Then a few days before the test, the phone rang. It was a teacher from the music school. She said that Marta would be able to take the test on Saturday evening, after the Sabbath!

On the night of the test Marta played her violin so well that the teachers gave her a perfect score. She was accepted into the music school!

Even though Marta loved music school, she loved God more, and would always put Him first.
