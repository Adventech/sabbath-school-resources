Moses beamed with joy as he led his family to his tent to rest. He welcomed inside his father-in-law, Jethro; wife, Zipporah; and his two sons. It was so good to be with his loving family again. Now his tent felt like home.

They had so much to talk about! “Moses told his father-in-law everything the Lord had done to the king and the Egyptians. . . . Moses told about all the problems they had faced along the way. And Moses told him how the Lord had saved them” (Exodus 18:8, ICB).

Imagine Jethro’s eyes when Moses described the Passover night, the dry path through the sea, and the water from the rock. There were so many miracles to tell! “Jethro was very happy when he heard all the good things the Lord had done for Israel” (Exodus 18:9, ICB). It helped him to trust God even more.

Jethro wanted to show God his thanks. What did he do? **Read Exodus 18:12.**

Everyone eating the special meal remembered what God had done, and were thankful. When we have family worship together, we can also help each other come closer to God. We can share Bible stories and tell each other how God has helped us. And like Jethro, we can thank God for saving us. The Bible says, “Give thanks to the Lord and pray to him. . . . Sing praises to him. Tell about all the wonderful things he has done” (Psalm 105:1, 2, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Which miracle would you have wanted to tell Jethro about first?
- What might you like to share with your family about God?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for His amazing, saving love for you.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
**Boys:** pretend to be Moses, talking to Jethro about the miracles God did in Egypt and in the desert.\
**Girls:** pretend to be Zipporah, talking to Moses about what he has told you. You might like to put on a costume, or tie a cloth around your head as you talk.

^[Mission Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Show or tell your grandfather or grandmother how much you love them and how much God loves them. If they live far away, call them to share this with them.

```=Memory Verse

“Let all that you do be done with love” (1 Corinthians 16:14).
```
