**Opening Activities**

Source containers to make simple joyful shakers for the children to decorate, then play. You could use **cardboard rolls, yogurt tubs, or juice bottles**. Fill with **beans, rice**, or **grains** and **seal**. If your container needs a lid, you can make one from **paper** and fix in place with **rubber bands**.

Make several joyful shakers ahead of time for children who may come later. Decorate with **markers and stickers**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: Wrap a **box in plain brown paper** and draw sad face onto it. Place inside **smiley-face stickers or fruit** that’s ready and easy to eat.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
