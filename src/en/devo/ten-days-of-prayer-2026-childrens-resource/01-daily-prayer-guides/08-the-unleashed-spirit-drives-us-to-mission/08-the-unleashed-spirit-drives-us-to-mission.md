The floating map showed they had arrived...

“It’s right here,” Orel said, pointing to the South Wall of the New Jerusalem. “But I can’t quite see where the person we’re looking for is.”

“I’m sure he’ll show up soon,” Luca said, gazing at the tall wall of the city, which was made of jasper. The wall reflected varied colors—red, yellow, brown, and dark blue. “Look at the wall—it’s truly amazing.”

“Not just the wall—have you seen the foundations?” Orel asked, looking all around as he kept watch for the person who, according to the map, should be nearby.

“Wow! So many precious stones! I only recognize some of them.

Here is jasper, like in the wall, and these green ones must be emeralds. And there are several of deep blue color—they must be sapphires…”

Out of nowhere, a man appeared behind Luca and said, “Little boy, you have a very good eye for details—and I must add, a very curious mind.”

“Luca, let me introduce you to Philip the Evangelist,” Orel said with a wide smile. “He always enjoys making dramatic entrances and today is no exception. For a moment, I thought my map had an error. But how good to find you, Philip—or was it you who found us?”

“I saw you arrive a few minutes ago and wanted to surprise you,” Philip said with a smile.

“I remember reading somewhere that you were guided directly by the Holy Spirit,” Luca said thoughtfully. “Orel, what does the Bible say about Philip?”

“It says many things, Luca. Philip was a powerful instrument in God’s hands,” Orel said, searching for a text on the floating glass.

“You’re talking about me while I’m standing right here!” Philip laughed. “I can tell you myself how miraculous it was to work with the Holy Spirit. I have so much to share.”

“We’re learning about what the Holy Spirit did when He was unleashed in the lives of some of Jesus’ followers,” Luca said enthusiastically. “In your case, the Spirit used you in the mission of evangelism—bringing people to the feet of Jesus.”

“That’s right!” Orel said. “Here’s a special moment we want to know about. I’ll read Acts 8:29. The Bible says, ‘The Spirit told Philip, “Go to that chariot and stay near it.”’ Did the Spirit really lead you directly to a person to evangelize them?”

“Oh yes! It was definitely the Holy Spirit!” Philip quickly responded. “Ever since I met Jesus, my heart burned with the desire for Him to return soon. Listening to the apostles, I understood that for Him to return, the gospel had to be preached in every nation, tribe, language, and people.

“So I committed myself to becoming an evangelist, a preacher of the gospel, to see Jesus return in my own generation. That’s when the Holy Spirit was unleashed upon me, and I simply let myself be guided by Him in the great work of saving the lost.”

“What a wonderful testimony, Philip!” said Luca. “I also wanted to be a preacher on Earth. Did the Spirit guide you to a chariot in the middle of the road? What happened?”

“I was just about to tell you,” Philip said. “The Holy Spirit led me to preach to an Ethiopian man. He had traveled far to reach Israel and learn about Jesus, but no one in Israel had explained it to him, and he was returning home confused and discouraged.

“The Spirit identified those who sincerely longed to know Jesus and sent us to meet them,” Philip said joyfully. “The rest of the story is the Spirit unleashing Himself in their lives, transforming them into disciples of Jesus and driving them to fulfill the mission...”

“There you are, my friend Philip!” A deep, calm voice spoke from behind them. The three of them turned around.

“Well, we were just talking about you!” Philip said, running to embrace the man who had just appeared. “I present to you my friend from Ethiopia—yes, the very one who was in that chariot and was baptized along the way. I met him again here in the Holy City!”

“Pleased to meet you, friends of Philip,” the Ethiopian said. “Forgive me for having to take Philip away, but I have gathered more than 500 people who came to Jesus in Ethiopia—thanks to God and to my missionary work—and now they are also here in heaven! They all want to thank Philip. So, I must take him with me. Don’t worry—we’ll have plenty of time to talk and get to know each other better.”

“500 redeemed! Wow!” Luca and Orel exclaimed at the same time. “No problem—we’ll see you soon, Philip.”

Orel lit up his floating map, tapped a few buttons, and the familiar red light began to blink…

“You won’t want to miss the next character,” Orel said. “Even I am excited to go meet him. Are you ready?”

“Now you’ve got me excited too! Let’s go!”

### Let’s Get to Work!

1. Memorize Psalm 23 or another psalm, and ask your pastor or teacher to give you the opportunity to recite it at your church or school.
2. Prepare a sermon or a short devotional and share it with your family, your friends, or—if possible—at your church or school.

### Let’s Pray!

Here are some prayer topics. **Pray several times throughout this day.** Remember to pray personally and also involve your family and friends.

1. Pray that the Holy Spirit may be unleashed in your life and drive you always to fulfill the mission.
2. Pray that the Holy Spirit may help you win other people for Jesus.
