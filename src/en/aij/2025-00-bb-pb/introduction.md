### Parent Book

The Baby Steps Parent Book is written specifically to encourage parents and caregivers by sharing ideas and offering practical strategies as you nurture the spiritual life of your baby.

Toward the end of this book, you will find four simple story programs. These programs will be mirrored in Sabbath School (one per quarter).

Each day, when your baby is content, you can follow the simple worship ideas. These same ideas will be repeated every Sabbath at Sabbath School.

Your baby will learn to love the repetition of the songs, stories, and props as they’re repeated every day at home and every week at Sabbath School.
