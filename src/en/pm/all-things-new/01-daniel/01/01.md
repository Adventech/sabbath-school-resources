[[Daniel 1:1-8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Daniel and his friends objected to the king’s food for at least two reasons. First, it was unhealthy and would make them weaker physically and mentally. Second, the food almost certainly had been dedicated to the gods of Babylon before being placed on the king’s table. To eat the food would have shown allegiance to those gods—something that Daniel and his friends could never do.

[[Daniel 1:9-21]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Today we know better than ever that Daniel and his friends were right: A healthy diet that also avoids alcohol and other mind-clouding substances makes us stronger mentally and physically.
- Eating the healthiest foods we can find and avoiding drugs and alcohol will also give us strength as we become people of justice, goodness, and love.
- Daniel and his friends would not compromise their loyalty to the God of heaven and earth. In return, God gave them special skills and abilities that will be dramatically put to the test in chapter 2.
