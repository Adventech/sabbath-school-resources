The fever was bad. Simon Peter’s mother-in-law lay in her bed, very hot and very sick. Her family looked at her with worry. Then they looked up into the kind eyes of Jesus. Yes—surely Jesus could make her better! They “asked Jesus to do something to help her” (Luke 4:38, ICB).

What does the Bible tell us to do when we have worries or problems? **Read 1 Peter 5:7.**

Jesus walked across the room to stand beside the sick woman. Her eyes were closed; her body was still. Jesus tenderly picked up her hand. With power He told the fever to leave.

Everyone in the room held their breath and watched. Suddenly her eyes fluttered open. She sat up and looked around.

Simon’s mother-in-law looked up at the lovely face of Jesus. She smiled a big smile, and Jesus smiled back. She felt so much better! Her body had been made strong and healthy again. What an amazing miracle!

She was so thankful to Jesus. After many smiles and words of thanks, she ran to the kitchen. The sound of clinking cups and clattering pots could be heard. Then Simon’s mother-in-law began serving food and drinks for all her guests.

Jesus must have smiled as she handed Him a plate of food. What a lovely way to say thank You to Jesus for His tender care and healing miracle.
