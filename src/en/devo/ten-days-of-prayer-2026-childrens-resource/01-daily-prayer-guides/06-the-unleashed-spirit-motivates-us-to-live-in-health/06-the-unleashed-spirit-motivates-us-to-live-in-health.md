Bushes full of flowers and fruits all lined up in perfect order, divided by their kind and species. Beautiful butterflies shining in the sunlight, fluttering everywhere. The most incredible birds are running or flying freely across the vast and beautiful field.

“We have arrived at the Heavenly Meadow!” Orel said, pointing toward the bushes. “Although there are delicious fruit trees scattered throughout the New Jerusalem, here almost every variety of fruit and vegetables are planted and harvested to feed all the residents of the Holy City.”

“Wow! I just hope broccoli tastes good here!” Luca laughed loudly. “On Earth, we used to eat meat, but I see that here no one needs that.”

“The answer is simple,” said a man stepping out from among the bushes. He wore a beautiful white robe with a red border at the hem.

“When God created the Earth, He designed human beings to eat only plants and seeds. In them they would find everything necessary to live in health. But with the entrance of sin, humanity moved away from God’s ideal plan and began killing animals for food.”

“God allowed meat to be introduced into the human diet after the flood,” Orel added.

“There weren’t many options for vegetables after the flood! But even then, God established what was clean to eat and what was not. Yet people continued to drift away from God’s original plan, and this brought serious consequences to their health.”

“There’s no need to remember the sad past! Everything is new here!” the man said, extending a hand to Luca. “My name is John. On Earth they called me John the Baptist, but here in heaven everyone is already baptized by the Holy Spirit.” John laughed joyfully. “So, I don’t have much baptizing work to do here, right?”

“John the Baptist! What an enormous privilege!” Luca said with excitement. “The one who prepared the way for Jesus to come to Earth. Wow! My name is Luca, and this is Orel.”

“Pleased to meet you!” John said, shaking their hands. “I often come here. I love feeling the wind and being surrounded by nature. In the quiet, I can meditate and learn more about my good Jesus. He was my cousin on Earth, but I always knew He was the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world—and He did!”

Orel came closer to Luca, holding the floating glass where a Bible verse was highlighted. “Here’s an interesting verse about our friend John.” With his finger he pointed to Luke 1:15 and read, “…for he will be great in the sight of the Lord. He is never to take wine or other fermented drink, and he will be filled with the Holy Spirit even before he is born.”

“What memories!” John said. “My father Zechariah couldn’t believe it, and God took away his voice until the day I was born. We still tease him about that…”

“We’re learning about the Holy Spirit,” Orel told John. “Your case is interesting, John. You were chosen before you were born to prepare people’s hearts to accept Jesus as the promised Messiah. The Spirit was unleashed in your life even from your mother’s womb. What did that mean for you?”

“From a very young age, my parents taught me to fear God and showed me the ministry I was to carry out,” John replied. “The Spirit was unleashed upon me and motivated me to live simply and in good health so I could face the challenges of preaching a message no one wanted to hear—especially to the political and religious leaders during my time on Earth.

“It was very hard… I lived a life full of persecution, mistreatment, and rejection by most people. But my greatest joy was knowing that Jesus truly was the Messiah God had promised through His prophets. He fulfilled every description perfectly.

“I had to hold on until He became the greatest—the Light who shines in the world and removes the darkness.” John spoke all this with great emotion in his voice.

“I had a tragic ending on Earth… They cut off my head, and my eyes closed… until I was resurrected with the righteous and saw my Savior in the clouds of heaven. Everything I suffered on Earth seems insignificant compared to what we live here in heaven with Jesus.”

“Wow!” Luca said. “I don’t know if I could have endured everything you went through, John…”

“The Spirit showed me I needed a healthy body,” John answered. “I climbed and descended mountains. I walked hundreds of miles carrying God’s message. That helped me a lot—it gave me physical, mental, and spiritual strength. As the verse said, I was prepared from before I was born for this task.”

“Your story is unique, John,” said Luca. “What will you dedicate yourself to now in heaven?”

“Good question, Luca,” John smiled. “I want to learn everything about nature. I’m curious about how plants produce flowers, and then how flowers become fruit. I’m learning so much here in the Heavenly Meadow. Anyway, I have eternity to discover all the mysteries of God’s creation.”

“We’ll have many opportunities to keep talking, dear John,” Orel said as the red dot blinked again on the floating map. “Luca, it’s time to go. Let’s go!”

“Thank you, John, for explaining more about the Holy Spirit,” Luca said. “I’m sure we’ll meet again.”

Both of them left for their next destination.

### Let’s Get to Work!

1. Prepare a basket with healthy foods and give it to someone in need.
2. Ask your parents or teachers to go with you when you deliver the basket.
3. Pray for that person, and ask your parents or teachers to help you find Bible verses about faith in Jesus to read to those who are going through difficult times.

### Let’s Pray!

Here are some prayer topics. **Pray several times throughout this day.** Remember to pray personally and also involve your family and friends.

1. Pray that the Holy Spirit may be unleashed in your life and give you the motivation to live in health.
2. Pray that the Holy Spirit may help you be a powerful instrument in preaching the message of Jesus.
