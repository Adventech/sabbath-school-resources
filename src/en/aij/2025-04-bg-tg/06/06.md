**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: I can be God’s helper.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- Welcome children and parents by smiling. Tell the children you are happy to see them today!
- Sing **^[“Tick-Tock.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give the children some **sticks** to tap together as they sing. Tell the children that it’s wonderful that they came to Sabbath School today.

**WEEK 6: I CAN HELP MY FAMILY**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag:** Pictures of animal families (dolphins, lions, penguins)

Ask: **Who is in your family?** Invite children to point to a parent or caregiver or say the name of a sibling. **God made families to love and help each other!**

Show the picture of the dolphin family. **God created dolphins to swim with their big families called pods. The mommies help their babies by giving them their milk to drink. The baby dolphins swim in the middle of the pod so they stay safe.** Ask: **Can you see them? They use their long noses to keep danger away. Dolphins help each other by working together to catch fish** (move your hands together like a fish).

Show the picture of the lion family. **Do you know the sound a lion makes? _Roar!_ God made lions to live in big families called _prides_. The mommy lions look for food and daddy lions care for the baby cubs.** Ask: **Can you point to the cubs? God made mommy lions to be very fast so that they can catch their food. The daddy lions look after the cubs while the mommy lions find dinner.**

Show the picture of the penguin family. **God made penguins to help their families too! The mommy penguin lays an egg and then carefully gives it to the daddy penguin. The egg needs to stay warm in the snow, so the daddy puts the egg on his feet and covers it with his tummy.** Ask: **How would you stand if you had an egg on your feet? He looks after the egg for a long time while the mommy is away catching fish to feed her new chick. All the daddy penguins help each other stay warm by huddling close together.**

Open your Bible, point, and say: **God tell us in the Bible that “Through love serve one another”** (Galatians 5:13). **God made dolphins, lions, and penguins to help their families. He made our families that way too. What do you do to help your family? It makes God happy when we work together to help in our families.** Invite the children to give their parent/caregiver a hug.

Say Together: **I can help my family.**

```=Craft

Cut out a beak and two feet from orange paper, an oval shape from white paper, and two oval wing shapes from black paper. Glue a black strip of paper around a cardboard cylinder. Alternatively, you can glue a black semicircle onto an upside-down paper cup. Glue a smaller semicircle out of white paper onto the black. Then glue on a beak and feet out of orange paper. Last, stick on googly eyes and two wings out of black paper. Provide a cotton ball as a pretend egg. Children can show how daddy penguins keep the baby egg warm by resting it on his feet and covering it with his tummy (placing the penguin over the top so the cotton ball is hidden).

**^[What you need:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#418fcd"}}})** cardboard cylinder (or paper cup); black, white and orange paper; googly eyes; pencil; scissors; glue; cotton ball.
```
