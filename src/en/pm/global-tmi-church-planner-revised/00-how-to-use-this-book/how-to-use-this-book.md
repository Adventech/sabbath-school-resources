title: How to Use This Book
---

According to the _Seventh-day Adventist Church Manual_, the top priority of every church board is to develop an “active” and “ongoing” disciple-making plan (p. 137). This plan should aim to “enlist all members and children” in “personal outreach” (p. 138). The spiritual life and growth of the church prospers when it devotes its “first interests and highest energies to involving every member in proclaiming the good news and making disciples” (p. 135). Therefore, the programs and ministries of the church should support, train, and equip the members for this vital work.

This _Global TMI Church Planner_ will help local church ^[pastors]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) and ^[lay leaders]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) develop an active and ongoing disciple-making plan for their church.

1. ^[Read]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). On pages 3-17, learn how local churches can implement a disciple-making culture and strategy based on Christ’s method.
2. ^[Create]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Review the instructions on pages 18-21 on how to create a disciple-making evangelism plan. Use the blank planning form on pages 22 and 23 to make a plan for your church each year. An electronic version may be downloaded at [globaltmi.org](https://globaltmi.org).
3. ^[Communicate]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Present the completed plan to be approved by your church board and/or by the whole church at a business meeting.
4. ^[Implement]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Use the companion _My Disciple-Making Journal: A Global TMI Personal Planner_ to inspire and equip your members for their personal disciple-making mission. Access additional resources to help implement your plan at [globaltmi.org](https://globaltmi.org).
5. ^[Evaluate]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Periodically review the progress and results of your plan—not only in terms of numerical and spiritual growth but also in the number of members actively involved in making disciples.
