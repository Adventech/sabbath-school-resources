The woman whose back had been bent over, now stood straight and tall, gratefully praising God. Jesus watched and smiled. Others sat in surprised wonder. Some wrinkled their brows, confused.

The synagogue leader frowned. He believed Jesus had broken the “rules” by healing on the Sabbath. The leader said to the people, “There are six days for work. So come to be healed on one of those days. Don’t come for healing on the Sabbath day” (Luke 13:14, ICB).

The angry synagogue leader misunderstood the Sabbath. He had missed the truth about who God really is and how much He loves to help people. All he could see was a broken rule. Jesus knew how much that synagogue leader, and others in the crowd, needed to know the truth. Why? **Read John 8:32.**

Jesus shook His head. He said, “You people are hypocrites! All of you untie your work animals and lead them to drink water every day—even on the Sabbath day! This woman that I healed is our Jewish sister. . . . Surely it is not wrong for her to be freed from her sickness on a Sabbath day!” (Luke 13:15,16, ICB).

The synagogue leader and those who believed him were silenced. They had seen and heard the truth.

The people had seen and heard the truth too, and they were very happy. Of course the woman was more important than an animal! Of course one of God’s children deserved to be healed on the Sabbath! The truth made so much sense.

Jesus is a wonderful Teacher. We can learn the truth by reading about the things He did and learning about the things He taught. We can follow His example by loving and helping others, as God loves us, on the Sabbath.
