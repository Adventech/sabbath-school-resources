---
title: Use Things, Love People
date: 31/01/2026
---

As we prepare to return our tithe and give our offerings today, remember this wise saying: “In an age that loves things and uses people, Christians are called to love people and use things.”

Jesus showed us how to do this. He used mud to love a blind man. He used sand to love an adulteress. He used water and clay pots to love some newlyweds. He used loaves and fish to love a crowd of hungry people. He used his garment to love a bleeding woman.

And finally, He used a cross and three nails to build a bridge of love.

Jesus used things to _love_ people.

But, in a divine twist of amazing multiplication, Jesus also uses the people He loves. He uses us to love others—to love everyone—even our enemies, the people who have unjustly used us.

As His stewards, God invites us to use our things to love people. We should use our time, talents, treasures, and testimony to lead people to Jesus. What a privilege it is to be coworkers in God’s vineyard.

### Appeal

Do you love God enough to be used by Him? As you return your 10% tithe and give your Promise offerings today, ask yourself how you can use things—your home, finances, talents, and time—to love people. It’s a beautiful way of living. It’s God’s way. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
