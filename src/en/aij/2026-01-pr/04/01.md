Lucifer, the Magnificent Angel

God created many, many beautiful angels. There were thousands and thousands, and ten thousand times ten thousand of them! How does the Bible describe the number of angels God created? Read Hebrews 12:22.

Their glorious songs of praise and happy voices filled every part of heaven. All those shining angels loved God and enjoyed being with Him. Each one of them was delighted to serve God and go as quick as a flash of lightning to obey their God. But one was God’s special angel. He sparkled with twinkling jewels and sang with a clear voice as he led heaven’s choir.

This magnificent angel was Lucifer. Everyone loved and respected Lucifer. He had an important and responsible place in heaven, right beside God’s throne.

He was God’s talented and strong covering angel, called a cherub. Happiness filled his heart as he served God as the leader of the angels. Heaven was filled with joy and wonder.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What would it be like to live in heaven, where all the angels love and serve God?_
- _Just like the angels, we can happily enjoy God’s presence and serve and worship Him too._
