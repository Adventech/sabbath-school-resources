**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child with a smile and by name as they arrive. Ask them if they had fun playing with anyone special this week, and if so, what they did together.

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to play **“Toss the Balloon”** to each other until you have about six children. Encourage them to respect each other by giving everyone a turn. Once you have a group, play **“Under and Over”** with the balloon. Children can stand in a line, two feet apart. When you say go, the children will pass the balloon under their legs, then over the next person’s head. Time them to get faster. Add another line of children (with an extra balloon) when more children arrive.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Sing **“Pack It Up”** as you transition into the Sabbath School program.

**OFFERING AND MEMORY VERSE** (6 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#E6B349"}}})** Share a **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission offering.

```=Mission Fast Facts #10

In Côte d’Ivoire, pastors can talk freely about Jesus and teach from the Bible. They use many creative ways to tell others about His love. Some use their smartphones (WhatsApp) to send devotionals and sermon recordings. These are very helpful in areas where not many people can read or write.

```

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal lots of **small paper hearts**. Share that last week we learned that God gave us His love (sprinkle hearts over everyone’s heads) and that He wants us to show our love back to Him by obeying the **first four commandments** (the first part of our Ten Commandments song).

In today’s story we find out how the **last six commandments** tell us how we can live as God wants us to live by showing love to those around us, and by thinking of them before we think of ourselves (the second part of our Ten Commandments song).

Invite **children to collect five hearts** each from the hearts on the floor around them. Then play **joyful music** and have children see how many people they can **give their hearts** to (one per person).

- **Can you give away more than you get?**

God gives us so much love. We can show Him our love in return, and also give our love to others. Share the **Big Idea: God’s way is best. He teaches me to love others**.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to **show someone in your family what you’re learning about God’s laws of love. You might like to use pictures or toys to help you do this challenge**.

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned that God loves us so much that He gave us the Ten Commandments. His first four commandments teach us how to love God and grow closer to Him. Commandments 5-10 teach us how to love others.

- **Can you remember the Big Idea?** Say it together one final time: **God’s way is best. He teaches me to love others.**

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Sing **“The Ten Commandments”** song again, but this time, with actions. For the first four commandments, place your hands over your heart, then stretch your arms up to God. For the next six commandments, place your hands over your heart, then sweep your arms out in front of you to demonstrate loving others.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Sing the **“Goodbye Song.”** Share that next week we will learn about a time the Israelites disobeyed one of the commandments.
