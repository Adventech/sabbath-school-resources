**Word of the Week**

**Follower:** A follower is someone who follows a leader. They listen to the things their leader says and copy the things their leader does. Jesus wants us to be His followers.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus returned to the Jordan River after His temptation, and John the Baptist recognized Him and pointed Him out. Two of John’s disciples followed Jesus. One of them, Andrew, went to find his brother Simon and introduced him to Jesus. Later Jesus called Philip and Nathanael. One night the disciples fished all night but caught nothing. The next morning Jesus asked them to put out from the shore and lower the net down. They did so and brought back a huge catch of fish. This experience led them to leave everything and follow Jesus completely.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants them to follow Him.
- **Feel** happy to give their hearts to Jesus.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to help them follow Him each day.
