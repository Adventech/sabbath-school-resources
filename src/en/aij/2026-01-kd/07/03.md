For 300 years “Enoch walked with God” (Genesis 5:24, ICB). Enoch thought about God, talked about God, and showed God’s love to others. What a faithful friend!

Then one day something truly amazing happened. God did something special for His friend. What was it? Read Genesis 5:24.

He might have been plowing a field or cleaning dishes at home. Maybe he was praying alone, as he did every day. Wherever Enoch was, it must have been a wonderful surprise. Just like that, God took Enoch to heaven! (Hebrews 11:5, ICB).

Imagine Enoch’s excitement! How different from earth his home in heaven looked. Heaven has glittering golden streets, singing angel choirs, friendly animals, and flowers that never die. Best of all, it’s where God lives. Enoch could be with his Best Friend Jesus forever!

Jesus can’t wait to take you to heaven too. He is such a faithful friend! One day soon “the Lord himself will come down from heaven. There will be a loud command with the voice of the archangel and with the trumpet call of God. . . . We will be taken up in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And we will be with the Lord forever” (1 Thessalonians 4:16, 17).

What a wonderful day that will be!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- What is your favorite way to spend time with Jesus?
- What is the first thing you would like to do when you see Jesus in heaven?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
