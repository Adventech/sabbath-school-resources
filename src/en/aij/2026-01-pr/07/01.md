After the Fall

We don’t know how long it was after Abel died that Adam and Eve had another son, but they had other children. Later these children grew up and had children of their own. Soon there were hundreds and thousands of people on the earth!

One of Adam and Eve’s children was Seth. How old was Adam when Seth was born, and what does the Bible tell us about Seth? Read Genesis 5:3. Although Adam and Eve had other children, it was Seth’s family that Jesus would eventually be born into.

Adam lived for 930 years. In this time he faithfully told all of his children and grandchildren what it was like living in perfect Eden, and about the sad day they had disobeyed God. They could even go to the gates of Eden to see the angels guarding the garden, and Adam’s family often worshipped there. Adam told the story of his fall, how very important it was to follow God, and the promise God had given to send Someone to deliver them from their bad choices. But not many people listened. Cain’s family became proud, mean, and selfish. They hurt themselves and others in terrible ways.

God loved every single one of Adam’s descendants. But in the same way that He wouldn’t make Adam and Eve obey Him in the Garden of Eden, He also let people decide if they wanted to be faithful to Him or not.
