Learn

Choose an animal family from the lesson and look for a book or short online nature video about them. Practice the noises they make together. Act out the way they move, and the way they help each other as a family.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8BA94B"}}}
Create

Draw a picture of your family. You may like to draw outlines for your child to add faces and features to, as well as color in. Thank God for each person in your picture.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8BA94B"}}}
Model

Remember to always treat your child with care and respect, even when you’re tired or frustrated. Whenever you ask your child to help around the home, get down on their level and whisper your instruction. Let your child practice saying, “Yes, Mommy” or “Yes, Daddy,” with a smile on their face.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8BA94B"}}}
Play

Make cleaning up the toys or house into a game. Play energetic music and let your child know that by the end of the song, ________ needs to be done. Change it up by pretending to be different animals while you clean. Move in the way that animal does as you do your jobs.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8BA94B"}}}
Pray

Each day this week, ask God to help your family love and help each other.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#8BA94B", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Through love, serve one another” (Galatians 5:13).
