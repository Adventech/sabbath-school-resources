**Big Idea:**\
God is loving. He created our world.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Genesis 1:1)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Create”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To create is to make something new.

“Let There Be . . . Light!”

Do you like making new things? Maybe you like to paint beautiful pictures or build fun machines out of boxes or LEGO bricks. Did you know that God loves to create things too? The Bible tells us that “in the beginning God created the heavens and the earth” (Genesis 1:1).

It all started when God looked out over a deep, dark space covered with dark water.

The Spirit of God was there too, moving over the waters. God had been planning to create something very special here. He knew the very first thing He wanted to make. What was it? Read Genesis 1:3. God said the words out loud and . . . _shimmer! glimmer!_ Light was there! Just—like—that!

“God saw that the light was good” (Genesis 1:4, ICB). He was happy. But He wasn’t finished yet. The Bible says, “God divided the light from the darkness. God named the light Day and the darkness He called Night” (Genesis 1:4, 5). There was an evening, and there was a morning. It was the first day ever in God’s new world!

How amazing that God could create light by saying words out loud!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you finish this sentence? “God saw that the light was [_]()” (Genesis 1:4).
- Why do you think God thought the light was good?
- What do you love about light?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Drape blankets over chairs to make a playhouse. Invite a family member inside and tell them the story.\
Turn on a flashlight when you say, “God saw that the light was good!” (Genesis 1:4).

^[Pray]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Pray inside your blanket playhouse. Thank God for making light so you can see everything He has made.
