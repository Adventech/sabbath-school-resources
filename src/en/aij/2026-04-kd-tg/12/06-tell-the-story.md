**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He brings me joy.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 12 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Suggested Storytelling Props: **bag with something to pack, sandals, joyful shakers**—hand out at the end
- Use **peg people** (Jesus, woman, son) or a **felt set** if desired.
- Ask children to join you in some actions.
- Speak with expression to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

Jesus, His disciples, and a crowd of followers packed their bags and put on their sandals. ^[(Pack bag, put on sandals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a wave ^[(wave)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), they said goodbye to their friends and headed off down a dusty road to the mountain village of Nain.

The disciples were always happy to walk and talk with Jesus. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Along the road Jesus stopped to heal the sick and speak words of kindness. People loved meeting Jesus! Many joined the group and followed Him up the rocky road to Nain.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As they drew near, they heard sounds of sad crying. Coming out of the city gates was a very sorry sight. A young man who had died was being carried out on a stretcher. His mother followed. She had no husband to hold her hand, and no other children to cry with. This son had been the only family she had left, and now he had died too. How very sad!

Together the mother and villagers began the slow journey out the gates toward the rocky tombs not far out of town.

Jesus watched this poor woman walking along the road with tears streaming down her face. He felt her sadness deep in His own heart. He wanted to do something! He wanted to bring comfort to this sad mother.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus moved close beside her, and said, “Do not weep [cry]” (Luke 7:13).

The woman looked up at the Man with the kind voice. She watched as He stepped closer to her son. Jesus reached out and gently placed His hand on the stretcher.

Everyone stopped in surprise. The people who had been crying loudly went quiet. Not a whisper could be heard.

Jesus leaned in close to the lifeless man and said, “Young man, I say to you, arise [get up]” (Luke 7:14).

The villagers and the visitors looked at Jesus with surprise. Would the man, who had died, really get up? That would be impossible!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The stretcher tipped and wobbled. The widow’s son suddenly sat up and looked around.

Jesus smiled at the mother and her son. Jesus had turned her sadness into joy!

The mother hugged her son tightly. She kissed his cheeks. Then she hugged him some more. It was a miracle! The man, who was dead, was now alive!

The crowd couldn’t believe it—Jesus had done the impossible! **He can do anything.** Instantly the sad funeral turned into a joyful celebration. ^[(Hand out joyful shakers.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) People clapped happily. Then the crowd broke into songs of praise to God. ^[(Shake shakers some more, then stop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “They said, ‘A great prophet has come to us! God is taking care of his people’ ” (Luke 7:16, ICB).

The sad town of Nain was filled with joy because of what Jesus had done. ^[(Shake shakers, then stop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

News traveled throughout the land about the caring, all-powerful Jesus. He had brought a young man who had died back to life! ^[(Shake shakers. Stop and collect.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) And someday, when Jesus returns, He will raise all those who have died loving Him. What an exciting day that will be!
