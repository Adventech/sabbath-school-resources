The afternoon sun shone brightly as Jesus walked down the dusty road toward Jairus’ house. Jairus stuck close to His side. The excited crowd followed. But as they neared his house, everyone went quiet. What was that terrible noise up ahead? They could hear wailing and crying. Flute players were making sad music. It was so noisy! And it was coming from Jairus’ home. They were making sad noises because the sick girl had died.

What did Jesus say to them? **Read Luke 8:52.**

The loud noise stopped. The people were silent. Did they hear correctly? Did Jesus just say she was sleeping? The crying crowd started to make fun of Jesus. They thought, _Jairus’ daughter isn’t asleep—she is dead!_

Of course, Jesus knew the little girl was dead. He was trying to explain to the crying crowd that her death would not be forever. He was telling them death is like a deep, deep, peaceful sleep.

Jesus went into the girl’s room. The little girl’s mommy and Daddy Jairus, along with Peter, John, and James, quietly followed Him.

On the bed, as still as still could be, lay Jairus’ daughter. She didn’t breathe or move. Her face looked peaceful because she wasn’t sick anymore. Jairus’ daughter was resting in the sleep of death. She was waiting for Jesus, the One whose powerful words could give her life!

From the beginning of sin, every person has eventually died. They too are resting peacefully in the sleep of death. They are not alive. They have no breath and no beating heart. They are waiting to hear the voice of the only One who has the power to wake them up. First Corinthians 15:22, says, “. . . in Christ all shall be made alive.” What wonderful news!
