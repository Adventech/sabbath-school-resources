**Opening Activities**

Collect lots of **leaves** in different colors and shapes. If unavailable in your season/location, use paper flowers or circles. Provide a **hole punch** and cut some lengths of **string** for the garland.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: small clear bag** full of **little colorful things that look messy together** (beads, buttons, craft items, paper, string). Also source a **kaleidoscope or a media device** (phone or tablet) to show a virtual kaleidoscope (search “kaleidoscope video” online). Place the kaleidoscope/device in a **large paper bag** and **tape** it shut.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: toy picnic set, small bag of grain, cup covered in aluminum foil, bags of grain from last week, small bag of coins, larger bag/sack.

Source a **felt set** or **wooden peg people** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Print the heart template and cut out lots of paper hearts for the children to find. Hide them around your Sabbath School space. Make a colorful map of the space, drawing simple features that the children will recognize, showing where you have hidden the paper love hearts.

**Nature/Mission**

Print **^[Nature Card 24: Sunflower]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Nature Lesson: Source sunflower seeds (if easily available) and a carboard sun. Share the story, “The Happy Sunflower.”
