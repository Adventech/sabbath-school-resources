**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a timer and tell them that for two minutes no one is allowed to talk or make any noise. Provide them with paper and pencils and tell them they can only use hand signals or write notes to communicate with each other.

Then do a silent task for two minutes: Have children respond to the following question by brainstorming on their papers without talking:

- **What is God’s plan for us?**

**^[DEBRIEF:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Discuss and debrief:

- What did it feel like to be unable to talk? In our story today, Zacharias is unable to talk for months, not just two minutes!
- How do you think Zacharias felt suddenly not being able to talk? How do you think he felt after he was finally able to talk again? (Encourage children to think of a range of feelings.)
- What kind of things would you miss saying if you couldn’t talk?

**Teaching Point:** In our story today God wanted Zacharias to be part of His big story. Even though Zacharias doubted God at the beginning, he was still faithful and willing to be used by Him.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read **Proverbs 3:5, 6**, “Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths.”

**Teaching Point:** When we surrender to God and lean not on our way of thinking but on His way of thinking, He leads us in a path that is part of His big story.

Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard/poster paper or Program PowerPoint: **God looks for people who love Him to be part of His big story.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[TUG-OF-WAR:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Give a long rope with a ribbon tied in the middle to two children. Create two markers on the floor that are three feet (one meter) in front of each person. Invite them to have a tug-of-war and try to pull the ribbon on the rope to the line closest to them. After one or two turns, ask:

- What are the two participants showing us through their actions? (They are the strongest; the most powerful; the best, etc.) Have them try again, but before they begin, whisper into one participant’s ear “surrender.” When the battle stops, ask, “What did we just see happen?” Lead discussion toward this important word of the week: surrender.
- How hard was it for you to surrender the rope war?
- Who found it hard to surrender in our story today?
- Who found it easier? What helped them surrender? (Putting all their trust in God.)
- How easy is it for you to surrender? (To stop resisting and obey.)

**Teaching Point:** When we surrender to God and obey what He is asking of us, we show our love for Him and get to move His story forward.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Collector Card: John: Prepare the Way** to place in/on their storage container/bag. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the memory verse.

**Teaching Point:** Mary probably never thought she would have God’s Son as her baby. But she was humble and surrendered to God. We may not think we are able to help God with His big story, but He works in our lives when we surrender and obey Him. Nothing is impossible with Him.

Sing the **Scripture Song** together: **“Luke 1:37.”**

- **What impossible thing might God do through you? What might His plan be for you?**

**Memory Verse Activity:** If time allows, invite the children to say the memory verse to a partner without using their voice, just facing each other and creating gestures. Then, using their voices again, see if they can repeat the memory verse saying one word each until they have completed the memory verse.

- **Who could you share the memory verse with this week?**

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your **world map or globe** and identify Georgia, where this week’s mission story comes from. Share the Mission Story, **“A Gift From God,”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Zacharias served in the temple. Think which people in your community serve others. Share your thoughts next Sabbath.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Look your children in the eye and share how each one of them can become part of God’s story—both now and into the future. Each person is **unique** and **special**.

In advance, try to find out the **meaning** of each child’s **name**. Write their names and the meanings of their names on the back of a piece of cardstock shaped like a **bookmark**. Leave the front blank. Include blank extras for visitors.

Ask the children if they know what their name means. Does anyone remember the meaning of John’s name from our story today? Remind them it means “Jehovah (God) is gracious.”

- **What does “gracious” mean?** (Courteous, kind, and pleasant, showing God’s grace.)

Give each child their **Name Bookmark** and invite them to write their name in big letters on the front. Children may like to punch a hole at the top and tie a piece of ribbon, then decorate it as a reminder that God knows each person by name, and He wants each of us to be part of His big story.

When the craft is completed, invite children to close their eyes, and imagine God saying their name. To do this, read **Isaiah 43:1**, “I have called you by your name, _____, _______, ______ [insert children’s names]; you are Mine.”

Explain that as God’s special children, He will love us, guide us, lead us, and help us in that special part of His story that He has just for us.

Say the **Big Idea** again: **God looks for people who love Him to be part of His big story.**

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Play the song **“Lord, Please Guide Me”** or **“I Surrender All”** in the background as children make their craft. Display the **words** of the song. Ask the children what words in the song are meaningful to them.
