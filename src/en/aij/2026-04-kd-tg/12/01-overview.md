**Word of the Week**

**Joy:** To feel joy is to feel happiness and delight. The joy that Jesus gives us is better than just a wonderful feeling. This joy can make us content, even when things are sad or hard.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus and His disciples set out for the mountain village of Nain, and Jesus spread joy along the way by encouraging and healing people. Outside of Nain He met a funeral procession for a young man, the only son of a widow. Jesus felt compassion for the woman and told her not to cry. He then went to the coffin, touched it, and told the lifeless young man to get up. Immediately the young man heard, sat up, and began to talk. There was a joyous reunion. Those who saw this were amazed and called Jesus a prophet sent from God.
