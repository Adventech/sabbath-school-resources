The excited crowd soon hushed. Shh! Kids stopped giggling. Moms and dads stopped whispering. Everyone looked and listened. Jesus had another story to tell . . .

_Dig, dig, dig!_ A builder was building a house. His shovel dug deep into the dirt. Thud. The man had reached a hard, strong rock. Perfect! He would build his house on this rock. First the man built a solid floor. Then he built tall walls, and finally, on went the roof. Done!

A mighty storm rumbled in. The rain came down, and the floods came up. But the man wasn’t worried one bit. The flood wouldn’t knock his house down, “because the house was built well” (Luke 6:48, ICB). It had been built on a rock.

Then Jesus told His listeners about another builder . . .

_Bang, bang, bang._ Up went the tall walls and on went the roof. This builder didn’t care about digging down to strong rocks. He didn’t bother to build a solid floor. He chose some sandy land, then quickly built his house. Done!

Down came the rain and up came the flood. Then with one mighty crash the whole house fell . . . down!

Our lives are like a house, and God is the rock. What does the Bible tell us about God our rock? **Read 2 Samuel 22:3.**

When we listen to God’s words from the Bible and obey them, we will stay strong through every storm and problem.
