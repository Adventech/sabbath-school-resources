The Sétimo dia was a be no ordinary dia! Adam e Eve spent time com seu wonderful Deus, exploring, learning, e praiCante Ele para Seu amor. Deus had rested, e eles had followed Seu example. O que else did Deus Faça? **Leia Gênesis 2:3.**

Nosso Deus is então bom e santo, e Seu amor is perfeito. Quando Ele made the Sába santo, ou sanctified isso, Ele was making isso unique e especial, just like Himself. Deus was setting apart a whole dia a compartilhe Seu perfeito amor com Seu children. Wow! A whole dia de perfeito amor. O que Façaes Deus Pergunte nos a Faça então nós can passar a whole dia com Ele? **Leia Êxodo 20:9, 10.**

Deus gives nos six dias a work hard, then Ele sets apart the Sába então nós can stop being busy Façaing school e homework, cleaning, e shopping. Ele knows nosso brains need a break de learning, planning, chooCante, e organizing. Ele also knows nós need a break de worry e stress.

Com todos those things out de the way, nós can descanso e be close a Deus. Isso’s a especial time a lembre-se e celebrate nosso Creator Deus. Quando nós are com Deus, nosso hearts want a amor Ele. Nós go a church em Sába a worship Deus com others em Seu santo dia. Nós want a Cante e talk a Ele. Nós want a give nosso time ou money as an offering a Ele. Nós want a Faça something especial para somebody else porque eles are precious a Ele. Nós want a thank Deus para todos the bom things Ele criou. Each de these things is a lindo way a show Deus that nós amor e respect Ele porque Ele is nosso mighty Creator Deus. Each de these things is an act de worship.

^[FOR YOU, PARENTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) God sees the work you constantly do for your family, so He blesses you with Sabbath rest too. “God’s love has set a limit to the demands of toil. Over the Sabbath He places His merciful hand. In His own day He preserves for the family opportunity for communion with Him, with nature, and with one another” (Ellen G. White, _Education_, p. 251).
