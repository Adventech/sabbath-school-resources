_Crack!_ An odd noise woke up little Amy late at night. Lifting her head, she smelled something unusual. She decided to take a look.

Peeking out of the door, Amy saw that the television looked like a big ball of fire. The TV was in a wooden cabinet in the living room. On top of the TV cabinet was a big flower vase.

Amy’s eyes opened wide in fear as she saw the hot orange flames reaching up to the ceiling. The fire was quickly spreading toward the kitchen and garage.

“Fire!” Amy screamed. “Help! Help!”

But the fire was so hot that no one dared come out of their rooms. Her father, mother, and brother quickly ran to a window at the back of the house and jumped out. But Amy did not run. Quickly, just for a moment, she fell on her knees.

“God, please save us,” she prayed.

At that moment the big flower vase sitting above the TV began to wobble. Then . . . crash! Amy watched as the vase of water fell straight onto the fire. The flames instantly died down. They stopped climbing up the ceiling. They stopped racing toward the kitchen and garage. The fire shrunk down until it disappeared completely.

God had heard and answered Amy’s prayer.

Sin is a lot like that big fire. It began in the Garden of Eden, when Adam and Eve disobeyed God. Since then, sin has spread like fire. It has caused pain, sadness, and even death. It is a problem we just can’t fix ourselves.

That is why God made a plan to send Jesus to destroy sin and death forever. “For God loved the world so much that he gave His only Son. God gave His son so that whoever believes in Him may not be lost, but have eternal life” (John 3:16, ICB).
