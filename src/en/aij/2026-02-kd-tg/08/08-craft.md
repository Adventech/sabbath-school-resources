**Preparation instructions:**

Draw and cut a heart shape out one of the paper cups (one per child).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/08/8b.png)

**Create it together:**

- Decorate the heart cup with a red marker and stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/08/8c.png)

- Cut out each of the pictures and glue onto either side of the second paper cup.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
