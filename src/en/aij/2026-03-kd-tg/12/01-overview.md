**Word of the Week**

**Change:** Change means to make something different. You can change clothes and put on different clothes. Or you might make a different choice to play outside instead of inside—you could change your mind. Jesus can change our selfish, sinful hearts so we can be loving and kind, as He is.

**Lesson Summary**

Zacchaeus, a tax collector in Jericho, heard that Jesus was coming to his town. He ran to meet Jesus but couldn’t see over the heads of the crowd because he was short. So he decided to climb a sycamore tree to see better. When Jesus was about to pass under the tree, He stopped and invited Himself to Zacchaeus’ house. Zacchaeus was surprised and joyful. He decided to change his ways and pay back the people he had cheated.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants to change our hearts from being selfish to loving.
- **Feel** thankful that Jesus can help us make wrong things right.
- **Respond** by inviting Jesus into their hearts so He can change them.
