The hot midday sun sparkled on the Jordan River as more and more people came to listen to John’s powerful words. “Repent,” he cried to the people (Matthew 3:2). That word stuck in many people’s hearts like a sharp arrow in a target. Repent. God was helping them realize their hearts were selfish, proud, and full of sin. They didn’t want those rotten hearts anymore, so what did they do? **Read Matthew 3:6.**

They decided to respond to God by saying they were sorry and letting Him take away their sins. To show their need for Him and faith in His power to forgive, they dipped under the waters as their “old” selves and came up spiritually “new.” Imagine how happy they were after that!

Some of Israel’s wealthy rulers and teachers came to be baptized too. But God showed John they didn’t really mean it. They were fakes who thought they could continue to be proud, selfish, and cruel. John told them, “You must do the things that show that you have really changed your hearts and lives” (Matthew 3:8, ICB).

The truly repentant people wanted their baptism to be the start of a beautiful life with God, not just an act. God was working in their hearts to change them, and they responded in faith. They asked John, “What should we do?”

John replied, “If you have two shirts, share with the person who does not have one. If you have food, share that too” (Luke 3:10, 11, ICB). He told the tax collectors not to be greedy, the soldiers not to be violent, and the grumblers to be happy with their pay.

God had led the people to repent. They had listened to John the Baptist, and the Holy Spirit had prompted them to feel sorry for their sins. They had asked God for forgiveness and been baptized. Now they wanted to live kind and good lives, sharing God’s love.
