**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

We’ve all been in situations in which we have wanted to do something that we know we shouldn’t. It’s called temptation—a tug-of-war in the mind. We are faced with a choice—to give in to temptation or to resist it.

Jesus experienced this same battle, and the stakes were so much higher for Him. The salvation of the world, all through the ages, was in His hands. He absolutely understands the difficulties we face when we are tempted. Hebrews 4:15, 16 tells us: For we do not have a High Priest who cannot sympathize with our weaknesses, but was in all points tempted as we are, yet without sin. Let us therefore come boldly to the throne of grace, that we may obtain mercy and find grace to help in time of need. He does not just sympathize with us—He empathizes, for He has been there. What reassurance!

The Bible also tells us that even though Jesus faced temptations, He was victorious every time. Satan tempted Jesus when He was at His weakest. Yet Jesus relied not on His own earthly power but on God’s Word. Jesus wants us to be victorious using the same strategy and power. God can give us His power through His Living Word.

All of heaven celebrates when we rely on God and His power to resist temptation. Ephesians 6:10 tells us to “be strong in the Lord and in [His] power.”

It is God’s power that will defeat the enemy. But we must stay strong in His power. Let’s spend time in God’s Word every day, reading the Bible and committing scripture to memory. Let’s carve out time to both listen and talk to God and daily seek the power of the Holy Spirit to strengthen us against every temptation.

May God help you as you teach the children in your care, so that they may know that Jesus can help them overcome temptation too.
