---
title: Old, Poor, and Alone
date: 28/11/2026
---

He was surviving the pains of old age, living on a meager income alone in government housing. He’d read a book on stewardship and wanted me, the author, to visit with him. The only reason he gave me to visit was, “I just want to tell you something.”

I delayed the visit because I knew in my heart that he’d just ask me for money, but I arranged an appointment anyway, secretly hoping that he wouldn’t be home.

After chatting about his old furniture and how he budgets every coin into separate containers to try and pay his bills, he got to the point. He began, “I just want you to know how much your book about material blessings challenged me.”

“Really?” I said. I was surprised that a book on affluence, written for wealthy Christians, would have had any impact on a poor old man living alone.

“Yes. I’ve always returned a 10% tithe to God, but a few years ago, I set a goal to give a regular second 10% of my income for mission projects, and I recently reached that goal. But while reading your book, God convicted me to give a third 10% for mission. I just wanted to let you know that I’m nearly at 30%, and God is blessing me so much!”

The tears of joy in his eyes reflected the tears of conviction in mine. He’s not old, poor, and alone. He’s looking forward to his new pain-free body, rich in the Lord and truly part of God’s family.

As I walked back to my car, I asked God to forgive me for hesitating to visit him, for judging his heart by his circumstances, and for being too selfish with the material blessings God had given to me.

### Appeal

As we now return God’s 10% tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s each consider how God is convicting us to prayerfully and systematically set aside a greater percentage of our income for His mission of saving the lost.
