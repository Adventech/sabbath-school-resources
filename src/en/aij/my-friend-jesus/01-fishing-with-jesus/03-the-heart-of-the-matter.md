Jesus is calling you to follow Him, just as He called His disciples.

Maybe you are finding it hard to focus on His call over the clamor of crying babies, toddler tantrums, or the pressure of working and providing for the family. Maybe the TV, your social calendar, or social media is trying to drown out His voice.

But Jesus is right there, by your side. He knows your name (Isaiah 43:1). He knows how many hairs are on your head (Luke 12:7) and created you intricately in your mother’s womb (Jeremiah 1:5). He knows your thoughts before you do (Psalm 139:4), and He has a wonderful plan for your life (Jeremiah 29:11). He knows everything you have done and everything you will do, and yet His love for you is higher than the heavens and stretches out farther than the ocean (Romans 8:39).

It may not always be easy—but what a wonderful blessing is to be gained from saying yes to His invitation.
