**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** and show the pictures of Children’s Faces. Place the faces in a line and ask:

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Which face do you think shows happiness? disappointment? surprise? confusion? anger?** (You may like to invite different children to stand by a photo to answer each question while the remaining children make the expression.)
- **Which faces show how our loving God makes us feel on the inside?**

The way we feel inside will often show on our faces and in our actions.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Adam and Eve’s family had **inside feelings** that showed on their **outside faces**. Those feelings made them make good choices and bad choices. Sometimes they obeyed God, and sometimes they didn’t.

Share the **Big Idea**. Today, we are learning that **God is loving. I will want to obey Him.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **child’s hat** and an **adult’s shirt**.

Invite a child to pretend to be a grown-up (put on an adult shirt), while you pretend to be a child (wear child’s hat). Have the “grown-up” (child) ask you to make your bed. Respond in the following ways: obey mostly, obey happily, grumble while you obey, disobey. After each response, invite the other children to give you a “thumbs up” for obedience or a “thumbs down” for disobedience. Act out other scenarios, such as: take out the trash, help with the dishes, collect the vegetables from the garden, be kind to your sister.

Explain how it’s only when we have love in our hearts that we will want to obey His rules of love with a happy, willing heart.

**Teaching Point:** God will always let people make their own choices—because that is what love does. God is happy to see people choose His way and obey Him.

Ask the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. I will want to obey Him.**

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Craft Activity: Card.** Make a card to give to somebody. Ask the children to share the message of their card with their family and to give it to someone who might need this message.

(Messages could include one of the following: When I love God, I will want to obey Him. Jesus can help me with my feelings, so I don’t hurt others. God’s way is my way.)

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Show the children the following objects from today’s lesson: **children’s faces, altar and cross picture, male lion**. Then ask:

- **What big, important lesson from today’s story does each object remind you of?**

After each lesson is shared, invite everyone to clap in celebration of what God has taught you.

- **Children’s Faces**: We’ve learned that our outside faces show our inside feelings. Let’s choose to ask Jesus to help us control unhappy or angry feelings so we don’t hurt other people.
- **Fruit/vegetables and a toy lamb**: We can say, “God’s way is my way.”
- **Picture of male lion**: When we choose peace, we choose love. This is God’s way.

Ask the children to repeat the **Big Idea** with you: Today we’ve learned that **God is loving. I will want to obey Him.**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Choose a helpful job to do every day for a week to practice doing something faithfully (e.g., water the plants, make your bed, set the table). You may like to mark a calendar square every day you do this.**
