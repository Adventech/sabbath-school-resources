Abram grew up in the city of Ur, near where the ark rested. The people who lived in this area had once been God’s faithful followers. But not anymore. Nearly everyone in Ur worshipped idols, even Abram’s father, Terah (Joshua 24:2). But not Abram! No, Abram didn’t want to be like everyone else. Abram chose the one true God.

Abram knew that his God was alive and could hear him. He talked to God every day. He learned to listen to God. And as he grew, he learned to trust and obey Him. God was Abram’s very best friend. Abram knew that God is loving, so he could follow Him.

One day, when Abram was all grown up and married, God asked him to leave the city of Ur. Abram; his wife, Sarai; his father, Terah; and many other family members loaded up their donkeys and camels. With a big wave goodbye, they left their home. Where did they go? **Read Genesis 11:31.**

While in Haran, Abram’s very old father, Terah, died. But God was with Abram and wanted to lead His friend on a new adventure. Our memory verse says, “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delights in his way” (Psalm 37:23).
