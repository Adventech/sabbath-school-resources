**Focus:** Jesus used humble barley loaves to feed the multitude. He gives us what we need, so we can share with others.

In Bible times barley was an important food that poor people ate.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/10/nature-lesson-a.png)

People planted barley in the wintertime or springtime. It started out as a little seed ^[(show picture of barley grains)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then it grew into a little plant that looked like bright-green grass ^[(show picture of small barley plant)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Eventually, it grew a tall stalk with seeds on it. The seeds had long spikes on top.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/10/nature-lesson-b.png)

In summertime the whole plant would turn a beautiful golden color. This meant it was time to harvest the barley! ^[(Show picture of ripe barley.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Men would use a special tool called a scythe to cut it down ^[(show picture of scythe)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then they would separate the grains from the stalks. Sometimes they had animals walk over the stalks of grain to help them do this. Eventually they would grind the grains into flour to make into bread.

In today’s story Jesus didn’t give the people fancy food that only rich people could afford. Instead, He multiplied the humble barley loaves the little boy shared with Him, and everyone had plenty to eat.

- **What do you have that you could share with others?** ^[(It might be something like barley, but Jesus can use it if you are willing to share it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
