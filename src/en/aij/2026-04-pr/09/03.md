“Stand up. Take up your mat and walk” (John 5:8, ICB). Walk? The sick man hadn’t done that for 38 years! But that didn’t stop him from believing. He didn’t hesitate, he didn’t doubt, he didn’t stop to ask questions. He simply obeyed.

As the man began to pull himself up, his muscles responded. He felt no pain as his stiff legs bent and his weak ankles flexed. With sheer joy he sprang to his feet, realizing he could jump and skip and run again! He was healed!

With a burst of strength and energy, he stooped to pick up his bed mat and blanket, just as Jesus had told him to. It felt so good to move again.

Clutching his bedroll under one arm, the man paused and looked around to thank the One who had healed him, but Jesus had quietly blended into the crowd. After looking around, the man happily walked away. His steps were firm and free, his heart was hugely grateful, and his lips praised God for the unexpected miracle.

We can have simple faith, as the sick man did. Sometimes Jesus will heal our bodies, but He’ll always heal our hearts. Sin is a disease that makes our hearts sick, messy, and broken. We are all sinners. Jesus says to us, “Do you want to be well?” (John 5:6, ICB). God promises, “I will heal them and reveal to them the abundance of peace and truth” (Jeremiah 33:6). When we want to be healed, we can believe and act on God’s words, and He will forgive and heal us from sin, just as He healed the sick man.
