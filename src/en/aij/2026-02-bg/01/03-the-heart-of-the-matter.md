Jesus is calling you to follow Him, just as He called His disciples. Will you, like Peter, jump in feet first?

Maybe you are finding it hard to focus on His call over the clamor of crying babies, toddler tantrums, or the pressure of working and providing for the family. Maybe the TV, your social calendar, or social media is trying to drown out His voice.

But Jesus is right there, by your side. He knows your name (Isaiah 43:1). He knows how many hairs are on your head (Luke 12:7) and created you intricately in your mother’s womb (Jeremiah 1:5). He knows your thoughts before you do (Psalm 139:4), and He has a wonderful plan for your life (Jeremiah 29:11). He knows everything you have done and everything you will do, and yet His love for you is higher than the heavens and stretches out farther than the ocean (Romans 8:39).

It may not always be easy—but what a wonderful blessing is to be gained from saying yes to His call. Imagine if one of the disciples had said, “Now is not a convenient time,” or “No, I have to focus on my business.” Oh, what they would have missed!

When Jesus called Matthew, he didn’t hesitate. “He gave no thought to the lucrative business he had exchanged for poverty and fatigue. It was enough that he would be in the presence of Christ, that he could learn wisdom and goodness from His lips, behold His marvelous works, and be a colaborer with Him in His arduous toil” (Ellen G. White, _The Spirit of Prophecy_, vol. 2, pp. 187, 188).

What a privilege to have the opportunity to be a co-laborer with Jesus! May you be encouraged by the faith of those early disciples to say yes to following God in every part of your life.
