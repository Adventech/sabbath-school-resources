**Focus:** Nature reveals God’s love, giving everyone the opportunity to know Him.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/10/nature-lesson.png)

**“Then God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good” (Genesis 1:31).**

Have you ever lain on the grass at night and looked up into the starry heavens? Have you ever picked a flower and smelled its fragrant perfume? Maybe you have skipped rocks across a beautiful shining pond, watching as they bounced along. Or maybe you have hiked up a mountain and looked down to the valley below, amazed at the beauty of the view. It was Jesus who created our beautiful world. It was His hand that hung the stars in the sky and the moon in its place. It was His hand that formed the delicate flowers and tall, majestic mountains.

Nature is all around us, revealing God’s love. And God created us with our five senses so we could have the opportunity to know Him better.

We can feel the wet dew on the grass under our feet, smooth pebbles in our hands, or a soft feather being tickled under our chin.

We can hear the birds as they sing their morning songs and the leaves as they rustle on the trees in the wind.

We can see the vivid colors of a rainbow arc across the sky and the flash of bright blue from a flapping butterfly’s wings as it lands on a yellow flower.

We can smell the perfume of a freshly picked rose, a zesty orange, or the woody scent of a forest.

We can taste the sweet, sweet honey that the busy bees make, and the crunchy carrot picked fresh from the garden (or marketplace or store).

God’s love is revealed through nature and reminds us that He wants to make His children happy. Through nature He wants everyone to have the opportunity to know Him. If ever you’re feeling distant from God, go outside and spend some time in His nature.

- **What are some of your favorite things God created?**
- **How do those things help you know His love better?**
