In the stillness of the night in Bethlehem, God’s precious gift had been given. While the town slept, Baby Jesus drew His first breath. Mary had wrapped Him, cuddled Him, and fed Him. Such a tiny body for God to fit into! But from His tininess would come big, BIG things. Jesus would live and die to show the whole world a beautiful picture of God’s love.

One day as an adult, the grown-up Jesus called Himself “the bread of life” (John 6:35). He said this, for He knew that people cannot live without bread (food), and neither could they live happy and meaningful lives without Him in their hearts. They couldn’t live forever without Him either. Jesus could do for us what no one else could.

Jesus truly was God’s precious gift, given in the town of Bethlehem, which means “house of bread.” Jesus, the “bread of life,” was born in the “house of bread”! How amazing that even the name of the town of Jesus’ birth could speak a message about Him into the hearts of people.

What message is God speaking into your heart today?
