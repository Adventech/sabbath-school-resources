**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a “storm” in a jar **object lesson**.

1. Place the water and sand inside the jar and shake it up to create a “storm.” Talk about how when we sin, tell lies, and hurt others, our lives can become chaotic, as the water is now in the jar.
2. Put the jar on a table and watch as it settles down. Talk about how when we stop and ask God to forgive our sins, He is faithful to forgive. With His forgiveness, our hearts become peaceful, as the water in the jar is now.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** The lives of the people in Jacob’s family became like this storm jar: messy and chaotic. When we sin, our life can become like the storm in the jar too. But when we pray and ask God to forgive us, only then do we have true peace.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **God forgives me and is always with me.** In our story today we’ll see an amazing sign that God showed Jacob so that he would always remember this truth.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children to **stand up**, close their eyes, and imagine what Jacob saw in his dream. The children can then sit down while you ask for a few children **to describe what they pictured** and **how they felt**.

- **How do you think Jacob felt as he lay down that night and put his head on a hard pillow?** (List some words.) **How do you think he felt when he woke up?**
- **Why did God give Jacob this dream?** (To show Jacob He still loved him, would protect him, and had forgiven him.)

Explain that there was something very special about the actual ladder in Jacob’s dream. Ask:

- **Think back to your lesson this week. Who is the ladder like?** (Jesus.) **What makes you say that?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Clear a big space and show the children the two places you have marked out with a long space between. Explain that you are going to have a jumping competition (no run-ups) to see who can get the closest to your mark. Be inclusive of children with different abilities and adapt the challenge as needed. You (teacher) can jump first, but jump only a very small distance. Give each child a turn, affirming their efforts. Have a helper track the longest jumps.

Share with the children that you forgot to tell them that in between the two marks was a giant canyon. Stand where you jumped to (the smallest jump) and compare with the longest jump. **Did either of us make it?** (No, we both fell short.) Lay a long piece of cloth or tape across the “canyon” space and ask them to imagine that the cloth is a ladder. Invite one or two children to walk across.

Explain that sin has separated us from God. No matter how hard we try (or jump), we can’t save ourselves. Like Jacob, we all make mistakes and sin. Jesus came to earth and died for our sins. He is like a ladder whose perfect love connects us back with our Father. We just need to be truly sorry for our sins and ask for His forgiveness.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Jesus died to save us from sin and connect us to God. He is like the ladder in Jacob’s dream. **The Bible says, “There is only one way that people can reach God. That way is through Jesus Christ” (1 Timothy 2:5, ICB).**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Who else was on the ladder in Jacob’s dream?** (Angels.)
- **What are they doing for you and me?** (They keep us safe, help us.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God loves us so much He sends His angels to also help us stay close to Him.
