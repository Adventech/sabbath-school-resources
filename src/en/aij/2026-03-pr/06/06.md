As Jesus grew up, His heart always belonged to God. He had lived a perfect life (1 Peter 2:22). Jesus had never sinned and never needed to repent, because He did nothing wrong. He chose to be baptized by John for very different reasons.

When John felt unworthy to baptize God’s Son, Jesus said, “Permit it to be so now, for thus it is fitting for us to fulfill all righteousness” (Matthew 3:15). Jesus was baptized as an example for us to follow. He wanted to show us that His life was committed to God, just as our lives can be. When we are baptized, we also commit our lives fully to God to show others that we now belong to God.

Baptisms are special times when people show that they have given their hearts completely to Jesus and want to belong to His family. They are letting others know they are serious about asking Jesus for His power to help them trust and follow Him always.

God’s family will celebrate with you when you choose to follow His example and be baptized one day.
