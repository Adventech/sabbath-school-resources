The men smiled as they gazed up at the bright, twinkling stars that filled the inky black sky. These wise, educated men loved to study the stars. The men, who lived in a far country to the east of Jerusalem, loved to study many things. They even studied the Bible scrolls to try to learn more about them. As they studied, they also learned about the true God and the prophecies that showed Jesus would soon be born. They were excited!

One night as they gazed at the stars, the Wise Men noticed an especially bright star that was really a cluster of shining angels. God told them in a dream to follow it, for it would lead them to the promised Savior they were devoted to learning about. They followed the brilliant star at night until they arrived in Jerusalem. What did they ask the people there? Read Matthew 2:2.

The Wise Men were surprised when no one knew what they were talking about. Why don’t the people know? Can’t they read? Don’t they care? the Wise Men wondered. They had expected to find the city celebrating.

When news about the baby “King of the Jews” reached King Herod, he was not happy! He tricked the Wise Men into believing he wanted to worship the baby King too. What did he tell them to do? **Read Matthew 2:7, 8.**

The priests and religious leaders weren’t happy either. They were upset because they thought God hadn’t told them about the birth of the promised Savior. Those jealous leaders refused to accept the news from richly dressed strangers from a far country. Oh, what a blessing they missed! And all because they overlooked what God had told them in the Bible scrolls. They hadn’t eagerly studied the way the Wise Men had.

The Wise Men were devoted to learning all they could about the promised Messiah. A devotion to learning can lead us to worship Jesus too.
