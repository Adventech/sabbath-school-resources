**Big Idea:** God is loving. He created our world.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Swish **water** in a **sealed container** for the sound of water throughout the story. **Flashlight.**
- Blow **bubbles** to illustrate air, or a **water spray bottle** to show mist.
- Lay a **green blanket** on the floor, share **fresh herbs** for children to smell and **fresh or toy fruit** to touch.
- Do actions with the children of a blooming flower and a growing tree.
- Lay a **black blanket** on the floor and invite children to place a **paper moon and stars** in a “night sky.”
- Use the Creation, Days 3 and 4, audiovisual (**^[Sights and Sounds Video]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Throw **feathers** up and watch them float down. Play **bird sounds**, or have the children make bird sounds.
- Have the children place **toy fish** and **ocean creatures** on a piece of **blue fabric** you have laid out.
- Use a **cardboard sun** to move up and down to show how the sun ”sets” each Creation day.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 1 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- God loves to create! He created the heavens and the earth.
- God looked out over a deep, dark space covered with dark water.
- DAY 1: God spoke and created light. He created day and night ^[(container of water, black blanket, and flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- DAY 2: God spoke and divided the waters. He created the sea below, and air to fill the sky above ^[(blow bubbles, water mist from spray bottle, breathe in and out)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- DAY 3: God spoke and made dry land appear. He filled the land with plants, flowers, fruit, and trees ^[(green fabric, grow like a tree, use hand to make blooming flower, fresh herbs, fresh/toy fruit)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- DAY 4: God spoke and created the sun, stars, and moon ^[(cardboard sun, black blanket, moon, and stars. Show the Creation Days 3 and 4 Sights and Sounds audiovisual from AIJ website)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- DAY 5: God spoke and created the fish of the sea and birds of the air ^[(blue fabric, toy sea creatures, feathers to throw in the air)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God saw that it was good.

**TELL THE STORY**

Do you like making new things? God loves creating! The Bible tells us that “In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth” (Genesis 1:1).

On DAY 1 God looked out over a deep, dark space covered with dark water ^[(swish container of water)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The Spirit of God moved over the waters. God had been planning to create something very special here. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God said, “Let there be light” (Genesis 1:3) and . . . shimmer! glimmer! Light was there! ^[(Invite children to look underneath a black blanket and turn on a flashlight.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

“God saw that the light was good” (Genesis 1:4, ICB). He was happy. The Bible says, “God divided the light from the darkness. God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night” (Genesis 1:4, 5). It was the first day ever in God’s new world!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On DAY 2 God spoke out loud again, saying, “Let there be something to divide the water in two!” (Genesis 1:6, ICB). He created a wide band of sky that split the water into two parts. Lots of little water droplets floated above the sky ^[(blow bubbles to float above the children or mist a water spray bottle)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and the rest of the water splished and splashed in the sea down below (swish container of water). Now fresh air filled the sky ^[(invite the children to stand up and take a deep breath in and out before sitting down again)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On DAY 3 God spoke, and dry land appeared—just like that! “Then God said, ‘Let the earth produce plants’ . . . and it happened” (Genesis 1:11, ICB). Lush green grass suddenly spread out like a carpet across the ground ^[(place green fabric on the floor)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Beautiful flowers appeared in all shapes and colors! ^[(Children use their hands to make a blooming flower.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Tall, majestic trees towered ^[(stand tall, arms up)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) above the short, leafy bushes ^[(crouch down)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Juicy fruit hung on trees, ready to be picked ^[(show different types of fruit)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The smell of fresh green herbs filled the air ^[(pass around fresh herbs to smell or try)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). What did God think about what He had created? [Children repeat with you: “God saw that it was good” (Genesis 1:12).]

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On DAY 4 God spoke again saying, “Let there be lights in the sky to separate day from night. . . . They will be in the sky to give light to the earth” (Genesis 1:14, 15, ICB). A big, blazing sun appeared in just the right place to keep the earth warm during the day ^[(have a child hold the cardboard sun high)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God also made the softly glowing moon and twinkling stars to shine in the dark night sky. ^[(Place a black blanket on the floor. Children place the moon and stars on it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

What did God think about what He had created? ^[(Children repeat with you: “God saw that it was good.”) (Show the Creation Days 3 and 4 Sights and Sounds audiovisual.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On DAY 5 God said, “Let the water be filled with living things” (Genesis 1:20, ICB). Suddenly fish and creatures of every kind appeared in the sea! ^[(Place blue fabric on the floor. Children place toy sea creatures on it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Little fish, big whales, floating octopuses, and slinky seals swam through the waters. They were all happy together.

Then God said, “Let birds fly above the earth” (Genesis 1:20). Suddenly the sky was filled with thousands of birds of all colors, shapes, and sizes ^[(have children throw a feather up in the air and watch it float)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). There were squawks, chirps, whistles, and hoots on the land ^[(play or make bird sounds)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The earth had come alive!

God looked at His creation and . . . ^[(children repeat with you)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), “He saw that it was good.” He was so very happy.

All that God made in these five days was to be a perfect gift for us. God is loving. He created our world.
