[[Daniel 9:1-19]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- God’s people had been taken captive by the Babylonians because of their great, persistent sins. But Daniel also knows that the most high God is a God of justice, goodness, and love. God will forgive His people, just as He does anyone who will place their trust in Him today. You do not need to live a life of guilt or fear! God stands ready to forgive and begin restoring you today.

[[Daniel 9:20-23]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Gabriel’s purpose here is to interpret what was left uninterpreted in chapter 8. That chapter concluded with these words: “And the vision of the [2,300] evenings and mornings which was told is true; therefore seal up the vision, for it refers to many days in the future.” Notice the 2,300 days were not explained, and no way of dating them was given. Gabriel now rectifies that.

[[Daniel 9:24]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Seventy weeks = 70 x 7 days = 490 days = which, as we have seen, equals 490 years. This is the amount of time Daniel and the rest of God’s followers were given to do a number of important things: “finish transgression,” “bring in everlasting righteousness,” “seal up vision and prophecy,” etc.
- These “seventy weeks,” or 490 years, “are determined.” Some translations say “are decreed.” Both are correct. In Hebrew the word translated as “determined” or “decreed” is _khathak_. It literally means “to cut off.” This is because important government decrees in ancient times were often cut or carved in stone to preserve them. In Daniel 9, 490 years are being “cut off.” But cut off from what? The period left unexplained in the vision of chapter 8. According to the angel Gabriel, _the 490 years are being cut o ff from the beginning of the 2,300 years_. This is wonderful news, for this means that if we can discover when the 490 years started, we can simultaneously find out when the 2,300 years started—and therefore when the judgment process that will end all evil on earth begins.

[[Daniel 9:25]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- “Messiah the Prince” is a reference to Jesus Christ, God the Son, beginning His ministry on earth. Here’s why that is incredibly important:
	- Every one of us has sinned against God. We have broken His laws, ignored His leading in our lives, and oppressed others in various ways. We have chosen oppression over justice, evil over goodness, and selfishness over love. All of these sins have separated us from God, and unless something changes, that separation will last forever, leading to our destruction.
	- But the most high God does not want to be separated from us! He loves us and wants to be with us for eternity. He wants to save us from the penalty of our sins.
- So Jesus did for us what we could never do for ourselves. He came to this earth, fully God yet in the form of humanity, and He became one of us. He lived with us, walked with us, taught us, and showed us the ways of His kingdom. And finally, after three and a half years of ministry, He willingly gave up His life by being crucified on a Roman cross. _Jesus died as a substitute for us, dying for our sins._
- Because of this substitution, anyone who puts his or her trust in Jesus as their Savior and Master will receive at least two things.
	- First, their sins are forgiven. In the heavenly judgment process we first saw in Daniel 7, our names are moved from “guilty” to “forgiven,” from being “lost” to being “found.” Jesus’ perfect life record is counted as our own in the heavenly judgment, and we are welcomed into Christ’s kingdom as His own family.
	- Second, as we wait for Jesus’ forever kingdom to be established on earth, He gives us another profound gift. After Jesus died on the cross, He was buried in a grave. But on the third day He was raised back to life. This is known as Jesus’ “resurrection.” And because of His resurrection and the power He showed over death and sin, Jesus now gives us power to also conquer death and sin. He freely and daily gives us His strength to become the kind of people He has always known we could be: people of justice, goodness, and love. And even if we physically die before His kingdom is fully established, He will resurrect us to life at the end of time to spend eternity with Him. This is the kind of life that Jesus died and rose again to give us!
- And when would this incredible earthly ministry of Jesus begin? Verse 25 is clear: after “seven weeks and sixty-two weeks,” for a total of 69 weeks, or 483 literal years. And when would those 483 years start? Precisely when the decree “to restore and rebuild Jerusalem” was given—not to restore just part of Jerusalem, but all of it. Fortunately, we know exactly when that decree was issued: in 457 B.C. by King Artaxerxes I of the Medo-Persian Empire. We now have the start date of the 70-week/490-year and 2,300-year prophecies!

[[Daniel 9:26-27]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- We saw previously that Jesus would start His ministry on earth after “seven weeks and sixty-two weeks,” or after 483 literal years (verse 25). We also now see that after that 483 years Jesus will “confirm a covenant with many for one week,” or for seven literal years (verse 27), and that in the middle of that seven years, “He shall bring an end to sacrifice and offering.”
- If we count forward 483 years from 457 B.C., we come to the year A.D. 27 (accounting for there being no “zero year” between 1 B.C. and A.D. 1). In A.D. 27 Jesus was baptized and anointed as the Messiah (see Matthew 3:16; Acts 10:38). Three and a half years later (“in the middle of the week” as verse 27 puts it), Jesus was “cut off, but not for Himself” (verse 26) when He was crucified on a cross by the Romans in A.D. 31.
- His crucifixion in the “middle” of that seven-year period also brought “an end to sacrifice and offering.” For thousands of years God had required His followers to offer for their sins the sacrifice of an unblemished animal—often a lamb—at God’s sanctuary on earth. But when Jesus died on the cross, the massive four-inch-thick curtain in that temple “was torn in two from top to bottom” (Mark 15:38). The message from the most high God was clear: No more lambs are to be sacrificed, for THE “Lamb of God,” Jesus Christ, had given His life for “the sin of the world” (John 1:29)—a sacrifice to end all the previous sacrifices that had foreshadowed it. Now people can come directly in prayer to Jesus, confessing and forsaking their sins, and finding abundant forgiveness in Him.
- More than four centuries later _Jesus fulfilled Daniel 9’s prophetic timeline with perfect accuracy_. Bible prophecy is trustworthy, and Jesus is the Messiah and Savior of the world.
- And what of the 2,300-day prophecy and the beginning of the final judgment of all humanity? Using the day-for-a-year principle, if we count forward 2,300 years from 457 B.C., we end up at the year A.D. 1844 (again, remember that there is no “zero year” between 1 B.C. and A.D. 1). In that year began the final “Day” of Atonement in the heavenly sanctuary.
- Exactly how long this “Day” will last is known only to God. But we do know that when it is completed, Jesus will return to earth a second time and take His people to be with Him in heaven.
- Until the Second Coming, we are called to put sin and selfishness away, to seek forgiveness from God, and to find new life in Jesus Christ as people of justice, goodness, and love.
- The year 1844 is thus filled with significance! We will learn more about this final judgment time and the end of all evil when we study Revelation.
