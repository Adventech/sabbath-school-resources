**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) Play “Race to The Pool.”** Place a hula hoop or other circle shape or outline on the floor at one end of the room to symbolize a pool. Have a volunteer stand next to it with their back to the rest of the class. The children will stand at the other end of the room. When you say “Go,” the game begins. Students will sneak closer to the pool with the goal to get into it without being caught. But when the person standing beside the “pool” turns around, everyone must freeze. If they see anyone moving, that person must return to the starting line. The game ends when someone gets into the “pool.”

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Talk about how difficult it was to get into the pool.

- **How did the person who made it into the pool feel when they finally got there?**
- **How did others feel when they missed out or had to go back to the start of the line when they were caught moving?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Today we’re going to hear about a man who was desperately trying to make it into a pool. He kept wanting to for years and years, but he couldn’t. Until one day Jesus came along and showed him lovingkindness.

- **What does lovingkindness mean?** (When you care for someone but you also act and do something for them.)

Share: Jesus has lovingkindness for all people. Let’s discover how Jesus showed His love to this man.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **We see how much Jesus loves us through His words and His kind actions.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Display a **bowl of fruit or healthy edible treats** on a table in the middle of the room. Tell the children you care about them all very much and you hope they will know how much God loves them. Then tell the children that you have a treat for each of them, but first, you need to see if they meet the requirements. Tell them what the requirements are. (Intentionally make the conditions absurd and hard to achieve. For example: You are more than six feet tall, you have a pet snake, you can say the alphabet backwards, you can do a handstand, you rode a tricycle to church, etc.). Ask the children if there is anyone who meets all the requirements. (No).

Tell the class that you are taking away all the requirements. You want to extend lovingkindness to each person. Offer a treat to each child.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How did you feel when you saw the treats?** (Excited, happy.)
- **How did you feel when you realized you didn’t meet the requirements?** (Disappointed, sad.)
- **How did you feel when you were told that you could receive a treat anyway?** (Thankful, glad.)

**Teaching Point:** If we had to be a certain way before God would love us, it would be hopeless. Yet God’s lovingkindness toward us is deeper and wider and greater than any love you’ll ever receive from anyone else. It’s free, and He offers it to you today. Do you want to accept it?

Say the **Big Idea** again together: **We see how much Jesus loves us through His words and His kind actions.**
