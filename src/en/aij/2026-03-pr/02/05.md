Inside the little stable a lantern glowed soft and warm. Joseph and Mary gazed in wonder at the tiny baby snuggled in a manger of hay. This was God’s Son! God’s precious gift had been born in the night. He was the best way God could say to everyone in the whole wide world, I love you!

Peace and joy overflowed from Mary’s heart as she softly spoke her baby’s name: “Jesus.” Such a beautiful name, so full of meaning. What does the name mean? **Read Matthew 1:21.**

The meaning of a name was very important in Bible times. Whenever Jesus’ name was spoken, people would know He was their Savior.

Jesus would be called by other names too, and each one had a special meaning. Isaiah 7:14 says, “She will have a son, and she will name him Immanuel” (ICB), which means, “God with us” (Matthew 1:23).

Several Bible writers called Jesus the “Messiah,” which means “the anointed one, the chosen one.” God’s priests and kings were anointed to show that their important work had His blessing. Jesus had God’s blessing when He was chosen for the important work of saving people.

Isaiah also wrote about other names that Jesus would have. In Isaiah 9:6 the Bible says, “For unto us a child is born. . . . And His name will be called Wonderful, Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace.”

Jesus was just too amazing to have only one name! All of heaven rejoiced at His birth, for they knew that Jesus’ names described Him well. He truly was the wonderful, mighty, peaceful, everlasting God who came to our world as the Savior.

What an amazing gift!
