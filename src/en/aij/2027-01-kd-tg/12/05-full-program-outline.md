**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a picture of seven different **animal homes**: a rabbit’s burrow, spider’s web, a bear or tiger’s den, a beehive, a bird’s nest, an ant’s hill, a duck or frog’s pond.

- **Who lives here?**
- **Why is this a good home for a ________ ?**

**Teaching Point:** Homes provide a place where animals can live with their families and be safe.

- **What’s something you like to do with your family at home?**

Share that home is a place where we belong. It is a place to feel loved, to be together, to share, and to be happy.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** A long time ago God asked His people to make Him a special home so He could live close to them. It was called the tabernacle, or sanctuary. It was right in the middle of their camp so everyone could see that God was close and know that He was their Friend and God. Let’s find out what it looked like and how special it was.

This story will help us learn our **Big Idea**. Say it together: **God’s way is best. He wants to be with me.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story, “Building the Sanctuary,” in an interactive and engaging way, using the storytelling suggestions, and the Program PowerPoint.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **child’s birthday invitation in an envelope** and some of the storytelling props: **fabric, tools, messages, baskets, beads**.

Invite a child to open the envelope and share what’s inside.

- **How would you feel if you were invited to a friend’s home for their birthday party? What would you say? What would you do?**

**Teaching Point:** It’s so special when good friends want to be with us. In our story God showed that He wanted to be very, very close to His friends—He wanted to live with them!

- **What did the people do? Why?** (Gave God gifts and helped Him to build the sanctuary. They were so happy and excited; they loved Him!) Tell the children that they did a very good job as helpers in the story.
- **What would you have liked to do for God to help build His special home?** Ask the children to stand behind the item/job they would have chosen: sewing (fabric), building (tools), carrying important messages (papers), lifting baskets (basket), working with special jewels (beads).

Thank the children for wanting to show God their love. Ask them to return to their seats.

**Teaching Point:** God wants to be close to us too! He wants to live in our hearts. We can be like the Israelites and be excited to make a home for God. We can invite Him to come into our hearts today!

Share the **Big Idea: God’s way is best. He wants to be with me.** Then sing **“Into My Heart”** as a prayer to invite God into their hearts.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Thank the children for sharing Jesus with their family as a Mission Challenge each week this quarter. **This week in family worship, explain to your family what you love about God.**

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Ask two or three children what they learned today. Affirm their answers and share how today we’ve learned that God loves us so much that He wants to be close to us. He asked the Israelites to build Him a special home. He wants to live in our hearts. We can invite Him to come in.

- **How can we show Jesus how happy and thankful that makes us?** (We could sing, tell Him we love Him, thank Him, give Him good gifts, use what we are good at to help Him.)

Invite the children to put one hand over their heart, spreading out their five fingers. Invite them to say “Thank you” five times as they raise each finger. Repeat, starting with a low voice and ending with a high voice.
