Life in a New World

The ark rested on top of a high mountain. Noah and his family stood by the boat and looked all around. The world had changed so much and looked very different now. There were no more smooth mountains; no more jewels on the ground; no more grand, tall trees.

Even though their new world was very different, Noah and his family could still trust that God would be with them. God would help them turn this soggy earth and rubble into a beautiful place to live. Just like the bright green grass shoots poking through the mud, they would start again.

As always, God had a plan. Right before He hung the rainbow of promise in the sky, God asked Noah and his family to do something. He said, “Have many children. Grow in number and fill the earth” (Genesis 9:1, ICB). Noah’s small family were to become a big family of God’s children.

And so God’s family began their new life in the freshly cleaned world. What did Noah become? **Read Genesis 9:20.**

They planted new farms, grew new food, and had many children and grandchildren. Each day Noah chose to follow God and His ways. God says in the Bible, “My son, do not forget my law, but let your heart keep my commands” (Proverbs 3:1). Just like Noah, you can choose to follow God’s way every day.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever traveled to a place that looks very different from the way your home does?
- What choices would Noah’s family need to make in this new world?
