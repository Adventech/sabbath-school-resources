**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

There’s so much we can continue to learn from the Exodus story, as familiar as it might be to us. God’s people were slaves who needed deliverance. God provided miraculous freedom and promised His people a true home. The Israelites had so much to unlearn, and God was so patient with them. He is the same toward us today.

God desired to be near to His people. He called the nation of Israel to serve Him in the wilderness. He wanted to teach them about Himself, His love, His law, and His plans for their future home. The same is true for us today.

Before you begin to teach this new quarter, step aside to spend time with Him in a quiet place. Pray and spend time reading the Exodus story for yourself, again. Recommit your life to Him. Remember how He led His people and believe that He is leading you, too, as you lead His little ones, and their parents, in Sabbath School. Our same faithful God wants them to know about His character, His love, His law, and His plans for eternity.

God is with you as He was with His chosen nation during their wilderness wanderings. In the busy moments in your week, as you plan this program for the Beginner children and their parents, and in the personal lows and highs of your life, He is there. Always steady; always faithful; always true—God is there for you.

“I bore you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself. Now therefore, if you will indeed obey My voice and keep My covenant, then you shall be a special treasure to Me above all people; for all the earth is Mine. And you shall be to Me a kingdom of priests and a holy nation” (Exodus 19:4-6).
