Sabbath was the most amazing day of all! It was a day for Adam and Eve to be with our Creator. They had lots of questions! Perhaps they asked, _Why is the sky blue? or How do the clouds hold so much rain?_

God had so much to teach them! Maybe God told Adam and Eve how He had made light with His voice. Maybe he told Adam how He had shaped and placed the teeny, tiniest (three-millimeter) bone deep inside Adam’s ear, so he could hear. Perhaps God pointed to a hummingbird and explained how He created it to flap its wings superfast, while hovering in front of a flower to get nectar with its perfectly shaped long beak. Adam and Eve must have been amazed at God’s creative power!

Every Sabbath moment with God was special. It was like unwrapping a present from God every week.

Sabbath is a beautiful gift for you too! It is a time we can worship God together with our family and church friends. It is a time we can rest and enjoy God’s creation and His friendship. And it is an extra-special time to share His love with others. On Sabbath we can smile and say, “Lord, you have made me happy by what you have done” (Psalm 92:4, ICB).
