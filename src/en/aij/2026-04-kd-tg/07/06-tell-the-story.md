**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He has power over death.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 7 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- **Peg people**: Jesus, Jairus. You may also like to use the **craft sample** for the little girl in her bed.
- Use a **felt set** if desired.
- Give children simple **musical instruments** to use on cue (toy or hand-made).
- Ask children to join you in some actions.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jairus sat down on his daughter’s bed and brushed away his tears. He was worried. His 12-year-old daughter was very sick. She lay in bed, pale and still. She was dying ^[(Jairus peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Jairus needed help, and he needed it now. Suddenly he remembered that Jesus was close by. Yes! Jesus would be able to heal her.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jairus hurried down the dirt road, leaving a trail of dust behind him. Jairus wasn’t a priest, but he was in charge of the synagogue. His long robe flapped as he raced toward town. He must find Jesus quickly!

Jesus was at his friend Matthew’s house. A big crowd of people waited in the street outside. Jairus couldn’t wait. He had to see Jesus! The crowd parted as the important ruler rushed inside.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jairus went to Jesus and bowed down at His feet. He said, “My little daughter is dying. Please come and put your hands on her. Then she will be healed and will live” (Mark 5:23, ICB). ^[(Show Jesus peg person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jairus spoke such believing words! He had so much trust in Jesus.

Jesus looked at him with love and care. With a nod Jesus stood up and went with Jairus. Quickly Jesus’ followers and the crowd scrambled to their feet and followed Him.

Together they set off. Jarius smiled as he walked. Jesus was going to help him! Jesus was coming to heal his daughter.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The big crowd pressed in close to Jesus. They walked slowly down the road. Step. Step. Step. Suddenly a man from Jairus’ home ran up to him. He said sadly, “Your daughter is dead. Do not trouble the Teacher” (Luke 8:49).

Oh, no!

Before Jairus could do or say anything, Jesus spoke. “‘Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be made well” (Luke 8:50).

Just believe. Yes, he would believe in Jesus! On they walked.

As they neared Jarius’ house, everyone went quiet. What was that terrible noise up ahead? They could hear wailing, crying and sad flute music. ^[(Play the musical instruments.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) People were making sad noises at Jarius’ house because the sick girl had died.

Jesus walked up to the noisy crowd and said, “Don’t cry. She is not dead; she is only sleeping” (Luke 8:52, ICB).

The crying crowd started to make fun of Jesus. They thought, Jairus’ daughter isn’t asleep—she is dead!

Of course, Jesus knew the little girl was dead. He was trying to explain that her death would not be forever. He was telling them that death is like a deep, deep peaceful sleep.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus went into the girl’s room. The little girl’s mommy and Daddy Jairus, along with Peter, John, and James, quietly followed Him.

On the bed lay Jairus’ daughter. ^[(Invite children to lie down on the floor and be very still.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She didn’t breathe or move. Jairus’ daughter was resting in the sleep of death. She was waiting for Jesus, the one whose powerful words could give her life!

Jesus took her by the hand and said, “Little girl, arise” (Luke 8:54).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Instantly her heart began beating again. Her eyes opened. Her lips smiled. Jesus helped her to stand up on her feet. ^[(“Awaken” children one by one.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Her mother and father joyfully picked her up in their arms and gave her the biggest hug! Their daughter, who was dead, was now alive! ^[(Hooray!)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
