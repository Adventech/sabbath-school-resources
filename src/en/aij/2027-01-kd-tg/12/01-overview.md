**Word of the Week**

**Home:** The place where someone lives. Home might be an apartment, a house, hut, tent, caravan, or hotel. Some animals make their home in trees, burrows, nests, or caves. God has a beautiful home in heaven. But He also wants to live with us here—in our hearts. He wants to be with us always.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God wanted to be near to His people, so He gave Moses detailed instructions about how to build Him a home called the sanctuary. The Israelites willingly donated the materials needed, then used the talents God had given them to make this beautiful place for God. Each part of the sanctuary would teach God’s people about how He would save them; how Jesus would one day die as the Lamb of God, to take away all the sins of everyone who has ever lived in the world.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God wants to be close to them.
- **Feel** a desire to be close friends with God.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to live in their hearts.
