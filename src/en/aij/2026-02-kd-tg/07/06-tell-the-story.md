**Big Idea:** God is with me. I can be brave.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Invite the children to come underneath a blanket with you to see how dark it is.
- Shine a **flashlight** while underneath the **blanket**. Share how God was still with Joseph.
- Use a **colorful coat/cloth** as Joseph’s coat, or fabric you can rip.
- Invite children to smell some spices.
- Jingle **coins** in a bag or invite the children to count it out with you.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 7 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Joseph runs to his father. Jacob has a job for him: to find and check on his brothers.
- With courage Joseph sets off. God is with him ^[(colorful coat/cloth)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- After a long walk Joseph sees his brothers. They see him and his fancy coat and are jealous and angry.
- The brothers decide to hurt him. Reuben tells them not to—he says they should throw Joseph into a pit instead.
- The brothers grab Joseph, rip his coat, and throw him into the pit.
- _Thud_. Poor Joseph is sad and scared at the bottom of the dark pit. God is with him ^[(blanket over children for darkness; flashlight for God’s presence)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Brother Judah sees some traders going to Egypt and has an idea ^[(smell spices)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Brother Reuben is not with the others, so they sell Joseph to the traders as a slave.
- Brother Reuben returns and is so upset. What will they tell their father? Will they have the courage to tell the truth?
- No. All 10 brothers decide to tell Jacob a lie. They show him the ripped coat ^[(colorful coat/cloth)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and let him believe Joseph was killed.
- Joseph cries as he is led to Egypt. God reminds him of the stories of His love. God will be with him wherever he goes—even to Egypt.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Father Jacob smiled as Joseph ran to see him. He had a special job to give his son Joseph. His older sons had been away from home for a long time, looking after the sheep. “Go and see if your brothers and the sheep are all right. Then come back and tell me,” he said (Genesis 37:14, ICB).

Joseph put on his colorful coat ^[(colorful coat)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and waved goodbye to his father. Then he set off on his long, lonely trip. But Joseph knew he wasn’t really alone, because God was always with him. God would give him courage.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph walked and walked. Finally he found his brothers! With a big grin he ran toward them, waving.

Joseph’s brothers sat and watched the sheep munching grass. Suddenly one of the brothers pointed. Who was that man waving at them? He was wearing a colorful coat. It was Joseph!

“Here comes that dreamer,” they said to each other (Genesis 37:19, ICB). Their hate and jealousy bubbled up and over, like a volcano. They decided to get rid of Joseph—right there and then!

Oldest brother Reuben heard their plans. He didn’t want Joseph to be hurt! He told the brothers just to throw him into a deep pit. Secretly Reuben planned to rescue him later and take him safely home.

Joseph grinned as he finally caught up to his brothers. But they didn’t smile back! With rough hands they grabbed him and ripped off his coat. _Rip!_ ^[(Strip off coat or rip a piece of fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Picking him up, they threw him into the empty pit.

Thud. Joseph landed on the hard ground. He was so sad! It was dark and scary. He sat at the bottom of the pit and cried ^[(blanket to show darkness)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God was with Joseph in the darkness. He would never leave him ^[(shine flashlight to show God’s presence)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Ignoring Joseph’s cries, his brothers sat down to eat. They looked up to see a long line of camels coming closer and closer (place camels in sandbox). The smell of spices drifted on the breeze (let children smell the spices). The camels carried Ishmaelite traders on the way to Egypt to sell their spices.

Brother Judah had an idea! They could sell their brother to the traders as a slave—a worker who wasn’t free and was forced to work. The traders could take him to Egypt to sell to a rich family.

Reuben was far away, minding the sheep. But the other brothers nodded. Yes! They would make lots of money and be rid of Joseph forever!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The brothers pulled poor Joseph out of the pit. Then ching-ching, they sold him for 20 pieces of silver! ^[(Count out 20 coins from moneybag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Reuben was so upset when he found out! What would they tell their father? Would they have the courage to tell the truth?

No! All 10 brothers chose to tell a terrible lie. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They carried Joseph’s colorful coat to Jacob ^[(colorful coat)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It was all torn. They let Jacob believe that Joseph had been killed by a wild animal.

Jacob was so sad! He cried and cried. No one could comfort him. The brothers realized they had made a horrible mistake.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Poor Joseph was sad too, as he walked with the traders and plodding camels to Egypt. Big tears spilled down his cheeks ^[(pretend to cry)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He thought about his father and brother Benjamin. Would he ever see them again? He felt so alone. And so scared.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But God was very close to Joseph. He reminded him of the stories Father Jacob had taught him about God, and how He always loves and protects His children.

Courage filled Joseph’s heart. He wasn’t alone. God was with him wherever he went—even in faraway Egypt.
