Jesus had used fishermen’s language when He called Peter, Andrew, and John for their new special task. They understood what He meant when He said, “Follow me, and I will make you fishers of men” (Matthew 4:19). Instead of catching fish, they would “catch people” and help them get to know Jesus.

Jesus knew how much He would need some special friends to help Him with His mission. They would learn from Jesus, and then help Him show the people what God is really like. These disciples were given the important work of calling others, just as Andrew called his brother Peter, and Philip called his friend Nathanael.

Jesus had 12 close disciples. One was Matthew. What did he do for a job, and how did Jesus call him? **Read Matthew 9:9.**

Can you name the other disciples? Check them in Matthew 10:2-4.

Jesus’ disciples came from different jobs and different social groups and had very different personalities. Yet they all had two things in common: they knew how much Jesus loved them, and they chose to love Him back by following when He called.
