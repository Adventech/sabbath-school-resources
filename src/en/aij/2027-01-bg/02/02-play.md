Create

Have your child help you bake a healthy treat to share with a friend. Talk about all the ingredients that make it yummy. Share that there are also lots of good things we can do to make good friendships. This includes using gentle hands, sweet hugs, kind words, smiles, laughter, and helping hands.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#b04b9c"}}}
Explore

Practice friendship skills with your child. Have a pretend picnic or trip to the playground and role-play (using stuffed animals or dolls) what it looks like to be a friend. Practice taking turns and using kind words. Build blocks together, and take turns placing blocks to make a tower; then let each having a turn to knock it down.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#b04b9c"}}}
Learn

Plan a playdate for your little one. Before the get-together, talk about what makes a good friend. Say hello and share a smile. Use soft hands and take turns with toys. Laugh and have fun. Say “thank you” and “goodbye” when the play time is over. On the way home reflect with your child on the playdate. Ask questions such as “What made you smile?” “How did you feel when ______________?” For older toddlers you can begin to build empathy with questions about how they think their friend felt.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#b04b9c"}}}
Thank

Take a picture of your child with their friend (with a parent’s permission). Print out two copies. Help your child to make two picture frames by sticking colored craft sticks onto cardboard. Add stickers to decorate. Talk with your child about what made their friend a good friend. Practice saying “Thank you for _(friendship characteristic)_. I like being your friend.” Visit their friend to tell them thank you and share one of the framed pictures with them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#b04b9c"}}}
Pray

Each day at prayer time, ask your child to name someone who is a friend: a family member, a pet, or anyone they enjoy spending time with. Pray together:

Dear Jesus,\
Thank You for my friend, ________.\
Help me to be a good friend too.\
Amen.
