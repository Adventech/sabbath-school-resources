Learn

Talk about how Jesus fills us up with love and kindness when He lives in our hearts. We then have lots of kindness to give to others. Think of ways God uses our senses to be kind to others. For example: eyes _(without asking, look out for a job that needs to be done)_, ears _(listen for someone who needs help)_, smell _(give flowers to a friend)_, touch _(give a hug to someone who is sad)_, and taste _(bake something healthy and yummy to give to someone else)._

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#337ABC"}}}
Explore

If weather permits, go on a nature walk and find a nice grassy place to lie down and watch the clouds. Alternatively, look up “cloud watching” videos online. Look at the clouds; talk about what it would be like to follow God’s cloud and see the cloud right near your home. Thank Jesus for leading you and being close to you.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#337ABC"}}}
Connect

Ask your child to close their eyes tightly. Hold your child’s hand. Say, “Even though you can’t see me, you know I am here because you can feel my hand. God is like that too! We can’t see Him, but we can feel He is close to us.”

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#337ABC"}}}
Sing

Share that when Jesus lives in our hearts, He helps us make good choices and helps us to be loving and kind. Ask your child if they want to invite God into their heart. Sing a song, such as “Into My Heart,” together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#337ABC"}}}
Create and Pray

Draw and cut out a large heart shape. Have your child color it in. Then let them hold it as you pray together and ask Jesus to come into your hearts.
