**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **blindfold**. Place the blindfold over a child’s eyes so they cannot see. Choose another child to be the leader. Instruct the blindfolded child to follow the instructions of the leader, but not to listen to anyone else. Have the leader give instructions to the child to walk around the classroom and back to their seat. Meanwhile, choose two or three other children to give false instructions to the blindfolded child. Finish the activity when the blindfolded child returns to their seat.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Was it easy to hear the voice you needed to follow?**
- **What made it hard?**
- **How important was it to follow the right person?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Sometimes Satan tries to distract us from following Jesus. But we can learn to hear God’s voice so that we can follow Him, especially when we read the Bible.

Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. I will follow Him.**

In our story today, Jesus chose some special people to follow Him. Let’s find out who they were.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

DO: Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the Story Explorer Box, revealing the following **labels/pictures**: “School,” “Home,” “Others,” and “Worship/Church”; **along with pictures that represent different ways we spend our time, and a picture of Jesus**.

- **What are some things we do each day?** (Play, read, spend time with family, eat, brush teeth, take a bath, time with Jesus, etc.)

As the children suggest an activity you have a picture for, place it by the appropriate label. If they make a suggestion you don’t have a picture for, discuss which label it might go with. Place the picture of Jesus with the Worship/Church label. Point out that there’s lots of labels for other things, and one label for Jesus.

- **Is Jesus with us when we eat or when we play? Is He with us when we do other things?** (Yes. We can ask Him to be with us always.)

Take the picture of Jesus from the “Worship/Church” label. Place His picture above all the pictures/labels.

**Teaching Point:** Jesus sees every part of our lives, and He wants to be part of everything we do. When we are followers of Jesus, we’ll ask Him to be with us always. We can talk and listen to Him throughout our whole day. A true follower wants Jesus to be part of everything they do!
