**Word of the Week**

**Supply:** Supply means to provide, or to give what is needed. When someone gives you lunch, they supply food. God gives us what we need because He loves us. God supplies us with many things, like rain, sunshine, fresh air, and the food we need to live.

**Lesson Summary**

Joseph became the governor of Egypt. God had shown him that there would be seven years of plenty, then seven years of famine. During the seven years of plenty, Joseph built storehouses and had the grain gathered and stored there. He worked hard with God’s help. When the seven years of famine came, Joseph was able to open the storehouses and supply the people with grain. Joseph’s brothers in Canaan were also affected by famine and went to Egypt to buy grain. They bowed before Governor Joseph, just as Joseph’s dream had shown all those years ago. The brothers did not recognize him. Joseph kept one brother in prison and sent the others home to bring back brother Benjamin. Joseph secretly supplied them with extra grain and returned their money.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Feel** loved by God because He cares so much for them.
- **Respond** by thanking God for providing for them and loving them.
