Create

Create a picture of the crowd from the story. Use finger paints and your child’s fingerprint as the people’s heads, then draw bodies. Draw Jesus and the woman in the middle of the crowd and draw a heart above them. Display your picture this week to remind your child of this lesson story.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explore

Use natural objects from the backyard or a park to retell the story of Jesus and the woman. Make a crowd from sticks, leaves, or stones. Get the woman to find Jesus in the “crowd.” Thank Jesus for noticing and healing her.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Play

Play a game of hide-and-seek. After a couple of rounds, cuddle your child on your lap and explain that God loves them so much. He always knows where we are, and He loves us all the time! Share how even though no one else noticed the sick woman, Jesus saw her and loved her! We can notice people no one else notices, and share Jesus’ love with them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Connect

Jesus sees us and loves us. He always has time for us. We can do the same for others. Model friendliness this week in your interactions with others. Teach your child to look into people’s eyes when speaking to them. Teach them to say “Hello” to neighbors and smile. Give your time to someone in need. Show others that they are noticed and loved, just as Jesus did when He was on earth.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

Show your child photos of themselves when they were born. Read Psalm 139:13, 14 and explain to your child that they are fearfully and wonderfully made! Jesus planned them in their mother’s tummy. He knows their name and hears their prayers. Say a prayer, thanking God for seeing us, knowing us, and hearing our prayers.
