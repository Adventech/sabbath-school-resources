**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut pieces of string, approximately 19 inches (50 centimeters) long.

**Create it Together:**

1\. Cut out the pieces.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4b.png)

2\. Fold the side tabs of the backdrop, then glue the rectangle pieces to each side (along the water).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4c.png)

3\. Fold the side tabs of the front water piece, then glue them to the rectangle pieces on each side of the backdrop.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4d.png)

4\. Tape craft sticks to the back of “Jesus” and “Peter.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4e.png)

5\. Glue the boat onto the water. An adult can help punch 2 holes in the top and tie on a piece of string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4f.png)

6\. Children can hang the puppet theatre around their neck, then move the stick puppets to re-enact the story!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/4g.png)
