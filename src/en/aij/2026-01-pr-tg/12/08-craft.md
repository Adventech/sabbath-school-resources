**What you need:**

Two paper plates, stapler, green cellophane/tissue paper, colored paper (white, black, red, and green), scissors, glue, marker.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/12/12a.png)

**Instructions:**

- Fold two paper plates in half. Cut one paper plate down the middle. Leave the other folded.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/12/12b.png)

- Staple each half to the back of the folded paper plate. This will make pockets for your hands.
- Cut up small squares of green cellophane/tissue paper and glue onto the top of the puppet.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/12/12c.png)

- Frog eyes: cut out two semicircles of white paper. Draw eyes with marker and glue on the top.
- Frog mouth: cut a semicircle out of black paper. Glue inside the mouth.
- Frog tongue: cut a strip of red paper and wind around your finger to create a coil. Glue inside the mouth, half sticking out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/12/12d.png)

- Frog legs: cut four strips of green paper, folding each one both ways so they “spring.” Glue to the bottom of the puppet.
