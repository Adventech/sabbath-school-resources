In six days God had spoken a whole new world to life. It was a magnificent gift, wrapped up in God’s love. But whom was this gift for? **Read Genesis 1:27.**

God didn’t speak this time. He wanted His next creation to be special and different, for He was about to make someone like Himself in many, many ways. God took some dust and began to gently shape it. Love poured through His hands as He molded an arm, a tummy, a mouth, and a heart, until a man lay peacefully still. He was perfect to look at, but not alive. Then God bent over his silent face. How did God bring this first man to life? **Read Genesis 2:7.**

Just imagine what it would have been like for this man, Adam, to stir, open his eyes, and see God’s kind, smiling face. Adam was healthy, happy, and handsome, with bright ideas and an incredible brain.

Made in the image of God, Adam was designed to also love and care for others. To celebrate this special role, God gave him the fun job of naming all the animals. “Giraffe” popped into his head for a tall, gangly creature, and “rabbit” for a little furball. Strong, bouncing creatures were called “kangaroos,” and cooing white birds became “doves.”

Maybe a horse nuzzled his arm and a dog licked his hand, wanting to be best friends. God had made Adam responsible for looking after them and everything else on the earth. What an awesome, kind caretaker God had given them.

Our loving God created us in His image and also made us responsible for looking after His creatures. This is called stewardship—when we take care of what God has given us. When we do this, God knows how much we love Him and appreciate the wonderful things He made.
