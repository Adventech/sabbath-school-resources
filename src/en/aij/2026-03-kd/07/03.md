_Rumble, rumble, rumble._ Jesus’ stomach was empty. Completely empty. He had been praying in the desert for 40 days and 40 nights without any food at all. Jesus was really hungry and very weak.

Suddenly someone appeared right in front of Jesus. Who was it? **Read Matthew 4:1.**

He had come to tempt Jesus to do something wrong.

Scattered across the sand lay hard rocks. These rocks looked a lot like delicious loaves of bread. Yum! Jesus could really do with some fresh bread right now. But even though the rocks looked like bread, they definitely wouldn’t taste like bread.

Satan pointed to the rocks. “If You are the Son of God,” he said to Jesus, “command that these stones become bread” (Matthew 4:3).

Jesus knew Satan was trying to trick Him. He shook His head. No! He would not do a miracle like that. His power was for helping others—not Himself. Instantly Jesus remembered a Bible verse. He turned to Satan and boldly said, “Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God” (Matthew 4:4).

Jesus told Satan that food was not as important as God’s words. He trusted God would give Him the strength He needed. Jesus wouldn’t do this to help Himself.

Satan frowned. His plan to trick Jesus had failed. He would have to try something else.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- How can you tell when you are hungry? What does it feel like?
- Why did Jesus have strength to say no to Satan?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for giving you food.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
