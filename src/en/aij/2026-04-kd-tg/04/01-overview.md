**Lesson Summary**

After feeding more than 5,000 people, Jesus sent His disciples across the Sea of Galilee while He stayed behind to pray. As the disciples sailed across the lake, a fierce storm arose. Jesus came to them—walking on water. When Peter realized it was Jesus, he asked to walk to Him on the water. Jesus invited him to come.

Peter walked on the water, but then began to sink when he looked at the storm instead of Jesus. He cried out to Jesus for help. Jesus lifted him up and guided him back to the boat. The storm stopped. The disciples saw that Jesus was the Son of God.
