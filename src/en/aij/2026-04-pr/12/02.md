As Jesus and His crowd of dusty followers finally neared the village of Nain, another crowd passed through the town gates. Unlike the happy crowd surrounding Jesus, this was a sad group of people. They walked slowly toward the local cemetery to bury a young man from their village.

Paid mourners filled the air with their weeping and wailing. Friends bowed their heads and dabbed tears from their eyes. At the front of the crowd, strong men carried their dead friend on a stretcher, his body covered in a white cloth. His weeping mother walked along behind. All moved sadly and slowly toward the graveside.

Jesus quickly took in the whole scene, and His heart was filled with sympathy. The heartbroken mother was a widow whose husband had died sometime before. Now she had lost her only son, the one who took care of her. With no one left to support her, the lonely woman would probably become poor, hungry, and homeless. That is, unless she could glean in a kind farmer’s fields for leftover kernels of grain. Her future looked hopeless as she wept and stumbled toward the graveyard.

The weeping woman didn’t know that “when the Lord saw her, He had compassion on her” (Luke 7:13). She didn’t know that He felt her sadness and sorrow. In fact, she didn’t even know He was there.

Jesus is always there in our sad and difficult times. What does Jesus promise to do for those who mourn? **Read Matthew 5:4.** Jesus will do the same for us. His sympathetic heart feels our sadness and suffering too.
