As they do this, encourage discussion about the process. For example: Do you like building/making things? What do you like about it? Share that in today’s story you will learn about someone who built something. This person did exactly what God said. Listen to **“It’s About Love”** in the background.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a picture of Greyfriars Bobby. Briefly share the story of this faithful little dog.

When you are faithful, you do what you say you will do. People who are faithful can be trusted. They don’t give up, just as Greyfriars Bobby did not give up waiting for his master.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s story is about our faithful God and some of His faithful friends who did what He asked them to do.

Point to the boat(s) made by the children who arrived early and ask:

- **Can you guess the name of one of them?** (Yes, Noah! He and his family were faithful.)

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is loving. I can be faithful to Him.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Noah Builds a Boat”** in an interactive and engaging way using the storytelling suggestions and Program PowerPoint.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** In today’s story, Noah was faithful to God in lots of ways.

- **How did Noah show that He was faithful to God?** (He was God’s friend, listened to God, obeyed, built the ark exactly as God had said, told the people about God, invited them to come into the ark, etc.)

**Note**: You may like to have the children stand up and take one step toward you for every answer given to see if they can get to you!

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a Bible, money purse, photo of a mom and dad, and a picture of praying hands. Lay out the items and invite the children to respond to the following question:

- **How can these objects remind us to be faithful to God, as Noah was?** (Allow them to share their thoughts.)\
Prompt the children to make the connections before sharing:
	- **Bible** (spend time with God)
	- **Praying hands** (praying to God, asking Him to help us be faithful)
	- **Money purse** (sharing with those in need)
	- **Photo of a mom and dad** (loving our parents)

**Teaching Point:** With God’s loving help, you can be faithful in these things. Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. I can be faithful to Him.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 7: Golden Retriever** Dog and share the following facts:

- Golden retriever dogs are very loyal to their owners and are always happy to be with them.
- They are wonderfully gentle, very kind, and extremely intelligent.
- They have soft, furry coats, big smiles, and are very friendly.
- They like to obey their owner’s instructions.

As the golden retriever is faithful to its owner, we can also choose to be faithful to God every day.

Give each child **Nature Card 7** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the mission story: “Sharing God’s Words.”

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Make a “Talking Noah.”** Explain that Noah used his words to faithfully talk about God and to encourage others to choose God. He did this every day. Have the children make a “Talking Noah” with a movable mouth. Encourage them to pretend they are Noah and talk to others about God, and affirm them.

- **What words do you think Noah used?**
- **What words would you like to say to others from your heart?** When we do this, we are growing in faithfulness.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Review what has been learned today.

We’ve learned that **God is faithful**. With His help, we can be faithful too. We can do this by listening to His words in the Bible. We can do this by sharing God with others who don’t know about Him.

Invite the children to repeat the **Big Idea** with you: **God is loving. I can be faithful to Him.**
