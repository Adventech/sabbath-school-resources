For 40 long days it rained and rained. Then the wind died down and the rain stopped. All was quiet and still. The ark now rocked gently while monkeys chattered, rabbits snuffled, and lions purred.

Outside the ark, water covered everything! The Bible tells us, “The water rose so much that even the highest mountains under the sky were covered by it” (Genesis 7:19, ICB).

Noah and his family were safe in the floating zoo. But still they must wait and trust the God who had saved them.

After 150 days, with a whoosh and a whirl, “God made a wind blow over the earth. And the water went down” (Genesis 8:1, ICB). Finally the floating boat went bump! It had come to a sudden stop. Where had the ark landed? **Read Genesis 8:4.**

No more floating; no more rocking! They were on dry land at last. Noah and his family were so excited! But still the door stayed shut.

One day, after waiting and waiting, Noah opened the window. He gently lifted a raven, and later a dove, into the sky. The birds flew off with a flutter and a flap. Would they find a new home? No, they both came back to the ark. There was still too much water around.

After a week Noah sent out the dove again. This time it came back with a treasure: “A fresh olive leaf was in its mouth” (Genesis 8:11, ICB). The waters were going down. Plants were growing again. Their time on the boat was nearly over!
