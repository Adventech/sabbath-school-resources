It was a beautiful day! The world was full of color _(point to colors/items in picture)_. White clouds floated across the blue sky. Yellow birds sang in the trees. White sheep ate fresh new grass. Bright-orange flowers grew on the hillside. Noah and his family felt the warm sun on their skin as they gathered at the stone altar. They were so happy. They prayed and said “Thank You” to God for His love and care.\
Noah and his family had lots to thank God for. He had cared for them, just as He’d promised. Waves had rocked the ark, but God had kept them safe. The rain had changed the world in many ways. Mountains were now rugged and steep, and oceans and canyons so deep. But God’s love is always the same _(hug child)_. Trees would grow big and strong again. The waters were back where they belonged. Fish swam and leaped in a trickling stream. A family of happy ducks swam together as Noah and his family thanked God _(praying hands)_ for all He had done.

God was happy to hear their thank-You prayers. Now He had one more promise to share. God said He would never cover the whole world with water ever again. God would keep this promise until the end of time. He showed Noah’s family a special sign. High, high up in the sky was a beautiful rainbow _(make an arch motion)_, shining bright—full of colors and brilliant light.\
Bright colors stretched across the sky. Red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. A rainbow is God’s special promise—one that lasts forever and ever! This promise was for Noah’s family and their families, and all people, always. It’s a promise for me and for you! The rain will fall, the water may rise, but the earth will never be covered with water again.\
After it rains, look up in the sky _(point up)_. Can you find a rainbow?

The world is full of beautiful colors that God has made _(point to each picture)_. Red strawberries and orange pumpkins grow down low. Yellow and green fills forests of trees. Blue is the color of oceans so deep. The sky turns indigo as stars shine bright at night. Pretty violet flowers grow near paths in the sun.\
The colors of the rainbow are all around. But the most special rainbow is the one in the sky. It reminds us of God’s special promise, His love so big! _(Hug child.)_ God always keeps His promises.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for Your rainbow! Thank You for Your love.
