**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He gives us the Sabbath.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 8 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Distribute **ribbon sticks** to the children and instruct them to hold them up and wave them whenever you say the word “delight.” Use a simple **paper scroll**.
- Use **peg people** (Jesus, a woman, two men, Pharisee) or a **felt set** if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was Sabbath morning. Jesus walked into the synagogue with a smile on His face. He loved the Sabbath. He knew it was “a delight, the holy day of the Lord” (Isaiah 58:13). ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus went to the synagogue to be with God and worship Him. This Sabbath He also went to the synagogue to teach the people all about God’s love. Jesus carefully took out a scroll (which was their Bible) and began to read. ^[(Open scroll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

As Jesus read, He noticed a man who couldn’t use one of his hands at all. Jesus wanted to help him. He called the man forward.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Jewish leaders frowned and watched closely. They whispered angrily to each other. Surely Jesus wouldn’t dare break our laws and heal someone on Sabbath!

Jesus knew what they were thinking. He said, “I ask you, which is it right to do on the Sabbath day: to do good, or to do evil? Is it right to save a life or to destroy one?” (Luke 6:9, ICB).

Everyone became quiet. Of course, it was better to help someone on Sabbath! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus turned back to the man and kindly said, “Stretch out your hand” (Luke 6:10). The man stretched out his hand and it was completely healed! His heart was filled with delight. ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The Jewish leaders looked on with grumpy faces. They didn’t like Jesus healing people on Sabbath. But Jesus is “Lord of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:28). He wanted the people to see that Sabbath is a day to show God’s love, and to help people.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On a different Sabbath morning, a woman walked slowly into the synagogue. She had a very sore, bent-over back. She hadn’t been able to stand up straight for 18 years!

Jesus saw her. He gently called her to come close. Then He said, “Woman, your sickness has left you!” (Luke 13:12, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus put His hands on her, and instantly she could stand up tall. She saw the kind face of Jesus, her Healer, smiling at her. Joyfully she began praising God for her now tall, straight back.

Jesus told the watching crowd that God saw her as precious and important. He said that it was a good thing to heal her on Sabbath because it showed God’s love and care (Luke 13:15, 16).

The woman was full of delight. ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She mattered to God the Father! She mattered to Jesus!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was another delightful Sabbath day. ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus was having a Sabbath meal at a Pharisee’s house. The Pharisee had invited his proud Pharisee friends too. They liked to show off to others.

At the lunch a man was brought to Jesus. This man had a sickness called dropsy that made his arms and legs swell up.

His skin was tight and puffed up like a balloon.

The Pharisees didn’t show any care for this man. They were too busy trying to prove how important they were! But Jesus cared about him.

Jesus reached out and touched the man. Right away the man’s body was healed! The man was so happy and thankful! He was filled with delight. ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

On that special Sabbath Jesus showed this man, who had been sick, that he mattered. He also showed the Pharisees that Sabbath was a good day to make someone feel better. Sabbath is a delight ^[(wave ribbon sticks)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and a day when we should show others how much God loves them.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus can do anything! He gives us the Sabbath.**
