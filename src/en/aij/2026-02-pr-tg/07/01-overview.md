**Lesson Summary**

Jacob sent Joseph to check on his brothers, who had taken the sheep to find green grass. When his jealous brothers saw him coming, they plotted to get rid of him. They threw Joseph into a pit and took his beautiful, colorful coat. The brothers then sold him as a slave to Ishmaelite traders on their way to Egypt. Scared and alone, Joseph chose to trust God with his future, knowing that God was with him.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they can trust God to be with them.
- **Feel** God’s presence in their lives, strengthening them.
- **Respond** by choosing to trust God, even when they feel alone.
