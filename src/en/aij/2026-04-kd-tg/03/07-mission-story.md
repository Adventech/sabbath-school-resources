**Focus:** Jesus helps Wellington find a way to bring his friends back to Sabbath School.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Brazil** on your **globe** or **world map**.

_Note: If possible, bring a kite (or draw one) to show to the children as you tell the story._

Wellington was 10 years old, and he loved flying kites. One day, while he was flying a kite near his home in Brazil, a man and woman walked up to him. They invited him to join the Pathfinder Club at their church. They said the Pathfinders did fun activities and learned about Jesus.

Wellington didn’t know anything about Pathfinders, but he thought it sounded interesting. His parents gave him permission to join the club. Wellington loved Pathfinders. Soon he began going to Sabbath School and church every Sabbath and studying the Bible too. After three years he was baptized.

But then Wellington noticed something. Some of his friends from Pathfinders had stopped coming to Sabbath School. Then more of them stopped coming. Wellington was sad. He wondered what he could do to help his friends.

Then Jesus gave him a big idea. Wellington decided that if his friends didn’t want to come to Sabbath School, he would take Sabbath School to them.

The next Sabbath Wellington and a friend got up early. They went to the home of one of their friends who had stopped coming to Sabbath School. They knocked on the door. “Good morning!” Wellington said. “I would like to have Sabbath School with you in your home today.” The next Sabbath he and his friend went to another home and did the same thing.

After three months five boys and girls had returned to church. After six months 22 children were coming to Sabbath School every week at the church.

Wellington was so happy! God had used him to bring his friends back to Jesus. He felt like a kite, flying high in the sky.

**Teaching Point:** Like Wellington, we can ask Jesus to help us know how to bring our friends to Him.

Repeat the **Big Idea: “Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He loves to help me and my friends** [stand and link arms together]**.”**
