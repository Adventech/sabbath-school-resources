**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **tub or container** and fill with **water**—use two tubs if you have a big class. Source **household items to discover which float and which sink**. Items that float may include: sponges, bottles, leaves, plastic, oil, ice, wood, cork, paper, bubble wrap, Lego bricks, crayons, ping-pong balls. Items that sink may include: metal, keys, marbles, scissors, toy cars, dried pasta, metal spoons.

Source a **bucket, masking tape, scissors**, and **two beanbags** (or balls if beanbags are unavailable) for the Focus Activity.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare the **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 43: Peter Walking on the Water]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Source items for the memory verse activity: **globe/map, stickers, soft balls** for children in pairs.

Source an **orange/mandarin** with peel on, and one without its peel, to use in a **bowl of water**.
