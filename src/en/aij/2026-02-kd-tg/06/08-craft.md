**What you need:**

Brown paper bag, colorful fabric and/or ribbon, scissors, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/06/6a.png)

**Preparation instructions:**

Make an example prior to the lesson so you can walk the children through the process.

**Create it together:**

- Cut a strip from the bottom of a paper bag, roughly 1.5 inches (3.5 centimeters) wide.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/06/6b.png)

- Cut this strip in half and save for later.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/06/6c.png)

- Fold the rest of the paper bag vertically in half and glue together.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/06/6d.png)

- Cut a straight line down the center of the top layer, so it opens out.
- Glue each saved strip to the back of the paper bag, like sleeves.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/06/6e.png)

- Glue rows of colored ribbon/pieces of colorful cloth on the front to make a colorful coat (6-8).
