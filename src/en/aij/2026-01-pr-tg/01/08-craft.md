**What you need:**

Week 1 craft template on white cardstock, crayons, nature stickers, marker, pencil, scissors.

**Instructions:**

- Color each section of the circle a different color in the following order: red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, violet. Make sure the colors are bright. Write numbers 1-7 on each segment.
- Cut out your circle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/01/1b.png)

- Put stickers of things God created on days 1-5 on the back of your circle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/01/1c.png)

- Carefully push a sharp pencil through the center of your circle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/01/1d.png)

- Play a game with your spinner. Take turns with a friend to spin.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/01/1e.png)

- Whatever number the pencil is pointing to when it stops, share something that God made on that day of Creation!
