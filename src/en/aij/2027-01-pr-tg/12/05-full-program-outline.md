**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children your house floor plan (AIJ website).

- **What is this?** (A house floor plan.)
- **How important is this? Why?** (Very important, because it tells the workers exactly how to build the house. Otherwise, you could end up with a house that leaks or falls down, etc. You wouldn’t be able to live in it.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Divide your class into teams of three or four. Instruct them to work together to build the structure of the house based on the simple floor plan you just showed them. They will use toothpicks and mini marshmallows (or blocks, Legos, strips of cardboard, etc.). Allow two minutes for building. Children will show their structures to others when the time is up.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How successful was your team at building the house structure? Why?**
- **Did any of your team members have skills that made the job easier for all of you?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Display the sanctuary floor plan and a picture of the completed sanctuary from the **Program PowerPoints** or lesson. Highlight that in today’s story you will see how God’s people built the sanctuary exactly to the plan God had given Moses. He asked for the sanctuary to be made because He wanted to be close to them. Point to the **Big Idea** on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God wants to be very close to His people.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story to bring it to life for the children.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Hold up a photo of you with your spouse or a good friend known to the children. Unpack how you got to know them. You may like to invite the children to guess how you met.

- **Who is someone you have a close relationship with?** (Mom, dad, sibling, relative, friend at church or school.)
- **How did you build a friendship and become close to that person?** Pair share with a friend, then share as a group. (Talked, did things together and for each other, loved each other.)
- **How does it make you feel, knowing you are close to that person?** (Special, cared for, important, safe.)
- **How did the people show they wanted to be close to God in the story today?** (They gave their best treasures, their talents, and their time to build the beautiful sanctuary.)

God wants to be very close to you too!

- **How can we build a friendship with God and come close to Him?** Write their answers on your whiteboard and draw a heart around them when done. (Spend time with Him every day; talk with Him; give Him our love and worship; read His words in the Bible; trust and obey Him, etc.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Just as you are close to your mom, dad, or friend, God wanted to be very close to His people too. That’s why He asked them to build the tabernacle, so that He could “dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8). God wants to be very close to you today, because you are very precious to Him.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 64: Moses and the Sanctuary** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: “And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8).

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/12-exodus-25-8.mp3] “Exodus 25:8” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Highlight the word **“let”** in the memory verse. Explain that when someone lets us do something, it means we are allowed to do the thing we want to do. It is often a privilege. When God said “Let,” He was saying that it was a privilege for the Israelites to be able to build Him a sanctuary.

- **Why do you think God wanted a sanctuary built instead of just visiting the people?** (He wanted to live with them always, not just now and then.)

It is a privilege for us to have God as our very best friend too. Think about it: the God of the universe wants to be your best friend!

Invite the children to do a “crowd wave” as you all say, “Thank You, God!” They are to sit or crouch down; then the people on the far right are to stand and put their hands up. The person beside them follows; it repeats.

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Nature Lesson, **“Clever Barn Swallows,”** and engage the children in the discussion questions.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Make a **Tabernacle Flip Book** to show the different furniture in the sanctuary.

Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** in the background as children make their craft. As the children work on their craft, explain that next week we will learn about the special meaning of each of the parts of the sanctuary and its furniture. Affirm them for working together to make God’s tabernacle beautiful.

**^[REVIEW AND COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Hold up today’s craft and discuss:

- **Do you think God needed a tent to live in? Why?** (No. God is everywhere, but He chose the tabernacle to help His people understand that He wanted to be close and live with them.)

Say the **Big Idea** together: **God wants to be very close to His people.**

Today, instead of living in a tent, God wants to live in our hearts. Point to the heart you drew around your earlier brainstorm and go through the ways we can be close to our loving God. Invite the children to stand and, if they would like to, choose one or more to focus on this week then silently pray to God, committing to this special time with Him.
