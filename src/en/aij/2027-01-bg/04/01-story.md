_Chop, chop, chop (make motions with each sound)_ went the ax on the big strong trees. Thwack! The tall, tall trees came tumbling down. _Sh, sh, sh_ went the saw making long boards of wood. 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 _(count fingers)_ boards stacked up high. _Tap, tap, tap_ went the hammer from morning to night. Noah and his family worked each day to build the ark. There was much work to do. There had never been a boat like this. It would be taller than a giraffe and bigger than an elephant.\
God told Noah how to build the great big boat. Noah and his family built it bit by bit, board by board, and nail by nail, just as God had told them. It would be so big! _(Arms wide.)_ It would be bigger than a truck, and bigger than a rocket! There would be a place for all the people who wanted to come on board. There would be space for the tigers, and lions, and even the tall, tall giraffes _(reach up high)_. There would be room for the hippos, and panda bears, and even the tiny caterpillars _(thumb and finger almost touching)_.

Day by day, year by year, Noah and his family worked on the ark. It got bigger and bigger. The sides went higher and higher. God told Noah to make an opening around the roof, filling the ark with air and light. Noah was God’s helper. He did all that God told him to do. Inside the ark would be a place for all God’s friends, food to eat, a room for the kangaroos, and one for the monkeys too.\
Noah and his family worked hard for six days _(six fingers)_ and then stopped to remember God’s special Sabbath day. Every Sabbath they rested from their work. They spent time with God and each other. On the seventh day _(seven fingers)_ of each week the ax didn’t chop, the saw didn’t sh-sh, and the hammers didn’t tap. Noah and his family loved this day the very best! They talked and sang and prayed to God. Every Sabbath they thanked God for all He had made. They thanked Him for His love and care.

Noah and his family had lots of work to do. They did everything just as God told them to. They were so happy to be God’s helpers! They built the ark from the tall, strong trees. They made a place for the animals—big and small. They made room for all the food they would need. But who would help the people remember God’s love for them? _(Tap heart.)_ Who would tell them about God’s big plan? Who would tell them to come inside the ark and be safe? Noah would! He would be God’s loving helper.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for loving me. I love You too! I’m happy to be Your helper.
