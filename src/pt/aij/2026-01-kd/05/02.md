Little insects and happy green frogs sang together beneath tall trees. Eve smiled as she walked along. What a beautiful day it was! Suddenly Eve stopped. Her eyes opened wide. Right in front of her was the “don’t touch it” tree, the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. _Wow! The fruit is so beautiful_, Eve thought to herself (Genesis 3:6).

A smooth voice came from inside the tree: “Did God really say that you must not eat fruit from any tree in the garden?” (Genesis 3:1, ICB).

Eve looked closer. She saw a serpent with sparkly golden wings. What a surprise! Serpents don’t talk! But this one did.

“We may eat fruit from the trees in the garden,” Eve replied. “But God told us, ‘You must not eat fruit from the tree that is in the middle of the garden. You must not even touch it, or you will die’ ” (Genesis 3:2, 3, ICB).

What did the serpent say next? **Read Genesis 3:4.**

What a big lie! God had very clearly warned Adam and Eve that if they ate from this tree they would die. But the serpent said they wouldn’t die. He called God a liar! Do you think you might know who this lying serpent was? Yes, Satan!

Would Adam and Eve believe Satan and eat the fruit, or would they obey their loving Creator and Friend?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What could Eve have done when she felt tempted?
- What is something you could do the next time you feel tempted to do something wrong?
