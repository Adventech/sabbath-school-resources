Exciting News in Galilee

The road out of Jerusalem was particularly busy on this bright, happy day. Family and friends walked and talked excitedly together as they traveled back to their homes far away. They had just spent a whole week in Jerusalem for Passover. Passover was the biggest celebration of the whole year. It was a special time to remember how God had saved them.

But this year, Passover was even more special. Jesus had come to Jerusalem. Mothers and aunties chatted loudly, children talked as they ran and played, and fathers waved their arms as they spoke. Everyone talked about Jesus. Jesus had said that the temple was His Father’s house. He had chased away the greedy people who were treating it like a marketplace. Was God His Father? The people thought about the amazing things Jesus had said and done. Some began to believe in Him.

Jesus and His disciples were also leaving Jerusalem after Passover. They were walking toward Galilee. “When Jesus arrived in Galilee, the people there welcomed him” (John 4:45, ICB). They had already heard so many wonderful things about Him and were happy that He had come to visit their town.

“Jesus traveled through all the towns and villages. He taught in their synagogues [churches] and told people the Good News” (Matthew 9:35, ICB).

What else did He do? **Read the rest of Matthew 9:35.**

Jesus loved spending time with people. He loved to heal and help them. He still loves to heal and help you!
