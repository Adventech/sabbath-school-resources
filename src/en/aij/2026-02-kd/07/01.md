**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. I can be brave.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Isaiah 41:10)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Courage”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Courage is being able to do something even when it seems scary or hard. To have courage is to be brave. When we have our big, strong God by our side, we can be filled with courage knowing He will help us.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Genesis 37:12-35 and _Patriarchs and Prophets_, pp. 210-214.

Happy to Help

Joseph swung the hammer at the tent peg. Ting. Ting. Ting. He paused and listened. _Someone was calling for him. It sounded like Father Jacob._ Carefully Joseph returned the hammer and raced toward his father’s tent.

Jacob laughed as Joseph burst through the doors of the tent with a big grin. Joseph was always so quick to obey. It made him happy to see his son being so honest and eager to help. Then Jacob’s smile faded. He remembered why he had called Joseph. He was worried.

It wasn’t another wobbly tent peg that worried Jacob. He was worried about his older sons. They had been looking after the sheep in far-away fields for a long time. _Were they safe? Were they well and happy?_

“Go and see if your brothers and the sheep are all right. Then come back and tell me,” Jacob said to Joseph (Genesis 37:14 ICB).

The last time Joseph had seen his 10 older brothers they had been very unkind to him. Would Joseph want to go find them? **Read Genesis 37:13.**

Joseph packed his bag with food, put on his colorful coat, and waved to his father. Then he started his long, lonely trip. But Joseph knew he wasn’t really alone because God was always with him. God would give him courage.

God can help you be brave too. He says, “Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you” (Isaiah 41:10).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- If you were Joseph, would you have wanted to help your dad with his work?
- What do you like to do to help your family?
