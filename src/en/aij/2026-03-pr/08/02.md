The day after seeing Jesus return to the Jordan River, John the Baptist saw Him again. A smile lit up his face as he shouted, “Behold the Lamb of God!” (John 1:36).

Two fisherman, Andrew and John, heard John’s bold words about Jesus. They wondered what “the Lamb of God” meant. They longed to find out, so they followed Jesus.

Hearing John and Andrew’s footsteps behind Him, Jesus turned and said, “What do you seek?” (John 1:38). Then what happened? **Read John 1:38, 39.**

Imagine the wonderful time those two friends had sitting at Jesus’ feet and listening to Him speak. They had recognized the Holy Spirit in John the Baptist’s preaching; now they heard the same Spirit in Jesus’ voice. John and Andrew knew that Jesus truly was the Messiah and that He loved them very much. They had followed Him with their feet; now they followed Him with their hearts. They were the first to follow Jesus, and it made Him very glad.

Andrew couldn’t wait to tell his brother the good news! Excitement rippled through him as he ran to find Simon. “We have found the Messiah!” he cried. “And he brought him to Jesus” (John 1:41, 42). Jesus welcomed Simon right away and gave him a new name to suit the good things in his character. He would now be called “Peter,” which means “stone.”

After John and Andrew followed Jesus, they spent time with Him, getting to know Him. This is the most important thing for us to do when we follow Jesus too. He calls us to be His friend and learn from Him. Then, when we truly know He is our Savior, our hearts will bubble over with His love, and we will want to tell our family and friends how awesome Jesus is. Just like Jesus, we will say to them, “Come and see” (John 1:39); come and get to know Jesus for yourself.
