**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite volunteers to **mime stories about Jesus** for the other children to guess. Tell them to think about the previous 10 lessons and think of stories where Jesus did amazing things so that it’s easy for others to guess.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children to all return to their seats. **Hold up your Bible** and share how the stories about Jesus and what He taught are all recorded for us in the Bible. But in Jesus’ day these true stories were told from person to person and traveled from town to town. As they were told, people’s faith in Jesus grew. The stories made their way to a Roman centurion, a Roman official in charge of 100 other soldiers. Let’s find out what happened as he heard these stories and when he met Jesus.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **Jesus sees my need and responds to my faith in Him.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the Bible story, using the **Program PowerPoint**, photos of yourself when you were young, and the discussion questions.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to **stand up and form pairs**. Ask them the following questions, then after each question, give them 60 seconds to share together in their pairs. Use a **timer** with a tone as their cue. After each question, invite two volunteers to share their answers with the group. Then invite them to **change pairs** to answer the next question, and so on. Affirm their answers.

- **What in this story surprises you? Why?**
- **What does this story tell you about Jesus’ words?**
- **Why can we be sure that Jesus sees our needs and wants to help?**
- **Do we need big faith or faith in a big God? Why?** (Say this slowly a couple of times or write it out on the board.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus’ words are so powerful, and they can give life. With His words He can help and heal us too. His Word tells us the truth about who He is and how He loves, so we can be assured that He will always respond to our needs. We need only a little faith in this big God to see Him work in our lives.

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite each person to move out of their pair and to find their own space in the Sabbath School room/area. Invite them **to silently pray** to God and share with Him about something they need.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 50: The Roman Centurion** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to say Jesus’ special words that are in the memory verse: “Go your way; and as you have believed, so let it be done for you” (Matthew 8:13).

Ask the children to say this verse in their own words. Then **sing** the **Scripture Song** together: **“Matthew 8:13.”**

God wants us to believe that He can do great things!

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Prayers of Faith,”** and discuss the follow-up questions.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display the **Roman Shield craft**. Invite the children to create a new design for the centurion’s shield that shows the theme of faith and healing.

Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly in the background as children complete their craft.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Hold up the seeds and plantings from the Early Arrivals activity.

- **What does this remind you of in today’s story?** (Our faith starts small, but it can grow, just as the seed does when it’s in the right growing place. As the Roman centurion learned more about God and heard stories of Jesus, His teachings, and the miracles He performed, his faith grew to be like a very large tree!)
- **How can your faith grow this week?** (By believing that God is good and He cares. By trusting His words and promises in the Bible, acting in faith, trusting God, and knowing that He sees your needs and will respond to your faith at the best time.)

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing **“God Be with You.”** Then pray, thanking God for giving each person faith that can grow just like a seed when it’s planted.
