**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

How wonderful it is to be close to God, our friend! When we hold Him close to our hearts, we experience connectedness with Him that brings peace and joy to our lives.

But have you ever felt far away from God? Perhaps He felt distant because of the distractions of your busy life. Or perhaps He felt out of reach because of a difficult situation you were going through and couldn’t see a possible solution. Perhaps you felt distant because of sins you held on to. Whatever the reason, we feel empty and alone without a close relationship with God. Our hearts long for it, because that’s what we were made for.

This was Jacob’s experience as he ran from his brother, Esau. Guilt weighed heavily on him as he wondered whether he had gone too far for God to reach him. As he fled, Jacob confessed his sin. He repented, knowing that the burden of his deceit and lies had put a wedge between him and his family. But more important, it had also separated him from God. How he wanted to repair all the brokenness in his life!

It was at this low point, alone, humbled, repentant, and seeking, that Jacob was given a special dream of a ladder with angels busily flying up and down from heaven to earth. This ladder symbolized Jesus, our Savior, and the salvation we receive through Him. “The Lord compassionately revealed just what Jacob needed—a Saviour. He had sinned, but his heart was filled with gratitude as he saw revealed a way by which he could be restored to the favor of God” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 183).

As you review this story and teach it this week, may you once again choose God—coming to Him with a humble, repentant heart so that nothing can come between you and your Savior. And may you and the children in your Sabbath School know that God’s arms are open wide, ready to embrace you all.
