**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **piece of paper** for each child and **colored pencils/markers**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source a **small bowl of flour** with something of value (e.g., a coin) hidden in it.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: **pipe cleaners** to make into simple characters that you can change as Jesus heals them: Jesus; the woman whose back was bent over, then straightened; the man with a disability in his hand; a man with a swelling sickness. Be sensitive and careful not to make them comical.

Source a **piece of paper** and **pen** for each child.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 47: Jesus Heals on the Sabbath]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Source **cardstock** for the memory verse activity, and write one word of the memory verse on each piece of cardstock.
