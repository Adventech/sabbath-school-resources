With a sad but gentle voice God told Adam and Eve that life would change. God had wanted to keep them from experiencing pain and death, but they had chosen to disobey Him and eat from the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. Now Adam would have to work hard to grow food. Eve would feel pain when she had children.

Then God told them they would have to leave their beautiful garden home. Once again, their hearts filled with sorrow . . . leave their home?

Yes, for God always means what He says. So with one long last look at all the beauty God had wanted them to enjoy forever, they left Eden. Never to return.

Why did God tell them they would have to leave the Garden of Eden? Read Genesis 3:22, 23.

God had given them the tree of life so they would live forever. But living forever with sin would be horrible! God had to keep His word. He put angels with flaming swords at the gates to the garden to guard the tree of life.

Adam and Eve now knew good and evil, and it was no fun at all. The one good thing in the middle of all the sorrow was that God would never leave them, just as He won’t ever leave us. They couldn’t see Him face-to-face anymore, but He would always be there. For He says in His Word, “I am with you always” (Matthew 28:20).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _When have you had to leave something or someone you love? How did you feel?_
- _What would you like to say to God, knowing that He will never leave you?_
