**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God’s mighty power had protected the Israelites through nine drastic plagues in Egypt. This week’s story finds them poised for one of the greatest escapes in history.

Whole families were gathered under one roof, filled with suspense. They checked the “to-do list” once again. The blood of the lamb covered the doorpost. The Passover meal was prepared. The bags were packed. The sandals were laced up. The animals were corralled. And they waited . . .

Piercing wails marked the stroke of midnight, and Egypt was plunged into grief. But in the hearts of the Hebrews there was jubilation, and the freedom march began. Courage and triumph marked every step on this night that divides their history, forever to be remembered and celebrated.

God protected His people with His mighty power. And in the deliverance was a Passover promise. The Messiah would come and truly save all who put their trust in Him. Jesus would be “the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” (John 1:29), and there would be “redemption through His blood” (Ephesians 1:7).

God’s saving grace extends all the way through history to you. Thanks to Jesus, your heavenly escape plan is set. Do you feel the urgency? Do you believe the promise to be true?

As you and your Sabbath School class discover the deep and beautiful truths embedded in this deliverance story, our prayer is that you will have your hearts enlarged by God’s steadfast love, and His mighty power to protect and deliver.
