As Jacob lay there in the quiet darkness, he smiled and thought about the glowing ladder and the shining angels. What an amazing dream! He didn’t feel lonely anymore. God had said, “I am with you . . . I will not leave you . . .” (Genesis 28:15, ICB).

He looked at the dark, distant hills and the starry sky. Yes, this place where God talked to him was very special. He said, “Surely the Lord is in this place” (Genesis 28:16, ICB).

As the early morning sun began to creep across the land, Jacob got up. He couldn’t sleep, he was too excited. He had been forgiven! God had promised him a wonderful new life. He had to do something to show God just how thankful he was. What did he do? **Read Genesis 28:18.**

This would be a special reminder rock. Whenever Jacob walked past this place, the rock would remind him of the dazzling dream and God’s great love. He would stop in this special place and worship God.

It is so important to remember God and thank Him for the wonderful things He does for you. Remembering and praising God can help you to be confident in His love when you are sad or worried.
