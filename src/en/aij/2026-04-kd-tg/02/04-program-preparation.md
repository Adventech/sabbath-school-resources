**Opening Activities**

Source or make **Creation items** for a Creation hunt. You may like to cut out simple shapes in cardstock or use props from quarter one, toys, pictures, or living things. Include: stars, moon, sun, plants and flowers, fruits and vegetables, fish and birds, animals.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box:** feathers or cotton balls, or lightweight paper scraps.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
