Isn’t it wonderful that Jesus was able to make so many hungry people happy and full?

Now it’s your chance to share some surprise food blessings too. This week you have been planning and preparing meals to share with others. Together as a family, deliver the meals today. Who knows, maybe the surprise food will create smiles, joy, and even new friends you can share Jesus with. Share this week’s Big Idea with your new friend in the community.
