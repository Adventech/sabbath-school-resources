**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He wants to be my close friend.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

- Use **white dot stickers** to represent leprosy.

**TELL THE STORY**

In today’s Bible story Jesus was a kind and loving friend to three men ^[(hold up three fingers/figurines/felts)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) who really needed His care.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The first man ^[(peg man)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) sat under a little shelter, far outside the city gates. He looked sadly back toward his home. Oh, how he missed his family.

The man had a terrible sickness called leprosy. His skin was blotchy and covered with sores. Sometimes he couldn’t feel parts of his body or use his arms or legs well. But even worse, he was sad and lonely. People with leprosy were told to leave their home and their families. They were to go away—and stay away!

Wherever the man went, he had to call out, “Unclean! Unclean!” (Leviticus 13:45). The people would rush away. They wouldn’t talk to him. They wouldn’t touch him. They wouldn’t even come close.

But one day the man heard about Jesus. Jesus didn’t tell people to go away. Jesus was kind. And Jesus loved making people better. The man jumped to his feet. He had to find Jesus. He had to ask Jesus to heal him!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After a long walk the man saw Jesus by the lake. He bowed down at Jesus’ feet, and said, “If You are willing, You can make me clean” (Mark 1:40).

Jesus reached out and touched the man with leprosy. He hadn’t been touched in years! Then Jesus said, “I am willing; be cleansed [healed]” (Mark 1:41).

At once the man was healed! He looked up with a happy smile into Jesus’ kind, caring eyes. He was all better!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The healed man raced home to find his family and tell them the good news. There were happy hugs and joyful shouts.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The second man ^[(peg man)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) met Jesus on a Sabbath afternoon in Jerusalem. Jesus noticed the man sitting on the side of the street, begging. Jesus always notices the people who need kindness and love. This man had been blind since he was born. That means he had never been able to see anything.

Jesus crouched down in front of the man and spat on the ground. He then used His hands to mix His spit with the dirt to make some mud. ^[(Use play-dough and mime action.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He put the mud on the man’s eyes. Then He told him to go and wash his eyes at a nearby pool.

When the cool water splashed on his face, his eyes flickered open. There was a bright burst of light. He squinted and looked around for the first time ever. He could see!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But not everyone was happy. When the Pharisees heard that Jesus had healed the man on Sabbath, they were angry. They didn’t believe Jesus was anyone special. And they didn’t believe Jesus should break their rules and help others on the Sabbath. The Pharisees called the healed man a liar and treated him unkindly. Then they told him loudly to leave the synagogue.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Like a loving friend, Jesus went and found the man. He told the man that He was the Son of God. The man bowed and worshipped Jesus. Now he was truly happy.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus met the third man (peg man) in a big crowd in a town by the sea. “This man was deaf and could not talk” (Mark 7:32, ICB). His friends and family begged Jesus to put His hands on the man and heal him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus wanted to be alone with the deaf man, so He gently led the man away from the noisy crowd. With kindness in His eyes, Jesus put His fingers in the man’s ears and touched the man’s tongue. Jesus looked up to heaven and said, “Be opened” (Mark 7:34).

Immediately the man could hear and speak clearly. He was healed! The first thing he heard was the kind voice of his new Friend, Jesus.

^[Show your three peg people (or felts).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Say: **Here were three people who had felt left out. Three people who now felt included and loved. Three people who were now close friends of Jesus. Thank You, Jesus.**
