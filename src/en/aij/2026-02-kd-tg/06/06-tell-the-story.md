**Big Idea:** God is with me. He cares for me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use colorful **blocks** to make an altar together with the children.
- Use a **baby doll wrapped in a blanket** as Benjamin.
- Use **yarn** for the children to feel and touch, dip into **containers** to pretend to dye and weave.
- Have a **colorful coat/cloth** to invite children to try on.
- Count out **11 silver stars** on a **black blanket** as you share the dream.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 6 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

- God told Jacob He would care for his family: with land; many children. Some would be kings.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob stacked the stones ^[(stack blocks)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). A big one, then a little one, another big one. He was making an altar to God in Bethel. This was the special place where God had given him a dream about a ladder of angels. God had promised to be with him wherever he went!

When the altar was finished, Jacob and his family gave God an offering of thanks. What a faithful Friend God had been to their family. Then God spoke to Jacob once again. God promised to care for Jacob’s family: they would grow bigger, they would have land, and some would even become kings.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As they left Bethel, Rachel gave birth to a beautiful baby boy ^[(pass around baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was named Benjamin. Now Jacob had 12 sons!

Sadly, after Benjamin was born, Rachel died. Jacob was very, very sad. But he knew God cared and would comfort him.

As little Benjamin grew, Father Jacob loved to tell him and bigger brother Joseph stories about God. Joseph listened closely. His heart was gentle and caring. He really wanted to follow God’s ways.

When Joseph was older, he went to work with his brothers, caring for the sheep and goats in the fields ^[(hold toy sheep, staff)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Joseph was a good, honest worker. But he noticed his big brothers often doing and saying bad things. He tried to tell them to do the right thing, but they wouldn’t listen.

Father Jacob was thankful Joseph was a kind and honest son he could trust.

One day Jacob decided to give Joseph a gift to show his love. Heading out into the field, Jacob brought in some sheep. He clipped off their fluffy wool and began twirling and whirling it into long threads ^[(pass around yarn; dip in containers)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then Jacob dipped the threads in little clay pots of colorful dye.

When the wool dried, Jacob began to weave it together: over and under, over and under ^[(pretend to weave)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Slowly and carefully he wove a bright, beautiful coat for Joseph.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finished! Proudly Jacob gave Joseph the special gift. Joseph’s mouth opened wide in surprise! It was so beautiful. He loved it! ^[(Children try on coat/cloth.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But his big brothers didn’t like the coat at all. Their father hadn’t made any of them a coat of beautiful colors! It made them jealous and angry. “So they hated their brother and could not speak to him politely” (Genesis 37:4, ICB).

Poor Joseph! It was hard to have angry, mean brothers. But he could give his worries to God, for God cared for him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One night tired Joseph took off his coat, lay down, and fell fast asleep. As he slept, God gave him a dream. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He dreamed that he and his brothers were cutting wheat in the field. His bundle of wheat stood up straight, while their wheat bundles bowed down to his.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph told his brothers about his strange dream. The brothers were mad! How dare he try to be the boss of them! “Do you really think you will be king over us?” they said to their little brother. “His brothers hated him even more now” (Genesis 37:8, ICB).

As he slept one night, Joseph had another dream. Confused, he told his brothers about it again. “I had another dream. I saw the sun, moon and 11 stars bowing down to me,” he said (Genesis 37:9, ICB). ^[(Count out and place 11 stars, sun, and moon on black blanket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Joseph’s brothers glared at him, their faces turning red with anger. Did he say 11 stars? Joseph had 11 brothers! It sounded as if Joseph thought he was the best in the family!

It sounded strange to Jacob too. God had given him a very special dream long ago. Maybe God was trying to tell Joseph something special too.

Yes, God was caring for this family! They could trust Him.
