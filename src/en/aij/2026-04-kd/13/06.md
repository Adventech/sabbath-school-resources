Everyone was still. Everyone was silent. The powerful words of Jesus echoed off the rock: “Lazarus, come out!” (John 11:43, ICB).

At once, “the dead man came out” (John 11:44, ICB). Lazarus was alive!

The people must have gasped! It seemed impossible. The special friend of Jesus who had lain still in a tomb, without a breath or beating heart, for four days, was now alive!

Lazarus stood there at the door to the cave, wrapped from head to toe in tight bandages. Lazarus waddled slowly forward.

“Take the cloth off of him and let him go,” said Jesus (John 11:44, ICB).

Racing forward, Lazarus’ family and friends unwrapped him. He was the most wonderful present they had ever been given! Lazarus glowed with health and strength. As soon as he was unwrapped, he ran forward and fell at Jesus’ feet in worship.

The crowd could not believe what they had just seen! At first, they were quiet, unsure how to react. Then they began to praise God with joy! Mary hugged Lazarus. Lazarus hugged Jesus. And Jesus hugged Martha. The whole crowd clapped and sang praises to the all-powerful God who had given life back to Lazarus! There were smiles, happy tears, and laughter all across that mountain top.

One day there will be more smiles, happy tears, and laughter when Jesus returns and wakes up the people who have died loving Him. People who have been dead for weeks, months, and even thousands of years will be given life. Jesus truly is all-powerful!
