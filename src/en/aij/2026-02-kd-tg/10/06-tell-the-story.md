**Big Idea:** God is with me. He gives me everything I need.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- **Fancy fabric/robe**.
- **Grain**, **rice**, or **beans as grain**. **Containers, cups, small bag**. Invite the children to transfer cups of grain into a large container as the storehouse.
- A **bag of coins** to hide in a bag of grain.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 10 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Pharaoh was happy. Joseph’s God and wise Joseph would save Egypt from a time of hunger.
- Pharaoh gave Governor Joseph a king’s ring, robe, golden chain, royal chariot. ^[(Dress a child in a fancy robe, golden chain, and ring.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God had told Joseph there would be seven years with lots of food, then seven hungry years. He would store up the food from the good times to get through the bad times.
- Joseph’s workers built storehouses, planted and harvested grain, and stored it in the storehouses. ^[(Children to fill container with cups of grain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- One, two, three, four, five, six, seven good years ^[(count on fingers)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God gave them more than they needed! The storehouses were full!
- The famine started, just as God said. Hungry people came to Joseph, and he sold them grain—God had supplied their needs. ^[(Pour some grain from a cup into a small bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Joseph’s family in Canaan needed food too. His 10 older brothers traveled to Egypt to buy grain.
- The brothers bowed down to Governor Joseph. They didn’t recognize him. His dream had come true!
- Joseph tested his brothers—had they changed? He said they were spies and put them in prison.
- After three days he said they could go home if they returned with Benjamin. Simeon was to stay in prison until then.
- Joseph’s servants filled their sacks with grain, and secretly put in the money they had paid Joseph. What a surprise! Food and money! God was supplying their needs ^[(bag with grain, coin bag)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Pharaoh stood tall and smiled at his new governor, Joseph. Joseph’s God had told him the meaning of his strange dreams. And now this wise man and his wise God would help save Egypt from a time of hunger.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Pharaoh took off his Egyptian royal ring and slid it onto Joseph’s finger ^[(ring)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). This ring was like Pharaoh’s signature. With it Joseph could do anything the king would do! Pharaoh clapped his hands. His servants dressed Joseph in fancy robes and put a shiny golden chain around his neck. ^[(Clap, dress a child in a robe and chain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The king gave him a fast chariot and servants too. With a quick bow to the king, Joseph left the palace right away. Then he “traveled through all the land of Egypt” (Genesis 41:46, ICB). He counted the fields of wheat and barley. He chose good leaders and strong helpers. Then he had bricks made and giant storehouses built in all the cities.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had told Joseph that there would be seven good years with plenty of food. During this time he would need to store lots and lots of food away. Then there would be enough food in the storehouses to feed everyone during the next seven bad years.

Like busy little ants, Joseph’s helpers did whatever he asked. The builders built, the farmers planted and harvested, and the helpers carried the extra grain straight to the storehouses in each city. ^[(Children to transfer grain in cups to empty container.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

(Count on fingers.) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After one, two, three, four, five, six, seven years, the storehouses were filled to the very, very top. God had supplied so much more than they needed!

Then came the time of no rain. With a windy whoosh, the dust blew across Egypt’s dry fields. Not a single green leaf could be seen. The cows and sheep grew thin. Soon everyone was hungry! ^[(Rub tummy.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Pharaoh sent the hungry people to Joseph. Joseph swung open the doors to the storehouses. He began selling bags of grain to the hungry families. There was more than enough! God had supplied for all their need ^[(fill small bag with grain)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

In faraway Canaan, people were hungry too. Joseph’s family was very hungry! His brothers all still lived with Father Jacob, looking after their sheep. When Jacob heard there was food in Egypt, he was excited! He sent his 10 older sons to go to Egypt and buy lots of grain.

Governor Joseph looked up from his work to see 10 shepherds walking toward him to buy grain. He bent his head and looked a little closer. Could it be . . . ?

Yes! It was his 10 older brothers!

Joseph quickly hid his surprise. His brothers didn’t know it was him! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Quietly he watched as they bowed low to him. Suddenly Joseph remembered the dreams he’d had as a boy! They had come true!

Governor Joseph looked at his brothers carefully. Were they still angry, jealous men? He would test them and see.

Joseph frowned and pretended to get very, very upset. He told his brothers he thought they were enemy spies. He had them put in prison! The brothers were so confused! But God was with them in prison, just as He had been with Joseph.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After three days Joseph freed them from prison. He told them they could go home with their grain if they brought back their younger brother Benjamin. Joseph really wanted to see his younger brother! He would keep brother Simeon in prison until they returned.

The brothers didn’t like this idea one bit! But they had to agree.

Joseph had his servants fill their sacks with grain to take home. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph told his servants to also hide, inside the bags of grain, the money his brothers had given him. Free food! What a kind and generous surprise ^[(sack of grain, surprise bag of coins inside)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God was looking after Joseph’s family, just as He looks after you.
