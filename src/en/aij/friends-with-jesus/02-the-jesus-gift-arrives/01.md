Time to Go!

Toot, toot, toot, the trumpets blasted. The announcement boomed across cities and towns far and wide. Ruler Caesar Augustus had ordered everyone to go to their family’s hometown. Everyone had to go now! He wanted to count all the people. And he wasn’t asking, “Please, if you don’t mind.” No, it was an order! A command. If people didn’t listen, they would be in big trouble.

So what did all the people do? **Read Luke 2:3.**

_Hurry, hurry!_ Moms and dads, brothers and sisters, grandmothers and grandfathers busily packed their bags. Whether their hometown was close by or far away, they must go there—and they must go there now!

There were no cars, motorbikes, trains, or planes. Instead, people had to walk the long, dusty roads or ride a bumpy donkey or horse.

Before they left, many families bowed together to humbly pray. They knew that God promises to “guard you as you come and go” (Psalm 121:8, ICB). They wanted God to be with them and keep them safe on their long journey.
