**Bread for Trash**

**Location:** Help a child locate **Angola** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Adna was a grumpy person. She liked to argue and fight with her neighbors in Luanda, Angola.

Every time Adna saw her neighbor Mary, she yelled at her. But Mary only smiled and greeted her happily. “It’s good to see you today!” Mary said. “May God bless you!”

Adna yelled louder and tried to pick a fight, but Mary would simply smile.

This made Adna very angry. She took a bag of garbage from her house and set it beside Mary’s front gate. Mary didn’t complain. She didn’t say a word. She picked up the bag of garbage and took it down the street to the big trash container.

Then Mary went and gave Adna some freshly baked bread. “It’s good to see you today! This bread is for you,” said Mary. Adna was so surprised! But she still wanted to fight. So when she had another big bag of garbage, she left it at Mary’s front gate again. Mary picked up the bag and threw it away. Then she came to Adna. “It’s good to see you today!” she said. “This bread is for you.”

This happened again and again. Adna left bags of garbage at Mary’s gate, and Mary brought over bread.

One Sabbath morning Mary came out of her house just as Adna was leaving a bag of garbage at the front gate. Mary was dressed up and ready to go to church, but she smiled and took the garbage away. Then she went to Adna’s house with bread.

Adna began to cry. “Thank you very much for the bread,” she said. “Please wait for us. We also want to go to church with you. My family and I would like to learn to love your Jesus.”

Today Adna and her family are happy Seventh-day Adventists. She no longer likes to be grumpy or unkind. Instead, she smiles at her neighbors and says with a smile, “It’s good to see you today! May God bless you.”

- **How did Mary show that she honored grumpy Adna?**

Showing kindness through our actions even when someone is unkind to us is honoring them and sharing God’s love.
