**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The world tells us that being confident is a good thing. But on what, or whom, is our confidence based?

God has given us every reason to have confidence and recognize that He is the ultimate reason we can be confident in facing the challenges of life. He created us, saved us, and loves us. He invites us to cease our worry, our fretting and stress, and depend on Him for peace, joy, and love. He alone is the one who can give us the peace that passes all understanding.

This kind of peace is not something we can achieve ourselves. The Bible tells us to “trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths” (Proverbs 3:5, 6).

Despite seeing God’s promises so faithfully fulfilled, Jacob fell into the trap that so easily ensnares all of us: depending on self instead of God. His choice to take advantage of his brother was the beginning of much trouble and trauma. “We need to have far less confidence in what man can do and far more confidence in what God can do for every believing soul” (Ellen G. White, _Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 146).

May you and the children in your Sabbath School class grow in confidence as you see God’s goodness and His sovereign hand at work in the lives of Isaac, Rebekah, and their family. And may you be like Abraham’s wise servant and ask God to lead you every step, every day.
