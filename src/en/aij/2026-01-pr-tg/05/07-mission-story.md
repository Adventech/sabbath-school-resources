**Focus:** Learning about Jesus and choosing to worship Him instead of the gods in the temples.

**Location:** Help a child locate **southeastern India** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Hadassah was nervous and worried about going to a new school. _What if the children don’t like me? What will my teachers be like? Will my classmates be kind to me?_ she wondered.

Hadassah’s parents weren’t Christians, but they had enrolled her in an Adventist school in southeastern India because they wanted her to learn English. They warned her, “Be careful! Don’t listen to what those Christians say about their God! Remember the gods we worship in the temple!”

Hadassah tried to obey her parents, but as her teachers told stories about Jesus, she felt drawn to Him. _How can this God who made the world and everything in it love me?_ she wondered. She didn’t feel like her family’s gods loved her!

The more Hadassah heard in Bible class at her new school, the more she realized that Jesus is the one true God and that she loved Him. She accepted Jesus as her Savior, but she couldn’t tell her parents. If they knew she had stopped worshiping their gods, they would most likely take Hadassah out of school. Then she would have to go to another school where she would be forced to worship gods she didn’t believe in anymore.

Hadassah is praying that her parents will see the difference in her life and that they will one day accept her decision to follow Jesus.

Hadassah says, “I hope other children will choose to follow Jesus and will be ready when Jesus comes back. No matter what temptations come your way, don’t wait. Give your life to Jesus now.” Hadassah has given good advice. God has given each one of us the freedom to choose Jesus.

Let’s pray for children like Hadassah who have made the choice to follow Jesus when it isn’t easy.
