Opening Activities

Source **gray paper** and cut into strips to create a memory verse paper chain, as well as pencils/crayons, glue, memory verse card, stapler.

Prepare ingredients for the lemonade object lesson, as well as a bigger batch of lemonade to share with the class after Sabbath School. Make **homemade lemonade** with **water, lemons, and sugar or honey**. Have the same ingredients in smaller quantities for the object lesson. You will need a **jug** and **cups** for both. Cut up **pieces of lemon** for the children to try.

Provide extra **coins** for visitors to give as offering.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.
