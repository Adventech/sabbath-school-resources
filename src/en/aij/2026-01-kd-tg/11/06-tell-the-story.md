**Big Idea:** God is loving. I will put others first.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **peg people** or a **felt set** to tell the story.
- Use **plush animals** for Abram’s flocks, and **coins in a bag** for his silver and gold.
- Create “landscapes.” Set up **green fabric** over pillows for mountains, **blue fabric or paper** for streams, and different shades of **green fabric** for flat plains.
- Use the **cardboard altar** from week 8.
- Use **heart stickers (or draw hearts) on craft sticks** to place inside a **sandbox** to illustrate how Abram and Sarai made friends in Canaan.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 11 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Abram had lots and lots of animals and riches. He always remembered His blessings came from God and thanked Him. (Felt or peg man. Toy animals on green fabric. Coins in bag.)
- Abram was humble and made others feel important (stick or peg people).
- Lot had lots of animals too (more toy animals).
- There wasn’t enough grass. The workers began to fight (peg people arguing).
- Abram asked God for help. Abram said he and Lot should separate.
- Abram let Lot choose the land first. (Walk around and look at the “landscapes” with children.)
- Lot chose lush land near the bad city of Sodom.
- Abram chose a peaceful spot and made an altar (cardboard box).
- God promised to give Abram all the land, forever, and a big, big family! (Point to “landscapes” again.)
- Abram and Sarai made friends, honored others, and built altars to God. (Smiling peg people in sandbox. Heart craft sticks.)

**TELL THE STORY**

^[► (Use a peg or felt man for Abram.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram looked left, then right. He looked ahead and behind. Everywhere he turned there were wandering sheep, skipping goats, clip-clopping donkeys, grunting camels, and bellowing oxen. He owned so many animals. ^[(Give toy animals to children to place on green fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) There were too many to count! God had already blessed Abram with so much—and it wasn’t just animals. God had blessed him with silver and gold too! ^[(Jingle coins in a bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Wherever Abram went, people saw how God was blessing him. In fact, they honored him like a mighty prince. But Abram never acted more important than others. Instead, he made others feel important. He showed love and kindness to everyone ^[(use peg people or felts)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Like Abram, Lot was rich with his own herds, flocks, and tents. ^[(Give children other toy animals to place on a different part of the green fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had blessed him too! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) There was just one problem on Abram’s farm: there wasn’t enough grass for all the hungry animals. Abram and Lot’s workers began to argue and fight ^[(use expressive words and actions)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Pointing their fingers angrily, they may have shouted: “Keep your sheep away from the best grass on the hill—it’s our turn!” ^[(Move some sheep away.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “No, it’s not! You’re just greedy!”

Abram thought about his problem and prayed to God for wisdom. He wanted everyone to be happy and peaceful. He told Lot that they should separate and live in different places.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram pointed across the land and told Lot to choose the fields he would like. How kind of Abram! God had given all this land to him—but Abram was choosing to share his land.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lot’s eyes opened wide with delight ^[(open eyes wide and smile)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He could choose first! Lot should have stopped and said, “No, Uncle Abram, you choose first.” But he didn’t. Lot wanted the very best for himself. Greedily he searched for the greenest and most beautiful land. ^[(Look at the various “landscapes” and talk about why each one might be best.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally Lot pointed. “There! Down on the lush, flat, green land!” That was where he wanted to live. He thought it looked perfect. It had lots of green grass, good food, and water. And it was really close to the busy city of Sodom. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He moved his family, workers, and animals to that place ^[(move animals, peg people away)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was so happy with his me-first choice! But the Bible says that “the people of Sodom were very evil” (Genesis 13:13, ICB). Uh-oh, that doesn’t sound like a good place to live, does it?

Up in the hills Abram hummed as he set up his tent. It was so peaceful in the spot he had chosen for his new home. His tent looked out at green grass and big, shady trees. But best of all, it faced an altar to God. ^[(Use cardboard altar from week 8 to build an altar.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God loved seeing Abram set up his home around the altar. He had a surprise to give His friend. All the land in every direction would be his, and his family’s, forever! ^[(Point to the “landscapes” around the room.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God promised Abram, “I will make your descendants as many as the dust of the earth” (Genesis 13:16, ICB). Descendants are children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren. A family too big to even count? That is a very big family! What a kind gift from his loving Friend, God.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram and Sarai made lots of friends in Canaan ^[(place smiling peg people in a sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They told all their friends about God and His love for everyone ^[(place hearts on craft sticks around the peg people)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The people in Canaan noticed that Abram was different from other people. He was happy, friendly, and kind. He put others first instead of himself. He left thank-You altars to God everywhere he went. Abram was different because he had God’s love in his heart!

The Bible says, “Be humble and give more honor to others than to yourselves. Do not be interested only in your own life, but be interested in the lives of others” (Philippians 2:3, 4, ICB). And that is exactly what Abram did. He chose God’s way and honored Lot more than he did himself.
