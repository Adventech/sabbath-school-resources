Baskets of gold and silver gleamed in the sunshine. The people smiled just as brightly. They had so many treasures to give to God! Moses was happy too. God had given Him exact directions to build the sanctuary that would be His desert home.

Excitedly Moses told the people that God had chosen Bezalel and Aholiab to be in charge of work teams. God had given these men special skills and wisdom for the building of His sanctuary. Who else did God give special skills to? **Read Exodus 36:1.**

Bezalel and Aholiab got themselves organized. Pretty soon the whole camp was filled with activity. The men in Bezalel’s work teams swung axes, sawed long planks of acacia wood, and melted silver dishes over a scorching fire. Still others worked with gold or with polished precious stones.

In Aholiab’s work teams, steady fingers spun fine gold, blue, purple, and scarlet thread. Other workers used the fine thread to make beautiful colored cloth for the tabernacle curtains. Another group of workers spun white linen thread that was woven into white cloth. This would be used to make the fence that would surround God’s tabernacle.

Everybody was busy doing something to help build God’s tent church. Helpers fetched and carried. Others swept up wood shavings and timber offcuts. Back in the tents, great cooks made lunch for the workers.

All the busy people were following God’s wise advice. The Bible advises us on how we should work too. “In all the work you are doing, work the best you can. Work as if you were working for the Lord, not men” (Colossians 3:23, ICB).

God gives us talents and skills just as He did the Israelites. Working to use and develop them for Him is a special way we can respond to His love for us. Our actions tell God that we love Him, that He is important in our lives, and that we want to serve Him.
