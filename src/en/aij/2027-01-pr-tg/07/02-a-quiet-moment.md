**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Every day we wake to new miracles from our Creator. The sun rises with distinct colors and cloud patterns. Dew glistens on spiky desert plants, flowers stretch forth their petals, and the wind blows through leaves creating its own tune. In some places snow crystals form and fall—each one boasting its own unique, stunning design.

And every day God offers us His grace. “Through the Lord’s mercies we are not consumed, because His compassions fail not. They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness” (Lamentations 3:22, 23).

The Israelites had witnessed God’s provision with the water. But they were yet to grow a daily trust in Him. As the people started to feel hungry, their gratitude dissolved into grumbles and greed. Once again Moses put the problem where it belonged—he turned to God for help. And God met their grumbles with grace. He rained sweet, honey-flavored manna down from heaven.

Each day, the children of Israel would “see the glory of the Lord” (Exodus 16:7). Six days a week manna carpeted the ground, waiting to be collected. It was “manifest tokens of His love and care” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 292). It spoke of God’s gracious provision, day after day. And on the Sabbath day its absence spoke of God’s desire to be remembered, worshipped, and adored by His grateful, nourished children.

Our loving God is so gracious. Come to Him afresh each morning in His Word this week and open your hands wide to accept His provision. Indeed, great is His faithfulness.

May you and the children be awed by the magnitude of God’s grace. Grace that gives in the face of rejection, that shows up anew every day; grace that is worthy of gratitude—every single day.
