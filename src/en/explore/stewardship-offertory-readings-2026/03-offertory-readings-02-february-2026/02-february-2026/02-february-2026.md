---
title: Do You Have Nothing or Everything?
date: 14/02/2026
---

The disciples must have asked Jesus, “Are you sure, Lord? Do you really want us to go out empty-handed? No gold or silver, not even copper? No bag and no spare clothes? What, not even a pair of sandals?”

Jesus replied, “Yes. Just go. Heal the sick. Cleanse the lepers. Raise the dead. Cast out demons.”

In Matthew, we read his eyewitness account of this faith-challenging command from Christ. Jesus equipped the disciples with something that can probably be described as “nothing/ everything”—they were to carry _nothing_, but with trust in Jesus, they had everything—the Bible calls it faith.

A few years later, Peter and John, filled with the Holy Spirit, were walking to the temple when a lame man begged for some coins. Peter’s response, even with no money in his purse, shows his faith and trust in God. Peter said: “Silver and gold I do not have, but what I do have I give you: In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk” (Acts 3:6).

The man jumped up and leaped around, praising God! What a miracle!

In the padded-pew affluence of the 21st century, we can no longer honestly say, “Silver and gold have I none” because we do have some money. However, today, we find it hard to say, “In the name of Jesus Christ of Nazareth, rise up and walk.”

Have our finances stolen our faith?

Peter’s purse was full of nothing/everything . . . and he used it to buy a miracle.

### Appeal

As we now return God’s 10% as tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s ask God to break the bonds of whatever limits our faith. Let’s pray that God will help us experience the mighty work He can and wants to do, whether our purse is empty or full. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
