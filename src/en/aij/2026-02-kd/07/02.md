Down the road, over hills, and through big open fields, Joseph walked and walked. The sun was hot, but Joseph didn’t slow down. He was happy to help his worried father. He would find his older brothers and their sheep. He would bring back good news to his father, as soon as possible.

Finally after a long, long journey, Joseph arrived in Shechem. But where were his brothers? Where were the sheep? No, not on that hill. No, not in that valley. Walking in a big field, Joseph looked left and he looked right. But nothing. He was so confused. _Where were they?_

Just then a man walked over. He had noticed that Joseph looked lost. He asked Joseph, “What are you looking for?” (Genesis 37:15, ICB).

What did Joseph say to the man? **Read Genesis 37:16.**

Oh, yes! The man had seen the brothers and their flock of sheep. He had even overheard them talking. “I heard them say they were going to Dothan,” he answered (Genesis 37:17, ICB).

Dothan would be at least another five or six hours of walking. Joseph could have whined about being tired. He could have stomped his feet and refused to go any further. But Joseph thought about his father’s worried frown. He wanted to bring back good news, so his father could smile again. He wanted to make sure his brothers were safe.

Joseph thanked the stranger. Then with courage, he slung his bag over his shoulder and continued down the long path to Dothan.
