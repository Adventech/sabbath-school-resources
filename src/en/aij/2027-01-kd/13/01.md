**Big Idea:**\
God’s way is best. I will remember all His good works.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Remember His marvelous works which He has done”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ (1 Chronicles 16:12).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Remember”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ - To remember means to bring into your mind something that has happened in the past. You might remember a fun game you played yesterday or how to tie your shoes. These are things your mind knows from the past and remembers. God wants us to always remember the loving and caring things He has done for us.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Ex 30:7-10; 36-39; 40:31, 34-38; and _Patriarchs and Prophets_, pp. 343-358.

Moving In

The Israelites crowded around God’s new home. Children touched the outer walls curiously. Mothers pointed to the little stitches Aunty had made, or the walls Father had helped lift. Every wall, curtain, and piece of furniture had been done exactly the way God had asked. All the Israelites stood to admire the sanctuary. Their hearts bubbled up with happiness.

Suddenly, up, up, up rose God’s tall cloud. It floated toward the sanctuary and then down, down, down. God’s great cloud covered His new home. “The greatness of the Lord filled the Holy Tent” (Exodus 40:34, ICB). It shone with God’s brightness and goodness.

God had chosen to live with them, just as He’d promised! The people cried with joy to see Him move into His home.

Now every time they looked toward the middle of camp, they could see God’s cloud and His holy glow. He was so close!

Sometimes God’s cloud would lift high above the sanctuary and start to move. Why? **Read Exodus 40:36, 37.**

The people would pack up and quickly follow. God was still leading them. Whenever they looked at His cloud they could remember His promise to them. God had said, “I care about you. . . . I will lead you to the land of the Canaanites” (Exodus 3:16, 17, ICB). He would always keep His promises!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- What could the Israelites look at to help them remember that God always keeps His promises?
- What can you look at to help you remember God’s promises?
