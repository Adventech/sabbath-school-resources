> What does the Bible teach?

Wilhelm Reich, considered one of the key philosophical architects of the sexual revolution of the 1960s, taught that suppressing sexual impulses is bad for your mental health. He helped set the table for the present-day belief that if something feels good, you should do it—a situation in which our fallen passions are permitted to override moral reason.

**1. How does the apostle Paul counter Reich’s advice?** Why is there merit to Paul’s solution? What advice or caution would you give to a young person who is trying to apply this counsel? (1 Cor. 7:8, 9)

**2. In much of the world, the amount of time during which a person is required to exercise self-control has lengthened.** Puberty arrives earlier than it used to, and marriage is delayed much longer than in previous generations. What advice would you give to young people faced with this new reality? Are there passages in the Bible that illustrate or confirm your advice?

**3. What other challenges are people now facing regarding sexual purity?** How should Christians deal with them?

**4. The Bible teaches that both men and women were made in God’s image, and yet He created two versions of humanity: male and female.** What do you suppose men and women have to teach each other about God? What does this imply about the nature of marriage? In a world that doesn’t always share our values, has this become more important? (Gen. 1:26, 27; 2:22-24; Matt. 19:4-6)

**5. There have been times throughout history that people treated sex as shameful and/or dirty.** Unfortunately, Christians have been guilty of this too. What does the Bible say about appropriate sexual relations? (Heb. 13:4)

**6. The Song of Solomon is well known for its passionate descriptions of a relationship between a man and a woman.** Why do you think God included this book in the canon of Scripture? What important lessons can we derive from it?

**7. In Song of Solomon 6:4 the author compares the woman he loves to the city of Jerusalem.** Does this comparison hold spiritual significance? What do appropriate sexual relationships teach us about our relationship with Christ? (Eph. 5:30-32; Rev. 21:2, 3)

**8. In the face of rising divorce rates in much of the world, what can Christians do to help divorce-proof their marriages?** (Phil. 2:3, 4)
