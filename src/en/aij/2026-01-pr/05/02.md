Life for Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden must have been one great, joyous adventure! That is, until they made a sad mistake. It seemed such a little thing at first, but it turned out to be very big.

It all started when Eve found herself at the forbidden tree, face-to-face with a dazzlingly beautiful, striking, winged serpent.

Suddenly the serpent began talking in the sweetest voice! Satan was speaking through it, so a string of smooth lies poured from its mouth. What were the serpent’s lies? Read Genesis 3:4, 5.

The lies were confusing, but they sounded so good. As the serpent slithered closer and held out the fruit, she took it and crunched her first bite. It was juicy and sweet, and nothing bad happened, so she quickly picked some for Adam.

Adam’s face fell as Eve stepped toward him holding the fruit. He took a bite.

Satan had tempted Adam and Eve by making things look, sound, and feel good when they weren’t good at all.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Think of a time you’ve been tempted to make a bad choice. What thoughts were going on inside your head? How did Satan make it sound OK?_
- _Read Jude 24 together and talk about what it means._

God lovingly works in our hearts to help us see the truth and make the right choices. On the other hand, Satan spins lies to make wrong look right and right look wrong.
