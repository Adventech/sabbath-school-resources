**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **cloth, sweet popcorn or oats, cups, bowl of popcorn, jar**.

Make **seven cards** and write the **days of the week** on each card. Prepare **statement cards** with the following statements on each: No manna fell. Twice the amount of manna fell. The people collected manna. Extra manna was stored for the next day. The people spent the day worshipping God. The people ate quail. (You may like to make doubles for larger groups.)
