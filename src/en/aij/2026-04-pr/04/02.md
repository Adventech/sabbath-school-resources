The air on the hillside was electrified with excitement as the crowds waited to crown Jesus as king. Jesus was sad, for He knew “that they were about to come and take Him by force to make Him king” (John 6:15). He knew that this was a very bad idea. The people couldn’t see it, but Jesus knew it would lead to fighting. He had come to inspire them with peace and happiness, not war and fighting.

Jesus quickly acted to stop their plans. He called His disciples together. He told them to get in their boat and return to Capernaum at once. That was the last thing they felt like doing, but they turned in silence and walked toward the lake.

Meanwhile, Jesus told the excited crowds to go home. His quiet yet strong words stopped them in their tracks. They began to leave, dragging their disappointed feet. What did Jesus do next? **Read Matthew 14:23.**

Jesus always gained strength when He focused on God and not on His problems. He spent hours praying for power to help the people understand His mission. He prayed that Satan wouldn’t be able to confuse them. And Jesus may have had tears in His eyes as He asked God to be with His disciples. He knew that if they didn’t keep their eyes focused on Him, it would be impossible for their faith to stay strong. “But with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26).

We can be confident that Jesus is very focused on helping us to stay close to Him too.
