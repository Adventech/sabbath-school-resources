**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He is all-powerful.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 13 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Suggested Storytelling Props: Use a **shoebox** or other box with a door cut in the side for Lazarus’ tomb. Cut a round **piece of cardboard** for the stone to roll across the entrance of the tomb. Use **Lazarus craft sample**.
- Use **peg people** (Jesus, Mary, Martha, disciples—use **Lazarus craft sample**), or make cardboard-roll characters, or use a **felt set** if desired.
- Use an expressive voice as you tell the story to engage the children’s interest.
```

**TELL THE STORY**

Mary and Martha whispered worried words to each other ^[(peg Mary and Martha)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). _What should they do?_ Their dear brother Lazarus was very sick. _How could they help him?_

They decided there was only one person who had the power to make their brother better. Quickly they sent a messenger to their good Friend, Jesus. “Now Jesus loved Martha and her sister and Lazarus” (John 11:5). They knew He would care. They knew He would help.

The messenger found Jesus ^[(peg Jesus)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and told Him, “Lord, the one you love is sick” (John 11:3, ICB).

Jesus didn’t rush to pack His bag or race down the road to visit His friends. He told the messenger, “This sickness will not end in death” (John 11:4, ICB).

The disciples were very surprised that Jesus didn’t go to His friend, but they trusted Jesus ^[(peg disciples)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Two days later Jesus told His disciples some very sad news—Lazarus had died. They would now go to him and his sisters in Bethany.

The disciples’ eyes opened wide in shock. _Why had Jesus waited until Lazarus had died?_ They didn’t understand, but again they chose to trust Jesus

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After two more days Jesus and His friends arrived in Bethany. Martha heard that Jesus had come, and ran to meet Him. ^[(Peg Martha runs to peg Jesus.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

As soon as she saw His loving face, she began to cry. Even though Martha didn’t understand why Jesus had taken so long, she trusted that He could do anything.

Jesus can do anything! He told Martha that He could give Lazarus life. He said to Martha, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in Me, though he may die, he shall live” (John 11:25).

Looking into Martha’s eyes, Jesus said, “Do you believe this?” (John 11:26).

“Yes, Lord. I believe,” Martha replied (John 11: 27).

Martha went back to her home and whispered in Mary’s ear ^[(Mary peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), “The Teacher has come and is calling for you” (John 11:28).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Quickly Mary got up and ran to Jesus. ^[(Peg Mary runs to peg Jesus.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Kneeling at His feet, she said, “Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died” (John 11:32).

Jesus’ eyes filled with tears, and He cried.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus walked with His friends toward the place where Lazarus was buried. They were followed by a large, crying crowd. ^[(Move peg people to “tomb.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lazarus had been buried in a tomb; a cave. It was shut tight with a big heavy stone. Jesus asked them to move the stone away.

Martha was surprised, “But, Lord,” she said, “it has been four days since he died. There will be a bad smell” (John 11:39, ICB).

Jesus reminded her that she needed to believe in His power. So some strong men rolled the stone away from the door. ^[(Move cardboard stone.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus looked up to the sky and prayed. Then in a loud, all-powerful voice, He said, “Lazarus, come out!” (John 11:43, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At once “he who had died came out” (John 11:44). Lazarus was alive! ^[(Show Lazarus.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Lazarus stood there at the door to the cave, wrapped from head to toe in tight bandages. “Take the cloth off of him and let him go,” said Jesus (John 11:44, ICB).

Racing forward, Lazarus’ family and friends unwrapped him. He was strong and healthy. He was alive!

Mary hugged Lazarus. Lazarus hugged Jesus. And Jesus hugged Martha. The whole crowd was so happy and sang praises to the all-powerful God who had given life back to Lazarus!
