Mary hummed to herself as she walked along in the warm sunshine. It was a long trek to her cousin’s home, but she was excited. Her cousin Elizabeth would have a special miracle baby—in three months’ time. Mary couldn’t wait to share her baby news too!

Finally Mary arrived at Zacharias’ home with a cheerful hello. As soon as Elizabeth heard Mary’s greeting, she was filled with the Holy Spirit. Elizabeth knew Mary’s news before she could even tell her! “God has blessed you more than any other woman. And God has blessed the baby which you will give birth to,” she said (Luke 1:42, ICB).

Elizabeth rested a hand on her tummy. “When I heard your voice, the baby inside me jumped with joy (Luke 1:44, ICB).

Mary’s heart filled with happiness. “My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior,” she said (Luke 1:46, 47). What else did Mary say? **Read Luke 1:48-55.**

Old Elizabeth and young Mary sat down together to share their stories, and to share their thanks to God. He had been so kind to both of them.

Mary and Elizabeth held each other’s hands tightly. They would soon be mothers! They knew other people might not share their joy. People might think and say such things as, Look at that old woman. She’s far too old to have a baby! Or Mary’s pregnant? But she’s not married! But these two women just kept smiling. They wouldn’t worry about what other people said. They wouldn’t let gossip or mean talk steal their joy. God loved them! He had chosen them to be an important part of His big story, and that was all that mattered.
