All Sabbath afternoon the people of Capernaum sat together in their homes talking excitedly about one thing: Jesus. They remembered His words and amazing healing miracles.

They all really, really wanted to ask Jesus to heal their sick families too. They knew Jesus was at Simon Peter’s house, so “when the sun was setting, all those who had any that were sick with various diseases brought them to Him” (Luke 4:40). Some shuffled slowly to the house, leaning on a walking stick. Others who couldn’t walk were carried on stretchers. Little sick children were carried in a parent’s arms. They all had one question. _Please, Jesus, can You help and heal us?_

As Jesus looked at the crowd, He saw their pain and noticed their worry. He cared for each one as though they were His own child.

The Bible says, “God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good” (Acts 10:38).

“Doing good” is exactly what Jesus did there in Capernaum. Shouts of joy filled the streets as Jesus began to lay His hands on the sick and heal them. How many of the people did Jesus heal? **Read Luke 4:40.**

What a busy night! Hour after hour, people came to be healed. Finally, when it was nearly morning, the last healed person waved goodbye and went home. Jesus sighed happily. He felt tired, but His heart was filled with joy at seeing so many sad faces smiling again.
