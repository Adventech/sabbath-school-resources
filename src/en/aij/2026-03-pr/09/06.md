Like ripples in a pond, the news of the wedding miracle spread out to the streets of Cana. Then it spread all the way down to Jerusalem and into the temple courts. When the priests and rulers heard it, they were curious. They searched the Scriptures about the Messiah, trying to understand what was happening.

Here was a chance for the leaders to discover who Jesus was. But they couldn’t get over their pride. The old priests shook their heads. The young priests and rulers put their noses in the air. Surely the Messiah would turn up in Jerusalem and perform miracles for them. Not at some simple wedding in a village! Their hearts were not open to Jesus.

Jesus could have worked a miracle in Jerusalem to show off His power to the priests and rulers. He didn’t, because He could see they wouldn’t believe that His authority came from God. Did they prove Him right? **Read Mark 11:27, 28.**

So instead, Jesus chose to use His power to add happiness to a wedding in a small town. That way, common people with open hearts would know that He was interested in them and that He loved them very much.

Jesus loves you very much too. Every day He longs to do a miracle in your life by forgiving your sins and changing your heart to be like His. “Oh, give thanks to the Lord of lords! . . . To Him who alone does great wonders” (Psalm 136:3, 4).
