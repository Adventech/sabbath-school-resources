A Coin\
^[and a]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#dde56d", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}}) Fish
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/02/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#284145"}}}
Splish-splash went the water on the sparkling Sea of Galilee. All was still. Then splish-splash went the water again. What made the water splish and splash? It was a fish! A fish jumped through the water. What fun! This fish would be Jesus’ special helper today. Peter smiled (smile) as he walked through his town by the sea. He felt happy that Jesus had come to visit! He couldn’t wait for everyone to meet his friend Jesus. But some men who collected money came toward him. Maybe they would like to meet Jesus too. But the men frowned at Peter (frown). Clink-clink went their money bags. They asked Peter if Jesus paid money to the temple. Peter nodded (nod). Yes, Jesus did what was right.   
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/02/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}}}
Peter felt sad as he walked home (sad face). He didn’t have any money to pay the temple. Jesus spent all His time loving and caring for others. Jesus didn’t have a purse full of coins that went clink-clink. He didn’t have a jar full of gold. He didn’t own a donkey. He didn’t have a house. How would they pay money to the temple? Jesus smiled at Peter. He knew just where to find the money. It wasn’t in a purse. It wasn’t in a jar. Peter’s eyes opened wide in surprise as Jesus told him what to do. Then Peter obeyed. He went fishing! He threw the fishing line into the water. Plop! Down, down, down it went. Peter quietly waited for a fish. Tug, tug, tug . . . Oh, he’d caught one! He pulled the fish up out of the water and opened its mouth. There it was—a bright, shiny coin! Peter found the money exactly where Jesus said he would—in the mouth of a fish!

Jesus is amazing! He put the bright shiny coin in the fish’s mouth. He knew where Peter would find the money to pay the temple. Jesus’ friends saw that even the fish obey Him! Jesus cared for Peter.\

Jesus cares for me too. He gives me all I need.\
