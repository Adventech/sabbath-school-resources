Without a second thought, Zacchaeus scrambled to his feet and ran down the road. He didn’t stop to think about his money table. Zacchaeus had only one thought—he must see Jesus.

The big crowd up ahead pushed, shoved, and bumped each other. They squeezed in close, trying to get a glimpse of Jesus as He walked down the road.

Oh, no. Zacchaeus stopped and looked at the crowd. He had a big “little” problem. What was it? **Read Luke 19:3.**

Standing on tiptoe, Zacchaeus tried to peer over the tall shoulders. Holding in his breath, he tried to squeeze between gaps, but it was no use. The big strong wall of people stopped Zacchaeus from getting any closer to Jesus.

Zacchaeus couldn’t see Jesus, but God knew He was there and cared for him. The Bible says, “The Lord looks down from heaven. He sees every person” (Psalm 33:13, ICB). Even though Zacchaeus was small and had made so many bad choices, God loved him.

- What would you have done if you had been Zacchaeus?
