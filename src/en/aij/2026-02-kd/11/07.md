Kids, it’s your turn to be the storyteller! Create a play scene of Canaan and Egypt and use it to retell the story of Joseph using your own voice.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#21A0A9"}}}
**To do this:**

Collect natural materials like stones, sticks, leaves, etc., to create the landscape.

You may like to drape colored fabric for land/water surfaces.

Make some small people from a material of your choice (e.g., play-dough or clay).

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#21A0A9"}}}
**Places to create:**

- Home in Canaan
- Potiphar’s house
- Prison
- The palace
- Across the land of Egypt

**^[Parents]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#C17EB6"}}})**, your willingness to interact with your child to guide and encourage the sharing of Joseph’s story and God’s leading in it will be key to this Sabbath experience.
