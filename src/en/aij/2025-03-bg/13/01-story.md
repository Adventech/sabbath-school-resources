A New\
^[Home]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3A5125", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})
^^^

God’s people had a new home. They looked out over the land God had led them to. There were grassy green fields with wildflowers for little lamb and old donkey. There were good places for Father and Uncle to build strong houses. There were sparkling streams and little water wells _(point to well)_ for Mother. There were gardens with fresh, juicy fruit for Big Sister. And lots of space for Little Brother to run and play.\
What a beautiful new home! There would be no more marching in the desert. No more sandy toes. No more hot tents. No more dusty beds. Thank You, God!\
Joshua told the people to move to different places in the Promised Land. Some moved to the mountains. Others lived on farms. Some made cities together. God was with them wherever they went. He is with you too.

What does your home look like? Some people live in houses made of bricks, wood, mud, leaves, or straw. Some people live in apartment buildings that are very tall. Some live in huts, close to birds that sing. Some live in city hotels, where the elevators go “bing.” Some are still looking for a safe place to call home. They take their beds with them wherever they go.\
Some people live in homes on top of tall sticks, way up high. When it’s rainy or muddy, their homes stay dry. Some live in caravans with wheels that move. Some live in homes made of ice, called igloos. A round tent or a floating boat can be a wonderful home too. Wherever you live, God is with you.

Jesus wants to be with you forever. He promises, “I am with you always” (Matthew 28:20). When you are happy, sad, afraid, or mad, you can talk to Jesus. He loves you. He is right beside you, and He will always help you. When Jesus comes back from heaven soon, you will be able to see Him right next to you. And He has a big surprise to give you—a new forever home with Him. What will your home look like? It will be perfect!\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for making a home for me.
