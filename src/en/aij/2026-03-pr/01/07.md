Judson Van DeVenter struggled over the problem in his mind yet again. For five long years he had wrestled with the dilemma: become a recognized artist or work as a minister for God?

Born in 1855 in the U.S. state of Michigan, Judson had always been passionate about art and became an art teacher. He also loved Jesus and was very busy in his church, especially when revival meetings were being held.

His friends noticed his strong faith and his work for the church. They tried to encourage him to give up his art teaching and become an evangelist. But his love for art was too strong, and he kept on teaching.

For five years God kept tugging at his heart.

Finally he gave up the struggle and surrendered all. He left teaching and became a full-time preacher. God rewarded Judson with a new creative talent. Thinking about that point in his life, he said, “A new day was ushered into my life. I became an evangelist and discovered down deep in my soul a talent hitherto unknown to me. God had hidden a song in my heart, and touching a tender chord, He caused me to sing.”

This is why Judson wrote the hymn “I Surrender All.” It became well known wherever he preached in America, England, and Scotland. It is now a favorite all over the world and is included in The Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal (no. 309). Judson is fondly remembered as a kind, deeply spiritual gentleman, all because he lived the words of his hymn and surrendered all to Jesus.

I Surrender All

All to Jesus I surrender,\
All to Him I freely give;\
I will ever love and trust Him,\
In His presence daily live;

I surrender all, I surrender all;\
All to Thee, my blessed Savior,\
I surrender all.

All to Jesus I surrender,\
Humbly at His feet I bow,\
Worldly pleasure all forsaken;\
Take me, Jesus, take me now.

All to Jesus I surrender,\
Make me, Savior, wholly Thine;\
Let me feel the Holy Spirit,\
Truly know that Thou art mine.
