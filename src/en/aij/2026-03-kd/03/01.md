**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God. He wants me to seek Him and find Him.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Jeremiah 29:13).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Seeking”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Seeking is searching for something. Jesus wants us to seek for Him with all our hearts. Some things we can do to spend time with Jesus include praying, reading the Bible, going to Sabbath School, and singing worship songs.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Matthew 2:1-12; Luke 2:8-38; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 43-67.

By the Fireside

The flames of the fire flickered yellow and orange. It crackled as smoke rose up into the starry night sky. Huddled around the warm flames sat some men.

Who were these men, and what were they doing? **Read Luke 2:8.**

The grassy fields were their home. During the day they would lead their sheep to crunchy green grass and cool streams. At night they would camp under the stars, still looking after their flocks. No hungry wolves would come near their fluffy sheep!

The shepherds laughed and talked together as they watched the sheep. They may have chatted about the cool breeze, their families back home, and the new little lambs. Perhaps they pointed to the field in the distance filled with long, new grass. Yes, that would be a great place to take the sheep tomorrow.

But as the night grew still and quiet, the men began to talk about their most favorite thing of all—the coming Savior.

When would He come? What would He look like? What would He do? Oh, how they wanted it to be soon!

Bowing their heads together, the shepherds prayed for the King whose “rule will last forever” and whose “kingdom will never be destroyed” (Daniel 7:14, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#A0BA3B", "size": "lg"}}})

- What would you have liked/disliked about being a shepherd?
- Who were the shepherds seeking?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#A0BA3B", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for putting His love in our hearts so we want to seek Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#A0BA3B", "size": "lg"}}})\
Build your own pretend campfire, using things from around the house. Sit around it and chat about what excites you the most about Jesus’ second coming.
