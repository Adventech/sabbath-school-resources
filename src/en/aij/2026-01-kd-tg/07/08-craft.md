**What you need:**

Cardboard tube, colored fabric, rubber band, ribbon or string, red paper, cotton ball, craft stick, googly eyes, craft glue, and scissors.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/07/7a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut a small “mouth” shape out of the cardboard tube.
- Cut some squares of fabric ready for the children to “dress” their “Noah.”

**Create it together:**

- Glue a small piece of red paper to a craft stick.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/07/7b.png)

- Glue a piece of fabric around the cardboard tube.
- Make the headpiece by securing a piece of fabric with a rubber band.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/07/7c.png)

- Tie a ribbon or string around the tube as a waistband.
- Glue on googly eyes or draw with markers.
- Glue on the cotton ball for the beard.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/07/7d.png)

- Children can move the craft stick, with the red paper on it, up and down inside the tube to make it look as if “Noah” is talking!
