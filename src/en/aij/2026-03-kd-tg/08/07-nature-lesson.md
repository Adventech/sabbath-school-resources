**Focus:** Jesus wants us to follow Him, just as Mario the Goose followed Dominic.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/08/nature-lesson.png)

Mario was a Toulouse Goose. He lived by a big lake in a park in Los Angeles, California, in the United States. One day something special happened. A man named Dominic came to the park to go for a walk. Mario wanted to go too! So Mario started following Dominic. They walked together all the way around the lake. Dominic was surprised, because he hadn’t given Mario any food or done anything to encourage the goose to follow him. Mario had decided to follow him, and that was that.

The next day Dominic came to the park again. What do you think Mario did? Yes, Mario followed Dominic everywhere he went! This became a pattern. Every day Dominic would go to the park to walk with Mario. Mario would wait for him beside the road and follow him around the park. Mario loved Dominic so much that he started trying to protect him. Mario would sometimes honk and flap his wings at dogs or people if he thought they were getting too close to his friend Dominic.

One day Dominic came to visit Mario as usual. Mario was so happy to see his friend! But there was a problem. Mario didn’t want Dominic to leave. After they had walked around the park together, Dominic got onto his scooter to go home. Mario wanted to keep following his friend! Dominic revved the engine and started driving home. Mario flapped his wings and started flying right behind Dominic! What could Dominic do? He didn’t have a place for Mario to live at his house! Dominic slowly drove back to the park and sat down on a bench. He waited for Mario to fall asleep, and then he went home. Dominic still visited Mario every day, but he had to wait for him to fall asleep before he could leave to go home.

One day, the people at the park decided to drain the lake and clean it out. The geese needed to find a new home. Mario and his goose friends were taken to live at the zoo. But that didn’t stop Mario’s friendship with Dominic. Dominic came to the zoo every morning to visit him. Mario and Dominic were faithful friends for the rest of Mario’s life!

Jesus wants us to follow Him faithfully for our whole lives, just as Mario followed Dominic.

- **Will you choose to follow Jesus today?**
