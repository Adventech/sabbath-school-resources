The sun shone brightly as it rose up over the hill. Noah and his family thanked God for another beautiful day. They’d finished all God had asked them to do. The food was carefully packed, enough for all. The animals had a room, but . . . where were the animals? How would they come? God knew. The animals would be on time! They would all be safe when the rains came _(hug child)_.\
_Woosh!_ A mighty wind rushed by. _Boom! Boom! Rustle, rustle._ What could that be? Noah and his family stared, their eyes wide with surprise. The animals were coming! Eagles swooped and soared over the trees _(arms out as though flying)_. Ducks flapped their wings above the streams. Tigers marched out of the forest as wolves howled for all to hear. Elephants stomped up the ramp, and skunks scampered through the grass. The sounds of the animals grew louder and louder as they came toward the ark.
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](assets/08-02.png)

As the animals came closer and closer, Noah saw that they lined up as neatly as could be. There were one, two pandas. One, two kangaroos. There were two zebras and two baboons. Each came together, matched with their mate, male and female _(boy and girl)_. The sweet hedgehogs snuffled and waddled to keep their place in line. The lions yawned as they patiently waited behind. The turtles kept pace one step at a time. There were pairs of little lizards and spotted ladybugs. With a snort, two big rhinoceroses joined the line.\
Two by two, all in a row, the animals came. Silly monkeys chattered, and fuzzy brown bears grunted. The bunny rabbits hopped, and the ostriches tiptoed up the ramp and into the ark. Seven pairs of goats hurried into place. Seven pairs of gentle cows and strong bulls and seven pairs of woolly sheep came two by two. They knew exactly what to do. Even the tall giraffes made it right through the big doorway.

The fast fox and the sleepy sloth would be safe on the boat that Noah had made. Little chipmunks knew just where to be, as the giraffe stood nearby, as tall as can be. The animals had come, from near and far, led by God’s angel.\
There was still room on the big boat for God’s special friends and everyone who wanted to come inside. God promised to keep them safe.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for caring for the animals! I want to care for animals too.
