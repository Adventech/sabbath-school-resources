### How to Run a Church-based Bible School

Learn how to start your own church-based Bible school.

This book provides step-by-step instructions to help you:
	
- Make friends in the community
- Connect people with Jesus and help them become His disciples
- Acquaint people with the Bible and the Seventh-day Adventist message
- Show people how the Bible meets their daily needs
- Prepare your community for evangelistic meetings
- Help people take the step to become baptized church members who make witnessing their way of life-
