**Focus:** When we listen closely, we can recognize God’s voice speaking to us, just as the sugar glider learns to recognize its owner’s voice.

- **Have you ever wondered how much fun it would be to fly from tree to tree?**
- **Let’s find out about a little animal called a sugar glider that can do just that.** ^[(Show picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Sugar gliders are clever little tree-dwelling mammals that look a bit like flying possums. When the trees are too far apart for them to scramble between the branches, they hurl themselves into the air and spread out their legs. A flap of loose skin between their front and back legs creates a cupped surface that catches the air and helps the glider sail to another tree. It can glide as far as 150 feet (almost 46 meters).

That would be cool to watch! But to see one, you would have to stay up late because they come out at night to eat fruit, insects, and leaves. They have large eyes that give them good night vision. But they also have very good hearing. Their big, hairless ears rotate like a cat’s ears so that they can easily hear a predator approaching or the slightest sound among the trees. To communicate, they “yap” softly to each other, but only one sugar glider yaps at a time while the others listen. Their lives depend on how well they see and how carefully they listen.

If a sugar glider is tamed as a pet, it becomes very good at recognizing and listening to its owner’s voice, just as Abram became good at recognizing and listening to God’s voice. We can learn to recognize and listen to God’s voice by reading His Word and listening and talking to Him in prayer—we just need to be still and focus, like the sugar glider.

- **Why not ask God to help you recognize and listen to His voice?**
