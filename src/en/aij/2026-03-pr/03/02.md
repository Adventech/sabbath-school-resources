The angels disappeared, the light faded away, and the shepherds found themselves in darkness once again. The words of the angel seemed to echo in the still night’s air: “For there is born to you this day in the city of David a Savior, who is Christ the Lord. And this will be the sign to you: You will find a Babe wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger” (Luke 2:11,12).

Amazed and filled with great joy, they ran off to Bethlehem in search of the place where Baby Jesus lay. They gently pushed open the stable door, and there was the Baby—the Baby! Wrapped in soft white cloth and lying in a manger. It was just as the angel had said. They tiptoed in, knelt down, and bowed to worship the tiny Jesus. Their eyes sparkled with happiness, for they knew that tiny Baby Jesus was worthy of all their worship and praise.

Then the shepherds told Joseph and Mary about the beautiful angel choir and the message from the dazzling angel. Mary cherished this news in her heart; her Baby was indeed very, VERY special.

With a last look at Baby Jesus, the shepherds quietly left. As they stepped onto the street, their excitement bubbled over. They shared what they had seen and heard with everyone. What else did they do as they returned to their fields? **Read Luke 2:20.**

The shepherds couldn’t help praising God for the things they had heard from the angels, for the Savior they had seen in the manger, and for what Mary and Joseph had told them. What a wonderful night it had been.

Baby Jesus was worthy of the shepherds’ worship, and He is worthy of our worship today. Our hearts were created to worship Him. Like the shepherds, we can praise Jesus for what we have heard about Him in His Word, what we have “seen” or experienced of Him ourselves, and for what others have told us of their love for Him.
