Fishing\
^[with]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#f3da45", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}}) Jesus
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/01/01.png)

The sun sparkled on the water of the Sea of Galilee. Whoosh . . . whish . . . The waves of the sea washed softly onto the shore.  Crunch . . .crunch . . . went Jesus’ sandals as He walked on the sand. Splish . . . splash . . . The water went against the sides of the boats as they gently rocked back and forth. Drip . . . drip . . . The water fell into the sea from the fishing nets. The fishermen, Peter, Andrew, James, and John, washed and carefully folded their nets. A long, long night of fishing was over. Had they caught any fish? No (sad face). A crowd of people gathered on the sandy shore. They moved closer to hear Jesus talk. Jesus stepped into a boat. He asked Peter to push Him away from the shore. Now the people could hear and see Him better. Rock . . . rock . . . went the boat on the sea. Jesus spoke about God, His Father in heaven. God loved the people very much! Jesus wanted the people to know God and to tell others about Him too.
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/01/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block": {"backgroundColor": "#ffffffad"}}}
Peter and Andrew listened to Jesus. Jesus finished talking. He smiled at the people. He smiled at Peter (big smile). Then He told Peter to take the boat out on the lake and throw his fishing net into the water. Peter knew the only time to catch any fish was at night. How would they catch any fish now in the morning? But Peter didn’t shake his head. He nodded yes to Jesus (nod). He did as Jesus asked. Over the side of the boat, out into the deep water, he threw his fishing net. Splash! It went out, then down into the sea. Pull . . . pull . . . pull . . . Peter and Andrew tugged at the net in the water. It was so heavy. It was full of fish! Come, help! They needed their friends, James and John. There were so many fish in the net that the boats began tipping. Hold on, Peter! Hold on, Andrew! Hold on, James and John! The friends tugged and pulled. Thud! The net full of fish was finally in the boats.

Shiny fish filled the boat—right to the very top. Peter, Andrew, James, and John were amazed. There were so many flip-flopping fish in their boat! Peter jumped out of the boat and knelt at Jesus’ feet. He knew that Jesus had done a miracle. Only the Son of God could have put all those wiggly fish into their nets. Jesus looked at His friends. “Follow Me,” He said (Matthew 4:19). Jesus wanted them to be His special helpers. He wanted them to learn to be like Him. The four fishermen left their fishing boats and followed Jesus. Jesus loves you! He wants you to learn to be like Him too. He says, “Follow Me, (say child’s name).” (Say together) Yes, Jesus, I want to follow You!
