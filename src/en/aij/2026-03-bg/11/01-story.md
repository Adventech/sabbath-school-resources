God’s people marched happily toward their new home. _March, march, march._ God was leading them to His Promised Land. It was going to be beautiful! There would be big, juicy grapes. There would be big, shady trees. There would be big cities. There would be big flower gardens for bees to make lots of sweet honey _(mmm, yummy)._ But suddenly, the people stopped marching. There was something very big in their way. Can you see what it is? _(Point to river.)_ Yes, it was a big, big river. What would they do?\
The people looked at the big River Jordan. They couldn’t jump over it. It was too wide _(stretch out arms)._ They couldn’t walk around it. It was too long _(stretch out arms)._ They couldn’t walk through it. It was too deep _(stretch arms up and down)._ They couldn’t even float on the fast river. They had no boats. They shook their heads _(shake head)._ They couldn’t cross the big river. But God knew how to get the people to the other side! He told Joshua how, and Joshua smiled at God’s words. He told the people that tomorrow God would do “amazing things” (Joshua 3:5, ICB).
^^^
^^^

In the morning God’s helpers, the priests, lifted up the very special ark from God’s tabernacle. They carried it to the river, just as God had asked. God was going first! The first two priests put their feet into the water. Splash! The rushing water stopped! There, in front of all the people was a dry path right through the big river! All the people smiled in surprise. God was the best leader!\
The priests took the ark to the middle of the river and there they stopped. They waited while all the people crossed over to the other side. The girls and boys, mommies and daddies, aunties and uncles, all walked through the river on dry ground! God’s tabernacle home didn’t get wet. Their tents didn’t get wet. Their animals didn’t get wet. And all the people stayed nice and dry too. God had helped them cross the big river!

The priests crossed the river too, holding God’s beautiful golden ark. Then, swish, swosh. Splish, splash. All the water came rushing down the river again. The river was wide _(stretch out arms)._ The river was long _(stretch out arms)._ The river was deep _(stretch arms up and down)._ But the river had not been too big for God. He can do anything! He would keep helping His people, always. He is the best leader. They just needed to trust God and follow Him.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, Jesus, for leading me! I want to follow You!
