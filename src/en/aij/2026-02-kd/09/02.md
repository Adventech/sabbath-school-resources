The butler and the baker were amazed by Joseph’s words. His God could tell them the meaning of their strange dreams!

Quickly the butler began. “I dreamed I saw a vine. On the vine there were three branches” (Genesis 40:9-10, ICB). Sweet, juicy grapes had grown on the branches. He had then picked the grapes and squeezed them into a cup for Pharaoh to drink.

As Joseph listened, God did something amazing! He showed Joseph what the strange dream meant. What did Joseph say? **Read Genesis 40:13.**

The butler’s eyes opened wide and his grin grew big. He would be free! What wonderful news.

Seeing the smiling butler, the baker was excited to tell his dream to Joseph too. “I dreamed there were three bread baskets on my head” (Genesis 40:16, ICB). The baskets were piled high with delicious baked food for the king. But birds swooped down and began eating the food instead.

When the baker finished telling Joseph the dream, God did something amazing again. He showed Joseph what this dream meant too. This time Joseph’s eyes misted with sadness as he told the baker that he would not serve the Pharaoh again. Sadly, in three days he would die.

Three days later the baker died, and the butler went back to his job in the palace. God knows exactly what will happen tomorrow, next year, and ten years from now! Psalm 139:6 (ICB) says, “Your knowledge is amazing to me.”
