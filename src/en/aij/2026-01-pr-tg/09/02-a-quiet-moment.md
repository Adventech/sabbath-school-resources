**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It was a simple instruction: “Be fruitful and multiply, and fill the earth” (Genesis 9:1), followed by a renewed covenant. Given by a dependable God to His dependent children, it was the road map forward in a flood-ravaged world. If it were followed, an abundance of blessings and fulfilled promises would be enjoyed in its wake.

But the renewed covenant was ignored and ultimately replaced by the choice to depend on self. It was a path that indulged pride, aimed for fame, and ultimately led to the Tower of Babel.

We may wonder how wickedness wormed its way back into the world so quickly. But our “me-first” culture is just as prone to self-reliance and pride. We can easily find ourselves chasing after the wrong things and depending on ourselves rather than on God. We may not build a skyscraper, but we may build a tower of independence.

Our trustworthy God invites us to something so much better. “The name of the Lord is a strong tower; the righteous run to it and are safe” (Proverbs 18:10). A strong tower—what a powerful metaphor! Let’s choose to run to our strong tower, God, and be safe.

As you and the children unpack the story of building the tower, our prayer is that you will see the futility of building towers of independence and that you will each be filled instead with the desire to depend on our loving and reliable God.
