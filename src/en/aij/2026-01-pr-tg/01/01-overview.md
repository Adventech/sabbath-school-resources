**Word of the Week**

**Attribute:** An attribute is a quality or feature of something. It can describe a color, shape, or size. It can also describe what a person is like.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson looks at God’s creative power as seen in the first five days of Creation at the beginning of this earth’s history. In five days God created the light, sky, plants, sun, moon, stars, fish, and birds, as special gifts to us. We can appreciate the unity of the Father, Son, and the Holy Spirit and their love and creative power as we enjoy the beauty and wonder of all that He made during those first five amazing days.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is love and that He created the earth as a special place for us to enjoy.
- **Feel** grateful that God’s creation shows how much He loves us.
- **Respond** by joyfully focusing on God’s love as seen in nature.
