---
title: Distributing Record Booklets and Supplies
---

To avoid confusion and disorder, everything should be in readiness at the beginning of each Sabbath School program. The secretary should arrive early enough to distribute necessary supplies for each division and class.

Class record cards and envelopes should be given to the teachers just before the opening of Sabbath School. This serves to minimize unnecessary moving about on the part of the secretary and consequent confusion during the opening exercises. When teachers have taken the offering and completed the record, they should place the class envelopes in a prearranged place so that they may be retrieved by the secretary without disturbing the teacher.

Bible study guides for the succeeding Sabbath should be delivered to division leaders before Sabbath School opens; these leaders may then arrange for them to be given to the pupils at the close of the school. In small schools, where there are no division leaders, the supplies can be given to the teachers, but they should keep papers and cards out of sight until the close of Sabbath School.
