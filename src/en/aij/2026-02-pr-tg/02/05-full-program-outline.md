**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Play a game of **Guess the Truth**. Give each child a **piece of paper** and a **pencil**. Ask them to write down, in any order, two true facts and one untrue fact about themselves. (For example: I like swimming. My pet’s name is Dusty. I play the piano.) Invite the children to read aloud their three facts. Can the other children guess which statement is untrue?

- **Which was easier to write: the things that were true or untrue?** (Accept all answers.)

Often it takes far more thought and energy to come up with lies than it is to simply tell the truth. And as we’ll see in today’s story, lies hurt ourselves and others. God is always honest, and He wants to help us to always be honest in a tactful way too—no matter what.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint** and read it together: **God delights in us when our words and actions are truthful.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Play a game of **“Who Am I?”** Put a blindfold on one child and have them stand with their back to the class. Ask for volunteers to go close to the blindfolded person and say “Who Am I?” in a funny voice. The blindfolded person is to guess who it is. Repeat the activity for others wanting to have a turn.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What was it like to try to guess who was speaking when you couldn’t see them?** What made it extra hard? (Not using their true voice.)
- **Jacob started off telling one lie. What were the other ways he deceived his father?** (Lying about who he was, the food, the clothes he was wearing, the feel of his skin.)
- **What does the Bible say about lying and being dishonest? Read today’s memory verse, Proverbs 12:22.** (“Lying lips are an abomination [disgrace], but those who deal truthfully are His delight.”)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** When we tell one lie, it often leads to other lies. Sin is like that—when we do it, it’s easy to do it again next time. It’s always better to be honest and truthful from the beginning. God delights in us when we are truthful.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Share an object lesson. You will need **sweet cut fruit** (small amount in bowl; more to share with class), **salt**, and **honey**.

- Share with the children that honesty is sweet. Have a child come and taste the fruit.
- Shake some salt on the fruit and give an example of when they might cover up a mistake or problem with a lie. Ask a child to taste the fruit now. (YUCK!)
- Share that when we tell a lie, we often put ourselves in a position where we feel we have to tell another lie to cover up the first lie—just as Jacob did. Pour some honey over the top of the salty fruit and have the child taste the fruit now. (YUCK!)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to find a place in the room to silently pray that God will help them to always be truthful.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Lies cannot be dissolved by covering them up. One lie easily leads to another lie. They ruin a wonderful, sweet life. If you have lied, the only way to deal with the lie is to have the courage to say you are sorry and ask God for forgiveness. Then God can wash the nasty rubbish out of your life and give you a clean start.

You may like to share the **fresh, sweet fruit** with the class. Remind the children that God loves us very much and loves to forgive us when we are sorry. He can help us learn to trust Him more and more.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to briefly report about last week’s Mission Challenge. Remind them of the question: **If you had a friend who doesn’t come to church, what would you want them to see, hear, or do at Sabbath School?**

Affirm their answers and emphasize how special it is to learn about Jesus together at Sabbath School. Share that they can be a missionary for God by inviting their friends to Sabbath School anytime. This quarter we are planning a special Sabbath School to bring our friends to in week 9 or 10.

Share that this week’s Mission Challenge is to **pray each day for God to lead us to the person we can invite to Sabbath School.** Affirm that they don’t need to know right now—God will show them when the time is right.
