Naomi’s eyes grew big when she saw the large bundle of barley that Ruth carried home. Wow! Boaz had been so kind to her!

Every working day, Ruth went back to Boaz’ field. He would welcome his workers with kind words like, “The Lord be with you!” (Ruth 2:4, ICB). He made sure Ruth had water to drink and food to eat, just like his happy servants.

Ruth worked very hard, all day long. Then with a big sigh she would pack all the grain she’d found into a sack. One handful, two handfuls . . . on and on she’d go. Finally her bag was so full she could hardly carry it! How did she find so much grain on the ground? **Read Ruth 2:15, 16.**

What a nice, secret kindness!

When their pile of grain grew big enough, Ruth or Naomi could take it to the market and swap it for things like oil, fruit, or wool. They had all they needed.

Have you ever done something kind for someone without them knowing it? It can make them feel really special. It may even get them thinking about the goodness of God.

Jesus said, “So when you give to the poor, give very secretly. . . . Your Father can see what is done in secret, and he will reward you” (Matthew 6:3, 4, ICB). Jesus, Himself, will thank you when you secretly give to those in need.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- What would it be like to be thanked for a secret kindness by Jesus?
- Do you think Jesus will thank Boaz for helping Ruth?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
