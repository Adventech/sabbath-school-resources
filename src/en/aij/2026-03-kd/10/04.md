The disciples sighed as they looked at the big crowd on the hillside. How were they to feed all these hungry people? There were more than 5,000 people with no food. What a big problem! The disciples were very worried—but Jesus wasn’t.

“How many loaves do you have? Go and see,” Jesus told the disciples (Mark 6:38).

The disciples rushed off to ask the people. Did the clever teachers and rulers have food? Sorry, no. Did the farmers? Not a crumb. Did the fishermen? No, nothing. The shopkeepers, builders, mothers, and aunties all shook their heads. No one had any food to give.

Then Andrew, one of Jesus’ disciples, stepped forward with a little boy. What did Andrew say? **Read John 6:9.**

One little boy, in the big, big crowd, had something to share. The disciples peered into the boy’s lunch basket. Carefully they counted, one, two, three, four, five little barley loaves. Plus two small fish.

Jesus smiled thankfully at the little boy. The boy’s own tummy must have rumbled with hunger too. But he didn’t keep his lunch to himself. He gave it to Jesus.

Jesus loves giving us what we need. And He loves for us to be like Him and give too. He promises, “Give, and you will receive. You will be given much. It will be poured into your hands—more than you can hold” (Luke 6:38, ICB).

Everyone watched Jesus as He took the five loaves and fishes in His hands. What would He do with this little boy’s lunch?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think the little boy had some food?
- Do you think the boy was happy to give his lunch to Jesus? Why?
- What do you think Jesus will do next? Why?
