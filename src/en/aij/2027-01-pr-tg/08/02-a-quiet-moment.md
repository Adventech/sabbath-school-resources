**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

In the Garden of Eden, God created family and established it as the heart of all human relationships. Despite the devastating impact the fall has had on familial relationships, God’s original purpose for our families hasn’t changed. He wants our families to be a safe, giving, circles of love. He empowers us all to love “because He first loved us” (1 John 4:19).

God longs to restore the fractured relationships that result from disagreements or outright hostility. He longs for families to flourish and thrive in the sunshine of healthy relationships. He longs to draw His children to each other and to Himself so that the joys of God-filled family relationships can be experienced and enjoyed.

With God at the center of Christian families, the home can be a little patch of Paradise for “the sweetest type of heaven is a home where the Spirit of the Lord resides” (Ellen G. White, _The Adventist Home_, p. 15). “His peace and grace and love will pervade the family circle like a precious perfume” (_The Adventist Home_, p. 17).

Jesus bids us, “This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you” (John 15:12).

May you and the children be richly blessed as you explore and identify the characteristics of healthy family relationships through the story of Moses and his joyous reunion with his precious family. May you be inspired to prayerfully pursue strong and loving relationships with your own family members. And may your hearts be filled with this little piece of heaven.
