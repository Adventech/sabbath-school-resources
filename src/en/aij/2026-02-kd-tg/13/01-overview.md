**Word of the Week**

**Welcome:** To welcome is to warmly greet someone. It also means to make someone feel as though they belong. You can welcome someone by smiling and saying a big hello or inviting them to play or sit with you. When you are friendly and kind to someone, they will feel welcome.

**Lesson Summary**

Naomi returned home to Bethlehem with Ruth by her side. The women of Bethlehem were so excited to welcome them and hear about Ruth’s choice to follow Naomi and God. To find food, Ruth went to a farmer’s field to pick up grain behind the harvesters. She worked very hard, and the field’s owner, Boaz, welcomed her with food, water, and extra grain. He had heard of her kindness to Naomi and her choice to follow God. Under Naomi’s guidance, Ruth visited Boaz one night and showed him that she would like for him to marry her. Boaz was very happy. After they were married, the couple welcomed a son, Obed, into their family. Later Jesus would be born into their family line too.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they are a part of God’s big, loving family.
- **Feel** a desire to welcome others into God’s family.
- **Respond** by asking God to help them be welcoming to others.
