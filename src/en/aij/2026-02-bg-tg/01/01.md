**WEEK 1: FISHING WITH JESUS**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: Small net, toy or paper fish, toy boat, blue fabric, toy or peg men

Place blue fabric and a small tub of water on the floor. Take a small plastic boat out of your story bag and push it on top of the water as you talk.

Open your Bible, point, and say: **The Bible tells us a story of some men; Peter, Andrew, James, and John** (show toy or peg men). **They wanted to catch some fish. Can you help them?** Invite children to “fish” with you, by throwing out a small net onto the blue fabric. Pull the net in, empty.

Ask: **Where are the fish? There are none. Peter and Andrew were fishing all night, but they didn’t catch any fish either.**

**Jesus saw that they didn’t have any fish. He climbed into Peter’s boat and said, “If you put your nets into the water, you will catch some fish” (Luke 5:4, ICB). Jesus smiled, and the fisherman obeyed. They threw their nets on the other side of the boat. Splash! Down, down into the sea the nets went.**

Invite the children to throw out the net again. As they do so, throw lots of colored paper fish into the net. Share the excitement of the miracle catch with the children by clapping your hands with them.

Say: **So many shiny fish filled the boat—right to the top! Peter, Andrew, James, and John were amazed. Jesus asked these men if they would be His special helpers. He wanted them to learn to be like Him. They said, “Yes!” and followed Jesus.**

Invite children to step on paper footprints that you have placed around the room as you talk about the fishermen leaving everything to follow their kind new friend, Jesus.

Say: **Jesus wants to be your special friend too** (encourage a big hug between parent/caregiver and child). That’s why Jesus invites you to follow Him and learn about Him. Do you want to say, “Yes, Jesus, I will follow You”?

Say together: **Thank You, Jesus, for caring for me. I want to be Your friend too.**
