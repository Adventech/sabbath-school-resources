**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** **Warmly welcome** each child by name at their eye level as they arrive. Ask: **Did you have to wait for something this week? How did you feel about waiting? What helped you to wait patiently?**

Then ask them individually, or as a small group, how they felt when there was nothing inside, or when the object inside didn’t match the “promise” of the picture on the container. Finally, ask them how they felt when the picture did match what was inside, just as the picture “promised.”

**^[DO (Quarterly Theme Exploration):]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#D34D5E"}}})** Place the golden throne where the children can see it and ask:

- **What are some things that are important to you?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#D34D5E"}}})** When something is more important to us than anything else, we could say it is our god. We think about it all the time. We spend as much time with it as we can. After a while it can start to control us. It is as though we have a golden throne in our hearts, and we place whatever is most important to us on that throne. Let’s think about some things that might be on the throne in people’s hearts. Place each item or photo onto the golden throne, one at a time. (Items could include: money, toys, picture of a movie star, singer, and/or athlete, wood or stone idol, smartphone or tablet, fancy house, expensive car, pet, yourself, Jesus.) As you place each item on the throne, ask:

- **Is this the most important thing in life?**
- **Should I leave this on the throne?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#D34D5E"}}})** Finally place the picture of Jesus on the throne.

- **Is Jesus the most important thing in life?**
- **Why should Jesus always be first in our hearts?**

**Teaching Point:** Out of all these things, Jesus is the only true God. He is the one who created our world. He is the one who died on the cross to save us from sin. And He is the one who will come and take us home to heaven one day. Jesus is very different from anyone or anything else. Point to the quarterly mural and say together: Jesus is my God.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **baby doll** and **some of the things that babies need**. Show the children the baby doll and pass it around the group. Invite the children to welcome the new “baby” with a hug, or in some other happy way.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How do people usually feel when there is a new baby in the family?**
- **How do families celebrate when there is a new baby?**

Show them the things that the baby will need: diapers/nappies, clothes, a little blanket, bottle, etc. Ask:

- **Why do babies need these special things, and how do we use them to care for them?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** God promised to send a Messiah to rescue His people. They were expecting a mighty king! But God sent Jesus as a little baby, and He chose a special family to take care of Him.

Say: Today I promise to give you a little gift before the end of the class! Make sure I don’t forget! This week’s **Big Idea** is **Jesus is my God. He is my promised Savior.** I wonder how the baby doll will help us with our story today?

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box**, revealing a **gift box** with a **lid** or **gift bag**. Ask:

- **What was the promise I made to you earlier today?** (To give them each a small gift.)

Open the box to reveal some treats and share them with the children to fulfill your promise.

- **How would you have felt if I had forgotten to keep my promise or if I had chosen to ignore what I’d promised?** (Disappointed, sad, upset.)
- **Why should we be very careful not to make promises unless we are very sure we can keep them?** (It is important to keep our promises so that we don’t let other people down or make them feel sad. When people don’t keep their promises, it’s difficult to trust them. It’s best not to make promises we cannot keep.)

**Teaching Point:** God did make a promise. He promised to send Jesus, and He did send Jesus to our earth. Everything God says is true. In fact, God cannot not keep a promise! We can always trust Him.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He is my promised Savior.**
