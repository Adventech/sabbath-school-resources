The question, “Who touched me?” (Luke 8:45, ICB) echoed across the crowd, straight to the ears of the woman who was quietly walking away. Spinning her head around, she saw Jesus looking right at her. She didn’t know what to do. Her hands began to tremble. Her legs began to wobble. She had heard the words of Jesus, “Who touched me?” And she had seen Jesus looking right at her.

She knew she couldn’t hide. Slowly she squeezed through the crowd toward Jesus. “She came and bowed at Jesus’ feet. Shaking with fear, she told him the whole story” (Mark 5:33, ICB). She explained how she had been sick for 12 years and that no doctors could help her. She told Jesus she had finally touched His robe and instantly been healed!

Jesus smiled at her with kind, gentle eyes. He wasn’t upset with her! No, Jesus wanted to give her even more than a healthy body. He wanted her to feel happy and loved on the inside.

Jesus said, “Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well. Go in peace” (Luke 8:48).

Jesus loved that she had reached out to Him in faith, being sure that He could help her. The woman’s heart bubbled up with love for Jesus and so much joy.

Jesus longs to help us too. He wants to give us peace and happiness. What does the Bible say Jesus can do, if we believe, and have faith like this woman? **Read Mark 9:23.**
