**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The story begins with an arrogant pharaoh who is worshipped by a confident bunch of Egyptians. Slogging away in the shadows are a discouraged, slumped-shouldered nation of Israelite slaves who hunger for freedom. Above all is the only powerful and true God of heaven. He is the God who promises to “rescue you from their bondage” and “redeem you with an outstretched arm” (Exodus 6:6).

Red blood, croaking frogs, pesky lice, swarming flies, dying cattle, festering boils, pounding hail, munching locusts, and oppressive darkness. All nine plagues are designed and executed for one specific purpose: to grab the attention of all and etch into their minds a knowledge of the one true God.

For the Egyptians it’s a dark story. Pharaoh, their revered leader, arrogantly ignores the warning that precedes each plague. As each plague delivers its catastrophic consequences and plunges the country into chaos, the terrified Egyptians get a frightening taste of the suffering they have caused God’s people.

Meanwhile, the Israelites in Goshen are bathed in daylight, God’s light. It is the light of His love, the warmth of His presence, and the assurance of His miraculous protection. The dark and bright sides of this incredible story reach a pinnacle of contrast when the ninth plague descends. God’s children discover that there is no better place to be than in the light of the powerful and true God of all.

As you and the children take a journey with Moses as the plagues descend on Egypt, our prayer is that the children will understand and be in awe of God’s mighty power to both deliver justice and lead His people to a bright new future with Him.
