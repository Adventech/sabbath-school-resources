Four Good Friends
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/04/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block": {"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
The little house was too full! There wasn’t enough room. There wasn’t even room for a little mouse! Mothers and fathers squished together (squish close). Grandfathers and grandmothers squeezed in close (pull child close). Young, old, short, tall—everyone pressed together to be near Jesus. Children peeked through windows to see and hear Jesus. People waited outside the little house to see Jesus too. Four friends carried a man who lay on a mat to the little house. The man’s legs didn’t work anymore. He couldn’t move them to walk and run. His four good friends wanted to take him to Jesus. They knew Jesus could make his legs work again. If only they could get to Jesus! But there were so many people in the little house. What could they do?  
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}

The four friends tried to carry the man through the front door of the little house, but there were too many people! They couldn’t help him through a window—it was too small! How would they help their friend see Jesus? The four friends cared for the man on the mat. They were kind and helpful friends. They would look for another way to Jesus. The four friends looked and looked (hand at forehead searching). Ah, there was a way! The roof—through the top of the house! Very carefully they climbed one step . . . two steps . . . They carried their friend all the way up to the flat top of the little house. They scratched away the straw and mud to make a hole in the roof. The man lying on his mat smiled at his four good friends. He was going to see Jesus!
^^^
^^^

Down, down, through the roof went the man on his mat. His friends carefully made him go lower and lower into the room, right to Jesus. The people in the little house looked up with surprise! Jesus smiled at the man. Jesus knew the man wanted forgiveness for the bad things he had done. Jesus said: “ ‘Friend, your sins are forgiven.’ . . . ‘Stand up! Take your mat and go home’” (Luke 5:20-24, ICB). The man’s legs moved. He picked up his mat and began walking! All the way home he thanked God for forgiveness and for legs that could walk and run again. He praised God for good friends. Thank You, Jesus, for my friends, and for being my best Friend! I want to bring my friends to You.\
(Say together) Please help me be a good friend too.
