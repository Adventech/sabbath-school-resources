**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite an **adult assistant** to stand next to you **with arms by their sides**. Say: “Let’s see how strong they are.” Invite a child to wrap one or two layers of cling wrap around the adult. Ask the adult to break out. Then wrap another layer. Can they break out again? Then wrap the whole box around the person. Bind from their shoulders to their knees. See if the person can break the bind. (They won’t be able to). Finally, use scissors to cut them free, while being careful not to cut their clothing.

(Note: Be sure you do not wrap a child. It must only be demonstrated with an adult volunteer.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How is this cling wrap like sin?** (It slowly ties us up and keeps us from doing what God wants us to do. At first it seems easy to break, so we don’t think we’re in danger, but then it gets stronger and stronger.)
- **How do we get wrapped in sin?** (Satan tempts us and we sometimes give in.)
- **What is temptation?** (The strong wish to do something that’s wrong or unwise.)
- **What kind of things does Satan tempt us to do?**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Hold up a Victory Flag (from the Early Arrivals activity).

Share that today, we can know that (point to the **Big Idea**): **I can rely on God and His power to help me when I am tempted.** God and His words in the Bible will always help us say no to Satan.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SCRIPTURE MATCHING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** As a class or in small groups, give each group a set of Temptation and Bible Verse Cards. Children will match the temptation to a Bible verse that they could say out loud to help them resist temptation.

- **Why do you think Jesus spoke words from the Bible when He was tempted by Satan?** (God’s way is best; God would give Him power to defeat Satan.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus won the victory over Satan by quoting words from the Bible. Satan also tempts us to disobey God by sinning. He wants to turn us away from God. But when we are feeling tempted, we can follow Jesus’ example.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Collector Card 33: Jesus in the Wilderness**, to place in their storage container/bag.

Show a **board** with several **nails** slightly nailed into it. Invite the children (one at a time) to try to hammer in a nail with the tool you give them. Provide a **hairbrush**, a **spoon**, a **screwdriver**, and finally a **hammer**.

- **Why didn’t the first three tools work very well?**

**Teaching Point:** It doesn’t make sense to use the wrong tool. It also doesn’t make sense to go to the wrong place when we are looking for good, true answers. We can turn to **God and His words in the Bible** to help us **resist temptation**. Distribute the **flags** made before Sabbath School (if done) for children to **wave** in the air as you **say and sing the memory verse: “1 Corinthians 15:57.”**

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Tempted to Cheat,”** and engage the children in the discussion question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Place a few empty plastic **cups** together in a group on the floor. Use a small ball to bowl the cups over. Ask children to take turns rolling a ball to knock them over. Next, place all the cups in a **box and close the lid**. Ask the children to now try knocking them over with the ball.

- **How easy was it to knock the cups over in the beginning? What difference did it make when the cups were protected by the box?**

**Teaching Point:** This is similar to how **God and His words in the Bible protect us from temptation**.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your **Memory Verse Box**. Explain how Jesus used the words directly from the Bible to resist and overcome Satan.

Explain that today they’ll make their own memory verse box to help them remember Bible verses too. Share instructions for making and decorating the box. Give children some cards with verses ready to decorate and some blank cards they can later write Bible verses on that are meaningful to them. Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** in the background as children make their memory verse box.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Thank the children for contributing to your special gratitude gifts for the people who serve in the community. If appropriate, put the gifts together now (gift bags/buckets) and plan to give them away later today. Include a special note from your Sabbath School. Pray for the people who will receive these gifts. Invite the children to pray each day this week for these people, that they may come to know and love Jesus too.

**^[SHARING CIRCLE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** If time allows, invite the children to sit in a circle. Invite children who feel comfortable and are willing to share which Bible verse from their Memory Verse Box is particularly meaningful or helpful to them.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **I can rely on God and His power to help me when I am tempted. When Jesus was tempted, He won! We too can win when we say no to Satan’s temptations. God will help us.**

Invite children to stand and wave the Victory Flags again as they repeat the Big Idea. Remind them that this truth really is worth being joyful about!

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Thank God He has the power to help each of us when we’re tempted, and that He is so much more powerful than Satan.
