**Big Idea:** God is with me. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Invite children to run on the spot.
- Invite children to copy your praying hands.
- Use a big **rock** as a pillow.
- Use a **flashlight** for the bright dream and to shine on picture of ladder.
- Use **angels on craft sticks** for the children to fly up and down your **picture of the ladder**.
- Count out **10 items** together to illustrate tithing.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 3 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Jacob ran faster and faster—away from home; away from angry Esau. He was scared and sorry for lying ^[(invite children to run on the spot)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- He felt alone and guilty for doing wrong by lying, cheating, and stealing the birthright blessing.
- Jacob prayed and said he was sorry to God for the sins he’d done, and asked for forgiveness ^[(praying hands)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- He went to sleep on the ground, with a rock for a pillow. **What would that be like?** ^[(Discuss. Invite a child to try.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God sent Jacob a dream of a shiny ladder, with angels going up and down the ladder, and God at the top. God was sending love, help, and forgiveness from heaven to earth. The ladder symbolizes Jesus as our Savior. ^[(Flashlight; use angels on craft sticks for children to fly up and down the ladder.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God told Jacob He would be with him wherever he went. He would give Jacob a big, big family.
- Jacob was so thankful! He set up his rock as a pillar for a reminder (rock upright).
- Jacob decided to give God a tenth (tithe) of all that God would give him (count out 10 items, then hold up one).
- Jacob started walking again. This time he was happy. He had told God he was sorry for doing wrong. And God had forgiven him!

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob ran faster and faster ^[(invite children to run on the spot)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Darkness was coming. He must hurry—hurry away from home; hurry away from his angry brother!

Spiky bushes and tree branches scratched his legs as he ran on. He felt so sad. If only he had been honest! He shouldn’t have lied to his father and tricked him into giving the birthright blessing to him instead of Esau. His lies had caused so much hurt.

Jacob thought of angry Esau. His own brother hated him! He quickly looked at the path behind him. Feeling scared, he ran even faster ^[(invite children to run faster . . . then stop)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As darkness fell, Jacob felt afraid and alone. The wrong things he’d done felt like a heavy rock on his back.

Jacob didn’t know if God still wanted to be his friend. Did God still love him after everything he’d done? He’d lied to his father and cheated his brother! God had seen it all. He had heard it all. _Surely God wouldn’t want me as His leader_, he thought.

Even though he was scared to talk to God, Jacob stopped to pray ^[(invite children to copy your praying hands)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He cried as he said he was sorry to God for all his lies. He asked God to forgive him for the wrong things he had done.

After a long day of running and walking, Jacob lay down. He was so tired he used a rock as his pillow. **What would that feel like?** ^[(Discuss, then invite a child to try.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Closing his eyes, Jacob fell fast asleep.

God spoke to Jacob as he went to sleep on the cold, hard “pillow.” God had watched over him every step of the way. He accepted Jacob’s prayer for forgiveness. And God planned to show Jacob something very special to prove it!

Suddenly a bright shining light burst through the darkness of his sleep ^[(flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God was giving Jacob a dream!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob saw a shiny ladder that stretched up, up, up, far into the sky. The ladder seemed to reach all the way from earth to heaven ^[(flashlight on ladder)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

As Jacob looked at the ladder, he noticed angels everywhere. Some were busily flying up the ladder, and others were busily flying down the ladder. At the very top of the ladder was God!

God’s strong and loving voice spoke. He told Jacob that He would bless him with a big, big family! Then God promised, “I am with you, and I will protect you everywhere you go” (Genesis 28:15, ICB). The ladder was a picture of what Jesus does. He saves us and connects us with God.

Jacob woke up feeling peaceful and thankful. God had heard his prayers and had forgiven him. God was with him and would bless him with a good future. He didn’t need to worry or fear, because the broken connection with God was now fixed. He smiled as he thought about all those angels flying up and down the ladder from heaven to earth. What a good, kind, forgiving God! ^[(Give angels on craft sticks to children to go up and down the ladder.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jacob got up. It was still early, and the sun wasn’t up yet. But he couldn’t sleep; he was too excited. He had been forgiven! God had promised him a wonderful new life. He had to do something to show God just how thankful he was.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He took the rock he had slept on and set it up like a pillar ^[(stand rock upright)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). This would be a special reminder rock. Whenever Jacob walked past this place, the rock would remind him of the ladder dream and God’s forgiving love.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He had a great idea—a way to show God his thanks wherever he went! Jacob promised to give God tithe. Every time he counted 10 gifts from God, he would give one back to God. ^[(Count out 10 items with children, and then hold up one.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob started walking again with a big happy smile. God loved him and had forgiven him!
