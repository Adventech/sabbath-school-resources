**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a “Who am I?” Object Lesson. Take out the bag with the face puzzle pieces inside. Hold up the piece with the least recognizable part of the face.

- **Do you know who this is?** Attach it to the whiteboard, wall, or a poster.

Invite a child to come and select another piece without looking. Attach to the whiteboard, wall, or poster. Ask the same question. Continue trying to fit the pieces together until the children guess correctly.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **Is it easy to recognize someone, even someone you know well, when you can’t see all of them?**
- **How much easier did it become each time I added another piece of the face?**

Share that God knew that after 400 years of slavery the Israelites didn’t recognize Him very well. So He did things in the wilderness to show how much He loved them.

- **What things had He done to show them His love?** (Parted the Red Sea, pillar of cloud/fire, provided water and manna.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God showed His love in so many marvelous ways! Each thing He did was like the hair or the nose or the eyes on this picture. It told the people more about their loving God. Only then could He teach them how to love and respect Him in return. And that leads us to today’s story and our **Big Idea: God teaches me how to show love and respect for Him.**

Invite the children to kneel in reverence for silent prayer. Before they pray, say: The children of Israel were so amazed that God had chosen them as His special treasure. They said, “All that the Lord has spoken we will do” (Exodus 19:8). **What will you say to God today?**
