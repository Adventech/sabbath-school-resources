**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It does our souls well to pause and recount all the things God has done for us. Awakening to a new morning, hearing laughter fill our home, or watching clouds roll above rain-kissed fields are all signs of God’s love and care. When we praise God, we are acknowledging who He is and all the wonderful things He has done in our lives.

When we see the power of God and His great love for us, we cannot help praising and worshipping Him. This was the experience of the shepherds in the field, Simeon and Anna in the temple, and the Wise Men from the East. These individuals were deeply devoted to God and waited patiently for their Redeemer. They lived life in a posture of expectancy and worship. God did not disappoint. They were the first people to welcome His Son; the greatest gift the world would ever know. What a reward! Their thanks naturally overflowed into worship and praise.

“You are worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honor and power; for You created all things, and by Your will they exist and were created” (Revelation 4:11).

As you and the children explore this week’s story, may you be reminded anew of who God is and His mighty works, and know He alone is worthy to be praised.
