**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The King of the universe sits on a grand, majestic throne, a rainbow encircling Him. Peace and joy radiate all around, and in His holy presence angels bow and sing to Him. The Bible tells us, “You are worthy, O Lord, to receive glory and honor and power; for You created all things, and by Your will they exist and were created” (Revelation 4:11). As you imagine the scene, how might you honor your Creator, Redeemer, Lord, and King?

We don’t need to wait for eternity to show our adoration, reverence, and love to our God. We can do it daily through the life choices we make, just as Joseph did. He resolved in his heart to honor God, no matter his circumstances. He honored God’s faithfulness by being faithful in his daily work. He honored God’s righteousness through his moral choices. He honored God’s goodness by showing integrity. He honored God in doing his assigned work with dedication, perseverance, and excellence.

We too can honor God in how we act, what we say, and how we treat others. You can honor Him by using your finances wisely, by working faithfully despite feeling unappreciated in a job, or by giving your time to a friend who is lonely. Through these ways and many others you honor God and reflect a little of His character to others.

God truly deserves our honor, and when we see Him face-to-face, we’ll be drawn to praise Him, to fall in adoration at His feet, in love toward Him. May you and the children be inspired this week to show God the honor He deserves.
