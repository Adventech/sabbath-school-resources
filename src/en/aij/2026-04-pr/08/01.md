Sabbath Blessings Forever

When the first six days of Creation came to an end, “God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good” (Genesis 1:31). In fact, God’s wonderful new world was so “very good” that the angels in heaven “shouted for joy” (Job 38:7). Then God set aside the seventh day as the very best day of all. He blessed it and made it holy (Genesis 2:1-3).

This special holy day was a gift from God to His children. Sabbath is a 24-hour rest in which we can remember God’s love and creative power. It is a time to be close to God, to hear His voice, and see His creative power in the crashing waves, the soaring eagles, and the blades of fresh green grass.

After Creation, God’s people enjoyed worshipping Him on the Sabbath. Their hearts were drawn to their Creator God, and the day was a blessing. But over time, more and more people forgot about God. They became too busy to spend time with Him on Sabbath.

By the time Jesus came to earth, the Sabbath truth was lost to most people and misunderstood by many others. This made Jesus sad, so He planned to show people the real meaning of the Sabbath, and the blessings that come from keeping it.

Our loving God knew how easy it would be for people to forget about His Sabbath over time. That’s why He said, “Remember the Sabbath day . . .” (Exodus 20:8). He wants us to remember the day so we can always experience His Sabbath blessings and know the truth about His love.
