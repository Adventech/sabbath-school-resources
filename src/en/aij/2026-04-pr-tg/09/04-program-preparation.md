**Opening and Focus Activities**

Print or write **messages** on small pieces of paper for the treasure hunt. Messages include: “Jesus loves you no matter what.” “You are forgiven.” “Nothing can separate you from God’s love.” “Even when you mess up, Jesus still loves you.” Hide these around your room so that there is enough for one message per child.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source a **hula hoop** or some other circle shape (or rope) to represent a pool.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: **Peg people**: Jesus, man who was healed, Pharisees, and a **cloth** or towel for mat/bedroll.

Source a **bowl of fruit or healthy treats**, enough for each child.

**Craft Materials**

Print **^[week 9 craft template]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** on colored paper, and source **colored-paper strips** one-inch-thick for weaving (2.5 centimeters), **scissors, a craft knife** for teachers to use, and **tape**. Using the craft knife, follow the craft instructions to prepare the base plate ahead of time.
