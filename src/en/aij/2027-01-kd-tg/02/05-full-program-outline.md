Show the children how to find the memory verse, **Psalm 51:10. Say it together**: “Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me” (Psalm 51:10).

Share that this Bible verse is a prayer to God. Whenever we make bad choices—such as being selfish, lying, or hurting others—it’s as if our hearts get messed up and dirty with sin.

- **Who can forgive us and change our hearts to be clean again?** (Yes, God!)

Share that a **“steadfast spirit”** means to be strong; to stick up for what we know is true and right.

Share that in our story today, Moses liked to stand up for what was right. But he didn’t always do it the right way. He needed God’s help to change Him and help him to follow God’s ways.

**Teaching Point:** God is so good and loving. We can ask Him to help us to be like Him. He loves to forgive and change us when we ask Him to. He can help us to be strong, stand up for what is right, and follow His good ways.

Listen to and sing the **Psalm 51:10 Scripture Song** together.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your bell or sound maker. Show the children the **Secret Surprise Box, but don’t open it yet**. In a quiet voice, invite the children to close their eyes. Ask them to **silently** imagine what it might be like to meet God later today! Ask them to imagine what He might look like . . . and what He might say. Then invite the children to open their eyes and quietly put up their hands to answer the following questions. When they are chosen, **invite them to jump to their feet to answer**.

- **What do you think God might look like? Why?**
- **What do you think He might say to you? Why?**

Choose a few different children and accept all answers.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal the **“flames”** you have made. Share that we don’t fully know what God looks like, but in today’s story someone heard God and saw something very surprising! (Hold up “flames.”) Moses saw warm, beautiful, powerful, holy fire. Wow! Let’s find out what God said to His friend Moses—and what Moses might do.

This story will help us learn today’s **Big Idea: God’s way is best. I can follow Him.**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children to each take some “flames” and stick them to your drawn tree/plotted plant.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, but don’t open your **Story Explorer Box** yet. Meanwhile, have a helper discreetly hide the Bible promises in your drawn tree/potted plant (slot between flames; stick on branches, etc.). Invite the children to follow you. Lead them around your Sabbath School area, finally arriving at your drawn tree/potted plant. Invite the children to sit down in front of it. Ask:

- **If you were Moses, how would you have felt to see the bush that didn’t burn and to hear God’s voice?**
- **What did God ask Moses to do?** (Take off his sandals—it was holy ground; return to Egypt to set His people free.)
- **If God asked you to do something important, how would you feel and what would you say? Why?**

Share that Moses didn’t need to be afraid to follow God, and neither should we. Let’s find out why. Invite the children to look for the promises on your tree/bush. Then invite them to follow you back to their seats.

Read out the promise: God said, “I will certainly be with you” (Exodus 3:12). Then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a Bible.

**Teaching Point:** Share that God “speaks to us” in the Bible. We can’t hear Him out loud, but we can read and hear His words in His holy Word. He promises to be with each one of us! He has shown that He can do anything—even the impossible. He can change a stick into a snake, and He can change us too. We can trust His great plans for us and follow Him.

Invite the children to close their eyes and say a silent prayer to Jesus, asking Him to help them follow His words and ways.
