**Opening Activities**

Print out one **^[sheep and goat template]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child and cut them out.

Source **markers**, or **stamps and stamp pad**, to make spots on the sheep and goats. You can make stamps out of something round, such as bottle tops, marker lids, or a chopped carrot.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: **masking tape**, a **spoon**, and a **ball**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: cup, rock to sit on cup, sandbox, peg people (Jacob, Laban, Rachel, Leah with a veil taped to her head).

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: colored sticker dots.

**Nature/Mission**

Print **^[Nature Card 17: Spotted Sheep]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
