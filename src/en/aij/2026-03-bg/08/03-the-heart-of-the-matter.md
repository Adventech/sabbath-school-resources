God wants to be close to you. Let that sink in. He desires more than anything to be your closest friend, your encourager, and your leader. How blessed the Israelites were to have physical evidence of God’s closeness and guidance in their lives.

When you look back over your life, I’m sure you can pinpoint moments you have felt closest to God. How amazing is it that God desires to be close to us always! He wants to have a continuous relationship with us, not one that stops and starts as our emotions or situations change. God promised to be with the Israelites always. Through all their travels, in the hills, and in the valleys. That promise is ours too. He is willing to show up every day, in every moment. May we open our eyes to see the many ways God’s wonderful cloud of comfort and protection is surrounding us today.

When we choose to let God in and be close to Him, everything changes! We’re able to speak softly and kindly to our children, even when they misbehave. Our burdens lighten, our joy is not dependent on circumstances, and we can love more freely. We are so blessed to be able to say that our hearts can be His sanctuary and that He can dwell in us.

“For this reason I bow my knees to the Father . . . , that Christ may dwell in your hearts through faith; that you, being rooted and grounded in love, may be able to comprehend with all the saints what is the width and length and depth and height—to know the love of Christ which passes knowledge; that you may be filled with all the fullness of God” (Ephesians 3:14-19).

As you walk through this week knowing that Christ is dwelling in your heart, may you begin to comprehend the many dimensions of God’s love and may you be filled to overflowing with His never-ending joy and peace.
