When we do things “with love” we do them in a loving, caring, helpful way.

- **If you were to speak “with love” what kind of words might you use?**
- **If you were to listen “with love” what might that look like?** (Ask them to show you.)
- **What kind of things might you use your hands to do “with love”?**

**Teaching Point:** God wants us to do everything with love. That means being helpful and caring and thinking of others more than ourselves every day. God can fill us up with His love, so we can share it with everyone else.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a family photo of your (teacher’s) family; a large piece of cardstock with an empty **photo frame** drawn on it; a **marker**. Show the children the family photo and identify the people in the picture. Share a brief affirmation about each person: E.g., my brother is very funny; my sister is kind and thoughtful. Invite the children to come forward and draw a simple smiley face to represent someone in their family, and tell the class what they love about them.

Share that it’s fun spending time with our families. And it’s extra special to spend family time telling stories about God. **How does your family shares stories about God?** (Prompt: At the dinner table, in books before bed, when you read your lesson, in worship, playing Bible games . . .?)

In our story today, Moses met his family after a long time apart. He was so thankful to God for the gift of his family. He had lots of stories to tell them about God. God is so good! Let’s find out what happened next, and how God used Moses’ loving family to help him.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** This story will help us understand our **Big Idea**. Invite the children to repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God’s way is best. He made families to love each other.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **peg (or felt) Moses.** Invite a child to come to the front and stand on tiptoe, reach both arms as high as they can, and hold Moses above their head. Ask them to keep standing in that position.

Share that Moses had a very big, important job to do. It was as tiring as being on tiptoe, reaching up high all day long. Invite all the children to copy and hold that position while you count to 10, then 20. Give them a clap, then invite them back to their seat.

- **What was that like?**
- **Imagine if you saw your mommy or daddy doing that all day. What would you do?** (Why would you do that?)

**Teaching Point:** God made families to love and care for each other. God can help us notice and help our families when they are tired, have a problem, or need extra love and care—just as He did with Moses’ family. The Bible says to “help each other with your troubles. When you do this, you truly obey the law of Christ” (Galatians 6:2, ICB).

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})**

- **What was the first thing Jethro did when he saw that Moses had a big problem?** (Prompt with prayer hands if needed.) Yes, he prayed! God gave him a great idea; a way for Moses to share all the hard work with happy helpers. God loves to help us do everything “with love.”

Invite the children to close their eyes, and if they would like to, they can repeat this prayer: “Dear Jesus, thank You for my family! Please help me show them love this week. Amen.”
