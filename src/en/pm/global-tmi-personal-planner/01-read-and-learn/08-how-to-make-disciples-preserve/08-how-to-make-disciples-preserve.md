^[Nurture, integrate, and train newly baptized church members]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Here are several ways to mentor a newly baptized member into an active ^[disciple-maker]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), and to continue developing spiritual habits, church involvement, and soul-winning abilities:

- ^[Establish weekly discipleship studies with your friend soon after his or her baptism (using _Discipleship Handbook_ or a similar resource).]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Help your friend begin a Bible and Spirit of Prophecy reading plan.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})<sup>*</sup>
- ^[Invite and involve your friend in church events, social gatherings, and personal evangelism training.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Participate in outreach activities and church ministries with your friend.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})

“Let all be taught how to work. Especially should those who are newly come to the faith be ^[educated to become laborers]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) together with God” (_Christian Service_, p. 69).

<sup>*</sup> A correlated reading plan, as well as many recommendations for the nurture, integration, and training of new members, can be found in the appendices of the _Discipleship Handbook_.
