**Word of the Week**

**Trust:** To trust is to rely on something or someone. When you trust someone, you believe their words are true. You know you can count on them. We can trust God. He is always loving. His words are true. And He always keeps His promises.

**Lesson Summary**

The Israelites were free! They praised and thanked God. God led His people out of Egypt with a pillar of cloud during the day and a pillar of fire at night. They came to the Red Sea and camped. When the Israelites saw the Egyptian army pursuing them, they realized they were trapped. Faced with an impossible situation, they were overcome with fear. But God made a way for them when it seemed impossible. He divided the waters of the Red Sea so they could escape on dry land. God then closed the waters of the Red Sea, and the pursuing enemy was destroyed.
