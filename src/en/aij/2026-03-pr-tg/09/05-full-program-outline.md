**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Share with the children how our story today is about Jesus performing His first miracle. I wonder what He will do!

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Before the lesson, put a few drops of red food coloring into the bottom of an empty glass. Tell the children that you are going to turn water into something else. Pour water from the jug into the empty glass, holding the glass so the children can’t see the food coloring at the bottom of it. As you pour the water in, it will look like the water is turning into grape juice.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask:

- **How did I do this? Did I perform a miracle?** No, I just did a trick.

Explain that a miracle is an extraordinary event caused by God that can’t be explained and is something that a person in their own strength could never do. Talk about how Jesus performed many miracles in the Bible. In 30 seconds, brainstorm together, listing as many miracles as you can that Jesus did.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **Jesus loves to surprise me with good things to grow my faith.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Show the children some **seeds**. Pass some around for the children to look at and touch. Notice how small they are. Then show a range of **fruit, vegetables**, or **flower plants** and discuss how such different plants come from small seeds.

Talk about how even though a seed is small, it has the ability to grow into a big plant. In many ways this is a miracle. Just as Jesus changed “ordinary” water into delicious grape juice, Jesus changes dry little seeds into beautiful fruit, flowers, and trees.

**Teaching Point:** Faith and trust in Jesus also often begin as something small. But as we spend time with Jesus by reading His Word, praying to Him, and learning all we can about His love and truth, it’s like watering seeds, and our faith grows into something big, beautiful, and strong.

**Memory Verse Challenge:** Divide the children into two groups and place a set of six jars (one set of jars for each group) on the other side of the room. Inside each jar are different **words from the memory verse**. One at a time, children from each team walk to a jar, take out a word, and walk back to their team to tag the next person to take a different word. When each team has collected all their words, they will arrange them to make their memory verse.
