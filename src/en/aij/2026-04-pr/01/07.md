Joao, a boy who lives in Brazil, knows that God answers prayer. One evening a splinter of wood broke off a door and pierced his mother’s eye! Father couldn’t get it out, and neither could the doctor unless he did an operation. Father did not have money for surgery, so Joao’s parents went home.

When Joao learned what had happened, he told his mother, “I want to pray for you.” He knelt down by her bed and prayed. “Dear Jesus, please fix my mom’s eye and help her to be OK. Thank You, Jesus. Amen.”

During the night Mother dreamed that an angel came and touched her injured eye. When she woke up the next morning, her eye did not hurt. The splinter was gone, and her eye looked fine! “Joao,” she said, “Jesus answered your prayer. My eye is fine!” Joao smiled sleepily and hugged his mother.

That Sabbath Joao joyfully told his Sabbath School class how Jesus had answered his prayer. The young boy said, “Prayer is the best!”

When things seemed impossible, Joao knew that God had power—He could save his mother’s eye. He knelt down by his mother’s bed and prayed.

In this week’s lesson the disciples cried out to God in prayer too. When they were desperately fighting a storm on a lake, they prayed a short, three-word prayer: “Lord, save us!” (Matthew 8:25).

The storm the disciples faced, and the storm Joao’s mother faced, were very different. But Jesus answered both prayers with a miracle, and the storm passed.
