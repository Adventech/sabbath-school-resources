Opening Activities

Prepare six **Mystery Sensory Bags** containing items for children to feel and guess. Number the bags for identification. (Prepare two or more sets for a larger group.) Place household objects or objects from nature in the bags. For example: **keys, grapes, cooked cold spaghetti noodles, potato peels, raisins, leaves, pumpkin seeds, a baby rattle, cotton balls, fur.**

Source paper and pencils to play **Guess the Truth**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **furry blanket/fake fur fabric** and two **bowls** for pretend “stew.”

Source a **blindfold** to play **“Who Am I?”** game.

Source **sweet cut fruit** that’s common in your culture **(e.g., bananas, mangoes, strawberries, apples)**. Prepare enough to share with the class, and a small bowl for the object lesson. **Salt** and **honey**.
