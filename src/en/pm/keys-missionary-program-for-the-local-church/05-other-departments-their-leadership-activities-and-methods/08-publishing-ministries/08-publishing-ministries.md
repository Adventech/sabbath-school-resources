---
title: Publishing Ministries
---

The publishing ministries coordinator and publishing ministries council lead out in literature and DVD distribution.

Members should be encouraged and trained to carry and distribute a variety of tracts, DVDs, booklets, and leaflets advertizing media programs, etc. We have been instructed, “Let every believer scatter broadcast tracts and leaflets and books containing the message for this time.” <sup>66</sup> What would happen if every member who is in contact with people carried material and prayed daily, “Lord, guide me to someone today with whom I can share and show me what to say and what to leave with them?”

In addition to total membership involvement, God’s messenger says, “We need colporteurs, who will go forth to circulate our publications everywhere.” <sup>67</sup> And “God calls for workers from every church among us to enter His service as canvasser evangelists [now called literature evangelists].” <sup>68</sup> Their work can provide a constant flow of interests to the church. We know of nothing that can better assist in finding Bible study interests and interests to attend an area-wide evangelistic campaign than the literature ministry. If homes are visited with tracts and literature months before the campaign, the harvest can be abundant.

It is the happy privilege of the publishing ministries coordinator to give practical training to the membership and to find, train, and encourage literature evangelists in this work that is equal to the gospel ministry.<sup>69</sup>
