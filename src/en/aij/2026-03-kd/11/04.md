Everybody knows that plums grow on plum trees, and grapes grow on grapevines. And of course, berries grow on orange trees. No! Berries grow on berry bushes!

Jesus stood at the bottom of a mountain teaching and healing many people. Everyone listened carefully—they didn’t want to miss any of His wonderful words. Then Jesus said to them, “Each tree is known by its fruit. People don’t gather figs from thornbushes” (Luke 6:44, ICB).

The people must have smiled at the silly thought. Imagine someone searching in a thornbush for figs. Ouch! How spiky and scratchy! The only thing to find in a thornbush is prickles and pain.

Jesus then explained that we are like trees. Of course, we don’t have green leaves and branches. But the way we act, and the things we do, are like fruit on a tree.

Without God’s Spirit living inside our hearts, we are a lot like a prickly thornbush. We can hurt others and don’t grow good fruit. But when the Holy Spirit lives in our hearts, we will grow His beautiful fruit. What type of fruit will we grow? **Read Galatians 5:22, 23.**

The people must have smiled as they ate their fruit the next day. Jesus had taught them a lesson that was so easy to remember! They had listened. They could remember. Now they could ask God to help them do what Jesus said. The Bible says, “More than that, blessed are those who hear the word of God and keep it!” (Luke 11:28).
