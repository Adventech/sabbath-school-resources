---
title: The Spirit Was Unleashed
date: 07/01/2026
---

Wow! My ears are still ringing… so many trumpets blasted together! It was incredible to feel the earth tremble at the sound of Jesus’ voice. From the ground, people were rising, shining with extraordinary light. Joyful angels were bringing the redeemed out of the graves and embracing their friends for the very first time. Suddenly, a light like a rainbow covered me. At first, I was scared, but then I realized that several around me also began to shine like I did… I felt an unimaginable peace, my body was being restored, I was filled with complete happiness. I really don’t know how to describe it… Wow!

“Luca! Luca! I’m talking to you,” said Orel.

“Sorry, Orel, I’m still in awe… Everything is wonderful here,” Luca replied, walking toward a beautiful tree that had caught his attention.

“I know, Luca. I’ve waited so long to talk to you and show you the New Jerusalem,” Orel said as he ran after Luca. “You were always so curious, little child! Though you didn’t know it, I’ve been caring for you for a little more than eight years.” Orel laughed joyfully and hugged Luca.

“Thank you, Orel… I like your name… Orel… What does it mean?”

“Orel means ‘Light of God.’ I was given that name a long time ago… but I’ll tell you the story another time. Luca, I just saw someone I want you to meet,” Orel said as he took Luca by the hand and led him toward someone special.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/devo/ten-days-of-prayer-2026-childrens-resource/01-daily-prayer-guides-01-the-spirit-was-unleashed/image2.png)

Standing before this special person, Luca realized it was Jesus. He opened his mouth to say something, but words wouldn’t come out—he was so excited! He had seen Jesus return to Earth in great glory and power and now he was standing right in front of Him.

“Oh, my dear Luca,” said Jesus with a great smile. “In heaven we all love to talk a lot,” Jesus laughed. “I’m so happy you’re here. Thank you, Orel, for accompanying him.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/devo/ten-days-of-prayer-2026-childrens-resource/01-daily-prayer-guides-01-the-spirit-was-unleashed/image3.png)

Luca ran and hugged Jesus, tears of joy streaming down his face. In that moment, all he could manage to say was, “Thank you!” He repeated it over and over again, “Thank you! Thank you! Thank you, Jesus!”

“Oh, my precious son!” Jesus said tenderly. “I did everything for you. You believed in Me, you listened to My voice, and even as a child, you didn’t hesitate to give Me your heart. The Holy Spirit convinced you and worked in your heart, and that’s why you’re here now. We will have eternity to talk, play, laugh, and learn together.”

“The Holy Spirit? I never really understood about Him… On Earth, no one explained it to me,” replied Luca, looking at Jesus with eyes brimming with tears of joy.

Jesus turned to speak with Orel, “Dear Orel, shine the light of God upon this child so he can learn about the Holy Spirit. I leave this important task in your hands.”

“Of course, my Lord Jesus,” Orel replied joyfully. “I’ll do my very best.”

Jesus said goodbye to the two of them and continued on His way.

“Luca, now that you’re in heaven, it’s important for you to know about the Holy Spirit,” Orel said as he made a kind of thin glass appear in the air. “Here, let me show you a text from the Bible. If I’m not mistaken it’s recorded in… yes… here it is… John 14:16–17.” Orel said all this while moving his finger on the floating glass.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/devo/ten-days-of-prayer-2026-childrens-resource/01-daily-prayer-guides-01-the-spirit-was-unleashed/image4.png)

Luca came closer to read what appeared on the glass. At first glance, he saw some strange letters. Orel told him it was ancient Greek. As he made the effort to read the text, he realized he understood it all!

He read, “And I will ask the Father, and he will give you another advocate to help you and be with you forever— the Spirit of truth. The world cannot accept him, because it neither sees him nor knows him. But you know him, for he lives with you and will be in you.”

“When the Lord Jesus was on Earth,” Orel told Luca, “He knew He couldn’t remain with His disciples forever. As a human being, Jesus couldn’t use all His power. For that reason, He promised to send the Holy Spirit to accompany and guide us.”

“How interesting all this is, Orel!” said Luca with great excitement. “I was curious to know more about the Holy Spirit…”

“Well then… When Jesus ascended to heaven, the Holy Spirit was fully unleashed and sent to Earth to guide everyone to Jesus,” Orel continued. “The Spirit worked silently but very powerfully. You are an example of His work, dear Luca! That’s why you’re here with me in heaven now!”

“Wow! I want to know everything about Him! Orel, tell me more,” said the curious Luca.

“I just thought of a better idea,” Orel said quickly. “I’ll introduce you to some people who can share their own experiences with the Holy Spirit.”

“I can’t wait any longer!” said Luca as Orel showed him a floating map of the New Jerusalem. A red dot was flashing in a place near where they were.

“All right, get ready! We’ll both enjoy meeting these people… Follow me!” Orel said as he ran ahead of Luca toward the point marked on the map.

“Let’s go!”
