---
title: Charity Is Our Luxury
date: 18/07/2026
---

They were an anomaly to my young, ambitious, profit-driven mind: a husband-and-wife team who ran their businesses for God.

Over the years, I studied their financial reports and even helped structure their network of companies, trusts, and foundations to maximize gifts to charities and ministries. The process allowed me to uncover their deepest values, firmly rooted in 2 Corinthians 8:9, and challenge my heart’s motivations.

They came from an era when wealthy Christians didn’t flaunt their blessings. They believed that every cent they owned was God’s and was reserved solely for His glory, never their own.

They could have lived a luxurious lifestyle, but they lived frugally yet comfortably on far less than the average household income. They made any evidence of their wealth almost invisible.

On observing the husband making yet another unnecessarily frugal decision, when the selection of the upgraded option could have made their lives much more comfortable, I asked the question, “Dad, why don’t you and Mum spend more on yourselves? Why not enjoy more of life’s luxuries?”

Two decades later, his simple yet life-changing answer is still ringing in my ears: “Charity is our luxury.”

### Appeal

It’s now time to return God’s tithe and give the Promise offerings, which we are encouraged to preplan and set aside as a percentage of our income this week. Let’s each prayerfully consider what luxuries we enjoy—large or small—and decide to sacrifice some or all of them for the luxury of growing the kingdom of heaven. Thank you for your faithfulness.
