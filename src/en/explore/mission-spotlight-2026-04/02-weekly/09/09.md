Every day, people are encountering messages online. Scrolling, watching, searching.

“OneVoice27” is a global call for the church to come together in mission, to reach the unreached for Christ, with a special focus on those in the 10/40, Post-Christian, and Urban Windows. It’s all about sharing Jesus Christ. The goal is one unified message shared through personal connections and the use of media as we move together toward 2027.

The year 2027 marks 2,000 years since Jesus’ baptism and anointing, when His public ministry began.

This is a simple idea, and each of us has a part to play in its execution.

At its core, “OneVoice27” invites every member to take part in digital discipleship, using the platforms we already engage with each day to reflect Christ’s love and point others to truth. It can be as simple as sharing a message, starting a conversation, or intentionally creating content that leads people to Jesus.

And it won’t end with these digital moments. What begins online can lead to real relationships, Bible studies, and a more meaningful journey of faith.

Around the world, church members of all ages are using their gifts in different ways but with the same purpose.

As we look toward 2027, our shared movement will connect what happens online with what happens on the ground. Relationships will open the door for lives to be transformed.

This is the invitation of “OneVoice27.” We’re all called to be part of it; to notice the people around us, to care enough to be closer, and to share real hope with them.

Because when our mission is united, our individual efforts become something stronger.

One voice, reaching the world.
