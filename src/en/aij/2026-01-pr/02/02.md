God had someone precious on His mind when He made the animals—you! He knew animals would be very important to all people and the planet.

Some animals He created to help us directly.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![God knew that we would need animals like horses to ride and oxen to pull heavy loads.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/02/image3.png)

Others play a role in helping the planet to work perfectly.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![Beavers dam up rivers, which reduces flooding and helps fish to flourish.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/02/image4.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![Squirrels are natural tree planters, stashing nuts that take root and grow into forests.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/02/image5.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![Monkeys eat fruit and spread the seeds so that new trees grow in forests.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/02/image6.png)

Forests create the oxygen we all need to breathe and live.

God’s love for us is so great that He also designed animals to bring us joy. Think about mischievous monkeys, playful otters, and parrots that learn to talk.

God made other animals to be our special friends. When we love them, they love us right back, like playful cats or loyal dogs.

There’s a story told of Greyfriars Bobby, a little dog who belonged to a man in Scotland named John Gray. They were faithful friends. When Mr. Gray died, Bobby stayed on his grave and wouldn’t leave, even though many people wanted to take him home and care for him. He stayed there until he died 14 years later. He was a faithful dog who kept loving his master as long as he lived. Greyfriars Bobby reminds us of how much God loves us with a love that never ends. What does the Bible call God’s love that never ends? Read Jeremiah 31:3.

People should care for the animals, but we can also learn life lessons by looking at some of God’s creatures. The Bible tells us we can learn something big and important from the tiniest ant. What can we learn from an ant? Read Proverbs 6:6.

God’s love is selfless; it is always giving. He gave us animals to help us and the planet, and to teach us, love us, and bring us joy.
