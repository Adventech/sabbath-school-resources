Find 3 sunflowers, 4 tiger lilies, 7 daisies, and 1 rose.

Did you know that broccoli is a flower? Who would have thought that flowers could be so deliciously healthy for you! Not only are some flowers healthy to eat but they also add pops of color and scatter bright joy wherever they grow in nature.

What is your favorite flower?

This week we have learned how Jesus wants to comfort us and bring us joy when we are sad.

Your Sabbath Challenge this week is to be like Jesus! Can you think of someone who may be sad who you can comfort or bring joy to? Maybe someone who seems lonely, or someone who has had a bad week?

Make, or collect, a bunch of joyful, colorful flowers and deliver them to someone, along with a card.

**^[Option 1:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Go for a walk to collect a bunch of wildflowers.
