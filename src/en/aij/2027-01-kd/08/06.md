Moses didn’t rush to tie his sandals or gobble down his morning manna. He took his time. Then with a big smile he invited father Jethro to follow him outside. Moses had done everything Jethro had told him to do. And things in the camp already looked different. Jethro nodded to Moses. Yes, this was very good!

There were no big lines of people waiting to tell Moses their problems. Instead, there were leaders all over the camp meeting with people. Moses had chosen leaders for small groups, then bigger groups, and even bigger groups. What did the leaders do? **Read Exodus 18:26.**

Moses would help his helpers, and everyone would be happy. Jethro was so pleased. He had wanted the best for Moses and the people of Israel. That’s what good families want for each other. The Bible says, “Let all that you do be done with love” (1 Corinthians 16:14).

The people of Israel were starting to work together like a family. God must have been even happier than father Jethro!

With a smile, Jethro packed up his things and kissed his family goodbye. It was time to go back home to Midian. He would leave his family in good care. His family was part of a bigger family now—the family of God. And God would continue to love and lead them.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you work together with your family?
- Who is someone special in your church family? What could you do this week to show them you care?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for being your loving Father and making you part of His family.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Invite others into God’s family. On balloons, draw a heart and write (with help if needed) the words “God loves you.” Have a parent/caregiver blow up the balloons and tie strings to the bottoms. Make a plan to go somewhere busy (market/park) and hand them out as a family.
