And then . . . Water! Water! Someone had spotted a spring of water! The exciting news rippled through the tired and thirsty crowd. Thirsty mothers, fathers, and little boys and girls, all raced toward the spring. Their eyes were wide with excitement as they looked at the pool of water right in front of them. After three days of seeing only dirt, dust, and sand, the water looked so good!

Splashing their hands into the water, they happily scooped it up into their mouths. But . . . yuck! To their horror, the water tasted bitter. Their happy smiles disappeared. Now the Israelites were thirsty and grumpy!

What did they do next? **Read Exodus 15:24.**

They had forgotten about the tall cloud leading them. They had forgotten about their powerful God who had sent plagues and parted the Red Sea to save them. They just complained and grumbled, and blamed Moses!

Moses listened to their bitter, angry words, and then he “cried out to the Lord” (Exodus 15:25). He prayed. What a good idea. Grumbling doesn’t turn bitter water into sweet water. But Moses remembered that God was with them and could do anything.

When you have a problem, you can go straight to God too. He will always listen, and His kindness never ends. “Oh, give thanks to the Lord, for He is good! For His mercy endures forever” (Psalm 136:1).
