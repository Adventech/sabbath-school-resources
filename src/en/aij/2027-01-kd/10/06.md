God had now given nine commandments. He had one more rule of love to share. What was commandment number 10? To find out, read Exodus 20:17.

Mother arrived back to the car, waving a chocolate bar in the air. “Just a little treat for you girls to share,” she said happily to her daughters Milena and Bela, who were in the back seat.

“Thanks!” the girls replied with a grin. Mother unwrapped the chocolate, snapped it into two pieces, and handed half to each of the girls.

Milena looked happily at her chocolate. She looked across to Bela, and suddenly her smile turned into a frown. “It’s not fair! Bela’s chocolate is bigger than mine!”

Mother asked Bela for her chocolate, snapped off a piece of it, ate the piece, and handed the chocolate back. Now it was Bela’s turn to fuss, “But now her chocolate is much bigger than mine!”

Mother decided to teach her daughters an important lesson about wanting things that belong to someone else. She snapped off an extra-big piece from Milena’s chocolate and ate it. And then when Milena complained, Mother snapped off another huge piece from Bela’s.

Suddenly the girls realized that their chocolates were quickly disappearing, and the only person eating any of it was their mother! They quietly ate their little pieces as they thought about the lesson Mother had just taught them.

Wishing for things we don’t have steals the joy of the things we do have. God wants us to be thankful for the things God has given us.
