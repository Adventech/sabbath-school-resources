The centurion rushed to get ready. He had just heard the most exciting news. Jesus was on His way to Capernaum, the very town where he and his sick servant lived! He must go to Jesus right away!

But suddenly, he stopped still. He scratched his chin, deep in thought. Would it be okay to speak to such an important person as Jesus? He didn’t think he could. He was a Roman soldier. He wasn’t a teacher of the law, or a Jewish leader.

The centurion smiled. He had a great idea. Who did he ask to help? **Read Luke 7:3.**

Yes. That would work. He could trust his wise Jewish friends to take the message to Jesus. Surely, they would speak the right words to this important Man.

When he asked them, the Jewish leaders happily agreed to help their kind friend. They headed off down the road to look for Jesus.

When Jesus arrived in Capernaum, the group of men found Him and “begged him saying, ‘This officer is worthy of your help. He loves our people, and he built us a synagogue’ ” (Luke 7:4, 5, ICB).

Jesus could see that the centurion had been very kind and generous to the Jews. But something else made Jesus even happier. This Roman soldier, who had grown up worshiping idols, trusted the all-powerful, one true God.

Jesus smiled at the helpful friends. Yes, He would gladly help. Jesus followed the group of leaders down the road toward the centurion’s house.
