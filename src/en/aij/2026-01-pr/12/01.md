Home in the Wicked City of Sodom

Abram and his household of tent dwellers loved God and lived peacefully under the oak trees in the hills of Mamre. Abram’s new home suited him. Here he could live a simple life, learning more about God’s love from the beauty of nature and by caring for the land, the animals, and his household. This quiet spot in the countryside helped him become closer friends with God. With a thankful heart Abram built an altar to God beside his tent.

Meanwhile, the excitement of the big city of Sodom had grabbed Lot’s attention, and moving through the lush plains of the Jordan, he pitched his tents close by. The Bible says, “The men of Sodom were exceedingly wicked and sinful against the Lord” (Genesis 13:13). But now, instead of looking at God’s good things, Lot was focused on these very men and their sinful city.

The dazzling city of Sodom soon lured Lot into faulty thinking: It would be an exciting place to live . . . I can get rich quick selling in the busy markets . . . The people aren’t as wicked as they seem . . . We won’t worship idols . . . In the end he believed his faulty thinking and made an unwise choice to move right into wicked Sodom! What had he forgotten? **Read Proverbs 14:12.**

Abram and Lot each made a choice, one wise, one unwise. God will always help us to make wise choices if we ask Him and put Him first.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Abram looked out of his tent at an altar to God, while Lot looked out of his tent at Sodom. How did this guide their choices?_
- _What can you look at in your day to help you make wise choices? What might you avoid looking at?_
