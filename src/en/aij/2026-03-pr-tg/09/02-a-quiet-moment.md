**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Lively chatter and vibrant laughter floated through the air. A fresh breeze carried the fragrance of flowers and slow-roasted food. A wedding was always a delightful reprieve from life’s burdens. Guests ate and drank and basked in the joy of the happy couple.

Jesus showed His infinite love for people and His desire to bring them abundant life when He chose to perform that first miracle at a wedding in Cana. Although “not yet His time,” His heart saw need, and His love responded with more than what they expected. What Jesus did to the water in those jars He would go on to do in people’s lives wherever He went—He would make the ordinary, extraordinary. Many who saw or heard of this miracle would grow closer to God and believe with greater conviction.

“And truly Jesus did many other signs in the presence of His disciples, which are not written in this book; but these are written that you may believe that Jesus is the Christ, the Son of God, and that believing you may have life in His name” (John 20:30, 31).

Whether we observe a miracle or receive one ourselves, Jesus delights in seeing our joy—and our growth toward Him.

As you prepare this week’s lesson, may your faith grow as you see Jesus’ love expressed in this powerful, tangible way. May the children also see just how much Jesus loves them and wants to grow their faith in Him.
