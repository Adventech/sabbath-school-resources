The soldiers stood at attention as 12-year-old Moses waited for the huge palace gates to open. On this side of the gate, he had been an Israelite slave’s child. On the other side, he would become a prince of Egypt.

Moses must have been sad, scared, and excited all at the same time! The princess had sent for him to go and live in the palace. He would miss the peaceful and happy days his loving family had spent together in their little mud-brick home. He would miss worshipping God and praying with them too.

But 12-year-old Moses had no choice; he had to walk through the gates into a very different life. He now dressed like an Egyptian prince, and learned to talk and act like an Egyptian prince. He was taught all the many things an Egyptian prince needed to know, such as how to fight and be the leader of the army, how to rule the people, and how to be the future king of Egypt.

All this learning was another part of God’s plan to prepare Moses for the big job of leading His people out of Egypt. Moses needed to understand fully how Egyptians thought, and the different ways they did things. But, as Ellen G. White tells us, there was one thing Moses refused to learn and do. He would never bow down and worship the Egyptian gods. Ever. No matter how hard the priests threatened and tried to make him. In his heart he was a prince of God, not a prince of Egypt.

You are also a prince or princess of God. **Read Galatians 3:26 to find out why.**

Moses never forgot what Mother Jochebed had taught him, and he never forgot that God was with him. He knew that “it is God who works in you both to will and to do for His good pleasure” (Philippians 2:13). God was working in Moses’ life, and one day Moses would work for Him.
