> Review+ has quality material for your family

We’ve all been there: It’s Friday night, you’ve already had worship, and you just kind of want to watch something—as a family. You’re not about to turn on the TV— not on Sabbath. You glance over at your aging Christian DVD collection for something appropriate, but you know that the theology isn’t _quite_ like ours, and you’re going to have to carefully screen them to make sure that your kids aren’t going to be learning something we don’t believe—_especially_ on Sabbath.

_Adventist Review_ has got you covered. _Review_+ is a free streaming platform designed for everyone in your home. You can watch _Tell the World_, the powerful story of how our movement began. Or _The Evidence of Biological Machines_, for the teen who asks hard questions about faith and science.

Every moment is designed to be in harmony with Adventist principles—you don’t have to worry about what your children might learn. It’s one more way of stepping up our commitment to helping you deepen your faith and equip you for service.

_Review+_ replaces ARTV, our old streaming platform. You can now safely delete the ARTV app from your devices, because this is the upgrade.

It’s free. It’s our gift to you.

You can likely already access it: it’s on Roku, Fire TV, Apple TV, LG, Android . . . and of course, the web. If you have a screen in your home, you have _Review+_. Search _Review+_ in your device’s app store, or watch right now at reviewplus.org. The install is . . . three minutes.

This Friday night, you’ll be ready.
