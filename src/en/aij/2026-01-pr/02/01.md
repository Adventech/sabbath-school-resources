God Created Animals

Flowers opened their petals and drank in the sunshine, trees swayed in the breeze, birds sang a chorus of praise, and fish darted through crystal waters and over colorful coral reefs. The world was beautiful! The scene was set for Day 6 of Creation.

The Bible tells us, “Then God said, ‘Let the earth be filled with animals. And let each produce more of its own kind. Let there be tame animals and small crawling animals and wild animals. And let each produce more of its kind.’ And it happened” (Genesis 1:24, ICB).

Wow! At God’s command horses galloped, dogs barked, and lions roared. Great elephants and tiny field mice nibbled the plants side by side. All of God’s animals looked, behaved, and sounded different, but they lived happily together.

Read Genesis 1:25. What did God mean when He said each animal was made according to “its kind”?

God’s creatures would have babies just like themselves. He made worms to always be worms; they couldn’t turn into fish! He made monkeys to always be monkeys; they couldn’t turn into people! Each kind of animal was cleverly designed to stay just the way God made it.

After creating all the animals, “God saw that it was good” (Genesis 1:25). We too can say, “It is good.”

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Which animal do you like to think about to remind you of God’s creative power?_
- _Is there an animal that you’d like to learn more about?_

Remember to look for clues in animals and nature, because they always tell us something about God.
