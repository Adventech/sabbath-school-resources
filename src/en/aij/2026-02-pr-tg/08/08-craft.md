**What you need:**

Week 8 craft template (on white cardstock), 16 craft sticks, craft glue, scissors, 4 pieces of twine or string, tape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/8a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/craft-template.png)

```

**Instructions:**

- Cut out the template and glue two craft sticks on opposite sides.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/8b.png)

- Glue two more craft sticks to the other sides to make a square frame.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/8b.png)

- Continue to glue on four layers of craft sticks.
- Once dry, thread a piece of twine through the frame at the top and tie a knot.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/8c.png)

- Repeat with the remaining pieces of string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-pr-tg/08/8d.png)

- Thread the end of each string through the frame at the bottom and tape to the back.
