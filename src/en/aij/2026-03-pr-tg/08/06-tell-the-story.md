**Big Idea:** Jesus loves me so much He calls me to follow Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- You may like to use the following props: **flashlight, paper strips** to make a paper chain (any color), **one yellow strip with Jesus’ name written on it, fishing net, paper fish, stapler.**
- Ask children to open their Bibles and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask them.
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A crowd of people lined the shores of the Jordan River. They listened quietly to the powerful words of John the Baptist. As he preached, Jesus slipped into the crowd.

John looked across the sea of faces. Then he stopped. It was Jesus! “Look, the Lamb of God. He takes away the sins of the world!” he cried. “He is the Son of God” (John 1:29, 34, ICB). ^[(Take a yellow strip of paper with Jesus’ name on it and staple it to make a paper chain link as you speak.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The next day John the Baptist saw Jesus again. A smile lit up his face as he shouted, “Look, the Lamb of God!” (John 1:36, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Two fishermen, Andrew and John, heard his amazing words. So they followed Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus heard their footsteps behind Him. He turned and asked them, “What do you seek?” (John 1:38).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did they say to Jesus? Read John 1:38, 39.** (They asked Jesus where He was staying, to which Jesus said, “Come and see.” They saw where He was staying and stayed with Him that day.) ^[(Create two more paper links, staple, and add them to the yellow link.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Imagine the wonderful time those two friends had with Jesus. They knew that Jesus truly was the Messiah and that He loved them very much.

Andrew couldn’t wait to tell his brother the good news! He ran to find Simon. “We have found the Messiah!” he cried (John 1:41). Quickly he brought Simon to Jesus. ^[(Create another link and add to the paper chain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus welcomed Simon right away and gave him a new name to suit the good things in his character. He would now be called “Peter,” which means “stone.”

The next day, walking toward Galilee, Jesus saw a man named Philip and said, “Follow Me” (John 1:43). And Philip did. ^[(Create and add another chain link.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But not before running off to call his friend Nathanael to come and meet the Messiah too. ^[(Create and add another chain link.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Nathanael had heard John the Baptist’s words. But he wasn’t sure if Jesus really was the Messiah. He was under a fig tree praying for God to show him, when Philip rushed up and said, “We have found him. He is Jesus, the son of Joseph. He is from Nazareth” (John 1:45, ICB).

The words seemed like a direct answer to his prayer, and yet . . . Nathanael still had doubts. Jesus didn’t look like Nathanael thought He should. And he didn’t come from an important city—just the small village of Nazareth.

Philip simply said: “Come and see” (John 1:46).

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What made Nathanael sure that Jesus truly was the Messiah? Read John 1:47-51.** (Jesus knew his heart and his action. He knew Nathanael was an honest man, and he knew what Nathanael had been doing before Jesus had seen him.)

^[(Show all your chain links on your paper chain)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus now had five followers, known as disciples: Philip, Nathanael, Andrew, Peter, and John. There would be more disciples ^[(keep adding links and naming the other disciples: James, Thomas, Matthew, James, Thaddaeus, Simon, Judas)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They would all stay with Jesus ^[(cluster the chain together)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

They would all go where He goes ^[(move the chain from side to side)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They would all stay together (cluster close), eat together, and walk together. This is what disciples did. They learned every day of the week from their Rabbi. Jesus was their rabbi. **They wanted to be just like Him.**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Time passed. Early one morning four very disappointed men pulled their boats onto the shore of Lake Galilee. Peter, Andrew, John, and his brother, James, had fished all night and caught nothing—not even one tiny fish. Peter felt so very discouraged.

Jesus came walking along the beach with a huge crowd of followers. Seeing Peter’s boat, Jesus stepped into it and began to teach the people about God’s love and how they could be saved from their sins.

When Jesus had finished teaching, He spoke to Peter. “Take the boat into deep water. If you will put your nets in the water, you will catch some fish” (Luke 5:4, ICB).

Peter looked at Jesus in surprise. “Master, we worked hard all night trying to catch fish, but we caught nothing. But you say to put the nets in the water; so I will” (Luke 5:5, ICB). It made no sense—there people fished only at night—but Peter trusted anyway.

Out in the deep water, Peter threw the nets into the water. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They were instantly full! His eyes bulged in surprise! He and Andrew tried to pull in their net full of wiggling, squiggling fish, but it was way too heavy. It was breaking!

They called out for James and John to row their boat over and help. Together the four fishermen loaded the slippery fish into both boats until they were full. Slowly the disciples rowed to shore.

Peter was amazed—and overwhelmed. Jesus had power to control nature! He bowed before Jesus and said, “Go away from me, Lord. I am a sinful man!” (Luke 5:8, ICB).

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did Jesus say? Read Luke 5:10.** (“Do not be afraid. From now on you will catch men.”)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The men “left everything and followed Jesus” (Luke 5:11, ICB).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Discuss:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What does it mean to be a “fisher of men”—to “catch men”?** (To bring people to Jesus.)
- **Does Jesus call anyone else to be “fishers of men”?** (Yes, we can all be His disciples.)
- **What did Jesus do in our story to show that He would help us follow Him?** (Jesus filled Peter’s net with many fish! Jesus gives us all we need. We can trust Him.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Teaching Point:** Jesus wants us all to bring others to Him so they can be saved too. We can trust God to help us as we follow Him. Just as He provided the miracle catch for the disciples, He will provide for us ^[(show your cluster of connected paper chains again)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Say the **Big Idea** again: **Jesus loves me so much He calls me to follow Him.**
