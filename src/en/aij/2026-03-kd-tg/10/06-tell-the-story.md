**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He gives me all I need.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Give a **basket** containing **five bread rolls** (or pita breads) and **two toy/paper fish** to a child to represent the little boy who shared his lunch.
- Spread out a **picnic blanket** for the children to sit on.
- Give children some bread (or crackers or a **healthy snack** that’s relevant to your culture to represent the loaves and fish; be mindful of allergies).
- Use a basket to pretend to collect the leftovers.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 10 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus’ disciples were tired, really tired ^[(children copy yawn)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It had been a big and busy day, with lots of crowds.

“Come with me,” Jesus said. “We will go to a quiet place to be alone. There we will get some rest” (Mark 6:31, ICB).

The disciples slowly followed Jesus down to a boat on a lake. They pushed it out into the water and climbed in. Ahh, time for some quiet rest. The boat glided through the water toward the other side of the lake.

But back on the shore the people noticed Jesus leaving. They weren’t ready to say goodbye to Jesus yet! They began to run, run run ^[(running action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) around the lake! ^[(Lead the children around the room, then back to their seats.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The people ran so fast that they got to the other side of the lake before Jesus and the disciples did!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus looked at the people with love and care. He could see that they needed Him. He began to heal those who were sick. Then He told a story, and then another story.

Days passed and it was another evening as the sun began to set. What a wonderful day it had been with Jesus! No one wanted the day to end. But the people’s tummies began to rumble. They were very hungry! ^[(Invite children to rub their tummies.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Oh, no. What would they do? They were a long way from home or any town. And they didn’t have any food. In the rush to follow Jesus, they had forgotten to pack a lunch or a dinner!

The disciples told Jesus about the hungry people. They said, “Send the people away. They need to go to the farms and towns around here to buy some food to eat” (Mark 6:36, ICB).

Jesus smiled and shook His head. He had a better idea. “You give them something to eat,” He said (Mark 6:37).

The disciples frowned. They didn’t have any food or any money to buy food for so many people. There were thousands in the crowd!

This problem was way too big for the disciples, but not for Jesus. He asked the disciples, “How many loaves do you have? Go and see” (Mark 6:38).

^[(Ask each child, except the boy with the basket, if they have any bread.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Nobody had any food!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally Andrew stepped forward with a little boy ^[(invite the boy with the basket to come to the front)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). One little boy, in the big, big crowd, had something to share. What was in his lunch basket? ^[(Take out items as you count.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) There were one, two, three, four, five loaves of bread, plus one, two small fish.

Jesus took the little basket in His hands. He smiled thankfully at the boy. The boy’s tummy must have been rumbling too, but he was happy to share with Jesus.

Jesus asked the people to sit down. ^[(Spread picnic blanket and invite the children to sit down on it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then looking up to heaven, Jesus prayed. He thanked God for the basket of food that the boy had shared with Him. Then He divided the bread and fish into pieces and gave them to His disciples to pass to the crowd.

^[(Invite two children to help you pass out some bread.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Some for you, some for you, and some for you too!_

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was then that something amazing happened. The bread and fish kept coming. No matter how much food the disciples handed out, there was always more to share!

Hooray! The disciples laughed, and the crowds smiled. Jesus was feeding thousands of hungry people from just five loaves and two small fish. It was a miracle!

_Mmm, yum!_ The people happily ate until they were full. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then Jesus sent the disciples to collect the leftover pieces of bread and fish that hadn’t been eaten. They collected 12 full baskets of food!

Jesus gave the people what they needed and so much more. He will also do the same for you.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He gives me all I need.**
