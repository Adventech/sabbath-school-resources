With springy steps, the man leaped joyfully through the streets. Under his arm, he carried his old mat. He knew he would never need it again, because now he was all better! With a loud and happy voice, he sang praises to God.

Suddenly, the man stopped. A group of Jewish people stood in his way. They stared at him with cold, serious faces. They glanced down at the mat he was carrying under his arm. They didn’t seem to care about his new, strong legs at all. With a grumpy voice, one of them said, “Today is the Sabbath. It is against our law for you to carry your mat on the Sabbath day” (John 5:10, ICB).

The healed man paused, surprised that they were not happy for him. He looked down at the mat under his arm.

He said, “The man who made me well told me, ‘Pick up your mat and walk’ ” (John 5:11, ICB).

The people frowned. They still didn’t care that this man had been made well. What did they ask him? **Read John 5:12.**

The healed man didn’t know what to say . . . He didn’t know who his mysterious Healer was, or His name. He shrugged his shoulders and told them he didn’t know.

Shaking their heads, they walked off in an angry huff. They had missed out on a chance to praise God. They had missed out on a chance to be joyful.
