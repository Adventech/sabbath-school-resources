While Abel grew up a amor Deus e follow Seu plan, Cain did not. Ele let selfishness grow until there was pequeno room left em seu heart para Deus. Instead, like flourishing weeds, bad things took root e grew there. Thistles em the field annoyed ele, seu younger brother irritated ele, seu parents’ choice em Eden made ele angry. Ele was angry even com Deus. Then one dia ele stubbornly disobeyed por not bringing a lamb as an offering. O que did Cain bring a Deus as an offering instead? **Leia Gênesis 4:3.**

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/06/image5.png)

Cain saw Deus’s instructions através selfish eyes, e ele thought ele could Faça things seu own way. Depois todos, wasn’t seu fruit de seu garden that ele had grown bom enough para Deus?

Isso wasn’t o que Deus had Pergunteed para, então fire didn’t fall de Céu. Quando fire burned up the lamb Abel had offered, a knot de anger tightened em Cain’s heart.

Abel saw the anger e tried a calmly encourage seu brother a choose a Faça o que was right. Abel reminded Cain de como Deus had forgiven seu parents, como Ele had promised a save, e como the sacrifices pointed a Jesus. Abel really hoped Cain would listen. Mas Cain did not.

Deus saw the anger e spoke pleading words de encouragement. “Se você Faça well, will você not be accepted?” Deus said (Gênesis 4:7). Mas Cain wasn’t sorry, e ele didn’t listen.

- _How well Faça você listen a those quem amor você? Quando você listen e obey como Faça você feel?_
- _Has anger ever stopped você de obeying? Se então como Faça você feel?_

^[FOR YOU, PARENTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Sometimes we may speak in anger to our children. Then comes guilt and regret. But take heart; just as God sought to encourage Cain, He knows your situation and He encourages you. His promise in Isaiah 49:16 is yours to claim today. And don’t hesitate to ask your child for forgiveness when you have not dealt with them gently!
