The smoke from the altar rose into the big sky. God was happy to see Noah’s thank-You sacrifice. God spoke and said, “I make this agreement with you: I will never again destroy all living things by floodwaters. A flood will never again destroy the earth” (Genesis 9:11, ICB).

Suddenly, as Noah and his family looked up into the sky, a bright, colorful gift from God appeared. What was it? Read Genesis 9:13.

Noah and his family looked up to see an arch of colored light in the sky. They could see red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet. It was the first rainbow! What a beautiful sight!

God told them that every time they saw a rainbow they could remember that God loves and saves His children. It would also remind them that God would never flood the whole earth ever again.

God is still keeping His promise today. And He always will.
