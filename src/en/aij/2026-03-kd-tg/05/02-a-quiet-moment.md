**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Nathan came home from school with a little pot of soil. It contained something that looked like a tiny blade of grass. “This is for you, Mommy!” he said. “It is called an amaryllis!” Nathan’s mom looked after that little plant for many years. It grew long, glossy leaves and a strong stem. Every day the stem grew taller, and buds appeared. One morning Mom discovered huge pink and white flowers, like angels’ trumpets, on top of the stem. Her dedication had been rewarded with a gorgeous flower.

Growing takes time. Jesus was born as a tiny baby. There was nothing to indicate that He was the Son of God. He had to start at the very beginning and learn to walk and talk and feed Himself, just like other children.

Jesus was always looking for ways to grow and learn more about God, about His role as the Messiah, and about understanding and caring for people. He was Immanuel, God with us, helping us to understand what God’s love is really like. “And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).

God invites us to grow by learning more about Him, and more about His love and grace. He wants our hearts to be filled with His love, so we’ll naturally share it with others. As we grow to love God and others, God does something in us so that we also increase “in wisdom and stature and in favor with God and men.”

“It is the Lord’s desire that His followers shall grow in grace, that their love shall abound more and more, that they shall be filled with the fruits of righteousness, which are by Jesus Christ, unto the praise and glory of God” (Ellen G. White, in Signs of the Times, June 12, 1901).
