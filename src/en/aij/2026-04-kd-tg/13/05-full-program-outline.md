**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **sealed box** (with a picture of Jesus inside) and a **flashlight**.

- **What’s a flashlight used for? When have you used one?**
- **What do you think is inside this sealed box?**

Make a small hole in the top of the box with some scissors so that you can see inside. Allow a few children to look inside.

- **What do you see?** (Nothing; it’s too dark.)
- **What could we use to help us see?** (The flashlight.)

Turn on the flashlight and try shining it into the box.

- **What can you see? Does the flashlight help? I wonder what the problem might be?** (It needs batteries.)

Put the batteries into the flashlight and try again.

- **What can you see?** (A picture of Jesus.) **Why did we need to use batteries?** (Because without them, there’s no power in the flashlight.)

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **covered-up printout of our Resurrection picture from the Program PowerPoint**. Keep it covered while you discuss this first question. Invite each child to put their hand up, then stand up if you choose them to answer, then sit back down.

- **What was your favorite part of today’s Bible story? Why?** Affirm and discuss their answers.

Then uncover the picture and give them time to look at it closely.

- **What’s happening in this picture?**
- **Who can you see in this picture?** Point out some of the individuals: Jesus, angels, children. Explain that there are people from many different countries who lived at different times in history. Look at their expressions. Talk about the children, and if any look like you.
- **What is your favorite part of this picture?** Affirm and discuss their answers.

**Teaching Point:** One day Jesus will come again and wake up all the people who love Him, just as He woke up Lazarus. Then we will all get to live together with Him, and no one will ever die. What an amazing day that will be!

Invite all the children to jump to their feet, keeping their eyes on Jesus in the picture, invite them to say together, “Come soon, Jesus!” if they would like to.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 52: Silverback Gorilla** and share the following facts:

- Silverback gorillas live high in the mountains of Africa in family groups.
- A male silverback gorilla has lots of big muscles and is the big leader in his family.
- They have long arms, thick black fur, and a shiny black nose.
- Gorillas have very powerful jaws that eat through bamboo, bark, and roots.

God created the silverback gorilla to be super strong and powerful in his family because He can do anything!

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 52: Silverback Gorilla** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Miracle at Sea.”**

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) “Wake Up, Lazarus!”** Make a craft of Lazarus to remind the children of Jesus’ love and power. As the children are working on the craft, discuss:

- **What do you think Lazarus’ first thought was when Jesus called him out of the tomb?**

Explain how Lazarus must have wondered what was happening when he “woke up” from being dead! He must have felt very thankful to be with his sisters and his loving Friend, Jesus, again. Invite the children to hold up their Lazarus craft as you say the **Big Idea** a final time: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He is all-powerful** [flex muscles]**.”**

**^[REVIEW and SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Sing **“What Can Jesus Do?”** Show the **Program PowerPoint Summary**, and for each picture, invite the children to finish your sentence: **“Jesus can . . .”** (calm a storm; heal the blind man, etc.).
