**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He cares for me.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“. . . casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (1 Peter 5:7)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Care”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Care means to be really interested in something or someone. Care also means to look after. God cares about you. He thinks you’re very important, and He’s interested in everything you say, think, and do. He also cares for you—He looks after you with great love. He protects, comforts, and helps you every day.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Genesis 35:7-21; 37:1-11; and _Patriarchs and Prophets_, pp. 204, 209, 210.

A Promise Filled

Jacob grinned as he sat under the shade of the big old tree in his new home in Shechem. He loved watching his grandchildren play. Their giggles and chatter made him so happy. Some sat in the grass playing games with sticks and stones. Others whispered as they hid behind tents waiting for their cousins to find them. His family had grown over the years. God had definitely kept His promise to give him a big, big family!

What else had God promised him long ago, when Jacob had run away from his family home? **Read Genesis 28:15.**

God had done what He promised! Jacob was back in beautiful Canaan. God had cared for him all along the way. When he was scared of Esau, God was with him. When he was angry at unfair Uncle Laban, God helped him do his best. And when he was worried about meeting Esau again, God gave him peace.

God promises to look after you too. If you’re ever worried or afraid, you can tell Jesus. He will take those worries, or cares, from you. The Bible says, “. . . casting all your care [or worries] upon Him, for He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).

Jacob was so thankful, he built an altar next to his tent, just like Abraham did. Here at the altar, in both the morning and the evening, Jacob called his family together to worship God. He had been so faithful. Jacob knew he—and his big, big family—could always trust Him.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What stories might Jacob have told his grandchildren about God’s care for him?
- What story would you have liked to hear the most?
