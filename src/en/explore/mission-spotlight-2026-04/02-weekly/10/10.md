Jesus gave the call to go  
to every tribe and land,  
to spread His love around the globe  
with our own hands.

We make this sacred vow  
to serve in every place  
till all nations know  
the power of His grace.

So with one voice  
we proclaim redemption story  
and with one voice  
we commit to tell the world.

Let us all rejoice  
in the Father’s love and glory  
as we reach all nations  
with one voice.

Every way we can  
we’ll share the story of His grace,  
heart to heart,  
we will show His loving face.

Every language known,  
every tribe and every tongue,  
we will declare Your name  
until His kingdom comes.

So with one voice

and with one voice  
we commit to tell the world.

From east to west  
and north to south we’ll go,  
every heart will meet the One
