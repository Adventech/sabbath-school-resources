**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The Israelites woke up to a miracle every morning. Along with the dew, there it was—manna. God sent His people all that they needed to thrive, and they gathered it one day at a time. Not too much, or too little—just enough for today. And so, for 40 years, they would practice trust in the One who provides . . . again and again.

Centuries later Jesus Himself would tell people not to worry about what they would eat or drink, but to trust in their heavenly provider. “For your heavenly Father knows that you need all these things” (Matthew 6:32).

Lillies don’t fret. Sparrows don’t store things for tomorrow. But all of nature is provided for. God will also provide for you, your family, and the families in your Sabbath School. He knows the many and varied needs we have, whether it be financial needs, healing, our daily bread, or parenting with wisdom and patience. We can live with contentment, rather than worry or want, trusting that our “heavenly Father knows” and will provide.

“Our heavenly Father has a thousand ways to provide for us of which we know nothing. Those who accept the one principle of making the service of God supreme will find perplexities vanish and a plain path before their feet” (Ellen G. White, _The Ministry of Healing_, p. 481). What a beautiful reminder to put God and His ways first, knowing that He will take care of the rest. For His mercies “are new every morning” (Lamentations 3:23). That’s a promise worth saying at the start of each new day.

This week may your trust grow in the One who will always provide. And may the children in your Sabbath School be assured that they can always follow Him, knowing He will take care of their needs—always.
