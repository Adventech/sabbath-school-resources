**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He welcomes everyone into His family.

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Welcome”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To welcome is to warmly greet someone. It also means to make someone feel like they belong. You can welcome someone by smiling and saying a big hello or inviting them to play or sit with you. When you are friendly and kind to someone, they will feel welcome.

The Journey Home

The donkey gave a snort and a snuffle and swished his tail as he plodded down the dusty road. Naomi patted his head softly as she walked beside him. She was going home—home to Bethlehem.

Naomi looked over at Ruth, who was leading the donkey. She thought about Ruth’s words. “Every place you go, I will go,” Ruth had promised (Ruth 1:16, ICB).

Each day as they traveled under the warm sun, Ruth was there. Every night as they camped under the stars, Ruth was beside her. Naomi’s eyes sparkled with tears. She had lost her husband and her two sons, but Ruth had promised to stick with her like superglue!

Naomi smiled as Ruth also gave their tired donkey a little pat. She knew Ruth wasn’t just following her. Ruth had chosen to follow God too. She had promised: “Your God will be my God” (Ruth 1:16, ICB).

When we choose to love God, just like Ruth, He does something amazing—He welcomes us into His very own family. The Bible says, “Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called children of God!” (1 John 3:1).

God is the very best Father. He looked after His two daughters, Ruth and Naomi, as they walked with little donkey. He was with them when they were hot and tired during the day. He was with them when it was dark and lonely at night. Would He ever leave them? **Read Deuteronomy 31:8.** Isn’t it wonderful to be part of God’s forever family?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Ruth made a choice to stick close to God and follow Him forever. Would you like to make that choice too?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that we can be part of His forever family.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask someone if you can try to stick to them like superglue for a little while. Everywhere they go, you go; everything they do, you do. Was it hard or easy? Thank God that He is always with you.
