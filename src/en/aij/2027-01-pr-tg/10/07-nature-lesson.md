**Focus:** God’s Ten Commandments keep us safe like the safety web a jumping spider spins.

- **Have you ever seen a jumping spider?**
- **Let’s find out what a jumping spider can teach us about God’s Ten Commandments.** (Show picture.)

You’re crouching behind a bush while playing hide-and-seek. Out of the corner of your eye you notice movement on a leaf. You watch as a colorful spider leaps and grasps a fly in its front legs, then eats it.

There are at least 300 species of jumping spiders in North America. They have thick bodies, short hairy legs, and two large eyes at the front of their head. Those eyes give them the best eyesight of any creature their size.

Jumping spiders can leap forward, backward, or sideways, and up to 40 times their body length! When hunting, they move quietly until they spy a small insect. Then, raising their front legs, they leap at the insect, spinning a safety web just in case they miss their target. That safety web keeps them from falling.

God has given humans a safety web with 10 strands. It outlines what we should do to live in harmony with God and other people. It’s the Ten Commandments.

Just as the spider can leap freely when connected to its safety web, we can live freely as long as we follow God’s laws. Your safety web doesn’t limit you, but gives you the freedom to grow according to God’s plan for you.

In his song to God, David said, “Oh, let me not wander from your commandments!” (Psalm 119:10). He knew that following God’s 10 laws of love would be a safety net for him.

**Discuss:**

- **What can we learn from the jumping spider about following God’s commandments?** (Talk with a person sitting next to you.)
