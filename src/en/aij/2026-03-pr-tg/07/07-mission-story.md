**Focus:** Jesus can help me be strong and not cheat when tempted

**Location:** Help a child locate **Côte d’Ivoire (Ivory Coast)** on your **globe** or **world map**.

It all began one morning at breakfast. As Eli-Ann ate her boiled rice with a little sugar on it, Mom said, “Your exams start today, don’t they?”

“Yes,” Eli-Ann replied. “I’ve studied hard, but I know that the tests will be hard. The teacher told us that people from the government will be there to make sure nobody cheats.”

“Do your best,” Mom replied. “Your uncle and I will stay near the school and pray that Jesus will help you to remember all the things you have studied.”

“Thank you,” Eli-Ann said. “It will make me happy to know you are near. When we get our break between exams, I’ll be able to run out and tell you how I’m doing.”

Eli-Ann’s mother and uncle walked to school with her. When they arrived at the school, Eli-Ann waved goodbye. Jesus, help me to do my very best for You today, she prayed silently.

In class the children began their exams. The government inspectors walked around the room making sure the students didn’t have any notes with them.

One of the inspectors stopped by Eli-Ann’s desk. “You’ve spelled that word wrong,” he whispered to her. Then he began telling her how to spell it correctly.

Eli-Ann looked up at the inspector in surprise. “I won’t tell anyone,” he said.

“No, thank you, sir,” Eli-Ann replied. “I don’t want to cheat. By God’s grace I would rather fail this exam than cheat!” The examiner frowned and walked on. _I hope he doesn’t get mad and fail me_, Eli-Ann thought. _But I know that I did what is right!_

When that exam ended, the teacher let the children go outside for a short break. Eli-Ann ran to where her mother and uncle sat under a large shade tree. “I’m sure glad that one is finished!” Eli-Ann exclaimed.

“Was it really hard?” Mom asked.

“Yes, it was,” Eli-Ann admitted. “I misspelled a hard word, and one of the inspectors told me how to spell it correctly. I told him that I’d rather fail than cheat. I don’t think he was very happy with me.”

“You did the right thing,” her uncle said.

“Don’t worry, sweetheart,” her mother assured her. “God will bless you for being honest.”

“I sure hope you’re right,” Eli-Ann said as she turned to go back inside for the next exam.

A few days later the results arrived, and Eli-Ann had passed. She squealed and clapped her hands. She had not only passed but had earned high marks! God had honored her for her faithfulness!

“I want to thank Jesus for helping me do well,” Eli-Ann said. So Eli-Ann and her mom knelt down and prayed.

“Dear Jesus,” Mom said, “thank You for helping Eli-Ann do so well. Thank You for helping her do what was right when the inspector tempted her to cheat.” Eli-Ann thanked God too for His help during the tests. Then Mom hugged Eli-Ann and whispered, “I’m so proud of you, and I know Jesus is too.”

**How did Eli-Ann rely on God’s strength when tempted to cheat on the exam?**
