**Big Idea:**\
God’s way is best. He provides for all of my needs.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ (Psalm 145:16).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Provide”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ - To provide is to give something that is needed. It is God who provides the food you eat and the water you drink. God also provides love and forgiveness.

Grumbling Mouths and Rumbling Tummies

The grumbling started in their minds, but then the people started to say their thoughts aloud. Little girls and boys whined. Mothers and fathers complained. Soon all the Israelite people “complained against Moses and Aaron in the wilderness” (Exodus 16:2). They were hungry!

They were so hungry that they even started to wish they’d never left Egypt. They said to Moses, “There we had meat to eat. We had all the food we wanted. But you have brought us into this desert. You will starve us to death here” (Exodus 16:3, ICB).

They were so forgetful. They had forgotten how badly they had been treated as slaves. They also chose not to remember God’s miracles and how He’d cared for them all this time. They had even forgotten that it was God who had led them out of Egypt.

But God would still provide for them. He knew they were hungry, and He loved them. He said, “I will rain bread from heaven for you” (Exodus 16:4).

Heavenly bread? Wow! Moses and Aaron rushed to tell the Israelites about God’s miracle. Now they would know it was God who was their leader and provider. They said, “Every morning He will give you all the bread you want” (Exodus 16:8, ICB).

Suddenly the people saw something amazing out toward the desert. What was it? **Read Exodus 16:10.**

Their eyes grew wide as they watched the bright, beautiful cloud. God was with them, and He was their true leader. “You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing” (Psalm 145:16).

- Who did God choose to lead the Israelites? Who was really leading them all the way?
