The warm sun set over the earth, announcing Day 5. What would our creative God do next? The answer came as His voice rang out across the heavens. “Let the waters abound with an abundance of living creatures” (Genesis 1:20).

Suddenly the sea was alive with many creatures, all unique and special. Fish flapped, dolphins dove, and giant whales swam through the deep waters. Crabs scuttled in the rock pools and stingrays glided in the shallows.

Streams and lakes teemed with life. Goldfish cut a bright-orange path across the ponds, salmon shot up rivers, and lakes filled with minnows.

But God wasn’t done with Day 5 yet. What else did God create? **Read Genesis 1:20** (second half).

God’s voice filled the sky with birds. Soaring eagles, swooping swallows, and dabbling ducks appeared. Some soared in the air currents high above the earth, while others flitted from branch to branch in the trees. The air was alive with clucking and screeching, chirping and warbling, as winged creatures praised their Creator, God.

From big to small, pretty to plain, and silent to squawking, God designed and created them all. He was pleased with His work;
He liked what He saw. The Bible says, “And God saw that it was good” (Genesis 1:21). He knew how much we would like it too.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What kind of bird or fish would you like to have as a pet?_
- _Why might God have created so many different types of bird and fish species? What clue does this give you about His attributes?_

Scientists believe there are about 11,000<sup>1</sup> different bird species, and more than 243,000<sup>2</sup> known species in the ocean. God created them on Day 5 to fill our earth with variety so that we could know and see how amazing our Creator God is.
