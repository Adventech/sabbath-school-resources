Football, swimming, tennis, gymnastics, skiing—there are so many different types of sports that are played all around the world. Do you have a favorite?

There’s even one sport that has been played on the moon! Astronaut Alan Shepard played golf way up on the moon. After landing the spacecraft, he went outside in his spacesuit and swung and hit two golf balls into the air.

It doesn’t matter which sport you watch or play—there are always rules. Even such games as tag have rules. Rules are really important for a fun, fair game. Without them the game could become very unfair, very dangerous, and very confusing—very quickly!

God too knew that rules were important. He gave us the Ten Commandments to help us see what is right and wrong. The Bible says, “The truly happy person is the one who carefully studies God’s perfect law that makes people free. . . . He listens to God’s teaching and does not forget what he heard. Then he obeys what God’s teaching says” (James 1:25, ICB).

God’s first four commandments all help us to love God and put Him first. “You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength” (Deuteronomy 6:5). When we do, He fills us with love. What else does the Bible say comes from following His ways? **Read Psalm 16:11.**
