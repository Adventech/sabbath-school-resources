All was dark as Jacob finally lay down after his busy day of giving gifts. He had moved his family to a new camp across the river. Then he’d crossed back to their old camp by himself. Closing his eyes, he began to pray.

Suddenly strong arms grabbed Jacob. Jacob grabbed them back. He twisted and turned, pushed and pulled. Who was this enemy? he wondered. It was so dark that he couldn’t see His face.

As Jacob fought, he remembered all of his mistakes. Then he remembered God’s promises. With new strength Jacob kept fighting. The unknown stranger would not give up, but neither would Jacob. On and on they wrestled.

How long did they wrestle? **Read Genesis 32:24.**

Jacob was exhausted, he had been wrestling for so long! But he held on tight. Then suddenly, the One Jacob had been wrestling with reached out and touched Jacob’s hip. Instantly Jacob fell down to the ground in a heap—his hip was badly hurt. That was no ordinary touch! Jacob instantly realized he had been wrestling with God.

Jacob held onto God even tighter and cried, “I will not let You go unless You bless me!” (Genesis 32:26). He knew he needed God’s forgiveness, strength, and peace.

God heard him and “He blessed him there” (Genesis 32:29). God also gave him a brand-new name. Jacob’s name had meant tricker. But God changed his name to Israel which means blessed prince. He would be a special leader, just as God had promised.
