{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: Stars, sun, toy or real fruit, ball to roll, bandaids, small blanket, balloons, play-dough, red love hearts, picture of Jesus (www.aliveinjesus.info)

Say: **In all our Bible stories, we can see just how much Jesus loves you, and how kind Jesus is. His love is bigger than the sky** (have children reach up high)**, and wider than the ocean wide** (reach arms across).

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you are sleeping** (dangle some stars on a string over the children inviting them to reach up).

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you are playing** (take turns rolling a ball to each child in a circle, and/or use blocks to build together).

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you are hungry** (give real or plastic fruit to the children and invite them to place them in a basket or on plates).

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you are sad** (use play-dough and make a sad face, then make a happy face).

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you feel afraid.** (Hide underneath a blanket, and then say “peek-a-boo!” Repeat a few times.)

Say: **Jesus cares for you when you are sick** (place a band aid on each child’s clothing or hand. Invite parents/caregivers to hug their child).

Say: **Jesus is your very special friend, and He loves you!** (Invite children to look around the room to each find a small heart with a picture of Jesus on the back. They can take this home to remind them of how much Jesus cares for them.)

Open you Bible, point, and say: **The Bible says, “He cares for you”** (1 Peter 5:7, ICB). Point to each child as you repeat the quarterly memory verse, **“Jesus cares for you** (child’s name)**.” He is our very best Friend.**

Give each child a balloon, each with a smiling face drawn on one side. Say together: **Jesus cares for me!**
