Smiling and laughing, the servants carried their jars of miracle grape juice around to the thirsty guests. Jesus had provided delicious, unfermented wine to fill every single empty cup.

The guests sipped and smiled and slurped some more. What sweet, delicious juice! Where had this come from? they wondered. They had never tasted anything like it!

The guests called for the servants and began to ask questions about the grape juice. The servants couldn’t hold in their excitement. They told everyone the amazing story of Jesus and His miracle.

Wow! Everyone began to talk about Jesus. His disciple friends began to talk about Him too. This was Jesus’ first miracle. The Bible says, “He showed his glory, and his followers believed in him” (John 2:11, ICB). They were so glad they had left everything to follow Him.

Finally, after many days, the wedding party came to an end, and people returned home. But their excitement went with them. Wherever the guests went, they told their friends the story of Jesus and the miracle juice. Their friends told their families, and they told their friends. Soon it seemed as if everyone was talking about Jesus.

Even the priests and leaders became curious when they heard about this humble yet powerful Teacher.

What things does the Bible tell us to talk about? **Read Psalm 145:4-6.**
