**What you need:**

Week 6 craft template, two shades of blue paper, paper cup, craft sticks, scissors, glue.

**Preparation instructions:**

Cut wavy strips of paper out of different shades of blue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6b.png)

**Create it together:**

1\. Glue a strip of wavy blue paper around the top of a paper cup.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6c.png)

2\. Continue gluing wavy strips of paper onto the cup, overlapping them until the whole cup is covered.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6d.png)

3\. Cut out the picture of Jesus and glue to a craft stick. Adults can use scissors to poke a hole through the bottom of the cup for the craft stick to slide through.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6e.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6f.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/06/6g.png)

4\. (Optional: Cut out the dove and glue to a second craft stick. Glue craft stick to back of cup. Retell the story while you move Jesus below and above the water.
