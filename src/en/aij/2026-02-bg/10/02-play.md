Learn

During bathtime, play with objects that float and sink. Have your child attempt to stand on top of the water. Ask, “Can you do it?” Share how Jesus can do amazing and impossible things because He is God. We call them miracles. We can trust Him with our problems. He can make miracles happen.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Retell

Paint a stripe of water on the pavement outside, or on a long strip of paper from a paper roll. Stand at one end of the stripe while your child stands at the other. Call them to walk on the water to you, just as Peter did. Then, with you beside them, ask them to walk with their eyes closed, as when Peter forgot to keep looking at Jesus. Be ready to hold their hand when they need you.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explain

Being brave doesn’t mean you aren’t ever afraid. It means you trust God when things are scary. It can also take bravery for a young child to try new things. Remind them that Jesus helps them to be brave.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Sing

“Jesus Takes Care of Me” (Janet Sage, in Little Sabbath Songs for Cradle Roll, no. 33).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Create

Draw and cut out a large circle as a bravery badge. Have your child color their bravery badge. Whenever you see them being brave, give them a sticker to place on their badge. Say, “Thank You, Jesus, for helping (child’s name) be brave today.”

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

Jesus, I know You are always with me.\
When things are so scary\
Or I try something new,\
I remember the ways You care for me.\
Help me be brave with You.\
Amen.
