**Word of the Week**

**Respect:** Respect is treating others the way you would like to be treated. When you show respect for others, you care about their feelings and thoughts.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This week we continue to explore the Ten Commandments by looking at the last six commandments. After learning how we can show
our love to God, these next commandments show how we can respond to others, because they are precious to God.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** what the commandments are.
- **Feel** a desire to obey the commandments.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to help them love others.
