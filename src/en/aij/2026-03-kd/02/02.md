Joseph looked across at Mary with a smile. Her tummy was big and round. Mary was pregnant with precious Baby Jesus. And He could be born any day now!

Because Mary was about to have her baby, it wouldn’t be easy to travel. But they had to go to Bethlehem. They had been ordered to go to their hometown for a “people count,” so they needed to go. After packing their bags, Joseph gently lifted Mary onto the donkey’s back. With a clip-clop, they were on their way.

Where were they going? **Read Luke 2:4.**

Mary and Joseph traveled for many days along hot, dusty paths, and busy roads.

Mary smiled and held her tummy tenderly as she swayed left and right on the donkey’s back. They didn’t need to worry on this long trip. God was with them. His angels were busy keeping them safe. One day very soon they would be able to say, “For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given” (Isaiah 9:6). The greatest gift ever given to the world would soon be safely cradled in their arms.
