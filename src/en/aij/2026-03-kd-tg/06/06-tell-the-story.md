**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use the following storytelling props: a **baby doll** to represent baby John; **coat or robe**; **fruit**; and **heart shape**. (Write on **whiteboard** and make messy, then erase. Or cover a **heart shape in mud** to show a sinful heart and wash in a **bowl of water** to show a clean heart.) Also use a **flashlight**; **dove picture** on a **stick**; and this week’s **craft** of Jesus being baptized.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 6 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Old Zacharias held his son and smiled ^[(hold baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had given him and old Elizabeth a miracle baby.
- Because Zacharias doubted, he hadn’t been able to speak since the angel had promised him a son. He wrote, “His name is John” (Luke 1:63) and could speak again. Another miracle!
- Their neighbors were amazed. What would baby John grow up to do?
- Zacharias knew! The angel had told him. John would get the people ready to meet Jesus!
- John became a preacher called John the Baptist. He lived in the desert, wore camel’s hair clothing, and ate locusts and wild honey.
- The people needed a change. Not of clothes or food ^[(hold up coat, fruit, shake head)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They needed God to change their hearts ^[(dirty heart)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- John told people to repent—to be sorry.
- John baptized people to show them that they were washed clean of their sins, and they could have a fresh new start ^[(clean heart)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Jesus came to the river Jordan. He asked John to baptize Him, even though He had a clean heart. He wanted to be our example.
- After He was baptized, God sent a light from heaven to shine on Jesus ^[(flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The Holy Spirit came down looking like a dove ^[(dove on a stick)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God told Jesus, “You are My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11).
- God is pleased when we say sorry for our sins. He loves to forgive us and to give us a clean heart ^[(heart)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He also wants us to turn away from our sin.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Zacharias held his son and smiled ^[(hold baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had done the impossible. He had given this old priest and his old wife, Elizabeth, a baby!

He asked one of his friends to quickly pass him something to write on. He hadn’t been able to talk since the angel had visited him. The angel had promised this miracle baby, but Zacharias had doubted him. And Zacharias had been silent ever since!

^[(Pretend to write.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “His name is John,” Zacharias wrote (Luke 1:63).

John. Baby John. Just as the angel had instructed. Suddenly Zacharias could speak again! It was another miracle!

Their neighbors were amazed. They told their friends about baby John. Soon all the people in the towns around them were talking about the miracle. “What kind of child will this be?” they wondered (Luke 1:66).

Zacharias knew! The angel had told him. John would be God’s messenger. He would get people ready to meet Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Baby John grew up to become a preacher, called John the Baptist. He lived in the desert near the river Jordan.

The people all rushed to the desert to hear him speak. What a surprise they got! He didn’t look or act like anyone they had ever seen before. He didn’t wear a fancy robe. He wore a coat made of camel hair. He didn’t eat fancy food. He ate locusts and wild honey.

John was different from other preachers, and his message was different too. God wanted it that way. It was time for a big change!

The people didn’t need to change their clothes ^[(hold up robe, shake head)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They didn’t need to change their food ^[(hold up fruit, shake head)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). John said, “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2). ^[(Hold up heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Priests, tax collectors, fishermen, farmers, and families all hurried to the river Jordan. They listened carefully. John the Baptist’s voice boomed loud and strong. “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2).

Repent? The children in the crowd didn’t know what that word meant. Their mothers told them quietly that it meant to be sorry for their sins. We have all done wrong. God wants us to be sorry for our sins and tell Him so. His love and forgiveness are for everyone!

As John spoke, the Holy Spirit began to do something deep inside the people’s hearts. They realized how sinful they had been. Right there, in the desert, they said they were sorry, and asked God to forgive them. They wanted God to make them clean and new, and they didn’t want to go back to their old sinful ways.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) John baptized them in the river. He lay them back in the water until they went right underneath the water and then brought them back up again. This was to show everyone that God had cleansed their hearts and made them new ^[(clean heart)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

One day Jesus came to the Jordan River. He passed through the crowds and went straight toward John the Baptist.

Jesus asked John to baptize him. Jesus didn’t need to be sorry or to ask for forgiveness. Jesus’ heart was already completely clean, because He never did anything wrong. But Jesus wanted to be our example. He wanted to teach us about God’s forgiveness and power to make our hearts clean. He wanted to show us what we can do when we have sinned and are sorry.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) So John baptized Jesus, right there in the Jordan River ^[(show this week’s craft)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). And then something amazing happened. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

A very bright light poured down from heaven, shining onto Jesus ^[(shine flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then the Holy Spirit came down and rested on Jesus. It looked like a dove ^[(hold up the dove on a stick)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then God spoke, “You are My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11).

God is also pleased with you and me when we are sorry and ask for His forgiveness. He also wants us to turn away from our sins. He loves you and promises to give you a clean heart.
