The Bible tells us all about the day when Jesus will come back to this earth. This is God’s story for you. _(Insert your child’s name on the blank lines and choose the appropriate pronoun.)_

________stands staring up into the sky with his/her family. His/Her eyes watch a small dark cloud in the distance (Psalm 18:9).

There is something different about this cloud . . . it glows with a dazzling light and seems to be getting closer and closer, and brighter and brighter (2 Thessalonians 2:8). A rainbow glitters above the cloud like a crown (Revelation 4:3).

“Could it be?” ________ whispers with excitement, “Is that Jesus coming?”

A distant angel choir begins to sing, “Gloria!”

“Look! I see Jesus and the angels,” points ____________ (Matthew 25:31). In the middle of the cloud of angels stands kind King Jesus wearing a shining, golden crown (Revelation 19:12).

Trumpets echo across the sky (1 Thessalonians 4:16). Toot-toot. The earth shakes (Psalm 18:7) and the beautiful, booming voice of Jesus speaks from the sky, “Awake, you who sleep, arise from the dead” (Ephesians 5:14).

The family’s eyes open wide as graves crack open. People wearing shining white robes begin to step up and out of the ground (1 Thessalonians 4:16). They look toward Jesus with smiles and arms stretched upward.

“Look!” shouts ___________. “They’re flying.” The family watches the people raised from the dead become as light as a feather and float up to the cloud where Jesus is waiting (1 Thessalonians 4:16). His smile shines brighter than the sun.

In an instant, _________ notices she/he has changed. His/her whole family has changed too! They suddenly look fresh and healthy. Their clothes have changed to glowing robes of light (1 Corinthians 15:51, 53).

Looking down, _________ squeals, “I’m flying!”

Together, the family floats higher and higher toward Jesus, the cloud of angels, and other believers (1 Thessalonians 4:17). Together, they are led to heaven, by their King, Savior, and Friend, Jesus. There is no more sin, sadness, or death. They will live with Jesus in perfect love, forever! What a day that will be!
