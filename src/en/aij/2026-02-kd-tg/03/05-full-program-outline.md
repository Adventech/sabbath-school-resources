**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child as they arrive. Ask: **What was the best part of your week?**

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite children to participate in a **game**. Explain that you will call out three different words, and they are to do the action for each word. Words and actions include:

1. Ladder: taking BIG steps on the spot and using arms to climb.
2. Rock: children will crouch down into a ball (to look like a small rock).
3. Dream: children will lie down with eyes closed.

Try it out in order, doing the actions for the children to copy. Then stop doing the action and only use words, then mix up the order, then get faster and faster to see who can keep up!

Share that these three things—a ladder, a rock, and a dream—will be in today’s special Bible story.

**^[SING and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#E6B349"}}})** Invite a child to hold the special class Bible. Sing **“God’s Word.”** What does the Bible teach us about today’s Big Idea?

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/assets/gods-words.mp3] “God’s Words” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Amy Cherry"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Glockenspiel","value":"David Williams"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2024 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **Put your hand up if you sometimes make mistakes and do the wrong thing.** (We all do!)
- **What should we do when we make mistakes?** (Say “I’m sorry,” ask for forgiveness, and do our best not to do the same thing again.)
- **What is forgiveness?** (God forgives us when we humbly say we’re sorry. It means He wipes away the wrong things we’ve done and doesn’t remember them anymore. When Jesus died on the cross, He made a way for us to be forgiven.)

Share that God loves us so much. He doesn’t want sin to separate us and Him. What does the Bible say God does when we say we’re sorry to Him? Show the children how to find the **memory verse, 1 John 1:9. Say it together:** “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins” (1 John 1:9). Explain that “confess our sins” means to say we’re sorry for our sins. It’s when our hearts are soft and when we say we’re sorry to God and the people we hurt that God forgives us and makes everything right again.

Listen to, and sing, the **1 John 1:9 Scripture Song**. Share that God is our very best friend who forgives us when we are sorry.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal pieces of **chalk** or **whiteboard markers**.

Ask the children to take turns using these supplies to make the messiest drawing they can on a wipeable surface. (You may use a chalk/whiteboard or pavement.)

Explain that sin makes a big, terrible mess. Not only does it make a mess, but it badly hurts everyone around us, and God. But God doesn’t stop loving us when He looks at our ugly mess. As our Friend, He does something that no one else can do. Using a **cloth**, wipe away the scribbles. When we say we are sorry and ask God to forgive us, He does. He wipes away our sin as if it never happened.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s Bible story is about a man who made some terrible mistakes. Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Jacob Finds His Savior”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your bell or play your sound maker, then invite a child to open the Story Explorer Box, revealing a **small, wrapped box with a bow.** (Don’t open yet.)

- **What’s this?** (A special gift!)

Put the gift to one side for now. Invite the children to stand up as you remember the actions of the game you played before Sabbath School. Do a quick recap: **rock, ladder, dream**.

Ask the children to show you the **dream action** again, then sit down. Share that Jacob felt very sad, sorry, and alone. He said he was sorry to God and asked God to forgive him.

- **Did God forgive him?** (Yes!) **Why?** (He will always forgive us when we say we are sorry. He is our loving friend.)
- **How did God encourage Jacob?** (He sent him a dream with a special message.)

Ask the children to stand up and show the ladder action again, then sit down.

- **What was very special about this ladder?** (It connected earth and heaven, with angels flying up and down. It shows us what Jesus does for us when He saves us and connects us with God.)

God loved Jacob, and He loves you. He had forgiven Jacob, and He will also forgive us when we say we’re sorry.

Invite a child to open the gift, then to pass out the hearts inside. Share that God’s forgiveness is a wonderful gift of love.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.**

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Forgiveness Rock**. Invite the children to stand again, then do the rock action.

- **When you’re given a wonderful gift, what do you want to say to the gift giver?** (Yes, thank you!)
- **How did Jacob say thank You to God?** (With his rock, a tenth of all God would give him.)

Invite the children to make a **Forgiveness Rock** to remind them to say “I’m sorry” and “Thank You” to Jesus for loving and forgiving us.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})**

- **What did you learn in Sabbath School today?** (Wait for the children to respond.)

Today we learned that God loves us so very much and wants us to always be close to Him. When we sin, we should say we’re sorry. God’s love is so great that it stretches down from heaven to forgive us. God knew we’d need Jesus to be like a ladder to keep us connected to God, so even before we were born Jesus died on the cross for us.
