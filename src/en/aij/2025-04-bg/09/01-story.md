Mary sang as she tidied her little house. She was very happy. She was going to marry a man named Joseph. Mary was also happy because she loved God with her whole heart _(cover your heart with hands)._ The sun shone through the window as she worked. _Sweep, sweep,_ went the broom as she sang. Suddenly the room got brighter . . . and _brighter_! It wasn’t the sunshine—it was a beautiful angel standing right there in front of her!\
Light swirled around the beautiful angel. Shimmer, shimmer, shine. Mary was so surprised! _(Surprised face.)_ The angel said gently, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, because God is pleased with you. Listen! You will become pregnant. You will give birth to a son, and you will name Him Jesus” (Luke 1:30, 31, ICB). The shining angel told her that this baby would be very, very special. “He will be called the Son of God” (Luke 1:35, ICB).

Mary smiled at the shining angel. She trusted God and His words that the angel spoke. Yes, she would be God’s helper! She would be the mother of Baby Jesus, God’s own Son! She ran to tell Joseph the news. He was very surprised too! _(Surprised face.)_ He scratched his head (scratch head). He rubbed his chin _(rub chin)_. Was what Mary said really true? _(Nod.)_\
Joseph thought about the angel all day long. When the sun went down, he kept _thinking . . . thinking . . . thinking . . ._ Finally, he shut his eyes and went to sleep _(sleep action)_. Suddenly, he saw an angel in his dreams. Light swirled around the beautiful angel. _Shimmer, shimmer, shine._ The angel had come to tell Joseph that it was true—Mary was going to have a baby, even though they weren’t married yet! Joseph trusted God.

Mary rested her hand on her growing tummy and felt her baby move. She was so happy God had chosen her to be the mother of His Son, Baby Jesus! Joseph was happy too. He knew he could trust God. He would look after Jesus and be God’s helper.\
God sometimes chooses us for special jobs too. Like Mary, we can trust God and say “yes” when He asks us to do something for Him.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for choosing us to be your helpers. I can trust You and say “yes” to You!
