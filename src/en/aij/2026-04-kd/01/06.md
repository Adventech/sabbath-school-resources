The disciples looked at Jesus with big, wondering eyes. Jesus had stopped the storm with just His words! Everyone was amazed. Jesus, the kind teacher and healer, also had power to tell nature what to do.

In the stillness, voices whispered, “Who can this be, that even the wind and sea obey Him!” (Mark 4:41).

We know the answer! Jesus is God. He is all powerful. We can always trust Him to help us. He makes our hearts peaceful.

When the thunder booms out the window and the rain pours down, don’t be afraid. Remember that Jesus is with you.

When a friend says angry words, or when something seems unfair, remember that Jesus is with you. He can keep you calm.

When you try something new and scary, remember that Jesus is with you. He can give you His peace.

“You, Lord, give true peace. You give peace to those who depend on you. You give peace to those who trust you” (Isaiah 26:3, ICB).

- What can you do next time you feel this way?
