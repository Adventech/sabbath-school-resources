**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The line from a favorite worship song goes, “I’ve got peace like a river in my soul.” What a beautiful description of the way God can change our turbulent times into calm, content moments of trust. No matter the swirls that come—conflict, separation, brokenness—God can carry us through on His river of peace.

Thank you for taking the time this week to nurture the children at Sabbath School to first find peace with God and then become peacemakers for Him. “Blessed are the peacemakers, for they shall be called sons of God” (Matthew 5:9).

It’s not easy to know how to repair friendships or make peace after conflict. It was one thing for Jacob to be sorry for his deceit as he fled from home, and quite another to think of facing furious Esau again. Only God could fill him with the courage and calm he needed to attempt to make peace. And only God could assure him that however Esau responded, Jacob would still be blessed for following His way of love.

May you seek God’s peace first and foremost this week. Fill up on the promises in His Word like one who thirsts in the desert. It is from the overflow that you will be able to teach, and model, that it’s God’s peace alone that really satisfies.

“The heart that is in harmony with God is a partaker of the peace of heaven, and will diffuse its blessed influence all around. The spirit of peace will rest like dew upon hearts weary and troubled with worldly strife” (Ellen G. White, in _Signs of the Times_, December 27, 1905).
