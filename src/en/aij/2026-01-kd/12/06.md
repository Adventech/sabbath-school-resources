Another king came to meet Abram after his big rescue. It was the king of Sodom. He was happy that Abram saved all the people from his city! His friends and neighbors were all safe.

The king of Sodom saw all the treasures–the silver and gold and precious things–that Abram had collected from the enemy kings. He said to Abram, “You may keep all these things yourself. Just give me my people who were captured!” (Genesis 14:21, ICB).

Wow! The king of Sodom was offering to give Abram _everything_ that had been taken from the city of Sodom, except for the people. All of that gold, silver, and animals would make Abram _very, very_ rich.

What would you say? Abram shook his head. What did he say to the king of Sodom? Read Genesis 14:22, 23.

Abram wouldn’t take anything!

Abram had been loyal to Lot and now showed his loyalty and trust in God. He knew he didn’t need a king’s riches. God would always provide for him. God had promised Abram, “I will bless you” (Genesis 12:2).

For a long time the people of Sodom talked about Abram’s bravery as they sat by the city gates or walked through the marketplace. Abram was a loyal and brave hero.

- What did Abram teach the people of Sodom about his God?
