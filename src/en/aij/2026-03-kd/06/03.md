John the Baptist’s voice boomed loud and strong across the desert. “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2).

Everyone in the big crowd by the Jordan River listened quietly. People had traveled from all over the country to see and hear what John the Baptist had to say.

Repent? The kids in the crowd didn’t know what that word meant. Their mothers told them quietly that it meant to be sorry. God wants us to be sorry for our sins and tell Him so. He also wants us to turn away from our sin.

The clever teachers and important Pharisees looked at their feet. Did they need to be sorry?

The rich rulers took a big, deep breath. Did they need to be sorry?

The mothers, fathers, builders, shopkeepers, and farmers all stopped and thought too. Did they need to be sorry?

Who does the Bible say have sinned and need God’s forgiveness? **Read Romans 3:23.**

Yes, we have all done wrong. But God’s love and forgiveness are for everyone!

As John spoke, the Holy Spirit began to do something deep inside the people’s hearts. They realized how sinful they had been. Right there, in the desert, they asked God to forgive them.

“Lord, you are kind and forgiving. You have great love for those who call to you” (Psalm 86:5, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What might you say to Jesus, right now?
- What does the Bible tell you that Jesus will say back to you?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Say “I’m sorry” to Jesus. Tell Him how sad you feel for something you did wrong today and ask Him to forgive you.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Help your family with the washing of clothes or dishes. Watch how messy things are when they go into the water. Our hearts are like that, messy with sin. When we are sorry and tell Jesus so, He forgives us and makes our hearts sparkly and clean.
