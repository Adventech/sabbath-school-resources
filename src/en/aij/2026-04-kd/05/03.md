The man stood up on his new healthy feet, reached out with his new perfect hands, and hugged Jesus tightly with his new strong muscles.

Then Jesus spoke softly to him, “Go and show yourself to the priest. And offer a gift to God because you have been healed” (Mark 1:44, ICB).

The crowd was so amazed. Jesus could do anything! They watched as the man went, jumping and leaping down the road. He sang praises to God all the way into town.

The healed man headed straight to the temple just as Jesus had told him to. It was the priest’s job to say if he was sick or better. The priest looked over his skin closely and told everyone the man was healed and clean. He no longer had leprosy! He was free to walk through the streets, hug his family, and see his friends.

Imagine the surprise on the faces of his family when he burst through the door of their home! They must have been very shocked! The whole street would have heard their excited voices. They must have hugged him tightly and cried with happiness.

Wherever he went, the healed man couldn’t keep his excitement and thankfulness inside. His heart was like a happy volcano, exploding with joy! He couldn’t help but share his story with every person he saw. He had to tell others about his kind Friend Jesus who loved and healed him.

Because of his sharing, what happened? **Read Luke 5:15.**

You can tell others about Jesus too! Think how happy they will be to know Jesus as their kind Friend.
