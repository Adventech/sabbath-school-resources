Do you ever dream about doing impossible things—like soaring high in the air with birds or swimming in the deep ocean without needing to hold your breath? Maybe there are other things you can almost do—like touching your nose with your tongue, licking your elbow, or saying the alphabet backwards as fast as you say it forwards.

It’s amazing that Jesus can do anything! There is nothing too hard for Him, but He also never did things to get attention or make Himself look good. Jesus is the kindest, wisest, most caring, wonderful person who ever lived. The stories you’ll read this quarter show us how much He loved to help others feel better and know and love God more.

If you feel sick right now, or if you’re sad about something, remember how much Jesus wants to help you feel better too. We might not see these same kind of miracles, but the very same Jesus that you meet in the Bible is your Friend, just as He was to the people in these stories. Don’t ever forget how much He loves you.
