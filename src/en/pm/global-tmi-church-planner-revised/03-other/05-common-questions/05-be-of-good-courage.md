#### Does the strategy in this church planner work with small churches?

The Global TMI disciple-making framework is based on Christ's method. Not only does it work for small churches, but it can work for even one member! _My Disciple-Making Journal: A Global TMI Personal Planner_, which is designed to aid individual church members, is based on the same disciple-making strategy as this church planner.

While small churches or groups may have fewer departments, leaders, resources, and events, creating a disciple-making plan using this church planner will still be very helpful. They will also benefit greatly by providing the personal planner to each of their members.

#### Do we need a large budget to implement disciple-making evangelism?

While money is helpful to procure resources and conduct events, disciple-making evangelism is based on the principle of personal labor, which can still be accomplished at little to no cost if funds are not available.

#### Could using this _Global TMI Church Planner_ stifle the work of the Holy Spirit?

If we rely only on plans and methods, it is possible to forget that conversion is the work of the Holy Spirit. At the same time, God does instruct His church to make careful plans to equip members for service: “In all our conferences there should be well- organized plans for the instruction and training of those who desire to give themselves to the work of God” (_Gospel Workers_, p. 75). While we prayerfully seek God, we should also lay “well-organized plans” to carry out the mission. Using the church planner for this purpose can help keep a church’s focus on the mission of making disciples.

#### Should I expect a significant increase in baptisms if I use the Global TMI strategy?

When a church and its members focus on getting every member involved in making disciples, it will generally result in both spiritual and numerical growth. However, don’t be discouraged if this doesn’t happen initially. Developing a disciple-making culture takes time. Even if you don’t see an increase in baptisms, remember that getting members involved is itself a measure of success. The goal of our mission is not merely baptisms, but active disciples. So be of good courage. And rest assured that when getting the members involved in this vital work becomes the focus, growth will eventually come. And when it does, the rate of those who leave the church will decrease.

#### How should ministries and departments relate to the church’s disciple-making plan?

Sometimes our local church departments can work in silos and even compete more than collaborate with each other. The beauty of the Global TMI disciple-making framework is that it brings together every department, ministry, initiative, and institution of the church. We all share the same basic mission and strategy—to make disciples. Some departments may specialize in preparing the soil, such as health ministries and community services. Others may place special emphasis on planting seeds of truth, such as literature and media ministries. Still others may focus on making disciples in a particular demographic (women, children, etc.). But ministries and departments can each contribute to one or more phases of the church’s overall disciple-making plan.
