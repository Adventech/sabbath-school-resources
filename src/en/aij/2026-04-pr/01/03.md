Suddenly, a wild wind began to blow. The disciples held on tight as a wild storm blew across the lake. Dark clouds rolled across the sky covering the moon and the stars. Lightning flashed. Thunder crashed. Giant waves splashed into the boat and sloshed around the disciples’ feet. Then “the boat began to fill with water and they were in danger” (Luke 8:23, ICB).

The disciples knew all about sailing and fishing. They knew all about boats and storms. But this was a storm like no other! They tried everything good fishermen do in a storm, but nothing helped. They couldn’t control the sails. They just couldn’t bail the water out of their boat!

The disciples looked at the rolling waves, and they felt scared. They looked down at the water splashing around their feet, then up at the lightning blazing across the sky. They were even more frightened. They were terrified they would die!

What had the disciples forgotten? **Read Psalm 50:15.**

Panicked and filled with fear, they had forgotten that Jesus was with them. All they could think about was what they could do to save themselves from the scary storm.

Sometimes we find ourselves in scary, messy, troubled situations that are a lot like storms. These “stormy” times may scare us, or make us anxious or worried. Maybe someone we love is very sick, or our family is changing or moving. Maybe a good friend doesn’t want to be our friend anymore, or there’s a big fight in our neighborhood. We can easily try to solve the troubles by ourselves, as the disciples did. But we’re not alone! God promises, “I will be with him in trouble; I will deliver him and honor him” (Psalm 91:15). What a beautiful promise to remember!
