**Word of the Week**

**Friend:** Friends are people who love each other no matter what. Good friends put the other person first. They care when their friend is hurting, and they love bringing happiness to each other.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

As Jesus was teaching in a house in Capernaum, four men brought their friend with paralysis to Him to be healed. But the large crowd made it impossible to get the man close to Jesus, so the friends carried him up to the roof, made an opening, and lowered him on his mat down to Jesus. Jesus healed the man and told him that his sins were forgiven. The man stood, took up his bed, and went home. Everyone was amazed.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus longs to help us and our friends.
- **Feel** a desire to come to Jesus because He will always help us.
- **Respond** by telling my friends about Jesus.
