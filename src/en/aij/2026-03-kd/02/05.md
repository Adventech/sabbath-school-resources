Mary tenderly wrapped Baby Jesus in cloth to keep Him from getting cold. Then she laid Him down to sleep.

What was Baby Jesus’ bed? **Read Luke 2:7.**

A king sleeping in a bed like that? Usually kings sleep in fancy royal palaces, on giant silky-soft beds. But not this humble King.

Thousands and thousands of invisible angels looked down at Baby Jesus lying in that manger. They were amazed. They knew Jesus as king of the universe! In heaven Jesus had a shiny throne, with angels singing praise songs to Him day and night. And now He had swapped it for a stable, a manger, and noisy animals.

The Bible says, “He gave up his place with God and made himself nothing” (Philippians 2:7, ICB). Wow! What a humble God and Savior He is.

The angels knew their King deserved the very best. But Jesus had given it all away to save the world. He had come down, down, down, to give His life—the greatest gift ever given to the world.
