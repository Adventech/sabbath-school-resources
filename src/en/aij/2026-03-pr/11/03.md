Jesus didn’t just tell His disciples about the kingdom of heaven—He showed them. Jesus, the Master Teacher, knew that life is a wonderful lesson book. Every day that His disciples were walking, talking, and being with Jesus, they were learning by watching Him.

The Bible doesn’t tell us a lot about Jesus’ life in the 30 years before His baptism, but Jesus was quietly showing people how to live God’s way then too; His life was an example. When He began His public work for God and called His disciples, His life became an even bigger example.

While other rabbis were teaching their followers what they thought it meant to be holy, Jesus was busy showing His disciples about giving. When Jesus turned water into wine at the wedding in Cana, they learned about giving surprise blessings. When Jesus fed the multitudes on the hillsides, they learned about giving real food and spiritual food. When Jesus healed the sick and sad people that flocked to Him, they learned about giving healing and comfort. And when He taught the crowds, He was giving them knowledge about a loving God.

Why did Jesus focus so much on showing His disciples about giving? **Read James 1:17.** Jesus was following God’s example when He nurtured people’s faith by giving them what they needed. It was important for the disciples to learn to follow Jesus’ example too, so that they could continue giving to the people and nurturing their faith long after He had gone back to heaven. As the disciples watched the different ways Jesus gave, their faith was being nurtured too.
