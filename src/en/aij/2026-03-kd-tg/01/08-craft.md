**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the shapes, then fold each of the rectangles back and forth like an accordion, using the horizontal lines as a guide. (An adult can help if needed.)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/01/1b.png)

2\. Place each folded page vertically side by side.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/01/1c.png)

3\. Fold along the horizontal line, one to the left and one to the right, then glue the end folds together to create “wings.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/01/1d.png)

4\. Loop a piece of ribbon/string and glue between the two circles, leaving some of the string below.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/01/1e.png)

5\. Glue the string below the circles between each of the “wings.” (Optional: Tie a bow out of some ribbon and stick to the front.) Now your angel is ready to hang!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/01/1f.png)
