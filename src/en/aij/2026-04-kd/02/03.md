The nobleman galloped down the road on his horse, leaving a dusty cloud behind Him. His colorful robes flapped in the breeze. He leaned forward, his eyes looking straight ahead. He must hurry! He needed Jesus to heal his very sick son. Jesus was his only hope.

He had never met Jesus before, but he had heard from others how kind, gentle, and generous Jesus was. He was also powerful! Jesus had made sick people well, and blind people see again.

The nobleman thought that if he could just speak to Jesus himself, he would tell Him about his sick son. Maybe Jesus would come back to his house and make his son all better.

The nobleman thought about what he would say to Jesus as he sped down the road. He had to make sure Jesus came back home with him. Without Jesus, he had no hope that his son would live.

What words can we pray when we really need Jesus to help us? **Read Psalm 39:7.** You can pray these words to Jesus, knowing that He cares for you.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- The nobleman had to keep believing and hoping. What is something you are hoping for that you have to wait for?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Being very careful, watch an adult boil water in a pot. Watch the bubbles rise from the bottom to the top. Just as the bubbles rise, we can rise above our troubles when we trust in Jesus.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to help us always believe in Him and His goodness—even when we can’t see it yet.
