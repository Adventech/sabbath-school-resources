**Opening Activities**

For the color sorting and matching game, collect **a large range of items in different colors**. Try to use **nature items**. You may also like to use **colored blocks** or **household items**.

You will need **nine pieces of white paper or cardstock**. Using **markers, draw a giant heart** in a different color on each piece of paper: **red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink, gray, brown**. The children will place the colorful items inside the heart of the same color, so consider size when choosing items and paper.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a small **gift-wrapped box** with a bow and silver paper **stars** inside.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: colorful blocks; baby doll in blanket; yarn; containers (for pretend dye); bright, colorful coat or cloth; black blanket; 11 silver stars.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Make **colorful ribbon sticks** by attaching ribbons to the end of craft sticks or buy premade. You will need **red, purple, yellow, blue, green, orange,** and **pink ribbons**. Make enough so each child can have one. Place inside the box.
