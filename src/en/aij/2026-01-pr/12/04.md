Abram’s army hurried on their way to track down the four greedy kings and their captives. One night as they neared the city of Dan, they spied the enemy camp. Quietly, ever so quietly, they crept closer to check things out.

What they saw made them wonder why they’d bothered to sneak up silently! The enemy armies were celebrating the victory with a lively party. They had figured there was nothing at all to worry about, for hadn’t they just overthrown five kings, stolen their goods, and captured their people?

While thinking they were such clever warriors, they were really very unwise. If only they knew how God was working just outside their camp. Not wasting even a minute, Abram quickly organized his troops. What do you think Abram did? Read Genesis 14:15.

What a wise plan that was! Surprised beyond belief at the sudden attack, the stunned soldiers quit partying and leaped up to flee. Never mind the captives, never mind the stolen food or treasures. Leaving everything behind, they ran for their lives, with Abram’s men chasing hot on their heels.

Seeing his kind Uncle Abram filled Lot with relief and made him hugely grateful. What a blessing! Abram had wisely trusted God and lived by the words “Learn to do good, seek justice, rebuke the oppressor [stand up for those who are not free or don’t have the power to act]!” (Isaiah 1:17).
