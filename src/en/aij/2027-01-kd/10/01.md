Respect and Obey

God had shared four commandments with the Israelites about how to love Him. Now God began sharing rules about loving and respecting others. What was commandment number five? **Read Exodus 20:12.**

Brian had been taught the importance of respecting and obeying his parents. His family were missionaries living on the Island of Penang. One night he and his father were late walking home. The sun began to quickly sink in the sky. It wasn’t long before they were walking in complete darkness. It was a clear but moonless night.

Suddenly an unusual flash of lightning lit up the clear sky. The light showed something frightening! Coiled up on the ground right in front of Brian was a cobra snake.

With a quiet yet urgent voice, Father said one word: “STOP!” Brian instantly obeyed. He stopped with one leg still in the air.

Suddenly another flash of lightning lit up the sky. Father was able to quickly take care of the snake. Then he spoke one more word: “RUN!” Brian ran with his father until they reached safety.

Brian knew God had saved his life by sending the miracle lightning at exactly the right time. He was very thankful that he respected and quickly obeyed his father. God has given us parents to protect, love, and guide us. God’s words were wise when He said, “Honor your father and your mother. Then you will live a long time” (Exodus 20:12, ICB).
