on Water

^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/10/01.png)

It had been a good day. Now it was time to go home. Jesus’ friends waved goodbye to the people (wave). They waved goodbye to Jesus (wave). They pushed their boat out onto the calm lake. Flip-flap, flip-flap went the boat’s sail in the breeze. The wind over the Sea of Galilee started to blow. Jesus’ friends felt cold as they sat in their boat (brrr). They looked up at the dark clouds that blocked the moon. They felt little raindrops land on their hands (tap on child’s hands). Drip-drop, drip-drop. Oh, no—a storm was coming! The boat rocked back and forth on the water. Rock, rock (rock with your child). Crash went a wave into the boat. The men were scared! (Hug child close.) Row, row went their oars! The men tried to keep the boat straight. But they couldn’t. The storm was too big!
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/10/02.png)

Jesus saw the big storm. He saw His friends from far away. He saw how tired and scared they were. Then Jesus went right to them. He didn’t swim. He didn’t take a boat. Jesus walked on top of the waves! (Walk fingers across picture.) The men in the boat were very surprised. Who was walking on the water? Jesus said, “Have courage! It is I! Don’t be afraid” (Matthew 14:27, ICB). Peter asked Jesus if he could walk on the water to meet Jesus. “Come,” Jesus said with a smile (Matthew 14:29). Peter stepped out of the boat . . . and onto the water. He didn’t sink! He looked at Jesus’ kind face as he walked closer to Him. Step, step, step. Peter came closer. He was walking on water!

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/10/03.png)

Peter thought about the big waves (clap hands). He thought about the strong wind (blow/whistle). He felt scared, and he looked down. He forgot to look at Jesus. Peter started to sink down, down into the water. He cried out for help! Suddenly Jesus was right beside him. He took Peter’s hand and pulled him up out of the water. Together they walked over the waves to the boat. Then the water became still, and the wind was quiet again. Peter forgot that Jesus was near, right there to help him. Jesus loves you and will help you be brave too.\
(Say together) Jesus, please help me remember that You are always with me.
