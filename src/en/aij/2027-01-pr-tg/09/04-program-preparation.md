**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **globe/world map**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Source **number outlines 1-4, markers/colored pencils** and other **decorative craft items**.

Source **a picture of someone well known to the children**. Cut it up into puzzle pieces. Make some pieces really obscure, so they won’t easily guess who this is until there are many pieces to fit together. Source a **paper bag** or similar to store the pieces in, for a random draw.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **flashlight and trumpet sound effect**. Source **four large pieces of paper/cardstock and draw a large heart on them**. Attach to the walls with **sticky tac** in the four corners of your room or place within your area. Have extra sticky tac (or similar) for the children to later attach their decorated numbers to the wall around each heart. Source **pens** and **Post-it notes** for each child. You will also need a set of **markers**, and **small heart stickers**. (Alternatively children can draw hearts with pens or stamp both thumbs in an inkpad to then press on paper to create hearts—you will need wipes/handwash if using this option.)
