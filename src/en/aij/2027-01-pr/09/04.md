The lightning ceased, the thunder stopped, and the trumpet blasts were silenced. Mount Sinai was quiet and still. God had some very important things to say, and He wanted every man, woman, and child to listen with reverent attention. They held their breath in the silence and waited.

Finally God’s deep, rich voice spoke into the stillness. First, He reminded the Israelites who He was. “I am the Lord your God, who brought you out of the land of Egypt, out of the house of bondage” (Exodus 20:2).

### I AM

_Not I might be, or I was, or I could be._ “I am” means I exist for real, right now! **“I AM”** is saying, “I love you,” and I will continue to love you as long as I exist.

### The LORD

This means that God is “the Existing One,”the Living One” (_The Seventh-day Adventist Bible Commentary_, vol. 1, p. 601). God is reminding His people that He’s true; He’s alive and with them. Even when they grumble and feel as if He’s forgotten all about them, they can know that He’s right there with them. **“The LORD”** is saying, “I love you.”

### YOUR God

Not somebody else’s God, not the neighbor’s God. God is saying He is a personal God, a personal friend. **“YOUR God”** is saying, “I love you.”

### OUT

“Who brought you **OUT** of the land of Egypt, **OUT** of the house of bondage.” God is reminding His people how important it is to remember that He has brought them out of a terrible situation with His mighty power. He has given them freedom from bondage, which means slavery. Now He wants to show them how He can also bring them out of slavery of a different kind—slavery to sin. **“OUT”** is saying, “I love you.”

God gave four beautiful messages of love to the children of Israel, and God’s children of all time. God tells us today that He is our I AM, our Lord, our God, and our rescuer who brings us out of slavery to sin.
