**Opening Activities**

Source music for the **musical statues game**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: Listening Picture Cards** with common things parents teach their children (how to make a bed, how to tie shoes, how to put things away to keep a room neat and tidy, how to wash dishes, and safety skills, such as not touching a hot stove and looking both ways before crossing a street, etc.) and a **fly swatter**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source items for **suggested Storytelling Props**: a candle, matches, and a bowl; mustard seeds and several other kinds of seeds to compare sizes (e.g., cucumber, pumpkin, and avocado); figs (or a local fruit) for children to taste, if appropriate; two houses built from Lego bricks or other materials; a tray of sand and a brick; a spray bottle filled with water; and a hair dryer.

Prepare to play **“The Lord’s Prayer”** song.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

**Story Explorer Box: a phone.** Arrange to call the pastor or another person the children will recognize. Instruct the person you call to tell the children how important it is to stay connected to Jesus, just as the house stayed firmly connected to the rock.
