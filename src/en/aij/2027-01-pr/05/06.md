Walking, running, skipping, and trotting, the people of Israel, and all their animals, hurried across God’s miracle path right through the middle of the Red Sea.

But wait. What was that noise behind them?

The Bible tells us, “The Egyptians pursued and went after them into the midst of the sea, all Pharaoh’s horses, his chariots, and his horsemen” (Exodus 14:23).

God was watching. He was ready. Suddenly “He took off their chariot wheels, so that they drove them with difficulty” (Exodus 14:25). Crashing and smashing together, they suddenly realized that God was Israel’s leader. They cried, “The Lord fights for them.”

That’s exactly what God did. With the Israelites safely on the other side, God told Moses to stretch His rod over the water once again. Moses obeyed. What happened next? **Read Exodus 14:27, 28.**

God had done everything possible to help them choose the true God, but they had rebelled. He couldn’t allow them to hurt and try to kill the Israelites ever again.

While the whole Egyptian army drowned, not one Israelite had been lost or hurt! Great shouts of victory and praise burst from their lips. For their joyful thank-You service, Moses wrote a beautiful song of praise that began with the words “I will sing unto the Lord, for He has triumphed gloriously. The horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea!” (Exodus 15:1). They continued to praise God for being their wonderful leader, their protector, and for defeating their enemies.
