**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Play several rounds of **Memory Wipe**. Show the children a tray of 10 small objects (e.g., toy car, coin, pencil, marble, paper clip, spoon, key, button, rubber band, piece of Lego, etc.). Give them 30 seconds to look closely and remember everything on the tray and its position. After 30 seconds, cover the items with your tea towel/cloth so none of the objects are visible. Instruct the children to silently write down as many objects and their order on the tray as they can remember. Uncover the tray and see which objects they remembered in their correct location.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How easy was it to remember the objects and where they were on the tray?** (Difficult. There wasn’t enough time to remember the object and their location.)
- **Did you notice that you remembered some objects quickly but forgot others?**

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** In our memory game it was easy to forget some of the objects even though you saw them only a moment ago. People are like that with God’s blessings too. In today’s Bible story God’s people forgot all the amazing ways He had already cared for them.

- **How had God provided for His people since they left Egypt?** (Freeing them from Egypt; guiding, cooling, and protecting them with His cloud during the day; protecting and warming them with His cloud at night; parting the Red Sea; giving them water in the desert.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God showed His grace and care for the Israelites many times since they had left Egypt. However, they still complained about what they didn’t have. It’s important for us to remember all the good things God has done for us, so we don’t start grumbling when life gets tough.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God is gracious every day, even when we forget how He has provided for us in the past.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**UNPACK THE STORY** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Place the words from the days of the week in order on the floor. Invite six children to come to the front. You will read six statements and for each one, the children should stand beside the day that was correct for the statement.

**Statements:** 1. No manna fell. 2. Twice the amount of manna fell. 3. The people collected manna. 4: Extra manna was stored for the next day. 5. The people spent the day worshipping God. 6. The people ate quail.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- Ask the children standing on the days from Sunday to Thursday: **Why do you think God sent the people food to collect each day—just for this day?** (To teach them to rely on Him each day.)
- Ask the children standing at Friday: **What does it tell you about God that He sent extra manna on this day?** (It’s important to prepare for Sabbath on Friday so that the blessings of the Sabbath can be enjoyed fully, and we can rest as God has commanded us to rest and spend time with Him and others.)
- Ask the children standing at Saturday: **What can we learn about the Sabbath from what happened on this day?** (It is important to God; we should keep it special and holy.)
- Ask the child/children with the quail statement (who could have stood anywhere): **Why do you think God sent the quail?** (He is gracious; He is wise; He wanted to teach them that His way, not their way, was best.)

Display your **jar of manna (popcorn)** and the **Gratitude Jar** from last week. Share that Aaron’s jar would help the Israelites remember these lessons and to rely on God to provide and guide them. It would help them stay grateful, content, and trusting.

- Imagine the Israelites had a gratitude jar. **What could they have written down and put in it?** (Rescued from slavery in Egypt, crossing the Red Sea, pillar cloud of guidance and warmth, water in the desert, etc.)
- **What would you add to our jar of thanks that you have learned today? “Thank You, God . . .”** As the children share, write responses on craft sticks and add them to the Gratitude jar.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God is loving, kind, wise, gracious, and forgiving. He faithfully provides for us, even when we don’t trust or obey. God wants to spend special time with us every Sabbath, His special, holy day. This is something we need as much as bread. We can be thankful for His constant love and grace—every day.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 59: Moses and the Manna** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: “You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing” (Psalm 145:16).

Stand up and throw the **manna memory verse pieces** out in an open space. Explain to the children that each manna piece has a word from the memory verse on it. They are to gather the “manna” pieces and put the memory verse in order. Read the memory verse aloud together when all children have finished.

- **How did God “open His hand” in today’s story?** (Accept answers.)

Share that God provided the food they needed: manna. He showed them grace when they grumbled. He also gave them a lesson by sending the quail. We can trust God to truly meet all our needs each day.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Manna in Angola,”** and engage children in the discussion question.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to: **Share your memory verse in a beautiful way (say, write, sing) with someone who might not know God well.**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show children your completed **Manna in the Desert** craft to make as a reminder that God provides for us every day. Provide a template for each child, and the materials needed to complete it. While children are making their crafts, share with them some **health facts** and how we can use the food God has given us to help our bodies be healthy and strong.

**^[REVIEW AND COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring the children back together as a group and discuss the following:

- **Can you think of something God has done for you in the past that you sometimes forget?** (E.g., helped me when I was sick, gave me peace in a stressful situation.)
- **What might happen to our faith if we forget God’s past blessings?** (We might stop trusting Him and think He doesn’t care, even though He always does.)
- **How can you remind yourself of God’s goodness every day?** (Write them down in a notebook before bed each night, tell my family/friends about God’s blessings, etc.)

Say the Big Idea together: God is gracious every day, even when we forget how He has provided for us in the past.

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing the closing song, **“God Be With You,”** together. Thank God for graciously providing for us every day, including our food. Pray for the children and their families, that they will be grateful for the way God provides for their needs in the coming week.
