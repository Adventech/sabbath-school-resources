### Jesus Is the Savior God Promised

> <p></p>
> “Anyone who falls on this stone will be broken to pieces; anyone on whom it falls will be crushed.” Matthew 21:44 (NIV)

Have you ever heard a promise and wondered if it would really happen?

Long before Jesus came to earth, God gave Daniel a special prophecy. God told His people that the Messiah, the Savior, would come. God gave Israel time to listen to Him, turn back to Him, and prepare for the Messiah.

Then, at the time God had promised, Jesus came! But many people did not understand that Jesus was the promised Messiah. They expected Him to do something different. God’s prophecy had said that the Messiah would die, not for Himself, but for others.

That is exactly what Jesus did. Jesus died for us because He loves us. He is the Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world.

After Jesus rose from the dead, He gave His followers an important mission. He told them to tell others about Him, first nearby, and then farther and farther away, until people everywhere could hear the good news.

That mission is ours too. Even when we are little, we can help others know about Jesus. We can tell someone, “Jesus loves you,” pray for others, and show His love by the way we live.

God’s prophecy came true: Jesus came to save us. And now He wants everyone to hear the good news.
