After many good years the kind pharaoh in Egypt died. A new evil pharaoh sat on the royal throne. As the new pharaoh looked across his kingdom, he became very worried. There were so many Israelites!

One day he said, “Look! The people of Israel are too many! And they are too strong for us to handle. . . . They could fight us and escape from the country” (Exodus 1:9, 10, ICB). He thought and thought. _We can’t let the Israelite people grow stronger and bigger than us Egyptians. We must stop them!_

So he decided to make their lives very hard. He forced the Israelites to work long days as slaves. They carried heavy loads, made mud bricks, and planted fields. Sweat dripped from their faces, and their muscles ached. All they did every day—from early morning to late at night—was work, work, work! The people were so very tired.

But God protected and blessed them. What happened? **Read Exodus 1:12.**

Their families kept growing, and growing, and growing! God had plans to give His people “a future and a hope” (Jeremiah 29:11).

Pharaoh was furious that _his_ plan wasn’t working, so he came up with a new one. He told his soldiers to throw all the Israelite baby boys into the Nile River. What a horrible plan. But even though Pharaoh treated them so badly, God kept blessing His people. No evil king could stop God’s plan for them. Their families kept growing, and growing, and growing!

^[Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#00929C", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ants are one of the hardest working creatures on earth. They work all day and all night, collecting food and keeping their nests clean. They have time for only short power naps that last about eight minutes.
