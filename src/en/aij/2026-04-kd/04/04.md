The disciples heard Jesus’ words above the roar of the winds. “Be of good cheer! It is I; do not be afraid” (Matthew 14:27).

Instantly, the disciples felt peace. Jesus was there.

Because their eyes were on Jesus, they stopped thinking about the howling winds and the rolling waves.

Peter, especially, wanted to jump for joy! He felt big and brave and called out through the noisy storm, “Lord, if that is really you, then tell me to come to You on the water” (Matthew 14:28, ICB).

What did Jesus say? **Read Matthew 14:29.**

What an exciting word!

Feeling very brave, Peter stood up in the tipping, rocking boat. He put one foot over the side of the boat, and then the other. He looked at Jesus with amazement. He was standing on the water!

He took one step, and then another. He couldn’t believe it. Jesus was helping Him to do something completely impossible! Peter wasn’t sinking down in the water. Somehow his feet were walking on the watery waves, just as Jesus was.

With a big smile, he slowly began walking toward Jesus.

With Jesus beside you, you can be brave like Peter! Jesus can help you to be brave when something seems scary or new, or when you want to do the right thing. You can come close to Jesus when you pray to Him, when you say a memory verse out loud, or when you read His Words in the Bible.
