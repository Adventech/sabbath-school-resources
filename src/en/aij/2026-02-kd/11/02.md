With a shout of joy Simeon ran to greet the brothers. He was free!

The brothers hugged, and smiled, and hugged some more. They were so grateful the governor had set Simeon free. God was truly looking after them. Together they followed the servant to Joseph’s home for lunch.

When the governor arrived home, the brothers gave him their gifts and bowed low on the ground. They still had no idea the governor was their brother, Joseph!

With a kind smile Joseph asked them about their father. They replied, “Your servant our father is in good health; he is still alive” (Genesis 43:28).

What good news—Joseph’s father was alive! Then Joseph’s eyes met Benjamin’s. Here was the little brother he had missed so much. All Joseph’s feelings bubbled over. Quickly he rushed out of the room so his brothers couldn’t see him cry.

Joseph’s servants seated the brothers at a big fancy table. It was a long time since they’d seen so much food in one place. What a treat! The brothers then noticed something even more amazing than the beautiful food. What was it? **Read Genesis 43:33.**

When all the yummy food was served up for the brothers, Joseph did something extra special for his youngest brother. For every one plate of food the big brothers were given, Joseph gave Benjamin five plates of food! Were the brothers jealous or angry? No! They smiled at Benjamin and just kept eating and chatting. Joseph could see that God had changed their hearts. This made Joseph very happy!
