The people sat on the hillside and listened carefully to Jesus. They had smiled at the stories of the crashing house and the mixed-up fruit. They had nodded at the stories about seeds and bright lights. So this was what God’s love was like!

Jesus smiled at the people. He really wanted them to come even closer to their loving God. He would teach them how. He would teach them how to pray.

Looking up into the sky, Jesus began a beautiful prayer.\
“Our Father in heaven,\
Hallowed be Your name.\
Your kingdom come.\
Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven.\
Give us this day our daily bread.\
And forgive us our debts, as we forgive our debtors.\
And do not lead us into temptation,\
But deliver us from the evil one.\
For Yours is the kingdom and the power and the glory forever.\
Amen” (Matthew 6:9-13).

Jesus started by praising God—giving Him honor, love, and celebrating who He is—and putting all His trust in Him. Then He asked God for the things they needed, such as food, forgiveness, help, and strength. Jesus finished by praising God.

Jesus wants us to pray like this too. He is our best teacher. He shows and tells us how to be close to God and show His love.
