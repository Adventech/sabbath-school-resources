When we let God into our hearts, we will trust Him and ask Him to guide us. King David gave God this role as king of his heart, and wrote in Psalm 25:4, 5: “Show me Your ways, O Lord; teach me Your paths. Lead me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; on You I wait all the day.”

Wise King Solomon also gives us advice about God’s guidance. **Read Proverbs 3:6.**

When we tell God what’s happening in our lives and ask for His guidance, He will help us to make wise choices. We will let Him show us what to do, which path to take. Sometimes the path may not be clear at first, but we can keep praying for guidance, and trust God’s promise in Isaiah 48:17: “I am the Lord your God. I teach you to do what is good. I lead you in the way you should go” (ICB).

God is the best guide we could ever have, because He has such loving care over us. Psalm 139:1-3 tells us He knows when we sit, lie down, and stand up. He knows what we are thinking, and everything we say and do. God knows all of this because He loves us so much. We can love him back by saying, “You will guide me with your counsel, and afterward receive me to glory” (Psalm 73:24).
