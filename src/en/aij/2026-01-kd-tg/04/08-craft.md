**Sunflower**

**What you need:**

Paper plate, large craft stick or cardboard tube, yellow and green paint or crayons, nontoxic craft glue, tape, and sunflower seeds (1).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Use yellow paint or a crayon to color paper plate (2).
- Use green paint or a crayon to color large craft stick or cardboard tube (2).
- Make cuts around the edge of the paper plate, bending them in and out to look like petals (3).
- Use nontoxic glue to stick sunflower seeds onto the paper plate (3).
- Tape craft stick to the back of the paper plate, to serve as a handle/stem (3).
