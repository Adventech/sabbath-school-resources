**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. He wants to be with me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 12 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Suggested props: **metallic items, sparkly items, beautiful cloths, wood, olive oil**, and **spices**. Show some props and hide others for the children to find and bring to a **big basket**. Have items to involve children in making the tabernacle: **fabric** to pretend to sew, **tools** and **shiny foil** to wrap **cardboard** “walls”; **small paper messages** to carry; **baskets** to carry; **beads** to carry as jewels.
- Use peg people (Bezalel, Moses, Aaron and two sons, others) or a felt set if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses felt happy as he left his tent and hurried to meet the people. He had good news to tell them. God had asked the Israelites to build something very important.

- {"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
- **What was it?**

God had said to Moses, “And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8).

God wanted to be close to His people. He wanted to make a home with them. He was going to make it an extra-special, holy home.

God had an exact plan for His home. He gave Moses a list of things He needed. He would like gold, silver, and bronze; sparkling stones, and precious jewels too. They would need beautiful cloths, wood, animal skins, olive oil, and spices. ^[(Show props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Would the people like to give these things as gifts to God? Yes, they truly would!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) People rushed to their tents to look for gifts. They sang out and smiled as they found something. _Yes, a sparkly ring! Hooray, a shiny silver bowl! Here’s a beautiful tablecloth!_ ^[(Children to find hidden props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The people placed their gifts in baskets. ^[(Children to put in basket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Soon they had more than enough! It was time to start making the sanctuary.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God knew just the right person to be in charge—Bezalel ^[(peg man)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had given Bezalel some special talents so that he was very good at making things. Bezalel was so happy to help! He and a team of makers began right away.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) All over camp, people began to look for ways to use their talents too. The women sewed beautiful curtains for inside God’s home. ^[(Give children fabric to pretend “sew.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Men sawed and hammered as they made flat wooden walls. They covered the walls with gold so they would glow with light. ^[(Children to use tools and wrap foil over cardboard.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Some people who could run fast became messengers. ^[(Children to carry messages.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Others, who were strong, carried heavy baskets. ^[(Children to carry baskets.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Even the leaders carried the special jewels. ^[(Children to carry beads.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Everyone wanted to do what they were good at to help make God’s special home.

God’s home, the sanctuary, would also be a traveling home. It was a tent that could be easily packed up and moved. God wanted to live with His people, the Israelites, wherever they went.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Aaron and his sons were excited ^[(peg Aaron and two men)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They had special jobs too—they would be priests. Aaron would be the high priest. He wore a holy vest, shining with jewels.

God’s new home would be a holy place of worship. The priests would teach the people about God’s love and forgiveness. They would help the people come close to Him.

At the front of God’s home was a courtyard. It was a place for the people to bring their lamb offering, and other offerings, to God and ask for forgiveness.

Their small offerings in God’s home would remind them of God’s big promise. He had promised to send a Rescuer to take the place of the lamb offering. It would be Jesus! He would one day die for every person.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally everything was finished. Moses smiled as he checked every detail. The sanctuary had been made with gifts from the people and by those who used their special talents.

The sanctuary was golden and glowing on the inside, and it could be moved. There was a place for all the people to be forgiven, and they would learn about the promised Savior too. But the best part was the Most Holy Place. This was where God’s presence would be.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God wants to be close to you too! He wants to live in your heart.
