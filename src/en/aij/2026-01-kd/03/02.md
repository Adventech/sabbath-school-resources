Birds sung out their morning calls. It was Sabbath! The first Sabbath morning ever. Imagine Adam and Eve waking up to a full day with God.

Every new day in the Garden of Eden was an exciting adventure. But the seventh day—the Sabbath—was a _very special_ day. God did something wonderful. What did He do? **Read Genesis 2:3.**

The Sabbath was _blessed_ by God. When God blesses something or someone, He gives them a special kind of happiness. God blessed the seventh day, Saturday, to be an extra-special time for us to come closer to Him.

Imagine Adam and Eve happily talking, laughing, and singing with God. Imagine God bending down to show Adam and Eve a small seed that would grow into a beautiful flower, or pointing to an eagle and explaining how it flies. Imagine patting friendly rabbits and foxes, and tasting a juicy watermelon together for the first time.

God made the Sabbath _holy_. It would not be like any other day; it would be a special day to rest, worship, and be with Him.

God wants to be with you in a special way on Sabbath too. He wants us to stop all our work and worry to spend time with Him. Our memory verse says, “Remember the Sabbath day to keep it holy” (Exodus 20:8).
