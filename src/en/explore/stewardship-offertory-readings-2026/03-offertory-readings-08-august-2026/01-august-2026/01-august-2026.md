The names of Latimer and Ridley are tied together in history because they were tied to the same stake to be burned as martyrs on October 16, 1555. On the evening before Ridley’s execution, a friend offered to stay by his side all night to comfort him, but Ridley politely declined his offer, saying, “God willing, I plan to sleep as quietly tonight as ever I did in my life.”

How could Ridley have such peace when he was about to lose literally everything on earth, including his life? And why are some rich, healthy people so stressed while some of the poorest enjoy deep peace?

As we get more life experience, we begin to understand that peace is not the absence of problems; it’s the presence of God. Ultimately, true peace doesn’t rely on externals but internals.

Perfect health is not required. Great wealth is not required. Fairy-tale relationships are not required.

We find peace when we find God. As Paul wrote in Philippians 4:6, 7, “Do not be anxious about anything, but in every situation, by prayer and petition, with thanksgiving, present your requests to God. And the peace of God, which transcends all understanding, will guard your hearts and your minds in Christ Jesus” (NIV).

Are you looking for peace? Look for God first, and you’ll sleep well.
