---
title: Glossary of Terms and Symbols For the Books of Daniel and Revelation
subtitle: (Includes only the main terms, symbols, and Bible references)
---

**Abomination of desolation** – False religious system operating in opposition to the true worship of God, resulting in the destruction of Jerusalem and the persecution of God’s people. Daniel 11:31; 12:11; see also Daniel 8:13; 9:27; Matthew 24:15; Mark 13:14; Luke 21:20.

**Abaddon/Apollyon** – Hebrew and Greek names meaning “Destroyer”; the angel associated with the abyss. Revelation 9:11.

**Abyss/Bottomless pit** – Realm of demonic activity; place of Satan’s temporary imprisonment. Revelation 9:1, 2, 11; 11:7; 17:8: 20:1, 3.

**Alpha and Omega** – The first and last letters of the Greek alphabet used as a divine title to show God’s and Jesus’ eternal existence and Their universal rule. Revelation 1:8; 21:6; 22:13.

**Amen** – Means “truly” or “so be it”; a title for Christ as the true and faithful witness. Revelation 1:6-7; 3:14; 5:14; 7:12; 19:4; 22:20.

**Angel** – Refers to a “messenger,” whether a human or a heavenly angel. Daniel 3:28; 6:23; 9:21; Revelation 1:1, 20; 5:2, 11; 7:1-2, 11; 8:2-6, 13; 9:11, 15; 10:1, 5; 12:7, 9; 14:6, 8, 9-10; 15, 17-19; 15:1; 17:1, 7; 18:1, 21; 19:17; 20:1; 21:9, 12; 22:6, 16.

**Antichrist** – Term found only in 1 John 2:18, 22; 4:3; 2 John 7, it refers to a power usurping Christ’s prerogatives, which antichrist powers in Revelation do; see Revelation 13:1, 5-6; 17:3.

**Armageddon** – Symbolic location for the final battle between heaven and earth.

**Babylon the Great/Harlot** – Corrupt religio-political system opposed to God from which God’s people are called out. Revelation 14:8; 16:19; 17:5; 18:2, 10, 21.

**Blood of the Lamb** – Refers to Christ’s atoning death through which believers receive redemption, cleansing, and victory. Revelation 1:5; 5:9; 7:14; 12:11; 19:13.

**Beast** – Symbol of a kingdom. Daniel 7:3-7, 11-12, 17, 19, 23; 8:3-8, 20-21; Revelation 13:1-4, 11-12, 14; 14:9, 11; 15:2; 16:13; 17:3, 8, 11-13, 16-17.

**Book of Life** – God’s record of all who profess faith in Christ as Messiah/ Savior. Daniel 7:10; Revelation 3:5; 13:8; 20:12, 15; 21:27.

**Bowls/Seven last plagues** – Judgments described as God’s wrath poured out on the wicked. Revelation 15:7; 16:1; 21:9.

**Bride/Wife of the Lamb** – Redeemed people of God, symbolized by the holy city, New Jerusalem. Revelation 19:7-9; 21:2, 9; 22:17.

**Chaldeans** – Founders of the Neo-Babylonian Empire; also refers to a class of wise men who functioned as priests. Daniel 2:4.

**Crown** – Symbolic of victory and authority. Revelation 2:10; 3:11; 4:4, 10; 6:2; 9:7; 12:1, 3; 13:1; 14:14; 19:12.

**Cyrus the Great (c. 600 – 530 B.C.)** – Founder of the Achaemenid Empire, unifying the Persian tribes. After conquering Babylon in 539 B.C., he allowed the Israelite captives to return to Judah (Ezra 1:1-4). Daniel 1:21; 6:28; 10:1. See also Isaiah 44:28-45:1.

**Day** – Symbolizing a year in the time prophecies of Daniel and Revelation.

**Dragon** – Identified as Satan and the pagan Roman Empire used by him. Daniel 7:7; Revelation 12:3-4, 7, 9, 13, 16-17; 13:2, 4, 11; 16:13; 20:2.

**Dura** – Means “walled place” and may refer to the area between Babylon’s inner and outer walls. Daniel 3:1.

**Earth-dwellers** – Those whose future is tied to this perishing world, not heaven. Revelation 3:10; 6:10; 8:13; 13:8, 14; 14:6; 17:8.

**Edom, Moab, Ammonites** – Distant relatives of the Israelites, enemies of God’s people, possibly also those saved from spiritual Babylon. Daniel 11:41; see also Revelation 18:4.

**Elders** – 24 members of the heavenly council. Revelation 4:4, 10; 5:5-6, 8, 11, 14; 7:11, 13; 11:16; 14:3; 19:4.

**Eunuchs** – Possibly literal eunuchs, but the Hebrew word can also refer to high officials. Daniel 1:7-11, 18.

**Gog and Magog** – Symbolic of the wicked nations raised in the second resurrection, gathered by Satan to oppose God after the millennium. Revelation 20:7-9. See also Ezekiel 38-39.

**Hades** – The grave. Death and Hades surrender their dead and are ultimately destroyed. Revelation 1:18; 6:8; 20:13-14.

**Harvest** – Final gatherings of the saved and the lost at the Second Advent. Revelation 14:14-20.

**Horn** – Symbol of power, referring to a king or kingdom. Daniel 7:7-8, 11, 20, 21, 24; 8:3, 5-9, 20-22; Revelation 12:3; 13:1, 11; 17:3, 7, 12, 16.

**Image of the beast** – Church-state union operating like the medieval Papacy. Revelation 13:14-15; 14:9, 11; 20:4.

**Ishtar Gate** – A monumental entrance to Babylon, built c. 575 B.C. by King Nebuchadnezzar II, having blue-glazed bricks with reliefs of lions, bulls, and dragons.

**Jezebel** – Symbolic of an apostate religious system like the harlot Babylon. Revelation 2:20-23.

**Lake of Fire** – Place of fiery judgment and final destruction of the wicked. Revelation 19:20; 20:10, 14-15; 21:8.

**Lamb** – Symbol of Jesus Christ who was sacrificially slain and yet victorious. Revelation 5:6, 8, 12-13; 6:1, 16; 7:9-10, 17; 13:8; 14:1, 4, 10; 15:3; 17:14; 19:7, 9; 21:14, 22, 23; 22:1, 3.

**Lampstands** – Source of light identified as the seven churches. Revelation 1:12-13, 20.

**Lion of the Tribe of Judah** – Messianic title for Christ as the victorious king. Revelation 5:5.

**Locusts** – Means of divine judgment. Revelation 9:3, 7. See also Joel 1:4; Amos 4:9.

**Magicians, astrologers, and sorcerers** – These claimed the ability to communicate with the unseen world, practicing divination, magic, exorcism, and astrology. Daniel 1:20; 2:2, 27; 4:7; 5:11; Revelation 21:8; 22:15.

**Mark of the Beast** – Symbolic of loyalty to the beast and participation in its system of false worship in contrast to the Seal of God. Revelation 13:16-18; 14:9-11; 20:4.

**Michael** – Name for Jesus meaning “who is like God.” Daniel 10:13, 21; 12:1; Revelation 12:7-9.

**New Jerusalem** – City where redeemed humans live while in heaven during the millennium and thereafter located on the new earth. Revelation 21:2-22:5.

**Patmos** – Island in the Aegean Sea where John was exiled and received visions. Revelation 1:9.

**Rainbow** – Symbolic of God’s character of love, mercy, and justice. Revelation 4:3; 10:1.

**Remnant** – End-time believers in Christ who keep God’s commandments and have the testimony of Jesus. Revelation 12:17.

**Robes/white garments** – Symbolic of purity, vindication, and final victory. Revelation 3:4-5, 18; 6:11; 7:9, 13-14; 19:8.

**Satrap** – Means “protector of the kingdom,” referring to a variety of subordinate officials in Babylon. Daniel 3:2-3, 27; 6:1-4, 6-7.

**Seal of God** – Symbolic of loyalty to God and His commandments in contrast to the mark of the beast. Revelation 7:2-4; 14:1; 22:4.

**Second death** – Final death and destruction in the lake of fire. Revelation 2:11; 20:6, 14; 21:8.

**Seven Spirits of God** – Symbolic of the omniscience and omnipresent influence of the Holy Spirit. Revelation 1:4; 3:1; 4:5; 5:6. See also Zechariah 3:9; 4:10.

**Shinar** – Ancient geographical term for the region of Babylonia. Daniel 1:2.

**Spirit of Prophecy** – The Spirit-imparted gift of prophecy; the testimony of Jesus. Revelation 19:10; 22:9.

**Stars** – Heavenly bodies, but also symbolic of God’s people, messengers, or angels. Daniel 8:10; 12:3; Revelation 1:16, 20; 6:13; 8:10; 9:1; 12:1, 4.

**Sword** – Word of God by which all are judged. Revelation 1:16; 2:16; 19:15, 21. See also Ephesians 6:17; Hebrews 4:12.

**Testimony of Jesus** – Prophetic messages from Jesus through His prophets that carry divine authority like the Word of God. Revelation 1:2, 9; 12:17; 19:10.

**Three Angels’ Messages** – Three global end-time proclamations: the gospel in the context of the judgment and a call to worship the Creator; announcement of Babylon’s fall; and warning against worship of the beast and his image. Revelation 14:6-12. See also Revelation 18:1-4.

**Time** – Period of 360 days (or years in apocalyptic time prophecies). Daniel 4:16, 23, 25, 32; 7:25; 12:7; Revelation 12:14.

**Watcher** – A heavenly messenger or angel. Daniel 4:13, 17, 23.

**Waters/Sea** – Symbolic of peoples, multitudes, nations, and languages. Daniel 7:2-3; Revelation 13:1; 17:1, 15.

**Woman clothed with the sun** – Symbolic of God’s faithful people throughout history. Revelation 12:1-6, 13-17.

**Wormwood** – Notoriously bitter herb, symbolizing Satan, the great fallen star, who spreads poisonous heresy. Revelation 8:10-11.

**Zion** – Mount for worship of God, where the Lamb stands with the 144,000. Revelation 14:1.
