Explore

Set up a cubby house. Use fairy lights, flashlights, or candles (with careful adult supervision) to shine light inside the cubby house. Read the story again, sitting close with your child. Share how the Wise Men never gave up following the star. We can choose to always follow Jesus too.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#1D5A81"}}}
Play

Act out the story together, dressing up as Wise Men. You may also like to be a camel and give your child a ride (_plod, plod, plod; bumpity, bump, bump_). Have fun finding or making some props to be the three gifts for King Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#1D5A81"}}}
Serve

The Wise Men had three very special gifts to give Jesus. What gifts can you give Jesus? One gift we can give Jesus is to love and serve people around us. See if you can brainstorm an act of kindness to do every day this week for someone else, OR use the ideas below:\
**Sunday:** Make a healthy treat to eat and give to neighbors or friends.\
**Monday:** Write a card to someone you know who lives far away or someone who is lonely. Put it in the mail together.\
**Tuesday:** Look through clothes/toys you may have grown out of and donate to charity.\
**Wednesday:** Today, ask your child to surprise you by doing a job without being asked. See if you can spot them doing it.\
**Thursday:** Choose some Christian books you would like to give children who don’t have many. Donate them or wrap them up and give them to someone you know.\
**Friday:** When eating supper together, go around the circle and share something you love about each person in your family.
**Sabbath:** Invite someone to your house for a special Sabbath lunch. You could hand-deliver an invitation.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#1D5A81"}}}
Pray

The Wise Men kept following the star. Ask Jesus to help your family to always follow Jesus.
