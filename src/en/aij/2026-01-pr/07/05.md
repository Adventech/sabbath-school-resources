Finally the 120 years had passed. The ark was finished. Everything was ready. For the last time, Noah begged the people to trust God and come into the ark.

Imagine Noah’s voice echoing through the valley as he pleaded with the people again to believe God’s words. Imagine the crowds mocking him: _What a crazy man! How could the whole world be covered with water?_ Water never fell from the sky! Noah had heard this for years and years, but he still believed every word that God had said.

Suddenly the people stopped laughing. Their eyes opened wide in surprise. What was happening? **Read Genesis 7:2, 3.** Animals plodded and pranced toward the ark! The animals came in pairs; a male and female of every kind. Some animals (those referred to by God as clean animals) came in seven pairs. They spilled out of the woods, across the fields, over the hills, and went quietly, obediently, into the ark. Birds came too, flapping through the ark’s door. For seven days the animals kept coming and coming and coming—led by angels that no one could see.

Imagine Noah climbing the ramp leading to the ark’s door, his voice echoing across the valley one last time, asking all to choose God and be safe. He waited. No one came.

The Bible says, “Then the Lord said to Noah, ‘Come into the ark, you and all your household, because I have seen that you are righteous before Me in this generation’ ” (Genesis 7:1).

Just eight people obeyed God and went into the ark: Noah, his wife, and their sons—Shem, Ham, Japheth—and their wives. Only eight people were faithful and trusted God’s words.

Then the boat’s huge door started to close! Strong hands, invisible to everyone, pushed it shut. Yes, God had closed the door, and not even the strongest man could open it.

Though so sad about what would happen, God loved how Noah and his family had faithfully obeyed Him.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Why were the animals in twos and sevens? (Read about it in Genesis 7:2.)_
- _Why is it so hard to stand up for what’s right?_

God has always wanted to draw people (us!) close to Himself. In His love He still invites us to come to Him to be safe from the stormy troubles in our lives. He offers to carry our worries and burdens (Matthew 11:28-30). There is plenty of room inside His heart of protection and comfort.
