**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a rainbow stick and a card/poster that says “Yes, God was always with them!” Practice saying this phrase enthusiastically with the children. They will say it in response to your question after they have mimed each of the activities below.

1. **_“The animals are hungry!”_** (Mime feeding animals.)\
**Teacher:** “Was God with Noah and his family when they fed the animals?”\
**Children:** “Yes, God was always with them!”
2. **_“The wind’s getting stronger!”_** (Wave arms side to side.)\
**Teacher:** “Was God with them when the wind was getting stronger?”\
**Children:** “Yes, God was always with them!”
3. **_“The rain’s falling harder!”_** (Slap hands on thighs.)\
**Teacher:** “Was God with them when the rain was falling harder?”\
**Children:** “Yes, God was always with them!”
4. **_“We can leave the ark!”_** (Mime excitement.)\
**Teacher:** “Was God with them when they stepped onto dry land?”\
**Children:** “Yes, God was always with them!”

**Teaching Point:** God was with Noah and his family in the ark on the stormy sea, when they came out of the ark, and when they saw His beautiful rainbow promise in the sky. God will always be with us too!

Invite the children to wave their rainbow sticks as they repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. He is always with me.**
