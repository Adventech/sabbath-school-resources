**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source paper clips, a string and magnet, and a stick for a fishing game. Write names/places from the past few lessons on slips of paper or fish shapes (e.g., King Herod, John the Baptist, Nazareth, Simeon, Anna, Wise Men, angel, Peter, Philip).

Create footprints from various colors of papers and write the following words on the back: care, forgive, friend, give, good, grateful, happy, honest, kind, love, obey, patient, pray, trust, tell the truth, share, study God’s Word. Also source a picture of Jesus and a flashlight. Place the picture of Jesus with the flashlight shining on Him in a spot in the room. Then make a path out of the footprints that lead to the picture of Jesus.

Write the name of each disciple on a piece of paper, then fold and hide them in different places around the Sabbath School room or outside: Simon Peter, Andrew, James (son of Zebedee), John, Philip, Bartholomew, Matthew, Thomas, James (son of Alphaeus), Jude, Simon, Judas. To make it more difficult, also add some blank pieces of paper.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Share the **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.
