On the first day of Creation, God said, “Let there be light” (Genesis 1:3). And there was!

The next day, beautiful light shone brightly over the waters. It made the waters shimmer and sparkle.

God knew exactly what was needed next. He spoke out loud again. _What did He say?_ Read Genesis 1:6.

God created a wide band of sky the Bible calls “the firmament” (Genesis 1:8). The firmament split the water into two parts. Lots of little water droplets floated above the sky—and the rest of the water splished and splashed in the sea down below. Now fresh air filled the sky.

God made the wide band of sky on the second day because He knew that the plants, animals, and people He would soon create would need air to live. How creative and loving is our God!

Share with your child one of your favorite things that God has made. If possible, show pictures.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever tried to hold your breath? We can’t do it for very long—our faces get red and our eyes get watery after just a little while. God created our lungs to need air. He created air on the second day of Creation.
- How powerful is God’s breath? Read Psalm 33:6 to find out.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Take a big, deep breath, hold it for a moment, then thank God for air to breathe.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Have fun with the air that God made.

1. Blow up a brightly colored balloon to play with, or
2. Make a simple kite to fly on a windy day, or
3. Blow bubbles and watch them float.
