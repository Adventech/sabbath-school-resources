Pharaoh knew Joseph was right: he would need a wise man to help Egypt through this crisis. But where would he find a man like this? He sat pondering, and then the answer came to him. Of course! Joseph was just the man his kingdom needed! He said to his servants, “Can we find a better man than Joseph to take this job? God’s Spirit is truly in him” (Genesis 41:38, ICB). Then what did he say to Joseph? **Read Genesis 41:39-41.**

Joseph’s beautiful character, his wise words, and his God-given ability to interpret dreams had convinced the king that Joseph’s powerful God was real and that He was working in Joseph’s life. Potiphar believed this, the prison guard and prisoners had seen this, the king of Egypt knew this, and one day soon the whole of Egypt would know of Joseph’s God too.

Joseph’s dreadful prison days had finally come to an end. To his surprise, governor’s clothes and the king’s ring were quickly given to him. He was crowned second-in-command over all the land of Egypt. Truly, “the Lord was with Joseph” (Genesis 39:2).
