^[Word of the Week—GUIDANCE]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#1f5695", "size": "xl"}}})\
Guidance means to give advice and information to help do something, or solve a problem or difficulty. Guidance leads and directs us when we need help—like a light would guide someone on a dark path at night. Not all advice is good advice.

A Rotten Heart

King Herod stomped down the halls of his beautiful palace. The visit by the Wise Men from the East had left the king in a terrible mood. His robes swirled around him as he began to plot a wicked scheme.

The Wise Men had gone happily on their way. They didn’t know that Herod was hiding hatred and jealousy behind the nice words he had spoken to them when they had passed through Jerusalem searching for Baby Jesus. The King had said, “Go and search carefully for the young Child, and when you have found Him, bring back word to me, that I may come and worship Him also” (Matthew 2:8). But it was all a lie. Instead of worshipping Baby Jesus, King Herod secretly planned to kill Him!

Herod hated the way the Wise Men had called Baby Jesus the “King of the Jews” (Matthew 2:2). Herod was jealous; he wanted power all to himself! The more he thought about it, the angrier he became.

Where does the Bible say bad actions come from? **Read the words of Jesus in Mark 7:21-23.**

Herod is a sad example of what can happen to people’s hearts when they reject God and think only about themselves. The Wise Men had given him the opportunity to worship Jesus and get to know the wonderful God of heaven as they had. But Herod rejected their knowledge and didn’t want God’s guidance. A hard, hateful heart was the result. Herod would never know that God will “guide our feet into the way of peace” (Luke 1:79). How very sad.
