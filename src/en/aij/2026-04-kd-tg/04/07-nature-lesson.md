**Focus:** Jesus can help us to be brave, like the golden eagle.

_Note: Invite the children to do the actions in bold along with you._

Show the picture of the **Golden Eagle**.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/04/nature-lesson.png)

- **What do you notice about this beautiful creature?**

Let’s look at its wings. The Golden Eagle can fly really fast. It can fly faster than you can run. It’s faster than a cheetah. In fact, when it swoops down from the sky, it can fly more than 100 miles per hour (160 kilometers per hour). That’s even faster than a car! Can you **flap your arms like wings** to show how the Golden Eagle flies very fast?

Now let’s look at its eyes. The golden eagle is not as big as you are, but its eyes are about the same size as yours! That means that for its size, the Golden Eagle’s eyes are really big. They’re even bigger than its brain! The Golden Eagle’s big eyes help it to find food. It can see food even if it’s a mile or two (two or three kilometers) away. It might be flying high up in the sky, and it sees something for lunch. It locks its eyes on it and swoops down—very fast. It grabs it and flies off to its nest. The Golden Eagle keeps its eyes locked on its lunch, and that helps it to find enough to eat. Can you **put your hands like this (make hands into circles and place over eyes, like binoculars)** to show me the Golden Eagle’s big eyes?

God gave the Golden Eagle fast wings (**flap arms**) and big eyes (**hands form circles over eyes**), but the most amazing thing about the Golden Eagle is what it does when there is a storm. Golden Eagles are brave. When they see a storm coming, they don’t get scared and fly away from it—they fly right into it! That’s because Jesus made Golden Eagles to soar high in stormy winds. The Golden Eagle actually uses the winds of the storm to help it fly higher than it could just by flapping its wings. The strong winds push it high into the sky. Sometimes the Golden Eagle goes so high that it flies right over top of the storm—even higher than the clouds and rain! Isn’t it brave? Can you **flex your muscles** like this (demonstrate flexing your arm muscles), to show how the Golden Eagle is brave, even in a scary storm?

- **Can you use your body to show me three special things about the Golden Eagle?** (Fast wings—flap arms; big, clever eyes—hands in circles over eyes; brave—flex muscles.)
- **Who can make you brave, just like the Golden Eagle?** (Yes, Jesus! He can do anything. He can make us brave.)
