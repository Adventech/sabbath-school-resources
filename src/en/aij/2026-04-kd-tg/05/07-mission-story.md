**Focus:** Sofia learns that Jesus loves her and wants to be her friend.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Ukraine** on your **globe** or **world map**.

_Hand each child a happy face/sad face on a craft stick. Invite the children to hold up the face they think Sofia would have had at each point in the story._

Sofia liked to smile and laugh and talk with her friends at the public school in Vorzil, Ukraine ^[(smiling face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). But then she had a big fight with her best friend, Vernika ^[(frowning face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Vernika said some very unkind words to Sofia. “Nobody needs you,” she said. “You will never have any friends.” Then Vernika pushed Sofia against the school wall. Sofia’s head hurt. She had to go home from school early.

Sofia’s head got better, but her heart still hurt because of Vernika’s unkind words. Sofia stopped smiling and laughing. She became very quiet ^[(frowning face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She wondered if Vernika was right. Maybe she would never have any friends.

Mother saw Sofia’s sad face. That summer she asked Sofia, “Would you like to go to the new Adventist school that has opened up?”

“Yes!” said Sofia ^[(smiling face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

On the first day of school, Sofia felt very shy and lonely ^[(frowning face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). A girl in her class greeted her.

“Hi,” the girl said.

“Hi,” Sofia replied.

“What’s your name?” the girl asked.

Sofia didn’t answer. She just went to sit at her desk. She didn’t say anything else all day until it was time to go home.

“Bye,” said the boy sitting next to her.

“Bye,” Sofia said. And that was all.

Sofia hardly spoke at all for the first two weeks of school. She felt lonely and sad ^[(frowning face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She didn’t have any friends. _Nobody needs me,_ she thought. _Nobody even wants to talk to me._

A few days later Sofia’s teacher approached her after school. She asked why Sofia didn’t talk to anyone. Sofia told her teacher about the mean girl at her old school. The teacher listened to Sofia’s story. When she was finished, she asked, “Would you like to hear about Someone who forgave people who hurt Him?”

Yes, Sofia wanted to hear.

Sofia’s teacher told her about Jesus. Sofia had never heard of Jesus. She wanted to know more. She began to read the Bible at home. At school she began to talk to her classmates. And that was the start of the “new” Sofia. She became happy and friendly ^[(smiling face)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She even preached a sermon at church! And she wasn’t lonely anymore.

“Jesus loves everyone,” Sofia said. “Jesus changed my life.”

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
**Teaching Point:** When we feel lonely, we can talk to Jesus. Just as He helped Sofia, He will help us too.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He wants to be my close friend** [hug arms around own shoulders]**.”**
