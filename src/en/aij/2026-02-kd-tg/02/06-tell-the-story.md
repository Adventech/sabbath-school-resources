**Big Idea:** God is with me. He helps me to be honest.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- **Peg people:** Isaac, Rebekah, Jacob, and Esau, with a simple scene (fabric for a tent, grass, hill), small goat toys.
- Use **woolen fleece** or a furry blanket to cover a child as goat skins.
- Use a **bowl, toy vegetables**, and a **spoon** to mix up the stew together.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 2 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

- God had told Rebekah that Jacob would be the family leader ^[(Jacob peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Isaac wanted Esau. He would choose Esau when no one was looking ^[(Esau peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Isaac asked Esau to hunt and kill an animal and make him a stew. He would eat it, then bless him.
- Rebekah heard ^[(Rebekah peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She told Jacob she would make a goat stew. He was to take it to Isaac, lie, and pretend to be Esau to get the blessing.

**TELL THE STORY**

Isaac sat down with a sigh. He was very old now ^[(show Isaac peg man near a “tent”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He couldn’t see, and his feet moved slowly. He thought he wouldn’t be alive much longer. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was time to give one of his sons a special blessing and make him the new family leader. This son would lead everyone to love and follow God. Should it be Jacob or Esau? ^[(Hold up peg brothers.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Old Isaac smiled. Of course, it should be Esau! He was the firstborn after all, and birthright blessings were always given to the firstborn ^[(hold up Esau peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Mother Rebekah did not agree ^[(hold up Rebekah peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had told her long ago: “The older will serve the younger” (Genesis 25:23). She knew the blessing was for younger brother Jacob. ^[(Hold up Jacob peg.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

But Father Isaac had made up his mind. He would choose Esau—while no one was looking!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Quickly he called Esau to his side. In a whisper he asked him to go hunting, then make a stew for him to eat. “Then I will bless you before I die,” he said (Genesis 27:4, ICB). ^[(Esau peg.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Rebekah heard the secret whispers. Oh, no! Isaac was going to give the family blessing to Esau—today!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She raced off to find Jacob. She would fix this—her way! ^[(Rebekah peg person running to Jacob peg person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

She told Jacob to quickly bring her two goats. She would make them into a stew. Then Jacob would pretend to be Esau, take it to his father, and get the birthright blessing instead.

Jacob couldn’t believe it. His mother was telling him to lie!

Rebekah stirred the stew. Mmm! It was just as Isaac liked it. Quickly she poured it into a bowl ^[(vegetables, spoon, bowl)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Finding Esau’s clothes, Rebekah handed them to Jacob. He slid them over his head. Now Jacob smelled like his big brother, the hunter. But there was still one big problem. Jacob’s smooth skin didn’t feel like Esau’s hairy skin. She covered him in goatskins. Problem solved! Now Jacob smelled and felt like Esau. ^[(If possible, dress a child in a furry blanket, so others can feel their “hairy arms.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob took the stew to his father. “Father,” Jacob said in his best Esau voice. “I am Esau, your first son. I have done what you told me. Now sit up and eat. . . . Then bless me.”

Blind father Isaac frowned. “Are you really my son Esau?”

“Yes, I am,” Jacob answered (Genesis 27:18-24, ICB).

Oh, no! So much lying! Jacob held a lying stew, wore lying clothes, and had lying goat hair wrapped over his hands and neck. And now his words were lies too.

Old Isaac was tricked. He spoke God’s special blessing to Jacob. The birthright blessing was now his forever!

Jacob stepped out of his father’s tent. Soon after, Esau stepped inside, holding his bowl of stew. “Father, rise and eat the food that your son killed for you. Then bless me,” Esau said (Genesis 27:31, ICB).

What? Old Isaac was confused. “Who are you?” he asked.

“I am your son—your firstborn son—Esau,” came the reply (Genesis 27:32, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Old Isaac began to tremble all over. He had been tricked!

Esau was so upset he cried. ^[(Show Esau peg person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was very sad and angry! He begged his father to bless him also, but it was too late. The birthright blessing had been given to Jacob.

Isaac was very sorry that he had not trusted God’s plan to give the blessing to Jacob in the first place. Now his family had hurt each other with lies and tricks.

Esau began to hate his brother. He even planned to kill him! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When Rebekah heard about that, she told Jacob to run away ^[(Rebekah and Jacob peg people talking behind the tent privately)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was to go and live with her brother Laban, far away, where he would be safe.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob hugged his parents and cried as he said goodbye. Although Jacob had made many bad choices, God still loved him. He would always be with him.
