The sounds in the new world were restful. Soft breezes gently rustled the leaves in trees. Ocean waves curled and gently crashed onto sandy beaches. They were peaceful sounds.

But God wasn’t finished yet! The best was yet to come. On the fifth day God called out in a powerful voice. What did He say? Read Genesis 1:20, 21.

As soon as God spoke, fish and creatures of every kind appeared in the sea! Striped clown fish darted through coral, giant octopuses with their sticky tentacles blobbed and floated gracefully, friendly gray dolphins leaped playfully, and crabs scuttled along the ocean floor. All were happy together.

But that wasn’t all! God kept speaking! “Let birds fly above the earth” (Genesis 1:20). Suddenly the sky was filled with thousands of birds of all colors, shapes, and sizes. Tiny, speckled sparrows flitted here and there. Pink flamingos stood tall in shallow waters. Rainbow parrots and big-beaked toucans perched high in the trees.

There were squawks, chirps, whistles, and hoots on the land. There was splashing, diving, and swishing in the ocean. The earth had come alive with lots of different sights and sounds as amazing new creatures swam deep in the water and flew high in the sky.

God looked at His creation and saw that it was good. He was so very happy. The sun dipped behind the trees and gently turned the sky orange, then pink, then purple as the fifth day came to an end.
