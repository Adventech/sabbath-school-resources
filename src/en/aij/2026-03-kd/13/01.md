Not Welcomed in Nazareth

It was Sabbath, and Jesus had stopped to visit His family and friends in Nazareth. Nazareth was Jesus’ hometown—the place He had grown up. Jesus went to the synagogue and began to teach them from the Bible scrolls.

Jesus told the people that He was the promised Savior. The people “were amazed at the beautiful words he spoke” (Luke 4:22, ICB). But they didn’t think He was the Savior—they thought he was just Joseph’s son.

Jesus knew they didn’t really believe His words, and He told them so.

The Pharisees were furious! The people were angry too. They grabbed Jesus and pushed Him out of the synagogue. They shoved Him down the streets and out of town and wanted to throw Him off a cliff.

As the crowd dragged Jesus roughly down the road, something amazing suddenly happened. God’s angels made Jesus invisible. He turned and walked straight “through the crowd and went on his way” (Luke 4:30, ICB). It was a miracle! God had rescued Jesus from the angry crowd.

Jesus loved the people of Nazareth. But if they didn’t want to listen to Him, Jesus would go and do good somewhere else. Where did Jesus go? **Read Luke 4:31.**

- What do you think some people in the crowd might have thought when Jesus disappeared?
