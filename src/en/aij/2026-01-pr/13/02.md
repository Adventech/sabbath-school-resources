God’s promise to His friend Abram was very, very, very important. So important that God shared it again! Abram learned that the promise was part of something special called a covenant, in which God and he would make a promise to each other (Genesis 15). In Genesis 17 God’s part of the covenant was His promise to make Abram the father of a great nation. Abram’s part of the covenant was to love and obey God as his very best friend. To show that he understood how special the covenant was, Abram bowed facedown to the ground and reverently listened as God continued to speak. What else did God tell Abram? **Read Genesis 17:5.**

In Bible times names were chosen carefully, because the meaning of a name was important. It described the person or told something special about them. The name Abraham means “father of a great number,” so when God changed Abram’s name, it became a reminder that He would fulfill His promise.

Sarai also had a part in God’s covenant, so He changed her name to mean princess. What was it? **Read Genesis 17:15.**

God knows our hearts, so when we get to heaven, He will give each of us a new name too. These new names will reflect our friendship with God and all we have been through.

God tells us that He will write our new name in two places: “I will give him a white stone, and on the stone a new name written which no one knows except him who receives it” (Revelation 2:17). And “for each there is a crown, bearing his own ‘new name’ ” (Ellen G. White, _The Great Controversy_, p. 646).

A white stone with your new name and a sparkling crown will be such precious gifts. We can patiently wait to receive them, for our loving God fulfills His promises.
