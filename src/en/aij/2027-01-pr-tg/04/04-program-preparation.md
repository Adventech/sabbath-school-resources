**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source Suggested Storytelling Props: **plague pyramid craft, flatbread, coins, backpack, small leaved branches tied together, sandals, staff, sand, bitter herbs (dandelion or bitter leafy greens)**.

Source a picture of a lamb, and reuse the following Storytelling Props to **Unpack the Story**: bread, branches, gold and silver coins, sandals/staff, bitter herbs.

Source two pieces of paper. On one, write: “The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, ‘Behold! The lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!’ ” (John 1:29).

On the other piece of paper, write: “In Christ we are set free by the blood of His death. And so we have forgiveness of sins because of God’s rich grace” (Ephesians 1:7, ICB). Fold both pieces of paper until small.
