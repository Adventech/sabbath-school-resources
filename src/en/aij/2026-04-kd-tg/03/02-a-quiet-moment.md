**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

A strong arm grabbed the corner of his mat; another arm gripped the opposite corner; while above him, two more hands wrapped tightly around his mat. In unison the four friends lifted him up. The man with paralysis, who lay on that mat, had many challenges. But thanks to his four friends—and their faith in Jesus—he had hope.

His friends were willing to do anything to get him to Jesus. They strained, and heaved, and risked embarrassment and rebuke to not only carry their friend to Jesus but to get him to the very feet of Jesus. That’s what love does. Nothing stopped those four men from seeking healing for their friend.

Jesus rewarded their faith with a double miracle. “Son, your sins are forgiven. . . . I say to you, arise, take up your bed, and go to your house” (Mark 2:5-11). He healed the man from the sin in his heart and from its effects on his body. The man became whole, and his friends rejoiced in the miracles.

Is there someone in your life who needs to be lifted up this week? Jesus has the power to transform their lives—and yours—at the same time! Invite the Holy Spirit to show you how to best encourage and bring them to the feet of Jesus. All sorts of obstacles may be in their way: shame, rejection, pain, or lack of resources. Be a friend who can clear the way so they can connect with the Savior.

May you be inspired today to also bring the children in your Sabbath School class to Jesus, as you challenge them to do the same with those they love.
