In all He did on earth Jesus showed His heart of love—a heart that overflowed with boundless love for everyone He met. His love for us is just as personal and limitless. “So Christ, the heavenly Shepherd, cares for His flock that is scattered throughout the world. He knows us all by name. He knows the very house in which we live, and the name of each inmate. He cares for each one as if there were not another in the whole world” (Ellen G. White, _The Story of Jesus_, pp. 75, 76).

Take a moment to consider these verses that express God’s love for you and your child. Place your name in the verses:

“Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called children of God!” (1 John 3:1).

“Nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 8:39).

“The Lord your God in your midst, the Mighty One, will save; He will rejoice over you with gladness, He will quiet you with His love, He will rejoice over you with singing” (Zephaniah 3:17).

The Creator of the universe, who is capable of amazing miracles, cares for _**you**_ as a loving Father. He knows _**your**_ name, He knows _**your**_ children, and He loves _**you**_ with His great, enormous love! May you “be able to comprehend with all the saints what is the width and length and depth and height—to know the love of Christ which passes knowledge; that you may be filled with all the fullness of God” (Ephesians 3:18, 19).
