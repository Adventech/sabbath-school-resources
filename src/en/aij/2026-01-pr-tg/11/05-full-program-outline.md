**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children as they arrive. Invite them to share how they were able to shine as one of
God’s twinkling stars last week.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Have the children use their **Character Cards** to engage with each other.

- **Which is your favorite memory verse?**
- **How many memory verses can you recite?**
- **Describe one of the characters for each other to guess.**
- **Mime part of a character’s story for each other to guess.**

**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“God Is My Faithful Friend”** and a song of your choice. Sing the prayer song, **“Sabbath Song.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-is-my-faithful-friend.mp3] “God Is My Faithful Friend”

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/sabbath-song.mp3] “Sabbath Song”

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Invite several children to pray, remembering to thank God for being with you as you learn together.

**^[BIBLE FOCUS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“The Books of the Bible.”** Share the **Bible Fast Fact:** There are 185 songs in the Bible. From these, 150 of them are in the book of Psalms. Psalms is the Bible’s songbook and poetry book.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/books-of-the-bible.mp3] “The Books of the Bible”

**^[GIVE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Have a child collect the offering as you all sing **“Thank You, Jesus.”** Later, after the money has been counted by an adult, invite a child to color the next section of your **Offering Chart** to show how much has been collected this quarter. Then share this week’s **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission project. Say a short prayer for God to bless and take the offering to where it’s most needed.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/thank-you-jesus.mp3] “Thank You, Jesus”

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Warmly welcome any visitor and sing the **“Visitor Song.”** Sing **“Happy Birthday”** to a child who has had a birthday this week, and pray for God to bless them.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FEF5E4", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a quick **walk ‘n’ talk** activity. Draw their attention to the five **Making the Right Choice cards** attached to the walls around the room. Explain that they will work their way clockwise around each of the cards and discuss what the right choice would be in **small groups**.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Briefly review each card.

- **What did each of the examples require you to be?** (Unselfish.)
- **How easy/hard is it to be unselfish?**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** As we learn more about Abram in today’s lesson, we will be reminded of our Big Idea. Read the **Big Idea** together: **When God is my friend, He will help me put others first.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF0E8", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite two children to choose one of the **two small gifts** on your serving tray. Commend a child if they let the other person choose first. Repeat the activity with pairs of children until everyone has a gift. Prompt for unselfishness, commend acceptance of a lesser gift or offers to unselfishly swap, etc.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How natural was it for you to be unselfish and let the other person choose first?**
- **Don’t answer out loud: When choosing a gift, were you more like Abram or Lot?**
- **Why did Abram let Lot choose first?** (Abram prayed, God showed him what to do, and he obeyed.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Because of sin, we are naturally selfish. But when we are God’s friend, He will help us to be unselfish
like Him.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 11: Abram and Lot** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and have the children recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song “Jeremiah 31:14.”** Share that Abram was satisfied with God’s goodness and had unselfishly put Lot first.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/11-jeremiah-31-14.mp3] “Jeremiah 31:14”

**^[MEMORY VERSE [OPTION]:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** If time allows, invite your children to quickly find the **memory verse words** hidden in the room and work together to assemble the memory verse. Before beginning, challenge them to act unselfishly during this activity.

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your jar of sand or dust.

- **How many grains of sand (or dust) might there be in this jar?**
- **Could you count them?**

Remind the children of God’s wonderful promise to Abram and how we are Abram’s descendants when we are God’s friends. Millions and millions of other people are too. What a privilege!

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) Use your Sabbath School craft as a prop, and share last week’s story with someone who ives near you and may not know God as their friend yet.**

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring children back together to sing **“God Bless You.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-bless-you.mp3] “God Bless You”

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Before you pray, say that you will have a silent part in the prayer where they can ask God to help them put others first this week, just as Abram did.

Ask God to be with the children and their families and to guide them in their friendship with Him this week. Pause for personal prayer. Ask God to help them choose to unselfishly put others first.
