### Harvest Decisions for Christ and Baptism

After cultivating interests with Bible studies, it is time to reap a ^[HARVEST]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) of spiritual decisions that culminate in baptism. Full-grown wheat is a blessing only when someone gathers the harvest. In the same way, we are counseled to make appeals to those studying Bible truth, urging them to follow Christ and the light revealed to them. While this can and should be done in personal settings, one of the most proven means of gaining decisions has also been public evangelistic meetings conducted in traditional or small-group formats.

“The ^[harvest truly is great]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), but the laborers are few; therefore pray the Lord of the harvest to send out ^[laborers into His harvest]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (Luke 10:2).\
“And now why are you waiting? ^[Arise and be baptized]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), and wash away your sins, calling on the name of the Lord” (Acts 22:16).

### ^[ESTABLISH AN EVANGELISTIC REAPING MINISTRY:]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#EFAF44", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})

- ^[APPOINT]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) an evangelism coordinator and other necessary team members<sup>*</sup> annually for your harvest activities and events.
- ^[BUDGET]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) annually for public evangelistic efforts.
- ^[PLAN]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) ways to inspire, equip, and involve members in gaining decisions and preparing people for baptism, while also scheduling at least one evangelistic series (any format) each year with an emphasis on securing decisions for Christ and baptism.
- ^[COMBINE]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) personal effort with public meetings—prayer, friendship, guidance, invitations, visitation/contact, Bible studies, appeals, baptismal preparation, etc.
- ^[PROVIDE]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) leadership support—prayer, promotion, financial support, and participation.

<sup>*</sup> Public evangelism teams may include speakers and study leaders, as well as coordinators for prayer, visitation, advertising, registration, children’s program, platform host, music, audiovisual, materials, refreshments, etc.
