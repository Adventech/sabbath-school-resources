**Focus:**  Disobedience has consequences.

**Location:** Help a child locate **New Guinea** on your **globe** or world **map**.

“Come on, William,” his friends called. “We’re going to the forest to play!”

William liked playing with his friends, but his mother had warned him, “Don’t play in the forest. It’s dangerous, and you could get hurt.”

But playing hide-and-seek in the forest was so much more fun; there were so many good places to hide. William jumped up and ran toward his friends. He’d go this once. Mom wouldn’t know.

William crouched low as one of the boys searched near his hiding place. When the boy had passed by, William darted toward the tree that was home base. He didn’t see the vine that curled across his path. It tangled around his leg and tossed him into the air. He landed in a deep ditch. He tried to stand up and reach the vine to pull himself up, but he couldn’t reach it.

“Help!” he cried. “Help!” Some of his friends heard William call and ran to find him. They pushed the vine down so William could grab it, then they pulled him out of the ditch. He tried to stand, but his bare foot hurt. Two of his friends helped him walk home.

William was glad his mom was asleep when he arrived home. He didn’t want to tell her he’d gotten hurt disobeying her. He washed his foot and looked at a cut on the bottom. It hurt to walk on it, but maybe no one would notice.

But moms notice things, and William’s mom noticed he was limping. “What happened to your foot?” she asked.

“I got a cut while I was playing,” William said. At least that much is true, he thought.

“Where were you playing?” she asked, as she took a close look a William’s foot. “In the field,” he lied.

William’s mom found out the truth, and William was punished twice—once for disobeying and playing in the forest, and once for lying.

“I learned that it doesn’t pay to lie,” William said. “And it doesn’t pay to disobey, either. Mom reminded me that parents don’t make rules just to keep us from having fun. They want to keep us safe.’”

Ask: **What choice should William have made?**

**Teaching Point:** When we do not obey, it can have consequences that cause pain and make us sad. Our big loving God can help us to make good choices.
