Before long, Baby Jesus was 40 days old, and it was time to dedicate Him to the Lord at the temple in Jerusalem. Joseph couldn’t afford to buy a lamb, so he put two young pigeons in a cage for a sacrifice, and five silver coins in a money bag to pay the dedication fee. Then his little family made the five-mile trip to Jerusalem.

Mary held Baby Jesus close. Joseph smiled at their little Baby as they walked. Soon they could thank God for the privilege of looking after His precious Son. They wanted to dedicate Jesus to God. And to see Him receive God’s dedication blessings from the temple priest.

Many Christian parents today dedicate their babies to God too. A little ceremony is held at church. The baby doesn’t understand what is happening, but the parents and the church members do. Mothers and fathers are publicly saying that they want their child—their precious gift from God—to grow up loving and trusting Jesus. What else are parents committing to at the dedication of their baby? **Read Psalm 78:4.**

Parents know they need God’s help to be wise and kind parents, so the pastor prays for both the parents and the baby, asking God to bless the little family with His love as the baby grows up. Church members may also commit to teach the child to love Jesus.

When Jesus was an adult, He took children in His arms and blessed them too. **Read about it in Mark 10:13-16.** What a wonderful thing to have God’s blessings and be dedicated to Him as a little child.
