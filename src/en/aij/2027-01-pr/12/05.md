Exciting news rippled around the Israelite camp. The workers likely cheered when they heard it. Mothers smiled and children clapped. Everything for God’s beautiful sanctuary was finished!

Moses blessed the people because they had done exactly what God had said. All that needed to happen now was for the sanctuary to be set up. After about six months of hard work, this was a wonderful day. What did God say to Moses? **Read Exodus 40:1, 2.**

The spots for the courtyard fence posts were mapped out. The places for the walls of the tabernacle were measured. Aaron and his sons were chosen to be the priests. The Levites, one of the Israelite tribes, were chosen to take care of the tabernacle.

In the early-morning sunlight on New Year’s Day, Moses brought the gold-covered timber boards and hooked them together to make a frame. When the glittering frame was in place, the linen curtain roof was stretched over the top. Then it was covered with curtains of animal skins for the walls. They would protect the beautiful tabernacle and its golden furniture from the desert sand and winds.

Everyone watched Moses take the golden ark into the Most Holy Place. Then in front of the ark he hung beautiful curtains embroidered with angels to separate the two rooms. Next the altar of incense, the table of showbread, and the lovely golden lampstand were carefully arranged in the Holy Place. A front curtain was hung, and then the altar and the washbasin were moved into place in the courtyard. Finally, the courtyard fence curtains were hung.

The people gazed in silent awe at God’s beautiful finished sanctuary. Now their loving God could come close to dwell with them; closer than ever before. They couldn’t wait.
