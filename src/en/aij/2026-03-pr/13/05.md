News of the miracle spread like wildfire around Capernaum that Sabbath afternoon. The people had been amazed by the Great Teacher in the synagogue, but then Jesus had healed Peter’s mother-in-law as well! The story was told from house to house and around every street corner.

The people’s hearts filled with hope—maybe they could be healed too! But they didn’t dare come to Jesus on Sabbath, as they were afraid of making the rabbis, who thought it a sin to heal on Sabbath, angry. As soon as the sun set, their doors flew open, and the people of Capernaum surged out into the streets.

From all over town everyone headed toward the humble home where Jesus was staying. Limping, leaning on walking sticks, and supported by friends, the people came. “And He laid His hands on every one of them and healed them” (Luke 4:40). What else did Jesus do for some of these faith-filled people? **Read Luke 4:41.**

Jesus shared His love and God’s healing power as He blessed every single person. And as each one was healed, the joy was shared right down the line of people waiting to meet Jesus.

The night was dark, and the hour was late when the last person left. Silence finally settled down upon Capernaum. Grateful silence, contented silence, joyful silence. It had been a day like no other. God’s promise “For you shall go out with joy, and be led out with peace” (Isaiah 55:12) had come true in a special way that night.

When Jesus blesses people’s lives in wonderful ways, the news—and the joy—can quickly spread. Become a messenger of this good news! Jesus also loves to be able to bless many people at once, just as He does at church, so they can experience His love together.
