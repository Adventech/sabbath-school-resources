The crowd pressed in closely around Jesus. The sick woman kept trying to get to Jesus. “If only I may touch His clothes, I shall be made well,” she said to herself (Mark 5:28). As Jesus was passing, she drew on all her strength and stretched out her arm. Her fingertips barely managed to touch the hem of His clothes. Every little bit of the woman’s faith was focused in that one touch.

Then what happened? **Read Mark 5:29.**

Imagine how the woman felt to have no more pain or bleeding, no more tiredness or worry. It felt so good to be well!

Her heart bubbled over with joy for the miracle that Jesus had done for her! Jesus knew her need, and His power had healed her. Her body was healthy, her faith had grown, and her heart was very happy.

Deep down inside, she knew the truth of Jesus’ words “I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly” (John 10:10). Her suffering had ended when she reached out to Jesus. And He had given her “life in all its fullness” (John 10:10, ICB).

Jesus loves to heal us, too. Sometimes He works an amazing miracle to heal our bodies instantly, and sometimes His power heals our cuts and fevers slowly.

When we reach out to Him, He also heals our broken hearts (Psalm 147:3) and our weak faith (2 Corinthians 12:9). His death on the cross heals us from sin when we accept Him as our Savior (Isaiah 53:5). We can always reach out to Jesus for different kinds of healing.
