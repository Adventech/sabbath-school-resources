No doubt, your hands are constantly in motion as helping hands. Clasp them together now. What a gift from God! You have been wonderfully created to be a contributor. God knows that we will find purpose and joy in giving and doing for others. “To perform every duty as unto the Lord throws a charm around the humblest employment and links the workers on earth with the holy beings who do God’s will in heaven” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 574).

It does us well to remember this at the end of a long day, when hands, head, and heart have worked hard. In serving your family, you have served our Creator. Such a perspective puts rays of joyful light on those heavy tasks that never seem to end.

“Integrity in little things, the performance of little acts of fidelity and little deeds of kindness, will gladden the path of life; and when our work on earth is ended, it will be found that every one of the little duties faithfully performed has exerted an influence for good—an influence that can never perish” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 574).

Like Samuel’s, your helpful hands matter. Your faithfulness, joy, and kindness in the little deeds you do this week make a difference. As you go about your work this week, remember that you are working for the Lord.

“And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men, knowing that from the Lord you will receive the reward of the inheritance; for you serve the Lord Christ” (Colossians 3:23, 24).
