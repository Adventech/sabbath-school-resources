**Big Idea:** I can be faithful to God by spending time with Him and obeying His voice.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images. Props: **Faithfulness T-shirt, marker**, bag of **building objects**. Use a **model ark** and **animals**, or a felt set and board. **Spray bottle with water** inside.

**Note** Write Genesis 6:5, 6 and Genesis 7:10, 11 on the whiteboard and teach the children how to use the references at the top of the pages in the Bible to help them find it.
```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam lived to be 930 years old! During those years he faithfully told all of his children and grandchildren what it was like to live in perfect Eden, and about the sad day they had disobeyed God. They could even go to the gates of Eden to see the angels guarding the Garden. Adam told the story of his fall, how very important it was to follow God, and how God promised to send Someone to save them from the evil world. Some people listened, but many didn’t. They became proud, mean, and selfish.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) There was one special man named Enoch who loved God very much. He was kind and humble. The Bible says, “Enoch walked with God” (Genesis 5:22). ^[**What does this mean?** (It means he and God were on the same path. Enoch loved God with all his mind and heart. He agreed with what God said and was faithful and obedient to God’s law.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) God hadn’t yet given the Ten Commandments to His people, but He had taught them to follow His law of showing love to Him and to others. God helped Enoch to show this love by being kind and gentle. He also gave him a strong, wise, and growing mind. ^[(Show T-shirt: it was as if they were in the Faithfulness T-shirt together.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Enoch preached to the people about God’s love. He also pleaded with them to follow God and stop doing wrong. Then one day, when Enoch was old, his best friend God took him to heaven!

Hundreds of years went by. ^[**What were the people like, and how did God feel about them? Read Genesis 6:5, 6.** (They were very wicked; they did evil continually. God was very sad.)]]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

God knew that if He didn’t do something, there would be no one left through whom the Savior could be born. God tried to turn people’s hearts back to Him again and again, but only a few listened.

God decided He would send a huge flood to cover the whole world. But first He wanted to warn everyone because He loved them. He needed a faithful man to help Him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God chose Noah ^[(show felt and board)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He told Noah to build a big boat, called an ark, and told him exactly how to build it. Noah listened and obeyed all of God’s instructions.

Noah and his sons got to work, sawing, hammering, and building the ark ^[(show building tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The ark had rooms for people and animals, and one door on the side. Everyone who chose to go in through that door would be safe from the flood.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah told everyone about God’s plan to wash the world clean. Most people thought he was crazy, but that didn’t stop Noah. He faithfully built and preached for 120 years!

Spending time with God every day helped Noah to be so faithful.

Finally, the ark was finished.

Noah tried one last time to invite the people to come into the ark to be saved from the coming flood! They just laughed and made fun of him. _Rain? Water had never fallen from the sky!_

Suddenly the people stopped laughing. Their eyes opened wide in surprise.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Animals plodded and pranced toward the ark! ^[(Felt set or toy animals and ark.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They marched in twos, out of the woods, across the fields, over the hills, and into the ark. The clean animals came in seven pairs. Birds came too, flying through the ark’s door. For seven days they kept coming and coming—led by angels that no one could see.

But only eight people trusted God and came into the ark: Noah, his wife, their sons—Shem, Ham, Japheth—and their wives.

Then God closed the door. Not even the strongest man in the world could open it.

For seven days Noah and his family patiently waited inside the ark, trusting that the flood would come just as God had said. ^[**What happened after seven days? Read Genesis 7:10, 11.** (It began to rain.)]]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lightning flashed, and thunder roared. Huge raindrops began to fall, and water exploded out of the ground. ^[(Spray water bottle lightly over children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) What a huge storm!

Some of the people pounded on the ark door—but it was closed tight. It was too late. The water slowly covered the whole world. All living things outside the ark were destroyed.

Inside the ark, Noah and his family and all the animals were safe. Angels hovered over the ark to keep it safe through the storm—just as God had promised. He is always faithful.
