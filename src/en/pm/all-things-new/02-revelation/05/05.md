[[Revelation 5:1-5]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- In 1962 a scroll sealed with seven seals was found in caves near Jericho. Further study revealed it to be an ancient legal document. Such scrolls usually contained contracts or wills. In this case it contained heavenly records that were deeply meaningful for the future of God’s people.
- John is therefore deeply interested in the contents of this heavenly scroll, for it contains a complete record of God’s covenant with His people! It records humanity’s response to that covenant, as well as Satan’s crimes against it. The seals contain prophecies of how Jesus would work out the details of that covenant to preserve and care for His followers down through the ages.
- So no wonder John wept! For if evil is ever to be destroyed; if Christ’s justice, goodness, and love are ever to rule the universe; then this covenant scroll must be unsealed.

[[Revelation 5:6-7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Jesus is here portrayed as a lamb that has been killed and resurrected—the very acts that enable Him to take the covenant scroll, open it, and work from the heavenly sanctuary on behalf of His followers.
- This heavenly scene most likely takes place shortly after Christ’s death, resurrection, and ascension. But it is also undoubtedly a representation of how Christ’s followers will look to Him and His heavenly mediation for them.

[[Revelation 5:8-14]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The heavenly scene in chapters 4 and 5 indicates that all heaven sees Christ’s sacrifice for humanity as completely sufficient and effective. Jesus, our rightful Lord and High Priest in the heavenly sanctuary, has “redeemed us to God by [His] blood out of every tribe and tongue and people and nation” (verse 9).
- Because of what Jesus did on the cross and is doing for us in heaven, humanity now has hope!
