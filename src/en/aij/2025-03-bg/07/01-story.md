^[Home]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#702413", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})

_Clink, clink_ went the silver coins into a basket. The little girl clapped her hands. She was so happy to give to God! Another child waited behind her, carrying a gold pot. It was for God too! Everyone in camp lined up to happily give their precious things to God. They had shiny gold rings, pots, and other things. They had cups and bowls in silver and bronze. They had jewels that sparkled in the golden sun. They gave these things to make God’s new home.\
God’s new home would sparkle. God’s new home would shine. But best of all, God’s new home would be right in the middle of camp! God had told Moses to build Him a home called a tabernacle. He wanted to be with His people, right there in the middle of them all.

^^^

The baskets of coins filled up . . . up . . . up. _Clink, clink._ The people kept giving. The piles of pots grew higher . . . and higher . . . and higher. Until . . . stop! Moses held up his hands. He counted the baskets. There was more than enough! He was so happy. The people had worked together to give lots and lots of precious things to God. Now they could make God’s beautiful home.\
The people looked for ways to help. Some became builders _(point to builders)._ Hammer, hammer, hammer. The builders used wood, gold, silver, and bronze to make beautiful things for God’s home. Some people carried heavy things, and others showed them where to put them _(point to picture)._ Some people became sewers _(point to sewers)._ Sew, sew, sew went their little needles. They made beautiful purple, red, and blue curtains for God’s home. Some people became helpers _(point to helper with food)._ They brought food and water to the workers.

The people were so excited that God would live in the middle of their camp! They were so happy to help God by working together. Just as the people gave with happy hearts, we too can give to Jesus. Jesus loves it when we give Him our prayers, songs, and time to listen to Bible stories. He loves it when we give Him our offerings. He loves it when we work together to help His work.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, Jesus, that I can work with others to help You!
