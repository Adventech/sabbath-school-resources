On a different Sabbath morning, a woman walked slowly into the synagogue. She had a very sore, bent-over back. She couldn’t stand up straight.

How long had her back been bent over? **Read Luke 13:11.**

She hadn’t been able to stand up straight for 18 years! Can you bend over and count to 18? That is a lot of years. Imagine not being able to look up to see fluffy white clouds or a bird’s nest high in a tree. Imagine not being able to see people’s smiles or watch their faces as they tell a funny story. For 18 years the woman had been leaning over, looking at the dusty ground.

As the woman walked into the synagogue, she could hear the voice of someone reading from the Scripture scrolls. She couldn’t see His face properly, but she began to listen closely to His gentle, powerful words.

Who do you think this Man was?

Yes, it was Jesus! Jesus was the best Teacher. He “did not teach like their teachers of the law. He taught like a person who had authority [power]” (Matthew 7:29).

Jesus is still our best Teacher. We can read His words in the Bible.

The woman smiled to herself as she listened. She felt like she was in the presence of a kind king, whose words of wisdom and love were to be respected and listened to.
