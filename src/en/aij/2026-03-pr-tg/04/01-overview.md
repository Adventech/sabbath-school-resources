**Word of the Week**

**Guidance:** Guidance means to give advice and information, to help do something, or solve a problem or difficulty. Guidance leads and directs us when we need help—like a light would guide someone on a dark path at night. Not all advice is good advice.

**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the way God guides the lives of those who are willing to listen to Him. God guided the Wise Men to baby Jesus so they could worship Him and He guided them safely home again. He guided Joseph and Mary to flee King Herod’s wrath and anger at finding out there was a baby threatening his reign on the throne, then guided them back home again. Four times God sent an angel messenger to Joseph and each time Joseph listened and obeyed, trusting God’s protection and guidance.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can guide their lives.
- **Feel** they can trust God to guide their lives.
- **Respond** by giving their hearts to God and listening to Him and following His ways.
