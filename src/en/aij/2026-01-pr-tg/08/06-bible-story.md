**Big Idea:** I can thank God for His protection and trust all His promises.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images. You may like to use a **felt set** to create the story as you tell it. Or you may like to give two children **two birds** to use as the raven and dove. Props: the **altar** made by children who arrived early; a **toy lamb**. Numbers 1 to 10 written on individual sheets of paper.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Safe inside the ark, Noah and his little family were protected by their loving God. It rained for 40 days and 40 nights. That much rain buried everything and washed all the badness away.

Right on time, just as God had promised, He stopped the rain! In its place, He sent howling winds to help dry up the water.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then God guided the ark into peaceful waters on top of a group of mountains. The little family was grateful that their home had stopped rolling and tossing around!

They continued to care for all the beautiful, slimy, fluffy, squawky, growly animals in the ark. The family threw grain to the birds and bananas to the monkeys. They swept out the lions’ area and shoveled out elephant poo. Some jobs were fun, and others weren’t, but they faithfully did them all.

They waited and wondered how long it would be until they could leave the ark. **^[How long did the water cover the earth? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Genesis 7:24.** ^[(150 days, which equals five months!)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

Then one day Noah let a raven go to test if the earth was dry or not (toy/picture). It returned—there was too much water around for it to land. A week later he let a white dove fly away, but it returned too.

Noah waited . . . Seven days later he released the dove again. This time it came back with an olive leaf in its beak— there were growing plants somewhere out there! Noah waited another week . . . Then he let it go again. This time it didn’t return—it had found a new home!

Noah and his family were so excited! Soon they would be free to explore their new home. But they would be patient. God had shut the ark’s door, and God would let them out. Their job was to wait patiently while God allowed the land to dry out and plants to grow so the family could survive outside the ark. They kept on patiently waiting and trusting God to protect them.

Let’s pause the story for a moment. _(Note: Take no longer than five minutes to do the following activity.)_

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3", "size": "lg"}}}
**DISCUSS**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}
- **What did God protect Noah and his family from during the year they were in the ark?** (Stormy seas, howling winds, the ark capsizing, animals that would normally be wild, boredom, fear, etc.)
- **What has God protected you from during the last year?** (Share how God protects us from many dangers or scary things we don’t even know about!) Briefly share how the story of Noah reflects how God will save us from this world of sin through Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3", "size": "lg"}}}
**DO**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}
Lay the numbers 1 to 10 on the ground to create a rating scale. Invite children to stand on a number in response to the questions. 1: not at all; 10: very much.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}
- **How patient do you think Noah and his family were while they waited in the ark?**
- **How patient are you while waiting for something you have been promised?**
- **How big a difference was there in where you stood for both questions?** (Discuss possible reasons for a difference.) Remember, God will help us to wait patiently!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Let’s continue our story. One day it happened! God opened the heavy door to the ark. Eight happy people leaped to their feet and set the animals free. The waiting was finally over!

How wonderful it was to stand upon dry ground. **^[What did Noah do first? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Genesis 8:20.** ^[(Built an altar and offered animal sacrifices to thank God.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah and his family had so much to thank God for! ^[(Use altar of building blocks; toy lamb.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When an altar was built and the animals placed on top, the little family knelt together and began to worship God. They thanked Him for protecting and saving them through the roughest seas and the wildest storm. God accepted the gift of their praise and thanks; He loved His faithful children.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah and his family were shocked when they took a good look around. All the beauty had been washed away. The land was rough and dirty, without trees, and with jagged, bare mountains.

God saw their sadness, and He understood, for He must have felt it too. God had a special surprise to share with Noah and his family. It was a beautiful thing they had never seen before, and He knew how happy it would make them.

With a sudden burst of color, a beautiful rainbow blazed in a big arch across the sky. They gazed up in awe at the first rainbow this world had ever seen.

Even more beautiful than the rainbow was the message of love it sent. It was God’s way of telling them that He would never flood the whole earth again.

**^[Did you know there’s a rainbow in heaven? Where is it? Read]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) Revelation 4:2, 3.** ^[(There is a rainbow around His throne in heaven. God was sharing a very special piece of heaven with His children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58B0E3"}}}) ^[► (Show the picture of God’s throne in the Picture PowerPoint.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

A rainbow would always remind people that God keeps His promise to love and care for His children.
