**Focus:** We can trust Jesus for all things!

**Location:** Help a child locate **Indonesia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Armi and his friend smiled as they began their long hike—eight hours up to a far-off mountain village. Armi was a missionary teacher in Papua, Indonesia. One of his students had told him about a woman in her jungle village who couldn’t walk. Armi didn’t know much about medicine, but he hoped to cheer her up.

Armi and his friend found the woman crying in pain on a bed. Her right knee had an open wound, and it was swollen. She had fallen out of a tree seven years earlier and hadn’t been able to walk since that time. There were no doctors or hospitals. And after the fall her husband left her and their four children.

The woman was glad for the visit. She had lost all hope.

Armi and his friend stepped outside of the hut to pray. “Lord, please show us how to help this woman even though we don’t know much about medicine.”

Back inside the hut, Armi and his friend carefully cleaned the wound. They didn’t really know what they were doing. Neighbors gathered to watch. Armi said, “Only one Person can heal you—Jesus.”

The woman had never heard of Jesus. None of the villagers had heard of Jesus. But together they prayed, and the woman’s face filled with peace.

Armi promised to return next week. As he and his friend hiked home, they prayed. How could they get medical advice? They had no cell phone (mobile) signal. They were living in the mountains and hadn’t had a signal for months.

Then a miracle happened. Just a few days after Armi had visited the woman, his phone rang. He had a cell phone signal! He thanked God and quickly called a friend who was a nurse. She told him how to clean the wound and what pills to give the woman. The call lasted two minutes; then the signal disappeared. It was just enough time!

For the next two months Armi and his friend visited the woman every two weeks and cared for her wound. They urged her to pray to Jesus, and shared stories about Him with her and her neighbors.

The swelling slowly went down, and the wound healed. One day she could even walk again! “I believe that it’s because of Jesus,” the woman said. She had learned to trust Him even when things seemed hopeless.

In time she began to walk eight hours every week to worship in Armi’s church. She also sent her children and all the village children to the jungle mission school.

“Things that are impossible for me are not impossible for God,” Armi said.
