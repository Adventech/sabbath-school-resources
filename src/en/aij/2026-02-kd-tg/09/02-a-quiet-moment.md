**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Your heart beats, your lungs fill with air, your brain sends messages to all parts of your body as your hands and feet move without you really thinking about it. We were created in an amazing way by an amazing, loving Creator God.

Sometimes we underestimate what God can do for us, because we lose sight of how amazing He really is and focus on our problems. Like Joseph in prison, we may not be able to see a solution. Yet like Joseph, we can trust God to bring about a good outcome in His time. “We need to have far less confidence in what man can do and far more confidence in what God can do for every believing soul. He longs to have you reach after Him by faith. He longs to have you expect great things from Him. . . . Put your talents into the work, ask God for wisdom, and it will be given you” (Ellen G. White, _Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 146).

When we focus on who God is, it puts everything else into perspective. Stars glittering and hanging in perfect balance across a night sky can suddenly seem brighter when we know that God put careful detail and thought behind each one. Everything around us demonstrates how truly amazing God is.

God wants to do something amazing in your life as you teach this week’s lesson to His precious little ones. Through you, may the children know that their amazing God can do the same for them.
