`When we accept Christ what will be seen in our life? John 15:1-2, 5, 16`

`How important is it that the Christian bear fruit in his life? John 15:2`

`How does Christ abide in us? 1John 3:24`

`Which member of the Godhead is especially involved in bringing this fruit in our life? Galatians 5:22-23`

`List the fruit of the Spirit Christ led Paul to write about. Galatians 5:22-23`

`Whose character qualities are these fruit of the Spirit? John 15:11; 14:27; Revelation 14:12`
