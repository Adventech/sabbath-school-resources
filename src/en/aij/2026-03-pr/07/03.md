After 40 days Jesus’ special time with God was over. He was tired, weak, and hungry. Satan had been watching Jesus, trying to find the right moment to tempt Him, and now he saw his chance.

God had said about Jesus, “This is my beloved Son” (Matthew 3:17). Satan decided to use God’s words against Jesus. He thought that if he could tempt Jesus to doubt God’s words, he would win forever! And God’s Son would lose.

Satan suddenly appeared beside Jesus, pretending to be an angel from heaven. It was as if the shining angel had come to answer Jesus’ prayers, but his words told a different story. What did Satan say? **Read Matthew 4:3.**

Jesus thought about that little word “if.” Of course He was God’s Son! An angel from heaven would never say “If you are God’s Son . . .” How dare Satan tempt Him to doubt that God was His Father! Jesus struggled against the temptation to explain who He was, but He didn’t let Satan trick Him into working a miracle to prove He was God’s Son. He would never use His power to work a miracle for Himself—it was meant only to help others. And besides, God could give Him food anytime.

Jesus looked Satan straight in the eye and said, “It is written, ‘Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God’ ” (Matthew 4:4).

Jesus had won!

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- What might Jesus have prayed when Satan tempted Him?
- Has Satan ever tried to tempt you into doubting that you are God’s child? Remember you are God’s child; He loves, helps, and forgives you.

Satan sometimes tempts us when we are tired and hungry because he knows we might not be so strong to resist. When this happens, our loving God wants us to rely on Him and His power. We can pray “right away” and claim a Bible promise, as Jesus did. **Read Psalm 119:11.**
