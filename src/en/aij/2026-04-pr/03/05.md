As the Pharisees stood in stony silence, Jesus looked right at them and asked another question. What did He ask? **Read Mark 2:9.**

Not one of the Pharisees could answer. Jesus continued speaking: “ ‘But that you may know that the Son of Man has power on earth to forgive sins’—He said to the man with paralysis, ‘I say to you, arise, take up your bed, and go to your house’ ” (Mark 2:10).

Instantly the man sprang to his feet. He was healed all over! His body pulsed with new life as he rolled up his bed and tucked it under one arm. He strode toward the door, and this time the crowd let him through. In amazement they praised God and said, “We never saw anything like this!” (Mark 2:12). “Today we have seen amazing things!” (Luke 5:26, ICB).

Jesus had proved that He was the Son of God! He had power to heal hurting sinful hearts with forgiveness, and hurting bodies too.

As the healed man headed home, he praised God! God had healed him, and that was worth shouting about! You can be sure his four friends jogged along beside him too, praising God with their happy friend. They had helped him find Jesus, and now they could all rejoice together in the miracle of his new life.

Many others who saw the amazing healing and heard his story were also blessed. This man was proof that Jesus didn’t push away people who had made mistakes. He didn’t ignore their pain, as the priests and doctors had done. Instead, Jesus invited them to be forgiven, to be happy, and to live a new life with Him. Your best Friend Jesus will always do the same for you.
