Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **colorful fabric** that can be torn, **sweet spices** (cinnamon sticks, citrus peel, cardamom, etc.), and a **bag of coins** or a bag filled with things that might sound like coins clinking together.

**Write out the first part of Psalm 145:18**—half on one piece of paper and half on another. Fold a couple of times and label 1 and 2 so children will know which one to read first. Do enough for each pair in the class to have both halves. 1. “The Lord is close to . . .”; 2. “. . . everyone who prays to him” (Psalm 145:18, ICB).
