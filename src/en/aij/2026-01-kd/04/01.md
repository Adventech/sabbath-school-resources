Peace in Heaven

Long before God created our world and made Adam and Eve, He created some very special helpers. Angels! God made thousands and thousands of angels. They were wonderful, with powerful wings and a bright light that made them shine wherever they went.

Each angel had a special job. Some angels took messages from one side of heaven to the other. Other angels sang joyful worship songs in the heavenly choir. Some angels, called cherubim, stood next to God and covered Him with their wings as He sat on His beautiful throne. The angels were content, and they chose to work with a smile and a song.

Love was everywhere in heaven. God is love, and everyone in heaven followed His law of love, which showed them how to love Him and one another. Because of God’s great love for all the angels, they chose to love Him too. They also loved each other. Their voices were always kind and gentle. Everyone only ever thought about bringing happiness to each other. What a wonderful place it was. Everyone was content.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Which job do you think you would enjoy the most if you were an angel in heaven? A messenger, a singer in the choir, or a cherubim? Why?
