After speaking the first five commandments, God then told the Israelites commandment number six. What was it? **Read Exodus 20:13.**

God never wants us to kill, hate, or hurt others. God wants us to show His love and kindness with every word, thought, and action.

Madeline learned this lesson from the Holy Spirit when she was 8 years old. She had just finished playing her favorite board game with her brother. “Mathieu, you need to help me clean up,” she said firmly. She placed a board game piece into the box.

“You can do it,” he replied.

Madeline’s anger bubbled and boiled inside. Her face grew red. _How dare he?_

“Mathieu, come here and help me right now!” shouted Madeline with her hands on her hips.

“No. I don’t want to,” Mathieu answered.

With that, Madeline charged at Mathieu like an angry bull. Her little brother screamed in fright; then the chase began. Finally Madeline had Mathieu cornered. She lifted her hand and hit him on the leg.

Mathieu began to sob. “You should have listened to me and helped me,” she said.

But then Madeline saw her little brother’s fear-filled eyes. She noticed the big red mark on his leg. Guilt washed over her. Then these words from the Holy Spirit came clearly to her mind, “Madeline, you must not hate or hurt your brother. That’s not what Jesus would want you to do.”

Madeline said she was sorry. She said sorry to Jesus and asked Him to help her to be kind and respectful every day.

- Have you ever become so angry that you wanted to hurt someone?
- What should you do if you feel like that?
