**Focus:** God cares for us and answers our prayers when we seek Him.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Botswana** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Five-year-old Katie was enjoying camp meeting with her grandmother. She went to play with her friends in the beautiful
countryside close to the camp. After a while she started to get hungry, so she decided to walk back to the tent where her grandmother was making food. But she accidentally took a wrong turn and went in the opposite direction.

She walked and walked, but she didn’t find the camp or her grandmother. When suppertime came and there was no Katie, Grandma started to worry. When she still hadn’t come back for evening worship, a man told everyone that Katie was lost. He asked people to pray for her and help them look for her. They prayed that she would be safe because there were lots of wild animals in the Botswana bush.

Many campers came to search for her, and those who stayed behind to care for their own children prayed that God would lead the searchers to Katie. Suddenly someone found a tiny shoeprint in the damp sand on the edge of a lake. They followed the footprints into the grass. It was very dark by now, but they found a big searchlight to help them look for her. They found more shoeprints and followed them carefully through streams and grass and into the thick forest.

Finally, long after midnight, they found Katie sleeping peacefully on the ground, with her little shoes next to her. She
woke up, saw her pastor, and smiled at him. The searchers wrapped her warmly in a blanket and carried her back to the camp, singing praises to God. Then they had a special praise service together! They were all so happy for the miracle of God guiding them to find Katie’s little footprints and to follow them!

This special miracle helped people understand how much God cares for each of His children. Just as He wanted the searchers to find Katie, He wants us to search for Him too and He will help us find Him.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He wants me to seek Him and find Him.**
