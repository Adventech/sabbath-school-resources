**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Floating in a basket on the Nile River, Baby Moses may have appeared to have been abandoned to luck. But the odds of his survival were small. But the odds of his day ending with hungry crocodiles, murderous soldiers, or hostile Egyptians were great. But thankfully, life is not a game of chance.

God had placed Moses, the son of slaves, in a loving home with God-fearing parents. And they had prayerfully placed their baby under His watchful care. God would not only save Moses but use all the challenges, successes, and failures in his life to prepare him for great things. Nothing could stand in the way of God’s personal, powerful plans for him.

The same is true for you. Ephesians 2:10 says, “Now we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works.” You are part of God’s plans—a masterpiece He continues to shape. With His grace and forgiveness, He continues to lead your life as part of His plans.

Can you see how the many experiences in your past have prepared you for your present? Only God could have worked through those challenges and triumphs to position you. Have you praised Him lately for the ways in which He is equipping and leading you? Spend time this week reflecting on how He works all things together for good—for you.

As you teach the children in the same way Jochebed taught Moses, may you also appreciate the importance of your role in influencing their lives for Christ. He has called each of them to a purpose in His kingdom. Your patience, encouragement, kindness, and guidance is just what God has planned to help prepare them.
