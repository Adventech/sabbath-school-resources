**Lesson Summary**

At Mount Sinai God told Moses to build a sanctuary so He could live among His people. The people willingly gave generous offerings of gold, silver, fine cloth, and animal skins. God gave special skills to Bezalel, and many workers to build the tabernacle. For months they joyfully worked together making precious furniture for the sanctuary. When it was finished, Moses set everything in place. Then God’s glorious cloud filled the tabernacle, showing He wanted to live close to His people.
