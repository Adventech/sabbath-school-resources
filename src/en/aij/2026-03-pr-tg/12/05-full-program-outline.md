**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Tape some bells to a ruler or a long stick and see if the children can jump to touch them. Someone in today’s story put everything into reaching and searching for Someone great.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a lump of clay or play-dough. Ask them what they see. Explain that with this lump of clay/play-dough there is **potential** to make something special. Give some to each child and invite them to create something in four minutes.

Explain that our story today is about Jesus seeing the potential for good in a man who had been making wrong choices, I wonder what happens to this man when He realizes Jesus sees His potential and worth?

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **Jesus sees potential for good in everyone.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Why did Jesus go to Zacchaeus’ house?** (To show Zacchaeus, and everyone else, how much he loved him and wanted to be part of his changed life and future.)

Show the folded heart that you prepared earlier. Explain that sometimes when people don’t feel loved, their hearts can feel closed up like this. They might feel lonely or not good enough. But when someone believes in us, our hearts open and bloom like a flower. (Place your flower in a tub of water and watch it bloom.) Jesus helped Zacchaeus’ heart to open to Him and grow when He called Zacchaeus by name and ate dinner at his home. Zacchaeus suddenly felt noticed, loved, and sorry for the things he had done to steal from others.

**^[EXPLAIN and DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Zacchaeus had made many wrong choices before he met Jesus. Sometimes we do the same thing. We sin and do things that hurt God and others. We break His Ten Commandments, and this separates us from God and those around us.

Give each child a folded heart and invite them to think about something they are sorry for; something they need God to help them with. Write this on the heart (or simply write “X” because God knows the details). Then invite them to come and place their hearts in the bowl of water to show they are sorry. (Be sure that you, Teacher, do this too.) First, pray a silent prayer, and then a group prayer. **Ask God to forgive you** and that He will help you **make things right**, just as Zacchaeus did.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) With your family, leave Bible pamphlets (tracts) or notes about Jesus somewhere as you walk or play at the park, or as you talk to others.** Zacchaeus also loved to talk to others about Jesus after his heart was changed.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sit in a circle. Place small pieces of paper (with an affirming message on each one) in the middle. Invite each person to choose a message to give to the person on their right.

- **How did it make you feel to hear those encouraging words?** (If needed, break into pairs for this activity.)

Share that Jesus was like this. He encouraged others with His words and actions. He wanted everyone to be His friend, and He wants us to be His friends too. Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus sees potential for good in everyone.** (Point to everyone around you.)

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Conclude with a final prayer, thanking God for loving us and seeing the potential in all of us.
