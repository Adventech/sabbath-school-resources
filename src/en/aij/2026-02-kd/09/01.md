Puzzled Prisoners

The guard’s keys jingled and jangled as he walked past the prison doors. Joseph opened his eyes and smiled. It was a new day in prison. He bowed his head and thanked God for giving him peace and love in his heart.

Joseph had been chosen to “take care of all the prisoners” (Genesis 39:22, ICB). It kept him very busy and made him happy too.

But this morning Joseph noticed two other prisoners were not happy at all! One was a butler, whose job was to serve Pharaoh drinks. The other was a baker, who had cooked fresh bread for the Pharaoh. Pharaoh had become angry with the men and put them in prison.

Kindly, Joseph asked the two men, “Why do you look so unhappy today?” (Genesis 40:7, ICB). The men said, “We both had dreams last night. But no one can explain the meaning of them to us” (Genesis 40:8, ICB).

Strange dreams? Joseph knew what it was like to wake up feeling puzzled by an unusual dream!

Could Joseph help explain the butler and the baker’s dreams?

He would need to know their thoughts and the future to be able to do that! Who is the only One who could do something so amazing? **Read Psalm 86:10.**

“Joseph said to them, ‘God is the only One who can explain the meaning of dreams. So tell me your dreams’ ” (Genesis 40:8, ICB).

He wanted them to know that his God could do amazing and impossible things. His God could help them!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you remember Joseph’s two dreams? What were they?
- Can you think of two amazing things that God can do that we can’t?
