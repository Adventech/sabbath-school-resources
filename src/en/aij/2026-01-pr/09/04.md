God had given beautiful promises to Adam and Noah that showed how much He loves sinners and faithful people alike. But a promise is beautiful only if it is believed; and the proud city dwellers didn’t believe the promise.

Deep down, the city dwellers were angry with God because they believed He had flooded and wrecked the earth for no good reason. A God like that could do it again, they may have thought. They would not trust His love; they would not trust His rainbow promise. They would protect themselves should disaster strike again! They came up with a crazy and dangerous idea, something their faithful relatives would never ever have thought of. What was their idea? Read Genesis 11:4.

Their foolish idea caught on, and soon an army of workers were making bricks, collecting tar, and carrying supplies. The longer they worked, the prouder they were of the gigantic tower that was taking shape. They truly believed it would reach so high that it would save them from another flood and make them world-famous for many years to come.

Soaring up into the clouds, the tower could be seen for miles around. Children’s eyes sparkled as they squinted up at workers scurrying busily around like tiny ants at the top. They had never seen such an enormous building, and they were in awe.

God had seen it too, and He was not in awe. For “those people who are ruled by their sinful selves cannot please God” (Romans 8:8, ICB). Without a doubt their choices broke His heart. They had depended on themselves and not God’s promises. Every promise in God’s Word is a true message of love from Him.
