Imagine the scene when pairs of every kind of animal began flocking toward the ark. The antelope leaping next to the lions, the bears lumbering behind the cows, the rabbits hopping beside the foxes, all peaceably led by the hand of unseen angels. We are told that “beasts of every description, the fiercest as well as the most gentle, were seen coming from mount and forest and quietly making their way toward the ark” _(Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 97)_.

God was gathering the animals and getting ready to make the earth new again.

Another day is coming soon when God will make the earth new again. When the fiercest as well as the gentlest animals will dwell peaceably together. Isaiah 11 paints a beautiful picture of what the new earth will be like under the rule of Jesus, our king:

“The wolf also shall dwell with the lamb,\
The leopard shall lie down with the young goat,\
The calf and the young lion and the fatling together;\
And a little child shall lead them.\
The cow and the bear shall graze;\
Their young ones shall lie down together;\
And the lion shall eat straw like the ox.\
The nursing child shall play by the cobra’s hole,\
And the weaned child shall put his hand in the viper’s den.\
They shall not hurt nor destroy in all My holy mountain,\
For the earth shall be full of the knowledge of the Lord as the waters cover the sea” _(Isaiah 11:6-9)_.

A day is coming when we no longer need to fear for the safety of our little ones. What a wonderful day that will be! Let us pray for the Lord to make us ready, like Noah, as the final events of earth’s history unfold, and the earth is made new!
