Mary and Martha both sighed as they stood in the doorway of Lazarus’ bedroom. Their usually bright, happy home seemed very gloomy today. Their eyes were tired and red. Their backs ached. They had hardly slept. They had been too busy taking care of their brother. He was very sick.

The sisters whispered worried words. _What should they do? How could they help their dear brother?_

They decided there was only one Person who had the power to make their brother better. Quickly, they sent a messenger to race down the road to their Friend Jesus. They knew He would care. They knew He would help.

The messenger found Jesus and told Him, “Lord, the one you love is sick” (John 11:3, ICB).

What did Jesus say? **Read John 11:4.**

Running back home, the messenger told Mary and Martha Jesus’ words. _What a relief! Lazarus would get better!_

Mary and Martha cared for their brother. Two days passed by. Jesus didn’t come. Finally, with great sadness, they watched as Lazarus took his last breath and died.

Back in the town where Jesus was, the disciples were confused. It had been two days since the messenger had come. Two whole days! Why hadn’t Jesus left right away to help His sick friend?

Jesus hadn’t forgotten Lazarus. He never forgets His children. Jesus is always “near to those who have a broken heart” (Psalm 34:18). Jesus was thinking of His friends the whole time. And Jesus had a plan.
