**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Play a **memory game**. Sit in a circle and start by saying, “We’re going on a trip, and I’m taking [one item].” The next person repeats the phrase and adds one more item. Continue around the circle until it becomes too difficult to remember all the items.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How important is it to prepare for a trip?** (Very important—if you want it to be a fun time.)
- **What could go wrong if you didn’t prepare well?** (Unable to do fun activities, go hungry, not be able to brush your teeth, get lost, etc.).

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** It’s always important to be prepared! Did you know that God prepares well for things too?

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read **John 14:1-3**: “Let not your heart be troubled; you believe in God, believe also in Me. In my Father’s house are many mansions; if it were not so, I would have told you. I go to prepare a place for you. And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you to Myself; that where I am, there you may be also.”

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What is God preparing for us?** (A place—many mansions)
- **Why would Jesus want to come back and take us to be with Him?** (He loves us, and wants us to live with Him forever.)
- **How can we prepare our hearts for Jesus’ return?** (By praying to God and listening to Him, reading the Bible, and obeying Him and loving others as He does.)

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Our story today is about Moses and how God prepared him for a special job. Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard/poster (acknowledging how beautifully it has been decorated by the children): **God prepares people to do a big work for Him.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to play a guessing game with you: **Guess What I’m Preparing For?**

Hold up one of your three mystery bags. Invite the children to guess the activity you’re preparing for based on the items you are taking or gathering. Pull out each object one at a time. (Marathon: running shoes, sunglasses, hat, water bottle, ID number. Summer holiday: swimsuit, sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, flip-flops, towel, insect repellent, water bottle. Snow Skiing: skis (cardboard), jacket, helmet, goggles/sunglasses, gloves.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Just as you pack or prepare for an activity, God prepares us for His work.

**^[REVIEW and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What did God do in this story to begin preparing Moses for the big job He had in mind for him?** (God gave him godly parents, a caring big sister and brother, a home he could be hidden in, protection by the princess, royal schooling, life and privileges of a prince.)
- **What, or who, might God be using to prepare you for a future with Him?** (Loving family/good friends/church family, the Bible, Sabbath School teachers, schooling, learning about nature, hobbies.)

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** It can change someone’s life to know that God is interested and involved in all that they go through. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **use your craft to share the story with someone and encourage them that God has an awesome plan for their life.**

**^[RESPOND:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** This is just the start of Moses’ life. Do you believe he should trust God to keep leading him? Why?

- **Do you believe God has awesome plans for your life?** If so invite them to say “Amen” but to do it in a whisper, just as Moses’ family may have had to whisper as they hid him away. Invite the children to repeat their “Amen” a little louder, louder, then much, much louder again!
- **How might you show that you are willing to let God, or godly people, prepare you for God’s plans this week?** (Read my Bible and lesson, pray, listen in family worship and Sabbath School, be involved in church activities, be respectful of others.)
