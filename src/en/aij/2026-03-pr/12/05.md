Jesus’ smile was broad and bright. He had just heard Zacchaeus say that he would give half his money to the poor, as well as make things right with all the people he’d cheated. Jesus knew that Zacchaeus meant every word, and that he would truly obey the Bible-scroll verses: “Blessed is he who considers the poor” (Psalm 41:1) and “You must not cheat each other” (Leviticus 25:17, ICB). Jesus was happy to see that Zacchaeus would now let God help him become a giving, kind man who would live up to his potential.

Then in front of the whole crowd Jesus smiled and said, “Today salvation has come to this house, because he also is a son of Abraham” (Luke 19:9). Jesus was letting the priests and rabbis in the crowd know that Zacchaeus was one of Abraham’s descendants too—they shouldn’t think they were better than Zacchaeus! What else did Jesus say? **Read Luke 19:10.**

Jesus had come to save sinners! The rabbis, priests, and rulers were sinners, just like Zacchaeus. But Zacchaeus had listened to the Holy Spirit telling Him to change. He had been sorry and had done everything He could to meet and know Jesus. Jesus now said he was saved; he was one of God’s precious children.

Zacchaeus and his family had been shut out of the synagogue because the rabbis really didn’t like them. So Jesus went to their house to show them how much they were loved and accepted by Him. He taught them about God’s kingdom of peace, and their hearts began to heal.

Zacchaeus was the happiest man in Jericho that day, for Jesus had visited him, loved him, and welcomed him into the family of God. The lost sinner was saved!

Jesus longs to save you too. He wants to come into your heart and stay with you, just as He wanted to go to the home of Zacchaeus. His love and acceptance are for you and for everyone, for God has designed us all with the potential to love as He loves.
