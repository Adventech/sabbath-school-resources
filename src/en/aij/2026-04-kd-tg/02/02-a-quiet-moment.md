**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The nobleman looked up at Jesus with the anguish of a parent about to lose his precious son. Jesus knew his thoughts, his doubts and fears, and He saw the belief in the nobleman’s heart. Jesus didn’t move; He didn’t turn and rush to the boy’s side. He simply spoke: “Go your way; your son lives” (John 4:50). There was no sign or touch, just words that declared the truth of the miracle.

Convicted by these powerful words, the nobleman’s faith took hold of his life. His worry and fears vanished as his belief bloomed. The nobleman left Jesus’ presence that day “with a peace and joy he had never known before” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 198, 199). He traveled all the way home, believing a promise. And oh, what a reunion was had!

Jesus has also given us a mighty promise to cling to. It is the promise of this week’s memory verse: “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved, you and your household” (Acts 16:31). The promise of eternal life with Him hangs from this simple, beautiful promise. Like the nobleman, we can travel toward our “home” with peace, believing in the truth and power of His words.

“For we walk by faith, not by sight” (2 Corinthians 5:7).

May you and the children in your Sabbath School class choose to trust in His Word—to believe that He is good, even before we see the good He can do. We can know that His Word is truth; that He can heal every hurt and fill every need.
