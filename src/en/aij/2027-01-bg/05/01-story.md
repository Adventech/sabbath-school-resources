Day by day, year by year, Noah and his family worked hard to build the ark. Board by board, nail by nail, the boat grew tall. It grew taller than a horse, then taller than a camel. _(Start with your hand low and raise higher with each animal)_. It grew taller than a moose, then taller than an elephant. It grew even taller than a giraffe! Noah built the ark just as God had said. God had a plan to take care of all His people. When the rain came, those who wanted to be God’s friends would be safe inside the ark!\
God loved all the people so much and wanted them to be His friends. He’d made the world for them. He’d filled it with soft green grass, and beautiful flowers in so many colors—red, yellow, purple, and blue. God made trees that reached up so high _(reach up)_ and filled the sky with birds that fly _(fly actions)_. God had made cheetahs to run fast _(running motions)_ and frogs to jump high _(jump)_. He’d done all of this for the people He had made. But the people had forgotten His love for them. Noah was God’s friend. He was God’s helper. He would tell the people about God’s love.

_Tap, tap, tap_ went Noah’s hammer as he built the ark. People came from all over to watch. Why would Noah build a boat so tall? It was too big for the ponds and streams. Where would it float? _(Shrug with hands out.)_ Noah told them all God had said. He told the people that rain would fall from the sky and cover the earth. It would rain until the rivers were too full. It would rain until the lakes were as big as the ocean and the hills were covered. The rain would fall until water covered the whole world. Everyone should come inside the ark!\
The people shook their heads _(shake head)_. There had never been rain! They didn’t need an umbrella. They didn’t need a raincoat or boots to stay dry. They had never seen water fall from the sky. How could this happen? The people didn’t believe Noah, and they didn’t believe God. Noah told the people that God loved them _(hug)_. He told them they should be kind. They didn’t want to be God’s friend. They didn’t want to listen to God or come inside God’s ark!

God loved the people _(hug)_. He wanted them to be dry and safe when the rain came. God was sad they didn’t want to be His friend. Noah and his family were God’s friends! They would be safe in the ark when the rain came. Noah would keep telling others about the ark, and maybe they would choose to be God’s friend too. God loved them so much!
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, that Your love is so big! _(Arms out wide.)_ I want to tell others of Your big love too!
