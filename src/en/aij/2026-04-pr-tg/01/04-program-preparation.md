**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source **shallow trays or bowls of water**, and **play-dough** for children to mold and make a boat to float on the water. (Optional: Source **marbles** for children to put into their boat and see how many it will hold before it sinks.)

Visit the AIJ website for information about the location of this quarter’s mission offering. Print and assemble the **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Source a **globe** or **world map**. Print the **^[13 Mission Fast Facts]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** so you can share a Mission Fast Fact each week.

Source items for Focus Activity: **Blue blanket/sheet or tablecloth** (disposable party ones are inexpensive), **items to put in the middle of the blanket/sheet**. Possible items could be a big ball, collection of small balls, inflated balloons, soft toys, or a number of small paper/plastic boats.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display. (Do this every week.)

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **spray bottle filled with water, strong fan**, and **toy figures or peg people** to represent Jesus and His disciples.

Source **masking tape**, scrunched-up pieces of **blue** or **white paper**.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 40: Jesus Calming the Storm]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child. Fold and glue the two sides together. Source a container, envelope, bag, or key ring for children to store these cards in your Sabbath School room this quarter.

Print the memory verse on **blue paper** and cut out each word for the memory verse shuffle. Source **music** to play during this time.
