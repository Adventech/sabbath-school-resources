**Focus:** I can hide God’s word in my heart by memorizing Bible verses.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Ghana** on your **globe** or **world map**.

It was summertime, and Dacosta was at a special program for kids along with 290 other children. On Sabbath a 10-year-old girl named Gifty preached a sermon about Jesus coming again. Dacosta was only 7, but he listened carefully. He liked Gifty’s sermon. He couldn’t wait for Jesus to come again and take him to heaven!

After the sermon an adult asked the children, “How many of you would like to preach like Gifty?” Dacosta raised his hand high. If Gifty could preach like that, maybe he could too one day!

A few Sabbaths later there was a special meeting called a preachers’ club at Dacosta’s church. Dacosta was excited! Now he could learn how to preach. The teacher challenged him and the other children to memorize John 14:1. “Come back next week and recite it from memory,” the teacher said.

Dacosta worked hard all week. He didn’t understand why he needed to memorize a verse. He wanted to learn to preach! But he memorized it anyway. “Let not your heart be troubled; you believe in God, believe also in Me,” he repeated over and over. On Sabbath Dacosta was ready. He said the verse perfectly!

The teacher was pleased. “Now I want you to memorize Psalm 100 this week,” the teacher said. Psalm 100 had five verses, so it took even more practice. Dacosta worked hard to learn all five of them by heart. “Make a joyful shout to the Lord, all you Lands!” he repeated over and over. That Sabbath Dacosta repeated the whole psalm perfectly. The teacher gave the children more verses to memorize. Dacosta wondered why he needed to learn all these verses. When would he get to preach?

Soon the teacher told Dacosta it was time to preach his first sermon. Dacosta was excited! He told Jesus’ story about the lost boy who came home again. The people at church were pleased with Dacosta’s sermon.

But that wasn’t the end of his preaching. Before long, Dacosta preached for seven days in a row for some children’s meetings. At the end of the meetings five people gave their hearts to Jesus and were baptized. Suddenly Dacosta realized why his teacher had asked him to memorize so many Bible verses. It had made it easy to memorize his sermons! And because he had learned to “hide God’s Word in his heart,” five people had chosen to follow Jesus.

- **What will happen if you decide to “hide God’s Word in your heart”?** (Maybe you will become a preacher like Dacosta. Or maybe you will find other ways to share God’s Word with others.)
