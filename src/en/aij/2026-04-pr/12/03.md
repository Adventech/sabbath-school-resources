The weeping woman staggered along behind her dead son’s stretcher, unable to see through her tears. She was totally unaware that Jesus was approaching. He quietly walked up beside her and gently spoke. What did He say? **Read Luke 7:13.**

Jesus’ words must have sounded strange. The woman couldn’t help weeping over the loss of her only son whom she loved so much. It was almost impossible not to weep! But she heard the compassion and felt the comfort in Jesus’ voice as He spoke.

Jesus said those words for good reason—He wanted to inspire her with hope and trust before He performed a miracle. Then Jesus reached over and touched the stretcher.

Suddenly the stretcher carriers stopped. The wailing stopped. The two crowds surged forward, then stopped. They merged together as one excited crowd, and gathered around the stretcher.

Many people had seen Jesus work miracles to heal the sick, so they stood on tiptoe and craned their necks to catch a glimpse of what He would do next. They thought, Maybe, just maybe, Jesus’ power can raise the dead man! They wondered, and hoped… They maybe even held their breath as they fixed their eyes on Jesus and the young man.

Jesus was there right beside the sad mother, offering comfort and peace. Jesus has sympathy for all sad and suffering people and His words today can bring you and others hope. When you are sad, remember, “The Lord is close to the brokenhearted. He saves those whose spirits have been crushed” (Psalm 34:18, ICB).
