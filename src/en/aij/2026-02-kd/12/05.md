Naomi woke to a new morning and a new plan. Quickly she set to work cleaning, gathering, and packing. It was time to go home!

Ruth and Orpah loved Naomi too much to let her go alone. They knew she needed them. They decided to leave Moab with her. They bundled up all the things they could carry and set off on the long journey to Bethlehem.

As they walked slowly down the dusty road, Naomi looked at brave Ruth and Orpah. They were so kind to come with her. But it was such a big move for them to leave their home and their country.

Suddenly Naomi turned to Ruth and Orpah and said, “Go back home. Each of you, go to your own mother’s house. You have been very kind to me and to my sons who are now dead. I hope the Lord will also be kind to you in the same way. I hope the Lord will give you another home and a new husband” (Ruth 1:8, 9, ICB).

What did Ruth and Orpah say? **Read Ruth 1:10.**

But Naomi had made up her mind. She wanted the best for them.

Putting others first is God’s way. The Bible says, “Do not be interested only in your own life, but be interested in the lives of others” (Philippians 2:4, ICB). All three women were shining God’s love. With God’s help, this is something you can do too.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of a time someone missed out on something just so you could be happy? Thank them for shining God’s love.
