**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source **paper** and **colored pencils**.

Source a **globe/world map**, update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source a **heavy object** that a child will need help to lift.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: a **Bible-times map**, a **stone and a bowl of water**, a **ball**, and a **plant with a cloth cover**.
