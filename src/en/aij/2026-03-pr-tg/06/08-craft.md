**What you need:**

Week 6 craft template printed on white cardstock, scissors, glue, blue cellophane, markers, fish stickers (optional).

**Instructions:**

1\. Decorate the water section of the printable (under the wavy line) with fish and sea grass.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/6b.png)

2\. Glue a piece of cellophane over the top and cut out Jesus and John.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/6c.png)

3\. Fold the pieces along the lines. Make it so the water is standing up, and glue Jesus and John along the base of the card.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/6d.png)

4\. Show Jesus standing up in the water, then push the underwater picture backward as though He is being baptized.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/6e.png)
