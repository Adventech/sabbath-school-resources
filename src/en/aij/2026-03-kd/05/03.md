Jesus was growing into a young man—He was now 12 years old! And now that He was 12, He could do something special with His family. “His parents went to Jerusalem every year at the Feast of the Passover” (Luke 2:41). And this year Jesus could go too. Yay!

Off they went, down the road toward Jerusalem. Jesus and His family weren’t the only ones—it seemed as though all of Nazareth was going too. Everyone was on their way to a special feast in the big city of Jerusalem.

The trip would take a few days, but no one seemed to mind. They walked, talked, laughed, and sang together. The fields were full of bright, colorful flowers and sweetly singing birds. Jesus loved being in nature.

As they walked, Joseph may have told Jesus the story of God’s people, and His plan for us. Perhaps they shared with Jesus the great miracles that God had done—right there in their country, long ago.

What does God tell us in the Bible that we should remember? **Read 1 Chronicles 16:12.**

Mary and Joseph wanted Jesus to learn and remember God’s goodness. If we remember God’s miracles and blessings, we will grow in faith and wisdom, just as Jesus did.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go around the circle and do a “popcorn” prayer. Take turns saying one thing to God you are thankful for. Move around the circle again and again, thanking God for who He is and what He does for you.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go for a walk together in nature. Find as many “special” things as you can along the way that God has made. Look carefully at the designs in flowers, plants, and trees.
