Mary and Joseph held Baby Jesus close as they walked along the stone streets of Jerusalem. Today was a very special day. Today they would take Jesus to the temple. It was time to thank God for the gift of Baby Jesus.

The happy parents smiled as they gave Baby Jesus to the priest to say a prayer of thanks. The priest held the tiny Baby in his arms, but he had no idea he was holding the King of heaven! He said a prayer in front of the altar and handed Jesus back to His mother.

But someone else noticed who Jesus was. It was an old man in the temple named Simeon. Simeon was really close to God. When he saw Baby Jesus cradled in Mary’s arms, he instantly knew that this baby was the promised King and Savior. He took Jesus in his arms and said a special thank-You prayer.

What did Simeon say? **Read Luke 2:30.**

Old Anna, with her wrinkly skin and gray hair, was also in the temple that day. Anna loved seeking God with her whole heart. She would pray “day and night,” and she “never left the Temple” (Luke 2:37, ICB). As Simeon stood holding Baby Jesus, Anna’s face lit up with God’s bright joy. She knew this child was special. She just couldn’t keep inside her words of praise, thanking God that Jesus, the promised Savior, had come.
