^[GET READY]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#6D6E71", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“Nothing is more needed in our work than the ^[practical results of communion with God]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Gospel Workers_, p. 510).\
\
“Through ^[much prayer]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) you must labor for souls, for this is ^[the only method]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) by which you can reach hearts” (_Evangelism_, p. 342).\
\
“The work of God in this earth can never be finished until the men and women comprising our ^[church membership]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) rally to the work and ^[unite their effort]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) with those of ^[ministers and church officers]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 9, p. 116).\
\
“Let every church member become a working member, to build up ^[spiritual interests]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Evangelism_, p. 65).\
\
“There is need of ^[personal labor]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) for the souls of the lost” (_Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 57).

^[PREPARE]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“Christ’s method alone will give true success in reaching the people. The Saviour ^[mingled]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) with men as one who desired their good. He showed His ^[sympathy]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) for them, ^[ministered]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) to their needs, and won their confidence. Then He bade them, ‘Follow Me’ ” (_The Ministry of Healing_, p. 143).\
\
“When properly conducted, the ^[health work is an entering wedge]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), making a way for other truths to reach the heart” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 6, p. 327).\
\
“The sowers of the seed have a work to do in ^[preparing hearts]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) to receive the gospel” (_Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 57).

^[PLANT]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“The ^[truth is to be scattered by all]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) who claim to be disciples of Christ” (_Christian Service_, p. 68).\
\
“Unvarnished truth must be spoken, in ^[leaflets and pamphlets]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), and these must be scattered like the leaves of autumn” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 9, p. 231).\
\
“Everyone who has heard the ^[invitation]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) is to ^[echo the message]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) from hill and valley, saying, ‘Come’ ” (_The Acts of the Apostles_, p. 110).

^[CULTIVATE]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3C8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“Let ministers teach church members that in order to grow in spirituality, they must carry the burden that the Lord has laid upon them—the burden of ^[leading souls into the truth]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Christian Service_, p. 69).\
\
“The plan of holding Bible readings [studies] was a ^[heaven-born idea]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Christian Service_, p. 141).\
\
“The more one tries to explain the Word of God to others, with a love for souls, the ^[plainer it becomes to himself]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 354).\
\
“In visions of the night, representations passed before me of a great reformatory movement among God’s people. . . . ^[Hundreds and thousands]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) were seen visiting families and opening before them the Word of God” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 9, p. 126).

^[HARVEST]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#EFAF44", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“If there is not a decided application of the truth to their hearts, if words are not spoken at the right moment, ^[calling for decision]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) from the weight of evidence already presented, the convicted ones pass on without identifying themselves with Christ, the golden opportunity passes, and they have not yielded, and they go ^[farther and farther away]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) from the truth, farther away from Jesus and never take their stand on the Lord’s side” (_Evangelism_, p. 283).\
\
“The test of discipleship is not ^[brought to bear]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) as closely as it should be upon those who present themselves for baptism” (_Evangelism_, p. 311).\
\
“Too much ^[hasty work]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) is done in adding names to the church roll. . . . Those who admit them say, ‘We will first get them into the church, and then reform them.’ But ^[this is a mistake]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (in _Review and Herald_, May 21, 1901).

^[PRESERVE]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#BE353A", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
“After individuals have been converted to the truth, they need to be ^[looked after]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). . . . These ^[newly converted ones]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) need nursing—watchful attention, help, and encouragement” (_Evangelism_, p. 351).\
\
“When souls are converted, ^[set them to work at once]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). And as they labor according to their ability, they will grow stronger” (_Evangelism_, p. 355).\
\
One soul, won to the truth, will be instrumental in ^[winning others]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), and there will be an ^[ever-increasing]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) result of blessing and salvation” (_Christian Service_, p. 121).
