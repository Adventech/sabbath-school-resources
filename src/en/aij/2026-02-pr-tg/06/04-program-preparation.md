Opening Activities

Copy the **memory verse** onto **different colored pieces of paper** and cut it into puzzle pieces. Make them all the same number of pieces so children will know how many they need to find. Make enough for each pair in your class. Hide the pieces around the Sabbath School space.

Create a **large poster** and **draw a giant thought bubble** (a cloud shape) onto the poster. Source different colored markers to use. Print or write out this Bible verse (ICB) big enough to reach across the thought bubble: “You are precious to me. I give you honor, and I love you” (Isaiah 43:4).

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.
