As the sun began to sink in the sky, the Israelites rushed to get ready. The last plague was coming. And God had told Moses exactly how they could be saved.

Using a leafy hyssop branch, the fathers painted the front door of their home red with lamb’s blood. They painted it over the top of the door and down both sides. This was like a big, bright sign that said, “We love God and trust Him.”

That night God would send an angel over all the land of Egypt. This angel would bring death to all the firstborn children and animals in Egypt. When the angel saw the painted doorposts, He would pass over their home. God said, “Nothing terrible will hurt you when I punish the land of Egypt” (Exodus 12:13, ICB). They would be saved!

Inside the homes, the people of Israel were busy getting ready too.

A roast lamb was cooked over the kitchen fire, and the family made some fresh flatbread and a plate of bitter herbs. God had asked them to make and eat this special meal together. What was it called? **Read Exodus 12:27.**

Some Egyptian families also believed in the one true God after seeing His mighty power. They wanted to be saved too. The Hebrew families happily welcomed them. God’s love is for anyone who believes.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that He offers to save anyone who believes in Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Using a red crayon or pencil, draw a big heart on a piece of paper. With help, stick this on a door. This can be a sign to Jesus this week that you love and trust Him.

^[Mission Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
