For seven days it didn’t rain. Oh, how the people outside the ark scoffed and laughed at Noah and his family. How wrong the old man was! they must have thought. But Noah and his family patiently waited inside the ark, praying to God and encouraging each other. They trusted that the Flood would come just as God had said.

What happened after seven days? **Read Genesis 7:10, 11.** Then, on the eighth day, dark clouds smothered the sky. Lightning flashed, and thunder roared. The people had never seen anything like it! They were terrified!

Huge raindrops began to fall, and water exploded out of the ground. The people outside the ark watched as the terrible storm ruined their beautiful houses and their idols. Suddenly they realized that Noah had been right. God was bringing a flood on the earth.

Some of the people pounded on the door of the ark—but it was closed tight. Some ran to the top of the highest hills to escape the water. Others climbed tall trees. But gradually water covered the whole world, and the ark was the only thing that could be seen on top of all the water. Angels hovered over the ark to keep it safe as the storm raged.

The Bible says that God “destroyed all living things which were on the face of the ground: both man and cattle, creeping thing and bird of the air. They were destroyed from the earth. Only Noah and those who were with him in the ark remained alive” (Genesis 7:23).

Inside the ark Noah and his family and all the animals were safe. They may have felt a little scared, but inside the ark was the safest place to be. Out of the thousands of people who were living on the earth, only eight chose to follow God. How happy Noah and his family were that they had walked with God, listened to Him for all those years, and had done what He had asked them to do!

Besides Noah’s family and the animals in the ark, only one other thing was saved—the beautiful Garden of Eden. God took it to heaven before the Flood started, to keep it safe for Adam, Eve, and all of us to enjoy on the brand-new earth after Jesus comes again (see Revelation 2:7; 22:1, 2).
