Life para Adam e Eve em the Garden de Eden must have been one grande, joyous adventure! That is, until eles made a sad mistake. Isso seemed such a pequeno thing em Primeiro, mas isso turned out a be very grande.

Isso todos started quando Eve found herself em the forbidden tree, face-a-face com a dazzlingly lindo, striking, winged serpent.

Suddenly the serpent começou talking em the sweetest voice! Satan was speaking através isso, então a string de smooth lies poured de seu mouth. O que foi o serpent’s lies? **Leia Gênesis 3:4, 5.**

The lies were confuCante, mas eles sounded então bom. As the serpent slithered closer e held out the fruit, she took isso e crunched her Primeiro bite. Isso was juicy e sweet, e nothing bad happened, então she quickly picked some para Adam.

Adam’s face fell as Eve stepped toward ele holding the fruit. Ele took a bite.

Satan had tempted Adam e Eve por making things look, sound, e feel bom quando eles weren’t bom em todos.
