God’s words filled the people with awe as they stared up at the cloud-covered mountain. God had reminded them of who He was and how He’d shown His love for them. Now it was time to teach them how to love Him back.

God would do this by speaking His 10 laws that show His beautiful character of love.

When God spoke His first commandment, what did He say? **Read Exodus 20:3.** God was telling the Israelites, and every human being down through time, that He wants to be their very best friend. All the love in the whole universe comes from God. So when God loves His children, He is also inviting them to love Him the most—more than anyone or anything.

Then God spoke the second commandment. He told the people not to make or worship idols that looked like anything in heaven or on earth (Exodus 20:4, 5). God said this because the Israelites had been surrounded in Egypt by people who worshipped many gods. Some of the Israelites had copied them and also worshipped idols made of stone or carved wood.

An idol is not just an image of a false god; it is anything that becomes more important to us than God. Idols can be money, or trendy new shoes, or the latest bike, or the newest smartphone. God may give us money, or trendy new shoes, or a new bike, but He wants us to worship Him as the gift-giver; not to love the gift more than Him. James 1:17 says, “Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and comes down from the Father of lights.” Only God is worthy of our worship.
