**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He is my promised Savior.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Stick **printed cardboard angels onto craft sticks** for children to hold up and wave whenever an angel is mentioned in the story. The angels are God’s messengers, sharing good news from God with His people.
- Use the **baby doll** from the Secret Surprise Box to represent baby John, and let the children say, “His name is John.”
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 1 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

Priest Zacharias prayed silently as he walked into the temple. Oh, how He loved God! He knew God always keeps His promises.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had made a special promise to His people. He promised to send His only Son to our earth. Jesus would come and save everyone from sin.

God’s people had been waiting a long, long time. But finally the right time had come.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly a bright angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) appeared.” Do not be afraid, Zacharias,” the angel said kindly, “your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John . . . . He will also be filled with the Holy Spirit” (Luke 1:13-15). The angel said that John would make people ready to meet the Lord. Zacharias found it hard to believe the angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He and his wife, Elizabeth, were very old—much too old to have children. The angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) told him that because he hadn’t believed the promise, he wouldn’t be able to speak again until after the baby was born.

Zacharias stepped outside the temple. He opened his mouth to speak to the people, but . . . no words came out! He couldn’t talk.

Sure enough, his wife, Elizabeth, soon became pregnant with their miracle baby. One day this baby would grow up to be God’s special helper. He would help people get ready to meet Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In another little town called Nazareth a young woman called Mary was doing her chores. Suddenly she jumped in surprise. Standing before her was a beautiful shining angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, because God is pleased with you. Listen! You will become pregnant. You will give birth to a son, and you will name him Jesus. . . . He will be called the Son of God” (Luke 1:30-35, ICB).

Mary nodded her head and bowed with respect. She believed the angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Yes, she would be the mother of Jesus.

The angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) also told Mary about her cousin Elizabeth and her miracle baby. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary was so happy that she went to visit her. By this time the baby in Elizabeth’s tummy was quite big. When Elizabeth met Mary, Elizabeth felt her baby boy bounce for joy inside her ^[(put hand on stomach)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Everyone was so happy that the promised Savior would soon be here!

Mary went home, and in time, Elizabeth’s baby boy was born. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Friends came to see him. They wanted to call him Zacharias, like his daddy. “No, he shall be called John,” Elizabeth said (Luke 1:60).

Their friends disagreed. No one in their family had the name John. They asked Zacharias. What would he like to call his son?

Zacharias still couldn’t talk, so he found something to write with and wrote, “His name is John” (Luke 1:63). Yes, John, just as the angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) had said! As soon as he finished writing, Zacharias could speak again!

Everyone knew it was a miracle. This would be a very special baby. Zacharias said he would help prepare the people for God’s promised One. ^[(Pass around the baby doll and let the children say, “His name is John.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Back in Nazareth, Joseph tossed and turned in his bed. He was very worried. Soon he would be marrying Mary. But he was confused. Mary had told him that an angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) had told her that she was pregnant with the promised Savior, the Son of God. Should he believe her? What should he do?

Finally Joseph fell asleep. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) While he slept, God sent an angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) to him in a dream. “Joseph, . . . don’t be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The baby in her is from the Holy Spirit,” the angel said (Matthew 1:20, ICB).

“And she will bring forth a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21).

Mary’s story was true. Joseph chose to trust God. Mary and Joseph would be the parents of the long-promised Savior of the world!
