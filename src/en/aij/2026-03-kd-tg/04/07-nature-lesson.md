**Focus:** God has taught penguins how to protect their babies and find their food, just as He has provided moms and dads or community workers to look after you.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-kd-tg/04/nature-lesson.png)

Mama and Papa Penguin were so happy! They looked down their beaks at the beautiful egg that lay on the sparkling white snow. Inside the egg their new baby chick was growing. Now they had the very special task, given to them by God, to care for their baby.

Papa Penguin kept the egg nice and warm, so their chick would grow big and strong. He used his fluffy, feathery body to keep the egg warm. He rested it on his webbed feet, to keep it away from the cold snow. And he learned how to wiggle his feet from time to time to turn the egg around to keep it warm all over.

Mama Penguin had a special job too. God guided her on a long walk over the snow to the sea. She waddled over the slippery snow many days to get to the ocean. There she swam and ate lots and lots of fish, until she couldn’t eat any more, so that she could carry them back to Papa Penguin and their baby penguin.

She made the very long walk back across the snow to Papa Penguin and their new chick. Then they would eat all the fish that she had saved for them in her tummy. ^[(Scatter lots of paper fish on the floor in one area of the room. Have the children pretend to be penguins and waddle behind you as you lead them to the fish. Then ask the children to collect the fish as quickly as they can and waddle back to a toy penguin, or a picture of a penguin.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

By the time Mama Penguin got back, their baby chick was pecking at the shell, ready to hatch. Soon their baby, Penny Penguin, popped out of the shell. Mama and Papa were so delighted to see their beautiful new chick. She was covered in fluffy feathers too, and they snuggled together to keep warm as they ate Mama’s fish!

God had taught Papa Penguin how to protect their special egg from anything that would try to hurt it. God had guided Mama Penguin all the way to the sea and back to bring them food to eat!

- **God has very special ways to guide and protect all His creatures. What other stories do you know about God guiding and protecting His creatures and His children?**

**Teaching Point:** God teaches the birds, like penguins, how to protect their chicks. He guides them to find food. God guides and protects us too!

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He protects me.**
