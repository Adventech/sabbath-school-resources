The Time Had Come

God’s story for us, recorded in the Bible, is the greatest true story there is. It is a love story, an adventure story, and best of all, it will end as a happily-forever-after story. It begins with God. The Bible says, “He chose us in Him before the foundation of the world” (Ephesians 1:4). He knew and chose you—yes, you—before He created Adam and Eve and gave them life! And the ending is not really an ending at all—how’s that for a good story? It’s forever with God, enjoying the love and friendship He created us for.

But the story gets messed up in between. After sin entered heaven and our world, things became dark and ugly. Satan tried to ruin the story. God immediately made a promise to destroy Satan and sin (Genesis 3:15). But Satan still continued to do his best to convince people that God was a cruel and unforgiving judge whom they couldn’t trust. He filled hearts with doubt, discouragement, pain, and suffering. And worst of all, he encouraged people to blame God for all the badness.

But God was still in control of His story; He would never let it be ruined! He would always fulfill His promises. God would show that Satan was wrong—very wrong. God would show people His true character up close, in a way they could understand. He would show them that “God is love” (1 John 4:8).

And so, when the world was at its very worst and Satan thought he’d almost gotten everyone on his side, God chose willing people to play a very special part in His big story. He chose people who really loved Him to help Him fulfill the big promise He had made to Adam and Eve; the promise to send His Son to the earth to stand in the place of all people and die for their sins. Satan—that enemy who had tried to ruin our story—would be crushed. The time had come. The world needed a Savior right away!
