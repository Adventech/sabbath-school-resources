**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He loves to help me and my friends.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Ask children to join you in some actions.
- A doll and small mat
- Use peg people or a felt set, if available.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 3 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- A man with paralysis lay on his mat. He couldn’t move parts of his body. He was also sad and felt bad. ^[(Lay doll on a mat.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- One day the man’s good, kind friends visited him. Jesus was in town. They wanted to take their friend to see Jesus. He could help! ^[(Children to take turns carrying doll with friends.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The friends carried him to Peter’s home, where Jesus was. But it was so crowded. How would they get their friend to Jesus?
- The good friends had an idea! They carried their friend to the roof. Then they made a hole in the roof ^[(demonstrate)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- People inside the house looked up, surprised. Jesus looked up too!
- The friends lowered the man on ropes down through the hole to Jesus’ feet. ^[(Lower doll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Jesus looked into the man’s eyes. He said, “Young man, your sins are forgiven” (Mark 2:5, ICB).
- Jesus’ words healed his heart. He was so happy and peaceful. He was forgiven!
- Then Jesus said, “I tell you, stand up. Take your mat and go home” (Mark 2:11, ICB). The man got up. He could walk. He was healed! ^[(Stand up doll and walk it away.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The people were amazed! Jesus had forgiven and healed this man.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a small stone house, on a small mat, lay an unhappy man. He had paralysis. This meant that he couldn’t move parts of his body at all. He could only lie still. ^[(Lay a doll on a mat.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

It had been such a long time since he had been able to walk. Oh, how he wished he could run again. He also wished that he could feel happy and peaceful. His mind was filled with bad, sad feelings because of the mistakes he had made.

One day the man’s good friends came to visit him. They were happy, kind friends. They would visit and help him.

Today they had very exciting news to share. Jesus was in town! He could help—He had even healed a man who was sick with leprosy!

The man with paralysis couldn’t believe it. This was too wonderful to even imagine!

Lying on his mat on the ground, the man saw his friends looking down at him with big, smiling faces. The group of friends was going to take their sick friend to Jesus. The friends each took a corner of his mat and began carrying him down the road to Peter’s home, where Jesus was. ^[(Give children turns carrying the doll on the mat together, then bring them back to their seats.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They stopped outside Peter’s house. There were people everywhere! The house was full and overflowing onto the street. People leaned in on tiptoe. Shoulders bumped each other. Bodies squeezed in tight. How would they get their friend to Jesus?

These good friends didn’t want to give up. Finally, they thought of a good idea. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Carrying their friend on his mat, they climbed up the stairs to the roof. ^[(Pretend to climb stairs holding the mat.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then they got busy. Soon “they . . . made a hole in the roof” (Mark 2:4, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Yes, a hole!

The people inside the house looked up. They were surprised! Dust and dirt fell on their heads. Jesus stopped talking and looked up too. Sunlight streamed in, and smiling faces peered down from above.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The four friends then used ropes to gently lower their friend down, down, down through that hole right to Jesus’ feet. ^[(Lower doll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus looked down into the man’s eyes with kindness. Jesus knew all about him. He knew his heart needed peace. He knew his body needed healing. Jesus said, “Son, your sins are forgiven” (Mark 2:5).

Jesus’ words healed his heart right away. He felt forgiven and full of peace. He smiled up at Jesus. He was so happy to be forgiven!

But Jesus wasn’t finished healing him. With a kind voice, He said, “I say to you, arise, take up your bed, and go to your house” (Mark 2:11). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Immediately the man stood up. He took his mat and walked out while everyone was watching him. ^[(Make doll stand, fold up the mat, and “walk” away.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The people stared in amazement. “We never saw anything like this!” they said to each other (Mark 2:12).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) This man had come to Jesus, sad and sorry. Now his smile was wide, and his eyes shone with peace. The man had come to Jesus, unable to move his body. Now he leaped up strong. Jesus had fully healed him!

**Big Idea: Jesus can do anything. He loves to help me and my friends.**
