Imagine you are Joshua, the new leader of Israel. You know these people well—you’ve spent 40 years in the desert because of the disobedience of their parents. You have seen how they squabble and complain, like tired children on a long car trip. Moses needed great patience to lead them. Now it is your turn to lead. Imagine the huge weight and responsibility this role would bring.

God never leaves us to carry our burdens or responsibility alone. God encouraged Joshua by saying, “No man shall be able to stand before you all the days of your life; as I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not leave you nor forsake you. . . . Have I not commanded you? Be strong and of good courage; do not be afraid, nor be dismayed, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go” (Joshua 1:5, 9).

What a beautiful promise! And how reassuring it would have been as he stepped up to the Jordan River knowing he had God on his side.

This promise is not just for Joshua but for us too! As a parent you have been tasked with the huge responsibility of leading your family. You too bear the brunt of complaining and whining, little people’s emotions, and watching children choose to disobey. There may be days you doubt yourself and your abilities as a leader for God. Remember that God is saying to you, “As I was with Moses, so I will be with you.” Take heart, take comfort, and take joy in knowing that the God of the universe will help you as you guide the children He has given you.
