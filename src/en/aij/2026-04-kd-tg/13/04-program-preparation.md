**Opening Activities**

Source and set out **small weights, tin cans**, or a range of household items of various weights. Paper and crayons.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: picture of Jesus** to go inside a box. **Flashlight**—remove **batteries** and keep these somewhere close to the activity, ready to insert into the flashlight. Have **scissors** or another tool handy to make a hole in the box.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source suggested storytelling props: a shoebox or other box with a door cut in the side for Lazarus’ tomb. Cut a round piece of cardboard for the stone to roll across the entrance of the tomb. Use **^[Lazarus craft sample]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **peg people** (Jesus, Mary, Martha, disciples—use **^[Lazarus craft sample]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**), or make **cardboard-roll characters**, or use a **felt set** if desired. Use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Printout a copy of the **^[Resurrection picture]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** from the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Keep it upside down while you ask the first question.
