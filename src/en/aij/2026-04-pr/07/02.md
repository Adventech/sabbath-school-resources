While Jesus was enjoying His meal with new friends at Matthew’s house, a man named Jairus came to see Him. He was a ruler of the Jewish synagogue. Out of breath and very upset, the ruler threw himself down at the feet of Jesus. People in the room hushed as the desperate man began to plead for help. What did Jairus say? **Read Mark 5:23.**

There was no doubt in Jairus’ mind that Jesus could heal his daughter. Jairus had been encouraged by the many stories he had heard about Jesus. So many true stories had already spread across the nation. Stories of Him turning water to wine; healing the locals of Capernaum; healing a man with leprosy; of calming a storm, and blessing the children.

When Jesus heard Jairus’ request and saw his faith, His heart was touched. Jesus immediately stopped eating, stood up, and left Matthew’s house. The disciples stood up and left with Him. As they stepped out through the door, the waiting crowds left with Jesus too. Everyone wanted to be close to Him. No one wanted to miss out on hearing His words or seeing any of His amazing miracles.

The tight crowd moved very slowly, especially when Jesus stopped along the way to heal someone, or encourage and comfort another. Jairus’ daughter was terribly ill and needed healing, but others needed Jesus too. And He was never too busy to care for them all.

Jairus’s faith had grown by hearing about Jesus and His wonderful works for others. Your trust in Him can grow stronger this way too! Our faith can also grow when we see beautiful things He has created, hear people share what He has done for them, or see how God answers a prayer.
