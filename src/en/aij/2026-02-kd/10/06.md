A shout echoed through the camp. The brothers had returned!

With happy hearts and hungry tummies, all the families rushed to greet them. But wait—someone was missing! Where was Simeon?

Quickly, the brothers told their strange story. They had met the great Governor of Egypt and been given lots of grain. But he thought they were spies. They’d been thrown in prison! Simeon was still there, waiting for them to come back with Benjamin.

What a story! What trouble they were in!

Poor Father Jacob watched as the brothers then emptied the grain from their sacks. As it slid out so did their money! “When they and their father saw it, they were afraid” (Genesis 42:35, ICB). _Would the Governor think they’d stolen it? What would happen to them now?_

Big brother Reuben looked at his father bravely. He would take Benjamin back to Egypt and sort out all this confusion. “Trust him to my care. I will bring him back to you,” he said (Genesis 42:37, ICB).

The other brothers nodded in agreement. They weren’t mean brothers anymore. God had changed them into caring, loving brothers.

What did Father Jacob say? **Read Genesis 42:38.**

The brothers looked at each other sadly. What would they do now? They didn’t know. But God did. They could trust Him to supply all their needs.
