**What you need:**

Week 10 craft template, crayons, scissors, small paper plate, rice, bells (optional), stapler, colored paper, two pipe cleaners/chenille sticks, craft feathers, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/craft-template.png)

```

**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut strips of colored paper 1 inch (2.5 centimeters) wide.

**Create it Together:**

1\. Color and cut out the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10b.png)

2\. Fold a small paper plate in half and place some rice and bells inside.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10c.png)

3\. Staple the two halves of the paper plate together, around the inner circle, placing staples close together. Leave a 1-inch (2.5 centimeter) gap on the right-hand corner. Trim the outer edge of the paper plate below the staples.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10d.png)

4\. Curl the strips of colored paper around a crayon. Then vertically fold the lower third of the paper strips to flatten.

5\. Poke the flat end of the paper strips, the bent pipe cleaners, and the feathers through the gap in the paper plate to make the tail. Staple into place.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10e.png)

6\. Glue the craft template onto the paper plate to complete your craft.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/10f.png)
