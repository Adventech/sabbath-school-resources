**Preparation Instructions:**

Print the craft template, and use a craft knife to pre-cut the slits.

**Instructions:**

1\. Color in the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/01/1b.png)

2\. Cut out the waves. Glue the bottom edge of the first wave onto the bottom flap of the second wave.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/01/1c.png)

3\. Slide the waves through the two slits on the page with the boat.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/01/1d.png)

4\. Tell the story, then pull the waves down when the sea becomes calm.
