Noah looked out of the ark’s window at the clear blue sky. No more rain fell, and the ground was almost dry. Soon God would open the very big door of the ark. It was too tall _(reach high)_ for Noah and his wife. Too big _(reach wide)_ for his sons or their wives. The door was even too heavy _(flex arms)_ for the strong ox and the mighty elephant! Noah knew God would open it when the time was just right. Noah and his family believed God’s promises were true. God would open the door soon.\
The white dove had left the ark; she had a new home. Noah and his family were happy! _(Clap.)_ Soon they would have a new home too! What would the world be like after all the rain? Would the hills be covered in green grass again? Would the new trees have yummy foods to eat, like apples, mangoes, and cherries? Would the bushes be filled with juicy berries? _(Rub tummy.)_ Soon they would see! God had kept them safe in the boat. His promises were true. God would take care of Noah and the animals too.

![](assets/12-02.png)

_C-r-e-a-k, c-r-e-a-k (hand motion for opening)_. Slowly the big door swung open. God sent an angel to open the heavy door. Now was the time to leave the big boat. The water was back where it belonged, running in rivers and filling up ponds. They would be able to walk on dry ground! Everyone was so excited! The door opened, and it was time to go! Hooray! _(Clap.)_ The lions roared, and the monkeys chattered. The elephants trumpeted, and the horses neighed. This was a great day!\
Fuzzy grizzly bears, spotted leopards, and raccoons with stripy tails each took their turn going through the great big door. The tall giraffe and the mighty elephant took giant steps. The tigers and sheep hurried to find something yummy to eat. In a flash of feathers, the falcon and the eagle spread their wings _(spread arms)_ to soar high above. A pair of zebras kicked in delight. The turtles took their time. Then came the hedgehogs, curious as could be. What would they see?

Pretty orange flowers filled grassy meadows. Birds chirped happily in the new, small trees. What a wonderful day it was! Noah and his family knew what to do. They would thank God that His promises are true. He had cared for them, just as He’d said.\
They gathered stones: _(count on picture)_ 1 . . . 2 . . . 3 . . . 4 . . . and so many more. Noah built an altar, stacking each stone one by one. This would be a place to remember all that God had done. Noah’s family came together to pray. They would give an offering to say thank You to God.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for caring for me too. I want to remember to say thank You for all You do.
