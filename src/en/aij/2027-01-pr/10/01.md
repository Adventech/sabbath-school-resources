Loving God

The Israelites stood in reverent awe before their mighty God at Mount Sinai. Their ears and hearts were wide open as God’s voice boomed out from the cloud and rolled across the plains.

“You shall have no other gods before Me. You shall not make for yourselves a carved image. . . . You shall not take the name of the Lord your God in vain. . . . Remember the Sabbath day . . . ” (Exodus 20:3-8). God’s first four commandments had told the people important ways to show love and respect for their mighty God.

Now it was time for God to teach them how to love and respect each other so that they could live together peacefully. God knew exactly what He was about to say to them. His next six commandments would describe His beautiful character of love and the way He and the angels lived in harmony with one another in heaven.

God had created His children on earth to live the same way. But because of sin, they often forgot God’s ways. Now it was time to remind them with a set of loving instructions. Those loving instructions are also for you. And for all people who live on the earth. Because we are loved by God, we are called to love like God. His commandments tell us how.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#8d575c", "size": "xl"}}}
THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- How does the Bible say we need to love others? **Read Matthew 22:39.**
- When your family buys something new, such as a phone or some kind of kitchen appliance, it comes with a set of instructions that explain how to care for it.
- Why does the maker of this device give the buyer a set of instructions?
- Who knows the most about operating the phone or appliance: the maker or your family?
- How does this remind you of God, His commandments, and His people?
