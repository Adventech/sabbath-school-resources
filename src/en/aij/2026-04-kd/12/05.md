The joyful mother hugged her son tightly. She kissed his cheeks. Then she hugged him some more. It was a miracle! The young man, who was dead, was now alive!

Instantly, the sad funeral turned into a joyful parade. People clapped happily. They were so amazed! After hugging each other with joy, they probably hugged the mother and son too!

Then the crowd broke into songs of praise to God. “They said, ‘A great prophet has come to us! God is taking care of his people’ ” (Luke 7:16, ICB).

The sad town of Nain was once again filled with joy and loveliness, because of what Jesus had done.

News traveled throughout the land about the caring, all-powerful Jesus. He had brought the woman’s son back to life!

When Jesus returns, He will bring our friends and family who loved Him back to life too. **Read about this in 1 Thessalonians 4:16, 17.**

What a joyful celebration that will be! We will be hugging each other, singing, and praising God even louder than the people of Nain. The Bible promises that our “happiness will last forever. Their gladness and joy will fill them completely. Sorrow and sadness will go far away” (Isaiah 35:10, ICB).

Thank You, Jesus!
