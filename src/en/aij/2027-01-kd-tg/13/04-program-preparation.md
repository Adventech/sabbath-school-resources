**Opening Activities**

Source matching picture cards for memory game or make your own using cardstock.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: **toy or treasured item** given to you by your family in your childhood, such as a teddy bear. Alternatively, ask a helper, or use a picture of you with this item. Source **blank note cards (palm cards)** (at least nine) or make by cutting up **cardstock**, and a **marker**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **storytelling props**: **memory box craft sample, tambourine, cup of water, flatbread**. Make five circular cards on cardstock by drawing around a cup and cutting out. Drawing a symbol of one of the following on each card: pillar of cloud and fire; bush; painted doorpost; tambourine; Red Sea; water/manna. It doesn’t matter what your drawing is like; it will just come to represent that story. After the story, place the five **cards** in the **Story Explorer Box**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: **sticky notes**. Make up the craft example and include here also.

Use peg people or a felt set and **^[week 13 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
