**Word of the Week**

**Faith:** When you have faith in someone, you trust that what they say is always true. Some people put their faith in things or in other people, but God is the only one we can always trust.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God led Abram, Sarai, Lot, and all their household from Ur into the land of Canaan as an act of love. God promised to make Abram the father of a great nation. As Abram started this journey of faith, his connection to God through prayer enabled God to grow his faith with every obedient step.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that following God means to have faith in Him and trust His words.
- **Feel** a desire to follow God.
- **Respond** by choosing to follow God.
