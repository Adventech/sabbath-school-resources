In 1915, a lady named Ellen G. White passed away. She had spoken and written about health, education, theology, family, devotional life, prayer, and leadership for seventy years.

Her writings are now available in more than 150 languages—more than any other American author. She wrote more than 5,000 articles and 40 books and left behind more than 100,000 handwritten pages.

Some people say that one of her most powerful paragraphs is the one below. When fully understood, it covers our entire life, including our faith and our finances:

“The Father’s presence encircled Christ, and nothing befell Him but that which infinite love permitted for the blessing of the world. Here was His source of comfort, and it is for us. He who is imbued with the Spirit of Christ abides in Christ. The blow that is aimed at him falls upon the Savior, who surrounds him with His presence. Whatever comes to him comes from Christ. He has no need to resist evil, for Christ is his defense. Nothing can touch him except by our Lord’s permission, and “all thing” that are permitted “work together for good to them that love God.” Romans 8:28” (Ellen G. White, Thoughts from the Mount of Blessings, p. 71).

What an incredible promise! When walking with Christ, any blow that the enemy aims at us falls on Jesus first, and He only lets through those challenges that will work together for our good.

Our life is in God’s hands. What an awesome place to be!

### Appeal

As we now return God’s tithe and give our regular and systematic Promise offerings, let’s do it with a joyful and grateful heart. Jesus is our Protector and our Provider.
