Opening Activities

Source **^[outline of a sack]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child, and **dried corn, glue, pencils/crayons**.

Source a **plate with raised sides, milk or soy milk, food coloring (four colors), dishwashing liquid, cotton swabs/balls**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Have a **map** available to show the children the journey Jacob’s sons took from Canaan to Egypt.

Source and prepare the following props to use throughout the story when prompted: **kernels of grain or corn in a small sack or paper bag**.

Source **paper** and **scissors** to create a snowflake.

Source a **whiteboard** or **poster board** and **markers**.
