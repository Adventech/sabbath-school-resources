**What you need:**

Week 6 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, four small colored rocks or pebbles, a die, resealable bag.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/6a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/2027-01-pr-craft-06.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Use colored pencils to shade in 10 or more numbered spaces on the board.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/6b.jpg)

2\. Cut out the action cards. Use the blank cards to write more action cards.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/6c.jpg)

3\. Play the game with a friend/s. Choose a colored rock/pebble and place them at the starting point in Egypt. Take turns rolling the die. If you land on a colored space, pick up an action card and read it aloud. The game ends when the first person makes it to the Promised Land. When you’re done, keep all the pieces in a resealable bag so you can play it again.
