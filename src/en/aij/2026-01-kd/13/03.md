After God helped Abram understand His promise again, the sun came up, and the sun went down . . . again and again and again. Days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into years, and still there was no baby for Abram and Sarai.

Now it was 24 years since God had first promised Abram a son. It was hard to be patient for that long! How old was Abram now? **Read Genesis 17:1.**

That’s a grand old age for Abram! The Bible says that Sarai was nearly 90 years old. After all this waiting, God spoke again to Abram and said, “I am changing your name from Abram to Abraham. This is because I am making you a father of many nations” (Genesis 17:5, ICB).

A new name, given by God. How special! Now whenever anyone said his new name, they were calling him a father and reminding him of God’s promise.

Then God said, “I will change the name of Sarai, your wife. Her new name will be Sarah. I will bless her. I will give her a son” (Genesis 17:15, 16, ICB). God also told Abraham that his son should be called Isaac.

While waiting for their promise, God was changing them into something better. God is changing us too, while we wait for His promises.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think God took a long time to fulfill His promise to Abraham?
- What does your name mean?
