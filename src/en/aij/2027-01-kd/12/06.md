Bezalel waved at Moses. All was finished. The builders and sewers, makers, strong lifters, and messengers all gathered around. It was time for Moses to look over all the different parts of God’s home.

Moses smiled as he checked every detail. The sanctuary had been made with gifts from the people, and by those who used their special talents. Every piece was beautiful!

It would be golden and glowing on the inside, and it could be moved. There was a place for all the people to be forgiven, and to learn about the promised Savior too. But the best part was the Most Holy Place.

God’s home had two rooms. The one at the front was called the Holy Place. This was where the priests worked. The back room was the Most Holy Place. God had said, “And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8). The Most Holy Place was where God lived. In the middle of the Most Holy Place was the Ten Commandments.

Moses finished checking all the parts that would make God’s home. Was it perfect? Could they set it up and invite God to move in? **Read Exodus 39:42, 43.**

There is another place God wants to live too—it’s inside your heart! Will you invite Him in?
