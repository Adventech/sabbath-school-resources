**Show children the butterfly life cycle poster and point to each step as you talk about it.**

Mother butterfly lays some eggs on a crisp, green leaf. Then she flutters away. Soon the eggs hatch. Out comes a little caterpillar. She is hungry. Munch, munch, munch. Caterpillars love to eat leaves for their lunch!

When the caterpillar has grown big and fat, it’s time to make a special home called a chrysalis. The caterpillar weaves the chrysalis around herself, and there she stays for a long time. While she’s in the chrysalis, an amazing thing happens. The caterpillar doesn’t stay a big, fat caterpillar. She turns into a beautiful butterfly! Soon she pops out of the chrysalis, spreads her wings, and flies away.

Isn’t it amazing how Jesus can change caterpillars into butterflies? And isn’t it amazing how Jesus’ love changed Zacchaeus when he decided to give his life to Jesus? Jesus can make big changes in your life too!

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. His love changes me.**
