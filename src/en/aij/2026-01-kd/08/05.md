Noah hummed as he fed the animals. Soon they would find new homes and food for themselves. The water around the boat was getting lower and lower. But still Noah and his family waited. God had shut the door to the ark, and now God would be the one to open it.

_Creak . . . creak . . ._ Could it be? After all this time? Yes, it was! God was opening the big ark door! Finally, after a year on the ark, the washed-clean world was ready for them to live in.

God said to Noah, “Go out of the ark, you and your wife, and your sons and your sons’ wives with you. Bring out with you every living thing” (Genesis 8:16, 17).

Noah and his family smiled excitedly as they stepped off the boat and wiggled their toes in the fresh new grass. They pointed and laughed as they watched the horses prancing, lions wrestling, and foxes chasing each other’s fluffy tails.

There was so much to do in this clean earth to make it their home! What did Noah do first? **Read Genesis 8:20.**

Noah collected big, heavy rocks from the ground and stacked them on top of each other to build an altar. He and his family wanted to say a big thank-You to God, so they offered a sacrifice.

Beside the altar, Noah and His family gave thanks to God. God had watched over them and saved them. God promises, “I will never leave you nor forsake you” (Hebrews 13:5b). Thank You, God.
