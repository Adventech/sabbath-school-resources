Joseph tossed and turned in his bed. He was so upset. He was supposed to be marrying Mary soon. But he was so confused. He thought about what Mary had told him. The story seemed impossible! She had told him that an angel had spoken to her. That she was pregnant with the promised Savior, the Son of God! Could he believe her? What should he do?

Finally Joseph fell into a restless sleep. While he slept, God sent an angel to encourage him.

“Joseph, . . . don’t be afraid to take Mary as your wife. The baby in her is from the Holy Spirit,” the angel said (Matthew 1:20, ICB).

“And she will bring forth a Son, and you shall call His name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21).

Mary’s story was true! God wanted Mary and Joseph to be the parents of the promised Savior of the world.

What did Joseph do? **Read Matthew 1:24.**

Joseph chose to trust God’s plan. He knew that God would be with them. God would be their helper as they raised this special baby, Jesus. One day He would save the world from sin and death!
