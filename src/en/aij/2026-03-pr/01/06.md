Elizabeth held her miracle son close to her heart. Her prayers had been answered—in such an amazing way. God’s Savior was on His way. And her little one would grow up to help prepare everyone to surrender their hearts to Him.

The stage was set in God’s amazing story. Jesus was coming! This was the best news sinful people could ever hear. Read this good news in **John 3:16**. The tiny baby growing inside of Mary would one day give eternal life to everyone who loves God.

We are living near the end of God’s big story, and He will soon give us the eternal life John spoke of. Jesus is preparing for that time right now. John 14:2-4 says, “I go to prepare a place for you. And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and receive you to myself; that where I am, there you may be also.”

Hebrews 6:20 tells us something else about Jesus in heaven. It says, “Where the forerunner has entered for us, even Jesus . . . ” Pause and think about that: Jesus is our forerunner!

What a surprising and beautiful thing! One day soon Jesus may tell all of heaven something like this: “Look at the people coming behind Me; look at all these faithful people from the earth! They have loved Me and surrendered their lives to Me. Now they’re coming to join us in heaven.”

John was Jesus’ forerunner. Jesus is our forerunner.
