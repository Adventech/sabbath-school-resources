**What you need:**

Week 8 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, craft sticks (one per member of family), colored/patterned paper, glue, pipe cleaners/chenille sticks, tape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8a.jpg)

**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut large and small, triangles and rectangles, out of colored and/or patterned paper.

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2027-01-kd-craft-08.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color and cut out the faces from the craft resource to match each member of your family (e.g., Mom, Dad, brother, and yourself).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8b.jpg)

2\. Choose some “clothing” shapes and glue onto the craft sticks (e.g. large triangle for Mom, large rectangle for Dad, small rectangle for brother, small triangle for yourself).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8c.jpg)

3\. Glue the faces at the top of the craft sticks.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8d.jpg)

4\. Wind a small loop at both ends of a pipe cleaner/chenille stick and tape to the back of each craft- stick person.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8e.jpg)

5\. Have fun playing with your own craft-stick family!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/8f.jpg)
