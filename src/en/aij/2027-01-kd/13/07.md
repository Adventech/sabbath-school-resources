The Israelites had so many amazing things to remember! And their big adventure with God had only just begun . . .

**^[Game: Remember When . . .]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg", "color": "#7775A3"}}})**

Now it’s your family’s turn to remember.

Have all family members spend five minutes collecting “remembering objects” from around the home or garden. These objects might remind you of fun times, holidays, outings, adventures, celebrations, or special church days. For example, you might find a shell you collected when you went to the beach. Or a sweater Grandma knitted when you went to visit her. Bring your objects back together. Take turns guessing the memory that belongs to that object.

**^[Remembering Gallery]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg", "color": "#7775A3"}}})**

This Sabbath afternoon, take your family on a tour through your remembering gallery. Show them the pictures and tell them the stories of what happened. We have learned all about the Israelites, from Moses’ birth to God’s new sanctuary home. There are so many “memory moments” to share. If there are some more pictures that you’d like to add first, do so. Then take your tour! (Parents, you may like to read Psalm 105 for your own enrichment, which was written to help God’s people remember.)

**^[Remember God]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg", "color": "#7775A3"}}})**

Remembering helped the Israelites to be thankful and to trust God. We need to remember God’s goodness and love too! Bring out the 10 things you collected on Tuesday that help you remember God is our mighty Creator. Your family may like to add some more. Thank God for all His miracles in nature and for taking care of you. Talk about the good things God has done for you and your family, and why you want to always remember them.

“Remember His marvelous works which He has done” (1 Chronicles 16:12).
