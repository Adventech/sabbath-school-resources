Eve looked at the tree. She looked at the beautiful fruit. She made a choice. Reaching out her hand, “she took some of its fruit and ate it” (Genesis 3:6, ICB). Crunch. Oh, no!

Soon Eve stood in front of Adam, holding the fruit proudly in her hands. Adam’s eyes opened wide in shock. _That was fruit from the “don’t touch it” tree! Why was Eve holding it?_ Eve gave the fruit to Adam and told him that she had eaten it and was fine. There was nothing to worry about.

What did Adam do? **Read Genesis 3:6.**

Instantly “the eyes of both of them were opened” (Genesis 3:7). They saw things differently. They looked at each other in surprise. Their robes of light had faded away! They were suddenly naked, cold . . . and scared!

Quickly Adam and Eve stitched fig leaves together to try to cover themselves up. They felt terrible!

God had seen the whole thing. He knew they had eaten the fruit, and it made Him so sad. He knew they were now experiencing the sad consequences of sin. But God wanted to give them another choice. He loved them so much.
