**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show two pictures of a boy (one happy and one sad). Share how these two pictures remind you of some words in **Psalm 32:1-3, 5**. Repentance can turn sad into happy.

**Turn-around game:** Play a game with the children to demonstrate what repentance means. Have children all face the same direction of the room. Tell them that you will say a number and a way of moving forward in one direction. For example: “Jump 3 steps,” “Move 1 step,” “Slide 5 steps.” When you say, “Turn around,” they need to do a complete **U-turn** and quickly go back to their starting place. Repeat with different moves, and finally a U-turn.

**Teaching Point:** When we **repent from the wrong things** we have done, we also change direction. We tell God we’re sorry for the wrong things we have done, and we ask for forgiveness. We then turn away from our sins.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **God prepares hearts to receive Jesus.** (He prepares our U-turns.)

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Give each child a washable marker and a cutout person that is laminated or placed inside a plastic sheet. Invite them to write or draw “sins” (such as lying, fighting, being disobedient) on their cutout person. Then let them wash the sin away with water.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How did it feel to wash away those sins?**
- **As we repent, what kinds of thoughts or actions will replace the things we used to do?**
- **When John baptized people, it symbolized the washing away of their sins and the start of a new life following God. But Jesus didn’t have any sin. Why did He need to be baptized?** (He was baptized as our example.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus offers to wash away our sins and give us a new life when we repent and are baptized.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 32: John Baptizes Jesus** to place in their storage container.

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children a glass of water. Explain how the water represents our hearts, and how we want clean pure hearts. Show some red food dye and how it represents sin in our lives. Add one drop of red food dye at a time, stating different sins each drop might represent (for example: telling a lie, being mean to a friend, taking something that is not yours, etc.). Observe how the water is now red and not clean any more. Ask: **How can we clean our hearts?** (By repenting and choosing not to do those things anymore.)

Add some bleach (representing how Jesus cleans us) and watch as the water turns clean again.

**Teaching Point:** Repentance isn’t just about saying “I’m sorry”—it’s about turning away from those wrong actions and inviting God to change us from the inside out. God loves to do this because He loves us so much.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** **Whom could you share today’s story with?**

**Challenge the children to use their craft to share the story and its message with at least one other person in the coming week.**

Also, encourage the children to **place their craft in a spot where they will notice it during the week to remind them to turn away from sin and repent**, and turn toward God, who wants to give us a new heart. Remind children that next week you will be collecting the final thank you notes and crafts, or goods, to give as a thank-you gift to those who serve in your community.

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God prepares hearts to receive Jesus.**

Refer to the picture of Jesus on your classroom mural, and talk about how He is our example, our Savior, and our friend. Give each child a sticky note and invite them to write their name on it and place it around the picture of Jesus if they too would like to give their hearts to Him to show that they belong to Him.

**Teaching Point:** God hopes we will follow Jesus’ example by giving our lives to Him and one day being baptized as Jesus was.
