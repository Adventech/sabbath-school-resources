[[Revelation 11:1]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Chapter 10 ended with the call to “prophesy again.” The very next verse (Rev. 11:1) points us to the work of Jesus in “the temple,” the heavenly sanctuary. Thus, a major focus of “prophesying again” is the ministry of Jesus as our high priest in the heavenly sanctuary (see previous discussion on Daniel 7:13, 14; 8:14, etc.). He is our comfort, our strength, and the one who, through His work in the sanctuary above, is helping us become people of justice, goodness, and love.
- We should also expect a reappearance soon of the 2,300-day prophecy of Daniel 8:14, since that prophecy specifically points to Jesus’ work for us as our high priest in the heavenly sanctuary.

[[Revelation 11:2-3]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The “forty-two months” in verse 2 is equal to the “one thousand two hundred and sixty days” of verse 3 (an apocalyptic month is 30 days). Both refer to the 1260-year prophecy we first learned about in Daniel 7:25. Those were the 1260 years of persecution by the little-horn power against the true followers of Christ.
- The reappearance here of the 1260-year period is significant. For our readers, it means that the time has come to identify whom this pivotal, end-time, little-horn power is: It is the Papacy, the ruling body of the Church of Rome.
- To see how this is so, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r11a/)**.
- It is extremely important to note that God, in identifying the Papacy as the little-horn power, is speaking of the Roman governance _system_, and not _individual_ Catholic _members_.
- Millions of Catholics are upstanding individuals who are conscientiously serving God to the best of their abilities. _No one should take God’s condemnation of the Roman system and use that as an excuse to disparage individual Catholic members._ Such misguided people, if unrepentant, will certainly not receive the seal of God at the end of time.
- As was pointed out in the preceding QR code, with the deliverance of Rome from the Ostrogoths in A.D. 538, the Papacy began its initial reign of 1260 years.
- During that time the Papacy persecuted millions of sincere followers of Christ.
- The Papacy’s initial reign ended in 1798 when the French general Berthier ordered that Pope Pius VI be taken captive. The pope died in 1799 while exiled in the French city of Valence.
- God’s prophecies are thus trustworthy and true. The 1260-year prophecy came to pass exactly as predicted.
- As we shall see in Revelation 13, the Papacy is predicted to return to global prominence in the last days of earth’s history.

[[Revelation 11:4-10]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The “two witnesses” of verse 3-12 most likely symbolize the Old and New Testaments of the Bible. God never leaves Himself without witness even during the 1260 years of papal persecution. To see how this is so, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r11b/)**.
- At the end of the 1260 years of persecution the two witnesses are killed (verse 7) in a city that is symbolically called Sodom and Egypt, where Jesus was crucified afresh.
- This refers to the attempts of the late-eighteenth-century French Revolution to destroy the Old and New Testaments. To see how this is so, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r11c/)**.

[[Revelation 11:11-12]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The “three-and-a-half days” of verses 9 and 11 are most likely a reference to the three and a half years from 1793 to 1797. In 1793 the leaders of the French Revolution banned the Old and New Testaments and the churches of those that followed Christ. In 1797 those banishments were lifted.
- Astonishingly, after 1797, France experienced a vibrant time of growth among the churches that followed Jesus, a fact most likely reflected in the words “the breath of life from God entered them” (verse 11).
- This revival struck fear in the hearts of those who previously had tried to outlaw God’s Word and His followers.

[[Revelation 11:13-19]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- While not showing the events causing it, heaven here declares that, with the blowing of the seventh trumpet, Satan has been vanquished (verse 15), and Jesus has won.
- As in Revelation 11:1, John’s focus once again turns to the heavenly sanctuary. But this time we see an even more finely tuned focus. Not only is the heavenly sanctuary revealed, but “the ark of His covenant” in that sanctuary is brought front and center.
- The ark of God’s covenant in the temple contains the Ten Commandments, the authoritative expression of God’s law and His character.
- The law of God, rightly understood and kept, is a foundational part of God’s kingdom of justice, goodness, and love.
- This focus on God’s law shows that it will play a crucial role in the last days of earth’s history (more on this when we study Revelation 13 and 14).
