Trees and flowers, the heavens and light, stars and the sun—all show us God’s love. His laws work inside of them, so they cannot choose to show their love in special ways, as animals and people can. God’s love inside the hearts of His sixth-day creatures can shine out in deep and special ways.

Lawrence Anthony, a famous explorer and nature lover, rescued and adopted wild and destructive elephants in Africa to stop them from being shot. He loved them, made friends with them, and cared for them. They loved him back. Two days after he died in 2012, his family was surprised to see two herds of elephants arrive at their house. The elephants had walked for 12 hours to quietly grieve with Lawrence’s family, even though they hadn’t been to his home for a year and a half, and no one could have told them he had died.

God’s love made their hearts start grieving when Lawrence’s heart stopped beating. His family and friends had loved him very deeply too, so it was comforting and special for them to all be able to grieve together with the elephants Lawrence had loved so greatly.

We can thank God for giving animals the ability to share and receive His love in special ways.
