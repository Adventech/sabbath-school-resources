- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- At the time of the end, the meaning of the “sealed” (unexplained) portions of the book of Daniel will be revealed. This is good news! Some of these prophecies even Daniel failed to understand, but thankfully, through the book of Revelation, these mysteries have been unsealed, so keep reading!

[[Daniel 12:5-7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Here reiterated is the 1,260-day-for-a-year prophecy, which we’ve already studied.

[[Daniel 12:8-12]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Here are two new prophetic time periods: 1,290 days/years, and 1,335 days/years. Nearly all are agreed that both of them are tied to the 1,260-year prophecy but focus on separate events with which this period begins and ends.

[[Daniel 12:13]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- As we have seen in the book of Daniel, Jesus Christ is our high priest in the heavenly sanctuary right now. He is working to bring us into His kingdom, which will be established soon, never to fall. Let’s refuse to be distracted by other gods or idols, for every false god in the book of Daniel failed to provide for those who unwisely trusted in them. Put your trust instead in Christ, the most high God, the embodiment of justice, goodness, and love. Only He can save you! And in saving you, as citizens of His eternal kingdom, He will transform you to become a person of justice, goodness, and love, as well.
