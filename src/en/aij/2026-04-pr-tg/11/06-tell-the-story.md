**Big Idea:** Jesus sees my need and responds to my faith in Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- You may like to use the following props: **Bible-times map** of the Sea of Galilee showing the town of Capernaum, **pictures** of you as a baby, child, and adult for the illustration.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[(Show the town of Capernaum on a map before you begin. This is where this story takes place.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Roman centurion sat beside the bed of his faithful servant and friend. He was worried. Very worried! His servant was terribly sick, and the doctors hadn’t been able to cure him. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The centurion and his servant were unlikely friends. The Bible says the servant was “dear to him” (Luke 7:2).

The Roman centurion had many Jewish friends and neighbors. Over time he heard about God and came to believe in the religion of the Jews. He even helped them build a synagogue to worship God.

The centurion also came to hear about Jesus and His teachings. A seed of faith was planted in his heart. The more he heard about Jesus’ power and God’s kingdom of love, the more the seed of faith grew.

_(Note: take no longer than five minutes to do the following activity.)_

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DO:** Show the children photographs of when you were a baby, then as a child/teenager, and a present one. Talk about how different you look and how you changed and grew. Talk about the things you came to be able to do over time as you grew up.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DISCUSS:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What things did I need to grow?** (Food, water, shelter, love, etc.)
- **Did you know that in a similar way as we grow physically, we can also grow our faith?**
- **In what ways do you think we can grow our faith?** (Hearing God’s Word, trusting in Jesus to help us, praying, obeying and acting on what we know to be true, sharing God with others, etc.)

The more the Roman centurion heard about God and Jesus, the more his faith grew. Jesus wants to grow our faith in Him more and more also.

**Teaching Point:** We can grow our faith by listening to and trusting God’s words in the Bible.

Let’s continue the story. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The centurion desperately needed help. He believed Jesus was the only one who could heal his servant and friend.

But how would he ask Jesus for help? The centurion didn’t feel he deserved to have Jesus come to his home and heal his dying servant. He was also aware of who he was—a Roman soldier, not a Jew. Romans and Jews didn’t get along very well. But these differences of culture didn’t matter to Jesus. In Him we are all one. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

So the centurion went and begged the Jewish elders to ask Jesus to heal his dying servant. The elders quickly agreed and hurried to meet Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did they say to Jesus? Read Luke 7:4, 5.** (They begged Jesus to help him, saying he was deserving because he loves our nation and has built a synagogue.)

The centurion didn’t deserve a miracle because of his good actions. It was simply his faith in Jesus and his need that made him worthy. Jesus immediately set out toward the officer’s home.

News that Jesus was on His way reached the Roman officer. The centurion still didn’t think he was worthy to have Jesus come to his house, so he sent some friends who hurried down the road to tell Jesus not to bother Himself by coming to his house.

Jesus kept walking anyway. When the centurion realized that Jesus was getting closer and closer, he finally found the courage to leave his house and meet Him on the road. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He say to Jesus? Read Luke 7:6-8, ICB.** (“Lord, you don’t need to come into my house. I am not good enough for you to be under my roof. That is why I did not come to you myself. You only need to say the word, and my servant will be healed. I too am a man under the authority of other men. And I have soldiers under my command. I tell one soldier, ‘Go,’ and he goes. And I tell another soldier, ‘Come,’ and he comes. And I say to my servant, ‘Do this,’ and my servant obeys me.”)

Just as the Roman government had authority over him and he had authority over his soldiers, he believed that Jesus, and Jesus had authority over all things on earth.

When Jesus heard those words, He was amazed at the man’s faith! Here was a Roman who believed that Jesus’ words had so much power that He didn’t even need to see or touch a person to heal them! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus turned to the crowd and said, “Assuredly, I say to you, I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel!” (Matthew 8:10).

Then Jesus spoke loudly to the centurion.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jesus say? Read Matthew 8:13, your memory verse.** (“Go your way; and as you have believed, so let it be done for you.”)

At that very second it happened! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the centurion got home, his servant looked and sounded very different. God’s power had surged through his body and made him well.
