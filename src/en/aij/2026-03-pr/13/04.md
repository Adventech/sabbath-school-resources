Peter’s mother-in-law felt amazing! With a spring in her step and light in her eyes, she headed straight to the kitchen to serve her guests. Jesus had healed her, so she immediately passed the blessing on by doing what she could for others. She happily served Sabbath lunch to her family, Jesus, and the disciples.

Imagine the cheery chatter around the table as they ate that meal together in Peter’s house. You can be sure they weren’t fighting or arguing. Neither would they have been bossing Peter’s mother-in-law around—no, they would have been very grateful and eager to help her. Every time they looked at her they would have been reminded of the wonderful healing miracle Jesus had performed. This joy is one they would need to share with others too. Read Matthew 5:16.

Just as Peter’s mother-in-law served others after she was healed, we can be a blessing to others after God has blessed our lives in wonderful ways. If you have been blessed with the ability to read well, for example, you could help someone else learn to read. Blessed with strong legs, you could do a chore for an elderly neighbor. Blessed with a car, your family might give someone a ride to church.

Proverbs 11:25 says, “The generous soul will be made rich, and he who waters will also be watered himself.” When we are a blessing to others, our lives will be blessed as well! It’s like a big circle of blessings, going round and round. Part of the blessings is the way we feel inside after we have done something good for someone else.
