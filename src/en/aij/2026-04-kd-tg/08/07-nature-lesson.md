**Focus:** Lions can teach us how to work and rest.

- **There’s an animal that is known as the king of the jungle. Do you know what it is?** (The lion.)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/nature-lesson.png)

Lions are big cats that live in parts of Africa and India. They live together in groups called prides. They’re the only cats that live together like this. A pride is like a lion family with moms, cubs (lion babies), and a few dads. The mother lion does most of the hunting, and the father lion helps protect the family. He has a big fluffy mane around his head—kind of like a giant furry crown. The little lion cubs love to play and learn how to roar.

- **Can you roar like a baby cub? Can you roar like a father lion?**

Big father lion’s loud roar can be heard up to five miles (eight kilometers) away! They roar to talk to each other and say,

“This is our home!”

Lions are very strong and run very fast. They are smart hunters. Chasing animals takes a lot of energy, so they also spend a lot of time each day resting. They save their strength for when it’s really needed to find dinner for the family. Lions run very quickly, but they don’t always catch each animal they chase. They usually eat only every two to four days. How clever they are to spend their days working and resting.

Lions teach us three important things as we think about our story this week:

1. They teach us that **being strong** means knowing when to rest and when to take care of each other.
2. They teach us that it’s good to **work when needed**, and it is wise to **rest when needed**.
3. They also teach us that **being strong** doesn’t mean always being busy—even the “king of the jungle” knows when to stop.

(Give instructions for the children to prowl like a lion and then pause and rest. Prowl . . . rest. Prowl . . . rest.)

God gave us the special gift of the Sabbath every week so that our hearts can be happy and our minds can be wise. We can rest from doing busywork, and spend time learning about God and being closer to Him and also loving others on His holy day.

Work . . . and rest. Work . . . and rest. Thank You, God, for the Sabbath!
