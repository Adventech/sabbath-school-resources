**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Option 1**—Ask each child to name a favorite food and tell why they like it. Give each child some **modeling clay** and invite them to make the food they like to eat best. Talk about how it’s nice to invite people over to your house and share food together for a meal. Explain that in our story today, a little boy shares his food with Jesus who then performs a miracle and shares it with many, many people.

**Option 2**—Use a food dropper and guess how many drops of water you can fit on a coin of choice. (It will be far more than expected.) After the experiment, say, “It makes me think of the word ‘abundant.’ ” Abundant means having plenty of something—having more than enough. The coin can hold an abundant amount of water. **I wonder what will be in abundance—more than enough—in today’s story?**

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a piece of bread. Ask:

- **What would I do if I wanted to share it?** (Cut or break it into smaller pieces.)

Then cut or break the piece of bread into smaller pieces.

- **Can I share it now?** (Yes.)
- **Do I now have more bread?** (No, just more pieces. It is still just one slice of bread.)
- **Last week we learned what a miracle is. How would you explain this to someone?** An extraordinary event or happening caused by God; something people could never ever do or explain.

**Teaching Point:** In today’s story, Jesus made more bread and fish, not just more pieces, to feed thousands of people. This was a miracle: something extraordinary that only God could do.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **Jesus feeds me with His words and with food.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** If possible, show the children an assortment of bread, including whole wheat, whole grain, focaccia, etc. Allow them to smell and taste some of the bread.

- **How does it smell? How does it taste?**
- **Does Jesus love to supply our needs?** (Yes!)

Jesus doesn’t just feed us with physical food, though.

- **What other kinds of food does Jesus want to feed us?** (Spiritual.) **What does this mean?** (Our thoughts and beliefs about God, and our need for Him are spiritual).

**Teaching Point:** Through our quiet times with Him—in Sabbath School, church, and Bible class at school—Jesus feeds us spiritual food too. He also knows we can’t live a happy Christian life without growing our hearts and minds with Him. This is why we have the Bible. He gives both types of food—food for our body and food for our mind and heart.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})**

- **What do you have that Jesus can multiply?** On the back of their craft, invite the children to draw a quick picture or write a list of things they have that they could give to Jesus to multiply. (For example: music, artistic ability, smiles, cheerful attitude, money, clothes, food, toys.)
- **How can we give these to Jesus so He can multiply them and use them to meet the needs of others?** (Sing songs, make cards for others, be friendly and greet people with a smile. Give money in offerings or give clothes, food, and toys to those in need.)

**Teaching Point:** When you give something to Jesus, you aren’t losing it; He will increase it, so you have more to give.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** In this story, Jesus, with His words and with His gift of food, showed He cared about people. This week, show that you, with your words, care for others. **At the store or at an after-school activity, share a smile and tell someone what a good job they are doing. Share the simple statement “Jesus loves you” or “God bless you.”**

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus feeds me with His words and with food.** Say together joyfully: **Thank You, Jesus!**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Jesus blessed the food that day. He also blesses you. Conclude with a prayer of blessing:

“Dear Jesus, Thank You for these children who You created and love so much. You provide every blessing, both big and small—a home full of love, hands to catch when they fall. Food on their table, warm clothes to wear, friends who bring laughter, a family who cares. Please guide their choices, both day and night, feed them with kindness, truth, and light. Help them to trust, to grow, to be strong, to know You are with them their whole life long. In Jesus’ name, amen.”
