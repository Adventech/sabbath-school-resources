God’s little family didn’t feel like a happy family anymore. Isaac was disappointed. Jacob was weighed down with guilt. Rebekah was bitterly sorry. And Esau cried and raged in anger. They had all been reminded why God says it is never a good idea to lie.

If only Isaac had listened to God, if only Rebekah had waited for God, and if only Jacob had trusted God, things would surely have turned out differently. But God didn’t want them to stay angry and hurt and discouraged by “If only . . .” They couldn’t change the mistakes they had made, but they could remember how huge and wonderful God’s love is.

What else could they remember about God’s love? **Read Psalm 118:1.**

This message of love is so important that God says it again in Lamentations 3:22, 23: “The Lord’s love never ends. His mercies never stop. They are new every morning. Lord, Your loyalty is great” (ICB).

God’s big heart of love can forgive us. We can remember how much He loves us and learn to trust Him more and more.
