**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

“For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life” (John 3:16).

What familiar words. How often have we repeated them, but how deeply do we ponder their breadth and depth? God loves us that much. He gave the most precious, best gift imaginable: His one and only Son. All we need to do is believe in Him, know Him, and love Him in response, and we can be assured of eternal life with Him.

Ellen White says, “The gift of Christ reveals the Father’s heart. . . . It declares that while God’s hatred of sin is as strong as death, His love for the sinner is stronger than death. Having undertaken our redemption, He will spare nothing, however dear, which is necessary to the completion of His work. No truth essential to our salvation is withheld, no miracle of mercy is neglected, no divine agency is left unemployed. Favor is heaped upon favor, gift upon gift. The whole treasury of heaven is open to those He seeks to save. Having collected the riches of the universe, and laid open the resources of infinite power, He gives them all into the hands of Christ, and says, All these are for man. Use these gifts to convince him that there is no greater love than Mine in earth or heaven. His greatest happiness will be found in loving Me” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 57).

In our humanity we will never truly comprehend the vastness of this gift. Jesus really is heaven’s “best and greatest” (Ellen G. White, Education, p. 73).

May you and the children be in awe this week of the amazing Gift God has given us, the best Gift of all, the gift of Jesus.
