**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **storytelling props** and **Story Explorer items**: Create “Happy Hearts” using **six pieces of paper**. Write “Happy Heart” on each with a small number 5-10 on each, and plan to stick them to walls or objects in your room, or lay them on the floor. **Giant heart from last week** with **numbers 1-4** stuck to it. You will need **numbers 5-10** for this week, and **sticky tac** or similar so children can stick them to Happy Heart stations then the giant heart.

Prepare six activity stations:

**Station 5:** Source a **small pile of clothing to fold, toys, and a toy container**.

**Station 6:** Source **six card-sized pieces of paper**. Write the following words on them: Hit, Hate, Push, Yell, Hurt, Pinch. Also cut some **paper hands** (enough to cover words); and source **markers; tape or glue**.

**Station 7:** Make **paper hearts the size of a hand and cut in two** (with unique lines) so they are a puzzle that fits together. Make enough for each child to have a whole heart. Hearts of different colors will make it easier. Source **tape** and have a helper tape the floor while you are at the previous station.

**Station 8: Toy, book, ball**.
