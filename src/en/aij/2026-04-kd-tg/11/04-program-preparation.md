**Opening Activities**

Write out the following Big Ideas from the first 10 lessons of this quarter and put on slips of paper in a bag for lucky dip. Do not include numbers; this is just for your reference.

1. Jesus can do anything. He can keep me calm.
2. Jesus can do anything. His words can heal and are always true.
3. Jesus can do anything. He loves to help me and my friends.
4. Jesus can do anything. He makes me brave.
5. Jesus can do anything. He wants to be my close friend.
6. Jesus can do anything. He can change me when I have faith in Him.
7. Jesus can do anything. He has power over death.
8. Jesus can do anything. He gives us the Sabbath.
9. Jesus can do anything. He wants to save me.
10. Jesus can do anything. He hears me when I call out to Him.

Match with **printed pictures from the Program PowerPoints** or adapt activity to use the lesson guide or digital images.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: Make a sample of the craft, a Roman soldier helmet, and place in box.** Prepare or set up stations with **balls** to roll into a **bucket**; **items** to pick up and put in a **container**; **bells/instruments** from past quarters.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
