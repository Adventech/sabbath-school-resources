Jethro had an idea! He knew just how to help his tired, too-busy son-in-law. First, Moses must talk to God. “You must tell him about their disagreements,” said Jethro (Exodus 18:19, ICB). As Moses told God about the people’s problems, God would help Moses know what to do. Yes, God is always the very best helper!

Then, Moses was to teach the people all about God’s ways. “Tell them the right way to live and what they should do,” Jethro said (Exodus 18:20, ICB).

With God’s help and His wisdom, there would be fewer problems. But Moses still needed helpers. Jethro told Moses to pick some Israelite leaders. For them to be good helpers, what did they need to be like? **Read Exodus 18:21.**

These helpers could have the job of solving the simple problems. Moses could help with the hard problems. Together they would share the work.

Moses was so happy to have good advice from his family. “So Moses listened to his father-in-law and did everything he said” (Exodus 18:24, ICB).

God has given us parents, caregivers, and leaders in the church to love and guide us. When they are following God’s ways, their advice will lead to peace and happiness in our home and in the church. God wants us to work together and help each other. It’s how we can show God’s love.
