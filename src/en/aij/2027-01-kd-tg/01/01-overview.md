**Word of the Week**

**Protect:** To protect is to keep something or someone safe. A parent or caring adult might hold your hand when you cross the road, so they can protect you from traffic. God protects His children too. He wants to save us and keep us from danger.

**Lesson Summary**

The children of Israel had settled in Egypt after Joseph was governor. God blessed them, and they grew in numbers. Threatened by the population boom, a new pharaoh ordered midwives to kill all newborn baby boys. When the midwives didn’t do this, Pharaoh told his people to throw the baby boys into the Nile River. A Hebrew woman named Jochebed hid her baby boy for three months before making a basket, then hiding him in the basket on the river. God had special plans for Moses. Sister Miriam and unseen angels watched over him. Baby Moses was discovered by Pharaoh’s daughter, who decided to keep and raise him as her own. Miriam offered to find someone to nurse the child, and fetched her own mother. Baby Moses was reunited with his family and given a hope and a future.
