**Opening Activities**

Source **cans of food**, or other heavy objects, and **cloth bags**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: colorful slime**, a small **Lego brick set**, a **cute teddy bear**, and/or other items the children would be tempted to touch or eat.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
