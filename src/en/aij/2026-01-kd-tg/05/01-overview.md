**Word of the Week**

**Consequence:** A consequence is something that happens because of a choice we make. Often good choices lead to good consequences, and bad choices lead to bad consequences.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God is love, and love gives freedom to choose. Eve and Adam had to choose whether to trust God’s words and obey His voice. Sadly, they chose to doubt and disobey. Sin entered the world because of this choice. There were terrible consequences for them, and for all the world, ever since. But God had a plan to send His Son to this world and save us from those consequences.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that choices have consequences.
- **Feel** thankful that God always lets us choose.
- **Respond** by choosing to say “Thank You” to God for saving us.
