**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He wants to save me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 9 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use **peg people** (Jesus; a man) or a **felt set** if desired. Put a **heart sticker** on the Jesus peg person.
- Use a piece of **blue cloth** to represent the water in the pool.
- Ask children to join you in some actions.
- Use facial expressions and an engaging voice.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was a beautiful Sabbath day. Jesus walked slowly through the streets of Jerusalem ^[(Jesus peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He stopped at the Pool of Bethesda and looked around. ^[(Stop next to blue cloth “pool.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was very busy. The pool had five large porches around its edges. Sitting or lying under the shade of these porches were lots of people. ^[(Place other peg people.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Some were sick. Some couldn’t see. Others were so unwell that it was hard for them to move.

The people believed that if they could be the first person to get into the water when it moved, they would be healed. So they sat there, watching the water, day after day.

Jesus felt a deep care for every person there. ^[(Place heart sticker on Jesus’ peg person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then Jesus noticed one man. He had been unwell for a very, very long time ^[(peg man, lying down)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus walked over to him and asked kindly, “Do you want to be made well?” (John 5:6).

The man began to tell Jesus all about how helpless he felt. When the water moved, he would try hard to move his weak body toward the pool. But there was no one to help him. Someone else always got to the water before him.

With love in His eyes, Jesus said, “Stand up. Pick up your mat and walk” (John 5:8, ICB).

Immediately the man obeyed! With faith in his heart, he sprung to his feet. (Stand up peg man.) His legs stretched out straight. His arms felt strong. His whole body felt the buzz of health and energy. He was well!

The man was so happy. God cared about him!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The man rolled up his rug and tucked it under his arm. He turned back to thank his Healer. But He was gone. The man didn’t even know the kind Stranger’s name!

With springy steps, the man leaped joyfully through the streets. Under his arm he carried his old mat. With a loud and happy voice he sang praises to God.

Suddenly the man stopped. A group of Jewish people stood in his way. With a grumpy voice one of them said, “Today is the Sabbath. It is against our law for you to carry your mat on the Sabbath day” (John 5:10, ICB).

The healed man said, “‘He who made me well said to me, ‘Take up your bed and walk’ ” (John 5:11).

The people frowned. They weren’t excited that he had been healed. They didn’t understand that the Sabbath was a day to show love for others. Instead, they asked who had healed him. The healed man shrugged his shoulders and told them he didn’t know. Then he ran on to the temple. He really wanted to say thank You to God for healing him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus found him in the temple. The healed man’s eyes lit up. It was the same caring, kind Man who had healed him. He was so excited to thank Jesus properly!

Then, with a serious voice, Jesus said, “See, you have been made well. Sin no more” (John 5:14).

Jesus was worried he might get stuck making the same mistakes that he had made many years before. Those choices had ended up making him sick. Jesus didn’t want him to be sick, hurt, or unhappy. Jesus loved that man so much! What a wonderful Savior and Friend.
