Crowds of people pressed into every tiny space in Peter’s house. Many more craned their necks at the open doors and windows. It seemed that everyone wanted to be near Jesus. The rich, the poor; the tall, the short; the curious, the unbelieving. No one wanted to miss out.

The man with paralysis decided he didn’t want to miss out either. He asked his four good friends to take him to Jesus. Gladly they picked up his bed mat and carried him toward Peter’s house. But when they arrived, they couldn’t get through the dense crowd. They tried again and again, but it was no use. The crowd wouldn’t let them inside. Jesus was so close, and yet so far away.

Then the friends had a brilliant idea. The man smiled and nodded. His friends carried him up the outside stairs. Setting him down, they quickly got to work grabbing, digging, pulling, and lifting. What were they doing on this roof? **Read Luke 5:19.**

The surprised crowds below looked up to see . . . sunshine . . . four smiling faces . . . then a bed mat with a man on it being slowly lowered from the roof! Down, down it came, until the man with paralysis was lying at Jesus’ feet.

The friends watched Jesus from above as their friend looked up at Jesus with pleading eyes. “When Jesus saw his faith, as well as the faith of his friends, He said to the man, “Son, your sins are forgiven you” (Mark 2:5).

Jesus already knew these four friends believed in Him, but now everyone could see they had put their faith into action. They had helped their friend, given him hope, and allowed his faith to grow too. Jesus now gave the man with paralysis what he needed most. He was forgiven!
