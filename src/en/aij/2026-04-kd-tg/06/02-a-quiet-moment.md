**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

She was truly desperate—this nameless woman with a painful and embarrassing illness. She’d used much of her energy, spent all her savings, and cried many tears, yet still she’d found no relief. It seemed she was destined to live out her life in pain, weakness, and solitude.

Yet there was a glimmer of hope: Jesus. She set out to find Him, and despite the great challenges in reaching Him, she persisted. Seeing her opportunity, she bent low, reached out, and touched Him. “In that one touch was concentrated the faith of her life, and instantly her pain and feebleness gave place to the vigor of perfect health” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 343). Her life was forever changed!

Jesus not only restored her health but affirmed her faith and value. She was seen, known, loved, and restored. “Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well” (Luke 8:48).

Your week may be long and challenging. The obstacles between you and quality time with Jesus may be many. But have faith and persist. Reach out to Jesus, for He longs to transform each of us. Trust Him with your weaknesses, your hopes, your shame—your all. He will surely say, “Son, Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well.”

May you be blessed as you draw closer to Jesus this week, and as you encourage the children in your care to have faith in our loving Savior.
