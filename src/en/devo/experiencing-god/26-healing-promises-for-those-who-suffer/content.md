---
title: Healing Promises for Those Who Suffer
---

> <callout></callout>
> The Great Physician still works in our world today.\
Whether He heals you physically, mentally, or spiritually, He will not fail to satisfy your deepest desires. Invite Him to walk through the valley of suffering beside you.

1. “I call on the Lord in my distress, and he answers me.” (Ps.120:1)
2. “Praise the Lord, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits—who forgives all your sins and heals all your diseases.” (Ps. 103:2, 3)
3. “Before they call I will answer; while they are still speaking I will hear.” (Isa. 65:24)
4. “He gives strength to the weary and increases the power of the weak.” (Isa. 40:29)
5. “He has sent me to bind up the brokenhearted, to proclaim freedom for the captives and release from darkness for the prisoners, to proclaim the year of the Lord’s favor and the day of vengeance of our God, to comfort all who mourn, and provide for those who grieve in Zion—to bestow on them a crown of beauty instead of ashes, the oil of gladness instead of mourning, and a garment of praise instead of a spirit of despair.” (Isa. 61:1-3)
6. “Heal me, O Lord, and I will be healed; save me and I will be saved, for you are the one I praise.” (Jer. 17:14)
7. “Therefore I tell you, whatever you ask for in prayer, believe that you have received it, and it will be yours.” (Mark 11:24)
8. “For the pardon of sin, for the Holy Spirit, for a Christlike temper, for wisdom and strength to do His work, for any gift He has promised, we may ask; then we are to believe that we receive, and return thanks to God that we have received.” (Education, p. 258).
9. “Is any one of you sick? He should call the elders of the church to pray over him and anoint him with oil in the name of the Lord. And the prayer offered in faith will make the sick person well; the Lord will raise him up. If he has sinned, he will be forgiven.” (James 5:14, 15)

_Bible verses on this page quoted from NIV (1984 edition)_
