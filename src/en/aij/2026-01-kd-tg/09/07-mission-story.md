Even when Shanker was a little boy, he often made trouble. It seemed he always felt angry inside, so he argued with anyone who didn’t agree with him. He even tried to hurt other people, pushing them when he got angry. Now that he was a big boy, Shanker was making more and more bad choices and being friends with other troublemakers.

His mom and dad were sad that their son was hurting himself and other people. They knew that they needed to do something to help him. They decided to send him away from their home city of Mumbai, the biggest city in India, to a Christian school in another city.

Shanker’s journey to the new school took two days by train. He felt alone and a little scared. What would it be like in this new school? Also, where would he live?

His teachers were kind to him, and his classmates didn’t try to fight back when he argued with them. A kind teacher helped him with his math and encouraged him to take time to read his Bible every day.

He was surprised when he was told he would go to worship every day, where he heard about Jesus for the first time. “As I learned about Jesus, my life started to change,” Shanker says. “My classmates and teachers helped me know more about God, and I started to see that only Jesus could make me happy.”

Now Shanker loves reading the Bible and praying. He takes part in worship and is a leader in his favorite sport. As team captain he gathers his teammates together before a game with another school to pray that God will protect them from getting hurt and help them be good examples to the other team.

Sometimes members of the other team try to make Shanker’s team angry. They use unkind words, but Shanker’s friends ignore those words. If they lose, they are good sports; and if they win, they praise God. Shanker says, “My life changed completely after I asked Jesus into my life. Now Jesus comes first for me.”

- **What changed Shanker from being proud and angry to being kind and loving?**

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. He is never proud.**
