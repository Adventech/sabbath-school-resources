title: Unsung Heroes
date: 14/11/2026
---

Global Mission pioneers are some of the unsung heroes of the Adventist mission. Around the world today, hundreds of these dedicated laypeople are entering new areas and reaching previously unreached people groups to start new Seventh-day Adventist congregations.

Many of the places they go are areas where the church has struggled or never succeeded in the past, but no one has told the pioneers that it can’t be done.

Living on a modest stipend, they move into these communities and put Christ’s method of ministry into practice. They mingle with the people, working alongside them in the rice fields or playing soccer with children in the city. They show sympathy, meet practical needs, and earn the confidence of those they serve. Then, they invite them to follow Jesus.

Like Jesus, these pioneers immerse themselves in the communities they serve. They sacrifice their time and efforts to live simply and bring others into a loving relationship with God.

And it is to support them that the church has the Annual Sacrifice Fund. If your territory has adopted the Combined Offering Plan, a portion of your regular and systematic offerings will be allocated to this fund. In addition to that, once a year, members around the world are invited to sacrifice another portion of their income, besides their tithe and Promise offerings, and send that portion to this fund.

Just as God has called these Global Mission pioneers to give up so much to serve Him, He calls us to sacrifice too—even if we remain at home.

### Appeal
