**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Jesus cared for everyone He met. Whether it was on a boat, sitting on the mountainside, in a fisherman’s home, walking the streets, or simply standing under an olive tree, Jesus cared. He saw the needs of every person; of every situation; and He lived to gently draw people to Himself and to His Father. “. . . the Saviour’s work was not restricted to any time or place. His compassion knew no limit” (Ellen G. White, ^[Ministry of Healing]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "CenturyGothic-Italic"}}}), pg. 1).

Jesus still cares. You might teach this to your children and believe it in your heart, but when did you last feel the embrace of heaven upon your weary body or your empty soul? Perhaps, like many of these miracle stories, you have significant needs, fears, or challenges

that seem too great to bear. Yet, despite living a few thousand years later than the stories in this children’s program, we can believe that Jesus cares for us too.

As you teach these lessons about Jesus’ care and His miracles and healings this quarter, pause to notice that it’s not solely about a physical healing or provision. It’s about so much more. Jesus drew people to Himself. He wanted to have a close friendship with every person He met. He wants this with you too.

Yes, Jesus cares for you, dear Teacher. May you know it as you teach this timeless truth this quarter. Indeed, “He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).
