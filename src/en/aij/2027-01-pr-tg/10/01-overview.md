**Word of the Week**

**Respect:** Acting and speaking in ways that show care and honor to others. Treating others as you would like to be treated.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

The Israelites listened reverently as God spoke the last six commandments at Mount Sinai. As God delivered His loving advice, they learned what it meant to love and respect others so that everyone could live safe and happy lives together. These laws are for all people of all time. They are so important that God spoke them aloud, wrote them with His finger, recorded them in the Bible and writes them in our hearts and minds.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that because God loves us, He teaches us how to show love and respect for others.
- **Feel** a desire to live in ways that love and respect others.
- **Respond** by asking God to put His ten-commandment law of love in their hearts.
