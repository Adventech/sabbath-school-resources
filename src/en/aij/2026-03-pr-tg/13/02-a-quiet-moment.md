**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God spoke these powerful words to Abraham: “I will bless you . . . and you shall be a blessing” (Genesis 12:2). When we’ve experienced God’s power in our lives and the ways He helps and blesses us, we want others to know this too. Every blessing given to us by God is not a treasure to be hoarded, but a gift to be shared with another. Every kind word and gentle deed becomes a tender echo of His love—a ripple that reaches far beyond our sight.

When Jesus was on earth, He lived to reflect His Father’s true character, to restore the broken relationship between humankind and heaven, to teach and show God’s wonderful truths. When we know God, we will want others to be blessed with this same knowledge and connection with Jesus. As He gives to us, He wants us to give to others. When we rise in the morning, we will want to ask God, “How can I be a blessing today? How can I help someone, love someone, encourage someone on Your behalf today, Father?” And as we go about our day, He will show us who needs a special blessing from above. As we do His work, we find that we are blessed more.

May God bless you, and the children you teach, abundantly as you endeavor to be a blessing to each other.
