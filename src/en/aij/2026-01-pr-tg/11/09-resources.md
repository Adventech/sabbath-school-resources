```=Making the Right Choice Cards

Create a set of Making the Right Choice cards. Take five pieces of paper and write one of the following on each piece:

- There is one piece of pie left and you want it, but others might want it too. What should you do?
- You are building a very tall tower with your LEGO building blocks. Your younger brother comes along and wants to build one of his own, but there aren’t enough pieces. What could you do?
- Your mother asks you to set the table for dinner, but it isn’t your turn. What should you do?
- You are given a bag of candy when you are having lunch with your friends. You want to quickly hide it and take it home. What should you do?
- A bowl of fruit is being passed around the table. You love grapes, so you want to take them all. What should you do?

```
