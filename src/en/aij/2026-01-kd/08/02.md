Pigs grunted, parrots whistled, and hyenas squealed. Noah and his family of just eight people must have laughed as they looked around the ark. This was their new home. What a noisy, smelly . . . amazing place!

Outside the ark, the people stared at the boat. They had watched all the animals walk into the ark. They saw the big open door. But they chose not to come into the boat. God longed to save everyone, but He wouldn’t force those who didn’t want to be saved.

Suddenly the big, heavy door swung s-l-o-w-l-y shut. The Bible says, “Then the Lord closed the door behind them” (Genesis 7:16, ICB).

God closed the door, and only He could open it.

That evening the stars twinkled against the still night sky. When morning came, the sun rose, as usual, in the clear blue sky. For seven days everything in nature stayed exactly the same.

The people outside looked up at the blue sky. _Noah must be wrong, they thought. It wasn’t going to rain!_ They banged loudly on the thick sides of the boat and made fun of Noah and his family.

Inside the ark Noah and his family calmly waited. Why weren’t they worried that the rain hadn’t come? What had God said to Noah? **Read Genesis 7:4.**

How kind of God to tell Noah exactly what would happen. The family waited patiently, knowing God always keeps His word.
