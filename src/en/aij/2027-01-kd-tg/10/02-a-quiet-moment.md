**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Picture it—a world in which every child listens to their mother’s gentle instruction and honors their father’s wise counsel. A world in which no doors are locked because nothing is ever stolen, and hearts are never broken because promises are never betrayed. Imagine streets filled with laughter, not lies; homes filled with peace, not pain; marriages strong and families united; neighbors content; no one stung by jealousy.

This is God’s plan for us. In this week’s lesson we are reminded that He carved it in stone for us. His law is perfect, reflecting His character and will for our lives.

However, God knew that as sinners we would fall short. In love He sent Jesus to make that picture possible. “For without Me you can do nothing,” Jesus said (John 15:5). Wonderfully, “with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26). The strength we lack, He gives us. The obedience we long for, He makes possible. When our will is linked to His, the impossible becomes possible.

Be encouraged that Jesus is both our example and our helper. He will grow us to love God, and love others with generosity, respect, and grace. It is a beautiful picture of how to live: one hand reaching up to God and one reaching out to others.

Solomon said it best: “Fear God, and keep his commandments: for this is the whole duty of man” (Ecclesiastes 12:13, KJV). Yes, it is. And with God’s Spirit within us, it is beautifully possible.
