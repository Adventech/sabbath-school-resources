**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Print **^[Bible reference cards]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and **^[nature object cards]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for matching game. Use the following prompts for the cards:

- They do not worry, because God feeds them (Matthew 6:26)—Birds
- It doesn’t fear drought or sun (Jeremiah 17:7, 8)—Tree
- Hardworking and wise (Proverbs 6:6)—Ant
- They do not worry about what to wear (Matthew 6:28)—Flowers
- They tell of God’s work (Psalm 19:1)—Skies
- God knows the exact number (Luke 12:7)—Hairs on our head
