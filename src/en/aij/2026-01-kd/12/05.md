After his hands were untied, Lot gave Uncle Abram a big hug. He felt so happy that Abram had saved him! Even though Lot had made some sad choices, Abram loved him very much. What did Abram do then? **Read Genesis 14:16.**

It was now time for Abram to go home to his tent in the hills. On his way home, a special king came out to greet him–Melchizedek, the king of Salem. Melchizedek was king and the temple priest who worshipped and followed God.

“Abram,” said Melchizedek, “may God Most High give you blessings. . . . We praise God Most High. He has helped you to defeat your enemies!” (Genesis 14:19, 20, ICB).

Abram’s heart filled with joy. He was so happy to hear these words. God had helped him win the battle. All the things Abram had won from the battle weren’t _really_ his. God had won the battle, so Abram wanted to give some back to God.

“Abram gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything he had brought back from the battle” (Genesis 14:20, ICB). So for every 10 pieces of gold, he gave one of those to the king and priest Melchizedek! Abram gave back to God what was already His.

Today we can be like Abram. If we get ten coins, we can give one back to God. It’s our way of showing God that we give back to Him what belongs to Him because He asks us to do this. We call this “tithe.” In this way we also show God that we are loyal to Him.
