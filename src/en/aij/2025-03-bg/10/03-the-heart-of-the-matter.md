Rahab was a woman of faith. She risked her life in taking the two spies into her home; she treated them with great kindness and quite literally saved their lives. She had heard about the God of the Israelites, and unlike everyone in her wicked city, she wanted to know more. She felt the beckoning of God in her life. Not only did He save her when the Israelites captured the city—He included her in the lineage of Jesus! (Matthew 1.) No one is beyond the reach of God’s family. His arms are open and waiting for all!

What a loving God we serve! He doesn’t have favorites. He sees us, loves us, and reaches out for us. Better still, He uses us to reach out to others on His behalf.

We are God’s ambassadors to the Rahabs in our community. All around us are people God wants to reach; minds that are searching for truth and meaning; hearts that are awakening to His love. “How then shall they call on Him in whom they have not believed? And how shall they believe in Him of whom they have not heard? And how shall they hear without a preacher? . . . As it is written: ‘How beautiful are the feet of those who preach the gospel of peace, who bring glad tidings of good things!’ ” (Romans 10:14, 15).

God invites us to be part of the weaving of His great tapestry, His salvation plan. As you go about your week, look for the people God sends you, those He wants you to smile at and ask about their day, those who are lonely or lost, and those who need to know of His saving plan for them. Offer them a taste of God’s inclusive, overflowing love, just like the love Rahab was shown.
