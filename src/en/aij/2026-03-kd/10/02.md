Jesus looked down from the hill at the big crowd of people. They were all there to see Him.

The crowd looked like a flock of little lost sheep. They didn’t have fluffy wool or clip-clopping hooves. But just like sheep, they were looking for a shepherd. The people wanted Jesus to be their shepherd. They wanted Him to guide them and look after them.

Jesus wanted to be their loving shepherd too. So what did Jesus do? **Read Luke 9:11.**

The people leaned in and listened to Jesus’ lovely words. His words were so wise and true. Everything Jesus said felt like bright, warm sunshine. The Bible says, “The entrance of Your words gives light; it gives understanding to the simple” (Psalm 119:130).

Old grandfathers and little granddaughters sat together and learned from Jesus. His stories about God’s love were just what they needed! No one wanted to miss a word.

People also brought their sick friends and family to Jesus. With kindness Jesus lay His hand on them and spoke words with healing power. Instantly they were healed. Shouts of joy echoed across the hills and valleys.

Spending the day with Jesus was so wonderful!

- What would you have asked Jesus to do to help you?
