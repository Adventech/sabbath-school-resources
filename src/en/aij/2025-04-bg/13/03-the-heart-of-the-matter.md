The Wise Men were from a different land, far from Israel. Yet their hearts were open to God. With every step the Wise Men followed God’s leading. They were obedient to their calling. They diligently followed the star. They searched the Scriptures. They even stopped to ask for help. What an example! God’s own children didn’t know enough about Him to even take the short trip of 5.6 miles (9 kilometers) from Jerusalem to Bethlehem.

> <callout>Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 60</callout>
> “While they had the star before them as an outward sign, they had also the inward evidence of the Holy Spirit, which was impressing their hearts, and inspiring them with hope.”

The Wise Men looked up. They got their direction and their purpose from above. They didn’t allow themselves to become distracted or discouraged along the way. They listened to the Holy Spirit, and they just kept following.

How many times do we get distracted by the things around us and forget to look up? All we ever need is found in Jesus and the wonderful saving truths in His Word, the Bible. Look to Jesus for confidence and encouragement. Because when He is our source of light, we too can share His light with others.

> <callout>Hebrews 12:1, 2</callout>
> “Let us run with endurance the race that is set before us, looking unto Jesus, the author and finisher of our faith.”
