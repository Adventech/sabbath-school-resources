Deus allowed Lucifer a gossip e Conte lies sobre Ele until the whispering became então loud that isso turned into open rebellion. Deus allowed this porque Ele is amor. Ele didn’t Faça robots. Ele criou beings quem could choose a amor e serve Ele ou choose a go seu own way.

Todos de Céu took sides. Thousands de Anjos chose Lucifer’s side. Ele tricked a Terceiro de todos the Anjos. The other two Terceiros were loyal a Deus e stood waiting a see o que would happen.

Then a war começou em Céu. Nós Façan’t exactly know o que kind de war isso was, mas em the end Deus won the war porque Ele is wise, strong, e mighty. O que happened a Lucifer? **Leia Isaías 14:12 e Apocalipse 12:7-9.**

O que a sad way para things a end para Deus’s most glorious Anjo. Quando Lucifer realized ele had lost the war, ele openly declared seu rebellion against Deus’s law e Seu government. Lucifer had become Satan.

That was a sad, sad dia para todos de Céu. Deus had Façane everything possible a save Lucifer, mas the seed de sin had grown into pride então grande e ugly that ele just didn’t want a be rescued e forgiven.
