```=Mission Fast Facts #9

In Ghana, the most common foods are maize, rice grains, and root tubers such as cassava. Fufu is a popular dish (enjoyed in many West African countries) made from a mash of cooked cassava and green plantains. Children enjoy these with soups and stews that have lots of seasoning and flavor. Peanut soup is also popular. Most foods in Ghana are seasonal, so children only get to enjoy them when they grow.
```

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, and invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **child’s helmet**, a **small toy car**, and a **Bible promise**.

- **Who likes to ride a bike or scooter?**
- **What should you put on first?** (A helmet.) Ask a child to show you how to put on a helmet.
- **Why must you wear a helmet?** (To keep safe.)

Show the children the toy car. Explain that it can go really, really fast. Ask a child to demonstrate making it go fast.

- **What must you do as soon as you get in the car?** (Put on a seat belt.) Ask the children to show you. **Why?** (To keep safe.)
- **Who helps you to remember to put on a helmet or put on a seat belt?** (Family.) **Why do you think they want to keep you safe?** (They love me.)

Share that God loves the children very much—more than anyone else in the whole world! Hold up the Bible promise, sharing that this comes from God’s Word, the Bible. God says, “I love you people with a love that will last forever” (Jeremiah 31:3, ICB).

**Teaching Point:** Because God loves us, He wants the very best for us. He has given us 10 special rules—also called laws—to keep us safe and happy and to help us live well. These laws are called The Ten Commandments. We show we love Him back when we obey His words and His special rules.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Let’s find out what the first four are today! They will help us learn our **Big Idea**. Share the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. I will put Him first.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 61: Clownfish and Sea Anemone** and share the following facts:

- The most common clownfish are bright orange with white stripes, but some clownfish are yellow, red, or black.
- These bright fish make their home in a very surprising place. They live among sea anemones. Sea anemones look like plants swaying in the water, but they’re actually animals.
- Sea anemones have long tentacles that they use to sting and catch other sea creatures. But God has made the clownfish special, so that they don’t get stung by an anemone! Clownfish live safely in the sea anemones, protected from bigger fish.
- Clownfish help the sea anemones keep clean and help them attract food. Clownfish and sea anemones live better when they live together.

The anemones keep the clownfish safe and happy so long as they stay close. This is a special reminder of how God’s commandments keep us safe and help us live a happy life. We want to obey and stay close to Him.

Give each child a **Nature Card 61: Clownfish and Sea Anemone** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
