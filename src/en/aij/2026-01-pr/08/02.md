Floating safely on the water, Noah and his family continued to care for all the animals. Can you imagine sharing a giant home with all those beautiful, licky, slimy, spiky, fluffy, squawky, growly animals? Which animals would you have loved to care for each day? The family faithfully looked after them all. They threw grain to the birds, cabbages to the hippos, and bananas to the moneys. They swept out the lions’ area and shoveled out elephant poo. Some jobs were fun, and others weren’t, but they faithfully did them all.

Throughout their busy days they waited and wondered . . . How long would it be until they could leave the ark? Weeks and months went by. Sometimes they must have felt tired of waiting. But they trusted God, for they knew He had a lot of water to dry up! How long did the water cover the earth for? Read Genesis 7:24.

That many days equals five long months. It was a good thing they had work to do and animals to love while they waited.

Then one day Noah released a raven. It circled back and forth but couldn’t land because there were still tons of water everywhere. Then seven days later he let a white dove fly eagerly away. It flapped around, and then returned, a sign that it had nowhere to land. After seven more days of patient waiting, Noah let the dove go again. This time it returned with an olive leaf in its beak—there was dry land and growing plants somewhere out there! Then one week later it soared away and didn’t return—it had found a new home.

This was happy news for Noah’s family! The happy eight continued waiting, knowing that they too would soon leave the ark.
