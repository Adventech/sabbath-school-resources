Hannah knelt at the entrance of God’s house with a big sigh _(sigh)_. A little tear fell down her face. Then another. And another. Poor Hannah was sad _(sad face)_. She was sad in her quiet home. She felt sad in the happy street outside. She even felt sad in God’s house, the tabernacle. Hannah had no children. She wanted a child to cuddle _(hug)_, to tickle _(tickle)_, and to giggle with _(laugh)_.\
Hannah was God’s friend. She knew God loved her. She knew He heard her when she prayed _(praying hands)_. She asked God to send her a baby boy. She promised God that if she had a baby boy, she would bring him back to God’s house, so he could be God’s special helper.\
Priest Eli was God’s special helper _(point to Eli)_. He lived and worked in God’s house. He saw Hannah crying. Eli couldn’t hear her prayers. But God could! God always hears.

Hannah told Priest Eli all about her prayer. Oh, how she wanted a child to cuddle _(hug)_, to tickle _(tickle)_ and to giggle with _(laugh)_. Eli smiled. He knew God hears and answers prayers. “Go in peace,” he said kindly to Hannah. “May . . . God . . . give you what you asked” (1 Samuel 1:17, ICB).\
It wasn’t too long before God did just that. Hannah had a little baby! She was so happy _(clap hands)_. Thank you, God! She named her baby Samuel. His name means “God has heard.” Hannah cuddled Samuel close _(hug)_. She tickled his toes _(tickle toes)_. And as he giggled, she giggled too _(laugh)_.

Hannah loved her baby so much! She watched Samuel learn to crawl, then walk, then run! When Samuel was old enough, she brought him back to God’s house, just as she had promised. God had given him to her. Now she would give him to God to be His special helper. Samuel would grow up to serve God with Priest Eli.\
God hears your prayers too! Any time of the day, you can stop and pray to God. He loves to hear your voice.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for listening to our prayers!
