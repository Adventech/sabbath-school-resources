The shepherds were the lowest in society; the outcasts. But they were the ones watching for Jesus to come. They were alert and waiting while they looked after their sheep. “Through the silent hours they talked together of the promised Savior, and prayed for the coming of the King to David’s throne” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 47).

God honored these humble, faithful men with the heavenly announcement of His Son’s birth. What a privilege! They were the first to hear that Jesus had come to save us and to bring joy to all men. That joy was theirs to see, hear, touch, and know.

You have been chosen as a faithful, humble parent. While you watch over your little “flock,” be alert. God has a message for you to share too—the good news! Jesus saves. He brings peace and joy for all people. It’s a powerful message of hope for our hurting friends, neighbors, and family members.

Not sure where to start? Run to Jesus first. Bow down, like the shepherds in prayer, and thank Him. He will fill you with the joy you need for your heart, and joy to pass on to others. When we know Jesus, we won’t be able to contain the joy in our hearts! Remember, the shepherds ran back to their sheep, loudly praising God all the way.

“For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life” (John 3:16). This is joy, eternal—to know Jesus Christ, whom God has sent.
