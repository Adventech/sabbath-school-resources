Sadie stood on the porch of her house in a village in Belize. While she stood there, she noticed something very strange. A young, kind-looking man began walking down a narrow road toward her. Then he suddenly stopped, turned around, and walked away.

_How very unusual!_ Sadie thought.

A few minutes later the man did the same thing. Sadie was very puzzled. As she watched, he came toward her a third time. But this time he kept walking until finally he stood on her porch.

“We are having a meeting about God,” he said softly. Sadie could see he was a very quiet missionary man from another country. “Would you come along?” the man asked shyly.

“No,” Sadie crossly told the visitor. “I am not interested.”

The man walked away. But Sadie couldn’t get his kind, soft voice out of her mind. She wondered why she had spoken to him so angrily. She decided to go to the meeting that evening.

That night the young man welcomed her warmly and gave her a new Bible. Sadie learned a lot about Jesus at the meeting. She enjoyed it so much that she kept going back.

She had heard so many wonderful things that her mind couldn’t stop thinking about it all. Lying in bed one night, she just couldn’t sleep. Finally Sadie got up and prayed, “God, You know I want to serve You . . .”

After praying, she felt a peace in her heart and fell asleep. In the morning she felt as though she had a new heart. God had changed her, and she wanted to serve Jesus.

Since then Sadie has helped more than 50 people learn about Jesus and be baptized. And all because God gave a shy missionary man a “steadfast spirit” (Psalm 51:10) to boldly invite her to a meeting during which she learned about Jesus and His love.
