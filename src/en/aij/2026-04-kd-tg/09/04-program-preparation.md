**Opening Activities**

Source different **games and activities** relevant to your culture to challenge the children. These may include nature puzzles, nature memory cards, blocks for towers, or symmetry matching. You can make a simple Lego symmetry match by dividing a baseplate with tape and building 3-D shapes on one side to be matched on the other.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: Before class begins, push a **peg person** (man) into a **plastic bottle**. Have **scissors** ready to cut the top off during Sabbath School.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props: blue fabric** for the pool, **small heart sticker**.

Source **peg people** (Jesus, man who was healed) or a **felt set**, and **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Small **dish towels** or **rags** so each child can have a “mat” like the man in the story.
