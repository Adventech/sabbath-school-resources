Jesus walked through the streets of Jerusalem one Sabbath. He came to the Pool of Bethesda and looked around. He felt sad because He saw many sick people. Some sat with friends. Others sat alone. Some sat near the edge of the pool, and under the shady porches. Jesus felt a deep care for every one of the people.

Then Jesus’ eyes stopped on one particular man.

What does the Bible tell us about this man? **Read John 5:5.**

He had been unwell for a very long time. But this man had never given up hope that one day he would win the race to the rippling water. When the water moved, a shout would go up. Amongst the pushing and shoving crowd, the man would slowly lift his body off the ground. He would try hard to move his weak body toward the pool. But every single time someone faster would dive in first.

The man felt helpless and was almost ready to give up. He couldn’t do it on his own and he didn’t have a friend to carry him to the pool. He felt so alone.

Jesus watched him closely. He “knew that [the man] had been sick for a very long time” (John 5:6, ICB). Jesus also knew that the man was miserable because he believed God would never forgive him for the bad things he had done when he was younger. The man thought God had forgotten him.

But God never gives up on us—even when we make mistakes. He is always there to help us.

If only the man had realized that Jesus’ eyes of love were watching him at that very moment! Jesus saw his sadness and helplessness and started walking toward him.
