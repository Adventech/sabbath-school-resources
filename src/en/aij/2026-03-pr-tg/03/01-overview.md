**Lesson Summary**

This lesson follows the arrival of Jesus and focuses on those who were ready and waiting to give Him praise and worship. Angels visited a group of shepherds who were waiting for the promised Messiah. The heavens filled with praise. They were told where to find Jesus and ran to worship Him, then told others of His arrival. Simeon and Anna lived devoted to God, who had told them they would see the Savior. When they did, they praised and worshiped Him. Wise Men in the East had been studying the Scriptures and learned of the Savior. They followed God’s star until they found Him in Bethlehem, where they worshiped Him with gifts. Jesus is still worthy of our praise and worship.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus is worthy of praise and worship.
- **Feel** that they want to follow Jesus and be devoted to Him.
- **Respond** by praising God and wanting to worship Him.
