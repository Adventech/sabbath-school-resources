**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal the **Listening Picture Cards**, showing common things parents teach their children, and a **fly swatter**.

Invite children to take turns playing a short game. Lay out the cards on the floor and have the children gather around them. Give the first child a fly swatter and ask them to quickly swat the card that represents what they hear you say. For each child, call out their name and tell them to do a task represented on one of the cards. (For example: “Emma, please make your bed.” Emma swats the card showing a child making her bed. “Nolan, let me show you how to tie your shoe.” Nolan swats the picture of tying a shoe.)

Praise the children for listening well and following your instructions.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Why do our parents teach us how to do these things?** (Because they love us and know we need to learn these things, because knowing these will help us as we grow older.)
- **What kinds of things does Jesus want to teach us?** (How to stay close to Him, how to be kind to others, how to live the best way while we wait for Him to return, etc.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Jesus used stories to teach people important things He wanted them to know. Today we will get to hear some of the stories Jesus told.

Share **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He wants to teach me.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box**, revealing a **phone**.

- **What’s this?** (A phone.)
- **What can I use it for?** (Talking to people who aren’t nearby.)

Explain that in our story today, Jesus taught the people about how to stay connected to Him. One way is by praying, which is a little like talking on the phone. In our prayers, we can talk to Jesus, and we can also listen to Him.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** With the phone on speakerphone, call your church pastor or another adult the children will recognize. Ask the children to greet the adult, then ask the adult if they have a special message for your class. Invite the children to listen as the adult reminds the children to stay connected to Jesus, just like the house stayed attached to the rock.

- **How is talking to [adult’s name] like praying to God?** (He listens to us, He hears us even though we can’t see Him.)
- **How is praying different than talking on the phone?** (We don’t need a phone to talk to God—He hears us no matter where we are! We don’t always hear God’s voice speaking straight to us, but He communicates with us through the Bible.)

**Teaching Point:** When we have a best friend, we like to spend time with them. It’s the same with Jesus. He loves spending time with you because He loves you! You can spend time with Him by praying and reading His words in the Bible. This is what it means to build a house (your life) on the rock.

Together, repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He wants to teach me.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (7 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 37: Long-Eared Jerboa** and share the following facts:

- This tiny mouselike creature lives in remote parts of China and Mongolia.
- It catches insects to eat by leaping quickly into the air.
- For its body size, it has the largest ears of any animal in the world.
- It is very good at listening. With its large ears it can hear predators from very far away.
- Its large ears also help it to cool down in the hot desert, where it lives.

Let’s learn to **“listen like a jerboa!”**

Give each child **Nature Card 37: Long-Eared Jerboa** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
