**What you need:**

Week 3 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/3a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/2027-01-pr-craft-03.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color the pictures.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/3b.jpg)

2\. Cut out the pyramid templates.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/3c.jpg)

3\. Fold along the lines and glue the tabs to make a pyramid. The last triangle side will be tricky, so use plenty of glue and gently pinch the corners together on the outside of the pyramid to stick in place.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/3d.jpg)

4\. Use your pyramids to help you remember the order of the plagues in Egypt.
