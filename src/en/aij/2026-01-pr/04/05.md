Lucifer’s selfish thoughts took root and grew like a weed. When the angels loved his music, Lucifer’s pride grew. When he saw Jesus and God the Father talking together, jealousy grew and began to strangle the love in his heart.

God the Father and His Son, Jesus, loved Lucifer so much that they tried to stop his poisonous, jealous thoughts. Jesus talked with Lucifer many times and showed him how kind and fair God’s government was. God’s law was all about love and selflessness.

Lucifer knew Jesus was right and he was wrong. He had gossiped and told lies. He had been selfish and proud. He thought about how he’d acted and wondered if he should change. Jesus told him he needed to say he was sorry to all the angels and admit that he was wrong. Lucifer knew God would forgive him if he did this. How did Lucifer know this? Read 2 Chronicles 7:14.

Even though this verse had not yet been written, Lucifer and all the angels knew this was exactly what God’s big heart would do.

But the proud thoughts had deep roots in Lucifer’s heart. How could he say he was sorry? How could he admit in front of the whole universe that he had been wrong? No, he couldn’t! So he hardened his heart and chose to continue his rebellion.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How hard do you find it to say you are sorry and admit you have been wrong? Why do you think it is hard for us to admit something we have done wrong?_
- _Think of a time you did say you were sorry. How did the other person respond, and how did it make you feel?_

Sometimes it might be difficult to say we’re sorry when we have made a mistake or hurt someone, but God fills us with peace when we ask for forgiveness!
