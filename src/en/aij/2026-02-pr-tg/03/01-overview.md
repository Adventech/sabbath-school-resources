**Word of the Week**

**Forgiveness:** Giving up the right to punish someone when they have done wrong or hurt us; letting go of hurtful feelings such as anger or hatred toward a person who has hurt us or others. If we repent, God forgives us.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God’s forgiveness and ever-present love is shown through the story of Jacob. After deceiving his brother, Jacob fled for his life. He left home with his father’s blessing, but felt lonely, guilty, and sorry. God sent him a dream showing a ladder that reached from earth to heaven, with angels walking up and down it. Jesus stood at the top of the ladder and promised Jacob that He would always be with him and protect him. Jacob knew he was forgiven and loved.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God will always forgive us when we repent, no matter what.
- **Feel** assured that God is with us.
- **Respond** by forgiving others who wrong us.
