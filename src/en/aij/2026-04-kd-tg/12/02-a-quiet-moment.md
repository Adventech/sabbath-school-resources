**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Wherever Jesus went, He loved people, spoke truth, noticed those who were downcast, and spread joy. He transformed the most difficult situations and changed people’s lives. And in today’s story Jesus transformed a funeral procession in Nain so it turned into a joy-filled celebration!

These stories are a foretaste of what we will experience when He comes back to take us home to heaven. His kind eyes will look into ours. His nail-pierced hands will wrap around our own. And the feeling in our hearts will surely be pure joy, as He leads us to the home He has prepared for us.

“We shall ever dwell with and enjoy the light of His precious countenance. My heart leaps with joy at the cheering prospect. We are almost home. Heaven, sweet heaven! It is our eternal home. I am glad every moment that Jesus lives, and because He lives we shall live also. My soul says, Praise the Lord. . . .

“Our mortal bodies may die and be laid away in the grave. Yet the blessed hope lives on until the resurrection, when the voice of Jesus calls forth the sleeping dust. We shall then enjoy the fullness of the blessed, glorious hope” (Ellen G. White, _In Heavenly Places_, p. 352).

This Sabbath, may you and the children anticipate this wonderful event, and experience the joy that can be ours today because Jesus is near.

“For I will turn their mourning to joy, will comfort them, and make them rejoice rather than sorrow” (Jeremiah 31:13).
