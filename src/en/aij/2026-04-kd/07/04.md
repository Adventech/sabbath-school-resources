Jairus walked slowly beside Jesus. All the worry and hurry of the morning was gone. Jesus was going to help him! Jesus was coming to heal his precious daughter.

Everyone outside was happy to be close to Jesus too. The crowd squeezed tightly around Him and they walked slowly because of all the people.

Step. Stop. Step. Stop. It was so crowded. It was getting harder and harder for Jesus and Jairus to walk up the road to his house.

Suddenly, Jesus stopped. A woman who had been sick for 12 years had touched His robe and been healed. Jesus had felt her touch. Jesus was so happy. He praised her for her faith in Him.

Jairus must have wanted to leap for joy too. Jesus had the power to heal! And Jesus cared for everyone. He cared for important rulers like Jairus, and poor, sick women like the one He had just healed. Jesus would care for his child.

Jesus will care for you too!

While Jesus was still speaking words of love to the healed woman, someone tapped Jairus on the shoulder. It was a man from his own home. What did he say to Jairus? **Read Luke 8:49.**

The man’s words were like a bucket of icy cold water being poured over Jairus’ head. He felt shocked. _His little girl . . . dead?_

Before Jairus could do or say anything, Jesus spoke. “Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be made well.” (Luke 8:50).

Just believe. Yes, he would believe in Jesus!
