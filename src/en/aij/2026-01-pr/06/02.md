God taught Adam more about His amazing promise to save, sometimes called the plan of salvation. And then Adam taught his children. He taught them well. How sad it would be if they made a bad choice, as he had.

Teaching by example, Adam took Cain and Abel out to find a perfect little lamb—the best one in the flock. Adam shared how this lamb was a reminder of God’s Son, Jesus, who would one day come to the earth to die for their sins. How do we know the lamb is a reminder of Jesus? Read John 1:29.

Then the bleating lamb was gently placed on a stone altar. The family said they were sorry for their sins, and asked God to show His mercy. And then came the saddest part. With falling tears, Adam killed the little lamb. Sorrow filled the boys’ hearts as they looked away in horror. It was a sad reminder that they were sinners who needed a Savior to die for them.

Fire flashed down from heaven to burn up the little lamb offering. God was saying thank you for doing this hard thing to show they knew Jesus would one day die for them. So the family saw God’s great love and His mercy. They were truly grateful.

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/06/image3.png)

Abel grew up to be a strong young man who loved God very much. He happily chose to follow God’s instructions to sacrifice a lamb. How did God feel about Abel’s offering? Read Genesis 4:4.

God loved Abel, and Abel loved God . . . Abel loved God, and God loved Abel . . . It was like a beautiful circle of love.
