{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God is love, and heaven was filled with love. Lucifer (“son of the morning”), a beautiful, talented, leading angel, allowed pride to grow in his heart. It grew bigger and bigger until there were lies, rebellion, and war in heaven. As there can be only love in heaven, God sent Lucifer, now Satan (“the enemy”), and the fallen angels away.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God shows His love by letting us choose.
- **Feel** joy in choosing God’s way.
- **Respond** by being content to put God first when making choices.
