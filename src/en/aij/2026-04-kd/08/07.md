There are so many things to enjoy on Sabbath that can make us smile with delight. Nature is one of those happy gifts.

Let’s see if these nature facts can bring a smile to your face!

**^[1]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKHobgoblin", "size": "lg", "color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Sea otters sometimes hold hands with each other while they sleep floating on the water. They do this so they don’t drift apart.

**^[2]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKHobgoblin", "size": "lg", "color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Squirrels accidentally plant many new trees each year because they forget where they bury their acorns.

**^[3]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKHobgoblin", "size": "lg", "color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Japanese macaque monkeys make snowballs just for fun!

**^[4]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKHobgoblin", "size": "lg", "color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Butterflies taste with their feet.

**^[5]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKHobgoblin", "size": "lg", "color": "#F26A5C"}}})** Fitzroy River turtles can breathe through their bottoms.

Now that you are smiling, go for a walk and collect things God has made that bring you joy and delight (e.g., flowers, leaves, rocks to plop in water, sticks for building teepees).

Bring them back home and find other things around the house that bring delight on Sabbath (e.g., Bible, musical instrument). Lay them all out, in the shape of a big smile.

Sing “Jesus Loves Me” together. Maybe you can come up with some actions this time!
