Create

Create a collage using natural objects of what you think the Promised Land would have looked like. Use grass to make fields and bark and leaves to make trees. God wanted to give His people good things. He wants to give us good things too. We can trust Him.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#AD6956"}}}
Discover

Give your child something plain, such as a cracker, plain bread, or rice, to eat. Then give your child some cut-up fruit if you can. Talk about how yummy it would have been for the Israelites to eat fruit. What a treat! Share how it took two men to carry one big, big bunch of grapes!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#AD6956"}}}
Explore

Walk around your home and find five things that God has given your family (e.g., food, water, clothes). Our God gives us good things! Thank God for these things in your family worships.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#AD6956"}}}
Connect

Play a game of hide-and-seek together. Share how the 12 men had to be quiet and careful when they went into the Promised Land! End the game with a surprise feast of fruit.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#AD6956"}}}
Prayer

Share how we want to be like Caleb and Joshua. We want to trust God. Ask God to help you trust Him today.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#D75A2C", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Trust in the Lord with all your heart” (Proverbs 3:5).
