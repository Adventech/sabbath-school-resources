**Create it Together:**

1\. Color in the craft templates, then cut out the arm and torso of the woman from page 2.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/06/6b.png)

2\. Join the arm to the torso by pushing a craft split pin through the dots.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}

3\. Join the torso to the rest of the body on page 1, by pushing a craft split pin through the remaining dot.
