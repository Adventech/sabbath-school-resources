A Special Speaker

_“Shabbat Shalom!”_ echoed through the streets one bright and beautiful Sabbath morning. This was the way the Jews said, “Happy, peaceful Sabbath to you!”

Sabbath is always the best day of the week. Why is it so special? **Read Genesis 2:3.**

Jesus walked into the synagogue with a smile on His face. He loved the Sabbath. He knew it was “a delight, the holy day of the Lord” (Isaiah 58:13). He went to the synagogue to be with God and worship Him. This Sabbath, He also went to the synagogue to teach the people all about God’s love.

Jesus walked into the synagogue with a smile on His face. He loved the Sabbath. He knew it was “a delight, the holy day of the Lord” (Isaiah 58:13). He went to the synagogue to be with God and worship Him. This Sabbath, He also went to the synagogue to teach the people all about God’s love.

As Jesus stepped through the large doors, He looked around. He noticed the Jewish leaders standing with their arms crossed and their chins pointing up proudly. They looked very important and serious.

As Jesus watched them, it made Him sad.

He carefully took out a scroll (which was their Bible) and began to read and teach a beautiful message.

As Jesus spoke, the synagogue leaders watched Him closely. Hot anger and jealousy flickered like flames in their hearts.

Jesus taught the people the right way to understand God’s law, and clearly showed them God’s love. “They wanted to see Jesus do something wrong so that they could [blame] him” (Luke 6:7, ICB). They really wanted to get Jesus into big trouble, or even better, get rid of Him altogether.

But Jesus wasn’t worried. He had created the Sabbath! Our memory verse says, “Therefore the Son of Man is also Lord of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:28).
