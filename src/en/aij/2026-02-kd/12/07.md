Jume and his mother were new believers in the Adventist church. They had learned all about Jesus and the Sabbath. Jume worked hard growing and selling food from his family’s garden.

One day Jume decided to ask for extra work on a tea farm. But the farmer, Mr. McDonald, said that he must work on Saturday. Jume shook his head and said he couldn’t. Saturday was the Sabbath, God’s day of rest. As Jume quietly walked away, Mr. McDonald yelled out mean words and called him horrible names.

A few days later Jume stopped at the farm to sell Mrs. McDonald some vegetables on his way to market. She bought everything he had! As he happily left the farm he walked past Mr. McDonald’s office. Suddenly he stood still. His eyes opened wide in horror. A deadly snake slithered through the open door into the room!

Jume saw Mr. McDonald outside and ran over to him. As soon as Mr. McDonald saw Jume he began shouting at him again. “I remember you! You are the one who will not work on Saturday. I do not want you on my farm!”

Calmly and kindly Jume replied, “I came to tell you that a dangerous snake just crawled into your office. I was afraid you might get bitten.”

Together the two walked carefully into the office and began looking around. “Look,” said Jume, as he pointed to a basket of letters that were moving slightly on the desk. Jume took a stick, slowly lifted up the top letter, gave the snake a whack, and threw it outside.

Mr. McDonald stood quietly. He was shocked at what had just happened. “I might have been killed,” he said finally. “I was on my way back to this office to answer that very letter.” After a pause he added, “I am going to say more than thank you. You can come and work in my office tomorrow. . . . I believe I can trust you.”

Just like Jume, God wants you to “let your light so shine before men, that they may see your good works and glorify your Father in heaven” (Matthew 5:16).
