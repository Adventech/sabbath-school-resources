**Big Idea:** God is loving. He is never proud.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **^[happy and sad peg people]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** in a **sand tray** as you share about those who did or didn’t choose to follow God.
- Lay **vegetables and fruit** on **green fabric** to illustrate what Noah and his family planted.
- Use **blue fabric** for the river and **brown fabric** for the flat plains.
- Use **blocks** or **other building materials** to build the tower.
- Use a piece of **yellow fabric** as “lightning” to strike the tower down.
- Lay a **world map** on the floor/use a **globe**.
- Invite children to place **paper hearts** on the map.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 9 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use **felts**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- God told Noah’s family to have many children and to live around the world. Ham’s family turned away from God. They built a city near a river ^[(brown and blue fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and decided to build a tall tower ^[(wooden blocks on brown fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- The tower grew higher and higher ^[(children to build the tower)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God knew pride would hurt not help them. So God confused their language.
- Lightning hit the top of the tower, and it was smashed. ^[(“Strike” the tower with yellow fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- People soon found others who spoke the same words, and they moved all over the world ^[(world map or globe)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God’s plan for people to fill the whole world happened ^[(paper hearts on map)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Noah and his family began their new life in the freshly cleaned world. They planted new farms and grew new food ^[(place vegetables and fruit on green fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God told them: “Have many children. Grow in number and fill the earth” (Genesis 9:1, ICB). Yes, that’s just what they would do!

Time passed, and Noah’s family grew bigger and bigger. Sadly, some chose to turn away from God and disobey His law of love. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Ham’s family learned to be unkind and mean. Their children learned to be like that too. They were proud and selfish ^[(children place sad peg people in the sand tray)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). People who had chosen Satan’s ways didn’t like their cousins talking about God. They packed up and moved down the mountains to live on the flat land near a big river ^[(blue fabric river with sad peg people beside it)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The people who lived on the flat land ^[(children place sad peg people on brown fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) slowly became more and more proud. “We are the best!” they said. “We are the strongest and the smartest!” They thought a lot of themselves. They didn’t want to spread out around the whole earth, as God had told them to.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Instead, “They said to each other, ‘Let’s build for ourselves a city and a tower.’ ” ^[(Empty a pile of wooden blocks onto brown fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) ‘ “We will become famous. If we do this, we will not be scattered over all the earth’” (Genesis 11:4, ICB). They wanted to build a fancy city and a big, tall tower to show how important and strong they were. They wanted to be powerful like God. But God is loving and kind, unselfish and humble; they were not like God at all.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people were very busy, making bricks, baking bricks, and laying bricks. The tower grew higher and higher and higher ^[(children to build the tower)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They thought that if they worked very hard, they could even reach heaven.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God knew the people would never be happy with their proud, selfish leaders. Pride would hurt them and others! So God said, “Come, let us go down and confuse their language. Then they will not be able to understand each other” (Genesis 11:7, ICB).
^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly everyone in the city started speaking strange words they had never heard before. Workers shouted for more bricks, but different sounds came out! Some may have called out, “Ziegel!“; some may have called out, “Ladrillos!”; and others may have called out, “Batu bata!”

The messengers looked on, confused. People yelled louder, but it didn’t help. Faces turned red and angry. Whenever anyone opened their mouth to speak, strange new words came out! Everyone was confused and frustrated!

Work on the huge tower stopped. A bright streak of lightning flashed through the sky, hitting and breaking the top of the tower. ^[(“Strike” the tower down with yellow fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It fell to the ground with a big crash. The tower was never finished! The proud tower builders had learned the hard way that people can never save themselves—only God could do that.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) People soon found others who spoke the same words, and they moved all over the world ^[(lay out a world map or use a globe)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Some went to the hills, some to the seaside, and others sailed on boats to new lands. Wherever they went, God would be there. Some would stay proud and not choose God. But God would always work on their hearts and love them. ^[(Start to give paper hearts to children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God’s plan for people to fill the whole world happened. Nothing can stop God’s plans or His love for all people. ^[(Children can spread hearts across the map.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
