The blinking red dot on the map stopped at the edge of a great river. There were many people on both sides of the river. Lions, lambs, and many other animals were playing peacefully with the people there. Everything was calm, and there was so much peace.

“What is this place, Orel?” Luca asked, intrigued.

“We are on the riverbank of the Water of Life,” Orel quickly replied. “This river flows through the entire New Jerusalem, and the most amazing part is that it comes straight from the very throne of God!”

“Wow! What a beautiful place,” said Luca as he ran to hug the leg of a giraffe that was eating fruit from a huge tree by the river.

Many people came closer when they saw the giraffe’s enormous tongue kindly lick Luca’s head. The giraffe had greeted him friendly but left him covered in slobber.

“Here, use this to clean yourself,” said Orel, handing Luca a very soft cloth. “It’s made from a super-absorbent material that’s produced in…”

Someone interrupted him, “What the boy really needs is a good dip in the river. That giraffe greeted me the same way the first time I met her,” said a man, looking at Orel.

Luca noticed something special about this man. His face shone with a gentle light, his gaze radiated love, and his voice carried peace.

“Who are you?” Luca asked. “Somehow I feel like I know you.”

“You are standing before the famous Moses,” Orel replied.

“Famous? Me?” Moses said with a smile on his face. “I’ve been here for several years now, ever since God resurrected me and brought me to live with Him. That’s why I know this loving giraffe firsthand. Come, Luca, wash yourself in the waters of the river.”

Luca followed him to the riverbank and was amazed at the perfection and calmness of the water. It was like crystal and yet liquid at the same time. Moses knelt down, scooped up some water with both hands, and sprinkled it over Luca. “Wow! How refreshing!” He felt completely clean, but how?

“Luca, you’re standing before Moses. Let’s not waste the chance to learn from him,” Orel said as he approached with the floating glass, a text of the Bible was highlighted.

“There’s one particular scene from Moses’ life that will help us understand the Holy Spirit.” Luca read aloud Numbers 11:16–17, “The Lord said to Moses: ‘Bring me seventy of Israel’s elders who are known to you as leaders and officials among the people. Have them come to the tent of meeting, that they may stand there with you. I will come down and speak with you there, and I will take some of the power of the Spirit that is on you and put it on them. They will share the burden of the people with you so that you will not have to carry it alone.’”

“Oh!” said Moses. “I remember writing that so many years ago. Praise be to our good God who fulfilled everything He promised us! Luca, I’m amazed at your ability with ancient Hebrew… Astounding!”

“Moses, the Holy Spirit was upon you, and God shared Him with the others? Wow!” Luca hurried to ask, “Were you the first among the Hebrew people to receive Him? That must have been supernatural. How did you feel? What could you do? Were you afraid?”

“Dear child,” said Moses, “your questions bring so many memories and stories to mind. I was just an ordinary man who tried to do his best for God. I failed many times, but the Lord in His mercy forgave and restored me. Was I afraid? Of course! I left Egypt with more than 2 million people, a difficult people. I didn’t know what to do, and if it hadn’t been for the Spirit of God guiding me, I don’t know how my story would have ended.

“God unleashed His Spirit upon me to lead His people through the Red Sea and the desert. The problems were huge! We had no food, little water, and there were dangerous animals that wanted to harm us. The people spoke against God. For this reason, the Lord chose other men to help me lead the Hebrew people, and He gave His Spirit to all of them too, so they could do things the right way.

“The Spirit of God equipped us to bring the people to Canaan—the Promised Land. I didn’t enter that land, but I entered this heavenly land! God keeps His promises! The Holy Spirit gives us the ability to lead the people of God!”

“Wow, Moses! That’s extraordinary! I don’t think there is another man with the experiences you’ve had,” said Luca. “I want to hear all your stories.”

“Of course! Whenever you want to talk, I’ll be waiting by that tree whose roots reach both sides of the river.” Luca eyes opened wide when he realized that the tree Moses pointed to was the Tree of Life. In his mind, new questions were already forming.

Orel noticed this and said, “Luca, it’s time to go. We must reach a very distant place.” He pointed to the floating map. Now the map was no longer flat. There were small glowing spheres, and a red dot rested on one of them.

“Are we going to another planet?” Luca asked excitedly.

Orel smiled and, in Moses’ same tone, said: “Of course! What are we waiting for?” Both of them said goodbye to Moses and left the galaxy.

### Let’s Get to Work!

1. On a sheet of paper, draw and color one of the scenes from Moses’ life that impressed you the most. It could be about the plagues that fell on Egypt, how he opened the Red Sea, how manna fell from heaven, etc.
2. Send your drawing to your pastor or church elder. On the back, write a message of encouragement for their task of leading the church. Tell them that you promise to pray for the Spirit to be unleashed upon them so that they may be leaders who guide the church toward Jesus.

### Let’s Pray!

Here are some prayer topics. **Pray several times throughout this day.** Remember to pray personally and also involve your family and friends.

1. Pray that the Holy Spirit may be unleashed in your life and make you a powerful leader in the Adventist Church in the end times of the world.
2. Pray that Jesus may give you opportunities to use your leadership to prepare many people to be ready for the Second Coming of Christ.
