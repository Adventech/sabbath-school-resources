**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

When God gave Moses the detailed instructions for building the tabernacle, He wasn’t simply giving blueprints for a tent. He was giving His people a picture of salvation. Every curtain, every piece of furniture, and every ceremony whispered the same truth: Jesus would one day die to save all sinners who put their trust in Him. There truly is no god “who is so great a God as our God” (Psalm 77:13).

God’s plan of salvation is detailed, full of grace, and shows us the extent to which Jesus will go to save us from sin.

The Israelites may not have understood the full meaning of the symbols, but God was gently training their hearts to recognize His plan. Just as the lamb was sacrificed each morning and evening, God was pointing to the day His own Son would be offered for us. The Israelites (and we today) were to have “constant dependence on the atoning blood of Christ” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 352).

As you take a walk through the sanctuary and unfold the plan of salvation with the children, our prayer is that each of you will experience anew the wonder of the precious gift that Jesus is to this sin-saturated world, and to you personally. May your hearts be touched by the Savior’s love and His desire to re-create in each of us a likeness to Himself.
