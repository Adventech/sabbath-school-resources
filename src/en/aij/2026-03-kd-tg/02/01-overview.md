**Word of the Week**

**Humble:** Humble means thinking less about yourself and more about God and others. People who are humble don’t show off. Instead, they point others to God. They say and do things that help others feel loved and valued.

**Lesson Summary**

Not long before Baby Jesus was to be born, Mary and Joseph traveled a long way to Bethlehem to be counted by the ruler of their country. When they arrived in Bethlehem, it was crowded, and there was no place left for them to stay. But someone showed them a quiet stable where they could sleep in peace. Baby Jesus was born, and they wrapped Him in strips of cloth and placed Him in an animal feeding trough to sleep.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus is a precious gift from God.
- **Feel** happy that Jesus loved us so much that He chose to be humble.
- **Respond** by sharing the gift of Jesus’ humble love with others.
