**Big Idea:**\
Jesus can do anything. He wants to be my close Friend.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“The Lord is near to those who have a broken heart”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Psalm 34:18).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Kind”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - When we are kind, we are helpful, friendly, and good to others. A kind person will say and do good things to help someone and make them happy. Jesus is always kind and loving. We can be kind like Him.

A Spark of Hope

His skin was blotchy and covered with sores. It hurt for him to walk. It was hard for him to use his hands. The man sighed as he stretched. He had leprosy.

This terrible sickness made the man’s body hurt. But even worse, it made him sad and lonely.

People with leprosy were told to leave their home and their families. They were to go away—and stay away!

Wherever the man went he had to call out, “Unclean! Unclean!” (Leviticus 13:45). The people would rush away. They wouldn’t talk to him. They wouldn’t touch him. They wouldn’t even come close.

He sat under a little shelter, far outside the city gates. He looked sadly back toward his home. He missed big, warm family hugs. He missed laughing with his friends. Oh how he missed everyone!

But one day, things changed. The man with leprosy heard the wonderful stories about Jesus. His eyes opened wide. His heart began to thump. Jesus didn’t tell people to go away. Jesus was kind. And Jesus loved making people better. The Bible says, “Many people followed him, and he healed all who were sick” (Matthew 12:15, ICB).

Jesus was kind to everyone. He wants us to be kind to everyone too! **Read Ephesians 4:32.**

The man with leprosy jumped to his feet. He suddenly felt hopeful. _Maybe, just maybe, this Jesus could even heal me_, he thought. His hope grew, like the flame of a little candle, burning brightly in the dark.

He had to find Jesus. He had to ask Jesus to heal him!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever felt left out? Have you noticed others being left out?
- What kind things could you do to include others?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Think of someone you know who seems lonely. Make a plan to visit them, call, or send a kind message to them today.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus that we never need to feel lonely because He is our close Friend.
