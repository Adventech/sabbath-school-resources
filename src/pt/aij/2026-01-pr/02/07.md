Eles loved ele back. Two dias depois ele died em 2012, seu family was surprised a see two herds de elephants arrive em seu house. The elephants had walked para 12 hours a quietly grieve com Lawrence’s family, even though eles hadn’t been a seu home para a year e a half, e no one could have told eles ele had died.

Deus’s amor made seu hearts start grieving quando Lawrence’s heart stopped beating. Seu family e friends had loved ele very deeply too, então isso was comforting e especial para eles a todos be able a grieve together com the elephants Lawrence had loved então greatly.

Nós can thank Deus para giving animals the ability a compartilhe e receive Seu amor em especial ways.
