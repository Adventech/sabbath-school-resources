**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 6 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- **Peg people**: Woman and Jesus. Alternatively, you may wish to use a **felt set**.
- Props: **Empty money bag, cloak** or **piece of fabric** as Jesus’ coat.
- Ask children to join you in some actions.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.
```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The sick woman looked up and down the dusty road in surprise. It was full of people! Everyone was excited. Everyone was in a hurry. And everyone was happy to tell her the good news—Jesus had come to town!

The woman’s heart pounded. She had heard about Jesus, the great miracle worker. Maybe He could help her.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The woman had been sick for 12 long years. She had been to one doctor after another, but no one could help her. She had spent all her money ^[(empty money bag)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) on ways to get better. But she was still in pain. She was also lonely. No one would visit her, or touch her, because she was sick. Could Jesus help her?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The sick woman followed all the people to the lake. It was a very big crowd. In the middle of all the people was Jesus, smiling kindly as He made sick people well. The woman’s faith grew. She must get to Jesus!

The poor sick woman tried and tried to push through the crowd to get to Jesus. But she just couldn’t. There were too many people!

At lunchtime Jesus left the lake and went to His friend Matthew’s house to eat. The woman watched sadly as Jesus moved farther and farther away. She decided to follow Jesus and wait for Him. She wouldn’t give up! She had faith that Jesus would help her.

After lunch Jesus was back on the street. Quickly a huge crowd surrounded Him once more. Everyone was pushing and shoving and trying to squeeze in close to Jesus. The tired, sick woman tried her hardest to reach Jesus.

The woman thought, “If only I may touch His clothes, I shall be made well” (Mark 5:28).

Jesus knew all about the sick woman. He turned and walked toward her. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The woman dropped down low. She leaned forward with all her might and stretched out her hand. The very tip of her finger touched the edge of Jesus’ robe as it swished past her. ^[(Touch coat—allow children to touch it too.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Instantly she felt something like electricity zoom through her body from her head to her toes. She was healed!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly Jesus stopped. The people came to a stop too. “Who touched Me?” Jesus asked (Luke 8:45).

The people in the crowd laughed. Was Jesus joking? Everyone was touching Him! Peter said, “Master, the people are all around you and are pushing against you” (Luke 8:45, ICB).

Looking toward the woman, Jesus said, “Someone did touch me! I felt power go out from me” (Luke 8:46, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Slowly the woman squeezed through the crowd. “She came and bowed at Jesus’ feet. Shaking with fear, she told him the whole story” (Mark 5:33, ICB). She explained that she had been sick for 12 years and that no doctors had been able to help her. She told Jesus she had touched His robe and had instantly been healed!

Jesus smiled at her with kind, gentle eyes. He wasn’t upset with her! No, Jesus wanted to give her even more than a well body. He wanted her to feel happy and loved on the inside.

Jesus said, “Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well. Go in peace” (Luke 8:48).

She was loved and special. Jesus was so happy that she had reached out to Him in faith, being sure that He could help her.
