**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever felt desperate for Jesus to help you with something? Perhaps you’ve been sick or without work, or maybe you’ve needed Him to help you restore a broken relationship. You might have prayed and waited, thinking about the different ways God might resolve your challenging situation. Maybe you’ve thought about the possibilities, eager to see what God will do. Perhaps you’re in this very situation right now.

The story of Bartimaeus shows us that Jesus wants to give us joy.

When Bartimaeus called out, “Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Mark 10:48), it showed he believed that Jesus was the promised Messiah, the one with power and authority. He didn’t let others silence his desperate plea, but cried out in faith. When Jesus asked him what he wanted, Bartimaeus boldly declared his need, threw off his cloak, jumped up, and went to Jesus. He didn’t sit passively, but showed faith that Jesus could change his circumstances.

We can be like Bartimaeus too—believing, crying out to Jesus for His help, declaring our need, and acting in faith as we follow Him. As we do this we’ll also experience joy.

“Do not sorrow, for the joy of the Lord is your strength” (Nehemiah 8:10).

May excitement well up inside you all this Sabbath as you explore this week’s story and be reminded of the great things God wants to do in each of our lives.
