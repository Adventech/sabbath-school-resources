title: Introduction
---

God Keeps His Promises is a devotional resource created to help children discover the wonderful truths found in God’s Word and learn that God is always faithful.

Through ten Bible lessons based on prayer, prophecy, God’s promises, Jesus’ love, and the hope of His soon return, children will discover that God has a plan and that His promises are always true.

Each day’s message is divided into three sections:

1. Lesson, where children learn the biblical message in a simple and meaningful way;
2. Activity, designed for children who cannot read yet, using creative activities to help them explore Bible truths;
3. My Devotional Adventure, created for children who can read, helping them reflect on the lesson and apply it to their lives.

As children journey through these pages, may they learn to trust God, love His Word, follow Jesus, and remember every day that God keeps His promises!
