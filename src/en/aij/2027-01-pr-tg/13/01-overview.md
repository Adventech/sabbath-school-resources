**Word of the Week**

**Salvation:** The way sinful people are saved and restored to a friendship with God. Because Jesus was innocent and died in the place of every guilty, sinful person, we can be God’s friend now and live forever with Him in heaven.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

The Israelites, so recently released from Egypt, had limited knowledge of God’s promised Savior and the plan of salvation. So God embedded His plan to save and the work Jesus would do for sinners in symbolism in the sanctuary, its furniture, and its services.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God always had a plan to save them and all who believe in Him.
- **Feel** gratitude for God’s gift of salvation.
- **Respond** by accepting Jesus into their hearts anew.
