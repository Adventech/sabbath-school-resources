In this exciting new quarter, you’ll hear more wonderful Bible stories about Moses and how God did amazing things to help His people.

As you listen to these stories, imagine what it might have been like to be there when they crossed the Red Sea or when they collected yummy manna every morning. Imagine how hot and tired you would feel walking in the desert for such a long time, and how kind and loving God is to lead His people all the way to a new home.

One of the most amazing parts of our stories this quarter is that God wanted to be very close to His people. He chose to live in the middle of their camp. I know He wants to be close to you every day too. Do you want to ask Him to lead you today and always? His way is best, and He always wants what’s best for you.

Let’s start reading and learning more about our amazing God! I’m praying for you as you learn more about Him.
