**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He is loving and perfect.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use the following storytelling props: **water pot** and **bowl* for helping Mary; **toy carpentry tools** (or safe real tools) and **wooden blocks**, for helping Joseph; and **scrolls of paper**, for learning in the temple.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 5 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Little Boy Jesus jumped out of bed! He prayed to God, His heavenly Father and Helper ^[(praying hands)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Jesus was a helper too. He helped His mother with chores ^[(water pot, bowl)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and His earthly father too ^[(toy tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- He learned from nature: skipping lambs, busy ants.
- He grew taller. He grew wiser too. People liked His kindness, honesty, and goodness.
- Jesus grew . . . ^[(invite children to crouch, then grow)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was 12! He could go to the Passover to worship God, remembering His care.
- Jesus was so excited to see the temple. He watched the priests make the sin offerings. He would one day take away the sins of the world.
- Jesus wanted to keep learning. He found some temple teachers and asked them big questions ^[(scrolls)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- His parents started traveling home and didn’t realize that Jesus wasn’t with them ^[(search)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- They hurried back to Jerusalem and found Him three days later. Where was He? In the temple, learning ^[(scrolls)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Jesus returned to Nazareth with Mary and Joseph. He continued to grow “in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).

**TELL THE STORY**

Little Boy Jesus woke up, stretched, and yawned. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He began to hum a song of praise as He climbed out of bed. He always had God’s joy in His heart.

Happily, He straightened His blanket. Then bowing His head, Jesus talked to God, His heavenly Father and helper ^[(praying hands)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus loved being a helper too. Perhaps He ran to help His mother with breakfast and the chores. Perhaps He helped fetch the water ^[(carry water pot)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and set the table ^[(carry bowl)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Perhaps Jesus then happily followed His earthly father, to help him with his work as a carpenter.

_Bang, bang, bang_ ^[(toy tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus busily nailed the wood together. He always measured carefully and tried His very best. When it was time to pack up, Jesus carefully put away His tools ^[(pack up tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

As Jesus walked down the road outside, perhaps He noticed the little lambs jumping playfully in the field. Perhaps He watched the tiny ants hurrying across the dirt path. Nature taught Him so much about God’s love and power.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At home, Mary told Jesus stories from the Scriptures ^[(hold up Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus loved to learn about God.

Every day in Nazareth, as Jesus worked, ate, and played, He grew. Yes, young Jesus grew taller and taller. But He was also growing in another way (open Bible). The Bible says, “Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).

He was growing wiser! He was kind, honest, and obedient. People liked His cheerful, loving ways.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus grew ^[(invite children to crouch down and grow taller)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) . . . and grew . . . and grew. He was now 12 years old! He was old enough to go on a trip with His family to the Passover feast in Jerusalem. It was a special time when God’s people came from all over the land to worship together. They sang, prayed, feasted, and went to the temple.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus was so excited to see the beautiful temple! He watched the priest give the sin offering. He realized it was all about Him. He would one day take away everyone’s sins.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Young Jesus wanted to learn more. He found the teachers and Pharisees and sat quietly at their feet. He listened to their words ^[(show scrolls)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). And He asked them difficult questions.

The teachers and Pharisees were puzzled. They rubbed their chins. Who was this boy? He was so wise!

When the Passover ended, the people all started their long walks home. Mary and Joseph joined a big group of friends and family. They walked all day in the noisy, happy crowd. Suddenly they realized Someone special was missing. They looked left ^[(look left)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They looked right ^[(look right)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Oh, no! Where was Jesus?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary and Joseph looked everywhere, but they couldn’t find Him! Their hearts pounded. Where was Jesus? They raced back down the road to Jerusalem and looked . . . and looked . . . and looked for Him. After three days, they finally found Him.

Where was Jesus? He was sitting in the temple, still talking to the teachers ^[(scrolls)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary ran to Jesus. “Son,” she cried, “why did you do this to us? Your father and I were very worried about you. We have been looking for you” (Luke 2:48, ICB). Jesus wondered why they were looking for Him since He had been with His heavenly Father the whole time. He had been growing in wisdom.

Mary, Joseph, and Jesus returned home to Nazareth. Mary was glad that Jesus was learning about His heavenly Father, and she was glad that God was looking after Jesus all the time too.
