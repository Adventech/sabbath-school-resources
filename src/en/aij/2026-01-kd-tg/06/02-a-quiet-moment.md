**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It’s good to take a quiet moment to reflect on the responsibility you have of connecting your little ones with the Father. Showing them who He is is one of the greatest callings in our lives.

This week’s lesson holds a lot of big ideas. It deals with weighty matters that affect us all: choice, consequences, obedience (to God and parents), the sobering lamb symbolism, Jesus’ promised sacrifice, the damage anger can cause, self-regulation, and dealing with negative emotions. There is indeed much to consider. These concepts will be dealt with in as sensitive a manner as possible. We pray that you undertake this lesson with sensitivity, gentleness, and love.

“Let the instruction you give your children be simple, and be sure that it is clearly understood. The lessons that you learn from the word you are to present to their young minds so plainly that they cannot fail to understand. By simple lessons drawn from the word of God and their own experience, you may teach them how to conform their lives to the highest standard. Even in childhood . . . they may learn to live thoughtful, earnest lives that will yield a rich harvest of good” (Ellen G. White, _Counsels to Parents, Teachers, and Students_, p. 109).

May He help you with this task.

“The fear of the Lord is the beginning of wisdom” (Psalm 111:10).
