**Focus:** Jesus gives us His abundant peace, no matter the circumstances.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Tajikistan** on your **globe** or **world map**.

My name is Shukhrat [SHOO-khrawt], and I live in Tajikistan [tah-jih-kih-STAN].

Tajikistan is a beautiful country, with majestic mountains, lush green valleys, and crystal-clear mountain lakes and streams. But a terrible war broke out in our country, and many people were killed.

From the time that I was a small boy, I liked to read. One day my father, a soldier, brought me a small pile of books that someone had given him. I was only 11 years old, but I still remember these books. Three were Christian books: _The Bible in Pictures_, _The Gospel of Jesus Christ_, and _The Life of Jesus Christ_.

Those books changed my life. Up until that time, I knew little about God. But as I read those books, I started to love Jesus. When I found out how Jesus gave His life on the cross for me, I loved with Him with all my heart! I remember the night I knelt and prayed in my room. I gave my heart and my life to Jesus and accepted Him as my Savior! I was 12 years old.

When my father and other relatives found out that I had become a Christian, they were very angry. All I could do was bow my head and peacefully be silent.

Now another kind of war had broken out in my family. My relatives took away my Bible, but I got another one from a Christian church. They took that one too. I read another one secretly, but somehow they found out. My father beat me. My uncle even threatened to kill me. I was scared, and I didn’t know what to do. But I was comforted by the words of Jesus that I had learned from the Bible.

I kept reading my Bible. When I got older, I was baptized into the Seventh-day Adventist Church.

Even though it is dangerous to be a Christian, even though I could be beaten or threatened, even if I am killed, I will not change my mind. I love Jesus, and I will obey Him always. Jesus gives me peace.

- **Let’s thank Jesus for His peace, even when there is war and conflict in our countries and families. How can we hold on to His peace, as Shukhrat does?**
