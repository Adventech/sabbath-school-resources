Rain, Rain, and More Rain

Safe inside the ark, Noah and his little family got used to their floating home tossing around and the steady rhythm of the pounding rain. It had rained for so long . . . How long did it rain? Read Genesis 7:12.

Inside their floating home they were dry and protected. God had kept them safe by designing the ark to float perfectly in raging waters. It had a roof that the water ran off, thick walls, and a tightly closed door. The ark had no big windows, so they were kept from seeing the loss of their neighbors and their old home. God lovingly protected them and saved them from the flood. In the same way He will someday save us from this evil world when we choose Jesus and the safety He offers us.

Noah and his family may have counted the days and nights eagerly. 1, 2, . . . 38, 39, 40! After 40 days God stopped the rain! In its place He sent howling winds to help dry up the water.

Then God guided the tossing ark into peaceful waters inside a group of mountains. Noah and his family were grateful that their home had stopped tossing and rolling; they surely wouldn’t miss that!
