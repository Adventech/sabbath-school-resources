**Focus:** Ground squirrels are loyal to their families. They keep them safe by making loud noises to alert them of danger.

Show the children a whistle and invite a child to blow it to make a sound.

- **Can you whistle without using this whistle?**

Show the photo of the ground squirrel.

These little whistling creatures are very loyal friends.

These little ground squirrels live in the mountains among the sage bushes in North America. They eat mostly seeds, flowers, nuts, mushrooms, and green plants.

Bobcats, weasels, coyotes, eagles, and badgers live there too. Sadly, they like to eat ground squirrels. Ground squirrels need to look out for each other to stay safe and not become dinner for a bigger animal!

If they spot trouble, you would think that the very best thing for a ground squirrel to do would be to stay v-e-r-y still and v-e-r-y quiet.

Ground squirrels are loyal to their friends and family—they want to protect them, not just themselves. When the ground squirrel sees danger, it opens its mouth wide, pushes its body forward, and squeals a loud alarm call. There are even two types of this special call. If the enemy is walking on the ground, they make five trills; and if it’s flying in the air, they make one long whistle ^[(use the whistle to illustrate)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Wow! What a clever squirrel.

- **What do you think all the other squirrels do when they hear these sounds?**

They quickly run and hide. But the ground squirrel who sounded the alarm call doesn’t run and hide. Instead, he stands completely still, keeping his eyes on the enemy. He puts himself in danger to rescue his friends and family. What a loyal little ground squirrel!

- **How does this remind you of Abram in our story today?** (Abram also put himself in danger to save his family.)

**Teaching Point:** Being loyal to our friends and family means sticking close and putting them first. When we do this, we show that “a friend loves at all times” (Proverbs 17:17).

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. He can help me to be a loyal friend.**
