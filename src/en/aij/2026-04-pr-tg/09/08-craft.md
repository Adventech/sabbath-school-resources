**What you need:**

Week 9 craft template on colored paper, other colored pieces of paper one inch thick (2.5 centimeters), craft knife (adults only), scissors, tape.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/craft-template.png)

```

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Use a craft knife to cut along the vertical lines of the craft template.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9b.png)

- To save time, cut out long strips of different colored paper, roughly one inch (2.5 centimeters) wide.

**Instructions:**

1\. Cut around the outside of the craft template.

2\. Weave a strip of colored paper over and under the slits in the page.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9c.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9d.png)

3\. Take a different colored strip of paper. This time, weave it first under then over the slits in the page, so it is opposite to the strip above.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9e.png)

4\. Continue weaving strips of paper in this manner to create a checkerboard pattern.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9f.png)

5\. Tape the ends of the paper strips to the page (on both sides) then trim the edges to complete your woven mat.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/09/9g.png)
