Opening Activities

**Early Arrivals:** Source paper roll or poster-size pieces of paper, pencils.

**Focus Activities:** Source a collection of natural objects, used to create a Nature Table. Suggestions: feathers, rocks, shells, bark, wood, flowers, leaves, and anything else with interesting colors, shapes, or textures. Add small containers of sand, dried beans, bugs, etc. If possible, source a set of magnifying glasses and/or a microscope.

Source **^[Nature Closeup Pictures.]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**

Update **Offering Chart.**

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
