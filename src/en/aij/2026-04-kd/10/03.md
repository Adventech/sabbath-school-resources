The noisy crowd came closer and closer. Bartimaeus listened carefully to the excited chatter. Yes, Jesus was coming!

Bartimaeus took a deep breath. He knew that if he didn’t do something to get Jesus’ attention, his one chance at being healed might walk right past!

With a very loud voice, Bartimaeus began shouting above the noisy crowd. “Jesus, Son of David! Please help me!” (Luke 18:38, ICB).

Bartimaeus kept on shouting. Over and over again, he called for Jesus to help him.

The people who were leading the group down the road could hear Bartimaeus shouting. They thought his noisy yelling was very annoying. They angrily “warned him that he should be quiet” (Luke 18:39).

But no discouraging or cranky words would stop Bartimaeus from getting Jesus’ attention. What did he do? **Read Luke 18:39.**

He shouted, again and again, louder and louder.

The people sitting near him may have even needed to cover their ears because he was so loud! But Bartimaeus knew he needed Jesus.

We need Jesus too. Don’t let anything stop you from coming close to Him today.
