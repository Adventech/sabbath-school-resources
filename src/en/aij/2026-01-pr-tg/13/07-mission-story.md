**Focus:** God loves to do miracles for us, just as He did for Abraham and Sarah.

**Location:** Help a child locate **India** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Ruchi was very sad. She had been married for three years and didn’t have a baby. She and her husband had visited doctors, prayed to gods of wood and stone, and done everything else they could think of. But nothing worked.

One day a woman named Shakina came to visit some of her family members who lived with Ruchi. Shakina heard that Ruchi wanted a baby, so she spoke to her. “I believe in Jesus, who is the true God,” Shakina said. “Let me pray for you.”

Ruchi had never heard about Jesus, but she was delighted. Shakina invited her to kneel to pray.

“Lord, let Ruchi have a baby,” Shakina prayed. Week after week Shakina returned to pray with Ruchi. “You should pray too,” Shakina said. “You should have faith in Jesus, just like Abraham did.”

“Abra-who?” Ruchi asked. She had never heard of Abraham.

Shakina told the story of how Abraham and Sarah had prayed for a baby. God answered their prayers by giving Sarah a baby boy when she was 90 years old. Ruchi said to herself, _Wow, if God could give Sarah a baby when she was so old, then He can give me a baby. I’m only 24!_ Ruchi began to pray every day. “Jesus, please give me a baby too,” she prayed.

One day Shakina told the story of how God had given Hannah a baby named Samuel. Ruchi’s faith grew. She began to read the Bible.

Ruchi kept praying and reading the Bible for four years. One day she felt something unusual in her tummy. She immediately went to the doctor, and he announced that she was pregnant! Ruchi joyfully thanked God and called Shakina to tell her the news.

Ruchi gave birth to a baby girl named Cash. A year later she had another baby. Today Ruchi happily takes her two children to church every Sabbath. “Jesus is more powerful than anything else in the world,” she says.
