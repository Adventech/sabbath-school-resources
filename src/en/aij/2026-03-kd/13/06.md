All night long, shouts of joy and happy praises had echoed down the streets. Jesus had healed so many sick people in Capernaum. But now, shhh . . . the town once again slept peacefully.

At Simon’s house someone stirred. It was Jesus. Climbing out of bed, He quietly put on His sandals. Then He slipped out the door and down the street. Where was Jesus going? **Read Mark 1:35.**

Jesus loved spending time with God. Talking and listening to His heavenly Father gave Him strength and help. Whenever He could, Jesus would leave the busy cities and find a quiet, peaceful place in the mountains to pray.

Soon the people in Capernaum began to wake up with a yawn and a stretch. When Simon Peter woke up, He noticed that Jesus’ bed was empty. _Where had Jesus gone?_ He and the disciples began to search for their Teacher.

Finally they found Him praying in the mountains. “Everyone is looking for you!” they said to Jesus (Mark 1:37).

It made Jesus so happy that the people in Capernaum wanted to be with Him again. But He said to the disciples, “We should go somewhere else, to other towns around here. Then I can preach there too” (Mark 1:38, ICB).

The Bible says, “God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good” (Acts 10:38).

Jesus needed to show His goodness to other people in other towns too! So off they went. Jesus continued to teach and heal all throughout the area.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think the disciples knew where to find Jesus so quickly?
- What caring things did Jesus do for the people in Capernaum?
- What caring things does Jesus do for you?
