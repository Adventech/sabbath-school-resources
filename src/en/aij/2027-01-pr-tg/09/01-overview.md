**Word of the Week**

**Reverence:** A deep respect, awe, and love for someone or something. Reverence is shown through attitudes, feelings, and behavior. In this lesson reverence is defined as love and respect for God. In this lesson we will be thinking about reverence as love and respect for God.

**Lesson Summary**

God called Moses up Mount Sinai and shared His deep love for the Israelites, His intention for them to be His treasured possession, and His plan to visit and speak to them. After three days of preparation the people were ready for God’s visit. God descended in fire, thunder, and trumpet blasts, capturing their attention. Speaking the first four commandments, He showed them how to worship Him alone, avoid idols, honor His name, and keep His Sabbath holy. The children of Israel were in awe and gained new respect and reverence for God and His commandments.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that because God loves us, He teaches us how to show love and respect for Him.
- **Feel** a desire to love and respect God.
- **Respond** by choosing to put God first in our life and following the guidance of His first four commandments.
