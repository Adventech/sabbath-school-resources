Lucifer knew he was a very beautiful angel, and he thanked God for His amazing mind. He knew he was creative, and he praised God for making him strong and swift. He knew he was very smart, and he thanked God for His wisdom. Lucifer knew that God loved him, and he loved God.

One day, for a reason that no one can imagine, Lucifer looked at himself, and he didn’t think about how creative God was. Lucifer felt very proud of the strength and swiftness God had given him, but he didn’t praise God for his good looks or his many skills.

We don’t know how long Lucifer admired himself or whether he looked in the reflection of a still pond or a bright pillar. We don’t know how many times he adored his thick hair or his sparkling jewels. But the Bible tells us what started to happen in Lucifer’s heart when he admired himself. What was it? Read Ezekiel 28:17 (first half).

Something was hatching in Lucifer’s heart—something selfish and proud. Something was turning sweet into sour, and happy into sad. It was the seed of sin that leads to bitterness.

God knew what Lucifer was thinking, and He was so very sad that His beautiful angel was beginning to love himself more than anyone else—even more than God.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What talents can you praise God for? For example, do you sing well, or run well, or have a kind heart?_
- _Why do you think being proud and selfish is dangerous?_

We are happiest when we love God and are grateful for the special things we can do. But if pride creeps into our hearts, things begin to change.

^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Look for things in which you can see your reflection. Which works the best? Think about how Lucifer loved his own reflection. What does the Bible tell us is more important than thinking about ourselves? Read Philippians 2:3.
