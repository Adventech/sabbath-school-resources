Joshua smiled at the two men. He had a very exciting job for them. They were to go into the Promised Land to find out what it was like. They would go on an adventure, just as Joshua had done many years earlier. They were to go _looking, looking, looking._ They were to look up high. They were to look down low. They were to look everywhere in the big city of Jericho. Hurry, hurry . . . time to go!\
The two men packed their bags and set off. The city of Jericho had big, tall walls all around it. It had big, strong gates that went _clang-clang_ when they shut tight. The two men went quietly inside the city. _Shh!_ They looked up high. They looked down low. But they needed a friend to tell them where to go. They met a kind woman named Rahab. She offered to help them!
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/10/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
Rahab took the two men to her house. She sat up on her roof and talked with them. She knew they were friends of God. God had helped His people walk right through the Red Sea. He had sent them yummy manna to eat. She wanted to know their loving God too. This made the men very happy _(smile)._ It made God happy too _(hug child)._ Rahab gave the men some food and water. She told them all about her city.\
Soon it was time for the men to go. The stars came out. The big gates had been shut with a clang-clang. How could they leave Jericho? Rahab knew! She lived inside the big, tall walls. She put a red rope out of her window. Down, down, down the walls climbed the men. They waved back up at her from the ground _(wave)._\
Kind Rahab had helped them. They promised to help her too when they came to live in the land. Then they hurried quickly back to Joshua. Run, run, run. They were so happy. God had a new friend in Jericho—kind Rahab. God would help them make this new land their home.

Just like Rahab, we can look for ways to help others. We can show people Jesus’ love by being kind with our words and actions. Kind words say, “I love you” to your grandma, or “Thank you, Daddy, for my dinner.” Kind hands help clean up messes and fold the washing. Kind hearts give a hug to someone who is hurting.\
_(Say together)_ Dear Jesus, help me to look after others.
