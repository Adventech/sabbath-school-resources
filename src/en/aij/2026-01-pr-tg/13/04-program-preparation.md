Opening Activities

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
For **Early Arrivals**, use **sheets** to make a tent; and source a **basket, bowls, cups, tea towels/cloth for clothes**; other items that relate to camp living.\
**Focus:** create a set of four to six waiting cards. Write something the children in your context would find it hard to wait for (e.g., a new toy, a visit to friends or family far away, a special church event), or source **pictures** of these things.\
Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.\
Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
