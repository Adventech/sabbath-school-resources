**Focus:** Zhuen memorizes the Bible and shares with his friends.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Taiwan** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Zhuen, who lives in Taiwan, happily started memorizing the Bible when he was in grade 3. Mother promised that she would tell a Bible story every time he memorized a new verse. Zhuen loves listening to stories, especially from the Bible. His whole family memorizes the same verses and recites them out loud together during family worship.

Some church leaders heard about what they were doing and invited them to recite Bible verses at an important mission meeting. Many people were amazed. They decided to also memorize Bible verses with their children for family evening worships!

One day Zhuen’s father got a new job in a new city, so Zhuen and his family moved. At first Zhuen felt very sad about saying goodbye to his friends. But one day at his new school his teacher asked, “What does it mean to share?” Zhuen remembered a favorite Bible story that Mother had told him. “I know a story about a little boy who shared his lunch of five loaves and two fish, and it fed more than 5,000 people,” he said.

No one in the class had ever heard the story, not even the teacher. They had never read the Bible. Zhuen was asked to share the story with the class. He was so happy to share his love for Jesus in his new school.

Then one of Zhuen’s classmates had to move after his father got a new job. Zhuen saw that his friend was sad. He understood how the boy felt. He had felt the same way when he had moved. “Can I pray for you?” he asked. “God can make you feel happy again.”

The boy agreed, and Zhuen shared the Lord’s Prayer that he had memorized from the Bible, “Our Father in heaven, hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come. Your will be done on earth as it is in heaven” (for full prayer, read Matthew 6:9-13). Zhuen loves to pray with his classmates.

Zhuen is faithful.

- **How did Zhuen show he was faithful?** (He learned Bible memory verses faithfully and shared Bible stories with others.)

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. I can be faithful to Him.**
