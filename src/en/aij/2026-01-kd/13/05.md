The three strangers sat smiling and eating in the cool shade, under the trees near Abraham’s tent.

Though Abraham didn’t realize it yet, these men were not just men. Two of them were angels, and one of them was Jesus Himself!

Then one of the men (who was Jesus) looked at Abraham and said, “I will certainly return to you about this time a year from now. At that time your wife, Sarah, will have a son” (Genesis 18:10, ICB).

Tired, wrinkly, 90-year-old Sarah was in her own tent, secretly listening. When she heard these words, she thought it was so funny that she laughed to herself. “My husband and I are too old to have a baby” (Genesis 18:12, ICB).

Sarah thought her laugh was a secret. But Jesus heard her. He knew her thoughts. What did Jesus say to Abraham? **Read Genesis 18:13, 14.**

Sarah was afraid. She told a lie to Jesus and said, “I didn’t laugh.” But before she could walk away and hide, Jesus said, “No. You did laugh” (Genesis 18:15, ICB).

Sarah learned a lot that day. She learned that Jesus knows everything. She learned to always tell the truth. And she learned that God’s promises are always true. We can also believe Jesus’ words in the Bible: “With men this is impossible, but with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why did Sarah laugh when God spoke to them about having a baby?
- Have you ever laughed at the wrong time? What happened?
