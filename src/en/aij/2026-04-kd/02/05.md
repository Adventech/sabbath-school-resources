Early the next morning, the nobleman packed his things and climbed on his horse. With a song in his heart, he headed down the road toward his home.

As the nobleman came closer to his home, he noticed something up ahead. He squinted his eyes. What was it? Soon, he could see that it was his very own servants, running as fast as lightning toward him. Puffing and panting, they had big news to share. The nobleman’s son was alive and well!

But the nobleman wasn’t surprised at all.

“What time did my son begin to get well?” (John 4:52, ICB), he asked the servants.

They paused to think, “It was about one o’clock yesterday when the fever left him” (John 4:52, ICB).

They explained how the boy had been lying in his bed, pale and weak. Suddenly he had opened his eyes. His hot, sweaty skin had instantly changed to a soft, healthy glow and his eyes had sparkled with life.

The nobleman’s smile grew bigger and bigger. The one o’clock miracle was the very hour that Jesus had said, “Go your way; your son lives.” (John 4:50).

The words of Jesus are all-powerful. He tells us in the Bible, “All power on heaven and on earth is given to me” (Matthew 28:18, ICB). The nobleman was so thankful. We can be too!
