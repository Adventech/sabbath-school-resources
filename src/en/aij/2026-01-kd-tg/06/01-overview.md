**Word of the Week**

**Obey:** To obey means to follow a command. To obey God is to always follow God’s way, not my way.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God taught Adam and Eve to bring offerings that would remind them and their family of His promise and His plan to save. It showed His great love for them. God was pleased when they showed their love for Him by following His instructions. Cain chose to disobey God and offer his own sacrifice. Cain’s angry emotions turned into angry actions, and Cain killed Abel. The results of going his selfish way, instead of God’s way of love, were devastating.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that obeying God means following His way, not my way.
- **Feel** and understand that obeying comes from love.
- **Respond** by choosing to obey with a happy heart.
