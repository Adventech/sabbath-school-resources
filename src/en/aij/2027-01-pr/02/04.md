The little lamb bleated and darted about on her wobbly legs. With a gentle smile Moses scooped her up and carried her back to her mother. Forty years had gone by in the desert of Midian, and Moses had become a good shepherd. He had also grown closer to God—the best shepherd of all.

Suddenly Moses stopped. Up ahead he saw fire! Fire meant danger, so Moses watched the burning bush very carefully. That’s when he noticed something strange. The bright-orange flames kept leaping in the air, but the bush stayed green and alive!

Puzzled, Moses walked slowly toward the burning bush. Then “God called to him from the midst of the bush and said, ‘Moses, Moses!’ ” Moses’ lips trembled as he answered, “Here I am” (Exodus 3:4).

How did God respond? **Read Exodus 3:5.** Moses quickly obeyed and covered his face, for he knew he was in the presence of God.

Then God reminded Moses that He was the God of his ancestors: “the God of Abraham, the God of Isaac, and the God of Jacob” (Exodus 3:6). Just as He had been with his family before him, God would be with Moses too.

As God kept speaking, Moses listened reverently. “Come now, therefore, and I will send you to Pharaoh that you may bring My people, the children of Israel, out of Egypt” (Exodus 3:10).

God had finished preparing Moses in Midian. God had performed a miracle and spoken out loud. And God had promised to be with Moses. If Moses would submit to God and follow Him, he could be a wonderful part of God’s rescue plan!
