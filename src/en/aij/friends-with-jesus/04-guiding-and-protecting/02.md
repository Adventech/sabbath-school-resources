King Herod stomped up and down the palace hall with an angry scowl. Where were the Wise Men?

He thought about the day the Wise Men had arrived in Jerusalem. What a surprise it had been. Herod had invited the men to his palace, and they had asked him, “Where is He who has been born King of the Jews?” (Matthew 2:2).

Even now Herod grew angry at the thought of this question. There was only one king in this land, and his name was King Herod! How dare anybody even think that there could be a king other than Herod. No! No baby king would ever take his throne!

Herod grinned wickedly as he remembered how he had tricked the Wise Men. Hiding his anger, he had told them, “Go and search carefully for the young Child, and when you have found Him, bring back word to me, that I may come and worship Him also” (Matthew 2:8).

King Herod didn’t really want to worship Jesus or show Him deep love, honor, and respect. It was a trap! He wanted to find this baby King and get rid of Him. (Oh, no!)

But now many days had passed, and the Wise Men had not returned. King Herod frowned. Where were they? Surely they had found the baby King by now.

What had happened to the Wise Men? **Read Matthew 2:12.**
