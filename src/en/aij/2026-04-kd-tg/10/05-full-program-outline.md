**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal bag or box with folded strips of paper inside. On each paper is an animal name. There will be two of each kind within the box.

Invite the children to come and choose a slip of paper. Open and read it for them, whispering the name of the animal in their ear. Tell the group that on the count of three, they are to make the sound their animal makes. At the same time, they need to listen to find someone else that is making the same animal sound. When they find their partner, they can sit down together.

- **How did you find your partner?** (I listened for the right sound.)
- **What made it hard to do?** (There were so many other sounds.)

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story, **“Noisy Bartimaeus,”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and the **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **megaphone** made of cardstock, and a **Bible**. Put the megaphone to your mouth to demonstrate how it’s used to say something very loudly. Ask the children to quietly put their hands up to answer the two questions below. Select children to then stand and say their answers to the rest of the class by using the megaphone. Praise good listening.

- **Why did Bartimaeus keep calling out to Jesus?** (He believed in Jesus. He wanted to see.)
- **Why did Jesus listen and heal him?** (Jesus cared about him and loved him.)

Remind the children that Bartimaeus was so happy and thankful that he then followed Jesus down the road. Invite the children to stand and cup their hands to make a megaphone. When you ask the next question, they can call out their answers—all at the same time—as they follow you around the room/area.

- **What might Bartimaeus have called out now as he followed Jesus?**

Invite the children to return to their seats and be very, very quiet. Whisper your last question and invite the children to whisper and listen to each other for answers.

- **Why do you pray to Jesus?**

**Teaching Point:** Jesus loves us and will always listen to us when we pray. He listens to our happy words and our sad words. He loves to hear from us and help us. Point to your Bible and remind the children that the **Bible** tells us that “whoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved” (Joel 2:32).

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Noisy Lyrebird.** Make a noisy Lyrebird to prompt the children to pray to Jesus about anything and everything, knowing He cares and listens.

Share that today we learned that we can call to Jesus anytime. He is always listening to our prayers. Invite the children to stand and say the **Big Idea** with you three times: as a whisper; then louder; then loudest: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**.** [Cup hands either side of mouth] **He hears me when I call out to Him.”**

Let’s pray to Him by ourselves and also with our families all through this new week.
