**Word of the Week**

**Promise:** When a person agrees to do or not do something, they are making a promise. When God makes a promise, He keeps it every time!

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God promised His people that He would send a Messiah to save them. Now the time was just right to send two special babies to the world. Elizabeth and Zacharias were surprised to have a baby son in their old age. They named him John. He would help people to be ready for Jesus. Mary and Joseph were visited by an angel and told that Mary would have a baby son, Jesus, who would be the Messiah.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus is God, their promised Savior.
- **Feel** happy that God keeps His promises in such a wonderful way.
- **Respond** by thanking God for the wonderful gift of Jesus!
