**Word of the Week**

**Proud:** A proud person thinks they are more special or important than others. Usually people who are very proud of themselves feel as if they don’t need God or anyone else to help them.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After the Flood, God told Noah and his family to spread out all over the earth, but as time went by, people who hadn’t chosen God did not. They were proud in their own ways and decided to build a tall tower to protect themselves from another potential flood. God stopped the building of the tower by confusing their language, and they all separated and spread out over the earth.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is never proud but always loving.
- **Feel** happy knowing that God made us all different, and He loves us all.
- **Respond** by choosing to make God’s way my way.
