Jesus is a Friend you can always trust. “Behold, God is my salvation, I will trust and not be afraid” (Isaiah 12:2).

He can help you become a friend that others can count on too. Try these two fun trust challenges.

**^[Buddy Walk]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#F7925F"}}})**

Find a friend or family member to be your buddy. Create a short obstacle course together. Have a grown-up make sure it’s in a safe space. One buddy must be blindfolded, while the other buddy guides them through the course. The guider will need to use words and actions to help their buddy stay safe, and not bump into things. The super tricky part? The blindfolded buddy will need to walk through it backwards!

Take turns being the blindfolded buddy, and the trusted leader.

- How did you feel when blindfolded?
- What helped you trust your buddy to lead you?
- What helped you be a good, trusted buddy when leading?

**^[Food Surprise]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#F7925F"}}})**

Play this game with your parent or trusted grown-up. Ask them to make tiny tasters of surprise foods. With your blindfold on, you get to sniff, touch, and taste to try to figure out what it is. You may even have clues!

- How did you feel when trying these surprise foods?
- Did you think the things would be good or bad? Why?
- What helped you to try them?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus that you can trust Him. Ask Him to help you be a good friend that others trust too.
