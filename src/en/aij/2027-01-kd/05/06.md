The Israelites stood safely on the other side of the Red Sea. Their love and thanks to God bubbled over. What a wonderful Savior! He had done so much to lead them to freedom. They were no longer slaves. They were forever free!

Together they began to sing to God. Why did they want to sing together? **Read Exodus 15:1.**

Moses’ big sister, Miriam, “took a tambourine in her hand. All the women followed her, playing tambourines” (Exodus 15:20, ICB).

Everyone lifted their voices to sing this special song of thanks to God. With clapping hands they sang, “The Lord is my strength and song, and He has become my salvation; He is my God, and I will praise Him” (Exodus 15:2).

God had done the impossible. He deserved all their trust and worship.

If you have a problem that seems very big, don’t panic. “Do not be afraid. Stand still, and see the salvation of the Lord” (Exodus 14:13). You can trust Him to do the impossible for you too! And when He does, don’t forget to thank Him!
