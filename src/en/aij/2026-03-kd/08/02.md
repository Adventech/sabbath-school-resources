The sun shone bright and warm. It was another beautiful day by the Jordan River. John the Baptist stood near the riverbank talking with two of his friends, Andrew and John.

As the three men talked together, John the Baptist suddenly stopped. His eyes grew wide in wonder as Jesus walked past. “Behold the Lamb of God!” he said excitedly. (John 1:36).

Andrew and John looked at Jesus, amazed. Here was the promised Savior! What did they do next? **Read John 1:37.**

Andrew and John were the first followers of Jesus. Silently they walked behind Jesus. They didn’t know what to say. They didn’t even know what to do—other than follow Him!

Jesus kept walking. His eyes sparkled with joy. He knew Andrew and John were right behind Him.

Then Jesus stopped and turned. With kind words Jesus asked them, “What do you seek?” (John 1:38).

These two men looked at each other and then at Jesus. They wanted to be with Him. They wanted to listen and learn from Him.

“Where are you staying?” they asked.

Jesus smiled. “Come and see.” So they did! (John 1:38, 39). And they did! Together the two men went back to the place where Jesus was staying and spent the whole day with Him.

Jesus wants to spend time with you too! He says, “Come.” You can come to Him by listening to God’s Word in the Bible, and talking to Him in prayer.
