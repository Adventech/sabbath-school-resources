Imagine for a moment that you’ve been chosen to parent the God of the universe. What would your response be? What would the days, weeks, and months look like after hearing the news?

Mary is such an inspiring young person of faith. She was an ordinary, humble girl doing ordinary and humble things. Then an angel appeared and her whole life trajectory was changed in an instant. And after she heard the news, her response was “Behold the maidservant of the Lord! Let it be to me according to your word” (Luke 1:38). Later she sang her praises to God: “My soul magnifies the Lord, and my spirit has rejoiced in God my Savior. For He has regarded the lowly state of His maidservant; for behold, henceforth all generations will call me blessed. For He who is mighty has done great things for me, and holy is His name” (Luke 1:46-49).

Mary responded with _worship_—not _worry_. Her heart overflowed with praise. She didn’t doubt her calling; she embraced it! She said yes to God, despite her fears and her failings. She trusted God’s purpose for her life.

Jesus offers the same invitation to us. He desires to dwell in us. He has given us our own unique purpose. We can respond with worry or worship.

God still calls people like Mary and Joseph today. In this busy season may you respond to Jesus’ invitation and say “yes” to His calling for your life—first, as a parent who guides your child to know and love Jesus, and to whatever else He may be asking you to do. And may your song, as was Mary’s, be “For He who is mighty has done great things for me, and holy is His name” (Luke 1:49).
