_Clink, clink, clink._ Bartimaeus rattled the few small coins in his little cup. Would someone listen and give him some money? He waited. He listened.

It was just an ordinary day for Bartimaeus. He sat begging for money in his usual spot by the side of the road that led into Jericho. But it sounded different today. . . .

He could hear something. Footsteps. Lots of footsteps! Chatter. Lots of chatter!

Bartimaeus listened closely. His ears could hear a really large crowd coming toward him. The noise of the crowd came closer and closer. This swarm of people was extra large!

Bartimaeus turned to some of his friends sitting next to him. Friends who could see. What did he do? **Read Luke 18:36.**

His friends said excitedly, “Jesus, the one from Nazareth, is coming . . .” (Luke 18:37, ICB).

Jesus was walking on the road toward him! Bartimaeus had heard about this Man. He had hoped that one day he could meet Him. Jesus had healed people. People who couldn’t walk, people who couldn’t hear, and people who couldn’t see!

Jesus can do anything. He knows what we need, and He loves to help us. The Bible says, “My God shall supply all your need” (Philippians 4:19).

Bartimaeus clapped his hands together. He was so excited his heart pounded in his chest like a drum. He made up his mind. He would do whatever he could to meet Jesus. He would ask Jesus to heal him!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What would a very big, very excited crowd sound like?
- Do you think Jesus will hear Bartimaeus in this big crowd? Why?
