**Opening Activities**

Source **smooth stones/rocks, chalk, bowls of water, old cloths** and/or **sponges**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a **small backpack** and some **heavy stones/rocks**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props:** a wrapped baby doll; coat or robe; fruit; a washable heart shape, made dirty with something that will easily clean up; a bowl of water; a cloth; a flashlight; a dove picture on a stick, this week’s craft of Jesus being baptized.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: small bottles of **bubbles** (one for each child). (Or use cups with diluted dish soap and make pipe cleaner/chenille stick/bubble wands with handles. Make a loop about 1 inch [2.5 centimeters] in diameter at one end of the wire and leave the rest of the wire free as a handle.)
