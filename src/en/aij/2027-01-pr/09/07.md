Road signs are very useful things! They tell us which way to go (or not go!), help drivers to make turns, avoid dangers, and drive in a safe, orderly way. Without them there might be accidents, crashes, and chaos! Road signs keep people safe as they travel to their destination.

God’s Ten Commandments are like road signs that give us instructions as we travel the road of life toward our destination—heaven. They show us the very best and safest way to live on our journey.

**^[10 Commandments Road-Sign Model]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#a47b9b", "size": "xl"}}})**

**You will need:**

- A large piece of paper to draw a road and the surrounding environment on
- A baking dish or tray, breadboard, or large book to rest your road drawing on
- Markers, pencils, scissors, glue
- Craft sticks, skewers, or toothpicks to attach your road signs to
- Reusable sticky putty such as Blu Tack, or play-dough to rest your road signs in, to make them stand up along the highway

**^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#a47b9b", "size": "xl"}}})**

- Draw a road and the surrounding environment on a large piece of paper. Rest it in your baking dish, or attach it to your chosen flat surface.
- Draw 10 different road signs, cut them out, and write the first four commandments on four of them. Glue the signs onto your craft sticks.
- Place a blob of play-dough or Blu-tac on the end of the four signs you wrote on and stand them up along your roadway. Save the remaining six for next week.
