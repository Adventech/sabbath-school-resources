**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever thought about how remarkable it is that God gives us the ability to choose? He could have programmed us so that we would always obey and always do the right thing. Sin would never have been an option, and God’s perfect creation would never have experienced pain, suffering, and death.

Yet this would have gone against God’s character of love. His desire for humanity was to have a loving, genuine relationship with us—the kind that is possible only in the presence of authentic choice. Humankind was given freedom to choose. It was a huge risk, but God chose it out of love.

Every day, our choices can lead us closer to God or further away. They also impact the lives of others. Fortunately, God is ready to help us.

When we ask God to help us make good choices, He helps us do things we couldn’t do on our own. Like Joseph, we can choose to be happy amid the most difficult circumstances. We can praise God through pain and trust that He will carry us through. When we do this, it’s like a light shining in a dark place, pointing others to the Source of love and freedom.

You, friend, have made a choice to teach God’s precious children. Sometimes you may feel unequipped for the task. Commit your choices to God and allow His love to shine brightly through you to the children in your class.

“Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths” (Proverbs 3:5, 6).
