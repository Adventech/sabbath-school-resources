^[Word of the Week—Friend:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#087fb1", "size": "xl"}}})\
A person we have gotten to know well and like to spend time with. Someone we trust to be helpful and loyal. A good friend puts the other person’s needs first. Jesus tells us that He is our Friend (John 15:13-15).

In Need of Hope and Help

He couldn’t move his arms; he couldn’t move his legs. In fact, the man from Capernaum could scarcely move at all. His body was paralyzed. He could only lie still on his sleeping mat as time slowly passed by.

The man had a lot of time to think about his life and the choices he’d made. He knew that choosing God’s way of life is the best way to live. But he hadn’t chosen God’s way. Now he was really, really sorry.

He was sorry that he was in pain and couldn’t move. But he was even more sorry for the sinful choices he’d made that had led him to get sick. His heart was filled with guilt and regret.

He had asked the doctors and the priests to help him, but they had just turned up their noses and walked away. They wrongly believed his suffering was God’s punishment for his sins, so they ignored him. How cruel! If only they had remembered that God is not like that at all.

The Bible tells us, “There is no God like you. You forgive people who are guilty of sin. . . . You enjoy being kind. Lord, you will have mercy on us again. You will conquer our sins. You will throw away all our sins into the deepest sea” (Micah 7:17-19, ICB).

The man lay on his bed and wished his life could change. His guilt made him feel sad and trapped.

What does the Bible say the man should do? And what will God do in return? **Read Isaiah 55:7.**

He will do the same for you. Jesus loves to forgive and change us!
