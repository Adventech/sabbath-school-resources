Cain was angry! God had not burned up his offering, made his way. What had Abel done differently from Cain?

Abel tried to help his brother. With kind words he reminded Cain to choose a lamb sacrifice that would please God, but Cain wouldn’t listen. Instead he just got angrier at Abel. _How dare his little brother try to tell him what to do!_

Even God came down to Cain and tried to talk to him. What did God ask? Read Genesis 4:6. But Cain didn’t listen to God, either!

Instead Cain let his hate grow larger and larger, like a balloon. And while he was out in the field with Abel, his anger exploded, and he chose not to follow God’s rules of love. He hurt Abel so badly that Abel died. Cain ran away. But God saw what had happened.

“Where is your brother Abel?” God asked Cain.

“I don’t know,” Cain lied (Genesis 4:9, ICB).

God knew what Cain had done. He had seen everything, and it made Him very, very sad. God told Cain that he would have to leave his home and live far away in a land called Nod.

When Adam and Eve found out, their hearts broke. Their loving Abel was gone, and Cain had to go far away. What a sad, sad day. When we disobey, sadness comes to our home too. But Jesus forgives us when we say, “I’m sorry; please forgive me.” He can make our hearts happy again and help us to obey Him.
