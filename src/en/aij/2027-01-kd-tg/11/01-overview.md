**Word of the Week**

**Choose:** To choose is to pick out something. If you have three games to choose from, you get to pick which one to play. To choose also means to decide to do something. God loves you so much that He lets you make choices.

**Lesson Summary**

While Moses was on the mountain, the Israelites became impatient and demanded that Aaron make them an idol. Aaron chose to obey the people instead of God, and made them a golden calf. When Moses saw the idol, he was so upset, he broke the stone tablets. He asked those who wanted to choose God, to stand with him. Moses prayed to God to forgive them, and he cut a second set of tablets, which God wrote on.
