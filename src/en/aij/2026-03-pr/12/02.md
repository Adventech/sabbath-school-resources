The people smiled and chatted excitedly in the busy market of Jericho. Jesus. His name was on everyone’s lips! The people swapped stories as they bought their oil and olives. They listened as news came from the many traders and travelers who passed through their city. And they passed on the stories with wide eyes and big smiles as they lined up to pay their taxes to Zacchaeus.

Zacchaeus listened carefully as the coins clinked together in his money box. Hope began to stir in his heart. He overheard amazing stories about how Jesus gave blind people their sight and healed people who lived with disabilities. He heard that Jesus spoke beautiful things about God’s kingdom, that He treated poor people with kindness, and that He even had a tax collector as one of His disciples! When Zacchaeus heard that startling piece of news, he began to wonder if Jesus might see potential for good in him too. He longed for a better life.

John the Baptist’s words had been repeated up and down the country: “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2). What else may Zacchaeus have heard and recalled? **Read Luke 3:12, 13.**

The Holy Spirit was at work in Zacchaeus. His heart softened, and he began to believe that even he could repent and change his ways.

God always sees potential for change in people’s hearts. He helps them see that potential for themselves, even if other people around them don’t believe their lives could ever change for the better. “For the Lord does not see as man sees; for man looks at the outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart” (1 Samuel 16:7). The Holy Spirit may be working in the hearts of those around us, even though we may not see it at the time.
