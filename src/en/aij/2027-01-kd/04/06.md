Inside a nest, perched high on a rocky ledge, huddled three tiny baby eagle birds. Their soft baby feathers looked like a warm coat, and their little wet, tired bodies could barely stand. They were just a few hours old. Mother eagle gently pushed her warm body near her new babies to keep them warm. Without her warmth, they would die.

Father soared up high, looking for food. He saw something move way down below in the canyon, and swooped low to pick it up. Back at the nest, he broke it into pieces and held it to the chicks’ small beaks. They ate hungrily. In a few weeks’ time mother eagle would find food, but right now she needed to stay with her eaglets.

The hours passed by, and the hot sun rose high in the sky. It would burn the chicks if they were left alone. Father eagle spread his wings to give them shade. Without mother and father’s shelter, they would be too hot in the daytime and too cold at night. Finally the sun disappeared, and the stars came out at night. Father and mother eagle checked their babies: one, two, three. Yes, they were all sleeping.

Psalm 91:4 tells us God protects us too. It says, “He will protect you like a bird spreading its wings over its young” (ICB). Thank You, God! “You are my hiding place; You shall preserve me from trouble” (Psalm 32:7).

During the plagues of Egypt, God covered His children with love and protection, just as these eagle birds protected their chicks. And God promises to save you, too, when you ask for Him to live in your heart.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What did Jesus have to do so that we might be saved?
- What kinds of things does Jesus want to save us from? (Bad habits, anger, unkindness, etc.)
