How high can you bounce or jump? A red kangaroo can jump nearly 10 feet high! That means with one leap he could land on the roof of a minibus without any problem. That’s a pretty impressive bounce, isn’t it?

Find a ball and four other objects that won’t break when you drop them, and go outside (e.g., rice in a sock tied off).

Now guess whether the objects you have will bounce back at you or just drop to the ground, go “splat,” and stay there. Test each one. Were your guesses correct?

Sometimes people are like the objects that bounce. They might feel sad for a while when a sad thing happens and drop down low just as your ball did. But then they bounce back and feel good again. These “bounce” people are strong [resilient], just as Joseph was.

We all have times we go “splat” and hit a sad or tough time, but this is not where God wants us to stay. He is strong, and when we choose to have Him by our side, we can bounce again.
