Rebekah had never run so fast. Racing through the doors of her home, she breathlessly told her family what had just happened at the well. God had led Eliezer right to her!

Rebekah’s family welcomed Eliezer into their home. They kindly looked after his camels, washed his dirty feet, and prepared dinner for him. Then Eliezer told the family about his prayer for help and wisdom. He explained that Rebekah’s kindness was the answer to his prayer.

Everyone was amazed. They all agreed that God had chosen Rebekah to marry Isaac. “This is clearly from the Lord,” her father and uncle said with a smile (Genesis 24:50, ICB).

Turning to Rebekah her family asked, “Do you want to go with this man now?” (Genesis 24:58, ICB). What was Rebekah’s answer? **Read the rest of Genesis 24:58.**

Many days later, in the land of Canaan, Isaac walked slowly through a field under the early evening stars. He thought and prayed as he walked. Suddenly he saw 10 camels up ahead. It must be Eliezer, back from his special trip!

Rebekah’s heart fluttered as she watched Isaac coming near. This was the man God had chosen to be her husband! She climbed off the camel and pulled a veil over her face, like a bride on her wedding day.

Eliezer introduced them. He told Isaac that God had chosen this kind and thoughtful woman to be his wife. What a wonderful God! Isaac married Rebekah “and he loved her” (Genesis 24:67).
