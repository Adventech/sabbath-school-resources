A flash of bright lightning lit up the sky. The disciples gasped in fright. The crashing waves were filling up their boat! Finally, they went to Jesus for help.

What did they do and say? **Read Mark 4:38.**

Jesus stood up and faced the angry storm. He lifted His strong hands above the crashing waves—hands that He used every day to help, and heal, and love others. With a strong, calm voice He told the wind and the waves to stop.

“Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39).

The wind listened to Jesus’ voice. It stopped howling. The waves listened to Jesus’ voice. They grew still.

Everything was calm.

The dark clouds rolled away and the twinkling stars came out again in the clear, night sky.

Jesus looked into His disciples’ faces. He said, “Why are you so fearful? How is it that you have no faith?” (Mark 4:40).

Jesus wanted them to have even a tiny bit of faith so it could grow bigger like the seed and tree in the story He’d told the people that day. He wanted them to find peace by listening to His voice too.

You can also trust Jesus to help you feel safe, peaceful, and brave. Listen to His words in your memory verse. “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39). Thank You, Jesus!
