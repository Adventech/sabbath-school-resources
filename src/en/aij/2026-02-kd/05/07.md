In the warm waters between America and Africa lives an animal called a manatee. This large roly-poly creature looks a bit like a swimming elephant except it doesn’t have a trunk or legs. Instead this ocean giant has flippers and a flat tail, like a paddle.

A manatee spends most of its days swimming slowly through the water eating seagrass and other floating plants. When their front teeth fall out from all the munching, new teeth grow and move to the front of their mouth.

Manatees are usually quiet animals but sometimes they talk to each other with squeaks and squeals. Although they may have just a small brain, manatees have shown that they are very smart.

But there is one thing that really makes this creature extra special. Most wild animals can become dangerous or angry at times but not the manatee. It is known for always being gentle and peaceful.

They are happy to share their patch of water with any other manatee and will nuzzle and kiss each other. They even use their flippers to give hugs.

Manatees have been seen playing games like tag and bodysurfing together. How beautiful!

There’s a lot to learn from the manatee. God made a promise in John 14:27 when He said, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you.”

God’s Holy Spirit wants to give us the heart of a manatee; to be filled with His peace, kindness, and gentleness in every situation.
