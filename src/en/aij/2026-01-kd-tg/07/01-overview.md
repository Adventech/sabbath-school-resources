**Word of the Week**

**Faithful:** When you are faithful, you are a promise keeper. You can be trusted to do what you say you will do. God is faithful. He always keeps His promises.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Adam and Eve’s family grew. Sadly, more people chose Satan’s way than God’s way. Sin and its terrible consequences spread. Things became so bad that God asked His faithful servant Noah to build a boat in an effort to save those who wanted to follow His loving ways. God sent a flood to make things new again and to offer hope and salvation.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that being faithful to God shows our love for Him.
- **Feel** the strength that God gives to help us be faithful to Him.
- **Respond** by choosing to be faithful to God.
