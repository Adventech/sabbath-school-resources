**Word of the Week**

**Strength:** Strength is being strong and powerful. With strong muscles you can have the strength you need to lift heavy objects. Strength also means being tough and not being moved or pushed around by something. We can have strength inside our hearts—a strength to do what is right no matter what. God’s Holy Spirit and His Word can give us that strength.

**Lesson Summary**

Following Jesus’ baptism, the Holy Spirit led Jesus into the desert where He fasted and prayed for 40 days. There Satan, disguised as an angel from heaven, came to tempt Jesus. He challenged Jesus to use His power to turn stones into bread, presumptuously jump off the temple, and bow down and worship Satan himself. To each temptation Jesus responded by quoting Scripture. Finally Satan gave up in defeat, and God sent angels to strengthen and encourage Jesus.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God’s Word is powerful.
- **Feel** encouraged to know that God will help them when they are tempted.
- **Respond** by asking God to help them to hide His Word in their hearts.
