### Stay Close to Jesus and Be Ready

> <p></p>
> “Take your sickle and reap, because the time to reap has come, for the harvest of the earth is ripe.” Revelation 14:15 (NIV)

Have you ever seen fruit growing on a tree? At first, it is small and not ready to eat. But little by little, it grows until it is ripe and ready to be picked.

The Bible uses a harvest to teach us something about Jesus coming again. In Revelation 14, God shows us three important scenes. First, we see God’s people with Jesus. Then we hear the messages of three angels helping people get ready. Finally, we see Jesus coming again!

Jesus is like a farmer who waits until the harvest is ready. He wants His people to be ready.

What does being ready mean? The Bible says God’s people follow Jesus, obey God, and keep trusting Him. They love Jesus so much that they want to stay close to Him every day.

Being ready does not mean being afraid. It means having Jesus in our hearts. We can talk to Him, listen to His Word, obey Him, and ask the Holy Spirit for help.

Even when difficult things happen, Jesus does not leave His people. He stays close to them.

And one day, when the harvest is ready, Jesus will come again and take His people home!

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What can you do today to stay close to Jesus?

> <p>Remember</p>
> I want to stay close to Jesus and be ready when He comes!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear Jesus, thank You for loving us and staying close to us. Help us follow You, obey You, and keep You in our hearts. Please prepare us for the wonderful day when You come again. Amen.
