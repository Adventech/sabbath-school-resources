**What you need:**

Week 9 craft template, two paper cups, scissors, glue, colored marker, blue and purple paper.

**Instructions:**

1\. Cut out the pieces. Glue the jug onto a paper cup and trace the outline with a colored marker.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/09/9b.png)

2\. Color in the handles and glue to the side of the cup along the tabs. Adults will need to cut the jug shape out of the cup.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/09/9c.png)

3\. Stick the blue and purple pieces of paper around the outside of the second cup.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/09/9d.png)

4\. Place the second cup inside the jug cup and twist around to show the “water” (blue), turn to “wine” (purple).
