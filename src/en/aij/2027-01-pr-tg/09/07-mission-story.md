**Focus:** God became number one in Una’s life after he got to know Jesus at an Adventist boarding school.

**Location:** Help a child to locate **Botswana** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Una had the strangest dream the night before he went to pick up his school uniform at his new school, Eastern Gate Academy, a boarding school in Botswana. Sixteen-year-old Una dreamed that it was afternoon, and he was talking with a girl outdoors. Then he entered a building and was baptized.

Una awoke with a start. He didn’t go to church, and he couldn’t understand why he was being baptized. Finally he put the dream out of his mind. “I put it off as just another dream that a kid could have,” he said.

Later in the day Una moved into the dormitory at the school, and his parents returned to Zimbabwe, where his father worked. Una didn’t realize at first that he was attending a Seventh-day Adventist school, and he was surprised to be called to morning worship. At first he disliked getting up early, but after a while he became interested in Jesus. His love for Jesus grew throughout the school year, and he decided to be baptized.

On the day of the baptism, Una went outdoors and spoke with a girl near one of the dormitories. As they spoke, he sensed something familiar about the scenery. “But I didn’t understand what it was, and I pushed aside the thought,” he said.

A short time later the students boarded buses to ride to another church for the baptism. “When we got to the church, the entrance to the building looked familiar, as if I had seen it somewhere before,” Una said. “But I had never been there before.”

As Una stepped into the baptismal tank, he thought that the steps looked familiar. A moment later the pastor was praying as he put Una under the water. As Una emerged, water running down his face, he suddenly remembered his dream! Everything that had happened that afternoon had been in the dream. He couldn’t believe it.

“God works in mysterious ways, and I was fortunate to see my baptism in advance,” Una said. “After that, my faith increased.” Una later told the other students about the dream. The girl he had chatted to in his dream was amazed. Una’s parents were also surprised. The dream has allowed Una to share with them what the school teaches about Jesus. Una is now 18 and encourages his parents to go to church every Sabbath.

No matter what Una ends up doing after high school, he is determined to put Jesus first. “The dream showed me the path that I was to choose in life,” he said. “It showed me that I should stick to that one path—Jesus.”

**Discuss:**

- **How do you know that God is most important in Una’s life?**
- **How did Una show love and respect for God?**
