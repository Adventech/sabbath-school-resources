**Word of the Week**

**Faith:** The Bible tells us that “faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not yet seen” (Hebrews 11:1). This means that we can know that something is real, even if we don’t see it. You can trust that God loves you, saves you, and wants to spend eternity with you—you can have faith in Him!

**Lesson Summary**

A Roman centurion’s servant was sick, and he knew that Jesus was the only one who could help him. He didn’t feel worthy to have Jesus in his home, so he met Jesus on the way. He revealed his great faith by saying that Jesus didn’t need to go to his house, He could just speak, and the servant would be healed. Jesus declared He had never seen such great faith before.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God wants to work in their lives and respond to their faith.
- **Feel** they can have faith in Jesus, even if it starts small.
- **Respond** by sharing their faith with others.
