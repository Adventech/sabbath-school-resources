Joseph lay gently down on his bed. A soft smile played on his lips as he closed his eyes, ready for sleep. Life was good in Egypt. Little Jesus was growing well. He was about 3 years old. His family was safe, far away from wicked King Herod. There were many other Jews living there too, so they were not among total strangers. God had guided them well.

Soon Joseph was fast asleep. Then God gave Joseph a dream—his third dream from God. What did the angel in the dream tell Joseph to do? **Read Matthew 2:20.**

Once more, Joseph and Mary loaded up the donkey, maybe even with the help of little Jesus. And off they went.

Joseph planned to set up home in Bethlehem, but Herod’s meanest son Archelaus was now the ruler there. Joseph was afraid he would harm little Jesus. God spoke to Joseph in a fourth dream that guided him many miles north of Jerusalem to their old hometown of Nazareth. Once again the little family enjoyed God’s care and protection. It was a good thing they had God in their hearts and trusted His guidance!

God often guided people through dreams in the Bible and still leads many people by dreams and visions today. However, God’s guidance is available to us every day, not just in dreams. Can you think of three ways that God can guide us?

God uses His Word, the Bible. He guides us through people and circumstances—this special care is called providence. God also guides us by the Holy Spirit, who brings thoughts to our hearts and minds. God is able to constantly lead us in ways we will understand. “The Lord will guide you continually” (Isaiah 58:11).
