I Can Help\
^[God]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff", "typeface": "Omnes-Bold"}}}) 
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}

Blink your eyes three times: _one . . . two . . . three._ What do you see? Your eyes are a gift from God. You can use them to be His helper. Whisper your name, as quietly as can be. Now shout it out loud, on the count of three: _one . . . two . . . three._ Your words are a gift from God. You can use them to be His helper. God loves to see little people help Him! He has special jobs that you can do to show others His love.\
God made animals to be His helpers, just like you. Honey bees are very busy little helpers. Do you know what sound they make? Yes! They _buzz, buzz, buzz_. They collect sweet food from the flowers then fly back to their hive. Then these busy little helpers turn it into honey. Then off they fly to visit more flowers—_buzz, buzz, buzz_. When bees visit flowers, they help the flowers to grow. They drop little parts of the flower as they fly. This helps plants to grow fruit too. God uses bees to make honey, flowers, and fruit. What busy helpers! _Thank you, God, for bees!_

Can you find the line of worker ants? _March, march, march_, go the ants. They are very busy helping God. Ants march and dig and stir up the dirt as they work, work, work. They busily collect food to take back to their home. As they work, they help God. Ants help by putting air into the dirt so plants can grow. They also carry plant seeds and later drop them so that they can grow in new places. Even though they are very small, they can carry heavy things. God made them to be strong helpers. They all work together as a big, big team! _Thank You, God, for the ants!_\
Spot the squirrels in the tree. Up they climb, as quick as can be. They’re busy looking for food. Squirrels just love to eat seeds and nuts. They are also God’s special helpers; His little gardeners. Before the snow and cold winds come, the squirrels store nuts and seeds and hide them under the ground. In the wintertime they dig them up and eat them. But sometimes they forget some of the food they have hidden in the dirt. These seeds grow roots and grow into more trees. They help God by planting new trees for other animals to enjoy. _Thank You, God, for squirrels!_

What a wonderful world God created! He made the plants for us. He made the animals for us too. He even gave the animals special jobs to help God’s earth.\
God gives us special jobs too. We can use our eyes to look for ways to help Him. We can use our words to tell others all about His great, big love. We can also sing and smile! We can use our helpful hands to give our offerings to Him so that others can learn about God too. What other helpful things can you do for God?\
_(Say together)_ Dear God, I want to help You!
