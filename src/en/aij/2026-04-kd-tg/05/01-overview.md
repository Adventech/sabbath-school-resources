**Word of the Week**

**Kind:** When we are kind, we are helpful, friendly, and good to others. A kind person will say and do good things to help someone and make them happy. Jesus is always kind and loving. We can be kind like Him.

**Lesson Summary**

Jesus healed three lonely men. First, a man with leprosy approached Jesus in a large crowd and asked Jesus to heal him. Jesus touched the man and cleansed him. Second, on a Sabbath day Jesus and His disciples passed by a man who was blind from birth. Jesus spat in the dust, made mud, and put it on the blind man’s eyes. At Jesus’ command the man went and washed and came back seeing. The Pharisees were furious that the man had been healed on Sabbath and tried to exclude him. Jesus kindly found him and revealed that He was the Son of God. Third, Jesus also met a man who was deaf and speech-impaired. Jesus put His fingers in the man’s ears and touched his tongue, and he could hear and speak.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus cares about people who feel left out.
- **Feel** a desire to include others.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to be their close friend.
