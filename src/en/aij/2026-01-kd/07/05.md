_Bang, bang, bang! Saw, saw, saw!_ Noah and his three sons, Shem, Ham, and Japheth, began to build God’s huge boat. It would be the biggest boat they had ever seen! It would be a boat big enough to carry lots of people _and_ lots of animals.

Every day, except for Sabbath, Noah and his sons faithfully chopped, measured, sawed, and hammered. The sound of their axes, hammers, and saws shared a message: “A flood is coming! A flood is coming! Be ready! Be ready!”

It was a _big_ job! The cedar wood for the ark had to be covered with gooey pitch to make sure water didn’t get inside. The length and width of the ark had to be just right too. “Noah did everything that God commanded him” (Genesis 6:22, ICB).

Slowly the ark took shape. It was three stories high, with one door that opened on the side. Inside the boat were lots of little rooms. God had planned perfect spaces for people as well as animals. The giraffes with tall necks, heavy buffalo, big pandas, small meerkats, and little birds and beetles all needed a home in the ark.

Even though Noah and his sons had never seen a raindrop fall from the sky or seen a river flood, they built God’s big boat. Why? Read Hebrews 11:7.

God had said a flood would come, so they believed a flood would come. God had told them to build the ark, so Noah and his sons faithfully obeyed.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever had to do something that has taken a lot of hard work and time? How did you feel when it was finished?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you faithfully do what He asks every day, even when it is hard work.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
