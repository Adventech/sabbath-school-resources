**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever experienced a challenging moment when you needed to be faithful and stand strong against all odds? Such times need us to lean into our connection with God—relying on Him to make us strong, unmovable, and steadfast. “But the Lord is faithful, who shall establish you and guard you from the evil one” (2 Thessalonians 3:3).

Noah had such a moment. He could easily have questioned God’s unusual instructions to build an ark when no one had even heard of a flood. Yet he didn’t. He had cultivated a strong and respectful friendship with God. So in challenging moments he naturally and faithfully followed God’s instructions. The ark was meticulously built to every important detail. In the process Noah modeled his unwavering devotion to the God he loved and served.

“But Noah stood like a rock amid the tempest. Surrounded by popular contempt and ridicule, he distinguished himself by his holy integrity and unwavering faithfulness” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 96).

What an inspiring example this is for us today. We can also have a connection with God that helps us to grow steadfast in His ways, live out His priorities, obey His words of love, and boldly share His loving promises with others around us. We can stand as Noah did and live faithfully in a friendship with our God, who is always faithful.
