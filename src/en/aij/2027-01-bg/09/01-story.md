Door
^^^
^^^

Noah and his family smiled as the animals went through the _very big_ door of the ark. They were fat and thin. Big and small. Smooth and hairy . . . and even scary! _(Make animal sounds.)_ Cheetahs roared, and geese honked. Parrots chirped and pigs oinked. Horses neighed and cats purred. What other animal noises could be heard? God had sent them all to the ark. What a fun, noisy, wonderful zoo! The animals came inside, two by two.\
Noah was God’s friend. He listened to God’s big plan. He was God’s helper and had done all God had asked. God promised to keep Noah and his family safe in the ark when the big rain came. He promised to care for them and the animals when the big rain came. Sadly, no one else chose to come into the ark. No one else chose to trust God’s love. So God told Noah it was time to go into the boat. Noah and his wife went through the very big door _(arms out wide)_. His three sons and their wives all went through the very big door too.
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "bottom"}}}

The animals were inside. Noah and his family were now inside too. But who could shut the very big door? _(Shrug with hands out.)_ It was too big for Noah or his wife to shut! It was too heavy for Noah’s sons to pull. It was even too big for the elephants to close. Who could shut the big, heavy door? God would! It wasn’t too big for God.\
Nothing is too big _(arms wide)_ or too little _(hands close)_ for God! He promised to take care of Noah and his family. So with a bright flash of light, an angel shut the very big door _(clap hands)_. It was closed tight! No one could open it now, except for God. The rain would come. Noah, his family, and all the animals would be safe and dry inside the ark.

_(Count each day with fingers.)_ One day, two days, and three passed by. The sun shone brightly all outside. Four days, five, and six sped by, as Noah and his family prayed inside. Day 7 passed by and still no rain fell from the sky.\
God said the rain would come, so Noah and his family waited patiently, because God never lies. On Day 8 the clouds came by. The rain! There’s the rain! The rain came, just as God said it would.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for doing what You say You’ll do. Nothing is too big for You!
