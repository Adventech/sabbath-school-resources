^[Shepherds]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#D2AE77"}}})
^^^
^^^

The shepherds smiled as they watched over their sheep. The night was very dark. All their fluffy sheep were sleeping. _(Shh!)_ All their little lambs were sleeping too. _(Shh!)_ All was quiet in the fields outside Bethlehem. Suddenly, bright light swirled around them. _Shimmer, shimmer, shine._ The shepherds looked up in surprise. It was a sparkling angel! The angel said, “I am bringing you some good news. It will be a joy to all the people” (Luke 2:10, ICB). Jesus had been born!\
The shining angel told them where to find Baby Jesus. He was wrapped in cloth, sleeping in a manger in Bethlehem. Then the sky grew _brighter . . . and brighter . . . and brighter_! It was filled with lots and lots of shining angels. The angels were so happy. Jesus had been born! In sweet voices they sang, “Glory to God in the highest” (Luke 2:14). Jesus would bring joy to everyone.

^^^

The shepherds’ eyes opened wide _(look amazed)_ as they watched the shining angels sing. Their knees wobbled _(wobble knees)_. Their hearts beat fast _(tap chest quickly)_. Jesus had been born! As soon as the angels disappeared, the shepherds started to run. They smiled and sang as they ran all the way into Bethlehem. _Run, run, run._ They must find Baby Jesus! They ran to the stable. Would God’s Son be here? They knocked on the door. _Knock, knock, knock._\
The door opened and the shepherds quietly tiptoed inside. There was Baby Jesus, wrapped in cloth, sleeping in the manger. It was just as the angel said! The shepherds knelt down before Baby Jesus. Oh, how they loved Him! This tiny Baby really was from God. In a gentle whisper, the shepherds told Mary and Joseph about the angel’s special visit. This Baby would give joy to everyone!

Mary and Joseph smiled big, happy smiles at the kind shepherds. God had sent them to welcome His Son, Baby Jesus. The shepherds were so thankful. They sang and thanked God all the way back to their sleeping sheep. Their eyes had seen the angels from heaven _(point to eyes)_. Their ears had heard the angels’ song _(cup ear)_. Their hands had held Baby Jesus close _(pretend to hold baby)_. They knew God loved them. He loved everyone so much. He had sent Jesus.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for sending Jesus. Thank you that Jesus gives us joy.
