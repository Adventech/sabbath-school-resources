**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children to **see your dramatic rescue scene** (toys stuck in gelatin or in a closed container in a tub of other toys/sensory items). Share that something terrible has happened—your precious toys have become stuck and can’t get out! There’s absolutely nothing they can do on their own. **They need a rescuer with an awesome rescue plan!**

Show the children the “tools” they can use. Share that they are unable to touch the toys directly with their hands but can use these items to free the toys.

- **What should we do?** (Take several ideas, affirm, and discuss together, deciding on a plan.)

Invite a few children to put the plan into action. Conclude the activity by saying how glad you are that the rescue plan worked!

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God has a rescue plan for us, because we are stuck in sin. We can’t get out by ourselves. This is called the plan of salvation.

- **Why do you think that is?** (Jesus came to die on the cross to save everyone who loves God.)

We can understand more of God’s wonderful plan of salvation by learning about the symbols God put in the sanctuary. Everything there is a beautiful symbol of something important about God’s plan of salvation.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **The sanctuary shows God’s plan to save me.**
