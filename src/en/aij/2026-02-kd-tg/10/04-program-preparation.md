**Opening Activities**

Set out some play-dough.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box:** a collection of **household items that respond to a need**. For example, food for hunger, water for thirst. Include one for each child in the class, or enough to cover the group in pairs or threes, if needed. Include such items as: **a Bible, blanket, fruit, water bottle, clothing, shoes, umbrella/raincoat, first-aid supplies.**

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: fancy fabric/robe. Grain or rice or beans as grain. Containers, cups, small bag. A large container to use as the storehouse. A bag of coins to hide in a bag of grain.

Note: Keep grain, bags, and coins for next week’s lesson.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a **paper bag** for each child, **spoon, half-cup measuring cup, mystery bag** that the children can’t see inside. Place a **treat** for each child (sticker, edible treat, etc.) inside the mystery bag.
