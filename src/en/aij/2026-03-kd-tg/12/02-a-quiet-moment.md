**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

How has your life changed since you decided to follow Jesus?

Whether your decision to follow Jesus was an instant choice or a slowly growing conviction over many years, it has no doubt brought about major changes to your life. Think of a few simple examples that you could thank God for this week and share them with your own family as praise.

When we take time to pause, we may notice the things in our character that need changing. Maybe we need more patience or more gentleness to those around us. Perhaps we can be more generous with our time or resources. Growing the characters of our children takes intentional care and effort, yet God wants to continue to do this work in our lives too. We can be assured that “He who has begun a good work in you will complete it until the day of Jesus Christ” (Philippians 1:6). As we grow and allow God to refine us, let’s not forget to look back and thank Him for the miracles He has already worked in our hearts and lives.

The children in your class are starting to see what it means to give their hearts to Jesus. This week you can show them how Jesus’ love changed Zacchaeus’ life. Point the children to the moments that show His unconditional love: Jesus knew Zacchaeus by name; He wanted to spend time with Zacchaeus and share a meal at his house; He claimed Zacchaeus as part of God’s family. With Jesus’ love growing inside him, Zacchaeus began to change in amazing ways.

With Jesus’ love in our hearts, we will also continue to change. For truly, “if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation; old things have passed away; behold, all things have become new” (2 Corinthians 5:17).
