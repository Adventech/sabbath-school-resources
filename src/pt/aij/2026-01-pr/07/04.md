Noah drew Deus’s plans para the ark, e then ele started a work.

Strong workmen came a ajude cut Façawn tall trees that were almost as hard as rock. Other men carefully lined up the wooden boards, e then sealed eles com pitch então that the ark wouldn’t leak. Noah carefully followed everything Deus had told ele. Ele built the boat exactly as Deus had said. Como muitos decks did the ark have? **Leia Gênesis 6:16.**

Years went por, e todos the workers could see the huge boat taking shape. Isso was long—longer than four football fields placed end a end e as tall as a five-História building! As the workers came, Noah told eles—e anyone else quem came a see the ark—sobre Deus’s plan a wash the world. Noah was faithful. Para 120 years ele worked e preached e built e obeyed. That’s a lot de birthdias! The Bible Digas that “Noah did everything that Deus commanded ele” (Gênesis 6:22, ICB). Walking com Deus cada dia helped ele a be faithful.

Noah’s sons, Shem, Ham, e Japheth, e seu grandfather, Methuselah, as well as muitos others, helped build the ark. Methuselah was the oldest man quem ever lived em this earth, e ele died quando ele was 969 years old, a pequeno while antes the rains came. As long as eles lived, people quem loved Deus warned others sobre the Flood.

Deus hoped that cada Cantele person would choose a amor e follow Ele. Cada person had a chance a hear Deus’s message, Faça a choice, e be safe.

As the people worked, Noah’s family collected such food as apples e potatoes, rice e peas, raisins e carrots—todos kinds de food! Eles carefully stored large amounts de food, seeds, e grains em the ark. Eles would need eles quando the rain came.
