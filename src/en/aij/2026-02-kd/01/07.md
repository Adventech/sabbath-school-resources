A heavy white cardboard box arrived at a Seventh-day Adventist mission school in the Marshall Islands. It was from Grandpa Bob and was for the missionary families that worked at the school.

When Father and Mother saw the box, they couldn’t wait to open it! Big brothers Raijan and Jehuraian and little sister Jaira couldn’t wait either! The family gathered in the living room around the parcel.

“This box is a blessing from God,” Father said. “Let’s thank God now.” Together the family knelt down, and Father prayed.

“Heavenly Father, we are thankful for this blessing that just arrived. Help us to bless others through the blessing that we have received. Thank You for Grandpa Bob and his kindness. Please bless him. Amen.”

With Father’s approval, Raijan grabbed a pair of scissors and tore open the box.

Grandpa Bob had been sending their family boxes for three years because he wanted to show God’s kindness and love. The boxes would often come just when food was running out, when Father or Mother needed some encouragement, or when a child had a birthday.

“Oh, wow!” Raijan cried as he looked into the box and saw a toy car. “A yellow car!”

Juhuraian reached his hand into the box. “And a blue one!” he shouted.

Jaira couldn’t stop smiling. Her eyes were fixed on a doll. “My very own doll!” she said.

Mother and Father looked through the rest of the box and found chocolates, notebooks, pencils, red rice, nuts, and instant mashed potatoes. These would be wonderful to share with the student missionaries teaching at the school.

What a wonderful gift! Every time a box arrived, the children would jump for joy. The family always prayed before opening a box, saying, “Thank You, God, for showing Your love and kindness to us through our Grandpa Bob.”

The Bible says, “Blessed be the Lord, for He has shown me His marvelous kindness” (Psalm 31:21).
