As a family, put together a gift package for someone you know who is sick.

**You will need:**

- _an attractive box or bag_
- _wrapping paper and tape_
- _pens, crayons, or felt pens_
- _card or paper_
- _Small gift items from your home that would either cheer up a sick person or be useful to them. Think specifically about what the person would appreciate._

**Do:**

- _On small pieces of card paper, write Bible promises. Suggestions: 3 John 2, 1 Peter 3:12, Numbers 6:24, Psalm 86:15, Isaiah 41:10. Decorate the cards with pictures or stickers._
- _Collect healthy food items you are happy to share. Suggestions: muesli bars, fresh fruit, juice, nuts, canned food._
- _Share other small items such as: a small soft toy, tissues, a bar of soap, a book, fresh flowers._
- _Wrap each gift (except the flowers). Attach a promise card to each gift, and place it in the box or bag._
- _If your box is plain, you may like to either gift-wrap it, or draw pictures to decorate it._
- _Deliver your care package to the sick person. You may like to recite your memory verse or sing them a song as well._
