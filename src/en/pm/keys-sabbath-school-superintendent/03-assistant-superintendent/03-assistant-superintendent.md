---
title: Assistant Superintendent
---

This officer should serve, as the title indicates, as an assistant to the superintendent in carrying forward the regular work of the school, and in bearing such responsibilities as may be assigned by the superintendent. He or she should be ready to respond to any request of the superintendent for assistance in the administration of the Sabbath School affairs. In the absence of the superintendent, the assistant takes full charge of the school. The Sabbath School is a training school for workers of all classes. As a result of giving the assistants the proper training and experience, future leading officers will be provided. In the larger Sabbath Schools more than one assistant superintendent may be required.
