**Big Idea:** We are God’s special creation, made in His image.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images. Place one **Animal Card** under each child’s seat—facedown—until needed. Retain the cards that show mothers with their young. You may like to sculpt both Adam and Eve from play-dough or pretend to form these shapes on the ground as you share those parts of the story.

Source and show a few pictures of unusual animals (e.g., manatee, platypus, axolotl) for a quick name guess.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Flowers and trees swayed in the gentle breeze. The world was beautiful! It was Day 6 of Creation. “God said, ‘Let the earth be filled with animals’” (Genesis 1:24, ICB).

At God’s command, horses galloped, snakes slithered, and lions roared. All of God’s animals were different, but they lived together happily.

^[**What did God mean when He said the animals were made “according to its kind”? Read Genesis 1:25.** (They were designed to have babies just like themselves. God made monkeys to always be monkeys; they can’t turn into people!)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}) ^[(Show Animal Cards you have set aside—mothers with their young.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

When God created animals, He knew they would be very important to all people and to the world. ^[(Children to look at Animal Cards that were under their seats. How are these animals important?)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He created some animals to help us, such as horses to ride and oxen to pull heavy loads. Others, such as squirrels and monkeys, help the planet. They spread seeds so that more trees grow in forests. Forests create the oxygen we all need to breathe and live.

People should care for the animals. Animals can teach us a lot! ^[**What does Proverbs 6:6 tell us?** (That lazy people should watch how hard ants work and learn from them.)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})

Other animals bring us joy because of how they look and the things they do. Think about mischievous monkeys, playful otters, and parrots that learn to talk.

God designed some animals to be our loving, loyal friends—such as cats or dogs. ^[(Ask about their pets/animals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Their love can remind us of God’s love for us, which never ends.

_(Note: take no longer than five minutes to do the following activity.)_

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3", "size": "lg"}}}
**DO**

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
Display and read each **Animal Role Title** card and spread them out on the floor. Titles include (write on five separate cards):

- {"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
- God created animals to help us directly.
- God created some animals to help look after the planet.
- God created some animals that we can learn important lessons from.
- God created some animals to bring us joy.
- God created some animals to be our special friends.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
Invite children to place each of their animal pictures beside the label that best fits. Encourage them to discuss their choices with others to clarify their thinking and reasoning. As a group, review each title and collection of animal pictures.

- **Animals are a blessing to us. How are we to be a blessing to the animals?** (We can care for them.)

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3", "size": "lg"}}}
**TEACHING POINT**

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
God created the animals to be a blessing to us, and we can be a blessing to them as we responsibly take care of them. When we do this, we are being good creation caretakers.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Let’s continue with the story. It was now time for God’s most special creation. This time He didn’t speak. God took some dust and began to gently shape it into an arm, a tummy, a mouth, and a heart filled with blood. ^[(Kneel on the floor and pretend to shape a person out of air.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When He had finished, a man lay perfect and still. God bent over his face and breathed into his nostrils. ^[(Bend over and blow out.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The man came to life!

Adam opened his eyes to see God’s kind, smiling face. Adam was made in the image of God. He was designed to love and care for others.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had the best new job for him! He could name all the animals. “Giraffe” popped into his head for a tall creature, and “rabbit” for a little ball of fur. What amazing creatures there were to meet and name. ^[(Show unusual animal pictures for the children to quickly guess the names of each.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

All the animals had mates. Adam needed one too. ^[**What did God do? Read Genesis 2:21, 22.** (God created Eve from one of Adam’s ribs while he slept.)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam and Eve were created for each other as the very first male and female. Children would come from their special love for each other. Our families all come from this very first family. We’re all God’s precious children.

Adam and Eve wore a covering of God’s warm light and glory that fit them perfectly. ^[(PowerPoint picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Imagine never having to change your clothes and them never getting old and ripped!

Then God gave them a beautiful garden home called the Garden of Eden. It was a wonderful home with a blue sky and sparkling stars at night, green grass, lots of fun animal friends, and amazing places to explore. Adam and Eve enjoyed the beauty of their garden and talking with their loving Creator, God.
