**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a stack of paper** (heavy cardstock) **in the shape of circles**. You will call these “standing circles.” Count out the number of children and place that number of paper circles in a large circle. Invite the children to move around the big circle while the music is playing. When the music stops, they are to jump on a “standing circle.” Share that an adult helper is also playing. Play music and when it stops, the adult helper will intentionally not find a standing circle. Tell the helper adult they can’t play. (Have them look sad and sit down.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Is our helper having fun now?** (No.) **Why not?** (He/she has been left out; not included.)
- **Would you feel sad if you were left out of a game?** (Yes.)
- **What might Jesus do it He were here, playing with us?** (He would want to include them.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus cares when someone feels left out. He wants everyone to feel included.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play the game again, but this time, a “reverse” version. Take away a couple of standing circles but invite the children to work together to include their friends when the music stops. Who can quickly share their circle with a friend? Can you stand on the circle together? Remove a few more circles to play it one last time.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children to come back to their seats. Share that today’s story is about three men who had big problems and felt left out, but Jesus didn’t leave them feeling that way. He healed them and made sure they felt loved and included. Stand and say this week’s **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He wants to be my close friend** [hug arms around own shoulders]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **large heart**, a **picture of Jesus**, and a box of **Band-Aids** (sticky plasters). Give each child a Band-Aid. Tape the picture of Jesus to the heart and explain that Jesus wants to be your close friend. Invite the children to stick their Band-Aid on the heart around Jesus as they answer the following questions. Allow for multiple similar answers and prompt the children to respond.

- **In our lesson story, how did Jesus show that He was kind?** (He came close to people who were hurting, sick, or left out. He used gentle hands and kind words. He helped them.)
- **How did Jesus show that He was powerful and could do anything?** (He healed three men.)
- **Can you share about a time you felt sad or lonely?**
- **Whom can you trust to help you and be a good friend when you feel sad or left out? Why?** (Jesus. He loves me. My family and others who are kind too.)

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He wants to be my close friend** [hug arms around own shoulders]**.”**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 44: Mexican Axolotl Salamander**.

Ask: **Do you remember which animals we talked about a few weeks ago that heal in amazing ways?** (Starfish, deer, lizards.) Share that God has made these creatures—that look like they are always smiling—to also be fast healers. Share the following facts:

- Mexican axolotl salamanders live underwater and are found in only one lake in Mexico.
- They eat worms, some small fish, and insect eggs.
- They can’t see very well, but they can sense what is going on around them.
- If they have been hurt, lose a little arm or a leg, they are able to grow them back perfectly in a few months. They can even regrow parts of their heart, lung, or brain! Isn’t God incredible?

Jesus cares for the Mexican axolotl salamander, and He cares for you too.

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 44: Mexican Axolotl Salamander** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) “Healing Heart.”** Show children how to make a “Healing Heart” to remind them that Jesus cares for them and will be their very best friend. Play **“My Forever Friend, Jesus”** as the children do their craft to familiarize them with the words.

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Share that today we’ve learned that Jesus is close to us when we are sad, lonely, left out, or hurt. He wants to be our close friend. This week we can be like Jesus by reaching out to others with love and kindness.

Invite the children to stand up and, holding their healing heart’s edge, form a joined circle.

- **What can we do this week when we see someone who is feeling left out?**

Throw **balloons and/or curly streamers** inside the circle to represent the joy that comes when we invite people into our friendship circle. When we do this, we help heal their hearts and help them be happy.

Staying in the circle, sing together **“My Forever Friend, Jesus.”** Jesus will always help us be a good friend, just like He is.
