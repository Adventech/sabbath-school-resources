---
title: Eternity in Our Hearts
date: 20/12/2026
---

“Will what I do today make a difference in eternity?”

This question challenges us to rethink our plans for today and every day. We need to prioritize the time and blessings that God has entrusted to us in a way that will bring Him glory and praise forever.

As followers of Jesus Christ, we can know with absolute certainty that our years here on earth are just a fleeting, troubled start to a beautiful eternity. As the apostle John penned so encouragingly, “I write these things to you who believe in the name of the Son of God so that you may know that you have eternal life” (1 John 5:13, NIV).

Did you see that? We can know that we will live forever . . . and ever and ever. What a promise!

All we need to do is accept it. By accepting God’s offer, we become free to make eternity-effecting choices about our relationships, careers, education, spending, and giving.

We can all live our lives for eternity—starting today!

### Appeal

As you return God’s tithe and give your Promise offerings at the end of this year, please ask yourself, “Will my plans for today, for next year, truly make a difference in eternity?” God is inviting us to worship Him by setting up a regular and systematic giving system that shows our gratitude for His blessings. Through faithful stewardship, we can make a difference for eternity. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
