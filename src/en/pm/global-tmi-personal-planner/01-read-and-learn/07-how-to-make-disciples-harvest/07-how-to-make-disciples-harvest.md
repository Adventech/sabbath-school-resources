subtitle: How to Make Disciples 4

^[Encourage interests to follow Christ and be baptized]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#EFAF44", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}), or accompany them to evangelistic meetings, where appeals are made. Here are several ways to lead someone from a ^[study]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interest to a ^[baptismal]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#EFAF44", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interest, and ultimately a newly baptized member:

- ^[Encourage your friend to make decisions for Christ, including baptism.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Support your friend in completing the remaining topics in a Bible study or evangelistic series after a baptism decision has been made.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Prepare your friend for baptism by reviewing the fundamental teachings of the Bible and the commitment involved in baptism (using Fundamentals of Faith or a similar resource).]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Celebrate with your friend on the day of his or her baptism.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
