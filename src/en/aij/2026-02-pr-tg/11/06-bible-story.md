**Big Idea:** God sees the end from the beginning and can turn difficult experiences into blessings.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images. You may like to use the following props: **three or four small sacks or paper bags** with some **grain, a “silver” cup** (cup covered in aluminum foil).

```

**Pray**: Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph’s brothers looked at the sacks of grain they had brought back from Egypt. There was hardly any grain left! The famine had gotten worse. They needed more food! Finally, with Jacob’s blessing, the brothers—and Benjamin—set off for Egypt to free brother Simeon and buy more grain.

When the brothers arrived in Egypt, they once again bowed low before the governor of Egypt. They still didn’t know it was their brother Joseph! Joseph was so excited to see Benjamin at last! He hurried from the room and wept.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Simeon was released from prison, and lunch was served to all the brothers at Joseph’s house. To test them again, Joseph had Benjamin given five times more food than the others. Joseph listened closely to his brothers. Would they be jealous of Benjamin’s big meal? No! Joseph didn’t hear one jealous word. The brothers talked happily together as they ate.

Joseph was almost convinced his brothers had truly changed, but he decided to test them one more time.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he do? Read Genesis 44:1, 2.** (He commanded his steward to fill the brothers’ sacks with food, put each man’s money in his sack, and put his silver cup in Benjamin’s sack.) ^[(Ask a volunteer to put a silver cup into one sack of grain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The brothers left Egypt, happy to take their grain and brother Benjamin home.

But their happiness didn’t last long. Joseph’s steward raced after them and stopped them. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He accused them of stealing the governor’s silver cup! He asked angrily, “Why have you paid back evil for good?” (Genesis 44:4, ICB).

The servant searched the men’s sacks and found Joseph’s cup in Benjamin’s sack. ^[(Mix up the sacks of grain and have the children try to find the one with the cup.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The brothers were taken back to Egypt. As punishment, Joseph demanded that Benjamin become his slave, while the others could go free. Judah shook his head. His father would be so upset! He begged to take Benjamin’s place.

Joseph knew, without a doubt, that his brothers were truly sorry. He could see that God had changed their hard hearts. With tears in his eyes, he said, “I am Joseph” (Genesis 45:3).

The brothers gasped, amazed! Joseph! Could this man be their little brother? Then they became very afraid. This was the brother they had thrown into a pit. This was the brother they had sold to the traders.

Joseph realized how they felt.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he say to his brothers? Read Genesis 45:4-8.** (Joseph told them not to be sad, because God sent him ahead to become ruler of Egypt and to save lives.)

The brothers could hardly believe their ears. Joseph was forgiving them! And he was telling them how God overturned their cruelty and used him to save them from the famine.

But Joseph had still more good news for them.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What was that good news? Read Genesis 45:9-12.** (Come and live in Egypt, and I will provide for you.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The brothers sped home with the news. Father Jacob was shocked, but so excited. “Joseph my son is still alive. I will go and see him before I die,” he said (Genesis 45:28).

As Jacob’s wagon train rolled into Egypt, Joseph sprang from his chariot and raced to meet his precious father. He threw his arms around Jacob’s neck and wept for a long time.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob was so happy to see Joseph, and God gave him another 17 peaceful years with his long-lost son and his reunited children in Egypt. God had looked after his children, just as He’d promised.
