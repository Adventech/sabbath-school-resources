**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child by name as they arrive. Ask: **“What made you smile this week?”**

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to play a game of Musical Catch With Kindness. When the **music** plays, they are to gently throw a soft **ball**, **beanbag**, or **balloon** to each other. When the music stops, whoever has the ball is to say or do something kind to someone else, then throw the ball to them. Then the music and game will continue.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **sandpaper** and **cotton balls**.

Sit in a circle with the children and pass around the sandpaper, asking them what it feels like. Then pass the cotton balls around the circle.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- Do the cotton balls feel different from the sandpaper? (Yes.)
- Which one do you think kindness is like? (Kindness is soft and feels gentle, like the cotton balls.)
- Do you think God is more like the cotton balls or the sandpaper?

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** When we are unkind to others, our words or actions can feel like sandpaper. They hurt and feel a bit rough. When we are kind, our words and actions feel like the cotton balls. They are soft and gentle.

Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: God is with me. He helps me to be kind.

Our story this week is about someone who was very, very kind to someone she didn’t even know! She used her caring words and her helping hands. Let’s see what happened.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Share the story **“Isaac and Rebekah”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **a set of dominoes**. Invite the children to help you set up some dominoes in a row. Then have one child gently push over the first domino in the row to see what happens. Use this illustration to explain what happens when we show God’s kindness to others.

When the dominoes have all fallen over, sprinkle some **confetti** (or small pieces of colored paper) in each child’s hand to hold. Reinforce that they are to not let go of their confetti yet. Start sprinkling some confetti gently over the children. Explain that little acts of kindness can be sprinkled everywhere in their home, preschool/school, or even at Sabbath School.

- **What could you do to be kind to someone?**

After each child answers, invite them to throw their confetti in the air.

Use the following simple ideas as prompts:

- **Smile at someone who looks sad.**
- **Help someone who is younger than you.**
- **Pick a pretty flower to give someone.**
- **Give someone a big, warm hug.**
- **Share your toys or lunch.**

**^[SAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})**  We can be kind because God is so kind. There is no one in the whole world who is kinder than God. God made pretty flowers, big hugs, delicious food, and everything that is good in our world. He loves you more than anyone, ever. When you feel happy and peaceful in your heart, that feeling is from God. God smiles when we are kind to others.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He helps me to be kind.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD and NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 14: Capybara** and ask if anyone knows the name of this creature. Share that it’s called a capybara and ask them to say its name quickly three times. Share the following facts about the capybara:

- They are smart, friendly, and very gentle toward each other.
- They can dive underwater and hold their breath for five minutes! Wow!
- They look like a giant guinea pig and are from the same family.
- What a wonderful creature, created by our wonderful God!

Give each child **Nature Card 14: Capybara** for them to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

Share the Nature Lesson about capybaras: **“A Kind Friend.”**
