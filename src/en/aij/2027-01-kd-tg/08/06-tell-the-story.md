**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. He made families to love each other.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 8 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- **Suggested props**: create your own background by using **markers/crayons** on **colored paper** to depict a desert landscape with a mountain in the background and two tents—Jethro’s tent at the “start” and Moses’ tent at the “end.”
- Use peg people (Moses, Jethro, Zipporah, two boys [Gershom and Eliezer], a crowd of people) or a felt set
if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

- Moses’ wife, Zipporah, and their sons had stayed with her father, Jethro, in Midian. Now they could be together again. They set off to find Moses. ^[(Move peg family around backdrop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Jethro sent Moses a message when they were near, and he ran to meet them. Hooray! ^[(Moses peg person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Moses took them to his tent. ^[(Move family by drawn tent.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He told them stories about God. **^[What miracle stories might Moses have told?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**

- Jethro went home to Midian happy. (Move Jethro back to his tent.) Good families care for each other and want the best for each other (share memory verse).

**TELL THE STORY**

Father Jethro stood by his tent with a big smile! ^[(Place Jethro peg person by tent.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He had just heard the best news. God had set the Israelites free! Moses had led them out of Egypt and into the desert near Mount Sinai. Jethro rushed to tell his daughter Zipporah. Moses was safe! ^[(Move Jethro to Zipporah and children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Zipporah was Moses’ wife. They had two sons, Gershom and Eliezer. They had gone with Moses to Egypt, but on the way Moses sent them back to be with Father Jethro in Midian. But now they could all be together again!

Soon the family was all packed up. Jethro, Zipporah, Gershom, and Eliezer set off from Midian to find Moses. ^[(Move peg people around backdrop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

As they got closer, Jethro sent a message to Moses to let him know his family was on their way.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses couldn’t wait another minute. He traveled out to meet them! ^[(Move Moses to family.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They hugged and kissed. Now they were back together!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses beamed with joy as he led his family to his tent to rest. ^[(Move family by Moses’ tent.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was so good to be with his loving family again. Now his tent felt like home.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They had so much to talk about! Moses told his father-in-law everything God had done and how he had saved them.

**What miracle stories might Moses have told him?**

“Jethro was very happy when he heard all the good things the Lord had done for Israel” (Exodus 18:9, ICB). It helped him to trust God even more. He made a thank-You offering to God.

The next morning Moses was up early and ready to go. Father Jethro hurried to catch up with him. Moses had a big job to do. He had to help his people solve their problems.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jethro listened closely as Moses helped two people who couldn’t agree. Before long, more and more people crowded around Moses with their problems.

All day long the people lined up to see Moses. Jethro noticed Moses’ sagging shoulders and his tired eyes. Moses was completely exhausted.

Jethro cared for busy Moses. He could see that Moses had too much work to do. He would get too tired doing things this way.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jethro had a very good idea! First Moses must talk to God. God would help Moses know what to do. Yes, God is always the very best helper!

Then Moses should teach the people all about God’s ways. “Tell them the right way to live and what they should do,” Jethro said (Exodus 18:20, ICB).

With God’s help and His wisdom, there would be fewer problems. But Moses still needed helpers. Jethro told Moses to choose lots of good Israelite leaders.

These helpers could have the job of solving the simple problems. Moses could help with the hard problems. Together they would share the work.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses knew his father-in-law was wise. He listened to him and did what he said. Soon everything was better. There were no big lines of people waiting to tell Moses their problems. Moses wasn’t as worn out anymore. Instead, there were leaders all over the camp helping people.

Jethro went back home to Midian feeling very happy. ^[(Move Jethro back to his tent on background.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He had wanted the best for Moses and the people of Israel. That’s what good families want for each other. The Bible says, “Let all that you do be done with love” (1 Corinthians 16:14).
