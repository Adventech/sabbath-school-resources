The Israelites huddled together inside their homes as the night grew darker. Together they shared the special Passover meal by the flickering fire. They ate the roast lamb with the bitter herbs. Then they passed around the freshly baked flatbread for everyone to take a piece.

What were they wearing? **Read Exodus 12:11.**

They needed to be ready, at any second, to get up and leave Egypt forever. Soon they would be free! Their bags were packed. Their clothes were on. And they ate the Passover meal in a hurry.

This was no ordinary meal. God didn’t want them to ever forget it. “You are always to remember this day. Celebrate it with a feast to the Lord” (Exodus 12:14, ICB). Their families in the future were to always share the Passover together and remember how God had saved them.

Mothers and fathers cuddled their firstborn children close in their arms as the last piece of bread was eaten. Something very important was about to happen. They knew that this very night would be their last night as slaves in Egypt.

The night grew darker still. The angel was coming. But they trusted God. He would save them. The Bible says, “You are my hiding place; You shall preserve me from trouble” (Psalm 32:7).
