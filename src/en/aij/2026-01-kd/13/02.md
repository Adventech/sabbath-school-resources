Abram was worried. He _really_ wanted a son, but he still didn’t have one.

God saw that His friend was sad and worried. He wanted to encourage him, so one evening He spoke to Abram again.

God said, “Abram, don’t be afraid. I will defend you. And I will give you a great reward. . . . You will have a son of your own” (Genesis 15:1-4, ICB).

A son! Abram must have wanted to jump and clap–he was so happy!

God led Abram outside his tent. “Look at the sky,” God said. Abram looked up. The black sky was scattered with millions and millions of glittering stars. God kept talking: “There are so many stars you cannot count them. And your descendants [children, grandchildren] will be too many to count” (Genesis 15:5, ICB).

What happened next? **Read Genesis 15:6.**

Old Abram’s heart lit up with faith and hope in God’s promise. God would do the impossible! He and Sarai would have their own family! How exciting!

How kind of God to encourage Abram in such a special way.

God loves to encourage us with His words so we won’t worry. Try to say your memory verse. It will help you always remember that our God keeps His promises. “Know that the Lord your God, He is God, the faithful God” (Deuteronomy 7:9).
