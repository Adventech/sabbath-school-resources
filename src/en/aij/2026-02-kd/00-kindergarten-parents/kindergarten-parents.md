As you spend time with your child today, remember that God is with you. He is your heavenly Father and cares for you even more than you care for your growing child. Will you spend time with Him today, amidst the busy routines of life? Time with Him is time well spent.

So today, and in the days ahead, look for ways you can grow your own friendship with God. Here are some tips:

- Ask God to wake you early one morning. Go somewhere quiet and whisper aloud your prayer to God, asking Him to help you and bless your family. Read Ecclesiastes 3:1-15 and consider God’s words to you.
- Go for a walk in nature. Pray as you walk and notice the detail of God’s creation. Thank Him for caring for you more than the sparrow that falls (Matthew 10:31).
- In a journal or notebook, copy out Isaiah 55. Underline any words or ideas that stand out to you. Then, come back to those messages and think about them in relation to your life right now.

Talk with God about whatever is on your mind and heart. Tell Him that you trust Him and His plans for your future.

As you seek God more, you’ll naturally find that you want to lead your son or daughter to Him in a deeper way.

You’re in our prayers as you do this most important work,
