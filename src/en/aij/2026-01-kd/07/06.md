The people couldn’t believe what they were hearing. It seemed crazy! Noah, the big boat builder, was telling them that God was going to send a flood. They had never even seen rain. _Water falling in big drops from the sky? Water covering all the land in the whole world? No way!_ The people shook their heads and laughed.

For 120 years Noah and his family built the ark. And for 120 years Noah preached to the people. He warned them of the coming flood, and told them about the safe place on the boat. But nobody believed Noah. Not a single one.

Finally the ark was ready. One last time Noah begged the people to come inside the ark and be safe. God loved His people “with a love that will last forever” (Jeremiah 31:3, ICB). But the people didn’t love God. They refused to listen. Instead they just teased Noah and joked about the Flood. They had made their choice. It made God and Noah so sad.

Then God said something to Noah. What was it? **Read Genesis 7:1.**

The big door on the side of the boat was wide open. It was time to go into the ark. God would save Noah and his family, just as He had promised. Jesus promises to save us too when He comes back to take us to heaven from this sinful world.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Noah trusted in God’s faithfulness, even when everyone else didn’t. How can you show that you trust God, as Noah did?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for His faithfulness and love that lasts forever.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Build your own big boat out of chairs and sheets. Pretend to be Noah preaching. What might you say?

^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
_“Well done, good and faithful servant; you were faithful over a few things, I will make you ruler over many things” (Matthew 25:21)._

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#B4B5B4", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#FFFFFF"}}}
^[Family Journal]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})\
Have your child draw the outline of a big boat in your journal. Inside the boat, have an adult write the things that Adam, Seth, Enoch, and Noah did to be God’s faithful followers. Pray for God to help you be a faithful follower too.\
\
^[For You, Parents]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})\
In these last days God is calling you to build an “ark of faith” and to work for the salvation of your family. Spend time with Jesus in His Word daily. Pray over your children. “If parents have the love of God in their hearts, they will be able to bind their children to them by the tender ties of affection. It is in this way that you may be missionaries for God, you may be home missionaries” (Ellen G. White, in _Signs of the Times_, July 22, 1889).
