Moses stood at the door of his tent and looked out at God’s glowing home. He had grown up in the shiny palaces of Pharaoh. His home had been filled with gold and jewels. But nothing was as beautiful as seeing God’s bright cloud over His tent. God was so close.

Moses could remember the first time he had seen God’s brightness. Moses had been right here, near this very mountain, Mount Sinai.

The sheep had clip-clopped behind him as he had led them along the dusty path. Suddenly he had seen something bright up ahead. He had moved closer for a better look. There was a glowing bush covered in flickering flames. It was burning, but it wasn’t burning up!

A voice called out to him from the burning bush, “Moses, Moses!” (Exodus 3:4).

God was in the fire! Do you remember what He asked Moses to do? **Read Exodus 3:5.**

God had seen how sad and hurt His people were in Egypt. He had chosen Moses to be their leader. God would set His people free.

“I will be with you,” God said to Moses. “You will lead the people out of Egypt. Then all of you will worship me on this mountain” (Exodus 3:12, ICB).

That’s just what happened! Here they were, worshipping God in His new sanctuary at Mount Sinai. The Bible says, “Remember His marvelous works which He has done” (1 Chronicles 16:12).
