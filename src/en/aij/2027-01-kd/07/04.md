Little drops of dew rested on the dry ground each morning. As the warm sun rose, they melted away. Left behind on the ground was the manna. It looked like a white carpet all over camp. In this hot desert, where no food grew, the Israelites had yummy bread. God had provided all the healthy food they needed.

But some troublemakers wanted different food. “We want meat!” they said.

Soon everyone was complaining! “We remember the fish we ate for free in Egypt. We also had cucumbers, melons, leeks, onions, and garlic. But now we have lost our appetite. We never see anything but this manna” (Numbers 11:5, 6, ICB).

What did the people do next, and how did it make Moses and God feel? **Read Numbers 11:10.**

“Tell the people this,” God said to Moses. “Tomorrow you will eat meat. . . . You will eat that meat for a whole month. You will eat it until it comes out of your nose. You will hate it” (Numbers 11:18-20, ICB).

God chose to provide them with the food they wanted. But they would also suffer the consequences of their grumbling and greediness. Their choice not to follow God’s way would make them feel sick.

We can trust that God’s way is best. He provides all you need. God, “You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing” (Psalm 145:16).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Would the Israelites trust God’s way in the future?
- Will you trust God to provide what you need?
