It is Sabbath today—how wonderful! Today is a special time to celebrate and remember how wonderful God is and remember all He has created and given us.

Not everyone knows about God. They may not know that He is love and that His way is best. Maybe today you could share God’s love with the people who live around you.

**^[Activity:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg", "color": "#834A27"}}})** Go for a Sabbath chalk walk. Use chalk to write messages on a road that point people to God. (Or simply trace the messages on a dirt path.) Before you go on your chalk walk, you might like to learn and sing the chorus of the hymn “Trust and Obey” (The Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal, no. 590). While you’re out walking, you might hum it or keep singing along.
