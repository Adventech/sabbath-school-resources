title: Discussion Questions
subtitle: Chapter 8
---

`1. Have you ever been in a vineyard or grown grapes? If so, what did you notice?`

`2. Reread John 15:1-16. What most stands out to you from Jesus’ words in this passage? Is there something new?`

`3. Reflect on the questions on page 97 (Accept and Act). What is your honest response?`

`4. Has there been a time in your life when you have resisted the Holy Spirit? Has there been a time in your life when you may have looked like you’re abiding but you’ve actually been quite dried up, spiritually? What did you do to “grow” again?`

`5. How can we take encouragement from the fact that God prunes us all, at times in our lives?`
