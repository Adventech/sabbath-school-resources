[[Revelation 4:1-3]]

- The One on the throne is God the Father, ruler of all things.

[[Revelation 4:4]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- These may be 24 angels or, possibly, human beings redeemed from the earth who ascended to heaven with Jesus after His death and resurrection in A.D. 31 (see Ephesians 4:8). Note that they have received many of the rewards promised to those in the seven churches who overcome (verse 4).

[[Revelation 4:5-8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The four living creatures are an order of angels nearest the throne who constantly declare who God is.

[[Revelation 4:9-11]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- God’s right to rule all is based on the reality that He is the Creator of all.
- To say that God is the Creator is the most important thing we can say about Him.
- His Creator status will play an extremely important role as we near the heart of this book, as well as at the end of time.
