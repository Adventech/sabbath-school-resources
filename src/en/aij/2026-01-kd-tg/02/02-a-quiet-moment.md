**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Can you imagine all of heaven leaning in, keen to observe the detailed beauty in our emerging little world? Can you imagine the delight, the joy, the wonder, as they saw creation unfold?

“All heaven took a deep and joyful interest in the creation of the world and of man. Human beings were a new and distinct order. They were made ‘in the image of God.’ . . . They were to live in close communion with heaven, receiving power from the Source of all power” (Ellen G. White, in _Review and Herald_, February 11, 1902).

After sculpting man—complete with bones, DNA, and cells—God breathed life into him. What a beautiful moment it was for Adam to have God’s precious face up close and personal, as the very first thing that he saw. Here was love—pure, extravagant, and eternal. Adam had been made by love, for love. And so have you!

As you and the children unpack the sixth day of Creation together, may you bask in the love of our Creator God, and may you praise Him for how “fearfully and wonderfully” you have been made in His image (Psalm 139:14).

“‘God is love’ is written upon every opening bud, upon every spire of springing grass. . . . God has bound our hearts to Him by unnumbered tokens in heaven and in earth. Through the things of nature, and the deepest and tenderest earthly ties that human hearts can know, He has sought to reveal Himself to us” (Ellen G. White, _Steps to Christ_, p. 10).
