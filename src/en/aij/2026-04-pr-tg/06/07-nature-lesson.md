**Focus:** Just as the ants persist in taking food back to their nest, Jesus wants us to persist in reaching out to Him.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/06/nature-lesson.png)

The humble ant. We see them everywhere, scurrying about their day, always looking busy, knowing exactly what they are doing and where they are going. Have you ever stopped to watch and wondered, Where are they going? What are they doing?

Ants deserve our attention! They are amazing creatures, and there are more than 20 quadrillion of them on earth. That’s a whopping 2.5 million ants for every person! There are more than 12,000 ant species worldwide, and they are found on every continent on earth except Antarctica. Did you know an ant can lift 20 times its body weight? That is like a 200-pound (91-kilogram) man being able to lift a small car above his head! Isn’t that amazing?

A quality we can learn from the ant is persistence. Ants keep trying even when things are challenging, and they don’t give up. You may have noticed when out on a picnic that ants work together to move large pieces of food and crumbs from off the picnic blanket, through the grass, and into their nest. Even though they are so small, they persist and help each other with the task. They may need to move around and over obstacles, but they don’t give up until they have accomplished their goal. It takes time, but they continue until the job is done.

Just as the ants persist, we too need to persist. The most important thing we need to persist in doing is spending time with Jesus. Sometimes there will be obstacles in our way: we sleep in and rush out the door without spending time with Him. We may have too much homework or get busy playing our favorite sport and miss out on our time with Jesus. We can persist through these obstacles and remember that He is there for us and is just waiting for us to reach out to Him. He loves to see us persist in getting close to Him.
