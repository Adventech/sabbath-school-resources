Ruth’s life was not easy. She lost her husband, her family, and her cultural identity, and she moved to a foreign place. She was poor and had to work hard to get life’s basic needs. There were many times she could have given up, gone home, or abandoned Naomi and her God. But she was faithful.

Life is often filled with big, hard questions. _Why is this situation so hard? Why does it feel as if God is not providing in the way I need Him to? Why do I always feel as if I’m facing an uphill battle?_

Ruth could look back and see God’s provision. He cared wonderfully for her and recognized her faithfulness. He promises to do the same for you too. “Are not five sparrows sold for two copper coins? And not one of them is forgotten before God. But the very hairs of your head are all numbered. Do not fear therefore; you are of more value than many sparrows” (Luke 12:6, 7).

Whenever we feel anxious, we can trust that God will provide. Like Ruth, we should also do what we can to work hard in whatever situation we are in. While you might not know what tomorrow will bring, know that God sees all and offers peace that surpasses all understanding.
