A cow mooed and a sheep bleated as Joseph opened the creaky door and shone the lantern into the stable. Finally they had found a place to stay—in a stable. It wouldn’t be a nice place to sleep, but Joseph and Mary appreciated a roof over their heads. They quietly made a comfortable bed on a pile of clean straw among the animals. They were content, for they trusted God.

Lying on that bed of straw, Mary may have remembered what the angel Gabriel had told her about the baby she would soon give birth to. “He will be great, and will be called the Son of the Highest; and the Lord God will give Him the throne of His father David” (Luke 1:32). Even though Mary trusted God, she may have wondered why someone so important would be born where the animals lived, instead of in a palace fit for the King of kings.

But even this was part of God’s plan. If Jesus had been born in a palace, some people may have accepted Him only because of His noble place of birth, and others may have felt they were not worthy of God’s gift. God wanted people to know, right from the birth of Jesus, that the precious gift of His Son was for everyone, no matter who they were or where they came from. God’s gift is for you too!

Like Mary and Joseph, you can still trust God when things don’t go according to your plans. There are many things we can’t control, but we can always trust God to work through all our circumstances. In Philippians 4:11 Paul writes, “I have learned to be satisfied with the things I have and with everything that happens” (ICB).

God loves you dearly, and you have the precious gift of His Son. He can help you to appreciate and be satisfied with the things you have.
