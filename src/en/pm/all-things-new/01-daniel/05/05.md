- Belshazzar had no doubt heard all the stories of Nebuchadnezzar and his respect for the most high God, but chose not to reign with justice, goodness, or love. As a result, the Babylonian kingdom was destroyed.
- God is ultimately sovereign. He is patient with those who rule over various nations today, and blesses those who govern with justice, goodness, and love.
- But when a government continually oppresses others, eventually God’s patience will run out, just as it did with Belshazzar.
- If you are oppressed, remember the prophecy of Daniel 2 and put your trust in God. Justice is coming! The end of all things and the beginning of God’s everlasting kingdom of justice, goodness, and love is nearly here.
