Imagine if your outreach methods had no walls, borders, or limits to stop them from reaching billions of people. Adventist World Radio’s (AWR) broadcasts travel across the airwaves to places where missionaries cannot go, transforming lives for eternity.

Radio overcomes the barriers of government restrictions, cultural opposition, illiteracy, and geography. And radios are one of the cheapest forms of technology, accessible to most of the world’s population.

AWR broadcasts the Advent hope in Christ to the people of the world in their own languages. Producers in local studios create programs in more than 100 “heart languages”—the mother tongues that touch listeners’ hearts most profoundly. More languages are added as producers and airtime funding become available.

Many studios also operate Bible correspondence schools, through which they can communicate God’s beautiful messages with listeners. AWR receives more than 100,000 letters, phone calls, texts, and email messages from listeners yearly.

AWR’s goal is to reach every person with the saving message of God’s love through programs aired in over 200 critically important languages spoken in the world. This goal can become a reality through your financial partnership.
