**Paper Plate Honoring Crown**

**What you need:**

Extra-large paper plates, scissors, markers, craft glue, sticker jewels or printable jewels and shapes cut out of paper or contact paper (1).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Lightly fold paper plate in half and cut a line within the border (2).
- Fold the other way and cut another line (2).
- Fold all four pieces out to create the four crown points (3).
- Cut out small shapes from colored paper or contact paper.
- Print jewels to color from the AIJ website, if using.

**Create it together:**

- Stick the four jewels to each other’s crown while saying something kind to each other (4).
- Continue decorating the crown with markers and shapes.
