**Opening Activities**

Source **small boxes**, **wrapping paper**, **scissors**, **tape**, **stickers**, and **ribbons**. Print **memory verse cards on cardstock**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: **paper** or **plastic cups** (one per child) into the box, **wedding wrapping paper**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props:** ribbon sticks (used in Year A, Quarter 2), a fancy jug or pitcher, six mason jars (or other clear jars), an empty glass, cups from Secret Surprise Box, grape juice, a large container with water (enough to pour into each of the six jars), red and blue food coloring (place in the bottom of the glass).

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a set of **wooden blocks**.
