---
title: Protecting His Fingerprints
date: 21/03/2026
---

Presidents, prime ministers, and politicians encourage us to care for the environment, but shouldn’t Christians be exempt?

The Bible clearly and repeatedly tells us that this old world will soon pass away and that Earth 2.0 (the new and final version) will take its place. Surely, that must mean that we can care less about the environment.

It is true that you and I are called to be faithful stewards—managers and carers—of the time, talents, and treasures that God entrusts to us, and there’s great joy in doing so. However, an additional beauty of our lives is that we were also appointed stewards of all God’s creation.

Genesis 1:26 says, “God said, ‘Let us make human beings in our image, to be like us. They will reign over the fish in the sea, the birds in the sky, the livestock, all the wild animals on the earth, and the small animals that scurry along the ground’” (NLT).

So, we were appointed to be stewards of all creation—before we were even created!

What a privilege it is to care for an environment so thoroughly covered with God’s beautiful fingerprints. Yes, it is decaying rapidly, often because we don’t protect it as faithfully as we should, but it’s still the most beautiful thing this side of the ozone layer!

God’s signature is written across the entire earth. Let’s be faithful stewards of everything, including His creation.

### Appeal

Through our return of God’s tithe and the giving of our regular and systematic Promise offerings, God reminds us that we are stewards of everything He has given to us. Let’s ask Him for a greater sense of compassion for His creation and a real desire to properly care for what is left on this earth until Jesus returns. Thank you for your faithfulgiving today.
