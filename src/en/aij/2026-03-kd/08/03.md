Andrew and John couldn’t stop smiling as they sat and talked with Jesus. He was so wonderful and wise. His words were full of beauty and truth.

The day sped by so fast. Finally Andrew and John left the place where Jesus was staying. Their hearts overflowed with joy. They had listened to Jesus’ words, and they really wanted to be His followers.

Andrew couldn’t wait to tell his brother, Simon, all about Jesus too.

Racing down the street, Andrew felt his heart pump hard and fast. Puffing and panting, Andrew finally stood in front of his brother.

“We have found the Messiah,” he told Simon excitedly (John 1:41).

Simon didn’t waste a second. He wanted to meet Jesus—and he wanted to meet Him now! Together the brothers rushed to find Jesus. Jesus looked kindly at Simon. What did He say? **Read John 1:42.**

How amazing! Jesus already knew Simon’s name and his father’s name. And now Jesus had a new name for Simon too! He would now be called Peter, which means stone. Jesus wanted Peter to be His follower too.

Jesus knows your name too. He knows all about you, and He can’t wait to spend time with you. He says, “My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me” (John 10:27).
