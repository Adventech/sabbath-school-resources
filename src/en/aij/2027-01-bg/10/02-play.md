Retell

Do some water painting with your child as you retell the story in your own words. On a large patch of concrete, use a paintbrush and water to paint a big boat on land, then paint a cloud and flick some drops of water underneath for rain. Carefully pour some water under the ark to show how the water came up from the ground. Then paint some big waves around the boat with an angel above it.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#72c1c8"}}}
Play

Use props to act out the story during bathtime. Put some plastic animals and/or people into a toy boat or plastic bowl. Use a spray bottle for rain. Ask your child to help make waves by splashing the water while you hold on to the boat, as the angels did. Your child might want to have a turn telling the story too!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#72c1c8"}}}
Connect

Set up a play tent or make a cubby house in your child’s room. Make it comfortable and cozy with blankets and soft animal toys. Pretend that the tent or cubby house is the ark, and you need to go inside before the big storm comes! Use your phone to play rain/storm sounds while you snuggle up together. You might like to read the story in there before bed each night.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#72c1c8"}}}
Pray

Before you put your child to bed each night this week, sing the hymn “All Night, All Day,” by Otis L. McCoy. Then pray with them and thank God for sending His angels to watch over your child, just as they watched over Noah’s family.

All night, all day,\
Angels watching over me, my Lord.\
All night, all day,\
Angels watching over me.
