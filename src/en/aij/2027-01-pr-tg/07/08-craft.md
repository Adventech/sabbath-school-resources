**What you need:**

Week 7 craft template, scissors, glue, white chalk pen/marker.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-pr-craft-07.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the diorama template. Cut along the two vertical lines next to the glue tabs.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7b.jpg)

2\. Fold inward along all the lines. Glue the white glue tabs under the base of the diorama so it can stand up.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7c.jpg)

3\. Cut out the people figurines and fold the white tabs at the bottom.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7d.jpg)

4\. Glue the figurines onto the base of the diorama so they stand up.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7e.jpg)

5\. Use a white chalk pen/marker to dot the desert ground with manna. Place your diorama on display to remind you of the story.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7f.jpg)
