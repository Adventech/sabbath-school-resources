{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After God led the Israelites out of Egypt and through the Red Sea, they twice found themselves without water. When their water ran out, they complained bitterly, forgetting God’s miracles. At Marah God made the bitter water sweet after Moses obeyed His instructions. Later at Rephidim the Israelites again grumbled. Moses turned to God, who brought water from a rock. Each time, God provided for their needs, teaching His people to trust Him, be content, and live with gratitude.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that even in challenging times, they can trust God to provide for all their needs.
- **Feel** content and grateful for His care.
- **Respond** by developing a habit of gratitude instead of complaining.
