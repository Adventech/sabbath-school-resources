---
title: Knowing and Experiencing Our God
---

God has a passionate desire to have an intimate love relationship with us. This is the most amazing reality in this universe. Jesus prayed to His Father just before He risked everything, giving up even His own life for us, “this is eternal life, that they may know You, the only true God, and Jesus Christ whom You have sent.” John 17:3. The great apostle Paul said, “I also count all things loss for the excellence of the knowledge of Christ Jesus, my Lord...that I may know Him and the power of His resurrection...” Phil 3:8,10.

We have often struggled like most of you over the years in knowing how to have this close love relationship with our Lord. The materials in this compilation are some of the most helpful, practical counsels and deep insights we have found over the years to strengthen us and many others in this exciting life with Jesus!

The Book of Acts shares story after story of the amazing miracles of God, as the first Christians let Him totally lead in their lives. He led them into spiritual activities which changed them, and then the world. They gathered for earnest prayer, studying the Scriptures, self-examination, repentance, and confession, leading to a holy unity. It resulted in real humility, joyful praise and thanksgiving, and a clear focus on witnessing and Jesus’ mission. The Holy Spirit came upon them, and the results were unbelievable! As we approach the Second Coming of Jesus, we need that same upper room experience in our lives and churches today! We need the promised outpouring of the Holy Spirit’s power (Acts 1:8)

We hope that in these resources you may find help and encouragement in your walk with Jesus. However, we do not want you to think that there is some “right” way to pray or do all of these activities and become overwhelmed, feeling you will never do it right or be good enough at prayer and praise.

We love these statements in Steps to Christ: “Prayer is the opening of the heart to God as to a friend.” (SC, p.93); “Keep your wants, your joys, your sorrows, your cares, and your fears before God. You cannot burden Him; you cannot weary Him [...] His heart of love is touched by our sorrows and even by our utterances of them [...] The relations between God and each soul are as distinct and full as though there were not another soul upon the earth to share His watchcare, not another soul for whom He gave His beloved Son.” (SC, p.100)

Spend time with our Lord daily, asking Him to guide you into the disciplines and understandings you need most along your spiritual journey. Then enjoy Him as we get ready to be with Him forever!

We pray that the Lord will continue to guide you daily as you abide in him. We claim for you this promise of Jesus in John 15:7: “If you abide in Me and my words abide in you, you will ask what you desire, and it shall be done for you. By this My Father is glorified that you bear much fruit, so you will be my disciple.”

_Jerry and Janet Page_
