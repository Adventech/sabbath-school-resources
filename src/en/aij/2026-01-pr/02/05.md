The Bible says that “the man gave names to all the tame animals, to the birds in the sky and to all the wild animals. But Adam did not find a helper that was right for him” (Genesis 2:20, ICB). Adam must have wondered: Why isn’t there someone just like me?

God had the answer already planned. What was God’s surprise, and how did it come to be? **Read Genesis 2:21, 22.**

Once again, God used His own hands to create someone in His image (recite your memory verse).

First, He let Adam fall into a peaceful sleep. Then He carefully opened up Adam’s side, took out a rib, and gently molded it into a female. She was like Adam, but different. God had designed them to fit together always.

God took Eve to Adam, woke him up, then stood back to watch. Love must have filled Adam’s heart as he looked into her smiling eyes. What a beautiful surprise God had made, just for him. He welcomed her to the world as his very own special friend—someone who could talk with him, laugh with him, and understand his heart.

Adam couldn’t talk with the animals, but he could always talk with Eve. Words are one of God’s gifts to us because we are made in His image. Talking with people helps us get to know, understand, and love them. Kind words from a friend can drive away loneliness and help us feel close, just as Adam and Eve felt. What does the Bible say about kind words? **Read Proverbs 16:24.** The next time you eat honey or something sweet, think about how kind words can make us happy and healthy.

God made Adam and Eve to be special friends who could talk and be together. They were designed in His image, to each be special as man and woman, but to fit together in love.
