Little Samuel stretched his arms up high _(stretch)_. Then he stepped out of bed with a smile. It was a new day and there was lots to do! Samuel lived in God’s house, the tabernacle. He helped Priest Eli. Samuel made his little bed smooth and tidy. He folded his night clothes and put them away in his little box. He got dressed in the special robe His mother had made for him. He was ready for the day!\
Samuel had helpful hands _(hold out your hands)_. Every day he helped Priest Eli with his work in God’s house. Samuel loved helping. It made his heart happy to know that every little job he did was helping Eli. And every little job he did was helping God!
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/02/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block": {"backgroundColor": "#986F52ad"}}}
Swish, swish, swish went the broom _(pretend to sweep)_. Samuel’s job was to make sure everything was neat and clean. He used his little broom to sweep away the leaves the wind might blow into God’s house. Samuel was very careful with his work. He didn’t rush or miss things. He made sure he finished his jobs well.
Rub, rub, rub went little Samuel’s cloth _(pretend to polish)_. He used his cloth to polish the beautiful things used in God’s house. He rubbed them until they sparkled. It made little Samuel’s heart happy to know that He was helping God with so many different jobs. Whatever Samuel did, he did his very best work.\
“And the boy Samuel grew up serving the Lord” (1 Samuel 2:21, ICB).

Look at your hands. Did you know you can have helpful hands, as Samuel had? You can help your family by setting the table, or by putting the clothes away, or by making your bed. When you use your hands to help others, you are really helping God too! It makes Him smile when He sees us help each other and do our best for Him.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for little Samuel’s helpful hands. I want to be helpful to my family too.
