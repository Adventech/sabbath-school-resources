Share that today we will learn about Joseph, who needed to be brave in the dark. Having God with him was like having a light and a friend.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **stickers** and a **blindfold**. Share that the stickers are a reward for being brave.

- **What do you think it means to be brave?** (To do something hard when you are afraid.)

Invite the children to stand up, then help them get into pairs. Explain that they will do a jumping challenge—blindfolded! One child will be blindfolded while the other helps him/her to jump between three or four markers. Spread them out so that it’s a challenge but safe. Then the children will swap so that everyone has an opportunity to be brave and “jump.”

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Once the children have returned to their seats, give them each a sticker and affirm them for being brave. Discuss:

- **How did you feel when you were blindfolded?**
- **What helped you feel brave to take each leap?** (Having a buddy help you.)
- **How did you feel at the end?**

Explain that there are times in our life we need to be really brave! God promises to always be with us through the things that make us feel afraid. You trusted your buddy to help you in the jumping challenge. God is a friend you can always trust—no matter what!

Say together the **Big Idea: God is with me.** I can be brave. Our Bible story today is about Joseph. God helped him to be brave when things were really hard.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal some of the story prop items: **colorful coat/cloth, flashlight, spices, coins**. Name the items as you place them in a row in front of the children. Give each child a colorful ribbon from last week.

Place a piece of **fabric** over the top to hide the items. Ask the children to recall an item and what it reminds them of in today’s story about brave Joseph. As you take an item out, celebrate each answer by inviting children to wave their **colorful ribbons**.

**Teaching Point:** Even though we couldn’t see the items underneath the cloth, we knew they were still there! It is the same with our wonderful, loving God. We can’t see Him, but we know He’s always there helping us to be brave, just as He helped Joseph. Repeat the **Big Idea** again together: **God is with me. I can be brave.**
