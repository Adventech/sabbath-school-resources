---
title: Thinking It Over
---

When the superintendent is free after the Sabbath services, he or she would do well to sit down and take a quiet mental survey of the Sabbath School for that day. He or she should review the plans for the school, not ing the success of each and scanning the possible reason for any failure. The superintendent should seek privately the counsel and criticism of trusted friends and be willing to take counsel, but not to be persuaded to try out novel plans and methods against good judgment.
