**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** **Warmly welcome** each child by name as they arrive. Take an interest in their lives by chatting about the things that interest them (in preparation for a memory verse discussion).

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to play a version of the statue game with turn-taking. While the song **“It’s About Love”** plays, children walk around the room. When the music stops, everyone freezes like a statue. Then, with the music off, choose one child to do something fun—such as pretending to be an animal for others to guess. Before they do this, they should choose another child and say, “You go first.” That child will do their action first, followed by the first child, thus showing the importance of letting others go first. Then start the music again and repeat.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal the masking tape. Place two parallel lines of tape on the floor (five steps apart) for a **“You First” game**. One line will be the start line; the other the finish line. Explain that the goal is to get from one line to the other. Have the children stand shoulder-to- shoulder behind the start line.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** When you call a child’s name with a direction (for example, “Mario, take one step [or take two jumps]”), that child must first turn to the person next to them and say, “You first.” After the other person moves, then that child can move. If that child forgets, they must go back to the start line. Continue to call out simple instructions for different children.

- **How hard was it to let others go first, knowing that they might beat you to the finish line?**
- **How did it feel to be asked to go first by a friend?**

When you let others go first, you are honoring them. You think of them before yourself.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s story is about a man who honored other people; he thought of them before he thought of himself. He will help teach us our **Big Idea**. Stand and say it together: **God is loving. I will put others first.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a balloon, a container of confetti, and a piece of paper.

Place the piece of paper on a hard surface and spread the confetti on top. Blow up the balloon and rub it on your hair to develop static. Hold it several inches from the confetti for the children to see what will happen.

- **What happened to the confetti?** (The confetti will be attracted to static on the balloon.)
- **Why were people attracted to Abram?** (He was friends with God, was kind, and put others first.)

**Teaching Point:** Just as the balloon attracted the confetti, people were attracted to Abram because he was kind and put others first. When we treat others with kindness, they may want to know about our kind God too.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is Loving. I will put others first.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 11: African Cichlid Fish** and share the following facts:

- These clever little fish make homes out of rocks and shells.
- They have beautiful, rich colors of reds, blues, and yellows.
- The mother carries her baby fish eggs around in her mouth until they hatch.
- Sometimes the mother fish will go without food for days to keep her baby eggs safe, hiding them in her mouth. She puts them before her own needs.

Thank You, God, for teaching us through Your nature!

Give each child **Nature Card 11** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
