The beautiful dream of seeing Jesus kept coming back to her. Ellen smiled as she remembered His look of mercy. She knew without a doubt that Jesus loved her!

The dream had come right when the young girl needed it most. Ellen Harmon, who would one day become Ellen White, a founder and leader in our church, had felt alone and deeply discouraged. Suffering from pain and often unwell, she had thought that God was harsh and punishing. Ellen didn’t believe that she was good enough for God. But God had shown her a true vision of Jesus, her Friend and Savior. His love and kindness changed her instantly.

“My heart was so filled with peace and love to God.” It was so different to the gloom she’d felt before this time.

Ellen was shy, but she wrote about how she couldn’t keep what she knew about the true God to herself! She wanted to tell everyone the story of Jesus’ love. She especially wanted to help anyone who felt sad or discouraged by the heavy burden of sin. She thought about her friends. Oh, how she wanted them to know God!

It wasn’t always easy, but Ellen did everything she could to be a good friend and share Jesus with her friends. She organized special times together with them. Some of her friends were vain and didn’t think about others, and when they heard Ellen talk about God, they said it sounded like a fairytale, and they didn’t really want to listen.

Ellen really wanted her friends to know God. She spent a few nights awake all night, praying for them. They kept meeting together for many months until every one of them had given their hearts to Jesus.

Friends who love God are the closest kind of human friends we can have. When God is in our hearts, we share deeper and richer friendships because we want to live His way and encourage one another.
