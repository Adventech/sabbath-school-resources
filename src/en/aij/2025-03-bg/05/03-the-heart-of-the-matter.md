How easy it is to look back in disbelief on the Israelites’ journey and wonder at how they could be so quick to forget God’s leading. They could see Him in fire and cloud, and in the morning miracle of manna. But still they complained and doubted that He would provide.

Surely we would not be like that! And yet we too forget just who God is: love. We forget that the God who loved us yesterday will provide for us today, tomorrow, and always. We forget to fill our hearts with gratitude and praise, and let God turn that into trust.

When we forget to look to God, our focus falls instead on our problems, our to-do list, want list, pressures, and pain. And yes, we too complain! On this troubled soil doubt and distrust grow. “Why should we be ungrateful and distrustful? Jesus is our friend; all heaven is interested in our welfare; and our anxiety and fear grieve the Holy Spirit of God” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 294).

God is an ever-present Father, always faithful and kind. He does not forget us, and He provides the things we need. He forgives our complaining hearts, and lovingly guides us as we journey to the Promised Land. He says, “Come to Me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest. Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and lowly in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For My yoke is easy and My burden is light” (Matthew 11:28-30).
