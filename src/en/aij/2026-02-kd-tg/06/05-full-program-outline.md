**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **small wrapped present** with a bow. Don’t open it yet. Show the children the present and invite them to pass it around, guessing what might be inside.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What’s a special gift you’ve received?**
- **How does it make you feel when you’re given a gift?**
- **What type of gifts do you think God gives?**

Explain that God cares so much for us all. He knows what we need, and loves to give us good gifts.

Invite a child to open the present and show the children the silver stars inside.

- **I wonder what the stars are for in the story today. Can anyone guess?**

Affirm their answers. Share that our Bible story today is about Joseph. Things were not always easy for Joseph, but God cared. He showed His care in many ways. He gave Joseph two special dreams. That’s an unusual present! Let’s find out all about this story.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **colorful ribbons** (seven colors).

Give a colorful ribbon stick to each child.

Look at all these beautiful colors! Each one can remind us of how God cares for us, just as God cared for Joseph in our story today. **Can you find each color and repeat after me?**

- Red: God cares about my heart.
- Orange: God cares about my friends and family.
- Yellow: God cares about my happiness.
- Green: God cares about my food.
- Blue: God cares about me being healthy.
- Pink: God cares about my dreams.
- Purple: God cares about me!

**Teaching Point:** You can always ask God to help you if you feel worried or upset. He loves you so much and cares about every part of your life—how you feel and what you do. He loves to look after you—to care for you.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He cares for me. Invite the children to wave their colorful ribbon sticks in the air and say together, “God cares for me, and I love God!”**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold Up **Nature Card 19: Rainbow Lorikeet** and share the following facts:

- Rainbow Lorikeets are a type of parrot with beautiful rainbow-colored feathers.
- Both boy and girl Lorikeets have the same amazing colors and live together in flocks.
- They can fly very fast and are very loud! They like to happily screech as they search for food.
- Rainbow Lorikeets eat fruit, nectar, and flowers.
- They have a special tongue to gather pollen that looks like a little paintbrush.
- God made this beautiful bird with lots of rainbow colors! It can remind us of Joseph’s special coat in our story today.

Give each child **Nature Card 19: Rainbow Lorikeet** for them to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the **Mission Story**, “Special Gift From God,” about a mother’s prayer for her son.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Make **Joseph’s Colorful Coat**. To remind the children of the story they have learned today, help them make a picture of Joseph’s coat. Play some worship music in the background.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})**

- **What have you learned today?** (Can they think of something for every colored ribbon on their stick? Allow time for answers.)

Affirm their answers. Share that God cares about us. He cares for us when we are sad, when others are mean to us, and when we can’t understand the things that happen. We can take all our worries to Him. He loves us and can help us!

Like Joseph, let’s give our cares to God and trust Him to care for us.
