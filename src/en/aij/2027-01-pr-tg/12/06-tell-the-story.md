**Big Idea:** God wants to be very close to His people.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use the following props: **sanctuary felt board or pictures of items, toy tools—hammer/saw, cotton reels, woven tapestry, white tablecloth or sheet, tabernacle plan.**
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Children skipped and zigzagged through the maze of tents with their families as they made their way toward the meeting point in the Israelite camp at the foot of Mount Sinai. God had given Moses another special message for His people.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What had God said? Read Exodus 25:8, your memory verse** (make a sanctuary so He could live with them).

He gave Moses detailed plans, and the people must follow these plans carefully. It would be a movable tent church called a tabernacle that could be packed up and taken with them every time they moved camp.

God was always with the Israelites in the cloud, and He says to us, “I am with you always” (Matthew 28:20). But the sanctuary was to be a special place where the people could worship God, and learn more about His love and His awesome plan to send the promised Rescuer, Jesus.

It would be a beautiful sanctuary for God. He had designed every piece of furniture and decided every detail. They would need gold and silver, brass and precious stones, acacia wood, spices and oil, animal skins, fine linen, and colored thread to make the sanctuary.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Where would these materials come from? Read Exodus 25:2.** (The people were to give them willingly.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people hurried back to their tents to bundle up their treasures. They were glad they had so many beautiful gifts that the Egyptians had given them the night they had left Egypt.

Soon there were piles and piles of gold and silver earrings, bracelets, necklaces and vessels, baskets of fine linen and goat’s hair, and rolls of animal skins. They were so happy to give to God that there was soon more than enough.

Excitedly Moses told the people that God had chosen Bezalel and Aholiab to be in charge of work teams. God had given these men special skills and wisdom. God blessed other workers with special skills too.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Bezalel and Aholiab got themselves organized, and very soon the whole camp was filled with the buzz of busywork. The men in Bezalel’s work teams swung axes, sawed long planks of acacia wood, and melted silver dishes over a scorching fire ^[(toy tools—hammer or saw)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Still others worked with gold or polished precious stones.

In Aholiab’s work teams people spun fine gold, blue, purple, and scarlet thread ^[(cotton reels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Other workers used the fine thread to make beautiful colored cloth for the tabernacle curtains ^[(woven tapestry)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Another group of workers spun white linen thread that was woven into white cloth ^[(white tablecloth or sheet)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). This would be used to make the fence that would surround God’s tabernacle.

Runners fetched and carried or swept up wood shavings and timber offcuts. Back in the tents, cooks whipped up lunch for the workers. All those busy people were following God’s wise advice.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What advice were they following? Read Colossians 3:23.** (Work as if you’re working for the Lord, not for people.)

The tabernacle was to have two rooms: the Holy Place, where the priests would work, and the Most Holy Place, where God would be.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) All the furniture inside was very special: the table of showbread ^[(picture or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), a beautiful golden lampstand ^[(picture or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and the altar of incense ^[(picture or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Aaron and his sons were chosen to be the priests. The Levites, one of the Israelite tribes, were chosen to take care of the tabernacle.

After many months, all the different parts of God’s sanctuary were finished!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did God say to Moses? Read Exodus 40:1, 2.** (Set the tabernacle up.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses was so excited to obey! He put all the beautiful pieces together just as God had said. God’s beautiful, holy home shone in the sunshine. ^[(Show the picture and point out the different elements, but don’t go into detail—this will be done next week.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Suddenly, movement on Mount Sinai caught their eye. The pillar of cloud was moving! The people watched and held their breath as the cloud floated slowly until it hovered right above the tabernacle. It silently wrapped itself around the tent church as it shone with God’s dazzling glory. Then it filled the tabernacle and rested peacefully on the mercy seat between the golden angels on the ark in the Most Holy Place ^[(picture or felt)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The people looked on with wonder and reverent silence. Their loving God had come to live with them. They brushed away their tears and thanked God that He loved them this much.
