Lucifer’s selfish thoughts took root e grew like a weed. Quando the Anjos loved seu music, Lucifer’s pride grew. Quando ele saw Jesus e Deus the Father talking together, jealousy grew e começou a strangle the amor em seu heart.

Deus the Father e Seu Son, Jesus, loved Lucifer então much that eles tried a stop seu poisonous, jealous thoughts. Jesus talked com Lucifer muitos times e showed ele como kind e fair Deus’s government was. Deus’s law was todos sobre amor e selflessness.

Lucifer knew Jesus was right e ele was wrong. Ele had gossiped e told lies. Ele had been selfish e proud. Ele thought sobre como ele’d acted e wondered se ele should change. Jesus told ele ele needed a Diga ele was sorry a todos the Anjos e admit that ele was wrong. Lucifer knew Deus would forgive ele se ele did this. Como did Lucifer know this? **Leia 2 Crônicas 7:14.**

Even though this versículo had not yet been written, Lucifer e todos the Anjos knew this was exactly o que Deus’s grande heart would Faça.

Mas the proud thoughts had deep roots em Lucifer’s heart. Como could ele Diga ele was sorry? Como could ele admit em front de the whole universe that ele had been wrong? No, ele couldn’t! Então ele hardened seu heart e chose a continue seu rebellion.

pense . . . THEN TALK Sobre Isso

- _How hard Faça você find isso a Diga você are sorry e admit você have been wrong? Por que Faça você pense isso is hard para nos a admit something nós have Façane wrong?_
- _Think de a time você did Diga você were sorry. Como did the other person respond, e como did isso Faça você feel?_

Sometimes isso might be difficult a Diga nós’re sorry quando nós have made a mistake ou hurt someone, mas Deus fills nos com peace quando nós Pergunte para forgiveness!
