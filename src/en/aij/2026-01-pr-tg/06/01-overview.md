**Word of the Week**

**Mercy:** Being kind and forgiving to someone when they don’t deserve it, when it is unexpected, or when you have more power than they do.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God taught Adam, Eve, and their children to bring offerings that would remind them of His promise and His plan to save them. God was pleased when they showed their love for Him by listening to Him and following His instructions. He patiently reasoned with Cain, extended His mercy, and gave consequences when Cain chose to do things his own way instead of God’s.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God does all He can to help people accept His promise and plan to save.
- **Feel** the peace that comes from doing what God asks us to do.
- **Respond** to God’s love by listening to Him and doing what He asks.
