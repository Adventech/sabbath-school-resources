Janeesh is a natural storyteller whom God has blessed with a very special story indeed. The young man from a small village in southern India grew up loving God and decided to become a pastor. He left his village and went to study at a seminary. While there, he met some Adventist friends who invited him to a Friday night worship service. He had never heard about the Bible Sabbath, and after many questions and much study he accepted the Bible truth with joy.

Soon Janeesh was telling everyone about the Sabbath and about Jesus coming back soon. He just loved to share from the Bible. He preached to a Sundaykeeping church, and in time the whole church switched to worshipping on the Sabbath.

One day Janeesh began to feel ill. He had pain in his lower back, and his belly ached with a pain that didn’t go away. Finally he went to a doctor. After an examination and many tests, the doctor told Janeesh that he had cancer and that it had spread to several parts of his body. “You have only about six months to live,” the doctor said.

Janeesh went home feeling sad—not because he was going to die, but because he wouldn’t be able to share God’s love with more people.

Some people told Janeesh that God was punishing him for becoming an Adventist, or for arguing with the preacher. But Janeesh didn’t listen; he just prayed. Then he began a long treatment for the cancer.

When the treatments were finished, Janeesh returned home and held more meetings to talk about God and the Sabbath. From these meetings eight people were baptized!

When Janeesh returned for a checkup, his doctors couldn’t find any signs of the cancer. Janeesh is healthy and strong. Jesus has blessed him with a miracle of healing. “Jesus healed me so I can share God’s love with more people,” he says. Janeesh loves to tell people that Jesus loves them and will come again soon to take all those who believe in Him to a place where no one will ever be sick again.

(Note to Parents: Please take some time to explain to your child that God does not always heal people like this. Sometimes people die of their sicknesses. This does not mean that God does not love them, but we need to understand that there is a larger controversy going on in which we are a part. We need to trust God whether He decides to heal us or not.)
