Joseph’s brothers sat and watched the sheep munching grass nearby. Suddenly one of the brothers pointed. _What was that moving up ahead?_

It looked like a man coming their way. _Who could it be?_ He seemed to walk like Joseph. He even waved like their brother Joseph. Then they saw the colorful coat. It was Joseph!

“Here comes that dreamer,” they said to each other (Genesis 37:19, ICB). All their hate and jealousy bubbled up and overflowed like an angry volcano. Right then and there, the brothers decided to get rid of dreamer Joseph and his fancy coat.

Oldest brother Reuben heard their plans. He didn’t want Joseph to be hurt. He said to his brothers, “Throw him into this well here in the desert. But don’t hurt him!” (Genesis 37:22, ICB). Secretly Reuben planned to rescue him later and take him safely home to Father Jacob.

Joseph ran to greet them with a cheerful grin. But his brothers didn’t smile back. They grabbed him and pushed him down. Snatching and tugging they tore something off his back. _Rip!_ What was it? **Read Genesis 37:23.**

Then picking Joseph up, “they threw him into the well” (Genesis 37:24, ICB). _Thud._ The well was dry! Joseph landed on the hard ground of the dark, deep empty pit.

God was with Joseph as he sat in the dark, scared and confused. He promises us in the Bible, “Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you” (Isaiah 41:10).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of a time when your anger bubbled over into bad actions?
- What can help to calm an angry mind?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to give you courage to do the right thing when others around you aren’t.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
