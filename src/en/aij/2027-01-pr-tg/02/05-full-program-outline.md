**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Display the **Fisherman’s Eye Knot picture**. Distribute a piece of string or rope to each child and have them try and tie a fisherman’s eye knot. Allow time for the children to attempt this without adult assistance. When they are obviously struggling, ask if they need some help, then let them know that you are there to guide and help them through each step of the activity. Demonstrate each step of the knot tying and have them copy you as a group, step by step.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How easy was it to do this task on your own?** (Difficult!)
- **How were you able to get the knot tied in the end?** (It could be done when we had help.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Today we will be learning that God is our helper. When He asks us to do something, He will work with us to help us do it. Our story is about how God helped Moses to be ready and willing to do something big for Him.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the Big Idea written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **When God asks me to do something big for Him, He will help me to do it.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Load your backpack with heavy items (e.g., rocks, bricks, full bottles of water, bags of rice, etc.). Invite a few children to try to lift it and carry it to the door (they shouldn’t be able to lift it very easily). Invite one more child to lift it and then help them carry it to the door by lifting most of the weight.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How did Moses feel when God told him to go back to Egypt to help the Israelites?** (He was scared and didn’t think he could do it.)
- **What signs did God do to help Moses know He would be with him?** (Turned his staff into a snake, healed his leprous hand, and promised to send Aaron with him to Egypt.)
- **What is something you think God might want you to do—even if it feels big?** (Personal responses. May need to prompt: Tell a friend about Jesus, stand up for someone who is being picked on at school, give away something important to me for others.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Sometimes we might think that what God has asked us to do is too big or too heavy, just like the backpack. However, God helps carry the weight so we can do what He asks of us.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child a **Character Card 54: Moses in Midian** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and say this week’s memory verse: “Behold, God is my salvation, I will trust and not be afraid; ‘for Yah, the Lord, is my strength and song; He also has become my salvation’ ” (Isaiah 12:2). Then sing the **Scripture Song**: “Isaiah 12:2” together.

!a[2-isaiah-12-2.mp3] “Isaiah 12:2” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **What does it mean when it says that “God is my salvation”?** (God is the one who saves, forgives, and prepares us to live with Him forever.)
- **If God asked you to do something that seemed scary or hard, what can you remember from Isaiah 12:2?** (I can trust and not be afraid, because God is my strength, and He will help me.)

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, **“Swamp Beaver,”** and engage the children in the discussion questions.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your Burning Bush Pop-up sample. Explain that this card is a reminder that our holy, powerful God wants to be close to us, and asks us to do awesome things with Him. Distribute the craft template to each child. Have them color and cut it out, then glue it together so it will stand up. Ask them to think about who they want to give it to as they complete the craft. Then invite the children to hold them up and you **pray over the cards**, that the receiver may be encouraged.

**^[LISTEN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Introduce your special missionary guest to the children and Invite them to share how God asked them to do something special for Him—to serve, give, or go as part of mission. Follow up by asking how God has helped them to do what He has asked. Allow time for the children to ask any questions they may have. Encourage the children to thank him/her.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to **pray each day for God to show you how you might do something special for Him**. Pay attention to the people He brings to you, and look out for ways they may need help. Whatever it is that He asks of you, He will help you to do it!

**^[REVIEW AND COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Hold up your staff.

- **How did God use the staff in the story today to show Moses He would be with him while he completed God’s task?** (He showed Moses His power by turning the staff into a snake and back again. Like his trusty staff, God’s power would be with him.)

Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **When God asks me to do something big for Him, He will help me to do it.**

Lay a poster-sized piece of paper on the floor and read the words “I will go!” Invite the children to show God that they are willing and ready to do as He asks this week by signing the poster. They may also like to add a prayer or a short sentence to God.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Instead of your closing song, sing your **Scripture Song: “Isaiah 12:2”** together as you hold up the poster.
