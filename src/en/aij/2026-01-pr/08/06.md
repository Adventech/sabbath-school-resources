God’s heart was bursting with His plan to share something very special with Noah and his family! It was a beautiful thing they had never seen before, and He knew how much they would love it. What was the special thing God shared? **Read Genesis 9:13.**

What an awesome way for God to speak love to His children! The sudden burst of color blazed in a big arch across the sky. They gazed up at it in awe. It was the first rainbow this world had ever seen.

But even more beautiful than the rainbow was the message of love it gave. It was God’s way of telling them that He would never flood the whole earth again—ever! Every time the half circle of beautiful colors shone across the sky, it would remind them that God keeps His promises to love and care for His children. It was a lovely comfort to God’s eight faithful people after the Flood had changed their world so much. Why did God send a rainbow as a reminder of His promise? **Read Revelation 4:2, 3.**

God was sending a very special little piece of heaven to earth and sharing it with His children. Close your eyes and imagine that beautiful rainbow around God’s throne. Describe it. Won’t it be awesome to be there and see it one day!
