**Tell the Story**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source suggested Storytelling Props:\
• Sand tray and peg people: draw some with smiles and some with sad faces.\
• Blue, brown, green, and yellow fabric or paper, and toy vegetables and fruit.\
• Wooden blocks or building materials of your choice.\
• A world map or a globe.\
• A paper heart for each child.\
Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: 12 or 24 paper cups. On six (or 12) cups, draw a simple face with a straight line for the mouth—these are your pride faces. On the other six (or 12), draw a simple face with a big smile for the mouth—these are your happy faces.\
Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
