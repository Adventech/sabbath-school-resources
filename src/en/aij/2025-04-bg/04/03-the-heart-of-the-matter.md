Wherever you go, I will go; and wherever you lodge, I will lodge; your people shall be my people, and your God, my God” (Ruth 1:16). You may have heard this famous speech countless times, but have you ever stopped to ponder the amazing strength and determination behind these words?

Ruth was drawn to Naomi by much more than friendship. Ruth was compelled by love—God’s love. She wasn’t sacrificing her life at home out of kindness, Ruth was claiming the only life worth having; a life of love with God.

We can assume that Ruth had glimpsed God’s love through Naomi’s many kind acts and loving deeds as a mother-in-law. In a similar way, your tender care as a parent and friend can be a strong witness. Your patience, the investment of your time, your listening ear, and helpful hands are a ministry. Ellen White says that “the warmth of true friendship . . . is a foretaste of the joys of heaven” (_The Adventist Home_, p. 106).

Before you rush to phone a friend or sit patiently with a child, pause to spend time with your very Best Friend, Jesus. It is His love that draws and compels. Allow Him to fill you with His kindness, mercy, gentleness, and care—to the point of overflowing. Only then may we impact those around us.

Naomi led Ruth into God’s family—and more than that, she was included in Jesus’ own family tree! God has special people to reach through you too. Why not ask Him who, and how, today.
