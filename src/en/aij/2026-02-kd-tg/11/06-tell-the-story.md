**Big Idea:** God is with me. He will make everything good in the end.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a **toy picnic set** and invite children to set up a feast for the brothers in the story.
- Use **bags of grain** from last week, **small bag of coins**, and **silver cup** (cup covered in foil).
- Place a small cup inside a bag and invite a child to find what was inside.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 11 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Jacob’s family members were full. They had food from Egypt when there was none in the land.
- The food ran out. They had to go back to Egypt and take Benjamin so Simeon could be freed. Judah promised to look after Benjamin—no matter what.
- Joseph invited his brothers to his home for lunch. Simeon was freed. The brothers were together again!
- All 11 brothers bowed to Joseph. His dream was true!
- Were they kind to Benjamin? To test them, Joseph gave five plates of food to Benjamin for every one plate he gave the other brothers. They weren’t jealous or angry. ^[(Bring out the picnic set and have children help you set up a feast.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- As a final test, Joseph hid his silver cup in Benjamin’s sack, then sent his servant after the brothers. ^[(Invite children to make galloping sounds.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The cup was found in Benjamin’s sack! ^[(Cup in bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Back in Egypt, Joseph told them Benjamin would now become a slave. Judah offered to be Joseph’s slave instead.
- Joseph told his brothers, “I am Joseph.” He had forgiven them. God had worked things out for their family.
- Pharaoh invited Joseph’s family to come and live in Egypt. He sent wagons and animals as gifts.
- The brothers went home and told Jacob that Joseph was still alive and was governor of Egypt. He hurried to Egypt and was reunited with Joseph. God had guided them back together.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Ahh!_ Full tummies! What a wonderful feeling. Jacob’s family sighed with thankfulness as they munched on bread made with the grain from Egypt. At a time when there was no food in the land, they had good food to eat.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But as time passed, the bags of grain ran out again! There was only one thing to do—go back to Egypt and buy some more! But this time they would need to take Benjamin with them, just as the governor had said. Then Simeon would go free.

Brother Judah promised his father he would take care of Benjamin—no matter what! Jacob finally agreed. He gave them money and gifts for the governor. With a prayer he sent his 10 sons on their way.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After a long trip to Egypt, the 10 dusty brothers came before the Egyptian ruler again. Governor Joseph hid a smile as he noticed his little brother Benjamin with them. He looked so grown up! With a serious face Joseph invited them to his home for lunch.

Brother Simeon came too! The brothers were so happy to be together again.

When the 11 brothers came to Joseph’s house, they bowed low before him. It was just like his dream!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph’s brothers had been mean to him when he was younger. Were they kind to Benjamin? He would test them. For every one plate of food the big brothers were given, Joseph gave Benjamin five plates of food! Were the brothers jealous or angry? No! They smiled at Benjamin and just kept eating and talking. Joseph could see that God had changed their hearts. ^[(Bring out the picnic set and have children help you set up a feast.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Joseph decided to give them one final test. He told his most trusted servant to fill their sacks with grain and hide their money inside again. He was also to hide his own silver cup inside Benjamin’s sack ^[(grain, bag of coins, silver cup)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The next morning, as the sun rose, the brothers started toward home. What was that noise? The brothers spun around to see the governor’s servant galloping toward them. ^[(Invite children to make galloping sounds.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Oh, no! What was wrong? ^[(Give bag holding a cup to one child to open as you continue.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph’s silver cup was missing! Had they stolen it? The brothers shook their heads. Quickly they let down their sacks to prove it. The servant searched each brother’s bag until he came to Benjamin’s bag. There was the silver cup!

Shocked and scared, the brothers were led back to Joseph’s house. Here Joseph told them terrible news: Benjamin would become a slave forever. Oh, no! Bravely Judah stepped forward. He offered to swap places with Benjamin. This was a big mix-up, but he would take the blame. He would become a slave so Benjamin could go free.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph couldn’t pretend any longer. He burst into tears. “I am Joseph,” he sobbed (Genesis 45:3).

Joseph could see that they were scared. Would Joseph be angry with them? No. He had forgiven them. And God had used for good what they had done. He had been able to save them from hunger!

Pharaoh was so excited to hear about Joseph’s long-lost family. He sent them gifts and offered them the best land in Egypt. They could come and live with Joseph!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Arriving home, the brothers raced to Jacob’s tent. “Joseph is still alive. He is the ruler over all the land of Egypt,” they told him (Genesis 45:26, ICB). Father Jacob couldn’t believe his ears. But when he saw the gifts Pharaoh had sent, he knew they were telling the truth. Joseph’s older brothers told their father they were very sorry. They asked him to forgive their lies and trickery. Father Jacob forgave his sons for the lies they had told. Then he and his family hurried to Egypt with happy hearts.

Joseph couldn’t wait to see his father! He raced in his chariot to meet them. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Seeing his father, he jumped off the chariot and threw his arms around him. They hugged each other tightly and cried happy tears. God had guided them back together!
