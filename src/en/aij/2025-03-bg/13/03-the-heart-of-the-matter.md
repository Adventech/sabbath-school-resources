God keeps His promises. Do you believe it? Have you experienced this in your own life?

The Israelites had a front-row seat to the joy of a promise given and a promise kept. They certainly had an incredible adventure as they learned to trust God as the leader of their lives. As we come to the end of the Israelites’ journey in the wilderness, may you be inspired to realize that the God who led His people in such magnificent ways is your God too. He loves you so much! And though Israel’s journey looks different from yours, all our journeys take us to places of dependence on God. In our journey to the Land of Promise, God leaves special messages for us. His word is filled with love notes for you. Take a moment to dwell on them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-Black", "size": "lg", "color": "#7EA956"}}}
God Is Good.

“The Lord is gracious and full of compassion, slow to anger and great in mercy. The Lord is good to all, and His tender mercies are over all His works” (Psalm 145:8, 9).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-Black", "size": "lg", "color": "#7EA956"}}}
God Is Majestic and Powerful.

“Yours, O Lord, is the greatness, the power and the glory, the victory and the majesty; for all that is in heaven and in earth is Yours” (1 Chronicles 29:11).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-Black", "size": "lg", "color": "#7EA956"}}}
God Keeps His Promises.

“The Lord is not slack concerning His promise, as some count slackness, but is longsuffering toward us, not willing that any should perish but that all should come to repentance” (2 Peter 3:9).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-Black", "size": "lg", "color": "#7EA956"}}}
God Will Never Leave You nor Forsake You.

“And the Lord, He is the One who goes before you. He will be with you, He will not leave you nor forsake you; do not fear nor be dismayed” (Deuteronomy 31:8).

What other promises do you find encouraging? Make time this week to discover them and read them anew. When we dwell on these Bible promises, we receive courage and peace on our journey. May you believe without a shadow of a doubt that God both leads us and keeps His promises.
