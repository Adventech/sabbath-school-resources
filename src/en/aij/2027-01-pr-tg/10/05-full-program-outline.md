**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Display your premade **paper plane** and invite several children to fly it. (Choose older children whom you think will fly it well.) Point out that the person who made the plane knew exactly how to make it so it would glide well. They followed the laws of aerodynamics (how air flows around such things as planes and gliders that move through it). Share that you think it would look better if you changed it a little. Cut off the point, or part of a wing, and have several different children try to fly it.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **Why doesn’t the paper plane fly well now?** (Its design is no longer following the laws of aerodynamics.)
- **Who knew best how to make the plane fly well?** (The person who made it.)
- **How is God’s law a little similar to the way the plane was designed?** (Just as the plane works best when it’s made the right way, we will live our happiest, best lives when we follow God’s law.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God, who made us, knows the laws that will make it possible for us to live together in peace. He has given us His ten-commandment laws of love, but He also gives us freedom to choose to live by them or not. Things will not go well if we don’t learn to love and respect others in the ways He shows us in commandments 5 to 10.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **God teaches me how to show love and respect for others.**

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing **“Exodus 20:3-17”** together. Invite the children to hold up their fingers (1-10) for the number commandment they are singing about.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/exodus-20-3-17.mp3] “Exodus 20:3-17” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 62: Moses and God’s Law (No. 2)** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and ask children to recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: “Lead me in Your truth and teach me, for You are the God of my salvation; on You I wait all the day” (Psalm 25:5).

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/10-psalm-25-5.mp3] “Psalm 25:5” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **Who can we trust to be our teacher and why?** (God! He made us and loves us more than anyone else. He also saves us from sin and death. He wants only the best for me.)
- **What is something God has taught you from His Word today?**

God’s Ten Commandments are so important that He didn’t just speak them to the Israelites from Mount Sinai. He wrote them on two tablets of stone with His finger. God showed how important His holy law is; no one can ever change it or erase it.

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, **“Jumping Spider,”** and engage the children in the discussion question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Folding Commandment Plaque (Part 2). As the children complete this craft, remind them of God’s love and how His commandments bring peace and joy to everyone.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Encourage the children to put what they have learned about showing love to others into action this week. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **tell a friend what you like or appreciate about them. Then tell them why you love your Best Friend, Jesus.**

**^[REVIEW AND COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your **jug/bottle** with a **refreshing, healthy drink** in it (and a heart sticker on the front). Pour it into **a cup**, take a sip, and say how good it is. Ask:

- **Should I keep this goodness to myself or share?** (Share!) Have helpers pour and hand out drinks to give some to each child.
- **How might this be like the commandments we learned about today?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** God has given us so much love and goodness. He fills us up to overflowing! He wants us to be truly happy by sharing that love with others. God has taught us so much about Himself through His Ten Commandments. He is holy, good, and just. He wants us to be truly happy about sharing His love with others. When others see that we are different and respect others because of His love, they will want to love Him too!

Say the **Big Idea** together: **God teaches me how to show love and respect for others.**

Jeremiah 31:33 says, “I will put My law in their minds, and write it on their hearts; and I will be their God, and they shall be My people.”

- **What do you think this means?** (God will help us to remember His laws but also to know His love in our hearts, and we will want to respond to this love by loving Him and others.)
- **How does it make you feel that your mighty God will help change you to follow His laws?**

Invite the children to hold up their Ten Commandments craft if they would like to keep God’s law in their hearts.
