**Opening and Focus Activities**

Provide a selection of **colored cardstock, stickers, markers**, and other coloring materials. Gather some encouraging Bible verses for children to copy or paste onto their cards.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source **two clear glass cups, water**, two tablespoons of **oil**, two cut-out **paper hearts**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **Bible-times map** showing the location of Nain.

Source **small pieces of paper** with a Bible promise written on each. Promises could include: Psalm 107:8, 9; 119:76; Isaiah 40:29; 41:13; 43:1; Jeremiah 10:10; 20:11; John 6:47; 8:12; 11:25; Romans 12:12; 2 Corinthians 6:16; Job 23:10, Revelation 21:4, 5. Include additional promises for large classes (one per child). Fold these promises into small squares and hide them around the room before Sabbath School begins.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 51: Raised to Life]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Prepare for your special Mission Focus extra segment. Invite (remind) a **missionary** to come and share with your class. Be open to people who have been missionaries in your local area, serving others and sharing Jesus.

Create **pieces of paper with affirming messages** for children to share with each other. Make sure you have enough for each child to have one.
