The Pool of Hope

_“Baaaa,”_ bleated a sheep as a shepherd led his flock through a large gate into Jerusalem toward the sheep markets. They clip-clopped past the Pool of Bethesda, which looked cool and fresh on this warm day.

The Pool of Bethesda was a very popular and busy place. It had five large porches around its edges. Sitting or lying under the shade of these porches were hundreds of people. Some were sick. Some couldn’t see. Others were so unwell that it was hard for them to move.

They weren’t at the pool to go swimming or to have a bath. They were all there waiting . . . and waiting . . . and waiting some more. What were the people waiting for? **Read John 5:3.**

The people believed that if they could be the first person to splash into the water when it moved, they would be healed. So they sat, watching the water, day after day.

When the water did move, there would be a sudden shout! The people would push and shove as they tried to get into the pool the quickest.

But it wasn’t a very fair race. The first people to get into the water were always the healthiest and fastest. The people who were the sickest couldn’t get to the water in time, without help. Slowly they would go back to their mats on the porch, hoping that maybe next time someone would help them.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- The Bible says, “Do not be interested only in your own life, but be interested in the lives of others” (Philippians 2:4). Who could you help today? How?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Make a “Pool of Bethesda” with five porches. Inside: use blocks/carboard for the arches, and paper/foil for the water. Outside: use sticks for the arches or outline them in the dirt; dig a small hole to fill with water for the pool.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to help you notice others who need help. Then help them.
