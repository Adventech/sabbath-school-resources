**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

“I am the way, the truth, and the life,” Jesus declared (John 14:6).

What wonderful news: Jesus is the truth! Is it any wonder, then, that our enemy Satan seeks to undermine the truth? If he can weaken our dependence on truth, he weakens our dependence on Jesus.

In the Garden of Eden, Satan tried to present God as being the dishonest one: “You will not surely die. . . . You will be like God,” he said to Eve (Genesis 3:4, 5). And through the ages he has incited humans to operate according to his way, deceiving each other for selfish purposes.

Contrast this with the way God operates. He who knows and sees everything doesn’t need to be tricky or deceptive. His ways are clear and consistent. He values transparency, and He even invites us to evaluate His justice. We can depend on Him, for He is truth.

Jesus told us how to safeguard this dependence. “If

you abide in My word, you are My disciples indeed. And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free” (John 8:31, 32).

May you find extra time this week to dig deeper into the Bible and to abide in—truly trust and rest in—God’s love and direction for you. If only the family in this week’s lesson had trusted in God’s plan, His timing, His methods, and His help to be honest! Instead of brokenness and pain, they could have found freedom.

The good news of this week’s lesson, though, is that God never stops loving us, even when we choose our own way; even when we are dishonest. He is ready to forgive us and help us, for He is the way, the truth, and the life (John 14:6).
