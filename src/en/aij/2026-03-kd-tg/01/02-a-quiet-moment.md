**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The Meriden family was walking in Scotland one afternoon when they found an old, ruined castle. The door was open, so they went inside to explore. When it started to rain, they sat in one of the upper rooms and ate their picnic lunch. It was a fun adventure until they decided to walk back to their car. They discovered that the person looking after the castle had locked the doors for the night and gone home, forgetting that the family was still inside. Daddy tried to call for help, but there was no phone connection in the area. Eventually Mommy helped Daddy climb out of a high window on the ground floor. “I’ll come to rescue you soon!” he called as he walked back to a nearby village to get help.

It took Daddy several hours to find the person with the key so that he could rescue his family. They were tired and cold, but they weren’t afraid, because they knew that Daddy would keep his promise and come for them as soon as he could.

The Jewish nation had to wait a very long time before the promised Messiah arrived to be their Savior, because God was waiting for the very best time. “Therefore the Lord Himself will give you a sign: Behold, the virgin shall conceive and bear a Son, and shall call His name Immanuel” (Isaiah 7:14).

Just as the Meriden family knew that Daddy would keep his promise because he loved them and cared for them, we know that God will always keep His promises because He loves us more than we can imagine! He will always do what He says, at the very best time for everyone. How have God’s fulfilled promises helped build your trust in Him?

“To blot the promises of God from the Word would be like blotting the sun from the sky. . . . God has placed the promises in His Word to lead us to have faith in Him” (Ellen G. White, _My Life Today_, p. 338).
