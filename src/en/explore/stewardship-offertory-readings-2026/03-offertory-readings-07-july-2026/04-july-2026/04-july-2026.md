---
title: Giving Credit
date: 25/07/2026
---

Oswald Chambers wrote a book titled My Utmost for His Highest. It has been described as “the most beloved devotional of all time.” Chambers’ incredible ability to cut to the very heart of Christian living is almost unmatched.

One such example is this insightful statement about our talents and gifts: “We have the idea that we can dedicate our gifts to God. However, you cannot dedicate what is not yours.”

It’s so easy to think that our gifts, our money, and all that we have belong to us, and then accept any profit, praise, or applause that might come with it, but that’s not God’s way.

On page 68 of Prophets & Kings, Ellen G. White wrote:

“Raised to a pinnacle of greatness and surrounded with the gifts of fortune, Solomon became dizzy, lost his balance, and fell. . . . Thus it was that the temple of Jehovah came to be known throughout the nations as “Solomon’s temple.” The human agent had taken to himself the glory that belonged to the One “‘higher than the highest,” (Ecclesiastes 5:8). . . . Man cannot show greater weakness than by allowing men to ascribe to him the honor for gifts that are Heaven-bestowed.”

Let’s find rest and peace today in the joy of giving full credit where it is always due—to God our Creator, Sustainer, and Redeemer.

### Appeal

As you return God’s tithe and give your Promise offerings today, say a prayer to God to thank Him for giving you your talents, gifts, and money, and ask Him to guide you as you seek to be a faithful steward of it all.
