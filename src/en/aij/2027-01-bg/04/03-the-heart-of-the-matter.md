How do you build an ark? One wooden plank at a time.

How do you build character? One small daily habit at a time.

God asked Noah to build the biggest timber structure that had ever been known to humanity. It would be big enough and strong enough to save his entire family and every living creature! To say it was a daunting task would be an understatement. But by consistent effort and perseverance, Noah faithfully worked—plank by plank, nail by nail, till all was complete. In the end it took Noah more than 100 years to build the ark! In the same way, it takes a lifetime to build a person’s character.

Raising our children is the greatest work God calls us to do. “Upon all parents there rests the obligation of giving physical, mental, and spiritual instruction. It should be the object of every parent to secure to his child a well-balanced, symmetrical character. This is the work of no small magnitude and importance—a work requiring earnest thought and prayer no less than patient, persevering effort. A right foundation must be laid, a framework, strong and firm, erected; and then day by day the work of building, polishing, perfecting, must go forward” _(Ellen G. White, Child Guidance, p. 17)_.

So how can we, as parents, faithfully fulfill this incredible task? One day at a time. One small habit at a time. It could be as small as having a 10-minute worship time every morning, praying with your child every night when you tuck them into bed, or helping them memorize Scripture verses. Above all, it could be modeling first those Christian habits and character traits that you wish for your child to develop. All these small habits will add up to form a godly character that will serve your child for eternity.

As Proverbs 22:6 promises: “Train up a child in the way he should go, and when he is old he will not depart from it.”

What small thing could you start doing today?
