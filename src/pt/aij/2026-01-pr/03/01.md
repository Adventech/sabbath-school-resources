**^[MEMORY VERSE]({"style": {"text": {"size": "sm"}}})**\
**“Lembre-se the Sába dia, a guarde isso santo” (Êxodo 20:8).**

^[Word of the Week—Sabbath (noun):]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#7285a9", "size": "xl"}}})\
The Sétimo dia de the semana, de sunset Fridia a sunset Sába, Deus’s abençoou dia de descanso.

^[Sabbath (verb):]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#7285a9", "size": "xl"}}})\
A descanso, a cease. Em Hebrew, shabath means a stop work ou Atividade. Can você see the palavra “Sába” em shabath?

Deus criou the Sába

The sun sank slowly behind the distant hills, setting the sky ablaze com red e orange. The wonder de isso marked the end de the six dias de Criação. Everything was just as Deus had planned isso would be—lindo e perfeito. Seu final act de Criação, the Sétimo dia, would be like placing a diamond em top de a sparkling crown.

Deus didn’t criar Sába com a mighty command ou por sculpting com Seu hands. O que did Deus Faça? **Leia Gênesis 2:2.**

Psalm 121:3, 4 Digas Deus never sleeps, então Ele wasn’t feeling tired, e Ele didn’t need a nap. Mas Deus rested “de todos Seu work” (Gênesis 2:2). This was a different kind de descanso. This descanso meant that Deus made an extra-especial time just a be com Adam e Eve: Sába. Perhaps eles explored the garden, admiring each new plant ou animal. Maybe eles laughed em the birdcalls, tasted sweet new fruits, ou just smiled em each other em kind silence. Time together em the Sába was very especial.

De todos the beauty that Deus had given Adam e Eve a enjoy, Seu greatest gift was a be em a close, loving friendship com Ele. The Deus de the whole universe stopped todos Seu work a give nos this gift too.
