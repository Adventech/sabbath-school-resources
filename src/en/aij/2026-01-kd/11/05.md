Abram hummed as he set up his tent. It was so peaceful in the spot he had chosen for his new home. His tent looked out at green grass and big shady trees. But best of all, it faced an altar to God.

God loved seeing Abram set up his home around the altar. He had a surprise to give His friend. What did God say to him? **Read Genesis 13:14, 15.**

All the land in every direction would be his, and his family’s, forever!

God promised Abram, “I will make your descendants [children, grandchildren, and great-grandchildren] as many as the dust of the earth. If anyone could count the dust on the earth, he would count your people” (Genesis 13:16, ICB).

A family too big even to count? That is a _very big family_!

Abram was as old as a grandfather, and he still didn’t have a child of his own. But God wanted Abram to keep trusting Him. God would make it happen! God always keeps His promises.

God said to Abram, “Get up! Walk through all this land. I am now giving it to you” (Genesis 13:17, ICB). With a big smile Abram jumped to his feet. What a kind gift from his loving Friend.
