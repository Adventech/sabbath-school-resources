---
title: Simple Structure
---

There are necessary offices to be filled with specified duties to be performed but mere routine of formal service will be ineffective. The instruction given through the Spirit of Prophecy should be heeded in every plan: “The purity and simplicity of the Sabbath school must not be swallowed up in such an endless variety of forms that sufficient time cannot be devoted to religious interests. The beauty and success of the school are in its simplicity and earnestness in serving God.” <sup>5</sup>
