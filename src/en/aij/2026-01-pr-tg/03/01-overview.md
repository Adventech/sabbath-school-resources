**Word of the Week**

**Sabbath:** The seventh day of the week, from sunset Friday to sunset Saturday; God’s blessed day of rest. Sabbath (verb): to rest, to cease.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

The seventh-day Sabbath is one of God’s special gifts to us. On the very first Sabbath, God blessed the day and made it holy. Then He rested and spent time in fellowship with Adam and Eve. God invites us to remember the Sabbath by stopping our work and spending the day in fellowship with Him each week too. The Sabbath is a constant reminder of His love and creative power.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God gave us the Sabbath and blessed it as a very special day.
- **Feel** God’s loving presence with them on the Sabbath.
- **Respond** by worshipping God and choosing to spend time with Him on the Sabbath.
