King Herod sat on his throne fuming. His servants trembled as he began to rage. Where were those Wise Men from the East? As he counted up the days since they had visited, realization hit. The Wise Men were not coming back. He’d been tricked!

His anger boiling over, Herod called for his top soldiers. The people would pay for this! The Jewish priests and leaders hadn’t told him much about the newborn baby King. Herod was sure they were secretly planning to steal his throne and make this baby their king.

The soldiers bowed low. In a fit of rage, the king ordered that all the boy babies 2 years old and younger in Bethlehem and the surrounding districts were to be killed. What a terrible, terrible thing to ask his soldiers to do.

A very sad day followed. God had longed to protect His people from such a horrible thing, but they didn’t trust Him. Many of the Jewish leaders had become proud. They trusted themselves more than God. What promise in the Bible scrolls had they forgotten? **Read Psalm 25:9.**

God always draws us to follow His guidance, which will always be in harmony with the Bible. He says, “Your ears shall hear a word behind you, saying, ‘This is the way, walk in it’ ” (Isaiah 30:21). God speaks to our minds and hearts, and sometimes it’s as if we can hear Him out loud. We can choose to listen, as the shepherds and Wise Men had, or we can reject His guidance, as had Herod and the Jewish people. God will always let us choose.
