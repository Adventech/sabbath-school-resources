**Focus:** John trusts God to provide for his school fees.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Zimbabwe** on your **globe** or **world map**.

John was worried. He looked at his empty wallet and knew his bank account was empty too. He had arrived back at his university after the holidays, but he had no money to pay the fees. The head of the school had let him stay in a room for a night. But John would need to find the money by 5:00 p.m. the next night, or he’d have to leave.

John believed God really wanted him to study to become a minister. He went to the room, knelt down, and asked for God’s help.

John went to visit a friend and pray with him too. Instead of asking for God’s help, they thanked God for what He would do to help John. The money didn’t come that day, but John left it with God and went to sleep.

The next morning John met a friend on campus. “How are you? Is everything OK?” his friend asked him.

“Yes, everything is OK. God is in control,” said John.

“How’s your mom?” the friend asked.

“She’s fine,” John answered. Then without thinking, he added, “But she’s worried about my school fees.”

“How much do you need?” the friend asked. “Fifty thousand [Zimbabwean] dollars.”

His friend pulled out half of what he needed and gave it to him, on the spot! John thanked his friend warmly.

John hurried to a phone to tell his mother what God had done. “Mom,” he said, “can you please send my sister Mercy to the bank in town to put the rest of the money in my account?”

“John, you know I don’t have the money,” his mother said.

“Just send Mercy to town,” John said. “God will provide the money.”

A little while later he called his mother back. “I’ve been trying to reach you!” she said. “Mercy met a friend of yours in town who had planned to give you some money, but you had already left for school.” He gave John’s sister Z$25,000—the exact amount of money he needed!

John’s eyes filled with tears as he heard how God had answered his prayer and provided what he needed.
