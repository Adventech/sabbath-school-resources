The cold stone pillow hurt Jacob’s head, but he was so tired, he soon fell fast asleep.

Suddenly a bright shining light burst through the darkness of his sleep. God gave Jacob a special message in a dream!

Jacob saw a shiny ladder that stretched up, up, up, far into the sky. The ladder seemed to reach all the way from earth to heaven.

As Jacob looked at the ladder, he noticed angels everywhere. Some were busily walking up the ladder and others were busily walking down the ladder.

Who was standing above the ladder? **Read the first part of Genesis 28:13.**

God’s strong and loving voice spoke. He told Jacob that He would bless him with many children, grandchildren, and great-great-great grandchildren. What a big family! Then God promised, “I am with you, and I will protect you everywhere you go” (Genesis 28:15, ICB). That ladder was a picture of what Jesus does for us­—He connects us with heaven because He saves us.

Jacob woke up feeling peaceful and thankful. God had heard his prayers and had forgiven him. God was with him and would bless him with a good future. He didn’t need to worry or fear because the broken connection with God was now fixed. He must have smiled as he thought about all those angels flying up and down the ladder from heaven to earth. What a good, kind, forgiving God!

God’s love is so wonderful! It’s bigger, higher, stronger, and better than anything we can want or imagine!
