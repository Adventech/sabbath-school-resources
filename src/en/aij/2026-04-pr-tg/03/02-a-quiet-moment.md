**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

A good friend is a gift from God. Consider these descriptions from the book of Proverbs:

1\. A good friend is loyal and loving through both good times and hardship. “A friend loves at all times” (Proverbs 17:17).

2\. A good friend speaks the honest truth out of love and concern, even when it’s hard to hear. “Faithful are the wounds of a friend, but the kisses of an enemy are deceitful” (Proverbs 27:6).

3\. The people we choose as friends influence our character and decisions. “He who walks with wise men will be wise, but the companion of fools will be destroyed” (Proverbs 13:20).

4\. A good friend offers heartfelt advice and emotional encouragement. “Ointment and perfume delight the heart, and the sweetness of a man’s friend gives delight by hearty counsel” (Proverbs 27:9).

A good friend is loyal and helps us when we need it, such as the four friends in this week’s story. Maybe you’re the one on the mat today, longing for hope and too weak to stand. Or maybe you’re the friend, the one who won’t stop believing, who will dig through any obstacle to lay a loved one at the feet of Jesus. Either way, Jesus is waiting with love to do His work of transformation.

May you and the children be blessed as you explore the meaning of godly friendship and what it means to bring a friend to Jesus. “Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love, in honor giving preference to one another” (Romans 12:10).
