**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The sea was in front of them, Pharaoh’s army thundered behind them, and mountains cut off their only other escape route. There was no way through. What were they to do?

God had delivered the children of Israel with 10 signs of His power. His vow to lead them to the Promised Land was fresh in their minds. God was also physically present in a pillar of cloud by day and fire by night (Exodus 13:21). Yet still their fear took hold, overshadowing their fresh faith.

But Moses had learned to depend upon God. He had learned to listen, to follow God’s directions, and wait for His words. Moses declared, “The Lord will fight for you; you need only to be still” (Exodus 14:14).

As the people watched in wonder, God split the sea. He made a way where there wasn’t a way. And His people walked through the sea on dry ground—dry ground!

What a mighty God of miracles! When our “back is to the wall” and we see no way forward, we can also trust in Him. For nothing is impossible for God.

May you, like Moses, grow a daily habit of dependence on our God who saves. Start your morning in His Word, celebrating who He is—“You are the God who does wonders” (Psalm 77:14). Listen for the gentle reminders He gives you; look for the ways His words seek to guide you. The more time you spend in His presence, the more likely you will be to make faith, rather than fear, your first response when challenges arise.

Enjoy leading the children in your Sabbath School class to love God even more this Sabbath also, as they come to understand His miraculous love.
