**Opening Activities**

Set up a caring scene. Use soft **toys, dolls**, or ask adult helpers to be involved. Use **bandages/cloths** and other **first-aid items**, such as **Band-Aids, a thermometer**, and a **toy bed or blanket** for “sick” toys. Source a container of blocks.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box:** Create a cardboard **traffic light**. Cut out two circles in the middle of a **rectangular piece of cardboard** (the flap of a box would work well). Stick a piece of **green cellophane** over one hole, and **red cellophane** over the other. Source a **flashlight** to use with the traffic light.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: broom, cloth/duster, colorful paper strips and tape/stapler to make a paper chain. Keep this to use next week.

Source **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, a felt set, or wooden peg dolls.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a **jar** that you can write on or put a **sticker** on, with the words **“Good Choices.”**

Print out **red paper love hearts** for each child (or two for each if you have a small group).
