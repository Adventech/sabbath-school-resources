**What you need:**

A jar (or disposable cup), 12 large craft sticks, colored paper, scissors, tape, markers, stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/8a.png)

**Instructions:**

1\. Cut out a label from a colored piece of paper. Write “Sabbath Sticks”—or another title of your choice—and tape onto the jar.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/8b.png)

2\. Decorate the jar with stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/8c.png)

3\. Use markers to write family activities you can do to make Sabbath a blessing onto craft sticks.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/8d.png)

4\. Put the sticks into the jar, then pick one out each Sabbath.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/08/8e.png)
