As Ellen unwrapped the gift, she gasped. It was a beautiful silk dress.

A very expensive dress!

Ellen White was a messenger for God. She didn’t have much money, but all she had, she used to tell others about Jesus.

Ellen looked at the pretty gift that had come in the mail. It was lovely! She may have noticed pretty lace or ruffles. She may have smiled at a neat line of little buttons or a bow that sat perfectly in place. But Ellen didn’t try it on. She knew that this gift was exactly what she needed to help someone else.

Only days before, she had received an urgent message from Brother John Andrews. He and his family were busily sharing the Bible in another country far away. Brother Andrews had told Ellen that they were running out of money. If they were to keep sharing the wonderful message of Jesus, they would need money sent

to them as soon as possible. Ellen didn’t have much money. She wondered how God would supply the missionaries with what they needed.

As Ellen looked at the silk dress, she knew what to do.

Taking it up in her arms, she carried it to a local shopkeeper. She asked him to sell the dress for her, and he agreed. Someone came into the shop, saw the dress, and paid a lot of money for it. The shopkeeper returned the money to Ellen, who then mailed the money to Brother Andrews and his family far away.

Soon others heard about the silk dress and how Ellen had sold it to help the missionary family. They wanted to help missionaries too, so they gave Ellen even more money to share.

Who would have thought that God could use a dress to spread His love around the world?

Ellen trusted God with everything, and you can too. God promises to supply you with what you need, when you need it, because He loves you.
