The servants nodded and looked eagerly toward Jesus. Mary had told them very clearly, “Whatever He says to you, do it” (John 2:5).

Thinking quickly, Jesus looked around and saw six large stone jars that stood empty beside the door. A smile pulled at the corners of His mouth. Here was a chance to help the bride and groom, honor His mother’s faith, and grow the faith of His disciples. What did Jesus tell the servants to do? **Read John 2:7.** Without a word, they obeyed.

Then Jesus told the servants to dip a ladle into one of the jars, fill a cup, and take it to the host of the feast. Imagine the servant’s surprise! They expected a cup of water but got a cup of wine (grape juice) instead. It was a miracle!

Speechless, they took the wine (grape juice) to the host. As he drank it, he was amazed too. It was the sweetest wine he had ever tasted!

The host didn’t know that the wine had run out or that Jesus had performed a miracle. He took some to the bridegroom and exclaimed, “People always serve the best wine first. Later, after the guests have been drinking a lot, they serve the cheaper wine. But you have saved the best wine till now” (John 2:10, ICB).

Before the host and the groom even knew they had a problem, Jesus had quietly solved it with the very best grape juice ever.

Jesus is always interested in us and our happiness, and He loves to surprise us with the very best He can give. He quietly grows our faith and solves our problems in surprising and wonderful ways, sometimes even before we know we have a problem!
