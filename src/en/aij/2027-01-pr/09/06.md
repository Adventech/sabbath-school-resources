The Israelites had reverently listened to God speak the first two commandments from the billowing cloud at Mount Sinai. God had taught them how important it was to love and respect Him as their very best friend, and to worship only Him.

Then He spoke the third commandment to teach them about His name. What did He say? **Read Exodus 20:7.** Psalm 111:9 says, “Holy and awesome is His name.” When we love and respect God, we will use His name reverently instead of using it carelessly or as a swearword. This is another way we show reverence to our Father God, who calls us by His name (2 Chronicles 7:14).

When God spoke the fourth commandment, He said, “Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy” (Exodus 20:8). This day would be special and set apart from all the others—it would be holy. God told them clearly how to keep it holy. What did God say? **Read Exodus 20:9, 10.**

God knows that if we work seven days a week, we will wear ourselves out. But if we don’t have to wash our clothes, clean the house, go to school, or work for money on the Sabbath, we will enjoy the blessing of Sabbath rest. We will be blessed—happy, joyful, and content—in an even bigger way as we spend time with Jesus and reverently worship Him as our Creator. We will learn new things about God’s beautiful character that inspire us to love Him more. We will be blessed as we spend time with others who love Him too. And we will blessed as we serve other people, as Jesus did on Sabbath, showing them God’s love and kindness.

God wants us to know and worship Him. He has so much love and happiness to give us. If everyone showed their love for God by joyfully following His commandments, think how much happier our world would be!

^[FOR YOU, PARENTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) God’s Ten Commandments are a beautiful picture of His character of love. Yet so often we end up portraying them in terms of rules and obedience. In reality God’s love is what motivates obedience as a heartfelt response. “His love as a free gift will be received into the soul, and from love to Him we shall delight to obey His commandments” (Ellen G. White, _Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 283).
