**Lesson Summary**

In five days God created the light, sky, plants, sun, moon, stars, fish, and birds as special gifts for us. God created new things with just His voice—from nothing! Everything formed in the first five days of Creation by God expresses His amazing love for us.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** what was created by God in the first five days of Creation.
- **Feel** grateful for God’s power, creativity, and generous love.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His wonderful creation.
