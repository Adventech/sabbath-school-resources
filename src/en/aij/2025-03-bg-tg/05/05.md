**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time_. Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: God is always with me**. Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- Welcome children and parents by smiling. Tell the children you are happy to see them today!
- Sing **^[“Tick-tock Song”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** as the children **tap their sticks**. Ask the children: **What made you happy this week?** (Listen to, and affirm, responses.)
- Sing **^[“Happy All the Time.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Ask the children: **What do you love about God? It’s so wonderful to tell God how we love Him with our words and our songs.**
- Sing **^[“Praise Him, Praise Him.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **musical instrument or shaker** to play as they sing. **God gives us many beautiful things that show us He loves us and is always with us.**
- Sing **^[“Bubbles.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** **Blow bubbles** around the room for children to catch. Ask the children: **What good things has God given you?** (Food, water, somewhere to sleep, our families.)
- Sing **^[“All Our Needs.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child **a toy fruit or vegetable** to come and place in a basket. Explain that **God sees everything we do and hears everything we say. He knows everything about us. God is always with us.**
- Sing **^[“God Is With Me.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Invite each child to sit on the floor. Give each child a **small pillow**. When they sing about God being with them in the night, they can lie down and pretend to sleep. Explain that **God is always with us because He never sleeps!**
- Sing **^[“God Sees Me.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **toy binocular** to look through. Explain that **God is so big that He’s bigger than anything we can imagine. But God can come into our hearts when we ask Him to. It’s good to ask God to come into our hearts every day.**
- Sing **^[“Into My Heart.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **heart on a Popsicle stick.**

**WEEK 5: THE FOOD AND WATER SURPRISE**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: “Moses” peg person, sandbox, empty water bottle, peg people, large rock, small stick, cotton balls, fabric

Ask: **What is a desert like?** Invite the children to touch the sand. Shake an empty water bottle while you explain what it’s like to be really thirsty.

Open your Bible, point, and say: **The people had been walking for a very long time in the hot desert. Have you ever been very hungry or thirsty before?** (Rub your tummy.)

Say: **Moses asked God for His help. God didn’t want His people to be hungry. So God did something amazing and caused “food to fall like rain from the sky”** (Exodus 16:4, ICB). (Look and point up.) **This yummy heaven food was called “manna.” There were no more hungry tummies!**

Show the children some soft white cotton balls and invite the children to pretend they are manna food. (Be sure to tell the children not to eat them!) Place them in the middle of a piece of fabric you have laid out. Invite the children to take a side of the fabric and lift it up together as you say: **1 . . . 2 . . . 3 . . . manna!** Ask the children to collect the “manna” that has fallen.

Say: **But the people were also very thirsty. God had a plan for that too.** Open your Bible, point, and say: **God told Moses to “hit that rock with the stick, and water will come out of it. Then the people can drink”** (Exodus 17:6, ICB).

Place a rock on a tray, gently strike it with a stick, and pour some water over the rock. Say: **The people were so happy to drink fresh water! God always takes care of His people.** Give the children a drink of water, if appropriate.

Say together: **God looks after me.**
