**Talking Noah**

**What you need:**

Cardboard tube, colored fabric, rubber band, ribbon or string, red paper, cotton ball, craft stick, googly eyes, craft
glue, and scissors (1).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut a small “mouth” shape out of the cardboard tube (2).
- Cut some squares of fabric ready for the children to “dress” their “Noah.”

**Create it together:**

- Glue a small piece of red paper to a craft stick (2).
- Glue a piece of fabric around the cardboard tube (3).
- Make the headpiece by securing a piece of fabric with a rubber band (3).
- Tie a ribbon or string around the tube as a waistband (4).
- Glue on googly eyes or draw with markers (4).
- Glue on the cotton ball for the beard (4).
- Children can move the craft stick, with the red paper on it, up and down inside the tube to make it look as if “Noah” is talking!
