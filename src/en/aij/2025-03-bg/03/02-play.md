Explore and Create

Go out into nature and talk about the things Moses would have seen as he looked after the sheep. Make little sheep out of natural objects and share how the sheep always followed Moses’ voice. If indoors, print or draw a sheep and glue cotton balls to it.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3079BD"}}}
Connect

Play a role-playing game with your child. Pretend to be the shepherd while your child is a sheep. Call your child to you. Pretend to drink from the stream and lie down to sleep. Pretend to look out for danger and protect your little “sheep.” Thank God for watching over us like a shepherd watches their sheep.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3079BD"}}}
Help

Discuss ways children can help around the house with simple jobs at their skill level. Share with them that sometimes there are jobs that are hard, and we may not want to do them. But we can still obey, just as Moses obeyed God. Encourage your child when they help or complete a hard job around the house.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3079BD"}}}
Play

Make a game for obeying instructions. Ask your child to run around the room while you say stop and go. Your child will need to listen to your voice and obey. Change it up by whispering or speaking loudly, or going slow and fast. Talk about how important it is to listen to parents, grandparents, and caregivers.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3079BD"}}}
Pray

Thank God for speaking to Moses. Thank Him for choosing us to be His helpers too. Ask God to help us listen to Him and obey.
