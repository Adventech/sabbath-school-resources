**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

When we read the Bible, we see God’s character revealed:

God is love.\
God is faithful.\
God is merciful.\
God is gracious.\
God is longsuffering.\
God is good.\
God is patient.

And this is the character God wants to grow in us too. He wants us to become like Him.

On life’s journey each of us will face difficult times, which happen because we live in a sin-filled world. Thankfully, God can use those trials for our good. Through them He can correct our flaws and develop in us a well-rounded character that reflects His. This is what God did in the life of Joseph. God proved Himself faithful, and, in so doing, also proved Joseph’s faithfulness to Him.

While our lesson story is found in Genesis, take some time this week to read through one of the Gospels (Matthew, Mark, Luke, or John) in your devotional time with God. Here you will see Jesus up close in all His love and selflessness. Fix your eyes on Him. Journal the new things you notice and the prayers the Holy Spirit impresses upon you. For “as the flower turns to the sun, that the bright beams may aid in perfecting its beauty and symmetry, so should we turn to the Sun of Righteousness, that heaven’s light may shine upon us, that our character may be developed into the likeness of Christ” (Ellen G. White, _Steps to Christ_, p. 68).

May you and the children draw closer to God this week as you reflect upon His beautiful character.
