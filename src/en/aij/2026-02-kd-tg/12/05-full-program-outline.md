**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child with a smile as they arrive. Ask the following question and take time to listen to their answers.

- **What has made you smile this week?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to play a game of **musical flashlight**, a twist on the classic game musical chairs. Darken the room, if possible, and have a helper play and stop music as the children walk around a course you have set up, from Moab to Bethlehem. Explain that these are two places in our story today. As the music plays, you will use a flashlight to shine a light on a child’s feet, going from one child to another. When the music stops, the child who has the light shining on their feet will get a turn to hold the flashlight in the game (with help, and prompts to shine it on a child’s feet, not in their eyes).

Note: There are many activities in today’s lesson that include lights. Please be aware of the diverse sensory needs of the children in your class and adapt the activities as necessary. Talk to parents before the lesson to gauge what would make the children feel most comfortable.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **flashlight** and a small **blanket**.

Show the children the flashlight and turn it on.

- **What is this light used for?** (To see in the dark, to go camping, to find something.)
- **What do you think will happen if I cover the bright flashlight with this blanket?**

Leave the flashlight on, then place a blanket over the top so the flashlight cannot be seen.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Where is the light now?** (Hiding.)
- **Is the flashlight useful if it is hidden?** (No. You can’t use it to stay safe or find things. No one will know it’s on.)

Explain that God’s love is like a bright light. We do not want to hide our love for God. We can shine it brightly by using our kind words and actions.

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. I will let His love show.** Share that in our Bible story today we will find out about a kind woman who showed her love for God like a shining light—in happy and sad times.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Ask the children the following questions, not yet opening the **Story Explorer Box** (which has had a makeover!). As a child answers, invite them to stand up, and give them the **flashlight** from the Secret Surprise Box to shine onto the “reflective” Story Explorer Box to make it shine.

- **In what ways did Naomi shine God’s love in today’s story?** (She didn’t worship idols, she sang and prayed to God, she told stories about God, she was loving and kind.)
- **Why do you think Ruth chose not to go home, but to follow Naomi?** (She could tell that God was loving and kind because kind Naomi had shown her; she wanted to be God’s friend; she wanted to be kind too.)
- **Was Ruth now shining for God too?** (Yes.) **How?** (She was being a helpful friend and daughter to Naomi.)

Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **small candles** and **matches**. Place one candle for each child in a small sandbox (with tightly packed sand) or in a soft loaf of bread (push the candles into the bread).

- **What can you do this week to show others that your love for God is shining?**

When the first child answers, carefully light a candle and give them an unlit candle to hold. As more children answer, invite them to take an unlit candle. (Be sure to be very careful with the lighted candle and follow all safety procedures.)

Children’s answers might include:

- Smile at someone.
- Be with them when they are sad.
- Make a special card to cheer them up.
- Pick pretty flowers to give someone.
- Say kind words.
- Be loving and helpful.

Ask: **What would our Sabbath School room look like if we lit all our candles from this one candle?** (Bright!) Just as your little candle can share its light and shine brightly, we can do the same by sharing our love for God with others.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. I will let His love show.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (7 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Ask the children: **Can you think of an insect that lights up?** (Firefly.)

Hold up **Nature Card 25: Firefly** as you share the following facts:

- Fireflies are insects that have a special part on their body that lights up like a flashlight!
- Each one has a different flashing pattern to talk to each other.
- They can have different colors: orange, green, or yellow.
- They like to live in the forests and come out at night.

How amazing it is that God gave these creatures their own little light to shine!

Give each child **Nature Card 25: Firefly** to place in their Nature Collector’s Bag.
