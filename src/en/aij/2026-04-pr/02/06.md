The servant’s words “Your son lives!” (John 4:53) were like a precious gift to the happy nobleman. They warmed his heart as he rushed down the road, up the steps, and through his mansion doors.

Finding his little boy healed and healthy, the grateful father wrapped him in a big hug. He thanked God over and over again for the marvelous miracle. Then the nobleman told his household the amazing things that had happened in Cana. Soon they all knew about the seventh-hour miracle; how Jesus spoke in Cana and healed a boy in Capernaum at exactly the same time!

The nobleman’s words tumbled out as he shared about his new faith. He told how he was convinced that Jesus was the promised Messiah. Best of all, Jesus loved and cared for them. The whole household listened. How did they respond? **Read the last part of John 4:53.**

Many people were blessed through the faith of the nobleman. He had asked only for a healing miracle, but Jesus gave him so much more. His whole household now believed in the love and power of Jesus too. News of the miracle then spread throughout town, and still more people were prepared to accept Jesus and allow Him to change their lives.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- The nobleman shared his faith with his family and household. Who can you share your faith with? Pray for the opportunity to share with that person, and that they will choose to believe in Jesus.

^[Share and Praise]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#3d8b5a", "size": "xl"}}})\
• Draw Jesus on a piece of paper. On smaller pieces (one for each family member), write the word “faith.” Place your picture of Jesus on the floor in the middle of the room and sit beside it. Give family members a small slip of paper and a pen. Have them write a personal worry or doubt on it. Invite them to bring it to Jesus. Scrunch it up, and hand them a piece of paper with “faith” on it. Remind them that Jesus wants to replace our worries and doubts with faith.\
• Sing your Memory Verse. Look up the song “Cast All My Cares Upon You,” or simply make up a tune.\
