**Focus:** Jesus cares for each baby bird, and He cares even more for us!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/12/nature-lesson.png)

Have you ever come across a baby bird on the ground, all by itself? It’s small, and its feathers are still very fluffy. You realize it can’t yet fly, so you look around to see if you can find its nest in a nearby bush or tree. For many animals it’s often best not to touch new animals, since the mother will smell your scent and could abandon the baby. But not with baby birds. Birds recognize their offspring by their call, not by their smell.

If you find a bird that is older, it’s probably getting ready to leave the nest. Make sure it’s safe; then you may want to watch from a distance to see if its parents come to feed it. Another way to help is to call a wildlife caretaker who can help give you advice or even take care of the little bird.

In such times one of our first reactions is to reach out and help the tiny creature and give it love and care. We want it to grow big and strong into a healthy adult bird. We want it to have a wonderful life!

Jesus has given us compassion to care for both hurting animals and for people. The Bible says that not a single sparrow falls to the ground without our heavenly Father knowing about it (Matthew 10:29).

He cares even more for you! He cares for each of us when we are sad or hurting. He loves us so much that He offers us a wonderful eternal life with Him in a place where there will be no more sadness or pain! With Jesus as our friend, we can have hope and joy.
