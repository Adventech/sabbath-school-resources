**Lesson Summary**

After Jesus began His ministry, He and His disciples attended a family wedding in Cana. During the celebration the wine (grape juice) ran out, which was a big embarrassment to the hosts. When Jesus’ mother, Mary, found out, she went to Jesus with the problem. Jesus instructed the servants to fill six stone jars with water, then to pour some out and take it to the banquet master. They did this, and the water miraculously became wine.
