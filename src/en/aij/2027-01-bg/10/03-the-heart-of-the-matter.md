How truly frightening it would have been for Noah and his family as they heard that first crack of thunder. They had never experienced rain, let alone the world’s worst storm! As wave upon wave beat upon the ark, perhaps they worried, Will we make it through? “It often seemed to the family within the ark that they must perish, as for five long months their boat was tossed about, apparently at the mercy of wind and wave. It was a trying ordeal; but Noah’s faith did not waver, for he had the assurance that the divine hand was upon the helm” _(Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 105)_.

Storms will come in this life. As a parent you will know this well. God’s people are not removed from them. Yet, as believers we have with us a stronger, more powerful hand upon the helm: Jesus.

One evening Jesus told His disciples to hop into a boat and cross the lake. While Jesus slept at the helm, a furious storm came upon them. The waves beat against the little fishing boat, threatening to sink it and all its passengers. The disciples feared for their lives and in desperation called out to Jesus, “Lord, save us! We are perishing!” _(Matthew 8:25)_. Jesus rose and rebuked the storm. Then with surprise He asked them why they were so afraid. Did they forget who was at the helm?

Jesus is with you through any stormy relationship, parenting challenge, hardship, or overwhelming moment. He is more powerful, and He is in control. Thank Him now and claim His gift of peace.

> <callout>John 16:33</callout>
> “These things I have spoken to you, that in Me you may have peace. In the world you will have tribulation; but be of good cheer, I have overcome the world.”
