---
title: Think and Pray
---

^[Think]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) of everyone you know who needs to experience salvation through Jesus Christ, a greater understanding of Bible truth, or spiritual guidance, nurture, and support. Use the blank list provided to write down these names.

^[Pray]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) each day that God will open the hearts of the people on your list, and for wisdom to know how to lead them to become active disciples of Jesus.
