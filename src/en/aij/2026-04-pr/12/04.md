With His hand still resting on the dead man’s stretcher, Jesus looked down at the lifeless body. His clear voice rang out with power: “Young man, I say to you, arise” (Luke 7:14).

Responding to the words of Jesus, the dead man stirred. God’s power surged through his body, and his eyes blinked open. Life replaced death in every cell and muscle. Then Jesus took the young man’s hand and lifted him up. What did the young man do? **Read Luke 7:15.**

The mother held her son tightly in a hug. Tears of sadness became tears of great joy. Her beautiful boy had been dead but now he was alive!

Jesus promises us in the Bible, “For I will turn their mourning to joy, will comfort them, and make them rejoice rather than sorrow” (Jeremiah 31:13). He had done this in the most surprising way!

While the young man and his mom clung to each other in a long hug, the stunned crowd looked on, speechless. The Bible says, “Then fear came upon all” (Luke 7:16). They all felt as if God was right there with them, and they stood in stunned and reverent silence. They had seen with their own eyes that Jesus had the power to give life to someone who had died. It seemed unbelievable!

When Jesus feels sympathy for sad and suffering people, He steps in and does something. He may give comfort and peace, or prompt His people to show sympathy and support. He may even perform a miracle.
