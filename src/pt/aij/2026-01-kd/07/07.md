Bruno is a 7-year-old boy from Angola, in Africa. Bruno has the biggest, happiest smile you could ever imagine!

He is happy because He loves Jesus. Wherever Bruno goes, he loves to talk to others about God.

While riding the bumpy bus to school one day, Bruno began chatting to his bus driver about Jesus and how he goes to church on Sabbath.

“Sounds as though you really enjoy it,” said the bus driver. “I don’t go to church.”

“Well,” Bruno said bravely, “why don’t you come visit my church?”

“Maybe I will one day,” the bus driver said. He smiled and nodded.

Another time Bruno was at home, watching a carpenter hammering and fixing some things. “Would you like to visit our church on Saturday?” Bruno asked bravely. “I’m sure you’d like it!” The builder agreed.

A few weeks later Bruno’s church had some special programs. As Bruno sat in his seat, he couldn’t hold in his big, happy smile. Both his bus driver friend and his carpenter friend had come to the program! At the end of the meetings both men gave their lives to God.

Bruno was so happy for his new friends. God had done a miracle in their hearts.

God wants to use you as His faithful helper, just like Bruno. What can you do this weekend to share the good news of God’s love? You might invite a neighbor to church or to your home for closing Sabbath worship. You could send a drawing or card to someone. Or you might ask God to help you make a new friend.
