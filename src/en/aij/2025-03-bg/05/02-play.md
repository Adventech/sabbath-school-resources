Create

Choose to create a scene of either miracle manna falling or water coming from the rock and use it to talk about how God loves to give His children what they need.\
For manna, you may like to set up a desert camp scene using blocks, toys, or stuffed animals on a clean surface. Choose a common white food for manna, such as rice, rice puffs, or crackers. Sprinkle everywhere, then have fun collecting (and perhaps eating) it.\
For water from a rock, you may like to set up a scene in a sandbox or the dirt outside. Use natural objects to re-create the Israelites walking through the desert. Make little tents for the people using sticks and leaves. Find a rock that Moses could hit once with his stick for the water to come out. Use a cup or watering can to pour water over the rock. Talk about the amazing miracle God did.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#2F7ABE"}}}
Explore

Go outside and do lots of running, jumping, and sprinting together. When your child is hot and ready for a drink of water, give them a cup of cool water. Talk about how the Israelites would have been so hot and thirsty—just as your child is right now! God gave them a drink because He loved them and looked after them. God looks after you too!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#2F7ABE"}}}
Connect

One day this week, prepare a snack of honey on bread or crackers that are cut into small pieces. Talk about what manna might have tasted like. Talk about how the Israelites would have reacted to seeing it for the first time.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#2F7ABE"}}}
Pray

Write a list together of all the ways God looks after us (e.g., food, water, shelter, loving family). Draw a picture beside each item. Display the list on the fridge and remember to thank God for these things in your family prayers this week.
