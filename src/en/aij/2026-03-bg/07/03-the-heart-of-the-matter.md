Like Moses and Bezalel, God has tasked us with our own special job: to open our children’s hearts and minds to be dwelling places for the King. The time and talent we invest in bringing them into God’s presence is the greatest work we can do!

We know that Jesus loves children. He told us how much He values them when He said, “Let the little children come to Me, and do not forbid them; for of such is the kingdom of heaven” (Matthew 19:14). God wants our priority to be the hearts of our children. He wants us to commit to spend time at the family altar to open their hearts to Him. What does your family altar look like? Is it a bit rushed? Is it a bit inconsistent? Help is at hand. “Fathers and mothers, however pressing your business, do not fail to gather your family around God’s altar. Ask for the guardianship of holy angels in your home” (Ellen G. White, _Child Guidance_, p. 520).

Just as the Israelites gave with their whole hearts to the Lord, may we also give with their intentionality and joy. They were delighted to give to the Lord. In fact, they gave above and beyond what He needed. May we too be delighted to share the Lord with our children. May our daily family altar be a place where our children feel delight and love from both us and the Lord. And may we be filled with joy at the prospect of God living in our hearts and our homes.
