Lucifer’s selfishness puffed up like a balloon. Soon it was huge! It made him angry, jealous, and evil. He told lies about God, and caused trouble in heaven.

The Son of God knew exactly what Lucifer had been thinking, because “the Lord knows what is in every person’s mind” (1 Chronicles 28:9, ICB). He tried to help Lucifer change. But it was too late. Lucifer refused to listen. No one was going to tell him what to do anymore!

Lucifer was proud of the trouble he was making in heaven. So many angels had believed his lies—they thought God was unfair and unkind. The angels who believed Lucifer’s terrible lies were willing to fight against God.

This was a big problem! Lucifer had become the opposite of love, and only love could be in heaven. God had to do something. What does the Bible say happened? Read Revelation 12:9.

What a sad day it was! When Lucifer left heaven, his name became Satan—“the enemy.” Next week we’ll find out what Satan did next.

God is loving, so He lets us choose. He wants us to choose to follow Him. He will never force us to follow Him, but He hopes that we will choose to love Him with all our heart and put Him first.
