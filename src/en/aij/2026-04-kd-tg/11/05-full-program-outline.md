**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a Roman soldier helmet (craft sample). Introduce yourself as a captain and invite them to stand and join your army of peacekeepers. Promise that they will make their own helmet later. **Invite them to play “Captain Says” with you.** Have helpers set up little stations around the area with things of interest to interact with: **balls to roll into a bucket, items to pick up and put in a container, bells/instruments from past quarters to shake/play**. Have the children follow you and your commands around the room—only doing the actions when you say “Captain says . . .”

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children back to their seats and remove your helmet. Share that soldiers know to obey their captain. They trust that what he or she says is true, wise, and will keep them safe. Explain that in our story today we will meet an army captain who trusted someone very special. Share that our story will help us learn our **Big Idea**. Say it together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. I can trust Him.** [Prayer hands]**.”** Jesus is the very best captain.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a heart. Put it to the side as you invite a big and strong helper to the front. Ask for a brave volunteer child to join them.

- **Do you think** [helper’s name] **can safely catch** [child’s name] **if they fall backwards?**
- **What if the child is standing on a table?**
- **Why do you believe the helper will do this?** (They are big and strong; they are a good person; I trust them.)

Invite the child (if comfortable) to do a trust fall. Invite two or three others to also participate.

- **Why did the Roman captain believe Jesus?** (Jesus is God. He is powerful, good, loving; he had heard about all that Jesus had done.)

Hold up your heart. Share that the centurion trusted Jesus to help his friend, the servant.

- **What can you trust Jesus with?** (Pass the heart to each child as they answer.)

If the children would like to choose to trust Jesus this week, invite them to close their eyes and quietly say **“I trust You, Jesus”** as you pass them the heart, one by one.

**NATURE/MISSION** (5 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 50: Twinkling Stars** and share the following facts:

- Each tiny little star is a big, hot ball of fiery gas that is very far away.
- The nearest star to earth is the sun.
- Stars look like they are twinkling because the air around us is wiggling the light.
- Stars are different colors, such as red, yellow, or even blue.
- Even on a cloudy night, the stars are always still there, shining.

**Explain that as we look at the stars that always shine each night, they can remind us of Jesus, because we know He is always there for us, even if we can’t see Him.**

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 50: Twinkling Stars** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Roman Helmet.** Make a helmet to remind the children of the Roman soldier’s faith.

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Invite the children to stand and put on the helmets they have made.

- **What have you learned about Jesus today?**

Affirm their answers and share that our story today taught us that we can trust Jesus with _anything_.

Invite the children to follow you and march around the Sabbath School area as you call out the **Big Idea** (in army style) and they repeat it: **Jesus can do anything.** (Jesus can do anything.) **I can trust Him.** (I can trust in Him.) Repeat a couple of times as you march. Remind the children that we can follow Jesus in every part of our lives because He is the best captain of all.
