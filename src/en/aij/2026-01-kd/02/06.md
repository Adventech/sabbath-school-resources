God had created a new and beautiful world. It was filled with beautiful plants, birds, creatures, lovely smells, and delicious food. Most of all, God Himself was there! Adam and Eve couldn’t be happier!

On that first day, right there in the garden, Adam and Eve had a wedding. Side by side they stood, as God, their Creator and loving friend, married them. They loved each other purely and were very, very happy. What does God Himself tell us about getting married? Read Genesis 2:24.

The rest of that Friday must have felt like the best day of their lives for Adam and Eve. They had so much to explore and learn! There were raspberries to eat, roses to smell, and tigers to chase.

As the sun set at the end of the sixth day, Adam and Eve stood beside God in wonder. The sky turned orange, pink, and purple—God’s world was beautiful! Soon twinkling stars began to glimmer in the darkness. Adam and Eve were full of thanks to God for His great love.

All was perfect. All was peaceful. All of creation sang a song of thanks to God (Job 38:7). God was very happy too—His work was now done. “Then God saw everything that He had made, and indeed it was very good” (Genesis 1:31).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been to a wedding? What was it like?
- Why do you think God, on the sixth day, said His creation was **very** good?
