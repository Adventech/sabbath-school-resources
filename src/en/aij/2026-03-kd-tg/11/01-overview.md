**Word of the Week**

**Listen:** To listen is to pay attention to sound. When we pay attention to the words someone is saying, we are listening. Jesus asks us to listen to His wise words, and then do what He says.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus told simple stories about light, mustard seeds, and fruit. Then He told about two builders—one who built his house on shallow sand, and another who built on solid rock. When a storm came, the house on the sand fell down, while the house built on the rock was safe and secure. Jesus told His listeners that the rock is like God, and we are safe when we build our lives on Him. Jesus also taught the people how to pray so they would stay connected to God.

**Learning Goals**

- **Feel** excited to listen to Jesus’ stories.
- **Respond** by choosing to pray and listen to Jesus’ stories.
