Old Zacharias and Elizabeth closed their eyes and began to pray. They were praying and waiting, waiting and praying. The faithful couple from Hebron in the land of Judah were waiting for God’s promised Savior.

The couple knew they wouldn’t have to wait too long. God had given His prophets a message about the future—called a prophecy. God had told Daniel when the promised Savior would come (Daniel 9:25). That time was now! What did God tell His prophet Isaiah? **Read Isaiah 9:6.**

Zacharias and Elizabeth had read another beautiful prophecy in the Bible scrolls too. Before the Savior came to the earth, God would send a great preacher to help people’s hearts to accept Him (Isaiah 40:3-5). The couple eagerly prayed and perhaps wondered, When, oh, when will it happen?

Then one day as priest Zacharias was burning incense in the temple, a bright shining angel appeared beside the altar and lit up the room. The angel gave Zacharias a message. What was it? **Read Luke 1:13-17.**

For a moment Zacharias doubted the message. He said, “How can I know that what you’re saying is true? I am an old man, and my wife is old, too” (Luke 1:18, ICB). Too old to have a baby!

The angel explained that he was Gabriel, sent from God. The angel told Zacharias that because he had spoken his doubt aloud, he wouldn’t be able to talk until after the miracle baby was born.

Zacharias’ face lit up with joy. God had chosen his family to be God’s special helpers! They would be part of God’s big story! He would surrender—give up—his doubts and trust God. Zacharias left the temple unable to speak but with his face glowing.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Why did God choose Zacharias and Elizabeth? Read Luke 1:6. Imagine how they felt, becoming the parents of the one who would make people ready for the coming of the Lord!
