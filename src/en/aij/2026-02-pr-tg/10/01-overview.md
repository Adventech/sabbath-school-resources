**Lesson Summary**

Joseph became the governor over Egypt and worked to gather food during the seven years of plenty, to prepare for the seven years of famine. Through Joseph a whole nation was given the opportunity to know that the God of heaven is real and can be trusted. Back in the land of Canaan, Joseph’s family ran out of food, so Jacob sent 10 of his sons to Egypt to buy grain. Joseph recognized his brothers but didn’t tell them who he was. Then he tested them to find out whether their characters had changed, so he accused them of being spies and demanded they bring Benjamin to Egypt to prove they were not spies.
