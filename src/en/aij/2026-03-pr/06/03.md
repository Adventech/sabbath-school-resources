John’s voice boomed across the wilderness: “Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!” (Matthew 3:2).

With mighty words, a strong voice, and the power of the Holy Spirit, John cried out to people to change—the Messiah was coming! Dressed in his camel-hair clothes and calling out his message, John looked and sounded like a prophet. Some of the people thought he was an old-time prophet, raised from the dead! Some even wondered “whether he was the Christ or not” (Luke 3:15). But John told them firmly, “He who is coming after me is mightier than I” (Matthew 3:11).

John had everyone’s attention, and that was exactly what God wanted. He knew that John’s simple and powerful words would wake them up so that they could see and understand how bad their sins were, and how very far from God they really were. Over and over John earnestly preached about repentance and the Messiah who would appear very soon. What made John keep preaching? **Read the second half of 2 Peter 3:9.**

At first John’s message shocked the crowds who flocked to hear him. But then many did repent, for they realized his words were true. They were sinners in need of a Savior, just as John had said. They decided they didn’t want to keep sinning anymore. When they repented, all of heaven was happy, for “there is joy in the presence of the angels of God over one sinner who repents” (Luke 15:10).

Our loving God knows that we all make mistakes sometimes. But those mistakes never stop Him from loving us. He patiently calls us to repent and come back to Him again and again. His Holy Spirit gives us an uncomfortable feeling in our hearts when we’ve done something wrong. That feeling prompts us to say we’re sorry to the person we’ve wronged, and to ask God for forgiveness. Then, forgiven by God, it’s as if we had never sinned.
