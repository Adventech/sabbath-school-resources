**Focus:** A young sunflower is guided toward the sun to help it grow and thrive.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/11/nature-lesson.png)

^[(Use a sunflower seed and a cardboard sun as Storytelling Props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

- **Can you see the tiny little sunflower seed?**

A beautiful sunflower starts off as this very little seed. The seed is planted into the ground and watered, and very soon a strong little sprout pops up, becoming taller and taller each day! Finally, big, bright-yellow petals unfold in the warming light of the sun. Can you see their large and colorful flower heads? They look so happy and vibrant!

There is something else that’s special about this beautiful flower. As the sun moves throughout the day across the sky, the sunflower’s big face is guided by its light and turns toward it. A young sunflower can face one way in the morning, when the sun rises, and then face the other direction in the afternoon at sunset!

- **How high can you reach when you stand on your toes?** ^[(Invite children to stand and stretch up high.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) **You look like a sunflower!**

Now I am going to move the sun. Watch where it goes so you can be guided like a sunflower. Ready? ^[(Guide the cardboard sun around the room a few times so the children can face where it goes.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

You are such wonderful sunflowers!

The sun makes the sunflower strong and healthy and helps the roots to grow deep into the soil. God created the happy sunflower, and it can remind us of how everything He has made is good!

**Teaching Point:** Just as the sunflower is guided by the sunlight to grow, we can look to God to help us grow too.
