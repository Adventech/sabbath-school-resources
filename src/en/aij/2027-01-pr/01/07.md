Esther giggled as she played with her little sister on the mat in the middle of their home. The 3-year-old smiled back at her. The two girls lived with their family in a small village in the Solomon Islands during World War II.

The Seventh-day Adventists in Esther’s village prayed that the Allies (nations working together to help one another) would win the war. Someone told the enemy that the Christians in that village were praying for the other side to have victory. The enemy soldiers were so angry that they set off through the jungle to find Esther’s village and kill everybody in it. But a messenger raced ahead of them.

He dashed into the village shouting, “Enemy man, ’e come!” In a moment everyone in the village ran for the safety of the jungle, where they could hide. Everyone disappeared—everyone, that is, except Esther and her baby sister on the mat. For some reason Esther didn’t hear the warning call.

A few moments later she was startled by the noise and shouts of the raiding soldiers. She ran outside to see what was happening. As she came around the side of the building, she saw enemy soldiers going into every house. They threw out the owner’s things and burned and destroyed everything they could find.

{"style":{"block":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"},"rounded":false},"wrapper":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"}},"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr/01/image11.jpg)

Standing against the house as if frozen, she prayed, and God answered her prayer. The soldiers did not seem to see her. She was terrified, but never for a moment did she think of dashing for the safety of the jungle and leaving her baby sister.

Soldiers went into her home. They took things outside, smashed furniture, and stole all the food, but they did not start a fire, as they did in every other house. Esther was certain her little sister must have been killed, and tears dripped from her cheeks as she silently wept.

After destroying the village, the enemy soldiers left. Esther crept into the house all trembly and frightened. The place was in terrible confusion. Suddenly happiness flooded all over her! There on the mat in the middle of the floor lay her baby sister. Her tiny brown legs were kicking in the air, and her dark eyes were shining as she cooed baby noises. God’s angels had hidden both the baby and faithful Esther from the eyes of the cruel enemy.
