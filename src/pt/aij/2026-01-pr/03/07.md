José<sup>*</sup> loved a Faça shoes. Seu shoes were lindo, mas times were tough em Spain muitos years ago, e ele was very poor. Ele was então poor that ele owned only one hammer. Ele also had a radio that ele Brinqueed while ele worked.

One dia sobre this radio came the sound de a quartet Canteing a worship Canto. Then a man talked sobre the amor de Deus e the Bible.

“Bom sermon; bom Canteing,” José murmured a himself. “Eu must tune em again next semana.” Ele listened a the program muitos more times, e the more ele heard, the more ele liked isso.

Then one morning a man selling books entered seu shop. “Como interesting,” José exclaimed. “These books Diga the same things Eu hear em the radio cada semana.”

“Você mean em the _Voice de Prophecy_ program?” the book salesman Pergunteed.

“The very one,” José replied.

The delighted Adventist book salesman offered a compartilhe more com José e met com ele cada evening para a semana, studying the Bible.
José accepted todos the Bible truths except para the Sába. Ele just couldn’t give up work em this busy dia.

“Se Deus were a send você a sign Conteing você Ele wants você a guarde Sába santo, would você guarde isso then?” Pergunteed the bookseller.

“Oh, yes, yes, de course,” José replied.

The very next Sába José went a work as usual, mas ele couldn’t find seu hammer. Ele searched high e low, em the cupboards e sob the workbench, mas there was no hammer. E since that was the only hammer ele had, ele couldn’t work that dia.

Ele went back Sundia morning—e there was the hammer
as large as life!

“A strange thing,” ele whispered. “Is this the Senhor’s sign? No, isso couldn’t be.”

Então ele went a work the next Sába too. Ele had no trouble finding the hammer this time. Ele lined up a nail e struck the nail com the hammer. Mas as the hammer came Façawn, seu iron head broke off em two pieces!

“Isso is the sign de the Senhor!” José cried. Meu hammer wouldn’t work last Sába, e isso won’t work todia. E se isso won’t work em Sába, Eu won’t either. De now em Eu shall guarde the Sába.”

As soon as possible, José was baptized. Afterward, the pastor handed ele a gift: a new hammer.

_<sup>*</sup>Name has been changed._
