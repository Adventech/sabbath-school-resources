**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a collection of items that meet a need**. Items may include: a small blanket, fruit, a water bottle, clothing, first-aid supplies, an umbrella/raincoat.

Spread the items out around your space. Invite the children to stand up and, on the count of three, run and choose one item. Consider doing this activity in pairs if you have a large group. Encourage children to be kind and thoughtful as they choose an item.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ask the children to return to their seats with their object. Have each child, or pair, show what item they have. Then ask the following question, adapting it to their object.

- **When would you need this . . .** (say object)? (When I’m tired, hungry, hurt, etc.)

Then show the children your object: the class **Bible**. Explain that this precious Book also gives us what we need: love, hope, joy, encouragement, and God’s words to help us love Him and others.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain that God knows all the things we need. He promises to provide everything we need because He cares for us.

Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He gives me everything I need.** Share that in today’s Bible story God helps Joseph supply the people with something they really need.

- **I wonder how God provides. What do you think He’ll do?**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a stack of paper bags, a spoon, a half-cup measuring cup, and a mystery bag or container (with an appropriate small treat for each child inside). Put the mystery bag to the side for now. Give a paper bag to each child.

Invite the children to stand and walk over to the storage container of grain that was used to tell the story. Share that in our story, God gave Pharaoh and Joseph seven years to get ready for the time of hunger that was coming. God gave them time!

- **What did Joseph and the people do in the seven years?** (Built storehouses, grew lots of grain, filled the storehouses with grain.)

God gave them rain and sunshine (use hand motions to do actions together). He made the wheat to grow and grow (crouch down then stand up). Invite the **children to line up with their paper bags**. Put just a **spoonful of grain into each bag**. Explain that this represents all the people needed to live during the seven years of plenty. Invite the children to line up again to see what God gave them. Then put a **half cup of grain** into each bag. God gave so much grain that it fed all the people for seven more years!

- **How much grain did the people have to store away?** (Lots! More than what they needed.)
- **Who did God feed with all this grain?** (The people of Egypt, Pharaoh, Joseph, Joseph’s family from Canaan, and lots of others.)

Invite the children to line up again. Share that God had given Joseph more than what he needed so he could share it! Joseph gave some grain to his family and gave them a secret surprise. Reach into the mystery bag and secretly drop the treat into each bag as the children go past. Invite the children to return to their seats. Collect the paper bags but let them keep/enjoy their treats.

**Teaching Point:** God loves us so much that He gives us what we need—and often much, much more! God is so kind and good. No matter what you or your mommy or daddy need right now, God knows.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He gives me everything I need.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (7 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 23: Ant** and share the following facts:

- Ants are very busy and hardworking.
- These tiny insects are very, very strong and can lift things bigger than they are!
- Ants can live underground, in trees, plants, wood, and soil.
- All ants work together to provide for and look after the queen ant.
- Worker ants find food to bring back to the nest.

God is so amazing to make such a clever insect. The busy little ants can remind us of how God helped Joseph to store all the grain to provide for the people.

Give each child **Nature Card 23: Ant** for them to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the **Mission Story:** “The House Without a Roof” and discuss the follow-up question.
