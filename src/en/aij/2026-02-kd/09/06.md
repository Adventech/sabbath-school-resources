The king listened carefully as Joseph told him the good news and very bad news. There would be seven years of good times with lots of food. Then seven years of no rain and no food. Oh, no! The people of Egypt would be terribly hungry!

Pharaoh took a deep breath and frowned. But he didn’t need to worry. God had a plan to save this king, this mighty country, and His friend Joseph.

Joseph told Pharaoh God’s plan. It started with a man. “So let the king choose a man who is very wise and understanding. Let the king set him over the land of Egypt” (Genesis 41:33, ICB).

What would this wise man be in charge of organizing? **Read Genesis 41:34-36.**

The king smiled. What a good idea! He said, “Can we find a better man than Joseph to take this job? God’s spirit is truly in him!” (Genesis 41:38, ICB). All the king’s wise men and helpers agreed.

In a loud voice, the king said, “I will put you in charge of my palace. All the people will obey your orders. Only I will be greater than you” (Genesis 41:40, ICB).

Joseph was no longer a prisoner or slave—he was the Governor of Egypt! Only our loving God could do such an amazing thing. The Bible says, “For You are great, and do wondrous things; You alone are God” (Psalm 86:10).
