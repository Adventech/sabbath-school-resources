Do you love running around outside and playing tag with your friends? What is your favorite game?

Uriel is a boy from Mexico. He wished he could run around and kick a ball. But he couldn’t run. Since he was a little boy, he had been very unwell. He had something growing in his brain and other health problems too. As he grew older, the problems were getting so bad he struggled to even walk by himself.

Finally, when he was 11 years old, the doctor made a plan to fix his brain and his legs.

Uriel’s grandmother knew about Jesus and believed that prayer was powerful. She had read the stories of Jesus healing little boys like the nobleman’s son, when He was on earth. She knew Jesus could heal Uriel.

She convinced Uriel’s mother to go to the prayer meeting the night before Uriel’s big operation. That night Uriel’s mother and grandmother prayed together with their church family for Uriel to be made well.

The following morning, Uriel went to the hospital. The doctor did a final check of his legs before the operation. His eyes grew wide with amazement. He shook his head. The doctor couldn’t believe it—Uriel’s legs were all better! Then the doctor looked at pictures of his brain in a big machine. There was nothing growing in his brain anymore. Uriel was completely healed!

Uriel went back to school and was able to run around the playground. He was able to kick a ball and play soccer!

After seeing the power of prayer, Uriel and his mom realized that Jesus was definitely real! They gave their hearts to Jesus and began going to the local Seventh-day Adventist church. Eleven-year-old Uriel was so excited about what Jesus had done for him, he began to give Bible studies and preach. From those Bible studies, five people chose to be baptized too!

Not only did Jesus bring healing to Uriel’s body, but even more importantly, He saved Uriel, his mom, and others when they gave their hearts to Him.

The Bible says, “Believe on the Lord Jesus Christ, and you will be saved, you and your household” (Acts 16:31).
