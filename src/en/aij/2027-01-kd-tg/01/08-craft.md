**What you need:**

Week 1 craft template, scissors, paper plate, glue, blue cellophane/tissue paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1a.jpg)

**Preparation instructions:**

Cut blue cellophane and/or tissue paper into small pieces.

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2027-01-kd-craft-01.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the pictures.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1b.jpg)

2\. Fold a paper plate in half. With the flat edge at the top, glue two reed pictures back-to-back on both sides of the paper plate.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1c.jpg)

3\. Glue the basket pictures back-to-back, in between the reeds. Be sure to leave a section in the middle of the baskets without glue for baby Moses to slide into.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1d.jpg)

4\. Glue the baby Moses pictures back-to-back.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1e.jpg)

5\. Glue pieces of blue cellophane/tissue paper to cover the rest of the paper plate.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1f.jpg)

6\. Stand up the paper plate, slide baby Moses into the basket, and gently rock him side to side on the river.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/1g.jpg)
