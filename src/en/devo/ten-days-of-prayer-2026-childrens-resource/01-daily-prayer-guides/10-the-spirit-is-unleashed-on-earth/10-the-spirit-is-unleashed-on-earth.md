“Luca!… I’ve been calling you for ten minutes… Don’t make me come upstairs…”

Luca slowly opened his eyes. “Where am I?” he asked in a soft voice.

“Luca! You’re going to be late for school,” Mom said from the door. “Dad is already in the car waiting to take you… Get dressed quickly, son!”

“Mom! I’m home,” Luca said as he hugged his mom tightly.

“Where else would you be, my son?” Luca’s mom said, kissing him on the head. “But you’d better hurry, please. Today is the first day of school, and you don’t want to miss it.”

“Mom! I was in heaven! I visited other worlds, I saw incredible things and spoke with Bible characters,” Luca said excitedly.

“I think you had a very special dream,” Mom replied. “But you’ll have time to tell me later. For now, let’s pray and thank God for watching over you during the night.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}

They both knelt at the side of the bed and prayed. Luca got ready for school. His mom had his breakfast lunchbox prepared, and his dad was waiting in the car to take him to school before heading to work.

Sitting in the car, Luca asked, “Dad, who would you like to meet in heaven?”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/devo/ten-days-of-prayer-2026-childrens-resource/01-daily-prayer-guides-10-the-spirit-is-unleashed-on-earth/image3.png)

“Oh, many people,” Dad answered while driving. “Moses, David, and Paul…”

“I already met them!” Luca said quickly. “Or at least I dreamed about talking with them. The New Jerusalem is beautiful. You can’t imagine all the things I saw there…”

“One day we’ll go together—you, your mom, and I,” Dad replied. “But for the three of us to go, we must be faithful to God and do our part. Are you carrying your Bible?” Dad asked. “Open it to Acts 1:8 and read it, please, my son.”

Luca read, “But you will receive power when the Holy Spirit comes on you; and you will be my witnesses in Jerusalem, and in all Judea and Samaria, and to the ends of the earth.”

“To go to heaven, we must receive the Holy Spirit and fulfill the mission,” Dad explained. “The Spirit must be unleashed in our lives so that we can be used as instruments of salvation in the hands of Jesus. That way, many more people can know that He is coming soon in the clouds of heaven…”

“That’s right, Dad,” Luca said. “You’re right. Can I start studying the Bible with my classmates who aren’t Christians?”

“Of course, son!” Dad said. “Getting actively involved in the mission will help you grow strong spiritually, and you’ll be fulfilling Jesus’ command. That’s why the Spirit will help you accomplish God’s plan for your life.”

“Dad, I want to be a preacher like Paul,” Luca said with excitement. “I know I still have a long way to go, but I want to start right now!”

“My son, you don’t know how much joy it gives me to hear you say that” Dad said, wiping away a tear. “It’s the most beautiful thing I’ve heard so far.”

“Dad, I’ll also take care of my body and stop eating meat,” Luca said decisively. “John the Baptist taught me that I must be healthy if I want God to use me as an instrument in His hands.”

“Oh, Luca…” Dad replied. “That might be a little difficult. But your mom and I will see what we can do. Let’s take it step by step, okay?”

They arrived at school. Luca kissed his dad goodbye and ran off to his classroom. He sat in one of the first desks at the front. “I’ll work hard to learn all I can,” he thought with determination, “so I can be useful to God and help in the evangelization of the world. I want the Lord Jesus to return in my generation. I want to go to heaven and live what I dreamed.”

The Spirit had been unleashed in Luca’s life and was doing His transforming work in the heart of that little eight-year-old boy.

“Hi, my name is Luca,” he said to a girl who sat down next to him on his right.

“Hi Luca, my name is Amanda,” the girl replied shyly.

“Amanda, would you like to study the Bible with me and some of my friends?”

“Uh… yes… That would be nice,” Amanda answered.

“Great!” Luca said happily. “I’ll give you the Bible class schedule soon.” Luca had just found his first Bible student!

Though Luca didn’t know it, in one of the empty desks at the back sat Orel, smiling.

“You will be great in the Lord, dear Luca,” Orel thought. “Just a little longer, and we will meet again to live incredible things in heaven with Jesus.”

**THE END**

### Let’s Get to Work!

1. Make a list of five friends who don’t know Jesus.
2. Pray for them and ask if they would like to study the Bible with you.
3. With the help of your parents, organize your Bible class and give Bible studies to your friends.

If they are old enough, **invite them to accept Jesus** as their personal Savior through baptism.

### Let’s Pray!

Here are some prayer topics. **Pray several times throughout this day.** Remember to pray personally and also involve your family and friends.

1. Pray that the Holy Spirit may be unleashed in your life and make you a powerful instrument in God’s work.
2. Pray that the Holy Spirit may bring you family members and friends to study the Bible with you so you can help them know Jesus.
