“Wow! Super wow! What is this place?” Luca asked with great curiosity. “It’s all so beautiful! And what is that over there?”

“We are in the throne room of God in the New Jerusalem,” Orel said with deep reverence.

“This place is one of the most sacred in the whole universe, dear Luca. Do you remember the River of the Water of Life we saw some time ago?”

“Yes, it’s impossible to forget,” said Luca, looking at Orel.

“This is where the river begins—look,” Orel said, pointing to the lower part of the immense golden throne that stood at the center of the great hall.

“I see it, yes—it looks like crystal clear. Does that mean we are in the center of the New Jerusalem? This place is incredible, Orel! What did we come here to do?” asked Luca.

“Not what, but who we came to see here,” Orel replied with a smile. “Let me tell you something about myself. I am one of the angels who sing in the heavenly choir for God. My place is on one of these central platforms. It is a great privilege.”

“You have to teach me how to sing!” said Luca excitedly. “I was never good at music or singing… I wish I had learned on Earth.”

“It is never too late to learn, little one—especially when you have all eternity to do so,” said a very handsome man, dressed in a beautiful robe.

“There you are, dear friend!” Orel said, opening his arms to embrace the man. “Luca, I present to you King David.”

“Really? I can’t believe it! King David himself—what a privilege!” Luca exclaimed.

“Yes, I’m real,” David laughed joyfully. “But in heaven, I am not a king. There is only one King and Lord, our Savior Jesus.”

Orel lit up the floating glass to show a Bible text. “This time I found it quickly. David has so many verses connected to his name,” Orel said excitedly. “Please, my friend David, would you read this verse?”

David read aloud 1 Samuel 16:13, “So Samuel took the horn of oil and anointed him in the presence of his brothers, and from that day on the Spirit of the Lord came powerfully upon David. Samuel then went to Ramah.”

“I remember that day,” said David. “My brothers and my father were so surprised…”

“My dear friend David, Luca is learning about the Holy Spirit and how He worked with human beings on Earth,” Orel said. “Could you tell us about your experience?”

“Oh friends, what could I say that you don’t already know from the Bible?!” David said thoughtfully. “I was just a young shepherd boy, the youngest of all my brothers, and God chose me to be the King of Israel even though I was very young. The Holy Spirit anointed me and helped me develop many abilities.

“I became skilled in politics and leadership over Israel; I was a master composer of music; I loved building beautiful places. I became very famous, and the people loved me... and little by little, my heart drifted away from God.

“I did terrible things. As king, I was supposed to be the spiritual example for the people of Israel, but for a long time I became proud, deceitful, and even a criminal. I killed an innocent, honorable man just to take his wife.”

Orel and Luca listened very attentively to David’s words.

“Where was the young boy who had been anointed by the Holy Spirit?” David said, while heavy tears ran down his face. “Truly, friends, I am not crying for what I just told you, but my tears are tears of gratitude, because our good God forgave me and gave me another chance despite my mistakes.

“Jesus cleansed me, and after those terrible experiences in my life, the Holy Spirit was unleashed upon me again to help turn the people of Israel away from their evil ways and back to God with all their hearts.”

“What an intense and powerful story!” Luca said, wiping his tears with the edge of his robe.

“Thank you so much, David, because through your experience we’ve learned great lessons… God is good, He forgives us, and His Spirit restores us.”

“That’s right, little one. God is good—so good that He allows me to be near His throne and the angelic choir,” David said, looking reverently at the place of the throne. “I’m composing some songs for the choir; hopefully, one day the angels will sing them for our God.”

“Of course we will sing your songs, David,” Orel said with a big smile. “The angels know of your great musical talent. Perhaps soon you’ll join us as well. Time has flown quickly, Luca, we must leave for another place.”

The map already showed a blinking red dot to the east of the New Jerusalem.

“I’ll visit you again, David,” Luca said. “Thank you so much!”

“You know where to find me,” David said with a wink. “Goodbye, friends!”

### Let’s Get to Work!

The story of David shows us that God can forgive and cleanse us from all the wrong we’ve done. That’s why today’s challenge is divided into two groups:

- **Are you baptized?** _Do this:_ Talk with your pastor or elder to find out when the next Lord’s Supper will be held at your church. Participate and receive once again the forgiveness of your sins.
- **Not baptized yet?** _Do this:_ Talk with your parents, pastor, or Christian teacher about the possibility of being baptized. Take a Bible course to learn more about Jesus and make the decision to give Him your heart.

### Let’s Pray!

Here are some prayer topics. **Pray several times throughout this day.** Remember to pray personally and also involve your family and friends.

1. Pray that the Holy Spirit may be unleashed in your life and cleanse you from every wrong thought and action.
2. Pray that the Holy Spirit may help you remain always faithful to Jesus.
