The moon shone a silver path across the peaceful Sea of Galilee. The little group of boats made ripples in the water as their sails flapped in a gentle breeze.

All was calm.

But suddenly, a strong wind began to rumble. Calm, lapping water suddenly grew into big waves that slapped the side of the boat. Thick clouds swirled low and made the night even darker.

_Crack!_ Jagged lightning lit up the sky. Huge waves tumbled and crashed against the boat. The disciples became worried . . . then scared . . . then very, very frightened. This was a dangerous storm!

Jesus’ disciples used all their muscles to try and save their boat. But they weren’t strong enough. The storm was too big! “The waves began coming over the sides and into the boat. It was almost full of water” (Mark 4:37, ICB).

The disciples were scared.

Whenever we face big storms or times that make us feel scared, we can remember that we’re not alone. Jesus is with us! Jesus can keep us safe and calm. The Bible says, “Whenever I am afraid, I will trust in You” (Psalm 56:3).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been in a scary storm? What was it like?
- What do the disciples need to remember right now?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Place a “boat” (wooden block or toy) on the “water” (a small blanket or towel). With a family member or friend, hold up the blanket’s ends. Create the calm by pulling the blanket tight, then “create” the storm by flapping all four sides of the blanket. Watch the “boat” being tossed about “on the water.” Thank Jesus that He is always with us.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to remind us that He is always with us.
