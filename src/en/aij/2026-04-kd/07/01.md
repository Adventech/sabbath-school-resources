Jairus and His Little Girl

Jairus looked at his sleeping daughter and smiled. He loved his only daughter. Her happy chatter always made the day brighter. His heart filled with thankfulness to God that she was part of the family.

**What does Psalm 127:3 tell us about children?**

But Jairus didn’t smile for long. He was too worried. His 12-year-old daughter wasn’t just sleeping. She was sick. Very sick!

Usually full of life and laughter, she lay in bed, pale and still. She couldn’t sing happy songs, help around the home, or play with friends. She could only lie in bed and sleep. She was dying.

Jairus brushed away his tears. No! Not my big girl!

He needed help and he needed it now! But who could he ask for help?

His thoughts went to Jesus who always made time for children. Jesus often took children in His arms, put His hands on them, and blessed them (Mark 10:16).

Yes! Jesus would care about his sick daughter. Jesus would be able to bless and heal her.
