Abram bowed reverently and listened carefully, for God was still talking to him. God had shared an instruction, and now He shared a promise. What promise did God share? **Read Genesis 12:2.**

What a wonderful personal message to hear from the voice of God! It was especially meaningful for Abram and his wife, Sarai, as they had no children. So first it was the promise of a baby, and second a promise of many, many descendants.

Later on, when Abram wondered if God really would do this great thing, God took Abram outside into the inky darkness. His voice said softly, “Look at the sky. There are so many stars you cannot count them. And your descendants will be too many to count” (Genesis 15:5, ICB).

Gazing up at the twinkling stars, Abram felt humbled. God was going to bless him so that He could then bless many, many people in the future—so many they could never be counted! And they would shine as stars to show others about God, just as Abram did. He had always tried to live and talk in ways that would show what his loving God was like. Now God was going to help him show the whole world!

God had promised, “And in you all the families of the earth shall be blessed” (Genesis 12:3). Abram may have wondered if it could be that the promised Savior would one day come through his descendants to bless “all the families of the earth” (Genesis 12:3). He really wasn’t sure, but he may have carried that hope in his heart. Today we know that Jesus did come from Abraham’s family line and blessed the world by bringing us the gift of salvation from sin.
