I’ve spent a number of years traveling the globe, sharing the biblical principles of grateful living and the impact of money on our spirituality. I started my journey with the Three T’s of Grateful Living—Time, Talents, and Treasure—and have since identified a fourth, fifth, sixth, and seventh T.

However, while sharing in Arizona in the United States of America, a conversation with some of the local Apache and Navajo people opened my eyes to the eighth T, so now I have the complete set, hopefully!

The Eight T’s of Grateful Living teach me to live gratefully and generously with my:

1. Time—Dedicating time every day to God and His work.
2. Talents—Using my God-given abilities to serve Him.
3. Treasure—Faithfulness with tithes, offerings, and assets.
4. Testimony—Faithfully sharing what God has done and is doing in my life.
5. Temple—Caring for my mind and body for God’s service.
6. Territory—Faithfully caring for my land and the environment.
7. Truth—Faithfully protecting and sharing God’s Word.
8. Tribe—Caring for my family, church family, and global family.

These T’s are all things that God entrusts to us to be used carefully and prayerfully for His glory. Paul said it beautifully: “Whatever you do, do it _all_ for the glory of God” (1 Corinthians 10:31, NIV, emphasis added).

Let’s live gratefully and generously in all eight areas of life.

### Appeal

As we now collect God’s tithe and give our Promise offerings that we are encouraged to preplan and set apart as a percentage of our income during the week, let’s ask God to help us to be faithful in all of the 8 T’s: Time, Talents, Treasure, Testimony, Temple, Territory, Truth, and Tribe.
