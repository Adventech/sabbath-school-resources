**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **small white board**, **dry erase marker**, and **eraser** (or alternatives).

Explain that there are many people around us who care about us. How many people can you think of?

Invite the children to take turns drawing a picture of someone who cares about them. The other children can guess who it is (mom, dad, grandparents, doctor, teacher, pastor, or a Sabbath School teacher).

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How many people can you think of who care about you? Hold up the number of fingers.**
- **Who cares about you the most?** (Jesus.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus gives us moms and dads and other people in our lives to take care of us. But He cares about us too—even more than anyone else!

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He cares about me.** Let’s find out who Jesus cared about in today’s Bible story.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **thermometer**, a **bandage**, and a **small pack of tissues**. Explain that Jesus cared about people’s needs, and everywhere He went He helped people. We can be like Jesus and care for others too. God gives us the Holy Spirit to guide us so that we’ll notice when someone is sad so that we can care about them.

- **Who does each item remind you of?** (Thermometer: someone who is sick. Bandage: someone who is injured. Tissues: someone who is sad.)
- **What can we do to show we care about someone who is sick or injured?** (Take them flowers or a card, prepare some food for them, visit them.)
- **What can we do to show we care about someone who is sad?** (Give them a hug, offer to play with them, etc.)

Share that there will be times you will feel sick, hurt, or sad too. When you feel like this, you can talk to your mommy or daddy, a grandparent, or a friend and they can care for you. You can also ask Jesus because you know that He cares for you more than anyone.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He cares about me.**
