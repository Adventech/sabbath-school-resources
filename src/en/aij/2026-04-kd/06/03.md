The sick woman tried, and tried again, to push through the crowd to get to Jesus. But she just couldn’t. There were too many people! And now, Jesus was leaving to go have lunch with Matthew and his friends. She watched as Jesus moved farther and farther away.

The woman took a deep breath. She was very tired. But she wouldn’t give up! She had faith that Jesus would help her. She decided to follow Jesus and wait for Him.

After what seemed like hours, Jesus came out of Matthew’s house. The waiting crowd parted. The woman looked up to see His kind, caring face. Yes, she was sure He would help her.

But wait . . . Jesus was leaving in a hurry!

Leading Jesus away from Matthew’s house was Jairus, an important ruler of the temple, with a worried look on his face. He was talking to Jesus and pointing up the road. His daughter was sick and needed help. “Please come and put your hands on her,” he said to Jesus. “So Jesus went with the ruler, and many people followed . . . ” (Mark 5:23, 24 ICB).

As the crowd moved toward her, the woman saw her chance. Jesus was coming her way! She quickly took a step closer toward Him.

But as quickly as the crowd had parted, it once again pushed back in close around Jesus, like a huddle of penguins. Everyone was pushing and shoving and trying to squeeze in close to Jesus. The sick woman stumbled. Her body hurt. This was so hard!

**Read Joshua 1:9.** What do you think God wanted to say to her in this moment? Remember this verse whenever you feel like giving up too.

The woman felt God’s strength and courage fill her heart. No, she couldn’t give up! She had faith in Jesus. She must try to get to Him!

- Why did the people want to be so close to Jesus?
- Have you ever been in a busy, squishy crowd? How did you feel?
