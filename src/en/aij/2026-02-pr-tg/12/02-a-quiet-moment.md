**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever had a time in your life you felt as though you were in a deep pit of pain with no clear way out? Perhaps you grieved the loss of a loved one or felt wounded by someone you love.

Naomi was well acquainted with this kind of sorrow. Because of a famine in the land, she, her husband, and her two sons left their home and their people. However, 10 years later she returned as a widow with no husband or children. Yet through this wounded time God was with her. He provided special comfort through her daughter-in-law Ruth, who stood by her side, accepting Naomi’s people and Naomi’s God.

Like Naomi, you can have hope in whatever difficult season you’re currently facing. God’s Word offers you beautiful truths of hope. Jesus is with you (Isaiah 41:10), and you have a heavenly Father who cares deeply for you. He grieves with you, and He promises to heal the brokenhearted! (Psalm 147:3.)

As you explore the story of Naomi and Ruth with the children this Sabbath, may you feel comforted knowing that God still heals hearts today.
