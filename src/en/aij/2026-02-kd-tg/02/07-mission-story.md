**Focus:** Being honest is God’s way.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Taiwan** on your **globe** or **world map**.

^[(Place a bag of coins on the floor to pick up while telling this story.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

- **Have you ever found something that did not belong to you?**

Duh and Yu lived with their daddy in the country of Taiwan. Their mommy had died when they were very small, so Daddy took care of Duh and Yu. It was hard for him to find a job so they could have food to eat. Often the brothers would go to school with grumbling, hungry tummies.

There was a special kids’ program running at the Adventist church near their school. Duh and Yu would go there after school, and the kind teachers there would help them with their homework and give them a yummy meal. Their grumbling tummies wouldn’t be so hungry anymore.

Duh and Yu started to go to the children’s church there too. On Sabbaths they stayed all day and learned about God. Then they had a yummy lunch and played with the other children.

One day Duh and Yu and two of their new friends from the church walked to the library. “Look!” Duh said, pointing. There on the ground was a small cloth bag ^[(show bag of coins)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Inside it were some coins.

“It’s not ours,” one of their friends said.

- **What do you think they should do?**

They picked up the small cloth bag with the money inside and took it to the police station.

The police officer was surprised that they had brought in the lost bag of money. He shook the bag. Ching-ching went the money. Then he tipped out the coins and started to count them: ^[(Count coins from your bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) 1 . . . 2 . . . 3 . . . 4 . . . 5 . . . 6 . . . There was a lot of money! The police officer wrote down the names of the boys and where they lived.

The next day someone from a newspaper wanted to write a story about it! They talked to the brothers and their friends about the money they had found. “We learned to be honest at the Adventist church,” Duh told the reporter. “At church we are taught that God’s way is the best way, and we want to be like Him.”

Guess what? No one claimed the money, so the police officer gave the money to the children’s school to help pay for their school fees! Although the brothers did not have much, they wanted to follow God’s way by being honest.

I Wonder:

- **What would you do if this happened to you? Would you keep the money or give it to an adult or police officer?**
- **What did you learn from this story?**

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is with me. He helps me to be honest.**
