The people who lived on the flat land slowly became more and more proud. “We are the best!” they said. “We are the strongest and the smartest!” They wanted to become the most powerful leaders of the whole world.

God had asked them to spread out around the earth, but they didn’t like that idea. They wanted to live together in one place. What did they begin to make? **Read Genesis 11:3.**

_Hmmm, they thought, now we have all of these bricks. Let’s use them to build the biggest and best city in the whole world!_

“They said to each other, ‘Let’s build for ourselves a city and a tower. And let’s make the top of the tower reach high into the sky. We will become famous. If we do this, we will not be scattered over all the earth’ ” (Genesis 11:4, ICB).

Everyone on the flat land thought this was a great idea! They wanted lots of attention. They wanted to be powerful, like God. But although God is powerful, He is also loving, kind, and humble. God is loving and never proud. They were not like God at all. The Bible says, “When you do things, do not let selfishness or pride be your guide. Be humble and give more honor to others than to yourselves. . . . Think and act like Christ Jesus” (Philippians 2:3-5, ICB).
