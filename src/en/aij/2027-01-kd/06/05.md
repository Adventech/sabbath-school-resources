The Israelites kept on complaining. They were thirsty. Really thirsty. They wanted water now!

“What can I do with these people?” Moses asked God (Exodus 17:4, ICB). He didn’t know how to help their thirstiness or their complaining. But he knew God would know what to do.

God told Moses, “Go on before the people, and take with you some of the elders of Israel. Also take in your hand your rod with which you struck the river, and go” (Exodus 17:5).

Moses obeyed God and hurried ahead of the grumbling people. He stopped at Mount Sinai. What did God tell Him to do next? **Read Exodus 17:6.**

Water burst from the rock like a waterfall. It gushed over the dry stones like a river. God had provided miracle water, again! There was more than enough water for everyone in the camp.

Isn’t God amazing? He can make water come from inside a dry rock. He can do anything! The Israelites must have been so thankful. Even though they had complained and not trusted God, He still loved and cared for them.

Our loving God cares for us too. Why not thank Him now? The Bible says, “Give thanks to the Lord for His goodness, and for His wonderful works to the children of men!” (Psalm 107:21).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Was it hard or easy for God to bring water from a dry rock? Why?
- What did Moses do whenever he needed help?
