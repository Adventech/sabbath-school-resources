Sin had spread like a bad sickness across the new earth, but there were still some who loved God. A special man named Enoch was Seth’s great-great-great-grandson. Enoch loved God so much and was kind and humble. He chose God’s way as his way. The Bible says that “Enoch walked with God” (Genesis 5:22). God helped Enoch to be kind and gentle, and gave him a strong, wise, and growing mind.

Enoch began preaching about God’s love to anyone who would listen. He also pleaded with them to follow God and stop doing wrong. Sometimes he would go away where it was quiet so that he could pray and be with God. This special time with God gave him strength, energy, and joy when he came back to preach again.

After Enoch had been a friend of God’s for 365 years, what happened? Read Genesis 5:24. Yes, God took Enoch to heaven! Just imagine how Enoch felt when he saw his Best Friend, God, face-to-face!

The people living at this time were very tall and strong like Enoch, but most of them were not good and kind like him. They were violent and selfish. They took what God had given and used it to make themselves look good by taking the best things for themselves—gems and gold to become rich, and wood to build better houses. They made beautiful gardens for their idols and did whatever they wanted to do without thinking of how it hurt God, themselves, or other people.

What does the Bible say God saw when He looked at the people?

Read Genesis 6:5. God decided that if He didn’t do something, there would be no one left through whom the Savior could be born.
