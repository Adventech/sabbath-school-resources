**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **balloon** or **inflatable ball**. Blow it up in front of the children and comment on how each breath makes the balloon grow bigger and firmer. In a similar way, we can ask God to fill us with His love. When God’s love is in our lives, we can grow up in wisdom and in favor with God and man.

Play a game with the inflated object. Invite the children to throw it gently to each other. Each time someone catches it, they can name one thing they can do to show love to others.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How do you know that your heart is growing more like Jesus’ heart?**
- **How do you like to show love to the people in your family?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** One of the most important ways that we can grow is by learning about Jesus. When we learn about Him, we see how wonderful, good, and kind He is, and it makes us love Him more. When we love Him more and ask for His love to be in our lives, we become kinder and more loving to the people around us, especially to those in our family.

Ask the children to crouch down and then grow tall together as you say the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He is loving and perfect.**

Explain that in our story today we will learn about the time Jesus grew up, just as you are doing! He grew up to be kind and loving, and so can you!

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a bag containing some items. Invite the children to take turns removing an item from the bag. Ask the children how they could use the item to help at home:

- **Duster/dusting cloth:** Dust the furniture
- **Clothespin:** Help with the laundry
- **Trowel:** Help in the garden
- **Trash bag:** Take out the trash
- **Toy:** Pick up my toys
- **Broom:** Sweep the floors

**Teaching Point:** We can be like Jesus and help at home. We can also find ways to encourage others like He did.

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He is loving and perfect.**

**^[SHARE and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Explain that Jesus was a wonderful, loving, perfect boy. Imagine what He would have done if He came to our Sabbath School today . . .

- **What do you think He would like to sing?**
- **What do you think He would want to learn about?**
- **What questions might He ask?** (We’ve learned that He asked the teachers in the temple very difficult, interesting questions.)
- **Would you be Jesus’ friend if He came to our Sabbath School today?**

If Jesus had come to our Sabbath School today, I know He would want to be your friend. In fact, Jesus does want to be your friend! He knows what it’s like to be a child, just like you. You can talk to Him about anything, anytime. He always listens, and He loves you very, very much.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 31: Blue Whale** and then share the following facts:

- Blue whales are the fastest growing creatures on earth.
- They grow up to 100 feet long in the first year of life.
- They take very good care of their babies.
- They are kind to each other.
- They even sing songs to each other!

Give each child **Nature Card 31: Blue Whale** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Pedrito Grows.”**

**CRAFT**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Growing Person**. Children will color and fold paper children to remind them that they’re growing.

Ask the children to bend down low and stand up tall as they answer the question:

- **Do you want to grow like Jesus?**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Explain that like Pedrito, when we love Jesus, we’ll also want to love others. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **help your family with a cleanup project for someone else. You may help pick up trash, clean something at church or help people who live nearby**. Remember to show or tell others that God cares for them.
