The woman with the sore back listened carefully to Jesus as He taught everyone about God’s love. But suddenly . . . He stopped talking.

The woman could feel everyone’s eyes turn to her as Jesus called her to come over to where He stood. With surprise, the woman slowly walked over.

His gentle, yet powerful voice spoke again. “Woman, your sickness has left you!” (Luke 13:12, ICB). She was amazed!

What did Jesus do next? **Read Luke 13:13.**

As Jesus reached out His hands to tenderly touch her, her back stretched up tall and she looked around! She saw the kind face of Jesus, her Healer, smiling at her.

A big smile spread across her face too. She felt so joyful! It was like there were fireworks exploding inside her! She began loudly praising God for her now tall, straight back.

Jesus then turned to the watching crowd and called her their precious sister. He explained how God saw her as precious and important. He said that it was a good thing to heal her on Sabbath because it showed God’s love and care (Luke 13:15, 16).

As the woman listened to the beautiful, caring words of Jesus, her heart became full of happy delight. She mattered to God the Father! She mattered to Jesus!

What a happy Sabbath it was. Jesus had shown everyone that He is “Lord of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:28). Sabbath is a day to be with Him, to learn about Him, and to show His love to others.
