title: Summary
---

Sabbath Schools that manifest a community friendly presence will show an understanding and sensitivity to what is important to guests. Leaders, as well as regular members, will be sensitive to the mindset of people, and to what attracts and what repels. They will strive to use a common language and be ready to apply the Bible to daily living.
