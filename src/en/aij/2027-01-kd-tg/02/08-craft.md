**What you need:**

Week 2 craft template, white letter-size (A4) sheet of paper, scissors, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2027-01-kd-craft-02.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the pictures into strips, along the dotted lines. Arrange them in order, alternating one strip from each picture at a time.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2b.jpg)

2\. Fold a piece of paper and cut it in half (lengthwise). Glue the first strip from picture 1 onto the piece of paper, then glue the first strip from picture 2 beside it.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2c.jpg)

3\. Continue gluing the picture strips in order, side by side along the piece of paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2d.jpg)

4\. Fold the paper back and forth - accordion style. Make the folds in between each of the picture strips.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2e.jpg)

5\. Stand up the paper. When you look at it from one side, you will see the picture of Moses as a prince, and when you look at it from the other side, you will see the picture of Moses as a shepherd. Show someone your Moses agamograph and share the story of how Moses changed!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-kd-tg/assets/2f.jpg)
