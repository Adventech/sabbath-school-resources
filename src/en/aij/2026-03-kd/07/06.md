Finally alone again, Jesus fell to the ground, exhausted. He had just fought, and won, a mighty battle against Satan. Now He couldn’t even stand. He lay there hungry, tired, and weak.

As quick as a flash, help was by His side. Who came to help Jesus? **Read Matthew 4:11.**

They had watched the whole battle. Now the angels rushed to help Jesus and give Him strength. Lovingly, they gave Him food to eat and spoke kind words. They reminded Jesus that God loved Him. What caring angel friends!

God was looking after Jesus, and He looks after you too.

Feeling strong again, Jesus left the desert. Satan would try to tempt Him many more times. Life would be hard. But Jesus was determined to do what was right. He would need God’s strength and wise words to guide and help Him each day.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Imagine being one of the comforting angels. What words would you have said to Jesus?
- What do you think the angel food tasted like?
