**Opening Activities**

Source **blocks** (or boxes or containers) for children to build homes with.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: Print **“Animal Homes”** picture or show digitally.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source items for suggested **storytelling props: metallic items, sparkly items, beautiful cloths, wood, olive oil**, and **spices**. Show some props and hide others for the children to find and bring to a **big basket**. Have items to involve children in making the tabernacle: **fabric** to pretend to sew, **tools** and **shiny foil** to wrap **cardboard** “walls”; small **paper** messages to carry; **baskets** to carry; **beads** to carry as jewels.
Use peg people (Bezalel, Moses, Aaron and two sons, others) or a felt set and **^[week 12 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: **child’s birthday party invitation in envelope**. Place a few storytelling props (listed above) inside after story: **fabric, paper messages, basket, beads**.
