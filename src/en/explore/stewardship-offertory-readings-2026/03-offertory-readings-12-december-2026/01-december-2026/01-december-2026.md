---
title: Fundraising or Faith-Raising?
date: 05/12/2026
---

Somebody once said, “If you take care of the pennies, the pounds will take care of themselves.” It’s a sound piece of financial advice that crosses cultures and currencies. When we faithfully care for small amounts of money, we will have enough for the bigger expenses.

It’s also a good guide for people who manage the funds entrusted to churches and ministries. They’re also expected to be faithful stewards of every penny that comes into or passes through their hands on its way into God’s work.

But what if leaders have been faithful, even with the pennies, but there still aren’t enough pounds to do all God has called them to do? Should they use the best fundraising techniques and tools to get more money for ministry?

Yes, proven fundraising methods and technologies are very helpful. Still, as important as they are, there’s also a more sustainable and effective fundraiser than simply creating highly polished and well-crafted fundraising appeals.

Faith-raising is the key that unlocks God’s accounts, which are managed by His stewards. When we hear firsthand stories and testimonies of God’s faithfulness to others and then test His promises and experience His blessings, our faith and giving increase.

If you take care of the faith-raising, the fundraising will take care of itself.

### Appeal

As we now return God’s 10% tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s test His promises, just as He invites us to. Let’s remember the ways He has blessed us, and at every opportunity, let’s raise each other’s faith by sharing our testimonies about God’s goodness and faithfulness.
