**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Again and again God showed His love for His children in powerful ways as He led them through the wilderness. His comforting cloud guided them. It was a cooling shelter by day and cozy shelter by night. He protected, provided, and sustained them in a desolate desert. What a wonderful God!

Yet the Israelites didn’t fully understand the length and breadth of God’s love, and they didn’t know how to respond to it. God longed for them to love Him back, and so He shared His first four commandments to teach them how. These commandments would be for all of God’s people down through time.

God’s ten-commandment laws are a beautiful picture of His character of love and His desire to grow the same love in the hearts of each of us. Love and obedience go together like a hand in a glove. Jesus Himself said, “He who has My commandments and keeps them, it is he who loves Me” (John 14:21). Obedience is the bubbling overflow from a heart that has embraced God’s love.

“His love as a free gift will be received into the soul, and from love to Him we shall delight to obey His commandments” (Ellen G. White, _Christ’s Object Lessons_, p. 283).

Talk of God’s love in a personal, genuine way with the children in your Sabbath School and nurture a love for God in their hearts. Our prayer is that they will “delight to obey His commandments,” love Him more than anything or anyone, speak His name in reverent awe, and that the Sabbath will be the most special day of the week.
