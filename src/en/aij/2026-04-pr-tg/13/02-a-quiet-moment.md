**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The words of Jesus continue to echo down through time to encourage everyone who believes in Him who has lost someone they love. He said, “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in Me, though he may die, he shall live” (John 11:25). These words not only bring great hope but are also certain and true because they are spoken by the giver of life Himself.

Although we’ve all felt pain, we know in our hearts that one day, one magnificent day, “the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first” (1 Thessalonians 4:16).

Then there’ll be no more goodbyes, no more tears or sadness, and no more grief, for the old order of things will have passed away.

Although some of the children in your Sabbath School may have not yet tasted the bitterness of death, remember that the message you share with them this week can stay in their hearts as precious truth that can one day give them hope when they need it.

This week, may you all know that Jesus has the power to resurrect, and very soon He will come again to take those who believe in Him to heaven.
