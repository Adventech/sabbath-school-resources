**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

A stunning arch of color stretches across a rain-washed sky. We can’t help smiling. The vivid colors of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, indigo, and violet dazzle and delight. Mesmerized, we trace the beautiful beams of light with our eyes. We wonder at its vast size and ethereal glow. But best of all, we cherish the message of each rainbow—a message of God’s protection.

With bold brushstrokes, God painted the very first rainbow for Noah and his little family. He knew “that those who had been saved from the general ruin would have their fears awakened whenever the clouds should gather, the thunders roll, and the lightnings flash; and that . . . the pouring out of the waters from the heavens would strike terror to their hearts, for fear that another flood was coming upon them.”<sup>1</sup>

God understood, so He made a loving promise that “never again shall there be a flood to destroy the earth” (Genesis 9:11). Then, to further emphasize His words, God created the rainbow to be a striking symbol of the trustworthiness of His promise. The beauty of it chased away the fear in those eight hesitant hearts. They “looked with admiration and reverential awe mingled with joy upon this sign of God’s mercy.”<sup>2</sup>

God invites us to look with “admiration and reverential awe” at His rainbows too. Each arch of glowing color is a personal guarantee from God. You are safe in His protective care; you can trust Him. What a beautiful promise!

As you and the children explore God’s protection and promises through the lens of Noah’s story, may you be filled with praise and thankfulness.
