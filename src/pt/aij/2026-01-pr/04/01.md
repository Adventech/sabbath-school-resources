Lucifer, the Magnificent Anjo

Deus criou muitos, muitos lindo Anjos. There were thousands e thousands, e ten thousand times ten thousand de eles! Como Façaes the Bible describe the number de Anjos Deus criou? **Leia Hebreus 12:22.**

Seu glorious Cantos de praise e happy voices filled cada part de Céu. Todos those shining Anjos loved Deus e enjoyed being com Ele. Each one de eles was delighted a serve Deus e go as quick as a flash de lightning a obey seu Deus. Mas one was Deus’s especial Anjo. Ele sparkled com twinkling jewels e sang com a clear voice as ele led Céu’s choir.

This magnificent Anjo was Lucifer. Everyone loved e respected Lucifer. Ele had an important e responsible place em Céu, right beside Deus’s throne.

Ele was Deus’s talented e strong covering Anjo, called a cherub. Happiness filled seu heart as ele served Deus as the leader de the Anjos. Céu was filled com joy e wonder.
