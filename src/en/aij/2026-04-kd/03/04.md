Everyone looked up at the big hole in the roof. Jesus stopped talking and looked up too. Sunlight streamed in. Smiling faces peered down from above when they saw Jesus. Their plan was working!

The four friends then used ropes to gently lower their friend down, down, down through that hole right to Jesus’ feet. The man with paralysis had nearly given up hope of getting to Jesus—but here he was lying right at His feet!

Jesus looked down into the man’s eyes. Jesus knew all about him. He knew he needed peace. He knew how his body needed healing. He knew that his heart was full of trust. Jesus knew all these things without even asking his name.

Then Jesus said something beautiful and very surprising. What was it? **Read Mark 2:5.**

How special that Jesus started by healing the man’s biggest wish—to be forgiven and to have peace. Jesus’ words healed his heart right away. Peace shone like a bright light on his face. He was too happy to even speak.

Jesus wanted to give him a life full of good things. He says in the Bible, “I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly” (John 10:10).
