Gazing up into the night sky was a beautiful reminder of God’s wonderful promise to Abram. Imagine having millions and millions of descendants who couldn’t be counted! So even though He and Sarai had no children, Abram cherished God’s promise and looked forward to the day it would begin to come true.

Then, after Lot had moved away to the fertile plains, God gave Abram a daytime reminder of the promise too. What did God now say Abram’s descendants would be like? **Read Genesis 13:16.**

Have you ever watched specks of dust dance in a shaft of sunlight, or the way dust glides in front of the broom as the kitchen floor is swept, or seen a swirling dust storm on a hot summer’s day? These dust particles could never be counted, even by the world’s cleverest person at math! God’s words about dust were another beautiful way to tell Abram that many, many people would know and love God because of his faithfulness.

Abram knew that these descendants would unselfishly shine for God like stars in the night sky. And like specks of dust dancing in the sunlight, their lives would show others what a happy friendship with God looks like.

So even though Abram didn’t have the best land in Canaan, he was content to have God as his friend, and to carry God’s promise in his heart. He truly knew the blessing of unselfishly putting others first.
