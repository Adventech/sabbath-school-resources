**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Stand beside the table with your mystery items (covered by a cloth). Explain that each of the items under the cloth symbolizes how God knows everything. Invite a child, one at a time, to reach under the cloth, take an item, and **share what it tells us about what God knows**. Items include:

A **clock**: He knows what will happen in the future.

A **plant**: He knows everything about nature.

A **picture of a person**: He knows us, our feelings, favorite foods, friends, and more.

A **coin/paper money**: He knows our needs.

A **map**: He knows where we’re going.

A **locked diary**: He knows our thoughts and everything about us.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask for some children to show the class the memory verse page they colored before Sabbath School.

- **How does it make you feel to know that God (who knows everything about you and the whole world) made this promise to you?**

Tell the children that today you’ll learn more about a young man named Joseph. He learned to trust our all-powerful, all-knowing God, and knew God would help him, even when he was hurt by his own brothers.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **I can trust God to be with me.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}}) Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to **form pairs** and sit together, facing you.

**Draw a deep pit (U shape) on the whiteboard or on a poster.** Ask the children to close their eyes and imagine what it felt like to be Joseph, having just landed at the bottom of a pit with a thud. In pairs, have the children very briefly discuss these questions:

- **How might Joseph have felt to be thrown into the pit by his brothers?** (Scared, sad, betrayed, lonely, hurt.)
- **What problems can arise when we deal unjustly with others?**
- **What would be a better way to deal with someone who is favored and you are not?**

Sometimes people upset, reject, or hurt us. **Draw a stick figure at the bottom of the pit.** Explain that in these moments it can feel as though we are in the bottom of a pit too. But God has good news for us.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Explain that to discover this good news, we’re going to play a game of **“Hot and Cold”** in our pairs. Show the children the two, numbered, folded pieces of paper, each containing half of a Bible treasure (Psalm 145:18, ICB). One child will hide one Bible treasure, while the other searches for the other piece. They’ll then swap roles to find the second treasure. The **hider** can help the **seeker** by saying “cold,” “warm,” or “hot,” depending on how close the seeker is to finding the paper. As soon as one pair is finished, they are to sit down on the floor to show they are done.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the pair who finishes first to open their two papers, and each read their portion of Psalm 145:18. Then briefly discuss:

- **What promise does God give to those who call upon Him?** (That He is near to them.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Draw a heart around your drawing of a person in a pit. Explain that God is always with us, no matter where we are. We don’t have to search blindly for God—He doesn’t play “hot and cold” with us. We can pray to Him, knowing He is there for us.

Share that God was already helping Joseph through those around him.

- **How did God use Reuben to help Joseph?** (He convinced his brothers to throw Joseph into the pit, rather than kill him.)
- **How did God use the traders to help Joseph?** (They took Joseph away from his brothers, who may have hurt or killed him. God led the traders to the place they took Joseph to.)
- **What could you do during the week ahead that will help you to notice that God is with you?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God can work through our tough times to help us. We can trust Him even when it seems nothing will get easier. Our all-knowing, all-powerful, and all-loving God knows everything and is with us.
