Rosie let the raindrops hit her face, as she walked to the graveside. It was the sad day she had to say goodbye to her daddy. After being sick for a long time, he had peacefully died.

She sat with her big brothers under a shelter and cried.

But suddenly, just as the minister got up to talk, the rain stopped. The sun burst out and shone so brightly through the trees, that Rosie couldn’t help but smile. It was so beautiful.

The minister talked about how her daddy was peacefully asleep. He would wake up when Jesus returns. The very first thing he would see was the face of Jesus!

Rosie imagined that moment. She felt a brand-new feeling. She wasn’t crying anymore. She felt so warm inside, like her heart was full.

All morning, she felt calm. She felt like a blanket of love was wrapped tightly around her.

Her loving Jesus had sent the sunshine. He had helped her to feel His love. And one day, she would see her daddy again. Oh what joy!
