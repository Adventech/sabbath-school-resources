**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The young man rubbed the small key between his fingers. He’d forgotten all about this key. His grandpa had given it to him when he was just 9 years old. “For when you need it,” he’d said. Now the boy was 19. And Grandpa had passed away.

Sadly he began to try the key in all Grandpa’s old locks. But it was too small. With a glimmer of hope he ran to the old shed out back. Grandpa had always kept it locked and closed. Suddenly . . . his little key was the perfect fit! Pulling open the rusty door, the young man gasped to see . . . a car. A beautifully restored vintage car, with the keys in the door and a sign under the wipers that said, “With love, from Grandpa.”

God knows the needs you have, will soon have, and will have far into the future. In providence, God provides for your needs. What a relief and comfort to know that God has plans within plans—far beyond

what you can see—to ensure you make it into His arms for eternity.

Take a moment to consider His ultimate plan for you and pray to Him now, giving your life into His care and providence.

Joseph lived his life and trusted in God’s providence. When things were tough and didn’t make sense, he looked heavenward to his source of comfort and strength, knowing that God knows the end from the beginning. He told his brothers, “But as for you, you meant evil against me; but God meant it for good, in order to bring it about as it is this day, to save many people alive” (Genesis 50:20).

As you and the children spend one more week exploring the story of Joseph together, may you too put your trust in God, knowing that He has amazing things in store for you.
