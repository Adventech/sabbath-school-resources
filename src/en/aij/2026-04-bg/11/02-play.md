{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Create

Using clay or play-dough, create a little Nativity scene. Make a stable, manger, animals, and Mary, Joseph, and Baby Jesus. If using clay, let it dry and then place it somewhere in the house where you can see it for this season.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Play

Act out the story using a doll and stuffed animals that you have at home. Ask your child how to hold a brand-new baby. Wrap up your “Baby Jesus” in a blanket and sing Him to sleep. Set up the animals around Baby Jesus. Holding your “baby,” read the lesson story again together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Serve

Put together a little newborn package with your child. You could write a card, buy an outfit or some diapers and wipes, and deliver them to someone who has just had a baby. If you don’t know anyone in this situation, you could drop it off at your local maternity ward or shelter. Share with your child how Jesus came to serve others.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Sing

Learn and sing “Away in a Manger” and/ or “Silent Night” together as a family for worship. Talk about the place where Jesus was born. Talk also about the silent night Jesus was born into. What might you have heard? What might you have smelled? What might you have seen?

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Pray

Thank God for sending Baby Jesus so that everyone could know Him.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given” (Isaiah 9:6).
