A baby’s cry split the air. Friends and family crowded around to rejoice at the birth of Elizabeth’s newborn son. What would she call him? John. The happy crowd looked puzzled. Sons were often named after other relatives, but no one in their family was named John.

Zacharias reached for his writing tablet. He wrote, “His name is John” (Luke 1:63). Immediately he could speak. Zacharias explained that God Himself had named their little baby John, which means “Jehovah (God) is gracious.” What a miracle! The people were sure God had big plans for this baby. They spread the amazing news everywhere.

Precious baby John had an important part to play in God’s big story: He was to be “the forerunner of Christ” (_The Desire of Ages_, p. 97). In ancient times a forerunner’s job was to let everyone know that someone very important was coming behind them. For example, when people lined the roads to catch a glimpse of their king, a forerunner would run ahead shouting, “The king is coming!” Then everyone would eagerly look back down the road for the king.

What was John, as a forerunner, to do? **Read Luke 1:76-79.**

Luke 1:17 also tells us, “He will bring those who are not obeying God back to the right way of thinking. He will make people ready for the coming of the Lord” (ICB). What an important job, to prepare people’s hearts to accept God’s Son.

We can be like John and, in word or quiet action, help others come to know and love Jesus too.
