**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a bowl of flour. Tell them there is something valuable in the bowl. Invite a volunteer to come and see if they can find the **item of value** without using their hands. (The child won’t be able to find anything by simply looking.)

- **Were you able to see anything valuable in the bowl of flour?** (No.)
- **Why?** (Because the flour was in the way.)

Then allow them to use their hands to find the object. The valuable object was completely covered in the flour, hidden from sight.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Explain that this is how the Sabbath was in Jesus’ day. It was special, valuable, and needed, but the Pharisees had hidden its true value and blessings by covering it up with rules and more rules.

God made the Sabbath for people to enjoy and be a blessing to others, but these rules made the Sabbath a burden for many people. This is what Satan wanted. He wanted people to see God as a hard master. But Jesus came to show people the true meaning—and value—of the Sabbath.

- **How would you answer the question: Why did God give us the Sabbath?**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Share the **Big Idea: Jesus wants me to know and share the blessing of the Sabbath by caring for others.**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What did you learn from Jesus in these stories?** (To do good on the Sabbath.)
- **What are some activities you enjoy doing on the Sabbath?**
- **What makes the Sabbath special to you?**
- **What act of Sabbath kindness could you do for someone this Sabbath?** (Share ideas as a lead into the activity.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}}) Sabbath Planner:** Give each child a piece of paper/cardstock. Divide the paper into four sections. Write the dates of the next four Sabbaths at the top of each section. Ask the children to **think of an act of kindness they can do on each of these Sabbaths**. (For example: take flowers to an elderly neighbor, make a card for someone who is sick, give a warm hug to someone who looks sad, help the pastor/deacon set up the church before the service.) Encourage children to be specific and include one idea in each section and to carry out their wonderful plans.

Missionaries show God’s love in many ways: through friendships; friendship clubs; Bible studies; health care (doctors, nurses, dentists); teaching; helping after a disaster (such as a flood or earthquake); building homes, hospitals, or schools; developing radio, television, and social media stories about Jesus; running businesses to help people.
