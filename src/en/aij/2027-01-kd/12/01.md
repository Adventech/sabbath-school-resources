**Big Idea:**\

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Home”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ - The place where someone lives. Home might be an apartment, a house, hut, tent, caravan, or hotel. Some animals make their home in trees, burrows, nests, or caves. God has a beautiful home in heaven. But He also wants to live with us here—in our hearts. He wants to be with us always.

A New Home

Moses felt happy as he left his tent and hurried to meet the people. He had just returned to camp from being on the mountain with God. How good it was to be so close to his Friend. He had so much to tell everyone!

The Israelites had seen the new tablets of stone with God’s Ten Commandments. And because they had been truly sorry for worshipping the golden calf, God had forgiven them. Their hearts felt clean and new. What a good, loving God.

Now Moses had more exciting news to tell. God had asked the Israelites to build something very important. What was it? **Read Exodus 25:8.**

This really was the best news! God wanted to be close to His people. He wanted to make a home with them.

“I will live with the people of Israel,” God told Moses. “I will be their God. And they will know that I am the Lord their God. They will know that I am the one who led them out of Egypt. I did this so I could live with them” (Exodus 29:45, 46, ICB).

God’s home wouldn’t look like their old homes in Egypt. It wouldn’t look like the fancy palaces they had helped the Egyptians build. God was going to make it an extra-special, holy home that looked like His home in heaven. He said, “Build this Holy Tent and everything in it by the plan I will show you” (Exodus 25:9, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think God wanted to make a home in their camp?
- What might God’s new home look like?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that He wants to be close to you and be your God.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw a special home that you might like to live in. What would it look like inside and out? What things would you want inside? What good things could you do there? You might like to use this drawing as a plan, and make it out of blocks or Legos. God had a very special plan for His home!
