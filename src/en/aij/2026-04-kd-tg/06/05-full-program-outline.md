**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **small, closed box** or **bag with stickers** or a **treat** inside. Tell the children that you have hidden a **small, red heart** somewhere in the room. Ask them to help you find it. When they find it, they can have what’s inside the closed box. Keep the contents a secret but assure them that it is good. Encourage their efforts and remind them to keep trying even though it’s hard. Wait until some are ready to give up, then give hints so the children can find the heart.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Why were you so excited to find the heart?** (You told us we would get something good; we trusted you.)
- **Was it easy to find?** (No.)
- **Why didn’t you give up?** (We wanted the prize.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain to the children that they showed faith. They believed your words that something good was coming, and they kept looking for it until they found it. Happily give them the stickers or treat. Say: “Here is a reward for your faith.”

Today’s story is about a woman who was like you. She had faith that Jesus could give her something very good. She believed it was true and didn’t give up. Let’s find out what happened when she found Jesus.

Her story will teach us our **Big Idea: “Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He can change me when I have faith in Him** [fold hands together in prayer action]**.”** Stand and say it together.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** revealing a **blindfold**. Invite the children to play a gentle game of “Who touched me?” A few children can take turns being blindfolded while the other children take turns creeping up and gently touching them on the feet. The blindfolded child is to guess who touched them. Then invite the children to return to their seats.

- **How did Jesus know the woman had touched Him?** (He felt power go out from Him. He is God! He is all-powerful)
- **How was the woman changed by Jesus?** (She was made well! She was happy.)
- **Why was Jesus so happy to meet her?** (She believed in His power; she had faith.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus is our all-powerful God. We can have faith that He will always help us, just as He helped this woman. He can change us in amazing ways and heal our bodies from pain and our hearts from sin.

Invite the children to show that they believe in Jesus by closing their eyes and saying, “I trust You, Jesus,” with their hands outstretched, like the woman in today’s story.
