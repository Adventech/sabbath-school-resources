Two Trees

Deus criou Eden a be a lindo forever home para Adam e Eve. De delicate flower a towering tree, Ele placed everything together em perfeito order e beauty.

Two majestic trees stood em the middle de the garden. Each had a especial purpose.

The Primeiro grande tree was called the tree de life. The fruit de this tree would give life forever a Adam e Eve, e todos seu family a come.

The other grande tree was also important, mas Deus Pergunteed Adam e Eve not a eat de this tree—ever! O que was this tree called? **Leia Gênesis 2:17.**

Deus could have left this tree out de Seu perfeito garden, mas everything sobre Deus is true e fair. This tree was like a signpost Conteing eles that Lucifer, an Anjo em Céu, had rebelled, becoming Satan, which means “enemy.” Seu rebellion começou a war entre Deus e ele, bom e evil. Deus Pergunteed eles never a eat the fruit de this tree. Ele never wanted eles a know o que evil was: a experience hurt, pain, e death. Mas Ele let eles choose. Deus wanted eles a be able a choose a amor Ele, instead de being forced. ChooCante not a eat de the tree would show that eles had chosen Deus’s side em the war entre bom e evil.

Deus told eles eles would die se eles ate de this tree. Ele sent Seu loving Anjos a warn eles that Satan would try a tempt eles a Faça the wrong thing, a separate eles de Deus. Trusting Deus, staying away de this tree, e staying together would guarde eles de sinning.
