With great persistence the sick woman shuffled her way toward the lakeside where Jesus was teaching and healing. When she finally reached the edge of the crowd, it was impossible to force her way through. Everyone had come to see Jesus!

When Jesus left the lakeside, the crowd followed, and the woman followed along too. But again, she was unable to reach Jesus. Would she ever reach the Master Healer?

Just as she began to lose hope, Jesus turned and headed in her direction. This was it—her moment to meet Jesus!

She moved toward Him, but so did many others. As the crowd swirled around Jesus, she couldn’t get close enough to speak to Him, and she almost lost sight of Him! Afraid of losing her only chance, the tired woman pushed forward. With every step she said to herself, “If only I may touch His clothes, I shall be made well” (Mark 5:28).

The woman was focused on only one thing: reaching out to Jesus. First Timothy 6:12 describes her focus well. It says, “Fight the good fight of faith.” She wasn’t fighting against an enemy—she was fighting to get close to Jesus.

Jesus loves to see us persist in getting close to Him too. Because we live in a sinful world, He might seem far away at times, but He is always right there for us. We can “fight the good fight of faith” by persistently reaching out to Him. We can pray to Him and ask for the Holy Spirit’s help. We can read His Word and claim His promise to be close, even when we don’t feel as though He is. **Read the last part of Matthew 28:20.**
