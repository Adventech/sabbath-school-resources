**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Beforehand, on small pieces of paper, write one of the following scenarios. Ensure each of your children has one scenario:

1. Lying to your parents about homework
2. Hitting someone at school
3. Breaking someone’s toy on purpose
4. Sneaking out of the house when you should be doing your chores
5. Yelling at your parents
6. Stealing food from a classmate’s lunch box

Distribute a wrong choice card, a pen, and a sticky note to each child. Have them write a possible punishment on the sticky note and stick it over the wrong choice.

- **Would the punishment you have suggested be fair?**
- **What would it mean to be shown mercy in this situation?** (The punishment would be withheld.)

Instruct the children to take off the sticky note and write “Mercy” over the wrong choice. Have them place their cards under their chairs (to use at the end).

**Teaching Point:** Mercy is being kind and forgiving instead of giving someone the punishment they deserve when they have done something wrong. God continually shows His people mercy.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Today’s story is about Cain and Abel, the children of Adam and Eve in last week’s story. It is a very sad story, but we are going to look for the good parts, where God shows mercy and where His people listen to Him and obey.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from your whiteboard: **When God sees me listening to Him and obeying Him, He knows how much I love Him.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**, along with the altar activity.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Sprinkle **dried herbs (or pepper)** on your **plate of water**. Share how the herbs represent the bad things that can grow in our hearts: selfishness, jealousy, anger, grumbling, etc. Review how God’s love and mercy can clean up our hearts when we choose to listen to Him and follow His instructions. To demonstrate this, squirt a small amount of **dishwashing liquid** into the water and watch the herbs/pepper move away.

This is what God had wanted to do with the bad things in Cain’s heart. It wasn’t too late!

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** No matter how many bad choices we make, it is never too late for God’s love and mercy to change us. When we follow God’s instructions, repent (say we are sorry), and let Him change us, He knows how much we love Him.
