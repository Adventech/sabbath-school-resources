Lot’s eyes opened wide with delight. Uncle Abram was letting him choose to live wherever he wanted. He could choose first!

As Lot looked out over the land, what did he see? **Read Genesis 13:10.**

It was beautiful! Lot had a big choice to make.

Lot should have stopped and said, “No, Uncle Abram, you choose first.” But he didn’t. He should have asked God to help him choose. But no, Lot didn’t do that, either. Lot wanted the very best for himself. And Lot was sure that he would make the best choice.

Greedily he searched for the greenest and most beautiful land. Finally he pointed. There! Down on the lush, flat, green land! That is where he wanted to live. He thought it looked perfect. It had lots of green grass, good food, and water. And it was really close to the busy city of Sodom.

Lot’s decision was made. He said goodbye to Abram and Sarai. Then he gathered his family, his workers, and his animals. Together they walked down the hill and set up their tents near Sodom.

He was so happy with his me-first choice! But the Bible says that “the people of Sodom were very evil” (Genesis 13:13, ICB). Uh-oh, that doesn’t sound like a good place to live, does it?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})

- What had Abram done to be like the words in your memory verse? (See Philippians 2:4.)
- Do you think Lot’s choice was a good choice or a bad choice? What makes you say that?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to give you a heart that is happy to put others first.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})\
If possible, go to a lookout or a place in nature where you can admire the view. If this isn’t possible, perch yourself high on furniture and use your imagination. Pretend you were given Lot’s choice. What would you say and do?
