**Big Idea:** Jesus is my example as I grow up.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A gentle breeze swept over the grassy hills surrounding Nazareth. Sheep bleated softly under the shady trees. Birds chirped happily in the branches above. Tucked away from main roads and surrounded by hills, Nazareth was a quiet, peaceful place for Jesus and His family to call home. The village was small. The homes were simple ^[(show Lego or cardboard house)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

As Jesus grew, God kept Him close to family and nature.

Jesus loved roaming the hills near His home. ^[(Display small toy animals, flowers, or twigs.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The birds, wildflowers, cloud patterns, and tall trees all taught Him about God the Father. Mary encouraged His curious mind and helped Him learn new things.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did we say the Bible says about young Jesus? Read Luke 2:52.** (“And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.”)

Jesus grew taller, stronger, wiser, friendlier, and closer to God ^[(show Jesus figure)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When Sabbath came, Jesus worshipped in the synagogue (church). ^[(Show the Bible scroll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He listened and learned from the songs, prayers, and readings from the Bible scrolls. Most of all, He loved to worship His Father God there.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus didn’t go to school. His mother, Mary, taught Him to read and study the Bible scrolls at home. He did His best, and He learned well, for His true teacher was God.

He learned so well from God and learned so much in the Bible scrolls that when He grew up people were amazed at His knowledge.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At home, there were always chores to do. Dishes needed washing, beds needed making, and the floor in Father Joseph’s carpenter shop needed sweeping. Jesus saw what was needed, and He did it—without even being asked! Mary and Joseph smiled as they saw Jesus learn to be a willing and happy worker.

The Bible tells us, “People liked Him, and he pleased God” (Luke 2:52, ICB).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What qualities do you think Jesus had that made Him a great friend?**

The years sped by, and Jesus turned 12. He was so excited. Now He was old enough to travel to Jerusalem for the Passover celebration. He happily set off with His family and friends. Everyone was going to Jerusalem! The roads were crowded with travelers singing and chatting as they walked down the road toward Jerusalem ^[(show fabric or paper for the road)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Arriving in Jerusalem, Jesus stopped to look at the temple. ^[(Show simple temple from cardboard or Lego bricks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Its white stone walls glowed in the sunlight. Jesus was so eager to learn all He could on this trip, and the temple was a good place to start. God had given the temple to His people to teach them about the Messiah.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Inside He saw the priests doing their duties. He saw the Passover lamb being sacrificed on the altar ^[(show toy lamb)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He understood that these were symbols of what would happen to Him when He grew up. While here in Jerusalem, He came to fully know that He had come to earth to be the Lamb of God and die to take away the sins of the world.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a side room of the temple, rabbis (the teachers) taught their students ^[(show figures of rabbis)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus settled at their feet and listened carefully. He asked deep questions about God’s Word. Then the rabbis asked Him questions. They were amazed at His wise answers.

When the Passover ended, Mary and Joseph and all their friends began the journey home. The crowd of God’s people were like a big family. Old friends chatted together as they walked. Children ran and played together along the way. But Jesus wasn’t with them.

As evening came, Mary and Joseph suddenly realized that Jesus was missing! In a panic they hurried back to Jerusalem to look for Him. Finally, three days later, they found Him!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Where was Jesus? Read Luke 2:46** (He was in the temple!)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary ran toward Him. She said, “Son, why did you do this to us? Your father and I were very worried about you. We have been looking for you” (Luke 2:48, ICB).

Jesus replied, “Why did you have to look for me? You should have known that I must be where my Father’s work is!” (Luke 2:49, ICB). Jesus was gently telling His parents that He really understood that He was God’s Son too, and that He knew what He had come to the world to do.

Mary and Joseph looked puzzled. They didn’t understand. But Jesus did. He had learned from God. And He went home with His parents to continue to learn from, love, and obey them.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Back in Nazareth, Jesus worked alongside Joseph in the carpenter’s shop, crafting furniture and tools.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What does Colossians 3:23 say? Let’s read it together.** (“Work the best you can. Work as if you were working for the Lord, not for men” [ICB]).

Jesus always worked with joy and care, serving God happily in everything He did. He shared His food with the hungry, His time with the lonely, and kind words with those who were treated badly. Though some teased Him for being “too good,” Jesus remained gentle and unselfish, drawing people to Him through His example.

Jesus’ childhood in Nazareth was filled with love, learning, and growth. Through His life He showed us how to grow in wisdom, kindness, and obedience to God. Jesus is our perfect example as we grow up. He will be with us to help us make good choices every step of the way.
