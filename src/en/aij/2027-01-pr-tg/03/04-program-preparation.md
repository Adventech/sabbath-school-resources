**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[storytelling picture PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source an **opaque mystery bag to hold props for each plague**: 1. **Bottle of water and red food coloring**. 2. **Small toy frog or slime and frog picture**. 3. **Black sesame seeds as lice**. 4. **Small plastic flies or a picture of flies, or sultanas**. 5. **Toy farm animals**. 6. ****Circular adhesive bandages or red stickers for “boils.”** 7. **Ping-pong balls or mini marshmallows for hail**. 8. **Fake grasshoppers or picture of locusts**. 9. **Eye masks or blindfolds**.

Source a **food that is hard or soft**, depending on cooking, and bring examples of it in both forms: raw and cooked. For example, a carrot is hard but softens with cooking, as does rice, pasta, and some legumes.
