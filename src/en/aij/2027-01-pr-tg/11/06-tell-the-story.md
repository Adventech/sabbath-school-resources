**Big Idea:** God loves me and wants me to be His committed friend.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use **peg people** (Moses, Joshua, Aaron). Make a mountain by laying **fabric over containers** to create different levels. Lay **blue paper or fabric** over one level when the people see God there. Use a **toy cow** as the idol or make one out of **aluminum foil**. Alternatively, use a felt set if desired.
- Use your facial expressions and body language dramatically to express the range of emotions experienced by Aaron, Moses, and the Israelites.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to Exodus and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Thunder crashed, lightning flashed, and trumpet blasts echoed around the mountains. Then silence fell. God had finished speaking the Ten Commandments.

Staying at a distance, the people gazed in amazement as Moses headed back up the mountain. ^[(Move Moses peg man up your mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He disappeared into the swirling cloud where God was.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses listened carefully as God told him how all people, including servants, widows, orphans, poor people, and animals, should be treated—with love, respect, and care. He told Moses how the Sabbath and yearly feasts were a time to come close to God and celebrate His love and care for them.

Moses went back down the mountain ^[(move Moses back down to the ground)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and told the people all that God had said.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How did they respond? Read Exodus 24:3.** (We will do all that God has said.)

After the people had made their promise, God called Moses, Aaron, his two sons, and “seventy of the elders of Israel” (Exodus 24:9) up the mountain. ^[(Move Moses, Aaron, and Joshua to one level.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly they “saw the God of Israel” (Exodus 24:10) and worshipped Him from afar. ^[(Lay blue fabric on this level of the mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They glimpsed God’s feet standing on a pavement of sparkling blue sapphires. Wow! They ate and drank together before the Lord.

Then it was Joshua’s turn to climb the steep mountain with Moses. Aaron and the 70 elders went back to camp to care for the people. ^[(Move Aaron down; move Moses and Joshua up to another level.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

For six days Moses and Joshua prayed and waited for God. Then on the seventh day God called Moses higher up the mountain to talk with Him. ^[(Move Moses up.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

High up, God wrapped Moses close to Himself in the cloud and spoke for 40 days about many things. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Toward the end of their time together, God gave Moses a very special gift: the Ten Commandments written with His finger on stone tablets.

Back down in the camp, the people waited . . . and waited . . . and waited. Where was Moses? Some grumbled that Moses had deserted them or been burned up by the fiery cloud. Some even wanted to return to Egypt!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Before long, the worriers had become an angry mob that made its way to Aaron’s tent.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did the people say? Read Exodus 32:1.** (Make us gods to lead us.)

^[(Use Aaron peg man.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Aaron tried to talk the people out of their crazy idea, but he was afraid of the people. He let the crowd pressure him to do the wrong thing. He took their jewelry, melted it down, and made it into a shiny golden calf ^[(toy/foil cow)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then a wild party began. The people worshipped the calf idol. They offered sacrifices, feasted, and danced around it.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Meanwhile Moses was carefully carrying the precious ten-commandment stones down the mountainside. God told him to hurry—His people had sinned terribly! When Moses got closer, he saw the golden calf idol.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) What has happening? This was so terrible! How could the people do this to God?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In disbelief and anger Moses threw the commandment stones to the ground, where they shattered. The people had broken their promise to God. Now the stone commandments were broken too.

When Moses came to the bottom of the mountain where the people were, he seized the calf idol and threw it into the fire, where it melted in the flames. Then he crushed the gold from the fire into fine powder and scattered it on the drinking water, and made the people drink it. They needed to really think about what they had done.

If only Aaron had bravely stayed committed to God.

But God is always faithful, and He never breaks His promises. He hadn’t broken His commitment to the people. If they were sorry, He would forgive them and continue to teach them to be faithful, as He was.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses called out, “Whoever is on the Lord’s side—come to me!” (Exodus 32:26). The people of the tribe of Levi stood beside Moses. Many other people were really sorry for the terrible mistake they had made, and wanted God’s forgiveness. They stood with Moses too.

But there were others who weren’t sorry. The people died so they couldn’t lead anyone else astray.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**The Ten Commandments tablets still lay shattered, so what did God do? Read Exodus 34:1, 2.** (He asked Moses to make some more stone tablets and bring them up the mountain.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses again walked up the mountain with the new, empty tablets to talk with God for another 40 days. ^[(Move peg Moses up mountain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Once again God etched His law into stone tablets with His finger.

^[(Move Moses back down the mountain.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the people saw Moses walking down the mountain with the new tablets, his face was shining. Moses had been so close to God’s glory and goodness that his face now glowed too.
