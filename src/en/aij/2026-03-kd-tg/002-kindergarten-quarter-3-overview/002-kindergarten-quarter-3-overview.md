**Sabbath School Room Setup**

- Hang the **wall mural** at the front of your classroom, along with a **large title, “My God Is With Me.”** This quarterly theme should be reinforced each week.
- Find **nature items** to display near the mural, such as a tree or plant, bright flowers, toy animals, plastic fruit, etc.
- If your room is large enough, create **separate spaces** for the opening activities, the general program, a Bible story corner/mat, and a craft table. Transitioning from one learning space to another will help with reinforcement of ideas, engagement, and behavior.
- Purchase or make a **small paper bag for each child (Nature Collector Bag)**. Write their name on the front of the bag and invite each child to color or decorate their bag at the beginning or end of Sabbath School. Hang each bag on a wall using small hooks. Each week the children will place their nature card into the bag. At the end of the quarter they can take their cards home.
- Create a **special offering container** that can be given to a different child each week to collect the offering. You may like to add bright paint to a jar, make a money roll, or find a money box to use this quarter. Each quarter, use a different offering container to keep this special and fun for the children.
- Display the **Offering Chart** in your Sabbath School room (see www.aliveinjesus.info to print the chart). Each week, color the chart to show how much offering has been collected to give to the special mission project. Model the importance of giving and, if possible, bring a little extra money to give to children who don’t have anything to give.
- **Decorate two boxes (shoeboxes)** that will remain in your room for this program and be used each week. Label these boxes **(1) Secret Surprise Box, (2) Story Explorer Box**.
- Source a **bell** or another nice sound effect (mobile phone sound effect, triangle, xylophone). This will be used to transition between activities and before opening the Secret Surprise Box and **Story Explorer Box** each week.
- **Music** (Scripture Songs and original songs, including vocal recordings, backing tracks, lyrics, and sheet music) can be found on the AIJ website. You will find a playlist for each quarter on the Alive in Jesus website. You may also like to add songs from your culture.

**Other Helpful Information**

1. The **Bible Story** is a vital part of these teacher guides. Children learn about God and His Bible truths through narrative. Prepare to make the story portions engaging, memorable, and applicable each week. Familiarize yourself with the **story narratives** so that you can confidently share them without reading them. Use the simple Quick Cues to prompt you as needed, and keep the children’s interest by using props, sounds/music. Practice aloud beforehand, if possible, keeping in mind that the stories you tell will lay the foundation of a child’s faith. Always emphasize that the stories are true and from the Bible. Tell them how much Jesus loves them and wants to have a friendship with them.
2. **Mission Stories and Nature Lessons**: There is a two-week rotation for mission stories and nature lessons. Both reinforce the weekly Big Idea. For this reason they are in the latter part of the program after the **Big Idea** has been explored. In the first teacher guide each quarter, more time will be given to introducing the quarterly offering focus/division, so a short nature lesson is shared this week.\
The overall **Mission Challenge** focus for this quarter is: Make your home a welcoming place.\
**Mission Stories**: We have chosen the best age-appropriate mission stories that reinforce the weekly lesson focus. The mission stories won’t always come from where the quarterly mission offering is going, although you will share a weekly mission fact (from the AIJ website) that pertains to the global offering for the quarter.\
**Nature Lessons**: Each week you will give the children a nature card after sharing a nature lesson. The weekly memory verse will be on the back of the nature card. On the weeks during which a mission story is included, a short nature lesson will be shared. The alternative weeks will have a longer nature lesson with discussion questions.
3. The **_New King James Version_** and the **_International Children’s Bible_** are used within these teacher guides. The New King James Version is used across the curriculum for all memory verses for consistency.
4. Watch the **teacher videos** on the AIJ website for tips to help you teach Kindergarten.
