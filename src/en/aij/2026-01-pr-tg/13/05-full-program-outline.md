**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Talk about any blessings they had this past week.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Make a **tent** space with sheets draped over a table or chairs. Children can set up the tent and use camp items to pretend they are part of Abram’s tent-dwelling household.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What do you really look forward to and find it hard to wait for?**

To help the children think about this, spread your set of waiting cards out in a line across your whiteboard ledge or across the floor. Invite them to stand beside the card they would find it the hardest to wait for.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **Why is it hard to wait for something special?**
- **How much easier is it to wait when:**
	- you have a promise to hang on to?
	- you trust the promise giver?
	- the promise giver reminds you of the promise and lets you know it will happen soon?

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) As we learn more about Abram in today’s lesson, we will be reminded of our Big Idea.** Read the Big Idea together: **I can wait patiently for God’s promises to be fulfilled, because God is faithful.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Display your **two balloons** and ask:

- **Is it possible to poke a balloon with a pin and not have it pop?** (No!)
- **Are you sure . . . Really sure?** (Yes!)

Invite a child to use a pin to pop the balloon without tape. At the same time, poke a pin into your balloon in the center of the tape. (It won’t pop!) Exclaim that you’ve done what seemed impossible!

- **How does this activity remind you of God?** (He can do what looks impossible and fulfill His promises, just as He did for Abraham and Sarah.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** We can believe that God will fulfill His promises, even when doing so seems impossible to us. One of God’s big promises is that Jesus will come again and take us to heaven to be with Him.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 13: Baby Isaac**.

Discuss the picture and recite the **memory verse** together. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Hebrews 10:23.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/13-hebrews-10-23.mp3] “Hebrews 10:23” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Noah Jovinov"},{"key":"Cello","value":"Jared Chung"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **How well did Abraham and Sarah put this verse into practice?** (It was hard for them to believe at times, but God helped them to have faith and be hopeful). Give the children their collection of Character Cards from the quarter to take home.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the **Mission Story**, “Praying for a Baby,” and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Play **music** of your choice and invite the children to move around. When the music stops, have them find a **partner** nearby and **share** something they’ve learned or enjoyed in today’s lesson. Repeat several times, ensuring the children have a different partner each time.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your completed **Christian Hospitality Coloring Book** and share instructions for making it.

As the children work on their crafts, explain that God will give them a new name in heaven; it will be written on a white stone (Revelation 2:17), and on a crown (Ellen G. White, The Great Controversy, p. 646) God gives them.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) Put an idea from your Helping Hands craft into action this week to serve another family.** Challenge the children to pray about it and work with their families to adapt an idea to suit them.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring children back together to sing **“God Bless You.”**
