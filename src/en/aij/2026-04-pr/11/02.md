As the centurion spent time with his Jewish friends, he heard about God. Over time he came to believe the religion of the Jews was better than his own. The important leader even helped them build a synagogue to worship God.

The centurion had also come to hear about Jesus. With news of the Miracle Maker sweeping the land, even his own soldiers must have been talking about Jesus. When the centurion heard about His beautiful teachings, a seed of faith was planted in his heart. The more he heard about Jesus’ power and God’s kingdom of love, the more the little seed of faith grew. Roman’s 10:17 explains it this way: “So faith comes by hearing, and hearing by the word of God.” People hear the Good News when someone tells them about Christ.

Now, with his servant so sick, the centurion desperately needed help. He needed a miracle! His belief in Jesus had the opportunity to really grow. Would it? Yes! The kind Roman believed that Jesus was the only one who could heal his beloved servant, even though he had never seen Jesus perform a miracle or heard Him preach. He had the kind of faith Hebrews 11:1 tells us of: “faith is the substance of things hoped for, the evidence of things not yet seen.”

What does God promise us when we show this kind of faith in Him? **Read John 20:29.**

When we hear the good news about Jesus, we can let it grow faith in our hearts, as the centurion did. Then, when we need the help that only Jesus can give, we will believe that He can help and heal us.
