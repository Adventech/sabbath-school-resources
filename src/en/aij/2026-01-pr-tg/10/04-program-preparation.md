Opening Activities

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Make the spiral part of a **^[star mobile]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** using the **^[craft outline]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**. Have **stars** and **glitter** and **glue** ready for your children to make stars.\
Draw a **large tree** on your whiteboard or a piece of posterboard. (You will record names on it to create Noah’s family tree.)\
Update **^[Offering Chart.]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**\
Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
Source **^[Picture PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.\
Create **labels**: “The City of Ur,” “Haran,” and “Canaan/The Promised Land.” Prior to the lesson, attach the “City of Ur” label to the **whiteboard,** and the remaining two labels on other walls above a space where you can gather for subsequent sections of the story. If space is limited, remain below the **whiteboard,** and change the labels during the storytelling.\
Cut paper into one inch (two and a half centimeters) wide **strips** for each child to write their name on, then make into links. Cut another very **long, narrow strip** to thread through the links. **Glue** ends together. Write God on this long strip.\
Source enough **blindfolds** for half of the children.
