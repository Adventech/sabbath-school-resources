**What you need:**

Paper plate, large craft stick or cardboard tube, yellow and green paint or crayons, nontoxic craft glue, tape, and sunflower seeds.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/04/4a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Use yellow paint or a crayon to color paper plate.
- Use green paint or a crayon to color large craft stick or cardboard tube.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/04/4b.png)

- Make cuts around the edge of the paper plate, bending them in and out to look like petals.
- Use nontoxic glue to stick sunflower seeds onto the paper plate.
- Tape craft stick to the back of the paper plate, to serve as a handle/stem.

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/04/4c.png)
