After two days, Jesus finally told His disciples it was time to go to Bethany. “Our friend Lazarus has fallen asleep. But I am going there to wake him” (John 11:11, ICB).

_Oh, wonderful news_, the disciples thought. “If he sleeps he will get well” (John 11:12).

What did Jesus say? **Read John 11:14, 15.**

Their eyes opened wide in shock. _Why had Jesus waited until Lazarus had died?_

They didn’t understand, but they chose to trust Jesus and obey. Quickly they grabbed their things and followed Him down the quiet road to Bethany. Along the way, Jesus took His time to stop and help people. Jesus didn’t need to rush. Nothing, not even death, could stop God from doing what He planned.

They arrived outside Bethany and a messenger ran to find the two sisters. At their home, crowds of crying people were everywhere. Quietly walking inside, the messenger found Martha and whispered in her ear that Jesus was in Bethany.

Martha’s head flicked up. _Jesus had finally arrived!_ She stood up and walked right out the door to meet Him.

As soon as Martha saw the tender, loving face of her Friend, she began to cry. She sobbed, “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died. But even now I know that whatever You ask of God, God will give You.” (John 11:21, 22).

Even though Martha didn’t understand why Jesus had taken so long, she trusted that He could do anything!
