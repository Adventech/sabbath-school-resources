God’s clever plan to stop the tower builders had worked very well. Then to help them understand that they didn’t rule the world as they proudly believed, God sent a massive bolt of lightning to smash the top of the tower and send it toppling to the ground. What an impressive way to remind them that He was in charge! Scared and stripped of their boastful words, people scurried to their homes and shut themselves in. Gradually they found others they could understand. But everyone living together in one place was a nightmare of mix-ups and misunderstandings! What happened next? Read Genesis 11:8.

God had said to Grandfather Noah and his sons, “Fill the earth” (Genesis 9:1). But the city dwellers had ignored God’s wise words, so now His wise plan meant they had to split up and spread out. God was pleased that they would have a fresh chance to learn to depend on Him again as their friend.

God loves for us to depend on Him and follow His instructions. When we depend upon and obey Him, we show God that we choose Him to be the leader of our lives. When we don’t, God doesn’t give up on us. Instead, He uses different ways to help us.
