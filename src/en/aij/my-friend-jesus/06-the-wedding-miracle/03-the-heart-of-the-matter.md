Jesus’ first miracle was all about joy and compassion for human need. He is, after all, the Giver and Creator of joy. He desires for us to be filled with joy, overflowing with His love and peace. For His first miracle He could have marched into the temple and healed everyone in sight, or freed His people from their Roman oppressors, but instead He chose a humble Cana wedding to show He cares about our happiness.

Mary came to Jesus with a problem. This problem was stealing her joy and threatened to disrupt the joy of the wedding celebration. She came full of faith that Jesus could do the impossible. How amazing that Jesus honored Mary’s faith in such a public way! Whatever problems you face, nothing is too small to take to Jesus. He longs to give you, your family, and your friends the joy that only He can give.

The Bible assures us that, “in Your presence is fullness of joy” (Psalm 16:11).
