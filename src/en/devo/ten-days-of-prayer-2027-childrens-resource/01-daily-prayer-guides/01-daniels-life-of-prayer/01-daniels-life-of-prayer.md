### Daniel Talked to God Every Day

> <p></p>
> “Daniel always prayed to God three times every day. He continued to pray and give thanks to God, just as he had always done.” Daniel 6:10 (ICB)

Have you ever had something difficult happen and needed help? Maybe you felt afraid, worried, or unsure what to do. What can we do when those moments come?

Daniel knew the answer: He prayed to God.

Daniel loved God and decided in his heart to always be faithful to Him. Since he was young, Daniel had a special habit: He talked with God every day.

Daniel did not pray only when he had a problem. He prayed in the morning, in the afternoon, and in the evening. He thanked God and asked for His help.

One day, some important people became jealous of Daniel and wanted to stop him from praying. They made a rule that Daniel was not allowed to pray to God. But Daniel loved God, and he did not hide his faith. He opened his windows, knelt, and prayed just as he always did.

Daniel knew that God was with him. Prayer helped Daniel be brave and trust God, even when things were difficult.

We can be like Daniel. We can talk to God every day—not only when we need something, but also to thank Him for His love and care.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> When can you talk to God during your day?

> <p>Remember</p>
> I can pray to God every day!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You for loving me. Help me remember to talk to You every day, just like Daniel. Help me trust in You and be brave when things are difficult. Amen.
