I Can Help\
^[Others]({"style":{"typeface": "Omnes-Bold"}})
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/07/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
Hold your hands tightly. Now, open them wide. Wiggle your fingers as you give a big smile. You have helping hands. God loves you to use your helping hands to do kind things for others. The Bible says, “Do not forget to do good and to share” (Hebrews 13:16). We can use our hands to share our toys. We can use our hands to carry a friend’s bag. We can use our hands to draw a picture to make someone smile. God made us to be helpful.\
Big, stomping elephants are great helpers too. They live in groups called herds and they look after each other. They use their trunks to gently touch elephants who are sad or hurting. They also use their trunks to help baby elephants who get stuck in muddy water holes. They keep their babies in the middle of the herd so they can be safe. Elephants use their tusks to dig up dry water holes so that other animals have water to drink. _Thank You, God, for helpful elephants!_

What sounds do dogs make? _(Woof, woof!)_ Dogs are fun, furry friends. They can also be trained as helpers. Some dogs are trained to be guide dogs. These special dogs help people who can’t see. They show their owners safe places to walk and help warn them if something is in the way. Other dogs are trained as rescue dogs. These dogs have a great sense of smell. They can _sniff, sniff, sniff_, and help find people who are lost or hurt. _Thank You, God, for helpful dogs!_\
Geese _honk-honk_ as they waddle along. These big birds are helpers too! When winter comes, they fly a long, long way to warmer places. They fly in a very special pattern shaped like a “v” _(point to picture)_. When they do this, the bird at the front does lots of hard work so the birds behind him don’t have to fly so hard. They take turns being the leader so that they can all have a little rest. They also fly in a “v” to keep everyone in the group safe. _Thank You, God, for helpful geese!_

What are some ways you can help others? You are not too little to be a helper! God has given you helping hands. When we share our toys or let others go first on the playground, we are helping others. When we hug a friend who is sad, we are helping others and showing God’s love to them.\
_(Say together)_ Dear God, please come into my heart. Help me to use my helpful hands to help others.
