Quietly the baby was wrapped in a cloth and handed to his mother. Everyone in the little mud-brick house smiled with joy. His birth was such wonderful news, but it was a secret and couldn’t be shared with anyone. Shh!

Baby Moses must stay secretly hidden in their home—no crying and no cuddles out in the sunshine or walks on the dusty streets. Father Amram, Mother Jochebed, big sister Miriam, and brother Aaron must keep him safe. Pharaoh had ordered that all Hebrew baby boys should be killed. But that must never happen to Moses—ever! How long did Moses’ family keep him hidden? **Read Exodus 2:2.**

God had promised Abraham, “I will make you a great nation” (Genesis 12:2). The Israelite slaves weren’t doing so great in Egypt, and that wasn’t the land God had promised them. Canaan was the Promised Land (Genesis 12:5). God planned to free His people and take them there. He already had a leader in mind to take them to freedom.

Mother Jochebed didn’t know it yet, but God had chosen baby Moses to do a big work for Him! When Moses was all grown up, he would free the Israelites from slavery and lead them out of Egypt. It would be a very big job, but God would prepare him. In fact, God had already begun. He had given Moses godly parents, a loving sister Miriam, and a big brother Aaron. God would use them to help prepare Moses too, for that’s what God intended families to do.

- What do you think Moses’ family might do to help prepare him for God’s big work?
- How does your family help prepare you to serve God?
