**Big Idea:** God is loving. I will want to obey Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Rock a baby doll when introducing Cain, Abel, and Seth.
- Use Cain and Abel “peg people,” if desired.
- Use the cardboard altar.
- Show the children a toy baby lamb and invite them to “pat” it.
- Gently place the toy lamb on the “altar” and use red/yellow cellophane or fabric to “swallow” it when fire from heaven burns the lamb sacrifice.
- Use real or toy fruit and vegetables. Invite children to place them on the “altar.”
- Use the **^[Children’s Faces]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** throughout the story. Invite children to point to an emotion that a character is feeling. Look for the “point-to-expression” prompt to know when to use this prop.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 6 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**).
- Use a felt set, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Adam and Eve were so happy—they had baby Cain, then baby Abel ^[(point to facial expressions, baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God loved them! He would take away their sins and save them. As a promise and reminder of what Jesus would one day do, they were to bring a lamb sacrifice to the altar—God would forgive and burn it up ^[(lamb, pretend fire)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Adam and Eve obeyed God because they loved Him ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Cain grew up to be a good farmer. But Cain didn’t listen to others or obey—he thought his way was best ^[(peg people, fruit and vegetable, point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Abel grew up to be a good shepherd. He chose to obey God and his parents—it made him happy ^[(peg people, lamb, point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Abel obeyed God and brought a lamb to the altar. Cain brought fruit—he didn’t obey!
- God’s fire burned up Abel’s lamb, but it didn’t burn up Cain’s food ^[(pretend fire on altar)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Cain got very angry with Abel. He hurt him, and Abel died ^[(point to expressions)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God asked Cain where Abel was. Cain lied. God told Cain he would have to live far, far away.
- Adam and Eve were so sad to lose Abel and Cain. God gave them baby Seth, who loved and obeyed God ^[(point to expressions)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam and Eve were so happy ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had given them a sweet little baby ^[(rock doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They named him Cain. He grew and grew, until he was a little boy who could run and jump. One exciting day Cain became a big brother to a tiny baby boy named Abel! ^[(Point to expression.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God loved Adam and Eve and their boys ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Sin had caused sadness, pain, and death. But God had a plan to save them! ^[(Point to expression.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God asked Adam and Eve to build an altar ^[(cardboard box “altar”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They were to place a perfect lamb ^[(toy lamb)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) on top and tell God they were sorry for their sins. God would forgive them and send down fire to burn the sacrifice ^[(cover the lamb with cellophane or fabric “fire”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Adam and Eve obeyed God because they loved Him ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They came with Cain and Abel to the family altar, bringing a lamb. This special offering reminded them of God’s promise. God’s Son would be just like that lamb—perfect and pure ^[(invite children to pat the lamb)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus would come and die on the cross so that sins could be forgiven.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Cain and Abel grew up to be big, strong men, who did their jobs well. Cain was a very good farmer. He grew yummy fruits and vegetables for the whole family ^[(display fruit and vegetables)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). But Cain chose not to listen to anyone else because he thought his way was best ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Abel grew up to be a good, kind shepherd ^[(pat the toy lamb)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He chose to obey God and his parents. Following God’s rules made him so happy ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One morning Abel took a lamb to the altar, just as God had asked him to do ^[(place the lamb on the box)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He knew that God is loving, so Abel wanted to obey Him and have his sins forgiven. God sent down a blazing fire from heaven to show Abel that He had forgiven him ^[(cover the lamb with pretend “fire”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Abel was so thankful ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Then it was Cain’s turn. But he didn’t put a lamb on the altar; he put fruit instead. Fruit? That wasn’t what God had asked for! Fruit showed only what Cain could grow. ^[(Invite children to place the fruits or vegetables on the box.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was nothing like the lamb offering that showed what God’s Son would do one day.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Cain waited and waited for the fire from heaven . . . but there was nothing. Cain noticed Abel’s altar burning brightly, and he “became very angry” (Genesis 4:5, ICB). ^[(Point to expression.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With kind words Abel reminded Cain to choose a lamb sacrifice that would please God, but Cain wouldn’t listen. Instead, he just got angrier at Abel.

Cain let his hate grow bigger and bigger. Then his anger exploded ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He hurt Abel so badly that Abel died ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God saw what had happened. “Where is your brother Abel?” God asked Cain.

“I don’t know,” Cain lied (Genesis 4:9, ICB). God told Cain that he would have to leave his home and live far away in a land called Nod.

Adam and Eve were so sad. Their loving Abel was gone, and Cain had to go far away ^[(point to expression)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Cain’s bad choices had hurt everyone. God was close to Adam and Eve when they were sad. He loved them very much. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He gave them another precious baby boy, Seth ^[(rock doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). As Seth grew up, he loved God.
