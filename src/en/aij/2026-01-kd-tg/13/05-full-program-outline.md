**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal two balloons, tape, and a safety pin. Blow the balloons up and tie them.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What will happen if I stick this pin into this balloon?** (Pop!)
- **Could I stick the pin into this other balloon and not have it pop?** (That would be impossible! It could not happen!)

Place a two-inch (five-centimeter) piece of thick tape on the balloon, and then stick the pin in through the tape. (The balloon will not pop.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** What we thought was impossible became possible with a piece of sticky tape. Just like the tape, **God makes impossible things possible**. When He promises something, we can trust His words, because He is always faithful.

In today’s Bible story our faithful God did something impossible!

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is loving. He is always faithful.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Baby Isaac”** in an interactive and engaging way using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** If possible, divide the class into **three groups** and give three adults one of the following questions on a slip of paper. Have the three groups rotate to each adult and discuss their question with them.

- **How did God help Abraham and Sarah to wait patiently?** (Showed Abraham the stars and reminded him of the promise. Visited to tell Abraham he would have a son.)
- **How did God show that He was faithful in the story?** (His promise came true. Sarah had baby Isaac.)
- **How do you act when you have to wait patiently for something?** (Invite children to mime their answer.)

Play your **bell** or **sound maker** to call the children back together and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a Bible with a colorful ribbon marked at 2 Peter 3:13 and the matching **Promise Picture Card**.

- **What special promise are we faithfully waiting for that God has given us?**

Open the Bible and read 2 Peter 3:13 aloud: “But God made a promise to us. And we are waiting for what He promised—a new heaven and a new earth where goodness lives” (ICB). Invite the children to share what they like about what they see on the **Promise Picture Card**.

**Teaching Point:** Explain that God was faithful to Abraham and Sarah. His special promise to them came true! God’s promises to us will come true too, because He is faithful. Repeat the Big Idea: God is loving. He is always faithful.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 13: Penguin** and share the following facts:

- Penguins love their families a lot.
- Penguins wave their flippers when they are happy to see each other!
- Males find shiny pebbles to bring as a special gift to their female partner.
- Mother and father penguins take turns faithfully looking after their baby penguins.

God is amazing to have created such faithful little penguin families that love and look after each other for all their lives. Give each child **Nature Card 13** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}}) Share the mission story**: “Mother and the King Cobra.”
