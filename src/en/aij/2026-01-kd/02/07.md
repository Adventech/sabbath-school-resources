_(**Parents:** Use the following as a guide to spend time with your child outside, in awe of God’s nature.)_

**^[LISTEN:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#f26a5c"}}})** Close your eyes and have Daddy or Mommy lead you out to the middle of your yard, or a park nearby. Then sit down. Be quiet. What sounds do you hear? Maybe rustling leaves, or bird songs?

**^[LOOK UP:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994cc"}}})** Now open your eyes! Lie down and look up into the sky. What do you see? Do you see any clouds? What shapes do you see? Do you see any birds? Count them! When you see two of the same kind, think about how much God loves to make animals and people to be together.

**^[LOOK DOWN:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#8ac3a7"}}})** Now roll over onto your stomach. Look very, very closely in the grass, and you may be able to watch a whole new world! What do you see? Can you see any little bugs? Maybe an ant colony? Watch how the bugs climb all over the grass.

**^[LOOK AROUND:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})** Go for a walk. Look at everything God has made for you! All of creation tells us more about God and His love for you. He knew that some kids would like to collect colored leaves or different-shaped rocks. He knew other kids would like to pick up interesting bugs. God knew that some would love to climb trees, and that many would love to explore. He thought of YOU when creating all the wonderful things you see!

Every time you go outside, or every time you see beautiful pictures of nature, remember that God created the earth as a gift for you and others. What a creative, loving God He is!
