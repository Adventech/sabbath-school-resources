---
title: How to Use This Book
---

^[1. Read and Learn:]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Read ^[Section 1]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#36659F", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) of the book to learn why and how to make disciples using Christ’s method. Gain practical ideas for each phase of disciple-making based on the biblical analogy of the agricultural growth cycle.

^[2. Think and Pray:]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Think of everyone you know who needs friendship, salvation, Bible truth, or nurture and training. Write down these names on the Interest List in ^[Section 2]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#36659F", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) and pray for them each day.

^[3. Plan and Do:]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Plan how to reach out to your neighbors, church visitors, and other interests using the disciple-making logs provided in ^[Section 3]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#36659F", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). Do both planned and unplanned activities and record your efforts in the appropriate log. If you run out of blank lists or logs in this book, get free downloads at globaltmi.org.

^[4. Reflect:]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Reflect regularly on your disciple-making efforts and experiences and record your testimonies and reflections in the journal pages provided in ^[Section 4]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#36659F", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}).

{"style":{"block":{"backgroundColor": "#DEEBF1","rounded": true, "padding": {"top":"base","bottom":"base","start":"base","end":"base"}}}}
^[NOTE:]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) You can choose to start with only one or two names on your interest list and one or two activities on a disciple-making log, and then add to them over time. Don’t feel overwhelmed or pressured to use all the blank lines and pages in this journal. The most important thing is to ^[GET STARTED!]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) Those who are highly active, on the other hand, may run out of space. If so, you may print supplemental pages at [globaltmi.org](https://globaltmi.org) or simply start a new journal. Some may find it helpful to use a new journal every year.
