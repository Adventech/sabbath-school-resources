**Word of the Week**

**Remember:** To remember means to bring into your mind something that has happened in the past. You might remember a fun game you played yesterday or how to tie your shoelaces. These are things your mind knows from the past and remembers. God wants us to always remember the loving and caring things He has done for us.

**Lesson Summary**

When the sanctuary was finished, it was filled with the cloud of God’s glory. He would live with His people, as promised. The people were so happy. They remembered all the good things He had done for them: the burning bush and God’s promise to free His people and lead them to the Promised Land, His protection through the plagues and the Passover night, that He had parted the Red Sea so they could escape their enemy forever, that He had led and protected them with the pillar of cloud and fire, and that He had sent them manna and water to meet their needs. They could trust Him in the future.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** and remember that God is good and faithful.
- **Feel** grateful to remember what God has done for them.
- **Respond** by trusting God with all the things that are to come.
