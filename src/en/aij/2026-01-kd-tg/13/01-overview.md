**Word of the Week**

**Impossible:** When something isn’t able to happen, it’s impossible. For example, it’s impossible for humans to fly on their own. (Surprise: God makes impossible things happen!)

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Three men visited Abraham and Sarah’s home. They told them that Sarah would have a baby boy. Sarah laughed because she thought it was impossible. She was too old to have a baby, she thought. God fulfilled His promise, and baby Isaac was born to Sarah and Abraham. Abraham and Sarah loved their new baby very much. This was the beginning of the big family that God had promised them.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is faithful and keeps His promises.
- **Feel** that nothing is impossible for God to do.
- **Respond** by trusting God to help them each day.
