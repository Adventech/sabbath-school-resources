**WEEK 4: THE RED SEA**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: “Moses” and other peg people (Israelites), blue piece of fabric, wooden blocks, toy cows/ sheep, flashlight, pre-made cloud (cotton balls, glue, cardboard, red cellophane), musical instruments

Show your children “Moses,” peg people, toy animals, and cloud as you describe this scene. Say: **Moses marched out of Egypt with a smile. God had set His people free! After the many times that the king kept saying no, the Bible tells us that he finally said, “Get up and leave” (Exodus 12:31, ICB). There were so many people! There were so many animals! God went with them too. God sent a cloud in the daytime that gave shade from the hot sun. At night the cloud turned into fire that kept them warm. The people could follow God’s cloud, and He would be their leader.**

Place a blue piece of fabric on the floor. Invite children to help you “walk” Moses and other peg people with the blocks and animals to the edge of the fabric. Talk about what it would be like to walk with so many people and animals. Raise up the cloud pillar and shine a flashlight on it.

Say: **But the king quickly changed his mind. He told his army to bring all the people back! The people were camped by the sea and were afraid. They couldn’t go forward because there was a big sea in front of them. What could they do?**

Open your Bible, point, and say: **The Bible tells us that Moses said to the people, “Don’t be afraid”** (Exodus 14:13, ICB). **God told Moses to raise his “stick and hold it over the sea”** (Exodus 14:16, ICB). **A strong wind began to blow. Can you help me make a wind noise?** (Invite the children to make wind sounds with you.) **God is so mighty that He made a great wind to blow. It piled up water on one side and then the other. God opened a path in the middle of the sea! The people could walk safely across.**

Make a path through the center of the fabric, inviting the children to help you “walk” the blocks and animals through the sea, then close the path together. Play musical instruments together to talk about how the people praised God.

Say together: **God leads me.**
