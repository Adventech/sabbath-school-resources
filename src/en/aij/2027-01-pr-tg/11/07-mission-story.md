**Focus:** When I see God’s love in action, I will want to commit to Him.

**Location:** Help a child to locate **Ecuador** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Maria closed her eyes and began to pray. Her 5-year old sister, Angel, was very sick. She had a large tumor in her liver. “God, please save the life of my little sister,” Maria prayed.

Maria, age 21, had never prayed to God before.

When Angel’s teacher learned about the tumor, she also prayed. She asked her church to pray. She was the wife of a Seventh-day Adventist pastor.

The prayers touched Maria’s heart. She couldn’t understand why a church would pray for a little girl they didn’t know.

As the date approached for Angel’s surgery, Maria prayed even more. She promised to go to the Adventist church if Angel recovered. “If You heal her, we’ll go to that church,” she prayed.

The surgery went well, and Angel recovered completely after a month in the hospital. Their prayers to God had been clearly answered!

After Angel returned home, her teacher and her pastor-husband began visiting the family regularly. They gave many hugs. They asked about their well-being. They showed real interest in their lives, and they kept returning.

Maria had never seen such a loving couple, and she saw Jesus’ love in a whole new light.

After two years she and her family gave their hearts to Jesus. They showed their commitment to Him by being baptized. Even little Angel, who was 7, was baptized.

Maria was a social worker, helping children, and she wondered what she would do next. God had done so much for her, and she wanted to do something more for Him.

One day a friend asked, “Would you like to move to another country to work as a missionary?”

Maria thought about it. Then she attended a church meeting during which the speaker asked the same question. Maria prayed, “Here I am. Send me, Lord.”

Maria went as a teacher to Ecuador. At first it was difficult for her to adjust to life in a new country. She missed her parents and two sisters terribly, and life wasn’t very easy for her. But she stayed committed to God. And He gave her new gifts: once shy, she was able to boldly share her story with others.

- **How did God show His love to Maria and her family?**
- **How did Maria show her commitment to Him?**
