Empty. Empty. Empty. The wedding servant shook his head as he peered into another dry, empty pitcher that had once held the sweet grape juice (unfermented wine). _How did this happen? How did the grape juice run out so quickly?_

Inside, the servants could hear the happy music and the laughing, chatting people. The party was nowhere near finished. The bride and groom would be so upset if they knew the grape juice was all gone. The guests would be very disappointed too. Oh, no!

The servants frowned and whispered to each other. _How would they find enough grape juice to last the rest of the wedding party?_ There were no shops to go to. There was no way to get any more grape juice. But they had so many guests!

Soon Mary heard about their big problem. She knew that she could trust big problems to her even bigger God. What does the Bible say about impossible problems? **Read Matthew 19:26.**

God could provide for their needs.

Looking around the room, Mary saw Jesus talking with His disciple friends. She hurried over and stood in front of Him. In a quiet voice Mary said to Jesus, “They have no wine” (John 2:3).

Mary knew she could go to Jesus for help. You can too! No problem is too big for Him.
