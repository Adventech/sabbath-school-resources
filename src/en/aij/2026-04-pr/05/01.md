Compassion for the Crowds

Like metal to a magnet, huge crowds of people were attracted to Jesus. Some were just drawn to His healing power. But many wanted to be close to Him because He showed them how much God loved and cared for them. Before they met Jesus, their lives had felt hopelessly hard. The Roman rulers were cruel. They taxed the people unfairly, and tried to keep people poor and powerless. The temple priests didn’t treat people fairly and didn’t care for their spiritual needs very well.

Jesus could see exactly how the people were hurting. “He was moved with compassion for them, because they were weary and scattered, like sheep having no shepherd” (Matthew 9:36). What did He do for those crowds of weary and scattered followers? **Read the last part of Mark 6:34.** What else did He do? **Read Matthew 14:14.**

Moved by compassion, Jesus taught and healed; healed and taught. And slowly the people began to understand God’s love. Their minds began to heal, their hearts began to heal, and their bodies were healed too. Their lives had new meaning, all because Jesus was moved with compassion for them.

The Bible tells us in James 5:11, “The Lord is very compassionate and merciful.” Many people today don’t know this; they feel alone, hurt, and hopeless. Like the people in Jesus’ day, they need to see and experience God’s compassion.

This week we will learn more about how Jesus showed compassion to individual people within the crowds. His example will help us understand how we can treat others with compassion.
