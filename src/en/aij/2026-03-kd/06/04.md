The people by the Jordan River felt so happy. They were sorry for their sins, and God had forgiven them! John baptized them in the river to show everyone that God had cleansed their hearts and made them new.

But not everyone was happy. The important Jewish rulers in

Jerusalem wanted to know what was going on out there in the desert. They sent priests to ask John the Baptist, “Who are you? . . . What do you say about yourself?” (John 1:22).

John told them firmly, “I am not the Christ.” But John told them that the Savior would be coming soon! “I am not good enough to untie the strings of his sandals” (John 1:20, 27, ICB).

The Savior was coming soon! John’s words quickly spread all through the land. His message spread to the poor people living in the hills, and the fishermen by distant seas.

It even made it to the ears of a humble carpenter in Nazareth. When Jesus heard what John the Baptist had been saying, He put down His tools right away. He gave His mother a big goodbye hug and left Nazareth.

Where was Jesus going? **Read Matthew 3:13.**

The people by the Jordan didn’t know it yet, but Jesus was on His way. The carpenter from Nazareth would soon start a new job. It would be a job that God had planned for Jesus to do a long, long time ago. Jesus wasn’t just a man from Nazareth; Jesus was God’s Son. And Jesus was coming soon!
