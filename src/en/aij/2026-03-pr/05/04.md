The bells on the bottom of the high priest’s blue robe jingled softly as he walked toward the holy place. Jesus watched the priests in the courtyard performing their duties and saw the bleeding Passover lamb on the altar. The more Jesus watched, the more clearly He saw that these things were symbols of what would happen to Him. He had come to this earth to be the lamb and die one day. Such big thoughts made Him quiet.

God’s Son, the Creator of the twinkling stars and our wonderful world, is all-powerful, and knows all. While it’s hard to wrap our minds around this, He has also always existed, living with God the Father in heaven. But in a beautiful act of unselfishness, God the Son left it all behind. Now here He was as a human boy named Jesus, sitting in the temple, watching, thinking, and learning things about Himself and His future work on earth.

In a side room at the temple, the rabbis (religious teachers) taught their students. Jesus settled at their feet and listened carefully. He asked deep questions about God’s Word. Then the rabbis turned to Him with questions. They were “astonished at His understanding and answers” (Luke 2:47), for He understood God’s promises about the Messiah better than they did! Their hearts were touched.

Later, when Jesus was a grown man, He tried to teach the Jewish leaders again. Sadly, many didn’t want to listen to the truths Jesus shared. What did Jesus say would happen to those who want to learn about God’s goodness? **Read Matthew 5:6.**

Jesus still loves to teach you and fill you up with God’s goodness. He says, “As newborn babies want milk, you should want the pure and simple teaching. By it you can mature in your salvation. For you have already examined and seen how good the Lord is” (1 Peter 2:2, 3, ICB).
