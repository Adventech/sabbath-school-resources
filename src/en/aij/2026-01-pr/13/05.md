Abraham and Sarah would have a son! The incredible news must have made Abraham’s lonely heart nearly burst. But when Sarah heard the words of promise, she thought they sounded crazy! How could a 90-year-old woman ever have a baby? Impossible! And how could a very, very old man become a dad? Never! The very idea made Sarah laugh to herself.

No one heard her laugh, no one saw her laugh, but God knew about it. What did He say about her laugh? **Read Genesis 18:13.**

Filled with fear and shame at being found out, Sarah lied, “I did not laugh.” The Lord responded kindly but seriously, “No, but you did laugh!” (Genesis 18:15).

Sarah’s laugh and her lie must have saddened God, for His Word says that it is never good to lie (Proverbs 12:22). Sarah had been waiting 24 years after their arriving in Canaan for a baby to fulfill God’s promise, and that’s a long, long time to wait. Doubt had crept silently into Sarah’s heart, and now it burst out as a laugh.

But the laughter didn’t last. It was replaced by hope and joy as Sarah remembered God’s question “Is anything too hard for the Lord? No! I will return to you at the right time a year from now. And Sarah will have a son” (Genesis 18:14, ICB). Yes, God would fulfill His promise. The time had come.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How did God know Sarah laughed?_
- _Have you ever felt fear and shame after you were caught lying? What did you do to make things right?_

Lying is no small thing to God. Did you know that God cannot lie (Hebrews 6:18), because He is truth (John 14:6)? When we make mistakes, as Sarah did, God understands and helps us to make things right. When our hearts feel weighed down, He can use that to help us tell the truth and say we’re sorry. He sent Jesus to die for us so that He could forgive our mistakes and help us to do the right thing. Nothing is too hard for our God!
