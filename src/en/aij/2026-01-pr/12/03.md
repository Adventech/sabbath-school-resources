Somehow one of the captives made a daring escape from the clutches of the enemy kings. Thinking quickly he dashed up into the hills of Mamre to share the bad news with Abram. Puffing and barely pausing for breath, the runner told the terrible story of the battle and how Lot and his family had been captured. Imagine how Uncle Abram took this news.

Upon hearing the dreadful story, Abram decided immediately that Lot must be rescued. He could have done nothing, and let Lot suffer for his unwise choices. But he didn’t. Abram’s big heart loved Lot too much for that. The Bible tells us, “Whenever you are able, do good to people who need help” (Proverbs 3:27, ICB). Lot sure needed help right now!

The first thing Abram did was pray, for he knew he would be risking his life to rescue his nephew. But he also knew he could trust God to show him what to do, just as he had many times before. Then what did Abram do? Read Genesis 14:14.

These 318 men were Abram’s loyal servants. They loved God, respected Abram, and knew how to defend themselves against invaders in the hills. Three neighboring rulers joined in with their armed servants too. Together the newly formed army marched toward Dan on their dangerous mission. Lot’s captors far outnumbered them, but Abram’s men were brave. After all, they had God on their side. And He is very wise and powerful.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Why do you think Abram was so willing to risk his life to rescue Lot?_
- _How willing would you have been to join Abram on his rescue mission?_

When we pray to our powerful and wise God, He will show us what to do to help others who may be in trouble.
