**Word of the Week**

**Miracle:** A miracle is an extraordinary event or happening caused by God; something people could never ever do or explain.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores Jesus’ first miracle, turning water into wine at a wedding He attended with His disciples. Jesus’ mother was also at the wedding. When she learned that the wine was all gone, she told Jesus about the problem. She then told the servants to do whatever Jesus asked of them. Jesus told the servants to fill six large jars with water and then pour some out for the host of the feast. As they poured, the water was turned into wine, and the master claimed it was the best wine of all! Through this miracle, Jesus revealed His glory. He also demonstrated His love for humanity and desire to bring us happiness.

The children will:

- **Respond** by sharing their faith with others, telling them of the great things God has done.
