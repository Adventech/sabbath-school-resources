**Focus:** God shows His unconditional love through a missionary teacher.

**Location:** Help a child locate the **Marshall Islands** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Lomon was a 5-year-old boy who lived in the Marshall Islands. Every day he struggled to sit still and listen to the teacher. He wanted to be out in the street, playing with friends who weren’t in school yet. He began to pinch and punch the other children.

The teacher gave him a time-out, moving him away from the other children until he calmed down. But Lomon started howling like a wolf. “Ooooooo!” he howled. “Oo-oo-oo-ooooooo!” The teacher took Lomon to the principal, but his behavior stayed the same.

The teacher finally spoke with Lomon’s aunt. She learned that the boy didn’t live with his parents and that his family life was really hard.

The teacher’s heart felt sad for Lomon. She realized that he needed a lot of love and attention.

The teacher felt impressed to pray every day, not only for Lomon but also for each student, their families, and for God to fill the classroom with His love. She made a list of names and prayed for each student and family member by name every morning and evening. She was very determined and knew that God would help her children’s hearts to change.

At school the teacher kept Lomon after class when he was disobedient, and she prayed with him every time. Lomon didn’t know how to pray, so she taught him how.

One day after praying after all the other children had left, the boy said, “Miss, can I please push in all the chairs?” He wanted to help his teacher for the first time!

Within two weeks of starting to pray, the teacher noticed a big difference in her classroom.

Lomon began to clean up when other children made a mess. He tried to stop arguments by standing between the arguing children and asking them to hug each other instead of fight. The other children also started to show kindness. They learned how to say “I’m sorry” and “Please forgive me.” Those who were wronged learned to reply, “I forgive you,” and offer a hug. The teacher had been able to show her class God’s lovingkindness, and God’s love had changed them.

- **How was God able to use the teacher to show His lovingkindness?**
