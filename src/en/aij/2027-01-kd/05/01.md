God’s Fire and Cloud

Thousands of children and families hurried along together as they left Egypt. Their families marched together, one after another, as they left Egypt. Goats, sheep, cows, and donkeys all trotted along too. People smiled as they moved with the huge crowd. God had set His people free!

Their sandals made dusty footprints in the dry sand. On and on they walked. Soon their homes were far behind them. God was leading them to a new home—a Promised Land.

As they walked, everyone watched something at the very front of the crowd. It was something they had never seen before. It was a tall, tall cloud.

This was God’s special cloud. He used this cloud to show them He was leading them—He was in the cloud. They could trust God’s cloud to lead them to the Promised Land. The big cloud would also give the people shade from the hot desert sun.

What happened at night? **Read Psalm 105:39.**

They could trust God to protect and lead them on this big adventure.

His cloud gave cool shade during the day, and the fire gave light and warmth at night.

The Bible also says, “The pillar of cloud was always with them during the day. And the pillar of fire was always with them at night” (Exodus 13:22, ICB). He never left them alone on their journey to the Promised Land.

The same God is close to you, too, guiding and protecting you!
