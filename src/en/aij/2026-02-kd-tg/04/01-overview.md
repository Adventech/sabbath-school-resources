**Lesson Summary**

Jacob arrived at his family’s town of Haran. He met kind Rachel at the well, then went to stay with her father, his Uncle Laban. As payment for his excellent work for Laban, Jacob offered to work for seven years to marry Rachel. After seven years Laban tricked Jacob into marrying Rachel’s older sister, Leah. Jacob learned what it felt like to be on the receiving end of being tricked, but he didn’t get angry, as Esau had. Laban proposed that Jacob work another seven years for Rachel, which he did. Jacob did his best. God blessed Jacob with children, and Laban with large flocks. As payment to continue working, Jacob asked for all the spotted sheep and goats. Despite Laban trying to cheat him, God increased Jacob’s flocks. When Laban’s sons became jealous, God directed Jacob in a dream to return home. God was true to His promise to be with Jacob, despite challenges in his life.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God’s love and forgiveness makes us want to do our best in every situation.
- **Feel** that God is with them, even when things are hard or unfair.
- **Respond** by asking God to be in their hearts so that they can work joyfully for Him.
