**Word of the Week**

**Change:** Change means to make something different. You can change clothes and put on different clothes. Or you might make a different choice to play outside instead of inside—you could change your mind. God can change us so we are loving, faithful Christians.

**Lesson Summary**

Moses grew up in the royal palace to be a strong leader and prince. In anger he killed an Egyptian slave master and then fled Egypt. God looked after him and led him to the land of Midian. Moses calmly confronted an injustice at a well, helping the daughters of a priest—God was already changing him. Moses stayed with their family and worked as a shepherd for the next 40 years, learning to follow God more closely. One day Moses saw a burning bush and heard God speak to him. God called him to go back to Egypt and ask Pharaoh to let His people, who were slaves, go. God assured him that He was with Moses. Moses was afraid. God continued to show Moses that He would make this happen. Moses followed God.
