Mya Htet (a pseudonym) smiled as she listened to her teacher tell a Bible story. Mya Htet was in the eighth grade and had just arrived at the Taw Oo Adventist School (a pseudonym) in Myanmar. Her parents were Buddhists, and she had never heard such wonderful, true stories before.

As Mya Htet went to the prayer meetings and worship services, and as her teacher shared stories from the Bible, something started to change inside her heart.

It took time. Mya Htet’s family did not want her to become a Christian. But one day, during a week of prayer program, Mya Htet stood and gave her heart to Jesus. She was then baptized. She didn’t tell her parents, but they soon found out and were furious!

When Mya Htet went home, her parents demanded that she give up her new Christian beliefs. At first, they offered to give her whatever she wanted, if she would give up her faith in God. But when she refused, they got very angry. They beat her and locked her in a room.

While she was locked in the room, Mya Htet prayed to God and read the promises in her Bible. But when her family saw that she had a Bible, they grabbed it from her and began trying to tear it to pieces.

They pulled at the paper but somehow, the pages wouldn’t tear! Confused, her parents ran over to the fireplace and threw the Bible into it. Mya Htet’s horror turned to amazement when she saw that the Bible wouldn’t burn!

Her parents couldn’t believe their eyes, but they wouldn’t give up. Full of anger, they grabbed the Bible out of the fire and threw it into a bucket of water. But when they pulled it out, there wasn’t even a drop of water on the pages. Her parents saw they couldn’t destroy her Bible, so they locked her again in the room.

When Mya Htet saw that her Bible hadn’t been destroyed, her faith grew even stronger. She knew that God’s promises were true. She would keep trusting Him! She prayed and waited patiently to see what God would do.

God is always faithful, and soon after, when her parents left the house, a kind relative came and unlocked the door. Quickly, Mya Htet grabbed a few belongings and ran to hide in a field until someone from the school came to pick her up. They drove her back to the school.

Some years later, Mya Htet became a teacher in an Adventist school. She loves to tell others how God’s promises never fail.
