As God looked at the earth, His heart was filled with pain. “Violence was everywhere. And God saw this evil. All people on the earth did only evil” (Genesis 6:11, 12, ICB).

But God still had one true friend in the world. His name was Noah. The Bible says, “Noah was a good man. He was the most innocent man of his time. He walked with God” (Genesis 6:9, ICB).

Like Enoch, Noah made God his best friend. Noah loved God with all his heart, with all his soul, and with all his strength. Noah knew that God is loving, and Noah was faithful to Him.

God talked with Noah and told him about His plan to rescue the world from sin. God had already promised Adam and Eve that He would send Jesus to die for our sins. But the world was so evil that there would soon be no God followers left.

So God needed to make the world clean and new. “People have made the earth full of violence . . .” God explained to Noah sadly. “I will bring a flood of water on the earth. I will destroy all living things” (Genesis 6:13-17, ICB)

He asked His friend Noah to help Him. Noah listened closely as God told him what he must make. What were the instructions? **Read Genesis 6:14-16.**

This big boat would be called an ark. Even though the people in the world didn’t love God, God still loved them. He was still faithful. He would invite everyone into the ark to be safe from the coming flood.
