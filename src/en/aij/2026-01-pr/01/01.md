Our God

Have you ever wondered what it might be like to see God? (Revelation 1:12-16.) Have you ever wondered why God made the world, or what His plan is for your future? (Jeremiah 29:11-13.) When you think of God, what comes to your mind? What are some questions you would like to know the answers to?

We can find answers to many of our questions in the Bible; the most wonderful, true book in the whole world. The Bible tells us about where sin came from. It tells us about the beginning of our world, the many things that have happened since the world began, and how Jesus came to save us from our sins. Most important, the Bible tells us about who God is and how much He loves us.

Open your Bible, and you’ll see that the very first book is called Genesis, which means “beginning.” The first words in the Bible say, “In the beginning God . . .”

Yes, God was there at the very beginning of everything. It’s hard to imagine that because for us, everything has a beginning and an end. But not God. God was there before everything. He’s always been, and He always will be (Revelation 1:8). God knows everything—about everything, including everything about you! And He wants us to know Him too!

When we read our Bibles, we see that God has many attributes. Each attribute tells us something about what He is like. What is the word that those in heaven use to praise and describe God more than any other word? Read Revelation 4:8 (second part). Yes, this word, “holy,” means that God is good and pure, and there is not even one little bit of evil, sin, or selfishness in Him.

Because God is holy, He is also love. What does 1 John 4:8, 16 tell us?

God doesn’t just love us—He is love! God’s love only ever wants what is good for us. God invites every boy and girl, every mom and dad, to know Him and love Him in return. To do this, we need to read about God in His Bible, talk to Him, and be close to Him. Is this something you’d like to do?
