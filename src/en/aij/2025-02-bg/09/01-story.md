It was the end of a big day. The sky turned orange, then pink. Soon it would be bedtime. It had been a big day, but still the people by the lake asked Jesus for more stories. Have you ever asked for “just one more story . . . please”? Jesus climbed into a boat so that everyone could hear. He loved telling stories about God’s love! But finally, He had to say goodbye. “Let us cross over to the other side of the lake,” Jesus told His friends (Luke 8:22). The boat sailed away. The stars began to twinkle as the sky turned dark. Jesus was very tired. He stretched out and lay down in the back of the boat. The boat rocked softly back and forth on gentle waves (slowly rock your child). A soft breeze tickled His face. It was so peaceful, and Jesus fell fast asleep (lay head on hands with palms together).

Suddenly big clouds covered the moon. The gentle breeze began to blow stronger (blow, making sound of the wind). The waves rose higher. Still, Jesus slept. The boat began rocking faster and faster (rock quickly). Then big waves crashed into the boat (clap). Jesus’ friends got very wet and very worried! Was Jesus worried? (Shake head side to side.) No, Jesus wasn’t worried. He slept peacefully. The waves crashed (clap hands), and the wind howled. The disciples felt scared! Would the boat sink? “Teacher!” they cried. Jesus opened His eyes. He saw they were scared. He saw that they had forgotten to trust in God. He stood up and spoke to the wind and the waves, “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:35)

The wind suddenly stopped. (Whisper . . .) The water became quiet and still. All was calm. The disciples were amazed. They said, “Who can this be, that even the wind and the sea obey Him?” (Mark 4:41). This man was Jesus (point up), the Son of God. He had created the wind and the waves. They didn’t need to worry.\
(Say together) Thank You, Jesus, for being with me always.
