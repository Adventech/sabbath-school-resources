The world was in a huge mess! Adam and Eve had eaten the fruit and disobeyed God. Now sin and death spread everywhere. But God and His Son loved the world too much to leave us to die. As soon as Adam and Eve disobeyed, God and Jesus had a plan.

What was that plan? Read John 3:16.

This is the best news ever! Jesus loved sinful people so much that He came into the world as a little baby. He grew up and then died to save us. One day He’ll come back to this world to take those who love Him to be with Him forever in heaven and the earth made new. The Bible says, “How wide and how long and how deep [His] love is” (Ephesians 3:18, ICB).

God loved Adam and Eve when they chose to obey and when they didn’t. They had to live with the consequences, but He’d always love them and be there to help them every day. Jesus loves you that much too. Even if you were the only person in the whole world, Jesus would have come and died to save _just you_.

- Whom can you tell?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#00929c", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus for His loving plan to save you.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#00929c", "size": "lg"}}})\
