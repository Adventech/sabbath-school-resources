**What you need:**

Paper plate, paper circle, scissors, glue, marker, stickers, glitter glue, chenille stick or pipe cleaner, tape.

**Preparation instructions:**

- Draw guidelines on a paper plate for children to cut out the wedge. Make sure the height of the wedge is more than half the diameter of the paper plate, as shown.
- Cut out a small paper circle for the face.

**Create it together:**

- Cut out a wedge from a paper plate and glue to the opposite side of the plate.
