^[Word of the Week—DISCIPLE:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#812a1b", "size": "xl"}}})\
A disciple is a follower or student of a leader or teacher; learning from them and copying what they do. Jesus had 12 close disciples when He was on earth. He has many more today.

Two Fishermen

Jesus wound His way down the rocky desert trail and out of the lonely wilderness. Relying on God’s Word, He had won the victory over Satan’s temptations. Fed and revived by angels, He headed back to the Jordan River.

As He came closer to the river, Jesus heard the familiar voice of John the Baptist preaching to the people. Joining them, He mingled with the crowds, trying not to attract attention. But John spotted Him anyway.

God’s glory lit up John’s face, and his voice once again boomed out the words “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world! . . . I have seen and testified that this is the Son of God” (John 1:29-34).

Full of wonder and awe, the people stared at Jesus. Could this really be the Messiah? they wondered. Jesus was dressed in the simple clothes of the working class, likely worn and weathered from His time in the wilderness. He looked so ordinary, so poor, so much like them. Where were His kingly robes? Shouldn’t He look like a powerful and proud leader who could drive out the Roman rulers? Many in the crowd were disappointed and turned away.

But others saw more; much more than a poor, humble man. They noticed Jesus’ eyes. Kindness, gentleness, and indescribable love shone out from those eyes and reached their hearts. Even the atmosphere around Jesus seemed holy. Like a magnet to metal, they were drawn to Him. John the Baptist had preached to prepare them for this very moment, and now their hearts were full.

Jesus came to this earth as a poor, humble person so that His love and compassion could reach everyone, including those who were poor, vulnerable, or lonely. How did Jesus explain His mission later? **Read Matthew 20:28.**
