**Word of the Week**

**Sorry:** To be sorry is to feel bad about doing something wrong and wish you hadn’t done it. To say “I’m sorry” is to apologize for doing wrong. God asks us to be sorry for doing wrong and to ask Him for forgiveness.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Zacharias and Elizabeth’s miracle baby, John, had a special job—preparing the hearts of people to love Jesus. John preached and encouraged people to turn from disobedience, ask for forgiveness, make better choices in their lives, and be baptized. One day John baptized Jesus and saw a special sign from God shining on Jesus, showing that He is the Messiah!

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus forgives us whenever we are sorry and ask Him to forgive us.
- **Feel** free and safe to come to Jesus and tell Him when they have done something wrong.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His forgiveness.
