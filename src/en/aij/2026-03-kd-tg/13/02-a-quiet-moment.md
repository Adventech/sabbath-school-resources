**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Jesus cares how your day started and when it will end. He knows about the moments you felt rushed, stretched, or overlooked. He knows of the tasks done and still to be done. He cares about the state of your heart and the health of your body. In all things, He cares for you.

Let that sink in for a moment, then pray to God your response.

- How might appreciating that God cares change how you go about your day or your week?
- Will you notice Jesus’ care in your life today?

In this week’s lesson, the people in two different towns were given the same opportunity—only they were with Jesus in person! How would they respond to the care of Jesus? In Nazareth, Jesus revealed Himself as the Messiah. Yet, sadly, the people’s doubt and prejudice blocked any opportunities for them to experience His care in their daily lives. In Capernaum, however, the people drew close to Jesus in full belief. They couldn’t get enough of His presence, His miracles, and His stories. The people of Capernaum accepted Jesus with open arms.

It was here in Capernaum, that Jesus could reach out and transform bodies, heal hearts, and give peace to troubled minds.

Jesus longs to show you—and the children of your Sabbath School class—His care today. Make time for Him, listen to His Words, and embrace His presence in your life. He notices what brings you joy, and He is there to help in the challenges you face. Whatever your situation, He cares—and He is at work, bringing about good.

“For I am persuaded that neither death nor life, nor angels nor principalities nor powers, nor things present nor things to come, nor height nor depth, nor any other created thing, shall be able to separate us from the love of God which is in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 8:38, 39).
