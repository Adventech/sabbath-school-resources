“Tomorrow night let’s pull Mrs. White’s tent down while she’s sleeping in it,” a young man whispered with a grin to his friends.

It was camp meeting. People had come far and wide to hear Mrs. Ellen White speak about Jesus. Everyone was abuzz with excitement. Everyone, that is, except for a bunch of young troublemakers from a nearby town. They thought it was more interesting to throw stones at tents and cause trouble.

The camp guards were very worried. They heard about the troublemakers’ plan to pull down Mrs. White’s tent. Hurrying down to the police station, they asked if a policeman would come and guard her tent during the night. So that evening a tall, strong policeman arrived, ready to keep Mrs. White and her tent safe.

But Ellen White wasn’t worried one bit about the mischief-makers. So many times in her life God had sent His big, strong angels to protect her from sickness and danger.

She knew God was watching over her. He was much bigger and stronger than any gang of mischief-makers.

That night, after preaching, Ellen White returned to her tent and went to sleep. Outside, the policeman began his job as watchman. But the troublemakers never showed up. By midnight the policeman yawned and looked around. Everything was quiet and still.

Then the policeman saw something. He looked closely at Mrs. White’s tent, and his eyes opened wide in surprise. There above her tent shone a bright beam of light. The policeman stared as the light turned into a glittering angel.

The policeman dropped to the ground, amazed. For a few minutes he watched the angel standing guard above Ellen White’s tent; then he stood to his feet and walked away. His job was done! Mrs. White didn’t need his help at all. Not when she had God’s mighty angel protecting her.

God’s angels are busy protecting you too! “For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways” (Psalm 91:11).
