**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God who protects me.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Psalm 91:11).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Protect”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Protect means to keep someone or something safe from danger. God wants to keep you safe! He has given His angels the very special job of protecting you every day.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Luke 2:22-38; Matthew 2:19-23; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 59-67.

God’s Protection

Mary looked at little Jesus as He played. She couldn’t stop thinking about everything that had happened.

She would never forget the angel who had told her that she would give birth to the promised Savior! If she closed her eyes, she could still imagine the angel’s face and shining glow.

Her tummy had grown big with Baby Jesus inside. She remembered the long, bumpy donkey ride to Bethlehem and the sad feeling of having no place to stay.

Mary smiled as she remembered the animal stable and then what it was like to hold Baby Jesus for the first time. He was the best gift in the whole world!

She thought about the excited shepherds who told them about an angel choir. Then, of course, there was the moving star, and the Wise Men who followed it. So much had happened!

Mary knew that God had been very busy keeping their little family safe through it all.

Who else had been keeping Jesus safe? **Read Psalm 91:11.** This promise is for you too! Maybe the next time you are scared or worried, you can read or say this verse. Never forget that God’s big, strong angels are protecting you, just as they protected Baby Jesus.
