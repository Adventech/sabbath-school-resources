The village people of Nain stood and stared in stunned silence. Jesus had stopped a funeral! Jesus had raised the dead to life! They began to praise our amazing God.

The more they thought about the incredible miracle, the more the people tried to make sense of it. What did they say? **Read Luke 7:16.**

Because Jesus visited Nain early on in His public ministry, some of the village people had likely never seen Jesus or witnessed one of His healing miracles before. But they knew God’s prophets had done similar things long ago. The prophet Elijah had brought a widow’s son back to life (1 Kings 17:22), and Elisha the prophet had too (2 Kings 4:35). So, it seemed to them that Jesus must be a prophet. They could tell that through Jesus, God was “taking care of his people” (Luke 7:16, ICB).

The praising crowd turned around and headed back toward Nain. They had come out through the village gates as a crowd of sympathizing mourners. Now they returned as a crowd of celebrating friends.

What happened next? **Read Luke 7:17.**

Like all good stories, news of the miracle spread like wildfire from one village to the next . . . and the next . . . and the next. Many people came to know about Jesus and the sympathy He had for suffering people. They also learned about His power to bring dead people back to life.

Satan brought death into the world, but Jesus showed that He has power over death and the hurt it can cause. By dying for all, Jesus made sure we can live forever in heaven without death or sin. First Corinthians 15:55 says, “Death, where is your victory? Death, where is your power to hurt?” (ICB).
