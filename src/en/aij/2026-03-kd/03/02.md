The shepherds looked up at the starry sky as their sheep slept on the soft grass. All was quiet. All was still.

Suddenly a dazzling angel appeared before them. The angel’s light was so bright, it made the sky shine all around them. The shepherds jumped back in surprise. Their eyes opened wide, and their knees began to wobble. They were scared!

“Do not be afraid, for behold, I bring you good tidings of great joy which will be to all people” (Luke 2:10).

The shepherds gasped, amazed. An angel wanted to share good news for the whole world with them—a group of shepherds!

What did the angel say? **Read Luke 2:11.**

The promised Savior had come! The King they had just been talking about was here. They imagined His big, strong muscles, His crown, and His royal palace. Yes! They couldn’t wait to bow before their Ruler, the one they thought would save them from the mean Roman soldiers.

The angel said, “And this will be the sign to you: You will find a Babe wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger” (Luke 2:12).

Well, that’s a little strange for a king, isn’t it? But the shepherds looked at the beautiful angel and nodded. How special that God had sent them an angel messenger! With seeking hearts they believed the angel’s words.
