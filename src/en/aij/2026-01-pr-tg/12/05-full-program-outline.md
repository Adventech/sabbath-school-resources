**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Give them an opportunity to share one way they were able to put someone first last week. Were they able to share their story and craft with a neighbor?

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Play a game of **charades** using a set of **Character Cards**. Invite a child to take the top card, discretely look at it, then mime part of the story depicted on the card for the other children to guess. The child who guesses correctly then has a turn. You may need to add other Bible characters if you have a big group.

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** In our lesson stories so far, what kinds of prayers has Abram prayed? Sing the prayer song, **“Sabbath Song,”** and invite several children to pray, remembering to ask God to help us to remember these things when we pray.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/sabbath-song.mp3] “Sabbath Song”

Then share this week’s **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission project. Say a short prayer for God to bless and take the offering to where it’s most needed.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Warmly welcome any visitor and sing the **“Visitor Song.”** Sing **“Happy Birthday”** to a child who has had a
birthday this week, and pray for God to bless them.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite at least four older church members to join your Sabbath School for five minutes. Have them stand in a circle with the children. Give one of them a ball or soft toy. Ask the older members, “What is something wise you can share with the children here today?” (60-second answer.) Throw the ball/toy to a child to begin. They will throw it to an adult, who will catch it and answer the question. Then they will throw it to a child, who will then throw it to another adult, who will answer the same question until all adults have responded.

Discuss:

- **Why do older people have more wisdom than children?**
- **Which life lesson/wisdom did you most appreciate?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) Teaching Point:** People with more life experience than us are wiser than us, but God is the wisest of all!

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) As we learn more about Abram in today’s lesson, we will be reminded of our Big Idea.** Read the Big Idea from the whiteboard together: **God can help me make wise choices that are a blessing to others.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Like Abram, what could you do to help someone else in trouble or in need of help?** (Remember, the first thing is always to pray!)

To explore this question, engage the children in a **walk ‘n’ talk** activity. Place the five service cards around the room. Form **five groups**. Have them rotate around the **service cards**, discussing each question. If time allows, share responses as a class. Service card questions include (write the following on separate pieces of paper):

1. Your neighbor is sick. Their yard is overgrown. How can you help them?
2. A new person has joined your class at school. Others are not including them. What can you do?
3. Your parents have been at work all day. Now at home, they are working hard to get everything ready for Sabbath. What can you do to help?
4. The man next door has just fallen off his ladder and is lying on the ground. What should you do?
5. A friend has just found out their little brother has been taken to hospital. What could you do?

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Why did Abram give Melchizedek one tenth of all the goods he’d recovered?** (One tenth is a tithe; it belongs to God and it is given to Him because He asks that we return it to Him.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Tithe money supports pastors and workers who share the good news of Jesus’ love. This money allows more people to know and love God.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Display a **tithe envelope** and explain its use. In **small groups**, have the children work out the tithe on a **pile of coins** (or your chosen alternative), place it in a **tithe envelope**, and fill in the front. If you used coins, a child from each group could place it in the offering bag during church offering.

- **What did Abram reap for doing good and rescuing Lot?** (He had the peace and satisfaction that comes from doing the right thing and helping others.)

**^[COMBINED CRAFT and NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) Note:** As a response to the learning, today’s craft relates to the Nature Lesson.

Show the children your completed **Frog Craft** and share instructions for making it. Set the children to work on the frog craft without explaining its relevance.

Read the **Nature Lesson**, “Boiling a Frog,” while the children work.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** One unwise choice can lead to another and another until we don’t notice what we are doing wrong—as Lot did. Let’s be like Abram and ask God to help us make wise choices instead.

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) Display your frog in an eye-catching spot this week as a reminder to ask God to help you make wise choices.**

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring children back together to sing **“God Bless You.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-bless-you.mp3] “God Bless You”
