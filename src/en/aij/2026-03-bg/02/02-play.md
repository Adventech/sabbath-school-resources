Create

Make an angel out of paper or cardboard. Decorate with colors and glitter. Place in your child’s room where they can see it as they go to sleep. Say or sing the memory verse together each night.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#D7B339"}}}
Explore

Make a simple basket boat using foil, objects from nature, or a container. Place a tiny baby Moses in the basket and place in water (a puddle, pond, or the bath). Stop and listen to the sounds of nature—sounds that baby Moses might have heard from his basket boat. Talk about the angels who watched over baby Moses in his basket boat. Remind your child that God has an angel that watches over them too.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#D7B339"}}}
Sing

Find a lullaby, hymn, or other song about angels watching over us and sing it for worship each morning this week.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#D7B339"}}}
Discover

Re-create the scene of baby Moses in the basket boat on the river using everyday objects (e.g., use a blanket as the river). Act out the story using dolls and props from around the house. Talk about the ways God protected baby Moses on the river.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#D7B339"}}}
Pray

Thank God for sending His angels to watch over us. Pray that God will send angels to your home, and thank Him for His protection over your family.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#4073B1", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways” (Psalm 91:11).
