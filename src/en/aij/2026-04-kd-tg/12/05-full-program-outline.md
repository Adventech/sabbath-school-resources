**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a box **(containing smiley-face stickers or fruit for each child), wrapped in plain brown paper with frowning faces drawn on it.**

- **What do you think of my box?** (It looks plain, not very exciting, it makes me feel a bit sad.)
- **I wonder what’s in this box? What do you think it could be?**

Invite a child to open the box. Distribute a sticker or piece of fruit to each child.

- **How did you feel when you saw what was inside?** (Surprised, happy, joyful.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Share how today we will hear a story about something that happened that was very sad, but that Jesus turned into a wonderful surprise, like our box. This story will help us learn our **Big Idea**. Say it together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He brings me joy** [hug a friend]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **a box of tissues, a soft blanket, and a picture of heaven.**

Share how the mother in our story today felt very sad because her son had died.

- **What are some other things that make you feel sad?**

Choose a child to come to the front and stand beside you. Share how sometimes when we feel sad, we cry. The tears come from our eyes and roll down our faces. We might need a tissue to wipe them away. (Hand child a tissue and have them pretend to wipe their eyes.)

- **What else do we need when we feel sad?** (Someone to comfort us.)

Show the soft blanket then place it over a child’s shoulders. Point out how it’s soft and warm and cozy. It doesn’t take away our sadness, but it helps us to feel loved and content. (Place the blanket over each child’s shoulders in turn.) Share that Jesus can give us comfort like this blanket. This joy can make us happy and content, even when things are sad or hard.

- **How does this picture of Jesus in heaven make you feel?** Invite the children to stand up and show you with their actions and then sit down. (Excited, happy, joyful.)

Share that when Jesus returns, He will wake up the dead, just as He woke up the man in today’s story. Jesus will then bring all His friends (those who love Him) to heaven, where there will be no more dying, hurt, or sadness. We will be with Jesus always!

**Teaching Point:** Jesus can comfort us and give us joy now, even in hard times. We can have joy knowing we will be with Him in heaven.
