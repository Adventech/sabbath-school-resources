**Lesson Summary**

A nobleman whose son was desperately sick decided to go to Jesus to ask Him to heal his son. He decided he would believe in Jesus if He performed a miracle. Jesus revealed that He knew the man’s doubts and what was on his heart. Realizing Jesus truly was the Messiah, the nobleman cast all his worries and cares onto Him. He asked in faith for Jesus to heal his son, and Jesus told him his son was healed. In faith, the man took his time getting home. He found that his son had been healed at the exact moment Jesus had spoken. The nobleman told his whole household about Jesus, the Messiah, who cares for us.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants to take away our doubts and worries and grow our faith.
- **Feel** we can always come to Jesus because He cares for us.
- **Respond** by giving Him our doubts and worries.
