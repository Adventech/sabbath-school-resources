“Get up!” Joseph heard the angel say in a dream. He had been hiding in Egypt with Mary and little Jesus for a long time. Jesus had grown so much.

The angel kept talking as Joseph listened carefully. “Take the young Child and His mother, and go to the land of Israel” (Matthew 2:20). Mary, Joseph, and Jesus could now go home!

The angel told Joseph that King Herod had died. They didn’t need to hide anymore.

Once again they packed their bags and loaded the donkey. But this time things were different. There was no need to sneak away quietly in the middle of the night. No, they could travel safely in the daytime under the bright sun.

Along the way God gave Joseph another dream. God would guide them and keep them safe. In the dream God told Joseph to go back to the place where Mary and Joseph had once lived. Where was that? **Read Matthew 2:23.**

So that is exactly what they did. Mary and Joseph were so thankful to God for guiding and protecting them. God had sent His angels to look after them wherever they went.

God will always look after you too! “For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways” (Psalm 91:11).
