The great door of the ark swung open. Sunshine poured through the open doorway, and Noah gazed upon the new earth, green and luscious with the fresh scent of budding flora. How joyous that moment must have been after more than a year inside the musty confines of the ark! Out of all the things Noah must have wanted to do, at the top of his list was to build an altar to worship and thank the Lord.

May we stop every morning and evening to do the same. “It is for our own benefit to keep every gift of God fresh in our memory. Thus faith is strengthened to claim and to receive more and more. . . . . The soul that responds to the grace of God shall be like a watered garden. His health shall spring forth speedily; his light shall rise in obscurity, and the glory of the Lord shall be seen upon him. Let us then remember the loving-kindness of the Lord, and the multitude of His tender mercies. Like the people of Israel, let us set up our stones of witness, and inscribe upon them the precious story of what God has wrought for us _(Ellen G. White, The Desire of Ages, p. 348)_.

As your child grows, share with them often your own stories of God’s wonderful workings in your life. You might even like to write them down in a special book of remembrance. May we determine to keep every gift of God fresh in our memory.

> <callout>Psalm 92:1, 2</callout>
> “It is good to give thanks to the Lord, and to sing praises to Your name, O Most High; to declare your lovingkindness in the morning, and Your faithfulness every night.”
