Create

Make a tower (tube) of fire. Have your child do a big scribble of fire on a blank piece of paper, using orange and red crayons or markers. Fill the page with color! Cut out the flames and glue them onto another piece of paper. Wrap the flames around a tube or broomstick. When the people saw the fire, they remembered that God was with them. God is with us too.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Connect

Plan a special Friday night this week to celebrate that God is always with us. Make special dinner and let your child help you set the table nicely. Pick flowers or leaves outside to decorate the table and light candles, if you have them. Enjoy bringing in the Sabbath with singing songs and worshipping together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Play

Pretend to be an Israelite following God’s cloud. Talk about how the Israelites followed God, their leader, going behind the cloud and fire. Make a little tent together with a towel hung between two chairs. You may like to include the tower of fire you created. Pretend morning comes and God’s cloud moves.\
Say, “God’s cloud is moving; let’s follow Him” and quickly pack up the tent. Then have your child follow you as you pretend to follow the cloud. When the cloud stops, quickly set up the tent again.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Pray

God shows us His love for us in the Bible. Read the memory verse from your Bible. Try to say it together. Thank God that He is always with us.
