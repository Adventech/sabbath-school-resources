title: A.C.T.S. Prayer Format
---

> <callout></callout>
> Try using this simple acronym to guide your prayer time: ACTS. Conclude by asking God to guide your actions and choices for the rest of the day. “In all your ways acknowledge Him, and He shall direct your paths” (Prov. 3:6).

### Adoration

`Lord, I praise You for`

### Confession

`I confess the sin of`

### Thanksgiving

`I choose to thank You for`

### Supplication

`I pray for`

**Here’s what I need to do today in relation to**

`God:`

`My Family:`

`My Character:`

`My Ministry:`
