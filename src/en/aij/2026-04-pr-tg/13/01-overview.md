{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Lazarus, Jesus’ very good friend, was sick. His sisters, Mary and Martha, sent a message to Jesus. Having a greater purpose in mind, Jesus delayed coming, and before Jesus arrived at their home, Lazarus died and was buried. He had been in the tomb for four days when Jesus arrived. Mary, Martha, and the disciples didn’t understand Jesus’ delay, but Jesus lovingly wanted to give those who still were unbelieving more evidence that He truly was the Son of God and had the power to resurrect the dead.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that when we believe in Jesus, even if we die, we will live again one day with Him in heaven.
- **Feel** comforted knowing Jesus has the power over death.
- **Respond** by having faith in Jesus that He has power to do the impossible.
