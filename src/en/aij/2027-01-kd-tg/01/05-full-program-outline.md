**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **baby doll** (made of cloth or cotton yarn). Share with the children how precious this little baby is to you. Invite them to turn their backs and close their eyes while you hide it in your room/area. **Pretend to hide it** in a few different spaces then **give them a couple of minutes to look for the baby doll**. Then call them back to their seats. **Reveal the baby doll in a protected seed pod or basket** that was either hidden on your person or somewhere they wouldn’t have found it.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain how the baby was so precious that you wanted to keep it safe. Show them the little seed pod or basket. Share that it was very important that the baby doll didn’t get lost, as you have special plans to use this baby in our program later.

In our story today, we will learn about another precious baby who was hidden. I wonder who he was and what God had planned for Him? Let’s find out.

Invite the children to say the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. He has great plans for my life.** (Without the children noticing, have a helper place the baby in the **Story Explorer Box**.)

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story, **“A Baby and a Basket,”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal your **cloth baby doll and seed pod/basket** (from **Secret Surprise Box**), a **cardboard heart**, and a **paper crown**. Ask the following question and invite a couple of children to come forward and hold the baby doll and basket as they each answer.

- **Who looked after baby Moses and how?** (His family kept him quiet and safe; his mother made him a basket and hid him on the river; Miriam looked after him; angels looked after him; God sent a princess to save him; the princess adopted him.)

Invite a few children to come forward and hold the heart as they each answer the following question:

- **Who loves you? How do you know that you are loved and precious?**

Share that God had many special people (even angels) to keep Moses safe. And He has many people, including angels, to look after us too. God does this because He loves us and has a special plan for each of us.

Invite a few children to come forward to wear the crown as they each answer the following question:

- **What were God’s big plans for Moses?** (To be a prince; to one day free the Israelite slaves.)

**Teaching Point:** Just as God had very special plans for baby Moses, He has wonderful plans for your life. We don’t know what our lives will be like as we grow up, but we can trust Him. He promises to one day make us princes and princesses in heaven with Him. Now, that’s a very good plan for each of us!

Invite the children to stand. If they would like to say yes to God’s plan, invite them to put a pretend crown on their heads, then say the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. He has great plans for my life.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 53: “The Monarch Butterfly”** and share the following facts:

- Monarch butterflies are large, beautiful butterflies with orange, black, and white markings.
- The monarch butterfly starts out as a tiny egg. Then out of the egg hatches a small caterpillar.
- These caterpillars eat lots and lots of milkweed plants. This makes them poisonous to other animals and keeps them safe!
- When they have grown big, the caterpillar attaches to a leaf and forms a hard shell around itself called a chrysalis. It stays inside the chrysalis for two weeks while it totally transforms. Then it comes out as a beautiful butterfly.
- They aren’t just pretty—they’re flower helpers too! As they fly from plant to plant, they carry pollen, which helps flowers and fruit grow. They help us and our planet.

God has put His special plan inside each small caterpillar to become a beautiful, helpful, and very important butterfly. He has wonderful plans for you too.

Give each child **Nature Card 53: “The Monarch Butterfly”** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Moses’ Rocking Basket.** Invite the children to say the **Big Idea** again: **God’s way is best. He has great plans for my life.** Remind the children that God had a plan to protect baby Moses by sending angels to watch over his basket and by leading the princess to the river. **^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to **use your craft to share the story with someone in your family—perhaps a brother, sister, or cousin. They might like to learn more about Moses with you in the week to come.**

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned how God protected baby Moses and had a great plan for his life. We also learned that we are precious to God. We can trust Him every day, in good times and bad times, for He has a wonderful plan for each one of us.

Invite the children to crouch down low like little caterpillars in a chrysalis as you say the **Big Idea** one last time: **God’s way is best. He has great plans for my life.** If they would like to ask God to lead them, invite them to jump up like a beautiful monarch butterfly.
