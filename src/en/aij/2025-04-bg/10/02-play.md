Create

Create a little stable by gluing craft sticks around a small cardboard box. Make little animals peek out of the stable.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#396EA9"}}}
Play

Act out the story in your living room. Pretend to be the donkey and have your child ride on your back. _Clip-clop_ went little donkey all the way to Bethlehem. Joseph and Mary were trusting God to care for them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#396EA9"}}}
Explore

Go for a long walk somewhere in nature, if possible. Share how tired Mary must have been. Put down a blanket to sit on. Talk about how they would have slept under the stars on their way. It may have taken at least four days to walk there. God took care of them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#396EA9"}}}
Create

Make a heart out of paper or cardstock. Cut two doors that open in the middle of the heart. Behind the doors, glue a picture of Jesus. Place this somewhere where your child can see it often.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#396EA9"}}}
Pray

Sing your favorite Christmas song together, then pray. Thank God for sending Baby Jesus, and for taking care of Him. Thank Him for taking care of you.
