For three long days Mary and Joseph searched. What a long time! Finally, walking into the temple, they paused as they heard a familiar voice. Look! It was Jesus!

Mary wiped away her tears and ran over to Jesus. “Son,” she cried, “why did you do this to us? Your father and I were very worried about you. We have been looking for you” (Luke 2:48, ICB).

What did Jesus reply? **Read Luke 2:49.**

Jesus had been with His heavenly Father the whole time. He had been growing “in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).

Together Jesus, Mary, and Joseph returned home to Nazareth. Mary thought a lot about everything that happened as they walked. She was so glad that God was teaching her how to look after this very special Boy.
