- Remarkably, Daniel served in high office in both the Babylonian and Medo-Persian empires. He dedicated himself to God and His service, and because Daniel was a man of justice, goodness, and love, God was able to trust him with leadership at the highest levels of these two empires.
- It may seem surprising that Daniel did not try to use his formidable political authority (being one of only three governors over the whole kingdom) to harm his enemies, the satraps. But Daniel was concerned first and foremost about God’s values, not those of humans. And because of Daniel’s faithfulness, God moved mightily on his behalf and saved him from the lions.
- God is still able today to save us from our difficulties and trials. Ask Him for His help. Put your trust in Him, and He will provide the strength and courage you need—just as He did for Daniel.
