You might think that Satan would give up tempting Jesus after two failed attempts, but no.

He carried Jesus to a mountaintop where He could see beautiful cities, sparkling palaces, green fields, and fruit-filled orchards below. Jesus gazed in wonder at the wealth and beauty; it looked so much better than the barren desert He’d just spent time in. Then the devil spoke smoothly. **Read what he said in Matthew 4:9.**

What crazy things to say! First, Jesus had created the world, so it wasn’t Satan’s to give. And second, Satan knew the Bible says, “You shall have no other gods before Me” (Exodus 20:3), so why did Satan ask Jesus to worship him?

Because he is an evil liar. Satan knew that if Jesus became dazzled by riches and power, He could not save the world. If He bowed down and worshipped Satan, everyone would be lost forever.

It would have been easy to accept what Satan offered, but Jesus didn’t let temptation sidetrack Him from His mission. Even though Jesus knew His life on earth would be filled with sadness and suffering, His great big heart chose the hard path to save the sinners He loved so much.

Jesus firmly told the devil, “Away with you, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve’ ” (Matthew 4:10). And so Satan was forced to leave, angry and defeated.

Three big temptations, and three big victories! Jesus is a wonderful example of how to live by God’s words: “Therefore submit to God. Resist the devil and he will flee from you. Draw near to God and He will draw near to you” (James 4:7, 8).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Satan often tempts us to want cool new things we don’t need. What does he tempt you with?
- What can you do when he tempts you?

Our loving Jesus understands and keeps watch over us when Satan tempts us to believe that owning the latest cool thing, having lots of money, entertainment, and being popular will make us happy. These are some of Satan’s favorite lies.
