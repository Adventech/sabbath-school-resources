---
title: Jesus Saves, I Spend
date: 10/01/2026
---

Was Jesus a saver or a spender? He was the epitome of generosity—a big giver—but the Bible doesn’t have any references to Him being a big spender, even though He had 24/7 access.

Even when He “showed off” His unlimited wealth by generously creating a meal for more than 5,000 people out of thin air, He still wasn’t wasteful. He asked His disciples to collect the leftovers—not just the whole loaves and whole fish, but every last fragment. And what was His reason? “So that nothing is wasted” (John 6:12, NLT).

Did you see that? Jesus saved twelve baskets of scraps immediately after proving that He could instantaneously provide so much food that wasting twelve _thousand_ baskets wouldn’t have been an issue.

How easy it is, when God has blessed us abundantly, day after day, year after year, to start to be wasteful or overspend in some areas of our lives, often without even knowing it. We often spend, spend, spend on our homes, transport, holidays, recreation, and overall lifestyles.

Jesus saves. Shouldn’t we save too?

### Appeal

As you return God’s tithe and give your Promise offerings today, you can partner with Jesus in His saving of people. Let’s ask God for forgiveness for the times that we have wasted our blessings on useless things and ask Him for help to give as He gives and to save as He saves.
