More than 170 years ago Deus Pergunteed a man a preach para Ele, just as ele had Pergunteed Noah. Instead de preaching sobre a grande flood e finding safety em an ark, Deus Pergunteed this man a preach sobre Deus’s amor e the Segun coming de Jesus.

Seu name was João Loughborough, e ele was not quite 17 years old. Ele knew Deus wanted ele a preach, even though ele was young. Mas quando ele looked em himself, ele wondered quem would ever listen a ele! Seu trousers were hand-me-Façawns de a taller man, então eles flopped around seu ankles. The coat ele wore was just an overcoat that had been cut off em the bottom. Com only $1 em seu pocket, como was ele a buy nice clothes? Ele couldn’t, mas ele set off a preach anyway.

Thinking ele needed some preaching practice, João começou a preach em a pequeno place onde ele didn’t know anyone. Ele shouldn’t have worried, para the people were abençoou por o que eles heard. One man deu ele $3, others deu ele $5 worth de books a sell, e others encouraged ele porque ele was a bom preacher e could explain the Bible então well.

Moving em a another village, ele rented a church e put up some posters a let people know ele would be preaching para three nights. Pretty soon palavra got around that a boy was going a preach! Imagine João’s surprise quando ele found the church full a overflowing that Primeiro night, e then again the next evening! Ele was então glad that people had come a hear sobre Deus.

Mas there was one man quem wasn’t happy, e ele tried a cause trouble. Ele argued e said that João wasn’t preaching o que the Bible said. Antes long ele was very embarrassed, porque young João could easily show ele lots de Bible texts a prove the truths ele preached.

Sobre the years João Loughborough became a very well-known preacher quem worked hard para Deus em America, England, e Europe. Ele preached em churches, schools, tents, people’s homes, e even sob shady trees. Sometimes people tried a Faça cruel things a ele, mas Deus always kept ele safe.

Ele is remembered as a faithful Sétimo-dia Adventist pioneer quem worked hard com Tiago e Ellen White e other church leaders a Conte people sobre Deus e Seu truth para this time.

Enoch walked com Deus. Noah worked para Deus. João Loughborough preached para Deus. Nós can too.

- _What can você Faça para Deus?_

Quando nós walk com Deus por chooCante a amor Ele e live para Ele, Ele will show nos o que nós can Faça para Ele.
