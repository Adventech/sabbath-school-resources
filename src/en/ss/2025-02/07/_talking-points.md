### ^[Lesson Study Intro]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

This week, we will attempt to understand some of the divine interactions in carrying out the plan of redemption.

### ^[1. Angels are ministers for our salvation (Mon, Tue)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- Much of this week’s lesson revolves around the work of heavenly angels and their connection to God’s throne.
	- Angels are classified as “cherubim” and “seraphim,” and are “ministering spirits” to minister to our salvation (Heb. 1:14).
- The two cherubim at the gate of Eden (Gen. 3:24).
	- Cherubim are depicted throughout the sanctuary – on the mercy seat (see Ex. 25:19-22), inwrought in the veils (Ex. 26:1, 31; 36:8, 35), in Solomon’s temple beside the mercy seat (1 Ki. 6:23-27); and carved in the doors and the walls (1 Ki. 6:29, 32, 35).
		- “Cherubim… are often depicted as standing immediately next to God’s throne… . They are intimately tied to the throne of God.” Qtly, Tue, par 1, 5
- Ezekiel’s vision of the “living creatures” gives us insight into the ministry of the cherubim (Eze. 10:20).
	- The visions of Ezekiel 1 and 10 are heavy with symbolism, much of which is not directly explained.
		- Where such identification is lacking, the expositor is left to conjecture as to the application; hence dogmatism must be avoided.” SDA Bible Commentary, vol. 4, p. 577
	- We may, however, attempt to draw some conclusions –
	- They carried the throne of God, and did the bidding of God (v. 22, 26-28); the hand of the Lord sustained them (v. 8).
	- They went straight ahead, and never turned aside (v. 12; 10:11)
	- They conveyed light with great rapidity (vs. 13, 14)
	- They worked in conjunction with the machinery of the wheels (vs. 19-21; 10:16, 17)
- ^[The wheels were so complicated in arrangement that at first sight they appeared to be in confusion; yet they moved in perfect harmony. PK 535.3]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

### ^[2. The angels are an extension of the throne of God (Sun, Tue)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- “You who dwell between the cherubim…” (Ps. 80:1; Ex. 25:22).
	- The cherubim are in direct and immediate contact with the throne of God (Eze. 1:22, 26-8).
		- “Their angels always see the face of My Father…” (Mt. 18:10).
	- These angels are an extension of the love, mercy and compassion of God, and bring us direct communications from His immediate presence.
- ^[It is impossible to give any idea of the experience of the people of God who shall be alive upon the earth when celestial glory and a repetition of the persecutions of the past are blended. They will walk in the light proceeding from the throne of God. By means of the angels there will be constant communication between heaven and earth. 9T 16.1]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

### ^[3. We are an extension of the ministry of angels (Wed, Thu)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- God uses angels to empower His church on the earth.
	- The cherubim are described as having 4 faces (Eze. 1:10; Rev. 4:7). Some scholars believe these refer to the standards of the tribes bordering the sanctuary in the wilderness – Judah on the east, Reuben on the south, Ephraim on the west, and Dan on the north (Num. 2:3-25; Qtly, Wed).
		- This is a fitting representation of God’s church, and how He uses His angels to work through His people on earth.
- ^[All who engage in ministry are God's helping hand. They are co-workers with the angels; rather, they are the human agencies through whom the angels accomplish their mission. Angels speak through their voices, and work by their hands. And the human workers, co-operating with heavenly agencies, have the benefit of their education and experience. Ed 271.1]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})
	- Heavenly angels are cooperating with us to fill the vacancies left by the fallen angels, and they will triumph at last!
- ^[Heaven will triumph, for the vacancies made in heaven by the fall of Satan and his angels will be filled by the redeemed of the Lord. RH, May 29, 1900]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})
