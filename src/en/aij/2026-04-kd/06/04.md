Little did the woman know, but Jesus had heard all her thoughts. He saw her struggle. He felt her pain. And our loving Jesus was moving closer and closer toward her, on purpose.

The woman bent down low, tired and sore. Arms and legs bumped and shoved into her. But she didn’t move. Jesus was coming!

Between the moving legs and stomping feet, she saw a glimpse of the bottom of Jesus’ robe. “The woman thought, ‘If I can even touch his coat, that will be enough to heal me’ ” (Mark 5:28, ICB).

She had so much faith!

The woman leaned forward with all her might. She stretched out her hand as far as possible. The very tip of her finger touched the edge of Jesus’ robe as it swished past her.

Instantly, she felt a buzz like electricity zoom through her body from her head to her toes. What happened? **Read Mark 5:29.**

She stood up straight, feeling perfectly healthy and strong!

The woman wanted to thank Jesus, the mighty Healer, but she saw how busy He was. With a thankful heart and a smile shining on her face, she turned to go. She felt strong and well. Jesus had healed her!
