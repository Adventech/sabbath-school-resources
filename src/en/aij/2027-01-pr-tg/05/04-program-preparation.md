**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source **blocks, Legos, cardboard, foam squares or books** for “walls of water”

Source a **globe/world map**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Source **eight chairs** and **blindfolds**.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source Bible Story props: **white sheet, stick/staff, “walls of water” from Early Arrivals activity or two blue sheets/pieces of fabric**.

Source **strong magnet, bowl of water, iron-rich cereal** (e.g., cornflakes) or other iron-rich food that is local to you. Spinach, pumpkin seeds, oats, and seaweed have enough iron to be attracted to a magnet. You may also use black sand if available.
