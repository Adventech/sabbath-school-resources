**Opening Activities**

Source a **big container/tub** for sensory play. Fill with **grains or seeds** that are inexpensive and easy to find locally. You may like to use rice, puffed quinoa or puffed wheat, wheat berry, Indian corn, dried chickpeas or split peas. Include **toy farm animals, toy gardening tools** (rake, shovel) and **small containers** for play.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: A picnic blanket. Place the blanket in the box. Hide a picnic basket (or large covered basket) nearby and pack with a few eating utensils (plates, cutlery, cups).

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: Make some **“birds” from things that are plentiful in your location**: seedpods, pinecones, craft balls or balloons, or scrunched-up scrap paper. Draw or stick on wings. Source bowl of **crackers**, **wafers**, or **cereal** as “manna.” **Large mat.**

Prepare fake moldy manna prior to the lesson. Add two drops of **green food coloring** and one teaspoon of **vinegar** to a **jar** of **“manna”** (crackers/wafter/cereal) and shake.

Source peg people (Moses, families) or a felt set if desired, and **^[week 7 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: A variety of **pictures** or **real objects** of things that God provides (e.g., clothing, shoes, seeds, food, a bottle of water, a picture of a family, a love heart, Bible/lesson).
