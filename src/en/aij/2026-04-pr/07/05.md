The crowd of mourners became even louder! Wailing and weeping mixed with teasing and laughing. It was all too much! Jesus put a stop to it. He asked the noisy crowd to leave the house.

Then Jesus quietly called the young girl’s father and mother to Him, along with Peter, James, and John. Together they went into the room where the girl’s lifeless body lay. Jesus reached down and took the child’s cold hand in His warm, strong hand. He spoke softly in the language the family used every day. What did He say? **Read Mark 5:41.**

Instantly the girl trembled and her heart began to beat in a steady rhythm. Her eyes opened wide and she sat up. Then she jumped to her feet.

Jesus had done the impossible! Her parents were amazed. Their precious girl had died, and now she was alive!

Jesus smiled to see this beautiful young girl full of life, health, and energy. He had encouraged Jairus to keep trusting Him, even when things looked hopeless, and he had. Now Jairus knew without a doubt that our compassionate God has power over life and death. His daughter was alive!

Jesus encourages us to trust Him as Jairus did. His Word says, “Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding” (Proverbs 3:5). When we feel powerless to fix a problem, or it looks like all our options have run out, we can trust that Jesus understands. We can have faith that He can do the impossible for us, just as He did for Jairus.
