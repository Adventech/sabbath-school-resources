The crowd at the bottom of the sycamore tree grumbled and frowned. _Jesus was talking to cheating, lying Zacchaeus! Worse still, Jesus wanted to go stay at his house!_

The people of Jericho complained out loud. What did they say? **Read Luke 19:7.**

They didn’t realize that Jesus loved him anyway. And the love of Jesus had already changed Zacchaeus. He had decided to give away so much money. The tax collector from Jericho wanted to be just like Jesus and show kindness and honesty too.

Jesus looked from loving Zacchaeus to the frowning crowd. With a big smile He said, “he also is a son of Abraham” (Luke 19:9).

Zacchaeus was now a new creation. He belonged to Jesus’ big family. No longer a lying, stealing tax collector, Zacchaeus was a child of God. “Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation” (2 Corinthians 5:17). What a wonderful miracle!

Then Jesus told everyone that this kind of miracle—the miracle of changing people’s hearts—was what He had come to do. “The Son of Man came to find lost people and save them” (Luke 19:10, ICB).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What might Jesus and Zacchaeus have talked about over lunch at Zacchaeus’ house?
- Who showed more love, the crowd or Zacchaeus? Why?
- If you had been in the crowd, what would you have said to Jesus? To Zacchaeus?
