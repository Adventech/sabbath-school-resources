title: Love Gives
date: 19/09/2026

It sticks out like a swollen, bandaged thumb. It’s as plain as the nose on your face. It’s obvious, evident, plain, visible, as clear as day, and cannot be hidden. But I didn’t see it.

Anyone with a simple grasp of written language sees it. It’s unmissable, and it’s absolutely foundational to the Christian faith. I’m still unsure how I overlooked this two-word truth for so long.

I’ve spent years studying and sharing about faith, finances, and generosity, yet I still missed it. Maybe I overcomplicated it or skipped right over it, but it was staring me in the face right there, under my nose, in black and white. Nothing could be clearer. But I still didn’t see it.

It’s embedded at the start of the Bible’s most famous verse: John 3:16. Millions, maybe billions, of people have memorized it. I’ve heard five-year-old children say it and probably understand it. I’ve been able to recite this verse myself for more than forty years, and I’ve recited it regularly, yet it might as well have been invisible to me. I just didn’t see it.

Then, one day, there it was.

“For God so _loved_ the world that He gave . . .”

It’s simple.

Love gives.
