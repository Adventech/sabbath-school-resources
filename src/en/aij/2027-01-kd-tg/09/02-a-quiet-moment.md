**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Throughout this quarter we’ve walked the dusty wilderness together, following the footsteps of the Israelites. We’ve watched our faithful God rescue the broken, redeem the weary, and provide daily bread in barren places. With every step He has spoken His love. In every miracle He has given His grace. For “all the paths of the Lord are mercy and truth” (Psalm 25:10).

And now the story pauses at the foot of Mount Sinai, where God’s love and majesty come together to be powerfully seen by all. The clouds gather, the mountain trembles, and God speaks. He declares His law: the Ten Commandments.

These are not rules to restrain us; rather, they’re promises that will protect us. Each commandment is a glimpse into the Father’s heart and His desire for us to live a beautiful, blessed life. The Ten Commandments were to be “a revelation of His will, a transcript of His character, the expression of divine love and wisdom” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 52).

These commandments are a gift from God who doesn’t coerce but rather invites us to walk in His wonderful ways. “God never forces us to do right, but He seeks to save us from the evil and lead us to the good” (Ellen G. White, _The Ministry of Healing_, p. 114). These unchangeable 10 promises are intended to bless us “for our good always” (Deuteronomy 6:24).

So as you stand before your precious children this Sabbath, remember: you are not merely teaching the law. You are telling of a God who loves deeply, leads gently, and longs for His children to live fully. Let His love echo in your words. And may each little heart you teach come to see that God’s law isn’t merely a list—it’s a reflection of His wonderful character.
