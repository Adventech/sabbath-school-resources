**What you need:**

Week 12 craft template, scissors, glue, red tissue paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12a.jpg)

**Preparation instructions:**

Cut out small pieces of red tissue paper.

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-kd-craft-12.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the diorama template. Make two vertical slits at the bottom, as shown.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12b.jpg)

2\. Fold inward along the dotted lines. Stick the square glue tabs under the base of the sanctuary diorama.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12c.jpg)

3\. Cut out the tent template. Fold inward along the dotted lines and glue the glue tabs to the base of the diorama.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12d.jpg)

4\. Cut out the semicircle laver template. Glue the tab to make a cone. Fold the pointed end of the cone flat and glue onto the diorama (in front of the tent).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12e.jpg)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12f.jpg)

5\. Cut out the rectangle altar template. Fold in half and glue together.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12g.jpg)

6\. Continue to fold the rectangle in half and glue together another three times. Glue onto the diorama (in front of the laver).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12h.jpg)

7\. Scrunch up a small piece of red tissue paper and glue on top of the altar.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12i.jpg)

8\. Show someone your diorama and tell them about the sanctuary.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/12j.jpg)
