_Drip, drop . . . drip._ Mother Naomi tipped out the last drop of water from the jug. It was empty! She looked in the food basket. It was empty too! There had been no rain in her town of Bethlehem. And now there was no food or water. So, Naomi, her husband, and their two sons moved to a new land. Here, their water jug was full of water—_glug, glug, glug._ Their food basket was full of soft bread—yum! Her boys grew up to be good strong farmers.\
Naomi sung as she made bread. Her sons had married two kind girls, Ruth and Orpah. She loved and cared for them. She told them all about God. As she sang, they sang too. They filled the water jug for her—_glug, glug, glug._ They helped make the soft bread— yum! What a happy family.

One day, Naomi stopped singing. She was very sad. Her husband and sons had died. It was time to leave this land and go home to Bethlehem. God had sent rain there. He would look after her. Naomi packed up her full water jug—_glug, glug, glug._ She packed her food basket with lots of soft bread—yum! She took Ruth, Orpah, and little donkey, and started the big walk home. _Clip-clop, clip-clop,_ went little donkey.\
Suddenly, Naomi stopped. Ruth and Orpah stopped. _Clip-clop._ Little donkey stopped too. “Go back home,” Naomi said gently to Ruth and Orpah. She didn’t want them to be lonely in a new place. Ruth and Orpah gave her a big hug. Naomi was so kind. Then Orpah said goodbye and went home. Goodbye, Orpah! Ruth hugged Naomi too _(hug child)_. But she wouldn’t let go! Ruth wouldn’t say goodbye. She said, “Every place you go, I will go. . . . Your God will be my God” (Ruth 1:16, ICB).

Ruth smiled at Naomi as they walked to Bethlehem. _Clip-clop, clip-clop_ went little donkey, beside them. Ruth helped Naomi as they walked. She helped her when they set up their tent at night. She cared for her all along the way. Her heart was full of love and kindness for Naomi. God wants us to have a kind and caring heart, just as Ruth had. He wants us to make kind choices that help those around us.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for Ruth’s caring heart. Please help me to care for those around me too.
