God spoke again from the cloud-covered mountain. His next six commandments would teach His people how to live happily together with love and respect. It was time to share His next six commandments.

When God spoke the fifth commandment, what did He say? **Read Exodus 20:12.** To honor means to love and respect, but also to obey. Ephesians 6:1 says, “Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.”

Long before you could even walk, or feed yourself, or babble your first words, your mother and father took care of your many needs because they loved you. They still help you, but now you are old enough to help them sometimes too. As a family, you work and play and have fun together.

God wants all parents to patiently teach their children how to love God, and grow up to be kind and respectful men and women. Good or loving parents will sometimes make mistakes, but because they have lived longer and are wiser than you, God expects you to show love and respect by following their instructions. But if an unkind mother or father asks their child to do something that doesn’t show respect to God or others, it would not be wise for a child to obey them.

When God spoke the sixth commandment, He said, “You shall not murder” (Exodus 20:13). All life comes from God; He is the only one powerful enough to give life. God wants us to live happily together and never kill another person or ourselves, and take away His precious gift of life.

Jesus taught us that hate is like murder too (Matthew 5:21, 22). Thoughts and feelings of hatred can kill our love for others, or cause us to stop loving them. This anger and hate can lead to actions that cause harm. Leviticus 19:17 says, “You shall not hate your brother in your heart.” God’s plan for a happy world is that we respect others and regard their lives as precious.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Some children may not know their parents, or live with them. Whom should they honor?
- How do you feel when others love and respect you, and regard your life as precious? Think of a way you can thank them.
