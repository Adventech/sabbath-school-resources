**Opening and Focus Activities**

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare a **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Source **number outlines 5-10, colored pencils/markers and stickers/other decorative items**.

Display the **1-4 commandment hearts** from last week.

Make a **paper plane that flies well** and source **scissors**.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **six large pieces of paper/cardstock and draw a large heart on them**. Attach to the walls with sticky tac around your room or place within your area. Have extra **sticky tac** (or similar) for the children to later attach their decorated numbers to the wall around each heart. Source **pens and Post-it notes for each child**. You will also need **star stickers** (or children can draw stars).
