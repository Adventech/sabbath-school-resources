#### Gabriel Comes with the Answer

In answer to Daniel’s heartfelt prayer, the angel Gabriel came to finish his explanation of Daniel 8, especially the last part that the prophet found so distressing. As he listened to the angel, Daniel realized his people would return to Jerusalem as Jeremiah’s prophecy had stated, but there was more, much more to this new prophecy than he realized.

The Hebrew word _marʾeh_ (vision) in Daniel 9:23 refers specifically to the vision of 2,300 days (as it does in Dan. 8:15, 16, 26, 27). In other words, this new seventy-week time prophecy given in Daniel 9:24, 25 represents the first part of the longer time period of Daniel 8:14. That is, both time prophecies were to begin “from the going forth of the command to restore and build Jerusalem” (Dan. 9:25), a decree recorded in Ezra 7. Without going into too many details at this point, what is crystal clear is that Jesus, here called “Messiah the Prince,” would come at the end of sixty-nine prophetic weeks or 483 years, the first seven weeks of which (or forty-nine years) were allotted “to restore and build Jerusalem.” As Ezra indicates, there were actually three decrees—of Cyrus, Darius, and Artaxerxes (Ezra 6:14)—but only the third and final decree of Artaxerxes, issued in 457 BC, fulfilled all the stipulations of the prophecy. Counting 483 years from this date and remembering that from 1 BC to AD 1 is a single year (there is no year 0), brings us to AD 27, “the fifteenth year of the reign of Tiberius Caesar” (Luke 3:1).

#### Right on Time

Amazingly, it was in precisely this year, AD 27, that God anointed Jesus as the Messiah at His baptism “with the Holy Spirit and with power” (Acts 10:38). We know from the Dead Sea Scrolls that Jews were aware of this prophecy and believed it would soon be fulfilled. The New Testament confirms that it was fulfilled—right on time! Daniel need not have worried about what must have seemed to him a long time. God had everything in His hands, under His control. The city of Jerusalem and the temple would be rebuilt. The Messiah would come. And this year, 2027, marks the 2,000-year anniversary of the beginning of Jesus’ public ministry.

That’s something to think about. Everything happened just as God had said and exactly on time. We can trust that God will answer our prayers too, not necessarily according to our timetable but according to His, because His timing is always perfect, whether the need is great or small. Let’s pray together.

#### Praying God’s Word — Daniel 9:25

“Know therefore and understand, that from the going forth of the command to restore and build Jerusalem until Messiah the Prince, there shall be seven weeks and sixty-two weeks; the street shall be built again, and the wall, even in troublesome times.”

**“Know therefore and understand”**

_Lord, give us hearts that long to know and understand Your Word. As You opened the prophecy to Daniel, open the Scriptures to us by Your Spirit, that we may see how every detail of Your plan points to Jesus._

**“. . . until Messiah the Prince”**

_Father, we praise You because every prophecy points to Jesus, the Messiah, and every promise is fulfilled in Him. Thank You for sending Christ at exactly the right time to reveal Your love and bring salvation to the world. As we prepare for OneVoice27, unite us with one heart and one mission—to lift up Jesus so that every nation, tribe, language, and people may hear His voice. Strengthen our faith in Your Word and use us as faithful messengers to proclaim Christ to this generation._

**“The street shall be built again . . . even in troublesome times”**

_Lord, You rebuild and restore even in troublesome times. Build again what lies in ruins, and through OneVoice27 this year, make us builders of Your kingdom who carry the gospel and lift up one voice—even when the times are hard—until Jesus comes._
