Just as the Godhead planned Creation, we invite you to make special plans for the Sabbath so it will be a loved, treasured time with God. You may like to turn the activities you enjoy into family traditions, either inside or outside!

**^[On Friday evening you could:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#b3c5e6"}}})**

1. Have sparkling grape juice or another special drink.
2. Light candles.
3. Play special music.
4. Take turns to write or draw special thank-you messages to each person in the family and place them at each table setting.
5. Sing a special Sabbath song at the beginning of your meal or have a sing-along beforehand or afterward.
6. Speak a family Sabbath blessing together (e.g., Psalm 92 is “a song for the Sabbath day”).
7. Take turns doing different parts of Sabbath worship: singing a song, telling a Bible story you know, being thankful, saying prayer.

**^[On Sabbath afternoon you could:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#b3c5e6"}}})**

1. Play Bible charades.
2. Plan a Sabbath treasure hunt. You could use key words from Bible texts as clues.
3. Video a Bible story. Get everyone involved!
4. Make something together as a family and deliver it:
  - A potted flower with a note
  - Thank-you cards to people who have blessed your life
  - A bookmark or photos that will make someone smile
5. Call or FaceTime someone you haven’t made contact with in a long time as a family. Encourage and pray with them.
6. Create special Sabbath placemats. Draw the Garden of Eden or illustrate a favorite Bible verse. Laminate your designs and use for Sabbath meals.
