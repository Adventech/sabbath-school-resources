**Lesson Summary**

When the Israelites’ food stores ran low in the wilderness, they resorted to grumbling and blaming Moses instead of remembering how God had provided for them in the past and trusting Him to provide again. Yet God responded with kindness and sent manna from heaven to feed them daily. He gave clear instructions, performed miracles, and even gave meat when they complained again. God’s grace was constant—even when they forgot His past goodness.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God provides for our needs, even when we forget that He can and will.
- **Feel** grateful for the way God takes care of us, even though we forget that He does.
- **Respond** by thanking God for providing for our needs every day.
