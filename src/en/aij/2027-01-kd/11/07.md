Here are three creatures that glow, to brighten your day!

1. Fireflies, sometimes called lightning bugs, are little flying beetles. God has given them a glowing middle that shines like a star in the sky. They can even turn it on and off like a light switch!
2. Jellyfish are creatures that live in the ocean. They have a soft squishy body with long, dangling tentacles. Many jellyfish glow in the deep, dark ocean water. They can glow green, blue, and a pretty purple color.
3. Some mushrooms around the world can create a beautiful glowing light that can be seen at night on forest floors.

Moses’ face glowed because He had spent time with God. The Bible says that “God is light and in Him there is no darkness at all” (1 John 1:5). When He lives in our hearts, His goodness can show in our lives. How can you shine for Jesus this Sabbath?

**^[Sabbath Afternoon Activity:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
Visit an elderly/lonely person in your community and sing them some songs, such as “This Little Light of Mine” and “Shine, Jesus, Shine,” to bring God’s light and joy to their lives.
