**Lesson Summary**

The Israelites were slaves in Egypt under Pharaoh and were multiplying greatly. Fearing they might become too powerful, Pharaoh decreed all newborn Hebrew boys were to be killed. Jochebed gave birth to Moses, and when she could no longer conceal him, she placed him in a basket and set it afloat on the Nile River. Pharaoh’s daughter, the princess, discovered the baby and decided to adopt him. Miriam, Moses’ sister, offered to find a Hebrew woman to nurse him, and she brought their own mother, Jochebed, to care for him. In their home Jochebed taught Moses to love and respect God and to worship only Him. Once Moses grew older, he was brought to the palace to become a prince of Egypt. Moses never forgot what his mother taught him and that God was with him.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God prepares us during childhood to be responsible adults who will work in big and small ways for Him.
- **Feel** a desire to have God prepare them for His service.
- **Respond** by being willing to have God and godly adults prepare them for God’s work.
