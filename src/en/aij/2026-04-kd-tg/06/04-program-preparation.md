**Opening Activities**

Source several “touch and feel” boxes made from **shoeboxes**. Cut a hole in the side of each box big enough for a child’s hand to fit through. Place an interesting object in each box. For example, a smooth stone, a squishy succulent leaf, a key, a toothpaste tube, a kitchen sponge.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: small red heart, box or bag, stickers or treat**. Hide heart in Sabbath School area before class begins.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Nature/Mission**

Print a **^[Nature Collector Card 45: Luna Moth]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.
