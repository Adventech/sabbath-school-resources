**Lesson Summary**

Jacob learned to forgive others, just as God forgave him. Jacob fell in love with his Uncle Laban’s daughter Rachel and agreed to work seven years to marry her, but Jacob was tricked by Laban into marrying his firstborn daughter Leah instead. Jacob had to work seven more years to marry Rachel. He was steadfast, despite being cheated many times. God increased Jacob’s family and flocks, showing His love and good plans for him. After 20 years of work for Laban, God told Jacob to go back to the land of his family, and then brought about forgiveness and peace between his family and Laban’s.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God has only good thoughts toward them and that they are forgiven.
- **Feel** forgiven by God.
- **Respond** by choosing to forgive others.
