**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

There is much to admire about Abram’s character. His loyalty, both to God and to Lot, shines brightly and is a testament to God’s presence in his life.

We see his loyalty expressed in many ways—in his proud witness of Yahweh as he sought unity and peace with Lot, in his humility as he offered Lot the first choice of the land, and when he rescued Lot, despite Lot’s lack of gratitude.

Loyalty. It is so integral to who God is and His desire for our lives. God wants to help each of us to “choose to be a loyal friend”—loyal to Him and loyal to each other. As you explore this week’s story with the children, may they know how much our loving God desires to be their loyal friend too.

For truly, “a friend loves at all times” (Proverbs 17:17).
