Mothers clutched their babies, while little toddlers wobbled along beside them. Older children smiled as they led their elderly grandparents slowly by the hand. Sheep dawdled as daddies tried to guide all the animals forward. Ox-drawn carts loaded with bedding and food bumped slowly over the rough road beside them. No matter how anxious the Israelites were to get out of Egypt, the huge multitude moved slowly.

Many Egyptians left with the Israelites too. They were part of the “mixed multitude” of people who had joined God’s people. Some had come to believe in God. Many others were merely wanting to escape the plagues or find excitement. Whatever the reason, they were there, which shows that God’s protection and deliverance are offered freely to everyone.

As the early-morning sun peeped over the horizon, the great Egyptian cities and towering pyramids slowly disappeared in the distance behind the Israelites. With glad smiles they praised God for their deliverance. They were free!

All day the people walked. At last the sun sank in the western sky. The day had been long and exciting, and not many had slept even a wink the night before. When Moses gave the command to stop for the night, everyone was glad. The most wonderful day in their lives was coming to a peaceful end.

As they drifted off to sleep, God’s people thought about how they were free. Maybe they had to pinch themselves to prove it was really true! They knew the truth of our memory verse: “The Lord is my rock and my fortress and my deliverer” (2 Samuel 2:22).

When God delivers us from things, we are free too. John 8:36 says, “Therefore if the Son makes you free, you shall be free indeed.” Jesus, God’s Son, can free us from all sorts of things, such as bad habits and difficult situations. What is another thing He frees us from? **Read Romans 8:2.**
