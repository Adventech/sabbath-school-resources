**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** To unpack the word “descendants,” point to the **blank tree** you have drawn on the whiteboard. Write **Noah** at the top of the trunk and explain that you will record the names of his descendants (people related to him) branching out towards the top of the tree to create a “family tree.” Add Noah’s sons’ names above his name as you share a little about each. You may also like to add their son’s names (Abram in particular) to give context for the next four lessons:

**Japheth:** His family became known as Japhethites. Many lived by the sea and sailed all over the world to make new nations. Some of Japheth’s sons were **Gomer**, **Magog**, and **Javan**.

**Ham:** His family moved across the land, building many cities and kingdoms. One of Ham’s sons was named **Mizraim**, which means Egypt. Another son was called **Canaan**. Both family groups formed great nations, a long way from Babel. Ham’s other son, **Cush**, was the father of the Bible’s most famous hunter, **Nimrod**. This mighty man and his family formed the kingdom of Babylon, and he later built the city of Nineveh.

**Shem:** His family journeyed south, where they built villages and cities, including Ur. Shem had many children, who had many more children. The Bible records nine generations of sons between Shem and a very special son, **Abram**.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** As we learn about Abram in today’s lesson, we will be reminded that (read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard): **As God’s friend, I will live in a way that tells others about Him.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Organize your children into **pairs** and **blindfold** one. Explain that their partner will guide them with instructions to a place in the room. Whisper a different destination to each child giving the instructions (e.g., the door, the whiteboard, the cupboard).

**^[DEBRIEF:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Blindfolded children: How did you know where to go?** (Heard partner’s instructions.)
- **How easy or hard was it to follow your partners instructions? Why?** (Accept/discuss responses.)
- **How easy or hard do you think it was for Abram to follow God’s instructions? Why?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Abram was God’s friend, so he recognized and trusted God’s voice and instructions. We can too.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 10: Faithful Abram** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and have the children recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Isaiah 30:21.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/10-isaiah-30-21.mp3] “Isaiah 30:21” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singers","value":"Amelia Shipton, Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"Katie Strachan"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Try to whisper the memory verse very quietly to each other.

- **How carefully did you have to listen?**
- **How carefully did Abram listen to “the word behind him”?**

We can listen carefully to God too, just as Abram did.

**^[READ/REFLECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to sit quietly with their eyes closed and their faces turned upward as you slowly read:

“Gazing up at the stars in Canaan, Abram quietly thought about the descendants God had promised him. Each star shone brightly, reminding him that all those who would be God’s friend, right down through time, would shine as his descendants. He was a star. His wife, Sarai, was a star. His household members were stars. You are a star. How amazing to be a star descendant of Abram!”

**^[PRAY and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to say a personal prayer. After their short prayer time, encourage them to spend time this week listening, not just talking, in their prayer time with God. We can listen to God most by paying attention to what He tells us in His Word, the Bible. We can also pay attention to the promptings of the Holy Spirit.

**How else can you become closer friends with God?** (Reading the Bible, studying the lesson, being in nature, listening to and singing worship songs.)

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, “Sugar Gliders,” and read prompt for personal prayer.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your watercolor sky picture and invite each child to make one too with paper, a white crayon (for stars), watercolor paints, and a black cardstock mountain. Hang these in the classroom if possible as a reminder of God’s promise.
