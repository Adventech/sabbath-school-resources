**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God is faithful, even when things seem impossible.

Have you ever doubted this? Perhaps you’ve felt as though your situation is helpless and things are out of your control. Perhaps someone has promised you something, only to let you down—and you wonder if God is dependable when others aren’t.

God is faithful, even when we are not. He is constant, even when others are not. And He is there for you, even when you feel as though no one else is.

The promises in His Word testify of this and strengthen our friendship with Him. They are an anchor to our soul, grounding us in the One who loves us more than any other. “The unfailing promises of God will keep your heart in perfect peace” (Ellen G. White, letter 91, 1887, in _Letters and Mansucripts_, vol. 5, p. 94).

Perfect peace. God offers it to you today. Claim His promises as personal prayers, knowing that “with God all things are possible” (Matthew 19:26). What a precious promise to claim!

This Sabbath, as you lead little ones in their walk with God, may you personally rest in His promises.

“Let us hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering, for He who promised is faithful” (Hebrews 10:23)
