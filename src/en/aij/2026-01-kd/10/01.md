Helper for God

New homes, new farms, and even big cities popped up around the world. God’s plan to fill the world with people was happening! But sadly, most people had forgotten about God again.

God loved all the people. He looked for a helper to remind them of His great love. God began a kind of “treasure hunt”! He didn’t need a map, or even clues. He didn’t look for shiny rubies or sparkly diamonds. He looked for friends! God tells us that His friends are like a “special treasure” (Deuteronomy 14:2).

Adam, Abel, and Seth had been God’s true friends. Enoch, Noah, and Shem had all loved and obeyed God too. Even when most other people were choosing to do wrong, they kept being faithful.

Now God was looking for a new, faithful friend. He found a friend called Abram. Abram loved God. Like Enoch, he spent time talking to God. Like Noah, he was happy to tell others about God. God decided to use Abram to do great things.

You are also God’s special treasure. Read all of **Deuteronomy 14:2.** He has chosen you to be His friend and helper.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- How can you become God’s friend and helper?
