**Big Idea:** Families are one of God’s wonderful blessings.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use the following props: **set of emotion** cards from AIJ website.
- Use your facial expressions and body language dramatically to express the range of emotions experienced by Moses, various members of his family, and the Israelites.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to Exodus and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

Distribute an **emotion card** to various children and have them hold up their card at appropriate times in the story. Note that most of these descriptors are emotions, although some of them are verbs.

**Notes:** Try to just affirm rather than enter into a long discussion every time emotion cards are held up.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites packed up their tents, rounded up their animals, and set off again. They were coming close to Midian, Moses’ old home.

As Moses journeyed through the familiar countryside, he thought of his family. His two sons, Gershom and Eliezer, and his wife, Zipporah, were at home in the house of her father, Jethro. Oh, how he missed them! ^[(Show emotion card.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Moses’ family had begun the long journey to Egypt with him, but along the way Moses had made the hard decision to send them back home ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He loved them too much to let them see the suffering and destruction that he knew would come upon Egypt. They would be well cared for in Jethro’s home until it was safe for them to be with him again.

One day Jethro heard that the Israelites were camped close by. His heart skipped with joy! ^[(Show emotion card.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Bags were quickly packed, goodbyes were said, and the excited little family made their way across the plains ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

As the travelers neared the Israelite camp, Jethro sent a message to Moses.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did the message say? Read Exodus 18:6.** (I am bringing your family to you.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses was overjoyed! He ran out to greet them. First he bowed down respectfully before his father-in-law, Jethro, and kissed him ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then he hugged his wife and sons as happy tears were shed ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses had so much news to share with his family. He told them how God had displayed His power through the 10 plagues in Egypt, saved them on Passover night, then parted the Red Sea to deliver them from Pharaoh’s army! He told them how God made the bitter water sweet, about the manna falling from heaven, and how they would get to collect and eat it for breakfast in the morning.

Jethro rejoiced and said, “Blessed be the Lord, who has delivered you out of the hand of the Egyptians” (Exodus 18:10). ^[(Show emotion card.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

With a heart full of thanks, Jethro offered a sacrifice to God for His goodness. Then brother Aaron, Moses, father-in-law Jethro, and all the elders of Israel enjoyed a feast together to celebrate God’s goodness and mercy ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In the morning Zipporah, Gershom, and Eliezer awoke to the hustle and bustle of the Israelite camp. This was very different to what they were used to at home! Moses was up early and off to work. They watched as people came flocking to him with their complaints. Everyone wanted him to settle their problems and disagreements ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Moses listened and counselled—all day long! He was trying very hard to teach the people God’s laws.

Zipporah and Jethro were worried. They saw that this was far too much work for tired Moses ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They both cared deeply for him and didn’t want him to wear himself out.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Pause.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **Can you think of a time you’ve noticed a family member worn out and needing help?**
- **To love like Jesus, how should we respond?** (Help them; ask them if they need anything; pray for them; tell someone else in the family.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jethro went to talk to Moses—something must be done to help him. He said, “Listen now to my voice; I will give you counsel, and God will be with you” (Exodus 18:19). He told Moses to choose some honest men who loved God and could be trusted. These men were to be helper leaders over groups of people. Some would be in charge of thousands, others in charge of hundreds, and others in charge of fifties and tens.

The leaders of tens, fifties, and hundreds would settle the smaller problems, and anything more difficult would be handled by the leaders of thousands. Then only very serious matters would need to go to Moses. That way Moses wouldn’t be wearing himself out, and the people wouldn’t have to wait in line all day to have their problems heard.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Moses do next? Read Exodus 18:24, 25.** (He followed Jethro’s advice.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Very soon the people were taking their problems to the appointed leaders. And sure enough, the plan worked well.

Moses wasn’t so weary at the end of the day, and he no doubt had more time to spend with his family.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The little family was blessed by the wise advice of Jethro, an older family member. They were also blessed by Moses’ willingness to listen and follow his instructions. And best of all, they were truly blessed to have God as their heavenly Father, the One who loved and cared for them all the time ^[(emotion card)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
