**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Flames of fire flickered over the bush. As Moses drew near, God called him by name. “Here I am,” Moses replied to God.

The same God that lit up the desert with His holy fire wants to communicate with you today. He is there in your home, in your workplace, and as you prepare to teach the children this Sabbath. He calls you by name and invites you to respond with awe and reverence. For where He is, is holy.

God had a mission for Moses. Moses was asked to represent God to those who didn’t know Him; to be a leader for His people. God promised that He was about to make a history-changing move. He was going to deliver His people.

Moses’ quick first response to God—“Here I am”—was replaced with uncertainty, insecurity, and fear.

We are often quick to pledge our willingness to serve God, but it can be a lot harder to accept the roles that place us outside our comfort zone. (Even teaching Sabbath School!) Be encouraged by God’s words to Moses: “I will certainly be with you” (Exodus 3:12). He will be with you too, as you follow Him.

“The divine command given to Moses found him self-distrustful, slow of speech, and timid. He was overwhelmed with a sense of his incapacity to be a mouthpiece for God to Israel. But having once accepted the work, he entered upon it with his whole heart, putting all his trust in the Lord. . . . God blessed his ready obedience, and he became eloquent, hopeful, self-possessed, and well fitted for the greatest work ever given to man. This is an example of what God does to strengthen the character of those who trust Him fully and give themselves unreservedly to His commands” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 255).

How will you choose to follow Him today?
