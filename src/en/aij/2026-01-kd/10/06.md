_Flip flop_ went Abram’s sandals as he walked. His footprints dotted the hot, sandy soil behind him. His footprints led all the way back to the palace in Egypt. But where would his feet go now?

Abram smiled at Sarai as he walked. He wasn’t going to be in charge anymore. He was going to let God be the leader, again. Our memory verse says, “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delights in his way” (Psalm 37:23).

Abram and Sarai had done a lot of walking! But they’d also done a lot of learning. They’d learned that God was their very best friend. And that He had chosen them as His friends and helpers!

As they walked on, they knew that God would be with them in good times and bad. They could have faith in Him always.

Abram and Sarai stopped as the sun began to set. It was time to thank God for His love and care. Talking to Him, and remembering His words of promise, made their faith strong.

What a wonderful friend God is!
