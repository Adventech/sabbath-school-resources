**Word of the Week**

**Disciple:** A disciple is a follower or student of a leader or teacher learning from them and copying what they do. Jesus had 12 close disciples when He was on earth. He has many more today.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

At the beginning of Jesus’ ministry, He called 12 men to follow Him, spend time with Him, and learn from Him in order to later teach others the truth about God. After Peter, James, John, and Andrew spent a night of unsuccessful fishing, Jesus told them to cast their fishing nets back into the water, resulting in the biggest catch of their lives. Jesus then gave them a mission to catch people instead of fish.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus calls them to follow Him.
- **Feel** happy that Jesus loves them and wants to lead their lives.
- **Respond** by accepting His invitation to follow Him and tell others about Him too.
