**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

“Now the man Moses was very humble, more than all men who were on the face of the earth” (Numbers 12:3). Such words of spiritual affirmation were a far cry from the accolades Moses received as a handsome (Acts 7:20) and naturally charismatic prince in Egypt. As a fearless army commander, a learned scholar, and a future pharaoh of the great Egyptian empire, life looked promising for Prince Moses.

But God “does not see as man sees” (1 Samuel 16:7). Looking into the future, God could see a different Moses; one better equipped to lead the Hebrews from slavery to a closeness with their God. One refined and humbled by the solitary life of a desert shepherd. One distrustful of self. One who would carry the Israelites in his heart as God did.

God shaped and molded Moses in Midian, and 40 years later he was ready. Ready for God’s call; ready for the challenge. The only problem was that Moses didn’t think he was ready. Distrustful of self and looking for an out, Moses tried to derail the call . . . five times!

Can you relate to Moses’ “I’m not good enough” excuses? Perhaps there are areas of service—outside your comfort zone—that God is asking you to step into now. Or perhaps children’s Sabbath School is that area! Be encouraged by God’s patient promises to help Moses. It is after all, always His mission that He will achieve; He just knows how awesome it will be for you to be included!

As you lead the children this week, may you all be inspired to say yes to the things He asks of us. You can count on Him to equip, empower, and energize. He promises, “I will certainly be with you” (Exodus 3:12).
