Happy music filled the air. People chatted and smiled. The wedding was going wonderfully. The bride and groom sat back together, eating delicious food and sipping grape juice. What a celebration!

Their families had come from all over the country to be with them. Jesus and His disciples had come too.

Mary smiled. She was so happy that Jesus had come to the wedding. She hadn’t seen Him for so many weeks. She gave Him a big hug hello.

Mary looked into Jesus’ face. “He had no special beauty” on the outside, but His words and actions were always pure and lovely (Isaiah 53:2, ICB).

Mary noticed that the disciples watched Jesus closely too. They showed Him great respect and called Him Master. There were others at the wedding who kept glancing over at Jesus too. Excitedly they talked to their friends about the wonderful things they had heard about Him.

But happy, humble Jesus wasn’t at the wedding to take the attention away from the bride and groom. He had come to quietly enjoy the wedding party. And to spread God’s goodness and happiness wherever He could.

How can we be lovely like Jesus? Read 1 Peter 3:3, 4.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- What instruments do you think they might have played at the wedding?
- What food do you think they ate?
- Have you ever done something to show God’s love in a quiet, gentle way?
