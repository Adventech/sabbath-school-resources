[[Daniel 3:7-30]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- What an incredible test for those three friends! And yet the secret that guaranteed their victory is still available to everyone today: _They trusted God_, no matter what the future seemed to hold.
- Shadrach, Meshach, and Abed-Nego’s lives show that when we trust in God, He can provide for us in ways that He could not do if we did not trust Him.
- The story of Daniel 3 shows that trust in God does not guarantee freedom from difficulties in our lives. But it also shows that during those difficulties God is just, good, and loving. He will give us strength to prevail, even when powerful forces are oppressing us.
