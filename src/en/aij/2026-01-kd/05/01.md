Two Trees, Two Choices


Every day Adam and Eve smiled and laughed together. They loved their home in the Garden of Eden. They had the important and fun job of being the gardeners. Together they planted seeds and shaped the vines. In the cool evenings they would walk and talk with God. Life couldn’t be more perfect.

But one evening, as they walked together, God told Adam and Eve a very important story. He told them about an angel called Lucifer. This angel had wanted to be more important than God. He had tricked other angels with his lies. His name was now Satan. God warned Adam and Eve that Satan would want to trick them into disobeying God too.

God also pointed out two special trees in the garden. A “help yourself” tree and a “don’t touch it” tree. The “help yourself” tree was called the tree of life. It looked amazing! It grew over the beautiful river and had 12 different delicious fruits! Yum! If they ate this fruit, they would live forever. What a special tree.

Then God’s voice became very serious as He told Adam and Eve about the “don’t touch it” tree. It was called the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. It also had yummy-looking fruit, but what did God say about it? **Read Genesis 2:16, 17.**

Adam and Eve nodded their heads and listened closely to God’s warnings and rules. God is loving, so He let them choose to follow Him or disobey.
