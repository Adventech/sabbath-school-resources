The gift of Jesus is the best gift ever! In small ways we can show Jesus how much we appreciate the gift. What can we do? **Read Matthew 25:34-40.**

There are so many people that need help and love in our world. In this beautiful verse Jesus tells us that every time we are kind to others, we are being kind to Him. This is a practical way we can tell Jesus that we appreciate what He has done for us. Today, make creative plans as a family to show kindness to others.

Create a Kindness Box

- Find a little box or container (preferably with a lid), paper, scissors, and colorful pens or pencils.
- Cut the paper into smaller pieces that will fit inside the box.
- Invite family members and/or friends to think of something kind they could do for someone else. It may or may not be someone who is with you right now
- Give everyone a small piece of paper and have them write the name of their chosen person at the top. Then they can write or draw something kind they will do for that person during the week. Alternatively, they could write something kind about that person that they will then give to them.
- Have them place the completed pieces of paper in the box and explain that next Sabbath you will have a sharing time when each person will tell what they did, how it went, and how they felt afterward.
- Gift-wrap the box to be opened next week. You might like to write Matthew 25:40 on a tag and attach it to the box. “I tell you the truth. Anything you did for any of my people here, you also did for me” (Matthew 25:40, ICB).
