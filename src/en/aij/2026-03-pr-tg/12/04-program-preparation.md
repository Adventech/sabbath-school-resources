Opening and Focus Activities

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **paper heart**, folded up small (or a dry sponge), and a **bowl of water**.

Source **small pieces of paper, water-soluble markers** (or diluted food coloring and a cotton swab), a **bowl of water**, and **bleach** (optional).

Tie It All Together

Print **^[Character Card 38: Zacchaeus in a Tree]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Write a series of **statements** on small pieces of **colored paper**—enough for one per child. Statements can include: “You’re a good friend”; “Your thoughtfulness reminds me of Jesus”; “I like your smile”; “You are kind”; “You are friendly”; “You’re awesome”; “You are brave”; “You are gentle”; “You are as nice as a birthday cake”; “I see Jesus in you”; “Thanks for helping me,” etc.
