Two Trees

God created Eden to be a beautiful forever home for Adam and Eve. From delicate flower to towering tree, He placed everything together in perfect order and beauty.

Two majestic trees stood in the middle of the garden. Each had a special purpose.

The first great tree was called the tree of life. The fruit of this tree would give life forever to Adam and Eve, and all their family to come.

The other great tree was also important, but God asked Adam and Eve not to eat from this tree—ever! What was this tree called? **Read Genesis 2:17.**

God could have left this tree out of His perfect garden, but everything about God is true and fair. This tree was like a signpost telling them that Lucifer, an angel in heaven, had rebelled, becoming Satan, which means “enemy.” His rebellion began a war between God and him, good and evil. God asked them never to eat the fruit of this tree. He never wanted them to know what evil was: to experience hurt, pain, and death. But He let them choose. God wanted them to be able to choose to love Him, instead of being forced. Choosing not to eat from the tree would show that they had chosen God’s side in the war between good and evil.

God told them they would die if they ate from this tree. He sent His loving angels to warn them that Satan would try to tempt them to do the wrong thing, to separate them from God. Trusting God, staying away from this tree, and staying together would keep them from sinning.
