Tithing is the biblical system of returning 10% of your increase, such as profits, wages, and salary, to God’s storehouse. It is clearly requested by God in the Bible, but it is still a somewhat controversial topic across Christianity.

Some who argue that tithing was only for the Jews, and that today, we are called to give our “all” for God, often end up returning far less than 10% to Him. In comparison, other Christians meticulously return an exact 10% tithe—even on the estimated value of gifts they receive—but have little interest in caring for the poor or refugees or showing mercy to others.

Jesus Himself said we should do both—return an honest tithe _and_ actively uphold justice, mercy, and faith (Matthew 23:23). And God told Moses that “all the tithe of the land . . . is holy to the Lord” (Leviticus 27:30, NKJV).

From years of testing God, as instructed in Malachi 3:8–12, millions of Seventh-day Adventists have become firm believers in tithing. Maybe you have also heard of the families from the business world whose surnames are now the famous brands Heinz, Kraft, Wrigley, Hershey, and Colgate. Did you know that they were all faithful tithers?

Jesus encourages us to return a faithful tithe to God so that the everlasting gospel can be preached far and wide to hasten Jesus’ soon return.

Let’s ask ourselves this important question today: Are my tithes in proportion to my blessings?

### Appeal

As we now return 10% of our increase to God as tithe and give our regular and systematic Promise offerings, let’s ask God to reveal to us if we have left out anything essential in our tithe calculations. Then, let’s step out in faith and follow His Word in our giving. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
