Bartimaeus cocked his head to one side and listened carefully as he sat beside the road near Jericho. He couldn’t see, but he could hear the sounds of a happy crowd getting louder and closer. He couldn’t work out what it was all about. He knew only there must be hundreds of noisy people coming toward him.

When the noisy crowd reached Bartimaeus, he asked what was going on. What did the people tell him? **Read Luke 18:37.**

His hopes were coming true; it was the best reply a passerby could ever have given him! Excitement bubbled up inside as he thought about the great Teacher and Healer being so close. He had waited and longed for this moment.

Bartimaeus stretched his neck upward and shouted at the top of his voice, “Jesus, Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:38). Those eight words were filled with hope and excitement, joy and desperation—all mixed up together.

His cry to Jesus also showed his faith. Bartimaeus believed the Bible prophecy that said the Messiah would be a descendant of David (Isaiah 11:1). He couldn’t see Jesus, but he knew exactly who He was: the Son of David, the promised Messiah.

Even though his request got swallowed up by the noisy crowd, Bartimaeus didn’t give up. He didn’t know what would happen next, but he believed Jesus would heal him. And that excited him.

Sometimes we don’t get an answer from Jesus right away. Like Bartimaeus, hold on to your faith. Jesus will hear and answer you!
