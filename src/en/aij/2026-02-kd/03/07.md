Have you ever seen or touched snow? Close your eyes and imagine your hands are holding a ball of snow right now. What might it feel like?

Snow looks like a fluffy white powder, but if you could look really closely through a microscope, you would see that snow is actually made up of thousands and thousands of little snowflakes. Snowflakes are tiny drops of frozen water. Each snowflake is see-through and looks like a fancy-shaped glittering star.

Did you know that no two snowflakes are the same? God has made each one its own special shape.

You’ll only find snow in places that get very cold. Way up high in the clouds, it gets even colder! And when it’s freezing cold in the clouds, they make little ice crystals. These crystals get heavy and fall through the sky, down to the ground as snowflakes. Each snowflake takes a different trip from the cloud to the ground, and this gives each one its own amazing shape.

When snow covers the ground, everything looks different! Look how beautiful, white, and clean it looks in this picture, after the snow has fallen.

Fresh snow looks a lot like God’s forgiveness. When we sin, it’s as though we are messy with mistakes. God wants to make us beautiful and clean again, just like a white blanket of snow. He says, “If we confess our sins, He is faithful to forgive us our sins” (1 John 1:9).

If you live in a place where it snows, look at the tiny detail in each different snowflake. Thank God for His blanket of snowy-white forgiveness that covers our sins and makes us beautiful again.
