**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Tonight was the night! After years of oppression and pain, freedom was coming. God had shown His power, and now He would fulfill His promise. He would save all who put their trust in Him.

Imagine the nervous excitement and anticipation as the children of Israel rushed to obey God’s instructions; to spill the blood of a lamb, paint doorposts, gather bitter herbs, bake in haste, and dress to leave at a moment’s notice. With each task their faith must have grown deeper and stronger. Then every person, young and old, had to stop and wait. To do nothing but trust in God.

And God delivered—as promised.

With joy we too can place all our trust in Jesus. We are saved, not by our behavior, but by our Savior. His blood has been spilled to cover our sinfulness. Our future is secure through trust in Him. “Truly my soul silently waits for God; from Him comes my salvation” (Psalm 62:1).

As your heart fills with gratitude, journal your thanks. You may like to draw a doorframe and fill it with your prayerful response. May your heart be made ready to lead the children to put their trust in our loving Savior too.

“We look to self, as though we had power to save ourselves; but Jesus died for us because we are helpless to do this. In Him is our hope, our justification, our righteousness. . . . At this very time He is carrying on His work in our behalf, inviting us to come to Him in our helplessness and be saved” (Ellen G. White, _Faith and Works_, p. 36).
