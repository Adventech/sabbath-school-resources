**Word of the Week**

**Family:** God made families. He made Adam the daddy and Eve the mommy. Adam and Eve had children as part of their family. Extended families can include grandparents, caregivers, aunts, uncles, and cousins. God gave us families so we could love and care for each other.

**Lesson Summary**

It had been three months since Moses left his family in Midian and went to Egypt. Now his family was coming to him! Moses was happy to be with his family again. He told his father-in-law, wife, and two sons about all the wonderful things God had done. In honor of God’s goodness, Moses and his family ate a special meal together. The next day Moses’ father-in-law, Jethro, watched him help the people solve their problems all day long. Concerned for his well-being, Jethro gave Moses some godly advice. Moses listened to his advice and chose good men to help him lead the Israelites. Together they were able to accomplish so much more.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** God made families to love each other.
- **Feel** love for God and their family.
- **Respond** by thinking of ways to love and care for their family.
