**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Play **Make-a-Wave**: Use a blue sheet/blanket/tablecloth and ask the children to hold on to the edge of the “sea” so that it is stretched out. Place item/s in the middle. Call out a number between 1 and 10. Number 1 represents very gentle waves. Number 10 represents huge storm waves. The children will make the “sea” move according to the number you tell them. The object of the game is to see if they can keep the items on the sheet. At what number do things fall off the “sea”? (When they do, place them back on the sheet for another round.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** **Have you ever been in a storm that was so big and wild it felt like one of our 7-10 “waves”? How did it make you feel?**

Share with the children that our story this week is about the disciples and Jesus being in a big storm. It was so big and the wind was so violent that it was like being in an earthquake! **Let’s discover what the disciples learned from being in a storm with Jesus.**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **When I am scared, I can trust in Jesus to calm my fears and give me peace.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Create a **boat outline** on the floor using masking tape. Scrunch up pieces of blue and white paper into balls. Choose three or four children to volunteer to be “disciples” in the boat. The rest of the children will then gently throw the scrunched-up paper into the boat, representing the waves crashing into the boat in today’s Bible story. The “disciples” need to pick up the papers as fast as they can and throw them back out of the boat so it doesn’t “sink.”

Then say, “Stop!” and have the children immediately stop filling the boat with “water.”

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What did you notice as the “disciples” tried to save the boat from sinking?** (It got noisy and chaotic.)
- **How do you think the “disciples” felt during the storm?** (Ask the children outside the boat to share their thoughts first, then clarify these thoughts by asking the “disciples.”)
- **Have you ever been scared in a difficult situation? What happened to make you feel scared?**
- **What did you do? Did you try to solve the problem yourself, or did you ask for help?**

**Teaching Point:** In tough situations, it’s our normal reaction to look at what’s happening—and ourselves—and feel fear. When we are scared, we can often try to solve our problems ourselves, forgetting that Jesus is with us, ready to help us. Jesus wants us to have faith instead of fear. He wants us to remember that we can call on His help in any situation. When we focus on Jesus, He will calm our fears and give us peace.
