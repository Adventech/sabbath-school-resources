**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them. Share that today we want to think about something special. Show the word **BLESSING** in big letters.

- **What do you think this word means?** (The good and special things God put into someone’s life, or a gift that improves a person’s life, that makes them happy and content.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to make a blessing chain by writing blessings from God on strips of **paper**. Staple the strips together, interlocking them with one another to form a chain to hang in the room. These blessings can be celebrated during prayer time.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show a wrapped gift box. Explain that a blessing is like a gift that we give to others. It’s special when the gift gets passed on.

Show the children a smooth stone with the word “gift” written on it and explain that it represents a gift. Give each child a cardboard cylinder and have them stand in a line, connecting their cylinders to make one long channel.

The children will pass the stone from beginning to end trying to keep it from falling to the ground. (Line the children up from tallest to smallest, then start rolling the rock from the tallest child for momentum.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Personalize the following story with names of children or people known to your Sabbath School. As you mention each person, take the stone (“gift”) to that person. “[Jake’s] grandma gives him a hug and says, ‘You are special, and God loves you.’ [Jake] feels happy and helps his little sister [Jane] clean up her toys. [Jane] feels loved and draws a picture for her neighbor [Daniel], telling him God cares, and invites him to church. The neighbor [Daniel] smiles and decides to go to church. After church, the neighbor [Daniel] meets a woman [Mary] who taught his son many years ago. He affirms the teacher [Mary]. The teacher [Mary] feels encouraged and decides to pray with a student [Andrew] who is sad.”

- **Did [Jake’s] blessing stay with him?** (No, it kept going!)

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** As we learn more about Jesus in today’s lesson, we will be reminded of the Big Idea: When Jesus blesses my life in wonderful ways, I can pass a blessing on to others.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[PASS THE GIFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Have children sit in a circle. Let’s pass this blessing gift along a little more. Play music while they pass the “gift” around. When the music stops, the child holding the gift will give a blessing (a compliment, a kind word, or a prayer) to someone else. Keep playing until everyone has given and received a blessing.

- **How did it feel to bless and encourage someone?**
- **How did it feel to receive a blessing? Will these blessings happen by themselves?** (No; we need to choose to pass the gift on. God can help us choose to do this.)
- **How might you keep the blessings going this week?**

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 39: Jesus Heals Peter’s Mother-in-law** to place in their storage container/bag. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the **memory verse**. Explain that in Bible times people couldn’t look at the king’s face or eyes. They had to keep their eyes low. No one on earth has ever seen God’s face, but one day we will see His face! The Bible tells us that God’s face shines upon us, and He gives us His peace.

- **What are your favorite words in this verse?**
- **How does it feel to know that God’s face shines on you?**

- **How were you blessed?**

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Say the **Big Idea** together a final time: **When Jesus blesses my life in wonderful ways, I can pass a blessing on to others.**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Place the gift box in your circle. From inside the box, take out the words (written on separate pieces of paper): Home, Classroom, Church, Playground, and Neighborhood. Challenge the children to continue to bless others in each of these areas, because Jesus needs every one of us to be His active disciples.

Invite children to stand and bow their heads and pray **Numbers 6:24-26** as a prayer of blessing: “The Lord bless you and keep you; the Lord make His face shine upon you, and be gracious to you; the Lord lift up His [face] upon you, and give you peace.”

Then invite each child to say to the person next to them, “May God bless you and keep you.”
