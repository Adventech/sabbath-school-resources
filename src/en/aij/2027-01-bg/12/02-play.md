Sing

Sing a song of thanks to God. Either make up a song with your child or sing a familiar song you know to tell God you love Him and thank Him for His kindness.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#38af4a"}}}
Create

Paint some “thankfulness rocks” with your child. Decorate each rock with pictures/stickers of something your child is grateful for, such as a person, food, animal, place, toy, memory, or something in nature.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#38af4a"}}}
Play

Hide the “thankfulness rocks” around the yard for your child to find. Then build a little altar _(rock pile)_ like the one Noah built. Tell your child that every time they see it, it will remind them to say “Thank You” to God, just as Noah’s altar reminded him.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#38af4a"}}}
Pray

Make a family gratitude journal. Each night during worship, choose something as a family that you are grateful for, and draw it _(use a simple symbol)_ on a fresh page. Then have each person take turns decorating the page with markers and stickers. You might like to provide a box of cutout pictures from old books or magazines to use as well. Then pray to God as a family, thanking Him for that specific thing.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#38af4a", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Oh, give thanks to the Lord, for He is good!” _(Psalm 107:1)_.
