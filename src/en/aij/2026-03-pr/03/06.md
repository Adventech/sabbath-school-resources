As the evening sky darkened, the Wise Men left Jerusalem. And there was God’s star, twinkling above them once again! “When they saw the star, they rejoiced with exceedingly great joy” (Matthew 2:10) and they followed it to Bethlehem, right to the very house where Baby Jesus lay sleeping.

The Wise Men knocked and entered the cozy home. Here was Jesus, God’s Son, the Savior, the newborn King. They knelt and worshipped Him with love and devotion. They gave Him their hearts. Then they gave the other gifts they had brought. What were they? **Read Matthew 2:11.**

These were expensive and precious gifts, and each had special meaning for Jesus. They gave Him gold because Jesus was a king. They gave Him frankincense because Jesus would be a priest. Frankincense was the valuable perfume that the priests burned in worship ceremonies in the temple. And because Jesus would die one day to save His people, they gave Him myrrh. It was a sweet-smelling perfume used to prepare bodies for burial.

Jesus is our King, our Priest, and our Savior. He is worthy of our worship. We may not be able to give Him expensive gifts, but we can give Him our time, talents, gifts of money, and other things. And best of all, we can give Him our hearts.
