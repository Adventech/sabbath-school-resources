**Did you know that some animals give gifts to each other? Gentoo penguins are one such animal! (Show picture.) I wonder what they can teach us.**

Gentoo Penguins are large penguins that love to swim super-fast. They are the third-largest species of penguins and can grow up to 27 to 35 inches (70 to 90 centimeters) tall and weigh between 10 to 19 pounds (4.5 and 8.5 kilograms). They live in Antarctica and subantarctic Islands. A Gentoo Penguin has a reddish bill. It also has a black back and head, white belly, and a distinctive white stripe running eye to eye across the top of its head.

Gentoo Penguins do a lot of swimming and are the fastest species of penguins in the world. They can reach speeds of up to 22 miles (36 kilometers) per hour! Gentoo Penguins can also dive down deeply into the ocean when looking for food. They hunt for krill, fish, and squid. Have you ever tried to see how long you can hold your breath when swimming? The Gentoo Penguin can hold its breath and stay underwater for as long as seven minutes! They can make up to 450 dives in a day looking for food.

Something male Gentoo Penguins like to do is give gifts of pebbles! When they are ready to mate, they will search and search along the rocky shoreline, looking for the most beautiful pebble. They like to find the perfect pebble present and give it to their special female penguin. The female Gentoo Penguins use these pebbles to make their nests. So while this gift of pebbles is more for practical reasons, the male still needs to find the perfect pebble to show his female friend he is ready to start a family. If the female penguin accepts the gift, she will add his pebble present to her nest. Following some getting-to-know-you rituals, the male will help the female finish building the nest, ready for the eggs to be laid. His pebble was the perfect present.
