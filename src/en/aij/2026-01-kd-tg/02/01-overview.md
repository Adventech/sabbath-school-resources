{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

On Day 6 God created animals and man, and Adam was given the special responsibility of naming the animals. God created for Adam a beautiful wife named Eve. The creation work of our generous, loving God was finished at the end of the Creation week, and everything was very good.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that animals and people were created on Day 6 of Creation.
- **Feel** the deep love God has for us in giving us a wonderful world with so much variety.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His creation.
