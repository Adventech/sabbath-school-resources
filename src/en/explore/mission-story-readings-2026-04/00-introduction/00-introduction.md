Dear Sabbath School Leader,

This quarter we feature the Trans-European Division, which oversees the Seventh-day Adventist Church’s work in the following countries and territories: Aland Islands, Albania, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Croatia, Denmark, Estonia, Faroe Islands, Finland, Ireland, Isle of Man, Jersey, Latvia, Lithuania, Montenegro, Netherlands, North Macedonia, Norway, Poland, Serbia, Slovenia, Sweden, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland, and the southern portion of Cyprus.

The region is home to 208.3 million people, including 91,496 Adventists. That’s a ratio of one Adventist for every 2,276 people.

Part of a special offering collected on the last Sabbath of this quarter will go to support four projects in Finland, Poland, Croatia, and the United Kingdom. Those Thirteenth Sabbath projects are listed in the sidebar.

This quarter takes you behind the scenes in these four countries to see how God is working in wonderful ways, touching the lives of people and using them to bless others. I’m sure you, and those with whom you share, will be inspired as I was when I listened to these people share their stories.

### Special Features

If you want to make your Sabbath school class come alive this quarter, we offer photos, videos, and other materials to accompany each mission story. More information is provided in the sidebar with each story.

Follow us at https://facebook.com/missionquarterlies/ to read and share stories and download pictures and short videos illustrating the stories.

Download the PDF version of the Youth/Adult Mission quarterlies at https://am.adventistmission.org/mq-adult, and the Children’s Mission quarterly at https://am.adventistmission.org/mq-children.

Mission Spotlight videos are available completely free to download or stream at https://am.adventistmission.org/mission-spotlight.

Thank you for your support of Adventist Mission around the world—being grounded in the Bible and focused on the mission!

Gina Wahlen\
Editor

### Opportunities

This quarter’s Thirteenth Sabbath Offering, also known as the Quarterly Mission Project Offering, will support four projects in the Trans-European Division:

- Establishing the Family Mission Center OIKOS church plant, Helsinki, Finland
- Expanding KOMPAS, the first Adventist Primary School in Poland, Podkowa Lesna, Poland
- Rebuilding the Seventh-day Adventist Church, Zagreb, Croatia
- Provide “SafeSpace2B” outreach hubs in community schools, England, United Kingdom
