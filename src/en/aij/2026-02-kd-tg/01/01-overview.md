**Word of the Week**

**Kindness:** Kindness is love in action. When you are kind, you are helpful and friendly to others. We can show kindness with caring words, listening ears, willing feet, and helping hands.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Abraham sent his trusted servant Eliezer to find a wife for his son Isaac. Eliezer asked God for a sign so he would know whom to choose. Rebekah showed kindness by offering to water Eliezer’s camels. This was the sign Eliezer asked for. Rebekah chose to return home with Eliezer and marry Isaac. Years later Isaac and Rebekah prayed for children, and God gave them twin boys, Jacob and Esau. Esau, the elder twin, held the birthright—he would be the blessed leader of the family. But when the boys were older, Esau traded his birthright for a bowl of Jacob’s lentil stew.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is kind.
- **Feel** a desire to use their hands and words in sharing God’s kindness with others.
- **Respond** by asking God to help them show love and kindness to those around them.
