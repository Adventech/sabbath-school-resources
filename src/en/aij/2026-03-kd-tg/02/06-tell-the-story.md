**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He is the greatest gift ever.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Involve the children with hand actions and sounds for the following cues: trumpets, clip-clop of donkey, knocking.
- Use a baby doll as Jesus and wrap in cloth. Place in tub/container to act as a “manger.”
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **[(week 2 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})_Toot-toot-toot._ The trumpets blasted ^[(children copy noise and action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Toot-toot-toot. The announcement boomed across cities and towns all over the land. Ruler Caesar Augustus was ordering everyone to go to their family’s hometown. He wanted to do a count of all the people.

Joseph and Mary hurried to get ready. Mary’s tummy was big and round because she was pregnant with Baby Jesus. He could be born any day now! It would be hard for her to travel with a pregnant tummy. But a rule was a rule. They had to go to Joseph’s hometown of Bethlehem.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})After packing their bags, Joseph gently lifted Mary onto their donkey’s back. With a clip-clop ^[(clap hands on legs)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) they were on their way.

_Clip-clop. Clip-clop_ ^[(clap action on legs)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Mary held her tummy as she swayed left and right on the donkey’s back. What a wobbly ride! But she smiled ^[(smile)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They didn’t need to worry on this long trip. God was with them. His angels would keep them safe.

Finally Joseph could see a beautiful city up ahead. He pointed ^[(point)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and smiled ^[(smile)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It was Bethlehem!

The donkey’s slow clip-clop, clip-clop became clippity-cloppity, clippity-cloppity ^[(clap action on legs)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) as Joseph eagerly rushed toward the city.

As they entered the gates, there were people everywhere! Like Mary and Joseph, lots of people had come to be counted. Joseph, Mary, and their donkey squeezed past the crowds of visitors. They walked up and down the narrow streets looking for a place to sleep.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})Joseph stopped at an inn and knocked ^[(knock, knock)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). As the door creaked open, Joseph asked for a room to stay for the night. The owner shook his head and closed the door. “There were no rooms left in the inn” (Luke 2:7, ICB).

The only place to stay was a little stable. This place where animals slept would be their room for the night.

Joseph tied up their tired donkey. Then he helped Mary lie down on the scratchy hay. It had been such a long, bumpy journey! It was time to rest and sleep.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})On this night, in this very place, among these animals, something amazing happened. Baby Jesus was born! “For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given” (Isaiah 9:6).

God had given Jesus to the world. This was the greatest, most precious gift ever!

Joseph and Mary wrapped Him up and held Him close ^[(wrap and hold baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They knew their tiny Baby was someone very special. He was “God with us” (Matthew 1:23).

Mary and Joseph looked into His beautiful face and smiled. This special Baby needed a name. The shining angel had told them, “And you shall call his name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21).

Jesus. Yes, His name would be Jesus.

Mary placed Baby Jesus in a manger—a place where the animals fed. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Angels above watched over Him. Jesus had been born! Jesus was the very best gift that God could give the world to show how much He loves us.
