The nobleman’s horse galloped fast into Cana. Arriving at the gates, he quickly climbed off and was told where to find Jesus. Big crowds surrounded Jesus. But the nobleman pushed and squeezed his way toward Him. The nobleman didn’t have much time. He really, really needed Jesus’ help!

Bowing low, the nobleman “went to Jesus and begged him to come to Capernaum and heal his son. His son was almost dead” (John 4:47, ICB).

Choosing to believe in Jesus, what did the nobleman then say? **Read John 4:49.**

Jesus’ heart filled with compassion. He already knew about the pain and sadness the nobleman and his family had gone through. He knew the nobleman before they had even met. He wanted to help. What a wonderful, caring Savior!

Jesus answered, “Go your way; your son lives” (John 4:50).

Suddenly, all the worry in the nobleman’s mind went away. He was filled with peace.

The Bible tells us, “The man believed what Jesus told him” (John 4:50, ICB).

With a happy heart, the nobleman thanked Jesus. Then with a big smile on his face he walked away, knowing Jesus’ words were true.

The words of Jesus are always true. We can trust the Bible. Recite your memory verse and thank Jesus for His powerful words.
