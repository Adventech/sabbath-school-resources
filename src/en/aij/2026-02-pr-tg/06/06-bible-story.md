**Big Idea:** God gives me peace and helps me to make peace with others.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob waved to his son Joseph as he ran off to join his brothers in the fields ^[(wave)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jacob had 12 sons, but he wrongly loved Joseph best of all. Like little Benjamin, Joseph was Rachel’s son, born when Jacob was old. Sadly, Mother Rachel had died on the way home to Hebron in Canaan.

Joseph lifted his shepherd’s staff and waved it back to his father with a grin ^[(wave stick)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then he ran to find his brothers and their flocks.

The teenager was very close to his father. He had grown up listening to Jacob’s stories of God’s love, care, and special promises. He knew God had brought them safely back to Hebron to live, and had given them lots of animals and a good home. Joseph had chosen to be God’s friend and to obey Him. This made him happy and honest.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph’s 10 older brothers didn’t much care for the ways of God. They weren’t thoughtful. They lied, were rude, and caused trouble with their neighbors.

Out in the fields Joseph carefully looked after his father’s sheep. He noticed the bad things his brothers said and did while his father wasn’t looking. He felt sad to see them sinning against God.

Jacob’s heart was full of love for his honest son. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He decided to give him a gift that showed how much he loved him ^[(colorful coat)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Jacob smiled with pride as he carefully carried his gift to Joseph’s tent—a beautiful coat, woven with brilliant colors. It wasn’t like a shepherd’s coat; it was like a robe that rich and important people wore.

Joseph was so excited! He hugged the coat to his chest. It was stunning! Carefully he put it on and spun around ^[(put on colorful coat)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

His brothers were not happy. They didn’t have beautiful robes like that! Here was proof their father was playing favorites. The Bible says, “But when his brothers saw that their father loved him more than all his brothers, they hated him and could not speak peaceably to him” (Genesis 37:4).

The brothers no longer smiled at Joseph as they passed his tent. Out in the fields their mean words must have hit Joseph like stinging arrows.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One night Joseph lay his weary head down on his bed. All was quiet and still. No older brothers spoke nasty words to him or shot him angry glares. Soon he was fast asleep.

In the night God sent Joseph a vivid dream.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What happened in his dream? Read Genesis 37:7.** (Joseph’s sheaf of wheat stood tall while all his brothers’ sheaves bowed down to his sheaf.)

Joseph woke up and rubbed his eyes. What a surprising dream! Scratching his head in wonder, he went and told his brothers all about it.

His brothers stared back at their little brother and clenched their fists. They were furious! They replied, “Do you really think you will be king over us? Do you truly think you will rule over us?” (Genesis 37:8, ICB).

The brothers had been jealous. But now they were angry—really angry!

As the stars twinkled above their tent one night, God sent Joseph another amazing dream. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Against a jet-black sky, 11 stars, the bright silver moon, and the fiery sun all bowed down to him.

The dream felt too big to keep to himself, so once again he shared it with his family.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How did Joseph’s father respond to this dream? Read Genesis 37:10, 11.** (His father scolded him for thinking he would be better than everyone else in the family.)

Jacob believed God had sent Joseph the dream. It clearly showed Joseph ruling over his whole family. But Jacob spoke sharply to Joseph, for he knew the dream would upset Joseph’s older brothers.

Jacob was right. “Joseph’s brothers were jealous of him” (Genesis 37:11, ICB).

Joseph quietly treasured the precious dreams in his heart, wondering what they could mean. He remembered the wonderful dream father Jacob had been given about an angel ladder. He might not know today what the dreams meant, but he knew he would understand one day, for he trusted God, his caring and faithful friend.

- **What do you think Joseph should do about what he saw and heard in the fields?** (Accept all answers.)
- **What did Joseph do about it? Read Genesis 37:2.** (Told his father.)
- **Would it have been hard or easy for Joseph to tell his father about his brothers? Why?** (Hard; Jacob would be disappointed; his brothers would be angry.)
