**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How easy/hard was it to notice all the items on the tray and remember them?**
- **Do we always notice everything around us?**

God notices everything and everyone, and He notices our needs. He wants us to notice the needs of those around us too.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to stand and walk around your Sabbath School space to see what they notice. (They should notice your six folded papers, each with a question inside.)

Have a child read out the question marked with a number 1 (with help if needed). Discuss together before moving to question 2, until you’ve moved through all six questions. The six questions are:

1. **What needs did God notice in this story?** (Ruth and Naomi’s need for food, land, money. Ruth’s need for a husband, family, and home.)
2. **What did Boaz notice in this story?** (Ruth’s kindness to Naomi, and her faith in God.)
3. **What did Naomi notice in this story?** (That Boaz was a good man, that Ruth needed a husband, that God had led them to Boaz.)
4. **How did God show His kindness and care for Ruth and Naomi?** (He gave them food, led them to Boaz, gave them a new family and home. Included them in Jesus’ family.)
5. **How was Boaz like God?** (He was kind and caring, protected Ruth, saved her family.)
6. **What kind things might you do for others to show that you notice their needs?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to return to their seats. Show the class a variety of flowers. Pass the flowers around and allow the children to touch and smell them.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children:

- **What do these flowers do to get what they need? Do they go to work?**
- **Do they go to the food market? Do they go shopping for clothes?** (No.)

And yet God says that even Solomon in all his glory wasn’t clothed as magnificently as a lily (Matthew 6:28, 29). Our Father in heaven knows what we need, even before we ask Him. Just as God provided for Ruth and Naomi’s needs, He’ll provide for your needs too. You can be confident of this truth.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** We don’t need to worry, because our loving Father God promises to care for us. We can trust Him and live with peace instead.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Discuss with the children how they might notice and be encouraging to the friend they brought to Sabbath School. Their Mission Challenge this week is to: **Find out what would help your friend learn more about Jesus.** Encourage them to be good listeners (an important part of “noticing”), then talk with their families about how they can help their friend in small, practical ways. Share that their friend may need a ride to Sabbath School or the opportunity to come to their home to hear Bible stories.

**^[RESPOND:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Ask three children to share what they have learned from today’s lesson. Affirm their answers and the **Big Idea: God notices my needs.** God can use us to notice other’s needs and to show them care and practical help. Share that because **God knows what we need and gives it to us, we don’t need to worry**. Invite the children to physically “kick” worry out the door (kick the air) and instead trust in God this week to take care of all their needs.

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Conclude by praying, thanking God that He cares for each person and notices all our needs.
