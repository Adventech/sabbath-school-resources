**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Play **“Blind Pictionary.”** Give each child a piece of paper and pencil. Ask the children to close their eyes and give them step-by-step instructions about something they need to draw (a stick person, or a simple house with a tree next to it). When everyone is finished, ask the children to open their eyes, look at their drawing, and see how it turned out.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Talk about how difficult it was to draw without being able to see.

- **What are some tasks that would be difficult to do if you were blind?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Today we’re going to explore a Bible story about a man who was blind. His name was Bartimaeus. Share that while people who are blind or visually impaired can learn, work, travel, and lead full lives now, things were very different in Bible times and in this culture. This man’s life was very hard. When Bartemaeus heard of Jesus and the miracles He was doing, he became very excited!

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus loves to see me get excited about what He can do for me.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray. Ask God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the Bible story with enthusiasm.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Divide the class into small groups and give each group a **Bartimaeus puzzle**. On five pieces are ideas from

the story. On the other five pieces are messages for us from the story. The children need to match the story with the message. Then the children in each group should talk together about which of the messages is most relevant to them right now.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 49: Bartimaeus** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture on the card.

Ask children to close their eyes and imagine being blind for a long, long time. No wonder Bartimaeus loudly cried out to God.

- **Have you ever wanted to cry out to God because you knew He could help you?**

Ask the children to say the **memory verse**: “The Lord opens the eyes of the blind; the Lord raises those who are bowed down” (Psalm 146:8).

- **What do you think it means when it says, “The Lord raises those who are bowed down”? What kind of things make people feel “bowed down”?**

Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: **“Psalm 146:8.”**
