Mana wasn’t sure he was ready to join Pathfinders. But after seeking God’s guidance, he joined and became the first Pathfinder from his island to be baptized. His journey began with missionaries from Adventist Volunteer Service, whose work this quarter includes a new volunteer center at Chile Adventist University, training the next generation of missionaries.
