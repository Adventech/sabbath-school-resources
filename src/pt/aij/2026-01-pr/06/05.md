Out em the field, nobody saw the terrible thing Cain had Façane a seu brother, mas Deus knew. Como did Deus know? **Leia Psalm 139:1-3.**

Deus had seen o que was em Cain’s heart, e Ele saw o que Cain had Façane. Mas Deus’s amor could easily grow em Cain’s heart—se only ele would let isso. Então Deus deu Cain a chance.

Deus’s kind voice rang out a Cain, “Onde is Abel seu brother?”

Cain angrily replied, “Eu Faça not know. Am Eu meu brother’s keeper?” (Gênesis 4:9).

Deus was sadly disappointed. Cain had now added lying a seu string de bad choices. Cain didn’t want a new heart; ele’d chosen a guarde seu selfish one instead.

Porque Cain wouldn’t admit o que ele’d Façane e Diga ele was sorry, there would now be very sad consequences. Primeiro, Deus told Cain ele would have a work much harder a grow food em the ground, porque ele had killed seu brother there.

Mas even sadder, Deus had a send ele away de seu home e family a wander around the earth. Adam e Eve wept as eles watched seu son walk away. No Abel, e now no Cain. Seu hearts e seu home felt então empty. Like a dark cave filled com gloom.

Only Deus’s amor could fill the gap seu precious sons had left.
