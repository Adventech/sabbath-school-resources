Retell the Story

Go on a picnic. Ahead of time, cut some food into the shape of a fish. Put these “fish” along with small pieces of bread into a basket and hide them under a kitchen towel. This will be your miracle “surprise food.” Invite your child to help pack a basket for a picnic. Count together as you put five small pieces of bread and two pieces of “fish” into the basket, then cover those with a cloth. Sit on a picnic blanket to tell the story. When you get to the part when Jesus thanks God for the food, then take out five pieces of bread. Reach into the basket again to find there is more bread and more “fish.” Exclaim, “There is enough for everyone!” Show your child that you had hidden the extra underneath. What Jesus did was something amazing only He could do.

Play

Set up a “Caring Center” with dolls or stuffed animals, bandages, child-safe ice packs, pretend food, and books. Talk with your child about how they can also help care for others by using kind, gentle words, taking time to read or play with someone, and sharing what they have.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Give

Write a list of things we can do to help others. Let your child think of as many ideas as possible. Choose an idea from the list each day to complete together. Pack bags with nonperishable food, socks and warm clothing items, toothbrushes and toothpaste, soap, and other personal-care items. Donate bags to a homeless shelter or other community organization. Jesus can use your gift to help others who are in need and to show each person that He cares for them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Share

Bake small loaves of bread with your child. Help them measure and pour each ingredient and knead the dough with their hands. Make enough to share with a neighbor or someone they know who is sick or hurt.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Connect

Sit outside and talk about what it would have been like to be part of the crowd that day on the mountainside. Imagine the people’s faces as they saw the little lunch grow bigger before their eyes!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

Ask your child to list the foods they enjoy most and thank God for them. Teach your child a simple thank-You prayer or song to say together at mealtimes.
