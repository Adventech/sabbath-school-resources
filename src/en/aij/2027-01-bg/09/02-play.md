Connect

Share a story about a time in your life that God answered a prayer and solved a problem that was too big for you. Talk about how God is always bigger than even our biggest problems!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5e7b36"}}}
Learn and Pray

Look up some pictures of really big things, such as a big truck, whale, fridge, spaceship. God is bigger than all these things. He can always help us. Nothing is too big for God. Pray together, thanking God for being so big, so strong, and so mighty.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5e7b36"}}}
Create

Make an ark with a closing door. Using a cardboard cereal box, draw and cut out an ark shape. In the middle, draw a big square for the door. Cut along the top, one side, and the bottom. Let your child color it in with crayons or markers. Then trace around the ark on to another piece of cardboard and cut it out. On the back, glue around the edges and stick the two pieces of cardboard together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5e7b36"}}}
Retell

Retell this week’s story by using your play ark with either peg people, sticks, stones, or toy animals. Play together with your child. Talk about how kind and strong God is.
