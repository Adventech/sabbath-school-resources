God’s words rang in Moses’ ears—and he found them scary! Go to Pharaoh? Bring the children of Israel out of Egypt? Me? Really? Moses was terrified! He said, “Who am I that I should go to Pharaoh, and that I should lead the children of Israel out of Egypt?” (Exodus 3:11).

Again and again God had told Moses that He had a plan and that He would make it happen. God had said, “I have seen the troubles my people have suffered in Egypt. And I have heard their cries. . . . I have come down to save them. . . . I will bring them out of the land. I will lead them to a good land with lots of room. . . . So now I am sending you . . .” (Exodus 3:7-10, ICB).

Even though Moses was scared and didn’t realize God was in control, God was gracious and encouraging. He patiently told Moses what to do when he got to Egypt, what to say to the Israelites, how God would lead them out of Egypt, and what the Promised Land would be like (Exodus 3:7-17). And best of all, God said, “I will certainly be with you” (Exodus 3:12).

But Moses still had trouble trusting and obeying. He was worried the Israelites wouldn’t believe him. So God used actions to show that He could do the impossible.

God told Moses to throw his walking stick onto the ground. He did, and it became a snake! Moses fled in terror. “But the Lord said to him, ‘Reach out and grab the snake by its tail’ ” (Exodus 4:4, ICB). When Moses did, it became a stick again!

God performed another miracle to reassure Moses. What happened next? **Read Exodus 4:6-9.**

Even then, Moses didn’t feel confident. He said he didn’t speak well. God patiently listened, and then gave Moses another beautiful promise: “Now therefore, go, and I will be with your mouth and teach you what you shall say” (Exodus 4:12).

Just like Moses, we sometimes look at our strength instead of God’s. When we do, we find it hard to do what He asks us to do. But God is patient with us, just as He was with Moses. He speaks to encourage us, He gives us miracles and promises, and He listens as we talk honestly to Him.
