**Focus:** Jesus surprised Wyan and his mother with blessings because of Wyan’s faith.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Pennsylvania, USA**, on your **globe** or **world map**.

Wyan was excited! It was time to go to a new school. At this school he would learn about God.

Wyan’s family didn’t go to church. But one day there was a meeting at the school for the parents and students. Wyan asked his mother to go with him. She did. At the meeting they learned that Sabbath was God’s special day. Wyan wanted to go to Sabbath School the next Sabbath. But his mother frowned. “I don’t think I can afford to close my shop to attend church,” she said. But Wyan prayed, and a few weeks later Mother decided to trust God. She closed her shop and went to church with Wyan.

The church was planning a mission trip to Belize, a country in Central America. (Locate **Belize** on your **globe** or **world map**.) Wyan wanted to go on the mission trip, but he was too young to go alone. He asked his mother to go with him to Belize, but she explained that she didn’t have the money to go on the three-week trip. “If we go,” she said, “I would have to close my shop while we are gone. We can’t afford that.”

“God will work it out, Mom,” Wyan said. “We just have to trust Him.” Wyan prayed about the mission trip. Soon some church members learned of Wyan’s wish to go on the mission trip. They raised the money needed for Wyan and his mother to go. Wyan’s mom decided to trust Jesus and close her shop for three whole weeks so she could go with him.

The mission trip was an exciting time for Wyan, and at the end of the meetings 23 children said they wanted to follow Jesus.

When they returned home, God had a surprise for Wyan’s mother. “In the first week that Mom was back at her shop, she earned enough to make up for the three weeks the shop was closed!” Wyan said. “And her business just keeps growing!”

Wyan’s faith helped his mother learn to trust God too. They have both been baptized, and they want to trust Jesus always.

- **Wyan helped his mother learn to trust God. How can we help others to trust God?**

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus can do anything. I can trust Him!**
