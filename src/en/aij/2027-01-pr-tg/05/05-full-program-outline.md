**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** To illustrate the concept of trustworthy leadership, engage the children in the following activity:

Spaced well apart, set up two lines of four chairs with several feet between each chair. Divide your class into two teams and appoint a leader for each. Have the children form a line behind their leader at the first chair, and **blindfold all but the leader**. Children then place one hand on the shoulder of the person in front.

The team leader is to use verbal instructions and encouragement to guide his/her team as they zigzag safely through the line of chairs without touching them. As they go, prompt the leaders to give clear, specific prompts and praise.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How successful were you at this activity?**
- **What did your success depend upon?** (How well the leader guided and instructed; how much you trusted them; how trustworthy they were.)

Explain that in today’s story, we will be learning that Moses was a trustworthy leader for the Israelites, but what made him a good leader was that he relied on God as the most trustworthy leader of all.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God is the most trustworthy leader of all.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Fill a bowl with water. Take one cornflake (or other iron-rich cereal or food—see program preparation for ideas) and sit it on the surface of the water in the middle of the bowl (don’t let the cornflake get too close to the edge of the bowl or it will stay there). Place your magnet close to the cornflake and move it around the bowl, being careful not to get too close to the edge. Watch what happens (the cornflake should follow the magnet).

- **What does the cornflake and the magnet remind you of from today’s Bible story?** (God had the power to lead the Israelites; they followed.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Cornflakes contain a significant amount of iron, which is a magnetic material. When the magnet is close to the cornflake, the cornflakes are attracted to the magnet and follow wherever it goes. We are like the cornflakes and God is like the magnet. When we have God in our hearts, we want to be near Him and follow where He leads. God is the best leader ever—He always knows the way, and He never lets us down.

**^[DISCUSS and PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How does God lead us today? Read Romans 8:14** (The Spirit of God; the Holy Spirit).
- **How can we respond?** (By following; giving our hearts to Jesus and following what He teaches in His Word; by listening and obeying what the Holy Spirit tells us.)

Share a very brief example from your life when God has led you to a decision, a person, place, or experience. Invite the children to have a silent prayer asking God to be their leader, always.

- **What do you think the Israelites learned about God on this day?** (God is powerful, He keeps His promises, and He can do the impossible.)
- **When we “stand still” and wait on God, what are we showing Him?** (That we trust Him to take care of us and we know He is in control, not us.)

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Fleeing the Flood,”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show children your completed **Crossing the Sea Craft**. As the children make this craft ask them to imagine being there and what they might have thought, said, or done. Provide each child with an outline, and the materials needed to complete it.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Explain that younger children will often look up to you (as older children) as a leader they can rely on. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **give your craft to a younger person and tell them the story of God parting the Red Sea**.

**^[RESPOND:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Thank the children for exploring such an exciting Bible story with you today. Share that today we have talked about making God our leader. Ask for two or three children to respond to this question:

Invite the children to clap or cheer after each person’s response.

Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and say it together: **God is the most trustworthy leader of all.**

Invite the children to show God that they choose Him to be their leader by singing the song **“My Help.”**
