**Word of the Week**

**Peace:** Peace is the feeling of quiet, calm, security, and contentment we have in our hearts, even when there is trouble around us. Peace is also a time without fighting, or jostling crowds, or busyness. We have true peace when we trust in God.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After days of preaching, teaching, and healing, Jesus was very weary. He asked His disciples to take Him by boat to the other side of the Sea of Galilee, away from the crowds. Jesus fell asleep, and soon after, a big storm arose. The disciples tried to save themselves.

Finally they remembered that Jesus was with them. They called to Him for help. Jesus calmed the storm, and peace returned. Jesus reminds us that just as He calmed the storm, He can give us peace.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can help us when we face difficulties in our lives.
- **Feel** peace from God, even if we’re in the middle of something bad.
- **Respond** by trusting in Jesus to take care of us.
