^[Word of the Week—MATURE:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#236bb4", "size": "xl"}}})\
To mature is to grow up to become responsible and fully developed. People grow to be physically mature, as well as mature in their thinking, feelings, and how they speak and act. Some people mature more quickly than others. We can also say some children are mature for their age.

Fit For A King

A gentle breeze swept over the grassy hills surrounding Nazareth. Sheep bleated softly under a shady grove of trees. Birds chirped happily in the branches above. Tucked away from any main roads and surrounded by hills, Nazareth was a quiet, peaceful place for Jesus and His family to call home. As Jesus grew, God kept Him close to family and nature.

Much like the place of His birth, Jesus’ home and His village were humble places. Archaeologists have found that just a cluster of homes made up the small town of Nazareth at this time. Each home was basic: a few small rooms around an inner courtyard, with a small storage pit dug beneath. Many years later, when Philip told his friend Nathanael that he had found the Messiah, Jesus of Nazareth, what did Nathanael say about the place? **Read John 1:46.** Yes, it could!

Jesus roamed with Mary and Joseph in the hills near their home. His mind was like a sponge, soaking up everything around Him. The birds, the wildflowers, cloud patterns, and soaring trees all taught Him about God the Father. Mary loved to encourage His bright mind and help Him to learn all sorts of new things. We can be sure Mary and Joseph prayed for Him too.

Under God’s loving care, Jesus grew taller, stronger, wiser, friendlier, and closer to God. The Bible says in Luke 2:52: “And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.”

As a young child, Jesus learned God’s ways from nature and His family. People really liked Him, for He was kind, patient, unselfish, wise, and mature for His age. What a lovely example He is for us.
