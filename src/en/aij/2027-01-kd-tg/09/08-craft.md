Week 9 craft template, colored pencils, scissors, glue, string, beads.

**Create It together:**

1\. Color the craft template and cut out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9b.jpg)

2\. Fold each heart vertically in half.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9c.jpg)

3\. Glue the right side of heart 1 to the left side of heart 2, back-to-back.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9d.jpg)

4\. Do the same for heart 2 and heart 3.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9e.jpg)

5\. Do the same for heart 3 and heart 4.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9f.jpg)

6\. Tie a knot at the bottom of a piece of string. Thread beads onto a third of the string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9g.jpg)

7\. Place the string in between heart 4 and heart 1 and glue them back-to-back.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9h.jpg)

8\. Thread more beads onto the top third of the string and tie a knot.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/9i.jpg)
