**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Abram could have stayed in the comfortable, familiar place that was home—instead, he chose to listen and follow God’s voice. He left the land of his fathers, taking with him his large family and all that he had—never to return. He didn’t even know where he was going—but he went anyway. God called him and promised to lead him. Abram followed.

Is God calling you? Perhaps you’ve heard His call clearly and plainly, or perhaps it’s still a small prompting in your heart. Whatever it might be, we know that truly there’s no better place to be than within God’s will. He doesn’t call us without leading us and equipping us along the way.

It takes courage to step out in faith. It takes a firmness of spirit to leave the comfort of one’s home and all that is familiar. To follow God in every part of our lives is a high calling indeed, and one that we’ll never regret. He will direct every step.

“God never leads His children otherwise than they would choose to be led, if they could see the end from the beginning and discern the glory of the purpose which they are fulfilling as co-workers with Him” (Ellen G. White, _The Ministry of Healing_, p. 479).
