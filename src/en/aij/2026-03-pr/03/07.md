Organize and practice a special closing Sabbath worship for your family. Choose from the ideas below or create some of your own. Involve any family members who are willing to help you.

1\. Read the poem “What Can I Give Him?”

_What can I give Him, poor as I am?_\
_If I were a shepherd, I would bring a lamb._\
_If I were a wise man, I would do my part._\
_Yet what I can, I give Him._\
_Give my heart._

2\. Dress up as an angel, and read or sing Luke 2:14, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, goodwill toward men!”

3\. Dress up as a Wise Man and choose three objects to represent the gifts they gave. You might like to wrap them. Hold each one up and explain to your family what it is, and its special meaning for Jesus. (Refer back to Friday’s lesson.)

4\. Sing several of your favorite praise songs. If any family members play an instrument, invite them to accompany the singing. You might like to give family members something they could use, such as a percussion instrument to keep the beat or rhythm of the song. For example, two spoons to gently tap together, a rubber band stretched over a box to twang, a jar with some rice in it to shake.

5\. Have an adult write the words from Luke 2:25-32 (ICB) on a big sheet of paper so your family can read it as a reader’s theater.

**You:** _A man named Simeon lived in Jerusalem._

**Everyone:** He was a good man and devoted to God.

**You:** _He was waiting for the time when God would help Israel._

**Everyone:** The Holy Spirit was in Him.

**You:** _The Holy Spirit told Simeon that he would not die before he saw the Christ promised by the Lord._

**Everyone:** The Spirit led Simeon to the temple.

**You:** _Mary and Joseph brought the Baby Jesus to the temple . . . Then Simeon took the Baby in his arms and thanked God:_

**Everyone:** “Now, Lord, You can let me, your servant, die in peace as You said. I have seen your Salvation with my own eyes. You prepared him before all people. He is a light for the non-Jewish people to see. He will bring honor to your people, the Israelites.”
