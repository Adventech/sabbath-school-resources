**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a bag of objects and pictures** that are living and not living (seeds, plants, pictures of animals, people, kitchen things, toys, etc.)

Have the children take turns removing something from the bag.

- **Is this alive? How do you know?**

Sort them into piles of “alive” or “not alive.”

Share that plants are alive because they grow and change. People and animals are alive because they move, grow, and breathe—they need air to stay alive.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Our story today is about a girl who was alive, but then she died. She stopped breathing. Her story will teach us our **Big Idea**. Say it, then repeat it with the actions. **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He has power over death** [jump up again with both fists in the air]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a large cardboard **shield**, a few **scrap papers** with the word “death” written on each, the balls made by the children before Sabbath School, and a **marker**. Write on the shield in big letters “FAITH” and read it out to the children. Remind them that faith means believing and trusting in Jesus.

- **Who in our story showed big faith in Jesus? How?** (Jairus. He believed Jesus could help his little girl. He believed even when the servant said she had died.)
- **How did Jesus help the little girl?** (He brought her back to life!)

Show the children the scrap paper and read out the word “death” for them. Explain that Jesus is stronger than death. One day, Jesus will bring everyone who believes in Him back to life. There will be no more death, ever! Invite the children to each take a paper ball that was made before Sabbath School.

Share that because Jesus is stronger than death, we can have big faith in Him, like Jairus did. Even when we are sad, or we lose someone special, we can believe that one day, Jesus will give them life again. When He returns, He is going to raise to life everyone who has died following Him.

Invite the children to crumple their papers and throw their balls at the shield while you duck behind it. Give them several turns. Our faith is like the shield. It protects our minds and can keep us strong, even when bad things happen (like the scrunched-up paper balls).

Invite the children to hold their scrunched-up ball tightly and pray a silent thank-You prayer to Jesus.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 46: Maned Sloth** and share the following facts:

- This cute creature with a round face is found in the rainforests of Brazil.
- Sloths live high up in the treetops and only come to the ground once every eight days or so.
- They sleep for 15-18 hours each day, move VERY slowly, and don’t ever travel very far.
- Sloths can hold their breath for up to 40 minutes!
- Even though it sleeps a lot, moves slowly, and holds its breath a long time, the sloth is alive!

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 46: Maned Sloth** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Little Girl Dolly Peg.** Show children how to make a little girl toy. Invite them to use the peg toy to practice telling the Bible story to another child or family member today so they can know that Jesus has power over death.

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Like Jairus, we can be thankful that Jesus has power over death. One day He will come back from heaven and take away death forever. Isn’t that wonderful news?

Give everyone a **streamer** to throw. Count down from 10, then toss the streamers in the air and say **“Jesus has power over death!”** This week let’s use our craft or just our happy words to share this good news with someone.
