In a small stone house, on a small mat, lay an unhappy man. He couldn’t stand or even shuffle to the bathroom. He had paralysis. This meant that he couldn’t move parts of his body at all. He could only lie still.

It had been such a long time since he had been able to walk. Oh, how he wished he could wander through the fields by the trees and feel the sunshine again!

But even more than that, he wanted to feel peace and happiness again. His mind was filled with bad, sad feelings every single day. Lying on the mat, he felt completely hopeless.

But this man had one good thing in his life. He had good friends. His happy, kind friends didn’t forget him. They would visit and help him. What nice friends they were!

**Read Proverbs 17:17.** How were the sick man’s friends true friends?

One day, these good friends came knocking on the man’s door with exciting news. Jesus was here! He could help—He had even healed a man who was sick with leprosy!

The man with paralysis couldn’t believe it. This was too wonderful to even imagine!

The friends wanted him to be healed. They believed that Jesus was the answer to heal his body and his heart.

Jesus wants us to be saved, happy, and well. He tells us, “I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly [full of good things]” (John 10:10).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you have a friend who doesn’t know Jesus? How can you help them become Jesus’ friend?
