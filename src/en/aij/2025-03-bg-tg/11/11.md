**WEEK 11: THE BIG, BIG RIVER**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: Blue fabric, blue streamers, “ark” box

Say: **God’s people marched toward their new home. They wanted to eat big, juicy grapes! They wanted to grow pretty flower gardens! But suddenly the people stopped marching. Why?**

Place a piece of blue fabric across the floor. Invite some parents/caregivers to wave some long streamers across the fabric to illustrate the deep and fast-flowing river.

Say: **A big, big river was between God’s people and their new home, Canaan. What could they do?** (Point to the river.) **They couldn’t jump over the river, because it was too wide. They couldn’t walk around it, because it was too long. They couldn’t walk through it, because it was too deep. They had no boats.** Open your Bible, point, and say: **Joshua told the people that tomorrow God would do “amazing things”** (Joshua 3:5, ICB).

Invite two children to hold the “ark,” explaining that this special job was for only the priests. Say: **In the morning God’s helpers, the priests, lifted up the very special ark from God’s tabernacle. They carried it to the river, just as God had asked. God was going first! The first two priests put their feet into the water. _Splash!_** The two children can stand on the blue fabric. **The rushing water stopped!** Parents should stop waving the fabric/streamers. **A dry path was in front of them, right through the big river!**

**The priests took the ark to the middle of the river and stopped. They waited while all the people walked to the other side.** The children can all walk through the river. **The people didn’t get wet. The animals didn’t get wet. Their tents didn’t get wet. God’s tabernacle home didn’t get wet. God helped them cross the big river.**

**Nothing is too hard for God. He can do anything! He is the best leader. God is happy when we chose to follow Him.**

Say together: **God is the best leader.**
