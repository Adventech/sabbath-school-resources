Gege was a sick little boy in Mongolia. His mother was worried, so she fed him lots of bread and meat. She hoped it would make him stronger. As Gege ate, he grew stronger. But his tummy also grew. He became very big and weighed a lot.

Gege’s legs began to hurt. His legs hurt when he sat down. His legs hurt when he walked. He had to use crutches at school.

Mother took him to the doctor. “Gege is very sick,” the doctor said. “We will have to remove his legs.”

Gege was scared. Even though his legs hurt, he ran from the doctor’s office. At home, he told Mother that Jesus would heal him. Gege had been going to a Seventh-day Adventist church on his own. Mother was not a Christian. She begged him to go back to see the doctor. But he wouldn’t. He had read many stories in the Bible about Jesus healing people. Gege had faith that Jesus would help Him too! “I know that I will be healed,” he told Mother.

A short time later, Gege and Mother moved to another city. He had to walk a long way from his new house to his new church on Sabbath. But he didn’t mind. At church the pastor told him that walking was good for him. The pastor also invited Gege to take care of the vegetable garden. All summer, Gege walked a long way to the garden every day. He worked hard. Then he ate the yummy vegetables. Every day he prayed for God to heal his legs.

After just three months, Gege was slim, and his leg pains were gone! Mother took Gege back to the doctor and he said Gege was all better. Mother was so surprised. “Jesus healed you,” she said. Mother now believes in Jesus too!
