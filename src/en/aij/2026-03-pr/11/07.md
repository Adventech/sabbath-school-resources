The tallest mountain in North America is Denali (Mount McKinley) in Alaska. It is well named because Denali means “the high one.”

Denali is a towering mountain that is made of granite, a very hard rock. It covers an area of 7,400 square miles and sits on an earthquake fault line. This line is where two large, moving pieces of the earth’s outer layer meet. The moving pieces are called tectonic plates.

As the tectonic plates under Denali push against each other, the mountain gets squeezed and pushed slowly upward. This causes Mount Denali to grow by about .04 inches (one millimeter) each year. That might not seem very fast compared to how quickly you grow taller, but it means that in 100 years the mountain would grow four inches (10 centimeters) taller.

Just as the pushing tectonic plates under Denali help it to grow, Jesus helps you to grow as one of His disciples. And just as Denali keeps growing every year, your faith can keep growing every year too.

Nature Photo Safari

Head outdoors with your family this Sabbath for a closer look at God’s love and power in nature. Ask a grown-up if they’ll let you use their phone or camera (with help) to take some pictures of what you discover. A notepad and sketching pencil can be easily used instead.

^[Get Growing:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Take a picture of a living thing that starts out small. Can you photograph the same thing when it has grown bigger too? Look for a few examples. Trust that Jesus will nurture your faith too.

^[Bible Pictures:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Can you find any living things, or ordinary objects, that are similar to the ones Jesus mentioned in the stories we studied this week?

^[Close-Up:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) As a disciple you can watch the life of Jesus closely to learn His way of love. What close-ups of nature can you capture that show something unexpected and beautiful?

^[Big and Wide:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Take a photo that captures the land and sky and looks peaceful. God’s kingdom is one of peace. Look for an interesting focus, such as a shapely tree.

^[Portrait Shot:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Take photos of your family. Try to make them natural, not posed. (Get them to laugh or play a game!) Keep them to remember that God’s kingdom is about loving others.
