**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source green crayons, leaves from outside, paper, scissors, sticky-tac/tape. Draw a bush with branches on your whiteboard or a large poster—to be used during the story.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.


Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Print or digitally display **^[Fisherman’s Eye Knot picture]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** (AIJ website), **string or rope** for each child. You may like to watch an online tutorial (Search “Fisherman’s Knot”) to learn how to do this knot well yourself, so you can easily teach the children.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source Suggested Storytelling Props: **crown, bricks, staff, stuffed sheep, toy snake**.

Source a **backpack** and **heavy items** to go in it. For example, rocks, bricks, full bottles of water, bags of rice, etc.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 54: Moses in Midian]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Invite a **special guest** to Sabbath School to be interviewed. Choose someone who has been involved in a special mission project or is active in serving God—this may include your church pastor but can also include all members, including youth. Clearly communicate times, topic, and any special needs of your group. Encourage them to share a short simple account of how God asked them to do something for Him, and how He’s helped them to do that. Thank them for making time to connect with the children.

Source a **poster-sized piece of paper** and **markers**.
