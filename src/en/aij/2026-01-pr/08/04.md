At last eight happy people left the ark! With grateful smiles and a spring in their step, they stood upon dry ground. Then they immediately set about doing the most important thing. It wasn’t building a house, or cooking their dinner, or exploring what was over the hill. No, it was much more important than that. What did Noah do first when he got out of the ark? Read Genesis 8:20.

Noah and his family had so much to thank God for, and they wanted to do it in the way He had asked His people to, just as Abel had done. When an altar was built and the animals placed there, the little family gathered round. Kneeling together in a quiet huddle, they began to worship God. Grateful praise rose from their hearts and their lips as they thanked God for protecting them, saving them, and loving them through the roughest seas and the wildest storm.

God accepted the gift of their praise and thanks. He loved His faithful children, and He was happy they loved Him right back with their grateful hearts.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What would you have thanked God for if you had just left the ark?_
- _What do your family members do for you? Why not thank them right now!_

God loves it when we worship Him with thankful hearts. Just as Noah worshipped God first when he left the ark, our prayers can begin with thanks for God’s blessings.
