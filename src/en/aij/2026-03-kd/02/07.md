### Narrator Script

_Toot, toot, toot,_ the trumpets blasted. The announcement boomed across cities and towns far and wide. Ruler Caesar Augustus was ordering everyone to go to their family’s hometown for a people count.

Joseph and Mary packed their bags. Joseph gently lifted Mary onto their donkey’s back. With a _clip-clop_, they were on their way!

They traveled far, far away. Over hills and along dusty roads. _Clip-clop, clip-clop_. What a long trip! Finally, Joseph pointed excitedly. Look! It’s Bethlehem!

The donkey’s slow _clip-clop, clip-clop_ became _clippity-cloppity, clippity-cloppity_, as Joseph eagerly pulled the donkey with his precious load towards the city.

Joseph, Mary, and their donkey walked up and down the narrow streets of Bethlehem, looking for a place to sleep.

Joseph stopped at an inn. Knock, knock, knock. As the door creaked open, Joseph asked for a room to stay for the night. The owner shook his head and closed the door. “There were no rooms left in the inn” (Luke 2:7, ICB). Joseph tried the place next door, but there were no empty beds there, either.

All they could find was an animal stable. They made a bed in the hay and fell fast asleep.

On this night, in this very place, among these animals, something amazing happened. Yes! Baby Jesus was born!

Joseph found a manger for His bed. Mary wrapped the tiny baby King in cloth. Carefully they laid Him in the manger.

Jesus loved us so much that He came to save us. Here in the stable lay the greatest, most precious gift ever given to the world.
