Create

Build a little city of Jericho using blocks, dominos, paper cups, or empty boxes. Pretend to be the Israelites and march around it. Count seven times and then bring the walls down with a great big CRASH!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#57AD45"}}}
Explore

Share how the Israelites had to trust God and obey Him, even though they didn’t know what was going to happen. Blindfold your child and take their hand. Walk them around the room, going around obstacles, and talk about how they can trust you because you are leading them. Let go of their hand and give instructions. Ask your child to obey. Take the blindfold off. Share how we can trust God and know that when we obey Him, He’ll always lead us in the best way.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#57AD45"}}}
Learn

Practice counting to seven. Go outside and count seven things that are alike (leaves, flowers, rocks), or find seven items inside that are alike (toys, cutlery, socks). This was the special number. God had asked His people to walk around the city seven times on the seventh day.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#57AD45"}}}
Make

Decorate a piece of paper with crayons, paint, or glitter. Roll the paper to make a trumpet and tape together. Let your child walk around the room pretending to blow on their trumpet.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#57AD45"}}}
Pray

Act out the story of the Israelites marching around the city of Jericho for worship. After the “walls come down,” kneel together as a family and thank God for always being with us.
