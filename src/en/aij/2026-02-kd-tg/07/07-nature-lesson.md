**Focus:** We can be brave like the baby goslings, for God watches over us.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/07/nature-lesson.png)

This big, strong bird is called a Canada goose. **Can anyone guess where the Canada geese live?** They live in North America. ^[(Help a child locate on a map.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

These geese love the water and warm weather. They travel to warmer places during the winter, flying together in a big V shape.

Baby geese are called goslings. They are very cute and fluffy! When they are only a day old, they try swimming!

Baby goslings are very, very small, so they need to be brave to go into the water.

- **What things might be in the pond that could be dangerous to goslings?** (Large fish, bullfrogs, and turtles.)

A daddy goose is called a gander. He knows the danger in the pond waters, and he needs to keep his babies safe. As the goslings bravely jump into the water, he makes loud honking noises and flaps his wings fast against the water. “Honk, honk!” He makes a big noise!

- **Can you make a loud honking noise and flap your wings too?** (Invite the children to make a honking noise and flap their arms.)

Daddy goose keeps all the other creatures away so that the brave goslings can swim out into the water safely. They feel brave, knowing their strong daddy is with them, looking after them. How wonderful that God created the geese in this way to look after each other!

**Teaching Point:** Being brave is not as hard when we know that God is watching over us. We can feel safe knowing that God is with us.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is with me. I can be brave.**
