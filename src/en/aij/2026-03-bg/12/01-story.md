The Israelites had finally arrived at their new home, the Promised Land. _(Hooray!)_ God was so good. He had been with them, caring for them all the way from Egypt. The people camped near the big city of Jericho. It was another big thing, and it was in their way! Its walls were high _(stretch arms up)._ Its walls were thick _(stretch out arms)._ Its walls were very, very strong _(hold up strong arm)._ But God had helped them cross the big, big river. He would help them go past the big city of Jericho too! He told Joshua just what to do . . .\
The people woke up early. They left their tents, their sheep, and their warm bread behind. Quickly and quietly they marched around the big walls of Jericho. _March, march, march._ Shh! No one said a word. _March, march, march._ Then they came back to camp, patted their sheep, and ate their bread. The next day they did it again! They marched once around the city for one . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five . . . six days! The people didn’t know how this marching would help. But they obeyed God.

On the seventh day God told Joshua to do something new. Joshua told the people what to do. They smiled and nodded. Yes, they would trust God. Yes, they would obey God. March, march, march. The Israelites marched around the big city. But not just once. They marched one . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five . . . six . . . seven times! But this time they weren’t quiet. The priests blew their horns as the people marched. _March, march, march. Toot, toot, toot! March, march, march. Toot, toot, toot!_ It was so loud!\
After seven marches around the city, the priests gave one long blow on their trumpets. _T-o-o-t!_ Then Joshua told all the people, “Now shout!” (Joshua 6:16, ICB). The people shouted! The trumpets blew! Then a different sound came. _Rumble, rumble, CRASH!_ God brought the walls of Jericho tumbling down!

Nothing is too big or too hard for God! We can trust Him to be our leader. When God tells us in the Bible to be loving and kind to others, we can obey—even when it’s hard. When God tells us in the Bible to help others, we can obey—even when we’d rather be playing. When God tells us in the Bible to tell others about His love, we can obey— even if we feel shy. God wants the very best for us. He will help us. He crashed down the big walls of Jericho. He can do anything!\
Thank You, God. You are mighty! _(Say together)_ I want to trust and obey You!
