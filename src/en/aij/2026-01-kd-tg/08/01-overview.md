**Lesson Summary**

True to His word, God sent a worldwide flood to cleanse the earth from sin. His offer to save was accepted only by Noah and his family, who entered the safety of the ark. God also guided animals—in pairs and seven pairs—into the ark. God is our loving God who saves. God was with Noah and his family throughout the storm. He cares, guides, and provides. He set a rainbow in the sky to remind them of His loving promises.
