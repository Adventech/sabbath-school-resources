Mary jumped in surprise. Standing before her was a beautiful, shining angel! “Greetings!” said the angel. “The Lord has blessed you and is with you” (Luke 1:28, ICB).

Mary’s heart raced. Why was this special messenger of God talking to her? She was just an ordinary young woman from Nazareth. _What did it all mean?_

The angel said to her, “Don’t be afraid, Mary, because God is pleased with you.

“Listen! You will become pregnant. You will give birth to a son, and you will name him Jesus. . . . The baby will be holy. He will be called the Son of God” (Luke 1:30-35, ICB).

Mary could barely believe it. God was choosing her to be the mother of the promised Savior of the world! Then the angel smiled and told her something else exciting: “Elizabeth, your relative, is very old. But she is also pregnant with a son” (Luke 1:36, ICB).

What did the angel say next, to help Mary believe? **Read Luke 1:37.**

Mary was amazed at everything the angel said. Most of all she was amazed that she would be Jesus’ mother! God was doing what He had always promised. His plan to save sinners was finally happening.

“I am the servant girl of the Lord,” she humbly answered. “Let this happen to me as you say!” (Luke 1:38, ICB).
