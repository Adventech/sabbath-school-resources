Adam and Eve looked at the ground. They hadn’t told God the truth. They hadn’t said they were sorry!

God sadly explained the consequences of their sin. Sin caused pain, sadness, and death. They would one day die. So would the plant and animals.

Then God led Adam and Eve to the entrance of the garden and told them they must leave. God placed angels with flashing, fiery swords at the entrance. God had to make sure people could no longer eat from the tree of life, otherwise they would live forever in sadnes and pain.

Oh, how different Adam and Eve’s lives were now! Weeds and prickles began to grow among the grass and vegetables. The flowers drooped, the leaves fell. The fruit never again tasted as sweet and juicy. Adam and Eve would get sick, feel pain, and grow old. That is a lot of sad consequences. If only they had chosen to follow God’s ways.

But their loving God would still be very close. His great love would do everything to save them. The Bible tells us “For the wages (cost) of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 6:23).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- What do you think Adam and Eve missed the most after leaving the Garden of Eden?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that although “the wages of sin is death . . . the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 6:23).

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go for a nature walk and collect things that remind you of sin in the world (e.g., dead leaves, prickles). Place these things on the ground in the shape of a heart. Thank God that His love never changes.
