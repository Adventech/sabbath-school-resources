**Focus:** God miraculously provides manna for His people during a famine in Angola.

**Location:** Help a child to locate **Angola** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Namba Adventist Mission Station was set up more than 80 years ago in Angola. Hundreds of the local people lived here and learned about Jesus. Then one year the rains failed to come, and the crops shriveled and died. The closest shops were far, far away, and the people had very little money to buy food anyway.

Everyone became hungry and worried. The mission director’s wife called them all together. She told them the story of the manna in the wilderness and shared some of God’s promises from the Bible. After prayer her little 5-year-old daughter went out of the house. She soon returned eating what looked like little pieces of white bread. “What are you eating?” asked the mother.

The little girl told everyone that some white men outside had told her that God had answered her prayers and sent manna, and that she should pick it up and eat it. Everyone rushed outside and found many acres of land covered in the white substance. But they couldn’t see the white men anywhere!

The people picked up large quantities of the miracle food. It was sweet, and just as the Bible describes manna. But this special food didn’t go smelly and rotten when stored. Instead, it stayed fresh and sweet for as long as the people needed it.

The mission director, Carlos Sequeseque, carefully put some in a small box and sent it to another missionary. He included a letter that said: “It was on the 19th of March, 1939, even on this same day, that our God performed a miracle at Namba, for God caused to rain from heaven the bread of heaven which is called manna. The people of Namba ate and filled plates of it. There ate of it many men and women and young children. All ate of it, and therefore I send you a small amount of it to show you the manna, and to show you the certainty of the manna which fell on our village of Namba.”

The manna was still fresh seven years later! Yes, our gracious God is still able to provide miracle food for His children today, just as He did in Bible times.

- **What does this story show about our wonderful God?** (He provided for the Israelites in the desert, and He still provides for His people today.)
