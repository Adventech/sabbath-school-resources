It happened in a desert, on a dusty, ordinary track that Moses walked on, in the middle of nowhere. There, in a scrawny desert bush, God showed up in a dazzling, mighty, out-of-the-ordinary way. God turned the cracked dirt into holy ground, and He spoke to Moses, calling him by name. God showed up in the midst of Moses’ daily, common routine, and He captured Moses’ attention.

Exodus 3:3 tells us, “Moses said, I will now turn aside and see this great sight . . .” Moses had to be willing to “turn aside”—to interrupt his daily routine and pay attention to God. If he had looked the other way, he would have missed the Exodus, the people of Israel, and his calling.

What about you? How might you pause to notice God in your busy day-to-day tasks today?

Imagine hearing God’s voice in your living room, your car, or your workplace. Imagine your daily routine being interrupted by an attention-grabbing, beautiful, and holy encounter with God. He loves to meet us where we are. You may think you are in a dusty, ordinary season, but your home can be holy ground because the Almighty One is there with you. He says, “As I was with Moses, so I will be with you. I will not leave you nor forsake you” (Joshua 1:5). God asks you to lead your family to Him. Like Moses, will you “turn aside” to spend time with God today? Open His Word; talk with Him, for the God of the universe wants to be close to you too.
