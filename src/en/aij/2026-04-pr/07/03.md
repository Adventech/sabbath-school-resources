As Jesus and the big crowd walked slowly toward Jairus’ house, a messenger ran toward them from the other direction. What news did he have for Jairus? **Read Luke 8:49.**

Imagine how Jairus must have felt to hear this about his beautiful girl . . .

The messenger’s words showed that those back home believed it was too late for Jesus to do anything. They believed the great Teacher could have helped when the girl was sick. But now that she had died, the problem was too big for Jesus. Jairus was afraid that was true too (Luke 8:50). But Jesus knew differently.

As soon as Jesus overheard the messenger’s words, He said, “Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be made well” (Luke 8:50). Jairus had believed Jesus could heal his daughter. Now Jesus was encouraging him to have even greater faith—faith that Jesus could do the impossible and somehow make his dead daughter come alive.

“Jesus let only Peter, James, and John the brother of James go with him to Jairus’s house” (Mark 5:37, ICB). Encouraged by the words and actions of Jesus, Jairus hurried alongside Him.

Like Jairus, we can also trust Jesus, even when things look hopeless. Sometimes when we face a big problem and pray about it, things get worse before they get better. Jesus encourages us to keep trusting Him, even though the situation looks impossible.
