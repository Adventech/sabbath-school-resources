```=Option 1: Scroll

**What you need:**

A strip of white paper, a black tea bag and water, two large craft sticks, marker, tape, string.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/51a.png)

**Instructions:**

1. Write out the memory verse on a strip of white paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/51b.png)

2. Use a damp tea bag to dab around the piece of paper then leave to dry.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/51c.png)

3. Tape a craft stick on either side of the strip of paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/51d.png)

4. Roll the paper around the sticks toward the middle and tie a string around the “scroll.”

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/51e.png)

```

```=Option 2: Daddy’s Helper

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/52a.png)

**Instructions:**

1. Color in the craft template and cut out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/52b.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/05/52d.png)
```
