**Big Idea:** God is loving. He is always with me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a **^[carboard box “ark”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** with a flap door that you have precut, ready for **toy animals** to go inside.
- Children make **animal sounds** as they “walk” toy animals onto the “ark.”
- Use **puppets, toy birds, or birds on craft sticks** (one black, one white) for the raven and dove.
- Have a **branch** for the white bird to bring back to Noah.
- Children hold the edges of **blue fabric** and create a “stormy sea” with the “ark bottle” in the center.
- Place **green fabric** down as land appears.
- Children use **flat rocks or blocks** to make an altar with you.
- Children wave their **rainbow sticks**.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 8 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if available, and expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- God told Noah, “Come into the ark.” People made fun of Noah and his family.
- The animals marched into the ark—in pairs of twos and seven pairs of the same kind (males and females). ^[(Toy animals, into box ark.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God shut the door of the ark ^[(use your arms to close the door).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- After seven days the rains poured down and water came up through the earth. Noah and his family were safe in the ark—God was with them. ^[(Clap hands for rain, blue fabric for stormy sea, “ark bottle” in the center.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- After 40 days the rain stopped. Water covered the whole earth! Noah, his family, and the animals were safe ^[(blue fabric is still).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God sent a wind to blow the water away. The ark landed on a mountain.
- Was it time to leave? Noah sent a raven, and later a dove, into the sky. Would they find a new home? No, they both came back to the ark ^[(toy birds, ark).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- After a week Noah sent out the dove again. It came back with a leaf branch. The waters were going down! ^[(White bird, ark, small branch.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God opened the big ark door. Noah and his family went outside and made a thank-You altar to God. ^[(Green fabric, “walk” all the animals out of the ark, stones/blocks to make an altar.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God made a rainbow—a promise and reminder that He would never flood the whole earth again. ^[(Children wave rainbow sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God said to His friend Noah, “You and your family go into the boat” (Genesis 7:1, ICB). It was time! The ark was well-made and ready to float. God was ready to wash the world clean from evil. Sadly, the people didn’t believe God would send a flood. They made fun of Noah.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly something stopped the noisy crowd. What was happening? A big parade of animals was coming! ^[(Give children animals to bring into the toy “ark.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Some animals came in two of the same kind; and some, in seven pairs of the same kind, both males and females. They waddled, walked, crept, slithered, glided, and hopped their way toward the ark. Invisible angels led them in perfect order! What noises did they make? ^[(Children to make animal noises.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people watched all the animals enter the ark, but they refused to come in. God gave them a choice, but He always lets people choose.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After seven days God sent the rain, just as He had promised Noah. Lightning flashed and thunder crashed! Rain poured down from the sky. ^[(Children clap hands to make the sound of heavy rain.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Water gushed up through big cracks in the earth. The floodwaters rose higher and higher.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The ark tossed in the rough water and wild winds. ^[(Invite children to hold the edges of blue fabric. Place the “ark bottle” in the center. Gently toss the fabric up and down together to create a stormy sea.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But Noah and his family were safe inside. God was with them.

For 40 long days it rained and rained. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then the wind died down, and the rain stopped. All was quiet and still. ^[(Stop moving the blue fabric and place it down on the floor.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Outside the ark, water covered everything! Noah and his family were safe inside the floating zoo.

After many days God sent a wind to dry up the water. Finally, the floating boat went bump! They were on dry land at last—on the top of a high mountain. But still the door stayed shut.

Was it time to leave the ark? One day, after waiting and waiting, Noah opened the window. He gently lifted a raven, and later a dove, into the sky. The birds flew off with a flutter and a flap. ^[(Open a window in your cardboard ark. Bring out a black toy bird and then a white toy bird, flying them high above the children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Would they find a new home? No, they both came back to the ark ^[(fly the birds back inside the ark)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). There was still too much water around.

After a week Noah sent out the dove again. This time it came back with an olive leaf in its mouth. ^[(Fly the white bird from the ark window and back again with a small branch.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The waters were going down. Their time on the boat was nearly over!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Creak . . . creak . . . God was opening the big ark door! Finally, after a year on the ark, the washed-clean world was ready for them to live in. Noah and his family smiled excitedly as they stepped off the boat and wiggled their toes in the fresh new grass. ^[(Lay out green fabric and have the children walk all the animals out of the ark while making their sounds.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Noah was so happy and thankful to God! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He collected rocks and stacked them to build a thank-You altar. ^[(Children help you stack stones/blocks to make an altar.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He and his family said a big thank You to God for watching over them and saving them.

Suddenly a bright, colorful gift from God appeared in the sky. It was the first rainbow ever! ^[(Children wave rainbow sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God told them the rainbow was a reminder that He saves, protects, and loves His children. Rainbows would be a colorful promise that He would never flood the whole earth again. Whenever we see a rainbow, we can remember God’s goodness and His promise to save us.
