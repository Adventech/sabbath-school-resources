---
title: Conclusion
---

Under the guidance of the Holy Spirit, the missionary program of your church can be very successful if driven by a vision for the total involvement of the membership through the coordinated efforts of all departments and entities working together in unity with the passion Jesus has for every lost person. It would be difficult, if not impossible, to fully utilize all the ideas in this leaflet, but we trust you will find sufficient here to greatly enhance you efforts. May your church be richly blessed and filled with the joy of our Lord as you work with Him in preparing an abundant harvest for His return.
