**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He helps me to be honest.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Therefore, putting away lying, ‘Let each one of you speak truth’ ”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Ephesians 4:25)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Honest”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Being honest means telling the truth. God is always honest, He never lies. When God lives in your heart, you can speak honest words and not lie or cheat.

Time for the Blessing

Isaac sat down with a sigh. He was very old now. In fact Isaac was so old his eyes “could not see clearly” (Genesis 27:1, ICB) and his feet moved slowly. He knew he wouldn’t be alive much longer. But before he died, Isaac had something important to do. He must give one of his sons a special blessing and make them the new family leader. This son would lead everyone to love and follow God. Whom should it be?

Old Isaac smiled. Of course it should be Esau. He was the firstborn after all and birthright blessings were always given to the firstborn.

He knew Mother Rebekah did not agree. An angel had told her long ago: “The older will serve the younger” (Genesis 25:23, ICB). She knew the blessing was for younger brother Jacob, not Esau.

But Father Isaac wouldn’t listen. He had made up his mind. He would choose Esau—while no one was looking!

Quickly Father called Esau to his side. Then he whispered, “take your bow and arrows, and go hunting in the field. Kill an animal for me to eat. Prepare the tasty food that I love. Bring it to me, and I will eat. Then I will bless you before I die” (Genesis 27:3, 4, ICB).

Isaac thought no one was looking, but someone nearby was listening! Who was it? **Read Genesis 27:5.**

So much sneakiness. If only they had been honest with each other and asked God what to do.

- What do you think God thought of Isaac and Rebekah’s dishonest plans?
