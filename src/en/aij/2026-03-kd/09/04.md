Jesus looked at the worried servants and their empty pitchers. Yes, He would help them! By the door stood six empty water jars made of stone. Jesus turned to the servants and said, “Fill the waterpots with water” (John 2:7).

The servants raced off to do exactly what Jesus had told them.

_Glug-glug-glug. Glug-glug-glug._ The water splished and sploshed into the big heavy jars. One . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five . . . six jars were filled to the top.

The people at the party were getting thirsty. They had started to ask for their empty cups to be refilled. The servants looked at Jesus, waiting for His next instruction. What did Jesus say to them? **Read John 2:8.**

The servants were confused. But they chose to trust Jesus and obey. Carefully they poured the water into a cup and gave it to the master.

_Sip, sip._ His eyes opened wide. Then his lips turned up into a smile. This cup was full of the sweetest, freshest, most delicious grape juice he had ever tasted!

The master excitedly called the groom over. “People always serve the best wine first,” he said to the groom. “But you have saved the best wine till now” (John 2:10, ICB).

The servants were amazed. They had just seen a miracle with their very own eyes. Jesus had turned water into the most pure grape juice! He had provided just what they needed.
