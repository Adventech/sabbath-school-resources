You’re also in my oraçãos as you ler these wonderful stories to your criança. I esperança you’ll prioritize this special tempo each dia by giving just 10 minutes to sit with your criança as you compartilhar the História bíblica. Although our lives are busy, you won’t regret having this tempo together. Fazer it a daily habit and you’ll nãotice that Deus will shape your criança’s character and their amor for Him. It’s what naturtodosy happens when we spfim tempo with Him in His living Word.

On that nãote, have your Bíblia close by each dia as you ler these stories. You’ll ver a daily question and prompt where you’ll olhar in your Bíblia for the responder. Maisrar your criança how to use the Bíblia (the book, chapter, verse), and remind them that these stories are verdadeiro because they vir from the Bíblia.

So in the rush of vida, when the urgent pulls you away from the importante, may your daily oração be “Strengthen me according to Your word” (Psalm 119:28), as you perguntar Deus to ajudar you prioritize the growing fé of your criança.

I saber He’ll dar you strength,
