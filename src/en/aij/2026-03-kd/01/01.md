The Right Time!

King David thought about the words of the song. A smile spread across his face. “I wait for the Lord to help me. I trust His word” (Psalm 130:5, ICB).

_Yes_, the king decided, _that’s what I’ll do. I’ll wait._ He knew that God always keeps His promises.

Waiting for something really good isn’t easy. Waiting for birthdays, a playdate with your friend, or even a yummy treat can be hard! But this waiting was even harder. This waiting was for . . . Jesus!

God had made a promise to send His only Son on the biggest rescue mission ever. Jesus would come and save us from sin.

King David had learned all about God’s promise from his father, Jesse. And Jesse had learned about it from his father, Obed. And Obed had learned all about it from his parents, Ruth and Boaz. Now King David would tell his children and the people in his kingdom. Together they would wait.

God’s people waited . . . and waited . . . and waited. God sent other kings and leaders to remind the people to keep being patient. Their Rescuer was coming—when the time was right.

How might the words from the song in **Psalm 130:7, 8** have encouraged them? Many, many, many years went by. God’s people waited for a long, long time. Finally the waiting was over! The right time had now come.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- When have you waited for a very long time for something good? What did you wait for?
- What helped you to be patient while you waited?
