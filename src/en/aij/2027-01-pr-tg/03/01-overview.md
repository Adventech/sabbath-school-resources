**Lesson Summary**

Moses and Aaron returned to Egypt. God had sent them to lead His people out of slavery, but Pharaoh refused to let them go. In response, God sent a series of plagues to show His power and prove that the Egyptian gods were false. The Nile turned to blood, frogs invaded the land, lice and flies swarmed, and diseases struck people and animals. Boils, hail, locusts, and darkness followed, yet Pharaoh’s heart remained hard. Each plague revealed God’s power and care for His people, while Egypt’s gods were shown to be powerless and fake.
