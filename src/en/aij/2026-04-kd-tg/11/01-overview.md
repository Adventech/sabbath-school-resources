**Word of the Week**

**Trust:** To trust is to rely on something or someone. When you trust someone, you believe their words to be true. You know you can count on them. We can trust Jesus. He is always loving. His words are true. And He always keeps His promises.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

A Roman centurion’s servant was sick and at the point of death. He was a good man who loved God. Hearing of Jesus’ miracles, the soldier sent his Jewish friends to ask Jesus for His help. Jesus immediately started walking to the centurion’s home. A messenger ran to tell the centurion that Jesus was on His way.

The centurion ran to meet Jesus. He told Jesus that He didn’t need to come to his house—that He needed only to say the word and his servant would be healed. Jesus marveled at the centurion’s faith and healed his servant from afar.
