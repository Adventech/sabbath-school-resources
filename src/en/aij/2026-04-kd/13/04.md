Jesus looked with love at His trusting friend Martha. Even though she was very sad, she believed in Him. With comforting, caring words, He said, “Your brother will rise again” (John 11:23).

Jesus wanted to teach Martha that He, the Son of God, could give Lazarus life. “I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in me will have life even if he dies” (John 11:25, ICB).

Looking into Martha’s eyes, Jesus said, “Do you believe this?” (John 11:26).

“Yes, Lord. I believe that You are the Christ, the Son of God, who is to come into the world,” Martha replied. (John 11: 27). “I know that he will rise again in the resurrection at the last day” (John 11:24).

Martha knew that the all-powerful Jesus was her Savior. He would return in the clouds one day and wake the dead. But Martha didn’t realize Jesus also had amazing plans for that very day!

Martha headed back to her home and whispered in Mary’s ear, “The Teacher has come and is calling for you” (John 11:28).

Quickly, Mary got up and darted out the front door. The other sad and crying people followed her.

Now, it was Mary’s turn to kneel down at Jesus’ feet. “Lord, if you had been here, my brother would not have died” (John 11:32), she said sadly.

What did Jesus do? **Read John 11:35.**

Jesus’ eyes filled with tears. He was very sad in His heart. He was sad for His friends Mary, Martha, and Lazarus. He was sad for every person who had felt sad when someone they loved died. He was very sad for all the pain sin had caused the people on earth. Jesus cried.

Jesus is such a caring Friend and Savior!
