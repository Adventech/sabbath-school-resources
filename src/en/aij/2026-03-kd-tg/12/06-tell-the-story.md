**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. His love changes me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Have a **money box** and a **small money bag** for props. Place some **coins** into the money bag (at least three for each child).
- Make a **large tree** to represent the sycamore tree and attach it to the wall. Use **brown paper** or **cardboard** for the trunk and **green tissue paper** for leaves. Source a **toy** or print out a “Zacchaeus” for the children to place in the tree.
- Place a **small stool** or **stepladder** next to the tree.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 12 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- _Clink, Clink_ ^[(jingle coins in box)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Zacchaeus was a tax collector. Everyone lined up at his table to give him money.
- He was a thief and a cheat. He put one coin into the money box. He kept the other coin for himself. ^[(Give children money to hand to you. Place one coin into the money box and one in your pocket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The people were sad and angry as they left Zacchaeus’ tax booth. He had taken their money!
- One day Zacchaeus heard that Jesus was coming to town. He’d heard all about kind Jesus. He must meet Him! He ran to find Jesus.
- The people crowded around Jesus. Zacchaeus had a problem. He was short; too short to see over everyone’s heads.
- He had a brilliant idea. A tree! Yes, he could climb a tree to see.
- He ran ahead of the crowd and found a sycamore tree beside the road and climbed up into it. Now he could see! ^[(Children put toy/paper Zacchaeus in tree.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The crowd came closer and closer. Jesus stopped and looked up. Jesus said, “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down! I must stay at your house today” (Luke 19:5, ICB).
- Zacchaeus couldn’t believe his ears! Jesus knew his name! Jesus wanted to be with him.
- Jesus’ love began to change him. He was becoming a “new creation.”
- Zacchaeus said, “I will give half of my money to the poor. If I have cheated anyone, I will pay that person back four times more!” (Luke 19:8, ICB). Right away, Zacchaeus began to make things right.
- The crowd frowned and grumbled. They didn’t think Jesus should be friends with him.
- Jesus told them Zacchaeus was part of God’s family. Changing people’s hearts was what Jesus had come to do.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) _Clink, clink._ Zacchaeus slid two coins into the money box with a smile ^[(coins)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

“Zacchaeus was a chief tax collector, and he was rich, (Luke 19:2). He had the job of collecting tax money from everyone in Jericho and giving it to the Roman rulers.

Shopkeepers, farmers, and fishermen all lined up in front of Zacchaeus’ table. Quietly they waited their turn to pay. But Zacchaeus lied and cheated.

If someone needed to pay one silver coin, Zacchaeus made him pay two. Zacchaeus would drop one coin into the money box. _Clink!_ Then, shhh . . . when no one was watching, the other coin would disappear into his pocket—all for himself. ^[(Give children two coins to hand to you. Place one coin into the money box and one into your pocket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The people knew Zacchaeus was a thief. But they couldn’t stop him. They walked away from his table sad and angry. They had given their money to Zacchaeus. How would they have enough money to buy food now?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day, as Zacchaeus sat at his table counting coins, he heard a shout. Then another. Jesus! Jesus was coming!

Zacchaeus had heard so many stories about kind Jesus. He’d also heard of His wise words and mighty miracles.

Zacchaeus jumped to his feet. His heart filled with joy. Jesus was right here in Jericho! He must meet Jesus!

Quickly he left his table—and his money box—and ran down the road.

The big crowd up ahead pushed, shoved, and bumped each other. They squeezed in close around Jesus as He walked down the road. Everyone wanted to be near Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Zacchaeus ran up to the crowd. But he had a problem. Zacchaeus was short—too short to see over the big, tall crowd! Standing on tiptoe, Zacchaeus tried to peer over the tall shoulders ^[(invite children to stand on their toes)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). But it was no use. He still couldn’t see Jesus.

Then a brilliant idea came into his mind. A tree! Yes, he could climb a tree. Then he would be high enough to see over the crowd.

Zacchaeus jumped to his feet. His heart filled with joy. Jesus was right here in Jericho! He must meet Jesus!

Zacchaeus ran ahead of the crowd ^[(invite children to follow you to a tree)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He smiled as he saw a large, shady sycamore tree on the side of the road. Its branches stretched out over the path. Yes! That would be perfect. Quickly he climbed up into the branches ^[(child puts a toy/paper Zacchaeus into tree)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

He could see! His legs dangled down, and his eyes peered down the road.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus and the crowd came closer and closer. Soon Jesus was right below him!

Suddenly Jesus stopped. He “looked up and saw Zacchaeus in the tree.” His eyes shone with love and care. “Zacchaeus, hurry and come down! I must stay at your house today,” Jesus said (Luke 19:5, ICB).

Zacchaeus couldn’t believe it. Jesus knew his name! Jesus loved and cared for him and wanted to visit his house!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Quickly he scrambled down the tree ^[(child brings Zacchaeus down)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Jesus’ love and forgiveness started to change something inside of Zaccheaus—right then and there! He was becoming a “new creation.”

With a big smile on his face, he said to Jesus, “I will give half of my money to the poor. If I have cheated anyone, I will pay that person back four times more!” (Luke 19:8, ICB). Immediately, Zacchaeus began to make things right.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) This new Zacchaeus wanted to make things right. He felt sorry that he had hurt people. He wanted to give them more money than what he had stolen from them.

But the crowd at the bottom of the tree grumbled and frowned. They didn’t think Jesus should be friends with someone who lied and cheated. They didn’t realize that Jesus loved Zacchaeus and every other sinner in the crowd. The love of Jesus had already started to change Zacchaeus.

Jesus told everyone that Zacchaeus was part of God’s family. This kind of miracle—the miracle of changing people’s hearts—was what Jesus had come to do. He said, “The Son of Man came to find lost people and save them” (Luke 19:10, ICB).

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. His love changes me.**
