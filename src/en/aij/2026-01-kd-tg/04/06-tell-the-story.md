**Big Idea:** God is loving. He lets me choose.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Children can use the paper angels they made in the early arrival activity. Separate extra-shiny angel as Lucifer.
- Children hold on to one long piece of **string** as an illustration of how love connected everyone in heaven.
- Sing a song with their ”angels” when talking about the music of the angels.
- Paper hearts
- Use a **flashlight** to “shine” on the children’s angels, and then on Lucifer.
- Gradually blow up a **balloon** throughout the story to illustrate how much Lucifer’s discontentment and lies grew.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (week 4 Program PowerPoint).
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

- Each angel had a special job: messengers, singers, covering cherubim ^[(paper angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- The angels were content and happy—joined together with love ^[(paper hearts, ball of string-connected)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Lucifer was a beautiful, shining, leading angel. He stood next to God and was the leader of heaven’s choir ^[(sparkling Lucifer angel, shining light, paper angels sing together)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Pride began to grow in Lucifer’s heart ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Lucifer became jealous of God’s Son ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was not content anymore.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Long before God created our world, He created some very special helpers: angels! ^[(Have children hold their angels up.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God made thousands and thousands of angels. They were wonderful, with powerful wings and a bright light that made them shine wherever they went ^[(shine your light on the children’s angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Each angel had a special job. Some angels took messages from one side of heaven to the other ^[(invite some children to “fly” their angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Other angels sang joyful worship songs in heaven’s choir ^[(invite some children to use their angels to sing)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Some angels, called cherubim, stood next to God, and covered Him with their wings as He sat on His beautiful throne.

Love was everywhere in heaven ^[(throw out paper hearts)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God is love, and because of God’s great love for all the angels, they chose to love Him too. Everyone was content and happy. ^[(Roll out a ball of string and invite the children to stand up and hold on to it. Show how the string connects you all together. It is like the love that connected everyone with God and each other in heaven.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lucifer was a beautiful, shining angel who sparkled with light ^[(hold up your sparkling Lucifer angel and shine your light on it)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was the leader of all the other angels (see Ezekiel 28:14). He stood next to God and covered Him with his wings.

Lucifer was also the leader of heaven’s choir. Every day his beautiful voice led the worship songs, praising God and His Son too. ^[(Have children group all their paper angels together and sing a short song.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

All of heaven was full of peace, joy, and kindness. God loved the angels very much, and they loved Him too. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But one day something began to change in Lucifer’s heart. A little selfish thought began to grow, like a tiny seed that grows into a weed (blow a small amount of air into a balloon). Lucifer started to think all about himself: _Me, me, me_ ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Maybe he saw his reflection and admired his handsome face and sparkling robe ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Maybe he admired his own singing. As Lucifer thought more and more about himself, pride began to grow in his heart ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He forgot that it was God who had given him his special jobs, his beauty, and his singing voice ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

When the angels worshipped God’s Son, Lucifer became jealous. He thought to himself, _Why should God’s Son be more important than me?_ ^[(Blow into balloon.)({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was not content anymore. From his place beside God’s throne, Lucifer’s jealousy grew stronger and stronger.

If only Lucifer had talked to God about his bad thoughts and feelings. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But instead Lucifer began to talk to the other angels. He even began to tell lies about God and His law, which shows what it means to love God and others ^[(blow into balloon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He caused trouble in heaven. Oh, no! What bad, bad choices Lucifer was making.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had always shown Lucifer lots of love. Lucifer had changed so much, but God stayed the same: always loving, always true. God tried to help Lucifer change, but Lucifer refused to listen. No one was going to tell him what to do anymore!

Many angels believed Lucifer’s terrible lies—they thought God was unfair and unkind. These angels were willing to fight against God.

This was a big problem! Lucifer had become the opposite of love, and only love could be in heaven. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had to send Lucifer and the angels who believed Lucifer’s lies away from heaven. ^[(Pop the blown-up balloon and invite some of the children to place their angels aside.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

When Lucifer left heaven, his name became Satan, “the enemy.” What a sad day it was!
