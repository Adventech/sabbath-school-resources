Down by the Nile River, Mother Jochebed stood next to Miriam. Together they watched the little basket boat with their baby inside. The little basket rocked gently in the water among the reeds.

Mother couldn’t stay with her baby, but she trusted God to look after him.

The Bible tells us, “God is our protection and our strength. He always helps in times of trouble” (Psalm 46:1, ICB). God would not leave the little baby. He sent invisible angels to hover above the tiny basket as it floated on the rippling water.

Who else watched over the little baby in the basket? **Read Exodus 2:4.**

That little baby boy had many eyes carefully watching over him as he bobbed up and down in the little boat. God watched over him, God’s angels hovered above, and Miriam also stood close by, watching.

As Miriam waited down by the river, she pretended to do ordinary things that she would do any other ordinary day. Secretly she kept a very close eye on her little baby brother, to make sure he stayed safe.

God is watching over you too. He sends His angels to help you, and gives us loving family and friends to care for us. The Bible says, “Lord, you bless those who do what is right. You protect them like a soldier’s shield” (Psalm 5:12, ICB). Thank You, Jesus.
