**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He hears me when I call out to Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 10 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- When you point to the children, have them say loudly, “Please help me!”
- Rattle **coins** in a **cup**.
- Let children smell some **spice containers** (cinnamon, cloves, ginger, etc.)
- Use **peg people** (Jesus; Bartimaeus) or a **felt set** if desired.
- Speak with expression and engage the children in some actions.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Bartimaeus sat by the side of the busy road and tilted his head to hear. He listened to the traders chatting together as they walked into the big city of Jericho. _Chit-chat, chit-chat._ He heard their donkeys going _clip-clop, clip-clop_ beside them. He could smell their sweet dates and spices. ^[(Invite children to smell a range of spices.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But Bartimaeus couldn’t see anything. Bartimaeus was blind.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Bartimaeus would sit by the side of the busy road all day, asking people for money.

_“Clink, clink, clink.”_ Bartimaeus rattled the few small coins in his little cup. ^[(Rattle coins in cup.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Would someone listen and give him some money today? He waited. He listened.

He could hear something. Footsteps. Lots of footsteps! Chatter. Lots of chatter!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Bartimaeus listened closely. His ears could hear a really big crowd coming toward him. His friends said excitedly,

“Jesus, the one from Nazareth, is coming” (Luke 18:37, ICB).

Jesus was walking on the road toward him! Bartimaeus had heard of this Man. Jesus had healed people—people who couldn’t walk, people who couldn’t hear, and people who couldn’t see!

The noisy crowd came closer and closer. Yes, Jesus was coming!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a very loud voice Bartimaeus began shouting above the noisy crowd. “Jesus, Son of David! ^[(Point to children to say)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) **Please help me!”** (Luke 18:38, ICB).

Bartimaeus kept on shouting. Over and over he called for Jesus to help him. ^[(Children to say)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) **“Please help me!”**

The people who were leading the group thought his noisy yelling was very annoying. They were angry and told him to be quiet.

But nothing would stop Bartimaeus from getting Jesus’ attention. He shouted louder and louder, “Son of David! ^[(Children say)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) **Please help me!”** (Luke 18:39, ICB).

Bartimaeus knew he needed Jesus.

Jesus listened. He stopped and looked at Bartimaeus. Jesus kindly asked for Bartimaeus to be brought to Him.

Some of the men from the crowd walked over to Bartimaeus. “Cheer up! Get to your feet. Jesus is calling you,” they said (Mark 10:49, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Bartimaeus jumped up with a big smile on his face. Jesus had heard him calling out! Jesus had listened!

Bartimaeus couldn’t wait to come close to Jesus. He jumped up and left his coat behind.

^[(Invite the children to close their eyes like blind Bartimaeus.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus looked with deep care at Bartimaeus. Then He asked, “What do you want Me to do for you?” (Luke 18:41).

Bartimaeus answered, “I want to see again” (Luke 18:41, ICB).

Jesus said, “Receive your sight; your faith has made you well” (Luke 18:42). ^[(Invite children to open their eyes.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Instantly Bartimaeus could see! His eyes rested on the lovely face of Jesus. Jesus, who had listened to his shouts for help. Jesus, who had healed his eyes and saved him from his life of begging. Once again Bartimaeus began to shout—this time, with joy!

His thankfulness was contagious! “And all the people, when they saw it, gave praise to God” (Luke 18:43).

Bartimaeus decided to stay close to Jesus all the way down the road. He wanted to follow Jesus wherever He went.
