Moses stood on the mountain with God and smiled, amazed by His words. God loved His people as a mother eagle loves her little eaglets! He had dived into Egypt, rescued them, and brought them close to Him, like a snuggled-in baby bird.

Then God told Moses that His love was even greater than that. God said that He had chosen them to be His own “special treasure” (Exodus 19:5). They would become a great and holy nation if they would learn to listen to His voice and live the way He asked them to.

Moses hurried down the mountain to tell the Israelites the wonderful things God had said. He asked the people if they would listen to God, trust Him, and do what He asked. How did the Israelites respond? **Read Exodus 19:8.**

Moses climbed back up the mountain and told God all about the people’s promise. They wanted Him to be their God! Now God would come close, just as He wanted to. God said He would come down on the mountain in a thick cloud and speak to the people in three days’ time! He had never spoken directly to His beloved Israelite people before—they would really truly hear God’s voice!

The Israelites had to be prepared for such a holy and magnificent meeting. God instructed them to set a boundary around the mountain, and “do not go up the mountain or touch its base” (Exodus 19:12). Even though God wanted to be very close to them, this small distance helped them to understand that they would need to be reverent before their holy God.

The people were also taught that their camp, their tents, their clothes, their bodies, and their hearts needed to be sparkling clean and ready. They were learning how to show love and respect for God in every part of their lives.

All that day and the next, everyone worked quickly to get ready. Before climbing into bed that night, the whole camp was in perfect order. The people had made peace with anyone they had quarreled with. They had asked God to clean their hearts from sin and selfishness. God was visiting tomorrow, and they wanted to be ready!

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- The people could clean everything in their camp, but how well could they clean their hearts? While cleaning everything in the camp was important, the cleaning of the heart is even more important. Why do you think this is?
- How are our hearts cleaned? **Read Psalm 51:10.**
