**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Whether you’re in the early or late seasons of your life, and whether you’ve had a difficult or easy week, you can be sure that as you hold on to God, He is protecting and guiding you.

One of the clearest pictures we have of Jesus, our protector, is captured in the metaphor of a shepherd. A shepherd guides the sheep to green pastures, watches for predators, and carries little lambs who need to rest. A good shepherd keeps the sheep safe and leads them for their good. David knew what it meant to be a shepherd, and his picture of God as the Good Shepherd is beautifully captured in Psalm 23.

Many of God’s great leaders were shepherds. Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and Moses learned how to lead and protect people by caring for their flocks. Mary, Joseph, and Jesus would have been encouraged by these wonderful Old Testament stories.

In the story of Jesus’ birth we see many times that God and His angels protected and guided people to make sure that little Jesus was safe. Sometimes, like sheep, we can be oblivious to God’s guidance in our lives. It may take a few years before we can look back and notice that God was guiding us. How has God led and protected you in your life? Where is He leading you today? Thank Him for being your Good Shepherd and look forward to the day you will discover all the times He protected you without you ever noticing!

“Be strong and of good courage, do not fear nor be afraid of them; for the Lord your God, He is the One who goes with you. He will not leave you nor forsake you” (Deuteronomy 31:6).

“We have nothing to fear for the future, except as we shall forget the way the Lord has led us, and His teaching in our past history” (Ellen G. White, _Counsels for the Church_, p. 359).
