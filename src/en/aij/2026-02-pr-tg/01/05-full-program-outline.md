**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Demonstrate how to fold the paper and then cut around the outline to create two paper people. Talk about how they are identical. Did God make us all the same? Invite the children to create their own paper people and color and decorate each one so they are different. Talk about how in today’s story there were twin brothers who weren’t identical. God gives each one of us different gifts and talents to use for Him.

**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. Invite the children to look at the wall mural and ask them what they notice.

Open your Bible while you explain that this quarter we’ll continue to follow the story of God’s faithful care of Abraham and his family.

Ask the group:

- Which book in the Bible have we been reading? (Genesis)
- What did God promise Abraham’s family? (Prompt answer by showing a star or some dust. God promised to make them a big family, to bless them, and to use them to bless others.)
- Whom might we meet next in Abraham’s family? Invite them to look at the mural for clues.

Explain that we will learn through their stories, and in God’s Word, that God is with us and He leads our lives.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing the welcome song, **“Alive in Jesus,”** followed by **“Love My Enemies”** and **“God Speaks to Us.”**

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Seat the children in a circle and hand out pens and long strips of paper. Ask them to draw a facial expression of their choice in the first section, then fold the paper over, so it can’t be seen, and pass their drawing to the next person. Without peeking at the first section, have them draw the body of someone with an interesting job in the next section, then fold the paper and pass it along. The next person will draw a pair of feet, fold, and pass along. Invite the children to open their papers.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask a few children to show their pictures and kindly share a laugh together. Then invite them to pass the papers once more. This time, invite them to imagine they are looking at a picture of themselves in the future.

- **How do you feel about this picture?**
- **What’s one word to describe it?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** We have lots of choices to make in life, such as: the kinds of people I’ll choose as my friends, whether to be honest when I make a mistake, what I want to share with others, how I will include others into my circle of friends, and whether I should let my mood affect how I act. The good news is that we don’t have to rely on ourselves to make these choices. God sees the big picture and wants the best for us—we can trust His plans for us and ask Him to lead us in the big and small decisions.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or **Program PowerPoint**: **We can have confidence in God to lead us.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Set up an **obstacle course** using soft equipment (paper cups or cones) that children need to walk around to get to a marked destination. Children will work in pairs—one partner wearing a blindfold and the other acting as a guide. Guides can use only the words **forward**, **backward**, **left**, **right**, and stop to safely guide their partner through the “minefield.” If the partner with the blindfold walks into an object, they are to switch roles and start over.

- **Whom did you need to rely on to get through this obstacle course and why?** (Their partner; they couldn’t see the best or safest way to go.)
- **Who in the story showed they trusted God to lead them and how?** (Eliezer prayed for help to find Isaac’s wife; Isaac prayed and trusted God’s promise for a family.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Life is uncertain. Sometimes we won’t know which direction we should take or what we should do. God is the best guide! He is always with us and will lead us if we ask Him to.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children to stay standing and pair up. Have them help each other look up Proverbs 3:5-7. Invite them to read the verse and discuss together:

- **Which Bible character in today’s story would you share this verse with if you could, and why?**

Bring the children back together and have the pairs share their thoughts with the class. Affirm their answers, and then discuss the following questions as a group, encouraging different children to respond each time:

Show the children a large cardboard shaped heart “puzzle” with four pieces. Each piece will have a statement written on it, which you can show the children for them to read aloud after each of the following questions is discussed.

- **Why do you think God planned to give Jacob the birthright?** (God wanted to give Jacob the birthright, not because he was a quiet boy, but because he loved God, was thoughtful of others, and would lead the family to love and follow God; Esau was more interested in himself than the ways of God.) Show paper people. Show and read the statement on puzzle piece 1 (Jesus, help me to learn to love You and follow You rather than following my own ways.).
- **Jacob wanted what God wanted. So what was wrong with what he did to Esau?** (It was wrong to take advantage of his brother. He didn’t trust God to work things out His way, in His time.) Show pot of stew. Show and read the statement on puzzle piece 2 (Jesus, help me to wait for You to work things out in my life.)
- **Why is it better to have confidence in God rather than in ourselves?** (God knows what is best for us and others.) Show and read the statement on puzzle piece 3 (Jesus, help me to trust You.)
- **What will you do when you need to make an important decision?** (Pray and ask God to lead you.) Show and read the statement on puzzle piece 4 (Jesus, help me to pray to You when I need to make an important decision.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** We can’t see the future, but our loving God can. He always knows what is best for us. When we pray and ask for His help, He can guide us.

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 14: Jacob and Esau** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: “Proverbs 14:26.”

- **In this week’s story, how has God shown that we can have confidence in Him?**

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the **Mission Story**, “Child Missionary,” and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share that this quarter we’ll be focusing on **friendship** for our Mission Challenge. Each week we’ll learn or do something that will help us to eventually bring a friend to Sabbath School. Explain that Jesus wants us to share the good news of the Bible and His saving love with others. We can do this by sharing Sabbath School with our friends who may not yet know Him.

Share that this week’s mission challenge is to **bring** something for show-and-tell next week that answers this question:

- **What’s something you like about Sabbath School that you’d like a friend to enjoy with you? Is it a specific song, activity, the way your teacher tells the Bible story, the crafts, or something from nature you’ve collected at home?**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your **Pot of Stew craft** and explain how to make it. You may like to play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly in the background as children complete their craft. As the children create, talk about the exchange Esau made: the stew for God’s special blessing. How easy it can be to desire something we want instead of what God gives—which is so much better in the long term!

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Ask a child to share what they learned from: **the drawing game; the obstacle course; the Bible verse they looked up.**

Ask the class for a hands-up response:

- **Do you want God to lead your life this week?**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite each child to tell someone near them, “God wants to lead your life.”

Then pray for God to be with the children and their families and to help them remember that God is always with us. Give each child a joyful culturally appropriate gesture as they leave the Sabbath School room.
