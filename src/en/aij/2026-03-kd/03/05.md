Far, far away from Bethlehem lived a group of very wise and good men. They knew a lot about stars—the same stars you can see each night in your sky!

Where were these men from? **Read Matthew 2:1.**

These Wise Men spent their days seeking truth and reading. As they read the Old Testament Scriptures, they discovered that the Savior was coming soon. How exciting!

Then one night (the very night Baby Jesus was born) they looked at the sparkling stars in the night sky and noticed something different. It was an unusual light in the sky. Then the light faded and turned into a beautiful star that moved. How interesting!

They remembered the Old Testament scriptures that said “a star will come” and “a ruler will rise” (Numbers 24:17, ICB). With excitement the Wise Men realized this must be “His star” (Matthew 2:2), God’s special sign that their King and Savior had come, just as God had promised!

Not knowing where it might lead, they decided to follow the star. They saddled their camels, packed their bags, and filled their sacks with three very precious gifts for their King. No matter how far or how hard the trip might be, they would search for Him! They knew the truth of God’s promise: “And you will seek Me and find Me, when you search for Me with all your heart” (Jeremiah 29:13).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you know what the light and moving star was?
- What can the Wise Men teach us about seeking God and His truth?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that the Bible shows us the way to Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Sit together under the stars at night and try to find different stars. Your family may like to use an app to learn about the stars you see.
