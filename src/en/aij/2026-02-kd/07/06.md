The camels swayed from side to side as they plodded slowly toward Egypt. High up on their backs they carried a load of precious spices and perfumes. Beside them walked the Ishmaelite traders, chatting happily as they walked.

But behind one camel walked a very sad, scared-looking Joseph. He tried to hold in his tears but he couldn’t. They blurred his eyes and spilled down his cheeks. He couldn’t believe what had happened. His own brothers had sold him as a slave!

The camels plodded on, and so did he. Each slow step took him further away from home. He thought about his loving father and brother Benjamin. Would he ever see them again? Oh how he would miss them! He had never felt so alone.

Then Joseph’s heart filled with fear. _How would he be treated as a slave? Would life be hard and miserable?_

In this scary moment, God was very close to Joseph. He reminded him of the stories father Jacob had told him about God, and how He always loves and protects His children. What had God promised Father Jacob? **Read Genesis 28:15.**

Courage filled Joseph’s heart. He wasn’t alone after all. God was with him, wherever he went—even faraway Egypt.

The Bible says, “When I am afraid, I will trust you. I praise God for his word. I trust God. So I am not afraid” (Psalm 56:3, 4, ICB). And that is exactly what Joseph decided to do!
