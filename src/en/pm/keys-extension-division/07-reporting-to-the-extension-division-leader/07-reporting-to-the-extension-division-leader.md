---
title: Reporting to the Extension Division Leader
---

The extension division member should be instructed to report to the leader of the extension division within one week following the close of the quarter, the earlier the better. Be sure to instruct the member in how to complete the record on the envelope, and also on the supplementary card, if it is used.

The name, address, quarter, and its closing date can be filled in on the envelope and supplementary card by the extension division leader before they are sent to the member. The member should fill in the name and address at the bottom of the envelope. Total contributions should be summarized by the member and written in ink. The supplementary card should have the full name of each member listed, and his or her record should be marked each Sabbath.

The extension division membership and offerings are not included in the secretary’s report of the parent school each week, as is the case with other divisions of the Sabbath School. Since extension division members are often isolated, it is impossible for their records to be thus included. Instead, the extension division members keep the envelopes and record cards and then turn them in either personally or by mail to the extension division leader immediately after the close of the quarter. The leader in turn sum marizes the record of the entire extension division and passes this summary to the Sabbath School secretary.

The membership of the extension division is reported, as it stands, on the last Sabbath of each quarter and should be reported to the secretary of the parent Sabbath School, together with the var ious offerings given during the quarter (twelve Sabbaths, thir teenth Sabbath, birthday-thank offerings, Investment fund, and expense offerings). These figures are added to the main Sabbath School’s report at the end of the quarter.
