Day after day the people worked hard, making bricks, baking bricks, and laying bricks. So many bricks! Up, up, up—higher and higher the tower grew.

Oh, what a marvelous tower it was going to be! They couldn’t wait to hear people say, “Your tower is amazing! You are the best! Everybody knows about you and your tower!”

Every day they laid more bricks. They wanted their tower to reach right up to heaven! They worked hard and felt proud. And the Tower of Babel grew taller.

Some people started living in the lowest part of the tower. They filled their fancy rooms with wooden and stone idols. They thanked their idol gods for helping them.

Standing high on the walls, they looked down and proudly said, “Another flood will never reach _us_!”

They didn’t trust God at all. They were sure their plans were better than God’s plans. What does the Bible say about this thinking? **Read Proverbs 21:30.**

God saw what they were doing. He said, “These people are united. They all speak the same language. This is only the beginning of what they will do” (Genesis 11:6, ICB). Their sin and pride could hurt the whole world. The people must be stopped!
