**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a collection of your personal treasures or gifts. Talk about what each one means to you and how it helps you remember special people and special times.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What gifts would you keep in a treasure box if you had one?** (Prompt for responses as needed.)

Retrieve the final gift from your **Secret Surprise Box**: a picture or object that clearly depicts the Sabbath. Also invite the children who arrived early to come to the front and hold up the beautiful drawings they did.

**Our loving God gave us the Sabbath. It is a gift, a special treasure for us to enjoy.**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today we will be learning more about this special gift God gave us: the Sabbath. This week the **Big Idea** is: **God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath.** (Invite the children to stand up and repeat the Big Idea after you.)

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Sabbath Rest”** in an interactive and engaging way using the storytelling suggestions and/or **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a gift box containing five objects. Take each object out and explain that each one tells us about something we can do on the Sabbath.

Ask: **I wonder what each object tells us?** Guide discussion toward the following connections:

- **Bible** (learning about God)
- Small **heart** (enjoy family and friends)
- **Flower** or leaf (enjoy creation)
- Plastic **musical instrument** (worship God)
- **Happy emoji** (bring joy to others)

Have the children say after you: **“Thank You, God, for the Sabbath.”** (You may like to have the children form a line behind you and follow you around the room as you make up and sing the sentence in simple tunes for them to copy and sing back to you. Or say it with different actions for them to copy.) Sing, **“The Sabbath,”** together.

**Teaching Point:** The things we do on Sabbath remind us of how good, loving, and holy our God is.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 3: Bird of Paradise Flower** and share the following facts:

- This beautiful flower looks like a brightly colored bird.
- The colors are bright red, orange, and blue.
- They grow in warm places and are found mostly in South America and Australia.

When we notice the pretty details of this flower, and the care that went into creating it, we are delighting in the gift of the Sabbath. We are reminded of God’s big, amazing love for us.

Give each child **Nature Card 3** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** **Share the mission story:** “Anita’s Sabbath Adventure.”

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Craft activity: Sabbath Vase Picture. Make a picture of a pretty vase with flowers using fabric and paper. Play peaceful music in the background. (Alternatively, if you have access to small glass jars, you could have the children paint the jars to use as a Sabbath vase. Be certain to cover their clothing so they do not get paint on them.)

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He made the Sabbath.**

Invite children to say what makes them happy about the Sabbath. After each suggestion, have everyone say, **“Sabbath is the best day of all!”**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Look for ways to give others the biggest and the best this week. Think about mealtime, playtime, and helping around the home. This is God’s loving way.**
