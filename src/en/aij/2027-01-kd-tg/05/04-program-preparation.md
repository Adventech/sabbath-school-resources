**Opening Activities**

Source **materials to make percussion instruments**. E.g., paper plates folded in half and filled with beans/rice and stapled, sticks to color to click together, plastic containers of rice or beans, bells threaded on pipe cleaners wrapped around craft sticks, metal bottle tops nailed into wood, hitting spoons together. The children can use these in the story section and in Apply section, so you may like to make extras. Or you can supply tambourines/bells for these later segments.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: stack of fabric squares or hand towels.** Make the squares big enough for the children to jump onto. Include two sets for large groups.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: Source a **box** to use as an Egyptian chariot, **tambourines** for the children to play (or percussion instruments made earlier). Two pieces of blue fabric (or towels) to mark Red Sea.

Source peg people (Moses, families) or a felt set if desired, and the **^[week 5 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: **balloon, permanent marker, pin** to burst balloon.
