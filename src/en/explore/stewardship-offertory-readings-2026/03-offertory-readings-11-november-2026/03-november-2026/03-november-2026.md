title: God's Megaphone
date: 21/11/2026
---

Like Noah, Elijah, and John the Baptist, Seventh-day Adventists have been assigned one of the most exciting missions ever. We are the forerunners—those appointed to announce the soon-coming King. And what’s our cry? In its purest form, the three angels’ messages of Revelation 14 loudly proclaim, “The greatest rescue event in the history of our planet is soon to take place. Be ready!”

The original Greek words describing the loud voice with which these messages are given basically describe a megaphone. That’s us, God’s megaphone of love!

The Adventist movement has been entrusted with the privilege of being used by the Holy Spirit to tell the world. We are called to humbly, yet loudly, announce Christ’s return through loving acts of kindness, person-to-person sharing, evangelistic campaigns, media ministries, centers of influence, humanitarian services, schools, hospitals, and the list goes on.

John the Baptist prepared the way for the Lord by announcing the soon coming of Jesus, calling people to repentance, and helping them to prepare their hearts and characters for Jesus’ arrival. So, too, today, those who keep the commandments of God and have the testimony and faith of Jesus Christ have been invited to be the forerunners who loudly and clearly announce the soon arrival of Jesus.

### Appeal

The Seventh-day Adventist Church’s system of tithes and offerings is a powerful supply chain that resources God’s worldwide mission to announce the three angels’ messages. Thank you for revealing your love for God and everyone through your faithful giving today.
