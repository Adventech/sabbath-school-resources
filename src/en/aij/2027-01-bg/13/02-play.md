Sing

For worship each night, sing “Who Made the Beautiful Rainbow?”

Who made the beautiful rainbow?\
I know, I know.\
God made the beautiful rainbow,\
That’s why I love Him so.\

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Worship

Make a rainbow prop to use with the song. Tie colored ribbons/yarn/strips of colored paper to a craft stick or a stick you’ve found outside. Have the colored ribbons/yarn/papers hang long and loose for your child to wave as they sing.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Play

Have a rainbow scavenger hunt. Help your child find and collect 5-10 objects for each color of the rainbow. Then arrange the colorful objects on the floor to make a big rainbow shape. You may also like to make up a plate of rainbow-colored fruits, if easily accessible.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Create

Paint a rainbow picture as a reminder of the rainbow God painted in the sky.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Reflect

Throughout the day, make a few promises to your child that you can and will keep. For example, “I promise to make you lunch” or “I promise to read you a story before naptime.” At the end of the day, ask them if you kept all the promises you made to them. Then share how God always keeps His promises to us, just like His promise never to flood the whole world again.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Experiment

Catch a mini rainbow. In the morning or afternoon when the sun is low and strong, find a window where the sun is shining. Place a glass of water in front of the sunlight and ask your child if they can catch the rainbow on a white piece of paper. Alternatively, shine a flashlight on a CD and reflect it on to the white paper. Ask your child what colors they can see in the little rainbow.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#9e5f94"}}}
Retell

Watch a rainbow appear. Fold a paper towel in half. On the back half of the paper towel, use markers to draw a rainbow _(without your child seeing)_. Then on the front half, draw two gray clouds. Fill a shallow dish with water. Show your child the front of the paper towel with the clouds, keeping the rainbow hidden. Ask, “What did God make appear in the sky after the rain?” Then have them place the paper towel carefully in the water as they watch the rainbow appear in the clouds!
