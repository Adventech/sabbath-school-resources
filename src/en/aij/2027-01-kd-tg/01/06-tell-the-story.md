**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- A new evil pharaoh was fearful that there were too many Israelites, so he made them slaves.
- God protected them. Their families grew and grew!
- Pharaoh told his soldiers to throw all Israelite baby boys in the Nile River.
- Father Amram, Mother Jochebed, Miriam, and Aaron wouldn’t let that happen to their baby! They hid him ^[(baby)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). **^[What noises do babies make?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**
- After three months God gave Mother a new plan. She made a basket boat.
- Mother and Miriam put their baby in the basket boat and hid him in the reeds on the Nile River. Angels and Miriam watched over him ^[(basket, angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- The Egyptian princess found the boat. She wanted to look after him. She called him Moses.
- Miriam asked the princess if she would like a nurse. Yes. Miriam brought her mother. God had saved him!
- Moses lived with his family for 12 years, then moved to the palace and became a prince. God planned for Moses to one day save His people ^[(crown)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A new evil pharaoh sat on the royal throne of Egypt. As he looked across his kingdom, he saw so many Israelites, and he thought, We can’t let the Israelite people grow stronger and bigger than the Egyptians. We must stop them!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) So he decided to make their lives very hard. He forced the Israelites to work long days as slaves. They carried heavy loads, and made mud bricks and storehouses. All they did every day was work, work, work! The Israelites were so very tired.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But God protected and blessed them. Their families kept growing and growing! God had good plans for them.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Pharaoh was furious that his plan wasn’t working. So he ordered midwives to kill all the newborn baby boys. When the midwifes didn’t do this, Pharaoh told his people to throw the baby boys into the Nile River. Oh, no!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Father Amram and Mother Jochebed held their baby boy close. ^[(Hold baby doll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Big sister Miriam and brother Aaron shook their heads. There was no way they would ever let that happen to their little baby!

Quickly they hid their baby in their home. They would trust God to keep him safe. ^[(Hide baby doll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

But it was hard to keep a baby quiet! **^[What noises do babies make?]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After three months God gave Mother Jochebed a special idea. She collected some long green river reeds and began weaving them together to make a basket with a lid. She smeared it with sticky tar so water couldn’t leak inside. It would be a basket boat! ^[(Show basket.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Gently Jochebed wrapped her baby boy in a soft blanket ^[(show blanket)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), placed him inside and closed the lid. Mother and Miriam carried the secret basket boat to the river Nile. ^[(Place blue sheet on the ground as river.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then they hid the basket boat near the water’s edge among the tall grass. ^[(Add plastic or potted plant.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mother couldn’t stay with her baby, but she trusted God to look after him. He sent invisible angels to hover above the tiny basket as it floated on the water.

Miriam also stood close by, watching over him.

Someone was coming! Miriam watched closely. There, walking toward the water’s edge, was Pharaoh’s daughter, the Egyptian princess.

Suddenly the princess noticed the little basket boat. A servant brought it quickly to her. The princess opened the lid and saw a little baby inside. He was crying. What a surprise! The princess “felt sorry for him” (Exodus 2:6, ICB). She wanted to protect him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Miriam bravely came forward and asked the princess if she needed a nurse to look after the baby. “Yes, please” (Exodus 2:8, ICB), Pharaoh’s daughter replied.

Miriam knew just the woman to care for the baby—his own mother! Miriam raced home and brought her back to the river. The princess asked Mother Jochebed to look after him. She wanted to adopt him as her son when he grew into a big boy. His name would be Moses.

Jochebed nodded and smiled with joy. God had protected her baby boy in such a surprising way!

Little Moses grew up in a home in which his family loved him and loved God. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When Moses was about 12 years old, he left his little Hebrew house and moved into the royal palace. The princess adopted Moses and raised him as a prince ^[(crown)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God had the best plans for Moses. He planned to use Prince Moses one day to free the Israelite slaves from Egypt.
