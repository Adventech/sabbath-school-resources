**Big Idea:** I can rely on God and His power to help me when I am tempted.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images as you tell the story.
- You may like to use the following props: a Bible; three pieces of paper with a large number on each: 1, 2, 3; a few stones.
- Ask children to open their Bibles and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask them.
```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus smiled as He walked away from the Jordan River. God had just said, “This is My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Matthew 3:17). Leaving the river behind, Jesus walked up the rocky path and out into the wilderness. The Holy Spirit led Him to a quiet place in the desert.

Away from the crowds, Jesus spent time alone with His Father, God. During this time, Jesus didn’t rest or eat. It was a special time for Jesus. Because He was close to God, His faith grew stronger. He would need this strength to do the work He had come to earth to do.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After 40 days Jesus’ special time with God was over. He was tired, weak, and hungry. Satan had been watching Jesus, trying to find the right moment to tempt Him, and now he saw his chance!

^[(Show your Number 1 card.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly, Satan appeared beside Jesus. He was pretending to be a shining angel from heaven.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Satan say? Read Matthew 4:3.** (“If you are the Son of God, tell these rocks to become bread” [ICB].) ^[(Show stones.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus thought about that little word “if.” Of course He was God’s Son! An angel from heaven would never say “If you are God’s Son . . .” How dare Satan tempt Him to doubt that God was His Father!

Jesus didn’t explain. And He didn’t let Satan trick Him into working a miracle to prove He was God’s Son. He would never use His power to work a miracle for Himself—it was meant only to help others. And besides, God could give Him food anytime.

Jesus looked Satan straight in the eye and said ^[(hold up Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), “It is written in the Scriptures, ‘A person does not live only by eating bread. But a person lives by everything the Lord says,’ ” (Matthew 4:4, ICB).

Jesus had won!

Satan’s first temptation hadn’t worked, so he tried another one. ^[(Show your Number 2 card.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He took Jesus to the very top of the temple in Jerusalem. He said, “If you are the Son of God, jump off” (Matthew 4:6, ICB). Then Satan boldly recited a Bible verse from Psalm 91:11, 12: “For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways. In their hands they shall bear you up, lest you dash your foot against a stone.” Satan was using God’s own Word to try to trap Jesus!

Jesus wasn’t fooled. He knew He could claim God’s promise of protection while doing God’s work—that would be faith. But to claim God’s promise while doing something silly and dangerous? Never!

^[(Hold up Bible.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus answered, “It has been said, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God’ ” (Luke 4:12).

Jesus had won another victory!

Satan tried again. ^[(Show your Number 3 card.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He carried Jesus to a very tall mountaintop. Here they could see beautiful cities, sparkling palaces, green fields, and fruit-filled orchards below. Satan spoke smoothly.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Let’s read what he said in Matthew 4:9.** (“If you will bow down and worship me, I will give you all these things” [ICB].)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Why do you think Satan asked Jesus to worship Him?**

Satan is an evil liar. He knew that if Jesus became dazzled by riches and power, He could not save the world. If He bowed down and worshipped Satan, everyone would be lost forever.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How should Jesus answer? Why?**

Jesus didn’t let temptation sidetrack Him from His mission. Even though Jesus knew His life on earth would be filled with sadness and suffering, He chose the hard path to save the sinners He loved so much.

^[(Hold up Bible.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus firmly told the devil, “Away with you, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve’ ” (Matthew 4:10). And so Satan was forced to leave, angry and defeated.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus slumped to the ground, totally exhausted from the battle with Satan. Instantly angels were by His side. They cared for Jesus and fed Him food. God had sent the angels to remind Jesus of His great love. The rest of the angels filled heaven with their celebrations.

Jesus had won!
