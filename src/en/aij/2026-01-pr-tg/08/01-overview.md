**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the way Noah and his family trusted God to protect and lead them as their time in the ark came to an end and they embarked on building new lives in a dramatically changed world. God sent a rainbow as a promise of future care. They were grateful for God’s encouragement, blessings, fulfilled promises, and His promise to protect them in the future.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they can trust God to protect them and keep His promises.
- **Feel** thankful for God’s protection and fulfilled promises.
- **Respond** by choosing to actively trust God’s promises.
