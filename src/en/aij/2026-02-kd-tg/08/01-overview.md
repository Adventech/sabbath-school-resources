**Word of the Week**

**Choose:** To choose is to decide to do something. God loves you so much, He lets you make choices. He wants you to choose to love and follow Him, because His ways are best!

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Joseph arrived in Egypt and was sold into Potiphar’s wealthy household. Joseph chose to stay close to God and follow His ways. He worked hard and was honest, and God blessed him. He became head of Potiphar’s household. Potiphar noticed that God was with Joseph, blessing him. Potiphar’s wife told lies about Joseph, and Joseph was thrown into prison. In prison Joseph chose to trust God and follow His ways. He was kind and helped other prisoners. The man in charge of the prison noticed and put Joseph in charge of all the prisoners. Joseph chose God and showed His love everywhere he went.

**Learning Goals**

- **Feel** a desire to make good choices.
- **Respond** by making choices each day that follow God’s way.
