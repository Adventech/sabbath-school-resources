Llamas aren’t often everyone’s favorite animal, but they might be one of your new favorites—because llamas are super special!

Llamas are cousins to the camel. They have the same long neck, but no hump. Their soft, fluffy coat makes them look like a cuddly bedtime toy. And most of the time llamas are quite gentle and calm. Many even like to be petted!

But llamas aren’t always cute and cuddly. Do you know what llamas do when they get grumpy at each other? They stick out their tongue. It’s true! You might laugh at the thought, but if a llama pokes out its tongue, it’s saying, “Don’t mess with me! I’m not happy with you.” So much llama drama!

But the really cool thing about llamas is how they protect their herd. Their herd might be a flock of sheep, goats, deer, or even chickens. But when a llama decides that an animal is part of his family, he will protect that animal no matter what.

If a fox comes looking for some dinner, watch out! Loving llamas will make a loud warning noise, run toward the fox, chase it, kick it, or spit at it. They never stop watching over and protecting their herd. What caring creatures!

God has adopted us into His family, and He promises to protect and keep us safe from danger too. He even sends His angels day and night to watch over you and “to keep you in all your ways” (Psalm 91:11).
