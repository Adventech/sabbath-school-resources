Bartimaeus wouldn’t stop shouting! He yelled out loudly, with all his might. “Son of David, have mercy on me!” (Luke 18:39).

Many people in the crowd found his cry for help very annoying.

But not Jesus.

Jesus listened. From the middle of the crowd, Jesus heard the sweet sound of His precious Bartimaeus. He heard his cry for help.

Jesus stopped and looked at Bartimaeus. “Bring the blind man to me!” Jesus said with kindness in His voice (Luke 18:40, ICB).

With a hurry and a scurry, some of the men from the crowd walked over to Bartimaeus. They bent down in front of him. “Cheer up! Get to your feet. Jesus is calling you,” they said with a smile (Mark 10:49, ICB).

Bartimaeus jumped up with a big, glowing smile on his face. Jesus had heard him calling out! Jesus had listened!

Jesus always listens to us when we call out to Him. The Bible says, “God has listened. He has heard my prayer” (Psalm 66:19, ICB).

Bartimaeus couldn’t wait to come close to Jesus. He was in such an excited rush to meet Him, he left something behind. What was it? **Read Mark 10:50.**

The men led him by the arm past the watching eyes of the many people around. They stood Bartimaeus in front of Jesus.
