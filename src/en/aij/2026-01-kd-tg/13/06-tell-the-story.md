**Big Idea:** God is loving. He is always faithful.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **gray fabric** and a **walking stick** to illustrate how old Abraham and Sarah were.
- Create a **“tent”** by facing two chairs away from each other, a yard (one meter) apart, and draping a sheet over them.
- Use **flashlights** to flicker “stars” on the roof and show **pictures** of a starry night sky.
- Choose several children to wait as “Sarah” inside the tent or have all children sit in tent, space permitting.
- Use a **bowl, towel, cup**, and **toy fruit** to illustrate the kind things Abraham did for his visitors.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 13 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **baby doll** as baby Isaac.
- Use a **felt set**, if available, or peg people.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Abram and Sarah were very old. God had promised them a big family, but it seemed impossible! ^[(Gray fabric on head, walking stick.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God promised Abram that his family would be like the stars—too many to count! ^[(Flashlights on ceiling.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God gave them new names as a promise too. He called Abram Abraham—a father. He called Sarai Sarah—a princess ^[(children repeat names).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Three strangers came to visit Abraham. He was kind and friendly to them ^[(tent, wash feet, cup, play food).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The strangers knew Sarah’s name. Two were angels, and one was Jesus Himself!
- The stranger (Jesus) told Abraham that Sarah would have a baby in a year’s time ^[(use peg people or felts).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Sarah was listening and laughed in her mind! It seemed impossible—they were too old. She lied about laughing ^[(use peg people or felts).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Jesus knew Sarah laughed. He showed her that He knows everything. And His promises are always true.
- Baby Isaac was born. God had done the impossible! ^[(Baby doll.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- One day Jesus would be a descendant of this family. God always keeps His promises.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram was now an old, old man, and Sarai was a wrinkly old woman. ^[(Place gray-colored fabric over your head, use a walking stick.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was a long time since God had said to Abram, “I will make you a great nation; I will bless you” (Genesis 12:2). Poor Abram and Sarai still didn’t have a child of their own. It seemed impossible! ^[(Use facial expressions.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God wanted to encourage His friend. He told Abram, “You will have a son of your own” (Genesis 15:4, ICB). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then God led Abram outside in the middle of the night. “Look at the sky,” God said. Abram looked up. The black sky was scattered with millions of glittering stars ^[(children flicker flashlights on the ceiling)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God told Abram his family would be like the stars—too many to count. What a big family! God would do the impossible!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram and Sarai waited for their promised baby. They waited . . . and waited . . . and waited. Are you good at waiting for things?

It was hard to be patient for such a long time. Abram was now 99 years old, and Sarai was nearly 90! God helped them by giving them new names. These names would be a promise reminder. God called Abram Abraham, meaning father of many nations. He called Sarai Sarah, which means princess. ^[(Ask: What names did God give them?)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day, old Abraham was sitting in his tent’s shady doorway ^[(use peg people or felts)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He looked up and saw three men nearby ^[(count three children)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He ran from his tent to meet them. Abraham was kind and friendly. He helped to make the visitors feel important and special. He made sure their dusty feet were washed and that they had a restful seat. ^[(Use peg people or felts.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He gave them a cool drink ^[(show cup)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He made sure there was yummy food to eat ^[(show toy fruit)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The men asked their friendly host, “Where is your wife Sarah?” (Genesis 18:9, ICB). How did they know Sarah’s name, and why did they ask about her?

Abraham told them Sarah was inside the tent. ^[(Invite several or all children to be Sarah and sit inside the tent.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Though Abraham didn’t realize it yet, these men were not just men. Two of them were angels, and one of them was Jesus Himself!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then one of the men ^[(who was Jesus)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) said to Abraham, “I will certainly return to you about this time a year from now. At that time your wife Sarah will have a son” (Genesis 18:10, ICB). Sarah was secretly listening. When she heard these words, she laughed to herself, because she knew she was far too old to have a baby! ^[(Children to giggle.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Sarah hadn’t laughed out loud, but Jesus had heard her thoughts. Then something disappointing happened. Sarah told a lie to Jesus. She said, “I didn’t laugh.” Jesus spoke straight to her, “No. You did laugh” (Genesis 18:15, ICB). Sarah learned a lot that day. She learned that Jesus knows everything and that God’s promises are always true.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) How happy everyone was when little Isaac was born ^[(teacher cradles doll or passes around for children to have a cuddle)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Everyone knew that Isaac truly was a special gift from God. He was a miracle baby!

Isaac would have a son, and he would have a son . . . and one day Jesus would be a descendant of this very special family. God always keeps His promises. God is loving. He is always faithful.
