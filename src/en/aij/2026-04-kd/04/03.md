Down on the lake, a strong wind began to blow. The wind swept across the lake and stirred up the water. Little ripples turned into huge, crashing waves. The disciples clung to their boat as it rocked up and down and went from side to side. They were scared!

The disciples were in trouble, but they weren’t alone. Someone was watching very closely. Yes, Jesus.

Up on the hillside, Jesus had kept His eyes on His friends in their little boat. They didn’t need to worry because Jesus was near. What did Jesus do? **Read Matthew 14:25.**

The disciples looked up and saw something very strange in the moonlight. Could it be . . . ? No, surely not . . . but yes, it really was! It was a person walking on the tumbling, foaming sea!

_People can’t walk on water_, they thought. Their eyes opened wide. Their hands began to tremble. If they were scared before by the storm, they were extra scared now. The disciples started to scream!

“Jesus spoke to them, saying, ‘Be of good cheer! It is I; do not be afraid’ ” (Matthew 14:27).

Jesus didn’t want them to be scared. He was there to protect them! He was there to help them and save them from the storm.

Even though we can’t see Jesus, He sees us. He cares for us and will help us.
