“Good Morning, Friday!”

Five whole days had come and gone since God had started creating the new world. Now it was the sixth day. The golden sun shone brightly over the colorful gardens. Birds flitted between trees, singing happy songs. What a beautiful world!

God could see that it was good, but He wasn’t finished yet. What did He do next? Read Genesis 1:24, 25.

God spoke and it happened, just like that! When God’s powerful voice spoke, all kinds of different animals were created. Some animals had soft, silky fur. Others had tough, rough skin. Some had huge ears, and others had teeny, tiny tails.

God made a male and female (boy and girl) of every kind of animal: two barking dogs, two big elephants, two galloping horses, two fast leopards, and two mooing cows. God then commanded the animals to “be fruitful and multiply” (Genesis 1:22). He wanted all the pairs of animals to have cute baby animals, and for their families to grow bigger. Yes, God thought of everything!

What did God think of all that He had made? Read Genesis 1:25.

God had created something good, because He is good, and He is loving and kind and so very generous. Now it was time to make the most exciting creation of all.
