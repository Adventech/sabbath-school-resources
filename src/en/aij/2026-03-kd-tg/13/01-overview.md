**Word of the Week**

**Caring:** Caring is showing kindness and concern for someone. When you are caring, you look after someone and help them. Caring can be kind words, sharing a toy or some food, using gentle hands, or helping someone with a problem. There are many ways you can be caring, just as Jesus was.

**Lesson Summary**

Jesus visited the synagogue in Nazareth on a Sabbath and taught the people from the Scriptures that He had come as the Messiah. The people became angry and tried to kill Him, but God protected Him. Jesus then traveled to Capernaum, where He healed Peter’s mother-in-law and many others. After a long night caring for people, He spent time alone with His Father.
