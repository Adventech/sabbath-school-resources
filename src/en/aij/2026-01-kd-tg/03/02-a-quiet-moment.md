**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

How incredibly beautiful is the Sabbath gift of quality time with our God; how precious and how needed it is in our world today. God knew that gifting us the Sabbath would nurture sincere connections between Him and His children on this special day. “The Sabbath is a golden clasp that unites God and His people” (Ellen G. White, _Maranatha_, p. 244).

It is golden. It is a firm, secure clasp that safely links God, the Creator of the universe, with His loved ones. The Sabbath holds much potential for rich spiritual experiences.

Sabbaths can also be busy. Because you’re a Sabbath School teacher, your Sabbath morning will likely be very lively. God is in these creative, animated, sometimes chaotic moments with you. He loves to see you lead His children to praise Him in a special way on His holy day. If you open your heart to Him, His rest will fill your soul in whatever act of service, or moment of praise, you find yourself in.

This Sabbath may you personally delight in who God is, as you share with the children how wonderful He is today and every day. And may you enjoy the Sabbath as a special gift from God to be treasured.
