**Big Idea:** God is loving. He can help me to be a loyal friend.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Create a green landscape with a **green piece of fabric**.
- Use **peg people** for characters in this story.
- Have a piece of **cardboard with rows of “peg prisoners”** taped onto it, ready for the children to “rescue.”
- Use **plush animals, pots, spoons**, etc., to represent the stolen goods.
- Use **10 objects** of your choice to illustrate what tithe is. Use cardboard altar from week 8.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 12 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use **felts**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Abram’s home in the hills was happy and peaceful—with an altar to God, His loyal friend ^[(cardboard altar).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Lot lived down on the flat land near wicked Sodom. ^[(Point to the lush green “landscape” from last week.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Lot got closer and closer to Sodom . . . until he moved there! He made bad friends. ^[(Start to move back and forth in the room.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Four kings from far away attacked Sodom and their neighbors. They took all their things, food, and people as prisoners—even Lot! ^[(Peg people with sad faces, cardboard prison.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- A messenger told Abram. Abram was loyal and loving. _Oh, no! Poor Lot! I must help him!_
- Abram took 318 brave men from his camp, and they ran and rescued Lot and all the prisoners. ^[(Use peg people; rescue peg people from prison.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Abram took all the people and all the stolen things back to Sodom ^[(children load items into a box).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- A king and priest, who worshipped God, came out to greet Abram—Melchizedek. He asked that God would bless Abram ^[(peg person).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram’s home in the hills was a happy and peaceful one. Abram could talk with God under the shady trees. He could offer sacrifices to God on the altar, right near his tent ^[(cardboard altar)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He knew God was his loyal friend, and that was all he needed.

Down on the flat, green land were Lot’s tents, just down the road from the wicked city of Sodom. It wasn’t long before Lot started to think: _Hmm . . . Sodom is not far away. I can go there, but I won’t go there very often._ ^[(Start to move back and forth in the room.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Then later: _Oh, let’s go to Sodom and have some fun!_

Then later: _Oh, it’s too far to go back to our tent tonight. Let’s just stay the night in Sodom._

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lot slowly changed. He ended up moving right into Sodom to live beside people who didn’t love God and treated each other very badly. Before long they became friends.

Lot didn’t know that trouble was headed for the city of Sodom! Four troublemaking kings attacked Sodom and their neighbors. What a scary surprise! ^[(Peg people.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They took all the people as prisoners and stole all their things, and all their food too! Poor Lot was a prisoner! ^[(Show the peg people with sad faces taped to the cardboard “prison.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

A messenger ran as fast as he could and told Abram what had happened. Abram was very upset. _Oh no! Poor Lot! I love him! He’s in trouble. I must help him!_ Abram was so loyal and loving. He was like Jesus, who also came to this world to rescue us from our bad choices.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram thought and prayed, and then gathered 318 brave men from his camp to help him rescue Lot from the enemy camp. ^[(Peg people.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They raced off to find Lot.

Abram and his men crept up to the camp and chased the enemy away. What a surprise those soldiers got! Abram and his men rescued all the prisoners! ^[(Children to rescue peg people from the cardboard prison.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Lot gave Uncle Abram a big hug. He was so thankful that Abram had saved him. Abram took the prisoners and their things and set off for Sodom ^[(children to help you load items into a box)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

On their way a special king and priest who worshipped God came out to greet Abram. His name was Melchizedek^[(peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

“Abram,” said Melchizedek, “may God Most High give you blessings. . . . He has helped you to defeat your enemies” (Genesis 14:19, 20, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram was so happy to hear these words that he wanted to give a thank-You back to God. “Abram gave Melchizedek a tenth of everything he had brought back from the battle” (Genesis 14:20, ICB). So for every 10 pieces of gold, he gave one of those to the king and priest Melchizedek! ^[(Show 10 small objects, and then hold one up. Explain that this one is “tithe,” and it belongs to God.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Soon the king of Sodom appeared on the dusty road ^[(peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was very grateful to Abram for rescuing his people. He said, “You may keep all these things yourself. Just give me my people who were captured!”

Abram shook his head. No, no, no ^[(shake your head)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Abram wouldn’t take anything. He knew he didn’t need a king’s riches. His loyal God would always provide for him. He is our loyal God, and He will provide for us too.
