Noah smiled as he looked up at the bright rainbow, shining in the sky.What a beautiful reminder of God’s love! He hoped his big family would always smile when they saw it and always love God. But sadly, little by little, Ham stopped being God’s friend.

In time, Noah’s family grew bigger and bigger. Soon there were lots of people in the world again. Every person had a choice to follow God or not. Sadly, many chose to turn away from God. Ham’s children learned to be unkind and mean, like their father. Their children learned to be like that too. They were proud and selfish.

Everyone had been living in the mountains near the ark. But one day the people who didn’t want to follow God anymore became tired of listening to their cousins talk about God. They packed up their things and moved down the mountain to live on the flat land near a big river. Here they could forget about God and do things their own way.

Each day we can choose to follow God, or not. The Bible helps to guide us in making good choices. What does God say about obeying Him? **Read Proverbs 3:1.**

Sometimes it’s hard to choose God’s way, but He promises to help us when we ask Him.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you think the people who chose to forget God would be happy? Why or why not?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you make good choices every day so you can be happy and show His love to your family.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
