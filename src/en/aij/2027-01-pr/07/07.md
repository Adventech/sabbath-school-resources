A great way to think about caring for our bodies and staying healthy is found in the words NEW START. Each letter stands for an important way to care for our bodies and minds. Let’s take a look.

**N. Nutrition.** God created us to eat a range of fruits, vegetables, nuts, seeds, and grains so that our bodies get the nutrients they need to grow strong and healthy.

{"style":{"block":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"},"rounded":false},"wrapper":{"padding":{"start":"none","end":"none"}},"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr/07/image8.jpg)

**E. Exercise.** Being active for at least 30 minutes three or four times a week keeps us fit and our muscles strong. Exercise helps to keep our brains happy and healthy too.

**W. Water.** Children need to drink five to six glasses of water a day to keep their bodies functioning well. Without enough water, we can suffer from headaches, tiredness, brain fog, dull skin, and constipation.

**S. Sunlight.** While spending too long in direct sun can cause sunburn, getting enough sunlight is very important. Our bodies use sunlight to produce vitamin D, which helps us fight sickness. Sunlight also helps keep our bones strong and our minds happy.

**T. Temperance.** This means having self-control so that we don’t have too much or too little of what our bodies need, as well as avoiding what is harmful. For example, food is good. But if we overeat, we can feel gross or become sick. Temperance is also about allowing God to control our thoughts and feelings.

**A. Fresh Air.** When we breathe in fresh air, we are energized, able to concentrate longer and sleep better. Our bodies need clean air to help recover from sickness and to stay healthy.

**R. Rest.** Studies show that children need at least nine to 10 hours of sleep a night. During sleep our brains sort and store information, our bodies grow, and our germ-fighting immune system stays healthy. Not to mention how grumpy we can get without enough sleep!

**T. Trust in God.** God knows everything, and He knows what is best for us. Learning to trust Him to help and take care of us means we can have peace and not worry.
