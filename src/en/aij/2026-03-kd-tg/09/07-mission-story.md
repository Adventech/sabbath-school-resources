**Focus:** God provides for us when we choose to be faithful to Him.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Russia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Ivan loved to write, so he was excited when he heard about a big contest in which all children from across Russia were invited to write a story about outer space. The authors of the best stories would get to meet a real-life cosmonaut, a person who has been in space!

Ivan sat down and wrote his story. Then he prayed for God to bless it as he sent it off.

Three months passed. Ivan waited and waited. One day he received a letter that he had won! His story had been chosen as the best in his age group. Ivan was so happy—he knew God had heard his prayer.

Ivan was invited to go to a seashore town to meet a cosmonaut at a special prize ceremony. He was so excited!

But then he saw that the prize ceremony was scheduled for the Sabbath. He knew that he could not go. He wanted to keep the Sabbath, God’s special day of worship. Ivan was deeply disappointed, but he decided to pray again. He knew that God hears prayers.

On the Sabbath when Ivan was supposed to meet the cosmonaut at the prize ceremony, he chose to be faithful and meet with God at church instead.

Now, it just so happened that the cosmonaut saw that one of the winners of the story-writing contest had missed the prize ceremony. He wanted to meet the boy who was such a good storywriter. Ivan was very surprised when the cosmonaut showed up a short time later at his Seventh-day Adventist school. All the children gathered in the auditorium. The cosmonaut stood on the stage, and the principal called for Ivan to come to the front. All the children clapped and cheered loudly for Ivan.

The cosmonaut congratulated a beaming Ivan. He gave Ivan a collection of books about space, two hats, and several badges just like the ones cosmonauts wear on their spacesuits in outer space.

Ivan couldn’t have been happier. God had heard his prayers. He, and all the children at the school, got to meet a real-life cosmonaut because he had been faithful to God and kept the Sabbath holy. God had provided for him!
