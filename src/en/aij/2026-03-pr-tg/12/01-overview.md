**Word of the Week**

**Potential:** Potential means possible but not yet happened; the possibility, chance, or promise that something will develop and become real. When we use the word “potential” to describe people, we are talking about who they can become. Often we speak about someone “showing potential,” meaning we can see little signs that something good could develop in a person in the future.

**Lesson Summary**

The story of Zacchaeus shows that Jesus sees potential in everyone. Zacchaeus, a hated tax collector, cheated people by making them pay more than they should. He was a lonely, unhappy man. But when he heard about Jesus and some of the things He was doing, something stirred in his heart. He believed his life could change if he could just see Jesus. Zacchaeus was short, so he climbed a tree where he could see Jesus pass by. Jesus saw his potential for good and lovingly called him down and told him he would be visiting his house that very day. He called him a child of God and told the crowd that He can come to save the lost.
