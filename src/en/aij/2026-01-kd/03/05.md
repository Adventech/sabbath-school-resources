Elephants have the largest brain of all land animals. This means that elephants remember things _really well_! God wants us to have the memory of an elephant when it comes to the Sabbath. He wants us to always remember it and keep it different from every other day. The Bible tells us that God “has made His wonderful works to be remembered” (Psalm 111:4).

Every Friday evening when the sun goes down, the Sabbath begins. What else does the Bible say? **Read Exodus 20:8.**

In fact, God thought it was so important to remember the Sabbath that He wrote it in stone with His finger as part of the Ten Commandments.

There are a lot of things to remember and do on the Sabbath. God wants us to remember:

- To rest with Him
- To remember His love and power
- To enjoy His gift of creation
- To worship Him with our church family
- To share His love and truth with others

God knows that if we remember to do these things on the holy Sabbath day, it will bring us closer to Him and each other. Our hearts will overflow with joy.
