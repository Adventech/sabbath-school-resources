Create

Use play-dough or modeling clay to create Hannah and her little baby. Ask your child what Hannah’s face might have looked like when she cuddled her baby for the first time.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#EB9696"}}}
Explore

When the sun has set, take your child outside and hug each other. Share with them that even though we sleep every night, God never sleeps! He is always awake and listens closely to our every prayer. He knows the sound of our voice and even hears when lots and lots of people talk to Him at the same time. God is so amazing!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#EB9696"}}}
Role Play

Role-play looking after a “baby” (doll/teddy) together with your child. Pretend to bathe, feed, play with, and cuddle the baby. Share how Hannah was so excited that God said yes to her prayer, and how much she loved her little baby. Cuddle your child and let them know that they are an answer to your prayers!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#EB9696"}}}
Model

Set a timer on your phone for every hour or two, one day this week. Whenever the timer goes off, stop and pray with your child. Share how God wants us to talk to Him all throughout the day.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#EB9696"}}}
Pray

Make a conscious effort in your family prayers to pray over each of your children. Thank God for giving them to you as a gift. Pray that they will grow up to love and help Him.
