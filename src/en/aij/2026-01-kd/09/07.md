“The hour of His judgment is come,” called out the little girl. Her family watched the 5-year-old in wonder. Just minutes before, she had opened her mouth and sung out a beautiful hymn. She had never been taught the tricky words of the long song—she’d never even been taught to sing! In fact, the little girl could not even read or write. And yet here she was preaching powerfully to her family, telling them all to give their hearts to Jesus, for He was coming soon!

Her family knew her words were a miracle. They asked God for forgiveness. They wanted to be ready for Jesus to come!

It was the year 1843 in Sweden, and God was doing something amazing! From town to town kids just like you began to share the good news of Jesus as if they were grown-ups. At this time in Sweden it was against the law to preach anything that didn’t agree with the one religion of the country. Bible truths had been forgotten. People were proud and didn’t want to change. So God sent children to preach. He gave them the right words to say. God’s message of love would not be stopped!

One of the preacher kids was a little 8-year-old boy who had never learned his alphabet. A priest who was upset by the boy’s preaching tried to prove it was a trick. But he couldn’t! The young boy could quote Bible verses that he couldn’t read and had never heard before.

Soon teenagers joined in preaching too. They told others about the Bible and Jesus’ second coming. Large groups of people gathered, both day and night, to listen to the kids preach. God helped these children to tell others about Him!

“You are the God who does wonders; You have declared Your strength among the peoples” (Psalm 77:14).
