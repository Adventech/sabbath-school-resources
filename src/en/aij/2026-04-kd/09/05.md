The man who had been healed ran straight to the temple. He really wanted to say thank You to God. He took two offerings: a thank offering and a sin offering. He wanted to say thank You to God for healing him, and sorry for the many things he had done wrong when he was young.

Who came looking for the man? **Read John 5:14.**

Yes, Jesus! He walked right up to the man. The healed man’s eyes lit up, as he recognized who was standing right in front of him. It was the same caring, kind face that had asked him if he wanted to be healed. He must have been so excited to thank Jesus properly!

Then, with a serious voice, Jesus said, “See, you have been made well. Sin no more.”(John 5:14).

Jesus was worried he might get stuck making the same mistakes that he had made many years before. Those choices had ended up making him sick. Jesus didn’t want him to be sick, hurt, or unhappy. Jesus loved that man so much. What a wonderful Savior and Friend.

Just like that man was stuck by the pool, we are stuck in sin. Sin is like a sticky cobweb of mistakes that tangles us up. We can’t get rid of sin on our own. The Savior Jesus is our only Friend who can untangle us. Without Him we are helpless.

Can you say your memory verse? Thank You, Jesus, for forgiving and saving us.
