**Focus:** Like a sheep who watches and follows its shepherd, we are safe when we keep our eyes on Jesus.

Have you ever watched a flock of sheep? What did you notice about these woolly animals?

Sheep aren’t the smartest of animals. They get scared by small things. This might be because they can’t defend themselves very well, as they have no sharp teeth or claws, and they can’t run quickly. This means they’re easy prey for wolves or other predators.

Sheep can be calm inside a fence or sheep pen, but if they’re out in a field, they very easily get lost. They can also get stuck or even tip over onto their back without being able to get up. This is called being “cast down.” When this happens, the shepherd gently helps it stand again. For these reasons, sheep need a shepherd to watch over them.

Sheep don’t have good eyesight—but they have a strong sense of hearing. Poor vision makes them nervous in new places, but their hearing helps them stay calm when they hear the shepherd. Sheep can recognize their shepherd’s voice from other voices, so when a shepherd calls, the sheep usually come.

Sheep like to stay with the flock and feel safer when they’re not alone. They also naturally like to follow someone or something. They learn to follow the shepherd closely. When the shepherd is nearby, the sheep feel safe and calm. Often they’ll lie down around the shepherd and rest when the shepherd is nearby.

Sheep panic easily without their shepherd. If a sheep is left alone or gets separated, it can quickly become frightened or frozen with fear. But when the shepherd is near, sheep feel safe and calm.

Sheep also don’t know how to find good pasture or clean water without the shepherd’s help.

- **What does John 10:27 and Isaiah 53:6 tell us about ourselves?**

Sheep who stay near the shepherd are the safest, happiest sheep. They don’t need to worry, because the shepherd is always watching out for them. This is just like us and Jesus—when we keep our eyes and hearts on Him, we don’t have to be afraid. He’ll lead us and help us in any situation.
