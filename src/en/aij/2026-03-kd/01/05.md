Babies are such amazing miracles from God. Mary and Elizabeth’s babies were miracles too—extra-special miracles. Especially the baby growing inside of Mary. This tiny baby was the Son of God, the Creator of the universe, and the Savior of the world. Wow! What a miracle.

After a nice long visit with Elizabeth, Mary returned home to Nazareth. Soon Elizabeth gave birth to her little miracle boy. Their friends and family were so happy. They wanted to call him Zacharias, just like his daddy.

“No, he shall be called John,” Elizabeth said (Luke 1:60).

Their friends disagreed. “But no one in your family has this name!” (Luke 1:61, ICB). They asked Zacharias. What would he like to call his son?

What did Zacharias do? **Read Luke 1:63.**

Suddenly Zacharias could talk again! After months of being silent, Zacharias’ voice worked, “and he spoke, praising God” (Luke 1: 64).

Looking down at his precious son, Zacharias said, “Now you, child, . . . will go first before the Lord to prepare the people for his coming. You will make his people know that they will be saved” (Luke 1:76, 77).

What an important job John would have.
