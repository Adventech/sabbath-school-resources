A World of Sin

The warm flames flickered in the darkness. Adam’s big family huddled close to listen to Adam talk. Both Adam and Eve were now really old, and their family was really big. They were old enough to have great-great-great-great-great-grandchildren!

Adam was the best storyteller. He told his big family about happy times in the beautiful Garden of Eden, and the sad day they disobeyed God and ate the fruit. He reminded them to always trust and obey God.

Adam and Eve’s son Seth also talked to the people. He and his children loved God too. They were known as “the sons of God” (Genesis 6:2). They lived simple lives in tents in the country and were God’s faithful followers.

But not everyone loved and followed God’s ways. When angry Cain had been sent far away, he had had children too.His family built big cities and became very wicked and selfish. They stopped following God and instead did things their own way.

One day the “sons of God” began mixing with Cain’s family in the cities. They began marrying each other. The “sons of God” stopped living God’s way.

Sin had now spread everywhere! Every little thought, every single thing that people did, was always evil. What a horrible place the world had become. How did this make God feel? Read Genesis 6:6.
