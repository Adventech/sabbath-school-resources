**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He wants to teach me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Light a **candle** and place a **bowl** over it to illustrate Jesus’ story about light.
- Show several different kinds of **seeds**—mustard, plus other, larger seeds, such as cucumber, pumpkin, and avocado.
- Provide **figs** (or a local fruit) for children to taste, if appropriate.
- **Tools:** use a **spray bottle** of **water** for the storm.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 11 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Do you like stories? Jesus was the best storyteller. He told stories to teach people. Everyone loved to listen and learn from Jesus. We can too!
- One day Jesus told a story about a little lamp ^[(show light)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). At night lots of little lamps made their towns shine brightly.
- “People don’t hide a light under a bowl” (Matthew 5:15, ICB). ^[(Place bowl over light.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus wants us to be like a shining light and show His goodness.
- Jesus told a story about a tiny seed. “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed” (Matthew 13:31, ICB). ^[(Show seed.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) This tiny seed grows to be a big tree—what a miracle! Jesus can work a miracle in our hearts too, just as He does in that seed.
- Jesus told a funny story about fruit ^[(show fruit)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). “Each tree is known by its fruit. People don’t gather figs from thornbushes” (Luke 6:44, ICB). The only thing to find in a thornbush are prickles. Ouch!
- We are like trees. We can have good fruit ^[(good thoughts and actions)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) or thorns ^[(hurtful actions)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). With the Holy Spirit in our hearts, we can be loving and kind.
- Jesus told another story about two builders ^[(show tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). One man built a house on a solid rock. _Rumble, crash, splash._ A storm hit. The strong house stood firm.
- Another man built his house on a sandy beach ^[(show tools)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Rumble, crash, splash. A storm hit. The house fell down. **_CRASH!_**

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Do you like stories? Jesus was the best storyteller ever! He told stories to teach people. He talked about birds, colorful flowers, and fields of wheat. Because Jesus was outside when He told many of these stories, the boys and girls could look and see these things around them as they quietly listened. He told stories about naughty sheep, silly builders, and busy farmers. His stories were funny, exciting, and wonderful.

Everyone—both young and old—loved to listen and learn from Jesus. We can learn from Him too!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day Jesus told a story about a little lamp ^[(show light)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). At night every family used small pottery lamps to light their home. When everyone had their little lamps lit, it made their whole town shine brightly.

“People don’t hide a light under a bowl,” Jesus said (Matthew 5:15, ICB). ^[(Place bowl over the light.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the people heard this, they smiled. Some chuckled. What a silly idea! No one would ever light a lamp and hide it under a bowl. Lights were for shining, not hiding.

Jesus told them to each be like a little lamp and shine brightly. Jesus wants us to speak and do things that show His goodness. When we do this, we are like a bright light for others to see.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Another day Jesus talked about a tiny little seed ^[(hold up seed)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). “The kingdom of heaven is like a mustard seed,” He said (Matthew 13:31). That seed “is the least of all the seeds; but when it is grown it . . . becomes a tree” (Matthew 13:32).

A big tree from a tiny seed—what a miracle! Jesus wanted them to know that He could work a miracle in their hearts too, just as He could that seed. All they needed was a little bit of faith in Him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then Jesus told another story—a funny story about fruit ^[(show fruit)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He said, “Each tree is known by its fruit. People don’t gather figs from thornbushes” (Luke 6:44, ICB). Of course not! What a funny thought. The only thing to find in a thornbush are prickles. Ouch!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus explained that we are like trees. We can have good fruit, which is good, kind words and actions; or we can have thorns, which are mean, hurtful words or actions. When the Holy Spirit lives in our heart, He will help us to be loving and kind like a tree with good fruit, and to not have thorns.

Jesus had another story to tell. _Dig, dig, dig!_ A builder was building a house. His shovel dug deep into the dirt. Thud. The man reached a hard, strong rock. _Oh, good!_ Now he would build his house on this rock. First the man built a solid floor. Then he built tall walls. And finally, on went the roof. Done!

_Rumble, crash, splash!_ A big storm swept in. The rain came down, and the floodwaters came up higher and higher.

^[(Spray the house with water and blow it with the hair dryer.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But the man was safe in his strong house, for he had built it on solid rock.

Another man was building a house. Bang, bang, bang. Up went the tall walls and on went the roof. This builder didn’t care about digging down to strong rocks. He didn’t bother to build a solid floor. He chose some sandy land, then quickly built his house. Done!

_Rumble, crash, splash!_ A storm was coming! Down came the rain and up came the flood. _**CRASH!**_ The whole house fell to the ground!

Jesus told people that our lives are like the house, and God is like the rock. When we listen to God’s words from the Bible and obey them, we are like the man who built on the rock. When the storm comes, and when we have problems, we will stay strong, because we’re close to Jesus and we obey His word.

What amazing stories!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus also taught the people how to pray. Let’s sing it together now. ^[(Sing together “The Lord’s Prayer.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) You might also want to read Jesus’ prayer with your family later today ^[(hold up Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). We can always learn from Jesus. He is our best teacher.
