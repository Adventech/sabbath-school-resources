Abram and Lot had a big problem. There were too many sheep, too many cows, and too many fights on their farm! How could they fix this?

Abram thought about his problem. He prayed to God for wisdom. He wanted everyone to be happy and at peace.

Finally Abram said to Lot, “There should be no arguing between you and me. Your herders and mine should not argue either” (Genesis 13:8, ICB).

Abram pointed across the land. What did Abram say they should do? **Read Genesis 13:9.**

How kind of Abram! God had given all this land to him—but Abram was choosing to share his land with Lot. Not only that, but he was letting Lot choose the land he wanted. Abram would take whatever was left.

The Bible says, “Be humble and give more honor to others than to yourselves. Do not be interested only in your own life, but be interested in the lives of others” (Philippians 2:3, 4, ICB). And that is exactly what Abram did. He chose God’s way and honored Lot more than himself.

It’s hard to be generous. Without God’s help, it’s easy to be selfish and greedy: “I want the biggest; I want the best; I want to go first.” With God’s love in our hearts, we can be like Abram. We can give God our best and put others first.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- What does this part of the story tell you about Abram?
- How can you show honor to your brothers, sisters, and other family members this week?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you to put others first, especially when it’s hard.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
