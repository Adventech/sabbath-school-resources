A Promised Gift

Mary’s pregnant belly grew bigger and bigger. And so did her excitement. God’s promised Savior God was growing inside her, and He would soon be born!

The promise of a Savior had been made long ago to Adam and Eve in the Garden of Eden after they had sinned. God’s promise filled them with hope.

Then faithful followers of God such as Enoch, Noah, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, Joseph, and Moses had also looked forward to the arrival of the promised Savior. They too had believed, without ever seeing God’s promise fulfilled—they had faith (Hebrews 11).

Later Jesus would tell a doubting disciple how much He appreciates the faith of those who didn’t get to see Him in the flesh. He said, “Because you have seen Me, you have believed. Blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed” (John 20:29).

Mary must have patted her belly in wonder to think that she would see Him soon! She treasured the words the angel had spoken to her: the “Holy One who is to be born will be called the Son of God” (Luke 1:35). The promised gift would soon arrive, and it would be the very best gift of all. Why was God giving this gift? **Read John 3:16** (your memory verse).

The people in Mary’s village, across Judea, and all around the world didn’t know that God’s gift of love was about to arrive. They didn’t know that the tiny baby quietly growing inside Mary was God, God the Son, and that He was about to be born as a human being! Hardly anyone knew what was about to happen; but in time, God would make sure that everyone would have the opportunity to accept and appreciate His precious gift. Oh, how He hoped that everyone would.
