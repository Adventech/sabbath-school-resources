God called Noah to preach and build. Therefore, the people witnessed Noah’s faith not just by what he said, but by what he did. “While Noah was giving his warning message to the world, his works testified of his sincerity. It was thus that his faith was perfected and made evident. He gave the world an example of believing just what God says. Every blow struck upon the ark was a witness to the people” _(Ellen G. White, Patriarchs and Prophets, p. 95)_.

As the saying goes, sometimes our actions speak louder than our words. Especially when it comes to our children. Developmental psychologists all agree that children learn the most by imitating the people around them. This is because of little cells in the brain called mirror neurons. Every time your child watches someone doing an action, their mirror neurons fire up, making the brain think they are doing the exact same thing. Sometimes they are almost like a confronting mirror to our own faults and weaknesses!

So when it comes to our faith, do our works testify to our sincerity?

If our desire is for our children to have a committed faith in Jesus, then we must show them what that looks like in our own life. Do they see us spending time with Jesus every morning? Do they watch us turn to prayer in moments of stress? Do they see us serving others cheerfully?

May we take this moment for some self-reflection. Ask the Lord to reveal any habits or areas in your life that do not influence your child in a positive way. Then prayerfully ask for His help to change.
