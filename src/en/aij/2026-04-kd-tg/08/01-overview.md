{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus demonstrated that the Sabbath is a happy day for showing God’s love to others. On three different Sabbaths Jesus healed people who were suffering. While teaching in the synagogue, He healed a man with a withered hand. On another occasion He healed a woman who had been bent over for 18 years. During a feast on another Sabbath day, He healed a man who was sick. Each time the Jewish leaders were unhappy because they thought Jesus had broken the Sabbath rules, but Jesus showed that “the Sabbath was made for man, and not man for the Sabbath” (Mark 2:27), and that it was a blessing to do good on the Sabbath.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that the Sabbath is a special gift.
- **Feel** a desire to notice others’ needs on the Sabbath.
- **Respond** by finding ways to make the Sabbath a delight.
