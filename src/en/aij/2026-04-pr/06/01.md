^[Word of the Week—Persistent:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#a52c2e", "size": "xl"}}})\
Continuing to do, or trying to do, something in a determined way. A persistent person doesn’t give up or stop trying even when things are difficult, or if they are pressured to stop. God is persistent in His love for us—He never stops loving us.

Finding Hope

Wearily she stopped beside the busy road and caught her breath. She listened to the crowd of people as they hurried by. His name was on everyone’s lips: Jesus. Mothers and aunties whispered it with awe. Boys and girls called it out joyfully, and fathers and uncles spoke it with excitement: Jesus. Everyone was going to see Jesus!

Excitement surged through her veins as her eyes followed the crowd. She was going to see Jesus too. She had heard how He healed sick children and men who couldn’t walk. How He comforted women who had lost their husbands and encouraged mothers. Surely He can help a poor sick woman like me, she thought.

For 12 long years she had suffered from constant bleeding. She had seen every doctor and tried every treatment. The Bible tells us she “suffered many things from many physicians [doctors]” (Mark 5:26). Even though it was painful and expensive, she’d persisted in trying to get better. But after 12 long years her determination wasn’t enough. “She had spent all that she had and was no better, but rather grew worse” (Mark 5:26).

She had given up hoping for a cure long ago. But now, hearing about Jesus, she hoped for a miracle. What had Jesus come to earth to do? **Read John 10:10.**

She slowly set off down the road. She was determined to see Jesus. It didn’t matter how weak she felt—she would get herself to Him somehow. She believed that He could make her well.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Do you know someone who has been unwell or struggling with their health for a long time? How could you encourage them this week?
- When the sick woman heard about Jesus, how might she have felt? **Read Romans 15:13.**

^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Fold a long strip of paper to create three squares. In one square, write the names of people you know who are sick. In the next square, write medical workers you can pray for, such as doctors and caregivers. In the final square, write Jesus’ promise: “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you” (John 14:27). Tape the two ends of the strip together to create a triangular shape that will stand up to display what you have written.

^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Turn the triangle as you pray for the people on the first two squares. Claim the promise on the third square for them too. Use your standing triangle as a reminder for you to pray for others every day this week.
