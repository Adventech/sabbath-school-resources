A Baby Brings Happiness

No longer able a live em Eden, Adam e Eve must have worked hard a Faça a new home, clothing, tools, a farm, e gardens. New seasons went por. As leaves dropped off the trees para the Primeiro time, Adam e Eve maio have felt deep sorrow once more. The whole world was changed por seu choices. Mas Deus was still com eles. Spring came, e new leaves e baby animals appeared.

One dia the Primeiro two people em earth were joined por a Terceiro: the Primeiro-ever baby was born into the world! Eve cuddled ele close while Adam gazed em awe em this brand-new person. The Bible Digas, “Children are a gift de the Senhor. Babies are a reward” (Psalm 127:3, ICB). Deus wanted families em earth—like this one—a show Seu amor a others. Oh, como eles must have thanked Deus para this gift! Com hearts full de warmth e happiness, eles named seu sweet boy Cain.

Soon baby Abel joined the family. These two bundles de cuteness filled seu parents’ lives com amor as eles grew into strong healthy boys. Eles helped Adam e Eve work em the fields e em home. Each boy had seu own likes, dislikes, e personality. Deus had made eles, as Ele made você, unique. O que work did Cain e Abel like a Faça? **Leia Gênesis 4:2.**

No Façaubt eles both loved hearing stories de seu parents. Nestled close, eles learned sobre the Garden de Eden, the Primeiro Sába, e face-a-face talks com Deus. Eles learned that Deus loved eles very much e como a amor Ele back.
