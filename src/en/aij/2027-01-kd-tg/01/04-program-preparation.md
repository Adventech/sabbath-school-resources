**Opening Activities**

Source items for **“By the River Reeds”**: A **large container** filled with **water**, with **toy boats**, **people**, and **animals**. Include **river “reeds”** (bulrushes) made from cut sponges or plants (fake or real).

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: A “baby” made from **cloth or cotton yarn**. Simply cut a circle of fabric and pinch the middle, then tie some **yarn** around this pinched section to make it the head. Alternatively, use a peg person or baby made from a cotton spool. Source a **seedpod or basket** to hide it in.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: **blue fabric or towel, plastic or potted plant, basket, baby doll, small blanket, paper crown.**

Source **peg people** or a **felt set**, and **^[week 1 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: the cloth **baby** doll from the **Secret Surprise Box**, a **heart** made from cardboard, and a simple **paper crown**.

**Craft Materials**

Print one **^[week 1 craft template]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child. Source scissors, paper plates, glue, blue cellophane/tissue paper.

Cut 8-1/3 inch x 1/2 inch (21 x 1.5 cm) strips from two different colored pieces of **paper** (six strips of each color per child). Assemble other supplies: **staplers, small wooden spoons or peg dolls, pieces of cloth, tape, markers**.
