**WELCOME AND SENSORY TIME** (5 TO 8 MINUTES)

- Warmly welcome each individual child and parent/caregiver.
- Guide children to the sensory play areas.
- Acknowledge and intentionally connect with parents/caregivers.
- Learn all names of family members.
- Foster a warm, welcoming atmosphere (background music).
- Invite parents/caregivers to share prayer needs and pray together, for each other, and for the children.

**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the songs to keep the children’s attention.

**These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: I can trust God.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- **^[“Good Morning to You.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Welcome children and parents by smiling and shaking hands. Today’s story is a very exciting one! God asked Noah to build a big boat, an ark, for all the animals and the people who loved God to go inside.

- **^[“Noah Took a Hammer.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Use pretend hammers and saws to help cut down trees and build the ark. Show the children an ark (on the mural or an “ark” box you have created).

Pretend to put seeds into “soil.” Invite children to pour empty water cans over the garden, then take off the cover. Invite the children to “pick” the carrots. They may also “pick” paper apples from a plant/tree.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-bg-tg/assets/05-better-together.mp3] “Better Together” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation® of Seventh-day Adventists."}

Invite the children to “walk” these animals into your ark. God would keep the animals safe. They had food and Noah’s family to care for them. Noah asked everyone to come into the ark, but only Noah’s family came.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-bg-tg/assets/06-into-noahs-ark.mp3] “Into Noah’s Ark” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2024 General Conference Corporation® of Seventh-day Adventists."}

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-bg-tg/assets/07-the-big-door.mp3] “The Big Door” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation® of Seventh-day Adventists."}

**WEEK 1: GOD’S BIG LOVE**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag:** Flower, picture of Noah

Say: **In the beginning God made the world, and it was beautiful! He made the sparkling rivers and dolphins that jump. He made hopping kangaroos and soaring eagles** (do action for each thing God made).

Ask: **What is your favorite animal that God made?**

Say: **In those days the people chose to follow God. They thanked Him for His big love when they looked at the colorful flowers** (hold out flower). **They used their hands to be kind to others** (hold out hands). **They sang happy songs to God. They spent time with Him and talked to Him as their best friend.**

Say: **But later the people forgot about God. They forgot to thank Him for His beautiful creation. They forgot to be kind and gentle, choosing unloving and selfish ways instead. They forgot to look after the world He gave them. They forgot to spend time with God.**

Open your Bible, point, and say: **The Bible tells us there was one special man who remembered God.**

Show children the picture of Noah. **Noah loved God. He talked to Him every day. He was kind and loving. He looked after God’s wonderful creation. God was so happy with Noah. He had a special plan for Him!**

Say together: **I can remember God and spend time with Him!**
