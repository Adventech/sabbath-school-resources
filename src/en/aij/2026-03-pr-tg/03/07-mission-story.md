**Focus:** God is worthy of our worship, and He cares for us.

**Location:** Help a child locate **India** on your **globe** or **world map**.

It was Christmas Day in India. Mother stopped her work to listen closely to the beautiful singing drifting through her window. It seemed to fill her home with joy!

She had been so sad and worried lately. Her 4-year-old son Anurag couldn’t walk. He couldn’t stand. He couldn’t even sit. She had taken him to many doctors and hospitals, but no one could help him.

Mother peered out the window, looking for the source of this sweet sound. Sitting on the grass beside the Ganges River were a group of Christians, happily singing to Jesus. Mother loved the music. She’d never heard anything like it. It made her heart feel hopeful.

Mother wasn’t a Christian. She wondered if the Christians might be able to help her son. She scooped him up in her arms and carried him outside.

“Please pray for my son,” Mother asked the group. “Pray that he will be able to sit, stand, and walk.”

Sumanta, a Seventh-day Adventist missionary in his own country, nodded with a big smile. He had gathered people by the river to worship Jesus on Christmas Day.

“I am nothing. God is everything,” he told Mother. “If you know Jesus Christ, your faith will increase, and your faith will save your son.”

Mother realized she needed to have faith in this God. She had worshipped many false gods during her lifetime, but she had never worshipped Jesus.

Sumanta prayed for little Anurag. He also invited Mother to join a Bible study group. Three months passed. Mother faithfully studied the Bible every day—she was looking for Jesus. Her new friends in the Bible study group prayed for Anurag.

When six months passed, Mother had exciting news to share. “Anurag can sit up on his own!” Her smile was as bright as the sun. God was answering their prayers.

Mother’s faith grew. She decided to give her heart to Jesus and be baptized. There was only one God whom she wanted to worship now: Jesus, the God who was helping her son. He was worthy of her worship.

After Mother was baptized. Anurag kept gaining strength. Now this little boy can sit, stand, and walk! Mother is so happy. She is glad to worship her loving, saving Jesus. He has been so kind to her. “This is one of the greatest miracles,” she said.

Discuss:

- **How did God show Mother that He was the one true God, worthy of her worship?**
