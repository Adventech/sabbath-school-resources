**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut out the eyeholes.

**Instructions:**

1\. Color in the craft template and cut out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/10/10b.png)

2\. Tape a large craft stick to the back of the eye strip.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/10/10c.png)

3\. Place the eye strip behind the face, in between the two pages. Tape the edges of the pages together, being sure to leave the space between the black marks without tape. This will ensure the eyes can slide down.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/10/10d.png)

4\. Retell the story, using your craft as a prop to show how Bartimaeus was blind, then he could see!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/10/10e.png)
