The healed man raced home to find his family to tell them the good news. There were shouts of joy and happy hugs. The next-door neighbors heard the excitement and came outside to see what was going on. They were stunned! “They asked, ‘What happened? How did you get your sight?’ ” (John 9:10, ICB).

What was his reply? **Read John 9:11.**

“Where is He [Jesus]?” his neighbors asked (John 9:12).

Suddenly the man’s face became puzzled. He realized he didn’t know where Jesus was or what Jesus even _looked_ like! Oh no! How could he find Him to thank Him?

But not everyone was happy. When the Pharisees heard that Jesus had healed the man on Sabbath, they were angry. They didn’t believe Jesus was anyone special. And they didn’t believe Jesus should break their rules and help others on the Sabbath.

It was like they had their eyes scrunched up tightly. They didn’t want to see or know the truth about Jesus.

With frowning faces, the Pharisees called the healed man a liar, and treated him unkindly. Then they threw him out of the temple.

Like a loving friend, Jesus went and found the man. Jesus always draws “near to those who have a broken heart” (Psalm 34:18). Jesus leaned in close and asked the man a question. “Do you believe in the Son of God?” (John 9:35).

What did the man say? **Read John 9:36.**

Jesus said, “It is He who is talking with you” (John 9:37).

The man’s new eyes opened wide! He cried out, “Yes, Lord, I believe!” (v. 38). Then he bowed and worshiped Jesus.

The Pharisees didn’t want to know who Jesus was. But this man did! Now he was truly happy. He could see with his eyes. He could know Jesus as his Friend and God.
