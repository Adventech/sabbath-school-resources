The centurion looked up at the kind face of Jesus. Although he had first sent his friends to ask Jesus to heal his servant, now he stood before Jesus himself.

With a deep breath he said, “All you need to do is command that my servant be healed and he will be healed. I myself am a man under the authority of other men. And I have soldiers under my command. I tell one soldier ‘Go,’ and he goes. I tell another soldier, ‘Come,’ and he comes. I say to my servant, ‘Do this,’ and my servant obeys me” (Matthew 8:9, ICB).

He was saying that he understood that Jesus is powerful­—He can do anything. The centurion knew Jesus was the head of God’s heavenly army and could give orders as well. He knew with one word Jesus could make his sick servant better.

Healed with one word? This seemed impossible! But what does the Bible say God can do? **Read Mark 10:27.**

The centurion’s faith that Jesus could do the impossible, stunned Jesus. He was “amazed.” Turning to the crowd Jesus said, “Assuredly, I say to you, I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel” (Luke 7:9).

Jesus’ heart filled with joy to see how much trust and confidence the Roman soldier had in Him. He had never seen anyone believe so much in His power!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think Jesus was so amazed by the centurion?
- What do you trust Jesus to do for you?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Play a game of “centurion says” with your family or friends. One person is the centurion. They call out actions for the others to follow. Only do the actions if the “centurion” has said, “Centurion says . . .” first. For example, “Centurion says, clap your hands.” Listen closely. If they just say, “Clap your hands” without saying, “Centurion says,” don’t clap! Finish by discussing what happens when “Jesus says” something.
