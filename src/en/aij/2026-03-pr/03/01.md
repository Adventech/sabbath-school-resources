Heaven Rejoices

The Savior had been born! All of heaven rejoiced at His birth. A huge crowd of angels gathered above the hills of Bethlehem, eagerly waiting for the signal to share the good news with the world. Who would rejoice with them?

As Baby Jesus, the Son of God, breathed His first breath on earth, the people of Bethlehem slept on. What a day it had been! More travelers arrived for the Roman census, and every home was filled to the rafters with friends, family, and strangers alike.

God had chosen Bethlehem, naming this town in the Bible scrolls as the Savior’s place of birth. But some people in Bethlehem had been too busy to search the scrolls. They had been too busy chasing riches, too busy praying prayers they didn’t mean, and too busy trying to look holy. They had been too busy for God. And now God’s Son had arrived, while they slept on.

Would anyone notice? Would anyone care?

Down in the fields a group of shepherds was awake. These humble men who cared for their sheep by day and night were devoted to God. As they sat under the stars, protecting their sheep, they talked about the promised Savior and prayed for His coming. Suddenly something amazing happened! Read about it in Luke 2:9-14.

Praise God, those angels had found some devoted men who would notice, listen, and care! With great joy the angels lit up the night sky above the startled shepherds, singing and praising God for the newborn Savior of the world. The angels sang, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, goodwill toward men!” (Luke 2:14).
