**What you need:**

Blue, orange, yellow and brown paper, scissors, glue, fabric, craft glue, cotton swab, white paint.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7a.jpg)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Fold an orange sheet of paper in half, and cut with a wavy line to make sand.
- Cut mountain shapes out of brown paper.
- Cut tent triangles out of fabric. Make a small cut in the bottom of each triangle.
- Cut sun circles out of yellow paper.

**Create it together:**

1\. Glue mountain shapes along the middle of a blue sheet of paper (landscape).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7b.jpg)

2\. Glue the orange sand below the mountains and the yellow sun above.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7c.jpg)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7d.jpg)

3\. Use craft glue to stick the fabric tents on the orange sand.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7e.jpg)

4\. Dip a cotton swab into white paint and dot the orange sand with manna. Show your picture to someone and tell them how God provided food in the desert!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/7f.jpg)
