---
title: Birthday Thank Offerings
---

Thousands of dollars are given each quarter as Birthday Thank Offerings to missions.

Notice the term for this offering: the Birthday-Thank Offering. We give a gratitude offering in recognition of God’s blessings for another year of life. But sometimes there are other occasions for special thanksgiving that one desires to acknowl edge by a special gift to the Lord—preservation of life in an accident, recovery from a severe illness, the conversion of a soul for whom one has been laboring, or other blessings. All money coming into this fund is counted on the quarterly goal and the per capita giving.

The Birthday Thank Offering should never substitute for regular Sabbath School contribution, but should be an additional gift.
