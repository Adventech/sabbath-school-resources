Com a sad mas gentle voice Deus told Adam e Eve that life would change. Deus had wanted a guarde eles de experiencing pain e death, mas eles had chosen a disobey Ele e eat de the tree de the knowledge de bom e evil. Now Adam would have a work hard a grow food. Eve would feel pain quando she had children.

Then Deus told eles eles would have a leave seu lindo garden home. Once again, seu hearts filled com sorrow . . . leave seu home?

Yes, para Deus always means o que Ele Digas. Então com one long last look em todos the beauty Deus had wanted eles a enjoy forever, eles left Eden. Never a return.

Por que did Deus Conte eles eles would have a leave the Garden de Eden? **Leia Gênesis 3:22, 23.**

Deus had given eles the tree de life então eles would live forever. Mas living forever com sin would be horrible! Deus had a guarde Seu palavra. Ele put Anjos com flaming swords em the gates a the garden a guard the tree de life.

Adam e Eve now knew bom e evil, e isso was no fun em todos. The one bom thing em the middle de todos the sorrow was that Deus would never leave eles, just as Ele won’t ever leave nos. Eles couldn’t see Ele face-a-face anymore, mas Ele would always be there. Para Ele Digas em Seu Palavra, “Eu am com você always” (Mateus 28:20).
