**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Distribute a **magnifying glass** to each child and give them several minutes to explore the details in the nature objects on your **Nature Table**. Encourage them to notice the closeup texture, color, shape, patterns, etc. in each natural object.

**^[VIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Then compare these natural objects with human-made materials by looking at the **Nature Closeup Pictures**. Talk about the beautiful colors and detailed patterns on the **peacock feathers** when they are magnified. Show a piece of smooth **paper** and have children feel it before showing the picture of magnified paper.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **What do you think the paper will look like when it’s magnified?** (Accept all answers, then show the picture.)
- **When you look at what God has created and compare it to what people have created, what do you notice? (God’s creation is intricate, beautifully detailed, and patterned—even when highly magnified. Man’s creation is** random, disorderly.)
- **What did this comparing activity tell you about God?** (He is a God of order, beauty, intricate design, and careful thought.)

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard/poster paper: **We are God’s very special creation, made in His image.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story** with the animal card activity illustration and discussion.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[CREATE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** To recognize our limitations when compared with God’s creative power, give each child a piece of **modeling clay** or **play-dough**. Give them just a minute to sculpt a person and try to bring it to life.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Why couldn’t you bring your person to life?** (We can’t create life, only God can!)
- **What does the Bible say about how we are made? Read Psalm 139:14.** (We are “fearfully and wonderfully made.”)
- **What does God think of us? Read the first half of Isaiah 43:4.** (Precious, honored, loved.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Each of us are a “fearfully and wonderfully made” miracle, for only God can create a living, breathing person. We are all precious to Him.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 2: Adam and Eve** to place in their container. Discuss the picture
and recite the memory verse together. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Genesis 1:27.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/2-genesis-1-27.mp3] “Genesis 1:27” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singers","value":"Yelena Jovinov, David Williams"},{"key":"Cello","value":"Jared Chung"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"Katie Strachan"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Invite the children to discuss the following questions in pairs:

- **What does it mean to be “made in God’s image”?**
- **How do you feel, knowing you are made in God’s image?**

If time allows, have the children say the **memory verse** in pairs, saying one word each. Say it again faster.

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the **Nature Lesson**, “Eye See,” and engage the children in the discussion questions.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child a small **sticky note** and have them write “Thank You” on it. Invite them to stick it beside something on the Creation poster that they would like to thank God for. Alternatively, they could stick it beside their favorite animal card.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Distribute a **Creation Bookmark** craft outline to each child and have them make the bookmark.

While children complete their craft, play the song **“When God Speaks.”** Invite the children to sing along.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/when-god-speaks.mp3] “When God Speaks” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Braedan Entermann"},{"key":"Singers","value":"Alise Entermann, Braedan Entermann"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Braedan Entermann"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"Braedan Entermann"},{"key":"Producer","value":"Rhys Zacher"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2024 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Alternatively have the children use **play-dough** or **modeling clay** to **sculpt** something (object or scene) to represent each of the six days of Creation.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Bring children back together to sing the closing song, **“God Bless You.”**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/god-bless-you.mp3] “God Bless You” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singers","value":"David Williams, TVAC Choir"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Violin","value":"Tahlia Atcheson"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Bass","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Clicks","value":"David Williams"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}}) Share God’s special gift of the Sabbath with someone new.** Spend a moment talking and praying together about whom the children could invite to Sabbath School, on a Sabbath walk in nature, or to family worship next Sabbath. The gift of Sabbath shouldn’t be kept to ourselves!

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to form a **circle** and thank God for the part of God’s creation they stuck their sticky note beside. As you pray the final prayer, ask God to be with the children and help them always remember that they have been created in God’s image and that He loves them very much.
