Joseph was different from his brothers. He had a special friendship with God that made him kind, happy, and caring of others. He loved to choose God’s way of doing things. He was respectful and thoughtful with his words.

Make a coat of many colors, then put it on, and act as Joseph would.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F5C867", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
^[To do this, you’ll need,]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})\
• a brown paper grocery bag\
• scissors\
• glue\
• colored paper strips

1. Cut out the bottom of the bag. On one of the wider sides, cut from the top to bottom to make the front opening. Cut a circle on each of the shorter sides for armholes.
2. Provide glue and colored strips of paper and invite your child to cover the surface with the colors.
3. Leave it to dry while you read the story of Joseph from your favorite lesson, the Bible, or a Bible story book.
4. Then invite your child to put on the coat and respond to questions or situations politely and with kindness, just as Joseph would have done.

You may like to use such questions/situation prompts as:

- What do you think of your coat, Joseph?
- Mother Leah is carrying a big pot of water. What will you do, Joseph?
- Supper is now finished. What will you do, Joseph?
- There are three little lambs without a mommy. They are thirsty and bleating. What can you do, Joseph?
- Benjamin has tripped on a stone and fallen down. How will you help, Joseph?
