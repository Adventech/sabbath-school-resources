The Israelites listened quietly as God spoke from the top of Mount Sinai. God had told them the first three rules about loving Him with all their hearts. Then God gave commandment number four. What did He say? **Read Exodus 20:8-11.**

Every week, God asks you to keep the Sabbath holy. He invites you to a very special celebration. Below is what the invitation might look like if God wrote it on paper for you.

God made you, and He made the seventh-day Sabbath as a special time to be with you. Will you come to His Sabbath celebration this week? Will you choose to give Him your time and love? The Bible says, “You shall love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your strength” (Deuteronomy 6:5).
