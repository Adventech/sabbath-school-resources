Kermyt and his classmates wanted to do something to help others. They decided to feed homeless people in their town in Puerto Rico. “I’ll bring rice and beans,” said one student. Others offered to bring salad and juice. Arriving in the middle of town, the friends set up a table with food platters. Someone had brought 150 paper plates to serve the food on. Kermyt realized they had only enough food to feed about 50 people. They asked God to bless their meal.

The homeless people started lining up. Kermyt’s friends began spooning out large portions of food onto plates. Kermyt walked around inviting people to the meal.

Busily the classmates handed out plate after plate after plate. Lots of people came and took a full plate of rice, beans, and salad. Finally they scooped the last bit of food onto the very last plate.

Then they realized something amazing. They had had enough food to feed only 50 people, but somehow they had handed out 150 full plates! They had just fed 150 people with only a little food!

“We saw a miracle from God!” Kermyt said. “God multiplied the food.”

God provided miracle food that day, just as He had fed the hungry Israelites. Thank You, God, for always providing. “You open Your hand and satisfy the desire of every living thing” (Psalm 145:16).
