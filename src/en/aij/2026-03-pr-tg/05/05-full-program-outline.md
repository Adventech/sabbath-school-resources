**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them. Show several **baby photos** of Sabbath School leaders and see if the children can guess each one.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to work together to **draw a large picture of Jesus as a boy**. Offer some **books or the Sabbath School lesson** on which to base their drawing. What kind of clothes would He wear? What expression should be on His face? (This will be used during the rest of Sabbath School.)

**Large group option: While some children draw Jesus as a boy, you may like to invite others to draw** something they couldn’t do when they were younger, but something they can do now because they’ve learned it. It might be learning a musical instrument, a particular sport, or a hobby. Have children share their pictures and talk about how God helps us grow and change in all sorts of ways.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[BRAINSTORM:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Tape your picture of Jesus as a boy to the wall.

Ask:

- **What do we know about what Jesus was like as a boy?** Write responses around the drawn illustration. (If not mentioned, prompt some of the following by whispering an idea and inviting a child to mime it: loved to learn from **nature**, loved to **pray**, loved to read God’s words in the **Bible** scroll, loved to **depend on God** to obey and make the right choice.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** One thing we’ve written that Jesus loved to do is learn things about God from nature. Let’s do this, just like Jesus.

Show your **bag** with three things hiding inside. Ask: What are some things in nature that grow?

Pull out one item at a time and discuss how God grows each one.

- **What are the different ways in which we grow?** (Taller, stronger, smarter, more mature, closer to God, our faith grows. For example: “This seed can teach me that just as a seed grows into a tree, God can grow something great from me.”)

**^[BIG IDEA:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Just as Jesus did, we can learn from nature. Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my example as I grow.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Point to your note with the Boy Jesus and explain that He loved to read God’s words in the Bible. We can be like Jesus and learn from God’s Word too. We will pray and ask God to help us to learn something new from the Bible today. **Then creatively share the Bible story.**

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}}) What did our story tell us about how Jesus grew?** Read Luke 2:52 again.

**Teaching Point:** So Jesus grew taller, stronger, wiser, friendlier, and closer to God. Jesus is my example as I grow.

**^[MAKE CHOICES:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Point to your **note with the Boy Jesus illustration** and explain that He loved to depend on God to obey and make the right choices. Like Jesus, we can ask God to help us make good choices. Let’s pray before we make choices. Offer a prayer for wisdom, then **read the Situation Cards**. Decide on the best choice for each friend.

**Teaching Point:** Good choices can often be the hard choices. Like Jesus, we can depend on God to help us always make the right choice.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard: **Jesus is my example as I grow.**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Discuss:

- **How do we grow taller, stronger, wiser, and friendlier like Jesus?** (Pray; learn from God in the Bible and in nature; spend time with God.)

Ask for two volunteers to come to the front of the classroom. Place a small amount of **glitter** on the hand of one child and ask them to shake hands with the other child.

- **What happened to the glitter?** (It rubs off because of close contact.)
- **How is this like us and Jesus?** (When we come close to Jesus through His Word, prayer, and nature, we change to become a little more like Him.)

**Teaching Point:** We can follow the example of Jesus and spend time with God. He will grow good things in our lives and help us to make wise choices.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 31: Jesus in the Carpenter’s Shop** to place in their storage container along with their other collector cards.

**Just as Jesus did,** let’s share the words that we’ve memorized this week from the Bible. Our memory verse talks about growing two important things in our lives as we get older: grace and knowledge.

- **What is grace?** (Grace is showing love and kindness to someone when they do the wrong thing, or are unkind, or fail in some way.)
- **What is knowledge?** (Knowledge is what we know and understand about God, who He is, and what He’s like.) These are such special, important ways to grow!

Invite the children to squat down as low as they can. As they recite the **memory verse** they can “grow” taller and taller until their hands stretch up as high as they can go, and they’re on their tiptoes. Repeat several times. Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: **“2 Peter 3:18.”**

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“The Miracle of Music”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your simple **Scripture Scroll** craft sample. Explain that in Bible times they didn’t have books as we do today. The Bible was written down on scrolls instead. Jesus used scrolls like this when He was a boy. He learned many, many words from the Bible scroll from memory. Share the instructions for making the scroll and invite the children to write today’s **memory verse** on it. As children are doing this, invite them to share other verses from the Bible they have learned in the past (just as Jesus did), or play the **“Books of the Bible”** song and invite children to sing along. Or children can make a Daddy’s Helper craft.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** We can imagine that, as a boy, Jesus loved to sing.

- **Do you also like to sing?**

This week, **think about how you could use music** to help others grow closer to God. Share some Alive in Jesus music with a friend.

Encourage the children to keep collecting and creating items to contribute to your class gift to thank those who serve others in your community. What might they make this week that could be included? **Collect and create items for your gifts for community members.**

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together, **Jesus is my example as I grow.** We want to grow just as Jesus did.

Not only is He our example, but He is with us and will help us if we ask Him.
