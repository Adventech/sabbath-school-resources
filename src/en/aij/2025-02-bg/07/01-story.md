The\
^[Wedding]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#f0669c", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})\
Miracle 
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/07/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
It was a happy day! Jesus and His friends had come to a party—a wedding party. The people smiled and waved as they arrived (wave). Hello, Jesus! The tables were full of good foods. The figs, cheese, and olives smelled good. Mmm, yum. There were cups full of purple grape juice. It tasted sweet. Mmm, yum. The smell of beautiful flowers filled the air. The sounds of music and happy people filled the yard. Jesus’ mother was at the wedding too. Hello, Mary! (wave). But Mary wasn’t smiling. She had a problem. Mary hurried over to Jesus. She stood up tall on her tippy toes. Then she whispered her problem to Jesus. (Whisper) They have no more grape juice. Oh, no! There were lots of happy people at the wedding party. And lots of good food to eat. But they had run out of grape juice. And it was too late to buy anymore. What a big problem! 
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/07/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
Mary knew that Jesus would help. She called the servants. “Do whatever he tells you to do,” she told them (John 2:5, ICB). Jesus pointed at six big water jars (point to picture). He told the servants to fill them up with water. Water? Didn’t they need grape juice? The servants were confused. But they ran to obey. Glug, glug, glug went the water. They filled (count on fingers) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 jars—right to the very top! The servants carried the six full jars to Jesus. They put them down with a splish-splash. The jars were very full! Jesus told the servants to take the water from the jars and start filling up cups. The servants did just what Jesus said. They poured out the water . . . but it wasn’t water anymore! It was grape juice! Jesus had turned water into grape juice. It was a miracle! 
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/07/03.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
The servants carried the cups of grape juice out to the people. They smiled to see its deep red color. It smelled so good. Mmm, yum. Then they took a big sip. Mmm, yum! It was so sweet. It was the best grape juice they had ever tasted! The people were so happy. Jesus had cared about their problem. He had helped them. Jesus cares for me and my feelings. I can tell Him everything.\
(Say together) Thank You, Jesus, for caring about my problems.
