**Big Idea:**\

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well. Go in peace”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Luke 8:48).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Faith”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Faith means to trust and believe in someone or something. When we have faith, we are sure about something. Having faith in Jesus means to believe that He will do and say all that He has promised.

is based on Matthew 9:18–26; Mark 5:21–43; Luke 8:40–56; and _The Desire of Ages_, ch. 36.

No Hope

As she walked slowly out the door a tear slid down the sick woman’s cheek. She quickly wiped it away as she stared at the ground. The woman was in constant pain, and she felt as tired as an old great-grandma. The doctor had just told her that he had done all he could to help her. Nothing had worked.

For 12 years, she had been to one doctor after another, after another. She had tried many different treatments and yucky tasting medicines. But it always ended the same way. The doctors would shake their heads and send her on her way.

How much money had she spent? **Read Luke 8:43.**

She squeezed the empty money bag in her hand. What was she to do now? There was nothing left to try.

The woman walked slowly down the road. She sighed sadly. No one would visit her or touch her, because she was sick. She really needed help. And she really needed a friend.

What do you do when you’re feeling sick, sad, or lonely? It might help to hear kind words or be hugged by someone you love. It’s so good to know that people care for us. It’s even better to know that Jesus cares for us. The Bible says, “Give all your worries to him, because he cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7, ICB).

The woman didn’t know it yet, but she had a Friend who cared for her. She had a Friend who could help her. And He was coming her way.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Who might this special Friend be?
- You can give your worries to Jesus by praying to Him. What might you say?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Look for an opportunity this week to show someone who is feeling unwell or lonely that you care about them. Remind them that God cares about them too.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
