The soldier swung the prison door open with a creak and a clang.

Then the 10 brothers were led outside. Quickly, they were marched back to see the Governor. Their hearts pounded. What would happen to them now?

Dressed in royal Egyptian clothes, Governor Joseph said to them, “Do this thing, and I will let you live: If you are honest men, let one of your brothers stay here in prison. The rest of you go and carry grain back to feed your hungry families” (Genesis 42:18,19, ICB).

But Joseph wasn’t finished. He really wanted to see his younger brother Benjamin. Joseph told the brothers they could go home if they promised to bring back Benjamin.

The brothers didn’t like this idea one bit! They cared a lot for Benjamin and wanted to keep him safely at home with their father. But they had to agree. Brother Simeon would wait in prison until the other brothers returned with Benjamin.

Then their bags were filled with grain. But that’s not all! With whispering words, Joseph told his servants to hide money in the bags of grain. What a kind and generous surprise.

What did the brothers do next? **Read Genesis 42:26.**

As they travelled home the brothers had no idea of the treasure Joseph had hidden inside their bags of grain. God was looking after Joseph’s family, just as He looks after you. The Bible says, “And my God shall supply all your need” (Philippians 4:19).
