News had made its way from Egypt, across the many deserts, to the home of Jethro. And such amazing news it was! Jethro had heard about the plagues that had fallen on Egypt, and how God had miraculously delivered His people. Now he heard that the Israelites were camped close by.

His heart skipped with joy! He enjoyed having his daughter Zipporah and his grandsons living close by, but he knew how wonderful it would be for them to be reunited with Moses again. So he ran to share the good news with them. It was time for a family reunion!

Bags were quickly packed, goodbyes were said, and the excited little family made their way across the plains. Maybe Gershom and Eliezer tried to hurry the adults along—they were on their way to see their dad, and there was no time to lose!

As the travelers neared the Israelite camp, Jethro paused to have a message sent to Moses. What did the message say? **Read Exodus 18:6.**

What wonderful news! Moses ran out to greet them all. First he bowed respectfully before his father-in-law, Jethro, and kissed him. Then you can be sure he hugged his wife and sons as happy tears were shed. He had missed his wife and children, and now he could enjoy the relief and comfort of having them with him.

Jethro, Moses, and Zipporah knew that families are one of God’s wonderful blessings. They had unselfishly watched out for each other’s needs and put each other first. They weren’t always perfect, but they enjoyed strong family relationships. Jesus tells us in the Bible what it takes to have strong, good relationships like this. “Do for other people the same things you want them to do for you” (Matthew 7:12, ICB).
