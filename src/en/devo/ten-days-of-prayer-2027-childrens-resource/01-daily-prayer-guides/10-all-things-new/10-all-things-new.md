### Jesus Makes All Things New

> <p></p>
> “‘He will wipe every tear from their eyes. There will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the old order of things has passed away.” He who was seated on the throne said, “I am making everything new!” Rev 21:4–5 (NIV)

Have you ever cried because you were hurt or sad? God sees every tear.

The Bible tells us that one day, when Jesus comes, everything sad will be gone. There will be no more pain, no more crying, and no more death. We will see Jesus, face to face, and He will make all things new.

What a wonderful promise!

But the Bible also teaches us that Jesus can begin making things new in us today.

When we trust Jesus, He forgives us and cleans our hearts. He helps us become new people who love Him. His Holy Spirit helps us think kind thoughts, make good choices, forgive others, and grow more like Jesus.

We do not have to wait until heaven to begin a new life with Jesus. We can ask Him to be with us today and every day.

Jesus wants to help and guide us. He wants us to give Him our future, our thoughts, and our hearts because He knows what is best for us.

And someday, Jesus will finish what He has begun. He will come again and make everything new! As He promised.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What would you like Jesus to help make new in your heart today?

> <p>Remember</p>
> Jesus can make my heart new today, and one day He will make everything new!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear Jesus, thank You for promising a world without sadness, pain, or tears. Forgive me, make my heart new, and help me follow You every day. I want to be ready when You come and make all things new. Amen.
