^[Word of the Week—Reverence:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#693c87", "size": "xl"}}})\
A deep respect, awe, and love for someone or something. Reverence is shown through attitudes, feelings, and behavior. In this lesson we will think about reverence as love and respect for God.

Love in the Wilderness

The people slowed their steps, and all watched carefully as God’s white cloud moved ahead of them, then stopped and settled over the top of a majestic mountain, Mount Sinai. It was time to set up camp! Relief rippled through the great crowd of men, women, and children. The peaceful plain God had led them to was protected by towering mountains. It would be a wonderful place to camp. Men and women hurried to pitch tents, set up cooking areas, and turn the dusty plain into their home.

God had intentionally led His people to this peaceful place. It was time for them to learn more about Him. There would be nothing out in the mountains to distract them from what God wanted to teach them.

God had already taught the Israelites many things as they journeyed away from Egypt and through the hot desert. He had led them through the Red Sea and destroyed their Egyptian enemies to teach them that He was their powerful protector. He had led them in a cloud to teach them that He was their caring leader. He had given them manna and water from a rock to teach them that He was their loving provider.

Yet the Israelites still didn’t really understand what their God was like. They didn’t fully understand that He had done these things for them because “God is love” (1 John 4:16). He had showered them with love, but they didn’t know how to respond. God longed for them to love Him back. It was time for Him to teach them how.

God began by calling Moses to meet Him up on Mount Sinai. What did God say to Moses? **Read Exodus 19:3, 4.** What a beautiful picture God’s words painted! The Israelites knew how these majestic creatures carried and cared for their young. God had chosen a beautiful reminder of His love.

Love is always the best place to start. When we know how much God loves us, we will be open to His teaching and guidance.
