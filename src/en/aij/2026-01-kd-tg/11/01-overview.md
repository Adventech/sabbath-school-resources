**Word of the Week**

**Honor:** When you honor someone, you treat them with respect. You do things that show they are special and important to you. You put them first.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God blessed Abram and his nephew Lot in Canaan, and they become wealthy. Their servants argued over the grazing land because there wasn’t enough for them all. Abram solved the problem without fighting by honoring Lot above himself and letting him choose land first. They agreed to move to separate places to live.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can help us put others first.
- **Feel** a desire to put others first.
- **Respond** by choosing to ask God to help us honor others.
