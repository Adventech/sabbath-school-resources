The people had seen Moses climb the rugged mountain. They had seen God’s glory flash like fire from the thick, dark cloud. They knew Moses was talking with God.

But as the weeks dragged by, the people got tired of waiting, and they began to worry. Some grumbled that Moses had deserted them. Others thought he had been burned up by the fiery cloud. Some even wanted to return to Egypt!

Their commitment to God disappeared because they had gotten used to depending on Moses as a leader they could see rather than God Himself, whom they couldn’t see. God had given the cloud as a visible reminder of His invisible presence, but they forgot all about that.

Before long, the worriers had become an angry mob that made its way to Aaron’s tent. What did the people say? **Read Exodus 32:1.**

Aaron tried to talk the people out of their crazy idea, but he was afraid of the people. If only Aaron had trusted God! But instead Aaron let the crowd persuade him to do something wrong. He told them to take off their golden earrings, and then he made the jewelry into a shiny golden calf. The golden calf glistened brightly in the hot desert sun. The crowd then cried out, “This is your god, O Israel, that brought you out of the land of Egypt!” (Exodus 32:4). What? The calf was just an idol! It couldn’t see or hear. And it definitely didn’t bring the people out of Egypt!

Only a few weeks before, the people had committed to worshipping God and not making idols. But commitment needs to be more than words. It needs to be action and obedience too. Instead, the people should have invited God into their hearts each day and asked Him to help them stay committed to living His good law of love.
