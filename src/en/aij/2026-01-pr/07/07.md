More than 170 years ago God asked a man to preach for Him, just as he had asked Noah. Instead of preaching about a big flood and finding safety in an ark, God asked this man to preach about God’s love and the second coming of Jesus.

His name was John Loughborough, and he was not quite 17 years old. He knew God wanted him to preach, even though he was young. But when he looked at himself, he wondered who would ever listen to him! His trousers were hand-me-downs from a taller man, so they flopped around his ankles. The coat he wore was just an overcoat that had been cut off at the bottom. With only $1 in his pocket, how was he to buy nice clothes? He couldn’t, but he set off to preach anyway.

Thinking he needed some preaching practice, John began to preach in a small place where he didn’t know anyone. He shouldn’t have worried, for the people were blessed by what they heard. One man gave him $3, others gave him $5 worth of books to sell, and others encouraged him because he was a good preacher and could explain the Bible so well.

Moving on to another village, he rented a church and put up some posters to let people know he would be preaching for three nights. Pretty soon word got around that a boy was going to preach! Imagine John’s surprise when he found the church full to overflowing that first night, and then again the next evening! He was so glad that people had come to hear about God.

But there was one man who wasn’t happy, and he tried to cause trouble. He argued and said that John wasn’t preaching what the Bible said. Before long he was very embarrassed, because young John could easily show him lots of Bible texts to prove the truths he preached.

Over the years John Loughborough became a very well-known preacher who worked hard for God in America, England, and Europe. He preached in churches, schools, tents, people’s homes, and even under shady trees. Sometimes people tried to do cruel things to him, but God always kept him safe.

He is remembered as a faithful Seventh-day Adventist pioneer who worked hard with James and Ellen White and other church leaders to tell people about God and His truth for this time.

Enoch walked with God. Noah worked for God. John Loughborough preached for God. We can too.

- _What can you do for God?_

When we walk with God by choosing to love Him and live for Him, He will show us what we can do for Him.
