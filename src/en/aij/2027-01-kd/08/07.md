Charles Marshall Kinny had always wanted to belong to a special family of his own. Charles was a young boy who lived in the United States long ago, when some people had slaves. Charles was born a slave. He had an owner who made him work hard. For 10 years Charles was never shown care, love, or encouragement.

Eventually he was freed and traveled alone, in search of work. He settled in a town called Nevada. He found a place to live and a good job, but he still felt lonely.

One day, when he was 23 years old, he noticed a large striped tent being set up in town. Charles heard there would be preaching every night in the tent, so he decided to go. Night after night he listened to the preacher share the powerful Adventist message.

One evening a visiting preacher, Ellen White, stood up to speak. Charles listened closely to every word she said. She read a Bible verse that said, “Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed upon us, that we should be called children of God!” (1 John 3:1)

_Children of God_, Charles thought. _God loves me this much that He sees me as His child?_ It sounded too good to be true!

Charles decided to become a Seventh-day Adventist. He decided to become God’s child, and a part of the Adventist Church family. He became the first black pastor and began preaching all over the United States. He spent the rest of his life telling people that no matter who they were or where they came from, they belonged to God’s family.
