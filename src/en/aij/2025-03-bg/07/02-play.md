Create

Create a mini tabernacle using blocks or cardboard.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Explore

Talk about how the Israelites gave God their special things with happy hearts. Let your child help with some jobs this week to earn a few coins. Remind your child on Sabbath morning about the coins they earned and ask them if they would like to give them to Jesus. Put them in the offering at Sabbath School or church.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Practice

Practice teamwork throughout the week. Think of tasks your child can help with, such as folding clothes or tidying up. In all the little jobs this week, ask them to help as a team. Affirm their special qualities: cheerfulness, carefulness, eagerness, etc. God has made us all different so we can share our talents and work well together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Connect

Think about an act of kindness you could do together this week to be like Jesus and love others. It could be making a card together for someone sick, making rice pudding or baking muffins for a neighbor, or helping Grandma in her garden. Thank God that we can work together with Him to help others.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Prayer

Thank God that He asks for our help, even though He is the king of the universe! Ask Him to show you how you can help Him this week. As a family, think about or write down names of people in need whom you think God is asking you to encourage and help.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “God loves a cheerful giver” (2 Corinthians 9:7).
