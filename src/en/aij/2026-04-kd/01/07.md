Armi looked up at the sky and smiled. Yes, the weather was perfect for fishing! The 21-year-old missionary was excited. This was his first chance to make friends with the local people in the Maluka Islands of Indonesia. He hoped to tell them about Jesus.

Armi and two local men floated out into the ocean in a small wooden boat. But when the boat was far from shore, the weather quickly changed. A fierce wind began to blow. Rain started to fall. Large waves crashed against the boat.

Armi and his two friends tried to scoop the water out of the boat. When that didn’t work, they threw over their fishing nets to make the boat lighter.

As the rain fell and the waves rocked the boat, Armi suddenly sensed a small voice say, “Pray.” Grabbing hold of a rope, he closed his eyes, took a deep breath, and screamed, “Lord, help us! Save us because we know that You are able to save us!”

Armi repeated the prayer again and again, for three minutes. Then he said “Amen” and opened his eyes. At that moment, the storm vanished. The wind stopped, and the ocean was still. The dark clouds rolled away, and bright stars shone down.

Armi was shocked, and he cried out, “Lord, thank You so much!”

His two friends wept with joy as they steered the boat back to shore. On the way, they saw a fishing net that they had thrown out during the storm and stopped to collect it. The net was bursting with fish!

When the boat landed, Armi’s friends told everyone about their answered prayers. The villagers wanted to buy the miracle fish.

People kept coming to Armi to hear him tell the story about the storm. They asked many questions about Jesus. Four people were baptized. Within three years, the village had its own Adventist church.
