**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Welcome all children with a bright smile as they arrive. Talk with them about anything special that happened this week.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Build a big altar of sacrifice together with **LEGO, DUPLO, or other building blocks**. Share that this week’s story has an altar and that their altar will be used during the story.

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. Express how wonderful it is to be together to worship our loving God.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“God Is My Faithful Friend.”** Sing the prayer song **“Sabbath Song.”**

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[ACTIVITY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** View the collection of **objects that protect**, and decide what they have in common. If time allows, select children to quickly demonstrate how each object is used. Do this while the children are discussing what they have in common. Highlight how each object protects us by keeping us safe or minimizing harm.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **How can these objects remind us of God?** (He protects us.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Display a **picture of the ark** on a rough sea from last week’s Picture PowerPoint, and share that today’s story looks at how God protected Noah and his family.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: I can thank God for His protection and trust all His promises.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF0E8", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How and when can we worship and praise God in our daily lives, as Noah and His family did?** (Encourage children to think beyond Sabbath worship.)
- **What does God’s rainbow remind us of?** (His promise to protect His people, no more worldwide floods, the rainbow around God’s throne in heaven.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Rainbows remind us of God’s promise to love and protect us. They also remind us to thank Him for
His many colorful blessings.

**^[PRAISE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to color in the **rainbow** displayed. Then have the children say a color in the rainbow and think of something that is that color and thank God for it. We can thank God for all His many blessings!

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 8: Rainbow Promise** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and have the children recite the **memory verse** together. Sing the **Scripture Song, “Psalm 57:1.”** Invite them to stand and create actions to accompany the verse.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/8-psalm-57-1.mp3] “Psalm 57:1” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Aisha Chacon"},{"key":"Cello","value":"Jared Chung"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})**

- **What situations might you find yourself in that seem scary or hard to deal with? How could saying your memory verse help?** (It reminds us that God will protect us—we can trust Him.)

Explain that you will give the children an extra character card to take home as a reminder to say their memory verse and to trust in God whenever they are afraid.

- **Where could you display your card?** (On your wall at home, on the top of your school desk, on the bathroom door, etc.) Plan to put it there!

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, “Cheetah Protects Her Cubs,” and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your completed **Promise and Protection Spinner** and share instructions for making it. Distribute an **outline** to each child.

Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly in the background as the children complete their craft.
