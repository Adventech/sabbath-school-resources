**Focus:** God loves to see us get excited by what He can do.

Have you ever seen a parrot? There are more than 400 different species of parrots found all over the world, especially in warm places. Here are some examples:

**Macaws:** They live in Central and South America, in such places as Brazil and the Amazon rainforest. They’re bright, large, very loud birds! They often have blue, red, or yellow feathers. They can live up to 50 years or more.

**African Grey Parrots:** They live in Central Africa. They’re not very colorful, but they’re the best talkers! They can learn hundreds of words and even understand what some words mean.

**Cockatoos:** They live in Australia, Indonesia, and nearby islands. They have cute crests on their heads, which they raise and lower. They love people and need lots of attention!

**Quaker Parrots:** They live in South America and also now live in parts of the USA and Europe. They build huge nests (rare for parrots) and live in groups.

Parrots are very expressive, social birds. They like to be around people and are faithful to their owners. When they’re excited, parrots show it in very clear ways. They flap their wings, even if they’re not flying. They bob their head up and down. They make happy noises, such as chirping, whistling, or even mimicking laughter! They puff up their feathers to show they’re happy. They run around or climb playfully on whatever is nearby, and they talk even louder to let you know they’re excited! Sometimes parrots even copy human words when they’re excited.

When a parrot is excited, you will know it! They show it with their whole body.

Like parrots, we can let others know when we’re excited by not being shy about loving God. We can use our voices to praise God. What does the Bible say about this? (Ask two children to read the following two verses in their Bibles: Psalm 100:1 and Psalm 150:6.)

- **What do you do when you’re really happy?**
- **How can you show you’re excited about God today?**
