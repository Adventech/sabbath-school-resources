With a smile as wide and bright as a summer sky, the woman headed home. There was no shuffling, no bleeding, no weakness. She had been fully healed! She must have had so much energy. What do you think she may have done? Maybe she cleaned her house from top to bottom, or maybe she planted a garden. We don’t know what she did, but we do know that she must have done it with a grateful heart. Jesus had given her life “more abundantly” (John 10:10).

The healed woman wasn’t the only one who was grateful that day. Jesus was thankful too. He was thankful that a sick woman had reached out to Him and trusted Him. He was thankful that He could give her the miracle she longed for.

Jesus is thankful when we persist in reaching out to Him too. He hears our prayers and sees our faith. He loves to heal our sickness, our brokenness, and our loneliness when we believe in Him. He says, “The one who comes to me I will by no means cast out” (John 6:37). His plan is to give us a life that’s full of goodness. Will you persist in reaching out to Jesus so He can give you this life?
