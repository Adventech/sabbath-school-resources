**Word of the Week**

**Temptation:** Temptation is wanting to do something you know you shouldn’t do. Temptation is not sin; sin happens when you choose to follow the temptation. Temptation puts a Christian at risk of losing their closeness with God.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the way Jesus overcame the temptations of Satan in the wilderness. After 40 days with no food or rest, Jesus was at His weakest, and at that time Satan chose to attack his opponent. But Jesus did not rely on His own strength in this battle. He sought His Father in heaven to give Him the power to gain the victory over Satan. Jesus knew Scripture, and He used the Word of God to resist temptation. One, two, three times Satan came to Jesus with temptations in an attempt to discourage and break His firm hold on God. Each time Jesus’ weapon against Satan was the Word of God, and He was victorious!

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can give them victory over sin.
- **Feel** that they can rely on God and on His strength and power.
- **Respond** by spending time with God and storing up His Word in their minds to help them resist temptation.
