Opening Activities

Source **Modeling clay** or **play-dough** and **baking trays**.

Source a **remote-controlled vehicle** or drone (if able to go outside). If you are unable to source either, source a **picture** of a remote-controlled car or drone.

Prepare Mission Fast Fact.

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source **^[Picture PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.

Use **modeling clay story props** made by early arrivals, **remote-controlled car, 2 buckets** or jars filled with **sand**. Poke a small **tree branch** into each and label one “The Tree of Life,” and the other “The Tree of the Knowledge of Good and Evil.” Source **felts** of the story of Genesis 3.

Source a plate or tile: break into pieces and store in a container.
