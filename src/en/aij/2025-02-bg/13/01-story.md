^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/13/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
Can you see a shining star? Look for one in the dark night sky. See it twinkling, way up high. It’s higher than your biggest stretch (reach up high). It’s higher than the treetops. It’s higher than the hills. A bird can fly up very high, but a star is higher still. Jesus’ love for you is so, so big. It’s higher than the stars. Can you see a starfish? Look for it on the bottom of a bright-blue sea. See it down deep, as deep as can be. The sea is deeper than your biggest bend down low (reach low). It’s deeper than a swimming pool. It’s deeper than the big roots of a tall tree. Jesus’ love for you is so, so big. It’s deeper than the deepest sea.     
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/13/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
Can you see a sunset? Look for it in the sky when it’s nearly time for bed. See the golden sun sink down low in the sky. See the pretty colors side by side—pinks, purples, oranges, and yellows. The sky is so wide. It’s wider than your biggest reach (reach on each side). It’s wider than a mountain. It’s wider than a lake. Jesus’ love for you is so, so big. It’s wider than the sky. Can you see the clouds? Look for them as they float across the pretty sky. The sky is so long. It’s longer than your biggest reach in front and behind (reach in front and back). It’s longer than a plane. It’s longer than a train. It’s longer than you can ever see. Jesus’ love for you is so, so big. It’s longer than the sky.

Jesus wants to give you His big, big love! He cares for you. He listens to you. He helps you. He will be with you always. Yes, Jesus’ love for you is so, so big,  (say child’s name), the Bible tells us so. Would you like to say to Jesus, “I love You too. I want to follow You”?
