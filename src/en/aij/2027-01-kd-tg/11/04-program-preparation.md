**Opening Activities**

Source **play-dough**. Separate into small pieces for each child. **Tools** for children to write/draw with: craft sticks or twigs. Write your name on a piece before they arrive.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: **toy dog, dog treats**. **Media device** to play **dog obedience video** if choosing to do this option. You may like to use another toy dog to show disobedience if not using video.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source items for **Suggested storytelling props**: scrunched **newspaper** piled high and covered with **brown fabric** for “mountain,” toy cow for idol, **small cardboard** Ten Commandment tablets.

Source peg people (Moses, Aaron, Joshua, others) or a felt set if desired, and **^[week 11 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: **Two containers: one with good cut fruit** (enough for a piece for each child), the **other with a piece of rotten fruit**.
