Jesus is love, peace, and joy. Pride, selfishness, greed, and gossip began with Lucifer. When we choose Jesus, it affects what we do and how we treat other people. What would you do in each of these situations?

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![1. Imagine that you received two remote-control cars for your birthday. Your brother wants to play with one of the cars, but it is brand-new and you don’t want him to scratch it.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/04/image7.png)

^[What would you say?]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#73C3E2"}}})

- A. _“No, they are my cars, and it’s my birthday. I want to play with my cars by myself all day.”_
- B. _“You can play with one of them for two minutes while I watch you.”_
- C. _“Sure, let’s play together, but do you mind if we just play with them on the carpet until we learn how to drive them properly?”_

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![2. Imagine there is a shy girl in your class who often plays by herself on the playground. One day she drops her lunch box, and everything goes flying all over the ground. Most of the children laugh at her. She is very embarrassed.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/04/image8.png)

^[What would you do?]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#73C3E2"}}})

- A. _Laugh along with the rest of the class and watch her trying to pick up her lunch out of the dirt._
- B. _Smile at her and help her pick everything up. Tell her she can share your lunch._
- C. _Tell the story to everyone and whisper about how this girl is always so clumsy._

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![3. Imagine that you are really good at drawing. Everyone is amazed at your artwork.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/04/image9.png)

^[What would you do?]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#73C3E2"}}})

- A. _Tell all your friends, aunts, uncles, and your mom and dad that you are the best._
- B. _Refuse to help your little sister with her drawings because you don’t want her to learn to be as good as you. You feel annoyed at her because she always copies you._
- C. _Share your talents by making some beautiful cards with your artwork, and send them to people who are sick, sad, or lonely._
