Moses traveled on through the worksites, looking from weary slave to cruel taskmaster, from muddy hole to shiny palace. Suddenly he saw a sight up ahead that stopped him in his tracks. An Egyptian was beating a Hebrew slave right before his eyes!

Moses’ anger bubbled up into rage. The Bible says, “So he looked this way and that way, and when he saw no one, he killed the Egyptian and hid him in the sand” (Exodus 2:12).

Moses hadn’t waited for God’s plan of rescue; he’d chosen to be the rescuer himself. Except that his rescue had gone terribly wrong! It had ended with murder.

The very next day Moses was once again shocked to see a slave being beaten. This time, by a fellow Hebrew slave. “Why are you striking your companion?” Moses demanded (Exodus 2:13).

The mean slave glared at Moses and said, “Who made you a prince and judge over us? Do you intend to kill me as you killed the Egyptian?” (Exodus 2:14).

His secret was out! Fear stung Moses’ heart. His own people knew the terrible thing he had done, and had turned against him. Even though Moses knew what he’d done was wrong, “Moses thought that his fellow Jews would understand that God was using him to save them. But they did not understand” (Acts 7:25, ICB).

Pharaoh didn’t understand his actions either. “When Pharaoh heard of this matter, he sought to kill Moses” (Exodus 2:15). Moses was scared for his life! He knew he had to get out of Egypt. Now.

Even though Moses had messed up badly by trying to do things his own way, God was still with him and leading him. Moses would still do a big work for God one day, and God would help him do it. That’s how patient and forgiving our God is when we make mistakes.
