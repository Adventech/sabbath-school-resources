The man looked down at the blotchy white spots on his body, and he gasped! His body hurt and sometimes he lost feeing in an arm or a leg. Now he knew why. He had leprosy!

In Bible times, leprosy was thought of as a highly contagious disease with no cure. The man must leave his family, his friends, his job, and his home, and move into the countryside where no one could see or touch him. People weren’t just scared of getting sick—they also rejected people with leprosy because they mistakenly believed leprosy was caused by sin. If anyone came near the man with leprosy, he had to shout “Unclean!” so they could hurry away.

One day the lonely man with leprosy heard about Jesus. A seed of hope was planted in his heart, and he decided to look for the miracle-working Teacher. Maybe, just maybe, Jesus could heal him!

After wandering the mountain paths, He spotted Jesus, who was teaching beside the lake. The man’s faith grew as he saw others healed and heard their shouts of praise. Full of excitement, he rushed toward Jesus. He didn’t notice the horrified people pushing to get out of his way. He didn’t hear their shouts for him to stop. He saw only the Son of God. He knelt down and worshipped at Jesus’ feet, saying, “Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean” (Matthew 8:2).

Such wonderful faith! How did Jesus respond? **Read Matthew 8:3.**

Immediately the man’s skin glowed, his muscles firmed up, and his nerves could feel again. He was healed!

Jesus could have healed the man as he stood far away on the edge of the crowd, but Jesus knew he hadn’t been touched by anyone in a long time. He needed Jesus’ gentle touch so he would feel loved and accepted.

Jesus knows exactly what we need and when we need it. When we’re sad or hurt, others may give us a hug to comfort us. The Holy Spirit prompts those who love us to be compassionate and give us what we need to feel better.
