Sing and Pray

Teach your child the chorus of the hymn “Trust and Obey,” and sing it every night for worship before you pray together, asking God to help you to trust and obey Him. Talk about the words and how Noah trusted and obeyed God when he built the ark.

Trust and obey,\
For there’s no other way\
To be happy in Jesus,\
But to trust and obey

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#f0544b"}}}
Explore

Visit your local football fields _(or other sporting fields that are about 328 feet [100 meters] long)_. Explain to your child that you’re going to find out how long Noah’s ark was _(it was 510 feet [144 meters] long)_. Set up a visible marker _(e.g., a stroller or folding chair)_ at one end of the field. Walk the length of one and a half football fields and look back at your marker. Explain to your child that from the marker to where you are standing is how long Noah’s ark was! If possible, you could also show them a four-story building and show them how high the ark was. You can find other fun facts and comparisons on the Ark Encounter website.

Play

Do a picture puzzle with your child from start to finish. Talk about how Noah needed to put the ark together like a giant puzzle and how wonderful it must have felt when he finally finished! _(If you don’t have a puzzle, you could print or draw a picture of Noah’s ark, glue it to cardboard, and cut it into different shapes)_.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#f0544b"}}}
Create

Build Noah’s ark out of craft sticks. Tell your child that these will be like the big wooden planks Noah used to build the ark. First, get a piece of strong cardboard for the base, then use strong craft glue to stick down the first layer of craft sticks. You will need eight craft sticks, overlapping each other _(see example above)_.

Dot the ends of each craft stick with glue. Have your child help you by passing the craft sticks to you or placing them carefully on top, just as Noah’s sons helped him. Continue building it up about 15 sticks high. Leave to dry for a couple of hours, and then cut the excess cardboard off from around the boat. Your child will love watching the boat take shape and then playing with it later!
