Old Abraham sat in his tent’s shady doorway. It was too hot to work. It was the middle of the day—time to rest. As his sleepy eyes looked out over the fields, he saw something. Visitors! There were one . . . two . . . three visitors coming his way. Oh, what a surprise!

What did Abraham do? Read Genesis 18:2.

The visitors must have been happy to have such a friendly welcome!

Then Abraham said, “Sir . . . please stay awhile with me, your servant. I will bring some water so all of you can wash your feet. You may rest under the tree. I will get some bread for you, so you can regain your strength” (Genesis 18:3-5, ICB).

Abraham kindly led them over to the cool shade of a big tree. His home was out in the countryside. They would need water after such a long, hot walk. Quickly Abraham and Sarah rushed to looked after their guests.

The Bible tells us to “be gentle and polite to all people” (Titus 3:2, ICB). Abraham was just like this. No wonder people knew He was God’s friend! When we treat someone kindly, we’re really treating Jesus kindly (see Matthew 25:40). What a good thing to remember!
