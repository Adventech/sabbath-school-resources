Their tummies were full, their bags were packed, and their sandals were on their feet. They had painted their doorposts with blood and followed God’s instructions. The Israelites were ready to leave the land of Egypt! Quietly they prayed and waited. Outside, unseen, God’s angel passed over their homes and delivered all those who had obeyed God and put their trust in Him.

In Egypt the people had not put their trust in God. As the angel passed over the land, every family lost their firstborn son. Pharaoh rushed to his firstborn son’s room and found him lifeless. At that moment Pharaoh remembered the proud words he had spoken to Moses: “Who is the Lord that I should obey His voice to let Israel go? I do not know the Lord, nor will I let Israel go” (Exodus 5:2).

Now Pharaoh’s pride was broken. Pale-faced and trembling, he called for Moses and Aaron. What did he say to them? **Read Exodus 12:31, 32.** Terrified, the Egyptian people also urged the Israelites to go, or “we shall all be dead” (Exodus 12:33).

Before the sun rose, the Israelites left Egypt.

They moved as quickly as they could—all 600,000 men, as well as women and children.

Imagine their relief to finally be delivered from slavery and to be under God’s protection. God’s promise to Abraham, centuries before, had finally been fulfilled. **Read about it in Genesis 15:13, 14.** God had delivered them from slavery with His mighty power.

God wants to deliver us too. We may not be brickmaking slaves, but we are all hurt by sin, and stuck in it, just as if we were slaves. Satan wants us to stay that way. He tempts us to make wrong choices, be disobedient, selfish, and unkind. But God is mighty to save us! Matthew 6:13 tells us to pray, “Deliver us from the evil one.” Now, that’s a great prayer to pray!
