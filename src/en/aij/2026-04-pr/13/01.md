Good Friends

Welcome home.” These special words sound good in every language, no matter where your home is or what it looks like. Hopefully, your home is a place to feel safe and accepted; a place to laugh and play, love, and share honestly with people who care for you; a place to kick off your shoes and just be yourself.

When Jesus started His ministry, He left His family home in Nazareth. For three years He traveled from place to place. There were always new people that needed to meet Him, learn about God’s love, be healed and forgiven. Did Jesus have a home to go back to after a busy day of caring for others? **Read Matthew 8:20.**

Even though Jesus chose to go without a home, He was always welcome at the home of His close friend Lazarus, in Bethany. He often stayed with Lazarus and his two sisters, Mary and Martha. He enjoyed being with them because they loved Him and were like His family.

It must have been such a relief for Jesus to be in a peaceful home with friends. There He could speak freely and be understood. When Jesus was teaching and preaching to the crowds, not everyone understood or liked what He said. Especially some of the Pharisees. And sometimes they told Him so!

In the home of His friends, Jesus could escape from the misunderstandings and conflict. Unlike some of the Pharisees, Lazarus and his sisters treasured Jesus’ friendship and the things He taught them.

What a blessing it was for Jesus to have caring friends and a peaceful, accepting place to feel at home. Jesus also wants to be your best Friend and spend time with you. **Read Revelation 3:20.**

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- What would it be like to be on the road for three years, without a home? Why do you think Jesus chose to do this?
- How could you make your friends feel welcome and accepted in your home?
