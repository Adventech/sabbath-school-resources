Joseph smiled and pointed at the city ahead. Finally, after a very long and tiring trip, Mary and Joseph could see Bethlehem.

What was the other name for Bethlehem? Read Luke 2:4.

The donkey’s slow _clip-clop, clip-clop_ became _clippity-cloppity, clippity-cloppity_ as Joseph eagerly rushed toward the city.

As they entered the gates, they saw people everywhere! Lots of people had come to be counted, just as they had. Joseph, Mary, and their donkey squeezed past the crowds of visitors. They walked up and down the narrow streets looking for a place to sleep.

Joseph stopped at an inn. _Knock, knock, knock._ As the door creaked open, Joseph asked for a room to stay for the night. The owner shook his head and closed the door. “There were no rooms left in the inn” (Luke 2:7, ICB). Joseph tried the place next door, but there were no empty beds there, either.

With tired, sagging shoulders Joseph and Mary moved from one house to another, looking for a place to sleep. But not a single room could be found anywhere. Oh, dear! What would they do?

If only the people realized that the baby inside of Mary was God’s greatest gift to the world. If only they understood how special that unborn baby was. Maybe then they would have made room for Mary, Joseph, and the baby King.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Where would Mary and Joseph sleep?
- What would you have said or done, if you had been living in Bethlehem back then?
