**Opening Activities**

Write the **words** “God is with me” somewhere in your Sabbath School room (near the mural, if possible).

Source a **balloon**, small **beanbag**, or soft **ball**.

Familiarize yourself with this quarter’s mission project/offering destination to share with the children. Print out the **^[13 Mission Fast Facts]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for the quarter.

Print the **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and create a **colorful container** to collect the offering in.

Source a **bell** or **sound maker** to use to get the children’s attention before opening the Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box. E.g., a bell, toy flute, castanet, sealed cardboard tube filled with dried beans.

Source **two boxes** (shoeboxes) to be used for Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: sandpaper and cotton wool.**

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
