The man on the mat smiled up at kind Jesus. He was so happy to be forgiven!

But Jesus hadn’t yet finished healing him . . . With a kind voice, Jesus said, “ ‘I tell you, stand up. Take your mat and go home.’ Immediately the man stood up. He took his mat and walked out while everyone was watching him” (Mark 2:11, 12, ICB).

The people in the crowded room stared in amazement as they watched that man walk outside. “We never saw anything like this!” they said to each other (Mark 2:12).

This man had come to Jesus, pale and weak. Now his skin glowed healthy and pink.

He had come to Jesus, sad and sorry. Now his smile was wide, and his eyes shone with peace.

The man had come to Jesus, unable to move parts of his body. Now he could walk and run with legs so strong!

Jesus had said, “Take your mat.” How strange. His mat? The mat reminded him of all his gloomy, hurting days. Shouldn’t he leave it behind and forget it?

The mat would be good to keep. When he saw it, he could remember what life had been like before Jesus, and how much Jesus had changed everything. Whenever he told his story, he could show people his old mat and tell them how his Friend Jesus had made him better—on the inside and out.

Jesus says, “I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly” (John 10:10).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- The mat would always remind the man of Jesus. What special things do you have that also remind you of Jesus?
- What might the man’s friends have been doing and saying on the roof?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus for reminding us that He gives us peace and can help us, no matter how hard life might be.
