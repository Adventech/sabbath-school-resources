**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God. His words are powerful.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Psalm 119:11).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Strength”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Strength is being strong and powerful. With strong muscles you can have the strength you need to lift heavy objects. Strength also means being tough and not moved or pushed around by something. We can have strength inside our hearts—a strength to do what is right no matter what. God’s Holy Spirit and His Word can give us that strength.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Matthew 4:1-11; Mark 1:12, 13; Luke 4:1-13; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 114-131.

Strength Inside

Jesus stood on the shore of the Jordan River. His face glowed with God’s goodness. He had just been baptized and heard God say, “You are My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11).

These words made Jesus so happy! More than anything in the whole world, He loved God and He loved pleasing Him.

Jesus loved reading God’s Word and talking to God, His heavenly Father. It made Him strong on the inside. Every day Jesus followed God’s Word. Every day Jesus did what was right. The Bible says, “Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You” (Psalm 119:11).

It can be really hard to do what is right. Maybe you sometimes feel so angry you want to hurt someone with your words or actions. Maybe you have greedy or selfish thoughts. You might even want to lie, steal, disobey, or be rude.

But God promises that you can have the same strength Jesus had. You can make good choices too. For “super strength” like this you’re going to need help. Who can help you? **Read Philippians 4:13.**

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What helped Jesus stay strong on the inside?
- How can you have the strength to do what is right, as Jesus did?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to live in your heart and give you His super strength.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Have a “strength” challenge. Lie on the ground with your legs out straight. Lift your legs slightly off the ground and count how long you can hold them up in the air. What does strength really mean?
