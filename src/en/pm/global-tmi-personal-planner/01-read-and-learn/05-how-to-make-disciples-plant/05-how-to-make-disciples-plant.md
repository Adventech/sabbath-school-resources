^[Share literature, media, and spiritual invitations]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) widely to develop and find interested people. Consider these ways to lead someone from a ^[friendship]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interest to a ^[spiritual]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#694121", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) or ^[study]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3C8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interest, and to provide spiritual growth throughout the disciple-making journey:

- ^[Share truth-filled literature (tracts and books) and media.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Invite your friend to Sabbath School, church, and prayer meeting.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Share a personal testimony or a special Bible promise.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Invite your friend to go through a series of Bible studies together or on their own.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Invite your friend to a small group Bible study or evangelistic series.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Offer to pray together.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
