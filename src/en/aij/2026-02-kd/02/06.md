Esau’s anger bubbled and hissed like a pot of boiling hot water. Esau did not have God’s love and forgiveness in his heart. Instead, his hatred burned hotter and hotter until he wanted to kill his younger brother Jacob.

Rebekah knew it would be too dangerous for Jacob to stay. He must leave now!

“So, son, do what I say,” Rebekah cried. “My brother Laban is living in Haran. Go to him at once! Stay with him for a while until your brother is not so angry” (Genesis 27:43, 44, ICB).

Father Isaac also called for Jacob. He had been thinking and praying. He now knew God had planned to give Jacob the special blessing all along. Isaac told Jacob to marry a woman from Rebekah’s family who loved and believed in the one true God. Then Father Isaac gave Jacob one last beautiful blessing.

What did Isaac say? **Read Genesis 28:3.**

Jacob hugged his parents and cried. Isaac and Rebekah waved sadly as their son disappeared into the distance with only his staff and bag.

Although Jacob had made many bad choices, God was still with him. God loved him and forgave him for his mistakes. Did you know that God’s love for you never changes either, even when you make really big mistakes?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})

- What words would you say to Jacob to encourage him?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that His love never ends —even when we make mistakes.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})\
Give a hug to someone you know at church this Sabbath. Tell them that God’s love for them will never end.
