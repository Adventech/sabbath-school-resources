**Big Idea:** When Jesus blesses my life in wonderful ways, I can pass a blessing on to others.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**TELL THE STORY**

A gentle breeze blew through the pretty lakeside village of Capernaum. It set the palm trees swaying and cooled the crowds who had gathered in the streets. Families, fishermen, farmers, traders, and travelers all pressed together. Everyone was excited. Jesus was in town!

The people hurried to find the new Teacher. They gathered around Him, eager to listen and learn.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Sabbath came, and Jesus went to the synagogue, setting an example for us of keeping the Sabbath holy. The people tried to crowd into the synagogue to hear Jesus speak.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did they think of the way Jesus taught? Let’s read Mark 1:22.** (He taught with authority and not like the scribes.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After the service Jesus quietly slipped away for lunch at Simon Peter’s home. Jesus and His disciples had become like family to Peter. Peter gladly welcomed Jesus into his home.

As Jesus strolled into Peter’s house, He sat down wearily. But His rest didn’t last for long. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a worried look on his face, Peter came and told Jesus that his wife’s mother was sick with a very high fever. Her body felt hot, as if it were burning up. She was too sick to do anything except lie still in her bed.

Jesus went straight to the sick woman’s bedside and gently held her hand. “He stood very close to her and commanded the fever to leave. It left her immediately, and she got up and began serving them” (Luke 4:39, ICB).

At Jesus’ command the fever left: no headache, no aching muscles, no hot, burning body. What a wonderful blessing!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Peter’s mother-in-law felt amazing! With a spring in her step and light in her eyes, she headed straight to the kitchen to serve her guests. Jesus had healed her, so she immediately passed the blessing on by doing what she could for others. She happily served her family, Jesus, and the disciples.

News of the miracle spread like wildfire around Capernaum that Sabbath afternoon. Jesus had healed Peter’s mother-in-law! The story was told from house to house and around every street corner.

The people’s hearts filled with hope—maybe they could be healed too! But they didn’t dare come to Jesus on Sabbath, as they were afraid of making the rabbis angry. They thought it a sin to heal on Sabbath. As soon as the sun set, their doors flew open, and the people of Capernaum ran through the streets.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Everyone headed toward the humble home where Jesus was staying. Limping, leaning on walking sticks, and supported by friends, the people came. “And He laid His hands on every one of them and healed them” (Luke 4:40).

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What else did Jesus do for some of these faith-filled people? Let’s read Luke 4:41.** (He also healed those with demons.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus shared His love and God’s healing power as He blessed every single person. And as each one was healed, the joy was shared right down the line of people waiting to meet Jesus.

The night was dark, and the hour was late when the last person left. Silence finally settled down upon Capernaum. Grateful silence, contented silence, joyful silence. It had been a day like no other.
