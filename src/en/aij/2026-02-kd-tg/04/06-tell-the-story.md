**Big Idea:** God is with me. He helps me do my best.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Place a **cup** in a **sandbox** to use for a well, with a **rock** on top.
- Use **peg people** (Jacob, Laban, Rachel, Leah with a veil taped to her head) to “walk” in the sandbox as you share the story.
- Hide the **spotted sheep** and **goat** crafts the children made before Sabbath School around the room for them to find.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 4 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Whew! After a long walk, Jacob made it to his Uncle Laban’s town, Haran. He helped Laban’s daughter Rachel at the well ^[(Jacob and Rachel peg people in sandbox; move rock)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He was so happy to be with family!
- Jacob was a good worker for Laban ^[(swap Rachel for Laban peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). How would he like to be paid? Jacob offered to work seven years to marry lovely Rachel. Laban agreed.
- Jacob did his very best.
- After seven years Laban held a wedding feast. The bride came to Jacob in a long veil ^[(covered Leah peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jacob was so happy to finally marry Rachel.
- In the morning Jacob saw his new wife was Rachel’s older sister, Leah ^[(uncover Leah)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Laban had tricked him into marrying the wrong sister!
- Now Jacob knew what it was like to be lied to and tricked. It was terrible! But he didn’t get angry.
- Laban wasn’t sorry. He would let Jacob marry Rachel too, if he promised to work another seven years. Jacob agreed.
- Jacob did his very best. God blessed Laban with lots of animals ^[(add sheep to sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and gave Jacob lots of children. But Laban wouldn’t let him leave. What would he like to be paid? Jacob wanted all the spotted sheep and goats.
- Laban’s sons hid them. But God did a miracle! All the white sheep and goats had spotted babies. They were all Jacob’s! ^[(Invite children to find hidden spotted sheep.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- When Laban’s sons became jealous, God told Jacob in a dream that it was time to go home.
- Quick! The family packed up and left when Laban was away. God would lead them home.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[(Place Jacob peg person in sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob sat down by the well and smiled to the shepherds watering their sheep. Whew! After a long, long walk, he had finally made it to Haran, the town where his Uncle Laban lived.

“Do you know Laban . . . ?” he asked the shepherds.

“We know him,” they said. “Look, his daughter Rachel is coming now with his sheep” (Genesis 29:5, 6, ICB). ^[(Place Rachel peg person in sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The well was covered with a big, heavy stone. Quickly Jacob jumped up to help lovely Rachel. With a _push_ and a _pull_, a _wriggle_ and a _roll_, he moved the stone free ^[(move stone in sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then he kindly began to scoop water out for Rachel’s thirsty sheep.

Jacob had found his family! He was so happy; he cried happy tears.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob stayed with Laban ^[(swap Rachel peg person for Laban)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and worked hard for his family. Uncle Laban soon realized Jacob was a very good worker. He would need to be paid! What would he like to be paid?

Jacob smiled. He had fallen in love with Rachel! “Let me marry your younger daughter Rachel. If you will, I will work seven years for you,” Jacob said (Genesis 29:18, ICB).

Uncle Laban thought this was a great idea! Jacob started working right away. His love for God and for Rachel helped him do his best.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After seven years it was time for Jacob to marry Rachel. Uncle Laban held a big wedding feast. When it was dark, Jacob’s bride was brought to him wearing a veil that covered her face completely ^[(covered Leah peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jacob was so happy to marry Rachel!

As the sun rose the next morning, a sleepy Jacob slowly opened his eyes. He looked at his new wife to see her for the first time without her veil. His eyes opened wide in shock! (Reveal Leah peg person.)

Oh, no! It wasn’t Rachel; it was her older sister, Leah! Uncle Laban had tricked him into marrying the wrong sister!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Poor Jacob. He now knew what it felt like to be lied to and tricked. It wasn’t a good feeling at all! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Laban wasn’t sorry ^[(Laban peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He had a greedy plan. He said, “I will give you Rachel to marry also. But you must serve me another seven years” (Genesis 29:27, ICB).

Another seven years? Could Jacob really do his best and work seven more years for a man who had lied and tricked him?

Jacob took a deep breath and nodded. Yes, he would marry Rachel, and then he would work for Uncle Laban for another seven years with no pay.

Jacob kept working hard. God had forgiven him for lying and cheating. He would do his best for God!

God blessed Laban with lots of sheep and goats because of Jacob’s hard work. ^[(Add sheep to sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) And God gave Jacob lots of children. But Jacob missed his family far away. He asked his uncle if he could go home, but Uncle Laban didn’t want Jacob to go anywhere! Jacob’s hard work had made his uncle very rich. Laban offered to pay him. What would Jacob like to be paid?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob had a big family, but no sheep or goats of his own. He asked Uncle Laban to give him all the black and spotty sheep and goats.

“Agreed!” answered Laban. Then he whispered to his sons to quickly go hide all those animals.

God was with Jacob. As Jacob looked after Uncle Laban’s pure white sheep and goats, something amazing happened. Those white sheep and goats began to have lots of speckled and spotted babies. Jacob could keep them all! ^[(Invite children to go find the spotted sheep hidden around your Sabbath School space.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Because of God’s miracle, Jacob became very rich. Laban’s sons didn’t like it one bit! They became jealous and very unfriendly. God spoke to Jacob in a dream. He said, “I have seen all the wrong things Laban does to you. . . . I want you to leave here. Go back to the land where you were born” (Genesis 31:12, 13, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) While Laban was away, Jacob and his family quickly left. They packed their bags, rounded up their animals, climbed onto their camels, and raced off in a cloud of dust! God had been with Jacob and his family in Haran—and God would lead them home!
