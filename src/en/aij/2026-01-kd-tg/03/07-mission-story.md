**Focus:** The Sabbath is a special day with God.

“Happy Sabbath!” Father’s voice called through the darkness. “Rise and shine, Sunshine!” he said, poking his head into the door of Anita’s bedroom. It was 4:00 in the morning—but it was time to get up, have breakfast, and go! Anita’s eyes popped open. It was Sabbath—another chance to have a special adventure with God.

Anita and her family were missionaries from Greece who lived in Indonesia. Every Sabbath they would set off to a new village to worship God and share Jesus with the people there.

The family piled into the car with some student missionaries, and off they went. God’s Sabbaths were always so special. They passed tall forests, misty valleys scattered with villages, and beautiful rice paddies. “I’m so glad we don’t have to work on Sabbath,” Anita said to herself. “Having a special day of rest is such a good idea. Thank You, God.”

Finally bursts of blue ocean flashed between the trees. Soon the van was parked, and they were sailing on the ocean in a small boat. Everybody started sharing things they could see that they were thankful for—the blue-green water, the leaping fish, the clean golden air, the puffy clouds, their healthy bodies, and voices that could praise God today. What good things to talk about!

Then they swapped boats at a small island, sailed for another half hour, then began walking along a muddy trail. The cool mud squishing between Anita’s toes felt so good.

About 45 minutes later Anita saw a small village and a sign for the Seventh-day Adventist church. Sabbath School would begin soon, and the church was crowded already with happy people. “Hello, Anita. Hello, Pastor,” the people said. They were so happy to welcome the visitors. They knew that it had taken a long time and lots of effort to reach their village. Little children waved excitedly at Anita and came close. Anita smiled back.

Inside the church there were songs and Bible stories. And then Dad stood up to preach. How happy he looked to be there. There was lunch together and the chance to talk about Jesus. Anita even told a Bible story to the children all by herself.

Late in the afternoon Anita and the others began the long trip back home. She was tired but happy. Every Sabbath was a new adventure. Best of all, every Sabbath they got to worship God and talk about Him.

Anita loved being a missionary kid!
