The Jewish people loved to get together to share a meal on Sabbath. On this particular day, a very important Pharisee had invited Jesus and lots of people to his house for Sabbath dinner. Some sneaky spies were among the guests, watching Jesus very closely to see if they could catch Him disobeying their Sabbath rules.

As the food was passed around, Jesus noticed a man sitting nearby with a disease called dropsy. He had too much water in the cells of his body, especially around his stomach. The water made him look puffy and swollen.

Jesus planned to heal the sick man, but He knew that the Pharisees were watching Him like a hawk and they wouldn’t be happy. So He asked them a question first: “Is it right or wrong to heal on the Sabbath day?” (Luke 14:3, ICB). The Pharisees looked down. They knew the truth but wouldn’t answer.

Jesus took hold of the man who was sick and healed him. All the swelling in his body went away. He was well!

The Pharisees should have jumped for joy and praised God for the miracle. But they stayed silent. So Jesus asked them another question. What was Jesus’ question? **Read Luke 14:5.**

They knew the truth. They would help an animal in need, but they did not want to help people.

Jesus loved to pass on the blessing of the Sabbath by caring for others. He wants us to know the truth about Sabbath so we can do the same.
