**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Doubt and worry can so easily creep into our lives to weigh us down. Doubt is crippling and makes us question the foundation on which we stand. Worry can consume our thoughts and steal our peace. Someone once said, “Worry is like a rocking chair. It’ll give you something to do, but it won’t get you anywhere.” Both doubt and worry make us want to fix things on our own. But Jesus never meant for us to walk alone.

We’re told very plainly: “Cast all your anxiety on him because he cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7, NIV). To cast is to throw wholeheartedly, completely. This is what Jesus wants us to do, in a similar way that a small child might give their heavy bag to a parent to carry on a long journey. Our burden can be easy and light when we walk by His side.

“Worry is blind, and cannot discern the future; but Jesus sees the end from the beginning. In every difficulty He has His way prepared to bring relief. Our heavenly Father has a thousand ways to provide for us, of which we know nothing. Those who accept the one principle of making the service and honor of God supreme will find perplexities vanish, and a plain path before their feet” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 330).

Today, come to Jesus and give Him your worries. As He takes them and carries your burdens, you’ll find that your faith will also grow stronger.

May you and the children experience comfort and joy as you explore this week’s story, knowing that when you have worries, big or small, Jesus wants to help because He cares for you.
