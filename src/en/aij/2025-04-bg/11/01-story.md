{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}

{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}}}
All was quiet in Bethlehem. Little children were tucked in their beds, asleep. Big sisters and brothers were sleeping too. _(Shh!)_ Even mommies and daddies were asleep. _(Shh!)_ Inside the stable, old cow, woolly sheep, and little donkey were sleeping too. All was quiet and still. Until . . . What’s that sound? It was the cry of Mary’s newborn Baby! _(Wah, wah!)_\
Mary smiled as she lay on the hay in the stable. She held her new Baby close. Oh, how she loved Him! She loved His little nose _(touch child’s nose)_. She loved His little toes _(touch child’s toes)_. He was perfect _(hug child)_. Joseph smiled at his new Son and leaned in close. He gently whispered His name. It was the most special name ever . . . _(whisper)_ Jesus!
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-04-bg/11/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}}}
Mary wrapped little Baby Jesus in cloth. He needed a bed. Where would Baby Jesus, God’s Son, sleep? Joseph looked around the stable. He found a manger, the animal’s feeding tray. He filled the manger with fresh hay. The manger would hold precious Baby Jesus while He slept.\
Mary kissed Baby Jesus good night. Joseph squeezed His little hand. Old cow mooed softly _(moo, moo)_, and little donkey nuzzled Joseph’s arm. Woolly lamb baaed her welcome to Baby Jesus _(baa, baa)_. Angels watched the little Baby from above. God had sent Baby Jesus to the world! Mary cried happy tears.

Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. Angels are watching over you. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. God the Father is always with you. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. Mary and Joseph will love and care for you. Sleep sweetly, Baby Jesus. We will love you too!\
_(Say together)_ Thank you, God for sending Baby Jesus.
