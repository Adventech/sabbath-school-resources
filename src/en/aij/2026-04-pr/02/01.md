A Very Sick Little Boy

An anxious silence filled the nobleman’s mansion. Servants walked on tiptoes and spoke in hushed whispers. Inside a bedroom the nobleman’s face was haggard with worry as he looked down at his sick little boy.

Even though he had lots of money because he worked for the king, the nobleman couldn’t buy good health for the son he loved so much. No doctor and no amount of money had been able to make the sick boy well. In the end, the doctors had given up and said he would die, maybe even that very day. Anxious and heartbroken, the little boy’s parents stayed at his side.

Then surprising news was whispered along the mansion’s hallways and into the sick boy’s room. What was the surprising news? **Read the first part of John 4:46.**

The nobleman didn’t know a lot about the Miracle Worker the servants spoke of. But his hopes rose with every sentence he heard. Jesus could heal people without medicine. He spoke, and people with disabilities in their legs could walk. He touched sad people, and they found peace.

The nobleman thought of a plan. He would go to Cana and ask Jesus to return to Capernaum with him so his sick little son could be healed. Leaping up from the bedside, he shared his plan and said goodbye. Then he shot out the door and on toward Cana.

Worry and deep sadness prompted the nobleman to go and ask Jesus for help. The Bible encourages us to do the same. Philippians 4:6 says, “Be anxious for nothing, but in everything by prayer and supplication [asking], with thanksgiving, let your requests be made known to God.”
