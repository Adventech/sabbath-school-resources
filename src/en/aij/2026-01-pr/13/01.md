Waiting, Waiting . . .

Time ticked slowly by . . . God had promised Abram and Sarai many descendants, but they were _still_ waiting to cuddle a baby of their very own. Friends their age had become parents, grandparents, and some even great-grandparents. But still no baby came to Abram and Sarai. They waited.

When Lot and his family moved away, it must have been even harder to wait. Abram and Sarai now shared their household with their workers, instead of a family of their own. Abram wondered if it would always be that way—would a servant inherit everything God had given him? He asked God, “Lord God, what will You give me, seeing I go childless?” (Genesis 15:2).

God answered him in an amazing way. We learned all about it in week 10—can you remember the shining promise? Discover it again in **Genesis 15:5**.

Abram rested under a canopy of twinkling stars in the nighttime silence. “Abram believed the Lord. And the Lord accepted Abram’s faith, and that faith made him right with God” (Genesis 15:6, ICB).

It’s not easy to wait. But God promises more than just the end reward for us when we do. He kindly gave Abram a boost while he waited, and He says He will do that for you too.

The Bible says, “Let us hold fast the confession of our hope without wavering, for He who promised is faithful” (Hebrews 10:23).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _When have you had to wait a long time for something promised to you?_
- _Did you talk to God about it while you waited?_

God always keeps His promises, and He helps us wait patiently for them to be fulfilled.
