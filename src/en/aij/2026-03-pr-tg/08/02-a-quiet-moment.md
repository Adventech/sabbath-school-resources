**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

When Jesus began His ministry on earth and started calling His disciples, He didn’t call just the smartest, the best looking, or the strongest. He called ordinary people; people who were willing to give all to follow Him. Discipleship in the life and times of Jesus wasn’t just about knowing what the Rabbi knew; it was about being who the Rabbi was. They lived with and watched the Rabbi to become like him.

Jesus calls us to be His disciples too. He loves each of us and knows that when we follow Him, we will live lives full of purpose. Our time with Jesus will change us; we will be more like Him and desire to share His love with those around us.

With Jesus in our hearts, we become a mirror, reflecting His love and grace to others. “His disciples are His representatives among men, and one of the most effective ways of winning souls to Him is in exemplifying His character in our daily life. Our influence upon others depends not so much upon what we say as upon what we are” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 141, 142).

As you and the children explore this week’s story together, may you feel loved knowing that our God is calling each one of us to be His disciples too.
