God’s people kept marching through the hot, dusty desert. The sun shone brightly in the clear blue sky. But the people didn’t feel the sun burning their skin. God covered them with His special cloud. God’s cloud kept them cool in the hot desert. It gave them shade from the sun. Thank You, God!\
When the big cloud moved slowly across the desert, God’s people followed along behind it. Follow, follow, follow _(walk fingers on page)._ God was using the cloud to lead them! When the cloud stopped moving, God’s people stopped too. Quickly they set up their tents. When the cloud started to move again, they packed up everything and started walking. Follow, follow, follow _(walk fingers on page)._ It was so special to see God leading them!

At night, God’s cloud changed into a tower of fire. It glowed orange and red against the dark night sky. Flicker, flicker. It kept God’s people warm on the cold desert nights _(cuddle up close)._ The fire was like a beautiful night-light. It helped them to feel safe and loved. God was with them in the cool cloud during the day. He was with them in the warm fire at night.\
God promised to be with His people always! He called Moses to come and meet Him on a mountain. Up, up, up the mountain climbed Moses, to meet with God. God talked with Moses. He gave him a very special gift for the people. It was a list of good things to do to love God and love others. It’s called the Ten Commandments. It’s in our Bible today. Moses was so happy! He knew God loved him, and God loved all the people.

God loves you too! We can read God’s words to us in the Bible. We can learn how to love Him and others with all of our hearts. The Bible tells us that God is with us always. He is with you when you wake. He is with you when you play. He is with you all through the day! He’s with you when you lay down your sleepy head. He is with you all through the night!\
Thank You, God, for being with Your people in the cloud and fire. _(Say together)_ Thank You for being with me, always.
