Simeon walked quickly to the temple in Jerusalem. He had felt the Holy Spirit tell him to get there, now! Simeon quickened his pace and prayed as he walked. Prayer came often to Simeon. “Just and devout” (Luke 2:25), he had earnestly prayed for Israel to return to God. And he had watched, waited, and prayed with longing for their Savior to appear.

Breathlessly he scanned the temple court, perhaps wondering why the Holy Spirit had brought him here. Then Simeon’s eyes saw them. He saw Him!

Rushing to Mary and Joseph, Simeon took Baby Jesus in his arms and praised God. The Savior! At last the Messiah had come!

God, in His kindness, had promised His devoted friend Simeon that he would see the Messiah in his lifetime. What did Simeon say to God now that he stood holding God’s Son? **Read Luke 2:30-32.**

While Simeon praised God, a prophet named Anna entered the temple. An 84-year-old widow, Anna served God in the temple night and day—He was her everything! Seeing the baby in Simeon’s arms, her face lit up. It was the Messiah! She gave thanks to God and began to tell everyone the good news.

These two humble worshippers had hoped and prayed that they would see and be able to worship the Messiah. That very day God rewarded their devotion—all their hopes and dreams came true

Getting to know Jesus will give you great joy too! Like Simeon and Anna, you will want to share this joy with everyone.
