**Word of the Week**

**Excited:** Being excited is feeling extremely happy, enthusiastic, and eager. When you are excited, your emotions are all stirred up; it can be hard to relax because your happy thoughts are focused on something good.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores how Bartimaeus was excited at the thought of Jesus doing the impossible and healing him from his blindness. When Jesus passed by, Bartimaeus cried out in faith. He couldn’t see Jesus, but he knew who He was. He had faith that Jesus was God’s Son, the Son of David, the promised Messiah. When Jesus called Bartimaeus to come to Him, Bartimaeus obeyed. Then Jesus healed him, and in response Bartimaeus gratefully followed him.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus loves to see us happy by the things He’s doing in our lives.
- **Feel** excited about what Jesus can do.
- **Respond** by following Jesus and sharing the exciting things He is doing.
