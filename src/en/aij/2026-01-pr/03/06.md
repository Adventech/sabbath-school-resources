To help us understand and enjoy His creation, God gave us five senses. (Can you name the five senses God has given you?)

God knew our senses would help us enjoy His creation in special ways. Because we rest from our busyness on Sabbath, we have time to appreciate God’s world through our sight, hearing, touch, taste, and smell. How can you use your senses to experience the world in special ways on Sabbath?

You might like to do some of the following or plan your own Sabbath afternoon family activity.

- Explore a public garden and find the flower with the sweetest fragrance.
- Look for nature treasures as you explore a beach and rock pools.
- Add something special, such as a glass of grape juice, to your closing Sabbath meal.
- Take paper with you on a nature walk. Draw some things you see or write down what you hear. You may like to take photos too.
- Give someone a hug, cuddle a baby, or pat an animal to share God’s love through touch.
- Sing your favorite songs to God.
- Draw a picture of something God has created, or something you like to do on Sabbath.
- Encourage someone by writing them a note of thanks or kindness.
- Make a blessing book. Draw or take photos of blessings for every letter of the alphabet. Do it with some friends!
- Read about God’s Sabbath: “This is the day the Lord has made; we will rejoice and be glad in it” (Psalm 118:24). If you know this verse as a song, you may like to sing it now. Or make up your own song (perhaps to a familiar tune).
- With a family member, read and/or sing some of the Sabbath hymns in _The Seventh-day Adventist Hymnal_, such as hymn no. 390, “We Love Thy Sabbath, Lord.”

^[Friday Night]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
\
^[Share and Praise]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
It’s Sabbath, the best time of the week. Celebrate in a new way: light seven candles—one for each day of Creation; sing a song of praise; or take turns thanking God for something He’s made you. You might like to repeat something every Friday evening so it becomes a family tradition.\
\
^[Family Journal]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
What have you learned about God and His Sabbath that helps you love Him more?\
What might the world be like now if everyone had always accepted God’s Sabbath gift and worshipped Him every week?\
^[Parent Faith Talk]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
Share some very special standout Sabbaths you have enjoyed. Invite your child to share a special Sabbath they remember. Brainstorm ideas together to celebrate an upcoming Sabbath in a different way or place, to create memories together. Think about inviting someone to your home and sharing a meal with them.\
\
^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
Create a Sabbath “lucky dip” of praise. Have each family member write down what they love about Sabbath on a small slip of paper. Fold up and pop in a jar. Take turns dipping in and sharing praise.\
\
^[Pray]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
Pray a popcorn prayer of thanks to God for His Sabbath, during which family members say one thing, in any order, and as many times as they like.
