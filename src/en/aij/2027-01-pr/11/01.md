Committed to Friendship

Thunder crashed, lightning flashed, and trumpet blasts echoed around the mountains. Then silence fell. God had finished speaking the Ten Commandments. The people were in awe at the impressive signs of His mighty power.

Staying at a distance, the people gazed in amazement as Moses headed back up the mountain. He disappeared into the swirling dark cloud where God was. While they trembled with fright at God’s presence, Moses was attracted to God. They didn’t really understand that for all his life Moses had known God as a faithful friend, and that his friendship with God was based on loving commitment.

God wanted to help the Israelites commit to the same kind of loving friendship with Him too. He also wanted to help them obey the Ten Commandments in their everyday lives. So God had called Moses up the mountain to explain His laws in more detail. Moses listened carefully as God told him how all people, including servants, widows, orphans, poor people, and animals, should be treated—with love and care. He told Moses how the Sabbath and yearly feasts were a time to be close to Him and celebrate His love and care for them.

When God had finished speaking, Moses clambered back down the mountain to share God’s instructions with the people. How did they respond? **Read Exodus 24:3.**

Moses wrote God’s instructions down and shared them with the people. Again they repeated their promise (Exodus 24:7). It was the same promise they had made before God spoke His Ten Commandments aloud from the mountain (Exodus 19:8).

Three times the people spoke words of commitment and obedience to all of God’s laws and instructions. But did they really understand what they were saying? Did they understand that true commitment can come only from hearts in which God lives? from hearts that love and respect Him? Only time would tell.
