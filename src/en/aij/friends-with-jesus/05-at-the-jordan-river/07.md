**^[Challenge:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994CC", "typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**

Go for an afternoon walk and collect some rocks.

Look for:

- **Surprising shapes.** Can you find one that’s shaped like something else, such as a heart?
- **Cool colors.** Look closely to find rocks that are slightly different colors or have different colors going through them.
- **Textured rocks.** Look for smooth, scratchy, bumpy, or jagged rocks.

Bring them home and wash them clean in a big tub of water. Put one or two favorites near your bed to remind you of what you've learned this week.
