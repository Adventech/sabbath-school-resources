---
title: Sheep-Walking
date: 03/10/2026
---

Sheep are intelligent animals, yet they’re known for instinctively following the other sheep around them, sometimes all the way to the slaughterhouse!

Even amid the tragic impacts of pandemics, wars, and other disasters, much of the world is still focused on self—consumerism, real estate prices, holidays, and parties. Yes, the world will keep looking to self, but where’s my focus? Should I just sleepily follow the world, or should my mind be awake and focused on something greater?

In 1 John 2:15, 16, we read, “Do not love the world or anything in the world. If anyone loves the world, love for the Father is not in them. For everything in the world—the lust of the flesh, the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life—comes not from the Father but from the world” (NIV).

It’s so easy just to follow the flock’s fears and fantasies, but should followers of Jesus be sleepwalking, or sheep-walking, in the ways of the world? No. We need to wake up.

We’re called to live out God’s ways of love, care, and compassion. God has raised us to be in the flock but not of the flock, in the world but not of the world, so we can lead people to hope and healing in these challenging times.

As we see in Romans 13:11, “The hour has already come for you to wake up from your slumber, because our salvation is nearer now than when we first believed” (NIV).

Let’s lift our eyes above the worries and the ways of this old world. Let’s follow the Good Shepherd and help other sheep follow Him.

### Appeal

As we now return God’s 10% tithe and give our Promise offerings, let’s recommit ourselves to turning away from following the crowd into the world’s self-centered ways. Let’s live generously and gratefully, just as God intended. Thank you.
