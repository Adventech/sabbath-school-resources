The forgiven, healed man now moved and walked through the crowd with happy, bouncy steps. His sad face had turned into one filled with joy and peace.

The crowd stared in amazement as they stepped back to create a path for him. On this day, they had seen with their own eyes that Jesus had “come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly” (John 10:10).

The man ran home. He had to find his family to tell them the good news! He now had life in all its fullness.

Imagine his family’s surprise when he ran through the door with a big smile on his face! There must have been many thank-You prayers that night. Their home had seemed dark with sadness but now it felt bright with a happiness that only Jesus could give.

Jesus loves to help you and your friends. He wants to forgive you and give you peace. Then you can be like the friends in this story and help others get to know Jesus. Your friendship might even help their whole family!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- What do you think the family said when the healed man walked in the door at home?
- How have you seen Jesus’ kindness in your life?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go for a joyful wander, walk, skip, jump, roll, or hop in nature. Stop somewhere along the way to thank Jesus for His friendship and how happy you are to know Him.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that He can make hearts happy with forgiveness and bodies well with healing.
