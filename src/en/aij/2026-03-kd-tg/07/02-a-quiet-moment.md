**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God’s Word is powerful. We know this is true, but do we live as though we believe it?

Many of us spend our free hours on other distractions and neglect to spend daily time in God’s living Word. We can neglect growing a deeper connection with our loving Savior, when He longs to give us both peace and power.

God speaks to us through His Word when we seek Him. Sometimes He encourages and speaks words of love; other times He rebukes or corrects; but always He tells the truth, for His Word is truth. We’re told, “You are near, O Lord, and all Your commandments are truth” (Psalm 119:151). In fact, so much of Psalm 119 tells us about the beauty of God’s Word being enduring, giving direction, making us wise, giving understanding, showing us God’s love, lighting the way before us, and giving hope, among other things. Take some time to read this beautiful psalm this week, which lies at the very center of the Bible.

Yes, Jesus, who is our perfect example in all things, knew the Scriptures and spent time learning from them. He quoted Scripture in response to Satan’s temptations, and He continued to refer to the Scriptures throughout His ministry.

This week you have a wonderful opportunity to share God’s Word with the children in your class. As you prepare, take some unrushed time to abide in His Word; to hear from the Lord. He always has something more to teach us.
