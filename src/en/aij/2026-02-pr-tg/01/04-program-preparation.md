Opening Activities

Source the **Paper People outline**.

Cut **pieces of paper** into four vertical strips. Make enough strips of paper for each child.

Visit the AIJ website for information about the location of this quarter’s Mission Offering. Print and create an **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**. Source a **globe** or **world map**. Print the **^[13 Mission Fast Facts]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** so you can share a **Mission Fast Fact** each week.

Write the **Big Idea** at the top of your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Picture Powerpoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display. (Do this every week.)

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **big jug/waterpot**. **Star** from last quarter. **Pot and bowl** for pretend stew, **basket of vegetables**.

Source objects for obstacle course to scatter around the room, i.e., **paper cups**, **cones**, or anything else available, and a **blindfold**.

Cut a large cardboard heart shape into four pieces. On each piece, write one of the following statements:

1. Jesus, help me to learn to love You and follow You.
2. Jesus, help me to wait for You to work things out in my life.
3. Jesus, help me to trust You.
4. Jesus, help me to pray to You when I need to make an important decision.

Tie It All Together

Source **^[Character Card 14: Jacob and Esau]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child. Provide a container, envelope, bag, or keyring for children to store these cards in the classroom this quarter.
