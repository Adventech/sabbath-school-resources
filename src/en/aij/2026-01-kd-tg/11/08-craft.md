**What you need:**

Extra-large paper plates, scissors, markers, craft glue, sticker jewels or printable jewels and shapes cut out of paper or contact paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/11/11a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Lightly fold paper plate in half and cut a line within the border.
- Fold the other way and cut another line.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/11/11b.png)

- Fold all four pieces out to create the four crown points.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/11/11c.png)

- Cut out small shapes from colored paper or contact paper.
- Print jewels to color from the AIJ website, if using.

**Create it together:**

- Stick the four jewels to each other’s crown while saying something kind to each other.
- Continue decorating the crown with markers and shapes.
