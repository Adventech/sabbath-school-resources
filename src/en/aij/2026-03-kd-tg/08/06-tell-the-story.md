**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. I will follow Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **peg people** to represent Jesus and His disciples.
- Invite children to count with you.
- Have **a net** with some **toy fish** to illustrate fishing.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 8 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) John the Baptist’s voice boomed across the Jordan River as he told the crowds of people some very exciting news. The promised Savior was here, on earth, right now!

John the Baptist’s face lit up with excitement as Jesus walked toward him. He pointed at Jesus ^[(point)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and said, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” (John 1:29, 30). John told the people that Jesus was the One he was talking about.

The next day as John the Baptist talked with two of his friends, Andrew and John, he again saw Jesus. He pointed at him and said, “Behold the lamb of God!” (John 1:36).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Andrew and John looked at Jesus, amazed. Here was the promised Savior! They stopped what they were doing and started following Jesus ^[(Andrew and John peg people to follow peg Jesus)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They wanted to be with Him. They wanted to listen and learn from Him.

Jesus now had two special followers!

After spending a whole wonderful day with Jesus, Andrew raced home. He couldn’t wait to tell his brother, Simon, about Jesus. “We have found the Messiah,” he said (John 1:41).

Simon wanted to meet Jesus—and he wanted to meet Him now! Together they rushed to find Jesus.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus smiled. He already knew Simon’s name, and his father’s name! And now He gave Simon a new name—Peter.

Now how many followers did Jesus have? ^[(Point to each peg person as you count.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One, two, three.

Joyfully, Jesus set out for Galilee. There He found a man named Philip ^[(peg person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). “Follow me,” Jesus said (John 1:43). And that’s just what Philip did!

How many followers did Jesus have now? ^[(Point to each peg person as you count.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One, two, three, four.

When you become a follower of Jesus, it’s impossible to keep it a secret. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Philip was so excited! He went to find his friend Nathanael (peg person). Nathanael was sitting under a fig tree, praying. Philip told Nathanael that he had found the Messiah— His name was Jesus!

Nathanael wasn’t so sure. “Come and see,” Philip told him (John 1:46).

Nathanael followed behind Philip to go and meet Jesus. Jesus smiled as soon as He saw Nathanael and told everyone he was a good person.

Nathanael was shocked. He’d never met Jesus before! “How do you know me?” he asked (John 1:48).

“I saw you when you were under the fig tree. That was before Philip told you about me,” Jesus replied (John 1:48, ICB).

Nathanael was amazed! Jesus knew all about his prayers under the fig tree. He must be the Messiah! He decided to follow Jesus too.

How many followers did Jesus have now? ^[(Count peg people.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One, two, three, four, five. Soon there would be more.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One morning Jesus and His followers were beside the lake. Lots of people had followed them. They came from all directions. They wanted to listen to Jesus’ stories. Jesus sat down in Peter’s fishing boat and taught the people.

Peter sat in the corner of the boat as Jesus preached. He sighed and hung his head. He had been fishing all night and had caught nothing. Not one single fish!

Finally Jesus finished preaching. He turned to Peter and said, “Take the boat into deep water. If you will put your nets in the water, you will catch some fish” (Luke 5:4, ICB). Peter hadn’t caught any fish the whole night. But he chose to listen to Jesus and follow His instructions.

Splash! The nets plopped into the water and sank down out of sight ^[(throw the net out)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Peter began to pull up the nets—but something was wrong! The nets were too heavy! He couldn’t lift them out of the water! ^[(Put toy fish in the net.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Peter called to his friends to help him. The fishermen couldn’t believe their eyes. There were so many flipping, flapping fish! It was a miracle!

Jesus said to Peter, “From now on you will catch men” (Luke 5:10). After pulling his boat onto the shore, Peter left it there and followed Jesus.
