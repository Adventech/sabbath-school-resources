If you look at nature, you can see clues everywhere that point to God as the true Creator. Here are just a couple of the many clues you can look for.

**Clue 1: Birds in Flight**

Go outside and find a bird that is resting on the ground or in a tree. Watch what that bird does when it wants to fly. Does it go running down a long runway the way an airplane does? No! It flaps its wings, and straight up it goes!

The people who design airplanes are very, very smart. But even the smartest person has not yet figured out how to invent a plane that can flap its wings. That tells us that whoever designed birds must be smarter than even the smartest airplane designer. Yes, God is an amazing designer!

**Clue 2: Pecking Woodpeckers**

Have you ever watched (or heard) a woodpecker pecking at a tree? A woodpecker must have a special beak so that it can poke a hole in the tree. If a different bird tried to peck as fast as a woodpecker does, its beak would break!

But that’s not all. Pecking as fast as a woodpecker does, any other bird would get a terrible headache. Why doesn’t a woodpecker get a headache? It has a special cushion in its head to protect its brain. A woodpecker can peck at super-fast speeds and not hurt its head at all!

Those who don’t believe in a Creator God say that the birds and animals changed very slowly to become what we see today. Does that make sense for the woodpecker? Imagine a woodpecker with a hard beak but no brain cushion. What would happen when the woodpecker tried to peck at a tree? It would hurt its brain! What about a bird with a brain cushion but no hard beak? Its beak would break. For a woodpecker to peck, it must have the extra-strong beak and the brain cushion created at the same time by a wise Creator God.

There are so many more things you can explore in nature to prove that God is our Creator. What other clues can you discover from the pictures on this page?
