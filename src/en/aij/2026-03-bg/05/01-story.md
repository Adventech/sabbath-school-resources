The Food and Water\
^[Surprise]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#48426C", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/05/01.png)

God’s people walked slowly through the hot, dusty desert. _Plod, plod, plod._ They weren’t very happy _(frown)._ They were hot! They were hungry! There were no gardens that grew food in the desert. There were no markets to buy food either. They stomped their feet. “We need food! We need water!” (Exodus 16:3, ICB). They grumbled to Moses _(grumble, grumble)._ Then they grumbled some more _(grumble, grumble)._ But the people didn’t need to grumble—God would take care of them.\
When the people woke up in the morning, they rubbed their sleepy eyes in surprise. They saw little white circles that looked like snow all over the ground. What is it? they wondered. Moses smiled. It was food! God had sent them food, like bread, from heaven _(point up)._ It had fallen like rain from the sky _(wiggle fingers like rain)._ Thank You, God! They picked it up and ate some. Mmm, it was yummy! It tasted sweet like honey. They called this food manna. _(Can you see the manna?)_

The people licked their dry lips. Oh, they were thirsty! There was no water in this part of the desert. Plod, plod, plod. They walked slowly on. It was hard work carrying clothes and tents on their backs. The cows were thirsty. _(Moo!)_ The sheep were thirsty too. _(Baa!)_ God knew everyone needed a drink.\
When the people stopped at some rocks, God told Moses to hit the rock with his walking stick. Suddenly a fresh, cool stream of water poured out of the rock like a waterfall. _Splish, splash, splosh!_ The people cheered! Now they didn’t have to be thirsty anymore. God was so kind to give them water.

God loved His people like a new daddy or mommy loves their precious baby _(pretend to hold a baby in your arms)._ He wanted to care for them and give them what they needed. He sent them food and water. God knows what you need, and He loves to look after you.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for looking after me!
