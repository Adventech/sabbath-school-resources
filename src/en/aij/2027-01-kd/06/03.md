The people huffed and puffed. They were so very thirsty. And grumpy. Finally they had found lots of water in the desert, but it was horrible! The water was so bitter that it made them screw up their faces. Yuck!

While the people worried and complained, Moses prayed. After Moses prayed to God about the bitter water, “the Lord showed him a tree” (Exodus 15:25). Moses picked up the tree and threw it into the water. “When he cast it into the waters, the waters were made sweet” (Exodus 15:25).

The people rushed to the sweet waters. They gulped and guzzled. Then they slurped and sipped some more. Oh, it was wonderful! God had given them miracle water. They must have been so thankful!

God also made a beautiful promise to them right there by the water. What was it? **Read Exodus 15:26.**

Their loving God wanted to keep them healthy and happy. If they obeyed and followed Him, they would be blessed.

God has also showed us how we can be healthy and happy by caring for our bodies and minds. He wants us to eat the wonderful fruits and vegetables He’s created, drink pure water, enjoy sunlight, sleep well each night, and have a grateful, trusting heart toward Him. When we follow God’s ways, we too can be blessed.

The last water container was filled. The people had one long last drink of sweet water from the fountain. Then the Israelites continued on their journey as they followed the cloud. God led them to a beautiful place called Elim, with 12 fresh springs of water and 70 shady palm trees. What a cool, restful place!

Even though their journey had sometimes been hard, God’s way was best. He can guide us through any problem too. We can be thankful that He is such a perfect leader!
