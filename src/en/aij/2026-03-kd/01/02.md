The time had come! The waiting was over! God was ready to send His Son to save the world. But first He would need a helper—someone to tell everyone that the Son of God was coming.

This helper would need parents who would teach him God’s ways. What parents did God chose? **Read Luke 1:5, 6.**

One day an angel suddenly appeared to faithful Zacharias while he was working in the temple.

“Do not be afraid, Zacharias,” the angel said kindly, “your wife Elizabeth will bear you a son, and you shall call his name John . . . . He will also be filled with the Holy Spirit” (Luke 1:13-15). The angel said that John would make people ready to meet the Lord.

Zacharias couldn’t believe it! He and his wife, Elizabeth, were very old; way too old to have children. “How can I know that what you say is true?” he asked doubtfully. “I am an old man, and my wife is old too” (Luke 1:18, ICB).

The angel told Zacharias that Elizabeth would have this promised baby. But because Zacharias doubted, he would not be able to talk until baby John was born.

Zacharias stepped outside the temple. He opened his mouth to speak to the people, but . . . nothing! No sound came out at all. Quietly Zacharias headed home with his wife, Elizabeth. He thought a lot about the angel’s words.

Sure enough, Elizabeth soon became pregnant with her little miracle baby. One day this baby would grow up to become God’s special helper. He would help people get ready for Jesus!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think God chose Zacharias and Elizabeth to be the parents of this special helper?
- Can you remember another husband and wife in the Bible whom God promised to give a baby to, even though they were very old? (Abraham and Sarah.) Zacharias should have remembered them too and not doubted God’s promise.
