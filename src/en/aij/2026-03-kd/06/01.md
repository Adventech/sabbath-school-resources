**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God who forgives me when I say I’m sorry.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Repent, for the kingdom of heaven is at hand!”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Matthew 3:2).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Sorry”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To be sorry is to feel bad about doing something wrong, and to wish you hadn’t done it. To say “I’m sorry” is to apologize for doing wrong. God asks us to be sorry for doing wrong and to ask Him for forgiveness.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Luke 3:1-22; John 1:4-34; Matthew 3; Mark 1:1-11; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 109-113.

Baby John

Zacharias smiled with pure joy. God had done the impossible! He had given this old priest and his old wife, Elizabeth, a baby.

He asked one of his friends to quickly pass him something to write on. He hadn’t been able to talk since the angel had visited him. The angel had promised this miracle baby. But Zacharias had doubted him. He had been silent ever since!

“His name is John,” Zacharias wrote (Luke 1:63).

John. Baby John. Just as the angel had instructed. Suddenly Zacharias could speak again! It was another miracle!

Their neighbors were amazed. They told their friends about baby John. And they told the story of the miracle child to their friends. Soon all the people in the towns around them were talking about baby John. “What kind of child will this be?” they wondered (Luke 1:66). They could tell that God was with him.

Zacharias knew! The angel had told him. He said to his son, “And you, child, will be called the prophet of the Highest; for you will go before the face of the Lord to prepare His ways” (Luke 1:76).

John would help everyone know that they could be saved. “They will be saved by having their sins forgiven” (Luke 1:77, ICB).

What happened to baby John? **Read Luke 1:80.**

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Would you liked to have visited this miracle baby? What might you have said to this little family?
- How would John “prepare” people to meet Jesus?
- How can you prepare your heart for Jesus?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to forgive you and make your heart ready for Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Spend 10 minutes not speaking. You’ll have to find creative ways to tell people what you need instead. What did you most want to do when the time was up?
