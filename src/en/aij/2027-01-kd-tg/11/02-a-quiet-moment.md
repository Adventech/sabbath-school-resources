**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It is estimated that the average adult makes about 35,000 choices every single day. From the clothes we wear to the words we speak, from the routes we drive to the thoughts we allow, there are so many choices, and most are made without a second thought. But beneath these daily decisions is a deeper choice to be all in for God or for self. As you read through this week’s lesson, may you hear His call once more: Choose Me.

Romans 12:2 says, “Be transformed by the renewing of your mind, that you may prove what is that good and acceptable and perfect will of God.” Transformation starts with surrender, then it grows through prayer, and flourishes in God’s Word. And it’s all guided by the Helper, the Holy Spirit, who walks beside us with every choice we make.

God doesn’t ask us to be perfect decision-makers. He just wants us to be humble as we seek Him with surrendered hearts. James 1:5 reminds us that when we lack wisdom, all we have to do is ask. And Philippians 2:13 gently assures us that it is God who works in us “both to will and to do for His good pleasure.”

“You cannot control your thoughts, your impulses, your affections. . . . But you can choose to serve Him. You can give Him your will” (Ellen G. White, _Steps to Christ_, p. 47).

When we do this, God reshapes our hearts and our wants. He more and more closely aligns our will with His wise and perfect will. So today, amid your 35,000 choices, start with surrender. Let this one choice shape every other one—and watch what God will do.
