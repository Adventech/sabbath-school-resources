The disciples waited in the boat, hoping Jesus would come with them. Finally they gave up and headed out across the lake as darkness closed in. As they rowed, they wondered, _Why did we listen to Jesus instead of staying and crowning Him king? Why didn’t Jesus show the people His kingly power?_

Jesus had done the impossible that day. He had healed the sick and fed the hungry crowds with a miracle. But the disciples had forgotten all that. Their disappointment and doubt swallowed up the memory of anything good.

Suddenly a violent wind blew across the lake. It turned the gentle ripples of water into thunderous waves that crashed across the boat. Once again, the disciples were in the middle of a big, scary storm!

Hour after frightening hour, the disciples fought the storm. Their strong arms pulled on the oars, but the storm was stronger. Well after midnight, the weary disciples gave up the struggle.

When they felt totally helpless, they remembered their Helper, Jesus! Grumpiness had taken the disciples’ focus away from Jesus, but the storm brought their focus back to Him. They remembered Jesus was the God of the impossible. He could save them in the storm.

Sometimes we may get grumpy, just as the disciples did. Jesus may not answer a prayer the way we want Him to, or His Word may give us advice we don’t like. When we stay grumpy, our focus on Jesus gets shaky and things sometimes go wrong. Troubles will often remind us how much we need Jesus. Then we can say, Lord “Unto You I lift up my eyes” (Psalm 123:1).
