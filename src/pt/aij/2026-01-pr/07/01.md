Depois the Fall

Nós Façan’t know como long isso was depois Abel died that Adam e Eve had another son, mas eles had other children. Later these children grew up e had children de seu own. Soon there were hundreds e thousands de people em the earth!

One de Adam e Eve’s children was Seth. Como old was Adam quando Seth was born, e o que Façaes the Bible Conte nos sobre Seth? **Leia Gênesis 5:3.** Although Adam e Eve had other children, isso was Seth’s family that Jesus would eventually be born into.

Adam lived para 930 years. Em this time ele faithfully told todos de seu children e grandchildren o que isso was like living em perfeito Eden, e sobre the sad dia eles had disobeyed Deus. Eles could even go a the gates de Eden a see the Anjos guarding the garden, e Adam’s family often worshipped there. Adam told the História de seu fall, como very important isso was a follow Deus, e the promise Deus had given a send Someone a deliver eles de seu bad choices. Mas not muitos people listened. Cain’s family became proud, mean, e selfish. Eles hurt themselves e others em terrible ways.

Deus loved cada Cantele one de Adam’s descendants. Mas em the same way that Ele wouldn’t Faça Adam e Eve obey Ele em the Garden de Eden, Ele also let people decide se eles wanted a be faithful a Ele ou not.
