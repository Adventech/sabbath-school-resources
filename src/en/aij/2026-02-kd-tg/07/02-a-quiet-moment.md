**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

In our uncertain world, bravery and courage are needed more than ever. Standing up for truth, facing an unknown diagnosis, speaking publicly, or trying something new all require us to act with courage. Sometimes even day-to-day activities, such as tackling a long to-do list, might need us to face the day bravely!

Fortunately, we don’t have to be brave on our own. When we commit our lives into God’s hands, He gives us the courage to face any situation with bravery. Trusting God makes our trials smaller. Nothing is too hard for our God, and He never leaves our side.

Do you need more courage to face a challenge? Fix your eyes on a wonderful, caring God who deeply loves you, who cries with you, hurts with you, walks with you, and knows you. If your heart feels heavy right now, these beautiful words are for you: “Wait on the Lord; be of good courage, and He shall strengthen your heart” (Psalm 27:14).

You can step forward bravely, with God’s strong hand in yours. Hear him tenderly say to you, “Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you” (Isaiah 41:10).

As you teach the children this week about God’s closeness through Joseph’s trials, may you know deep in your heart that He is with you in yours too.

“His grace is sufficient for all our trials; and although they are greater than ever before, yet if we trust wholly in God, we can overcome every temptation and through His grace come off victorious” (Ellen G. White, _Early Writings_, p. 46).
