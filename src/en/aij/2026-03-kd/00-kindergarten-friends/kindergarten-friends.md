In this special new quarter, you’ll learn about Jesus and some of the amazing things He did while He was on our earth. Jesus came to earth as a little baby and grew as a kind, obedient, thoughtful boy. If I’d lived at that time, I would have loved to be His friend. Would you?

As Jesus grew into a man, He was the very best teacher, preacher, and miracle worker. Everything He did was very good, and Jesus never sinned. Although Jesus became a person—like you and me—He is also God. Jesus did many miracles, some of which you’ll learn about this quarter. He met and helped many people, and He pointed everyone to God.

Jesus showed us what God is like. The Bible tells us that “The Son reflects the glory of God. He is an exact copy of God’s nature. He holds everything together with his powerful word.” (Hebrews 1:3, ICB).

Jesus is my God and He is your God too. Let’s get to know Him more together!
