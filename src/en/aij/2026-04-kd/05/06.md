One sunny day, Jesus walked the dusty road with His disciples to the town of Decapolis, near the sea. As soon as the town people saw their special Visitor, they quickly spread the exciting news. _It was Jesus! The kind Healer had come to their town today!_

Quickly . . . quickly! It wasn’t long before crowds of people gathered around Jesus. They brought their unwell mothers, limping fathers, and sick children to Jesus to be healed.

One man who was brought to Jesus “was deaf and could not talk” (Mark 7:32, ICB). His friends and family begged Jesus to put His hands on the man and heal him.

All Jesus needed to do was speak words and he would be healed, but this time Jesus did things differently. He wanted to be alone with the man who was deaf, so He gently led the man away from the busy, noisy crowd.

With kindness in His eyes, Jesus put His fingers in the man’s ears and touched the man’s tongue. Jesus looked up to heaven and took a deep breath. Then He said, “Be opened” (Mark 7:34).

Immediately, the man could hear and speak clearly.

Guess what the man heard first? He heard the kind voice of Jesus. Jesus loves to have quiet times with you too.

The healed man praised God and told everyone the story. They were all amazed! What did the people say about Jesus? **Read Mark 7:37.**

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think Jesus wanted to do this miracle away from the busy, noisy crowd?
- Where do you go to spend alone time with Jesus?
