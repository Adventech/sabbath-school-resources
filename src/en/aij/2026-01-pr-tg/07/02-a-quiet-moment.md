**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Noah. The name elicits images of a bearded builder, an ardent preacher, a man of single-minded resolve. At first glance he appears to be the lead actor in this dramatic narrative. But strip back the story layers, and we find an incredibly loving and faithful God whose heart is grieved by the enormity of sin and the cancerous hold it had on almost every human He so lovingly created. He wanted to save them all.

It is undoubtedly God who has the lead role. And Noah knew this. His relationship with his Father God was profoundly strong and deep; this trust was built on mutual love, quiet time spent in each other’s presence, and listening—really listening—to one another’s voices. Stated succinctly, “Noah walked with God” (Genesis 6:9).

And the result? Noah’s faithfulness to God’s call and plan.

Preach about rain without ever having seen it? Faithful.\
Build a massive boat in the mountains? Faithful.\
Endure rejection and ridicule? Faithful.\
Preach with few results? Faithful.

Unswervingly faithful.

As you and the children delve into the story of Noah together, may you be humbled and inspired by the faithfulness of both God and Noah. And more important, may the children’s hearts be drawn like magnets toward their loving Creator God, for only then will they pursue lives of faithfulness to their friend Jesus.
