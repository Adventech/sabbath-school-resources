God’s people collected sweet, fresh manna in the early-morning light, just as God had told them to. Day after day they collected just enough and watched the leftovers dissolve in the sunshine. After all, Moses had said, “Let no one leave any of it till morning” (Exodus 16:19).

But at sunup one day an awful stench wafted out a tent flap and into the camp. What was that foul smell? It didn’t take long to find out that it was a pot of rotten manna, crawling with wiggling maggots! Someone hadn’t listened, and had stored extra manna in their tent, instead of only collecting what was needed for that day. What a yucky way to be reminded that their God was trying to teach them to obey and depend on Him to feed them every day.

Then Moses gave God’s people a special Friday message. He said, “Tomorrow is a Sabbath rest, a holy Sabbath to the Lord” (Exodus 16:23). Moses went on to tell the people to collect double the amount of manna, cook it, and store half of it for Sabbath, because no manna would fall on God’s holy day.

Puzzled, the Israelites obeyed. Would their Friday manna go smelly and rotten? Or wouldn’t it? They found out in the morning. What happened? **Read Exodus 16:24.**

What an amazing way to be reminded about God’s gracious Sabbath blessings! It had been hard for the Israelites to keep the Sabbath as slaves in Egypt, but now they could rest and spend time with God on His special day in the wilderness.

From then on, God’s three miracles reminded them every week of His gracious provisions. First, His “angels’ food” (Psalm 78:25) fell from heaven six days a week. Second, twice as much fell on Fridays. And third, the extra manna on Friday stayed fresh on Sabbath. Three beautiful manna-miracle reminders.
