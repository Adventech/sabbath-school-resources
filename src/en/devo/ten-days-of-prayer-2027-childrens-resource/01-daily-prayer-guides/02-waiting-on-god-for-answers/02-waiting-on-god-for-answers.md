### I Can Trust God While I Wait

> <p></p>
> “I, Daniel, understood from the Scriptures the number of years that the Lord had spoken about through the prophet Jeremiah.” Daniel 9:2 (ICB)

Have you ever had to wait for something you really wanted? Maybe you waited for a birthday, a special trip, or a gift. Waiting can be hard because we want things to happen right away, as soon as possible.

Daniel also had to wait. Daniel loved God and trusted His promises, but there were things he did not understand. He wanted to know when God would fulfill His promise to His people.

What did Daniel do while he waited? He studied God’s Word and prayed.

Daniel learned that God had not forgotten His people. God knew the right time, and He would do what He promised.

Sometimes we pray and ask God for something, but the answer does not come quickly. We may wonder, “God, are You listening?” But God always hears us. He knows what is best, and He works at the right time.

While we wait, we can pray, read the Bible, and remember that God loves us. Waiting helps us learn to trust Him more.

God kept His promise to Daniel, and God keeps His promises to us, too.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What is something you have had to wait for? How can you trust God while you wait?

> <p>Remember</p>
> I can trust God while I wait!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You for always keeping Your promises. Help me trust in You when I have to wait. Teach me to pray, listen, and believe that You are working for me. Amen.
