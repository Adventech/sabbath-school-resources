**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Ponder this: Jesus simply offered up a prayer of thanks to His heavenly Father, and then fed a crowd of 5,000 men, plus women and children, with just five small barley loves and two small fish. What an amazing miracle! Jesus supplied their needs in a profound and abundant way. God has also promised to supply our needs--the needs of our body, heart, and soul.

Our Father God cares for our health. He created seeds to grow into an abundance of fruits and vegetables. He sends the sun and rain to help them grow--all for our benefit. There are places and seasons where finding food, or healthy food, is incredibly challenging. Trust in God’s care and claim His promises. “And my God shall supply all your need . . .” Each day, regardless of where in the world we live, He supplies us with the food we need. And we can thank Him for this. The Bible says, “And my God shall supply all your need according to His riches in glory by Christ Jesus” (Philippians 4:19).

Just as Jesus supernaturally provided food for the crowds, He can also supernaturally supply us with food for our empty or broken hearts through His Word. When we ask the Holy Spirit to lead us, His Word will fill our innermost soul and permeate us. It gives us food—spiritual nourishment and strength to face the challenges of life.

Jesus has always been willing to supply our needs, both body and soul. But the choice is ours to accept His help, just as the disciples did.

As you and the children explore this week’s story together, may you feel loved knowing that our God is willing to supply all your physical needs and all your spiritual needs too.
