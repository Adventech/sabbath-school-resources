**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He gives me everything I need.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“And my God shall supply all your need”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Philippians 4:19)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Supply”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Supply means to provide, or to give what is needed. When someone gives you lunch, they supply food. God gives us what we need because He loves us. God supplies us with many things, like rain, sunshine, fresh air, and the food we need to live.

A New Job

His frown was gone. His worries were gone too. Pharaoh stood tall and smiled at his new governor, Joseph. Joseph’s God had told him the meaning of his strange dreams. And now this wise man and his wise God would help save his kingdom.

“Look! I have put you in charge of all the land of Egypt” the king said to Joseph (Genesis 41:41, ICB).

Pharaoh took off his royal ring and slid it onto Joseph’s finger. This ring was like Pharaoh’s signature. With this ring, Joseph could do anything the king would do!

With a clap of his hands, Pharaoh called for his servants. Quickly they ran to get everything he asked for. Then they dressed Joseph in fancy robes and put a shiny golden chain around his neck.

Joseph didn’t look like a slave or a prisoner anymore. He had been given the power of a king, and now he looked like one too. What else did the king do so that all the people would know Joseph was an important leader? **Read Genesis 41:43.**

Pharaoh gave Joseph all the things he would need to be a leader. God would supply all the wisdom he would need to be a good leader. God had helped him when he’d been thrown in a pit, sold as a slave, and put in prison. He would help him now too.

Joseph looked at his chariots and horses, his army of helpers, and his smiling king. He knew the truth of our memory verse: “And my God shall supply all your need” (Philippians 4:19).
