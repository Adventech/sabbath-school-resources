Angry grumbles rumbled through the camp. Laban’s sons were not happy! “Jacob has taken everything our father owned,” they complained to each other. “Jacob has become rich in this way” (Genesis 31:1, ICB).

Jacob did have lots and lots of strong, healthy, spotty animals. But he hadn’t taken them! God had done an amazing miracle and blessed him.

Jacob heard their jealous whispers. He noticed Laban and his sons becoming less friendly. God saw this too and spoke to Jacob. What did God say? **Read Genesis 31:3.**

Jacob secretly called Leah and Rachel out into the far field. He told them about a dream God had given him. “The angel of God spoke to me in that dream. He said, ‘Jacob. . . . I have seen the wrong things Laban does to you. . . . Now I want you to leave here. Go back to the land where you were born’ ” (Genesis 31:11-13, ICB).

Rachel and Leah had also seen their father’s greediness and lying. They agreed to go with Jacob back to his home in Canaan.

But they would need to escape when Laban wasn’t watching. One day, when Laban was in a faraway field, they saw their chance. Quickly they packed their bags and rounded up their animals. Then Jacob, his wives, and children climbed onto their camels and raced off in a cloud of dust. God would lead them home!
