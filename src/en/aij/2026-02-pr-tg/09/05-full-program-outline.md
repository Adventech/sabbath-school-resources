**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Hand out some small slices of fresh lemon for the children to try. Ask them how it tastes (sour!). Share that sometimes things happen in life that we don’t like. They can be very sour experiences.

- **What are some examples of “sour” times?** (Getting hurt, a toy breaks, we get in trouble for something, etc.)

Share that being friends with our sweet God makes all the difference in how we get through these sour times. When we trust God and spend time with Him as our friend, He can change our characters to be sweet, like His.

- **What are some words to describe a sweet character?** (Helpful, kind, giving, patient, cheerful.)

Remind the children that these are words that describe our Best Friend, God. When we spend time with Him, we become like Him.

Mix the lemons with some water and sugar/honey. Pour the water into some cups for the children to taste. Explain that you can enjoy more lemonade together after Sabbath School. God has the power to change us and can make the sour times turn sweet.

Share that in today’s true story from the Bible we will learn more about Joseph. (Point to the Wall Mural and briefly share an overview of Joseph’s life for the visitors who may not know the story.) Joseph patiently trusted God, and God helped him to be kind and patient throughout his time in prison. God used Joseph to show others that He is real and cares about everyone.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **God is real, and He works in my life to show this truth to others.**

Our story today is about when God helped Joseph to interpret dreams, showing that God is real and was working in Joseph’s life. He wants to work in your life too.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}}) Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Place the Animal Trait Cards on the floor in front of the children.

- **Of these characteristics, which ones would you use to describe Joseph?** (Invite each child to select one.) Are there any that do not describe Joseph?

Ask the children to read and show the card they chose and discuss:

- **When and how did Joseph show this character trait?**

Then ask all the children:

- **Why do you think Joseph had these character traits? What made him different from those around him?** (He was friends with God. God’s love was in Joseph’s heart, changing him to be like God.)

Share that we can learn about God through His Word, the Bible. Write the following verse on the board and invite the children to read it together: **Philippians 2:13:** “Yes, God is working in you to help you want to do what pleases him. Then he gives you the power to do it” (ICB).

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Explain that God’s character is perfect. When we make Him our loving friend, He starts to change our hearts. (Show the children a bowl of water. Add some red food coloring as you talk, demonstrating that He slowly makes our lives different when we come to know Him.) He can give us the power to do the right thing and have characters like His. God can help us to have open eyes for the people around us so that we can see where they need help.
