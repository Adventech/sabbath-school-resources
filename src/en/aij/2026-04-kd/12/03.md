On this particular day, the little village of Nain was not so . . . lovely. People wailed loudly, as they walked slowly beside the stretcher. They cried and walked. Everyone was sad to say goodbye to the young man who had died.

Jesus and His friends watched on, feeling sad too. The young man’s mother didn’t even notice Jesus and the other visitors watching. Her eyes were so flooded with tears that she could barely see where her next step was.

Jesus moved close beside her, and with comforting words said, “Don’t cry” (Luke 7:13, ICB).

The woman looked up at the Man with the kind voice. She watched as He stepped closer to her son. Jesus reached out and gently placed His hand on the stretcher.

Everyone stopped suddenly in surprise. The people who had been wailing loudly went quiet. Not a whisper could be heard.

Jesus looked with love at the young man who was lying completely still and peaceful. It was like he was in a deep, deep sleep. He didn’t breathe. He couldn’t think or move. The Bible says, “The dead know nothing” (Ecclesiastes 9:5).

Jesus, our powerful Creator, leaned in close to the lifeless man. Then He spoke these words: “Young man, I tell you, get up!” (Luke 7:14, ICB).

- Do you think the young man will get up? Why?
