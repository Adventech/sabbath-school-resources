**Big Idea:** God notices my needs.

**TELL THE STORY**

Settling into her new home in Bethlehem, Ruth knew she needed to provide food for herself and Naomi. They had come back from Moab as poor widows, with no land, work, or money. What would they do?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Ruth noticed the farmers harvesting the beautiful fields of ripe barley and wheat. Ah-ha! With a bright idea in mind, she rushed to Naomi. She asked, “Let me go to the fields. Maybe someone will be kind and let me gather the grain he leaves in his field” (Ruth 2:2, ICB). This was called “gleaning.”

Naomi knew this was God’s way of providing for them, because He had given Israel a law that said, “Do not harvest all the way to the corners of your fields. If grain falls onto the ground, don’t gather it up” (Leviticus 19:9, ICB).

So in the morning Ruth found a field and began to pick up the leftover grain. Soon Boaz, the landowner, arrived. He noticed Ruth and asked his workers who she was. Then he walked over and spoke kindly to her.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Boaz say? Read Ruth 2:8, 9.** (He told her not to glean in another field, but to stay in his field. He would make sure she was safe and provide her with water.) **How can we show similar attentiveness to others in need?**

Boaz had noticed how much Ruth, a foreigner, loved and trusted God. He told her the Lord would reward her for her kindness to Naomi. He said, “You have come to [God] as a little bird finds shelter under the wings of its mother” (Ruth 2:12, ICB). He knew God would protect and provide for her.

Boaz wanted to protect Ruth too. He told her to pick up grain only in his fields so she would be safe. Then secretly he told his men to drop lots of grain for her to pick up ^[(show a bowl of grain)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At the end of the day Ruth rushed home with a big heavy sack, full of grain ^[(big sack)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Naomi’s mouth dropped open in surprise! She asked, “Where have you gleaned today?” (Ruth 2:19).

Ruth told her mother-in-law all about Boaz’s kindness. Clapping her hands, Naomi told Ruth to keep going to Boaz’s field every day. God had noticed them and blessed them!

One day Naomi noticed that Ruth needed a new husband. Boaz would be the perfect man! He was kind and caring. He was also her husband’s closest relative.

Naomi told Ruth about the law in Israel to protect widows. It said that if a woman’s husband died before they had a son, she could ask her husband’s closest relative to buy the land that had belonged to her husband. If the relative bought the land, he would also marry her.

There was a very unusual custom Ruth would need to follow to ask Boaz to become her husband.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Naomi tell Ruth to do? Read Ruth 3:3, 4.** (To wear her best garment, and go secretly to the threshing floor. When Boaz lay down, she was to uncover his feet and lie down and wait until he told her what to do.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) That night Ruth crept to where Boaz slept on a bed of straw, lay down, and uncovered his feet. Then she waited . . .

In the night Boaz woke up and turned over. Suddenly he noticed a woman lying in the darkness at his feet. He was so surprised! “Who are you?” he asked (Ruth 3:9).

Ruth answered softly, “I am Ruth, your maidservant.” Boaz understood why she had come. Yes, he wanted to marry her! He was so happy she had noticed him and chosen him to care for her.

There was a relative who was closer to Naomi. In the morning he would find him and talk to him.

Before daylight Ruth quietly crept home. She soon had good news. The other relative had decided not to buy the land and marry her. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Boaz was free to marry Ruth! Now he would always be with her to love and protect her, as would our faithful God.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In time God gave them a baby boy they named Obed. Obed became the father of Jesse, and Jesse the father of King David. And one day Jesus would be born into her family! You never know how an act of kindness that you show toward others in need might lead to a great blessing in the future. How wonderful that so many needs were met for Ruth, Naomi, and even Boaz!
