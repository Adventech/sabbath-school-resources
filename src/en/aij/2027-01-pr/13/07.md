All of the beautiful pieces of furniture in the sanctuary symbolized something that Jesus does for all people. But none of these things could happen if no one brought a lamb into the courtyard to die for people’s sins. It was hard to see a perfect, innocent little lamb die, but this was the only way sin could be dealt with. It reminded everyone about how horrible sin is, and how there was no other way to get rid of sin from their lives. One day God’s Son, Jesus, would come to the earth to die like a lamb for everyone who wanted their sins to be forgiven. Jesus was perfect, and He would die so that people could choose to love and live in heaven with Him one day. It’s all so amazing, and so true.

Today we don’t need to offer lamb sacrifices, because Jesus has already died and risen. In many ways it’s easier for us, because we don’t have to see innocent lambs die. But in other ways it’s harder, because many people forget how horrible sin is, and how precious Jesus’ sacrifice is.
