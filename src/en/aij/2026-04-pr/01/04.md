The battered boat rocked from side to side as the disciples held on tight. The storm on the Sea of Galilee was getting wilder! Waves crashed over the side and water pooled at their feet, as thunder boomed overhead.

Wet and whipped by the wind, the terrified disciples suddenly remembered that Jesus was in the boat! They peered into the darkness and called His name.

A flash of lightning lit up the sky, and they all saw Him. The disciples stared with wide eyes; some gasped in amazement. “Jesus was sleeping” (Matthew 8:24, ICB). There He lay, with His eyes closed and His head down, sleeping peacefully in the middle of the wild storm.

God knew why Jesus could sleep peacefully through the storm, for His Word says, “When you lie down, you will not be afraid; yes, you will lie down and your sleep will be sweet” (Proverbs 3:24). Jesus trusted God completely. And God was with Him, right through that scary storm.

Our friend Jesus shows us that we can trust God to be with us, right in the middle of our storms and scary times too. God doesn’t always take our problems away. But He has promised that He will always be with us through our scary times, helping us to be brave. Psalm 46:1, 2 says, “God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. Therefore we will not fear, even though the earth be removed, and though the mountains be carried into the midst of the sea.” We can be like Jesus. We can trust God and rest peacefully.
