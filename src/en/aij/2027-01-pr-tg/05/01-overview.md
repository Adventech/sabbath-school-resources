**Lesson Summary**

As the Israelites journeyed through the harsh desert, God led them with a pillar of cloud by day and pillar of fire by night, showing His constant presence and care. When Pharaoh’s army trapped them at the Red Sea, the Israelites panicked—but Moses trusted God. God miraculously parted the sea, protected His people, and destroyed their enemies. Through it all God proved He was not only their protector but their most trustworthy leader, guiding them with wisdom and love.
