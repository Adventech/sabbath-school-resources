**Opening Activities**

Print **pictures of the children** in your class or source *paper* and **colored pencils** or **markers** for them to draw themselves. Source **string** and a **hole punch**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: Draw around a plate to create **a “standing circle”** template. Trace and cut out on **cardstock**—one for each child. Source **music** (instrument or device).

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props: small white dot stickers, brown play-dough.** (This can be made ahead using a homemade playdough recipe. Add five drops red, two drops yellow, and one drop of blue food coloring to make it brown.)

Source **peg people**: Jesus, three men. You could also use a **felt set** and the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box: large heart, picture of Jesus, box of Band-Aids** (plasters).

**Nature/Mission**

Print a **^[Nature Collector Card 44: Mexican Axolotl Salamander]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Print **^[happy and sad faces]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, one set for each child, and source **craft sticks**. Glue a happy face on one side of each craft stick and a sad face on the other.
