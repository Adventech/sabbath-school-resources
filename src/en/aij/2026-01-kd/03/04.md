Four days to go . . . Three days to go . . . Two days to go . . . Just one day until Sabbath! Every week Adam and Eve would count down the days until the very best day—Sabbath. Oh, how Adam and Eve looked forward to their special time with God!

For six days Adam and Eve were busy, busy, looking after the garden and animals. But God’s holy Sabbath was a day of rest. Adam and Eve did all their work before Sabbath, so they could be ready for it. Adam finished all his jobs early. Eve gathered extra delicious food, such as cashews, sweet mangoes, and strawberries. They didn’t want anything to distract them from their special time with God!

The Bible tells us that if we pay attention to God on Sabbath and not our own busy things, our Sabbaths will be . . . what? **Read Isaiah 58:13, 14.**

God must have smiled when He saw Adam and Eve keeping the Sabbath special because of their love for Him. God has said, “Keep my Sabbaths holy. They will be a sign between me and you. Then you will know that I am the Lord your God” (Ezekiel 20:20, ICB).
