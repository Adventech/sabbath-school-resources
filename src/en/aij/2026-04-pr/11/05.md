Jesus’ heart must have filled with joy when He heard the centurion declare his faith in Him. He knew that this man had grown up believing in the idols of Rome, and that he had only recently heard of the one true God. Yet he believed that Jesus was the Savior.

Jesus wouldn’t let such amazing faith go unnoticed. The Jewish leaders, the centurion’s friends, and the whole crowd needed to hear about the centurion’s wonderful faith!

Jesus turned to the crowd and said, “Assuredly, I say to you, I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel!” (Matthew 8:10). Wow! This “unlikely” friend of the Jews had more faith than anyone else, the Jews included! These words were meant to make people think. Jesus wanted the Jews to give up believing they would be saved because they had faithful ancestors, or because they kept all the rules. He hoped they would accept Him as their personal Savior instead.

Then Jesus spoke to the centurion, loudly enough for everyone to hear. What did Jesus say? **Read Matthew 8:13, your memory verse.**

At that very second the centurion’s servant was healed!

When the centurion got home, his servant looked and sounded very different. God’s power had surged through his body and made him well.

Jesus loves to respond to our faith too. He loves to answer our prayers, change our hearts, and give us peace. Jesus can do all this and more if we will put our faith in Him. “All things are possible to him who believes” (Mark 9:23).
