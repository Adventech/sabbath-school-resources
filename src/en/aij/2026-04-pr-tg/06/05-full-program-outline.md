**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) Hold or wear a prop that you might use when sick.** Invite the children to stand up and come to the middle of your room or area. Explain that you have some questions for them and **would like them to answer by moving to the left or right of the room, following your hand signals.** (Point to each side as you read the two options.) When you are sick, do you prefer to:

- **sleep or read?**
- **stay in bed or be in the living room?**
- **drink cool water or a warm drink?**
- **go to a doctor in a hospital or have them visit your home?**
- **have your parents look after you or your grandparents look after you?**
- **go to school or stay home?**
- **take good-tasting medicine or yucky-tasting medicine?**

**Do you find being sick boring or interesting?**

Share that the woman in today’s story had been sick for 12 years. She had tried everything—perhaps cool water, warm teas, staying home, going out, all the doctors, and lots of yucky-tasting medicine—but she was still sick. Let’s discover what she does next. Our Big Idea may give us a clue.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus can heal me when I reach out to Him.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Place some **tape high on a wall** that is very difficult for the children to touch unless they jump. Invite the children to take turns to try to reach the mark.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Was it easy to reach up and touch the mark?** (No.) **For those who could reach it, what did you have to do to be able to touch it?** (Reach up as high as you could or jump while reaching up.)
- **What is the lesson for us in this story?** (To persist, especially when it comes to asking Jesus for help.)

Then hold up the hem of an article of clothing or some cloth.

- **Was there something special about Jesus’ clothes that healed the woman?** (No. Her faith and God’s power healed her.)
- **Would she have been healed if she had given up?** (Maybe not.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus gave the sick woman a “more abundant” life (John 10:10). He wants to do that for us too. He invites us to show our trust in His power to change, help, and heal us, by asking Him—and not giving up. He sees our faith, and He will never ignore us.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Give each child a sticky note. Ask:

- **Is there something that feels difficult in your life right now?**

It might be something small or something big. We might need healing in our bodies or our minds, or we might have someone close to us who needs this. Living in a world full of sin, we have many opportunities to have faith in Jesus to help us.

Give each child a small piece of paper (or sticky note) and invite them to draw a small picture or symbol as a reminder to have faith that Jesus can help and heal when we reach out to Him. They can place this in their Bibles. Pause briefly and invite the children to say a silent prayer to remember this.

- **How can you help them reach out to God?**

This week’s Mission Challenge is to: **Listen to Adventist World Radio with your family. Prepare to share about a place where God is using global missionaries.** Perhaps you’ll find a story about missionaries who helped others feel better or who showed others that God gives us a better life when we know Him.

Invite the children to echo each answer that another child says. (Child: “Jesus cares for me.” Class: “Jesus cares for you,” etc.)
