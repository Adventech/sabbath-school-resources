Before Mary and Joseph knew it, their small Boy began growing bigger, taller, and more mature. He turned 5! Jesus’ fifth birthday was probably very different from yours, but it was very special too. When He was young, Jesus started to worship in the synagogue (church) on Sabbath. Jesus learned from the songs, prayers, and Bible scroll readings in the synagogue. But most of all, He loved to worship His Father-God there.

Jesus didn’t go to school. His mother taught Him to read and study the Scriptures in their humble home. The words came from His mother’s lips and in the written Bible scrolls, but they were really God’s words. God was His teacher.

What a good student Jesus was! He learned so well from God and His mom that when He grew up, people were amazed at His knowledge. What did they say? **Read John 7:14, 15.**

When Jesus taught the people when He was older, He wasn’t just sharing His knowledge of the Bible scrolls. He had become wise as He matured through childhood, so He could teach them how to use the knowledge for good in their lives.

Jesus invites us to let Him be our teacher too. What kind of teacher is Jesus? **Read Matthew 11:29, 30.**

Jesus was a gentle but hardworking student as He grew up and matured. He remembers what learning in our world was like, so He is the perfect teacher. Why not trust Him to teach you to be more like Him?

As Jesus grew and matured, He loved to worship God on the Sabbath, learn new things from His parents and the Bible scrolls, and wisely share what He learned with others. We can ask God to help us follow His example.
