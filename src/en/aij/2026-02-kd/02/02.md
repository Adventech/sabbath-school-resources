_Oh no!_ Rebekah’s eyes opened wide. Isaac was going to give the family blessing to Esau—today!

Rebekah’s heart began to pound. _The blessing must be given to Jacob, not Esau._ She raced off to find Jacob. She would fix this—her way! “Listen,” she whispered to her younger son, “I heard your father talking to your brother Esau” (Genesis 27:6, ICB). She told Jacob about the blessing Esau was about to be given.

“So obey me, my son. Do what I tell you,” Rebekah said to Jacob (Genesis 27:8). She asked him to bring her two goats. “I will prepare them just the way your father likes them,” she explained. “Then you will take the food to your father. And he will bless you before he dies” (Genesis 27:10, ICB).

Jacob couldn’t believe it. His mother was telling him to pretend to be Esau and lie to his father. Jacob wasn’t sure it was a good idea. He looked down at his arms, “My brother Esau is a hairy man. I am smooth! If my father touches me, he will know I am not Esau. Then he will not bless me” (Genesis 27:11,12, ICB). But Rebekah had a plan to trick old, blind Isaac.

What does the Bible say about lying? **Read 1 Peter 3:10.**

Not once did Rebekah pray and ask God for help. Oh dear, things are only going to go from bad to really bad without God helping and guiding.
