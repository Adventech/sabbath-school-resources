**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The story of the Tower of Babel is a sobering one. When we stare into its details, we see that pride and selfishness fueled the arrogance, defiance, and self- centeredness of Babel’s people.

“The Tower of Babel represented doubt of God’s Word and defiance of His will” (_The SDA Bible Commentary_, vol. 1, p. 285). “The whole undertaking was designed to exalt still further the pride of its projectors and to turn the minds of future generations away from God and lead them into idolatry” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 119).

Pride is a sin that still grips us. We often underestimate the damage it causes.

Now is the time to teach humility. Although they are egocentric by nature, now is the time to encourage and teach our little ones to be aware of humility so that they will come to say, _God’s way is my way_.

Humility allows us to be taught by God’s Spirit; to grow and be gradually transformed to be more like Him.

May our prayer be: _Give us all humility, Lord. And may it start with me._
