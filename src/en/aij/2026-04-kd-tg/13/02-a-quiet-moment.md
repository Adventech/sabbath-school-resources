**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The Bible contains countless miracle stories in which God answered immediately. But there are also others—such as Jesus’ prayer in Gethsemane and Lazarus’ sickness—in which God didn’t appear to respond right away. Yet each time, God did answer and act.

When Jesus prayed in Gethsemane, God answered no, yet He strengthened Jesus for the trial He went through. When Lazarus was sick, Jesus delayed so that His power could be wondrously displayed, providing hope for all. Our God always sees the full picture, although we see only a few scenes.

“Had Christ been in the sickroom, Lazarus would not have died; for Satan would have had no power over him. Death could not have aimed his dart at Lazarus in the presence of the Life-giver. Therefore Christ remained away. He suffered the enemy to exercise his power, that He might drive him back, a conquered foe. He permitted Lazarus to pass under the dominion of death; and the suffering sisters saw their brother laid in the grave. . . . He suffered every pang of sorrow that they endured. He loved them no less because He tarried; but He knew that for them, for Lazarus, for Himself, and for His disciples, a victory was to be gained” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 528).

Oh, what a victory He gained for you, for the children in your Sabbath School, and for all who put their trust in Him! For “Jesus said to her, ‘I am the resurrection and the life. He who believes in Me, though he may die, he shall live’ ” (John 11:25).

Like Mary and Martha, may you and the children be encouraged to trust Jesus even when you don’t understand how He is moving. And may you look forward to seeing Him soon, as He holds out a victory crown to you, and the gift of eternal life with Him.
