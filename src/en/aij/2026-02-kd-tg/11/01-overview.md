**Lesson Summary**

The famine continued in Egypt and Canaan, and Joseph’s brothers were forced to return to Egypt to buy more grain. The brothers, including Benjamin, were reunited with Simeon and bowed before Joseph, just as Joseph’s dream had shown many years before. Joseph tested them again to see if they had changed. He gave Benjamin more food, and the brothers weren’t jealous. He then planted a silver cup in Benjamin’s bag. As punishment for theft, Benjamin was to become a slave. Judah volunteered to take the punishment. Joseph saw that God had changed their hearts. He then revealed who he was and told his brothers they were forgiven. God had used Joseph’s experiences to save his family and many others. Pharaoh invited the family to live in Egypt, and Joseph was finally reunited with his father and brothers.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can bring good from any situation.
- **Feel** confident to trust God in both good and bad times.
- **Respond** by asking God to always be their guide.
