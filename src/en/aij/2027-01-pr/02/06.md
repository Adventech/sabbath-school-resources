God’s voice rang loud and clear from the burning bush. Moses would be the rescuer! All along God had been trying to teach Moses that he just needed to rely on God’s awesome power instead of his own. But still Moses didn’t feel as if he could do it. He said, “Please, Lord, send someone else” (Exodus 4:13, ICB).

God listened to Moses and said, “Your brother Aaron, from the family of Levi, is a skilled speaker. He is already coming to meet you. . . . I will tell you what to say. Then you will tell Aaron. I will help both of you know what to say and do” (Exodus 4:14,15, ICB). What encouraging words from God! Even though Moses was still learning to trust God, God would help him. He already had his brother on the way.

Moses submitted willingly to God, picked up his shepherd’s rod, and went to gather his family together. After saying goodbye to his father-in-law, he left his home and life as a shepherd behind him. It was time to return to Egypt.

After a long journey, Moses spied his brother, Aaron, in the distance. What a happy reunion that was, after 40 years apart! What did Moses tell Aaron? **Read Exodus 4:28.** Together they traveled on to Egypt: two brothers who would soon begin a big, BIG work for God.

When God asks us to do something for Him, He will give us strength and help to do it too. You can say “Yes!” to His plans. “Trust in the Lord with all your heart, and lean not on your own understanding; in all your ways acknowledge Him, and he shall direct your paths” (Proverbs 3:5, 6).
