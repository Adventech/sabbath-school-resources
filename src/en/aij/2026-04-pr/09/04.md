The man walked down the cobblestone streets of Jerusalem, smiling with every bouncy step, and praising God. He was healed!

Meeting some Pharisees, the man couldn’t help stopping and telling them of the wonderful miracle. What did they say? **Read John 5:10.**

They should have been leaping with joy and praising God too, but instead the Jewish leaders were worried only about Jesus breaking their made-up rules about Sabbath.

In his excitement, the healed man had completely forgotten it was the Sabbath! But he wouldn’t let the Pharisees make him feel guilty for carrying his bedroll on God’s special day. While he didn’t know who had healed him, he knew God had given Him the power to do it. Obeying the Healer was all that mattered to him, so he boldly said, “The man who made me well said to me, ‘Take up your bed and walk’ ” (John 5:11).

The Pharisees didn’t like his answer, so they asked another question: “Who is the Man who said to you, ‘Take up your bed and walk’?” (John 5:12). Of course, he didn’t know. But the Pharisees did. They were just looking for a reason to get Jesus into trouble. They didn’t see God’s lovingkindness for a lonely, suffering man. They saw only broken rules.

Like the healed man, we may not always be able to answer everyone’s questions about Jesus, but we can confidently share what Jesus has done for us. Even if others don’t believe us, no one can take away our story and our joy.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- **Read Exodus 20:8-11.** How is God’s way of keeping the Sabbath different from the Pharisees’ rules?
- How has Jesus brought you joy and happiness? Your answer can be part of your “story” (testimony).
