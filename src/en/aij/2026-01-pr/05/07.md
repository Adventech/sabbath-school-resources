There are more than 73,000 different types of trees in the world! Do you know that trees can help us understand God’s promises?

There are two main types of trees: deciduous and evergreen. In countries where it gets cold in winter, deciduous trees lose all their leaves during the cold part of the year. If a deciduous tree does not lose its leaves, then it will freeze in winter. Sap is what feeds the leaves. During the fall (autumn) the sap runs down to the roots, where it is stored for the winter. If the sap stayed up in the trunk and branches, then the tree would freeze. If you live in a country where it snows, your water bottle would freeze if you left it outside on an icy-cold winter day. Then during the spring the sap runs back up the tree trunk and through the branches to feed the buds that will turn into green leaves again!

Do you cry when the trees lose their leaves? No, of course not, because you know that there will be new green leaves again. You know this because you have seen it happen before. It is a promise of new life.

But did you know that Adam and Eve cried when they saw the first dead leaf fall to the ground? It broke their hearts to see the effect of their choice not to trust God. They knew that God had promised to make it right by sending His Son to save the world, but the leafless trees clearly brought to mind the sad fact that death would now come to every living thing.

We too have to see many sad things because of sin. But we live with the same promise—Jesus came once to die for our sins, and He will come again to take us home to heaven! The next time you see trees lose their leaves, you may be reminded about how terrible sin is. But think too about how wonderful it is to live with the promise that Jesus is coming again to make a world with no death, pain, or sin. What a wonderful place that will be!
