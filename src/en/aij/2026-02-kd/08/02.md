Potiphar was very rich. As captain in the king’s army, he had lots of money, servants, idols, animals, and land. And now he had bought a new slave named Joseph.

No one in Potiphar’s home believed in the one true God except for Joseph. Every day Joseph would have to choose. Would he choose to worship God or idols? Would he choose to be honest or steal? Would he work hard or would he be lazy?

Joseph decided to always follow God’s ways and worship Him. He would be honest and work hard to please His loving God. The Bible says, “And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men” (Colossians 3:23).

Joseph began to work hard for Potiphar. With a sweep and a swish of a broom, Joseph cleaned the dusty corners that no one noticed. With a wash and a wipe, he cleaned away the dust hiding under the furniture. With a big groan, Joseph lifted heavy sacks of grain onto his aching back. He faithfully did his best in everything.

The people in the palace liked having Joseph around. He was always kind and helpful. It wasn’t long before someone else noticed Joseph’s hard work too. Who was it? **Read the first part of Genesis 39:4.**

In fact, he liked and trusted Joseph so much that he treated him like his own son. From a slave to a son! God was definitely blessing and helping Joseph.
