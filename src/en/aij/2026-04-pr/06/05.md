The grateful woman had tried to blend into the crowd, unnoticed; but when Jesus looked toward her, she realized she couldn’t hide any longer. She slowly made her way toward Jesus. This time the crowd parted. Trembling, she fell down at His feet.

Through grateful tears, the woman began to tell her story. She told Jesus how the long years of suffering had left her weak and tired. She told Him how she had heard about Jesus and was filled with hope. She believed He could help her. She had desperately reached out to touch His clothes and instantly, miraculously, she had been healed!

Jesus must have had a smile in His voice as He gently said, “Daughter, be of good cheer; your faith has made you well. Go in peace” (Luke 8:48).

When Jesus felt healing power go out from Him, He had longed to comfort the humble woman with words she would always treasure. He had said, “Daughter . . .” There was so much meaning in that one word. She was loved, she belonged, and she was part of God’s family. Jesus didn’t want the faith of His daughter to go unnoticed by the pressing crowd. They needed to know what faith looked like in a person’s life. And what it could achieve when combined with God’s power to love and heal.

Our loving Jesus persistently reaches out to us and waits for us to come to Him so that He can comfort us too. Like the woman, we can’t hide from Jesus. His heart knows where we are, and He prompts us to come. Then what does He call us? **Read 2 Corinthians 6:18.**
