**Opening Activities**

Source items for early arrivals: Provide an assortment of “nature paintbrushes” by gathering different **branches of plants**, **bushes**, and **flowers**. Source **strong scissors or cutters** for an adult to shape plants into brushes. Fill half a **tub or bucket** with **water**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: two short paper chains as “arm shackles” ands scissors. Make by linking one-inch (2.5-centimeter) strips of paper together and taping or stapling each loop.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: **sticks** and **red or orange paper** to build a fire, **flatbread, large piece of cardstock/cardboard** with a door painted on it, **red paint** (optional—can use water), **leafy branch** as paintbrush, **bag**, and **walking stick**.

Source peg people or a felt set if desired, and **^[week 4 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: nature paintbrush, tote bag with child’s top, non-breakable cup and bowl inside, container of cut-up flatbread (enough for each child to have a piece). Picture of Jesus.
