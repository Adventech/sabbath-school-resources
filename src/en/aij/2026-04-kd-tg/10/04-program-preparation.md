**Opening Activities**

Set out **paper** and **crayons** for children to draw what they hear.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: A **bag or box**. Write out animal names—in pairs—on **small slips of paper**, then fold them and place them in the box or bag. Animals may include: cow, sheep, pig, horse, goat, chicken, dog, cat, lion, crocodile, duck, bird, monkey, mouse, frog.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props: coins** in a **cup** ready to make a jingling sound, **spice** in containers (cinnamon, cloves, ginger, etc.), **recordings of birds singing and sheep baaing**.

Source **peg people** (Jesus, Bartimaeus) or a **felt set**, and **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Make a **megaphone** out of cardstock by simply rolling it into a conical shape and taping it together. Include a **Bible** or slip in your class Bible.
