Create

Make a simple, healthy edible treat together. Reflect on the memory verse, “Do not forget to do good and to share.” Think of someone who might need some encouragement, and deliver these treats to them to brighten their day. Explain to your child that when we help others, we are helping God too.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5D5562"}}}
Learn

Talk with your child about ways you can look after the beautiful earth God has made us. The bees, ants, and squirrels need a clean garden home. Talk about not littering, and go on a little mission to pick up trash in a garden space near you. Consider lining up trash at home to talk about being careful about what you use and throw away. You may also like to tend or start growing a flowering plant to help the bees (even in a pot by a window).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5D5562"}}}
Connect

Plan a special activity that you could do as a family this week to help God. It could be picking up someone to take to church, looking after an animal, or inviting someone who is lonely for a meal.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#5D5562"}}}
Pray

Invite each person in the family to share their favorite animal that God made. Talk about why that animal is special and what we could do to help that animal. Thank God for each of the animals mentioned and for giving us nature to enjoy.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Trust in the Lord and do good” (Psalm 37:3).
