### Personal Ministries Leader

There is no greater privilege than to work with the Holy Spirit in inspiring, organizing, training, and assisting God’s people in sharing Jesus. If you need help mobilizing the membership of your church for outreach, this book is for you.

This book is full of practical advice to help you:

- Cooperate with church and conference leaders
- Develop a plan and strategy with specific goals
- Train members to witness
- And much more!
