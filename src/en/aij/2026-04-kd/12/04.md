The villagers and the visitors looked at Jesus with surprise. Would the young man, who had died, really get up? That would be impossible!

What happened? **Read the first part of Luke 7:15.**

With the life-giving words of Jesus, the stretcher tipped and wobbled. The woman’s son suddenly sat up and looked around. He must have been a little confused because he could see the crowd all looking at him with mouths open wide in shock.

_Why is everyone looking at me?_ he must have wondered. _And why am I being carried out of town?_

The crowd couldn’t believe it—Jesus had done the impossible! He can do anything. They stood there silent and stunned.

Jesus smiled at the mother and her son. He was happy to see them hug each other tightly as they cried happy tears.

Jesus had turned her sadness into joy!

Jesus knows when we need help, love, and even a big hug. He can help us have joy when times are happy, and when they are sad. “Now may the God of hope fill you with all joy and peace in believing” (Romans 15:13).
