The fruit didn’t taste bom para long. Tears stung seu eyes as Adam e Eve sat com shoulders drooped. A deep sadness filled seu hearts, along com guilt e shame.

Blinking back tears, eles realized Deus’s soft covering de light had disappeared de around seu bodies. Eles felt lonely, chilled, e naked! Fumbling com fig leaves, eles tried hard a Faça themselves some clothes. Then o que did Adam e Eve hear, e o que did eles Faça? **Leia Gênesis 3:8.**

Guilt, fear, e shame drove eles a run e hide de Deus. Seu choice a disobey Ele was sin, e sin had changed eles inside. Eles didn’t know como much Deus’s heart ached com sorrow e amor para eles.

Deus knew como Adam e Eve felt, e Ele knew onde eles were. Wanting eles a choose a see Ele, Ele gently called a Adam, “Onde are você?” (Gênesis 3:9).

Adam’s head popped up, e ele wondered se seu ears had heard right. Did Deus still want a be com eles? Em response a the kindness em Deus’s voice, eles quit hiding em the shaws. Com heads hung low, eles came out a see seu Deus. Ele looked em eles com eyes de both amor e sorrow e then começou a speak.

Deus Pergunteed o que had happened, e the blaming começou. Adam blamed Eve. Eve blamed the serpent. Mas the serpent had no one a blame. Eles had todos hurt Deus por not trusting Ele. Adam e Eve had disobeyed, e terrible consequences would follow. Mas Deus hadn’t left eles! E Seu presence calmed seu hearts.
