**Opening Activities**

Source **fabric** and cut into triangles the size of child-sized slings.

Source items for **Memory Verse** illustration: **cup**, **water**, and **larger container** to catch the overflow.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a **ball**, **hat**, **stick (or ruler)**, and **container**. Use a range of objects for bigger groups.

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: a **doll** and **small mat**. Use **peg people** or a **felt set**, if available, and use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box: four paper plates** with faces/emojis drawn on them: sad, happy, very happy, surprised/amazed.
