News about the straight-talking preacher in the wilderness spread like wildfire. Soon it reached Jesus in the carpenter shop in Nazareth, and He knew what He had to do. He put away His tools, said goodbye to His mom, and followed the crowds all the way to John.

Glancing up the hill, John spotted Jesus making His way toward the river. For months He had been telling people to get ready for the coming Messiah, and now the Holy Spirit had shown him that He was here! Excitement pulsed through John’s voice as he cried, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world!” (John 1:29).

John wanted everyone to notice Jesus.

Jesus smiled as He came to John to be baptized. Jesus is so holy that John didn’t feel that he, a sinner, was worthy to baptize Him. He said, “I need to be baptized by You, and are You coming to me?” (Matthew 3:14). But Jesus urged John to baptize Him in the Jordan River right away.

After His baptism Jesus stepped from the water and knelt on the riverbank. He prayed for power to reach hard-hearted people with His love so they would accept His gift of salvation. He asked for proof that God had accepted Him as His Son on earth. And in a flash God answered!

Dazzling light from God’s throne surrounded Jesus. He looked up. The Holy Spirit, in the form of a beautiful white dove, came down on Him. While many saw this amazing sight, only John and a few others understood this miracle. Everyone stood silent as Jesus glowed with holy light. Then God spoke from heaven. What did He say? **Read Matthew 3:17.**

Those wonderful words warmed the people’s hearts and made their faith grow. Those words are for everyone. Everyone.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Read Matthew 3:17 again. Can you imagine how happy God was when He said those words?
- You are also God’s child! Read the verse again and imagine God naming you as He says it.

When the people watched Jesus’ beautiful baptism, their faith grew, and their hearts were prepared to love Him. When we see others repent and choose baptism, our faith can grow too.
