**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

We all experience “storms” in our lives—times of uncertainty, stress, or anxiety. Sometimes a storm comes and goes overnight; other times the storms rage for what seems like far too long. Life’s difficulties can come at the most unexpected times. Like the disciples, we may be experienced “sailors,” yet the storms catch us off guard.

It’s so incredibly comforting to know that Jesus is with us through every storm. Not only is He in the boat with us, but He is at the helm as the one who directs the boat. Although He may at times be silent, He always hears our cries for help. We can still be assured that all will be well because all of creation knows His voice.

“He never fails to give us the help we need. Whether on the land or on the sea, if we have the Saviour in our hearts, there is no need of fear. Living faith in the Redeemer will smooth the sea of life, and will deliver us from danger in the way that He knows to be best” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 336).

Today Jesus wants to give you peace in any storm you’re facing. He will still your heart and the worries in your mind. He promises, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your heart be troubled, neither let it be afraid” (John 14:27).

Let your heart trust in God and rest in His peace today. He is with you and the children in your Sabbath School class this week and always.
