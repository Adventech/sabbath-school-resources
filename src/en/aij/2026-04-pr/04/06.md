The early-morning light began to spread across the sea as the disciples knelt around Jesus in the little fishing boat. They were hugely grateful for the way He had rescued them, right in the middle of the scary storm—again! Bowing humbly before Him, they said, “Truly You are the Son of God!” (Matthew 14:33).

Out on the sea, they had been reminded that only the Son of God could keep them safe in storms of all kinds. Peter thought his faith was strong. But when he took his eyes off Jesus, he was actually weak. He needed to focus on Jesus and depend on His power.

We are sometimes like Peter. When problems come, we focus on them instead of keeping our eyes on Jesus. Our loving Jesus wants us to trust Him to do the impossible, especially when we are weak and don’t know what to do. He can lift us out of our problems, just as He lifted Peter out of the water. Our part is to keep our eyes on Him and believe His promises. **Read Psalm 32:8.**

As we choose to stay focused on Jesus, we will talk with Him in prayer, read His Word, and listen to His Holy Spirit speaking to our hearts. Then we can say as the disciples did, “Truly You are the Son of God” (Matthew 14:33).
