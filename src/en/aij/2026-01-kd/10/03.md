Abram loved talking to God. One day, while he was alone praying, God spoke to him.

“Leave your country,” God said. “Go to the land I will show you. I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you. . . . And you will be a blessing to others” (Genesis 12:1, 2, ICB).

Leave? Without knowing _where_ he was going?

What did Abram do? **Read Genesis 12:4.**

What great faith! Abram didn’t even think about whether or not to obey—he just did it! He knew that “the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord, and He delights in his way” (Psalm 37:23).

Busily Abram and his family packed up “everything they owned” (Genesis 12:5, ICB). They strapped their bags and baskets onto their camels and donkeys. Then Abram, Sarai, their nephew Lot, and their servants said goodbye to their big family. With another big wave, they left town to go . . . well, they weren’t sure where!

Abram smiled with excitement as he walked along the dusty road. God had promised He would bless him, lead him, and make him the father of many, many people. He didn’t even have one child yet. What an exciting promise!

- What does faith mean?
- Who showed faith in this story?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help us trust and obey what He says in the Bible.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Wrap up a good surprise and give it to a family member. You might wrap up something you’ve made, found, or saved. Thank God that we can trust Him to give us good surprises.
