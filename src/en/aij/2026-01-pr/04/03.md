Lucifer watched Jesus speaking with God the Father. What were they discussing? How Lucifer wished he knew! Actually why didn’t he know? Wasn’t he the first of the covering cherubs, the highest order of angels? Wasn’t he the angel who was closest to the throne of God? Why was _Jesus_ invited to talk closely with God the Father, and he wasn’t?

Lucifer felt jealous of Jesus. The more he thought about Jesus, the more jealous and poisonous his thoughts became.

But _he_ kept thinking them anyway. God knew how poisonous jealous thoughts can be, so He warned us about them in the Ten Commandments. What did God say? **Read Exodus 20:17.**

God knows that when we really, really want _anything_ someone else has, our hearts will fill up with a poison that will never make us happy. If only Lucifer would stop to think about this.

But he didn’t. He didn’t think about how God created all things through Jesus. Why would Lucifer, a created angel, be jealous of the One who created him? Whatever was he thinking?

Thinking too much about himself was making his thoughts ugly and crooked; so crooked that he even said, “I will be like [God] Most High” (Isaiah 14:14).

No one had ever been jealous in heaven before. Every angel was happy that their angel friends had special gifts. No one even knew what jealousy was! How sad that Lucifer was inventing stinking thinking.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What would the world be like if there were no jealousy or pride?_
- _How do you feel when you really, really want what someone else has? (Remind your child that many people work hard for whatever they have.)_

God loves to help us keep our hearts loving and free from the poison of jealousy.
