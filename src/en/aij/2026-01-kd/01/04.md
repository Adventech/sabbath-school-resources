Water swirled and daffodils nodded. Branches swayed and tall grass Why? swished in the breeze. So much had changed in just three days! What would God create next in His special world?

On the fourth day God spoke again. The Bible tells us that He said, “ ‘Let there be lights in the sky to separate day from night. . . . They will be in the sky to give light to the earth.’ And it happened” (Genesis 1:14, 15, ICB). What did God create next? Read Genesis 1:16.

God made the big, blazing sun appear in just the right place to keep the earth warm during the day. God knew the sun couldn’t be too close to the earth or it would be too hot. It couldn’t be too far away, or everything would freeze. The brightness from the sun would help plants to grow strong and colorful and help people to stay healthy too.

God also made the softly glowing moon and more stars than you can count. They twinkled and blinked in the dark night sky. God’s nighttime lights remind us that He will always love us! The Bible says, “He made the moon and stars to rule the night. His love continues forever” (Psalm 136:9, ICB).

^[Fun Fact]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
When the sun shines on our skin, it makes vitamin D, sometimes called the sunshine vitamin. Our bodies need vitamin D to help our bones and teeth stay strong.
