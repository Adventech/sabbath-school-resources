Explore

Re-create the story using sticks, leaves, and bark to create a house. Take a piece of cloth, tie a thread to its four corners, draw and cut out a paper man, and lay him on the cloth bed. Make a hole in the “roof” and send down the “sick man.”

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Create

Invite one of your friends to play with you. You can be a good friend like the four kind friends in the story. Jesus wants to help you and be your Best Friend.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Play

Re-create the story using a blanket and some teddy bears. Read the story again. Take turns giving your child a turn to be carried around the room in the blanket by holding each of the corners.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Connect

Think about a friend (who may also be a family member) who may need your help or encouragement. Spend time making a card, gift, or practicing a song to sing, to encourage this person.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Pray

Choose a different friend to pray for each day this week. Ask God to help them and give them what they need.
