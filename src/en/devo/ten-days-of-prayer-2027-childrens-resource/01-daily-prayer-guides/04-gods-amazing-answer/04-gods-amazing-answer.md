### God Keeps His Promises

> <p></p>
> “Know and understand this: The Chosen One will come at the right time.” Daniel 9:25 (ICB)

Have you ever waited for a special day? Maybe for a special gift, a holiday, or a visit from someone you love. When we wait, we may wonder, “When will it happen?”

Daniel had been praying and waiting for God’s answer. God sent the angel Gabriel to help Daniel understand His plan.

God showed Daniel something amazing. He had a special promise! One day, the Messiah would come. The Messiah was Jesus!

Many years later, Jesus came exactly when God had planned. Jesus came to show God’s love and to bring salvation to the world.

Daniel learned that God never forgets His promises. Even when people must wait, God is always working. His plans are good, and His timing is perfect.

Sometimes we pray and hope for answers. We may want things to happen quickly, but we can trust God because He knows the best time.

God answered Daniel’s prayer, and God continues to keep His promises to us.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What is a promise that someone has kept for you? How does it make you feel when someone keeps a promise?

> <p>Remember</p>
> God always keeps His promises!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You because Your promises are always true. Thank You for sending Jesus at the right time. Help us trust Your plans and remember that You always care for us. Amen.
