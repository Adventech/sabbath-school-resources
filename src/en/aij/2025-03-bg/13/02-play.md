Create

Make a collage picture together of the people’s new home in the Promised Land. Make a horizon line on a piece of paper. Let your child color green below and blue above. Then collect things to lay over it and rearrange as they like (no need to glue). You could include leaves, grasses, flowers, herbs, sticks, colored papers, stickers, magazine or printed pictures, or other craft supplies.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#F0523C"}}}
Learn

Share how God has promised to come back and take us to heaven. He will then make our earth new again. He will give us a new home with Him forever! Share the ideas of Isaiah 11:6-11 with your child. Talk about what excites them most about this new forever home with Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#F0523C"}}}
Share

Share a yummy fruit snack together—something your child loves to eat but may not get often. Imagine all the yummy fruits that were in the people’s new home, the Promised Land. Thank God for providing good, healthy food for us. Talk about which foods you would like to eat with Jesus in our new forever home with Him.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#F0523C"}}}
Explore

Create a set of binoculars using two cardboard cylinders. Decorate them together. Share how the Israelites would have looked out over the land and been amazed at the special gifts God had given them—yummy food, water, big fields. Use your binoculars to search around the home and outside for special gifts God has given you! Thank God together for His love and care.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#F0523C"}}}
Pray

Thank God for being with us always. Thank Him for coming soon to give us a new home with Him where we can see His smile, hug Him, play with Him, and love Him forever.
