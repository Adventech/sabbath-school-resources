[[Revelation 3:1-6]]

- **[Click Here for Lessons From Sardis 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r03a/)**
- The mid-1500s, often known as the Protestant Reformation, and the century following it are well represented by the church in Sardis.
- Reformers then helped purify the church by pointing people back to God’s teaching and reminding them that salvation in Jesus Christ cannot be earned. Instead, Jesus offers salvation without cost to all who are willing to receive Him as Savior and Lord.
- Unfortunately, in the time immediately following these reformers, the church once again became sterile and cold.
- Jesus loves truth! But the Sardis era shows that truth, apart from a relationship with Jesus, will soon be reduced again to cold facts. Jesus longs for us to know Him _personally_, and not just know things _about_ Him.

[[Revelation 3:7-13]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- **[Click Here for Lessons From Philadelphia 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r03b/)**
- The positive condition of Christ’s followers in Philadelphia aptly describes the history of the church in general in the eighteenth and early nineteenth centuries. Those were years of tremendous faithfulness and growth known as the First and Second Great Awakenings. Followers of Christ all over the world shared the good news of salvation, and the church grew accordingly, even as believers’ energies were severely taxed.

[[Revelation 3:14-22]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- **[Click Here for Lessons From Laodicea 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r03c/)**
- Laodicea represents the condition of Christ’s followers from the mid-nineteenth century to the present. They with millions of others are saturated with conveniences, sinful media influences, and busyness, all of which distract them from the salvation Jesus offers them.
- Jesus calls to every individual on earth today to answer Him when He knocks on the door of his or her heart, that He may come into their lives and “dine” with them—that is, that He might have genuine fellowship with them. What is your answer to His invitation?
