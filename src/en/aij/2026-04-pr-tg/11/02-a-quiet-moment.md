**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

What did it take for Jesus to be amazed? It wasn’t something His disciples said or did. It wasn’t the Jewish leaders or their actions. Jesus was amazed at the faith of a Roman centurion.

This centurion didn’t ask for something for himself, but for his servant. As a powerful man he was ruler over many, yet he chose to humble himself before the Son of God to ask for a special favor. The centurion just asked for Jesus to speak.

Jesus’ words must have rebuked His disciples and encouraged the Roman centurion. “Assuredly, I say to you, I have not found such great faith, not even in Israel!” (Matthew 8:10).

“[The centurion’s] heart had been touched by the grace of Christ. He saw his own unworthiness; yet he feared not to ask help. He trusted not to his own goodness; his argument was his great need. His faith took hold upon Christ in His true character. He did not believe in Him merely as a worker of miracles, but as the friend and Saviour of mankind” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 317).

What is your faith like today? As a Sabbath School leader you might have a team working with you, or you might be a sole teacher in a small church. Jesus hopes that your faith will be strong, for He wants to do mighty things in the hearts of the children you teach. Believe this and watch the Spirit at work.

As you teach this story, may it also speak to you and encourage you.
