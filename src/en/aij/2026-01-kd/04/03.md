All of heaven was full of peace, joy, and kindness. God loved the angels very much, and they loved Him too. But one day something began to change in Lucifer’s heart. A little selfish thought began to grow, like a tiny seed that grows into a weed. Lucifer started to think all about himself: me, me, me. What does the Bible say about it? Read the first part of Ezekiel 28:17.

Lucifer may have noticed the angels watching him with respect. Maybe he saw his reflection and admired his handsome face and sparkling robe. Maybe he heard his voice sing louder and stronger above the other angels.

As Lucifer thought more and more about himself, pride began to grow in his heart. He forgot that it was God who had given him his special jobs, his beauty, and his singing voice.

Sad things happen when we put ourselves first. We think we’re going to be happier, but it doesn’t turn out that way. We end up being discontented (which is the opposite of being content). That’s why the Bible also asks us to “give your brothers and sisters more honor than you want for yourselves” (Romans 12:10, ICB).

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you put others first, or ask God to help you “be content with such things as you have” (Hebrews 13:5a).

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Put the words “Others First” on display somewhere in your home. Look for ways to give others the biggest and the best this week. Think about mealtime, playtime, and helping around the home. This is God’s way.
