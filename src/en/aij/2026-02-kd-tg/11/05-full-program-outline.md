**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child by name as they arrive.

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to a table to make a leaf garland. Show them the colorful leaves to choose from and give each a piece of precut string. Show them how to use a hole punch to make a hole in each leaf, then how to guide the string through each hole. Talk about how the leaves are all different but come together as something beautiful because the string ties them together. We all go through different times­—good times and bad times—but we can trust God to make our life into something beautiful too. I wonder how we will see this in our story today.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **small clear bag** full of little **colorful things** that look messy together (beads, buttons, paper, string), and a **large paper bag** that they can’t see through, taped shut (with a **kaleidoscope/device inside**).

Shake your clear bag. Ask the kids what it looks like. **It’s colorful, but is it beautiful?** (Not really; it’s a mess!)

Share that Joseph went through some hard times. He had been a slave and a prisoner! But unlike this bag, his life was not a mess. God had special plans for each colorful part of Joseph’s life.

Ask a child to open the larger paper bag to reveal the kaleidoscope (or a media device to show the children a virtual kaleidoscope). Let the children stand up to take turns looking at the beautiful patterns and designs. Talk about what they see and what they think of it.

**Teaching Point:** A designer put all these colorful bits together in surprising ways to make something beautiful. God can do the same thing with our good and bad times. We may not understand why some things happen, but we can trust God to make everything good in the end. If it’s not good, it’s not the end!

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children to all stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He will make everything good in the end.** In our story today, we see how God does this for His good friend Joseph.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **sound device**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a colorful **map** of your Sabbath School space (drawn by you).

Invite the children to search and find the **paper hearts** that you have hidden around the room, using the map that you have given them. Celebrate with a child each time they bring a paper heart back to you.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Invite the children to return to their seats.

- **Was it helpful to use the map as your guide?**

Share that we don’t know what’s going to happen in our lives, but God does! He has a wonderful map, the Bible, that each one of us can use. When we trust Him as our friend, He can guide us through the good times and bad times.

- **What was the good ending to Joseph’s story?** (His family was saved from being hungry. He forgave his brothers, and they were friends again. He saw his father, and his whole family came to live with him in Egypt.)
- **When Jesus comes back, He has a very, very good ending for our story too. What will that ending be?** (Talk about heaven and the new earth, and life with Jesus that never ends. Share that God wants every one of us to be there!)

**Teaching Point:** God guided Joseph all along the way. Sometimes it was good; other times it was hard. Like Joseph, we can trust God. He is our wonderful guide! He has wonderful plans to help us now, and we’ll be with Him forever.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He makes everything good in the end.**

Invite the children to put their paper hearts under their seats to use at the end.

**NATURE/MISSION** (7 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD and NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share that there is a beautiful flower in nature that reminds us of how God guides us. Ask the children if they can guess which flower it is, based on your clues.

- They grow very tall.
- They have a big happy “face.”
- They have big, bright-yellow petals.
- They love the sun, and bees are their friends.
- What is this flower?

Hold up **Nature Card 24: Sunflower**. Yes, it’s a sunflower! Share that God made these beautiful flowers to do something special. Let’s learn a little more about them.

Give each child **Nature Card 24: Sunflower** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**Share the Nature Lesson**, “The Happy Sunflower,” in an interactive way with **sunflower seeds** and a **cardboard sun** as props.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Activity: **Benjamin’s Sack Game**. The children can play a game during which one child hides the silver cup under one sack of grain and the other children take turns to find the cup. Print one game for each child. You will need to print the 11 sacks of grain and one silver cup. The sacks of grain can be stuck on one piece of paper (bottoms only, so that they can be flipped down).

Play worship music in the background.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** **Activity:** Invite the children to take out the paper hearts that they had placed under their seats. With helpers, stretch out a sheet or some fabric into a taut circle, and invite the children to stand around the edges.

- **What did we learn about God today?** (Allow time for their answers.)

Share that we learned that God is with us through good times and bad times. He will guide us and make everything good in the end, as He did for Joseph.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Invite the children to place their hearts in the center of the fabric, then to hold the edges of the fabric. Guide them to float the fabric up, and then to float it down as you sing, **“I Can Trust God.”**
