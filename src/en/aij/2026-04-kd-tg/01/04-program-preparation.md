**Opening Activities**

Source a **dishpan** or **large bowl of water** and **household items** to test for “sink or float activity” (paper clip, key, feather, leaf, etc.).

Familiarize yourself with this quarter’s mission project/offering destination to share with the children. Print out the **^[13 Mission Fast Facts]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for the quarter.

Print the **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and create a colorful **container** to collect the offering.

Source a **bell** or **sound maker** to use to get the children’s attention before opening the Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box. For example, a bell, toy flute, castanet, sealed cardboard tube filled with dried beans.

Source **two boxes** (shoeboxes) to be used for Secret Surprise Box and Story Explorer Box.

Source items for the **Secret Surprise Box**: **bedsheet** and **beach ball** (deflated).

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props: tub of water, toy boat** or **plastic containers** as boats, **flashlight, spray bottle of water**. Create storm sounds (or source online).

Source **peg people** (Jesus and disciples), or a **felt set**, and the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for the **Story Explorer Box**: Make **six small toy boats**, according to the pattern included, from Styrofoam or thick cardboard, toothpicks, white cardstock, and tape. **Tub** or large bowl of water.

**Nature/Mission**

Print a **^[Nature Collector Card 40: Florida Manatee]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child. Purchase or make a **Nature Collector Bag** for each child to use all quarter.
