Joseph wandered the fields wearing his new special coat. It was his favorite thing to wear. It had long sleeves and so many beautiful colors.

But his big brothers didn’t like the coat at all. They whispered jealous words to each other. Their father hadn’t made them a coat of beautiful colors! It made them angry. “So they hated their brother and could not speak to him politely” (Genesis 37:4, ICB). Poor Joseph!

One night tired Joseph took off his favorite coat, folded it carefully, and put it in a safe spot. Then lying down, he quickly fell fast asleep. As Joseph slept, God gave him a dream. It was a very strange dream. When he woke up, he kept thinking about it. It was so real! He wondered what it might mean. He wanted to talk to someone about it.

“Listen to the dream I had,” he began to tell his big brothers (Genesis 37:6, ICB). What did he say next? **Read Genesis 37:7.**

The brothers were mad! Their wheat bundles bowed down to Joseph’s wheat bundle? Never! “Do you really think you will be king over us?” they said to their little brother. How dare he try to be the boss! “His brothers hated him even more now” (Genesis 37:8, ICB).

It must have been hard and confusing for Joseph. But even though his brothers were mean and angry, he had a big Friend to care for him. The Bible says, “. . . casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why were Joseph’s brothers angry?
- Who can you talk to when others are angry or unkind?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C17EB6", "size": "lg"}}})\
