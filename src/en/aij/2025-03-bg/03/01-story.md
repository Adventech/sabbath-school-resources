Moses called the sheep to his side. Come, sheep! The woolly sheep ran to him _(baa)._ The little lambs ran quickly behind _(baa)._ Moses smiled at them. He loved looking after the sheep. As a boy, he had lived as a prince in a palace in Egypt. But now he was a man and lived far, far away. Moses was a gentle shepherd.\
The desert sun was hot, and the little lambs baaed and bleated. _(Can you make the sound of a sheep?)_ They were thirsty, hot, and hungry. Moses led them to a stream. He was a kind shepherd. He loved spending time outside in God’s nature. He listened as the stream splashed over little rocks. Splish, splash. He sang and prayed to God as he watched his sheep.

One day as Moses led his sheep to find water, he saw something that made him stop still. Up ahead a bush was on fire! _Flicker, flash._ The flames were big and hot. Flicker, flash. Moses looked closer. The bush was on fire, but the leaves and branches didn’t burn up!\
Suddenly Moses heard God call his name: “Moses, Moses! . . . Take off your sandals. You are standing on holy ground” (Exodus 3:4, 5, ICB). God was right there with Moses, inside the burning bush!\
God told Moses that He had a special job for him. God’s people in Egypt were sad _(make a sad face)._ They had a mean king, called Pharaoh, who hurt them. God wanted Moses to lead the people away from Egypt. God told Moses to go to Pharaoh and say, “Let my people go!” Moses was scared. This was a big job! But God said, “I will be with you” (Exodus 3:12, ICB).

Moses listened carefully to God _(cup ear)._ God loved Moses very much. He told him that He would send his brother, Aaron, to help him. Would Moses obey? Yes! Moses chose to do what God asked. He went back to Egypt so that he could help God’s people. Thank You, God, for choosing Moses to be Your helper. _(Say together)_ Help me to obey You too.
