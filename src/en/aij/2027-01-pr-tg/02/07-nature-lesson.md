**Focus:** We can trust God to help us develop the courage to face life with a smile, even when things go wrong.

**Have you ever had to adapt to something new and very different? How did you like it?**

Let’s find out about an animal who has had to adapt to different climates. ^[(Show swamp beaver picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](nature-lesson.jpg)

The nutria, also known as a swamp beaver, looks like a beaver with a rat’s tail. This mammal is clumsy on land, but has webbed back feet that help it swim well. It can even stay underwater for five minutes. But it can keep most of its body underwater all the time because its nose and eyes sit high on its head.

The swamp beaver has a thick undercoat of soft fur. This is covered by long fawn-colored “guard hairs” that act like a waterproof raincoat. Because the swamp beaver spends most of its time in water, it must regularly groom itself with oil from its mouth. Using its front paws, it combs the oil through its guard hairs to keep them waterproof.

The swamp beaver uses its long, ever-growing, orange-colored teeth to gnaw off tough water plants. Special bacteria in its stomach help it digest things many other animals can’t eat.

Though the swamp beaver has always lived in South American countries, fur farmers introduced it to other countries, so it now lives in other areas, including North America. It has had to adapt to different climates, especially those with harsh, unfavorable conditions. In very cold climates its long, bare tail may freeze and break off, but that doesn’t seem to harm the beaver.

During your lifetime you may have to adapt to unfavorable conditions too. Maybe you’ll attend an unfriendly school. Or maybe you will have to move to a very unfamiliar place, just as Moses had to move to the palace. Your family might face challenges. Or, like Moses, you might make a big mistake that changes things for you. But no matter what, God promises, “Fear not, for I am with you; be not dismayed, for I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes, I will help you, I will uphold you with My righteous right hand” (Isaiah 41:10).
