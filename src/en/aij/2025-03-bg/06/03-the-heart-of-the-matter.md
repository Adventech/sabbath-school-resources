God’s fire and cloud illuminate the loving protectiveness of our God. But God wanted more. He wanted to be even closer to His people. He desired an intimate relationship with them. He wants to have this with you too. When the people arrived at Mount Sinai, they were to prepare to meet Him. Here was a chance to learn more about the God who had freed and saved them.

For days the Israelites prepared to meet with God. They cleaned their tents, their clothes, and their hearts. With a sense of excitement and awe they bowed before the Lord at the edge of the mountain.

He came with an amazing display of splendor and power. When the Israelites saw the mighty signs of His presence, “they trembled and stood afar off” (Exodus 20:18). Their view of God had been shaped by their harsh experience as slaves. His power terrified them! Rather than come closer to see the amazing God who saved them, they shrank back in fear and missed their chance to directly receive God’s 10 laws of love close-up and personal.

God is always taking a step closer toward you. Still, God wants more. His aim is to dwell within you. When we accept Him as our Savior, He takes up the closest position possible. “Do you not know yourselves, that Christ is in you?” (2 Corinthians 13:5). The guidance, protection, warmth, safety, and care He shows His children in the desert is just a foretaste of what Jesus offers you when He is the leader of your heart.

Come closer to Him in prayer and Bible study today. He can’t wait to come close to you! “Draw near to God and He will draw near to you” (James 4:8).
