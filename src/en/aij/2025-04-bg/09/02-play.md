Sing

Mary was so happy she sang praises to God. As you go about your work this week, make a special effort to sing praises to God out loud. Learn some new songs about Baby Jesus or ask your child for their favorite songs as you do your work.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#504651"}}}
Play

Act out the story with your child. You might like to use finger puppets made from cardboard or printed from the Internet.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#504651"}}}
Connect

The angel came to bring the good news to Mary that the Savior of the world would come. You too can share the good news! Think of someone you may like to post a letter to in the mail; someone who needs to hear the good news of Jesus! Together with your child, make a card and decorate it, sharing the joy of Baby Jesus. Mail it together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#504651"}}}
Learn

Share with your child about angels and how they help God. Explain to your child that they have their very own angel who helps to guide and protect them. Imagine that an angel appeared in your home. What would you see and hear? What would your reaction be?

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#504651"}}}
Pray

Thank God for the angel’s visit to Mary and for her trusting heart. Ask God to help each person in your family trust Him.
