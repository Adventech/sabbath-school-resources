Suddenly, Jesus stopped. The people stopped too. They looked at each other. They looked at Jesus. Why was He just standing there?

Jesus began looking around Him, as though He had lost something. The crowd looked around too—although they didn’t quite know what they were looking for!

What did Jesus say? **Find the answer in the first part of Luke 8:45.**

The people in the crowd muttered and chuckled when they heard Jesus’ question. Was Jesus joking? Everyone was touching Him! But when they looked at Jesus, they realized He was serious. Jesus wanted to know who had touched Him!

Peter spoke up, “Master, the people are all around you and are pushing against you” (Luke 8:45, ICB).

Then Jesus looked in the very direction of the woman and said, “Someone did touch me! I felt power go out from me” (Luke 8:46, ICB).

With so many people squashing and squishing against Him, this woman was the only one, in that moment, who had touched Him needing something very special. Jesus knew that, and He wanted to encourage her for her faith and use her example to teach the crowd.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do we reach out to Jesus now?
- Would you like to reach out to Jesus and have Him fill you with the Holy Spirit’s power?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Find things around your home that need power. For example, a phone or a kitchen appliance. What happens if they don’t get power? The trusting woman had felt the great power of Jesus. You can too, when you read your Bible, pray every day, and talk about Jesus with others.
