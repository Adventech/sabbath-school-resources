Connect

Plan an activity with your child for the next day. You could choose something simple and fun, such as going to the park or having a picnic lunch. Follow through with your plan. Show your child all the things you brought along to take care of them: a hat to keep them safe from the sun; an umbrella/raincoat for rain; water to drink; a picnic lunch. Explain that God’s plans are very good. We can trust Him to take care of us and save us. He would take care of everyone who trusted Him and went onto the big boat. He will take care of you!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#61b69e"}}}
Pray

Thank God that He has a big plan to always take care of you. Pray and name each person in your family, thanking God for His big love and for taking care of them.

Explore

Create water play activities that engage your child’s senses.
ae5aa3
**^[Touch:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#61b69e"}}})** water spray bottle, a soft hose, extra play time when bathing/showering.

**^[Listen:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#61b69e"}}})** search for “rain” online and listen to light and heavy rain sounds.

**^[Taste:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#61b69e"}}})** drink and describe water (cool, clear, fresh).

**^[Talk:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#61b69e"}}})** water is a gift from God. It cleans us, makes us healthy, and makes everything fresh. God would use water to make the world fresh and clean.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#61b69e"}}}
Create

Look up Bible pictures of the ark. Together, draw a “plan” for the ark and color it. Share how it would have rooms for people and for all the different animals. God had a big plan to take care of everyone who wanted to come into the ark.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Make yourself an ark” (Genesis 6:14).
