**Focus:** Grass is forgiving. We can follow Jesus’ example and be forgiving too.

Grass grows in all kinds of places, all over the world. It even grows in the tiniest cracks in our streets and sidewalks. In fact, it is so common that we often don’t even think about it. But grass is truly amazing!

Grass is special because when we cut the top off grass, it keeps on growing. Many other plants can’t do this, because they grow from the tip of the shoot, and when we cut the tip, that shoot can’t grow any more. But grass grows from the bottom of the plant. So we can cut grass, and animals can eat it, and it will just keep on growing to make soft and green places for us to sit and walk, and more food for the animals.

When it hasn’t rained for a long time, grass can look dry and brown and dead. But when it rains, the grass soon puts out new shoots to make everything beautiful again.

Even when grass is flooded, or when there is snow, grass usually grows back again very quickly.

Grass is useful for us too. Many animals eat grass, and some of them can give us milk, wool, and other good things. Humans eat some kinds of grass too. We call them cereals or grains. Wheat, oats, barley, rye, corn, rice, and millet are all different kinds of grass. Bamboo is also a kind of grass, and we can use it to make all kinds of useful things, even fabric for our clothes ^[(show the children some different types of grass)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Grass is green. God might have chosen that color for grass because it is soothing for our eyes and looks pretty among the other plants.

Grass keeps growing, even when humans, animals, and the weather do all kinds of damage to it. It just keeps on growing to make the world beautiful.

We can say thank You to God for creating grass in such a wonderful way.

- **What does grass do when we treat it badly?** (It keeps growing.)
