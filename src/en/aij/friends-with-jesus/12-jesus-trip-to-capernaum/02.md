The city of Capernaum was so beautiful. It sat right on the shore of the shimmering Sea of Galilee. It was surrounded by green fields, tall palm trees, and bright flowers.

Capernaum was not only beautiful—it was also very busy. Lots of people lived there. One particular Sabbath day the city seemed extra crowded. Exciting news had spread throughout the area—Capernaum had a special Visitor. Jesus had come to town!

People left their things and rushed down the road. Along the way, they stopped to tell others the good news. Jesus was speaking in the synagogue right now! Together the desperate crowds squished and squeezed inside the building. They wanted to get as close as possible to the wise and wonderful Teacher.

Jesus loved the Sabbath. He loved reading the Holy Scriptures. And on this beautiful Sabbath day, in this beautiful city, “Jesus taught the people” (Luke 4:31, ICB).

The crowd listened closely. His words warmed their hearts and made them think of heavenly things. Every word Jesus spoke, every look He gave, showed His great love and care.

What else did the people think of His words? **Read Luke 4:32.**

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think there were so many people in the synagogue?
- The Sabbath is a special time from sunset Friday night to sunset Saturday. It is God's holy day to rest from work and other busy things, be with Him, read His Bible, celebrate His love at church, and show love to others. Would you like to love Sabbath like Jesus?
