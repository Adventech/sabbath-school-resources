The cows mooed. The sheep baaed. Mary and Joseph looked around the small, humble stable. This place where animals slept would be their room for the night. In all of Bethlehem it was the only room they could find.

Joseph tied up their tired donkey. Then he helped Mary lie down on the prickly hay. It had been such a long, bumpy journey! It was time to rest and sleep.

On this night, in this very place, among these animals, something amazing happened. Baby Jesus was born! “For unto us a Child is born, unto us a Son is given” (Isaiah 9:6).

God had given Jesus to the world. This was the greatest, most precious gift _ever_!

Joseph and Mary held Him close. They knew their tiny baby was someone very special. He was “God with us” (Matthew 1:23).

Mary and Joseph looked into His beautiful face and smiled. This special baby needed a name. The shining angel had told them, “And you shall call his name Jesus, for He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21).

Jesus. Yes, His name would be Jesus!
