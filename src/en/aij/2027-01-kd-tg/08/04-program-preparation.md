**Opening Activities**

Source **pipe cleaners and beads**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: A **family photo** of your (teacher’s) family recently or when you were a child. Make a large empty photo frame by cutting out a piece of **cardboard** from a **box** or using a large sheet of **cardstock**. Draw a frame around the edge. Source a **marker** for the children to draw faces on the picture frame. During the program you may like to have a helper draw the first simple smiley face with hair so the kids have a size and style model.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source items for suggested **storytelling props**: create your own background by using **markers/crayons** on **colored paper** to depict a desert landscape with a mountain in the background and two tents—Jethro’s tent at the “start” and Moses’ tent at the “end.”

Use peg people (Moses, Jethro, Zipporah, two boys [Gershom and Eliezer], a crowd of people) or a felt set if desired, and **^[week 8 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Place your Moses peg person or felt Moses in the box after the story.
