Excited whispers swept through the busy market of Jericho. Jesus was in town! Zacchaeus gasped to hear the news. Jesus, in his own town! He hurried from his tax booth and joined the endless stream of people that surged like a wave toward Jesus.

Elbows knocked elbows. Sandals stepped on toes. Noisily the people pushed and shoved forward. Everyone wanted to see Jesus! Zacchaeus moved with the crowd. The people of Jericho didn’t see potential in Zacchaeus, but he hoped that Jesus would.

Shorter than most men, Zacchaeus stood on tiptoe and tried desperately to see above the wall of jostling people. He must get to Jesus! But no matter how hard he tried, Zacchaeus couldn’t see or squeeze his way through the crowd.

In desperation Zacchaeus broke free from the crowd and ran up ahead. Here, towering higher than any of the men of Jericho, stood a great tree. Quick as a flash he scrambled up the sycamore tree and out onto a wide-spreading branch that overhung the street. Perched there, he eagerly scanned the crowds for a glimpse of Jesus, the one he longed to see. The only one he longed to see.

Our loving God works in our hearts to help us seek Jesus first, as Zacchaeus did. When we seek Jesus, we will look for ways to be close to Him.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Read Matthew 6:33 again. How well did Zacchaeus follow the teaching in this verse?
- What would it look like for you to seek Jesus first?
- What sort of things might be “added to you” (Matthew 6:33) when you seek Jesus first, as Zacchaeus did?
