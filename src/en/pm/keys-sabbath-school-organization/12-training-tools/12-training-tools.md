---
title: Training Tools
---

Many valuable training aids can be obtained from the General Conference and world division Sabbath School and Personal Ministries Departments and Adventist Book Centers. These would include the Leadership; Sabbath School leaflets, and teaching/training courses. Books written on various functions of Sabbath School work that should be studied by leaders and teachers of all divisions include Counsels on Sabbath School Work by Ellen G. White and The Sabbath School Manual. Training videos and filmstrips are available. Another excellent resource is the web site for the General Conference Sabbath School and Personal Ministries Department, https://SabbathSchoolPersonalMinistries.org.
