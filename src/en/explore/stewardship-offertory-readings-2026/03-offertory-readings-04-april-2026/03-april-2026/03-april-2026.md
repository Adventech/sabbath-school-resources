title: The New Necessary
date: 18/04/2026
---

Do you sometimes think of upgrading your bicycle, car, house, or holiday? I often do. It’s normal, but is it necessary?

A Volkswagen car advertisement reads, “Every so often, you realize you’re worth a bit more, and the things you once found extravagant become altogether necessary. Because once we get a taste of a better life, there’s no going back.”

What an incredible insight into human nature. We continually want more and better and nicer things, even though it may cause others to have less or distract us from our God-given mission.

Are the people who humbly follow Jesus of Nazareth and believe that they have an incredible eternity awaiting them called to upgrade their lifestyles at the same rate as people who don’t know God? Are we also to seek satisfaction and worth from the nice things we own?

The Bible clearly teaches that we can be conservative in our living so that we can be liberal in our giving. Maybe God allows us to be “worth a bit more” so that we can upgrade our standard of giving, not just our standard of _living_.

Maybe it is time for us to have an upgrade of our giving.

### Appeal

Your regular and systematic offerings, called Promise offerings, are your partnership with God. He promises, “Give, and it will be given to you: good measure, pressed down, shaken together, and running over. . . . For with the same measure that you use, it will be measured back to you” (Luke 6:38). If you doubt this promise, test God. Prove Him, and you will see for yourself.
