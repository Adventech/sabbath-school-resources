A friend smiles and giggles with you. They use gentle hands and kind words too. A friend wants to play and have fun with you. They wave hello and bye-bye _(wave)_. A friend has helping hands and will take turns on the slide. They’re happy to be with you. A friend cares when you’re sad and says “I’m sorry” too. A friend likes you just because you are you! Who is a friend to you? _(Pause.)_ You can be a friend too!\
Friends can be big or small. They may have brown skin, black, or white. Their hair may be curly, straight, wild, or wavy. Some friends are big people you know so well—grandmas and grandpas, and aunties and uncles. Friends can live next door or far, far away.\
Animal friends might have feathers or fur. They might snuggle up or purr. Not all friends are the same, but each is special—just like you! _(Hug.)_ Who is your friend today? _(Pause.)_ You can be a friend too!

Noah lived long ago. He had a very special friend: God _(point up)_. Noah and God talked every day. They talked in the morning as the sun came over the hills. Noah liked walking in the forest and talking with God—his best friend. Noah said “Thank You” to God for the yummy food growing in his garden.\
When the sun set low, Noah told God everything; all about his day. He listened _(point to ear)_ carefully to all God had to say. Noah was a friend of God. You can be God’s friend too!\
Noah was a good man. He was kind and loving to his family. He was gentle and caring to the animals. He was careful with everything that God had made. He obeyed God. Noah’s grandfather, Methuselah, taught Noah about God. Noah taught his family about God too. Noah wanted his family to be friends with God and talk with Him each day. You can be God’s friend too!

God wants to be your friend. He loves to see you play. He loves to hear you laugh _(tickle)_. God knows the funny things that make you giggle and what makes you sad. He loves you. No, we can’t see God’s face, because He lives in heaven. But He has given you a family to help you learn about God. And He is with you all throughout the day _(point at heart)_. He listens _(point to ear)_ carefully to all you say. God likes to be with you. God is your most special friend.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for being my friend!
