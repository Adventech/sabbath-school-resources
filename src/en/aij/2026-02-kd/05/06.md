Jacob limped back to his family camp, tired but filled with God’s peace. God had promised to be with him. God had blessed him.

As Jacob looked out into the distance, he saw Esau and his 400 men coming toward him. Had God been able to change Esau’s angry heart?

Jacob hobbled slowly toward his brother, his face bright with joy. Then he stopped. What did he do? **Read Genesis 33:3.**

Esau began running straight toward his brother smiling. “Esau put his arms around him and hugged him. Then Esau kissed him, and they both cried” (Genesis 33:4, ICB).

God had heard Jacob’s prayer. He had changed Esau’s anger and hatred into love and forgiveness. Esau was actually happy to see his younger brother! What a wonderful miracle God had done.

Sometimes it is hard to forgive people when they hurt or upset you. But God promises in John 14:27, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you.” God can change your heart too, so that you can love like Him.
