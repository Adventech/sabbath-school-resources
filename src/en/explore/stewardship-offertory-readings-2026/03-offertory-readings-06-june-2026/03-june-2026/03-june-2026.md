---
title: Fast-Moving Pilgrims
date: 21/06/2026
---

Author C. S. Lewis wrote, “Our Father refreshes us on the journey with some pleasant inns, but He will not encourage us to mistake them for home.”

We can be so focused on the material blessings that God gives us that we forget whose they are and what they are for. Our assets are not actually ours. Money, houses, investments, clothes, sports equipment, furniture, decorations, tools, and even talents are entrusted to us to be enjoyed, yes, but also to test us—to see whether we will use them for eternal interests or selfish purposes.

We are called, trained, resourced, and empowered to be fast-moving pilgrims. We have a simple but crucial mission—to help people fix their eyes on Jesus Christ. God supports and funds this mission with the talents and possessions He entrusts to us. What a privilege!

Let’s use God’s resources to encourage people to fix their eyes on Jesus. In doing so, we will also be empowered to keep our eyes on Him.

As Helen Lemmel penned, “Turn your eyes upon Jesus, look full in His wonderful face, and the things of earth will grow strangely dim in the light of His glory and grace.”

### Appeal

It’s now time to return God’s tithe and give the Promise offerings, which we are encouraged to preplan and set aside as a percentage of our income this week. Let’s ask God to help us live lightly and not be bogged down with desires for more worldly things. Let’s run the race set before us and lay off every weight that might slow us down. Thank you for your faithfulness.
