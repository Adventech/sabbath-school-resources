**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **paper towel strips, four clear plastic** or **glass cups, food coloring**, and a **spoon**.

Add a half cup of water to each of the four cups and place them in a line with a little space between each one. Then add a few drops of food coloring into each cup—a different color in each one. Stir the food coloring into the water. Give one paper towel strip each to three different children.

- **Do you think the paper towel is beautiful?** (Not really. It’s plain white in color.)

Explain that we’ll now all watch to see how these paper towels can become more beautiful. Say: **I wonder how they’ll change.** Ask one child to place one end of their paper towel into the first cup, and the other end into the second cup. The next child will do the same with the following two cups. (The first child places their strip into cups 1 and 2. The second child places their strip into cups 2 and 3, and the third child places their strip into cups 3 and 4.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How do you think the white paper towel will change?**

Tell the children you’ll leave the paper towel experiment while you listen to today’s Bible story—but you’ll come back to look at it later.

Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. His love changes me.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[REFLECT and DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Zacchaeus must have been so surprised that Jesus wanted to go to his house! Because he felt loved by Jesus, something started to change inside of him.

Jesus’ love can change us too. If we’ve been mean, or if we’ve lied, stolen, or cheated someone, we can ask Jesus to help, and He can help us make things right.

Invite the children to think of something they’ve done that hurt someone else (a parent, sister or brother, or friend).

- **What can you do to make things right? What did Zacchaeus do?**

1. Zacchaeus wanted to see Jesus. We can go somewhere quiet and ask Jesus to change our heart.
2. When Zacchaeus understood that Jesus loved him, he decided to make things right with others. We know that Jesus loves us, and He’ll help us make things right too.

Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box**, revealing a set of **“Make It Right” Cards**. These cards include pictures of **a hug, a gift, a child helping, sharing a toy, and praying**. Give each child one card and ask them if they can do what is on their card to make things right with someone they have hurt. Ask the children to stand up if they would like to do the thing on their card.

**^[OBSERVE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Invite the children to look at the paper towels from the **Secret Surprise Box**.

- **How do they look now?** (They’ve changed from plain white into a rainbow, just as Zacchaeus changed from a dishonest person into a kind, loving person. Only Jesus’ love can do that!)

**CRAFT**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Butterfly**. Talk about how butterflies go through big changes. They start as a caterpillar; then they create a cocoon, in which they change, and finally turn into a butterfly! Caterpillars remind us of how Jesus’ love can change our hearts, just as it did for Zacchaeus.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned that Jesus’ love can change our hearts. This week’s Mission Challenge is for you to think about how Jesus wants others to know about His love. Our Mission Challenge for the new week is to **pray each day for the new friends God has given you. Pray that they will make Jesus their very best friend**.
