The story is told of a man, let’s call him Peter, who received a new sports car as a gift from his brother. One day, he drove it into the city and parked it on the street while he did some shopping. On returning to his car, his arms loaded with shopping bags, he saw a young homeless boy staring at the shiny, new vehicle with wide eyes.

As Peter approached the car, the boy shyly asked, “Excuse me, sir. Is this your car?”

“Yes, it is,” Peter replied. “My brother gave it to me. It was a gift.”

The boy’s eyes lit up with surprise at the thought of such a generous and expensive gift. “Wow!” he said. “That’s incredible! I wish . . . I wish . . .”

But before he could finish his sentence, Peter finished it for him. “Yes, I know, son. You wish you had a brother like that.”

“No, no, sir. That’s not it,” the boy quickly replied. “I wish . . . I just wish I could be a brother like that.”

Just like Abraham of old, we are each blessed to be a blessing (Genesis 12:2). (Acts 20:35).

Jesus said it perfectly, “It is more blessed to give than to receive”

### Appeal

As you return God’s tithe and give your Promise offerings today, consider ways that you, too, can be a brother like that. You don’t need to be rich enough to give away a sports car. God simply asks you to look at the things He has entrusted in your care, big or small, and to use them to love people. Thank you for your faithful giving today.
