**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He cares about me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Make or source a **scroll** to use and source a **bowl and cup** to serve Jesus.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 13 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- It was Sabbath. Jesus was in the synagogue in Nazareth, where He’d grown up. He taught from the Bible scrolls ^[(unroll scroll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and told people that He was the promised Savior.
- The people didn’t believe Jesus was the Savior—they thought He was just Joseph’s son. Jesus knew this and told them so.
- The people became angry and dragged Him out of town. God worked a miracle and made Jesus invisible so that He walked straight “through the crowd and went on his way” (Luke 4:30, ICB).
- Jesus went to busy, beautiful Capernaum. On Sabbath He went to the synagogue and taught the people. They listened closely. His words warmed their hearts.
- Jesus went to Simon Peter’s house for the afternoon. Simon Peter’s mother-in-law was sick with a bad fever.
- Simon Peter’s family asked Jesus to please help. Jesus took Simon Peter’s mother-in-law’s hand and told the fever to go away ^[(take child’s hand)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- She was better! She hurried to the kitchen to prepare some food for her guests as a thank You to Jesus ^[(bowl, cup)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- After the sun went down, lots of people who were sick and hurt came to Simon Peter’s house. Would Jesus heal and help them?
- Yes! Jesus cared. Jesus healed them all.
- In the morning Jesus went somewhere quiet to pray. Jesus loved and needed to spend time with God. Then He went on to a new town to spread God’s love and do good.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was Sabbath, God’s special day of rest, and Jesus was in the synagogue in Nazareth to worship God. Nazareth was where Jesus had grown up.

Jesus stood up and began to teach the people from the Bible scrolls ^[(unroll scroll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Jesus told the people that He was the promised Savior. The people “were amazed at the beautiful words he spoke” (Luke 4:22, ICB). But they didn’t think He was the Savior—they thought He was just Joseph’s son.

Jesus knew they didn’t believe His words, and He told them so.

The Pharisees were furious! The people were angry too. They grabbed Jesus and pushed Him out of the synagogue. They shoved Him down the streets and out of town and wanted to throw Him off a cliff (Luke 4:29).

Suddenly something amazing happened. God made it so that Jesus could not be seen. He turned and walked straight “through the crowd and went on his way” (Luke 4:30, ICB). God protected Jesus from the angry crowd.

Jesus would find another place to do good. He went to the beautiful, busy town of Capernaum.

On another Sabbath Jesus went to the synagogue and taught the people. Here, the people listened closely. His words spoke to their minds and warmed their hearts with love.

After the service, everyone went home with joy!

Peter was one of Jesus’ disciple friends. Peter’s family lived in Capernaum. So he invited Jesus to his house.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But at Peter’s house something was terribly wrong. Peter’s mother-in-law was sick with a fever. The fever was very bad. Her skin was hot and damp, and she lay very still on her bed.

Jesus looked at her and He knew that Peter’s family was worried. Jesus cared about Peter’s mother-in-law and her family. They asked Jesus to help her. We can always ask Jesus to help us too!

Jesus walked over to her bed and took her hand. Then He told the fever to go away!

The woman’s eyes fluttered open. She sat up and looked at Jesus with a smile. He smiled back at her. She felt so much better! Her body was now strong and healthy again. What an amazing miracle!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She thanked Jesus, then stood up and hurried to the kitchen. Soon she was back with a special plate of food for Jesus and His disciples ^[(bowl and cup)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus had cared for her, and now she wanted to care for Him. It was a good way for her to say thank You to Jesus.

What a wonderful Sabbath! The people of Capernaum were very happy that Jesus was in their town. They all wanted Jesus to heal their sick brothers and mothers and fathers and friends too.

The afternoon passed by, and soon the Sabbath day was over. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the sun went down, many people came to Simon Peter’s house. Knock, knock, knock! Some people came with a walking stick. Others who couldn’t walk were carried on stretchers. Little sick children were carried by their parents. They all had one question: Please, Jesus, can You help and heal us?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Yes. Jesus cared for every person. He healed the person with a walking stick, the ones on stretchers, and the little children. Jesus healed every person who came!

Joyful shouts filled the air as one person, and then another, left the house to go home, healthy and strong.

Before the sun came up again after a long night of healing and caring for others, Jesus slipped out the door and walked down the street. He went to a quiet place where He could pray. Jesus loved spending time with God. He prayed to His heavenly Father, who gave Him strength and help. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then Jesus went to another town to teach, help, and heal so that more people could know about God’s wonderful love for every person—and for you and me.
