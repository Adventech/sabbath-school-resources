The Israelites got up early, ready for a big party. They were so excited about their new golden calf. They made a big feast and lots of sinful sacrifices to their new idol. Then they began to sing and dance around the golden calf.

God’s people had completely forgotten about God. But He had not forgotten about them. He saw the people worshipping an idol, even though they had made a promise to only worship Him, and it made Him very, very sad.

“Go down from this mountain,” God told Moses. The people “have made for themselves a calf of melted gold. They have worshipped that calf” (Exodus 32:7, 8, ICB).

Moses climbed down the mountain with Joshua. He carried something very important in his hands. What was it? **Read Exodus 32:15, 16.**

As they came into camp, Moses and Joshua stopped in their tracks. There was the golden calf idol. And there were God’s people dancing around it.

Moses was filled with holy anger! He lifted the precious commandments above his head. Then he threw them to the ground. Crash! They smashed into many pieces.

God’s rules of love had been broken. It showed the Israelites that they had broken their promise to love and obey God.

Sin is a choice that hurts us and others. It also hurts God. The Bible says, “There is a way that seems right to a man, but its end is the way of death” (Proverbs 14:12). God wants to give us a happy, blessed life instead. He asks us to choose to obey Him. We can pray, “Teach me to do your will, for you are my God” (Psalm 143:10).
