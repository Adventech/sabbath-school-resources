**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. It’s wonderful to be together in Sabbath School again today!

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“Alive in Jesus”** and **“God Is My Faithful Friend.”** Then sing the prayer song, **“Be Still, Be Silent.”**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Invite three children to pray: one to thank God for who He is and all the ways He blesses us, one to ask for the Holy Spirit to be with us and to help us learn, and one to pray for specific prayer requests from the children.

**^[BIBLE FOCUS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“The Books of the Bible.”** Challenge the children to memorize the books of the Bible in order by the end of the quarter. Remind them that they can ask their parents to find **“The Books of the Bible”** song on the Alive in Jesus website to listen at home. Share a **Bible Fast Fact:** The shortest verse in the Bible is only two words long: “Jesus wept” (John 11:35). I wonder if God may have wept in today’s story? Think about this as we learn together.

**^[GIVE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Have a child collect the offering as you all sing **“Thank You, Jesus.”** Later, after the money has been counted by an adult, invite a child to color the next section of your **Offering Chart** to show how much has been collected this quarter. Then share this week’s **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission project. Say a short prayer for God to bless and take the offering to where it’s most needed.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/assets/thank-you-jesus.mp3] “Thank You, Jesus”

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Warmly welcome any visitor and sing the **“Visitor Song.”** Sing **“Happy Birthday”** to a child who has had a birthday this week, and pray for God to bless them.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** This week’s story takes us back to the Garden of Eden on earth.

- **Who do you think the story characters might be?** Record their answers on the whiteboard (e.g., Adam, Eve, God. If they say snake or serpent, write this too).

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Operate the remote-controlled vehicle. If you feel comfortable doing so, invite several children to have a turn. Each time there is a different person in control, ask:

- **Who is operating the vehicle?**
- **Can the vehicle choose where it goes?**

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** After the first part of the story, we will unpack the important lessons the remote-controlled vehicle teaches us. Start thinking about this, and how it might connect to this week’s **Big Idea**.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard: **When I make a wrong choice, God still loves me and has a plan to save.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**,
including an activity with a remote-controlled car.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Close your eyes and count to 20 while everyone **hides** in the room without opening cupboards or moving things. (Most will be visible when you open your eyes.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How easy/hard was it to hide? Why?**
- **What does this remind you of in today’s story?** (Adam and Eve trying unsuccessfully to hide from God.)
- **Why did Adam and Eve try to hide from God?** (They felt guilty, afraid, worried, ashamed, naked, sad, lonely, etc.)
- **Why did God come to meet with them? Read Deuteronomy 31:8.** (God will never leave us.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Display your broken plate pieces and invite a group of children to try piecing the plate back together (carefully!).

- **How successful were you?** (Not very!)
- **What could the broken plate/tile and your inability to put it back together remind you of?** (Accept ideas.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Wrong choices affect us, like the breaks in the plate/tile. We can’t put ourselves back together, but our forgiving God promises to do just that when we accept Jesus and His plan to save us.
