**Big Idea:**\
Jesus can do anything. He loves to help me and my friends.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“I have come that they may have life, and that they may have it more abundantly”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (John 10:10).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Friend”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Friends are people who love each other no matter what. Good friends put the other person first. They care when their friend is hurting and love bringing happiness to each other.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Mark 2:1–12; Matthew 9:1–8; and _The Desire of Ages_, p. 267-271.

“Do You Know? Have You Heard?”

The people of Galilee were busily buzzing about, like busy bees. The town was a hive of excitement. Everyone had a story to tell about Jesus, the miracle man!

Some of them had seen Jesus heal a man with leprosy—a terrible kind of sickness. The Man had white, blotchy skin and parts of his body that were badly hurt because he couldn’t feel or move them anymore. Jesus had spoken a few words and the man’s skin and body had changed to be like new!

Others had seen a woman healed from a terrible fever. Jesus had held her by the hand, and immediately, the fever went away!

These stories traveled up and down the streets. Chatter, chatter, chatter.

The people couldn’t keep their excitement bottled inside. They had to tell everyone. Neighbors told neighbors, and shopkeepers told travelers. Jesus made the town people so happy! What amazing news this was to share. They wanted everyone to know all about Jesus.

It didn’t take long for big crowds to find and follow Jesus. They all wanted to see Him with their very own eyes. They wanted to meet Him and hear Him talk with their very own ears. Jesus’ disciples said to Him, “Everyone is looking for you!” (Mark 1:37, ICB).

What an exciting, busy, wonderful time it was in little Capernaum.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- What are some miracles in the Bible you know that Jesus did?
- What would it be like to see Jesus do a miracle right in front of you?
- Who would you like the miracle to be for?

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Print and cut out the picture of Jesus from the AIJ website (or draw Him and cut out). Attach a popsicle stick and make a “crowd” of people from play blocks or dry beans. Put them on a tray. Move Jesus and have the “crowd” follow. Pretend Jesus is talking to the people. What do you think He says?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus that He always wants to help people feel better again.
