---
title: Disciple-Making Culture
---

Global Total Member Involvement is not just a slogan or a theme. It’s a call for every church to develop an ongoing disciple-making culture based on the following three principles:

#### 1. Success is based on the number of workers, not just baptisms.

The goal is not just to reach everyone, but to involve everyone.

“The real character of the church is measured, not by the high profession she makes, not by the names enrolled on her books, but by what she is actually doing for the Master, by the number of her persevering, ^[faithful workers]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Gospel Workers_, p. 200).\
“When we are successful in the work of soulsaving, those who are added to the faith will, in turn, use their ability in ^[giving the truth]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) to others” (_Testimonies for the Church_, vol. 9, p. 86).

#### 2. Personal labor, which was Christ’s method, is the primary work of the church.

Church programs and events support personal ministry, not the other way around. Not only are large events not a substitute for personal labor, but they can’t be successful without it.

{"style":{"block":{"backgroundColor": "#F5F5F3","rounded": true, "padding": {"top":"base","bottom":"base","start":"base","end":"base"}}}}
“The Lord desires that His word of grace shall be brought home to every soul. To a great degree this must be accomplished by ^[personal labor]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}). This was ^[Christ’s method]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})” (_Christian Service_, p. 117).\
“If we can have but one part done, let it be the ^[individual labor]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) of opening the Scriptures in households” (_Christian Service_, p. 121).

#### 3. The church’s disciple-making plan is to be active and ongoing.

The role of the local church in disciple-making is twofold. First, it is to inspire, equip, and involve church members in personal labor year-round. Second, it should plan calendar events and programs that can minister to the friends and interests of church members, while also generating new interests and ministering to the community.
