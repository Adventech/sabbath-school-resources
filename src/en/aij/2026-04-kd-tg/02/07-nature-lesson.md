**Focus:** Just as God helps animals, He can help us in amazing ways.

In the beginning of our world, God created animals by speaking! He is so powerful. His words made so many wonderful animals. He even made animals that can quickly heal after they have been hurt.

**Has anyone seen a starfish before?** ^[(Show picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/02/nature-lesson.png)

Starfish live in oceans all around the world—deep down at the bottom of the sea in rock pools, on coral reefs, or in sea grass forests. They are also called sea stars because they are not really fish. They are called invertebrates because they don’t have a backbone (point to your backbone).

**Can you hold out your arms? How many do you have?** Most starfish have five arms. Their skin is hard and bumpy. Many bigger fish, sea turtles, and crabs like to eat starfish. But they often chomp down on only one arm. Ouch! A starfish can lose an arm—or two.

**Can you guess what happens to the starfish?** Its arm grows back! Even when a poor starfish has only one arm left, it can grow back another four arms! Isn’t God amazing to make them that way?

On the land there are also animals that God has made to quickly heal. Lizards can quickly grow back tails after losing them. And God has made a deer’s antlers to grow back every year! A deer’s antlers are made of bone. **Can you point to your bones?** Imagine growing a whole new bone every year!

**Teaching Point:** God helps the animals He has made in amazing ways. We can trust Him to help us too. Let’s say our **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. His words can heal and are always true** [use hands as an open book]**.”**
