Martha frowned as she pressed a cool cloth to her brother’s forehead. Lazarus had suddenly become very sick! Mary and Martha tried to care for him and help him but he grew worse and worse. The anxious sisters thought about their good Friend Jesus and how He would be able to heal Lazarus. He was a two-day journey away, so they sent Him a simple message: “Lord, the one you love is sick” (John 11:3, ICB).

When Jesus heard the message, His disciples were surprised that He didn’t seem worried or sad. His good friend was sick! They were even more surprised that He didn’t leave for Bethany right away so that He could heal Lazarus.

The disciples didn’t understand the whole situation, but Jesus did. He knew Lazarus would die. He planned to go to Bethany later and use His loving power to call him out of his grave and bring him back to life.

Jesus planned it this way. Here was an opportunity to prove He was truly God’s Son.

Jesus also knew the people would be blessed by seeing and experiencing the happy ending to Lazarus’ story. He could see that resurrecting Lazarus would help them believe in the final resurrection of the dead at the end of time. **Read about God’s resurrection promise in 1 Corinthians 15:52.** Now, that’s a beautiful promise!

Jesus sees and understands situations so much better than we do. It’s as if He has a whole jigsaw puzzle, while we have only a few puzzle pieces. We can trust Him to help us understand a bit more of the puzzle, just as Jesus did for the disciples when He said, “This sickness will not end in death. It is for the glory of God. This has happened to bring glory to the Son of God” (John 11:4, ICB).
