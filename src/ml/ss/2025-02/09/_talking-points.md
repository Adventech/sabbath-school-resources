> <p>Memory Verse</p>
> “Let the peoples praise You, O God; let all the peoples
praise You. Oh, let the nations be glad and sing for joy! For You shall judge the people righteously, and govern the nations on earth. Selah.” Psalm 67:3, 4, NKJV

### ^[Lesson Study Intro]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

This week’s lesson highlights some of God’s most important roles within the framework of prophecy.

### ^[1. God is our refuge and strength (Sun, Mon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- Prophecy foretells a “time of trouble, such as never was since there was a nation” (Dan. 12:1).
	- In light of this, it is encouraging to know that God is “our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble” (Ps. 46:1).
		- There are multiple Psalms that share similarly about God’s help in times of trouble (Ps. 27:5; 37:39; 41:1).
- We’ve studied previously of the uncertainty of our times, and the failure of every earthly kingdom.
	- “[T]he grand climax of earth’s history will mean the breakdown of worldy kingdoms – the miserable systems of human government that have caused so much suffering over the millennia… But we know that we have citizenship in the kingdom of God (Phil. 3:20), and, high above the chaos of this planet, there is a throne that cannot be moved.” Qtly, Mon, par 1, 4

### ^[2. God is our king and cause of rejoicing (Tue)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- Psalm 47 speaks of God as the King “over all the earth,” and the rejoicing that comes as a result (see Ps. 47:1, 2, 6, 7).
	- The lesson highlights how this has not always been the case; the dominion of the earth was given the human race (Gen. 1:26-28), who in turn delivered it into the hands of the devil (Lk. 4:6; cf. Job 1:6, 7) who Jesus afterward identified as “the ruler of this world” (Jn. 12:31; 14:30; 16:11).
	- However, prophecy reveals that the time will come when the kingdoms of the world will be returned to humanity in the person of Christ (Dan. 7:27; Heb. 2:5-9).
		- We are “heirs of God and joint heirs with Christ” (Rom. 8:17).
- ^[The divine Intercessor presents the plea that all who have overcome through faith in His blood be forgiven their transgressions, that they be restored to their Eden home, and crowned as joint heirs with Himself to “the first dominion” (Mic. 4:8). Satan in his efforts to deceive and tempt our race had thought to frustrate the divine plan in man's creation; but Christ now asks that this plan be carried into effect as if man had never fallen. He asks for His people not only pardon and justification, full and complete, but a share in His glory and a seat upon His throne. GC 483.3]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

### ^[3. God is our glory (Wed, Thu)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F19637"}}})

- We must first encounter God’s glory before we can reflect God’s glory (Ps. 67:1, 2).
	- God’s glory shines upon His people, so that they can in turn reflect that glory to the world around them (Rev. 18:1; 14:6, 7).
		- “God intends that we reflect His light to the rest of the world…. Tragically, God’s people have often failed in this task…. It is as if we have painted our hearts with an ultra-dark substance, content to absorb God’s light without reflecting it.” Qtly, Thu, par 3, 4
- ^[The last rays of merciful light, the last message of mercy to be given to the world, is a revelation of His character of love. The children of God are to manifest His glory. In their own life and character they are to reveal what the grace of God has done for them. COL 415.5]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})
- This proclamation of God’s character leads to the conversion of souls and an abundant harvest (Ps. 67:6), after which “all the ends of the earth shall fear Him” (v. 7) and give Him glory!
	- “When Christ told us to carry the gospel to the world, He did not leave us alone to figure out how it might happen. The work is directed from heaven’s sanctuary. Our work is intimately wrapped up in Christ: He guides and empowers us. This is His work, not ours: we are asked to follow His lead. You will notice this is how it worked with Israel: God asked them to follow His instructions, and then He made the impossible happen…. We do not have to invent new means and methods because we have never been in charge of the work.” Qtly, Fri, par 3

### ^[Conclusion]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})

^[Light is a blessing, a universal blessing, pouring forth its treasures on a world unthankful, unholy, demoralized. So it is with the light of the Sun of Righteousness. The whole earth, wrapped as it is in the darkness of sin, and sorrow, and pain, is to be lighted with the knowledge of God's love…. The message of hope and mercy is to be carried to the ends of the earth. Whosoever will, may reach forth and take hold of God's strength and make peace with Him, and he shall make peace. No longer are the heathen to be wrapped in midnight darkness. The gloom is to disappear before the bright beams of the Sun of Righteousness. COL 418]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#0070C0"}}})
