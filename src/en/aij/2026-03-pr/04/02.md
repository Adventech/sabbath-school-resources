The Wise Men were full of joy as they packed up their belongings and prepared to leave Bethlehem. They had found the Messiah and worshipped Him, so now it was time to begin their long journey home. Not knowing about Herod’s hate-filled heart, they planned to stop in Jerusalem and share their wonderful news with him. Then he, and all the Hebrew people, could find Baby Jesus and worship Him too. Or so they thought.

But God had a different plan. He was watching over those Wise Men, and He knew that stopping in Jerusalem was a very dangerous idea. He needed to warn them right away! That night God chose to give them guidance in a vivid dream. What did God tell them to do, and how did they respond? **Read Matthew 2:12.**

The Wise Men were truly very wise. They hadn’t grown up knowing God, but they had learned to trust Him because He had already guided them in a number of ways. At first God led them to the Bible scrolls to help them understand the amazing things they saw in the starry heavens. He guided them to accept Him as the Creator. Then God pointed them to the prophecies about Jesus’ birth so that they could begin to trust Him. The Bible promises that “Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path” (Psalm 119:105).

God then guided them through nature; they followed His brilliant star of shining angels in the night sky. Then after they had worshipped Jesus and given Him their hearts, God used a dream to lead them safely home.

God longs to guide us too! The Bible says, “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God” (Romans 8:14). As we grow closer to Jesus, we will get better at recognizing God’s guidance in the Bible, in nature, and in other ways.
