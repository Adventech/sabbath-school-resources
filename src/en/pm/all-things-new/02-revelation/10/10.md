[[Revelation 10:1]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- As with the seventh seal at the end of chapter 6, the revealing of the seventh trumpet is also delayed, and instead John is introduced to another prophetic scene.

[[Revelation 10:2-3]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- This “angel,” or messenger, is most likely Jesus Christ, as characteristics mentioned here are also mentioned in chapter 1’s description of Him (see Revelation 1:13-16; compare Daniel 10:5, 6).
- His standing on both the land and the sea shows His authority over the whole earth and that His message is of global importance.

[[Revelation 10:4-7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The book in this mighty angel’s hand is “open” (verse 2), that is, it is unsealed, unlike the scroll in chapter 5, which was sealed seven times over.
- This unsealed book likely represents the prophecies of the book of Daniel that were sealed. To see why this is so, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r10/)**.
- Because the book of Daniel is shown as being unsealed, we can expect that portions of Daniel not previously explained will now be revealed in the remainder of Revelation.
- This is a powerful reason to study the books of Daniel and Revelation together. God has clearly built strong connections between the two.
- Verse 6’s declaration that there “should be delay no longer” (literally, “there shall be no more [prophetic] time”—the underlying word is _chronos_) also indicates that since the 2,300-day prophecy of Daniel 8—the longest of all time prophecies God has ever given—reached its fulfilment in 1844, God will no longer give prophecies regarding time. In other words, God is saying that because the planet is nearing its end, no more prophetic time periods are called for. Justice is coming—and is almost here! And the answer to the people of God who cried out in Revelation 6, “How long, O Lord, holy and true, until You judge and avenge our blood . . . ?” is now given: it will be coming soon.

[[Revelation 10:8-11]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Prophecies about the very end of time can be exciting and inspiring. However, _living_ through the very end of time will not be easy.
- Revelation makes clear that until Jesus returns, the world will get worse, not better—a bit like eating a sweet dessert, only to have it turn our stomachs sour as time wears on.
- But those who follow Jesus will be given the strength needed to “prophesy again”—that is, to continue to live like Jesus and carry out His just, good, and loving will on earth.
- The phrase “prophesy again” is also significant in that it calls for a special message from Jesus in the last days of earth’s history to be given to all “peoples, nations, tongues, and kings.”
