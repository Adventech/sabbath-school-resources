**Focus:** We can sometimes let other things come between us and God.

- **Have you ever seen a full moon in the night sky?**
- **Let’s find out what an eclipse of the moon is and what it can teach us.** ^[(Show picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/06/nature-lesson.png)

Elaine Egbert stepped outside to check the full moon. This is what she saw.

I knew that there would be a total eclipse of the moon that night, hiding it from my view. It was hard to imagine because right when I stepped outside, the moon shone brightly from the cloudless sky, stretching my shadow out behind me.

I kept watching carefully. Soon our earth would pass between the moon and the sun, blocking my view of the full moon and creating an eclipse.

First, I saw just a hint of a shadow on the moon’s left side, as though a cloud had crowded it. Later, only half the moon shone. At last, just before midnight, the whole of the moon’s face was eclipsed, or hidden. It looked dark brownish-red, and my shadow no longer stretched out behind me.

As I gazed at the moon that had been so bright earlier, I thought about God, and you, and me. God is like the moon. He shines His blessings on us and promises to guide us. When we accept His blessings and follow Him, we feel peaceful and happy.

But sometimes we let other things come between God and us. We might choose to do things our own way, or we might choose to ignore God’s instructions. Then the joy and happiness Jesus provides gets blocked, and it’s as if He is eclipsed, or hidden from us. His love is still there—but our sin is in the way.

When we find ourselves in this position, it’s important to make things right with God. We can ask for His forgiveness and accept His mercy.

**When sin and selfishness get between you and God, what can you do?** (Pray. Say you’re sorry. Ask for His forgiveness.)
