**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He helps me to be kind.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Blessed be the Lord, for He has shown me His marvelous kindness”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (Psalm 31:21)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Kindness”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Kindness is love in action. When you are kind, you are helpful and friendly to others. We can show kindness with caring words, listening ears, willing feet, and helpful hands.

An Important Decision

Abraham walked slowly toward the door and stepped outside his tent into the fresh air and warm sunshine. He leaned on his walking stick. In the distance he could see his olive trees and golden fields of grain. Beside them were his green fields filled with fat, woolly sheep. Abraham smiled. God had been so kind and generous. He had given Abraham a family, land, food, money, and more sheep and cows than he could count. But Abraham’s best gift was God’s friendship. Abraham was so happy to be called a “friend of God” (James 2:23).

Abraham loved to tell others about his Friend, the one true God. But in the nearby towns in Canaan, people didn’t believe in God. Instead they prayed to wooden idols that couldn’t see or hear. These idols definitely couldn’t show love or kindness to others.

Abraham had taught his son Isaac about the one true God. Isaac loved God too. Now that Isaac was all grown up, it was time to find him a wife who also loved and worshipped the God of heaven.

Abraham called for Eliezer, his most trusted servant. What did he ask Eliezer to do? **Read Genesis 24:4.**

How exciting! Eliezer was to go on a big trip to Abraham’s faraway family. There, Eliezer was to find Isaac a wife who believed in God and lived for Him.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What kind gifts has God given to your family?
- How do you know that God is your Friend?
