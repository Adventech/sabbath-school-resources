**What you need:**

Week 5 craft template, crayons, scissors, two small pieces of yarn, tape, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-kd-craft-05.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color in page 1 of the craft resource and cut out. (Color the side flaps blue for the sea, and the middle section orange or brown for the dry land.)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5b.jpg)

2\. Fold the side flaps into the middle. Color this side of the flaps blue also.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5c.jpg)

3\. Tape a small piece of yarn onto each flap.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5d.jpg)

4\. Cut out the pictures. Fold a small section at the bottom of each picture.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5e.jpg)

5\. Glue these folded sections on the bottom of the Red Sea page so the pictures stand up.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5f.jpg)

6\. Use the yarn to slowly pull the flaps open while you retell the story of God parting the Red Sea.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/5g.jpg)
