Depending on God

In the world washed of wickedness Noah and his family set about finding a good place to plant gardens, begin farming, and make a warm home. For a time the family stayed in the mountain area near to where the ark had come to rest. Here Noah, Shem, Ham, and Japheth built cozy houses from whatever they could find.

With happy hearts they depended totally on God to guide them, for life was not easy in the flood-damaged world. Plants and forests had been ripped out of the ground, buried, or washed away. Rocks and rubble needed to be cleared, forests replanted, and vegetable gardens sown with seeds. It was hard work! Why were they happy? Read Psalm 144:15.

The Bible says it well in Philippians 4:11: “I have learned to be satisfied with the things I have and with everything that happens” (ICB). God was pleased that they were happy and content as they depended on Him for everything.
