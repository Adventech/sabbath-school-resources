Put others first as you explore, make, and do this Sabbath.

The Bible has wise words to help us live unselfishly. Think about this verse as you head out into nature and share the Sabbath with others. “When you do things, do not let selfishness or pride be your guide. Be humble and give more honor to others than yourselves” (Philippians 2:3, ICB).

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#BC3C2D", "size": "xl"}}}
Nature Walk:

- Let your family choose a favorite place to walk.
- As you walk, look for ways you can put them first. (For example, let them walk in front so they have the best view, be happy for them to stop and look at nature whenever they want, give them the best resting spot, carry water and snacks for them.)
- What else could you choose to do to put
them first?

Make and Do:

- Think about the adults in your life who put you first. For example, who prepares your meals, washes your clothes, works hard so you have a home and food to eat. How can you say thank you? You might like to prepare a very simple meal, sweep the floor, do the dishes, tidy the house, help weed the garden . . .
