The weeks quickly roll by, and here we are again—another quarter. Whether you’re stretched thin or enjoying this season of life, we hope that the messages in this quarter speak to you as well as your child.

Indeed, Jesus can do anything as much today as He did when He lived on this earth. So, whatever you’re facing today, remember that He is with you now and tomorrow, and in all the days ahead. He came to show us what His Father is really like, to connect us with heaven, and to do that which was impossible without Him—to save us from sin.

Reach out for Him today, for He loves you as much as He loves your child,

^[Nina]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#E5723F", "typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}})\
