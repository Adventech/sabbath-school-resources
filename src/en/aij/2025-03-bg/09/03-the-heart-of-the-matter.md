Isn’t it amazing that all 12 spies had exactly the same experience in scouting out the land and yet they had such different responses to what they saw? Two men were filled with hope and excitement for the adventure ahead. They chose to have faith that God would fulfill His promise. The other 10 were determined to focus on the impossible, and were full of doubt and negativity. “In their unbelief they limited the power of God and distrusted the hand that had hitherto safely guided them” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets,_ p. 388).

It is not always easy to be positive and believe the best. In fact, sometimes it is downright hard! We might face problems with our marriage, our children, our health, finances, or work, and these things can seem as huge as the giants the spies glimpsed from afar. But God still leads us; we are never alone. When we trust Him, He will continue to move us forward.

God has amazing things planned for us—things that are far beyond our comprehension! He doesn’t want us to be sidetracked by the problems Satan puts in our path. He wants us to be empowered to trust His leading and hand over any “giant” problems to Him. Joshua and Caleb assured the people, “The Lord is with us. Do not fear them” (Numbers 14:9).

As you go about your week this week, build your faith by telling others (especially your children) about the good things God has done, and is doing, in your life. May we spread positive and faith-filled messages of hope, just as Joshua and Caleb did!
