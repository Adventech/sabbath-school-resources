Peter’s eyes bulged, and his mouth hung open in surprise! He had thrown his net into the water, just as Jesus had said. Now he and Andrew tried to pull in their net full of wiggling, squiggling fish, but it was way too heavy. In fact, it was breaking! And the boat was tilting . . . sinking . . . almost overturning! They called out for James and John to row their boat over and help.

With strong, quick strokes they soon arrived. Then their eyes bulged too—what a catch! Together the four fishermen loaded the slippery fish into both boats until they almost sank. Slowly, carefully, the disciples rowed to shore.

By the time they got there, Peter was no longer thinking about the fish or his boat. He had been amazed by the miracle, for it showed Jesus’ power to control even nature. He felt sinful and ashamed of his doubt and discouragement when he compared himself to Jesus. He clung to Jesus’ feet, saying, “Depart from me, for I am a sinful man, O Lord!” (Luke 5:8). Peter’s clinging hands showed how much he wanted to be with Jesus, yet his words showed how sinful and unworthy he felt.

Jesus understood Peter’s confused heart. What did He say? **Read Luke 5:10.**

A new mission! Jesus also gave these encouraging words: “Do not be afraid.” Then Jesus called James, John, and Andrew too. So “they left everything and followed Jesus” (Luke 5:11, ICB).

Jesus had already called Andrew, John, and Peter to give Him their hearts and follow Him as a special friend. Now He called them to work with Him as close disciples. His miracle showed them that they didn’t need to fish full time anymore to earn money. Instead, they could trust Jesus to provide for their needs. We can also follow Jesus and trust Him to care for us and fill our lives with His goodness.
