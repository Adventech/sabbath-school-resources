**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He wants me to seek Him and find Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **angel sticks** from week 1 of this quarter to wave when angels are mentioned and a large star for the children to follow as the Wise Men did. For the gifts, include **something gold** or **a box covered in gold paper**, a little jar of herbs/incense for **frankincense**, a bottle of perfume or oil for **myrrh**.
- Use a **picture of Jesus in a house with Mary** to represent the home in Bethlehem.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 3 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The night was peaceful and quiet on the hills outside Bethlehem. Huddled around a fire sat a group of shepherds. They laughed and talked together as they watched over their sheep. Their most favorite thing to talk about was the coming Savior. When would He come? Oh, how they wanted it to be soon!

The shepherds looked up at the starry sky as their sheep slept on the soft grass. All was quiet. All was still. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly a dazzling angel ^[(wave angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) appeared before them. The angel’s light was so bright that it made the whole sky light up all around them. The shepherds jumped back in surprise. Their eyes opened wide. What was this? They were scared!

“Don’t be afraid, because I am bringing you some good news,” said the angel ^[(wave angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). “It will be a joy to all the people. Today your Savior was born” (Luke 2:10, 11, ICB).

The angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) told them Jesus was in Bethlehem, wrapped in cloth, lying in a manger.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Thousands of angels ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) filled the sky with brilliant light. In their loudest and happiest voices the angels ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) sang, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace . . . toward men (Luke 2:14). It was the most beautiful song the shepherds had ever heard. When the beautiful song finished, the angels ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) disappeared. The light faded, and the darkness returned.

The shepherds could hardly breathe, they were so excited! Jumping up, they ran to Bethlehem ^[(running action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Huffing and panting, the shepherds stopped outside a stable. They gently opened the door. Shh! There lay Baby Jesus! He was sleeping peacefully in a manger, just as the angel ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) had said. The shepherds knelt down and worshipped Him.

Then the happy shepherds ran to tell everyone. They had found their King and Savior!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When Baby Jesus was a little bigger, Mary and Joseph took Him to the temple in Jerusalem. It was time to thank God for the precious gift of Jesus. There were two special people who worked in the temple. They had been looking for Jesus for a very long time! One was an old man named Simeon, and the other was an old woman named Anna. As soon as they saw Baby Jesus, they knew He was the Savior that the scrolls had written about—the one God had promised. They were so happy to see Him. With tears in their eyes they thanked God for sending Jesus!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Far, far away others were looking for Jesus too. A group of Wise Men. One night (the very night Baby Jesus was born!) they looked at the sparkling stars in the night sky and noticed something different. It was an unusual light in the sky. Then the light faded and turned into a sparkly star that moved ^[(show star)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). How interesting! They remembered the Old Testament scriptures that said a star would be the sign of God’s promised King.

The Wise Men decided they must follow the star. They must meet and welcome this very special new King! Quickly they saddled their camels and packed their bags with three special gifts for Him. Then they set out to search for the new King ^[(lead children with star)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The Wise Men traveled across the sand, over hills, through distant lands. Every night they followed the star.

After a very long journey the Wise Men came to the royal city of Jerusalem. Of course, that’s where they would find the baby King! But King Herod didn’t know anything about a newborn King.

The Wise Men left Jerusalem. Where to now? The special star was moving again. It led them to a little home in Bethlehem ^[(children follow the star to the picture of Jesus in a home with Mary). ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Here they found Mary with Jesus—the King!

The Wise Men dropped to their knees. With hearts full of joy they worshipped Jesus. Then, opening their bags, they pulled out three gifts. They gave Jesus shiny gold, special frankincense, and a sweet-smelling oil called myrrh. These were very special gifts for a king.

The Wise Men were so happy. They had finally found God’s King!
