The Animal Parade

“Come into the ark,” God said to His friend Noah (Genesis 7:1). It was time! The ark was big, beautiful, well made, and watertight. It was ready to float.

God was ready too. He would wash the earth clean with a flood. With Noah’s help He had invited everyone to come into the ark and be saved.

Sadly, the people didn’t believe God would send a flood. With loud voices they made fun of Noah and his family, and their big boat sitting on dry land.

Suddenly something quieted the noisy crowd. Their eyes opened wide in surprise. What was happening? Read Genesis 7:8, 9.

Tall giraffes, strong tigers, beautiful peacocks, and little ducks came down from the mountains and out of the forests. White birds, black birds, and rainbow-colored birds flew over the trees. Rhinos, fluffy rabbits, and buzzing beetles came too.

Animals waddled, walked, crept, slithered, glided, and hopped their way toward the ark. Invisible angels led them in perfect order! Up, up, up the ramp and in through the door they went. What an amazing animal parade!

Some animals came in twos of the same kind; and some came in seven pairs of the same kind, both males and females. God told Noah, “This will allow all these animals to continue living on the earth after the flood” (Genesis 7:3, ICB).

“There was every creature that had the breath of life” (Genesis 7:15, ICB). The ark had become a great big zoo!
