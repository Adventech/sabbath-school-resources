When Jesus stopped for Zacchaeus, the thief who was up in the tree, He made it known that He loves all people and sees their potential. He knows that we are all lost sinners without Him. On that day Jesus preached the best news the world had, and still has, ever heard! “For the Son of Man has come to seek and to save that which was lost” (Luke 19:10).

Zacchaeus owned up to his sins and let God start to change him. There were many in the crowd who didn’t think their own pride, greed, ambition, mean talking, gossiping, or selfishness was a problem. They didn’t know they were lost.

The Bible teaches us that everything that is more important than God is a sin (Exodus 20:3). One day a very rich man walked sadly away from Jesus. He had decided not to follow the Savior because he didn’t want to give up his riches. As He walked away, what did Jesus ask His disciples? **Read Mark 10:23.**

The disciples wondered, “Who then can be saved?” (Mark 10:26). Jesus replied, “With men it is impossible, but not with God; for with God all things are possible” (Mark 10:27).

With God all things are possible for you too! You can’t save yourself, no matter how kind or giving you try to be. You need Jesus. With the love of Jesus working in your heart, you will want to please and obey God with your words and actions, just as Zacchaeus did. You can count on Him to help you. Others will be truly blessed when they see you living up to the potential God sees in you.
