On the very first Sabbath, Adam and Eve learned how amazing and creative God was when He made the world with just words. They must have had so many questions. “They may have asked God why a giraffe has such a tall neck . . . how He made the enormous sun and small bees (how do they make their sweet honey?) . . . and why grass is green.” God must have loved their curiosity. He knew that learning new things about Him and His creation would help them understand and love Him more.

Later, when Jesus came to earth, He also knew how important it was for God’s people to keep learning about God too. So He helped to teach them. How did Jesus help others learn about God on the Sabbath? **Read Luke 4:16.**

When Jesus lived on earth, His church was held in a synagogue. Jesus loved to worship there on Sabbath. He read to the people from the Bible scrolls to help them learn new things about His Father God so that they would get to know Him better.

Reading Bible stories on Sabbath is a special way for us to get to know God better too. The story about Jesus calming the scary storm helps us know that He is with us when we’re frightened (Mark 4:35-41). The story of Jesus blessing the children helps us know that He will bless us too (Mark 10:13-16).

We can also learn about God and His creation by spending time in nature on Sabbath, noticing all the small things that we can easily miss when we’re rushing.

What an awesome God we can learn more about every Sabbath!

^[NATURE CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Plan a Sabbath nature walk to see what you can learn about God outdoors. See who can find the smallest nature object and who can find the biggest! Find something in nature from each color of the rainbow, or something that reminds you of a Bible story you know well. What do these things teach you about God?

^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Find some nature books to browse through on Sabbath. You may learn such things as:\
The world’s tallest tree is as high as a 35-story building.\
Your heart beats about 115,200 times a day.\
A cat has 32 muscles in each of its ears.\
A dragonfly has six legs, but it can’t walk.\
Plan a treasure hunt and hide these facts for your friends and family to find.
