Young Ellen Harmon was not a strong young woman. She was often sick. She couldn’t take long walks or carry heavy loads. But God chose Ellen to be a special messenger for Him.

One day while Ellen was praying in her home with others, God gave her a vision. A vision is like an awake dream from God. While Ellen was having the vision, she picked up their family Bible and held it with just one hand. This Bible was very, very large. The people in the room must have wondered, How can she do this? Surely she cannot hold the Bible for long. It is just too heavy!

Her mother knew that Ellen couldn’t even lift the big, heavy Bible with both hands. She realized that Jesus must be giving her special strength.

The people were amazed. For more than a half hour Ellen held up that heavy Bible in one hand, while God showed her the vision.

Everyone there that day knew that the vision must have been from God.

This miracle also showed everyone that the Bible is very important to God. The words inside are powerful and show us what is right. When the Holy Spirit lives in our hearts, God can use the words in the Bible to change us, guide us, and strengthen us against temptation.

The Bible says, “Your word I have hidden in my heart, that I might not sin against You” (Psalm 119:11).
