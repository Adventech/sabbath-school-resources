**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Interact warmly with each child as they arrive at Sabbath School. Use their name, look them in the eyes, and smile as you connect with them. As much as possible, be free from Sabbath School preparation so you can engage meaningfully.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Ask children to **draw a picture of a friend** they enjoy spending time with. Around their picture they can write words to describe what makes this person a good friend. Share that our story today is about a man whose friends helped him in an amazing way.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Lay a large bedsheet on the floor. Invite a volunteer, preferably a small child, to lie in the middle of the sheet and pretend that they can’t move. Share that the man in our story lived with paralysis—he couldn’t move parts of his body, and he couldn’t walk.

Next, ask for four volunteers. Ask the children to each pick up a corner of the sheet and carry the child on the sheet around the room.

Explain that the man in our story today had some friends who carried him in a similar way.

Display any drawings done by children before Sabbath School with their descriptive words about friends. Read the words out loud and talk about how good it is to have kind friends.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **Godly friends bring me to Jesus, who has the power to heal my body and forgive my sins.** Let’s find out how that happened for the man with paralysis.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the Bible story with enthusiasm.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask one child to quickly **build a cup pyramid**, using five cups on the base, then balancing four cups on the next level, then three cups above, then two cups. Show the children the one special cup you have prepared earlier, with a rubber band with four strings attached around the rubber band. Explain that four volunteers need to hold only the strings (one each), to pick up the cup, move it, and place it on top of your cup pyramid. Repeat with different children as time allows.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Was this an easy challenge?** (Accept all responses.)
- **Did the four friends find it challenging to get their friend to Jesus?** (Yes.)
- **What were their challenges?** (Carrying him, not being able to get through the crowd, getting up onto the roof, making a hole in the roof, then lowering him down.)
- **Did they give up? Should we give up?** (No.)

**Teaching Point:** God wants us to remember that He helps us with difficult things. We can bring our friends to Jesus, even when we might face challenges. He wants us to persevere.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 42: Carrying a Friend to Jesus** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture.

- **What’s your favorite moment in this story?**

Say this week’s **memory verse**, “Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love, in honor giving preference to one another” (Romans 12:10). Then sing the **Scripture Song “Romans 12:10”** together.

- **What do you think “in honor, giving preference to one another” means?**

Give a child an object and invite them to show what it might look like to offer this to a friend first.

- **How did the four friends in this week’s story show preference to one another?**
- **How does this verse say we should treat each other?**

If time allows, invite the children to form a circle, with each child taking a turn to say the next word in the memory verse. Alternatively, children could pass a ball or beanbag to the next person as they say a word.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Goats and Friends,”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Ask the children if they know any words or sentences in another language. Explain that this week’s Mission Challenge is to **learn how to say “Jesus loves you” in another language and say it to someone who speaks that language**.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your **A Hole in the Rooftop craft** and explain how to make it.

**Note:** Allow at least 10 minutes for this craft. You may like to ask for the help of several additional adults.

Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly in the background as children complete their craft, and encourage them to retell the story in their own words as they make the story model.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Briefly recall how the friends use their hands to bring their friend to Jesus. Invite the children to hold hands with a friend next to them or with a helper. Ask:

- **Do you have a friend who needs your help in coming closer to Jesus?**

Ask them to gently squeeze their friend’s hand when they have thought of a name. Share that just as the man in today’s Bible story was blessed to have godly friends, we can choose to be a godly friend to someone this week.

- **How might you use your hands this week to start bringing them to Jesus?**

Remind the children that being a loving friend is an important first step.

**^[SAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** On the count of three, invite each child to swing their hands up (still holding another’s hand) and say, “You can do it, friend!”

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to stand in a circle and link arms like a chain. Thank God for friends. Sing **“God Be with You.”** Then pray for opportunities for everyone in the circle to be a good friend this week and share God’s love with others.
