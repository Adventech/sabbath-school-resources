**Big Idea:** Jesus encourages me to keep trusting Him when things look hopeless.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus sipped some cool water, then He sat back on His cushion. What a wonderful feast! He was at Matthew’s house in Capernaum. (Show Capernaum on the map.) Matthew the disciple had been a tax collector. The Jewish leaders did not like tax collectors, but Jesus was kind to everyone.

A plate of food was passed around. Suddenly a man named Jairus burst into the room. He was a ruler of the Jewish synagogue. Out of breath and very upset, the ruler threw himself at the feet of Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jairus say? Read Mark 5:23.** (“My little daughter lies at the point of death. Come and lay Your hands on her, that she may be healed, and she will live.”)

There was no doubt in Jairus’ mind that Jesus could heal his 12-year-old daughter. He had been encouraged by the many stories he had heard about Jesus. When Jesus heard Jairus’ request and saw his faith, His heart was touched. Jesus immediately left Matthew’s house with the disciples and a crowd of followers. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The tight crowd moved very slowly. Especially when Jesus stopped along the way to heal someone or encourage and comfort another. Jairus’ daughter was seriously ill and needed healing, but others needed Jesus too.

As Jesus and the crowd walked slowly on, a messenger ran toward them. He told Jairus, “Your daughter is dead. Do not trouble the Teacher” (Luke 8:49).

When Jesus heard this, He said, “Do not be afraid; only believe, and she will be made well” (Luke 8:50).

Jesus was encouraging Jairus to have even greater faith—faith that Jesus could do the impossible and make his dead daughter come alive. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

With Jairus close by his side Jesus hurried toward the ruler’s home. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jairus’ house was already filled with the noisy show of sorrow that was the custom when someone in Jesus’ day died. Hired mourners and neighbors wept and wailed loudly to show their sympathy. ^[(Have children pretend to be “mourners” and wail and cry loudly.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

In a loud voice Jesus said, “Why make this commotion and weep? The child is not dead, but sleeping” (Mark 5:39).

Jesus’ words made no sense at all to the crowd of mourners. After all, they had seen that her heart had stopped beating. Jesus’ words sounded silly to them, so they laughed and made fun of Him (Mark 5:40).

But Jesus wasn’t put off by their laughter. He knew that death is only like a sleep. However, He asked the noisy crowd to leave the house. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus quietly called the young girl’s father and mother, and Peter, James, and John. Together they went into the room where the girl’s lifeless body lay. Jesus reached down and took the child’s cold hand into His warm, strong hand. He spoke softly in the language the family used every day.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He say? Read Mark 5:41.** (“Talitha, cumi,” which means, “Little girl, I say to you, arise.”)

Instantly the girl’s heart began to beat in a steady rhythm, and her lips spread into a smile. Her eyes opened wide, and she stood right up and began walking! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus had done the impossible! Her parents were amazed. Their precious girl had died, and now she was alive!

Jesus smiled at the happy family. Leaning over, He asked her parents to give the girl something to eat. Quickly they rushed to make her a feast. Their child was alive!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DO:** Show children how to find their pulse. See if they can count how many times their heart beats in 30 seconds. Invite the students to do 10 jumping jacks and run as fast as they can on the spot, then recheck their pulse. What happens when they feel their pulse now?

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DISCUSS:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What does our pulse tell us?** (That we are alive, our heart is pumping, and how often it is pumping.)
- **What does our heart do?** (It pumps oxygen-rich blood all around our body.)
- **If someone has no pulse, what does that mean?** (Their heart has stopped beating, and they have died.)
- **What is the good news we can remember about death in our story today?** (Death is like a sleep. Just as He raised Jairus’ daughter, when Jesus returns He will raise those who know and love Him. He will make them alive again and take them to live with Him in heaven forever.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**TEACHING POINT:** Jesus wants to encourage us to remember that He can do the impossible and has power over life and death.
