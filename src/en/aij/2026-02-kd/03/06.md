Jacob began his big journey again, looking like a different man! His shoulders no longer sagged, they were straight and tall. He smiled instead of looking scared. His feet seemed to almost bounce with joy as he walked happily along.

Jacob felt new on the inside too. God had cleaned away his mistakes. Jacob had learned that, “If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins” (1 John 1:9).

Uncle Laban’s tents were still a long way away, but Jacob didn’t mind one bit. The long walk would give him time to talk to his friend God. Jacob now felt confident that he wasn’t alone. God had promised him,

“I am with you . . . I will not leave you . . .” (Genesis 28:15, ICB).

God had also promised to give him a big family and bless him. What a loving, kind God! Jacob just wanted to praise and thank Him. What might he have said as he walked along? **Read Psalm 145:1-3.**

It doesn’t matter how big your messy mistakes are, God promises to forgive and bless you too! And that is something worth praising God for.
