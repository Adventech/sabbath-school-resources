The Kind Centurion

The Roman soldier walked up and down the hallway. His eyes looked at the ground and his shoulders bent over with worry.

He had important shiny medals on his chest and an impressive looking helmet. He was a centurion soldier and was in charge of many soldiers. In battle he made big decisions that were obeyed without question. But this Roman centurion was different from most.

How did he treat the Jewish people? **Read Luke 7:5.**

What a kind man. But today, his usual smile was gone. He was full of worry. One of his friends, who was also his servant, had become very, very sick. If something wasn’t done quickly, he would die.

Back and forth, back and forth, the centurion walked—thinking, thinking, thinking. What could he do? He made quick decisions all the time, but he didn’t know how to help his sick friend and time was running out.

In the quiet hall, he began to think about the Jewish God. Even though he had grown up surrounded by ugly Roman idols, he had learned to believe in the one true God of the Jews. He also remembered his Jewish friends talking about Jesus. They told him Jesus’ wise words and talked about His healing miracles. The centurion loved hearing the stories of Jesus. Jesus had even said that He was “the way, the truth, and the life” (John 14:6).

That was it! He would trust Jesus! Jesus could heal his friend and give him new health and life.
