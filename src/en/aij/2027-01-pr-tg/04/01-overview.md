**Lesson Summary**

After nine powerful plagues, Egypt was left in darkness and ruin, but Pharaoh still refused to let the Israelites go. God gave one final warning: the firstborn in every home would die, but those who followed His instructions and marked their doorposts with lamb’s blood would be protected. That night God’s mighty power passed through Egypt. The Israelites, trusting God, were kept safe, and Pharaoh finally let them go. God had delivered His people! God told them to remember His mighty power through the Passover—a sign that He protects and delivers.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is able to protect and save with His mighty power.
- **Feel** grateful for God’s protection and His power to save.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His mighty power to protect and deliver us.
