**Lesson Summary**

God designed the Sabbath to be a special day of worship and communion with Him so we can share His love with others. When Jesus came to earth, the blessing of the Sabbath was lost to many people. Jesus wanted to show people the real meaning and beauty of the Sabbath—that it wasn’t a day of just following rules. Jesus sometimes healed on the Sabbath, showing by His example that we should love and help others on this special day.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** the truth about the Sabbath, that it is a blessed day during which we can be a blessing to others.
- **Feel** thankful for the gift of the Sabbath.
- **Respond** by looking for opportunities to be a blessing to others on the Sabbath and passing on God’s love.
