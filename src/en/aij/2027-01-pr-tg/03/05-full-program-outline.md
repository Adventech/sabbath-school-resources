**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them and ask them to share an exciting moment they had this week.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Show the children your stack of sticky notes and pens. Invite them to **draw simple pictures** (no words) **of the most powerful things they can think of**. Invite them to stick their pictures on one side of the whiteboard or a large poster (leaving the other side free for the Focus Activity), and to do as many as they can before Sabbath School begins.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children to look at the whiteboard or poster with the Post-it note pictures of powerful things. Point out a few, affirm ideas, and talk about them together. Ask for volunteers and give them a marker. Ask the class:

- **What are some powerful things that only God can do?**

For each answer, invite the volunteers to take turns drawing what has been said on the clear part of the whiteboard/poster. (It may be tricky, so let them write words and get help when needed.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})**

- **Why do you think it was harder to draw things that show God’s power?** (These are things we can’t do or measure; it is without limit.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** This reminds us of how God is much more powerful than any human; He can do things that no human being can. In today’s story God displays His power nine times to show the Egyptians and the Israelites that He can do things that Pharaoh and the Egyptian false gods can’t do. He is the only powerful and true God.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God is the one true powerful God.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s Bible story. Then creatively share the Bible story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What happened to Pharaoh’s heart after each plague?** (It became harder and harder each time he wouldn’t acknowledge God as the powerful and true God.)
- **What happened to the Israelites’ hearts after each plague?** (They became more trusting—they softened toward God as their faith grew.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite two children to come to the front and open your two containers and share with the class what they have discovered in each. Both will be examples of the same food (carrot, rice, pasta, or legumes); one hard and one cooked and soft.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ezekiel 36:26 says that God will take our hard hearts (hold up hard food) and give us a heart of flesh, a soft heart, instead (hold up soft food). When we believe God is the only powerful and true God, we will want Him to live in our hearts and soften them with His power instead of becoming hardened, like Pharaoh.

Sing **“Into My Heart”** as a prayer to God.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to form pairs. Have them put their plague pyramid crafts behind their backs as they test each other to say all the plagues. When they have finished, invite them to stand and say their memory verse again. Have the children give each other a clap at the end.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Our powerful God stops each week to be close to us in a special way on Sabbath. How amazing is that? This week’s Mission Challenge is to **share God’s special gift of the Sabbath with someone new. Pray this week about who you could invite to Sabbath School, to a Sabbath walk in nature, or to family worship on the following Sabbath**.

**^[RESPOND:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to stand in a circle. Share that God’s power, presence, knowledge, and love are limitless—like a circle.

- **God showed His power in big ways. How does He show His power in small ways for you and me?** (He gives us peace when we’re scared or worried; He gives us courage to stand up for someone or to do the right thing; we see signs of God’s power in nature.)
- **If someone said, “I don’t think God is real,” what could you say to them based on today’s story?**

Say the **Big Idea** together: **God is the one true powerful God.**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing the closing song, **“God Be With You.”** Quietly ask the children to stay in the circle, bow their heads and pray a **silent prayer** of thanks to God for who He is. To conclude, pray a prayer over the children, asking God to continue to work powerfully in their lives, and soften their hearts with His love.
