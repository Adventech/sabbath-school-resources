Before Eliezer had even finished his prayer, a lovely woman appeared. She had walked from the city to the well and started to fill her water jar. Eliezer saw that she was very beautiful. She was also kind to the other women at the well.

The Bible tells us he ran to her and said, “Please let me drink a little water from your pitcher” (Genesis 24:17). Rebekah gave a big welcoming smile and lowered the jar from her shoulder. “Drink, my lord,” she answered kindly as she poured him some water (Genesis 24:18).

When he had finished drinking, what did Rebekah say and do? **Read Genesis 24:19, 20.**

Camels can be very thirsty animals. And Eliezer had 10 camels! They would have needed enough water to fill two or three large bathtubs. That’s a lot of jars of water! That’s a lot of running back and forth. What kindness Rebekah showed.

Eliezer gave her a big thank you and gifts of gold. He asked her, “Who is your father?” Rebekah said, “My father is Bethuel. He is the son of Milchah and Nahor” (Genesis 24:23, 24, ICB).

Eliezer was amazed. Nahor was Abraham’s brother, which meant that Rebekah was Abraham’s relative!

What did Eliezer do next? **Read Genesis 24:26.**

Rebekah had used her hands and feet to care for a stranger. She had shown God’s kindness in a very helpful way. God had used this kindness to lead Eliezer right to her!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you think Rebekah felt after she had finished giving water to the camels?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you show His lovingkindness, even when it’s hard work.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Give each family member a cup of water—but not to drink. Invite each person to use their cup of water to show kindness. For example: to clean or cook with; as a drink for a person or a plant.
