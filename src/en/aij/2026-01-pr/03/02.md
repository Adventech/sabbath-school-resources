On that very first Sabbath day God did something different and very wonderful—He spent a whole day with Adam and Eve. This was Adam and Eve’s first full day together and with God. What do you think they might have done together on that first Sabbath?

God must have loved to hear Adam laugh at monkeys chattering in the trees. He must have smiled as Eve stopped to smell a perfect rose. Imagine all the questions they had for God. He must have enjoyed explaining the wonders of things such as armadillos and centipedes, why the hippo has a huge mouth, or how He made the tiny fireflies. As they spent time resting and exploring with their Creator God on Sabbath, Adam and Eve couldn’t help loving Him in return. His heart was so big and tender, and they got to know this without a doubt.

This is what Genesis 2:3 means when it says, “God blessed the seventh day.” He blessed this day with His very own special and perfect kind of love. God gave this Sabbath blessing to everyone at the beginning of our world—from Adam right down to you. **Read Mark 2:27.**

Just as He did for Adam and Eve, God invites us to spend every Sabbath resting with Him. We can come together in church to learn about Him, talk to Him in prayer, hear His words in the Bible, and praise Him for His great love. We can learn about Him in nature, share and help each other, and grow closer to Him. The seventh day, Saturday, is the most special day!

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _God wants to build your friendship with Him in a special way every Sabbath. How does that make you feel?_
- _What can you do this Sabbath to make it a special day with God?_
