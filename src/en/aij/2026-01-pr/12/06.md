The king of Salem had wisely not been involved in this war. The name Salem actually means peace. Salem was ruled by a true friend of God, King Melchizedek, the king of peace. The Bible tells us this king was also a priest, and he had a special blessing to give Abram, along with bread and grape juice:

“Blessed be Abram of God Most High, Possessor of heaven and earth; and blessed be God Most High, who has delivered your enemies into your hand” (Genesis 14:19, 20).

Abram had been a brave leader and now he was a blessed leader. With a grateful heart he gave the priest Melchizedek one tenth of all the goods he had recovered. The Bible calls this one tenth “tithe.” When Abram gave the tithe to Melchizedek, whom was he really giving it to? **Read Leviticus 27:30, 32.**

In Bible times people gave a tithe of food, animals, or goods. Today we give one tenth of the money we receive. We should always thank God for all He has given to us, but when it comes to tithe, God asks that we return it to Him. Just as Melchizedek blessed Abram, God says to us, “I will . . . open for you the windows of heaven and pour out for you such blessing that there will not be room enough to receive it” (Malachi 3:10).

If you don’t have tithe, ask your parents if you can do some work around the house to earn some money. Then calculate how much one tenth is and give this as tithe next Sabbath.
