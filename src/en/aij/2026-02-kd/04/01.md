There at Last!

Jacob suddenly stopped walking and listened carefully. Yes, he heard it again! Chatter, laughter, and the “baa” of sheep! With a big smile, he followed the sounds. He found a group of shepherds gathered around a big well with their thirsty sheep.

Jacob had been walking for a long, long time. A drink of water was just what he needed! He gave a friendly smile and said hello. Then he asked the men, “My brothers, where are you from?”

“We are from Haran,” the shepherds replied. Haran! That was where Jacob’s uncle lived. He had finally made it!

“Do you know Laban . . . ?” he asked them.

“We know him,” they said. “Look, his daughter Rachel is coming now with his sheep” (Genesis 29:4-6, ICB).

Jacob looked up to see a lovely young woman leading her sheep to the well. The well was covered with a big heavy stone. Quickly Jacob jumped up to help. With a push and a pull, a wriggle and a roll, he moved the stone free. Then he began to scoop water out for Rachel’s thirsty sheep.

After such a long trip, Jacob had found his family. He was so happy he cried happy tears! Then he told Rachel that they were family. She couldn’t believe it! She was so excited she raced home to share the news.

What did Jacob’s Uncle Laban do when he heard about their special visitor? **Read Genesis 29:13.**

What a warm and happy welcome. God was leading and blessing Jacob just as He had promised.
