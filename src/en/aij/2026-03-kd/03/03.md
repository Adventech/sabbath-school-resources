The shepherds trembled at the angel’s words. The promised King had been born!

Then, with a flash, the sky lit up brighter than the sun. Thousands of angels filled the sky with brilliant light! In their loudest and happiest voices, the angels sang, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace . . . toward men (Luke 2:14).

It was the most beautiful song the shepherds had ever heard. When the song finished, the angels disappeared. The light faded away, and the darkness returned.

The shepherds could hardly breathe, they were so excited! Jumping up, they ran to Bethlehem. Finally, puffing and panting, the shepherds stopped outside a humble stable. Shh! There, with Mary and Joseph, lay tiny Baby Jesus, sleeping peacefully in the manger. Bowing down, the shepherds worshipped Jesus.

They had found their King and Savior!

Then off they raced again, down the streets of Bethlehem. Stopping here, and stopping there, they shared their story everywhere. Angels! A stable! A baby King! What an exciting story it was for them to tell others. “Everyone was amazed when they heard what the shepherds said to them” (Luke 2:18, ICB).

Somebody else couldn’t stop thinking about their story. Who was it? **Read Luke 2:19.**

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- If you were in Bethlehem that night, and a shepherd told you their story, what would you have done?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to fill your heart with thankfulness and praise for His great love for sending Jesus to our world as a baby.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Make up a song using the angel’s words in Luke 2:14. Your family may like to also look up a choir singing “Glory to God in the Highest” from Handel’s Messiah and share it with you.

^[Mission Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw a picture of an angel and ask your mom or dad to write the words from Luke 2:14 beside the angel. Give the picture to someone and tell them the story of the angels coming to the shepherds.
