_Mmm, yum!_ The people happily chomped and chewed the miracle food. Their tummies had been empty, but now they were full.

Then Jesus turned to His disciples. “Gather the pieces of fish and bread that were not eaten. Don’t waste anything” (John 6:12, ICB).

Peter, Andrew, John, and the other disciples began moving through the crowds. They collected all the leftover pieces of fish and bread that hadn’t been eaten. How much did they collect? **Read Mark 6:43.**

Wow! Not only had Jesus fed more than 5,000 hungry tummies, but there were lots of leftovers too! The people had miracle food to take home and give to others. Oh, the stories they would tell their friends and families as they too ate Jesus’ miracle food.

God had truly given the people what they needed and so much more! He will do the same for you. You can trust Him every day, and pray, “Give us this day our daily bread” (Matthew 6:11).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think Jesus gave them more food than what they needed?
- What do the extra leftovers teach us about Jesus?
