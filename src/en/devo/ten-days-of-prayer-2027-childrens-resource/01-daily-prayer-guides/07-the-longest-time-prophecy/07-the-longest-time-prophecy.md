### Jesus Is Helping Us in Heaven

> <p></p>
> “He said to me, ‘It will take 2,300 evenings and mornings; then the sanctuary will be reconsecrated.’” Daniel 8:14 (NIV)

Have you ever wondered what Jesus is doing in heaven right now?

Long ago, God gave Daniel a prophecy about 2,300 days. In Bible prophecy, these days represent years. When those years were counted, they reached the year 1844.

Daniel’s prophecy said that the sanctuary would be cleansed. But this was not a temple on earth. The Bible tells us that there is a sanctuary in heaven, and Jesus is there working for us.

Jesus is our heavenly High Priest. That means He stands before God for us. When we do something wrong, we do not need to hide from God. We can tell Jesus we are sorry and ask Him to forgive us.

The Bible promises that when we confess our sins, Jesus forgives us and makes us clean.

The Bible also tells us that there is a judgment in heaven. God knows everything about us, and Jesus is there for us.

Because Jesus loves us, we can trust Him and ask Him to prepare our hearts for His return.

One day, Jesus’ work in heaven will be finished, and He will come again!

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> Is there something you would like to tell Jesus and ask Him to forgive today?

> <p>Remember</p>
> Jesus forgives me and is preparing me for His return!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear Jesus, thank You for being our High Priest in heaven. Please forgive us when we do wrong and make our hearts clean. Help us love You, worship You, and be ready for the day You come again. Amen.
