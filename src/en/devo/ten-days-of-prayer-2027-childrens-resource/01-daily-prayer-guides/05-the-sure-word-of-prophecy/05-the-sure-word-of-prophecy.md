### God’s Word Is a Light for Me

> <p></p>
> “The teaching of the prophets is like a light shining in a dark place. You should pay attention to it until the day begins and the morning star rises in your hearts.” 2 Peter 1:19 (ICB)

Have you ever been in a dark room? When it is dark, it can be hard to see where you are going. But when someone turns on a light, you can see the way clearly.

The Bible is like a light for us. God gave us His Word so we can know Him, trust Him, and follow His ways.

A long time ago, God gave messages to His prophets. These messages told people about God’s plans and His promises. Many years later, people saw that God’s promises came true.

The Bible tells us that Jesus would come, and Jesus came just as God promised. This shows us that God’s Word is true and we can trust Him.

Sometimes we may feel afraid because we do not know what will happen. Sometimes things around us can seem confusing. But God knows the future, and He is always in control.

When we listen to God’s Word, it helps us make good choices and reminds us that God is with us.

Just like a lamp helps us see in the dark, God’s Word helps our hearts know the right way to go.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What is something that helps you when you feel afraid? How does God’s Word help you?

> <p>Remember</p>
> God’s Word guides me like a light!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You for giving us the Bible. Help us trust Your Word and follow Your way. Let Your light shine in our hearts every day. Amen.
