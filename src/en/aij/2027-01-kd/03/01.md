**Big Idea:**\
God’s way is best. He is mighty to save.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“The Lord your God in your midst, the Mighty One, will save”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ (Zephaniah 3:17).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Mighty”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929C"}}})_ - Mighty means having or showing great power. Our God is mighty—He is all-powerful and strong. He made the heavens and the earth. There is nothing our mighty God cannot do!

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Exodus 4:18-Exodus 11; with insights from _Patriarchs and Prophets_, pp. 257-272.

Good News and a Good God

Moses lifted his hand up to his forehead to block the sun from his eyes.

He squinted, trying to see across the sandy desert near Mount Sinai. _Someone was coming! Could it be? Yes, it was!_ His heart pounding, Moses ran toward the man. God had led big brother Aaron to him—right in the middle of the desert!

The two brothers gave each other warm hugs, kisses, and happy hellos. What a miracle to see each other here, after so long!

Moses had many stories to tell Aaron about their mighty God. He told him about the burning bush, the snake stick, the leprosy miracle, and God’s promise to free the Israelite slaves. What exciting news!

Together the brothers traveled back to Egypt and organized a meeting with all the Israelite leaders. What did Aaron and Moses say and do? **Read Exodus 4:30.**

With eyes wide open and ears listening to every word, the Israelite slaves believed what Moses and Aaron shared. Their hearts bubbled over with thankfulness as they realized the “Lord was concerned about them and had seen their troubles” (Exodus 4:31, ICB). Together they knelt down on the ground and worshipped the loving God who is mighty to save.

God wants to save you too! You may not be an Egyptian slave, but you are also stuck in a world of sin. Jesus is with you now. And He has promised to free you and take you to heaven one day soon. “The Lord your God in your midst, the Mighty One, will save” (Zephaniah 3:17).
