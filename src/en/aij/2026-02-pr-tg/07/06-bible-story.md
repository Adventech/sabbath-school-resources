**Big Idea:** I can trust God to be with me.

**TELL THE STORY**

Father Jacob rubbed his hands with worry. His 10 oldest sons had been gone a long time. They had taken the sheep far away to the grassy fields of Shechem. Were they well? Were they safe? Jacob needed to know! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He called for his son Joseph and said, “Please go and see if it is well with your brothers and well with the flocks, and bring back word to me” (Genesis 37:14).

Joseph set off, happy to help his father. After a long search he finally spotted their camp in the distance. Excitedly he hurried to meet his brothers, his brightly colored coat flapping behind him ^[(fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The brothers looked up. _It’s Joseph! No one else has a colorful coat like that! Here comes our father’s favorite son. Here comes the younger brother who dreamed that one day we would bow down to him._

Their jealousy and hatred bubbled up into rage. The brothers said to each other, “Here comes that dreamer. Let’s kill him. . . . Then we will see what will become of his dreams” (Genesis 37:19, 20, ICB).

Such murderous words made big brother Reuben feel uneasy. As the eldest son, he was responsible for Joseph, and he didn’t want him to die. But he didn’t want to go totally against his brothers. So he came up with a halfway kind of plan.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What was his idea? Read Genesis 37:21, 22.** (Not to kill Joseph but to put him into a pit instead, then free him later.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Joseph trotted happily toward his brothers. But his smile quickly faded when he noticed their angry glares. Whatever was happening? These were the brothers he loved and trusted!

They grabbed him roughly, tore his coat, and stripped it from him ^[(tear fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Confused and terrified, Joseph begged them to stop. But it was no use. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Strong arms gripped him and hurled him into the depths of a deep, dark pit.

Thud. Joseph hit the bottom.

While poor Joseph lay in the dark pit, his brothers talked together and sat down to eat. Suddenly they heard a sound in the distance. “When they looked up, they saw a group of Ishmaelites. They were traveling from Gilead to Egypt. Their camels were carrying spices, balm and myrrh” (Genesis 37:25, ICB). ^[(Smell spices.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Brother Judah watched the traveling traders as they came closer. He had an idea!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Judah suggest? Read Genesis 37:26, 27.** (To sell Joseph to the Ishmaelite traders.)

Brother Reuben was far off in the fields, away from the rest. The other brothers agreed to the cruel plan. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They quickly pulled Joseph up from the pit and sold him to the traders for 20 shekels of silver ^[(bag of coins)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Joseph begged his brothers not to sell him as a slave, but they ignored his cries. The deal was done. The traders, their camels packed with spices, and poor Joseph soon disappeared in a cloud of dust.

Brother Reuben returned to the pit, just as he had planned. But there was no Joseph! Fearing that his brothers had killed him, Reuben tore his clothes and cried, “The boy is not there! What will I do?” (Genesis 37:30, ICB).

His brothers quickly confessed to selling Joseph as a slave. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then they took Joseph’s torn coat, dipped it in goat’s blood, and took it to their father ^[(fabric)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They lied, saying they had found it, and let him believe that Joseph had been killed by a wild animal.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Meanwhile, Joseph traveled farther and farther away from home. Tears spilled down his cheeks as he thought of his father, Jacob, and his little brother, Benjamin. He wondered if he would ever see them again. He was scared to think of going to a strange land all alone.

Then right there, on the road to Egypt, Joseph’s heart turned to God. The God who had always loved and cared for him. He prayed that God would be with him in the strange new land he was headed to. He decided to fully trust God. The God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob would be his God too.
