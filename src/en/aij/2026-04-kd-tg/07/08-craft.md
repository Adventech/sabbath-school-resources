**What you need:**

Week 7 craft template on cardstock, clothespin, black marker, small pieces of yarn, tape, two pieces of fabric, scissors, a piece of ribbon.

```=Craft Template

```

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut pieces of thick yarn (roughly 2-1/3 inches/6 centimeters long)
- Cut out pieces of fabric, for the clothing and blanket.
- Cut pieces of ribbon to tie around the dolly peg.

**Create it Together:**

1\. Use a black marker to draw a sleeping face on one side of the dolly peg.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/07/7b.png)

2\. Turn over the peg and draw a happy face on the other side.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/07/7c.png)

3\. Tape some pieces of yarn on top of the peg for hair.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/07/7d.png)

4\. Wrap a piece of fabric around the peg and tie with the ribbon.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/07/7e.png)

5\. Cut out the bed template and fold along the dotted lines.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/07/7f.png)

6\. Re-enact the story using the props.
