Everyone in Bethlehem was excited! They whispered to each other by the well and in the busy marketplace. What surprising news! Two women had just walked into town on their own. They had come all the way from Moab! The women of the town all rushed to welcome them.

“Is this Naomi?” the women asked each other (Ruth 1:19, ICB).

Could it really be their old friend? They hadn’t seen her since she had left for Moab with her husband and two sons. They looked a little closer. It was Naomi! She had been away so long, but finally Naomi was home.

The women gathered closely around Naomi and Ruth. With tears in her eyes, Naomi told them how her family had died. What sad, sad news. Then Naomi introduced them to kind and caring Ruth. She told them how Ruth had chosen to leave her home to follow Naomi and to follow God.

Would the people of Bethlehem be kind and welcoming to this woman from a different land? Yes, they would!

In God’s family it doesn’t matter where you’re from or what you look like. The Bible says, “Christ is all that is important. . . . He loves you. So always do these things: show mercy to others; be kind, humble, gentle and patient” (Colossians 3:11, 12, ICB).

That would make anyone feel welcome! And what should you especially remember to do? **Read Colossians 3:14.**
