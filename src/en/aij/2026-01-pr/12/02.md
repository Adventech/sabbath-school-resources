Trumpets of war blasted their message through the streets of Sodom and out across the plains. “Prepare for war! Prepare for war!”

For 12 long years five kings down on the plains had served and paid lots of money in taxes to a powerful, greedy king in the east. Now they were tired of it and rebelled. This may have been an unwise choice, for it made the king of the east extra mad.

After a while he and three of his king friends prepared for battle and marched with their armies onto the plains. The kings who lived there banded together to fight right back.

The battle raged, and the five kings on the plains were losing badly. They dropped their weapons, quit fighting, and ran for their lives to hide in pits, or up to safety in the mountains. The enemy rushed into Sodom and greedily took everything the people of Sodom and Gomorrah owned. They also took all their food (Genesis 14:11). What else did they take? **Read Genesis 14:12.**

Weighed down with their stolen goods, the proud invaders headed toward home, taking with them many people they had captured in the cities. Tied together and marching along with all the other captives, Lot and his family must have felt very frightened. And Lot had plenty of time to think. Maybe he thought, If only we hadn’t moved into Sodom. If only I had asked God to help me make the right choice.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Who in the story, besides Lot, made unwise choices?_

God wants to help us make wise choices. He knows that one bad choice can sometimes lead to another, and that trouble might follow, as it did for Lot.
