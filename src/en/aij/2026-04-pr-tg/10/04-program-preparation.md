**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source items for Mystery Bags: **Bags/containers** that children can’t see through. In each bag/container put a **different object** that children can touch. (Use a mix of nature and household items in different shapes, sizes, and textures.) The children will have to guess what they are without looking.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Share the **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **paper** and **pencils** for blind Pictionary.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: a **Bible-times map** showing Jericho; **coins** and a **basket**, optional prop related to **something that excites you**.

Make the **Bartimaeus puzzle**. Write out the following statements in one color (or in bold if printing), and the application on a separate piece of paper in another color (or not in bold).

**Don’t be afraid to talk to Jesus. Bartimaeus wasn’t shy, and he asked Jesus for help.**

- We can pray and talk to Jesus anywhere and anytime. He’ll always hear us.

**Keep going, even when something feels hard. People told Bartimaeus to be quiet, but he didn’t listen. He kept calling out to Jesus.**

- Sometimes people won’t understand your faith, but keep trusting Jesus!

**Jesus cares about everyone—whether we’re poor, blind, big, or small.**

- You’re never too young, too small, or too different for Jesus to care about you. While Jesus was surrounded by many, he heard the voice of one person, i.e., Bartimaeus.

**Our faith in Jesus can change everything! Bartimaeus believed, and Jesus helped him.**

- Believing in Jesus is powerful. Jesus will help you with anything you’re facing, even though it might not be exactly as you want it to be. Jesus knows best.

**We can follow Jesus. Bartimaeus didn’t keep sitting there begging. He got up and followed.**

- When Jesus helps us, we can be excited and thank Him and live following Him.
