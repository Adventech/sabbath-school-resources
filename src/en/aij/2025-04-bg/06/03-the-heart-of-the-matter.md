What is the secret to a happy family? Society loves to tell us it’s all about big houses, material wealth, and lots of possessions. The latest toys and technology are sure to keep parents and children happy! And yet never has our world been filled with so much anxiety, selfishness, depression, separation, and hurt.

God has good news for us, though. He has given us the key to a happy family. He says in His Word, “Through love serve one another” (Galatians 5:13). When we invite Jesus into our hearts, we are filled with His love for others. We do not have to rely on our own strength to help our families. We can serve our families with God’s love.

As human beings, we are inherently selfish. We want the best for ourselves—a prosperous, _easy_ life. Even our children can develop entitled characteristics very early on. But this is not God’s desire for us. When we serve our families with humble hearts, He turns selfishness into selflessness. He desires our families to be happy and He knows that when we serve each other, this will bring great joy and security to the family unit. “Our work for Christ is to begin with the family, in the home. . . . There is no missionary field more important than this” (Ellen G. White, _The Adventist Home_, p. 35).

_Lord, I want our home to be filled with laughter and joy. I want to raise my children to be happy helpers—to always look outside themselves and to have hearts that overflow with love and kindness. Come into our home this week. Amen._
