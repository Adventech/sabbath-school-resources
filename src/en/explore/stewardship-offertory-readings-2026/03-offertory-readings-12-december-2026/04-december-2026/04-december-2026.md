Systematic Benevolence is a method of returning tithe and offerings that focuses on intentionally and systematically dedicating a percentage of our income to God’s essential mission work at home and around the world.

As we look to the new year ahead and consider all that God has already done for us through Jesus and all the blessings He gave us in the year now ending, let’s each prayerfully reconsider our giving.

Has God prospered you in the year that is ending now? Would you like to learn how to trust Him more and depend more on Him? After faithfully returning the 10% tithe to God, you are invited to increase the percentage of your income that you regularly give as Promise offerings. What about increasing it by one, two, five percent, or more?

### Appeal

What percentage of your income is the Lord inviting you to commit as your Promise offerings, besides tithe, during the next year? As we make that commitment in His presence, let us worship Him with tithe and our Promise offerings on this last Sabbath of the year. Let us put ourselves last and God first!
