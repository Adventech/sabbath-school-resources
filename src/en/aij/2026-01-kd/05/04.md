_What was that noise?_ Adam and Eve froze and listened. It was God. He was coming in the cool of the day, as He always did! Normally Adam and Eve were excited to see God, but not this time. Their hearts pounded hard, and they ran away fast.

Hiding in the dark shadows, they heard God’s loving voice calling out, “Where are you?” (Genesis 3:9). Of course, God knew exactly where they were, but He wanted them to choose to come to Him to talk about their big problem and fears.

Scared and embarrassed, Adam and Eve finally came out of their hiding place with their heads bent low.

Standing in front of God, Adam spoke with a quiet voice, “I heard You walking in the garden. I was afraid because I was naked. So I hid” (Genesis 3:10, ICB).

God looked at both of them with tender care. He said, “Did you eat fruit from that tree? I commanded you not to eat from that tree” (Genesis 3:11, ICB).

What did they say to God? **Read Genesis 3:12, 13.**

Were they saying they were sorry? No! They were too busy pointing the finger and blaming others for their bad choices.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you feel when you do the wrong thing? Do you hide, or tell the person caring for you right away?
- It can be hard to say you’re sorry instead of blaming someone else. Who can help you?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that you can always tell Him about your problems and fears.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Play a game of hide-and-seek with a friend or family member. Think about how our loving God went looking for Adam and Eve to help them.
