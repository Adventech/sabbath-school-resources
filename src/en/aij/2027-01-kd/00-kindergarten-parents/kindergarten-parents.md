The amazing stories of the Exodus convey many messages for us as we lead our children to know God. Some of the lessons we learn through these stories include:

- God is loving and always wants the best for us and our families.
- God is powerful and can free us from the things that bind us when we allow Him to do so.
- God wants to provide for all our needs, even when we complain and doubt His goodness.
- God’s perfect law is unchanging. He wants to protect us and our relationships with Him and others, because relationships are everything.
- God wants to dwell among us and be part of our everyday lives. He wants to abide in your home and waits for your invitation.
- God’s way truly is best.

In the same way He led His people through the desert to a new home, God also wants to abide with you now, until He comes to take you to your heavenly home.

May God be with your family until that day,

^[Nina]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#7994CC", "typeface": "Angello", "size": "xl"}}}) and your General Conference Sabbath School Team
