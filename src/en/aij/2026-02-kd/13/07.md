Christiane loved being in Pathfinders! She always told her little brother, Eduardo, about all the great things they did, like camping, learning about nature, crafts, and other fun activities.

Finally Eduardo was old enough to join Pathfinders too. Even though their family didn’t go to the Adventist church, both Eduardo and Christiane were welcomed and loved by their local Pathfinder club. Eduardo had so much fun, just like his sister!

Sometimes the Pathfinders were asked to lead the main service at the Adventist church. Eduardo loved being a part of the church service. He joined in with the singing and praying and collecting the offering.

When Eduardo’s sister finished Pathfinders, she kept going to the Adventist church, because everyone at the church made them feel so welcomed and cared for. One Sabbath morning she asked her brother, “Do you want to come too?” Eduardo nodded.

The more time that Eduardo spent at church, the more he learned about God. Eventually he gave his heart to Jesus and was baptized. He invited his mother and father to join him at church, and they began to come along too. Before long Eduardo’s mother and father gave their hearts to Jesus and were baptized as well!

Someone from church welcomed Christiane to Pathfinders and church. Christiane invited her little brother, Eduardo, and Eduardo welcomed his parents to church! Their whole family now knew Jesus because someone had welcomed them.

Eduardo’s mother said, “Because my children kept inviting us to worship with them, we are all in God’s family today.”
