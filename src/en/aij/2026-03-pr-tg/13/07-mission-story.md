**Focus:** When we love God, we will want to be a blessing to those around us.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Egypt** on your **globe** or **world map**.

In Cairo, Egypt, there are not many places to put trash. Throughout the city, piles of trash can be seen burning along the side of the road. There is simply too much trash and nowhere to put it.

Day after day the wall surrounding Nile Union Academy, an Adventist high school, became one such rubbish dump for the community. People felt there were no other options to get rid of their rubbish.

But God has special workers around the world, and He uses them in unique ways to His glory. Rony owned a successful company that recycled electronic equipment in Brazil, but he felt that God was calling him to do something bigger.

“I have a passion for helping people, and I’m thankful that God gave me a wife who shares my desire,” says Rony. “Three years ago we dedicated our lives to serving others and asked God to send us to a place where we could be a blessing.”

God led Rony and his wife to Nile Union Academy and gave them a passion for cleanup! Rony noticed that the campus wall had become the community dump, and he wanted to change that. Since the city workers cleared the area only once every two months, he started his project by going out and cleaning around the wall every day.

Garbage continued to pile up, and people told him that he would never last and should give up this impossible dream. Nonetheless, Rony pressed on. He began working closely with the academy students to educate and employ them to be stewards of the earth. No one wanted to work with the garbage before Rony started his program. But now students are excited to help out!

He has created a special system to teach the students the proper way to recycle or dispose of the trash left along the walls of their school.

Since the beginning of the project, Rony and the student workers have already moved more than 250 tons of garbage! The program has earned the respect of city leaders, who recognized Rony’s hard work. Now they send trash pickup three times a week! Rony doesn’t want to stop there. His next dream is to build a beautiful community park outside the academy walls. He wants to be a blessing to the community and to bring others to Jesus.

- **How has Rony been a blessing in his community?**
- **How does Rony remind you of our story today?**
- **What does Rony’s story teach you about what it takes to bless others? (It takes time and hard work)**
