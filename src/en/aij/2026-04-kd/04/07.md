A goat stood at the top of the tree . . . No, this isn’t the beginning of a joke—it really happens! God has created some goats to be just like monkeys, and they actually climb trees.

The Argan goats in western Morocco live above the crowd. These clever climbers happily perch themselves high up in the tree branches to munch fruit. It’s almost too funny to believe. Imagine looking up from under a tree to see a cute little goat peering down at you between the leaves!

This goat isn’t the only clever climbing creature. Mountain goats take it a step further, in fact hundreds of steps further. These amazing animals are able to climb rocky cliffs that go straight up, with barely anything to grip on to. They definitely aren’t scared of heights or falling. They are super brave!

God asks you to be brave too—no matter where you are. Maybe you have just moved to a new town or have noticed a lonely child on the playground who needs a friend. Maybe you have been asked to speak in church. Or perhaps you need to do the right thing when others are doing the wrong thing. Whatever the scary situation, you can know that God is there to help and guide you. Just as He was with the disciples, He is with you too. He says, “Be of good cheer! It is I; do not be afraid” (Matthew 14:27).
