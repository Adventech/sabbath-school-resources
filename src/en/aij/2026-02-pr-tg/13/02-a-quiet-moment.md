**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Isn’t it wonderful that God notices every detail of our lives? The things that go unnoticed by others don’t miss the eye of our heavenly Father.

When we’re having a difficult day and when nobody else notices, God does. And He helps us through it. He might encourage us through His Word, a song, a conversation, thought, or memory. Or He may direct our attention to the signs that He is in control—the rising of the sun, the falling of rain, or the giggle from a happy child. His encouragement may seem insignificant to others, but to us His every gift is a reminder that we are loved and cared for.

“Our heavenly Father has a thousand ways to provide for us of which we know nothing” (Ellen G. White, _The Ministry of Healing_, p. 481). What’s more, He knows our needs even before we ask! (Matthew 6:8.)

This week’s story of Ruth demonstrates God’s great love for His children and how He notices our needs. As a widow from a foreign land, Ruth had many needs. But God didn’t leave her. He was always with her, and He provided for her.

God notices what you need too. He notices what you need both now and in the future. Matthew 10:29 tells us that not a single sparrow can fall to the ground without our heavenly Father knowing it. If He notices a small sparrow when it falls to the ground, He definitely notices, and cares about, what happens to you.

As you and the children explore the story of Ruth together, may you feel so loved knowing that our God is a God who notices each and every detail of our lives. He will provide and care for you.
