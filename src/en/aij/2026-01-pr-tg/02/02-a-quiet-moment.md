**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

“Am I important?” It’s a question we have all asked ourselves from time to time. It’s easy to believe we are anything but important when relationships unravel, when we feel tired, or when goals are missed, or projects fail. Important? I don’t think so!

God understands. He answered this question right at the beginning of time, before it took shape in our hearts. He spoke words with the power to dispel our self-doubt: “Let Us make man in Our image, according to Our likeness” (Genesis 1:26). God didn’t say, “Let Us make trees in Our image, or goats in Our likeness.” No, He voiced these beautiful words, “Let Us make man in Our image.” Men and women, boys and girls, would be created to reflect the Creator Himself.

We take after God. He shaped us, breathed life into us, loved us, made us like Himself. Sure, sin has disfigured the image, but not destroyed it. God continues to shape us, gently making us more and more like Himself when we allow Him to. We are of infinite worth to God, for He made us in His image.

As you explore this truth with the children, may you know without a doubt that you are God’s idea, God’s creation, God’s child. You are created in His image.
