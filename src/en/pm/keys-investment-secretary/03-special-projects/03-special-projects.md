---
title: Special Projects
---

As a means of encouraging increased participation in Sabbath School Investment, the General Conference Annual Council in 1976 voted to set aside each year 25 percent of all Investment funds for special projects as selected by each division committee. This 25 percent is divided as fol lows: 10 percent is allocated to projects in North America; 90 percent is divided among the other divisions, prorated according to their needs.

The divisions are to use these special funds for projects that open up new work (frontier evangelism) or for large city evangelism. The 75 percent of Investment not used for special projects is added to the World Mission Fund, which is divided among all the divisions to be used at the direction of the division committees.

Because of its unique nature and special opportunities, Investment has not been designated as part of the Personal Giving Plan operating in some churches. Rather, it provides additional funds to supplement those already flowing into the World Mission Fund through regular channels.
