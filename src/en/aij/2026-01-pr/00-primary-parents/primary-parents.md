What a special responsibility you have—to teach your child to know, love, and serve Jesus. It’s in your daily routine that you’ll foster an environment that nurtures this most special connection: the daily acts of praying, reading, sharing God’s living Word with your child, and inviting a response from them.

As this quarter’s lessons reach your child’s hands, we pray that they will not only impact your child’s walk with Jesus, but also yours, as you bring the messages on these pages to life.

Although your child may be a confident reader, these lessons were written to facilitate parent-child spiritual learning and engagement, and for this reason we encourage you to read these lessons to your child every day. Research reveals that there are many benefits of reading aloud to children:

- Builds a bond with your child as they sit close to you
- Activates imagination
- Encourages deep thinking and grappling of ideas, particularly when followed up with rich conversations and discussion
- Facilitates important character and faith-building conversations
- Teaches that reading is fun and can reduce screen time
- Improves listening, fluency, and general communication skills

May God bless you and your child. You’re in our prayers, wherever you are in the world,
