John Loughborough turned the pages of his Bible quickly. The Sundaykeeping preacher frowned as he searched for Bible texts to “prove” the Sabbath was wrong. He wrote down what he could, then went at once to listen to a new preacher, John Andrews, who was teaching people about the Sabbath. He fully expected to prove that the seventh-day Sabbath was the wrong day to worship God. But the exact opposite happened! He quickly became convinced that the Sabbath was the right day to worship. After listening to that sermon, John Loughborough began to keep the Sabbath.

Soon after, John had an opportunity to meet Ellen White. She told him that the Lord wanted him to spend his time preaching the Sabbath message. This was a BIG request for John. It would mean giving up his work. When he got home, he went alone to pray in his room. He asked God to open the way and show him if that was what God really wanted him to do.

For the next little while John’s work didn’t go well, since nobody was buying the door locks he sold. His wife burst into tears when she discovered they were down to their last three cents. She worried about how they would be able to support themselves if John went preaching. But while she was out buying matches and thread with the last of their money, a man ordered lots of door locks and paid for them all in cash! Now they had enough money for firewood, food, and their other needs.

John knew without a doubt that God had truly called him to preach about the Sabbath. He gladly followed God. For many years John Loughborough preached about the Sabbath, and many people followed God’s call and became Sabbathkeepers too.
