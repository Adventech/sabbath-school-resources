**What you need:**

Paper people cutouts, craft glue, craft sticks, fabric/colored paper, tape, scissors, and pictures of children from different countries for inspiration.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/09/9a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out people shapes in various skin tones.
- Print pictures of children from different countries or have a picture book to show.
- Cut up small pieces of fabric ready to use.

**Create it together:**

- Stick on fabrics and/or colored paper to “dress” the paper people.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/09/9b.png)

- Attach a craft stick to the back of the people with tape.
