Elimelech, Naomi, and their sons had God’s love deep in their hearts. It was like a bright light that they carried all the way to Moab. Here they decided to let it shine brightly to everyone.

Each morning they bowed down and prayed together as a family. They may have sung songs too. Then, cheerfully, they began their daily jobs. They worked hard and helped their neighbors. Their loving words and kind actions shone God’s goodness.

But one day something happened, what was it? **Read Ruth 1:3.**

Oh, no! How sad! What would they do? Ruth, Mahlon, and Chilion cried and hugged each other tightly. They were thankful they still had each other. They were really, really thankful God was with them too. He had promised, “I am with you . . . I will not leave you” (Genesis 28:15).

God is with you whenever you’re sad too. His love is like a warm shining light that can help you feel stronger and better. God says in the Bible, “I am the one who comforts you” (Isaiah 51:12, ICB). Thank You, God!
