!v[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/pm/studying-together-01-bible-studies-you-can-give/assets/05Salvation.mp4]

_1 Jn. 4:8,9_ — God is love. Love prompted Him to redeem us.

_Gen. 1:27-31_ — God created human beings in His image.

_Gen. 3:8_ — Sin destroys our fellowship with God so we run from His presence (Isa. 59:1,2).

_Rom. 6:23_ — Separated from God we deserve to die eternally.

_Rom. 3:23_ — All have sinned and are under the penalty of death.

_Rom. 5:18,19_ — Adam brought death, Jesus brings life.

_Gal. 3:13_ — Jesus bore our curse.

_Heb. 2:8,9_ — Jesus experienced death for us.

_2 Cor. 5:21_ — Jesus became sin (took its penalty for us).

_Acts 3:19_ — As we repent, He forgives.

_1 Jn. 1:9_ — As we confess, He pardons.

_Rom. 8:1_ — Receiving Jesus, we are no longer condemned.

_Eph. 2:8_ — Through grace by faith we are saved.

_Jn. 1:12_ — As we receive Him by faith we become sons of God.

_1 Jn. 5:11-13_ — Believing in Him, we receive the gift of eternal life.

_Jn. 3:16_ — Salvation is a present experience and gift of God for all who believe.
