The young girl ran into the arms of her loving parents. For the first happy moments, all they must have done was cry joyful tears and wrap their arms around their daughter. Jesus had done the impossible and brought their little girl back to life!

Jesus smiled joyfully at the happy family. Then He might have leaned in close as He asked her parents to give the girl something to eat. Of course! How thoughtful of Jesus! Quickly, they rushed to make her a feast.

Do you think you would have liked that done for you? Jesus always knows what people need, and He encourages us to notice and meet the needs of others too (Philippians 2:4).

What else did Jesus tell Jairus and his wife? **Read Luke 8:56.**

Even though Jesus didn’t want to draw attention to Himself, news of the incredible miracle just couldn’t be stopped. Quick as a flash, it made its way to the mourners outside. There was no more crying, doubt, or teasing. The house of sadness turned into a house of joy. She’s alive! She’s alive! Before long, “the report of this went out into all the land” (Matthew 9:26).

The amazing miracle had given people the opportunity to believe that death really is just like a sleep, and that Jesus has power over life and death.
