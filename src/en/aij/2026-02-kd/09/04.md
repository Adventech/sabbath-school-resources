Pharaoh sat up in bed and rubbed his head. What a strange dream, he thought. He had just dreamt that seven fat cows had walked up out of the Nile River to munch on green grass. They were followed by seven hungry, thin cows. The thin cows ate the fat cows but stayed skinny. How very strange!

With a big sigh, he lay back down. The night was still very dark. He closed his eyes and fell asleep.

Again Pharaoh woke suddenly. His heart beat fast, as he looked around the darkness. He’d just had another strange dream! This time seven hungry, thin stalks of wheat had eaten seven healthy, fat stalks of wheat.

A dream about hungry cows eating cows and now hungry wheat eating wheat. _What could it possibly mean?_

He would find out! With a shout, he called for all the magicians and wise men of Egypt to come to him at once. He told them his dreams—what did they mean?

The palace fell silent. The wise men didn’t know! “. . . no one could explain their meaning to him” (Genesis 41:8, ICB).

Who is the only One amazing enough to explain dreams? **Read Genesis 40:8.**

Suddenly the king’s butler remembered Joseph’s words! He remembered Joseph’s amazing God. And he remembered Joseph was still in prison.

The butler quickly told the king all about Joseph and his dreams. The butler had forgotten to help Joseph, but God had not forgotten Joseph. He never forgets.

- Who might help Pharaoh find out what they mean?
