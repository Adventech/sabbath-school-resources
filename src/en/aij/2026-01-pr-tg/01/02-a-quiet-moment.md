**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

**Let there be . . .** “light”; “a firmament”; “lights in the firmament” (Genesis 1:3, 6, 14). By the power of His word, God made our world come miraculously into existence. He spoke words powerfully from a heart of love. “Let there be . . .”

More than 6,000 years later, despite the ravages of sin, we can still be filled with wonder at the evidence of God’s “Let there be . . .”

“‘God is love’ is written upon every opening bud, upon every spire of springing grass. The lovely birds making the air vocal with their happy songs, the delicately tinted flowers in their perfection perfuming the air, the lofty trees of the forest with their rich foliage of living green—all testify to the tender, fatherly care of our God and to His desire to make His children happy” (Ellen G. White, _Steps to Christ_, p. 2).

Let’s open our eyes and hearts, and slow down for a moment to drink it all in. Let’s pause to enjoy the intricacy of a tiny fern; the delicate perfume of a budding flower; the striking grandeur of towering mountains; the warbling songs of birds at dawn.

Let’s look beyond the beauty of nature to the God of beauty. Fields of lilies and fluttering birds remind us of His provision and faithfulness (Matthew 6:26-30). The sunrise whispers of Christ’s soon return (Psalm 19:4, 5). The heavens speak of His righteousness (Psalm 97:6), and every gust of wind calls us to live our lives in the power of His Spirit (John 3:8).

As you and the children explore the beauty of God and the wonders of His creation together this Sabbath, our prayer is that you will come to deeply know that “God is love” (1 John 4:8).
