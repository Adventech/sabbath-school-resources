Thick dark clouds rolled and rumbled across the sky. Thunder crashed and lightning flashed. After seven days of waiting, the storm had come—just as God had promised.

Very soon there was water everywhere! Where had it come from? **Read Genesis 7:11.**

Rain poured _down_ from the sky like waterfalls from the clouds. Water that was under the ground gushed up through big cracks in the earth.

The floodwater rose higher and higher. “As the water rose, it lifted the boat off the ground” (Genesis 7:18, ICB). The ark started to float.

The ark tossed in the rough water as the strong winds blew. But Noah and his family were safe inside the ark. Even though they were in the biggest, scariest storm the world had ever seen, they knew that God is loving and that He was with them. They believed in the God who promises, “I will never leave you nor forsake you” (Hebrews 13:5b).

God had promised to save them, and He would. He sent strong angels to protect them in the ark while the rains came down and the waters came up. When we trust God He will save and protect us too.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever seen a big flood or been in a big storm? What did it look like?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for sending His angels to protect us in the darkness and in scary storms.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go for a nature walk and find some water. It might be a pond, a creek, a dam, an ocean, or even a large puddle. (If this isn’t possible, use a bathtub or a bucket at home.) Find something in nature to be your boat, such as a stick or a leaf. Place it in the water and see if you can make stormy waves big enough to knock it over or sink it. Talk about how God’s angels protected the ark.
