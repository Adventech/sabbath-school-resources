Jesus is amor, peace, e joy. Pride, selfishness, greed, e gossip começou com Lucifer. Quando nós choose Jesus, isso affects o que nós Faça e como nós treat other people. O que would você Faça em each de these situations?

![1. Imagine that you received two remote-control cars for your birthday. Your brother wants to play with one of the cars, but it is brand-new and you don’t want him to scratch it.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/04/image7.png)

^[What would you say?]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#73C3E2"}}})

- A. _“No, eles are meu cars, e isso’s meu birthdia. Eu want a Brinque com meu cars por myself todos dia.”_
- B. _“Você can Brinque com one de eles para two minutes while Eu watch você.”_
- C. _“Sure, let’s Brinque together, mas Faça você mind se nós just Brinque com eles em the carpet until nós learn como a drive eles properly?”_

Most of the children laugh at her. She is very embarrassed.](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/04/image8.png)

^[What would you do?]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "color": "#73C3E2"}}})
