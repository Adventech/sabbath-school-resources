Joseph wrapped a prisoner’s hurt arm and gave him a smile. It was good to be useful. If a prisoner complained about being hungry, Joseph shared his food. If a prisoner felt lonely or sad, Joseph spoke kind and encouraging words. If the floor was dirty, Joseph asked for a broom and swept it clean.

Joseph had made a choice to live for God, even in prison. The Bible tells us, “And whatever you do, do it heartily, as to the Lord and not to men” (Colossians 3:23).

It wasn’t long before the soldier in charge of the prison noticed Joseph’s honesty and helpfulness. “The Lord caused the prison warden to like Joseph” (Genesis 39:21, ICB). What did the prison warden do? **Read Genesis 39:22.**

The warden could see Joseph’s kindness shining like a light! He could see his kindness was “because the Lord was with him” (Genesis 39:23).

Because of this, the prison warden totally trusted Joseph. He didn’t even check if Joseph’s jobs were done properly, he just knew they would be! “The Lord made Joseph successful in everything he did” (Genesis 39:23, ICB). What a faithful God!
