**Big Idea:** God prepares people to do a big work for Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use your facial expressions and body language dramatically to express the range of emotions experienced by the different characters of Pharaoh, Jochebed, Miriam, the princess, and Moses.
- You may like to use the following props: **woven basket, blue sheet, baby doll, tiara/princess clothes, crown**.
- Ask the children to have their **Bibles open** to the book of Exodus ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A new pharaoh ruled Egypt, and he was cruel—very cruel. He’d noticed that God’s people in Egypt had grown into a huge nation. Pharaoh became worried. He was scared they’d soon be stronger than the Egyptians—maybe even take over Egypt. So he turned them into slaves.

The whip cracked through the air. Frightened, the Israelite slaves hurried to work faster and faster to make Pharaoh’s many bricks.

The wicked king was still worried. He made life harder and harder for the poor slaves.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What happened? Read Exodus 1:12.** (The Israelites grew in number; the Egyptians were afraid.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Why do you think the Israelites kept multiplying?** (God was looking after them. He had a plan. He was in control.)

Pharaoh was furious! He called his soldiers and made a new law. All baby boys born to the Israelites should be killed! They were to be thrown into the Nile River.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In a little Hebrew home a baby was wrapped in a cloth and quietly handed to his mother ^[(baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Shh! He was a secret. No one wanted baby Moses to be thrown into the Nile River! Father Amram, Mother Jochebed, big sister Miriam, and brother Aaron must keep him safe. Carefully they hid him in their home. Shh!

God had big plans for this little baby. One day he would lead the Israelites out of Egypt to freedom!

Three months went by. Moses grew bigger and louder. Mother ran to him as he cried. Shh! It was getting too hard to hide him! God gave her a plan: she would hide him in the river, and pray that God would keep him safe.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jochebed took some tall reeds and wove a little basket boat ^[(woven basket)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then she covered it with pitch to make it waterproof. Baby Moses was placed inside ^[(baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then Mother and Miriam took the basket to the river to rock gently on the water among the reeds ^[(blue sheet)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Sister Miriam hid between the reeds and watched. Unseen angels also watched over Moses to keep him safe.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly Miriam’s eyes grew big. There was Pharaoh’s daughter ^[(tiara/princess clothes)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) walking with her maids along the riverbank!

Spying the little basket boat, the beautiful princess stopped. The basket was brought to her. Carefully she opened the lid. It was a baby ^[(baby doll)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})!

Moses let out a cry, and her heart filled with compassion. She guessed that a Hebrew mother must have placed him there to save him from the soldiers. The princess decided right then and there to adopt him.

This was another important part of God’s plan to prepare Moses for his big work ahead.

Miriam rushed up to the princess and bravely spoke.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did they say to each other? Read Exodus 2:7, 8.** (She would find a nurse for Moses: his own mother.)

Soon it was all arranged. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Baby Moses was safely back with his family in their home. They were so happy!

Mother Jochebed began to prepare Moses for life in the palace.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What do you think she taught Moses and why?** (To love and respect God—the one true God. To worship only Him. To know God as his best friend; a friend who would always hear and help him.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Time went by, and Moses turned 12. The soldiers stood at attention as Moses waited for the huge palace gates to open. The princess had sent for him to go and live in the palace. He would now be a prince of Egypt ^[(crown)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Moses dressed like an Egyptian prince, and learned to talk and act like an Egyptian prince. He was taught how to fight and be the leader of the army, how to rule the people, and how to be the future king of Egypt.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How do you think this might prepare Moses for God’s plans?** (He would be a clever, brave leader. He would understand the Egyptians.)

Moses never forgot what Mother Jochebed had taught him, and he never forgot that God was with him.
