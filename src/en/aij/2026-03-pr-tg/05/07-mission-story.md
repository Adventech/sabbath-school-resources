**Focus:** I can share my talents to help others grow to be like Jesus.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Colombia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Moises was only 8 years old when his family moved from Venezuela to Colombia in South America. He noticed right away that many boys acted rowdy and spoke roughly in his new school.

He thought, _If they knew and loved Jesus, they wouldn’t act and speak like that._ He wondered how he could introduce them to Jesus. Then he had an idea.

At recess he invited some of the children to read the Bible with him on the playground. Three children agreed, and they gathered around him as he opened his Bible.

After two months the Bible group grew so big that they began to meet during lunchtime on the playground. The children ate as they talked about what they read in the Bible.

But after many months Moises noticed that his classmates were as rowdy as before. He wondered if there might be a better way for his classmates to learn from Jesus and grow to be like Him.

He thought and prayed about it. Then he noticed something. All the children seemed to like music. He also liked music. In fact, he could play the keyboard, guitar, violin, flute, and the ukulele. Moises decided to open a music club. He would teach his classmates how to play music that praised God.

Six children eagerly joined the music club. Moises provided musical instruments so children could practice at school. He even allowed his classmates to take his violin and flute to practice at home.

The club grew and grew. They became known for playing wonderful music. Before long the young musicians were playing at worship services and other school events.

Year after year passed. Moises moved to another school for tenth grade, but the music club kept meeting. After Moises left, the school decided to make music an official subject, and it asked 14-year-old Moises to teach the weekly music class after school.

Moises doesn’t receive any money for his work. He teaches music out of the kindness of his heart. He teaches music because he wants children to know Jesus and grow to be like Him. His work is showing results. One boy who used to act rowdy and speak roughly has given his heart to Jesus. He has changed his behavior and has been baptized.

Moises hopes many other children also will get to know and love Jesus.
