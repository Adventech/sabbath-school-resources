In Setúbal, Portugal, plans were underway to build a new school when God provided an even better opportunity.

History, community, and deep-rooted traditions are some of the most significant foundations of Portugal's culture. From its coastal cities to its quiet neighborhoods, families here place a strong value on education, not just for knowledge but also for character.

Just south of Lisbon, in the city of Setúbal, one small school has gained trust in the community.

"Our school has existed in this city for more than forty years. It began inside the church building, in classrooms underneath the church. As the school grew, the space became too small, and we knew we had to look for another solution." —Angela

In 2023, plans were underway to build a new school with support from the world church through the 13th Sabbath Offering. But soon after, another opportunity appeared.

"We had a plan to build a school on a plot of land with 1,500 square meters. But God gave us something far greater, a school with 10,000 square meters." —João

Instead of building from the ground up, a full school complex went up for sale just in time to purchase.

"We went from a very small space to suddenly receiving this gift of 10,000 square meters. It was a blessing." —Angela

Throughout the years, this school has impacted many lives. One former student was so shaped by her experience that she now returns to teach music classes each week.

"I was once a student here, and now I've returned as a teacher. Coming back to my roots means so much to me. Back then it was a very small school, but it felt like a family. That environment shaped me deeply.

The values they taught us about God helped shape who I am today. They helped me become a better person." —Raquel

The extra space now offers children the opportunity to play, explore, and learn.

"Before, the children had very little space. Now they are surrounded by nature and space and for them, it is a joy to come to school. We don't just teach knowledge. Above all, we teach children about the love of God." —Marta

"Only about ten percent of our students are Adventist. That means this is truly a missionary school. This school is an example of what donations from around the world can do. What we do here is mission." —João

Please continue to pray for this school and the students here. And thank you for your generous support of the 13th Sabbath Offering, which has helped this school grow and thrive.

"If we do our part, God will do His, and He always gives us more than we ask for." —Marta
