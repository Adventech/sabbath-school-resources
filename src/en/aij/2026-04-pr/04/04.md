From the shore Jesus saw the angry white-capped waves. He saw the disciples struggle to stay afloat. He heard the cry of their hearts, Jesus, save us! Immediately He set about answering their prayer. What did He do? **Read Matthew 14:25.**

The disciples looked up to see a man walking toward them in the darkness—on top of the wild waves. Terrified, they shrieked, “It is a ghost!” (Matthew 14:26). In a flash Jesus’ comforting voice could be heard above the roaring waves, “Have courage! It is I! Don’t be afraid” (Matthew 14:27, ICB).

Their hearts flipped from fear to relief. Jesus was right there with them! He had heard their cry; He had come. They had found it impossible to save themselves, no matter how hard they focused on staying afloat. But Jesus had easily done the impossible to get to them.

The storm still raged, but the disciples trusted Jesus, for His words are true: “The eyes of the Lord are in every place, keeping watch on the evil and the good” (Proverbs 15:3). They had no doubt that Jesus’ eyes were on them. And now their eyes were focused on Him in return. No looking away, no grumbling, no doubting anymore. Just faith, and focus, and trust.

Jesus says the same to us today: “Have courage! It is I! Don’t be afraid” (Matthew 14:27, ICB). We may lose our focus in a stormy time and forget about Jesus, but His eyes are always keeping watch over us. And He is always there when we cry out for help.
