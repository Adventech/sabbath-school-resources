Baby Benjamin soon grew into big boy Benjamin. Father Jacob loved story time with Benjamin and bigger brother Joseph. The boys would lean in as Father Jacob told them stories about Adam and Eve, Noah’s big floating boat, and great-grandfather Abraham.

As Jacob spoke he noticed Joseph listening extra closely. Joseph’s heart was so pure, gentle, and caring. He really wanted to follow God’s ways.

As Joseph grew bigger he was given a job with his brothers. What was it? **Read the first part of Genesis 37:2.**

Joseph worked with honesty. But he noticed his big brothers often doing and saying bad things. He tried to tell them to do the right thing but they wouldn’t listen.

Father Jacob was thankful Joseph was a kind and honest son he could trust. One day Jacob decided to give Joseph a gift to show his love. Heading out into the field, Jacob brought in some sheep, clipped off their fluffy wool, and began twirling and whirling it into long threads. Then Jacob dipped the threads in little clay pots of colorful dye.

When the wool dried then the fun really began! Over and under, up and down, Jacob wove the wool. Slowly and carefully he wove a bright, beautiful coat for Joseph.

Finished! Proudly Jacob gave Joseph the special gift. Joseph’s mouth opened wide in surprise! It was so beautiful. He couldn’t believe it was his! He gave his father a big thank-you hug.

- What gift has someone given you to show they care for you?
- What gifts does God give you to show how much He loves you?
