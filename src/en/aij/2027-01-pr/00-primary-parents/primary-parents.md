God’s way is best. When the hardships of Egypt and the desert wanderings felt overwhelming, the Israelites came to learn this eternal truth: God knows best. When they longed for meat instead of heaven’s manna, or did things their own way with the golden calf, they were reminded again and again that God is all-knowing, all-seeing, and all-powerful. His way is always best.

This quarter, as you read these powerful stories to your child, our prayer is that you, too, will be reminded that God’s purpose for your life and your family is always best. Rest in Him; rely on Him; follow Him. There’s never a better place to be than beneath His “cloud.”

Look up and you’ll sense His presence today,
