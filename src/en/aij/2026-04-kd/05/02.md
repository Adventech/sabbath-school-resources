The man with leprosy pulled his cloak tightly around his shoulders. Slowly, he walked toward the big crowd by the lake. He tried to hurry along the back roads as fast as he could. He had to be careful not to be seen. The man with leprosy wasn’t allowed to come into town. He wasn’t allowed to be close to people. But he just had to break the rules because Jesus was here!

As he walked, he tried to hide his big smile. He couldn’t wait to see Jesus! But he wondered, _Will Jesus want to see me? Will Jesus want to heal me?_

He moved closer to the crowd, and to Jesus. Then closer . . . then closer, still.

The man with leprosy stopped and listened to the kind words of Jesus. He saw Jesus reach out His hands in love. He watched Jesus heal one sick person, and then another, and another. Finally, the man couldn’t stand back any longer! Believing in Jesus with all of his heart, he moved closer toward Him. Some people, who saw the man with leprosy, told him to go away.

Instead, the man with leprosy bowed down at Jesus’ feet. In a loud voice, he said, “If You are willing, You can make me clean” (Mark 1:40).

What did Jesus say and do? **Read Mark 1:41.**

Jesus wanted to help him! Jesus always draws “near to those who have a broken heart” (Psalm 34:18). With lots of love Jesus touched the man with leprosy. No one had touched him in years!

“At once the disease left the man, and he was healed” (Mark 1:42, ICB).

God’s power ran through his body. Instantly, his skin glowed with health, and his muscles became strong.

He looked up with a sparkling, happy smile into Jesus’ kind, caring eyes. He was all better!
