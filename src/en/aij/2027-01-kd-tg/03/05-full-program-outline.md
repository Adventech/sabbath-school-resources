**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **stacking blocks** (or containers), **two containers**, and **confetti** in an **envelope**. Invite volunteers to do challenges that are very hard in a short time frame, and get progressively harder. Ask a few volunteers to build a tower using all the blocks, but give them only 30 seconds (adjust time, depending on blocks). At the end of the 30 seconds, crash it down. Invite a few other children to come to the front. Give them only 20 seconds to do the same challenge, then crash it down. Then invite a few more children to the front. Pour out the confetti into one container. Give them only 20 seconds to build the tower AND pick up all the confetti in one container and put it in the other container.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children to return to their seats. Ask participants from each group:

- **What was the first challenge like? Why?** (Hard. We needed more time.)
- **How did you feel doing the second challenge?** (Tired.)
- **How did you feel at the end of the third challenge?** (Tired. Like it was unfair.)

In our story today, God’s people were having a hard time as slaves. They were made to work and work. Then Pharaoh demanded they do even more work! It was very unfair and sad. Pharaoh didn’t care about the slaves at all. But Someone mighty did care for them. God! He always cares.

- **What do you think God will do? Why?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}}) Teaching Point:** God cares about us and for us. He knows when things are unfair and hurtful. And He has the power to help and save us. He wants to help us when others hurt us. He also wants to save us from sin, which hurts us.

Let’s find out what God did for His people in Egypt. Our story today will help us learn our **Big Idea**. Say it together: **God’s way is best. He is mighty to save.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal blindfolds and red stickers.

Invite the children to put on their blindfolds and think quietly as you ask the following questions. Give them a moment to think, then have them take off the blindfold as you ask them again.

- **What do you think the Egyptians thought about as they sat in darkness, after nine plagues?**
- **Why did God warn them about the plagues?** (He wanted them to trust and obey Him so they could be saved. He loved them too!)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Sometimes we are given warnings so we can make good choices. God loves us and wants to save us. We get to choose whether we will trust and obey Him.

Invite children to put red stickers all over a helper’s hands/arms, then hold out their own (child’s) spot-free arms.

- **If you were an Israelite slave, what would you have thought to see all the Egyptians with painful boils, and none on any of God’s people?**
- **Would you do what God asked you to do next? Why?**

Explain that God is mighty to save. We can trust and obey Him. He loves us. He wants to give everyone a chance to learn about Him and follow Him.

Invite the children to put their blindfolds back on and say a silent prayer to Jesus. If they’d like to, they can ask Him to help them trust and obey Him.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 55: Thunderstorm** and share the following facts:

- Thunderstorms are powerful storms with thick clouds, heavy rain or hail, strong winds, loud thunder, and flashes of lightning.
- Thunderstorms are packed with lots of electricity. It is made by the warmth and flow of air.
- Lightning is a huge electrical spark that travels very quickly through the air, creating a flash of light.
- Lightning releases a lot of very hot energy. This heats the air, creating the sound of thunder. Thunder comes after a flash of lightning.
- The clouds change shape. They often become like tall towers with a flat top.
- Thunderstorms show God’s mighty power.

Thank You, God, for showing us Your mighty power through nature!

Give each child **Nature Card 55: Thunderstorm** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
