The Israelites slept happily under God’s pillar of fire at night, and they walked under its cool protection during the searing heat of the day. But the cloud had another important function too. What else did the cloud do? **Read Exodus 13:21.** God used the cloud to show the Israelites that He wasn’t just their trustworthy protector—He was also their trustworthy leader.

Early one morning the people set out on their journey. But instead of going straight toward Canaan, the cloud led them south. This must have seemed strange to some of those who knew the way, but they followed the cloud anyway. They were learning to trust God as their leader.

God had a plan. If the people had traveled the quickest way to Canaan, they would have met a people called the Philistines and their strong army of fighters. God’s people knew nothing about war. They were unarmed and would have been terrified of the scary Philistines. They might have even changed their minds and wanted to go back to Egypt!

God loved His people, and He didn’t want them to end up in a frightening war with a powerful enemy, so He led them safely south. If they were troubled about the direction they were traveling, all the Israelites had to do was look up at the beautiful pillar of cloud that moved slowly ahead of them. Then they would be reminded that God is the most trustworthy leader of all.

Instead of being led by a pillar of cloud today, we are led by God through His Bible and the power of the Holy Spirit. The Holy Spirit can be like a still, small voice in our mind that tells us what’s right and wrong. It can help us think through choices well, and remember Bible verses. It can lead us to good grown-ups for advice. Romans 8:14 says, “For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God.”
