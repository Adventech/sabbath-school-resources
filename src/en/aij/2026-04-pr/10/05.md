Jesus looked compassionately at Bartimaeus, and Bartimaeus’ grateful eyes looked right back. He could see! And the best thing of all was that he could see Jesus—his Healer, his Savior, the Son of David. As Bartimaeus looked into the loving face of Jesus, he couldn’t help praising God with excitement (Luke 18:43).

All the people watching were just as excited! They began to praise God. What a wonderful miracle! They had seen that “The Lord opens the eyes of the blind; The Lord raises those who are bowed down” (Psalm 146:8). What else had they seen? **Read Psalm 145:8.**

Jesus must have been so pleased that the people had seen God’s love and His power. The miracle was just as much for the people in the crowd as it was for Bartimaeus. It is also for you, reading about it today!

The people watching the great miracle had seen what faith in God can do. Faith had given Bartimaeus hope and made him excited. In faith he had called out, again and again. In faith he had leaped up to see Jesus, and left his coat behind. His faith in God had given him the courage to tell Jesus what he wanted. And Jesus had given him a miracle.

Jesus hoped that the watching crowd might learn to have faith and get excited about Him, just like Bartimaeus. Jesus wants you to be excited and call out to Him in faith too. He said, “And whatever things you ask in prayer, believing, you will receive” (Matthew 21:22).
