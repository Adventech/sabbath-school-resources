Inside Matthew’s house, everyone was leaned in closely, listening to Jesus’ wise words. But it was now time to go. Jesus stood up and walked outside. Jesus’ followers and the crowd also stood to their feet and followed Him.

As they started walking down the street, a man came rushing toward Jesus. Stepping forward, what did this man, Jairus, do? **Read Luke 8:41.**

The people’s eyes opened wide with surprise. Jairus was a very important leader. Most people would bow to _him_ with respect. But Jairus was bowing low at the feet of Jesus.

Jairus said, “My little daughter lies at the point of death. Come and lay Your hands on her, that she may be healed, and she will live.” (Mark 5:23).

Jairus spoke such believing words! He had so much trust in Jesus.

Lifting his face up to look into Jesus’ eyes, Jairus saw His love and care. He knew Jesus wanted to help him.

Jairus eagerly pointed down the road toward his home. Jesus and the large crowd headed toward the home where the sick girl lay close to death.

Jairus almost smiled as he thought about seeing his daughter healthy again. He couldn’t wait for her to be walking, singing, and smiling!
