**Word of the Week**

**Save:** To save is to keep safe, or to rescue from harm. God loves to keep us safe. Sin has hurt all people and the world that we live in. God sent Jesus to save us from our sins. To do this, Jesus died on the cross.

**Lesson Summary**

In last week’s lesson God had told the king of Egypt to let His people go. When he refused, God showed His power as the one true God through nine plagues. In this week’s lesson God warned Pharaoh—through Moses—about the last plague, when the firstborn sons of Egypt would die. God promised to save His people if they showed their trust in Him. The Israelites, and the mixed multitude, which included Egyptians, obeyed God’s instructions and killed a male lamb and painted its blood on their doorposts, cooked a special meal, and got ready to leave. That night the angel of death passed over the homes with painted doorposts and saved the people inside. Pharaoh’s firstborn son died, and he ordered the Israelites to leave. They were free! God saved them.
