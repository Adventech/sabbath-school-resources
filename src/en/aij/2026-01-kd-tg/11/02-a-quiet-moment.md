**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Abram’s friendship with God affected every area of his life. As Abram walked daily with God, he became humble and generous, and had genuine respect for others. This is the fruit of an abiding relationship with God. God’s ways become our ways. Selfishness is laid aside, and we think of others rather than ourselves. We become a different kind of parent, spouse, and friend because we reflect a little of God’s character the closer we come to Him.

Sadly, many in our fallen world today don’t know this kind of friendship. Theirs is a selfish world of “me first.” This was Lot’s experience.

Yet, like Abram, we can rise above the culture around us and live close to God. Then, from the result of our friendship with Him, our selfless witness can reflect God to those around us, as Abram did. Others will come to know of God’s character through the little daily moments that speak of what we stand for and whom we lovingly serve.

As you and the children delve into this week’s lesson about honoring others by putting them first, our prayer is that you will choose God as your personal, best friend and that you will let His loving character saturate every area of your life.

“Be kindly affectionate to one another with brotherly love, in honor giving preference to one another” (Romans 12:10).
