**Globe Skimmer Dragonfly**

**What you need:**

Sheet of paper, narrow leaves you have collected, two craft sticks, markers, craft glue, googly eyes (if available), and pipe cleaner/chenille stem (1).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Collect some narrow leaves from nature that children can use for their dragonfly wings.
- Use scissors to “shape” them if needed.
- Cut the pipe cleaners/chenille stem smaller for antennae.

**Create it together:**

- Wind the pipe cleaner around the top of each craft stick for antennae (2).
- Glue on googly eyes or draw them with markers (2).
- Glue the craft sticks and leaves on the page to create two dragonflies (3).
- Use markers to draw “flight paths” for the dragonflies (3).
- Encourage children to share what they learned about the globe skimmer dragonfly with a friend, pointing to Jesus as the Creator.
