Lucifer knew ele was a very lindo Anjo, e ele thanked Deus para Seu amazing mind. Ele knew ele was creative, e ele praised Deus para making ele strong e swift. Ele knew ele was very smart, e ele thanked Deus para Seu wism. Lucifer knew that Deus loved ele, e ele loved Deus.

One dia, para a reason that no one can imagine, Lucifer looked em himself, e ele didn’t Pense sobre como creative Deus was. Lucifer felt very proud de the strength e swiftness Deus had given ele, mas ele didn’t praise Deus para seu bom looks ou seu muitos skills.

Nós Façan’t know como long Lucifer admired himself ou whether ele looked em the reflection de a still pond ou a bright pillar. Nós Façan’t know como muitos times ele ared seu thick hair ou seu sparkling jewels. Mas the Bible Contes nos o que started a happen em Lucifer’s heart quando ele admired himself. O que was isso? **Leia Ezequiel 28:17** (Primeiro half).

Something was hatching em Lucifer’s heart—something selfish e proud. Something was turning sweet into sour, e happy into sad. Isso was the seed de sin that leads a bitterness.

Deus knew o que Lucifer was thinking, e Ele was então very sad that Seu lindo Anjo was beginning a amor himself more than anyone else—even more than Deus.

pense . . . THEN TALK Sobre Isso

- _What talents can você praise Deus para? Para example, Faça você Cante well, ou run well, ou have a kind heart?_
- _Why Faça você pense being proud e selfish is dangerous?_

Nós are happiest quando nós amor Deus e are grateful para the especial things nós can Faça. Mas se pride creeps into nosso hearts, things começar a change.
