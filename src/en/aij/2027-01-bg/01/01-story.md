In the beginning God made the world wonderful in every way. The trees were strong and tall _(stretch arms wide, then tall)_. Pretty flowers—red, yellow, pink, and purple—filled the forests. Rolling hills were covered in soft green grass. Water bubbled up from the ground, making ponds and streams that were filled with swimming fish _(hand fish motion)_. Happy little ducks made their cozy nests beside the water. Eagles soared _(arms wide)_ from the tall, tall trees, skimming across the lakes and the sea. What a beautiful world God had made!\
The stars that God made to shine twinkled _(flick fingers)_ in the night as the owls called, “Hoo, Hoo!” Each morning the big round sun _(circle with arms)_ rose over the hills to warm the earth. The grass, wet with dew, sparkled as the bright sun filled the clear blue sky. Great big elephants marched _(march with fists)_ through the tall grass. Tiny ants scurried about, making tunnels in the warm brown dirt. Sweet juicy oranges hung low from the tree branches. The gardens grew carrots bigger than you’ve ever seen. What a beautiful world God had made!

God made people too! He made people that were strong _(flex arm muscles)_ and tall, bigger than you or me. They took care of all the wonderful things God had made. They would talk to God each day and say, “Thank You!” for the beautiful world. They would thank Him for the yummy food and the beautiful flowers that grew. They would thank Him for the big strong trees and the graceful giraffes that could reach the leaves. The people sang happy songs to God, thanking Him for the beautiful world He had made.\
Many of the people God made were happy to be His friends. Many of the mommies and daddies, grandmothers and grandfathers, loved God. They told their children about His great big love _(big hug)_. But as time went on, more and more people forgot how much God loved them _(sad face)_. They no longer talked to Him or sang happy songs to Him. They did not remember to say “Thank You, God!” The people forgot that God had made the world as a special gift for them. They forgot to be kind and gentle and made selfish choices instead. They forgot to take care of the beautiful world God had made.

God loved His people. He wanted them to remember _(tap forehead)_ how much He cared for them. He wanted them to love and care for each other. God wanted them to enjoy the beautiful world He had made for them. What was God to do? _(Pause.)_ He would find someone who could help His people remember Him _(tap forehead)_. Who would God ask?\
Noah was a friend of God. Noah and God talked every day. Noah remembered to thank God for the beautiful world He had made.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for the beautiful world You made! Thank You for loving me.
