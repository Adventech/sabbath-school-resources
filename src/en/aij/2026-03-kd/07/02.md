It was time to go. The Holy Spirit spoke to Jesus and told Him to leave the Jordan River and go somewhere else, alone.

Jesus obeyed. Leaving the busy crowds behind, He started walking down a dusty road. It led Him past grassy fields and green rustling trees. On it went, through sandy hills and past rocky mountains.

Finally, after a long journey, Jesus stopped. Where had the Holy Spirit taken Him? **Read Mark 1:12.**

The warm sun shone brightly on His back. It was very hot and very dry here. There were no other travelers or farmers. Just Jesus. The Holy Spirit had led Him to a place that was quiet and peaceful.

Jesus needed to think and pray. He would soon begin a very important and difficult job. He would save all the people in the world from sin and death. As Jesus prayed, He asked God to give Him strength to do everything right.

That’s just what God would do! He had given Jesus the Holy Spirit as a helper. And He’d given Jesus His wise words to guide Him too. When Jesus was a young child like you, He memorized lots of verses from Scripture. He knew such verses as “Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path” (Psalm 119:105). God’s Word would help Him choose to always do the right thing.

It is really important for us to learn and memorize Bible verses too. They help us become strong, just like Jesus.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you say two Bible verses you have memorized?
- Which Bible verse is your favorite?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you learn and remember His words in the Bible.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go somewhere peaceful in nature, if possible. Be really quiet for one minute. Just look and listen. Say a prayer to God and ask Him to give you strength.
