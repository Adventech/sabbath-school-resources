Como could the whole world be covered com water?_ Water never fell de the sky! Noah had heard this para years e years, mas ele still believed cada palavra that Deus had said.

Suddenly the people stopped laughing. Seu eyes opened wide em surprise. O que was happening? **Leia Gênesis 7:2, 3.** Animals plodded e pranced toward the ark! The animals came em pairs; a male e female de cada kind. Some animals (those referred a por Deus as clean animals) came em seven pairs. Eles spilled out de the woods, across the fields, sobre the hills, e went quietly, obediently, into the ark. Birds came too, flapping através the ark’s Façaor. Para seven dias the animals kept coming e coming e coming—led por Anjos that no one could see.

Imagine Noah climbing the ramp leading a the ark’s Façaor, seu voice echoing across the valley one last time, Pergunteing todos a choose Deus e be safe. Ele waited. No one came.

The Bible Digas, “Then the Senhor said a Noah, ‘Come into the ark, você e todos seu household, porque Eu have seen that você are righteous antes Me em this generation’ ” (Gênesis 7:1).

Just eight people obeyed Deus e went into the ark: Noah, seu wife, e seu sons—Shem, Ham, Japheth—e seu wives. Only eight people were faithful e trusted Deus’s words.

Then the boat’s huge Façaor started a close! Strong hands, invisible a everyone, pushed isso shut. Yes, Deus had closed the Façaor, e not even the strongest man could open isso.

Though então sad sobre o que would happen, Deus loved como Noah e seu family had faithfully obeyed Ele.
