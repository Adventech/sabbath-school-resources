Joseph held his breath in surprise. Brother Judah had just offered his whole life as a slave to free Benjamin. He truly had changed. Joseph couldn’t hide any longer. He called for everyone to leave the room except his brothers. Then he burst into tears.

“I am Joseph,” he said (Genesis 45:3).

The brother’s stared in amazement. _Joseph, whom they had sold as a slave, was now the second most powerful man in Egypt? He must be so angry about what they had done to him! How would he punish them?_

Joseph could see that they were scared. “Come close to me . . . Now don’t be worried. Don’t be angry with yourselves because you sold me here. God sent me here ahead of you to save people’s lives. . . . It was to keep you alive in an amazing way. So it was not you who sent me here, but God” (Genesis 45:4, 5, 7, 8, ICB).

God had guided their lives. He had worked everything out for good: for Joseph, for the people of Egypt, and for Joseph’s family. The Bible says, “And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God” (Romans 8:28).

What did Joseph ask his brothers to do next? **Read Genesis 45:9-11.**

They would all be together! “Then Joseph kissed all his brothers. He cried as he hugged them” (Genesis 45:15, ICB). What a happy day!
