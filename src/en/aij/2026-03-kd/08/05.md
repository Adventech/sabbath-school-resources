Jesus watched the water gently lap the stony shore. He sat by the peaceful lake and sighed. Ahh, a quiet moment to rest.

He had spent many days teaching and healing crowds of people. Wherever Jesus went He always seemed to have followers. He was happy that people wanted to listen to His words and follow Him. But He was also thankful for quiet moments to be alone with God.

In nearby towns and villages the news quickly spread. Jesus is down by the lake. Quick! Tell the others!

It wasn’t long before the quiet shore was busy with fathers, mothers, children, and grandparents who wanted to be close to Jesus. Soon the “people were pressing all around him” (Luke 5:1, ICB).

Jesus had an idea. There were two boats close to the shore, so He climbed into one of them. Whom did the boat belong to? **Read the first part of Luke 5:3.**

Peter pushed the boat off from the shore. “Jesus sat down in the boat and continued to teach the people” (Luke 5:3, ICB). He always made time to be with His followers. He’ll always make time for you too.

^[Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Young sunflowers are great followers. Their bright-yellow flower heads move to follow the sun from morning until evening each day. They are a little like the crowds who followed Jesus while He was here on earth.
