**Word of the Week**

**Give:** When we give something, we take something we have and make it someone else’s. Jesus loves to give us the things we need, such as food, family, and a warm, safe place to sleep at night.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Crowds of people followed Jesus, and He spent all day teaching them. As evening approached, the crowds were hungry. Jesus told the disciples to feed them. The disciples found a boy who had five barley loaves and two small fish, and he gave them to Jesus. Jesus blessed them, broke them, and gave them to the disciples to pass out to the people. Jesus multiplied what they had, and everyone had enough to eat, with 12 full baskets left over.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus notices when they have a need.
- **Feel** safe knowing they can trust Jesus to meet their needs.
- **Respond** by helping to meet the needs of others.
