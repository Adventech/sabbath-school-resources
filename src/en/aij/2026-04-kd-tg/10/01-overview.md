**Listen:** To listen is to pay attention to a sound. When we listen, we try our hardest to hear someone or something.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Bartimaeus, a blind man, begged beside the road to Jericho. One day he heard a crowd approaching and found out that Jesus was about to pass by. Although others told him to be quiet, He called out to Jesus to heal him. Jesus stopped and asked him what he wanted. Bartimaeus asked Jesus to heal his eyes. Jesus restored Bartimaeus’ sight, and Bartimaeus followed Jesus.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus will listen attentively whenever they talk to Him.
- **Feel** secure knowing that Jesus cares about their problems.
- **Respond** by sharing their problems with Jesus.
