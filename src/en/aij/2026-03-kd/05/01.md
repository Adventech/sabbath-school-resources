**Big Idea:**\

_^[“Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Luke 2:52).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Grow”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - To grow is to get bigger and stronger. When you are a child, your body grows a little taller each day. People can also grow to be more loving and kind. When Jesus lives in your heart, you can grow to be more like Him.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Luke 2:41-52 and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 68-83.

Home Sweet Home

Little Boy Jesus woke up, stretched, and yawned. He smiled at the sound of rain pitter-pattering outside. It didn’t matter if the sun shone bright or dark storm clouds rumbled. Jesus always had God’s joy deep in His heart.

He began to hum a song of praise as He climbed out of bed. Happily He straightened His blanket. Then kneeling and bowing His head, Jesus talked to God, His heavenly Father and Helper.

Jesus loved being a helper too. He ran to help His mother with breakfast and the chores. Then He happily followed His earthly father, to help him with his work. Jesus had a lot to learn being a _________________ son. **Read Matthew 13:55 to fill in the gap.**

_Bang, bang, bang._ Jesus busily nailed a wooden leg onto a chair. He always measured carefully and tried His very best. When it was time to pack up, Jesus carefully put away His tools.

As Jesus walked down the road outside, perhaps He noticed the little lambs jumping playfully in the field. Perhaps He watched the tiny ants hurrying across the dirt path. Nature taught Him so much about God’s love and power.

At home Mary would draw Jesus close. She told Jesus stories and other beautiful things from the Scriptures. Jesus just loved to learn about God.

Every day in Nazareth, as Jesus worked, ate, and played, He grew. Yes, young Jesus grew taller and taller. But He was also growing in another way. “Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).
