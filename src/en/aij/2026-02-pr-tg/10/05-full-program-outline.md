**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) Object Lesson: God wants all people to have the opportunity to know Him. We are His messengers.**

Follow these steps:

1. Pour milk onto a plate and dot different colors of food coloring around the edge of the plate (in the milk). Explain that many people in the world live without knowing God. It can be a lonely and dark life without God.
2. God helps us to tell others about His love. Explain that the cotton swab/ball represents us, and the dishwashing liquid represents God. Dip the cotton swab/ball in the dishwashing liquid, then use it to touch the dots of food coloring. Watch the small colored dots of food coloring swirl around and touch other colors. One person tells another the good news about God’s love, who then tells another, and so on. The plate becomes a beautiful swirly rainbow of colors! God asks us to tell His love to everyone!

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **God loves everyone, and He wants all people to have the opportunity to know Him.**

Our story today is about God giving the Egyptians the opportunity to know Him. He used Joseph, a follower of God, to interpret Pharaoh’s dream about a famine, a time of no rain and no food. Pharaoh appointed Joseph to gather enough food in the years of plenty to make sure there was enough food in the years of famine.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children to stand up and pair with someone wearing the same color they are. In their pairs they are to pretend to be Egyptians. Give them 60 seconds to say to each other:

- **What do you think of Joseph’s God (and why)?**

Ask a few children to share what they talked about.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** After the children have returned to their seats, **read together Galatians 6:10**.

Just as God used Joseph to show how He was with the people in Egypt, sometimes God calls us to do jobs so that others will learn that God loves them.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}}) Brainstorm** together (on a whiteboard or poster) different ways we can share God’s love with others. (Ideas might include: encourage others; tell others what God has done for you; tell others what you learned in Sabbath School; help others; give what you can to those in need; write out Bible verses to give to others; be a reliable, trustworthy, kind person.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Share that God wanted the whole nation of Egypt to know Him. He helped save them through the famine through His friend Joseph. In the same way, God also wants you and your family to know Him.

**Snowflake Object Lesson:** Show the children a piece of paper. Highlight how perfect it is, with no cuts or folds or creases. Begin to fold the paper, and as you are folding it and cutting it, explain how each fold or cut might seem like a bad thing. When you are finished, unfold the paper to show the children a beautiful snowflake. The folds and cuts created something beautiful.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Together, read **Romans 8:28**.

Discuss how sometimes things happen to us that are disappointing or make us sad. God can help us grow through hard times, even though they make Him sad too.

- **How can you hold on to God in both the good and hard times?** (Trust that God won’t ever leave you; believe that He will help you in His time and way; read His Word; pray to Him.)
- **What bad things happened in our story that would eventually lead to good?** (Bad things happened to Joseph; a famine came to the land. Joseph’s brothers also thought bad things were happening to them. But God brought good out of all these situations.) **What can you remember to do the next time you are going through a tough time?** (Talk to God; ask Him to help you trust Him.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Because our loving God wants everyone to have the opportunity to know Him, He works in ways we sometimes may not understand. We can trust that He has a good reason for everything He does.
