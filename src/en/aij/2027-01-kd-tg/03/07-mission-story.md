**Focus:** God is all-powerful—He saves.

**Location:** Invite a child to help you find **Bangladesh** on a **map** or **globe**.

Mr. Tripura worked at an Adventist church and school in Bangladesh. One Friday he saw that someone had put a fence around the church and taken over the land!

It was a priest from the big temple nearby. They worshipped idols and false gods in this temple—not the one true God. The priest yelled at Mr. Tripura and said terrible things about Jesus. Then he told Mr. Tripura that he was going to hurt him that very night!

Mr. Tripura trusted in God and was not afraid. “My God is a living God,” he said. “He is mighty.”

Many of the villagers watched. They were all afraid of the priest because they believed he had great power. Early the next morning they were so happy to find that Mr. Tripura was still alive!

The next Sabbath the staff and students spent many hours praying. “Lord,” they prayed, “please show Your mighty power so that the people will know that our God is a living God.”

That afternoon they saw a small cloud in the sky. Then they heard a rumble of thunder. Soon a big storm came. Suddenly a bolt of lightning struck the nearby temple, not just once, but twice! The temple caught on fire, and all the idols were destroyed.

The priest was terrified and asked Mr. Tripura to forgive him. When the villagers saw what had happened, they were amazed.

Mr. Tripura told the crowd of people, “I worship the true God, who is alive. He answered my prayer. God is mighty and powerful. There’s nothing that God can’t do for us.”
