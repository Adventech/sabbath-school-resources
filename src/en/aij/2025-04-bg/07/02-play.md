Create

Using play-dough, create some of the animals in this week’s lesson. You could create lines of footprints through the play-dough, and have your child guess which footprint belongs to which animal. Can you think of any other helpful animals? Make them too! Thank God for the animals when you have finished creating.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Reflect

How can we help people we meet every day? We can look in people’s eyes when we talk to them. We can ask them if they need any help. We can share our toys and play nicely with others. We can open doors for people and say, “You go first.” These are all ways we can help others.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Share

Go through your child’s toys together. Ask them to choose some toys they would like to give to someone who does not have as much as you do. Write a little card for the recipient, then donate them to a local charity or to a family in need.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Learn

Borrow some books from your local library or read some nature books from home. See if you can find any of the animals listed in the lesson, or any other animals who help others.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Pray

Ask God to open your eyes to see the people around you who need help. Talk about the Bible verse “Do not forget to do good and to share” (Hebrews 13:16). Today, find people you can help, so your child can see this put into practice in your everyday lives.
