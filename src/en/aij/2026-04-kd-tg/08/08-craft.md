**What you need:**

Colored paper, pencil, scissors, markers, stickers, hole punch, pipe cleaner/chenille stick.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/8a.png)

**Create it Together:**

1\. Use a pencil to help each child trace around their hand.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/8b.png)

2\. Using three different colors of paper, stack the paper behind the hand outline. An adult can cut around the hand outline.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/8c.png)

3\. On each paper hand, children can write or draw things they can do to make the Sabbath a delight for someone else (e.g., visit someone who is lonely, sing at a nursing home, take cookies to someone, give someone a GLOW tract, invite someone on a nature walk). Then decorate with stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/8d.png)

4\. Punch a hole at the bottom of each hand. Thread the hand outlines onto a pipe cleaner and tie it to make a circle (like a keyring).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/08/8e.png)
