In moments when things seem so busy, or when there are too many demands on the finite hours of your day, sometimes you might not take time to pray or remember to thank God.

**_Pause . . . remember . . . pray._** Taking a pause in the midst of all that needs to get done may be the only way it can all get done. You might sigh as you listen to your children squabble or make a mess on the floor you just swept. Pause and remember the happy moment you had while tucking the children into bed the night before, or the sweet hugs you saw them give each other just moments ago. Pray for the peace of God to fill your home.

**_Pause . . . remember . . . pray._** Pause to revel in a precious moment together as you and your child gather the bits of food from off the just-cleaned floor. Remember the smell of a freshly picked flower given to you by your child. Realize that these are the moments that truly matter. Pray and thank God for these messy moments that become opportunities for learning what it means to love.

**_Pause . . . remember . . . pray._** Pause with your child and watch the ants marching into their underground tunnels. Remember with your child that God made the beautiful sunshine that warms the grass between their toes and the raindrops that fill the puddles to splash in. Thank Him in prayer for His loving presence in your life and in your home.

> <callout>Deuteronomy 4:9, ICB</callout>
> “But be careful! Watch out. Don’t forget the things that you have seen. Don’t forget them as long as you live. Teach them to your children and grandchildren.”
