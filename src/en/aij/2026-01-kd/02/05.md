Adam had a wonderful time patting and naming all the animals. But after a while he noticed something. What was it? **Read Genesis 2:20.**

Adam saw a male and female (boy and girl) penguin; a male and female elephant; and a male and female koala—in fact, _every_ animal had a partner, except for him.

Where would he find another person to love—someone he could talk to and laugh with? But God already knew what Adam needed. He knew that creation was not finished without this special someone for Adam. He said, “It is not good for the man to be alone. I will make a helper who is right for him” (Genesis 2:18, ICB). He would give Adam a special partner.

“So the Lord God caused the man to sleep very deeply” (Genesis 2:21, ICB). Then God then did something very unusual. Do you know where your rib bones are? Can you feel them? While Adam slept, God took out one of Adam’s ribs, and used it to create something beautiful. What was it? **Read Genesis 2:22.**

Adam’s eyelids fluttered open. Who was this? His heart filled with joy. _Oh! She’s my match._ And Adam loved her.

Adam called her “woman” because she was like him. Later Adam called her Eve, because “she is the mother of everyone who ever lived” (Genesis 3:20, ICB). God made Eve from Adam’s side, so that she could stand next to him as his helper and friend.
