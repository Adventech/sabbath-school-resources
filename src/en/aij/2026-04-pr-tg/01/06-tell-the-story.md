**Big Idea:** When I am scared, I can trust in Jesus to calm my fears and give me peace.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Invite volunteers to produce some storm sound effects during the story: thunder—stomping feet; lightning—turning the lights on and off; waves—spray water from **spray bottles**; wind—wave a piece of **cardboard** or turn on an **electric fan**; rain—pat thighs or tap fingernails on tabletop.
- You may like to use the following props: **toy figurines** or **peg people** to represent Jesus and the disciples; **a strong fan; water bottle**.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus smiled as He waved goodbye to the crowds who lined the shores of Lake Galilee. It had been another wonderful day, teaching and healing. But now it was time for a rest.

Jesus knew a perfect quiet spot, far from the crowds.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Where did Jesus decide to go? Read Luke 8:22.** (To the other side of the lake.) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus stepped into a boat with His disciples and quickly set off. Others climbed into their own fishing boats and followed Him across the lake. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The tired Teacher sat in the back of the little boat as it rocked gently on the water. The sun had set, and the stars began to twinkle overhead. He rested His head, closed His eyes, and fell fast asleep. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Suddenly a wild wind began to blow. ^[(Use a fan or piece of cardboard.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Rain began to fall. ^[(Snap fingers or tap them on tabletop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The rain got louder. ^[(Pat thighs.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) An angry storm rolled across the sky. Lightning flashed. ^[(Turn lights on and off.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Thunder crashed. ^[(Stomp feet.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Giant waves splashed into the boat and sloshed around the disciples’ feet. Then “the boat began to fill with water, and they were in danger” (Luke 8:23, ICB). ^[(Spray water from spray bottle.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The disciples tried to control the sails, but the wind was too strong. They tried to bail the water out of their boat, but another wave soon filled it up! The disciples looked at the roaring waves, and they felt scared. They looked down at the water lapping at their feet, then up at the lightning in the sky. They were even more frightened!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
PAUSE the story and scrunch up a piece of paper. Then open it flat. **How does this piece of paper remind you of how the disciples felt at this point?** (It’s all “distressed” and creased with worry lines. Everything is all crazy and messy.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What had the disciples forgotten? Read Psalm 50:15.** (To call upon God in times of trouble and He will deliver.)

The other boats were in trouble too. They moved their boats together, trying to stay afloat. What could they do? ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Then they remembered: Jesus was in the boat! Their Best Friend and Savior was with them all this time. He was still sleeping peacefully—right in the middle of the wild storm! Jesus was not worried.

The disciples frantically cried out to Him, “Master! Master! We will drown!” (Luke 8:24, ICB).

Their loud cries woke Jesus, and He opened His eyes. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus stood calmly in the tossing boat while the waves broke and lightning flashed. He lifted His hand and spoke fearlessly to the angry storm: “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39).

Suddenly the wind stopped howling and the waves sank. The clouds rolled away and the stars twinkled. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The boat floated gently on the quiet sea while the disciples and all the people in the other boats stared at Jesus in amazement. Jesus, who created the water and the wind, also knows how to control the things He created.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did they say? Read Matthew 8:27.** (“Who can this be, that even the winds and the sea obey Him?”)

They had truly seen God’s power. He hadn’t been afraid in the storm, not even a tiny bit. He had simply stood up and spoken those three little words, “Peace, be still!” (Mark 4:39).

**Illustrate:** Take your scrunched, creased piece of paper and remind the children of the stress the disciples were feeling. Then place a smooth, blank piece of paper on top to cover the creases. Share how having Jesus as our Savior and Friend gives us peace in our minds, even when the world around us feels crazy. Sometimes He brings peace into our world, and sometimes He gives us peace in our mind and heart, even though things around us keep looking rough and

stormy. Both are miracles He does for us—we can’t do them for ourselves!

**Invite everyone to call out, “Thank You, Jesus!”**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**TEACHING POINT:** Just as the disciples tried to save themselves from the storm, sometimes we try to do things to save ourselves from sin and earn God’s gift of salvation. This leads only to greater fear and stress. This story reminds us that our efforts can’t save us. We’re saved through faith in Jesus, which gives us true peace in our hearts.
