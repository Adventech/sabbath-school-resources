A Happy Home

Abram woke up with a big yawn. The bright sun was up. It was time he was up too!

His home in the hills was a happy one. It was peaceful here. Abram could talk to God under the shady trees. He could listen to God while he looked after the little lambs and the cheeky goats. He could offer sacrifices to God at the altar, right near his tent.

Thanks to God, Abram had lots of animals, workers, and tents. But he knew the only thing he really needed was God.

Over the hills and down on the flat land were Lot’s tents. He looked out on beautiful land, all lush, green, and growing. But just down the road was the busy city of Sodom. Do you remember what the Bible said about the people of Sodom? Read Genesis 13:13.

Lot didn’t seem to care. This land was the best, and he wanted the best. Lot thought that the best would be the most beautiful place. But for Abram, the most important thing was God.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Are you more like Abram or more like Lot? What makes you say that?
- Are there things you would like to get? Would these things draw you closer to or lead you further away from God?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you make good choices this week that will help you grow closer to Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Draw or have someone write a list of things you would buy if you had lots of money. Cross off the things that could distract you from Jesus or make your heart more selfish. What is left?

^[Tell]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
