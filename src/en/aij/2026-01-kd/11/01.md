Blessed by God

Abram looked left, then right. He looked ahead and behind. Everywhere he turned there were wandering sheep, skipping goats, clip-clopping donkeys, grunting camels, and bellowing oxen. He owned so many animals. There were too many to count!

Abram smiled as he remembered God’s promise. God had told him, “I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you (Genesis 12:2, ICB).

There was no doubt God had kept His promise! God had already blessed Abram with so much—and it wasn’t just animals. What else had God given him? **Read Genesis 13:2.**

Wherever Abram went, people saw how God was blessing him. In fact, they honored him like a mighty prince. But Abram never acted more important than others. Instead, he showed God’s humble love and kindness to everyone he met.

Abram had been on a big traveling adventure with God. He had left Canaan in hard times. But now he had returned with more than he’d ever had before. He set up his tent right next to the altar he had built near Bethel. There he offered a sacrifice, saying “Thank You” to God for His many blessings.
