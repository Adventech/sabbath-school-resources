### A Global TMI Companion Resource!

Global TMI combines the power of unity in mission (every church working together) with the power of Christ’s method (every member personal ministry). _My Disciple- Making Journal: A Global TMI Personal Planner_ is a dynamic companion resource to this _Global TMI Church Planner_ that can be used to train and guide your members in the personal work of making disciples.
