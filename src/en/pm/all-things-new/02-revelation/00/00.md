Daniel and Revelation make clear that we are living in the last days of earth’s history. Oppression is indeed about to be ended. Justice is coming, goodness will reign, and love will be the rule and no longer the exception.

Before that, the battle of all the ages will have to play out first—the battle between Christ and Satan, between good and evil. We saw glimpses of this in Daniel 10 and 11 where Christ and heaven’s angels battle evil angels for influence over entire nations. But what you may not have known then and need to know now is that there is a profound difference between how Christ fights this battle and how Satan fights it.

Satan relies on brute force, manipulation, and coercion. Though he may first offer rewards for serving him, Satan eventually shows his true colors and demands allegiance. Christ, however, is love—and love, to be love, must never be forced. It must instead be freely chosen. Consequently, Christ never manipulates, never uses propaganda, never forces anyone to follow Him. He instead _shows us God’s love_, a love shown most clearly on the cross when Christ died for our sins. Moved by this love, we freely choose to give Him our allegiance. Jesus and Satan are completely different.

Sadly, Revelation makes clear that at the very end of time, most people on earth will choose Satan’s force over Jesus’ love. This means the world is going to get much worse, briefly, before it at last and forever gets better under the rulership of Jesus.

But do not be discouraged! A God of love tells His people ahead of time that dangers are coming, so that they can be prepared and not surprised when they arrive. Jesus is the Best Friend, Savior, and Leader a person can have, and He will not let those who choose Him be swept away by the coming disasters. At each turn of Revelation’s pages, keep your eyes focused on Him. You will be amazed at how He has made abundant provision for your care, even in earth’s darkest hour.

### About Revelation

Before we begin reading the book itself, there are four things you should know. First, the book of Revelation was written by (a man named) John around A.D. 95. He wrote it while he was in exile on the island of Patmos in the Aegean Sea off the coast of modern-day Turkey. We do not know the exact charges against John. But the fact that he was a dedicated follower of Jesus would have been enough for the Roman Empire of that day to imprison him. John knew firsthand what it meant to suffer for following Jesus.

Second, the Book of Revelation is written in what scholars call a “chiastic structure.” To learn more about this unique structure, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r00/)**. This structure shows that the great themes of chapters 12, 13, and 14 are the main reason Revelation was written. It also shows that three angels and their messages portrayed in Revelation 14:6-12 represent the most important messages in the entire book. They are the primary reason God inspired John to write the book and represent God’s final messages to the world before Jesus returns a second time. We cannot afford to miss what those three angels have to say!

Third, because of space constraints, there will be many symbols in Revelation that we will not have time to explain. But where possible, we have provided options for further study via QR codes. At the end of this book, we also provide a list of some important symbols and their meaning.

Fourth and last, Jesus is known throughout Scripture by different names, including Jesus, Christ, Jesus Christ, the Son of God, the Son of Man, the Lamb, etc. When you see these names, know that they all refer to the same person, Jesus.

Let’s begin! We trust that Daniel proved to be a worthy adventure for you, and that Revelation will be even better revealing God’s plan and earth’s final events. Justice, goodness, and love will triumph, and through Jesus Christ we can share in that victory.
