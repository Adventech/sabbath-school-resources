_Swish, swish_ went Ruth’s broom as she swept the floor. She sang as she worked and smiled at Mother Naomi. Their new home in Bethlehem was soon clean and tidy. Ruth was such a kind helper! She checked the water jug. It was full of water—_glug, glug, glug._ Then she checked the food basket. Oh, no, it was nearly empty! Naomi checked their money purse. It was empty too! _(Sad face.)_ They patted their hungry tummies _(pat tummy)_. How would they find food to eat?\
Ruth and Naomi knelt down. They folded their hands and prayed _(praying hands)_. God could help them find food! God gave Ruth an idea. There were lots of farms nearby that were full of grain. The grain could be made into soft, yummy bread. “Let me go to the fields,” she said to Naomi. “Maybe someone will be kind and let me gather the grain he leaves in his field” (Ruth 2:2, ICB). Naomi clapped her hands _(clap hands)_. What a great idea! Thank You, God!

Ruth walked slowly through a nearby field. Her back bent over. Her eyes looked at the ground. Ruth looked for grain that had been dropped by the farmers. _Looking, looking, looking . . . There’s some!_ Quickly, she picked it up and put it in her basket. The sun was hot. But Ruth kept _looking, looking, looking._ She found one . . . two . . . three bundles!\
A man named Boaz saw her working. He owned the field and wanted to help her. He loved God and was very kind. Boaz told his workers to do something nice for Ruth. _Whisper, whisper._ Shh! It would be a surprise.\
Ruth walked slowly through the field. She looked carefully for grain. _Looking, looking, looking . . . found some!_ Quickly, she picked it up and put it in her basket. She found one . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five bundles! Boaz had told his farmers to drop extra grain on the ground for Ruth to find. What a kind surprise! Then Boaz gave Ruth a cool drink and food to eat. He told her to come and look for grain in his field again tomorrow. He would take care of her.

Ruth ran home to Naomi. She held her basket tightly. When she saw it, Naomi’s mouth opened wide in surprise! _(Surprised face.)_ Ruth’s basket was full of grain! God had taken care of them. They had a home to live in. Their water jug was full—_glug, glug, glug._ They had food to eat—yum! And they had friends to help them. Kind Boaz married Ruth and looked after Ruth and Naomi.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for taking care of Ruth and Naomi, and thank You for taking care of me too!
