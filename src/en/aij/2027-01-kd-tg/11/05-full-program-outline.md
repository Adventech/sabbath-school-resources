**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then have a child open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **toy dog** (or picture of a dog) and **bag of food treats**. Introduce the dog with a dog name. Share that this friendly dog loves his master very much. His master is a very good master. They have spent lots of special time together.

- **Does anyone have or know a friendly dog? What are they like?**

Say: Let’s find out what happens when the master goes away and asks the dog to wait.

Put a treat in front of your toy dog and have him sniff the food but wait patiently. Reward the dog with more treats.

Share that not all dogs have learned to trust their good masters and obey. If it suits your context and resources, you you might like to find some videos online of dogs waiting patiently for their food, and others not obeying their master.If not, you could act out a scenario with a different toy dog failing to wait.

**Teaching Point:** Sometimes we can be a bit like the dog that didn’t listen or obey. We can choose our own wants over doing what God wants for us. But choosing God and following His ways is always best. He loves us and knows what will keep us safe and happy. He can teach us to choose Him.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In today’s story the Israelites struggled to wait patiently. Sadly, they made a very bad choice and disobeyed one of God’s important laws of love. Let’s find out what they chose, and what God did next. This story will help us understand our **Big Idea**. Say it together: **God’s way is best. I can choose Him.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your bell or sound maker, then have a child open the **Secret Explorer Box** to reveal **two containers** (one containing **good fruit**, the other **rotten fruit**). Share that there were lots of choices in our story today: good choices (invite the children to stand and show a thumbs-up, then sit down) and bad choices (invite them to stand and show thumbs-down, then sit down).

Show the children the bad fruit. **Would you choose to eat this?** (Thumbs-down.) **Why?** (It is bad for me; it will make me sick.)

Show the children the good fruit. **Would you choose to eat this?** (Thumbs-up.) **Why?** (It is good for me; it’s yummy.)

- **In our story Aaron had a big choice to make. What was it?** (If he would choose God or make a calf idol to worship instead.)
- **Aaron chose the idol. Was it a good or bad choice—thumbs-up or thumbs-down?** (Bad; thumbs-down.) **Why?** (Prompt: what could the idol do? What is God like? God loves us, made us, cares for us, saves us; we should worship only Him. An idol can’t do anything. Aaron disobeyed God.)
- **Why did Aaron choose to do it anyway?** (He wanted to be liked and be like everyone else.) Sometimes we can feel like doing our own thing or following friends instead of doing what we know is right.
- **Who can help us to be brave and teach us to choose well?** (God!)

**Teaching Point:** God was very sad to see the people make a bad choice that would hurt them. It was like eating bad fruit (show bad fruit). **Give the children some good fruit to eat**, and share that God’s ways are the best ways. He loved the people so much that He forgave them when they were sorry, and taught them how to make better choices.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 63: Gold**

- Gold is a very special shiny metal that is hard to find. It hides deep underground in rocks, or sometimes it sparkles in riverbeds.
- Gold is very precious. It’s worth lots of money.
- Gold is heavy, and it’s also soft. This means people can squish and shape it into such things as wedding rings, or crowns fit for a king or queen. You can flatten one tiny piece of gold into a super-thin sheet called gold leaf, which is thinner than a hair!
- People use gold in phones and computers and even in spacecraft that zoom through outer space!
- People have been using gold for a long time—since Joseph lived in Egypt.
Gold is special, but it’s not alive. It can’t help you. It can’t save you. It can’t love you. The Israelites chose to worship a golden statue as their god, even though it was just a piece of metal and wasn’t alive at all. We worship our living God, who loves us and helps us.

Give each child **Nature Card 63: Gold** to put in their Nature Collector Bag.
