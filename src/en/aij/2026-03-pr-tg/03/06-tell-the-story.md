**Big Idea:** Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images as you tell the story.
- **Props:** small **toy sheep** for the shepherds, a **flashlight or angel prop** for the angel appearance, a **toy/cardboard manger** with **toy/Lego figures** of Mary, Joseph, and Baby Jesus, a **doll or figure** to represent Simeon, a **figure** to represent Anna, a **bowl of fruit** (berries, bananas, grapes) for the activity, and a **star prop** to guide the Wise Men.
- Ask children to open their Bibles and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask them.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Stars dotted the dark night skies over Bethlehem. As Baby Jesus ^[(show Baby Jesus figure)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) breathed His first breath on earth, the people of Bethlehem slept on.

But on the grassy hills outside the town, a group of shepherds were wide awake. They sat around a fire, talking together as they watched over their sheep ^[(toy sheep)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). These humble shepherds were devoted to God. They often talked about the promised Savior and prayed for Him to come.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly the dark sky lit up. An angel appeared! ^[(Shine a flashlight or use an angel prop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The shepherds were surprised and terrified, but the angel spoke gently: “Don’t be afraid, because I am bringing you some good news. It will be a joy to all the people. Today your Savior was born in David’s town. He is Christ, the Lord” (Luke 2:10, 11, ICB).

The Savior had come! The angel said, “You will find a Babe wrapped in swaddling cloths, lying in a manger” (Luke 2:12).

The sky filled with angels, praising God and singing, “Glory to God in the highest, and on earth peace, goodwill toward men!” (Luke 2:14). ^[(Hold up “angels” prop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The light faded. The angels and their sweet song disappeared.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**The shepherds were amazed! After so much waiting, hoping, and praying, the Savior had come! What did they do? Let’s read Luke 2:15.** (They hurried to Bethlehem to see what the angel had told them.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Gently they pushed the door of the stable open. There, beside Joseph and Mary, was the Baby! He was wrapped in soft cloth and lying in a manger ^[(show Mary and Joseph figures and Baby Jesus in the manger)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It was just as the angel had said!

They tiptoed in, dropped to their knees, and bowed low to worship tiny Jesus. Their eyes sparkled with joy, and tears fell from their eyes, for they knew that Baby Jesus was no ordinary baby—He was the promised Messiah. He was worthy of their worship!

Full of excitement, they told Mary and Joseph all about the angel’s message and the angel choir. This was Christ, the Lord! Mary’s heart filled with joy.

With a last look at Baby Jesus, the shepherds quietly left. As they stepped onto the street, their excitement bubbled over. They couldn’t keep quiet any longer! They shared what they had seen and heard with everyone. With songs of praise they spread the good news!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Little Baby Jesus grew. When He was 40 days old, Mary and Joseph took Him to the temple in Jerusalem to dedicate Him to God. Mary held Baby Jesus close. Joseph smiled at their little Baby as they walked through the temple gates.

Simeon, a man who loved God with all his heart, walked quickly to the temple too ^[(show a figure to represent Simeon)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He had felt the Holy Spirit tell him to get there—now!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What had Simeon been promised by God? Read Luke 2:26.** (That he would not die before he saw the Messiah.)

Arriving at the temple, he scanned the temple court. Then he saw Baby Jesus—and instantly he knew! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Rushing to Mary and Joseph, Simeon took Baby Jesus in his arms and praised God. Here was the Savior! At last—the Messiah had come!

While Simeon praised God, a prophet named Anna entered the temple ^[(bring out a figure to represent Anna)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She was 84 years-old and devoted to God. She served Him in the temple day and night. Seeing the Baby, her face lit up. At once she knew He was the Messiah! With tears in her eyes she gave thanks to God and told everyone the good news. All her hopes and dreams had come true!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Direct children’s attention to the treat that has been sitting before them since the beginning of Sabbath School.

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **How did it feel seeing the treat but having to wait to receive it?**

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Pass it around now. Continue talking as they quietly eat it. Simeon and Anna had to wait for a much longer time to have God’s promise fulfilled. On this day they knew the wait was worth it!

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
Share the **Big Idea** again: (For) **Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship!**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Meanwhile, in a faraway country, a group of men gazed up at the twinkling stars. These wise, educated men loved to study the stars. They were devoted to learning. They even studied the Bible scrolls. As they studied, they learned about the true God. They read the writings of the prophets which said God was sending a new King and Savior.

The men gasped as they gazed up at the sky. There it was! A bright new, shining star! The brilliant star was really a cluster of shining angels. God told the Wise Men in a dream to follow the star—it would lead them to the new King.

The Wise Men packed up and set off on the long journey, following the star ^[(hold up a star prop)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They followed the shining star each night until they arrived in Jerusalem. Here they began to look for the new baby King. They said, “Where is He who has been born King of the Jews? For we have seen His star in the East and have come to worship Him” (Matthew 2:2).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But no one was waiting to worship Jesus in Jerusalem. Not King Herod—he sent them on their way. Not the Jewish leaders. Not even the people.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The star led them on to Bethlehem, directly to the house where Jesus was staying ^[(show Mary, Joseph, Jesus characters, without the manger)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). With hearts filled with wonder, the Wise Men bowed and worshipped Jesus—the new King. ^[(Place toy Wise Men near Mary, Joseph, and Baby Jesus.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What gifts did the Wise Men bring? Read Matthew 2:11.** (Gold, frankincense, and myrrh.)

These gifts had special meanings. Gold showed that Jesus was king. Frankincense, a perfume used in worship, symbolized His role as priest. Myrrh, used in burial, reminded them that Jesus would one day die for His people.
