title: Disciple-Making Strategy
---

There are three preliminary steps every church can take to get its members ready for their disciple-making mission:

### Getting Ready for Mission

- i. Emphasize spiritual revival.
- ii. Compile an interest list and establish an interest review process.
- iii. Provide personal evangelism training.

Because Jesus likened making disciples to the harvest cycle (see Matt. 9:37, 38; Luke 8:11; 10:2), the Global TMI strategy emphasizes five essential phases that align with the agricultural process. After taking the preliminary steps outlined above, the church should establish the following essential ministries in every phase of disciple-making:

These ministries should inspire, equip, and involve members in personal labor in each phase of disciple- making. They should also plan events and programs that minister to members’ friends and interests, as well as to new interests in the community. Each ministry should have a leader, a budget, and annual action plans.
