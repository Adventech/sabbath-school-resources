Trouble was headed for the city of Sodom! Four troublemaking kings from far away were coming to fight Sodom. What a scary surprise! Five kings from around Sodom joined together to fight back. There would be a battle!

Arrows flew and swords crashed. The four kings were stronger, and they won. The frightened kings of Sodom and nearby Gomorrah ran away. The Bible tells us the winning armies “took everything the people of Sodom and Gomorrah owned. They also took all their food” (Genesis 14:11, ICB).

Every little thing was taken! What happened to poor Lot? Read Genesis 14:12.

Oh, no! Lot and his family had become prisoners!

There was one man from Sodom who escaped. Very quietly he hid from the enemy soldiers. When the soldiers had gone, he ran to Abram’s home and told Abram what had happened.

When he heard the news Abram felt sad. Poor Lot! Right away Abram chose to be a loyal friend and help Lot. _But how?_ Abram thought and prayed hard. Then he made a big rescue plan. From his own camp he gathered 318 brave men to help him save Lot.

Even though Lot had made some bad choices and had not been kind enough to honor his uncle, Abram loved Lot. He was loyal to him. He returned selfishness with loving-kindness. He knew that “a friend loves at all times” (Proverbs 17:17). Abram was like Jesus, who came to our world to die for us, even though we’ve made bad choices. Jesus loves us so much!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8ac3a7", "size": "lg"}}})

- How do you think Lot felt when he was marched away with the enemy soldiers?
- Have you ever had to protect your younger brother, sister, or friend? What did you do?
