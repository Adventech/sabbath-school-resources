**Word of the Week**

**Confident:** Being confident means feeling sure that something is true. You can be confident that God loves to forgive you when you make mistakes—it’s certain and true.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After gaining the birthright blessing by deception, Jacob fled from home. He felt alone and guilty. He prayed and asked for God’s forgiveness. God gave Jacob a dream showing a glowing ladder connecting earth with heaven, with God at the top and angels going between. God promised to always be with him. Jacob awoke with a heart full of praise and thankfulness to God for showing His forgiveness and love. Jacob set up a monument to remember what God had done and shown him, and vowed to give God a tenth of all God would give him.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God loves and saves us.
- **Feel** a desire to seek and say they’re sorry to God for their mistakes, especially when they feel alone.
- **Respond** by asking God to be their friend.
