The Best Storyteller

There were so many people in the crowd! Stretching up tall on tiptoes, the children tried their very hardest to see Him. Elbows knocked as bodies squished together. Everyone wanted to be near Jesus.

Jesus looked at the growing crowd by the lake with kind, loving eyes. He was so happy to tell everyone about God’s love. But He could see how hard it was for the little children in the middle of the crowd to see. It was also hard for the grown-ups to hear if they stood all the way at the back of the crowd.

Jesus had a wonderful idea! What did He do? **Read Mark 4:1.**

Now, everyone could see and hear Jesus as He told them stories. He talked about a tiny seed growing to become a big tree. He explained that even a little bit of trust in God’s words would be like a tiny seed that can grow to become big and strong, just like a tree (Mark 4:31, 32). “Jesus used many stories like these to teach them. He taught them all that they could understand” (Mark 4:33, ICB).

Jesus also healed lots of sick people. He loved making people well and happy.

As the sun dropped low toward the mountains, people still huddled close and listened to Jesus. They loved Him so much. He was the best storyteller! They didn’t want to go home—they wanted to hear just one more story . . .

Jesus had been telling wonderful stories and healing people for many days now. He’d hardly stopped to rest or eat even a little flatbread.

Jesus loves teaching us too. We can learn from Him by reading His stories in the Bible.
