Joe was a 10-year-old boy who lived in the Solomon Islands. Joe didn’t know about Jesus, but he had one Seventh-day Adventist friend. One day this friend invited him to Pathfinders and church. Joe went, and he absolutely loved it! He kept going back again and again. Then Joe decided he wanted Jesus to live in His heart forever.

Joe started growing—he started becoming more like Jesus. He began to tell his friends at school about Pathfinders and the Bible. His friends wanted to hear more, so Joe invited them to his home so he could share more Bible stories with them.

Joe’s friends loved the stories so much that they began inviting their friends to come and listen and learn too. Soon 30 to 40 children were coming to Joe’s house every evening. They all wanted to learn more about Jesus.

Little by little Joe’s friends also started coming to church each Sabbath. Then something happened that made Joe really, really happy. His mom, cousin, and three friends all decided to be baptized! Wow!

Time passed, and Joe turned 13. Every Sabbath his living room was packed with about 70 people. With the help of the Holy Spirit, Joe grew his own church for Jesus!

Just as Joe did, you can also grow in God’s wisdom every day. You can share God’s goodness and truth with others so that they can grow too.
