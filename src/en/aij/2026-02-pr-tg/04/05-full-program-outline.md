**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Tell the children you have a challenge for them. Give each child a piece of paper and pen. Write a math challenge on the board while they close their eyes. When you say, “Go,” the children can open their eyes and work out the answer to the math problem on their papers. They’ll have just 60 seconds to find the solution (without talking or working together). For anyone who does find the answer, there will be a special prize (bookmark or healthy treat of your choice). Set your timer, then say, “Go!”

The challenge is: 5+9-15+7-2/3+106+9-15+23-4/5+1.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** The children won’t be able to find the answer in this time. Ask the children if they would like a prize. Yes? Give each child a prize anyway. In a similar way that none of you deserved a prize, when we ask for God’s forgiveness He gives it to us generously, even though we don’t deserve it.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Because God forgives us, we can learn to forgive others.** Our story today is about Jacob being tricked by his dishonest Uncle Laban, just as Jacob was dishonest with Isaac and Esau. God helped him to forgive Laban, just as God forgave him.

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}}) Label some heavy objects** (rocks or large books) with words that relate to unforgiveness, such as: lying, cheating, unfairness, hurt, anger, revenge, bitterness. **Place the objects in a bag**. Ask a child to pick up the bag and carry it around the room. **Set up two challenges** to do while they are carrying the bag: play a simple game with a friend who has no bag (catch a ball); then **help a different friend** to pick up paper that is on the floor. Invite two or three children to carry the bag and share what it was like.

Explain that holding on to hurts and carrying around unforgiveness is like carrying a heavy bag. When we do this, it feels hard to do what we like to do: have good friendships, help others, or be happy in life.

Take the heavy items out of the bag and ask one of the children to carry it now and describe the difference.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God can help us to let go of hurts and forgive others, even when they don’t ask for our forgiveness or say they’re sorry. He knows that we’ll feel better and live better when we forgive others.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children to form pairs. Explain that you are going to **think, pair, share**: think about the question on your own; discuss it with a buddy; then share your buddy’s thoughts with the class.

Read each question aloud, then give15 seconds for the kids to think, another 30 seconds to talk with their buddy, and finally, ask two different children to briefly share their buddy’s thoughts with the class. Follow this quick sequence with each question. (Don’t draw out this activity, as it’s intended to get everyone thinking, discussing, and sharing quickly.)

- **What unfair things happened to Jacob?** (He was tricked into marrying the wrong wife and having two wives, then working without pay, being cheated of pay.)
- **What is something Jacob, because of how he was treated, could have done but didn’t?** (Stayed angry at Laban, hurt Laban, gotten revenge, run away, got angry at God.)
- **What did Jacob do instead and why?** (Worked hard, forgave Laban, stayed calm and kind. God had forgiven him. God’s love changed him.)
- **What can you do to try to be like Jacob this week?** (Ask God for His forgiveness and to help you be forgiving, just as He is.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask children to think of someone they need to forgive. Then give each child a stone to represent the burden unforgiveness has had on them. Invite the children to come forward with their stone and place it in a designated place as a symbol of asking God to help them forgive this person and remove their burden.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God’s love and forgiveness changes us. He can help us to be forgiving. The Bible tells us, “Be kind and loving to each other. Forgive each other just as God forgave you in Christ” (Ephesians 4:32, ICB).
