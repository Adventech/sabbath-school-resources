**What you need:**

Sheet of paper, narrow leaves you have collected, two craft sticks, markers, craft glue, googly eyes (if available), and pipe cleaner/chenille stem.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/10/10a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Collect some narrow leaves from nature that children can use for their dragonfly wings.
- Use scissors to “shape” them if needed.
- Cut the pipe cleaners/chenille stem smaller for antennae.

**Create it together:**

- Wind the pipe cleaner around the top of each craft stick for antennae.
- Glue on googly eyes or draw them with markers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/10/10b.png)

- Glue the craft sticks and leaves on the page to create two dragonflies.
- Use markers to draw “flight paths” for the dragonflies.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/10/10c.png)

- Encourage children to share what they learned about the globe skimmer dragonfly with a friend, pointing to Jesus as the Creator.
