**What you need:**

Week 10 craft template, colored pencils/crayons, scissors, glue, craft knife (adults only), week 9 craft, ribbon (optional).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/10a.jpg)

**[Download Craft Template](assets/2027-01-pr-craft-10.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Color the craft template and cut it out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/10b.jpg)

2\. Cover the back of the page with plenty of glue. Carefully stick it to the back of the week 9 craft, using the lines as a guide.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/10c.jpg)

3\. Use a craft knife (adults only) to make a shallow cut along the solid lines only, on both sides. Children can fold along the dotted lines to make an accordion effect.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/10d.jpg)

4\. Tie the folding commandment plaque with a ribbon (optional).

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/10e.jpg)
