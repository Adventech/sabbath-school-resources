The sick woman walked slowly toward the main road and stopped. She looked up and down the dusty road in surprise. It was full of people! Everyone was excited. Everyone was in a hurry. And everyone was happy to tell her the good news—Jesus had come to town!

Her heart pounded. She had heard about Jesus, the great Miracle Worker. Maybe He could help her.

She felt very tired and weak, but she walked as fast as her legs would carry her. She followed all the people to the lake. By the time she arrived, “a large crowd gathered around him” (Mark 5:21, ICB). They were squished tightly together trying to get as close as possible to Jesus.

From behind the crowd, the woman leaned in and listened. Jesus’ words were like warm sunshine, filling her with hope and love. Then sick people began coming forward to be healed. The woman peered through little gaps. She saw glimpses of Jesus, who was touching people who were blind, deaf, and sick. Then she heard shouts of joy as they were instantly healed. Jesus could do anything!

_Oh, I just have to get to Jesus_, she thought.

The doctors couldn’t help her. But she believed that Jesus could! She had faith.

The Bible tells us, “Faith means being sure of the things we hope for. And faith means knowing that something is real even if we do not see it” (Hebrews 11:1, ICB).

With a new smile on her face, the woman tried to make her way closer to Jesus, the kind Healer.

Do you think Jesus will help her? **Read Psalm 34:17, 18.**
