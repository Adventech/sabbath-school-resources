The little white dove had found a place outside the ark to make a home. Noah wondered . . . If the dove was safe, what did the world look like now?

Noah just had to find out! So he “removed the covering of the ark and looked, and indeed the surface of the ground was dry” (Genesis 8:13).

Imagine Noah’s delight! His eyes feasted on blue sky and mountain peaks. His family crowded around, needing to see the dry land and blue sky too. Excitement rippled through the ark. Soon they would all be free!

But “soon” was not “now,” so they patiently waited. God had shut the ark’s door, and God was the one who would let them out. Their job was to patiently wait. What promise does God give to those who wait for Him? Read or recite Isaiah 40:31.

We can be sure that Noah and his little family were blessed in all these ways because they waited patiently for God to let them out. God was allowing the land to dry out enough so that plants could grow, food could be gathered, and the family could survive outside the ark. By waiting, they were trusting God to protect them.

Then one day it happened. The big, heavy door creaked slowly open. What excitement! Eight happy people leaped to their feet and let the animals free. Running and hopping, flying and crawling, the restless animals surged out the door—all eager to sniff fresh air. The waiting was finally over! They ran out into the clean, new world.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Think of things that you have had to wait for. How good are you at waiting?_

Just like Noah and his family, we can trust God to help us wait patiently for things to happen or for people to help us. He knows the best time for things to happen.
