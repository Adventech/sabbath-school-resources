**Focus:** The caterpillar shows how amazing our Creator God is.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/09/nature-lesson.png)

- **Can you make a little caterpillar with your finger?**
- **Have you had a caterpillar climb over your finger, or perhaps your toes?**

Caterpillars are hungry, wriggly insects. They have long bodies with a head at the front, then 12-13 segments behind.

- **How many legs do you have?** (Two!)

Caterpillars have six legs. But they have another five pairs of look-alike legs that come behind!

- **Where do you breathe in and out from?** (My mouth.)

Caterpillars don’t have just one mouth for breathing, they breathe through18 little openings on their sides.

- **How many eyes do you have?** (Two.)

If you were a caterpillar, you’d have six eyes on each side of your head. That’s 12 eyes!

Most caterpillars are plain green or brown with bare skin—they look a bit like worms. But some have bright colors and are very hairy.

These little creatures may look a little funny, but they go through a big change. God has special plans for the caterpillar. As soon as they hatch from an egg, they begin to eat. They munch and crunch on leaves all day long. Munch, munch, munch. Crunch, crunch, crunch.

When they have grown very big from all their eating, they build a cocoon using sticky goo from inside of them. The goo hardens to become a shell.

Then something truly amazing happens. Inside the cocoon, the caterpillar starts to change. Its long body changes. All their inside organs change. Its mouth and eyes change.

Finally, when it’s time, it breaks out of the cocoon . . . as a new, beautiful butterfly or moth!

With new, wonderful wings, the butterfly lifts up into the sky and flutters away. Isn’t that amazing?

**Teaching Point:** God is an amazing Creator. He can turn the caterpillar into a butterfly! We can be sure that God can do amazing things with us too!

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God can do amazing things!**
