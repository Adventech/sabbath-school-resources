---
title: The End of Ownership
date: 02/05/2026
---

Our human nature naturally wants to own things, and it seems that the longer we live, the more things we want to own. But God looks at things differently.

In Psalm 50:10–12, God says, “Every beast of the forest is Mine, and the cattle on a thousand hills. I know all the birds of the mountains, and the wild beasts of the field are Mine. If I were hungry, I would not tell you; for the world is Mine, and all its fullness” (NKJV).

Then, in Deuteronomy 8:17, 18, He says, “Then you say in your heart, ‘My power and the might of my hand have gained me this wealth.’ And you shall remember the LORD your God, for it is He who gives you power to get wealth, that He may establish His covenant which He swore to your fathers, as it is this day” (NKJV).

God owns everything, and He invites us to be faithful stewards of all that He entrusts to our care. A steward is a person who manages something on behalf of the owner. God owns this world and everything in it, and He asks us to manage it carefully for Him.

When we understand that God owns everything, our ownership ends, and stewardship begins.
