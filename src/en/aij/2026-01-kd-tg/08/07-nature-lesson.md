**Better Together**

There are lots of things in nature that are better together. The emperor shrimp and the nudibranch (pronounced new-di-brank), otherwise known as a sea slug, are great on their own, but even better together.

- **Which one is the emperor shrimp?**
- **Which one is the sea slug?** (Invite the children to point to each in the picture.)
- An emperor shrimp is very small—about the size of a paper clip.
- Emperor shrimp live deep in the ocean and have long front legs called claws.
- The ocean is a dangerous place, especially if you’re less than an inch (two centimeters) tall.
- The emperor shrimp wouldn’t be alive at all without the sea slug to protect it. You will never see an emperor shrimp all by itself.
- They are almost always riding on the back of a sea slug—the most brightly-colored, slowest-moving creature in the sea.
- The bright colors warn other creatures that the sea slug can hurt them with its stinging gills, so they stay away.
- The emperor shrimp can also quickly hide underneath the sea slug or inside its stinging gills, where it is completely safe.
- As the sea slug slowly moves and eats, it stirs up the sand, causing little pieces of food to float up to where the emperor shrimp can munch on them for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.
- The tiny emperor shrimp enjoys one of the safest and most pleasant rides in the sea—all thanks to its friend, the sea slug.
- **Why does the emperor shrimp need the sea slug?** (It keeps it safe and gives it food.)
- **How are the emperor shrimp and the sea slug like Noah and God?** (Noah was “better together” with God. God never left Noah and his family while they were safe inside the ark.)

**Teaching Point:** There is something else that is just better together: you and God! God knows what you need and provides such things as water and air and warm sunlight for you to enjoy every day. He also sends His angels to protect you and remind you to make good choices so that you stay away from danger. No matter how you look at it—life is just better with God in it! I’m so glad that He is always with us!

**_God and Me: Better together!_**
