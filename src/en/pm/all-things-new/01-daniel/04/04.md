[[Daniel 4:10-18]]

- Again, a divine clash is now set to occur. Who will prevail: Babylon’s gods? Or the most high God of heaven and earth?

[[Daniel 4:19-37]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Nebuchadnezzar has now witnessed three showdowns between his gods and the one true God of heaven and earth: first in chapter 2, when Nebuchadnezzar’s royal court failed to interpret his dream; second in chapter 3, when God miraculously preserved Daniel’s three friends; and now again in chapter 4.
- At last, at the end of chapter 4, after this bizarre illness lasting seven years, Nebuchadnezzar’s view of the most high God appears to have been permanently changed.
- He has clearly seen God’s justice, goodness, and love, and his respect for God has changed accordingly.
- What is your opinion of this most high God so far in the book of Daniel?
