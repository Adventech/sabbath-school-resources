**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **two short paper chains** and **scissors**. Invite two children to come forward and ask them to hold out their hands. Gently slip the end loops of each chain over their hands.

- **What would it be like to wear chains on your hands all day?** (Hard. It would be heavy and would stop you from playing or doing many things you like to do.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Explain that although the Israelites were not chained as slaves in Egypt, we do know they were slaves. They were made to work hard, and they couldn’t do whatever they wanted to do every day.

- **Would you have liked to be an Israelite slave? Why?** (Life was hard and sad for them.)

Ask the group to remember the Bible story from last week, and the week before. Invite them to stand up when chosen to answer the following:

- **What had God promised to do for His people who were slaves in Egypt?** (Set them free! Lead them to the Promised Land.)
- **Do you think God will do what He promised? Why?**

Take the scissors and carefully cut the chain in the middle for each child, then help them slip off the chains.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** God is love, and His words are true. He always does what He says He will do. He sent slimy frogs, biting insects, crashing hail, and more to prove it. God would save His people—just as He promised. Let’s find out how!

This story helps us learn our **Big Idea**. Invite children to repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God’s way is best. I am saved by trusting Him.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your bell or sound maker and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal pieces of **flatbread** in a **container**, a **tote bag** with a few items inside it **(child’s top, cup, bowl)**, and a **nature paintbrush**. Place the paintbrush next to a doorframe or your cardboard with drawn doorframe used in the story. Move the flatbread and bag (with contents laid beside it) to two different areas in your Sabbath School space. You now have three activity stations. You may like to duplicate this for larger groups or do it in pairs.

**Invite the children to play a game of trust with you.** If they choose to trust God, they should run to obey His instructions to the Israelites. When you say “Go,” they can run to the doorpost and brush the sides with a nature paintbrush, then run to the bag and pack it, then run to the bread and take a piece, and run back (and enjoy their bread!). Have helpers at each station to gently guide when needed.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Invite the children back to their seats. Affirm the children for being so quick to obey God’s words.

- **Why do you think the Israelites did these things and obeyed God?** (They trusted Him. They believed/loved Him. They knew that only God could save them.)
- **What happened to everyone** (including some Egyptians and others who had joined them) **who trusted God and painted their doorframes?** (They were saved!)
- **What do you think the people said to God as they left Egypt?** (Thank You! I love You!)

Share that we aren’t slaves, as the Israelites were, but we sometimes have troubles. We have hurts, sickness, sadness, and death because of sin.

**Show a picture of Jesus.** Explain that Jesus came to our earth to save us from sin. He wants to save everyone who puts their trust in Him. He wants to help and protect us each day. And He wants to one day take us to heaven.

Share that we can show Jesus that we trust Him by praying a very simple prayer. Invite the children to follow your actions and repeat your prayer, if they would like to. Kneel down, fold your hands, and bow your head. Pray, “Dear Jesus, I trust in You. Please save me. Thank You! Amen.”

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Painted Doorposts.** Make a painted doorframe with your memory verse as a reminder of today’s story and Jesus’ promise to save everyone who puts their trust in Him. **^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is **to be extra loving to your mommy (or a wonderful woman who cares for you). Help her and be kind to her or draw her a nice picture. Give her lots of hugs!**

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Ask two children what they learned today. Accept their answers. Add that today we learned that God kept His promise and saved His people. He gave them freedom.

We learned that God loves us and protects us from trouble too—He’s like a hiding place.

Best of all, we learned that Jesus saves us from sin. We can trust Him to help us now, and one day take us to be with Him in heaven.

Invite the children to call out “Thank You, Jesus!” while clapping the syllables. Show them, then repeat together three times.
