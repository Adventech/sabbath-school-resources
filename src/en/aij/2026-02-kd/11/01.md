Back to Egypt

_Ah, full tummies! What a wonderful feeling._ Jacob’s family sighed with thankfulness as they munched on their bread, made with the grain from Egypt.

But as time passed the grain began to run out. There was only one thing to do—go back to Egypt and buy some more! But this time, the brothers would need to take Benjamin with them so they could free Simeon and buy grain. Father Jacob shook his head, no. He still missed Joseph so much. He did not want to lose Benjamin too!

Brother Judah stood before their worried father. What did he promise to do? **Read Genesis 43:8, 9.**

What a good big brother! Father Jacob nodded his head slowly. With a prayer he sent all 10 sons back to Egypt. This time their empty sacks were filled with gifts for the governor. They also took money for new grain and the money Joseph had hidden in their sacks of grain before.

After a long trip to Egypt the ten brothers came before the Egyptian ruler again. Governor Joseph hid a smile as he noticed his little brother Benjamin was with them. He looked so grown up! With a serious face Joseph asked his servant to take them all to his home for lunch.

The brothers were nervous. Why were they being invited to eat with the Governor? But they had nothing to worry about because God was guiding them. He would make everything good in the end.
