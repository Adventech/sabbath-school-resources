The sky turned a golden pink at the end of another day as the sun slipped low behind the hill. What a special new day it had been with Jesus. He had taught them more amazing things! He had healed and helped them. No one wanted the day to end.

But as the afternoon sunlight faded, the people suddenly realized something. They were really, _really_ hungry! Their tummies began to rumble. Oh, no, what would they do? They were a long way from home or any towns. And they didn’t have any food to quickly munch on. In the rush to find and follow Jesus, the whole crowd had forgotten to pack lunch or dinner.

What a big problem! The disciples told Jesus all about it. They said to Him, “Send the people away. They need to go to the farms and towns around here to buy some food to eat” (Mark 6:35, 36, ICB).

Jesus smiled and shook His head. He had a better idea. “You give them something to eat,” He said (Mark 6:37).

The disciples frowned. _Feed 5,000 hungry men, plus families?_ What did they say to Jesus? **Read Mark 6:37.**

It seemed impossible! But the disciples had forgotten one very important thing. Nothing is too hard for Jesus.

Jesus loves to help us and give us what we need. He tells us in the Bible to ask God for what we need each day. We can say, “Give us this day our daily bread” (Matthew 6:11). This was just what the hungry people needed!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever forgotten to take food somewhere? What did you do?
- Would you like to ask and thank Jesus each day for your food? He loves to give you what you need.
