**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He helps me with any problems.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use **ribbon sticks** (from Quarter 2) where indicated.
- A **jug**.
- Use **six jars** to represent the stone water jars.
- Place an **empty glass** on a flat surface. Place a drop of red and a drop of **blue food coloring** in the bottom of it to make a purple liquid when water is poured into it. Or just pretend with an empty cup.
- Have **cups** available (from the Secret Surprise Box) and **grape juice** for children to taste at the end.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 9 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus smiled as he arrived at the wedding in Cana. Happy music filled the air. People talked and laughed. Everyone was full of joy!

The bride and groom sat together, eating delicious food and sipping grape juice. They were married! Hooray! ^[(Wave ribbon sticks.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Marriage is something very special that God created. It’s a forever promise between a man and a woman who love each other.

The bride and groom’s family had come from all over the country to celebrate. Jesus and His disciples had come too.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Mary saw Jesus, her Son. She was very happy! She hadn’t seen Him for many weeks. She must have given Him a big hello hug.

Lots of other people were excited to see Jesus too! They had heard wonderful things about Him. But Jesus didn’t want lots of attention. It was the bride and groom’s special day. He had come to quietly enjoy the wedding party and to share the joy of the bride and groom. Everywhere Jesus went, He also wanted people to know God more.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But over in the corner someone was frowning. The wedding servant shook his head as he peered into another empty jar ^[(pick up the empty jug)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It should have been full of sweet grape juice. But it was empty! _Oh, no_ ^[(show the jug is empty)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He picked up another jar. It was empty too! Empty. Empty. Empty. No more grape juice was left.

_Oh, no!_ The party was nowhere near finished. The bride and groom would be so upset if they knew the grape juice was all gone!

The servants whispered to each other. How would they find more grape juice? There were no shops to go to. There was no way to get any more grape juice.

Soon Mary heard about their problem. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She hurried over to Jesus and said, “They have no wine” (John 2:3). Mary knew she could go to Jesus for help. No problem is too big for Him!

Jesus looked at the worried servants and their empty jars. Yes, He would help them!

By the door stood six empty water jars made of stone ^[(point to jars). ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus turned to the servants and said, “Fill the waterpots with water” (John 2:7).

The servants obeyed. _Glug-glug-glug. Glug-glug-glug._ The water splished and splashed into the big heavy jars. ^[(Count jars.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One . . . two . . . three . . . four . . . five . . . six jars were filled to the top.

“Draw some out now, and take it to the master of the feast,” Jesus said (John 2:8).

The servants were confused. But they chose to trust Jesus and obey Him. Carefully they poured the water into a cup and gave it to the master ^[(pretend to pour into cup)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

_Sip, sip._ His eyes opened wide. Then his lips turned up into a smile. This wasn’t a cup of water! This was the sweetest, freshest, most delicious grape juice He had ever tasted!

“People always serve the best wine first,” he said to the groom. “But you have saved the best wine till now” (John 2:10, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was a miracle! Jesus had turned water into the purest grape juice! He had provided just what they needed.

The servants told the guests. The guests told their friends back home. Everyone began to talk about Jesus. He had done a miracle to solve their problem. Jesus loves to help us with our problems too. ^[(Let the children try a little grape juice in cups if appropriate.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
