From a distance, Miriam watched the floating basket that held her baby brother. Her eyes grew big when she saw Pharaoh’s daughter strolling with her maids along the riverbank—right toward the very spot where the little basket lay hidden! She didn’t know that the angels were guiding the princess there.

Spying the little basket boat in the bulrushes, the beautiful princess was curious. The basket was brought to her. Carefully she opened the lid. It was a baby! Moses let out a cry, and her heart filled up with compassion. She guessed that a poor Hebrew mother must have placed him there to save him from the soldiers. The princess was determined to save him, and decided right then and there to adopt him.

The princess may even have believed that the Nile River god Hapi had given her the child, because the Egyptians believed Hapi was the god who gave them long life and many children. Of course, it wasn’t Hapi, because God is the only God and there are no other gods. It was God who had given Moses to the princess. This was another important part of God’s plan to prepare Moses for his big work ahead.

Miriam had been secretly watching everything, especially the tender kindness Pharaoh’s daughter showed toward her little brother. Moving nearer, she spoke bravely with the princess. What did they say to each other? **Read Exodus 2:7, 8.**

Soon it was all arranged. Mother Jochebed would feed and care for her very own baby, and the princess would even pay her! It was a miracle! Mother Jochebed was deeply grateful.

God had heard her prayers and seen her faith. He had saved her baby! God hears our prayers and responds to them too, sometimes in unusual ways. Whatever our troubles might be, we can always pray, and God will always hear.
