God’s cloud led the people to the edge of the land He had promised them. This land would be their new home! God told Moses to send 12 men into the land to find out what it was like. Joshua and Caleb were chosen. This would be a big adventure! They were to look and look, all over the land. They were to look up high. They were to look down low. Hurry, hurry . . . they must go! _(Walk with fingers on the page.)_\
The 12 men packed their bags and set off. Walking, walking, walking. Looking, looking, looking. Their eyes grew big. Their mouths opened in surprise _(look surprised)._ Everything looked so beautiful! The grass was lush and green! The fields were golden with wheat. There was dark, rich soil. Shady green trees waved in the breeze. Water splished and splashed in rivers and ponds. And big purple, juicy grapes grew in the land!

The men rushed back to tell Moses everything. The land was beautiful up high. The land was beautiful down low. They had big, big, purple grapes to show! But . . . there was a problem. The cities were also big! They had huge walls and tall gates _(reach to the sky)._ The people were big too! And they had very strong soldiers. Some of the men felt scared—too scared to go into God’s Promised Land.\
But Joshua and Caleb weren’t scared. God had promised that this would be their home. They trusted Him to keep them safe. They told the people, “He will give us that land where much food grows. . . . Don’t be afraid!” (Numbers 14:8, 9, ICB).\
Who would the people listen to—the 10 scared men or brave Joshua and Caleb? Oh, no. The people chose to believe the scared men. They didn’t trust God to help them. God turned His cloud back to the desert. The people would have to live there for a while. He would teach them to trust Him more.
