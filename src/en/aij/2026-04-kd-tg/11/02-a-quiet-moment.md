**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

As a little child slips their hands in yours, leaps from a swing into your arms, or looks for you first in a crowd, they are expressing something wonderful: they trust you. What a privilege! So fully and freely do young, safe children give their trust to the caregivers in their lives. It is their sincere belief that you are the strongest, the fastest, the loveliest, the funniest . . . the best.

Jesus invites us to trust Him with the same simple faith (Matthew 18:2-5). For He truly is the most powerful, the most creative, the most generous, the most kind . . . the very best!

“Do not outgrow the simple faith and trust of your childhood. . . . Be a child again. Cast all your burdens and sorrows on Him who alone can give rest to the weary heart and peace to the troubled soul. If you would learn anew the precious secret of happiness in this life and how you may attain the future, immortal life, be assured you must again be a child in trust, in obedience, in love” (Ellen G. White _letter 56_, 1874).

The centurion in this week’s lesson had a new, childlike faith. Although he didn’t understand the ins and outs of religion, He trusted his friend to Jesus. He believed Jesus could do something beyond reason; beyond imagination. And Jesus did this miracle for them both.

May you be inspired by the faith of the centurion, along with the young children in your class, to experience the blessing of fully and freely trusting in Jesus this week. “Commit your way to the Lord, trust also in Him, and He shall bring it to pass” (Psalm 37:5).
