**Opening Activities**

Print the **Baby Jesus pictures**—at least one per child. Cut out three to four **large yellow or gold stars**—as big as your hand. Attach a clue to the first star that will lead to the next star picture, and place in a noticeable spot in the room. Place a different clue on each of the following stars that will lead the children around the room. These following stars should be hidden, and the clues lead to the last star which will have the pictures of Jesus with it. Provide crayons for the children to color their pictures before class.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: sawdust, sand, salt, or fine grains; plastic gold “coins,” small plastic or foam hearts** (or cutout from cardstock).

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props:** angel sticks from week 1; a large star (make it bright and sparkly, or source a star light that you can turn on); something gold or a box covered in gold paper; jar of herbs/incense for frankincense; perfume bottle or bottle with oil for myrrh; a picture of little Jesus in a house with Mary.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box: footprints** cut from paper; **objects** that lead us closer to Jesus (Bibles, songbooks, praying hand picture, bag of groceries to share, nature objects, etc.).
