**Big Idea:** God is loving. He took my consequences.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Set up two trees (tall plants in pots, artificial trees, or Christmas trees). Hang various fruits (either toy or real) on the trees. Hide a toy snake decorated with wings in one of the trees. Use a sparkling angel picture or prop.
- As the story is told, wander around the “garden” pretending to pick fruit except the forbidden one (you may like to invite the children to wander too).
- Draw an outline of a face. Use the **Face Emotions** pieces to show feelings throughout the story. Invite the children to place pieces on the face outline.
- Have large leaves to show the children what Adam and Eve had to wear.
- Use a flashlight to switch on, then off.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[Week 5 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Life with God in the garden was good. God warned Adam and Eve about Satan, his lies, and tricks ^[(Face Emotions, sparkling angel).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God pointed out two special trees. The tree of life was a “help yourself” tree that gave eternal life. The tree of the knowledge of good and evil was a “don’t touch it” tree. Adam and Eve were to stay together and away from it ^[(two plants in pots).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Eve went walking near the “don’t touch it” tree ^[(child wander).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- A voice came from the tree! The serpent tempted Eve to take the fruit ^[(toy snake decorated with wings)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). **Who do you think the serpent is?**
- Eve took the fruit and ate it. ^[(Have a piece of fruit in your hand and bite into it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Eve offered the fruit to Adam, and he ate it too! ^[(Choose a Face Emotion.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) **Did they make a good choice?**
- Their robes of light disappeared. They were naked, cold, and scared! They tried to cover up with fig leaves and hide from God ^[(Face Emotions, flashlight, shivering action, large leaves)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- God sees everything. He found them and explained the consequences of pain, sadness, death.
- Adam and Eve left the garden forever. Life was hard. ^[(Invite two children to stand up and “guard” the two trees.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God and Jesus had a rescue plan! Jesus would come as a baby, then die to save us. He’s coming back soon!

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam and Eve loved their Garden of Eden home, and they loved their Creator God. ^[(Invite a child to choose Face Emotions and place them on the face outline, to show how they felt.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One evening God told Adam and Eve about the angel Lucifer, now called Satan. ^[(Show the sparkling angel from previous week.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He had tricked other angels in heaven with his lies about God—he would try to trick them too.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God pointed out two special trees in the garden. A “help yourself” tree and a “don’t touch it” tree. ^[(Draw the children’s attention to the two plants/trees in pots.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The “help yourself” tree was called the tree of life. It looked amazing. It had 12 different delicious fruits! ^[(Place lots of toy fruits around the tree or hand them to the children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Yum! If they ate this fruit, they would live forever. What a special tree.

The “don’t touch it” tree was called the tree of the knowledge of good and evil. It also had yummy-looking fruit, but God said they were never to eat it—ever! They were to stay together and stay away from this tree.

One day Eve walked around the garden. ^[(Invite a child to wander through the two trees.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly she stopped. Right in front of her was the “don’t touch it” tree. _Wow! The fruit is so beautiful_, Eve thought to herself.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A smooth voice came from inside the tree: ^[(Bring the toy snake with “wings” out of hiding in the tree.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Did God really say that you must not eat fruit from any tree in the garden?” (Genesis 3:1, ICB). Eve looked closer. She saw a serpent with sparkly golden wings. What a surprise! Serpents don’t talk! But this one did.

Eve told the serpent that God had said they could eat fruit from the trees in the garden, just not the fruit from this tree. They weren’t even to touch this tree—or they would die! The serpent said to Eve, “You will not die” (Genesis 3:4, ICB). What a big lie! The serpent had called God a liar!

- **Do you think you might know who this lying serpent was?** (Satan!)

Eve made a choice. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Reaching out her hand, “she took some of its fruit and ate it” (Genesis 3:6, ICB). ^[(Have a piece of fruit in your hand and bite into it.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Crunch. Oh, no!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then she offered some to Adam. He also had a choice to make, and “he ate it” (Genesis 3:6).

- **Did they make a good choice?**

Instantly they felt different. They looked at each other in surprise ^[(use Face Emotions). ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Their robes of light ^[(flick light on, then switch it off)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) had faded away! Quickly Adam and Eve stitched fig leaves together to try to cover themselves up. They felt terrible! ^[(Have some large leaves to show the children what their “clothes” would have looked like.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Hearing God walking in the garden, they quickly hid! But God had seen everything. He called out to them in love. Then God sadly explained the consequences of their sin. There would be pain, sadness, and death.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They would one day die and so would the plants and animals. They must also leave their garden home forever. God placed angels with flashing, fiery swords at the entrance. ^[(Invite two children to stand up and “guard” the two trees.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He had to make sure people could no longer eat from the tree of life; otherwise, they would live forever in sadness and pain. Everything was different! Weeds and prickles began to grow among the grass and vegetables. The flowers drooped; the leaves fell. Adam and Eve would get sick, feel pain, and grow old. That is a lot of sad consequences. ^[(Invite the children to select some Face Emotions again.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) If only they had chosen to follow God’s ways.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As soon as Adam and Eve disobeyed, God the Father and Jesus had a plan. Jesus would come to earth to take the consequences for all people’s sins. This is the best news ever! ^[(Face Emotions.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus loved sinful people so much that He came into the world as a little baby ^[(use actions by cradling your arms and rocking)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He grew up and then died to save us. One day He’ll come back to this world to take those who love Him to be with Him forever in heaven.
