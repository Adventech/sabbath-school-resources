_Roar_ went the lions. _Buzzzz_ went the bees. _Neigh_ went the horse. Baaa went the lambs.

God must have smiled on the sixth day as He looked at all the noisy and playful animals He had just made. They were all so different: different colors, different sizes—they even lived in different places. Some swung up high in trees, while others burrowed underground. Some hid away, and others pranced in the wide-open meadows.

Yes, God was happy with everything He had made. And now it was time to make the best creation of all.

All of heaven watched as God bent down and “took dust from the ground” (Genesis 2:7, ICB). With His strong, kind hands God began to shape the dirt into two feet and two long legs. He made a chest, two arms, and 10 fingers. Shaping a head, God made ears, a nose, two eyes, and a mouth.

God was making a . . . ? Read the first part only of **Genesis 2:7** to fill in the gap.

The very first human ever! This man that God made was perfect. He was created with God’s very own hands!

When God made you, He also took special care. There’s not one other person in the whole wide world just like you. What a loving God He is!

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think God shaped the first person with His own hands instead of just talking, as He did when He made the animals?
- Do you think you could shape a person out of dust with your hands? We can be thankful that nothing is too hard for God!

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for making you and your family.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Make a person from some clay, mud, or snow. Making a person isn’t easy, but God was very creative and clever when He made Adam!
