**Focus:** Dennis discovers a turtle in his backyard and welcomes him into his family.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-02-kd-tg/13/nature-lesson.png)

Dennis loved God’s creatures. He enjoyed watching the tiny little ants climbing their anthills. He liked carefully looking into a busy bee’s nest to see inside. He climbed up high and peeked into a bird’s nest to look at some tiny, speckled eggs. Dennis loved exploring in his backyard and especially the little creek behind his house.

One summer day Dennis made an exciting discovery under a great big flower bush next to his backyard swing.

What was it? ^[(Show picture.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

How interesting! At first he thought it was a very big brown rock, but as he got closer he saw that it was a box turtle! He could not see any legs or head poking out, but when he picked it up, it was quite heavy. The little turtle had pulled its legs and head inside to hide. Isn’t the box turtle clever?

Dennis now had a new friend and wanted to make it feel welcome and special. So he filled a cardboard box with lush green grass and then added a bowl of clean water to drink. His big brother Rusty gave the turtle yummy fresh fruit and vegetables to eat. Ah, there! A special place, made with love for his new little box turtle friend. Carefully Dennis placed the box turtle inside.

Dennis thought that one thing was still missing. Yes! That was it! His new little friend needed a name! What should he call his box turtle? He needed a strong name. Dennis named the little box turtle “Samson” and then painted the name on its pretty shell.

After a few days Samson the box turtle was set free to roam around the yard. Dennis loved watching him explore and walk around. He was very happy about his new little family friend.

- **How does this story show us what God is like toward us?**

**Teaching Point:** Dennis welcomed the little turtle into his care and looked after it. God does the same for us. We can always feel safe in His family.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is with me. He welcomes everyone into His family.**
