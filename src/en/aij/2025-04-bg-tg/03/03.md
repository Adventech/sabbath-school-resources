**PRAISE TIME** (15 MINUTES)

_Invite parents/caregivers to sit on the floor with their child (where possible) or behind their child’s chair so that they can contribute to their child’s Sabbath School time._ Have props easily on hand to be able to transition through the song to keep the children’s attention. **These songs and props for each child will form the majority of the program. As you transition between songs, be sure to focus on the quarterly theme: I can be God’s helper.** Sing these songs together, encouraging parents/caregivers to sing with you:

- Welcome children and parents by smiling. Tell the children you are happy to see them today!
- Sing **^[“Tick-Tock.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give the children some **sticks** to tap together as they sing. Tell the children that it’s wonderful that they came to Sabbath School today.
- Share how good it is that it’s Sabbath—the best day of the week! Sing **^[“The Sabbath.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **musical instrument** to play as they sing to God.
- Sing **^[“God’s Little Helper”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** or **^[“I’m a Little Helper.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Give each child a **small brush or broom**. Invite the children to say together with you, “I can be God’s helper.”
- Share that something else we can do is tidy up our toys after we play. Sing **^[“Let’s Put All Our Toys Away.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Scatter **toys** on the floor as children put them away while they sing. Invite the children to say together with you, “I can be God’s helper.”
- Explain that we can be helpful when we’re obedient. Give each child a **soft toy animal**. Hold a larger toy at the front of the room and call to the baby toys. Ask the children to bring them to you; to obey. Then sing **^[“When a Mother Calls.”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Invite the children to say together with you, “I can be God’s helper when I obey.”
- Explain that when we invite Jesus to be in our homes, everything is happier. Sing **^[“With Jesus in the Family”]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** as you clap and point to the children, mother, father, etc.

**WEEK 3: SAMUEL LISTENS TO GOD**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag:** Headband with cardboard ears, items that make a sound (water in a bottle, bell, scrunching paper), pillow, blankets, pajamas, teddy bear, stars to dangle

Put on headband with cardboard ears. Ask: **Can you point to your ears? What is this sound?** Make a sound inside a bag with your chosen items. See if the children can guess the sound.

Say: **Isn’t it so amazing that our loving God thought to give us ears to hear so many different sounds?**

Say: **Today’s Bible story is about Samuel hearing someone calling him. Shh! It’s time for us to go to sleep** (put fingers to lips).

Show your items from the story bag that represent sleep. Encourage the children to lie down, put blankets on them, and dangle stars on a stick over them to represent “nighttime.”

Open your Bible, point, and say: **When Samuel was sleeping one night, he heard his name being called: “Samuel!”** (Encourage children to sit up.) **“He ran to Eli and said, ‘I am here. You called me.’ But Eli said, ‘I didn’t call you. Go back to bed.’ So Samuel went back to bed”** (1 Samuel 3:4, ICB). **Shh!** Ask children to lie down again.

Say: **Then he heard his name again. “Samuel!” Someone was calling him!** (Encourage children to sit up.) **“Samuel again went to Eli and said, ‘I am here. You called me.’ Again, Eli said, ‘I didn’t call you.Go back to bed’ ”** (1 Samuel 3:6, ICB). (Encourage children to lie down.)

**Who was calling Samuel? When Samuel came to Eli a third time, Eli then knew it was God calling. He told Samuel to say the next time, “Speak, Lord. . . . I am listening!”** (1 Samuel 3:9, ICB). **When God called him again, Samuel did just what Priest Eli had said. He listened to God.**

Say each child’s name out loud. Say: **God knows your names too!**

Say together: **God knows my name.**
