_Bang, bang! Clang, clang!_ It was early in the morning, but the builders were already hard at work. Their tower was now so tall that the people at the top couldn’t talk to the people at the bottom. Men stood along the path to call out messages.

God looked down on all the busyness. It must be stopped! God said, “Come, let us go down and confuse their language. Then they will not be able to understand each other” (Genesis 11:7, ICB).

Up until now, everyone in the whole world had spoken the same language. But suddenly everyone in the city started speaking strange words they had never heard before! Nothing made sense! Workers shouted for more bricks, but different sounds came out. Some may have called out “_Ziegel!_“; some may have called out “_Ladrillos!_”; others may have called out “_Batu bata!_”; and others “_Izitini!_”<sup>*</sup>

Brand-new words were shouted up and down the tower. The messengers looked on, confused. People became angry and yelled louder, but it didn’t help. Everyone was cranky and confused.

Work on the huge tower stopped. A bright streak of lightning flashed through the sky, hitting and breaking the top of the tower. It fell to the ground with a big crash. What a mess! What happened next? **Read Genesis 11:8.**

The tower was never finished. People soon found others who spoke the same words, and they moved away together, spreading out all over the earth.
