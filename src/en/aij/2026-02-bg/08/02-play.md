Create

Help your child create a love gift for a mother in their life: their mother (this may be you); grandmother; aunt; or caregiver. Draw a big heart and help your child to color and decorate it. Talk about how special mothers are to us. They are special to Jesus too! Pray together for this mother. Then have your child give them their love gift.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Connect

Spend time doing “helping” activities with your child. Patiently involve them in parts of household jobs that you or your spouse usually do on your own. Even just handing you items in the kitchen will make them feel like helpers and show them how much parents and caregivers do to help them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Play

Role-play doctors and nurses with your child. Build empathy and compassion by asking “patients” how they are and what they need. Take time to “bandage wounds, give medicine, and check temperatures.” Share with your child how happy it makes Jesus when we show kindness to people who are hurting.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explore

As you go for walks this week with your child (around your neighborhood or at the stores), be on the lookout for people you can help. Maybe you could open the door for someone at the stores or help someone carry something. You could watch out for any injured animals on your walk through the neighborhood, or practice caring for a pet at home. Praise your child for having helping hands like Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

In worship, think about some people you know who may be feeling down or unwell. Pray for them to feel better and to know God loves them. As a family, brainstorm some ways you could encourage them or help them feel better (for example, drop off a home-cooked meal, go for a visit, sing them a song). Choose one suggestion to complete on Sabbath afternoon.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “I am your God. I will strengthen you, yes I will help you” (Isaiah 41:10).
