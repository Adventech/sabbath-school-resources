**Little Things Grow**

**Show the children a variety of seeds.**

```=Seeds

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/04/nature-lesson.png)

```

- Look at the shape and color of each one.
- Comment on how unique each one is.
- Show real fruit or vegetables (or pictures, sourced by you) to show what grows from the seeds.
- Cut an apple (or another fruit) with lots of seeds in half, and remove the seeds.

**If I take these apple seeds and plant them in the ground, they will grow up to be a new apple tree. Each tree will have apples on it. If I take care of the apple tree, it will make more seeds—not just one seed, but many seeds! Then I’ll have a lot of seeds that can grow many more new apple trees. (Or use a different type of fruit seed as an illustration.)**

God wants us to be like seeds that grow good things. He will take care of you so you can grow all the goodness He has put inside you.

- **Were there good seeds growing in Lucifer’s heart?** (No. There was jealousy, selfishness, lies, and pride.)

Our choices can either grow good things or bad things in our hearts. ^[We can be like seeds planted into the ground and grow good things from the choices we make.]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Repeat **Big Idea: God is loving. He lets me choose.**
