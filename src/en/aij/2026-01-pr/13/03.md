The scorching midday sun drove Abraham into the cool of his tent. Glancing out the open doorway, he noticed three travelers approaching. Abraham had never seen them before, but without a second thought he leaped up from his mat and hurried outside to greet them. As was the custom, he bowed facedown to the ground before speaking politely to the leader of the little group.

“Sir, if you think well of me, please stay awhile with me, your servant. I will bring some water so all of you can wash your feet. You may rest under the tree. I will get some bread for you, so you can regain your strength. Then you may continue your journey” (Genesis 18:3-5, ICB).

Flashing Abraham a grateful smile, the leader quietly replied. What did he say? Read Genesis 18:5.

Leaving the dusty visitors to stretch out and rest in the shade of a spreading tree, Abraham rushed into the tent in search of Sarah. He quickly asked her to make lots of tasty bread. Meanwhile, he hurried out to get a tender calf for the servant to roast. Then Abraham grabbed a basin of water so the weary travelers could wash their dusty feet.

With everyone working together, the meal was soon ready. Abraham was delighted to share the best he had with his guests. Carrying steaming bowls of food, crusty bread, and cool milk, he served them himself.

When we share the best we have with strangers as Abraham did, we are sharing God’s love in a special way.
