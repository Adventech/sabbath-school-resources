**Word of the Week**

**Amazing:** When something is amazing, it is very surprising and makes you feel full of wonder. Think about a rainbow in the sky—that’s so amazing it makes you say, “Wow!” The things God has made for us, like our eyes to see and minds to learn new things, are also amazing. **Lesson Summary**

Joseph remained in prison, being punished for something he didn’t do. While there he met two troubled prisoners, Pharaoh’s butler and baker, who told him about their strange dreams. Joseph explained that God is the only one who can reveal dreams. God helped Joseph interpret the dreams, and they came true: the baker lost his life, and the butler returned to his position in the palace. The butler promised to help Joseph, but forgot his promise. But God didn’t forget Joseph. He sent Pharaoh two dreams that no one could explain. The butler finally remembered Joseph, and Joseph was called to help the king. With God’s help, Joseph told Pharaoh the meaning: a severe famine was coming. Pharaoh then made Joseph the governor of Egypt to help get ready for the famine.
