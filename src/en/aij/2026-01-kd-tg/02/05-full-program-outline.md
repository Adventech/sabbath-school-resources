**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **binoculars**. Share how binoculars make things bigger so we can notice more about them. Use them to “notice” special things about the children, objects in the room, and the animal footprints on the floor.

- **What animals do you think might have made these footprints?** (Discuss.)

Invite a few children to follow the footprints to find the **Creation Posters** spread around the room. Invite the children to use their hands to make pretend binoculars as they go and look carefully at the things God created on the posters. Encourage wonder and excitement at what God has created.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Do you remember what God created on the first five days?** (Point to each of the five posters.)
- **Why did God create all these amazing things?** (Our loving God created them as gifts to be enjoyed by the people He was about to create.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** On Day 6 God created something more. Do you know what He created? Let’s find out . . .

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Creation: Day 6”** in an interactive way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and/or **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a lump of **play- dough**. Have the children gather around the play-dough people made by the kids who arrived early. (Pick one up and whisper, “Wake up!” Tickle its toes, lightly shake it, ask it to walk or say “hello.”)

**Teaching Point:** Only our amazing God can create living, moving, breathing people who can do so many things. We surely can’t! God loves you so, so much. (As you say this, open your arms wide!)

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He created me.**

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Craft Activity: Garden Scene With Adam and Eve. Make a garden scene with Adam and Eve, using play-dough, nature items, and/or craft supplies.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we’ve learned that God is loving. We’ve learned that He made us because of His love.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Be extra loving to your brother, sister, or someone else who is close to you. Do something kind or make them a surprise. Then tell them you love them.**

Remind the children that God has given them an amazing body because He loves them.

- **What can you do this week to look after your God-made body?** (Accept answers.)

Challenge the children to wash their hands, brush their teeth, and eat healthy foods this week to care for their bodies.
