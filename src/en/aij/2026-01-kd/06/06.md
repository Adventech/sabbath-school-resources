Eve wiped away a tear. She missed her happy shepherd boy, Abel. She missed her strong gardening boy, Cain. Adam missed them too. Cain’s bad choices had hurt everyone.

But God was close to Adam and Eve when they were sad. He loved them very much. And He had a plan to bring them joy again!

God gave Adam and Eve another precious baby. It was another boy! What did they name him? Read Genesis 4:25.

Oh, how he looked like his daddy, Adam! As Seth grew, he loved and served God. He made good choices. When he grew up, he taught his sons to love God too. The Bible says, “At that time people began to pray to the Lord” (Genesis 4:26, ICB). Soon many people worshipped God because of Seth’s good choices.

God can help us make good choices too. When other people see the good choices we make, they might want to worship our loving God too.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- What good choices did you make today?
- Do you love God? What does our memory verse say will happen when we love God?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help your actions show others that you love Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
As a family, sing some of your favorite songs about God. Do any of these songs tell you about making good choices?

^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
_“If anyone loves Me, he will keep My word” (John 14:23)._

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#B4B5B4", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#FFFFFF"}}}
^[Family Journal]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})\
Brainstorm in your journal things you make choices about every day. These things can be written as words or drawn as pictures (e.g., clothes, food, words). When you have finished brainstorming, discuss each one. Ask, “How can we do this God’s way?”\
