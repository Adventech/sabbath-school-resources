In this exciting new quarter, some of the stories you’ll hear are about Adam and Eve, Noah, and Abram. These Bible heroes, who lived so long ago, tell us how we can love God and choose Him every day. But most of all, in every story, we see that God is loving.

Yes, God loves you so much! So today, as you run outside or as you play, as you rest at home or as you pray, think about this very important message: God loves me.

Don’t forget to ask your Mommy or Daddy, your older brother or sister, or your grandma or grandpa, to read these stories to you every day. As you listen to them, imagine being there in the story. What do you think it might have smelled like, sounded like, or felt like for the people in our Bible stories?

If you want to listen to the stories and look at the pictures, you can also go to www.aliveinjesus.info to hear the story every day in our podcasts, or learn your memory verse each week by listening to your Scripture Song.

God loves you SO much. I pray that you’ll know and feel this as you hear these stories.
