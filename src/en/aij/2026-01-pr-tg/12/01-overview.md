**Word of the Week**

**Wise:** Being able to make good choices based on experiences and knowing what is true and right.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the way Abram trusted God’s wisdom. Abram wisely chose to follow God’s guidance so he could be a blessing and rescue Lot after he and his family were captured by enemy kings near Sodom.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God can help us make wise choices that are a blessing to others.
- **Feel** excited to do good things for others.
- **Respond** by looking for opportunities to bless others every day.
