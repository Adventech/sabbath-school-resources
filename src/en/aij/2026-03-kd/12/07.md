A blob of little slimy balls floating in a creek may not look very exciting. But that little blob is far from ordinary, it is the beginning of an amazing miracle. This blob contains hundreds of tiny, slippery eggs. And these eggs will change into frogs!

These little eggs within the blob are called frogspawn. They have a tiny black dot and look a bit like an eyeball! This frogspawn will miraculously change into something that looks, sounds, and acts completely different.

Over time the little dot gets bigger and bigger. It grows a tail. Then after a few weeks, out it pops! The frogspawn is now a tadpole. But it looks more like a fish than a frog. It has a long tail and gills, so it can breathe underwater.

The tadpole wriggles and swims in the water, nibbling on plants. During the next few weeks something else begins to grow—its back legs! Then it grows front legs too. But it is not finished changing yet!

After the tadpole grows legs, its tail begins to shrink away. It grows lungs for breathing and eardrums to hear better.

Then when its amazing change is complete, it hops up and out of the water as a frog. It can now breathe air, jump high, and use its long sticky tongue to catch a quick meal. What an amazing change!

When Jesus lives in your heart, He changes you. Every day He is making you more and more beautiful inside. His love helps you to think differently, speak differently, and act differently than your old self.

The Bible says, “Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation” (2 Corinthians 5:17). What an amazing miracle!
