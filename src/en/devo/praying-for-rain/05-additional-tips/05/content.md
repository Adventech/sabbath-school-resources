---
title: Keep Individual Prayers Short
---

It’s important for you as the leader to model short, concise prayers, and the group will follow your example. There may be one or two (or a few) who will not pay any attention to the suggested guidelines you share, and once they start praying, they may go on and on. Just be patient and bear with them. **Remember, the main point is that they are praying!**

“Make **short prayers** in meetings, and lengthy prayers when you talk and commune with God in your closet.” Manuscript Releases, vol. 10, p. 130

“Learn to pray **short and right to the point**, asking for just what you need.” Our High Calling, p. 130
