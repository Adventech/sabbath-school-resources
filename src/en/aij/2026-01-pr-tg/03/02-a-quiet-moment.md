**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Our to-do lists run long as time seems to whizz by. For many of us, life feels increasingly busy. The antidote for life’s busyness is simple. It begins and ends with God.

In His infinite wisdom God gave and modeled something we all need: Sabbath rest. Not just physical rest or the absence of “doing,” but rather a restful state of “being”: being in God’s presence, being at peace, being spiritually nourished from His Word, being rejuvenated, being part of a communal worship experience at church, and being a blessing in service to others.

From the very first Sabbath, God has invited all people to enjoy being free from the burden of work. God invites us to intentionally withdraw from our everyday lives each Sabbath so that we can pause in His presence. Although it’s a privilege and a blessing to serve Him, each Sabbath He invites us to connect and draw strength and renewal from His endless supply of love and grace.

May both you and the children sense God’s presence as you explore the gift of the Sabbath and grow together on God’s holy day.

Shabbat Shalom.
