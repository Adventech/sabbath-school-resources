---
title: Handling Temptations
---

1. Satan is the originator of all our temptations (Jas. 1:12-15;Jn. 8:44).
2. God will never allow you to be tempted above what, with His strength, you can bear (1 Cor. 10:13).
3. Jesus faced every temptation common to us, in much greater degree, and overcame (Heb. 4:15,16).
4. Through Jesus' power, the victory over temptation is yours (1 Jn. 5:4; Rom. 8:5-15; 1 Cor. 1:27-30).
5. To maintain victory over temptation, it is necessary to avoid those specific places, pleasures, habits, or things which are the source of the temptation (Jas. 4:7,8; Phil. 4:7,8).
