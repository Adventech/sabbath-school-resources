**Focus:** Jesus sees our potential.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/12/nature-lesson.png)

Show the children an assortment of seeds. Invite the children to touch and compare the different seeds.

Have you ever looked at a seed? Seeds come in all sorts of different shapes and sizes. Maybe you have planted seeds in your yard. Perhaps you have helped your family grow some pretty flowers in your garden. Perhaps you have helped grow a veggie garden in your backyard with tomatoes, pumpkins, or corn. When you look at that tiny seed, it may look pretty ordinary and quite unspectacular. Who would believe it has the potential to grow into something amazing!

Every single seed carries within it all the potential and the promise of what it can become. A big plant is inside this tiny seed. From tall sunflowers to giant trees—from vines climbing and twisting along the trellis holding luscious juicy grapes, to vast fields of grain, swaying gently in the breeze and soaking up the sunshine.

Every single person also has potential. We have the potential to grow and become someone amazing! Try to see and discover the potential in other people and find ways to encourage them to see it too. When we give our hearts to Jesus and let Him change and grow us, we can become more and more like Him. Jesus sees the potential in each of us. He never gives up on us. He believes in us, and He invites us to give our lives to Him and then watch Him make something beautiful in our lives!

**Discuss**

- **How are seeds like people?**
- **How does this story remind you of Zacchaeus?**
- **Jesus sees the potential for good in each one of us and invites us to give our lives to Him and watch what He can do!**
