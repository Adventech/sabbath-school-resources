Everything about Jesus was different, not just His choice of disciples. He didn’t dress in rich robes like the other rabbis or draw attention to Himself in the streets and synagogue. He didn’t pray long prayers to impress others or think of Himself as more important. Jesus didn’t teach His disciples in the same ways the other rabbis did either, and He definitely didn’t teach them the same things!

Jesus’ mission on earth was to show everyone what God is like, and what His kingdom of love is like. Jesus had come from heaven, so He knew all about God’s kingdom of love. Now He wanted to show humans what God is like.

Kingdoms on earth are all about riches, crowns, palaces, and having lots of land and servants. Kings often go to war to try to steal land, or to protect their kingdom from other greedy kings. But God’s kingdom is not about riches or about fighting. **Read Romans 14:17** and say out loud the three words that describe God’s kingdom.

Jesus knew that the people were expecting Him, the Messiah, to overthrow the Roman rulers and set up a kingdom like all the others on earth. But Jesus made it clear that His kingdom is about living together peacefully. It is about having Jesus in our hearts—not about stone castles, or power, or owning lots of things. Accepting God’s love and loving others would bring joy. This was one of the first and most important things Jesus wanted to teach His disciples. Following Jesus’ example, Paul wrote, “So let us try to do what makes peace and helps one another” (Romans 14:19, ICB).

As a follower of Jesus, you are one of His disciples. Jesus is teaching you about His kingdom too. He knows that having lots of things or power or money won’t make you truly happy. But when you have Jesus in your heart, “the kingdom of God is within you” (Luke 17:21), and you will have joy.
