Create

Use paint or ink to stamp your child’s footprints on a blank piece of paper. Write on top of the footprints “I want to follow Jesus!” Share how we can follow Jesus by doing the kinds of things He loved to do: being kind and caring to those around us, helping, showing love with our words and actions, and obeying. Write or draw pictures of some things you could do to follow Jesus.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Play

Use a magnet, a string, and a stick to make a simple fishing rod. Put paper clips on some cut-out paper fish. Take turns catching fish. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explore

If there is snow, sand, or dirt nearby, go for a walk with your child. Go ahead of them and make footprints, encouraging your child to follow you by stepping in your footprints. Tell your child that Jesus wants us to follow Him. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Connect

Set up an obstacle course. Let your child watch you complete the course, and then say, “Follow me.” Complete the course together. Share how Jesus is there to help us with every hard or happy thing we face. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

In family worship, thank God for being the leader of your family. Share a prayer: “Jesus, we want to follow You!” Write it on a poster. Decorate this poster together with your child. You can draw a picture, decorate it with flowers, or glue footprints on the bottom. Place your poster in a prominent place in your home.
