Jacob stepped out of his father’s tent. Just a few moments later, Esau stepped in. Esau was holding a bowl of freshly made stew. He couldn’t wait to be given the special blessing from his father.

“Father, rise, and eat the food that your son killed for you. Then bless me,” Esau said with a smile (Genesis 27:31, ICB).

What? Old Isaac was confused. “Who are you?” he asked.

“I am your son—your firstborn son—Esau,” came the reply (Genesis 27:32, ICB).

Old Isaac began to tremble all over. “Then who was it that hunted

the animals and brought me food before you came? I ate it, and I blessed him. And it is too late now to take back my blessing” (Genesis 27:33, ICB).

He had been tricked! Esau begged his father to bless him too. What did Isaac say? **Read Genesis 27:35.**

Esau was so upset he wanted to cry. He was so mad he wanted to hurt Jacob. In fact, the Bible says, “Esau hated Jacob” (Genesis 27:41, ICB). He never wanted to see his younger brother again.

Old Father Isaac hung his head and cried too. He knew that God had planned to give Jacob the blessing. But Isaac had wanted to do things his own way. Now, his family had hurt each other with lies and tricks.
