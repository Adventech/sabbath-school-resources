**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God. I will follow Him.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“My sheep hear My voice, and I know them, and they follow Me”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (John 10:27).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Follower”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - A follower is someone who follows a leader. They listen to the things their leader says and copy the things their leader does. Jesus wants us to be His followers.

**This week’s lesson**\
is taken from Luke 5:1-11; John 1; Matthew 4:17-25; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 132-143; 244-251.

“Look, the Lamb of God!”

John the Baptist’s voice boomed across the Jordan River as he told the crowds some very exciting news. The promised Savior was here, on earth, right now! Jesus had finally come to save the world.

Whispers rippled through the crowd. Eyes looked left and right. Where was this king who would save them? Everyone in the crowd looked very plain and ordinary. They couldn’t see a mighty king or powerful ruler anywhere!

John had already baptized this very special Person. And here He was, back from the desert. Who was it? **Read the first part of John 1:29.**

John the Baptist’s face lit up with excitement as Jesus walked toward him. He said, “Look, the Lamb of God. He takes away the sins of the world! This is the One I was talking about” (John 1:29, 30, ICB).

Everyone spun around to see Jesus. And what a surprise they got. There stood a humble-looking man wearing simple-looking clothes.

But as the people looked closer, they noticed Jesus’ eyes filled

with the deepest love they had ever seen. He seemed so caring and gentle—yet powerful and holy at the same time.

The people were confused. They had been expecting a rich, proud leader who would make people obey him. The people weren’t sure they wanted to follow a humble, kind leader like this.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What kind of leader would be better—a proud, bossy leader or a humble, kind leader?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to help you to be loving and humble, just as He was on earth.

^[Mission Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Think of a kind, safe adult in your community who you might make friends with (together with your mom or dad). It might be a bus driver, gardener, or shopkeeper. What little things can you say to brighten their day?
