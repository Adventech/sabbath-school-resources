The wind is an amazing thing; you can’t see where it is coming from or where it is going, and you can’t hold it in your hand, but you can feel it on your face. ^[(Wave a paper fan over the children to feel the “wind.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Sometimes it blows so softly you can barely feel it, and at other times it can blow so hard that it can almost push you over!

Wind is made when hot air runs into cold air. Sometimes, in some places, this makes a “flyway” in the sky—like a highway of fast air. These flyways of wind help flying birds and insects reach faraway places more easily and quickly.

An insect that trusts the wind to take it to a new place is the globe skimmer dragonfly. The little dragonfly is about as big as your pointer finger. It can travel farther than any other insect on earth! Each season globe skimmers head out on a journey into the unknown. They cross big oceans and go to places they have never been before.

Do you know why they make these big dangerous journeys? They are looking for the best place for their eggs to hatch. When the wind changes, it lets them know that it is the right time to go. They catch a ride on the flyway and trust it to lead them to the right place at the right time, and it always does.

Just as the globe skimmer trusts the wind in the flyway, we can trust God to lead us safely. We can follow God, knowing that He wants what is best for us right now, when we are bigger, and when we are grown up. His ideas are far better than you could ever imagine!

- **How does the wind help the globe skimmer?** (It takes it where it needs to go.)
- **How was Abram like the globe skimmer?** (He went on a journey into the unknown.)
