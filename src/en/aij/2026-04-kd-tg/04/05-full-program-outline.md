{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal some paper clips and a bowl. Pour some water into the bowl. Give each child a paperclip and ask them whether they think it will sink or float. Allow each child to place their paper clip into the water. (They will sink to the bottom.)

Explain that these paperclips are like Peter—he couldn’t walk on water by himself.

Carefully place two paper clips on top of each other at right angles (to form an X). Gently lower them into the water. Remove the lower paper clip. The top paper clip will float. Repeat as desired.

**Teaching Point:** Jesus is like the paperclip on the bottom. He made it possible for Peter to do something he couldn’t do by himself. Jesus can help us to bravely do great things too!

- **What happened to Peter when he moved away from Jesus?** (He began to sink.)
- **Staying close to Jesus will help us stay brave. What kinds of things can we do each day to help keep us close to Jesus?** (Repeat the paper clip demonstration for every answer: talk to Him; read/listen to Bible stories; sing to Him; think about Him; talk about Him with others.)

Reinforce that when we keep close to Jesus, He makes us brave. Invite the children to silently **pray** with their **hand on their heart** and ask God to help them be brave.
