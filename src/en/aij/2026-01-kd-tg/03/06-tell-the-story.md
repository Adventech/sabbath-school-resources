**Big Idea:** God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a **cardboard sun** for the children to role-play when Sabbath begins.
- Use **Creation props** from the last two weeks to review the days of Creation.
- Have children make a heart shape with their hands or cross their arms over their chest when you talk about love.
- Provide a collection of items the children can use to pretend they are getting ready for Sabbath.
- Light a **candle** to illustrate how special Sabbath is.
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (week 3 Program PowerPoint).
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- At the end of Day 6 the sun set, and the first Sabbath began ^[(cardboard sun).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- In six days God had created everything ^[(Creation props).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Adam and Eve loved their new world, each other, and God ^[(love action).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God loved them and gave them the gift of Sabbath—time with Him ^[(love action).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Imagine the first Sabbath . . .
- God made the Sabbath holy.
- Sabbath was full of joy! Adam and Eve would count down the days until their special time with God! ^[(Count to seven; jump up and say, “Sabbath!”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God blessed the Sabbath.
- For six days Adam and Eve worked. They did all their work before Sabbath, so they could be ready for it ^[(props to pretend to get ready for Sabbath). What things could you do to help be ready for Sabbath at home?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The Sabbath begins at sunset every week—it’s a gift for everyone. God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath. Thank You, God!

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam and Eve watched the golden sun begin to set. When it dipped out of sight, the Sabbath would begin ^[(hold up cardboard sun and make it disappear behind a chair).]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Their smiles grew big and bright. What a beautiful world!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) In six days God had created everything. He created the light, sky, water, plants, sun, moon, stars, fish, birds, all the animals, and then Adam and Eve! ^[(Creation props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Adam and Eve thought about all the things they had seen in just one day ^[(show toy animals)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The giraffes had such long necks! And what about those playful monkeys? They loved their new garden home ^[(children show love action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). They loved each other too ^[(love action)]]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). And most of all, they loved their Creator ^[(love action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God loved ^[(love action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) them too. Because of His big love ^[(love action)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), He gave them something very special on the seventh day. The Bible says, “And on the seventh day God ended His work which He had done, and He rested on the seventh day” (Genesis 2:2). God gave them the gift of time with Him. On the seventh day, the Sabbath, God rested with joy. He wasn’t tired, but He stopped His work so He could spend a whole day with His precious children—Adam and Eve. This would be their first Sabbath together in the Garden of Eden!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Imagine Adam and Eve happily talking, laughing, and singing with God. Imagine God bending down to show Adam and Eve a small seed that would grow into a beautiful flower, or pointing to an eagle and explaining how it flies.

God made the Sabbath holy ^[(light a candle)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It would not be like any other day; it would be a special day to rest, worship, and be with Him.

Every week Adam and Eve would count down the days and look forward to their special time with God! ^[(Count to seven with the children; have them jump up and say “Sabbath!” at seven.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Sabbath was the best day of the week. It was filled with joy!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God blessed the seventh day, Saturday. He made it to be an extra-special time for us to come closer to Him. When God blesses something, He gives it a special kind of happiness.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) For six days Adam and Eve were busy, busy, looking after the garden and animals. Adam and Eve did all their work before Sabbath, so they could be ready for it. ^[(Invite children to use your collection of items to pretend they are getting ready for Sabbath.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam finished all his jobs early. Eve gathered extra-delicious foods, such as cashews, sweet mangoes, and strawberries. They didn’t want anything to distract them from their special time with God! God must have smiled when He saw Adam and Eve keeping the Sabbath special because of their love for Him.

- **What things could you do to help be ready for Sabbath at home?**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Every Friday evening when the sun goes down, the Sabbath begins. ^[(Give a child a cardboard sun and have them slowly lower it in the “sky.” Create a horizon line with a table edge or towel hanging on outstretched arm. Invite children to clap when they know Sabbath begins.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

God made the Sabbath a gift for everyone—from Adam and Eve, all the way down through time, to you and your family. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He has made it a happy time to talk to Him, read His words in the Bible, worship Him at church, spend time with our families, and show others His love.

Say together with the children: **God is loving. He gave us the Sabbath. Thank You, God!**
