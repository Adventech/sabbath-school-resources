Lying on his mat on the ground, the man with paralysis saw his friends looking down at him with big, smiling faces. The group of friends were going to take their sick friend to Jesus.

The friends each grabbed a corner of his mat and began carrying him down the road to Peter’s home where Jesus was.

Turning down the street, they could see Peter’s house ahead. But suddenly they stopped still. Oh, no! They had a problem. A big problem. What was it? **Read Mark 2:2.**

There were people everywhere! The house was full and overflowing onto the street. People leaned in on tippy-toes. Shoulders bumped each other. Bodies squeezed in tight. How would they get their friend to Jesus?

These good friends didn’t want to give up. Finally, they thought of a good idea. It was tricky, but getting to Jesus was the most important thing of all. Carrying their friend on his mat, they climbed up the stairs to the roof. Then they got busy.

The people inside the house were listening carefully to what Jesus was saying. But suddenly, they heard a scratching and a rustling up above. Everyone looked up. Parts of the ceiling fell to the ground with a crash.

The good friends kept on working. The Bible tells us, “They . . . made a hole in the roof” (Mark 2:4, ICB). Yes, a hole!

These friends, like good brothers, would do whatever it took to help their sick friend. Jesus wants us to be good friends like that too.
