Isso had been more than 1,500 years since Deus had made nosso lindo world. Deus tried a turn people’s hearts e minds back a Ele again e again, mas only a few had listened e responded a Seu voice (see Gênesis 6:5, 11-13). Como did Deus feel sobre o que was happening? **Leia Gênesis 6:6.**

Deus realized that Ele needed a stop wickedness de spLeiaing like a deadly sickness. Ele would send a flood a cover the whole world. Water would cover the houses, the land, e even the tallest mountains.

Deus loved todos people, e Ele wanted a warn eles sobre the Flood. Ele needed a faithful man a ajude Ele, e Ele chose Noah, para Noah “was a just man,” e ele “walked com Deus” (Gênesis 6:9).

Deus told Noah sobre Seu plans, e Noah listened carefully. Ele told Deus that ele would Faça whatever Ele Pergunteed. Deus told ele a build an ark e explained exactly como a Faça isso. Ele told ele seu size e shape, the type de wood a use, e como a put isso together. Isso needed a be almost as strong as stone, porque there would be a terrible storm. The ark would have one grande Façaor em one side, e rooms para both people e animals. Everyone quem chose a go através that Façaor e into the ark would be safe de the Flood.
