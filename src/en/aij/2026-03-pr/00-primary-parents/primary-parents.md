How well do you know Jesus as your friend? Perhaps you’ve known Him all your life, or maybe you’ve only recently met Him. This Jesus, whom you’ll read about to your child this quarter, knocks on the door of your heart and He wants to be close to both you and your child. Although you may know these stories well, we pray that you’ll also receive fresh insights for yourself and that you’ll grow closer to Jesus. Truly, it’s by beholding Him that we become changed (2 Corinthians 3:18).

Each week you’ll find a Parent Faith Talk prompt, which invites you to share something spiritual from your life that connects to the weekly focus. Don’t rush over these important prompts but take time to reflect and share with your child about how God has led in your walk with Him. When it comes to growing the faith of a child, research tells us that one of the most significant contributors is parents or caregivers sharing their faith. As you authentically share each week, these spiritual conversations will build a picture of how God has led your life and will reinforce that He also wants to lead theirs.

This quarter’s theme is Jesus is my God. As you invite Him to be the Lord of your life, and encourage your child to grow closer to Him, know that He is with you and your family. Together, we can pray, “I have set the Lord always before me; because He is at my right hand I shall not be moved” (Psalm 16:8).

May grace and peace be yours always,
