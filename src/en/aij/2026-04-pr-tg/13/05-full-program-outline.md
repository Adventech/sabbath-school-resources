**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Display some plants: some living, some heavily wilted/dead.

- **Which plants do you think will grow well? What makes you say that?**
- **Which plants do you think won’t grow well? Do you think they can be saved? What might happen to those?** (They might die.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** When Adam and Eve sinned, death became a part of our experience on earth. People, plants, and animals die. This means they no longer breathe, move, or grow.

- **What does the word “resurrect” mean?** (It’s our word of the week: The action of being brought back to life after someone has died.)
- **Can you or I resurrect something? Do you think I can resurrect these plants?** (No.)

Share how our story today is another story of someone close to Jesus who died, and again Jesus did something that seemed impossible! Has Jesus ever resurrected anyone in the stories that we’ve learned this quarter? (Widow of Nain’s son, Jairus’ daughter.) Let’s find out what happened with a very close friend of Jesus’.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **Jesus has the power to resurrect those who have died.**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Place the resurrection picture—available in the Program PowerPoint or as a PDF in the Resources folder—at the front of the room with a cloth over it, without the children seeing. Tell the children you have something special beneath the cloth, but keep the picture covered while you discuss this first question.

- **What was your favorite part of today’s Bible story? Why?** Affirm and discuss their answers.

Then uncover the picture and give the children time to look at it closely.

- **What’s happening in this picture?** (One day Jesus will come again and wake up all the people who love Him, just as He woke up Lazarus. Then we will all get to live together with Him, and no one will ever die. What an amazing day that will be!)
- **Whom can you see in this picture?** Point out some of the individuals: Jesus, angels, children. Explain that there are people from many different countries who lived at different times in history. Look at their expressions. Talk about whether any of the children look like those in your Sabbath School class.
- **What is your favorite part of this picture?** Affirm and discuss their answers.
- **How might knowing and thinking about this Bible truth change you or our world?**
- **What could you do about what you have heard?**

**Teaching Point:** We can trust that Jesus will one day resurrect all those who believe in Him, and we will be with Jesus forever. There will be no more sadness, death, or pain. This news can give us hope and joy. We can share it with everyone!

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the wilted plant again and remind the children that in this life things become old or broken, and people close to us may even die. Jesus cries when we cry and understands better than anyone else. He has power over death, and one day He will call to life those who have died who know and love Him.

Invite children to roll away the stone on their craft, and if they’d like to, call out the prayer **“Thank You, Jesus!”**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing the closing song, **“God Be with You.”** Then pray with your children and thank God that He has the power over death. Thank Him that we can look forward to eternity with Him and once again see friends and family who have died believing in Him.
