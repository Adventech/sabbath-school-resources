A Baby Brings Happiness

No longer able to live in Eden, Adam and Eve must have worked hard to make a new home, clothing, tools, a farm, and gardens. New seasons went by. As leaves dropped off the trees for the first time, Adam and Eve may have felt deep sorrow once more. The whole world was changed by their choices. But God was still with them. Spring came, and new leaves and baby animals appeared.

One day the first two people on earth were joined by a third: the first-ever baby was born into the world! Eve cuddled him close while Adam gazed in awe at this brand-new person. The Bible says, “Children are a gift from the Lord. Babies are a reward” (Psalm 127:3, ICB). God wanted families on earth—like this one—to show His love to others. Oh, how they must have thanked God for this gift! With hearts full of warmth and happiness, they named their sweet boy Cain.

Soon baby Abel joined the family. These two bundles of cuteness filled their parents’ lives with love as they grew into strong healthy boys. They helped Adam and Eve work in the fields and at home. Each boy had his own likes, dislikes, and personality. God had made them, as He made you, unique. What work did Cain and Abel like to do? Read Genesis 4:2.

No doubt they both loved hearing stories from their parents. Nestled close, they learned about the Garden of Eden, the first Sabbath, and face-to-face talks with God. They learned that God loved them very much and how to love Him back.
