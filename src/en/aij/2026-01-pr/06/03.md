While Abel grew up to love God and follow His plan, Cain did not. He let selfishness grow until there was little room left in his heart for God. Instead, like flourishing weeds, bad things took root and grew there. Thistles in the field annoyed him, his younger brother irritated him, his parents’ choice in Eden made him angry. He was angry even with God. Then one day he stubbornly disobeyed by not bringing a lamb as an offering. What did Cain bring to God as an offering instead? **Read Genesis 4:3.**

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr/06/image5.png)

Cain saw God’s instructions through selfish eyes, and he thought he could do things his own way. After all, wasn’t his fruit from his garden that he had grown good enough for God?

It wasn’t what God had asked for, so fire didn’t fall from heaven. When fire burned up the lamb Abel had offered, a knot of anger tightened in Cain’s heart.

Abel saw the anger and tried to calmly encourage his brother to choose to do what was right. Abel reminded Cain of how God had forgiven their parents, how He had promised to save, and how the sacrifices pointed to Jesus. Abel really hoped Cain would listen. But Cain did not.

God saw the anger and spoke pleading words of encouragement. “If you do well, will you not be accepted?” God said (Genesis 4:7). But Cain wasn’t sorry, and he didn’t listen.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How well do you listen to those who love you? When you listen and obey how do you feel?_
- _Has anger ever stopped you from obeying? If so how do you feel?_

^[FOR YOU, PARENTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular", "size": "xl"}}}) Sometimes we may speak in anger to our children. Then comes guilt and regret. And don’t hesitate to ask your child for forgiveness when you have not dealt with them gently!
