The soldier held Joseph’s arm tightly and shoved him roughly into the prison room. What did the soldier do next? **Read Psalm 105:18.**

Then the guard walked out and slammed the door with a bang. Joseph listened quietly as the jangling keys locked the door.

How quickly his life had changed! He had been in charge of Potiphar’s big, rich home and now this. Joseph looked around the dark and dusty jail. God had helped him make good choices. He had lived “the kind of life that honors and pleases the Lord in every way” (Colossians 1:10, ICB). But somehow he had ended up in prison.

Joseph had a choice to make as he sat quietly thinking. He could choose to be cross and complain. He could choose to yell, “That’s not fair!” and stomp his feet. But Joseph chose to live for God and please Him.

Joseph knew that God was with him. He decided to show others God’s love. When the soldiers were mean, Joseph chose to be kind. When the prisoners complained, Joseph chose to smile and say encouraging, happy words. He was like a light shining in that dark prison.
