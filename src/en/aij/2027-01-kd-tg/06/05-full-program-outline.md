**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal apple rings (or local fruit with a hole in the center).

- **How would you feel if I gave you this fruit?**

**Teaching Point:** I think you’d be very happy to have this yummy fruit! Wouldn’t it be a shame if instead of being thankful for the fruit, you complained about the hole in the middle? It’s easy to think only about what we don’t have (point to the hole) instead of enjoying all the good things we do have! (Point to the whole piece of fruit.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In today’s story the Israelites thought about just what they didn’t have. They forgot to be thankful for all the good things God had done for them. They forgot to trust that their kind God would always look after them. Thinking about what they didn’t have made them grumbly and grumpy.

Share the **Big Idea** and repeat it together: **God’s way is best. I will be thankful to Him.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal something you are personally thankful for (it might be a card from a loved one, a toothbrush, or warm socks). Explain to the children why you are thankful for the objects, then invite them to go on a **Thankful Scavenger Hunt**. Tell the children they will have two to three minutes to find something in the room or outside that they are thankful for. When the timer finishes, invite them to come back and share about the item they found.

- **Was it easy or hard to find an item you are thankful for?**

**Teaching Point:** If we look for it, we can always find something to thank God for!

- **Even though the Israelites had no water, what could they have been thankful for?** (Being free and not slaves, the miracle walk through the Red Sea, the pillar of cloud to shade them from the sun, or the pillar of fire to keep them warm at night.) The same God who gave these good things, would keep looking after them.

**Teaching Point:** The Bible says in **Psalm 68:19** that God “daily loads us with benefits.” In other words, God gives us good things to enjoy every single day! He also comforts us when we are sad or discouraged. So even on our bad days, there will always be something we can be thankful for.

Invite the children to stand as you say your **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. I will be thankful to Him.**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to **show or tell your cousins or uncles and aunts that you love them and that God loves them. If they live far away, call them this week to tell them.**

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})**

- **What did we learn about God today?** (Ask for two or three responses.)

Our God is so kind and loving. He gives us so many good things.

- **What can we always remember to say to God?** (Thank You!)

We can be thankful for all the miracles—big and small—that God does for us. He gives us so many good things. When we remember all the good things God does, it will help us to trust Him. Invite the children to gently tag as many others as they can in one minute. When they touch them, they must share a different thing they are thankful for. Remind them of a few things you’ve discussed before setting a timer and saying “Go.”Join back together and all do prayer hands as you say the following on the count of three: “Thank You, God!”
