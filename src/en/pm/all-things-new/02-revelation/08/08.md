[[Revelation 8:1-6]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The seventh seal signals the completion of the work of salvation for humanity.
- The vision of the seven trumpets continues to defy precise explanation. We know what portions of it mean. But some mystery remains.
- There is a correlation between the seven trumpets and the periods of time symbolized by the seven churches and the seven seals.
- The intercession for Christ’s followers in the heavenly sanctuary is symbolized by the angel offering up their prayers before the throne of God.
- The seven trumpets also have some substantial differences from the seven churches and seven seals. What is clear is that the trumpets represent seven successive periods of judgment upon God’s enemies because of their oppression of His people.
- We believe that before Christ returns, the meaning of the trumpets will become clear. But given the current lack of understanding, our comments on the trumpets will be few and brief.

[[Revelation 8:7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Trumpets in the history of God’s people were used for a variety of reasons. They called people to worship, warned of enemies approaching, or reminded people of important events. Trumpets also at times warned that the power of God was about to be shown in a mighty way in judging the enemies of God and His people.
- The seven trumpets seem to follow this same line, in that they warn of powerful judgments that will fall on those who oppress Christ’s followers.
