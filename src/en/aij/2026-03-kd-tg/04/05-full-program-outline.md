**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** **Warmly welcome** each child by name at their eye level as they arrive. Ask: **Who helped keep you safe this week? What did they do to protect you?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})**

- Invite children to come to the **tub of water** and ask them to help “rescue and protect” the **animals, fish, birds, and people** from the water.
- Provide **slotted spoons** for the children to help them “rescue” the things in the water.
- The children can place the “rescued” animals and people on a **large rock shape** to protect them from drowning.
- The children can place the “rescued” birds on a **soft white cloth or sponge** (cloud).
- The plastic **fish** need to be left in the water to protect them
- The children can remove the **plastic garbage** and put it in a **“recycling bin.”**

Use this activity to talk about protecting God’s nature by recycling garbage and not throwing it into the sea.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Sing **“Pack It Up”** as you transition into the program.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a toy that can be guided**. Let children take turns guiding the toy along a wavy chalk/string/drawn road on the floor. As they do this, talk about how we have parents who love us who also need to guide us and keep us safe.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Why does God want to guide us?**
- **Is it easy or difficult for God to guide you?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** God wants to guide us and keep us safe so that we don’t get hurt. Sometimes it’s easy to guide us, but sometimes it’s difficult because we try to go another way! But God always wants to help His children find the best way, because He wants to protect us.

Say the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is my God. He protects me.**

Share that in our story today we will learn how God sent His angels to protect and guide Mary, Joseph, and little Baby Jesus.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the Story Explorer Box, revealing **pictures of children doing various everyday activities**. Invite each child to choose one picture (or for large classes, have two children select one picture). Ask the children to come forward and, one at a time, answer these questions:

- **Who helps you do this?**
- **Who keeps you safe while you’re doing this?**

Encourage the children to include their guardian angels in their responses.

**Teaching Point:** God loves each of us so much that He gave us each an angel to be with us for our whole life. We can’t see our guardian angels, but we know they are with us, helping us with the big and small things we do. The Bible says we should “remember to welcome strangers into your homes. Some people have done this and have welcomed angels without knowing it” (Hebrews 13:2, ICB). (Remind the children that they should talk to strangers only when they are with their mommy or daddy, and not to talk to strangers when they are alone.)
