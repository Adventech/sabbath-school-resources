Adam and Eve were created for each other as the very first male and female. God wanted them to spend happy days together, just as God, Jesus, and the Holy Spirit do. We call this togetherness unity, and it’s a very big blessing.

Then God blessed Adam and Eve in another way. Children would come from their special love for each other. Our families all come from this very first family. We’re all God’s precious children. What words does God use to tell us how special we are? **Read Isaiah 43:4** (first half).

As God’s loved and precious children, Adam and Eve wore a covering of God’s warm light and glory that fitted them perfectly. Imagine never having to change your clothes and them never getting old and ripped!

Then God gave them a beautiful home too. He knew Adam and Eve so well that He gave them the perfect home that was just right for them. What was this home called? **Read Genesis 2:15.**

It was a wonderful home with a blue sky roof that sparkled with stars at night. Soft, green grass carpeted the floor, and the walls were lush, green plants. Sweet-smelling flowers perfumed the air, and birds tweeted happily. There were no windows to wash or floors to sweep. Instead, Adam and Eve enjoyed the beauty of their garden. They talked with God there, feeling close to Him in their peaceful home.

Even though we don’t live in a perfect world as Adam and Eve did, God still wants us to be close friends with Him. We can feel close to God in our homes when we read the Bible, or out in nature among the good things He has made.
