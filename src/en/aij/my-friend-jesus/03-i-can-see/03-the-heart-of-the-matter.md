Bartimaeus knew he needed a Savior, and he sought after Jesus with his whole heart. He did not give up at the first roadblock. He called out to Jesus with determination and faith! 

Make today a new day to become a “Bartimaeus.” Jesus longs to hear your cry out to Him. He will always respond. He sees you. He knows your darkest pains and heartaches, and He holds out His hand to make you well. He sees your faith and is willing to open your eyes to the new life He has for you.

Jesus can do miracles. He can heal broken relationships, forgive, take away shame, restore our mental and physical health, and so much more! Be honest with what you need and believe that He can do what you ask. Jesus said, "Until now you have asked nothing in My name. Ask, and you will receive, that your joy may be full" (John 16:24).
