**Big Idea:** God teaches me how to show love and respect for Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use a **flashlight** or other object to represent lightning, a **trumpet sound effect**, or similar.
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people slowed their steps, and all watched carefully as God’s white cloud moved ahead of them, then stopped and settled over the top of a majestic mountain, Mount Sinai. It was time to set up camp!

Relief rippled through the great crowd of men, women, and children. This peaceful plain was protected by towering mountains. It would be a wonderful place to camp.

God had a special plan for His people in this place. He had taught them many things as they journeyed away from Egypt. He had showered them with love—even manna from heaven. God longed for them to love Him back. It was time for Him to teach them how to do this.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God called Moses to come and meet Him on Mount Sinai. He said ^[(helper adult to read verse)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), “You have seen what I did to the Egyptians, and how I bore you on eagles’ wings and brought you to Myself. Now therefore, if you will indeed obey My voice and keep My covenant, then you shall be a special treasure to Me above all people” (Exodus 19:4, 5).

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What does this picture of the eagle tell you about God?** (He cares for us like a loving Father.)
- **How would you have felt to be called God’s “special treasure”? Do you think it’s true for you?**

Moses smiled, amazed by God’s words of love. He hurried down the mountain and told God’s people. The people were excited too! Invite the children to read Exodus 19:8 in unison, “All that the Lord has spoken we will do” (Exodus 19:8).

God then told Moses that He would come down on the mountain in a thick cloud and speak to the people in three days’ time!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites were to get ready to meet their holy God. The people were to clean their tents, their clothes, and their hearts from sin. They were to make peace with each other and ask God’s forgiveness. They were learning that God is pure and holy. He deserves our respect.

Everyone worked quickly to get ready. Before climbing into bed that night, the whole camp was in perfect order.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The next morning the people stared wide-eyed at Mount Sinai. The top was covered with a thick, dark cloud. All at once they heard the blast of a trumpet ^[(trumpet or similar)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), calling them to meet God.

Moses brought the people to the foot of the mountain—but they weren’t to go too close. Suddenly thunder crashed ^[(clap)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), lightning flashed ^[(flashlight)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), and trumpet blasts split the air ^[(trumpet or similar)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). The mountain smoked and shook as God came down in flames of fire.

God wanted to get their attention because He was their God, the great God of the universe, the great God who was worthy to be worshipped with reverence.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Everything and everyone became still. Finally God spoke. He reminded them that He was their loving God. Now it was time to teach the people how to love Him back. God would do this by speaking His 10 laws. These 10 commandments would show them His character, what God was like, and the things that were important to Him.

**Heart 1**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask the children to read Exodus 20:3.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think: ^[What does this mean to you?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**
- **Explain:** ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God wants to be our very best friend. God is the source of love. This means that all the pure love in the whole universe comes from Him. He asks us to love Him the most—more than anyone or anything. He deserves to always be first in our lives.
- **Apply: ^[How can you show God that He is first in your life this week?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** ^[(Children to write on Post-it notes and stick these on the heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**Heart 2**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask the children to read Exodus 20:4-6. Help define any difficult words.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think: ^[What does this mean to you?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**
- **Explain:** God loves us so much that He wants us to be faithful to Him. He wants us to benefit from a loving relationship with the one true, living, loving, saving God. He asks us not to make, worship, or serve any idols. Idols can be images or statues of false gods. They can also be such things as money or fancy things. Only God, who gives us everything, should be worshipped.
- **Apply: ^[What are some things that might take God’s place in our hearts?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** (Games, toys, people, hobbies.)
- ^[**What are some things we know about God that prove that only He deserves our worship?** (E.g., He created the heavens.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}) ^[(Give a heart sticker to each child who shares an answer for them to stick on the heart. Or they can draw a heart or use thumbs on an ink pad to create hearts. Try to fill the heart!)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- ^[**How can you make sure that other things don’t take God’s place—that He alone has our worship?** (Spend time with Him by reading His Word, praying, asking for His help, being careful about what I spend my time doing and surrounding myself with.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})

**Heart 3**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask the children to read Exodus 20:7.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think: ^[What does this mean to you?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**
- **Share:** ^[(Ask each child to say their surname and how they think it represents their family.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Is it important to your family that you act in ways that are loving and respectful toward others? (Yes. When you do this, you honor your family.)
- **Explain:** It’s the same with God. We are part of His family and bear His name in the sense that we represent Him. Whatever we say or do should honor and bring respect to His name, because others know we belong to His family. When we don’t live or act in a way that is worthy of someone who bears the name of God, we profane or take His holy name in vain.
- **Think: ^[How else could we break this commandment?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** When we love and respect God, we will use His name reverently instead of using it carelessly or as a swearword. The Bible says, “Holy and awesome is His name” (Psalm 111:9). It also says that He calls His people by His name (2 Chronicles 7:14).
- **Apply:** ^[**Why should we always use God’s name respectfully?** (He created us, is our leader, ruler, and friend; His name is our name when we follow Him.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}) This is why we are called “Christians”: because we are part of Christ’s family. Write God’s name in big letters in the center of your heart. ^[(Invite the children to all sign their name in the heart if they would like to show God their love by respecting His name.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**Heart 4**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask the children to read Exodus 20:8-11.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think: ^[What does this mean to you?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**
- **Explain:** God loves us so much that He set a time aside at Creation to be with us in a special, close way every week, starting at sunset on Friday evening and finishing on sunset on Saturday evening. He asks us to rest on the Sabbath day because He blessed it and made it holy. It’s not to be like other days. On Sabbath we are to stop our usual work. Instead, we can be blessed as we spend time with Jesus and reverently worship Him as our loving Creator God. We will learn new things about God’s beautiful character that inspire us to love Him more. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) We will be blessed as we spend time at church with others who love Him and with our families, and also show His love to others in need.
- **Apply: ^[What’s something special you can do on the Sabbath to enjoy time with Him and celebrate His love?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** ^[(Write their answers in different colored markers/pens in the heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
