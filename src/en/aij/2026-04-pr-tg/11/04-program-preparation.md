**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source a **paper cup** for each child, **markers, seeds**, and **soil**. Provide a way for children to wash hands after this activity.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Share the **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source items for prayer time: a **piece of paper** and a **pencil** for each child.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: a **Bible-times map** showing Capernaum, and photographs of you as a baby, child, and adult.

Source a **timer** for the discussion or use one on your phone/media device.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 50: The Roman Centurion]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Source a **seed** for each child.

Find another **mission guest** to come to Sabbath School next week and share about how they have been involved in sharing Jesus locally or another place.
