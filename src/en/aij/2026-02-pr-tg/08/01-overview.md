**Word of the Week**

**Honor:** To show great respect for someone: to treat them as special and important. The Bible says to “honor your father and your mother” (Exodus 20:12).

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

After being sold by his brothers to Ishmaelite traders, Joseph was taken to Egypt and sold as a slave to Potiphar, captain of the king’s guard. God was with Joseph and blessed him. Joseph honored God by doing his work carefully and with excellence, and he remained faithful to God in everything he did. When Potiphar’s wife tried to seduce Joseph, he remained faithful and refused to sin against God, even though it resulted in being falsely accused and thrown into jail.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is worthy of honor.
- **Feel** the desire to honor God.
- **Respond** by honoring God through their actions, even when pressured not to.
