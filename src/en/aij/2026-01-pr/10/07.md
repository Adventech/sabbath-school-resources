When God took Abram outside of his tent on that unforgettable night of promise, the stars filled the huge inky-black sky. There were no streetlights or city glow to dim their brightness, and Abram must have admired the flash of colors and twinkling of light for as far as he could see. Remember God’s promise from Wednesday’s lesson?

“Look at the sky. There are so many stars you cannot count them. And your descendants will be too many to count” (Genesis 15:5, ICB).

Scientists have tried to count all the stars but admit there are just too many! They think there are about 100-400 billion stars just in our Milky Way Galaxy. There are approximately 100 billion other galaxies, filled with stars too!

If you’ve tried stargazing on a clear, cool night, you might have tried to count them yourself. It’s nice to dream of reaching up and touching one. But while they look like sparkling jewels to us, stars are actually huge, glowing balls of hot gases. Gravity, the invisible force that keeps you standing on the ground, pulls the gases together. The cloud of gas then gets hotter and starts to glow.

Have you noticed that some stars flash a color? The color tells us how hot the star is. From coolest to hottest, stars can glow brown, red, orange, yellow, white, or blue. The sun is an example of a yellow star. It’s the closest star to earth, and while it is enormous, the sun is only an average size for a star.

To see a star with its cloud of colorful gases up close, check out this video from NASA: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T8X-QIePGKM.

Your family might also like to search for a local stargazing app. With it you can hold your device to the sky to identify all the different stars and planets.

Whenever you see the stars, remember God’s promise. His love is so big that it can’t be counted or limited. Because Jesus, a descendant of Abram’s, died for our sins, we can all be in God’s family of love forever. And with His love inside, we’ll shine as brightly as the stars.
