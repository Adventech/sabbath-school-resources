In two days God’s powerful voice had created light, split the water in two, and made fresh air.

On the third day God had more amazing things to create with His words. “Let the water under the sky be gathered together so the dry land will appear,” God’s voice commanded (Genesis 1:9, ICB). And it happened—just like that! God called the dry land “earth” and the water “seas.”

But God wasn’t finished yet. He had something else to do. What did He create next? **Read Genesis 1:11.**

When God spoke those words, lush green grass suddenly spread out like a carpet across the ground. Beautiful flowers, all shapes and colors, uncurled and nodded gently in the breeze. Tall, majestic trees towered above the short, plump bushes that dotted the land.

Peaches, bananas, mangoes, apricots, and oh, so many other fruits all hung on trees, ready to be picked. Broccoli, sweet peas, and crunchy carrots grew together. The smell of fresh, green mint and basil filled the air.

The Bible says that “God saw that it was good” (Genesis 1:12). God must have loved what He had made. It was beautiful. It was colorful. It all worked perfectly together. The light slowly disappeared, and the darkness returned. It was the end of the third day.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you finish this sentence? “God saw that it was [_]()” (Genesis 1:12).
- God could have made only one type of fruit, vegetable, and flower. Why do you think He made so many different plants?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for creating a tree, flower, vegetable, and fruit that you love.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
If possible, go outside and collect different-colored flowers that can be pressed in a book. Use these flowers to create a card to give to someone and tell them about God, our wonderful Creator.
