**What you need:**

Piece of cardstock, six craft sticks, craft glue, scissors, markers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8a.jpg)

**Create it together:**

1\. Glue two craft sticks vertically onto a piece of cardstock. Use a horizontal craft stick to roughly measure how far apart they need to be.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8b.jpg)

2\. Dot the top and bottom of each craft stick with glue and stick two horizontal craft sticks, on the top and bottom, to make a square.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8c.jpg)

3\. Allow a couple of minutes to dry, then cut around the outside of the square.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8d.jpg)

4\. Glue two more craft sticks to the top of the square to make a triangle roof.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8e.jpg)

5\. Use markers to decorate your frame and write the names of your family.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8f.jpg)

6\. At home, print or draw a picture of your family and stick it inside your frame.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](assets/8g.jpg)
