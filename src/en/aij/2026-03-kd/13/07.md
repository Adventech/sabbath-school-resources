Christian and Maribel didn’t know what to do. The two missionaries for Jesus had just arrived in the country of Ecuador. But there were no other Adventist Christians in the area. And the people didn’t seem interested to learn about Jesus at all.

How could they share Jesus’ love with their neighbors, if those neighbors weren’t interested?

Christian and Maribel thought and prayed. And then they had a great idea. They would be like Jesus and look for and care for the needs of others.

Christian and Maribel soon discovered that their community had many tail-wagging, sniffing, licking dogs! And these dogs were bored at home every day because their owners had to go to work.

Christian and Maribel began making new friends by walking the dogs every week. They soon had a team of missionaries helping them too.

The dogs and their owners were very thankful for their new caring and helpful Seventh-day Adventist friends. Some dog owners even started to meet up with their new dog-walking friends at the park. Over time some of the dog owners started asking for Bible studies. Then several people gave their hearts to Jesus.

God asks us in Ephesians 4:32 to “be kind and loving to each other” (ICB). We can do that in lots of creative, caring ways.

When you care for others, you will be showing them what Jesus’ great love looks like! Who knows—through a small act of kindness you might help someone find and follow Jesus, and make Him their best friend too.
