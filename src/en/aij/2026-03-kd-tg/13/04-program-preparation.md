**Opening Activities**

Source **stuffed animals**, **dishes of water** and **“animal food”** (seeds, dry cereal, etc.), **bandages**, and **blankets**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a small **whiteboard**, **dry erase marker**, and **eraser**. Alternatively, you could use a **chalkboard** and **chalk**, a **notepad** and **pencil**, or a **media tablet**.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
