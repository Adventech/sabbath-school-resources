^[Object Lesson 1:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#f8a074"}}})\
^[Wise Words]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f8a074", "size": "xl"}}})

Invite your child to put their hands in cold water. This is like a person who is peaceful and calm. Take their hands out and pour some water into a saucepan and place it on the stove to boil (hot water will quicken the process). Ask your child to stand back and be careful as they watch what happens as the water gets hotter. Share that sometimes people can say unkind words as it starts to boil, such as “You can’t play with me”; or “I don’t want to be your friend.” We should never talk like this to others. Pause between each statement and comment on the water beginning to boil. Explain how harsh words are like a person who is getting more and more angry, until finally things “boil” over.

Then take the water off the heat. As you do so, share some phrases that help things to “cool” down again, such as “I’m sorry.” “Please forgive me.” “That was my mistake.” “I want to be your friend.” As you say these, add cold water to the hot water until your child can safely put their hands in the water again to feel its coolness. Talk about how important it is to be kind and caring toward others.

Then brainstorm some kind, peaceful words you would like to have in your home. Write each on a separate piece of cardstock. Decorate each word card and hang them around your home to remind you all to use these types of words.

The consequence of your good-word choices will be a kind, peaceful, happy home where hearts feel safe, loved, and honored.

^[Object Lesson 2:]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#acc354"}}})\
^[Fill It Up!]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#acc354", "size": "xl"}}})

For Sabbath lunch, prepare some food that you need to fill before it’s complete and delicious (e.g., tacos, ravioli, sandwiches, spring rolls, dumplings, rice paper rolls, sushi, stuffed potato).

Talk about how we must never depend just on ourselves. Instead we should always remember how much we need Jesus each day.

Ask God to fill your hearts with His flavorful presence as you eat lunch!
