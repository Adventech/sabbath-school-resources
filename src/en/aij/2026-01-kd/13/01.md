Getting Old

Abram watched the children chase each other around the tents. He heard them squeal as they ran. What happy sounds! He wished he had a laughing, smiling son of his own. Poor Abram and Sarai still had no children.

It was a long time since God had said to Abram, “I will make you a great nation; I will bless you” (Genesis 12:2). Abram was now an old, old man. Sarai was a wrinkly old woman.

Abram thought about the times God had promised them children and grandchildren. He sighed. Those promises had become like a distant, dusty old memory.

Ever since Abram was young, he had been faithful to God and trusted His promises. Now, many years later, it was hard to believe that this promise was really true. Old wrinkly men and women don’t have babies! It was impossible, wasn’t it? Was God’s promise still true?

Waiting can be so hard. God encourages us to keep talking to Him while He teaches us to be patient. **Read Romans 12:12.** What should we be in prayer?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you like waiting for things? How do you act when it doesn’t come quickly?
- Who is patient in your family? How do they show they are patient?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you be patient, as Abram needed to be.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Put a puzzle together with your family or friend. It’s important to be patient while we trust Jesus to piece our lives together.
