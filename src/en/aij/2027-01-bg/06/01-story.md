Is Ready

The ark grew taller and taller. It reached up high into the big blue sky _(reach hands up high). Tap, tap, tap (hammer motion)_ went the hammer. _Sh, sh, sh_ went the saw. Noah followed the instructions just as God told him. He made it just the size God said. Noah and his family worked day by day and year by year. People came from all around to see the ark. Their eyes grew big with surprise. They had never seen a huge boat like this before!\
The ark grew taller and taller . . . to the very top! _Climb, climb, climb_ went Noah and his sons. _Up, up, up_ the ark they went—right onto the roof! _Tap, tap, tap_ went the hammer _(hammer motion)_, tapping in the last nails. Then _splish, splash, splosh_ went the brush. Noah and his family painted the ark with sticky goo to keep out the rain. They painted the whole big boat so it would float. There was an opening in the roof to let the light shine in. Noah made a window to open and shut _(hands flat together—open and shut)_. Then he made a door big enough for a truck to go through.

The ark was almost ready. God told Noah to build rooms inside for people and for animals. What size room would a hippo need? a hamster? A hairy gorilla? God knew! He told Noah how to build everything so it was just right. Noah made a place to keep all the animals’ food. And rooms for people too. All would have a place to be safe when the rain began to fall. But the people didn’t want to come inside the ark. They told Noah it would never ever rain. But Noah trusted God. Noah kept on building, board by board, and nail by nail.\
The sun rose day after day and went down night after night. The years passed by. It took a long, long time to build the ark! The people heard the chop of the ax and the tap of the hammer as the ark grew bigger and bigger. Then one day the people listened _(hand to ear)_. There was no more chop, chop, chop of the axe in the forest. There was no more tap, tap, tap of the hammer, or _sh, sh, sh_ of the saw. There was no more _splish, splash, splosh_ of the brush, painting the sticky goo over the ark. _(Shh . . .)_ There was . . . peace and quiet.

The ark was ready! The ark was w-i-d-e _(hand motion)_, and l-o-n-g _(hand motion)_, and t-a-l-l _(hand motion)_. It had a window that could open and shut and a door in the side. There were rooms for all the animals and people, just as God had said. The boat was ready. Noah had trusted God and obeyed all His plans. God’s ways are so good!\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God. Your way is good. I can trust You!
