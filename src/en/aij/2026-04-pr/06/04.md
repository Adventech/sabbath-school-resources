He had healed her! With a grateful heart she tried to pull back from the pushing crowd. But Jesus suddenly stopped, and the jostling people stopped with Him. With a loud voice He said, “Who touched My clothes?” (Mark 5:30).

The people were pressed together tightly; pushing, bumping, shoving. Everyone was surprised by His question! What did Peter say to Jesus? **Read Luke 8:45.**

Jesus replied, “Someone did touch me! I felt power go out from me” (Luke 8:46, ICB).

The Savior could easily tell the difference between the press of the crowd and a touch of faith. Jesus knew the answer to His question “Who touched My clothes?” But He asked it anyway, so that the woman would have the opportunity to tell others about the miracle. Everyone needed to hear her faith story; her testimony. They needed to hear about the beautiful miracle that happened when she reached out to Jesus. It would encourage them to reach out to Jesus too.

Jesus also asked the question so the people would never think His clothes had magically healed the woman. They needed to know the woman was healed when her faith connected with God’s power.

The crowd couldn’t see all that thinking going on inside Jesus’ head, and they weren’t sure what would happen next. But Jesus knew they were about to hear a beautiful story of persistence, faith, and healing.

One of the best ways for us to tell others about Jesus is to share our own faith story; our testimony. The Bible tells us, “Oh, give thanks to the Lord! Call upon His name; Make known His deeds [acts] among the peoples!” (1 Chronicles 16:8). When we tell others about our choice to follow Jesus, or about the wonderful ways He cares for us, their hearts may be touched so that they will want to reach out to Jesus too.
