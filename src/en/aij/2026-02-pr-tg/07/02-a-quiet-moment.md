**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Joseph found himself in a challenging situation. One moment he was an honored son, the next, a slave on his way to a foreign land. He had to make a choice: would he allow discouragement to completely overwhelm him, or would he draw his strength from God? He’d watched is father, Jacob, trust in God, and he made the choice to do the same. We see that God blessed and sustained Joseph through these very challenging times, as later described in this promise to Jeremiah:

“But blessed is the one who trusts in the Lord, whose confidence is in him. They will be like a tree planted by the water that sends out its roots by the stream. It does not fear when heat comes; its leaves are always green. It has no worries in a year of drought and never fails to bear fruit” (Jeremiah 17:7, 8, NIV).

Because Joseph trusted in the Lord, he could face the future bravely, knowing that God was with him.

God is with you too. Whether things are going well, or whether life is difficult and you feel as though you’ve landed in a deep, dark pit, as Joseph did, remember that God is with you and has a plan for your future. Don’t be discouraged. You are never alone, because our faithful God is your Father, and you are His beloved child.

May you and the children be encouraged by this story to fully trust God through the challenging times of life, knowing He is with you.
