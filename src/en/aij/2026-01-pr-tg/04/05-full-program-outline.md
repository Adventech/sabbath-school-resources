**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly welcome children as they arrive. Ask them about how their special day with God was last Sabbath.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** As the first five children arrive, give them a **strip of paper** with a Bible text. Ask if they will read the text aloud during the storytelling. Help them find the text and slip the paper into their Bible as a bookmark. Color or decorate a small angel.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Distribute a **magnifying glass** to each child and give them several minutes to explore the details in the nature objects on your **Nature Table**. **^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Share that the **Big Idea** gives us a clue that it must have something to do with choices. Read the **Big Idea** together: **God gives me freedom to choose because He is love.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story** with
props and PowerPoint pictures. Use the Strength Cards for a discussion partway through.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Display your two contrasting hearts—hard and soft. Allow the children to touch them and describe the difference between them.

- **Who does the hard heart make you think of in today’s story?** (Lucifer and one third of the angels.)
- **Who does the soft heart make you think of?** (Two thirds of the angels.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** The hearts of the one third of the angels hardened because they chose to listen to Lucifer and rebel. The hearts of two thirds of the angels chose to believe what they knew to be true about God—their hearts stayed soft.

- **What can help our hearts stay soft with the love of Jesus?** (Read the Bible, pray, sing songs about God’s love, choose to do and believe what is right.)

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** The most important thing we can do is choose to invite Jesus into our hearts every day. Let’s do that together now. Sing **“Into My Heart”** or another worship song of your choice.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 4: Fallen From Heaven** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and recite the **memory verse** together. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Job 34:4.”**

Reinforce that God gives us the choice to serve Him every day.

- **How do we do this?** (Pray and give Him our hearts. Ask Him to keep them soft.)

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the **Nature Lesson**, “Wild Sheep or Wild Goat,” and engage the children in the discussion questions.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child a **Good Choices Chatterbox** outline and have them follow the instructions to complete it. Highlight that it will remind us to choose to invite Jesus into our hearts every day. Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly as the children complete their craft.
