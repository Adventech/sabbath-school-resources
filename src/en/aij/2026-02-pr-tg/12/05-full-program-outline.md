**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Play a game of **animal charades** using the prompts you have prepared on small pieces of paper. Children will act out animals who are committed to each other (without talking).

**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. Talk about the game that you were just playing and point out some of the animals who stick with their mate for life (penguins, some monkeys), and those who are committed to caring for each other in a group (lions, dolphins). Share that today we’ll learn about God’s commitment to us, and how His love helped a young woman be loyal to her family when they were very sad.

Hold up your class Bible. Share that so far this year we have learned about God’s care for the first people who lived on our world, recorded in the book of Genesis. **Show them the book of Genesis in your Bible.** Turn to the book of Ruth, where today’s story will be from. Share that Ruth’s story came soon after, shared in one short book in the Bible.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“Alive in Jesus”** and **“He’s Able.”** Share that God is able to heal the brokenhearted. Then Sing the prayer song, **“Sabbath Song.”**

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Bring in a **first-aid kit** and talk about the different items found in the kit and what they are used for: a brace for a twisted ankle, a thermometer for taking someone’s temperature, Band-aids for cuts and scrapes, and a sling for a broken arm. Ask the children if they’ve used these items before to help someone else, or if someone has used them to help them.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Discuss how in a sinful world, bad things can happen to us. We may get hurt. The items in the first-aid kit are useful for a physical problem.

- **But will any of these items heal a broken heart?** (No.)
- **What does it mean to have a broken heart?** (We feel very sad because of something hard that has happened in our life, i.e., someone dying, a pet dying, our best friend moving away.)

Explain that today’s story will remind us of who can heal our broken hearts.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **God can heal broken hearts.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, then creatively share the Bible Story.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (8 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Show the children some buttons. Ask:

- **What are buttons for?**

Accept all answers, then explain that buttons bring two parts together (like two sides of a shirt) and keep them together.

Explain that **commitment is a little like buttons: it brings us together and keeps us together**. God is committed to us when we are hurting. His promises in the Bible remind us of this.

Open your Bible and **read the promise from Psalm 34:18**: “The Lord is close to the brokenhearted. He saves those whose spirits have been crushed” (ICB).

Invite the children to discover, through a game of **Bible Promise Match-up**, Bible promises that give encouragement. Ask them to **get into groups of three**. They may like to give their group a name that fits their group. Then invite the children to **sit down on the floor** so they can place the promises and scenarios out around them. They should **read a scenario** and then **find the Bible promise** that gives comfort for that situation. When they have matched all their promises, they can **choose a favorite** and prepare to share why it’s their favorite. When ready, they can **stand up**, so you’ll know they’re done. Spend time listening to each group. Affirm their favorite promises. You may like to also add your own.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God loves us and will always be there for us. He has given us promises in the Bible to comfort and encourage us, and to give us hope.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask the children to reflect back to the story of Ruth and Naomi:

- **Which promise would you have shared with them?**
- **How can a broken heart be healed?** (Through prayer; reading God’s promises; allowing time to heal; God working through people around us to comfort us.)
- **How can we relate to others when we see that they are sad? How would you like to be treated by others when you are sad?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God can see your heart and knows when it is hurting. He understands and wants to heal our hearts when we’re sad. We can also pray for and encourage others.

**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 25: Ruth and Naomi** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and have the children read the **memory verse** on the back. Then sing the **Scripture Song, “Psalm 147:3.”**

Engage the children in a memory verse challenge. Invite them to sit on the floor in a circle. The first child will say the first word of the verse, then will roll a ball on the floor to someone else in the circle. The child receiving the ball will say the second word of the verse and so on until the verse is complete. Repeat the activity with different children if time allows.

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the **Nature Lesson**, “The Bald Eagle Shows Commitment,” then discuss it.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your **medical kit craft** sample. Children cut out the medical kits and the items, and then paste each item into the medical kit. Then they can write the memory verse, **Psalm 147:3**, inside. Remind children that God’s promises in the Bible comfort and guide us in difficult times. You may like to play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** in the background as children make their craft.
