Jesus said, “I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved” _(John 10:9)_.

What a beautiful invitation it is to come to Jesus today and enter into His rest. Walk through the door He has died to open for you. It leads to eternal friendship with Him.

Noah stepped into a noisy, smelly boat that looked more like a floating zoo! But he also stepped into total trust and dependence. God, and God alone, would be His Savior as the storm raged. No matter the wait, no matter the uncertainty, his faith gave Noah peace.

Jesus wants to offer the same to you. Come inside, he says. “Come to me, all you who labor and are heavy laden, and I will give you rest” _(Matthew 11:28)_. Jesus offers us love, joy, and peace—forevermore! Like a very big door, Jesus also promises to stand between us and any threat. We are safe and saved in His care.

May we, as did Noah, build our relationship with Jesus. Noah built his every day. We can too. Your children will soon notice, even at a very young age, how you spend your time. Leave your Bible open. Show them how to spend time with God, while you do the same. And slowly, prayerfully, it will move them ever closer to Jesus—the Door.

“[Noah] lived out the message he preached and those who knew him best, his own family, could not avoid his holy influence” _(The SDA Bible Commentary, p. 254)_.
