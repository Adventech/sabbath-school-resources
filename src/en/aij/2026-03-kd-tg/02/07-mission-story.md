**Focus:** Jesus is the best gift we can share with others.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Nepal** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Kabita and Gita are sisters. They live in a town not far from Kathmandu in Nepal.

When Kabita and Gita were small, an Adventist missionary came to live in their village. The missionary’s daughter, Janie, invited the girls to come and play with her. Sometimes the missionary father told the children Bible stories and taught them beautiful songs about Jesus. Kabita and Gita went home very happy. The missionary’s songs were a special gift. The girls discovered that they loved to sing!

Sadly there was fighting nearby and the missionaries had to leave Nepal in a hurry. Kabita and Gita felt sad but when they sang the songs that the missionaries taught them, they felt better. The songs helped them to remember Jesus.

One day the police came and arrested Kabita and Gita’s father and took him to prison. Mother was very worried. Kabita remembered that Jesus hears our prayers and will help if we ask Him. She told her mother to pray to Jesus for help. Mother and the girls asked Jesus to help Father get out of prison, and he was released. The family were so happy—Jesus had answered their prayer! Now the girls knew for sure that Jesus loved and cared for them.

Before the missionaries left, they had told the girls about the Adventist boarding school in Kathmandu. The missionary father promised to find someone to pay their school fees so they could go there when they were older, and he did. Both girls were able to learn about Jesus every day.

“When we go home during holidays,” Gita said, “we tell our friends in the village about Jesus. We teach them to sing songs, and we pray for sick people. Sometimes they get well. We tell them they must pray to Jesus, for He is the living God.”

The girls are very grateful to the missionaries. They didn’t just give the girls beautiful songs, or a good school, they gave the girls the best gift ever—the gift of Jesus.
