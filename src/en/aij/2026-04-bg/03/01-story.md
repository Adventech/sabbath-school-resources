^[Listens to God]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#FFFFFF", "typeface": "Omnes-Bold"}}})

The sun had gone down. Samuel yawned _(yawn)_. It had been a busy day helping Priest Eli in God’s house. It was now time to sleep. Samuel got ready for bed. He knelt and prayed to God _(praying hands)_. He loved talking to Him! He thanked God for helping him to be a good helper all day. Samuel snuggled under his blankets and closed his eyes _(pretend to sleep)_. Good night, Samuel.\
Everything was quiet except for the owls hooting in the trees _(whoo-hooo)_. Suddenly Samuel heard a voice _(cup ear)_. The strong, kind voice called his name. “Samuel, Samuel!” Quickly, Samuel sat up! It must be Eli, he thought. He jumped out of bed and ran to Eli’s room. Pitter-patter, pitter-patter went his feet. “I am here. You called me,” he said. Eli rubbed his sleepy eyes _(rub eyes)_. He shook his head (shake head). “I didn’t call you. Go back to bed” (1 Samuel 3:4, 5, ICB). Samuel went back to bed and closed his eyes.

“Samuel, Samuel,” called the strong, kind voice. Quickly, Samuel sat up again! It must be Eli, he thought. He jumped out of bed and ran to Eli’s room. Pitter-patter, pitter-patter went his feet.“I am here. You called me,” he said. Eli shook his head again _(shake head)_. “I didn’t call you. Go back to bed,” he told Samuel (1 Samuel 3:6, ICB). Samuel scratched his head. He was sure he had heard a voice! Who was calling his name?\
Just as Samuel closed his eyes, he heard the voice call his name—again. “Samuel, Samuel!” Again he ran to Eli’s room. All of a sudden Eli knew who was calling Samuel’s name. It was God! _(Point upward.)_ He looked into Samuel’s eyes and said, “Go to bed. If He calls you again, say, ‘Speak, Lord. I am your servant, and I am listening’ ” (1 Samuel 3:9, ICB). When Samuel heard God’s voice calling his name, he knew what to say.\
God talked to Samuel. He told Samuel things that would happen to God’s people. Samuel listened very carefully.

God loves to say your name too. He wants to be your close friend. Sometimes we hear His voice in our minds as He helps us make good choices. Sometimes we hear His voice through His words in the Bible or through other people’s wise words. Will you have a listening ear, as Samuel did?\
