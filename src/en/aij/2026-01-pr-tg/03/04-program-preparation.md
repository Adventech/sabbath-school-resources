Opening Activities

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#FBFBFC", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Early Arrivals:** Ask **church members from across generations** to bring in a nature treasure and briefly share about it: pinned butterfly, pressed flower, gems or rocks, shells, a shed snakeskin. Children observe and ask questions.\
**Focus Activities:** Source a **small box** and **gift wrap**. Place a **gift for the children** inside the box and wrap it. Suggestions: something practical like a new set of markers or a set of magnetic letters for the class, or something for each child, such as a bookmark or eraser.\
Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.\
Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})**.\
Write the **Big Idea** at the top of the **whiteboard,** or print/write out and display.
