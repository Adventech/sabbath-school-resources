With a windy whoosh, the dust blew across Egypt’s dry fields. Not a single green leaf could be seen. Not a tiny flower or fruit grew anywhere. The cows and sheep grew thin. The time of no rain and no food had begun—just as God had said it would!

Soon everyone was hungry! Their tummies grumbled. This was terrible! They came to Pharaoh and begged him for food. “Go to Joseph,” he told them (Genesis 41:55, ICB).

As people lined up, Joseph swung open the doors to the storehouses. He began selling bags of grain to the hungry families. There was more than enough! God had supplied for all their need (Philippians 4:19).

In far-off Canaan people were hungry too. Joseph’s family was very hungry! His brothers all still lived with Father Jacob, looking after their sheep. Their flocks were thin, and grass was hard to find, just as it was in Egypt. Everyone in the family was worried. They were so hungry they thought they might soon die.

But one day Jacob heard some exciting news. What did he tell his sons? **Read Genesis 42:1, 2.**

Maybe they would eat again! Packing some empty sacks for grain, the 10 brothers saddled their donkeys and waved goodbye to Father Jacob, Benjamin, and their families. Then they began the long, dusty ride to Egypt.
