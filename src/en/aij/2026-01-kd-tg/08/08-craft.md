**What you need:**

Week 8 craft templates, sheet of blue cardstock, colored markers, scissors, and glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/08/8a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out the templates.
- Fold the blue cardstock in half.

**Create it together:**

- Color the circle and glue onto the front.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/08/8b.png)

- Fold the rainbow template in half (vertically) and repeat another three times.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/08/8c.png)

- Unfold and then refold along each crease, accordion-style.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/08/8d.png)

- Fold in half lengthwise and glue together to make a fan.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/08/8e.png)

- Open the card and glue the ends of the rainbow fan to either side of the card.
- Glue the promise clouds inside the card.
