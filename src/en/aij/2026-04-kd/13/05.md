With tears in His eyes, Jesus walked with His friends toward the place where Lazarus was buried. They were followed by a large, sad crowd.

Lazarus had been buried in a tomb; a cave cut into the side of a rocky mountain. A big heavy stone was rolled in front of the large doorway to the cave.

What did Jesus ask the people to do? **Read the first part of John 11:39.**

Martha was surprised, “But, Lord,” she said, “it has been four days since he died. There will be a bad smell” (John 11:39, ICB).

Jesus reminded her that she needed to believe in His power. So some strong men heaved and pushed and rolled the stone away from the door.

The loud, crying crowd hushed.

Jesus looked up to the sky and began to pray. “Father . . . I know that you always hear me. . . . I want them to believe that you sent me” (John 11:41, 42, ICB).

Then in a loud, all-powerful voice, Jesus said, “Lazarus, come out!” (John 11:43, ICB).

With these words, Jesus’ face glowed with a bright light. The crowd could sense His power and holiness. Their eyes watched the door to the cave. Their ears listened closely for a tiny sound of movement from inside. They wondered, _Is Jesus able to even raise someone who has been dead for four days? Is Jesus really that all-powerful?_
