The earth was bright with color and vibrant plant life as Day 4 began. What does the Bible tell us that God said on Day 4 of Creation in Genesis 1:14?

Yes, God’s mighty voice spoke again, causing the sun, moon, and stars to give light to the earth: one to give us light during the day and the others at night.

The silvery moon moved up into the night sky and millions of stars sparkled as tiny lights overhead.

Then, as darkness slowly gave way to morning, a fiery golden ball climbed high to warm and light up the earth for the very first time. Bathed in its glowing warmth, flowers turned toward the sun for the first time, as sunlight filtered through the trees and sparkled on the water.

The Bible tells us in Genesis 1:14, 15 that these lights also have four important jobs to do:

1. _Day and night._ The earth turns once in 24 hours. The part of the earth the sun shines on has daylight, while the other side has night.
2. _Time—days, months, and years._ The sun and the moon are like great big clocks. The sun marks off days and years, while the moon gives us months.
3. _Signs._ Sometimes God used the sun, moon, or stars to give people very important messages. (Extension Study: Read Matthew 24:29.)
4. _Seasons._ The pull of the sun and moon give us spring and summer, autumn and winter.

As God finished creating on the fourth day, the earth was warmer and lighter. God really liked His special lights. The Bible says again, “And God saw that it was good” (Genesis 1:18).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _What might life on earth be like if the sun and moon didn’t do their jobs properly?_
- _Read the first part of **1 Corinthians 14:33** and discuss what this tells you about God’s attributes._

We see that God carefully ordered every part of His creation. In the same way that God has given us laws to follow, did you know that He also gave laws for nature to follow? Recite your memory verse as you think about God’s thoughtful and creative power on show in nature.
