In and out went the little needle. Busily God’s people stitched and sewed. Every thread had to be perfect. Everyone wanted to make God’s home beautiful.

The people wove blue, red, and purple threads together to make wonderful curtains for inside God’s home. Then artists stitched little angels onto the curtains, just as God had asked. He would be surrounded by angels, just as He is in heaven. It would be a holy home.

Men sawed and hammered as they made the flat wooden walls of God’s home. What did they cover the walls with? **Read Exodus 26:29.**

God’s house would shimmer and glow with light. It would be a home fit for the King of the universe.

God’s home, the sanctuary, would also be a traveling home. It was a tent that could be easily packed up and moved. God wanted to live with the Israelites wherever they went. He had said, “And let them make Me a sanctuary, that I may dwell among them” (Exodus 25:8).

God wants to do the same with you. The Bible promises, “The Lord your God, He is the One who goes with you” (Deuteronomy 31:6). His home is holy, glowing with His goodness. He will stay close to you wherever you are.

**^[Challenge]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
Can you think of any animals that take their homes with them when they move?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- What might the angels have said as they saw the people making God’s beautiful home?
- God wants to live with you, too—where?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that He wants to live in your heart and stay close to you all day, every day.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Go for a walk outside, if you can. Try to find animal homes. Where might worms, ants, spiders, or birds live? What is special about their homes? Look in books or online if inside.
