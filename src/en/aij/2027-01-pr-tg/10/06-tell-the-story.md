**Big Idea:** My God teaches me how to show love and respect for others.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use the following props: rope/string, family photo, drum, treasure box.
- Six large **pieces of paper** stuck to different areas of your room area with a large heart drawn on them. Children will have attached their **large numbers** close by. **Post-it notes, pens, marker, star stickers** (or draw stars).
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites stood in reverent awe before their mighty God at Mount Sinai. Their ears and hearts were wide open as God’s voice boomed out from the cloud and rolled across the plains.

God had already shared His first four commandments that told the people important ways to show love and respect for God. Now it was time for God to teach them how to love and respect each other so that they could live together happily and peacefully.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite the children to follow you to the six hearts around your room/area. Ask them to bring your class Bible to discover what God said.)

**Heart 5**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:12.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think:**
	- ^[**What does this mean?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})
	- ^[**What’s the promise in this commandment?** (You’ll live a happy life.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})
- **Explain:** To honor means to love and respect, but also to obey, for Ephesians 6:1 says, “Children, obey your parents in the Lord, for this is right.” God wants all parents to patiently teach their children how to grow up to be kind and respectful men and women. Parents may sometimes make mistakes, but God expects you to show love and respect your parents by following their instructions. If you have learned to do this, it will be easier for you to obey the laws of the land as you grow older. It will be easier to follow God’s wise advice too.
- **Apply: ^[What does this commandment look like in action?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}})** (Invite the children to write their thoughts on Post-it notes and stick them on the heart. Read and affirm their ideas. Challenge them to put these ideas into action this week, remembering God’s promise that they will be blessed when they honor their parents.)

**Heart 6**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:13.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think:**
	- ^[**How might you say this in the positive?** (So you should . . . respect the lives of others, etc.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**Why do you think this is so important to God? Why is it important for us?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Explain:** All life comes from God; He is the only one powerful enough to give life. God wants us to live happily together and never take His precious gift of life away from ourselves or anyone else. Thoughts and feelings of hatred can destroy our love for others, which can then lead to actions that cause hurt, pain, and even death. Then they can lead to actions that cause harm. God’s plan for a happy world is that we respect others and regard their lives as precious.
- **Apply:** ^[**What can we do when feelings of hatred start or our tempers flare?** (Move away from the situation or people that make us angry or feel these strong emotions. We can also pray, because God will help us with our emotions. When we ask Him for forgiveness, He will hear us and forgive and can change us so that we can respect others--even those who treat us badly.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) ^[(With the assistance of helpers, invite the children to place their hand on the poster heart to be traced around, as a reminder.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**Heart 7**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:14.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think: ^[Why do you think God wants a husband and wife to be loving and faithful to each other?]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})**
- **Explain:** It was always God’s plan for children to grow up in families with both a mother and father who are loving, respectful, and faithful to each other for their whole lives. The Bible tells us that “the Lord your God, He is God, the faithful God” (Deuteronomy 7:9), so He knows how to help moms and dads be faithful to one another too.
- **Apply:** ^[**How might you be a faithful friend this week?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) ^[(Invite children to place a star sticker or draw a star on the heart as they answer.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**Heart 8**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:15.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think:**
	- ^[**How might you put this in the positive?** (So you should . . . respect the belongings of others, etc.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**Why do you think this is so important to God? Why is it important to us?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Explain:** For people to live together peacefully, it is important that everyone respects what belongs to others. Taking something that belongs to another person is not being fair, honest, or trustworthy. It hurts people. God would never, ever steal from us! So He tells us not to steal from others, either.
- **Apply:** Brainstorm some ways to show respect for people’s property, and write the answers in the heart.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(PAUSE: Walk from heart to heart, pausing between each, and ask:) ^[**Sometimes we will make mistakes and break God’s precious commandments. When we do this, what can we do? What does God do?** (We can come to Jesus and ask for His forgiveness and asl Him to grow and change us to be like Him.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) (Read 1 John 1:9 to the children if time allows.)

**Heart 9**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:16.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think:**
	- ^[**What does this mean?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**How might you put it in the positive?** (So you should . . . always tell the truth, etc.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**Why do you think this is so important to God? Why is it important to us?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Explain:** God’s Word also says the same thing as this commandment another way: “Do not lie to one another” (Colossians 3:9). As well as telling a lie, we can lie by exaggerating, keeping silent when the truth should be told, or giving an answer that isn’t honest, but rather deceitful. When we lie about something we have or haven’t done, other people will not trust us. This hurts us and our relationships. When we lie about others, we hurt them and their reputation (what others think of them). How much happier everyone would be if we always told the truth! ^[(Emphasize how very important it is to always tell the truth, no matter what. Explain that the truth always comes out, whether it’s sooner or later.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Apply:** Write BE HONEST in big letters in your heart. (Invite the children to form pairs and act out a scenario in which they are tempted to lie but choose to respect the other person and be honest.)

**Heart 10**

- **Read:** ^[(Ask a child to read Exodus 20:17.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Think:**
	- ^[**What does this mean to you?** (Can anyone explain what it means to covet?)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**How might you put it in the positive?** (So you should . . . be happy with what you hav, etc.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
	- ^[**Why do you think this is so important to God? Why is it important to us?**]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- **Explain:** Wanting something that belongs to someone else is coveting. We might covet someone’s things (toys, clothes, sporting goods, etc.) or someone’s popularity, their talents, or the things they get to do that you want to do. Wanting other people’s things is not a happy way to live. Ecclesiastes 4:6 tells us, “I say it is better to be content with what little you have. Otherwise, you will always be struggling for more. That is like chasing the wind” (ICB). What a waste of time that is!
- **Apply:** ^[(Write BE CONTENT in the poster heart.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Being grateful for the things we do have is a good “thought filter” that helps us be positive and content. When we covet what someone has, those things can take over our thoughts and become like an idol to us. ^[► (Invite the children to do the grateful clap they did a few weeks earlier. Children stand in a circle with palms up, one above and the other below the person next to them. Children are to pass on a clap and say something they are thankful to God for. Emphasize how very important it is to always tell the truth, no matter what. Explain that the truth always comes out, whether it’s sooner or later.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
