God spoke the third commandment. What was it? **Read Exodus 20:7.**

A man from Texas, in the United States, stopped by the side of the road and looked through the pile of dirty trash that had been dumped there. Something caught his eye. What was that? He looked again and pushed aside some smelly old food. Then he saw it. An old violin.

He bent down and picked up the violin. He brushed off the dust, food, and other trash that had stuck to it. This will be good, he thought. _H’mm, my wife has a broken violin. I’ll pull this one apart and use parts of it to fix my wife’s violin._

He drove down the road and then had another idea. _I should find out how much this old violin is worth_, he thought. So he stopped at a special shop and showed the old violin to the shopkeeper. Oh, what a surprise! It wasn’t trash—it was a very precious and very famous violin. It was actually a 1922 Giuseppe Pedrazzini violin. It had been beautifully made by one of the best violin makers to sound very, very beautiful. Then the shopkeeper told him something amazing. The violin was worth more than US$50,000! That’s lots and lots of money!

If the original owner of the violin had realized how valuable it was, there’s no way they would have thrown it away!

God is more special than all the treasures in the entire world put together. The Bible says, “You must respect the glorious and wonderful name of the Lord your God” (Deuteronomy 28:58, ICB).

Sometimes people use God’s name without any care, in the same way the violin was thrown away. They say God’s precious name carelessly or rudely when they are talking. God asks us to speak His name only with thoughtfulness. Because we love God, what we say and how we talk should always show others what God is like. Our words should show we treasure and love Him as the holy King of the universe.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever had a treasure/toy that someone was careless with? How did you feel?
