Jesus walked calmly into the dark room toward Jairus’ daughter. No longer alive, her body lay still and unmoving.

Jesus looked at her with a love that was bigger than all the oceans. He reached out and wrapped His big, strong hand around her small, cold hand. Then, with power, Jesus said, “Little girl, arise” (Luke 8:54).

Instantly her heart began beating again. Her eyes opened. Her lips smiled. Jesus helped her to stand up onto her feet.

Her mother and father joyfully picked her up in their arms to give her the biggest hug! Tears of happiness spilled down their cheeks. Their daughter, who was dead, was now alive!

One day, not too far away, Jesus will come back to earth. He will speak words of life to those who have died. “Awake, you who sleep. Arise from the dead” (Ephesians 5:14). God’s children who “sleep in the dust of the earth shall awake . . . to everlasting life” (Daniel 12:2).

Isn’t it wonderful that Jesus has power over death? He can do anything. Jesus will one day soon wake up everyone who died loving Him. We will live together with Him, forever.
