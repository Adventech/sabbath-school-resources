**What you need:**

Plastic bottle with screw-on lid, water, blue food coloring, cooking oil, plastic plate, fish stickers, craft glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1a.png)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut a sailboat shape out of a plastic plate for each child.

**Create it Together:**

1\. Help children pour water into the bottle so that it is about half full, then add a couple of drops of blue food coloring.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1b.png)

2\. Help children to pour oil into the bottle so that the bottle is about ¾ full.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1c.png)

3\. Drop the plastic boat inside the bottle. Place glue on the inside of the lid, and screw the lid onto the bottle tightly.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1d.png)

4\. Decorate the bottle with fish stickers.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1e.png)

5\. Turn the bottle side to side to create a “storm” and see how the boat floats!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/01/1f.png)
