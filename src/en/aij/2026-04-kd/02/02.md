Right next to the lapping water of the Sea of Galilee, where waves curled on windy days, was a little town called Capernaum. Dark-stone houses huddled together, not too far from the water’s edge.

In one of the larger dark-stone houses lived a nobleman. He was rich and worked for the king. He stood tall and wore special clothes. To someone walking past, it looked like everything was going very well for him. But things weren’t going well at all!

What was wrong? **Read John 4:46.**

In his dark, stone house, in a quiet bedroom, lay his weak, sick son. His little boy was so sick and tired he could barely open his eyes. He was very close to dying.

The nobleman didn’t want his son to die! What could he do?

Then he heard some exciting news from some friends who had just returned home from Jerusalem. They told him about Jesus, who had been healing the sick and doing great miracles. And then they told him the best part of all—Jesus was in Galilee! He was just an afternoon’s walk away in Cana.

Jesus is so near, thought the nobleman. He would go to Jesus and ask Him to save his son! He believed that Jesus could.

Jesus is always near to us—we can talk to Him whenever we pray. You can be sure that He wants to help you too. The Bible says, “Lord God, you are my hope” (Psalm 71:5, ICB).
