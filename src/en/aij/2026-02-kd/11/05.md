The palace echoed with whispers. It was such exciting news. Governor Joseph’s brothers were here in Egypt! The whispers spread from the servants to the soldiers. Soon the news was whispered to the king.

A big grin grew on Pharaoh’s face. He really wanted to do something kind for his friend and helper Joseph. This was his chance!

He told his servants to pack gifts for Joseph’s brothers. The servants brought out a long line of wagons and packed them full of food, silver, and clothes.

Pharaoh said to Joseph, “Tell them to bring their father and their families back here to me. I will give them the best land in Egypt” (Genesis 45:18, ICB).

What else would Pharaoh give them? **Read Genesis 45:20.** Such kindness and generosity!

Finally the brothers arrived home with their wagons of gifts. They raced to Father Jacob’s tent. Excitedly they told him, “Joseph is still alive. He is the ruler over all the land of Egypt” (Genesis 45:26, ICB).

Jacob was so surprised he felt like his heart stopped beating! He thought Joseph was dead. But no, he was alive, and a ruler in Egypt!

With a big, deep breath the brothers told their father the truth. They had sold Joseph as a slave and told him a big lie. They were very sorry. They asked Father Jacob to forgive their lies and trickery. Jacob listened to their words, and he looked at their sad faces. He saw the wagons filled with gifts. God had been with Joseph this whole time and had blessed his life. God had made “all things work together for good” (Romans 8:28).

In that moment Father Jacob forgave his sons for what they had done.
