Every time the rain clouds gathered, God knew that subsequent generations after Noah would tremble with fear, anticipating another worldwide flood. So in His tender mercy God chose to paint a beautiful rainbow in the sky as a continual reminder of His covenant of care. Still today we can look up at the rainbow and be assured of God’s unfailing love, which has stood the test of time.

Life is full of constant changes and uncertainties. Yet God assures us that no matter the storms we face today or the fears we have for tomorrow, there is one thing that will never change: His unfailing love and kindness. “ ‘For the mountains shall depart and the hills be removed, but My kindness shall not depart from you, nor shall My covenant of peace be removed,’ says the Lord, who has mercy on you” _(Isaiah 54:10)_.

We can experience so much joy and peace when we look to His promises and rest in His unchanging love. May you model to your child just what that peace looks like. May you use the rainbow as a constant cue to speak out loud that God keeps His promises; that His love never changes.

“As we look at the promises of God we find comfort and hope and joy, for they speak to us the words of the Infinite One. Properly to appreciate these precious promises we should study them carefully, examining them in detail. How much joy we might bring into life, how much goodness into the character, if we would but make these promises our own. . . . The unfailing promises of God will keep your heart in perfect peace” _(Ellen G. White, My Life Today, p. 338)_.
