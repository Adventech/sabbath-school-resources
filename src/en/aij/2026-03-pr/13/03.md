Jesus strolled into Peter’s house and sat down wearily. But His rest didn’t last for long. With a worried look on his face, Peter came and told Jesus that his wife’s mother was sick with a very high fever. Her body felt hot, as if it were burning up. She was too sick to do anything except lie still in her bed.

Jesus knew exactly what to do. He went straight to the sick woman’s bedside and gently held her hand. “He stood very close to her and commanded the fever to leave. It left her immediately, and she got up and began serving them” (Luke 4:39, ICB).

At Jesus’ command the fever left: no headache; no aching muscles; no hot, burning body. What a wonderful blessing!

Imagine the happiness and praise that filled Peter’s house. Jesus had healed many people in the streets and on the hillsides, but now He had healed someone they loved very much, in Peter’s very own family home.

Jesus blesses us with healing too. Sometimes He performs a miracle to heal us. At other times He heals us over time. When we are sick with a fever, our mom may put a cold cloth on our forehead to help cool us down, or the doctor may give us medicine to take. These are Jesus’ ways of helping our body to heal as we rest. This kind of healing may not seem like a miracle, but God is just as much at work in it. Peter’s mother-in-law was healed quickly, while we may be healed slowly. But both kinds of healing are wonderful blessings.

What might we say to God when He provides a way of healing for us and others? **Read Psalm 95:1.** You can also praise God by saying, “The Lord has done great things for us, and we are glad” (Psalm 126:3).
