**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. He protects me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Whenever angels are mentioned, the children can wave their **angel sticks**.
- Lay the **household items** (wooden bowls, pots, cloths, brushes, and spoons) around the class and have the children help to gather them up and put them into a **travel bag** or **pillowcase** when Mary and Joseph prepare to travel. Then unpack when they arrive in Egypt and Nazareth.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 4 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) King Herod stomped up and down the palace hall with an angry scowl. Where were the Wise Men? It had been many days since they had come to Jerusalem. Herod had invited the men to his palace, and they had asked him, “Where is He who has been born King of the Jews?” (Matthew 2:2).

Just thinking about their question made him angry! There was only one king in this land, and his name was King Herod! No baby king would ever take his throne!

Hiding his anger, King Herod had told the Wise Men, “Go and search carefully for the young Child, and when you have found Him, bring back word to me, that I may come and worship Him also” (Matthew 2:8).

King Herod didn’t really want to worship Jesus. It was a trap! He wanted to find this young King and get rid of Him. Oh, no!

But the Wise Men had not returned. King Herod frowned and stomped some more. Where were they?

God knew exactly where the Wise Men were! He was keeping them—and little Jesus—very safe. God sent the Wise Men a dream. They should not go back to see King Herod! Quickly and quietly the Wise Men snuck out of Bethlehem and took a different road home.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One night God also sent Joseph a special dream. In the dream an angel ^[(wave angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) talked to him. The angel said, “Get up! Take the child and His mother and escape to Egypt. Herod will start looking for the child to kill him” (Matthew 2:13, ICB).

Joseph woke Mary up and told her about the dream. God would protect them. He wanted to keep them safe from wicked King Herod.

As quiet as mice they collected their things. ^[(Children to help you pack up the household items into a travel bag.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They picked up sleepy toddler Jesus and hurried down the road. It was still dark. But they didn’t need to worry. God and His angels ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) would protect them.

It wasn’t long before soldiers marched through the town of Bethlehem. They banged on every door . . . looking . . . looking everywhere. King Herod had ordered his soldiers to find the young King, who would be “two years old and under” (Matthew 2:16).

But Jesus had already left Bethlehem. He was cuddled safely in his mother’s arms, on the road to faraway Egypt.

Mary, Joseph, and little Jesus lived safely in Egypt ^[(unpack bag)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Jesus grew to be a boy. Then one night Joseph heard the angel ^[(wave angel)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) in his dream, just as before. “Take the young Child and His mother, and go to the land of Israel” (Matthew 2:20). The angel told Joseph that King Herod had died. They didn’t need to hide anymore!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Once again they packed their bags ^[(pack up bag)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and loaded the donkey. But this time things were different. There was no need to sneak away quietly in the middle of the night. No, they could travel safely in the daytime under the bright sun.

Along the way, God gave Joseph another dream. God would guide them and keep them safe! In the dream God told Joseph to go back to Nazareth, where Mary and Joseph had once lived. So that is exactly what they did. Mary and Joseph were so thankful to God for guiding and protecting them. God sent His angels ^[(wave angels)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) to look after them wherever they went.

God will always look after you too! “For He shall give His angels charge over you, to keep you in all your ways” (Psalm 91:11).
