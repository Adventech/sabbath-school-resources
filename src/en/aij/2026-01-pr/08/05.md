Rising up from their reverent worship, Noah and his family now took the time to take a good look around. Shocked by what they saw, they sadly shook their heads. Gone was the beautiful world they had known before the Flood. In its place was a rough and barren landscape.

The raging waters had left the mountains jagged and bare and the land scattered with tree trunks that had snapped like matchsticks. Beautiful precious metals such as gold and silver were now buried deep under rocks and dirt. They would never sparkle on the surface of the earth again, as they had done in many places before.

Gazing at the ugliness surrounding them, their sad hearts now understood more of the far-reaching results of wickedness. First it had infected people’s hearts, and now it left scars on their world.

God saw their sadness and He understood, for He must have felt it too. Washing the world clean of wickedness was a very loving thing to do; but even good things sometimes come with sadness. Not wanting them to sit with their sorrow, God did something special. What did God do? Read Genesis 9:1.

What a wonderful thing God did! God’s blessing meant they had peace, joy, and a new start—because they had been faithful to Him. Noah knew what to do. With God’s blessing upon them, they needed to have families of precious children, just as God had said. They needed to begin building meaningful lives in their changed world. It wouldn’t be easy, but if they waited upon the Lord, He would renew their strength (Isaiah 40:31).
