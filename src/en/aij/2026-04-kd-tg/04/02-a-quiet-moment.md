**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It was another dark night, and again the disciples found themselves in a scary place—in another storm. Once again they fought the elements. Once again they floundered against impossible odds. This time Jesus wasn’t in the boat. But He had them firmly in His sights, just as He always does for each of us. As the storm raged, He walked over the stormy waves to be with them. Can you imagine the sight? Jesus, our rescuer, walking calmly above the waves.

Seeing Jesus, Peter was amazed! His fear melted, and courage grew. Putting all his faith in Jesus, Peter was willing to do the impossible. And so he bravely stepped out into the storm—toward Jesus.

With our eyes on Jesus, He can also help us to do what seems impossible. We can have confidence in the One who has power over all things, despite whatever challenges we may face. Whether it’s being brave to stand for the right when those around us don’t, or being bold about sharing our faith in Him with family, friends, or colleagues, we can have full assurance that Jesus can do anything. The storm around us—the hurt, uncertainty, struggle, and strife of this world—may continue. But we can know that Jesus is always above the storm.

Jesus invites you, and the children in your Sabbath School class, to “come.” Come closer. Leave your fear behind and walk boldy toward Him, giving Him all parts of your life. “Fear not, for I have redeemed you; I have called you by your name; You are Mine. When you pass through the waters, I will be with you; and through the rivers, they shall not overflow you. When you walk through the fire, you shall not be burned, nor shall the flame scorch you. For I am the Lord your God, the Holy One of Israel, your Savior” (Isaiah 43:1-3).
