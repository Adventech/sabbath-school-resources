Hello, friend!

Have you ever wondered what heaven is like? Or how the Holy Spirit works in your life? Well, get ready, because in these pages you will live a great adventure alongside Luca, a very curious boy, and his friend, the angel Orel.

Together they will walk through the New Jerusalem, meet Bible characters such as Moses, Joshua, David, Stephen, Paul, and even John the Baptist. Each of them will tell how the Holy Spirit was unleashed in special moments of their lives. And the best part is that you will discover that this same Spirit also wants to be unleashed in you!

Sometimes, the unleashed Spirit gives us extraordinary abilities; other times He helps us to be courageous leaders or invites us to read and understand the Bible. He also motivates us to care for our health, strengthens us when we suffer, restores us when we fail, and always inspires us to fulfill the mission of sharing Jesus.

In every chapter you will find fun activities: painting, drawing, writing phrases, praying, and even doing small challenges to share with your family and friends. All of this so that you don’t just read a story, but live it!

So open your eyes wide, set your heart ready, and join Luca and Orel on this journey. They will show you that the Holy Spirit is not a distant mystery, but a close friend who wants to be with you every day and be powerfully unleashed in your life.

Wow! Are you ready? Then…

Let’s get to work!
