[[Revelation 7:1-3]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Chapter 6 ended with a question: “Who can stand” in the great day of Christ’s wrath against evil oppressors? Chapter 7 immediately gives us the answer—but not by opening the seventh seal.
- Instead, there is a break in the progression of seal opening, for Christ in mercy is wanting to show how anyone who wishes can avoid destruction. He does not want anyone on earth to be lost! He instead wants every person possible to receive His free gift of salvation.
- To show who has chosen Him at the end of time, God offers “the seal of the living God.”
- What is this seal? We will discuss this more in chapters 13 and 14—and to prepare for that discussion, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r07/)**.

[[Revelation 7:4-8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The 144,000 of verse 4 and the 12 tribes of Israel in verses 5-8 are clearly symbolic. They do not represent the actual number of those who will enter Christ’s kingdom, nor will the blood descendants of Israel be the only ones eligible for membership in it.
- We know this because already in the letters to the seven churches it referred to followers of Christ as those “who say they are Jews and are not” (Rev. 2:9 and 3:9). Also, here “Israel” symbolizes the true followers of Jesus, which may include anyone, whether a literal Jew or not.
- We will understand more about the 144,000 when we study chapter 14.

[[Revelation 7:9-17]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The promises given here to this great and innumerable multitude of human beings are fantastic: No more hunger or thirst, nor blazing heat, and no more tears.
- Christ’s kingdom of justice, goodness, and love will be worth being a part of!
