God allowed Lucifer to gossip and tell lies about Him until the whispering became so loud that it turned into open rebellion. God allowed this because He is love. He didn’t make robots. He created beings who could choose to love and serve Him or choose to go their own way.

All of heaven took sides. Thousands of angels chose Lucifer’s side. He tricked a third of all the angels. The other two thirds were loyal to God and stood waiting to see what would happen.

Then a war began in heaven. We don’t exactly know what kind of war it was, but in the end God won the war because He is wise, strong, and mighty. What happened to Lucifer? **Read Isaiah 14:12 and Revelation 12:7-9.**

What a sad way for things to end for God’s most glorious angel. When Lucifer realized he had lost the war, he openly declared his rebellion against God’s law and His government. Lucifer had become Satan.

That was a sad, sad day for all of heaven. God had done everything possible to save Lucifer, but the seed of sin had grown into pride so big and ugly that he just didn’t want to be rescued and forgiven.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _How do you think God’s angels felt when they saw their friends choose to leave heaven with Lucifer?_
- _What helps you to make good choices, even when your friends do something that is wrong?_

Because God loves us so much, He uses true, strong, and loving words to help us choose His way every day.
