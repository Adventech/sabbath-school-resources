**Opening and Focus Activities**

Write the **Big Idea** on a piece of paper for children to later copy onto your whiteboard/display poster. Source whiteboard markers/other markers for children to decorate the Big Idea.

Print a new **Offering Chart** and prepare **Mission Fast Fact**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source a **woven basket, blue sheet, baby doll, tiara/princess clothes, crown.**

Source **three large opaque bags** to hold the mystery items for the Object Lesson. Marathon: **running shoes, sunglasses, hat, water bottle, ID number**, etc. Summer holiday: **swimsuit, sunscreen, sunglasses, hat, flip-flops, towel, insect repellent, water bottle**, etc. Snow skiing: **skis** (make from cardboard), **jacket, helmet, goggles/sunglasses, gloves**, etc. Alternatively, choose an activity that is suitable for your culture and climate, and find simple items as props. This may include: hiking, bike riding, camping, fishing, birdwatching, canoeing, or playing a popular sport.
