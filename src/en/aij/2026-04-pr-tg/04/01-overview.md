**Lesson Summary**

Awed by Jesus’ miracles, the disciples wanted to make Him king. Jesus had other plans. He sent them in a boat to cross the lake, while He went up a hillside to pray. While the disciples crossed the sea, a fierce storm blew in. Jesus walked out on the water to rescue them, again doing the impossible. The disciples were first afraid, then amazed. Jesus invited Peter to walk out to Him, and keeping his eyes on Jesus, Peter did the impossible too! But when Peter took his eyes off Jesus, he started to sink. Jesus reached out and saved him. When they stepped into the boat, the storm stopped.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus can do what is impossible for us to do for ourselves.
- **Feel** grateful that Jesus wants to help us in difficult situations.
- **Respond** by choosing to keep our eyes focused on Jesus.
