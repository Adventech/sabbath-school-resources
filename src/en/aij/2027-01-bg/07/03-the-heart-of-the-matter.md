Have you ever had the task of packing food for a family camping trip? It can require a lot of thought and careful planning to prepare enough food, even just for a few days. Imagine being Noah, given the enormous task of packing food for people and every kind of animal, not just for a few days, but a whole year! It is estimated that there could have been more than 6,700 animals on the ark. That’s a lot of food! But just as God provided instructions for the building of the ark, He would provide and preserve the food they needed to survive.

Throughout the Bible we see many instances in which God provides for His people. God led Joseph to store up grain in the years leading up to a famine in Egypt. When the Israelites wandered in the desert for 40 years, God rained down manna every morning for them to eat. God provided food for Elijah, first through the ravens, and then through a woman whose flour and oil never ran out. Then there were the miracles of Jesus, who, with just a handful of loaves and fish, fed the crowds of 5,000 and 4,000. Time and again, God shows Himself to be the Great Provider.

No wonder Jesus said, “Do not worry about your life, what you will eat or what you will drink; nor about your body, what you will put on. Is not life more than food and the body more than clothing? Look at the birds of the air, for they neither sow nor reap nor gather into barns; yet your heavenly Father feeds them. Are you not of more value than they?” _(Matthew 6:25, 26)_.

Come what may, God will sustain you and provide for you and your little ones. He promises to meet all your needs, whether big or small.
