Jesus looked down at the five barley loaves and two small fish. He smiled again at the boy who had happily given it to Him. Then looking up to heaven, Jesus prayed. He thanked God for the little basket of food that the boy had shared with Him.

Everyone listened quietly. Then “he divided the bread and gave it to his followers for them to give to the people. Then he divided the two fish among them all” (Mark 6:41, ICB).

Jesus passed the pieces to His disciples, and the disciples passed the pieces out to the crowd. Some for you, some for you, and some for you too!

It was then that something amazing began to happen. Something completely unbelievable. The bread and fish kept coming! No matter how much food the disciples handed out, there was always more to share. There was more than they could eat, and more than they could hold in their hands.

Hooray! The disciples laughed, and the crowds smiled. Jesus was feeding thousands of hungry people from just five loaves and two small fish! Happily they all ate their food and talked excitedly about the miracle.

What does God promise us in **Psalm 34:10**?
