Lot had chosen to live on the beautiful land near the wicked city Sodom. Perhaps he thought _I’ll just put my tent over here far away from the city. I don’t want to be too close. I think we’re far enough away from all those bad choices._

But it wasn’t long before Lot started to think: _Hmm. Sodom is not far away. I can go there, but I won’t go there very often._

Then later: _Oh, let’s go to Sodom and have some fun!_

Then later: _It’s not too bad here in Sodom; I have some good friends here now._

Then later: _Oh, let’s just move here to Sodom. They have really nice houses with walls and a roof—that’s so much better than living in a tent! Life will be so nice and cozy._

Then later: _Oh, Sodom’s not really a bad place: it’s a good place! I don’t know what I was thinking before._

As days turned into weeks, and weeks turned into months, Lot slowly changed. The people around him made terrible choices and treated others very badly. This wasn’t a good place for Lot!

It’s hard to make good choices when everyone around you is making bad choices. The Bible tells us that “bad friends will ruin good habits” (1 Corinthians 15:33, ICB).

The Bible also tells us how we can be wise. What does it say? Read Proverbs 22:3.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you think Lot was being wise or foolish? What makes you say that?
- Are you choosing good friends? Are you helping your friends make good choices?
