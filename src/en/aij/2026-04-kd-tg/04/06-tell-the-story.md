**Big Idea:** Jesus can do anything. He makes me brave.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Use a **sandbox** as a scene for the story, with **blue sand/cellophane** to represent Lake Galilee.
- A **boat**, made out of a cut-down tissue box or similar
- **Peg people** for Jesus and Peter and disciples, or a **felt set**, if available
- Use a **saltshaker** filled with water to make “rain” or use a spray bottle.
- Source some audio sound effects for a storm and play this as you tell the story.
- Place a few peg people as disciples on the edge of the “land” in the sandbox and on the boat at the edge of the “water” to start the story.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 2 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

- A wild storm blew over the lake (storm noises, “rain”).

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The water was cool on their feet as the disciples stepped into the lake. They waded out to their boat on the Sea of Galilee and climbed aboard. ^[(Put peg people in boat on “lake.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

It had been a wonderful day! They had watched Jesus teach a huge crowd of people on the mountainside. Then He had given them a mighty miracle. He had fed more than 5,000 people lunch from just five small loaves of bread and two fish.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Now the sky was bright with stars. The disciples pushed their boat out into the lake and set sail for the other side. Jesus had asked them to go. He wanted to stay, and said goodbye to the crowd.

Jesus waved goodbye to the people. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then He walked up the hill to find a quiet place to pray. He needed to spend time talking with God. ^[(Fold hands and bow head for a quiet moment of prayer.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Down on the lake a strong wind began to blow. The wind stirred up the water. Little ripples turned into huge, crashing waves. The disciples clung to their boat as it rocked up and down and went from side to side. They were scared! ^[(Start storm noises, “rain.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The disciples were in trouble, but they weren’t alone. Up on the hillside Jesus had kept His eyes on His friends in their little boat. They didn’t need to worry.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The disciples looked up and saw something very strange in the moonlight. It was a person walking on the wild waves! ^[(Jesus walks on water.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

People can’t walk on water, they thought. Their eyes opened wide. Their hands began to tremble, and they started to scream!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Jesus spoke to them, saying, ‘Be of good cheer! It is I; do not be afraid’ ” (Matthew 14:27).

Instantly the disciples felt peace. Jesus was there.

Peter, especially, wanted to jump for joy! Seeing Jesus helped him feel big and brave. He called out through the noisy storm, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water” (Matthew 14:28).

“Come,” Jesus said (Matthew 14:29).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Brave Peter put one foot over the side of the boat, and then the other. He was standing on the water! ^[(Peter on water.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Looking at Jesus, Peter took one step, and then another. He wasn’t sinking down into the water. Somehow his feet were walking on top of the watery waves, just as Jesus was.

But then he had a thought. _I wonder what the disciples think of me, back in the boat? They must think I’m pretty good!_ Peter looked back at his friends.

For a moment he lost sight of Jesus. All he could see were huge waves all around him. Peter didn’t feel brave anymore. He felt alone and frightened. Instantly he began to sink.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Reaching up his hand, Peter cried out, “Lord, save me!” (Matthew 14:30).

Immediately Jesus pulled Peter out of the water to be by His side. “O you of little faith, why did you doubt?” Jesus asked him (Matthew 14:31). Together they walked side by side and hand in hand across the water, back to the boat.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As Jesus stepped into the boat with Peter, the storm completely stopped. The wild wind disappeared. The water became flat and calm. ^[(Stop storm noises and “rain.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Peter and all the disciples were amazed. “Truly You are the Son of God,” they said (Matthew 14:33).

- ^[Invite the children to find a partner. Imagine one of you is Jesus and one is Peter. Hold on to each other and pretend you are walking on the water, back to the boat. What a special walk for Peter!]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})
