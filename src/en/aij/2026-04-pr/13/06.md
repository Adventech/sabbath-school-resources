Mary and Martha rushed to their brother and threw their arms around him. He was alive! Their good Friend Jesus had done the impossible! They thanked Jesus through tears of joy. What a wonderful gift from God.

While the sisters continued to hug Lazarus, the disciples praised God along with everyone else in the crowd. Now they understood why Jesus hadn’t rushed to heal Lazarus. They realized His special plan had shown everyone that He really truly is the Son of God. And that He has the power to resurrect those who have died.

This story helps us to understand and believe in the resurrection too. Romans 6:23 says, “For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Jesus Christ our Lord.” We don’t have to worry about death on this earth, because Jesus has promised to resurrect and give eternal life to those who love Him.

His followers may die and be “asleep” for two months, two years, or 2,000 years; it makes no difference to Jesus. God’s promise is always true: “Behold, O My people, I will open your graves and cause you to come up from your graves” (Ezekiel 37:12).

Other Bible verses also teach the resurrection truth:

“So I rejoice, and I am glad. Even my body has hope. This is because you will not leave me in the grave” (Psalm 16:9, 10, ICB).

“Your people have died, but they will live again. Their bodies will rise from death. . . . The ground will give birth to the dead buried in it” (Isaiah 26:19, ICB).
