**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some fresh cut-up or bite-sized fruit. Invite the children to take a piece. Talk about how good it is for us, and how the fruit grows from a tiny seed.

Invite the children to stand up. Ask the questions below and encourage the children to do the actions:

- **Can you make tiny fruit seeds grow into a tree? (Can you clap your hands?)**
- **Can you make the sun shine on a cloudy day? (Can you touch your toes?)**
- **Can you make the stars twinkle at night? (Can you hop on one leg?)**
- **Can you send rain in little drops and big showers? (Can you blink your eyes?)**
- **Can you make the wind blow? (Can you close your eyes and touch your nose?)**
- **Can you make a rainbow in the sky? (Can you wave goodbye?)**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite the children to sit back down.

- **What things couldn’t you do?**
- **Who can do all these things?** (God!)

**Teaching Point:** Nothing is too hard for God! He can do amazing things and can help us when things are hard.

Say together the **Big Idea: God is with me. He can do amazing things.**

Let’s find out what amazing things God did to help his friend Joseph—things that only God could do!

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Miracles in Prison”** in an interactive and engaging way using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **crown**, **paper chain**, and a **mirror**.

Place the **crown** on a child’s head.

- **What do you think Pharaoh, the king, learned about God?** (Prompt responses as needed. God cared about His people. God knows what will happen before it happens. God sends and explains dreams.) Invite the children to repeat after you: **God can do amazing things!**

Give the **paper chain** to two children to hold tightly between them.

- **What do you think Joseph learned about God?** (Prompt responses as needed. God cared for him, would always help him, is amazing.) Explain that even though Joseph was in prison, forgotten by many people, he still trusted God. Allow the two children to break the chain. Explain that God is a powerful helper. He had special plans for Joseph to be a leader! Invite the children to repeat after you: **God can do amazing things!**

Show the children the **mirror**. Share that we go through hard times sometimes too. Hold the mirror up to each child as they answer the following question.

- **When something is hard, what can you do?** (Pray, ask God for help, trust God.) Explain that our amazing God loves to help us. We are very special to Him. He knows what we think and dream. He knows what will happen next. We can trust God to help us.
