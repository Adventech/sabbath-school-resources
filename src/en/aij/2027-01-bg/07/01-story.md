One basket, two baskets, three baskets, and many, many more _(count with fingers)_. Up the ramp and through the great big door. Noah and his family had lots of food to store. They would be on the ark for a long, long time. God didn’t want anyone to be hungry! They’d need to store sweet juicy fruits, crunchy vegetables, grains, and seeds. God told Noah to gather food for his family and for the animals, both tiny _(squat low)_ and tall _(reach up high)_. Into the big, big boat they carried enough for all.\
God told Noah what to pack. Juicy berries, crunchy apples, and sweet mangoes filled the baskets to the top. There were bananas for the monkeys, and melons for the elephants. Don’t forget the hay for the horses or leaves for the giraffes! Peaches and plums, pumpkins and potatoes, olives and oranges would all be a yummy treat _(rub tummy and say “Mmm”)_. What would you like to eat? _(Point to child.)_

Bunnies like carrots, and pandas like bamboo. Bluebirds like seeds, and monkeys like peanuts. What do you like to eat? Noah and his family put all the food into the ark. They packed enough for everyone! Corn, squash, tomatoes, and grapes. God knew what everyone would need!\
Noah’s family thanked God for all He had made, with so many good things to eat _(praying hands)_. For pineapples, raspberries, and blueberries, picked from bushes down low. And for kumquats, avocados, and pomegranates plucked from high in the trees. But would there be food for the koala and the penguin? Yes! God made so many yummy foods—for everyone!

The ark was ready with lots of food. Soon it would rain. Who would come inside? Noah and his family and anyone else who chose to be God’s friend. Each kind of animal would have a place. There would be food for great, big animals _(reach up high)_ and little, tiny animals _(squat down low)_. Sweet, juicy, crunchy, or chewy, all God’s food was good! God would take care of them all.
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, for so many good things to eat! Thank You for giving me what I need!
