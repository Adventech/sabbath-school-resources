**Focus:** God can give me peace, like a peaceful koala.

- **Do you like to eat?**
- **Do you like to sleep?**

Koalas love to do both!

Koalas live in Australia in tall eucalyptus trees called gum trees. They have soft fur, big fluffy ears, and a leathery black nose. Their long claws and strong arms and legs make them excellent climbers.

They need to climb up very high to reach the juiciest gum leaves at the tops of the trees. These leaves are the koala’s food and water. And they are all they eat!

Koalas don’t use their sharp claws to fight. They don’t bother other animals. They are very peaceful. They gently care for each other and are mostly calm. Sometimes, though, they can make very strange noises—such as a low grunt or bellow! But mostly they softly nuzzle each other to say hello.

Koalas are kind mommies. They carry their tiny babies in their pouch on the front, then on their backs when they are bigger. They also look after little koalas who have lost their mommies.

If you see a peaceful koala in a tree, it’s most likely sleeping. Gum leaves don’t give koalas much energy, so they sleep a lot! Koalas can sleep up to 20 hours a day. That means they are awake for only about four hours each day.

When you get worried, angry, or scared, just remember the peaceful koala that God made. Ask God to give you peace like a koala. And lots of sleep too!

Say the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He gives me peace.**
