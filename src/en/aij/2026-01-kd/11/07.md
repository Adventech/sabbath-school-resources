God made so many amazing creatures. Whether you look for creatures at the beach, in the forest, or in the desert, you can find living creatures that teach us about God.

God made secret helpers in gardens for a special mission: to make flowers, fruit, and vegetables grow. Do you know what these creatures are? Here’s a clue: they have five eyes and six legs. Their wings flap so fast, some at 11,000 flaps per minute, that they make a buzzing noise when they fly. And did you know they make honey?

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd/11/image8.png)

You’ve got it: bees are God’s incredible secret agents! If you can, go outside and sit very still. See if you can see any bees near some flowers. These female bees are called worker bees. They have left their home, called a hive, to collect pollen and nectar from flowers. It’s also their job to build, clean, and protect their hive and raise baby bees. Their big family is called a colony, and their leader is called a queen bee. She has male bees who help her called drone bees.

We need to be careful around bees, because when they sting, it hurts! But bees have something very special to teach us.

Bees are called pollinators, which means that they go from flower to flower collecting nectar to bring back to the hive to make honey. Whenever they land on a flower, the pollen sticks to their legs. The bees pass the pollen from one flower to the next, like a secret agent. This is called pollination, and it is what gives flowers the food they need to be able to grow and become so beautiful. Their work of spreading pollen also means we end up with fruits and vegetables we need to survive.

Wherever we go, we can be like bees and spread God’s blessings. When we honor others, just as Abram did, we are God’s workers too. Choose to be like a bee, spreading love and kindness wherever you go.
