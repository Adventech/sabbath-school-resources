Thank you so much for being willing to teach your group of Primary children this year!

As you pray and plan from this new curriculum, may God lead you to know just how to make every Sabbath full of teachable moments that are eternally life-changing for the children.

- The main goal of this curriculum is to help your Primary students to **^[know and love Jesus and His living Word]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**, to **^[grow into His likeness]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**, and to **^[want to serve Him every day]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**.
- In all your interactions, the stories, and the group activities, remember to point the children to **^[God’s living, powerful Word]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. As you read the condensed narrative portions each week, be sure to have your Bible nearby to open so the children can read from it. Encourage the children to bring their own Bibles to Sabbath School each week.
- Encourage **^[parents and caregivers to read the lesson]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** to the children each day. Also encourage the children to **^[spend time with Jesus every day]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** (see ideas for this at the beginning of the _Primary_ Student Guide).
- Be **^[intentional about the culture you foster]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** in the classroom, helping all to have a sense of belonging. Notice those who might be on the fringes and involve them.
- Ensure that you always have at least one other **^[adult helper]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** in the room with you. They can welcome children who arrive late and assist you as needed.
- This new, global Sabbath School curriculum is written for hundreds of cultures. You will no doubt find some things that need adjusting and adapting to **^[meet the needs of the kids in front of you]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. If your group has very little knowledge, make the lessons simpler. If they know a lot, go deeper still. Look to adapt some of the activities for children with **^[special needs]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. See www.aliveinjesus.info for a training video on nurturing children with special needs. Pray for wisdom about what and how to use this curriculum, while staying true to its goals.
- The **^[Kindergarten and Primary Bible stories are the same]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**, so if you belong to a small church with combined Sabbath School classes, select the level that will best suit the knowledge and needs of your children.
- We have provided you with **^[music]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** suggestions in the program. You might also like to invite the children to choose songs and lead the singing time. Where possible, choose songs that apply to the weekly theme. If possible, display the words of the songs so that everyone can learn the songs together. Keep in mind the importance of choosing songs that share spiritual truths, as music will linger in our minds for years to come.
- Make an **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** at the beginning of each quarter from the template provided on our website. Together with the children, write weekly offering goals at the bottom of the page to encourage children to give to God’s work. Each week after the offering has been collected and counted, invite one child to color the new section to indicate the offering goal has been met. Display the Offering Chart in a prominent place in your Sabbath School room.

- You may like to make special mention of **^[birthdays]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** by singing a birthday song and giving a small gift to the child. If appropriate in your culture, encourage children to bring a Birthday-Thank Offering for your mission project. Also, be sure to acknowledge **^[visitors]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** and make them feel welcome in your Sabbath School. You’ll find a “Happy Birthday” song and a “Visitor Song” on the Alive in Jesus website.
- Establish a healthy understanding of **^[prayer]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. Model how special it is to talk with God as a friend through your own prayers, encouraging children to pray from their heart with reverence. Kneel for the opening prayer, explaining that this shows God we are humble.
- Encourage children to learn their **^[weekly memory verse]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. You can share various methods of doing memorization with them and point them to the weekly **^[Scripture Song]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**, which will help them to remember the verse. Please also spend a few moments reflecting on the meaning of each memory verse by discussing the questions each week. You may like to play the quarterly **^[Scripture Song playlist]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** as the children arrive, as they do their crafts, or as they leave Sabbath School, to reinforce the memory verses each quarter.
- Display the Alive in Jesus quarterly **^[mural]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})** at the front of your classroom. This is an important visual teaching tool and will bring color and life to your Sabbath School room.
- All resources can be found on the **^[Alive in Jesus website]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**, www.aliveinjesus.info, including crafts, music, training videos, podcasts, news and updates, and general information about Alive in Jesus.
- The Primary children will look to you as one of the most important adults in their lives, so **^[make your personal daily abiding with Jesus a priority]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58b0e3"}}})**. Pray for these children and the precious hour you have together each Sabbath morning.

Jesus said, “I must preach the kingdom of God . . . for this purpose I have been sent” (Luke 4:43). He has called you, and He will equip you, as you teach, nurture, guide, and love the Primary children in your care this year.
