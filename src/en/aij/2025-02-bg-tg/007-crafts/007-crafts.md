```=Week 1: Fishing With Jesus

Draw and cut out a 3-centimeter frame from a piece of cardstock. Children can decorate the front of the frame with sea-themed stickers. Adults can stick the frame onto clear contact. On a yellow piece of paper, trace around the child’s feet and cut around it. Children can stick their footprints onto the sticky backing, then sprinkle sand or cornmeal around it. Display their picture as a reminder of how the disciples left their fishing boats to follow Jesus.
```

```=Week 2: A Coin and a Fish

Fold a green sheet of paper vertically into even thirds and glue together. Fold in half to make a V shape. Flip over and fold each end into the middle to make a W shape. Glue these sides together so you end up with two pockets. This will be the mouth of the fish puppet. (If possible, prepare this step prior to the lesson.) Children can glue the fish body onto the pocket, then glue on the fins and oval shape inside the mouth, then draw on eyes. Children can place their hand inside, opening the fish’s mouth to reveal the yellow circle “coin” inside.
```

```=Week 3: “I Can See!”

Glue the sunglasses printable onto black cardstock and cut out. Sticky-tape the top of both sunglasses together, then tape two Popsicle sticks on either side of the “see-through” glasses. Children can hold up their sunglasses and experience what it would be like to be blind, and then to see!
```

```=Week 4: Four Good Friends

Poke a hole in each corner of a square piece of felt. Thread and tie both ends of a piece of string to one side of the felt, and then repeat on the other side. Children can color and glue a picture of the paralytic onto a cardboard cylinder. Retell the story while they lower the man down on the “stretcher” and then stand him up after he is healed.
```

```=Week 5: A Little Boy’s Lunch

Cut off the top third of a brown paper bag. Write “Jesus cares for me” on the front and have children decorate with markers and stickers. Fold up the bottom corners of the bag and stick- tape to make a flat base. Tape a strip of cardboard to either side of the bag to make a handle. Cut a fish shape out of two leaves and draw small lines on the top of five round rocks. Children can carry their five loaves and two fish inside their basket, like the boy in today’s story.
```

```=Week 6: One Thankful Man

On a sheet of paper, draw around child’s hands in black marker. Draw faces on 10 sticker dots. Children can stick a face on each finger and color in. Then they can put a love heart sticker on one of the finger “people.” Share how Jesus healed 10 lepers, but only one had a thankful heart!
```

```=Week 7: The Wedding Miracle

Trace around the top of a paper cup onto a piece of blue and purple cardstock. Cut around the inside of both circles, making them slightly smaller so they fit snugly inside the cup. Glue both circles together and poke a hole in the center. Cut off one fourth of a straw and slide the long piece of straw though the hole in the circles. Tape the straw into place. Children can then decorate the cup. Retell the story, showing the blue circle facing up, then flip the straw around to show how Jesus turned the water into purple grape juice.
```

```=Week 8: Jesus Heals a Mother

Fold and cut page 2 along the lines provided. Push the cut section of the card through, so it folds on the inside. Children can glue the blanket on the front of the card and the pillows and table inside the card as shown. Stick both pictures of the mother together and glue on the pop-up inside the card (with the happy side facing out). Retell the story while children close the card to show the sick mother lying down, then open the card to reveal the healed mother serving food.
```

```=Week 9: “Peace, Be Still”

Fold a paper plate in half and cut around the inner circle of the plate. Children can glue a brown semicircle onto the plate, then glue two colored triangles onto a Popsicle stick. Tape the Popsicle stick to the back of the paper plate and write “Jesus is always with me” in black marker. Children can rock the boat from side to side, then make it stand still, while you retell the story.
```

```=Week 10: Walking on Water

Children can draw wavy lines with white chalk onto a dark-blue sheet of cardstock, using their fingers to smudge the lines. Crinkle a sheet of clear cellophane and sticky tape onto the cardstock. Adults can assist cutting out the Jesus finger puppet, and children can color. Using the craft from last week, children can reenact the story, walking Jesus across the “water” to the boat, and hearing the “splashing” of the cellophane water.
```

```=Week 11: “Get Up, Little Girl”

Fold up 2 centimeters of a piece of cardboard to make a bed head. Children can glue on a piece of felt for a “pillow” and glue the bottom edge of a piece of fabric for a “blanket.” Make the girl puppet by gluing a triangle on either side of a Popsicle stick. Glue a circle shape at the top of each triangle with a sleeping face on one side and a smiling face on the other. Then glue a piece of wool on both sides of the face for hair. Retell the story, while children put the sleeping girl in the bed, and then stand her up and flip her around.
```

```=Week 12: “Who Touched Me?”

Children can decorate an empty tissue box using stickers and colored paper (or whatever you have available). On a piece of fabric, write the name “Jesus” with a marker. Children can fill their “sensory box” with the piece of fabric and other items. With their eyes closed, they can put their hand in the box and try to touch “Jesus’ robe.”
```

```=Week 13: Deep and Wide

Print and cut out the wheel printable onto white cardstock. Children can color in the pictures and decorate. Cut out the segment on the top layer. Place both circles together as shown and poke a hole in the center. Then use a pushpin, pipe cleaner, or sandwich tie to join both circles together. Children can spin the wheel around to reveal all the different times Jesus cares for them.
```
