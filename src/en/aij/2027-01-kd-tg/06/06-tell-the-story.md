**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. I will be thankful to Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 6 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Suggested props: a **long stick** for Moses’ staff, **cup** for each child, **blue fabric** for water, **tree branch**. A **box** as Mount Sinai, with **blue fabric** hidden inside, that can be pulled out as “gushing water.”
- Prepare a **bottle of “sweet” water** that tastes pleasant, by mixing some honey with water.
- Designate an area to be the waters of Marah. Lay a blue sheet on the ground with a small tree branch propped up nearby.
- Use peg people (Moses, families) or a felt set if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Boys and girls sang as they skipped along the dusty path. Their parents sang too as they walked behind. God had saved them all from the Egyptian army. They were so thankful!

They never wanted to forget to say “Thank You” to God. They would remember God’s mighty, saving power forever!

The new land they had crossed into was hot and dry. There was no green grass or leafy trees in this desert. But God was with them. His cloud gave them shade from the hot sun and it also led them on.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) For three long days the Israelites walked across the hot desert, but they didn’t find any water. They really, really wanted a drink. It was all they thought about and talked about.

And then . . . Water! Water! Someone had spotted a spring of water! ^[(Move to blue sheet on ground.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people rushed toward the pool. Splashing their hands into the water, they happily scooped it up into their mouths.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) But . . . Yuck! The water tasted bitter. Their happy smiles disappeared. Now the Israelites were thirsty and grumpy! They began to complain.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}}
**What had they already forgotten to say to God?** ^[(Children to copy prayer hands.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

They had forgotten to say “Thank You” to God for always taking care of them. God had been so good to them already. He had sent plagues and parted the Red Sea to save them. If the people had just remembered His miracles, they would have trusted Him. Remembering would help them to be thankful.

But Moses hadn’t forgotten God. He trusted God. While the people worried and complained, Moses prayed. ^[(Children to copy prayer hands.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Then “the Lord showed him a tree” (Exodus 15:25). Moses picked up the tree and threw it into the water. ^[(Throw branch on blue fabric.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Splash! And the waters were made sweet.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people rushed to the sweet waters. They gulped and guzzled. Then they slurped and sipped some more. Oh, it was wonderful! God had given them miracle water! They were so thankful!

^[(Hand out cups and taste sweet water.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The people had one last drink of sweet water, then continued on their journey as they followed the cloud. God led them to a beautiful place called Elim, with lots of fresh water and shady palm trees. What a cool, restful place!

Even though their journey had sometimes been hard, God’s way was best.

After they rested, the cloud started to move so the people followed it. One day God’s cloud came to a stop. Here the people set up camp again. But there was one problem. There was no water.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Give us water to drink,” the Israelites moaned to Moses. Only a little time had passed, and here they were again, thirsty, grumpy, and angry at Moses for taking them into the desert.

What had they already forgotten to say to God? ^[(Children to copy prayer hands.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

They had forgotten to say “Thank You” to God. God had made their bitter water sweet. If the people remembered His miracles, it would help them to be thankful.

Moses hadn’t forgotten. While the people worried and complained, Moses prayed. ^[(Children to copy prayer hands.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God told Moses what to do and Moses obeyed. He took some elders with him and went ahead of the people to a big rock. Then he raised his staff and struck the rock with his stick. _Whack!_ ^[(Hit “rock”; blue fabric to come out.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Water burst from the rock like a waterfall. It gushed over the dry stones like a river. God had provided miracle water again! There was more than enough water for everyone in the camp.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people were so thankful. What a powerful, caring God! God hoped they would stay thankful and trust Him.
