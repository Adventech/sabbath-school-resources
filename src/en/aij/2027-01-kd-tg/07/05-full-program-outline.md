**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a picnic blanket.

- **Can you find anything else that we could take on a picnic?** (Children to find picnic basket.)
- **Would you like to go on a picnic?**

Lay the picnic blanket on the floor and invite the children to sit around it.

- **What yummy picnic food might be inside?** (Accept answers and describe some delicious foods for the children to imagine.)

Place the covered basket in the middle and invite the children to take a look inside. They may find some plates, cutlery, and cups, but no food. Pretend to be surprised and disappointed.

- **How do you feel about there being no food in the basket for our picnic?** (Do you feel like complaining?)

**Teaching Point:** It’s hard not to complain when we don’t get something we really want. In today’s story the Israelites grumbled and complained about something. They forgot to trust that God would provide everything for them, just as He provided water.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite the children back to your **picnic blanket**. Show the children the picnic basket again, but share that this time it has been packed with things that God provides for us. (A helper will have replaced the utensils with items or pictures that may include a selection of delicious **picnic food, clothing, shoes, a bottle of water, a picture of a family, a love heart, Bible/lesson, etc.)**

Invite one child at a time to choose an item out of the basket and share what it is and why it is helpful to us (like “Show and Tell”). Invite different children to comment on each item, and then have the other children say, “Thank You . . . “ After each child shares, everyone can call out, **“Thank You, God, for giving us . . .”**

- **What did God give the Israelites to eat, and why was it so special?** (Manna. It came from heaven. It was all they needed to be full and healthy.)

**Teaching Point:** In today’s story God provided food for the Israelites in amazing ways. For 40 years He would keep providing manna every day so they wouldn’t ever be hungry in the desert. He’s still providing for us. Thank You, God!

Stand and repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. He provides for all of my needs.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 59: Seeds** and share the following facts:

- From little seeds we get yummy foods, such as fruits, veggies, nuts, beans, and grains (such as rice and oats!). Without seeds we wouldn’t have most of the food we love.
- Inside every seed is a tiny plant waiting to grow. With some water, sunshine, and fresh air, the little plant pops out of the seed like a surprise and starts growing up, up, up!
- Seeds come in all shapes and sizes. Some seeds are as tiny as a speck of dust (such as orchid seeds), and some are big and heavy (such as a coconut!).
- Animals help spread seeds. Birds, squirrels, and even your shoes can help move seeds to new places. Sometimes seeds hitch a ride on furry animals or float through the air like little parachutes!

God gives us seeds to provide the food we need. Thank You, God!

Give each child a **Nature Card 59: Seeds** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to **show or tell your grandfather and grandmother how much you love them, and how much God loves them. If they live far away, call them to share this with them.**

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Show the picnic basket again.

- Remember how disappointed you were not to get the yummy food when we first opened the basket? Think of another time you didn’t get something you really wanted. **What did you do?** (Give some time for quiet thinking.) **Did you complain, as the Israelites did?**

Challenge the children not to complain this week when they don’t get something they really want. Instead, challenge them to **thank God for something He’s already given** them, or say, **“It’s OK; God gives me all I need.”** Invite the children to practice saying this to a friend right now.
