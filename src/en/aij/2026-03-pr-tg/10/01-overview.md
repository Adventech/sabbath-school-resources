**Word of the Week**

**Spiritual:** Being spiritual has to do with people’s thoughts and beliefs about a higher power than themselves—God. Life is about more than what we can see and touch; we have a mind that has been designed to think about God and His big story for us. These thoughts about God, and our need for Him, are spiritual. We also call religious things that are about God spiritual. For example, we can read spiritual books about God, and we can sing spiritual songs.

**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the desire of Jesus to feed us spiritually while also meeting our physical need for food. When 5,000 men, plus women and children, came to Jesus, He had compassion on them. He healed the sick and preached about the kingdom of God. When the people were hungry, Andrew brought Jesus a boy’s five loaves and two fish. Jesus multiplied it for the people. Another time, Jesus again performed a miracle by multiplying a small amount of food to meet the hunger of 4,000 men and their families. Jesus demonstrated that God loves everyone and wants to provide for everyone’s spiritual and physical needs.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus cares about our physical needs and our spiritual needs.
- **Feel** the need to spend quiet time with Jesus to gain spiritual food.
- **Respond** by sharing what we know of God’s love with others, feeding them spiritually, and sharing our food with others in need too.
