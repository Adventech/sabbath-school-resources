---
title: The Visit
---

Approach the home in a cheerful, optimistic attitude. Bring greetings from the Sabbath School and your class (their class, if they are members). Ask ques tions about things you are sure they are interested in, such as the family. Sometimes it helps to transition into spiritual topics to ask how long they have been Seventh-day Adventists, if they are members. If you do not find the people at home the first time, try again, and if at all possible try to see them before the next Sabbath. Come to Sabbath School prepared to report on your visit.
