The news quickly traveled through the town, like ripples on a pond. _Jesus was here! Jesus was in Capernaum!_

As Jesus walked through the streets toward the centurion’s house, people saw Him. They stopped their shopping and marketplace chatter. They dropped their baskets of laundry and lists of things to do, and they all started to follow Him. The crowds squished in closely to the important Visitor.

A messenger raced ahead of the crowd to tell the centurion the good news. Jesus was coming to his home—right now!

The centurion’s face filled with surprise. Jesus was coming to heal his servant! If Jesus was coming, he would go and meet Him on the road.

Rushing outside, the centurion ran down the road to meet Jesus. Seeing Jesus, He said, “Lord, I am not worthy that You should come under my roof” (Matthew 8:9).

Jesus looked at the centurion with a smile on His face. He didn’t see the Roman centurion as “not worthy” or good enough. He loved him!

The Bible tells us, “For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him shall not perish but have everlasting life” (John 3:16).

God gives His great big love to “whoever” believes and trusts in Him. Would the centurion?
