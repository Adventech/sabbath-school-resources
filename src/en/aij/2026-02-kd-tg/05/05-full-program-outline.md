**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal **a clear container of water, dishwashing soap, and a straw(s)**.

Place the **container** on a **tray**. Add a small amount of **dishwashing mix to the water**, then blow air into the water through the straw. This will make lots of bubbles! Add more air if needed. You may also like to give straws to the children to **blow bubbles**. Allow the bubbles to overflow onto the tray.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What happened to the water?** (It bubbled up and made a mess.)

Share that sometimes when we are scared, worried, or angry, our feelings can bubble up inside us. It can feel very messy inside. Watch the bubbles dissipate together until the water is still and calm.

- **What is the water like now?** (Still, calm, peaceful.)

**Teaching Point:** When we feel worried or scared, we can ask God for His peace to help us feel calm and safe. Today’s Bible story is about Jacob. He had lots of worried feelings bubbling up inside him! His story helps us learn our **Big Idea: God is with me. He gives me peace.** Let’s take a deep breath and slowly let it out, then say our **Big Idea** together.

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Brothers Meet Again”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **glitter play-dough**.

Give each child a small amount of glitter play-dough. Ask the children to shape their play-dough into a sparkling heart. Share that we all need God’s peace in our hearts. Then invite them to break the heart in two.

**Hold up your peg people.** Share that Uncle Laban, Esau, and Jacob all needed peace. Their hearts had been broken by sad choices and bad feelings. But God helped them all! He loves to give us peace too. Discuss:

- **Who spoke to Uncle Laban in a dream when he was angry and ready to fight?** (God!) Invite the children to repeat as a reply: _God gave him peace._
- **When Jacob was scared, who showed him that angels were protecting him?** (God!) Repeat: _God gave him peace._
- **When Jacob was worried about seeing Esau and prayed, Who came close to hold him?** (God!) Repeat: _God gave him peace._
- **When Esau was angry and ready to fight, who helped him forgive?** (God!) Repeat: _God gave him peace._

**Teaching Point:** God loves to give us peace in our hearts. He helps us when we are angry, worried, or scared. He sends His angels to help us. He can make us feel calm, knowing that He is with us. Repeat the **Big Idea** together again: **God is with me. He gives me peace.**
