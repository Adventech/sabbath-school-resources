**Word of the Week**

**Relationship:** A connection between people. This can mean being related by blood, such as cousins, uncles, aunts, and grandparents. It can refer to the emotional feelings and behaviors between friends and family members.

**Lesson Summary**

While on his way to Egypt to free the Israelite slaves, Moses sent his family back home for their safety. When Jethro heard that Moses and the Israelites were close by, he returned Moses’ family to him. Their joyful reunion reminded them that families are a gift from God. Seeing Moses overworked, Jethro advised appointing leaders to help. Moses followed his advice, and the family enjoyed renewed time together, grateful for God’s blessings.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God intends for families to be one of God’s wonderful blessings.
- **Feel** grateful for their family or those they live with.
- **Respond** by doing what they can to strengthen relationships with family members.
