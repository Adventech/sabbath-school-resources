_Help!_ A loud scream came from inside Potiphar’s home. The servants and soldiers came running. They found Potiphar’s wife looking very upset. With pretend tears, she told them a story. It was a big made-up story. She said that Joseph had tried to hurt her.

Kind, hardworking Joseph? Joseph shook his head. He hadn’t hurt anyone!

But Potiphar’s wife kept lying. She was not a nice lady. She had asked Joseph to do something very bad, and he had said no. She didn’t like that one bit! So she pretended that Joseph had hurt her so he would get into big trouble. What a horrible thing to do!

She told the lie to her husband. Potiphar had always trusted Joseph’s honest words and good choices. But this story sounded bad! Potiphar “became very angry” (Genesis 39:19, ICB). What did Potiphar do next? **Read Genesis 39:20.**

Oh, no! Joseph hadn’t done anything wrong. He didn’t deserve to go to prison.

Thankfully God knew the truth. He knew Joseph had made the right choice. He knew that Potiphar’s wife was lying. As Joseph was dragged away God was close to him. He would give Joseph the strength to continue to do what was right, even in prison.

- What did you choose to do?
