The story of a great flood that covered the earth is found not only in the Bible; it’s also found etched into nature itself. All over the world we can still see proof from science that a worldwide flood reshaped the land and seas, while Noah and His family were protected on the ark.

The Bible tells us that water burst forth from the deep (Genesis 7:11, 12), as well as coming from heaven. This “bursting forth” moved the earth’s crust and shifted the land a lot. Earthquakes, tsunamis, and volcanic eruptions happen when the earth moves, so we can know that the earth would have changed really fast. When we look around the world, we see evidence (proof) of this rapid change.

There are continents (large areas of land) that look like they once fitted together but are now apart. Covering large parts of them are huge rock layers formed under water. People who don’t believe in the Flood say these layers formed very slowly over millions of years, but the layers are firm, without soil falling away between them. Animals and plants were buried quickly in these rocks, forming fossils. Along with land animals, many fossils are of fish and other creatures that lived in water.

Scientists have found thousands of whale fossils in the driest desert in the world. These must have been buried very rapidly to become fossils. Sometimes, animals such as dinosaurs died and rotted; then their bones were buried. But no dead animals can lie around for a long time before becoming fossils, because their bodies rapidly decompose (break down), even the bones, unless they are buried.

Satan wants to trick us into distrusting the Bible. But nature confirms the truth of God’s Word. You can count on it!
