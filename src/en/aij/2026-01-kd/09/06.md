High up on the mountain there were many of Noah’s relatives who chose to be God’s friends. They loved God and wanted to obey Him. They found out that “happy are the people whose God is the Lord” (Psalm 144:15, ICB). Shem’s family would one day have Jesus in their family!

The people who left Babel spread across the world. Some went to the hills, some to the seaside, and others sailed on boats to new lands.

Wherever they went, God would be there. Some would stay proud and not choose God. But God would always work on their hearts and love them. God tells us He looks after us like a . . . what? **Read Ezekiel 34:11.** What a loving God!

God’s plan for people to fill the whole world happened. Nothing–not even the evil choices of others--can stop God’s plans. And nothing would stop God’s plan to come down and save us from the sin in the world.
