**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

The Sabbath. It’s a reprieve from a busy week, a time to disconnect from the pressures of work—a blessed time set apart by our Creator for Him. It’s a day during which God can be our focus. It’s also a weekly reset before we continue into another busy week. God knew we’d need it for our health and for our families. This sacred time is such a blessing from Him.

As with everything good given to us from God, Satan has attempted to destroy or distort the gift of the Sabbath. Over the centuries the enemy has worked tirelessly to draw human beings away from the truth of the Sabbath, and ultimately away from Christ. He wants us to think it’s about keeping certain rules; he wants us to think that God is restricting us when really, Jesus tells us, “And you shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free” (John 8:32).

The Sabbath is one of the most beautiful gifts from God to express how much He loves us and wants us to have time with Him so that we don’t forget Him. He hopes we’ll share the blessing of Sabbath with others, as Jesus did while He lived on our earth.

This Sabbath, may you and the children in your Sabbath School class learn, believe, and feel the blessings of the Sabbath—both in your personal relationship with God and in how you reflect Him to others.
