Early one morning, Jenny, her brother Jeff, and their parents jumped into the family Jeep and headed off down the road. They were going on an exciting youth camp holiday, three hours away.

But the road to get there was very dangerous. It was bendy and steep and wound high up a mountain. On the side that looked out over the valley was a very, very steep cliff that dropped straight down. Far below was a small trickling creek.

The road was only just wide enough for the family Jeep to stay on. Dad had to drive very carefully to keep the car wheels on the road.

After a while Jenny got bored. She climbed through to the back door and leaned against the window to see out.

Suddenly the back door swung open. Jenny lost her balance and frantically grabbed the handle of the door as it swung out over the cliff.

“Mom, Dad!” she yelled. But the rattling car made it impossible for them to hear her. Looking down she could see the trickling creek far, far below. Jenny held on tightly with her sweaty hands.

“Dear Jesus,” she prayed, “please help Mom and Dad to see me, and help me to hang on tight.”

Jenny screamed out again and this time her mom and dad turned to see her hanging out the back of the car. Dad stopped the Jeep slowly and ran over to where Jenny was hanging. He carefully pulled her back to safety. Jenny fell into her daddy’s arms crying with relief.

As Jenny looks back on that day, she knows without a doubt that Jesus heard her prayer and saved her.
