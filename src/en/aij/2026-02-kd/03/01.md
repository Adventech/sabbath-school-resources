**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He forgives me when I say I’m sorry.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“If we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (1 John 1:9)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Confident”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Being confident means feeling sure that something is true. You can be confident that God loves to forgive you when you make mistakes—it’s certain and true.

Stumbling in the Dark

Jacob ran faster and faster. His feet pounded the ground, his sides hurt, and he was out of breath. But he couldn’t stop. Darkness was coming, he must hurry—hurry away from home!

Spiky bushes and tree branches scratched his legs as he ran on. His body hurt but not as much as his heart. How he would miss his parents! If only he had been honest! He shouldn’t have lied to his father and tricked him into giving him the birthright blessing. His lies had caused so much hurt and pain. And now his sin, and the things he’d done, felt horrible.

Jacob thought of angry Esau, shouting and waving his fist at him. His own brother hated him! He quickly looked at the path behind him. Feeling scared, he ran even faster.

As darkness fell Jacob felt afraid and alone. The wrong things he’d done felt like a heavy rock on his back. He wanted to get rid of it, but how? **Read Psalm 118:4.**

Jacob didn’t think God wanted to be his helper or friend anymore because he’d lied, cheated, and stole his brother’s blessing. He was sure his mistakes were too big for God to love him. But God saw Jacob’s hurt and the horrible feelings in his heart. The Bible says that when we’ve done something wrong, we can run straight to God. “Come back to God, and He will forgive your sins” (Acts 3:19, ICB).

God’s mercy lasts forever. He is such a good, kind, forgiving God. We can be confident of God’s love for us. We don’t ever need to feel alone.

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for His great big love.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Take a coin and cover it with dirt. Is it still valuable? Yes. Even when you are dirty with sin, God still loves and values you too. Now wash the coin. God’s forgiveness can clean our hearts.
