Explore

Re-create the story using sticks, leaves, and bark to create a house. Take a piece of cloth, tie a thread to its four corners, draw and cut out a paper man, and lay him on the cloth bed. Make a hole in the “roof” and send down the “sick man.” Thank Jesus together for healing the sick man in such an amazing way, and for his four kind friends who brought him to Jesus. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Create

Invite one of your friends to Sabbath School. Create a special invitation, then mail it or deliver it to their home.  

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Worship

In worship, re-create the story using a blanket and some teddy bears. Read the story from your Bible or the lesson again. Take turns giving your child a turn to be carried around the room in the blanket by holding each of the corners. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Connect

Think about a friend (who may also be a family member) who may need your help or encouragement. Spend time making a card, gift, or practicing a song to sing, to encourage this person. 

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#cca98b"}}}
Pray

Choose a different friend to pray for each day this week. Model praying for that person and then ask your child if they would like to pray too.
