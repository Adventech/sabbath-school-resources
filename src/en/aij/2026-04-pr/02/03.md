The nobleman looked up at Jesus, his eyes desperate. Would Jesus come and heal his dying son?

Jesus’ voice broke the silence: “Unless you people see signs and wonders, you will by no means believe” (John 4:48).

Those unexpected words hit the nobleman like a flash of light. They made everything clear. He saw that his reason for coming to Jesus had been selfish. He was now convinced that he was standing in the presence of God’s Son—the One who could read thoughts and do the impossible. The One who truly was the Messiah.

How did he reply to Jesus? **Read John 4:49.**

The nobleman’s words were no longer spoken from a selfish heart. They were spoken with faith! He had given Jesus all his cares, knowing Jesus cared for him.

Jesus didn’t make the nobleman wait any longer. He smiled and said tenderly, “Go your way; your son lives” (John 4:50).

Jesus could have sent the nobleman away because he doubted. Instead He lovingly showed him that he needed more faith and gave him the opportunity to believe. Then Jesus rewarded his faith with the promise of healing.

Jesus does the same for us when we doubt. When we come to Him, He shows us mercy and works in our hearts to wash the doubts away and replace them with faith. Our memory verse says, “. . . casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).
