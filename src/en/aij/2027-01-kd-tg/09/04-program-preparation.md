**Opening Activities**

Source a **large piece of cardstock or cardboard** and cut into a heart shape. Make it large enough to later fit 10 large numbers used in the story section (used next week also). Source **crayons, stickers, papers**, and **other craft materials** for children to use to decorate the heart.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: A child’s **helmet**, toy **car**, **card/paper**. Write the following Bible promise on the card: “I love you people with a love that will last forever” (Jeremiah 31:3, ICB).

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Tell the Story**

Source suggested storytelling props and Story Explorer items: Create “Happy Hearts” using four pieces of paper. Write “Happy Heart” with a small number 1-4 on each and plan to stick them to walls or objects in your room, or lay them on the floor. Cardstock to make numbers 1-10 (you will use only 1-4 this week). Ensure they will fit in the giant heart. You will need sticky tac or similar so children can stick them to Happy Heart stations, then the giant heart.

Prepare four activity stations.

**Station 1**: **tub, small paper heart for each child, collection of toys and paper, picture of the Bible**.

**Station 2**: collection of **cardboard boxes** (or plastic containers) **labeled with pictures, beanbag** (or you can fill a tied-up sock with dried rice).

**Station 3**: **paper towel tubes** for each pair of children.

**Station 4**: **nature items, Sabbath School lessons, Sabbath picture books, instruments**.

Ask a teen or youth to come to your class for the Apply section.
