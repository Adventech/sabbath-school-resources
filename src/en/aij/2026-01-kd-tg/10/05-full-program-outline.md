!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-kd-tg/assets/10-psalm-37-23.mp3] “Psalm 37:23” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Sean Nebblett"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Katya Jones"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2025 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

Invite the children to stand up and follow your instructions:

**Take two steps forward. Take four steps toward the window. Turn and take a step toward the door. Step back to your chair.**

Explain to the children that when you gave these instructions, you were “ordering their steps.” The memory verse tells us that “the steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord” (Psalm 37:23).

- **How do you think God “orders” our steps?** He doesn’t tell us to take steps around the room . . . (Accept answers for now.)

Share that today’s story will teach us more about what our memory verse means when it says, “The steps of a good man are ordered by the Lord” (Psalm 37:23).

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a blindfold. Invite two children (good friends) to engage in a trust activity. One child can wear the blindfold and follow the voice of their friend around the room. The “caller” is to talk to their friend to lead them (with their voice only) on a safe path while they follow instructions. After a short walk, ask the blindfolded child:

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How did you know where to go?** (Followed friend’s voice.)
- **Would you have followed the voice of a stranger you didn’t know?**
- **Why did you follow your friend?** (Know their voice, trust them, have faith they would lead you safely.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s story is about a man who trusted God and followed Him because he knew God’s voice very well.

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is loving. I will follow Him.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Abram, Man of Faith”** in an interactive and engaging way using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal paper clips, a piece of cardstock, and a strong magnet. Place the paper clips on top of the cardstock and lead them by moving the magnet around underneath.

- **What is leading the paper clips?** (A magnet we cannot see.)
- **How might the magnet and paper clips remind us of God and Abram?**

**Teaching Point:** Abram’s faith in God was so strong that he let God lead him, even though he could not see God. We may not hear God’s voice out loud as Abram did, but we can follow God by doing what His words in the Bible ask us to do.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. I will follow Him.**
