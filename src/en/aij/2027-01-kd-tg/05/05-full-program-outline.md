**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **stack of fabric squares or hand towels**. Place them around your Sabbath School area as a simple obstacle course so the children can jump from square to square, from start to finish. Tell the children to pretend that these are all “islands” with deep sea all around, and invite them to jump from “island” to “island” without falling into the water. Make sure it is doable for all ages in your group. For larger groups you may like to do multiple courses based on size/age.

Affirm the children for their good jumping. Share that your jumping challenge was tricky but possible—it could be done. Then move the squares very, very far apart from each other—way beyond what they could jump. Ask:

- **Do you think this is possible?** (No, it’s impossible. It can’t be done.)

**Teaching Point:** In today’s story the Israelites were faced with a very big problem. It was so big it was impossible! There was no way they could do it on their own. But they weren’t on their own. Someone very special was leading them, and He did something totally amazing! Let’s find out what. This story will help us understand our **Big Idea: God’s way is best. Nothing is impossible for Him!**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **balloon** and **marker**. Have a sharp **pin** nearby.

Ask a helper to blow up your balloon while you ask the following:

- **Why were the children of Israel afraid as they saw the Egyptian army coming toward them?** (The Egyptians were bigger and stronger than they were. There was nowhere to run. They thought they’d have to become slaves again.)

Write “scared” on your balloon.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})**

- **What is something that makes you a bit scared or worried?** (Accept answers.)

Share that even though the Israelites were scared at first, they learned something really amazing about God. **What might they have learned about God?** (He was with them; would always help them; could do the impossible.)

**Teaching Point:** Sometimes we can be scared of things that might hurt us, or others who are angry and bigger than we are. Other times we might worry about going somewhere new or making new friends. But we can remember that God is always with us. We can trust Him when we are scared, because God can fix impossible problems. He can do anything!

Pop your balloon. Invite the children to stand as you repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. Nothing is impossible for Him!**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 57: Bald Eagle** and share the following facts:

- Bald eagles are one of the largest hunting birds in the world. They have super sharp eyes that help them see fish (their favorite food) swimming in the water far, far below.
- They build some of the biggest nests in the world— sometimes as big as a small car!
- They are very protective parents. Both mommy and daddy bald eagle take turns looking after their eggs, sitting on them to keep them warm or spreading out their wings to shade them from the hot sun.
- Baby eagles are called “eaglets.” They’re soft and fluffy, and they grow very fast.
- Father eagles always stay near the nest, ready to protect the eaglets from any danger.

The Bible tells us that just as an eagle cares for its young, God protects us, leads us, and cares for us (Deuteronomy 32:11, 12).

Give each child a **Nature Card 57: Bald Eagle** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Parting the Red Sea.** Show the children a finished craft sample and demonstrate God’s miracle of the waters parting so His people could safely cross. As the children work on their craft, remind them that when we are faced with a big problem, we can trust God to make a way for us, even if it seems impossible.

Encourage the children to use their craft to tell the story to someone in their family.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is **to be extra loving to your daddy (or a wonderful man who cares for you). Help him, be kind, obey him, give him hugs, and tell him Jesus loves him.**

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned that **nothing is impossible with God.**

Explain that when we see what He will do to lovingly help and protect us, we will want to show Him our love too. We can do this by telling Him we love Him, thanking Him, telling others about Him, and singing to Him with joy.

Hand out percussion instruments to the children. Ask them to keep them still. Invite them to share something they want to thank God for. After each answer, have them shake/play their instrument.
