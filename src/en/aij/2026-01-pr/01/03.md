When God started creating life in our world, the earth was covered in water and darkness. There was no sky. There were no juicy apples, no crashing waves. Nothing, other than God the Father, His Son Jesus, and the Holy Spirit, who was “hovering over the face of the waters” (Genesis 1:2).

Imagine God’s big smile as He thought about all the amazing things He could create from nothing in the brand-new earth. The Bible says the earth was “without form, and void” (Genesis 1:2), which means that the earth hadn’t yet been prepared for plants and animals and people to live on it. God is the Creator of all things, and He saw how amazing the earth could be. So what did God do? Read Genesis 1:1. God’s love was so big, His plan for our world so wonderful. He would create our world and provide everything to care for it.

Imagine all of heaven watching to see what God would create on this special planet. The ability to create something from nothing is one of God’s amazing attributes. So He began by speaking powerful words into the emptiness. Do you know what God said? Read Genesis 1:3.

Suddenly beams of light flooded into the darkness, melting it away. God’s light shone warm and clear, brightening the beginning of His new world. “God saw the light, that it was good” (Genesis 1:4).

Then God gave the light another name. “God called the light Day, and the darkness He called Night. So the evening and the morning were the first day” (Genesis 1:5).
