Abraham was a perfect host. As his guests ate, he stood close by, ready to serve them more food or fresh milk. He may have wondered where they had journeyed from and where they were going to, but he waited for them to finish their meal before asking.

The travelers, however, paused their eating to ask him a question of their own. “Where is Sarah your wife?” (Genesis 18:9), they asked.

Looking back at the tent, Abraham replied, “Here, in the tent” (Genesis 18:9).

One of the men responded, “I will certainly return to you about this time a year from now. At that time your wife Sarah will have a son” (Genesis 18:10, ICB).

Abraham’s mind raced: how did the stranger know Sarah’s name; how did he know she would have a baby; and how did he know it would be a boy?

Suddenly he realized this kind man was the Lord Himself! Disguised as a man, God had come to deliver the good news. What a privilege to be able to serve the God of heaven and two of His angels!

Imagine if Abraham had simply let the strangers stroll on by. He would have missed the excitement of knowing he would soon be a dad; he would have missed the chance to meet and serve God!

Almost 2,000 years later a Bible writer remembered Abraham’s kindness and gave us some wise advice. **Read Hebrews 13:2.**

Just like Abraham, we may one day have the privilege of entertaining holy angels that look just like people. What a blessing that would be.
