**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. Nothing is impossible for Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 5 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Other suggested props: **a box** as an Egyptian chariot, **tambourines** for the children to play (or percussion instruments made before Sabbath School), **two pieces of blue fabric** (or towels) to mark Red Sea. Helpers can “part” them for the peg people as you tell the story.
- Use peg people (Moses, families) or a felt set if desired.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Children and families marched happily out of Egypt. God had set them free!
- They followed God’s tall cloud by day, His tower of fire at night. They could trust God.
- The people followed the cloud to the Red Sea to camp.
- Pharaoh was angry they’d left. He and his soldiers, horses, and chariots chased them ^[(chariot box)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- The Israelites were scared—they were trapped. Moses told them to trust God ^[(memory verse)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He would fight for them and save them.
- God’s cloud and fire went between the Israelites and their enemies. God hid His people.
- Moses stretched out his arm with his rod, and the sea parted. The people walked through on dry land! ^[(Separate blue cloth, peg people to walk through.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The Egyptians chased after them. God made their wheels stop turning, fall off, and get stuck! ^[(Show chariot stuck.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God told Moses to stretch his hand over the sea again. The waters returned and covered the army. ^[(Return blue cloth. Take away chariot.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The people sang their thanks to God, He had saved them! ^[(Use tambourines/bells and homemade percussion instruments.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Thousands of children and families hurried along together as they left Egypt. Goats, sheep, cows, and donkeys all trotted along too. People smiled as they moved with the huge crowd. God had set His people free! He was leading them to a new home—a Promised Land.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As they walked, everyone watched something at the very front of the crowd. It was a special cloud. ^[(Invite children to imagine a big cloud above them.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God was in the cloud, leading them. The special cloud also gave them shade from the hot sun in the daytime.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At night God was with them in a tower of fire. The fire gave light and warmth. They could trust God to protect and lead them.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The sun came up, and the people rolled up their beds and packed up their tents. They watched the cloud and waited.

The tall cloud began to move. Quickly all the people and the animals began to follow.

God’s cloud took them to the shores of the Red Sea. Here the cloud stopped, and the people stopped too. The Israelites set up their tents to camp.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Back in Egypt Pharaoh was angry and getting angrier. His slaves were gone, and he and his rulers wanted them back! The king and all his soldiers jumped on their chariots and raced after the Israelites ^[(chariot box)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

The Israelites saw them coming. Oh, no! Where could they run? On one side of them stood mountains, and in front of them was the deep Red Sea. They were trapped. Afraid, they began crying out to God for help.

Moses told them, “Do not be afraid. Stand still, and see the salvation of the Lord. . . . The Lord will fight for you, and you shall hold your peace” (Exodus 14:13, 14).

Yes, they could trust God—He would fight for them and save them!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly God’s tall cloud rose high into the air and came down between God’s people and the Egyptian soldiers. All night on the Israelites’ side God’s fire shone brightly. But on the other side it was very dark. God had protected His people from their enemies!

The Israelites obeyed God and rushed to the water’s edge. Moses held his hand with his rod over the sea, and the water parted. ^[(Separate blue cloths.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God made a dry path, right through the middle of the Red Sea! God had done the impossible.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people quickly walked on the dry path between two watery walls. ^[(Move peg people.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Egyptians stared in amazement, then started to chase them in their shiny chariots. Suddenly, their strong, shiny chariots started to wobble and crash. God had made their wheels fall off! God had stopped the chase! ^[(Show chariot stuck.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Safely on the other side, the Israelites watched. Their enemies were stuck in the mud! Then God told Moses what to do. Moses stretched out his hand, and the watery walls came crashing down. ^[(Move blue cloth back and take away chariot.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Those Egyptians died, and would never again chase the Israelites.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Israelites stood safely on the other side of the Red Sea. Their love and thanks to God bubbled over. Together they began to sing to God. Moses’ big sister, Miriam, and her friends played their tambourines. ^[(Hand out tambourine/bells.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Everyone sang their thanks to God for saving them!
