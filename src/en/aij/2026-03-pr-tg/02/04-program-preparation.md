Opening and Focus Activities

Provide **paper** and **pencils** for children to draw a picture of the best gift they’ve ever received.

Collect drawings/ideas from the Opening Activity and one of your own to share. Prepare two **gifts**: one with special wrapping but an ordinary “gift” (e.g., spoon or tape), and the other with a special gift (determined by you) in a wrinkled paper bag.

Source a **globe/world map**, update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, prepare Mission Fast Fact.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or print/write out and display.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Collect objects to share the story: **baking tray, pile of sticks, a small block of wood, leaves, round dried beans**, and a 15-inch (38-centimeter) length of **gold crepe paper** or fabric.

Source a **doll**, or if there is a newborn baby in your church, arrange to “borrow” this little one for an object lesson.

Write the following **statements** on strips of **paper**, place inside a large **envelope** labeled “GIFT,” and hide it in your room: heaven, being forgiven, feeling happy, feeling peace inside, knowing God loves me, being lonely, no hope in the future, no help with problems, living unforgiven, no heaven. Create two headings: “With God’s Gift of Jesus” and “Without God’s Gift of Jesus.”

Source a **gift box** with a removable lid to put the “With God’s Gift of Jesus” statements inside.

Source a small piece of blank **paper** and **markers** for children to write or draw something they want to thank God for because Jesus came into the world. (They will fold and keep till the end of Sabbath School.)

Craft Materials

Source **craft sticks, cardstock, craft glue, oval-shaped rocks, black markers, pieces of fabric, rubber bands, straw**. Source some **Christmas hymns** to play while children are completing their craft.
