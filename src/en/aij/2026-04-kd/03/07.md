Senitiki lay in the hospital bed with his wife by his side. He was sad, terribly sad.

That morning had started out like every other day. He had said goodbye to his wife, Vasemaca, and had gone to work. Senitiki and Vasemaca lived in Suva, the capital city of Fiji.

But on this day, Senitiki had a terrible accident at work and lost his leg.

Now, Senitiki lay in a hospital bed looking at the empty spot below his knee, where his leg used to be. He didn’t know what he would do. He was very upset!

“Come to church with me,” Vasemaca whispered. She had recently become a Seventh-day Adventist Christian and knew that Jesus could give him life “more abundantly” (John 10:10)—even in such a sad time.

Senitiki had not been interested in going to church. But now as he lay in the hospital bed, something began to change in his heart.

For a whole month Senitiki stayed in the hospital. During that time, his wife and many of her friends from the Adventist church came and visited him each day. They were so kind and loving!

Senitiki made a decision. He wanted to learn about the God who filled these Adventist people with such kindness. On his first Sabbath home from the hospital, Senitiki decided to go to church. He had a wonderful day with his wife and new Adventist friends. It wasn’t long before Senitiki and Jesus were best friends too—Senitiki even decided to be baptized!

Senitiki now shares the kindness and love of Jesus everywhere he goes. He is happier than he has ever been. He has even started a new Seventh-day Adventist church in his village and is planning to start another church in a village nearby. How do you show Jesus’ love and kindness to others?
