**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

“Since the war in heaven, Satan’s goal has been to separate humankind from a relationship with our Creator, God. Disguised as a serpent, and determined to pull off the first, worst deception, Satan tempted Eve to lose trust in God. He wanted her to trust herself instead; to do things her way. Sadly, Adam and Eve fell for this deception.”

The Bible tells us that there is no truth in Satan—he is a liar and the father of lies (John 8:44). His lies spread—first to Adam and Eve as they tried to shift the blame, then on throughout the generations.

Rebekah and Jacob followed in this path. Trusting themselves, they set out to get what they wanted, their way. To disguise himself, Jacob put on animal furs and wore Esau’s clothing. Rebekah substituted Esau’s food with her own version. And just as the story way back in the Garden of Eden spiraled downward quickly, so does the story of Jacob and Esau. Suddenly there were feelings of loneliness, anger, sadness, and grief beyond measure.

God wants so much more for us. The Bible tells us, “Lying lips are an abomination to the Lord, but those who deal truthfully are His delight” (Proverbs 12:22). God wants to show us how to live with truthfulness and integrity—attributes of His character. He wants the very best for us. He invites us to trust and depend on Him—the way, the truth, and the life. “Those who realize their dependence upon God will feel that they must be honest with their fellow men, and, above all, they must be honest with God, from whom come all the blessings of life” (Ellen G. White, _Child Guidance_, p. 152).

May we realize our need for Jesus every day this week and ask Him to help us to be like Him so that we can be honest tactfully.
