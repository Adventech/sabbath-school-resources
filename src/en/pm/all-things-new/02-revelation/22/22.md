[[Revelation 22:3-22]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Thus ends one of the greatest books ever written. In light of what you’ve read, do you see the incredible gift that Jesus has prepared for you?
- Currently our planet is often a dark place, and it will get darker still before Jesus returns. _But when Jesus comes back, all will be made right._ And you can be a part of that new kingdom of justice, goodness, and love if you choose.
- If you would like to accept Jesus as your Lord and Savior and the leader of your life, then say to Him the following prayer:
- “Jesus, I am a sinner. I confess that I have broken Your holy law. I have dishonored You, and I have harmed other people. Please forgive me for my sins. I accept Your free gift of salvation that You purchased for me on the cross. I ask You to be my Savior and the leader of my life. Please put Your Holy Spirit in my heart, that I may have the power to live a new life in You. I don’t deserve this gift. But I thank You for it. I ask this in Your name, Jesus. Amen.”
- If you pray this prayer from an honest heart, you are immediately welcomed into a personal relationship with Jesus! You are forgiven, set free from sin, and will now have the power of God’s Holy Spirit working within you to live a new life of justice, goodness, and love.
