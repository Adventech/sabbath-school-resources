**Lesson Summary**

After traveling to Moab from famine-stricken Israel, Naomi lost her husband and two sons. However, Naomi was close to God. She also had two daughters-in-law, Ruth and Orpah, who loved her. When Naomi set out to return to her home in Bethlehem, Ruth determined to go with her, saying, “Where you go I will go. . . . Your people shall be my people, and your God my God” (Ruth 1:16, ESV). Ruth was committed to both Naomi and Naomi’s God. God was able to comfort both women through their love and friendship.
