**Word of the Week**

**Providence:** God’s guidance and care for us, and His wise preparation for our future needs. We see God’s providence in our lives when we don’t know what we need or how our needs will be met.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Through the seven years of famine in Egypt and the surrounding lands, God had wisely prepared a way to supply for Jacob and his family’s needs through the leadership of Joseph in Egypt. Joseph’s brothers returned to Egypt to buy more grain, and Joseph continued to test them to see whether they had allowed God to change their hard hearts. When Joseph saw their changed hearts, he revealed who he was and how God had put him in his position so that he could provide for their needs. Jacob, his sons, and their families then journeyed from Canaan to Egypt, and Joseph was finally reunited with his father.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God sees that their needs can turn something bad into something good.
- **Feel** that they can trust God to take care of them.
- **Respond** by giving their hearts and lives to God because He cares for them.
