Create

Create a stormy sea in a bottle with your child. Fill a drink bottle half full of water. Add two or three drops of blue food coloring and one drop of green. Fill the bottle the rest of the way to the top with oil. You could add glitter or small plastic ocean animals. Close the lid very tightly. Tip the bottle side to side and watch the waves roll on the stormy sea.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#34c6f4"}}}
Explore

Set up a water play table by filling a big plastic container with water. Place a small plastic boat or other floating object (rubber duck) at one end of the container. Have your child blow on the toy to make it move across the water to the other side. Try other methods to move the boat across the water, such as using a straw to blow through or by making gentle waves with their hands in the water.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#34c6f4"}}}
Learn

Explore outside to find ways that God’s creation obeys Him. For example: the sunrise or sunset, the stars and the moon that come out at night. Talk with your child about how God’s creation shows us His loving care.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#34c6f4"}}}
Sing

Sing “See the Boat” (Little Voices Praise Him, no. 172) with the actions. This is a gentle lullaby-like song that tells the story of Jesus on the lake.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#34c6f4"}}}
Pray

Talk with your child about how Jesus was calm in the storm. When the wind was blowing, and the waves were crashing, He could sleep because He trusted in God to take care of Him and His friends. Even during a storm, we can be at peace because we know God is with us. Snuggle with a special stuffed animal or blanket and say a prayer, thanking God for watching over us all the time.
