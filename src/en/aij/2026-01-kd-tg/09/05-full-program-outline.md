**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal your set of picture cards. Hand a card to each child. Ensure that they keep the picture secret and that at least two to four children get the same one.

On the count of three, invite children to say out loud what their picture is and find others who call out the same word. Then ring your bell and ask the children:

- **Could you understand each other?**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Talk about how noisy and confusing this activity sounded! Share that in today’s story, there were noisy, confusing names being called out all at once too. This happened because people were very proud and wanted to do things their own way. They weren’t humble, like God. They should have thought, God’s way is my way.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In today’s story, the people of Babel became loud and proud too.

- **What does it mean to be proud?** (Feeling as if you are more special and important than others. That is not God’s way.)

Say the **Big Idea: God is loving. He is never proud.** Have the children stand and repeat it back to you.

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“The Tall Tower”** in an interactive and engaging way using the storytelling suggestions and Program PowerPoint.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker** and Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal six (or 12) “proud face” cups and six (or 12) “happy face” cups.

Show a cup with a proud face and ask:

- **Why did God confuse the languages and make the tower fall?** (The people were proud and thought only of themselves.) Review what it means to be proud. (A proud person thinks they are more special or important than others. Usually people who are very proud of themselves feel as if they don’t need God or anyone else to help them).
- **What kinds of things might proud people say?** (“I’m the best.” “I’m the strongest/smartest.” “I should have the best.”) As the children share their answers, build a tower together using the “proud face” cups.
- **Is being proud God’s way?** (No.) Knock the tower down.

Show the six “happy face” cups.

- **What kinds of things might people who are not proud say?** Invite children to build another tower as they answer this question. (“You are good at this.” “I’m glad you’re my friend, you go first.” “I will follow God.” “Thank you.”) God’s way is never proud. His way is kind; it puts others first.

God wanted the people in our Bible story to spread around the world to tell others about His great love. Show the children your world map or globe. Find your country. Show the world map again, with the hearts the children place on it during the story. Ask:

- **Did God’s plan to spread His love around the world happen?** (Yes!)

**Teaching Point:** God loves everyone—even those who are proud and have chosen not to follow His ways. He wants everyone in the world to have a chance to know His love.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. He is never proud.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 9: Mound-building Termite** and share the following facts:

- A tiny termite is no bigger than your fingernail.
- Inside the nest are lots and lots of little tunnels that help keep everything nice and cool.
- Termites never sleep.
- They are found in Africa, Australia, and North America.
- Together these little creatures can build a gigantic, tall nest above the ground!

Even the tiniest of creatures can build incredible things, reminding us of how loving and creative our God is. We can trust that He has great plans for us too. Give each child **Nature Card 9** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Paper People From Different Countries.** Reinforce how God loves ALL the people of the world. When we choose God’s way as my way, we will love all people too.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Invite the children to place their paper people on the floor (on the world map, if possible) and connect their hands in a circle to show how we are in a big circle of friendship. Sing **“Jesus Loves the Little Children”** as you look at the paper people. Then give a little time of prayer for children to pray for the people in these countries.

(Alternative lyrics: “Jesus loves the little children, all the children of the world. Stranger, friend, and neighbor too, He loves them as He loves you . . . Every color, every race, all are covered by His grace . . . Morning, evening, day, and night, they are precious in His sight . . . Jesus loves the little children of the world.”)

Today we’ve learned that **God isn’t proud. He is humble and kind.**

We’ve learned that **God’s love is for everyone**, and it goes on forever.

Today we can **choose to say, “God’s way is my way.”**

Repeat **Big Idea: God is loving. He is never proud.**
