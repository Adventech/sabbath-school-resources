Naomi couldn’t stop smiling. God had been good to her. She now had a daughter and a son. Ruth had married kind Boaz.

But soon Naomi had an even better reason to smile. She became a grandmother! Ruth and Boaz had a beautiful baby boy.

Everyone was so happy for the family. The women said to Naomi, “Praise the Lord who gave you this grandson. . . . This happened because of your daughter-in-law. She loves you” (Ruth 4:14, 15, ICB).

Naomi carefully took the teeny baby boy in her arms and cradled him close. Happy tears filled her eyes as she stroked his little cheeks. God had been so good. She couldn’t wait for this little boy to grow bigger, so she could tell him all about her faithful Father God.

Who named the baby? And what did they name him? **Read the first part of Ruth 4:17.**

This little baby boy grew up and became the grandfather of King David. And a long time later, Jesus, the Promised One, was born into this family too.

God showed Ruth His love and kindness. He welcomed her to His family. By sending His own Son, Jesus, to her big family, we can be sure that He welcomes everyone with love. Yes, **everyone**! The Bible says, “Behold what manner of love the Father has bestowed on us, that we should be called the children of God!” (1 John 3:1).
