### Testimonies and Reflections

It can be helpful to record your disciple-making experiences and personal reflections. Taking time to evaluate and reflect on successes, mistakes, and challenges, is an important key to growth as disciplemakers. This can not only help you recount how God has blessed your efforts, but can also be an inspiration to others when you share your testimony of God’s power in your life. “And they overcame him by the blood of the Lamb and by the word of their testimony” (Rev. 12:11).
