A sharp, loud cry pierced the silence. The baby boy was here! Happy shouts and praises erupted. Excitement rippled through the camp. God had fulfilled His promise! Sarah had given birth to a miracle baby at the grandmotherly age of 90. How old was Abraham when Isaac was born? Read Genesis 21:5.

Sarah lovingly wrapped her baby and snuggled him close. As she stroked his soft cheeks, happy laughter welled up from her heart. Only one short year before, Sarah had laughed in unbelief. Now she laughed with pure joy. “And Sarah said, ‘God has made me laugh, and all who hear will laugh with me’ ” (Genesis 21:6).

God must have known this tiny baby would bring peals of happy laughter, for He had said, “And you shall call his name Isaac” (Genesis 17:19). The name Isaac means “to laugh.”

Abraham must have gazed in wonder at this gift of happiness. This tiny son of promise was the first of Abraham’s many descendants: the first twinkling star, the first tiny speck of dust. And 25 years of waiting for this shining star made him even more precious.

God fulfilled His promise, as He always does. For nothing is impossible for God. The Bible says, “Ah, Lord God! Behold, You have made the heavens and the earth by Your great power and outstretched arm. There is nothing too hard for You” (Jeremiah 32:17).

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT

- _Think of a time a family member fulfilled a promise to you. How did you feel and what did you say?_
- _What promises has God made to you? Can you trust Him to fulfill them?_

God always fulfills the promises He makes to His people. We may have to wait a long time, but when a promise is finally fulfilled, we will appreciate it more.
