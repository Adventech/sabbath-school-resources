**Word of the Week**

**Confidence:** Trust that you or someone will act in the best possible way or that an outcome will be good.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Abraham’s growing family learned to have confidence in God and His timing. Abraham and his servant trusted God to find a wife for his son Isaac, and God delivered. Isaac and his wife trusted God for a baby, and God delivered—they had twins! God told them that the younger, Jacob, not the firstborn, would be the family’s leader. Jacob lacked confidence in God’s plan and timing, and instead tried to snatch it for himself.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is always with them and knows what is best for them.
- **Feel** confident that God will lead their lives.
- **Respond** by giving their hearts and lives to God, allowing Him to guide them.
