### God Helps Us Understand His Word

> <p></p>
> “Many will be purified, made spotless and refined, but the wicked will continue to be wicked. None of the wicked will understand, but those who are wise will understand.” Daniel 12:10 (NIV)

Have you ever had to wait before you could understand something? Maybe a puzzle looked confusing at first, but later the pieces began to fit together. Daniel also had questions. God revealed important things about the future to Daniel, but he did not understand everything. God told him that some of these prophecies would be understood later, at the time of the end.

Many years later, John saw something special in a vision. He saw an angel holding an open little book. John was told to eat the book. It tasted sweet like honey, but afterward it made his stomach bitter. What did that mean? The little book pointed to Daniel’s prophecies. After the long period of 1,260 years ended in 1798, people began studying Daniel’s prophecies with great interest.

They were especially excited about the prophecy that reached 1844. Many believed Jesus would return that year. Thinking about seeing Jesus was very sweet! But Jesus didn’t return in 1844. The people were very disappointed. That experience was very bitter.

They did not stop studying the Bible. They studied again and learned that they had misunderstood what the prophecy meant. Then God gave them an important mission: “You must prophesy again.” They were to keep sharing God’s message and telling others that Jesus is coming again.

We can learn from them, too. When we do not understand something in the Bible, we should keep learning, praying, and trusting God. He will help us understand His Word.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What should we do when we do not understand something in the Bible?

> <p>Remember</p>
> God helps us understand His Word and share the good news that Jesus is coming again!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You for giving us Your Word. Help us have wise and teachable hearts. Help us understand Your messages and tell others that Jesus is coming again. Amen.
