Many years before, when Jesus was 12, He had said to His mother, “I must be about My Father’s business” (Luke 2:49). Now that He was busy doing God’s work publicly, those words had really come true—especially on that long and exciting day in Capernaum when He blessed so many people.

- People were blessed by Jesus’ teachings in the synagogue.
- Jesus and His disciples had been blessed to feel part of Peter’s family.
- The family felt blessed to have Jesus stay with them.
- Peter’s mother-in-law had been blessed with healing.
- The whole town was blessed either to have been healed or to have seen the healings firsthand.

So many blessings flowed from Jesus as He went about His Father’s business that day. And when it came to an end, Jesus felt blessed by all He had been able to do for others. His heart had filled with sympathy when He saw the sufferings of those who had come to be healed. Then His heart sang with joy as He blessed the people with healing and comfort. Jesus was so glad He had the power to make them well and happy. It had been a wonderful day.
