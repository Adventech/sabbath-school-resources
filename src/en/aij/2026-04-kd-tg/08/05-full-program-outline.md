- **When did God make the very first Sabbath?** (On the seventh day of Creation.)

Share that God made the Sabbath during Creation week. Today’s memory verse tells us something else that’s very important about the Sabbath. Show the children how to find the **memory verse, Mark 2:28. Say it together:** “Therefore the Son of Man is also Lord of the Sabbath” (Mark 2:2).

**Teaching Point:** The “Son of Man” is another name for Jesus. Jesus created the Sabbath at the beginning of our world, and He is Lord of the Sabbath. This means He made it, He understands it best, and He’s in charge of it. He wants it to be a time of love, joy, togetherness, learning, and kindness for His children.

Listen to and sing the **Mark 2:28 Scripture Song**.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a parcel wrapped as a present.

- **Who likes to receive presents? Tell us about your favorite present.**

We know that today is the Sabbath. If Jesus is “Lord of the Sabbath,” having a Sabbath day is like getting a special present from Him.

Play **“Pass the Parcel”** with the present. Have the children sit in a circle. Pass the gift around the circle while music is playing or the teacher is singing. When the music stops, the child holding the present can say something they like to do on Sabbath (without opening the parcel, but simply passing it along). Show a number of play-dough “sculptures” that were made by those who arrived early to share more ideas.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}}) Teaching Point:** Jesus really, really loves us. He gave us the Sabbath because He wants us to remember how much He loves us and wants us to spend time with Him. Today we’ll learn of three good things Jesus did on Sabbath to pass on love, kindness, and joy, a little like we’ve passed the parcel on.

Say the **Big Idea** together: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything!** [Jump up with one fist in the air.] **He gives us the Sabbath** [make a heart-shape with both hands]**.”**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box**, which is wrapped with a ribbon. Inside the box are the following items. Invite a different child to come forward to select each item as you ask the question about each one, **“What do you think this object might want to tell us about the Sabbath?”**

- A small Bible (or picture of one) = Time with God
- A family photo = Family time
- A real flower or leaf = Enjoying God’s beautiful nature
- A small pillow or a picture of someone relaxing in nature = Rest from work
- An instrument or a songbook/hymnal = Worshipping God

**What is one thing you especially love about the Sabbath?**

**Teaching Point:** Jesus made the Sabbath so we can rest from our work, remember that He’s our Creator, be close to Him, and do good for others.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Helping Hands.** Invite the children to make a craft to remind them to use their hands to pass on Jesus’ love to others—just like our “Pass the Parcel” game. As they work, discuss the following question with lots of prompts.

- **How can you show someone kindness like Jesus did, on Sabbath?** (Smile, be cheerful, visit someone who is lonely, share a Bible story or verse, invite them to Sabbath School, give a gift, sing or play a musical instrument for them, etc.)

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Thank the children for praying for missionary builders and makers this last week. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **pray each day this week for the farmers and businesspeople who are missionaries. What could you grow or give to help others learn about Jesus?**

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Distribute the ribbon sticks from the story time. Share how today we learned that Jesus gave us the Sabbath day. We learned that He helped others and shared His love and kindness with them on Sabbath.

Let’s be like Jesus and make this Sabbath (today) a delight for others. Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus can do anything. He gives us the Sabbath.** Then wave the ribbon sticks and call out **“Happy Sabbath!”**

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Sing **“Jesus Can.”** Share that next week we’ll learn about someone else Jesus healed on the Sabbath.
