_Whoosh!_ The wind blew strongly through the sandy desert. It whistled in from the sea, over hills and through valleys, until it reached the Israelites’ camp. The dust swirled, and the tents flapped. Then the sky filled with flapping birds, carried on the wind. These little birds were called quail.

Thousands and thousands of quail flew straight into the Israelites’ camp. The quail were everywhere! “There were quail a day’s walk in any direction” (Numbers 11:31, ICB). God had given them far more than they could ever need.

The Israelites watched the quail landing all around their tents. They ran around excitedly. They hadn’t eaten meat in such a long time.

They filled their baskets, their bags, and their tents with birds. They filled up their tummies, too. But they didn’t stop. As the sun went down, they kept on gathering quail. They wanted more, and more, and more! The Israelites didn’t even sleep that night, they were so busy collecting quail.

Finally, all the quail had been gathered. It was time to feast! As they began greedily gobbling up the food, they suddenly became very sick. Their skin burned with fevers, and they felt so unwell.

Their greediness disappointed God. Their overeating showed they didn’t trust Him as their provider. What does the Bible tell us God will do for us? **Read Philippians 4:19.**

If only the Israelites had been more sensible and patient. They should have eaten the healthy manna God had already provided for them.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think the Israelites were so greedy?
- What are some things you have wanted that you didn’t need or weren’t good for you?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you trust Him to give you what you need, not just what you want.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
For your next meal, thank God for providing healthy food. Make an effort to eat and enjoy the food slowly together. See how many times you can chew each bite: 10, 20 times? When you’re finished, pray for those who may not have enough food to eat right now.
