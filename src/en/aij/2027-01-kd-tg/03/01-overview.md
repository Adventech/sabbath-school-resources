**Word of the Week**

**Mighty:** Mighty means having or showing great power. Our God is mighty—He is all-powerful and strong. He made the heavens and the earth. There is nothing our mighty God cannot do!

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

God sent Moses and Aaron to tell Pharaoh to let His people go, but Pharaoh refused to listen. He made life harder for the poor slaves. God promised to show His power and to save His people. God then sent nine plagues: water to blood; frogs; lice; flies; animal sickness; boils; hail; locusts; darkness. God protected His people and their land and animals. Each plague gave Pharaoh and the Egyptians a chance to see God’s might, believe in Him, and be saved. Some Egyptians did obey God’s warnings; most did not. Despite the clear signs of God’s power and care, Pharaoh would not believe; He would not let the Israelites go.
