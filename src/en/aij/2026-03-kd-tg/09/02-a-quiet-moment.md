**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever had a problem that seemed to have no solution? Something you felt powerless to do anything about?

That’s how the family in today’s story would have felt. The Bible doesn’t tell us how or why it happened. Perhaps there were more wedding guests than expected, or the family simply didn’t have enough money for more grape juice—but whatever the case, the results were the same: embarrassment, humiliation, and shame that would last for a long time.

Jesus hadn’t fully embarked on His public ministry yet, but He cared enough about this family to come to their aid. His first miracle wasn’t as big as multiplying food for thousands or stilling a raging storm. Instead, it was something seemingly small—providing the best possible grape juice for a family gathering. Through this miracle, Jesus showed that He is interested in every detail of each person’s life.

Jesus cares about you and your family. He cares about the challenges you are facing. Just as His heart went out to the embarrassed family in Cana, His heart goes out to us when we find ourselves in impossible situations. And the good news is that He has a solution. It may not be exactly what we are expecting, but He will be there, working behind the scenes. Even a family embarrassment can become a blessing for the whole community when we allow Jesus to take charge.

“Our heavenly Father has a thousand ways to provide for us of which we know nothing. Those who accept the one principle of making the service and honor of God supreme will find perplexities vanish and a plain path before their feet” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 330).
