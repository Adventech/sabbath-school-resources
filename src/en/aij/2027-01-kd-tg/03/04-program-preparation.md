Source suggested **storytelling props**: a **large stick** as Aaron’s rod, **toy snake/string**, **red food coloring** and **bottle of water**, **toy animals** (horse, donkey, camel, cow, or sheep), **red circle stickers**, **cotton balls**, **thick blanket**.

Use peg people (Moses, Aaron, Pharaoh) or a felt set, **^[week 3 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: **blindfolds**, **red stickers from story**.
