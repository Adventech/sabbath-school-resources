Moses walked quickly past the palace guards at the gates and hurried on toward Goshen. He had boldly warned Pharaoh. Now it was time to tell the Israelites. Arriving in Goshen, Moses quickly called the people together and shared God’s wonderful message: they would soon be leaving Egypt for good!

Excitement rippled through the crowd. Free at last! But to be delivered by God, they would need to follow His instructions very carefully. Each family was to select a perfect little lamb. Four days later, at twilight, the father in each family was to sacrifice the lamb and save some of its blood. Then he must use a hyssop branch to sprinkle the blood on the sides and top of the doorframe of his house. After that, everyone must stay inside together.

As the people silently listened, Moses continued: “The Lord will go through Egypt to kill the Egyptians. He will see the blood on the sides and tops of the doorframes. Then the Lord will pass over that house. He will not let the one who brings death come into your houses and kill you” (Exodus 12:23, ICB).

A murmur of understanding rumbled through the crowd. The blood of the lamb would be a sign of their faith in God’s promise. The destroying angel that God would send would see it and fly on over. By trusting in God, they would be safe!

Just a few short weeks before, Moses’ words would have terrified the Israelites and made their knees knock together! But God had shown His mighty power again and again as each plague fell on Egypt. The river of blood, the leaping frogs, the buzzing flies, the munching locusts, and the massive hailstones had grown their faith in God.

Now they would trust God with their lives. He had protected them, and they believed He would continue to protect them. Doing what God asked would show Him the depth of their trust, and their growing love for Him.

When we experience God’s protection and see His mighty power working in our lives, we will want to obey Him too.
