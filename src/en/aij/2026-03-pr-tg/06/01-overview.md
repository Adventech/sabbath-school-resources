**Word of the Week**

**Repent:** Repent means to be sorry for the wrong things you have done and seek forgiveness for your sins. To repent is to change directions, away from sin and toward God—like making a full U-turn on the road. Once a person has repented, they will live peacefully with God and other people.

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

John the Baptist had a very important work: to prepare people’s hearts for the soon coming of Jesus, the promised Messiah. John prepared to teach by spending quiet time with God and His Word in the desert. Then he called all people to repent. The Holy Spirit touched their hearts, and many said they were sorry for their sins and were baptized to show their need for God and His power to forgive. God had forgiven them and given them new, clean hearts. John taught the people to now live out this love with kindness toward others. One day, John saw Jesus and told everyone He was the Messiah, the Lamb of God. Jesus asked to be baptized as our example. The Holy Spirit came down as a dove and God declared that this was His Son, with whom He was well pleased.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God wants to prepare their heart to receive Jesus.
- **Feel** they can let God into their hearts to take away sin.
- **Respond** by repenting and turning away from sin and turning toward God.
