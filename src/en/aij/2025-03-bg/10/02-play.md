Create

Find three pieces of red string or yarn and twirl them together to make a little rope like the rope Rahab used to help the two men leave Jericho.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Invite

With your child, think of someone new you can invite to Sabbath School or over for a play date. Talk about ways we can show God’s love to that family. Pray that God will help us to show them kindness.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Connect

Make a list, using simple symbols, of practical things that your child can do this week to be kind (e.g., set the table, invite another child to play at the playground, share toys with a sibling, etc.). Check off the tasks or put stickers on them when they are completed. Celebrate your acts of kindness at the end of the week.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Explore

Go for a garden or park walk, if possible, to see how you could show kindness to God’s little creatures. You could feed some birds or leave out some water in a birdbath. You might plant some flowers that attract birds or bees. You might like to pile up leaves and twigs to make a home for insects.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Pray

In family worship, reflect on times when others showed you kindness. How did we show kindness today? Invite your child to share examples. Thank God for the ways He shows kindness to us, and ask Him to help us show kindness to others.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#D85A2C", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Let us do good to all” (Galatians 6:10).
