Connect

Once the sun has set on a clear night, make a bed (if possible) outside with a picnic rug, blankets, and pillows. Snuggle up together and look at the stars. Look at how high the stars are. Jesus’ love is even higher! Share how Jesus made all the stars and knows them all by name! How amazing that the God of the universe knows your child’s name and everything about them. Sing “Jesus Loves Me” together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Create

Draw different facial expressions on pieces of paper. Draw a worried face and share how Jesus can turn our worried faces into calm, happy faces. Use a big marker to draw over the worried face and turn it into a happy face. Thank God together for caring for us.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Explore

Look for things that are high, deep, wide, and long. Put like things together (wide with wide) to show their different lengths. Share how Jesus made lots of big things, but His love is the biggest!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Role-play

Share with your child about how Jesus helps us to be kind to others. Role-play situations in which you can use kind words and actions. When you go to the playground this week, encourage your child to let others go first. When you eat, encourage your child to say “Please” and “Thank you.”

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#3379be"}}}
Pray

Model praying in front of your child, throughout the day, to show that Jesus always listens. If you find yourself frustrated or cross, pray and ask God to help you. If something happy happens, pray and give thanks to God.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “I have loved you with an everlasting love” (Jeremiah 31:3).
