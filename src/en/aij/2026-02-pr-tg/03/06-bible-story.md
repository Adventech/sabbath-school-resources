**Big Idea:** God forgives me and is always with me.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

**Pray**: Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob dragged his feet as he walked to the fields to check on the sheep. He had cheated his brother of the birthright. He had lied to his father and to God! His lies had hurt everyone—and it made him feel terrible inside.

Esau watched his brother and frowned. He was furious! _How could Jacob do this to him?_ His anger grew and grew. Then one day he said, “I will kill my brother Jacob” (Genesis 27:41).

When Rebekah heard what Esau had said, she gasped with fright! She must keep Jacob safe! Quickly she called for Jacob and told him he had to leave home. He was to go to his Uncle Laban’s and stay there until Esau wasn’t angry anymore.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Rebekah then hurried off to Isaac’s tent. She didn’t want to worry old, frail Isaac by telling him of Esau’s cruel plan to kill his brother. Instead she told Isaac she was worried Jacob would marry a local Hittite woman if he stayed at home.

Isaac shook his head. He really didn’t want Jacob to marry someone who didn’t love God, as Esau had done. He loved Jacob and wanted the very best for his son. Even though Jacob’s lies had hurt him, Isaac had forgiven him.

Father Isaac called Jacob to his side. He told his son to go and find a wife from his mother’s family in Haran. Then Isaac blessed Jacob with the promises God had given to Grandfather Abraham, and said goodbye.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**How do you think Jacob felt when his father blessed him?** (Accept all responses. Some responses may be: relieved, loved, forgiven, sorry for what he had done.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Rebekah hugged Jacob goodbye and send him quickly on his way. Scared that Esau might hurt him, Jacob took off running. His heart pounded with fear. He felt guilty and all alone. Because of his bad choices, he had lost the trust of his family and couldn’t see his father and mother again. He even believed that he had lost God as his friend.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Do you think Jacob had lost God as his friend? What makes you say that?**

After two long days on the run, Jacob stopped. He cried out to God, telling Him how very sorry he was. He asked God to forgive him. And God did—right away!

But Jacob didn’t _feel_ any better—he still didn’t feel as though God was with him. He begged God to somehow show him that he was still his friend. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then he slumped down on the hard ground, laid his head on a stone for a pillow, and fell fast asleep ^[(large stone)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

God answered his prayer by giving him a beautiful dream.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jacob see in the dream? Read Genesis 28:12.** (A ladder reaching from earth to heaven and angels going up and down it.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The Lord stood at the top of the shining ladder. (The ladder represented Jesus, who would come to this earth to save us from our sins and reconnect us to God.) He spoke the promises to Jacob that He had given to Abraham and Isaac! God would give him a big, blessed family—like the dust of the earth—and all families would be blessed through him because Jesus would be born into his family line.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What very special promise did God give him next? Read Genesis 28:15.** (God would be with Jacob wherever he went.)

Jacob woke up in the stillness of the night. Oh, what a dream! He had seen God’s angels. He was truly loved and forgiven. The God of heaven and earth was with him!

He jumped to his feet. His loneliness and guilt were all gone. With joy he thanked and praised God. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then he took the stone he had used as a pillow and set it up as a pillar to remember this special dream about God’s love and forgiveness ^[(show stone)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
