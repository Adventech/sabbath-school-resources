**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask if any of the children remember whose story we’ve been studying the past few weeks. Encourage them to share any details they remember.

**Word Jigsaw Match:** Give each child one piece of the word jigsaw and invite them to go to each other and try to match the difficult situation in Joseph’s life to the solution God provided. Depending on the group size, you may need to give children more than one piece. For large classes, you will need to print all 14 pieces more than once.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Ask:

- **Did Joseph see the solution (matching jigsaw piece) to his trials right away?** (No, he didn’t see the result of some situations until many years later.) Discuss the fact that we may not understand how some things fit together in our lives for good until we get to heaven.

Explain that our story today is about how God cared for Joseph through good and bad times and provided for his needs in advance.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or the **Program PowerPoint**: **God sees the end from the beginning and can turn difficult experiences into blessings.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Show the children your **three sacks of grain**, in which you’ve hidden the star, heart, and ladder **symbol cards**. Invite three children to come forward to search for the cards within the sacks. Place these on the floor in three different places around the classroom.

Ask the children, in pairs, to move from one symbol card to the next to discuss what each one has taught us so far in the stories of Abraham, Jacob, and Joseph. Specifically, they should discuss God’s leading of this family.

Then, as a group, invite the children to briefly share their ideas before reading the following verses and discussing the questions together.

1. **Star.** Genesis 15:5: **What did God promise this family?** (To multiply their descendants like the stars.)
2. **Heart.** Genesis 12:2, 3: **What did God promise Abraham?** (To bless him and all people through his family.)
3. **Ladder.** Genesis 28:13-15: **What was the purpose of Jacob’s ladder?** (The ladder represented Jesus, who would one day come to earth to save us and connect us with God. God would bless the world through Jacob’s family—through Jesus!)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Together with all the children, discuss:

- **Did God keep His promises?** (Yes. All of them.)
- **Think about Joseph’s life. What were the hardest moments for him?**
- **Where, in Joseph’s life, can you see that God was always faithful?**

God is always faithful, even when it seems as if He’s silent through the hard times in our lives. For Joseph, those hard times lasted years (while he was in prison, for example), yet when he looked back over his life, God so clearly led Joseph through the valleys (hard) and mountaintops (good) experiences.

Ellen White says, “God never leads His children otherwise than they would choose to be led, if they could see the end from the beginning, and discern the glory of the purpose which they are fulfilling as coworkers with Him” (_The Desire of Ages_, pp. 224, 225).

God had a BIG plan for His chosen people, the Israelites, and He worked through Joseph’s life to make some of those plans happen. God wants for all people to know and love Him. He’s wanted this all throughout the ages of time . . . way down to you, now. (Hold up your open Bible.) God’s Bible is full of stories and promises that are true and trustworthy. We can depend on God. He will always keep His promises.

Invite the children to **recite their memory verse**, if they can.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to stand up again. Give each child a puzzle piece without showing them a picture of the finished puzzle. Give the children a few minutes to see if they can put the puzzle together.

- **Was it hard to do the puzzle without seeing the picture?**

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **Did Joseph know how his life was going to turn out and what it was going to look like?** (No.)
- **Why was he able to stay cheerful and kind while going through the ups and downs?** (He trusted God. He knew God is faithful.)
