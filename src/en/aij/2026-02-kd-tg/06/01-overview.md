**Word of the Week**

**Care:** Care means to be really interested in something or someone. Care also means to look after. God cares about you. He thinks you’re very important, and He’s interested in everything you say, think, and do. He also cares for you—He looks after you with great love. He protects, comforts, and helps you every day.

**Lesson Summary**

In the place God had sent Jacob his ladder dream, God reminded Jacob that He would care for his family. A sadness followed this encouragement. Benjamin was born to Rachel, but she died. God cared for sad Jacob and Joseph. Joseph grew up to love God and be honest when his brothers were not. Jacob made Joseph a colorful coat to show his love. His brothers were jealous, hateful, and mean. God cared for Joseph. God sent Joseph two dreams to show that He would be the family leader. His brothers were furious. Jacob was puzzled. They could trust God, for He cared for their whole family.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** they can trust God to be with them.
- **Feel** that God cares deeply for them.
- **Respond** by thanking God for His loving care toward them.
