**Focus:** When we share our faith in Jesus, faith grows.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Eastern Europe** on your **globe** or **world map**.

A humble church member in Eastern Europe wanted to be a missionary, but he did not have the gift of preaching. He was too shy and didn’t talk a lot.

So he packed a small suitcase and took a bus to a small, remote village. In the middle of the village he set up a chair and put on a white coat. ^[(Put a chair in front of the room. Put an oversized white shirt on a child and have the child stand by the chair.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Holding out his comb and scissors ^[(have the child hold out comb and scissors)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}), he invited the children to come for a free haircut.

Because the families were poor, the children did not often get nice haircuts. So the children quickly got permission and came running for a haircut. While they sat still for him to cut, the barber told them a Bible story. When they went home, the children had not only their nice haircuts but also Bible stories to tell.

The next day many parents came. The barber gave them free haircuts and told them stories. By serving a need, the humble missionary barber made friends for Jesus. After a while a new church was planted in that village.
