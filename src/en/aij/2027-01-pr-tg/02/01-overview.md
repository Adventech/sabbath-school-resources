**Lesson Summary**

After Moses killed an Egyptian, God directed him to the safety of Midian, where he became a humble shepherd. In the solitude of the great outdoors, God was able to continue to prepare Moses to lead the Israelites out of Egypt. This lesson explores the many ways God prepared Moses; how He asked him to lead Israel out of Egypt at the burning bush; and how God patiently encouraged and helped Moses to obey, despite his reluctance.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God will always help us accept and do something He has asked us to do.
- **Feel** a desire to humbly rely on God’s help when He asks us to do something for Him.
- **Respond** by being willing and ready to do as God asks.
