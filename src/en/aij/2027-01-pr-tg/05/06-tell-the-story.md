**Big Idea:** God is the most trustworthy leader of all.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- Use your facial expressions and body language dramatically to express the range of emotions experienced by the different characters.
- You may like to use the following props: **white sheet, stick/staff, “walls of water” from Early Arrivals activity or two blue sheets**.
- Ask the children to **open their Bibles** to Psalms and Exodus and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.
- Note: Before looking up the Exodus Bible verse, highlight how the word “Exodus” means a large departure of people. So Exodus, the second book of the Old Testament, is the story of the Israelites departure from Egypt to the Promised Land of Canaan.

```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The children of Israel walked as quickly as they could, away from Egypt. They were no longer slaves—God had set them free! They were finally on their way to the Promised Land. Through the night they quickly walked, away from Pharaoh.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As the sun rose, they continued to walk as quickly as they could. On and on they walked through the day. It was hot—really hot in the desert! When night fell, so did the temperature. It was very dark and cold at night! This was no fun at all.

God knew how much His people needed protection and encouragement in the wilderness, so He did something wonderful.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He do? Read Psalm 105:39.** (He sent a pillar of cloud to cover them during the day and a pillar of fire at night.) ^[(Hold white sheet over children for “protection.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At night the Israelites rested comfortably under the glowing warm cloud God had spread above them. During the day God’s special cloud gave moisture and shade from the burning desert heat. And best of all, God was in the cloud. No matter where they were, they could always see God’s protective presence and know that He was with them.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God used the cloud to lead them. When the cloud moved, they would trust and follow.

Early one morning God’s cloud moved, and the people packed up and followed. But instead of going straight toward Canaan, God’s cloud led them south. The people didn’t know why, but they followed. They were learning to trust God as their leader. God’s cloud stopped at the Red Sea. Here the people camped, with rugged mountains on either side.

Meanwhile, back in Egypt, Pharaoh was fuming! He was downright mad that his slaves weren’t there to rebuild and replant his broken cities and farms. He wanted them back now!

Quickly he called his troops together. Taking 600 chariots, they sped off in a cloud of dust toward the Israelites.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When the Israelites saw them approaching, they were filled with terror! They looked at the Red Sea ahead, the towering mountains all around, and the Egyptians behind. Oh, no—they were trapped!

Overcome with fear, the people screamed and cried out to Moses.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did they say to Him? Read Exodus 14:11, 12.** (Why did you bring us out of Egypt to die in the wilderness? We were better off there.)

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What had they forgotten?** (That God was their leader. They had forgotten His power, miracles, and care. They had not been better off as slaves.)

God was their real leader, and He was still in control. Moses stood calmly before the people.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did he say to them? Recite your memory verse, then read the next verse, Exodus 14:14, as well.** (God would fight for them, so they could be at peace.)

They could trust in their mighty, loving God!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As the Egyptian army charged closer, God’s great cloud rose into the sky, passed over the Israelites, and came down between them and the Egyptians. ^[(Hold up the white sheet.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) On the Egyptian side the cloud created darkness. But on the Israelite side the wall of cloud gave brilliant light.

The Egyptians could go no farther. The Israelites were protected, and their hope grew! God was in that cloud, standing between them and Pharaoh’s army. What a wonderful Leader!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) At God’s command Moses stretched out his rod ^[(stick/staff)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Instantly a mighty wind blew. It carved out a deep path through the sea, ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) as the water rolled up in a wall on either side. ^[(Point to your “walls of water.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God’s glowing light followed behind them.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}
(Invite children to walk through your blue sheets, held up by volunteers.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Walking, running, skipping, and trotting, the people of Israel, and all their animals, hurried across God’s miracle path right through the middle of the Red Sea.

But wait. What was that noise behind them?

All of Pharaoh’s horses, his chariots, and his horsemen were chasing behind them!

God was watching. He was ready. Suddenly “He took off their chariot wheels, so that they drove them with difficulty” (Exodus 14:25). Crashing and smashing together, they suddenly realized that God was Israel’s leader. They cried, “The Lord fights for them.”

As soon as the last Israelite had crossed, God told Moses to stretch His rod ^[(stick/staff)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) over the water once again. Moses obeyed.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What happened? Read Exodus 14:27, 28** (The Egyptians drowned.)

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The people were safe—and free! On the banks of the Red Sea, they began to shout and sing praises to God, their trustworthy leader.

Moses wrote a beautiful song of praise that began with the words “I will sing to the Lord, for He has triumphed gloriously! The horse and its rider He has thrown into the sea!” (Exodus 15:1). Everyone was so happy to sing their love and thanks to God for saving them.
