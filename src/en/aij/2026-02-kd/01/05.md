Rebekah frowned as she looked down at her big, round tummy.

_Kick. Kick. Thud. Kick. Wobble. Thud. What is this baby doing? It will not stop moving!_

Rebekah had waited 20 years for a baby. Isaac had asked God for help, and “The Lord heard Isaac’s prayer, and Rebekah became pregnant” (Genesis 25:21, ICB).

She smiled as she felt another surprising kick in her side. God had shown such great lovingkindness. Their family was finally growing!

But the bigger her tummy grew, the bigger the kicks became. She felt as though there was an endless battle going on inside her! “_If all is well, why am I like this?_” she thought to herself (Genesis 25:22).

Rebekah talked to God about it. What did God tell her? **Read Genesis 25:23.**

She was having not one but two babies! Twins! And already those two babies weren’t getting along very happily. No wonder it felt like a wrestling competition in her tummy!

Finally the day arrived for Rebekah to give birth. The first baby born was covered in thick red hair. They named him Esau. His brother quickly followed, holding tightly onto Esau’s heel. They named him Jacob.

Rebekah and Isaac were so happy! They’d asked God for a child of their own, and He had given them two.

Like Rebekah and Isaac, your family has been given a precious gift from God: you! Your church family is grateful for you too. Let’s say these words from the Bible, “How precious is Your lovingkindness, O God!” (Psalm 36:7).
