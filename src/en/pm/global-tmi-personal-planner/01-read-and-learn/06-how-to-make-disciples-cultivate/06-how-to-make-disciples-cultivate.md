^[Go through a full series of Bible studies]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3c8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) led by you or someone else. Consider these ways to lead a ^[study]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#3c8249", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) interest into Bible truth and to continue deepening biblical knowledge throughout the disciple-making process:

- ^[Go through a series of Bible studies together in person or over video calls.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Bring your friend to a small group Bible study or evangelistic series.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Watch a series of evangelistic videos together.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Provide Bible study guides for personal study, delivering a new lesson each week.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
- ^[Send a weekly link to online Bible studies your friends can study on their own.]({"style":{"text":{"typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}})
