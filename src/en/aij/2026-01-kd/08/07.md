The sun grew dim behind the trees. “We must hurry; it is getting very dark,” said Father.

Win and her family lived in a country called Burma. They had been walking home as carefully and quickly as they could. The path wound its way through a thick forest that led to their home. Normally they walked this narrow, bumpy path in the daytime, when the sun shone brightly and they could see clearly. But today was different. They had begun their trip too late, and now they had no choice but to keep going as best they could.

It wasn’t long before Win and her family walked in complete darkness.

Suddenly her brother tripped over a small hole and fell on his knees! “Ouch, that hurt!” he moaned. Win reached out her hand and helped him up. “Are you OK?” she asked kindly. Her brother nodded and dusted himself off.

How would they keep from falling on this dangerous trail?

Then Mother had an idea. “We need to pray,” she said. Everyone stopped and huddled together. Father wrapped his arms around the children as Mother said a pleading prayer, “God, give us light to find our
way home.”

Suddenly a bright light shone on the path ahead. They could suddenly see! The grateful family happily hurried home. The light led them right to the doorstep of their house.

Safe inside their home, Mother lit a candle. In that moment the light outside disappeared. “The light was a gift from God,” Mother whispered in amazement. The family knelt down together and thanked God for hearing their prayer and lighting their path.

Win and her family will always remember that day. Whenever Win has a big problem or feels scared, she remembers God’s bright light. God promises in the Bible that “I will never leave you nor forsake you” (Hebrews 13:5b).

And just as God was with Win in the dark, and Noah on the boat, God will always, always be there with you too.
