**Word of the Week**

**Loyal:** When you are loyal to someone, nothing can stop you from being their forever friend. You can always be trusted to stand for what is true and right.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

When Abram learned that his nephew Lot and his family had been captured by enemies, he prepared for a surprise attack at night. Abram and his men freed the prisoners, collected the items that had been stolen, and went back to Sodom. They then met Melchizedek. Abram gave him a tenth of all they had recovered. Abram returned the rest to the king of Sodom and kept nothing for himself.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God is loyal.
- **Feel** grateful for loyal friends.
- **Respond** by choosing to be loyal to God, friends, and family.
