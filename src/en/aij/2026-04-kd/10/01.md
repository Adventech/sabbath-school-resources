Begging by the Road

Bartimaeus sat by the side of the busy road and tilted his head to hear. He listened to the traders chatting together as they walked into the big city of Jericho. He heard their chickens clucking and donkeys braying. He could smell their sweet dates and spices. As he listened, he could feel the warmth of the sunshine on his back. But Bartimaeus couldn’t see anything. Bartimaeus was blind.

Today many people who can’t see can live the same wonderful life as those who can see. But in Bible times, people who were blind weren’t treated the same. They didn’t have jobs. They had to beg to earn money. Bartimaeus would sit by the side of the busy road all day, asking people for money. Many people would pretend they hadn’t heard him, and they would walk quietly past. But every now and then a kind person who knew Bartimaeus and understood that he needed help, would stop and drop a coin or two with a “clink, clink” into his little pot.

God loves it when we give to others who don’t have what they need. The Bible says, “Being kind to the poor is like lending to the Lord. The Lord will reward you for what you have done” (Proverbs 19:17, ICB).
