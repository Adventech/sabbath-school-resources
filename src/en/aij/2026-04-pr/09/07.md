Glistening white, the pearl is one of God’s great undersea miracles. Pearls have been set into royal crowns, worn with diamonds, and kept as treasures by the great museums of the world. This hard, roundish jewel is simply stunning. Not only did God make the pearl beautiful, He gave it a wonderful story, too.

The pearl’s story starts with a saltwater oyster. An oyster is a shellfish with a soft body, covered by two rough, rather ugly shells that clamp tightly together. Though not good-looking, these clever creatures work hard to filter and clean the coastal areas of warm oceans, just like a pool pump.

To eat, and to filter water, the oyster opens its shells slightly. Occasionally a grain of sand, salt, or particle of dust gets carried in by the water too. This hurts the oyster and it goes into defense mode. It produces a smooth liquid called nacre and covers the “intruder” (sand/salt/dust) to stop the pain and protect itself. Gradually the oyster adds layer after layer until a pearl is formed.

The inside of the shell, called mother-of-pearl, turns shimmering colors, but it is the pearl that is the true beauty, hiding within.

In life there are many things that hurt and irritate us, just like the oyster. The man in this week’s lesson suffered from his illness for 38 years. Jesus saw past his hurting body and lonely heart. He saw a precious pearl inside the suffering man. When Jesus loved him and God’s power healed him, the beautiful pearl could be seen.

Jesus sees a beautiful pearl inside you too. He sees past your mistakes and failures, and He offers you His love and grace. When He heals your heart, the beautiful pearl inside you can be seen by others.

^[Eco Fact:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Sadly, the oysters that make pearls have been overhunted, and “natural” pearls are considered extinct. Pearls are now made, or “cultured” in ocean farms by injecting an irritant into an oyster. The loss of natural pearl oysters, who filter and clean the water, hurts the whole ocean system.

^[Bible Fact:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}}) Jesus compared a precious pearl to the kingdom of God. **Read about it in Matthew 13:45, 46.**
