Once again, it was the Sabbath day, the best day of the week! Jesus entered the synagogue and began to teach about God’s love. While He was teaching, He noticed a man who had a disability in his right hand.

Jesus paused. He wanted to show everyone that Sabbath is a day to help people; a day to let them feel the love and power of God. He looked toward the man and said, “Arise and stand here” (Luke 6:8). The man quietly obeyed.

The Pharisees frowned and stared, watching closely to see if Jesus would break their Sabbath rules. Jesus knew exactly what they were thinking. So while the man stood waiting, Jesus asked the Pharisees some questions. “I ask you, which is it right to do on the Sabbath day; to do good, or to do evil? Is it right to save a life or to destroy one?” (Luke 6:9, ICB). What else did Jesus say to them? **Read Matthew 12:11, 12.**

The Pharisees looked at Jesus in stony silence, for they knew He had spoken the truth. They would save an animal on the Sabbath, but leave a man to suffer. And they had taught the people to do the same. Their twisted thinking and lack of compassion made Jesus very sad. He turned to the waiting man and said, “Stretch out your hand” (Luke 6:10). Instantly, miraculously, it was healed! Now he would be able to tie his sandal laces and hold a jar of water!

While the amazed man wiggled his fingers and stared at his healthy hand, the rabbis stormed out of the synagogue with stubborn hearts. But once again, the rest of the people began to understand the truth about God’s love and the Sabbath He had created.

God tells us in his Word, “Do not let anyone fool you by telling you things that are not true” (Ephesians 5:6, ICB). We can always trust God’s truth as we read it in the Bible.
