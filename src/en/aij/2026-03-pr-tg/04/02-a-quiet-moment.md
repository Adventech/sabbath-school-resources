**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Have you ever taken a road trip to an unfamiliar place, and lost your way? You may slow, stop, and try to get your bearings, but it’s still unclear which way to go. If you continue on this path, you may go further in the wrong direction. If you turn around, you will lose time and may still end up lost.

In the journey of life, it can be just as hard to find the right path. But we need not travel alone. God offers to be our guide. He has mapped out a unique purpose for each of us and He invites us to trust in His direction and care.

“For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works, which God prepared beforehand that we should walk in them” (Ephesians 2:10).

God had amazing plans for Mary, Joseph, and little Jesus. He guided them through threat, culture shock, and change. And His hand was over the Wise Men who had similarly put their whole trust in Him.

He has a wonderful journey mapped out for you too. You can trust Him with the big decisions—where to live, what job to do, how to educate your children—and each smaller one. The Bible says, “I will instruct you and teach you in the way you should go; I will guide you with My eye” (Psalm 32:8).

As you and the children explore this week’s story together, may you learn to trust Him more. He is the only One who will faithfully guide and teach us in the way we should go.
