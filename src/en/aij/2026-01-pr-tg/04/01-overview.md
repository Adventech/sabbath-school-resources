{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the harmony that existed in heaven and how it was shattered when Lucifer allowed pride and jealousy to grow in his heart. Throughout Lucifer’s campaign against God, all the angels were given the choice to believe either him or God. Just as He did the angels in heaven, God gives us the freedom to choose because He loves us so much.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God will never force anyone to love Him; He will always let us choose.
- **Feel** a desire to respond to God’s love and choose His ways.
- **Respond** by choosing to give their hearts to God every morning.
