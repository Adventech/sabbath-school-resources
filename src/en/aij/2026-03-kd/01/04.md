Mary had very exciting news. An angel had just told her she would be you? the mother of the Son of God, the Savior of the world! Mary couldn’t wait to tell someone. And she knew just whom to tell. Mary “got up and went quickly to a town in the mountains of Judea” (Luke 1:39, ICB).

Who was she going to visit? **Read Luke 1:40.**

As soon as Mary stepped through the front door, she happily greeted Elizabeth. The moment she spoke, Elizabeth was filled with the Holy Spirit. The baby inside Elizabeth’s round tummy leaped and wriggled with joy. Instantly Elizabeth knew that the Savior of the world was growing inside of Mary!

“God has blessed you more than any other woman,” Elizabeth said. “You are the mother of my Lord” (Luke 1:42, 43, ICB).

The two women hugged each other tightly and smiled with joy.

“My soul praises the Lord; my heart is happy because God is my Savior,” Mary said to Elizabeth. “God has done what he promised” (Luke 1:46, 55).

Yes! God’s plan was happening—Jesus was on the way. Aren’t you glad? For “He will save His people from their sins” (Matthew 1:21).
