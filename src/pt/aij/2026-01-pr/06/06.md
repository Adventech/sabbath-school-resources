Even depois o que Cain had Façane, Deus did something wonderful para ele. Deus listened quietly while Cain complained that Ele wasn’t being fair. Cain grumbled that ele didn’t want a work harder em the field, ou roam the earth, ou be hidden de Deus’s face. Mas more than that, ele really didn’t want someone a kill ele. E ele was very scared eles would, para hadn’t ele just killed seu brother? Ele didn’t even realize Deus had sent ele a roam the earth a ajude protect ele!

Imagine complaining a Deus like that! Deus wants nos a be honest quando nós talk a Ele, mas nós also need a be respectful. Como did Jesus teach Seu friends a start seu Oração a Deus? **Leia Mateus 6:9.**

Even depois listening a todos that grumbling, Deus replied com grande kindness. Ele put a mark de protection em Cain então that ele would not be attacked. Se anyone did try a kill Cain, eles would be punished.

Como loving is nosso Deus! Nós call that especial kind de amor “mercy,” para Deus was kind e forgiving quando Cain wasn’t sorry, ou honest, ou respectful. Cain surely didn’t deserve Deus’s mercy. Mas Deus deu isso anyway.
