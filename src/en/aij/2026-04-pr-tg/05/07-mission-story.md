Water of Life

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#F4793B"}}}
**Focus:** Compassion opens up a way for others to learn about Jesus.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Thailand** and **Myanmar** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Janiz watched the children set off with a bucket. They would need to walk a long way just to find fresh water. Then they would need to walk all the way back, carrying the heavy bucket.

Janiz was part of a group of 32 student missionaries to Thailand and Myanmar. They had come from an Adventist college and academy in Hong Kong and South Korea.

The students had all worked very hard back in their hometowns to raise money to help the people here. They had already helped fix up a kindergarten and had been teaching children music.

Now as Janiz watched an older woman carrying a heavy jar of water home, she knew another way to be helpful. Her heart was full of compassion for the families who lived across the border in Myanmar. They had to walk one mile (1.6 kilometers) to get water—back and forth, back and forth, every day.

The students decided to donate the rest of their money for the mission trip to lay a water pipeline to the village.

The villagers weren’t Christians, and they didn’t want anything to do with Christianity. But when they saw this simple act of kindness by Christians, they wanted a school for their children.

At the invitation of the villagers, the Seventh-day Adventist Church opened a school in the village in June 2016. The school now has more than 40 students—every school-age child in the village attends, as do several children from neighboring villages.

The village is so happy they have a water system and an Adventist school through the kindness and compassion of the missionary students.
