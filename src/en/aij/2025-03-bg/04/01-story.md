Moses marched out of Egypt with a big smile. March, march, march. God had set all His people free! They didn’t have to stay in Egypt with the mean king anymore. The people marched behind Moses. March, march, march. There were more people than you could count! Mooing cows and noisy sheep _(moo, baa)_ walked with them. God went with them too! He led them by a cloud in the day that gave them shade from the hot sun. At night the cloud turned into fire that kept them warm. This was God’s way of saying, “Follow Me! I will look after you” _(cuddle your child)._\
_March, march, march_ went the people _(march with fingers on the page)._ They marched until they came to the Red Sea. It was big! It was wide! They couldn’t swim through it. They couldn’t jump over it. And they had no boats. Behind them they could see a cloud of dust in the distance. Oh, no! Pharaoh was coming to take them back to Egypt! The people were afraid. They forgot about God’s cloud. They forgot God was leading them.

But Moses stayed calm. He trusted in God. He said, “Don’t be afraid! Stand still and see the Lord save you today” (Exodus 14:13, ICB). God told Moses to raise his walking stick over the water _(hold your arm up in the air)._ Then something happened. God sent a great wind to blow _(whoosh, whoosh)._ It piled up water on one side and then the other. God opened up a path in the middle of the sea!\
God’s people looked at each other with big eyes and open mouths! _(Can you make a surprised face?)_ God was so good! All the people marched through the sea on dry ground. March, march, march. Little children sat on their daddy’s shoulders and pointed at the walls of water—maybe they’d see a fish! Mommies held their babies close. Aunties, uncles, grandmas, and grandpas all walked quickly to the other side. The cows and sheep followed behind.

All of God’s people made it safely to the other side! _(Hooray!)_ Then God covered up the path with water once again. He did not let Pharaoh hurt them. God’s people were free! They sang to God and clapped their hands. God had looked after them! He would lead them to a special place—the Promised Land.\
Thank You, God, for leading the Israelites through the Red Sea. _(Say together)_ And thank You, God, for leading me!
