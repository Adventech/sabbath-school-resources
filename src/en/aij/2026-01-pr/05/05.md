Before Adam and Eve left Eden, the Bible says that “God made tunics of skin, and clothed them” (Genesis 3:21). This was the first time they had ever seen an animal friend die. It must have made them so very sad!

In the middle of all the sorrow, God shared a beautiful promise of hope and rescue with Adam and Eve. God would not let sin win! He says in Genesis 3:15 that someone will come to crush Satan and destroy all evil forever. God is talking to Satan, and He says that someone will come to crush Satan. This person, “He” in the verse, will be wounded but will destroy all evil forever.

God had a rescue plan—He would send someone to save them! Feeling miserable and fearing their own death, Adam and Eve needed to hear His promise. They needed to know that though they’d made their big mistake, God had a special way to fix it.

They couldn’t see what was happening in heaven. But moved by great love for them, Jesus had said to His Father, “Let the punishment fall on Me. I will stand in man’s place. He shall have another chance” (Ellen G. White, _The Faith I Live By_, p. 75).

Wow! It would be hard for God the Father to let His Son come to earth to stand in place of all people and die for their sins. But He knew Jesus was the very best His loving heart could give. And so this became the plan and the promise.

God used different words to share it with Adam and Eve, but they understood, and kept it in their hearts. They would always feel sad when they thought about what they had done, but God’s loving promise filled them with hope and a future. When Adam and Eve sinned, they passed the consequences of their sin on to everyone, so we all need God’s plan. He knows how much hope this amazing promise will give to His children all over the world, so the Bible records it in a special way. Read how it is written in John 3:16.
