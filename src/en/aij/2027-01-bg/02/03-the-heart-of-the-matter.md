What does it mean to be a friend? It has been said, “A friend is someone who understands your past, believes in your future, and accepts you just the way you are.” This is indeed the description of the friendship God offers each one of us. He knows everything there is to know about you. He does not turn away from the ugly details that may cloud your past or the brokenness that is part of your present. He knows the hurt, the joys, the struggles, and the victories of your life. He has a plan for your future, one that exceeds your past or your current situation. He sees who He made you to be, His beloved child. He calls you “friend” _(John 15:14)_.

God not only calls us friends, but He calls us to be friends to one another. He designed us to live in community, “bearing with one another, and forgiving one another” _(Colossians 3:12-14)_. We are to “comfort each other and edify one another” _(1 Thessalonians 5:11)_, and “above all things, have fervent love for one another” _(1 Peter 4:8)_.

God brings different people into our lives at just the right moment. Some may remain for a lifetime, while others for only a season. Each friendship is a precious gift from God, to be tended and cared for with love.

The characteristics your little one is learning now about friendship are ones that will shape them into being a good friend and learning to wisely choose good friends for themselves. Demonstrate through your own actions and boundaries what healthy friendships look like. Above all, remember that God has called you His friend.

> <callout>John 15:12-15</callout>
> “This is my commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you. . . . No longer do I call you servants, for a servant does not know what his master is doing; but I have called you friends.”
