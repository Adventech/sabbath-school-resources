The sun shone brightly through the window of the ark. Noah smiled as the sun warmed his face. All was still and quiet on the ark. There was no more drumming rain! After 40 days and nights of rain, it had stopped. God had done what He had promised. What a good Friend! Now Noah could hear the cats purr _(purr)_, the rabbits snuffle in the hay _(snuffle)_, and the doves coo in their nests _(coo)_. The ark floated gently over the waters.\
_Whoosh, whoosh!_ A big wind began to blow. God sent it to chase the water away. He knew what was best. He would care for Noah, his family, and all the animals. The wind blew and blew _(blow with child)_ as the boat sailed on the sea. The water went down, down _(start over head, move hand down)_. Still the big boat rocked on the waves. The wind blew and blew some more _(blow)_. The water went down, down _(hand lower). Thud! (Big clap.)_ The boat came to a stop on a mountain top. No more rocking up and down. Water was all around, but they were safe on dry ground.

The wind blew and blew _(blow)_. The water went down, down _(hand lower)_. Noah and his family waited for the water to go away. As they waited, they fed the colorful toucans, the fluffy mice, and woolly bison. They gave the bears berries and fed crunchy carrots to the rabbits. The wind blew and blew _(blow)_. The water went down some more _(hand lower)_.\
Noah could see rocks all around. Were trees growing? Was there a place to live? Noah knew how to find out. He opened the window and sent out a big black bird, a raven. The bird flew and flew, but she had nowhere to land. She flew back to the safe ark.\
The wind blew and blew _(blow)_. Noah waited. Then he tried again. He opened the window for a gentle white dove. She flew and flew, but still there was nowhere to land. There were no trees for a nest. Noah waited. God was slowly drying up all the water. Then Noah opened the window again. The dove flew out. Up, up, up into the sky she flew. Then out of sight. Would she find a tree? Noah hoped so! He would trust God as he waited to see.

Little dove flew to Noah. She held an olive leaf! The trees had started to grow. Soon there would be a place for the birds to build their nests. Noah waited. He trusted God.\
The water went down, down. Noah opened the window once more for the little dove. She flew and flew. Noah watched and waited _(hand above eyes)_. The dove didn’t come back. She had built a nest! The ground would soon be all dry. God would open the very big door when there was also a place for Noah and the animals to live once more.\
_(Say together)_ Thank You, God, that You know what’s best! Thank You for taking care of everyone on the ark.\
I choose to trust You too. Thank You for caring for me!
