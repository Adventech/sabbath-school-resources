**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It had been 12 years. Twelve long years of waiting in pain, hoping for healing. Nothing had worked, and her sickness had become part of her identity.

But one day hope flickered in her heart as she heard His name.

“Jesus.”

She decided to seek Him and pushed through the crowd to find Jesus. She reached out, her fingertips brushing the hem of His robe. Just a thread, just a touch—it would be enough. Power surged through her as healing came. Then Jesus told her, “Daughter, your faith has made you well. Go in peace, and be healed of your affliction” (Mark 5:34).

Whatever your affliction, Jesus wants to give you peace. Today you can reach out in faith to Him. The smallest faith, the most tentative hand—this is enough. Jesus sees and He meets our needs.

As you and the children explore this week’s story, may you be drawn to Him. Know that He loves us and that when we so much as reach out to Him, He is always there, willing and wanting to help us.
