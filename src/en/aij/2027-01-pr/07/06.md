Thousands and thousands and thousands of little gray birds blew in from the sea and across the sandy wilderness toward the Israelites’ camp. God was sending exactly what the grumblers wanted: meat to eat. Birds filled the sky in all directions. How close did God let them come to camp? **Read Numbers 11:31.**

As the quail fluttered in the air, the excited Israelites hitched up their robes and raced out toward them. All day, all night, and all the next day the Israelites greedily caught quail and gorged themselves on it. What couldn’t be eaten on the spot they spread out in the sun to dry.

Then whenever they felt like it, the greedy Israelites feasted on quail until their bellies were full to bursting. Their greediness made their tummies sick and their bodies burn with fever. Some of them even died. By the end of the month those who survived hated the very sight of quail.

God’s people learned the hard way that the manna He had graciously provided was best for them all along.

Today we are surrounded by both healthy and unhealthy food choices. God graciously provides what is best, and He knows we will be strong, well, and happy when we choose healthy foods that are good for our bodies.
