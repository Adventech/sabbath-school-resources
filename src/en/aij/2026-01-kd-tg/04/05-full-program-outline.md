**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **pictures of God’s creatures**. Hold up two cards at once and invite the children to choose between two exciting things they can do in heaven with one of these animals.

Invite them to stand on your left for the first option, or your right for the second. Options are: (1) swim with a dolphin or a shark, (2) fly with a hummingbird or an eagle, (3) ride a zebra or a cheetah.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** We will be able to make lots of fun choices in heaven, and all of them will be good!

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Long, long ago the angels in heaven chose only between good things too. But then one day they had to make a hard choice—would they keep loving God, or wouldn’t they? God will never force anyone to love Him. He will always let them choose because that’s what real love does.

Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is loving. He lets me choose.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Lucifer’s Sad Choice”** in an interactive way using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal the **“God” sign**. Invite the children to stand close to the sign. Share that our choices can lead us toward or away from God. Invite everyone to all step closer to, and then away from, the **“God” sign**. Tell them you will read out some choices. Ask them to show you where that choice might lead (toward or away from God) by stepping closer or away from the **“God” sign**.

- Someone yells at you, and you yell back.
- You draw a beautiful picture and give it to your neighbor to say thank you for their help.
- Your brother takes your toy, and you hit him.
- You see some garbage on the ground, and you pick it up and put it in the trash.
- You are kind to someone who is feeling sad.
- **Where did Lucifer’s choices take Him?** (Children will move away from “God” sign to show their answer.)
- **What can you do so that your choices help you to grow closer and closer to your Friend Jesus?**

**Teaching Point:** Our choices can either grow good things or ugly things in our hearts.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He lets me choose.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE LESSON and NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** **Share the Nature Lesson:** “Seeds: Little Things Grow.”

Hold up **Nature Card 4: Bamboo Plant** and share the following facts:

- Bamboo is not a tree, but a type of strong grass. Bamboo is hollow inside the stem.
- It is one of the fastest-growing plants in the world.

The fast-growing bamboo plant reminds us of how something small can grow very big in a short time. Good choices grow good things. Give each child **Nature Card 4** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** **Craft Activity: Sunflower**. Make a sunflower with seeds and craft paper to remind the children that when we choose God, He grows good things in our hearts.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we have learned that God loves us so much that He always lets us choose.

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He lets me choose.**

God hopes we will be content and make good choices. Invite the children to ask God to help them choose Him this week so that He can help good things grow in their hearts.

- **What good things does God want to grow in you?** (Love, kindness, honesty, obedience, to be content, to be a good friend, etc.) God loves to see these things growing in you!
