**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Engage the children in a quick **walk ‘n’ talk** activity. Draw their attention to the five **Making the Right Choice cards** attached to the walls around the room. Explain that they will work their way clockwise around each of the cards and discuss what the right choice would be in **small groups**.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Briefly review each card.

- **What did each of the examples require you to be?** (Unselfish.)
- **How easy/hard is it to be unselfish?**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** As we learn more about Abram in today’s lesson, we will be reminded of our Big Idea. Read the **Big Idea** together: **When God is my friend, He will help me put others first.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF0E8", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** **Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite two children to choose one of the **two small gifts** on your serving tray. Commend a child if they let the other person choose first. Repeat the activity with pairs of children until everyone has a gift. Prompt for unselfishness, commend acceptance of a lesser gift or offers to unselfishly swap, etc.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **How natural was it for you to be unselfish and let the other person choose first?**
- **Don’t answer out loud: When choosing a gift, were you more like Abram or Lot?**
- **Why did Abram let Lot choose first?** (Abram prayed, God showed him what to do, and he obeyed.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Because of sin, we are naturally selfish. But when we are God’s friend, He will help us to be unselfish
like Him.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 11: Abram and Lot** to place in their container. Discuss the picture and have the children recite the **memory verse**. Then sing the **Scripture Song “Jeremiah 31:14.”** Share that Abram was satisfied with God’s goodness and had unselfishly put Lot first.

**^[OBJECT LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your jar of sand or dust.

- **How many grains of sand (or dust) might there be in this jar?**
- **Could you count them?**

Remind the children of God’s wonderful promise to Abram and how we are Abram’s descendants when we are God’s friends. Millions and millions of other people are too. What a privilege!
