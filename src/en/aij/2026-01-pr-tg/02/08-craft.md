**What you need:**

Week 2 craft template on white cardstock, colored pencils, scissors, glue.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/craft-template.png)

```

**Instructions:**

- Cut out two rectangles from cardstock, then glue “God the Creator” onto one of the rectangles and color.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2b.png)

- Color the rest of your Creation bookmark and cut it out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2c.png)

- Fold along the dotted lines, flipping it over each time.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2d.png)

- Glue each end on the bookmark onto each rectangle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2e.png)

- Use it as a bookmark, or unfold it to tell the story of Creation.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-01-pr-tg/02/2f.png)

Alternatively, have the children use play-dough or modeling clay to sculpt something (object or scene) to represent each of the six days of Creation.
