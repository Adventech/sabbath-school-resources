Pastor John Matteson wanted to do his best. He had traveled all the way from his home in America to a country named Norway to preach that Jesus was coming soon.

He looked around with surprise. The room was full and overflowing with people who were squeezed in tight to listen to him.

He stood up and began to talk. Suddenly some people began to shout and interrupt him. Pastor Matteson realized that not all the people were there to listen. He did his best to keep preaching with all the noise and calling out.

But then in the middle of the sermon, someone the crowd knew well stood up and started shouting horrible things at Pastor Matteson. Some of the crowd cheered him on, and others started to hiss. Excitement swept through the crowd like a wave through a stormy sea. Pastor Matteson realized that if the people didn’t calm down, someone would soon get hurt. Some men were even walking toward the window with chairs, because they wanted to smash their way out of the crowded room.

“God, please help us,” Pastor Matteson whispered.

At that moment a tall man stood up in the balcony of the hall. In a very loud voice he ordered everyone to be quiet.

The strangest thing happened. Everyone sat down and became very quiet. Pastor Matteson closed the meeting with prayer.

After the meeting Pastor Matteson asked one of the helpers to find the tall stranger. He wanted to thank him. But within minutes, the helper came back, shaking his head. The tall stranger was nowhere to be found.

Pastor Matteson understood that God had sent His angel to help him. God had been pleased to see Pastor Matteson doing His best to share the Bible and had stepped in to help, at just the right moment.
