God had called His friend Moses up Mount Sinai many times. Now it was time to share this wonderful experience with Aaron, his two sons, and “seventy of the elders of Israel” (Exodus 24:9).

Together the leaders climbed the mountain toward God’s cloud. Suddenly they “saw the God of Israel” (Exodus 24:10). They glimpsed God’s feet standing on a sapphire pavement of sparkling blue. Wow! What an amazing privilege it was to be so close to God!

Then it was Joshua’s turn to climb the steep mountain with Moses while Aaron and the 70 elders cared for the people back in the camp. Higher and higher they climbed, until they reached the edge of the thick cloud.

For six days Moses and Joshua prayed and waited for God. Then on the seventh day the cloud grew brighter and opened wide as God called Moses higher up the mountain to talk with Him.

Far below, the people saw God’s fire fill the sky. The dazzling display was a powerful reminder that God was committed to being with the people He loved so much.

High up the mountain, God wrapped Moses close to Himself in the cloud. He spoke for 40 days about many things. All that time God strengthened Moses to live without food. Toward the end of their time together, God gave Moses a very special gift. What was it? **Read Exodus 31:18.**

Imagine how precious that gift was! God’s finger had written those words so His people would always have them to treasure. God’s words showed that He was committed to helping His people remember His beautiful character of love, and that He would help them live together with love and respect for each other.

God is committed to helping us remember and live by His laws of love too. What a wonderful God.
