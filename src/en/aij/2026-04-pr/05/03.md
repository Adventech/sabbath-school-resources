The man had never heard of Jesus, but here he was, standing right in front of the miracle-working Teacher. He was deaf and unable to speak. His compassionate friends believed that Jesus could restore his hearing and his speech, so they had kindly brought him to Jesus.

Jesus led the man away from the crowd to be alone with Him. Then what did He do? **Read Mark 7:33.**

Jesus touched the hearing- and speech-impaired man so that he would be healed in a loving way that he could understand. People who are deaf use their hands and the sense of touch to communicate, so Jesus did the same. The man’s ears didn’t work, so Jesus touched them. His tongue didn’t allow him to speak well, so Jesus touched it.

But that’s not all Jesus did for the man. He looked up to heaven because He wanted the man to know he would be healed by the power of the living God who lives in heaven. Then while still gazing upward, Jesus said, “Be opened” (Mark 7:34).

Instantly, the man could hear! He could speak! Birds and music, talking and singing, became wonderful sounds in his world. He was truly blessed.

Jesus acted with compassion when He healed the man in a meaningful way that made him feel valued and loved. God has so much compassion for everyone!
