**Lesson Summary**

Having committed her life to Naomi and to Naomi’s God, Ruth followed Naomi to Bethlehem. Naomi was without money or means, and the women struggled to find food. God noticed Ruth’s needs and rewarded her loyalty. He sent her to the field of Boaz, a relative of Naomi’s, who noticed her and helped her. Boaz was in a position to restore Naomi’s land and her family’s legacy by marrying Ruth, which he did. God honored Ruth’s commitment to Him through marriage and security.
