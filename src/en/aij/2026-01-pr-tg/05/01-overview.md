**Lesson Summary**

Even though Adam and Eve made a bad choice and ate the fruit from the tree of the knowledge of good and Evil, God did not abandon them. He forgave them, and then went on to share with them the promise of the greatest gift of all: the gift of salvation through One who would one day come and die to save them.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that God will never force us to choose His way; He will always let us choose.
- **Feel** thankful that God continues to love us, even when we make a wrong choice.
- **Respond** by choosing God’s way this week.
