**Big Idea:** God’s way is best. I can follow Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 2 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Suggested props: Dress up as “Prince Moses,” wearing a **crown** and **colored cloak**. Change to “Shepherd Moses,” wearing a **head covering**, **plain cloak**, and **shepherd’s staff** (long stick). Bush you have drawn on a **large piece of cardstock**, or **potted plant**, with “flames” from **Secret Surprise Box**. **Sheep** the children made before Sabbath School. A **toy snake (or piece of string)**.
- Use peg people (Moses, shepherds) or a felt set, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- Moses was now a prince in Egypt. He never forgot God, who had saved him ^[(Prince Moses props)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Moses saw an Israelite slave being treated badly by an Egyptian soldier. He got angry and killed the soldier.
- Pharaoh was furious. Moses ran far away to Midian.
- Moses saw some unkind shepherds chase some women from the well in Midian. He calmly stood up to the bullies. God was changing him.
- Moses met Priest Jethro, the women’s father. He loved God too! Moses married his daughter Zipporah.
- Moses became a shepherd. He learned to follow God ^[(Shepherd Moses props and sheep)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- One day Moses saw a burning bush that wouldn’t burn up! ^[(Drawn tree/plant “flames” prop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- God spoke to him from the bush. Moses was to take off his sandals. It was holy ground because God was there. God wanted Moses to go to Egypt to free His people.
- Moses was scared. God promised to go with Him. He showed him signs: a stick turned into a live snake! ^[(Stick and snake props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God promised to send Aaron as a helper.
- Moses didn’t need to worry. God would make this happen. He would help Moses to be strong and follow Him.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[(Prince Moses props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses now lived in Pharaoh’s palace as a prince. He wore beautiful robes and was a powerful man. But he never forgot the things Mother Jochebed had taught him when he was little. He knew God loved him and had saved him. God gave him a strong spirit so that He would worship God and not idols. He would stand up for what was true
and right.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day Prince Moses left the royal palace and went to visit his people, the Israelites, who were slaves. Life for his people was very hard and so unfair!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) As Moses watched them work, he saw an Egyptian soldier hitting an Israelite slave. This made Moses sad and angry. He “looked all around and saw that no one was watching” (Exodus 2:12, ICB), so he hit the soldier and killed him. Oh, no! He let his anger turn into bad actions.
When Pharaoh heard what Moses had done, he was furious! Moses knew it wasn’t safe to stay in Egypt any longer. ^[(Remove crown.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He quickly ran far, far away from Egypt. Even though Moses had done a terrible wrong, God still loved him and wanted him to trust God more, so God guided Moses to the land of Midian.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was such a long journey. Finally, Moses sat down near a well and took a deep breath. He noticed some women at the well. As the women tried to pour water for their father’s thirsty sheep, some unkind “shepherds came and chased the girls away” (Exodus 2:17, ICB).

That was very mean! Strong, caring Moses stood up to those rude shepherds—he wouldn’t let them pick on others. But this time he didn’t let his anger blow up. Moses was already letting God change him.

Moses brought the women back to the well, and he gave them and their sheep a cool drink.

The women told their father, Priest Jethro, what had happened. He welcomed Moses into their home. Moses found out that this family worshipped God, just as he did! He married Zipporah, one of Jethro’s daughters.

^[(Shepherd Moses props and sheep.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) No longer was Moses a prince with a shiny crown. Instead he lived a quiet life as a shepherd, looking after his sheep. Just as his sheep followed him, Moses was learning to follow God. And God was changing him as he spent many years in the desert. God was changing him.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day Moses was walking slowly through the desert with his sheep. He stopped. What was that? ^[(Drawn tree/plant and “flames” prop.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He could see a bush covered with bright flickering flames. The bush was covered in fire, but it wasn’t burning up!

A voice called out from the bush: “Moses, Moses!”

Trembling, Moses answered, “Here I am.”

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Take off your sandals. You are standing on holy ground” (Exodus 3:5, 6, ICB). It was God speaking to him!

God told Moses that He was sending him to the king of Egypt. “Go! Bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt” (Exodus 3:10, ICB).

God wanted to help His children who were being treated so unkindly. God didn’t want them to be slaves. He wanted them to be free and happy.

But Moses was afraid of doing such a big job. God promised, “I will be with you” (Exodus 3:12, ICB). What a promise! He didn’t need to be the biggest, strongest, or smartest. He just needed to trust God and follow Him.

God said, “I will use my great power against Egypt. I will make miracles happen in that land. After I do this, he will let you go” (Exodus 3:20, ICB).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Moses was still afraid, so God gave him miracles to show the Egyptians. He told Moses to throw his staff on the ground. ^[(Use stick and snake/string props.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Instantly it became a snake! Eek! But then God told him to pick it up, and it became a stick again.

God also promised to send Aaron, Moses’ big brother, to be his helper.

Moses didn’t need to worry. God was strong and powerful. God was going to make this happen! God would help Moses to be strong and follow Him.
