**Focus:** The Superb Lyrebird reminds us how Jesus listens when we pray.

Ask: **What kinds of bird sounds have you heard before? Can you show me?**

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-kd-tg/10/nature-lesson.png)

In God’s nature there are lots of special creatures that make sounds. On the leafy forest floors of the southeast coast of Australia you will sometimes hear jumping, running, scratching, and swift movements on the ground. This sound comes from a little bird called a lyrebird. Even though they have wings, they do not often fly, and use their wings only to jump up onto short tree branches if they feel a little scared. Hiding in the leaf litter on the ground are lots of yummy foods for them to eat, such as earthworms, insects, and even centipedes. The lyrebird’s four claws on each strong foot make perfect little rakes to dig into the moist dirt to find them.

Did you know that lyrebirds have something that is super special? A male lyrebird has beautiful long tail feathers that he can raise up over his head. When he does this, they look a little like a fan of feathers. He does this to attract the female lyrebird! He busily builds a dirt mound, stands on it, and then calls out a beautiful singing sound, shaking his spectacular long tail. What a performance!

There is something that is even more amazing about these creatures. Every lyrebird has its own special song, but they can also copy other sounds they hear. A lyrebird can listen to a particular call or sound in the forest—other birds, people, chain saws, and even the click of a camera—and then they can make the sound themselves! They are really good listeners.

**Teaching Point:** Jesus made the lyrebird to be a very good listener. But Jesus is an even better listener. He always lovingly listens to us when we talk to Him. He knows the sound of our voice.
