Larissa and Rosette laughed together as they ran and played. The two girls from Burundi, in Africa, were neighbors and best friends. Best friends never want a fun day to end! But all too soon Mother’s voice was calling Larissa to come inside. It was time for family worship.

Larissa, age 8, looked at Rosette, age 7, and smiled. She said, “Come on! It’s time for worship!”

Rosette followed Larissa into the house. The family sang a song about Jesus, then Mother read a Bible story and they all prayed.

“I liked Larissa’s family worship,” said Rosette. She wanted to know more about Jesus.

Then Larissa invited her friend Rosette to come with her to Sabbath School. Rosette asked her parents. “I’m so glad they said I could go,” said Rosette. “I love Sabbath School! I like the songs we sing and the Bible stories that we hear.”

Rosette kept going to Sabbath School with her friend. One day she asked her parents to come with her. At first they didn’t want to go, but Rosette kept inviting them. Finally her mother agreed to come with her. Rosette decided to keep praying for her father to come along too.

Jesus loves it when we ask others to come and follow Him. Larissa said it was easy to invite her best friend to become a friend of Jesus’. She hopes other children will ask their friends to come to Sabbath School too. “First, pray for them,” she said. “Then invite them. If they say yes, that’s great. If they say no, you can keep praying for them and inviting them. One day they’ll be glad you asked.”
