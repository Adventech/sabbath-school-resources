**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children who arrived early to share their drawings of the best gift they’ve ever received. Talk about your own personal best gift (with a drawing). If possible, add to the gift list more, if needed, and have someone write down on separate papers the ideas mentioned.

Then share how God has given a gift that is better than ________. It’s better than a_________ and a ___________. (Progressively read out all the gifts mentioned and place them in a pile on top of each other.)

God’s gift was small but would become big—and life-changing. THIS gift is better than your biggest wish. It’s the best gift ever!

Show two gifts—one beautifully wrapped, and one “wrapped” in a wrinkled paper bag. Invite a person to come and choose which one they’d like to have. Show how the contents don’t match the wrapping.

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** You’d think that such an important gift would be given in beautiful wrapping and placed in a fancy place. But our true story from the Bible today will show us that surprisingly, this best gift came in a plain, ordinary way. But we should always remember that the _wrapping_ is **not** the **gift** (show wrapping and gift). We’ll find out more about this gift today and how it makes all the difference in our lives.

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story. Use props, sing carols within the story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[SHOW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Hold a **doll** or invite a parent with a newborn **baby** to visit your room. Share how Jesus was as small and helpless as this baby, but He would grow to do something so amazing for us.

- **What was it?** Share the clues in the memory verse, John 3:16. **What happened when God “gave His only son”?**

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** To highlight how big the gift is, have children find a gift **“envelope”** hidden in your room. It will contain sentences. Place an open **gift box** on the floor with a title on the box: “With God’s Gift of Jesus.” On the floor beside the box, place another title: “Without God’s Gift of Jesus.” Then put each statement inside or outside the box. Statements include the following:

**With God’s Gift:** heaven, being forgiven, feeling happy, feeling peace inside, knowing God loves me.

**Without God’s Gift:** being lonely, no hope in the future, no help with problems, living unforgiven, no heaven.

**Teaching Point:** Jesus is the greatest gift we can ever receive. When we accept Him into our lives and believe in Him, everything changes. His love and forgiveness give us hope, peace, happiness, and eternal life.

**^[SHARE BIG IDEA:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is God’s precious gift to me.**

**^[RESPOND:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite children to **write or draw something from this list** (or another one) that they **personally want to thank God for**—something that they now have because Jesus came into the world (His love, His salvation, His gift of peace, kindness, happiness, forgiveness, hope, heaven, etc.). They will fold it and keep it till the end of Sabbath School.

Talk about how this is probably the most well-known verse in the Bible, but what does it really mean to you? Ask the children to repeat this verse with the word “me” included throughout (“For God so loved [me] that . . . ”). Then ask them to say it again with the word “you” and another name throughout (“For God so loved [you, _______,] that . . .”).

- **What have you learned about God by saying this verse in these two different ways?**
- **Who do you know that really needs to know the message of this verse this week?**

**Option:** If time allows, teach the children the following actions to the memory verse:

For God (point upward)\
so loved (hand over heart)\
the world (make circle with hands)\
that He (point upward)\
gave (stretch out hands, as if giving something)\
His only begotten Son, (hold up one finger)\
that whoever believes (point to others)\
in Him (point upward)\
should not perish (shake head)\
but have everlasting life (jump for joy)\
John 3:16 (palms together, then open hands)

- **What in this week’s story would you want to share with that person?**

**^[RESPOND and PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Invite the children to stand in a circle and bring their pieces of paper that show something they want to thank God for. Place a colorful gift box in the middle of your circle. Share how prayer is like giving a gift back to God—our love, our gratitude, and our desire to serve Him with our actions.

Then invite the children to take turns saying something from their prayer of thanks before placing it in the box or simply silently placing their prayer inside the open gift box.

Finish by praying on behalf of the children, thanking God for the incredible gift of Jesus.
