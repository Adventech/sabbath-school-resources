Jacob’s big family rushed to gather all their things and leave for Egypt. Pots were packed, clothes and bedding were rolled up, and sheep and goats were rounded up. Soon they would see Joseph!

Along the way, Jacob stopped in a place where his father had worshipped God. Together they offered a thank-You sacrifice to God. In the night God sent him a special message. “So He said, ‘I am God, the God of your father; do not fear to go down to Egypt, for I will make of you a great nation there’ ” (Genesis 46:3).

What a loving God to remind Jacob that He was always with him. With joy the family journeyed on to their new home. How many were in the family? **Read Genesis 46:26.**

Finally, with Egypt in the distance, Jacob sent Judah ahead of them to meet Joseph.

Puffing and panting, he arrived in Egypt and excitedly told Joseph that Father Jacob and the rest of his family were only just down the road. Just down the road? Hooray! Joseph jumped on his chariot and raced toward them in a cloud of dust.

As soon as he saw Father Jacob, he jumped off the chariot and threw his arms around him. They hugged each other tightly and cried happy tears.

God had guided them back together. What a faithful Friend. “And we know that all things work together for good to those who love God” (Romans 8:28).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- God was with Jacob, Joseph, and his brothers all through their story, in good and bad times. Do you think He is with you too? Why do you say that?
- Was God’s plan for the family—the children of Israel—good? What was it?
