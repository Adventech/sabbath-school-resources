After many days of celebrations, people packed their bags. They climbed onto their donkeys and waved goodbye to the beautiful city of Jerusalem. It was time to go home.

What did Jesus do? **Read Luke 2:43.**

Mary and Joseph had been traveling home for a full day when they realized someone special was missing. They looked left. They looked right. _Where was Jesus?_

_Surely He couldn’t be far away_, they thought. He was probably just collecting water for their donkey or playing with the other children. There were many friends and relatives with them on the road back home. Yes, they thought, _Jesus must be with them._

But after some time Mary and Joseph realized that Jesus was not with them. Quickly they asked their friends and family if they had seen Jesus. But the people just shook their heads.

After searching and looking for Jesus, they realized that Jesus was not there! Their hearts pounded hard in their chests. They had been so busy walking and talking with their friends on the road home to Nazareth that they hadn’t realized that Jesus wasn’t with them.

They must find Him—quickly! Turning back toward Jerusalem, Mary and Joseph raced down the road as fast as they could. They prayed desperately for God to keep their Boy safe.

Was Jesus safe? Yes! God was with Him. Jesus had been busy in the Temple, “sitting in the midst of the teachers, both listening to them and asking them questions” (Luke 2:46).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been lost? What did you do?
- Have you ever lost something precious? Whom can you ask to help you find it?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for always helping us when we get lost or when we lose something precious.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Hide a favorite toy or book. Make up two clues to help your friend or family member find it. Thank God that He always knows where things and people are and can keep us well and safe.
