Explore

Find a big space outdoors and collect lots of sticks and branches. Reenact the story with your child, using the sticks to make a big boat outline on the grass. _(Consider using gloves to prevent getting splinters.)_

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#279c48"}}}
Reflect

Give your child one small job they can do each day this week _(e.g., watering the plants, sweeping an area with a dustpan and brush, wiping down their table at the end of each meal)_. Affirm them for working hard and doing their best, just as Noah worked hard and did his best to build the ark. When Sabbath comes around, let them know they can have a rest from doing their job. Share how God gives us the blessing of both work and rest.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#279c48"}}}
Create

Talk about how Noah would have drawn up plans of what the boat would look like before he built it with wooden planks. Then draw the shape of an ark on a piece of cardboard using straight lines _(see example to the right)_.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#279c48"}}}
Pray

Make a prayer ark out of an old cardboard box. Use it this week in worship times, having your child climb inside to pray to God.
