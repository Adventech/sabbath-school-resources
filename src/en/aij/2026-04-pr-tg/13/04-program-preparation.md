**Opening and Focus Activities**

Source **colored cardstock, colored pencils, markers**, and other optional craft items such as stickers, ribbon to decorate their bookmarks. Have **encouraging Bible verses** available for the children to copy onto the bookmarks.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** and prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or write/print it for display.

Source a **globe** or **world map**.

Source items for the Focus Activity: **healthy living plants** and **wilted/dead plants**. Include **flowers** if possible.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: a **piece of cardstock** with a small hole cut in it and some **pictures from a book/magazine** to cover up with the piece of cardstock.

Show the **^[resurrection picture]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**—available in the Program PowerPoint or as a PDF in the Resources folder—and source a cloth to cover the picture before you show it.

Write or print the following verse on a card and place in your Bible before the lesson: 1 Thessalonians 4:16, 17.
