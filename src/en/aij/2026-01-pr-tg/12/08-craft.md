Frog Puppet

**What you need:** Two paper plates, stapler, green cellophane/tissue paper, colored paper (white, black, red, and green), scissors, glue, marker (1).

**Instructions:**

- Fold two paper plates in half. Cut one paper plate down the middle. Leave the other folded (2).
- Staple each half to the back of the folded paper plate. This will make pockets for your hands.
- Cut up small squares of green cellophane/tissue paper and glue onto the top of the puppet (3).
- Frog eyes: cut out two semicircles of white paper. Draw eyes with marker and glue on the top (4).
- Frog mouth: cut a semicircle out of black paper. Glue inside the mouth (4).
- Frog tongue: cut a strip of red paper and wind around your finger to create a coil. Glue inside the mouth, half sticking out (4).
- Frog legs: cut four strips of green paper, folding each one both ways so they “spring.” Glue to the bottom of the puppet (5).
