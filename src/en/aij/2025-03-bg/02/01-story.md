A Special\
^[Basket Boat]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}})
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/02/01.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block": {"backgroundColor": "#124926ad"}}}
Mommy Jochebed and Miriam hurried down the path to the river. In their arms they held a basket. Inside the basket was something very special. _(Do you know what was inside?)_ Baby Moses was safely tucked inside! Mommy stopped by the river and lifted the lid of the basket. She kissed baby Moses on his rosy cheeks _(kiss your child)._ She prayed that God would send His angels to watch over him. Then Mommy and Miriam gently hid the basket in the tall grasses.\
Miriam stayed by the river to keep watch over her baby brother. A soft breeze blew _(whoosh, whoosh)._ The water splashed at the edge _(splash, splash)._ The birds sang in the trees _(tweet, tweet)._ The little basket boat rocked gently on the water, this way, and that way _(rock side to side)._ Baby Moses liked to be rocked. He fell fast asleep.
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-03-bg/02/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block": {"backgroundColor": "#36160Dad"}}}
Miriam didn’t sleep. She watched her baby brother carefully. Then she saw someone coming to the river! It was the princess!\
Miriam looked from her hiding place as the princess walked right to the basket and opened the lid. The princess smiled. What a surprise—a beautiful baby boy! Baby Moses started crying. The princess picked him up and cuddled him close _(cuddle your child)._\
Miriam ran to the princess. She asked the princess if she would like her to find someone to care for the baby. “The king’s daughter said, ‘Yes, please!’ ” (Exodus 2:8, ICB). Miriam knew the right person to look after her baby brother! She ran as fast as she could, all the way home to her mommy. Mommy Jochebed hurried back with Miriam to meet the princess. The princess was so happy to have a kind mommy to look after baby Moses. Mommy Jochebed was so happy that baby Moses was safe! God had answered her prayer.

Mommy and Miriam laughed and sang songs to God all the way home. They held hands and knelt with Daddy Amram and brother Aaron. Together they thanked God for sending His angels to watch over baby Moses. God protects you too! He sends His angels to watch over you.\
Thank You, God, for keeping baby Moses safe. _(Say together)_ Thank You for watching over me.
