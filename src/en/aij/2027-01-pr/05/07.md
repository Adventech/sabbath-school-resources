The snow turned to rain as the horses pulled the sleigh on toward the great Mississippi River. The freezing snowstorms had stopped, but it was still awful weather to be on a trip. But Ellen White, her husband, and her friends Josiah and Elon weren’t on a holiday, they were on a God mission. God had shown Ellen a vision that she needed to visit some new believers in the town of Waukon, in Iowa, who had become discouraged.

But now, even though they’d made it through the terrible weather, the biggest river in North America ran before them, with no bridge for them to cross over. What could they do?

The river had frozen during the cold weather, so the men thought they could cross safely on the ice. People on the shore warned them, “Look, the rain has made the ice all mushy on top. Several teams of horses have already broken through the ice and barely escaped drowning.” Should they cross?

Josiah reined in the horses at the water’s edge. On top of the ice lay water a foot deep. He stood up and pointed at the broad river. “Is it forward to Iowa or back to Illinois? We have come to the Red Sea. Do we cross?”

Ellen White raised her voice, “Go forward, trusting in Israel’s God.”

Josiah eased the horses and sleigh down onto the ice. The horses splashed slowly forward. Everyone prayed. Any moment they might strike a weak spot and plunge into the icy waters.

People began to gather on the far bank to watch them cross. They had passed midstream now and approached the Iowa shore. The same God who had divided the Red Sea now froze the waters of the Mississippi in the path of the sleigh. When they reached the shore safely, the watching crowd cheered. What a relief to see them safe!

Pressing on through the sleet, snow, and chilling winds, the tired team of horses finally made it to Waukon four days later.

God had laid the bridge of ice under the sleigh of His servants that wintry day so that they could be a blessing to His people.
