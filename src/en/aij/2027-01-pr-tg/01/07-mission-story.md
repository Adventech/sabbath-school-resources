**Focus:** Jesus can help me be a missionary for Him as I grow.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Brazil** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Hadassa is an 8-year-old missionary who lives in Brazil. She gives Bible studies to her friends at school and preaches sometimes at church. She has been sharing Jesus with others for eight years—her whole life!

Her dad and mom really, really wanted to have a baby. They prayed and prayed. They waited and waited. But nothing happened.

“God, if You don’t give us a child, we will still serve You,” Dad prayed. “But if You do give us a child, we will give the child to You.”

Then one day Mom gave birth to little Hadassa. Dad remembered his promise. He and Mom dedicated her to God. They started training Hadassa to be a missionary.

When Hadassa was only a month old, Dad and Mom began to take her out to share Christian books on Sabbath afternoons. Dad or Mom carried her in their arms, and they visited with people waiting in their cars at red traffic lights. People were delighted to see the little baby. Many people accepted books and prayers.

When Hadassa was 2, she began to hand out books by herself. She also began to pray by herself with the people they met. People liked it when little Hadassa prayed for them. Some said, “I needed that at this exact moment. It was God who sent you.”

When Hadassa was 4 years old, she had a dream that she was playing on the roof with her brother, Caleb. Suddenly a big man dressed in white appeared. He was wearing the armor of God, including the shield of faith, the helmet of salvation, and the sword of the Spirit, which is the Word of God (see Ephesians 6:10-17).

The man came to Hadassa and said, “You were all called for a great mission.” The man dressed Hadassa and Caleb in the armor of God. Hadassa didn’t know how he did it. The armor just appeared on them. Then the man left.

Hadassa woke up and hurried to tell Mom. Mom was moved to tears. “It was Jesus who appeared to you,” she said.

From that moment Hadassa knew that she could be a missionary without help from Dad and Mom. She had help from Jesus. He was preparing her to do wonderful things for Him.

- **In her dream Jesus said, “You were all called for a great mission.” Do you think that includes you? Why?**
- **What help did Jesus give Hadassa that He promises to give you too?** (The armor of God.)
