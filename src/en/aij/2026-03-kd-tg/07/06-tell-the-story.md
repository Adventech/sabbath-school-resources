**Big Idea:** Jesus is my God. His words are powerful.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Set up three different stations in different areas of the classroom, one for each of Satan’s temptations: (1) an area with several **large rocks**; (2) a **stool** or **stepladder** and a **sandbox** filled with **peg people** nearby; and (3) an area with a **picture** from the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** from the lesson.
- Bible to take with you to the three stations.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story **^[(week 7 Program PowerPoint)]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus stood on the shore of the Jordan River. He had just been baptized, and God’s words were still in Jesus’ mind, “You are My beloved Son, in whom I am well pleased” (Mark 1:11).

The Holy Spirit spoke to Jesus and told Him to leave the Jordan River and go somewhere else, alone. Jesus obeyed. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He started walking down a dusty road ^[(invite children to follow you to station 1)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). It led him past grassy fields and green rustling trees. On it went, through sandy hills and past rocky mountains to . . . the desert!

It was very hot and very dry there. There were no other travelers or farmers. Just Jesus. The Holy Spirit had led Him to a place that was quiet and peaceful. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus needed to think and pray ^[(invite children to kneel with you)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Jesus would soon begin a very important job. He would save all the people in the world from sin and death. He asked God to make Him strong to do this work. It wouldn’t be easy.

Rumble, rumble (invite the children to stand and rub their tummies). Jesus’ tummy was empty! He had been praying in the desert for 40 days without any food at all. Yes, Jesus was very hungry.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly Satan appeared. He had come to tempt Jesus to do something wrong—to sin. He pointed at some big rocks that lay in the sand ^[(point to “rocks”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He said, “If you are the Son of God, tell these rocks to become bread” (Matthew 4:3).

Jesus knew Satan was trying to trick Him. He shook His head. No! He would not do a miracle like that. His power was for helping others—not Himself. Instantly Jesus remembered a Bible verse ^[(hold up Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He said, “Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God” (Matthew 4:4).

Jesus told Satan that food was not as important as God’s words ^[(hold up Bible)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He trusted God would give Him the strength He needed. Jesus wouldn’t do this to help Himself.

Satan frowned. He would try something else . . .

^[(Invite children to follow you to station 2 and stand around you as you climb up the stepladder or onto the stool.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Satan took Jesus way up high—to the top of God’s temple in Jerusalem! As Jesus looked down, He noticed the ground far, far below ^[(point to sandbox and peg people below)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). If someone fell from this spot, they would be hurt badly.

Then Satan said, “If you are the Son of God, jump off” (Matthew 4:6, ICB). Satan wanted Jesus to test God—to do something silly and see if God would send His angels to rescue Him.

Jesus knew another Bible verse to help Him! ^[(Hold up Bible.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He said, “It is written again, ‘You shall not tempt the Lord your God’” (Matthew 4:7).

Jesus knew He didn’t need to jump off a building to prove God cared about Him.

Satan frowned. He would try something else . . .

^[(Invite children to follow you to station 3.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Satan took Jesus up to the very top of a tall mountain. Wow! What a beautiful view! Jesus could see sparkling cities, marble palaces, green fields, and vineyards full of fruit ^[(show Program PowerPoint picture)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

As Jesus looked at the beautiful world, Satan said, “If you will bow down and worship me, I will give you all these things” (Matthew 4:9, ICB).

Jesus knew another Bible verse to help Him! ^[(Hold up Bible.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He said, “Away with you, Satan! For it is written, ‘You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve’ ” (Matthew 4:10).

Jesus knew that only God should be worshipped. He would bow down to only God. The Holy Spirit had made Jesus strong and wise. Jesus had used the words in the Bible like an invisible sword to fight against Satan’s lies.

Satan had lost the battle, and so he left. Jesus had won!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then, as quick as a flash, angels rushed to help Jesus. Lovingly they gave Him food to eat and spoke kind words to encourage Him. They reminded Jesus that God loved Him and would help Him.

With God’s strength you can resist Satan too. God’s words in the Bible are very powerful. They will help you be strong.
