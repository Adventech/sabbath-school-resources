The crowds in the synagogue sat in hushed awe as they thought about the powerful words Jesus had just spoken. Meanwhile Jesus slipped quietly out the door, leaving the people alone with their thoughts. Where did He go? **Read Mark 1:29.**

Jesus often stayed in Simon Peter’s home in Capernaum. Jesus and His disciples had become like family to Peter. His whole family treated Jesus as one of their own too. Jesus was blessed to be accepted and loved by them all. He knew He could go there for Sabbath lunch that day. He also knew He could rest quietly after preaching all morning to the crowds in the synagogue.

As Jesus traveled and taught all over Galilee, He needed people like Simon Peter who would welcome Him into their homes and treat Him like family. Many church members today are blessed when we welcome them into our homes too. Those who live alone, people who have lost their husband or wife, children who don’t have a mom or dad living with them, refugees and people new to the area or country, elderly people, and shy people are all blessed when we make them feel welcome and part of our family.

It is God’s plan that we all feel part of His big family. Ephesians 2:19 says, “Now you are citizens together with God’s holy people. You belong to God’s family” (ICB). We can travel all over the world and find members of God’s big church. Worshipping and spending time together is a wonderful, world-wide family blessing.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Where would you like to visit with other members of God’s family?
- How could you make others feel part of your church family? Challenge yourself to do something this week. For example: invite someone to sit with you in Sabbath School, invite someone to your house for Sabbath lunch, or greet them with a smile. Small actions can have a big impact.
