title: Bible Study Resources

The following list, while not exhaustive, provides some dependable and useful resources that will aid you in your study of the Bible.

### Online Websites:

[Bibleinfo.com](http://www.bibleinfo.com/). This website provides what the Bible has to say about more than 350 of life’s important questions, along with the opportunity to submit questions about the Bible.

[KidsBibleinfo.com](http://www.KidsBibleinfo.com/). Designed especially for children of grade school age, this site provides big answers for little people—from the Bible—along with character-building stories and lessons.

**The Word on the Web**. Free Bible study guides for developing a dynamic walk with God.

[Online Discover Bible Guides](http://www.itiswritten.com/discover): Learn how the major themes of Scripture point to a God of love and mercy.

[New Beginnings Video Bible Studies](http://www.itiswritten.com/new_beginnings): View a fascinating series of studies covering the whole Bible (English and Spanish).

[Search for Certainty](http://www.itiswritten.com/certainty): If you are searching for answers to many Bible questions, these easy-to-follow lessons are the place to start.

[Unsealing Daniel’s Mysteries](http://www.itiswritten.com/daniel): These studies will help you unravel the mysteries of the book of Daniel as you study it chapter-by-chapter.

[It Is Written Telecast](http://www.itiswritten.com/tvprogram). Visit the web archives to find Bible-based messages by Pastor Boonstra in streaming video, podcast and complete scripts.

[It Is Written Store](http://www.itiswritten.com/store). You will find many Bible study tools to strengthen your faith and walk with God here.

[Blue Letter Bible](http://www.blueletterbible.org/). This wonderful online scriptural reference commentary and study tool will help you discover rich lessons from His Word.

[Bible Gateway](http://www.biblegateway.com/). A free service for reading, studying and researching the Bible in the language or translation of your choice online. You may also listen to the Bible here.

### Concordances and Dictionaries (Available at your local Christian Bookstore or online):

**The New Strong’s Exhaustive Concordance of the Bible by James Strong**. This exhaustive research tool is the legendary classic that generations of Bible students have come to depend on for Bible study and research.

**Young’s Analytical Concordance to the Bible by Robert Young**. A standard Biblical concordance, Young’s casts all words in the Bible into alphabetical order and arranges them under their respective original words. This helps the reader to analyze more accurately the various uses of the original Hebrew and Greek words. Includes over 300,000 biblical references.

**Smith’s Bible Dictionary by William Smith**. A trustworthy classic of Bible scholarship, this Bible dictionary defines thousands of biblical words, and is packed with essential information such as maps and chronology tables.

### Computer Software (Available online or at Christian bookstores):

[e-Sword](http://www.e-sword.net/) is a feature rich and user friendly electronic Bible that makes the study of God’s Word both enjoyable and enriching. This software is free for the downloading and is available in many different languages.

**QuickVerse** is an electronic Bible study tool with many features to help you in your study of God’s Word. This software is available in PC or Mac versions.

**PC Study Bible** is simple enough for anyone to use, but provides power tools to aid your study of the Bible.
