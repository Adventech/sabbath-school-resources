**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Invite the children to stand in a circle with hands palms up near their sides. Invite them to place their right hand over the hand of the person beside them, and their left hand under the person beside them. When you say “Go,” they will start a **slow gratitude circle clap** where the person’s right hand claps the partner’s left hand, and so on until the claps go around the circle. As soon as your hand is clapped, they must say something that God provides. Go around slowly the first time as a practice, then encourage them to **go faster next time**.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God provides so many good things for us. When we think about it, we have endless things to thank Him for. When we thank God, our thoughts and attitudes become more positive, and our faith grows.

Invite the children to sit down. **Show them your jar, herbal tea, and filter** (strainer). Pour the water into the jar and show them what the filter catches. Share that filters catch impurities or unwanted things and let the good things flow through.

- **What things might we use filters for?** (Water, air, light, pictures.)

Gratitude is also like a filter for our thoughts, allowing us to see and experience God’s goodness more clearly.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** God is so good. He cares for us in countless ways. When we practice gratitude, it’s a helpful thought filter that lets us remember these good things. It leaves us more positive and content. In today’s story the children of Israel had so many things to be thankful for—just as we do. But something happened to shift their focus, and their mood! Let’s find out what, and how God responds.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God provides for my needs so I can be content and grateful.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Divide your class into two groups. Explain that you will give them 30 seconds to say as many uses of water as they can think of. Appoint a helper to each group to record their ideas with a simple tally mark. The winning group has the most tally marks. Say “Go,” and give the allotted 30 seconds. Watch and listen carefully, particularly when 30 seconds has passed.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Ask those who didn’t win to think about the thoughts they had in their mind—or even what they spoke out loud.

- **Did you grumble because you didn’t win? Or were you content to have done your best?**

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** We choose our attitude: we can grumble and complain or be content and grateful! How good God was to give the Israelites water, and how good He is to give us water too.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Invite the children to hold up their empty cups that contained their bitter/sweet water and discuss the following:

- **How did the people react when things didn’t seem to go their way?** (Grumbled, complained, were bitter.)
- **How did Moses react to these same problems? Why?** (He prayed. He trusted God as His leader. He had spent time with God and knew Him as His friend.)
- **What can give us a bitter or negative attitude, like the children of Israel?** (Thinking too much about our problems or bad things that happen, not remembering God’s blessings.)
- **What does God tell us to do when we are anxious or worried? Read Philippians 4:6, 7.** (Pray, give thanks, and ask God for everything we need.)

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** God wants to free us from bitterness and complaining. Instead of complaining, we can be grateful for the way God has already sweetened our lives with good things. Our faith and trust in Him will grow, as will our joy.

Then sing the **Scripture Song** together: “Oh, that men would give thanks to the Lord for His goodness and His wonderful works to the children of men!” (Psalm 107:8).

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Nature Lesson, **“The Grumbler,”** and discuss the follow-up questions.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** This week’s Mission Challenge is to let your thankfulness to God spill over into thankfulness to others. **Find creative ways to thank different members of your family for who they are and what they do.** Think of some examples together. Encourage and affirm their ideas.

**^[REVIEW AND COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your Gratitude Jar. Distribute a craft stick (or slip of paper) to each child and have them write on it something they are very grateful for. Invite them to say, “Thank You, God!” as they each place it inside the jar.

Point to the **Big Idea** written on your whiteboard/PowerPoint and read it together: **God provides for my needs so I can be content and grateful.**

Challenge the children to be alert to something they are grateful for during the week ahead. Explain that they will be given the opportunity to add something new to the Gratitude Jar next Sabbath.
