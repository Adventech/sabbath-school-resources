After Adam and Eve’s sad exit from the Garden of Eden, life fell into a steady rhythm for them. Their days became busy with work, just as God had said they would. Over time they noticed changes creeping into their world. Flowers faded, weeds popped up in the pumpkin patch, and leaves fluttered down and crunched under their feet.

Good and evil now lived side by side in all of God’s creation, not just in Adam and Eve’s hearts. Evil had stretched out its deadly fingers, but breathtaking beauty could always be seen. Now Adam and Eve could choose what to focus on: decay or beauty, good or evil.

Despite the way sin had changed their world, Adam and Eve held tight to hope in their hearts. Hope that they would one day see God’s Son come to earth to fulfill the promise. They didn’t know when it would happen, or how. But they truly believed it would, for God had promised. And He always keeps His promises. How can we be sure God keeps His promises? **Read Romans 4:21.**

There are many promises in God’s Word. We can hold them in our hearts, just as Adam and Eve did. Your memory verse is one of these; why not recite it now. It tells us that because God’s Son came and died for us, we can be forgiven and turn away from our sins to a new life of following Him.
