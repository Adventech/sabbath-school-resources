**Big Idea:** Jesus helps me do the impossible when I keep my eyes on Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- You may like to use the following prop: lightweight **broom**.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to Matthew 14 and be ready to read verses from the story when you ask for them.
```

**Pray:** Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#649b7c", "size": "lg"}}}
**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jesus called His disciples together. He could feel the crowd’s excitement on the hillside. The people “were about to come and take Him by force to make Him king” (John 6:15). He knew that this was a very bad idea. Quickly he told the disciples to return to Capernaum in their boat at once. Turning to the crowd, Jesus told the people to go home. Disappointed, they began to leave.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**Why didn’t Jesus want them to make Him king?** (He knew it was the wrong time. He knew He still had much work to do among the people.) What did Jesus do next? Read Matthew 14:23. (He went up onto the mountain by Himself to pray.)

The disciples waited in the boat, hoping Jesus would come with them. Finally they gave up and headed out across the lake. As they rowed, they wondered, _Why did we listen to Jesus instead of staying and crowning Him king? Why didn’t Jesus show the people His kingly power?_

Jesus had done the impossible that day. He had healed the sick and fed the hungry crowds with a miracle. But the disciples had forgotten all that. They were focused on their own disappointment. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Suddenly a violent wind blew across the lake. Once again the disciples were in the middle of a scary storm! Huge waves crashed against their boat. They tried to row the boat, but the storm was too strong. Well after midnight the weary disciples gave up the struggle.

When they felt totally helpless, they remembered their Helper, Jesus! He was the God of the impossible. And He could save them in the storm! He’d done it before.

From the shore Jesus saw the huge, whitecapped waves. He saw the disciples struggle to stay afloat. He heard the cry of their hearts, Jesus, save us. Immediately He set about answering their prayer.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He do? Read Matthew 14:25.** (Jesus went to them, walking on the sea.) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The disciples looked up to see a man walking toward them—on top of the wild waves! Terrified, they shrieked, “It is a ghost!” (Matthew 14:26). But Jesus’ comforting voice could be heard above the roaring waves, “Have courage! It is I! Don’t be afraid” (Matthew 14:27, ICB).

Their hearts flipped from fear to relief. Jesus was right there with them! He had heard their cry; He had come!

Peter was almost beside himself with excitement. Jesus was walking on water! He cried out, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water” (Matthew 14:28).

Jesus answered, “Come” (Matthew 14:29). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Peter scrambled out of the boat and walked toward Jesus with his eyes locked on his Master. He didn’t sink; he didn’t swim. He just kept walking straight toward Jesus.

Let’s pause the story for a moment. ^[(Note: take no longer than five minutes to do the following activity.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DO:**

Take a lightweight broom with a long handle and invite children to take turns, turning it upside down and trying to balance the broom, with the handle on their finger/palm of their hand. Allow all the children to try.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**DISCUSS:**

- {"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
- **What did you need to do to be able to balance the broom?** (Keep your eyes focused upward.)
- **What happened when you looked at yourself—your hand/finger?** (If you look at yourself, you can try as hard as you want, but the broom will always fall.)
- **What were the people focusing on at the beginning of the story?** (An earthly kingdom and crowning Jesus as king.)
- **When the disciples were in the boat, what were they focusing on?** (The storm and saving themselves.)
- **What was Jesus focused on when He went up the mountain to pray?** (God, His Father.)
- **What does Jesus want us to focus on?** (Him!) Share that Hebrews 12:2 tells us to look to Jesus, “the author and finisher of our faith.”

Let’s continue our story.

Peter felt pretty pleased with himself. He glanced back to see if his friends were watching. As soon as he took his eyes off Jesus, he stumbled. Then a huge wave rolled between them, blocking Jesus from his view. Peter panicked, and, losing faith, he began to sink. Peter shouted, “Lord, save me!” (Matthew 14:30).

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jesus do? Read Matthew 14:31.** (He stretched out His hand and caught Peter and said, “O you of little faith, why did you doubt?”) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Peter and Jesus walked to the boat and climbed in. (Imagine this walk—holding on to Jesus and walking on water!) The waves calmed down, and the wind grew silent. Peter was still and silent too. He realized his pride and lack of faith had nearly caused his death. Peter had been reminded to always keep his eyes on Jesus. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The early-morning light began to spread across the sea as the disciples knelt around Jesus in the little fishing boat. Bowing humbly before Him, they said, “Truly You are the Son of God!” (Matthew 14:33).

How amazing! **Jesus helped Peter do the impossible when he kept his eyes on Him.**

Personalize this idea by reading the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **Jesus helps me do the impossible when I keep my eyes focused on Him.**
