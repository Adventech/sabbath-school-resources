**Opening Activities**

Set out **play-dough** for children to show what they like to do on Sabbath.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box**: a **parcel or box wrapped as a gift**. Be prepared to play or sing music for a game of “Pass the Parcel.” (NOTE: please put aside to use next week also.)

Source a **birthday gift** of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source suggested **Storytelling Props**: a **ribbon stick** for each child. (Used in previous quarters. Buy or make your own by attaching ribbons to the end of a stick with a tack/pushpin/eye screw hook.) Use these also in the Review section. Source or make a paper scroll by simply rolling up paper.

Use **peg people** (Jesus; a woman and two men; Pharisee) or a **felt set** and the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: a small **Bible**, a **family photo**, a **real flower or leaf**, a small **pillow** or a **picture of someone relaxing in nature**, an **instrument or a songbook/hymnal**.
