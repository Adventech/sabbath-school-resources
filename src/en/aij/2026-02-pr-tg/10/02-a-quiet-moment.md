**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

God is love. He loves us so much that He was willing to die and take our sins as His own. He wants to give everyone the opportunity to know Him and accept His gift of eternal life. The Bible says He is “patient toward you, not wishing that any should perish” (2 Peter 3:9, ESV). Every day God draws people to Himself.

What a wonderful promise to know that God wants every person to know Him. He “desires all men to be saved and to come to the knowledge of the truth”

(1 Timothy 2:4). It doesn’t matter what they own, where they live, their occupation, or the color of their hair. God desires for all to be saved and to know Him.

Our story this week demonstrates how much God wants to reach everyone. He showed the Egyptians that He cared for them by using Joseph to provide food for them during the seven years of famine. “Through Joseph the attention of the king and great men of Egypt was directed to the true God” (Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_, p. 222).

God wants to use you to share His love with others and give them the opportunity to be saved too! Ask Him to work through your life right now. Commit to praying each day for the Holy Spirit to guide you to those whom God has already drawn toward Him and also toward you.

Like Joseph, may you and the children in your Sabbath School class be willing to be used by God to give others the opportunity to know Him.
