Sing

Sing “God’s Love Is So Wonderful” every night for worship.

God’s love is so wonderful\
God’s love is so wonderful\
God’s love is so wonderful\
Oh! wonderful love!

So high _(reach up high)_ you can’t get over it
So deep _(crouch down low)_ you can’t get under it
So wide _(stretch arms out wide)_ you can’t get around it
Oh! wonderful love! _(Hands on heart.)_

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#53ba5b"}}}
Explore

Go for a walk outside. Ask your child to help you find five things God has made and then tell them how it shows His love. For example, the sun to warm us and make food grow, the trees to clean the air and help us breathe, the flowers for our nose and eyes to enjoy. Talk about how God’s love is all around us every day.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#53ba5b"}}}
Share

Find a public sidewalk near your house or at a park. Ask your child to help you draw a big message with colorful chalk so others can be reminded of God’s big love for them. Write something like “GOD LOVES YOU” in big letters and decorate all around it with hearts and flowers _(or colorful scribbles!)_.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#53ba5b"}}}
Pray

During worship this week, pray with your child for an opportunity to share God’s love with someone. When an opportunity arises, involve your child as much as possible. Then pray together and thank God for using them to show His love.
