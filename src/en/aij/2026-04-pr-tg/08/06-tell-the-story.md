**Big Idea:** Jesus wants me to know and share the blessing of the Sabbath by caring for others.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Show the pictures from this week’s story in the **Program PowerPoint**. Ask a child to follow your cues and ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) click through the images.
- You may like to use **pipe cleaners** as simple characters: the woman whose back was bent over, then straightened; the man with a disability in his hand; a man with a swelling sickness. Be sensitive and careful not to make them comical.
- Use expressions and actions to illustrate the story.
- Ask children to **open their Bibles** to Luke and be ready to read verses from the story

when you ask for them.
```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was the Sabbath day, the best day of the week! Jesus stood up to teach in the synagogue. He loved the Sabbath day. A day of rest and worship. A day to remember God’s love and be close to Him. And a day to pass on God’s love and care to others.

The proud Jewish leaders and Pharisees were all in the synagogue on Sabbath too. But sadly, they had forgotten the truth about Sabbath. They had made it a day of rules. There were rules about everything! These rules made God seem like a hard taskmaster, and that’s just what Satan wanted. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus looked up as a woman shuffled into the synagogue, her eyes on the ground ^[(pipe cleaner person “bent over”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). For 18 long years her back had been bent over and she couldn’t straighten up. She couldn’t look up to see soaring birds or stars in the night sky.

Jesus knew how hard her life must be, and He desperately wanted to help her.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did He do? Read Luke 13:12.** (He called her over and told her she was free from her disability.) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jesus gently touched the woman. Immediately her back straightened up. ^[(Straighten pipe cleaner person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) She felt strong and flexible. She could see people at eye level. It was a miracle! The woman began praising God.

The synagogue leader believed Jesus had broken the “rules” by healing the woman on the Sabbath. The leader said to the people, “There are six days for work. So come to be healed on one of those days. Don’t come for healing on the Sabbath day” (Luke 13:14, ICB).

Jesus shook His head. He said, “You people are hypocrites! All of you untie your work animals and lead them to drink water every day—even on the Sabbath day! This woman is our Jewish sister. . . . Surely it is not wrong for her to be freed from her sickness on a Sabbath day!” (Luke 13:15, 16, ICB).

Of course the woman was more important than an animal! Of course one of God’s children deserved to be healed on the Sabbath! The truth made so much sense. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Another Sabbath while Jesus was teaching, He noticed a man who had a disability in his right hand (pipe cleaner hand with curled fingers). He said to the man, “Arise and stand here” (Luke 6:8).

The Pharisees frowned and stared. Jesus knew exactly what they were thinking. So Jesus asked them some questions.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What did Jesus ask? Read Luke 6:9.** (“Is it lawful on the Sabbath to do good or to do evil, to save life or to destroy?)

The Pharisees knew the truth. Sabbath was for doing good! Jesus turned to the man with the disability in his hand and said, “Stretch out your hand” (Luke 6:10). Instantly it was healed! ^[(Straighten pipe cleaner hand.) ►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Another time an important Pharisee had invited Jesus and others to his house for Sabbath dinner. Some sneaky spies were among the guests, watching Jesus closely to see if they could catch Him disobeying their Sabbath rules. At the

dinner Jesus noticed that one of the guests had a disease that made him look puffy and swollen ^[(puffy pipe cleaner person)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He had too much water in the cells of his body.

Jesus wanted to help this man and heal him. Looking at the Pharisees, Jesus asked, “Is it right or wrong to heal on the Sabbath day?” (Luke 14:3, ICB).

The Pharisees looked down. They knew the truth but wouldn’t answer.

Jesus took hold of the man who was sick and healed him. All the swelling in his body instantly disappeared! ^[(Slim pipe cleaner person.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He was well!

The Pharisees should have jumped with joy and praised God for the miracle. But they stayed silent. So Jesus asked them a question.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What was Jesus’ question? Read Luke 14:5.** (“Which of you, having a donkey or an ox that has fallen into a pit, will not immediately pull him out on the Sabbath day?”) ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

They knew the truth. They would help an animal in need. But they had not wanted to help people in need by showing compassion or mercy. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

The Sabbath was a blessed day to be filled with all the goodness of God. A day for being close to God and sharing His love with others. Jesus had shown the people how. He hoped they would always remember.
