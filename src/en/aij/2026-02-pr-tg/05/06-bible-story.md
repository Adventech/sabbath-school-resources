**Big Idea:** God gives me peace and helps me to make peace with others.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob and his big family walked slowly toward Canaan. Jacob smiled as he walked. He was happy knowing God had helped him make peace with Laban. But as he got closer to his old family home, his peace slipped away. He thought of how he had lied and tricked his brother and father long ago. He remembered that Esau had wanted to kill him! Jacob’s stomach knotted with fear. _Would Esau still want to hurt him?_

Suddenly, worried Jacob looked up from the dusty road to see an army of angels! Shining angels hovered in front of him. He spun around. There were more angels behind his big family!

Jacob remembered God’s ladder of angels that had given him hope when he had run away from Canaan. And now, 20 years later, God’s protecting angels were guiding him safely home.

“This is God’s camp,” said Jacob in awe (Genesis 32:2). He would always remember how God had given him peace instead of fear in this special place.

God’s peace settled over Jacob like a warm blanket ^[(blanket)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). But he still felt worried as he walked. Could he make peace with his angry brother? Jacob had let God forgive and change him. What would it take to show Esau he was truly sorry?

He had an idea! ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He would send his servants to find Esau and give him a kind message.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What was the message? Read Genesis 32:4, 5.** (They were to call Esau “my Lord,” and call Jacob “your servant.” Jacob asked to find favor with him.)

Jacob’s servants huffed and puffed as they returned. There was no message back from Esau. But there was news to share. Esau was coming their way with 400 strong men! Jacob’s heart pounded with fear.

As Esau and his band of fighting men marched closer, Jacob prayed. He gave his fear to God.

After his prayer Jacob was even more desperate to have peace with his brother. So he focused on what else he could do to soften Esau’s hatred. He chose some gifts for Esau.

{"style": {"text": {"color":"#58b0e3"}}}
**What were they? Read Genesis 32:13-15.** (Goats, sheep, camels, cows, and donkeys: 580 animals!)

The generous gifts trotted down the road with Jacob’s servants. Oh, how he hoped that the gifts would be accepted, and that he could soon be friends with his brother again.

Jacob sent his whole camp across the river while he stayed alone to pray. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Night fell. Still, he prayed alone in the dark. He felt scared and guilty. He knew it was his sin that had put his innocent family in danger.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) A strong hand suddenly seized Jacob. A Stranger had grabbed him in the darkness! Terrified, he wrestled with the Stranger. He struggled for hours, trying to get free.

At daybreak the wrestling Stranger touched Jacob’s hip. Instantly it popped out of its joint, and the muscle shrank. What power! Jacob realized this was no ordinary man! It was God!

Jacob clung to God pleading for a blessing: “I will not let You go unless You bless me!” (Genesis 32:26). God had been holding on to helpless Jacob with love. Now Jacob decided to cling on to God. God blessed him and gave him a new name: Israel.

As the new day began, Jacob’s family again traveled on. Clouds of desert dust swirled in the distance—Esau was approaching with his fighting men! But Jacob wasn’t the slightest bit afraid anymore. He had given all his worries to God and had accepted God’s peace ^[(blanket)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He would try to pass on God’s peace to His brother.

Limping bravely toward Esau, Jacob showed the utmost respect by bowing as one would to a king. His family held their breath as Jacob bent low before his brother, who still traveled toward them. They watched. And waited . . .

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) “Esau ran to meet him, and embraced him, and fell on his neck and kissed him, and they wept” (Genesis 33:4). What a beautiful reunion!

Esau met Jacob’s family and accepted all the gifts Jacob had sent. The brothers showered each other with kind words, smiles and hugs, forgiveness, and thoughtfulness. What a wonderful answer to Jacob’s prayers. Then the two brothers said goodbye. They had made peace ^[(blanket)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
