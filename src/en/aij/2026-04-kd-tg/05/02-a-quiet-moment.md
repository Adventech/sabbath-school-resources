**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Exclusion. We’ve all felt left out, lonely, excluded, or perhaps abandoned by those around us at different times in our lives.

The three men in today’s story were all too familiar with loneliness. The man with leprosy was physically excluded—forced away from family and community, and considered “unclean.” The blind man was excluded because people wrongly assumed his condition was the result of sin. The man who was hearing- and speech-impaired was excluded because of his inability to communicate with those around him.

Sin separates. It always has. It separates us from each other and from God. Yet Jesus came to change that. He didn’t use just His powerful words to heal, He also used His touch. He laid His hands on people who felt forgotten, untouched, and unloved. Jesus’ touch welcomed them and said, _“I love you and will restore you and make you whole.”_

Jesus invites you also to be His close friend and experience His transforming presence in your life. There is no greater friendship! “Your heavenly Father means to teach you to find in Him the friendship and love and consolation that will satisfy your most earnest hopes and desires. . . . You can be happy in Him if you had not another friend in the wide world” (Ellen G. White, _Our High Calling_, p. 259).

As you spend time with Jesus preparing this week’s lesson, may you be drawn to His kindness so you can then invite the children in your class to also experience the closest friendship of all, with Him.

“Greater love has no one than this, than to lay down one’s life for his friends” (John 15:13).
