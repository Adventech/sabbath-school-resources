**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

It happened in a beautiful garden on a perfect planet in a vast expanse of space. Unseen and undetected, the destroyer slithered into paradise and lurked in wait. With dazzling beauty and syrupy words, he spun the world’s first lies. The words were trusted, believed, and acted upon, and the downward spiral into darkness began.

It would have remained the very saddest of stories if not for our merciful God. Things took a remarkable turn when the Son of God pleaded with His Father, saying, “Let the punishment fall on Me. I will stand in man’s place. He shall have another chance.”<sup>1</sup> And so it was, that “as soon as there was sin there was a Savior. Christ knew that He would have to suffer, yet He became man’s substitute.”<sup>2</sup>

The choice to sin had been met with the choice to save. Oh the power of choice. The first choice dealt a fatal blow, while the second revealed the indescribable love of a merciful God. That love remains ever deep and strong as it calls the sinner back to God.

Guilty? Forgiven.\
Rebellious? Restored.\
Fearful? Reassured.\
Broken? Loved.\
Lost? Redeemed.

May you and the children be blessed as you explore the deep love of God that sits behind the power of choice that He gave to His earthly children. May you find hope and inspiration in the words of Paul: “For the wages of sin is death, but the gift of God is eternal life in Christ Jesus our Lord” (Romans 6:23).
