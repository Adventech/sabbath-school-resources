**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child with a smile as they arrive at Sabbath school.

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Show the children the “caring” scene** you have set up with some sick and injured soft toys, dolls, or adult helpers. Explain to the children that you are going to imagine that your helper has hurt their arm and needs a bandage; that teddy has fallen down and hurt his knee; or that the doll is sick and needs to go to bed. (You may need to invent some additional scenarios.) What will the children choose to do?

Give the children some cloth blankets, bandages, and Band-Aids. Once these children have chosen to help, call their attention to some blocks that need to be picked up. Now what will the children choose to do?

**^[SAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})**  Your good choices remind me of someone in our story today. His name is Joseph.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **flashlight** and a **cardboard traffic light**.

Hold up the cardboard traffic light and explain what both colors mean by shining the flashlight through both. Red means a “bad choice,” and green a “good choice.” Read out an example from the following list and invite different children to shine the flashlight through the green circle if they think it’s a good choice, or a red circle if they think it’s a bad choice.

- Helping someone up when they’ve fallen over.
- Continuing to play when you’ve been asked to pack up.
- Hugging someone special in your family.
- Sharing your favorite new toy.
- Going first when it’s not your turn.
- Throwing your toys when you’re angry.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Was it easy to choose these options?**
- **Have you had to make a hard choice before? What was it?**
- **Whom could we ask to help us choose well?**

Explain that every day we need to make choices! Sometimes it can be hard knowing what the right choice is, but we can ask our kind God to help us make good choices.

Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He helps me to make good choices.** Share that our Bible story today is about Joseph, who always chose the best choice—God’s way— even when it was hard! Let’s learn today’s story!

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS and DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a **jar** and some **red paper hearts**.

Give each child a red paper heart (or two if you have a small class). Show the children the jar with the words “Good Choices” written on it. Point to the words and say them with the children. Explain that today we learned about how God helped Joseph to make good choices.

- **What was a good choice that Joseph made in today’s story?** As they answer, invite them to place their red paper heart into the jar. (He was honest, did not complain, was helpful, was kind to others, prayed to God.)

**Teaching Point:** Our loving God will always help us to make good choices when we ask Him to. Even when there are bad things happening around us, we can still choose Him and show others His love.

- **What good choices can you make this week that will help others know about God’s love too?** Invite children who still have red hearts to place theirs into the jar. (Be helpful, share, tell others about Jesus, etc.)

Repeat the **Big Idea** again together: **God is with me. He helps me make good choices.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})**

- **What animal was at the very start of our story today?** (Camel.)

Give each child **Nature Card 21: Desert Camel** to place in their Nature Collector Bag. Share the following facts:

- Desert camels can store lots of water in their humps for long desert journeys.
- They have two rows of eyelashes that help keep the sand from their eyes.
- They can completely shut their nostrils during a sandstorm, so they don’t get sand up their noses!
- They can travel extremely fast and are very strong.

God made the desert camel to be well suited to the hot, dry, windy desert. God is amazing!

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the **Mission Story:** “Newspapers for Tony” and discuss the follow-up questions.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Paper Cup Slideshow.** As you create, talk to the children about how Joseph made good choices and helped people around him. His love was like a light in a dark place that showed people God’s love. God is so good and kind, and He loves it when others learn about Him by looking at our lives! Play worship music in the background.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Ask two children to say one word that describes what they learned today. Then summarize: Today we learned how God helps us make good choices.

Like Joseph, let’s choose to put God first in our lives and let Him help us make good choices.
