**Word of the Week**

**Faith** means to trust and believe in someone or something. When we have faith, we are sure about something. Having faith in Jesus means to believe that He will do and say all that He has promised.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

A woman who had been sick for 12 years was determined to ask Jesus to heal her. The crowds made it impossible for her to speak with Him directly, yet in faith she reached out and touched the edge of his garment. Instantly she was healed. Jesus asked who had touched Him, and when she admitted to the deed, He commended her for her faith.
