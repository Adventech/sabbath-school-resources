**EARLY ARRIVALS** (10 MINUTES BEFORE SABBATH SCHOOL BEGINS)

**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}}) Warmly welcome** each child at their eye level as they arrive. Ask them about their friends: who they are; how they met them; what they like to do together.

**^[PLAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Ask:

- **Have you ever broken or hurt your arm? Have you ever worn a sling?**

Invite the children to form pairs. Show the children your sling, and explain how to put a sling on someone who has a sore arm. Invite the children to take turns putting a sling on their partner. Explain that a sling helps our arm to rest and heal. Ask:

- **What are some other ways can we help our friends when they are hurt or sick?** (Visit them, sing to them, make them a “Get Well Soon” card, pray with them.)

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Transition into the Sabbath School program by singing **“Pack it Up.”**

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **ball**, **hat**, **stick** (or ruler), and a **container**. Explain that these items will be dropped on the floor as “obstacles.” Mark out a start and finish line and show the children they will need to hop over the obstacles. Help children get into pairs: one to pretend to have a hurt leg and to hop; the other to be their helper friend. Tie a sling around the “hurt” leg as a visual cue. Encourage them to lean on their friend to help them. (Include more obstacles if you have a larger class so multiple pairs can do it at the same time.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **What was it like to try to get to the finish line with a pretend hurt leg?**
- **What was it like to be the helper friend?**
- **How did you feel when you finally got to the end?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Today’s story is about some friends who tried very hard to help their friend who was sick. They wanted to take him to Jesus. It wasn’t easy, but they knew it would be worth it. Our **Big Idea** tells us why.

Say the **Big Idea** together and do the actions: **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He loves to help me and my friends** [stand and link arms together]**.”**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story, **“Bringing a Friend to Jesus,”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and the **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal four **paper plates with faces/emojis** drawn on them: sad, happy, very happy, surprised/amazed.

Invite the children to choose a face and talk about who in the story felt this way and why. (Sad man who needed healing and forgiveness. Happy friends who loved Jesus, or happy Jesus. Very happy man whom Jesus forgave and healed. Surprised people to see man coming through the roof and amazed to see that Jesus can do anything.)

- **How does it make you feel knowing Jesus loves to help you and your friends?**

Invite the children to close their eyes and say a silent prayer to Jesus. They may like to pray for a friend they can tell Jesus about, or ask for Jesus’ forgiveness or help for something in their life.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Collector Card 42: Long-Beaked Common Dolphin** and share the following facts:

- These friendly animals live in the ocean.
- They like to live in big groups, called pods, of up to 100 to 500 dolphins.
- They work together to hunt and help each other in the water.
- A few years ago scientists were watching a group of dolphins when they saw one that was hurt and it couldn’t swim very well. Some of the other dolphins in the group came and swam very close together underneath it, like a raft. They lifted it out of the water so it could breathe.

The long-beaked common dolphin teaches us about what being a good friend is like.

Give each child **Nature Collector Card 42: Long-Beaked Common Dolphin** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Wellington’s Big Idea.”**

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Healed in Two Ways.** Remind children how the man from today’s story was healed in two ways: his **sad, sinful heart** and his **broken body**. Demonstrate how they can make the man and his mat and use it to tell the story to a friend. Those who finish early can practice telling the story to each other.

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Share that today we learned that Jesus can heal our bodies and our hearts. (Hold up the very-happy-paper-plate face from the story.) He loves to help us and our friends. (Hold up the other happy face.)

- **How can you share Jesus with a friend this week?** (Tell them about Him, share our craft and stories about Him, and invite them to Sabbath School.)

Invite the children to find two or three friends right now to join hands with, in a circle. On the count of three, invite them to call out today’s **Big Idea** with actions—and very happy faces! **“Jesus** [point up] **can do anything** [jump up with one fist in the air]**. He loves to help me and my friends”** [stand and link arms together]**.**

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Remind the children of the fun welcome song and the languages that were shared. This week’s Mission Challenge is to **learn how to say “Jesus loves you” in another language and tell someone who speaks that language.**
