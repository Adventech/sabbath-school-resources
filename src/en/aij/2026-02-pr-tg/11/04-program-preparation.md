Opening Activities

Source the **^[Hieroglyphics printout]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child; as well as pencils/crayons.

Print out enough **^[word jigsaw pieces]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** so that each child can have one piece each. You may need to give some children more than one piece, depending on your group size.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **three or four small sacks or paper bags** with some **grain, a cup covered in aluminum foil**.

Source **three sticky notes** or small pieces of paper. Draw one of these three symbols on each: **heart**, **star**, **ladder**. You will hide these in the sacks during the Unpack the Story section.

Source a **small jigsaw puzzle** that doesn’t have too many pieces (about 10-20).
