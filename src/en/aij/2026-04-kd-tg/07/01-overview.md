**Word of the Week**

**Alive:** If you are alive, you have a beating heart. You can breathe and move and grow. When people or animals die, they can’t do any of those things.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jairus’ daughter was sick and about to die. The Jewish leader believed that Jesus could help her. He found Jesus and begged Him to come and heal her. Along the way, Jairus’ servant arrived with the news that his daughter had already died. Jesus encouraged Jairus to just believe. Jairus trusted Jesus, and they continued on to Jairus’ home. When Jesus arrived, He sent everyone outside except the girl’s parents and three of His disciples. Then He raised the little girl to life.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus has power over death.
- **Feel** a deep love for Jesus and His care for people who are hurting.
- **Respond** by telling others of Jesus’ power over death.
