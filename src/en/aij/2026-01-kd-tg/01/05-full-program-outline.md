**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Use your **bell** or chosen **sound maker** to get the children’s attention. Then point to the two special boxes: the Secret Surprise Box and the Story Explorer Box. Tell the children that each time they hear the bell ring, someone will be asked to open a special box.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Invite one child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** and take a look inside. (An empty black box with nothing inside it.) Invite everyone to look inside the box.

- **What’s inside this box?** (Nothing!) It is black and empty—just as the earth was before God began to create.
- **Could we live on the earth if it was just dark and empty?** (No.)
- **What things can you think of that we need to be able to live on the earth?** (Invite the children to stand up and mime their answers: eating food, breathing air, drinking water, washing their face, etc.)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** God is so loving. Before God created, He thought of us. He wanted to make a special place called earth for all the people He planned to create. The focus for this quarter is: **God is loving**. We will learn many reasons why God is so loving. But this week the **Big Idea** is: **God is loving. He created our world.** (Invite the children to repeat the Big Idea with you.)

- **Do you know how long it took for God to make our earth?** (Let’s find out the beginning of the story.)

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** If you have a separate story mat or corner in your room, invite the children to move there now. Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Creation: Days 1-5”** in an interactive way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a range of **objects** and **pictures** of things God created on Days 1-5, along with a set of **1-5 labels**. Tip them out and add your Storytelling Props to the pile. Lay the numbers out across the room and invite the children to sort the objects/pictures and place them beside the day they were created.

Beginning with Day 1, work your way along the line of sorted objects/pictures and ask for each day:

- **Why did God create . . . (light and darkness, air, land, etc.)?** (Accept answers, then always include “Because God is loving!”)
- **What do you love that was created on this day?** (After a child has shared, have the whole group say, “Thank You, God!”

Explain that our loving, creative God made all these wonderful things in five days because He wanted to make a special world for us to live in. Everything He makes is good. He loves us so much!

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 1: Sunset** and share the following facts:

- A sunset happens when the sun moves from the sky we see, to the other side of the world.
- Before the sun has totally disappeared from our sky, it leaves beautiful colors of red, orange, and yellow that look like a painting.
- The colors in the sky are created by the sun’s rays passing through the air.

As the sun sets each day, we can remember how our loving God created everything beautiful because He loves us. Thank You, God, for sunsets! Give each child **Nature Card 1: Sunset** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.
