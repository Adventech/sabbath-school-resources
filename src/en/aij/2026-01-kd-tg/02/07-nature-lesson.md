**Ask the children the following questions and invite them to do the matching action.** (Please be sensitive and adapt the following questions if you have special needs children in your class.)

- **Can you stand on one leg?**
- **Can you jump up and down?**
- **Can you wriggle your fingers?**
- **Can you give a big smile?**
- **Can you reach up tall?**
- **Can you turn around?**
- **Can you blink really fast?**
- **Can you shake?**

Wow! Your body can do so many amazing things. ^[(Share the fun facts below with the children, asking them to point to parts on their body or make actions that relate to the fact.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

**Did you know:**

- The human body needs food, air, and water, which is everything that God created for us in the first days of Creation!
- Your brain is active even when you are sleeping.
- Your nose cleans all the air you breathe in.
- You grow the most in the first two years of your life.
- Your ears and nose keep growing for all of your life.
- Your bones are stronger than steel.
- It takes only seven seconds for your food to reach your tummy.
- No one else has the same fingerprint as you.
- Your brain sends messages all over your body, telling it how to move and do things.
- Your heart pumps blood all through your body.
- Laughing is good for your heart.
- Your eyes are made up of about 2 million different working parts.

Thank You, God, for making us so special!

**Teaching Point:** God made something incredible and amazing when He made you. He thought very carefully about every part of your body. You were created in a very special and loving way. **God is loving. He created me!**
