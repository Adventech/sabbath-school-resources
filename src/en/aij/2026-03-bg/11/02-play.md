Explore

During bathtime, remind your child about the story of crossing the Jordan River. Using the water in the bath, try to make a dry space in the water. Can you do it? Can you stop the water in the middle? No! God did an amazing miracle to keep His people safe.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Learn

Trace around your feet and put 10 sheets of paper behind your drawing. Cut them out so that you have lots of feet. Place them on the floor around the house (within a child’s step distance). Invite your child to follow your footsteps and jump to each footprint. Share how this was a fun way that you led them. God led His people too! He used His special cloud to guide them. He made a dry path in the middle of the river too. Thank God together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Create

Share how the Israelites thanked God for helping them cross the big river. Make a little booklet by folding a piece of paper a few times. Draw pictures together of the things you are thankful for. Write “My Thanks Book” on the front. Each day, add something new, and thank God together.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#317ABD"}}}
Pray

In worship, take turns leading the family in a game of follow the leader. Give everyone in the family a turn at leading. Share how God is the best leader! Ask God to lead your family. Thank Him for His care.
