**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a **bubble container with only a little solution inside**.

Invite the children to look at the liquid inside the container.

- **What’s inside?** (Bubble solution.)
- **Is it very much?** (No.)

**Share:** Maybe there is only enough bubble solution for me.

- **Shall I keep it all to myself?** (No!)

Tell the children that you are going to share it with them, and that we will see how many bubbles we can blow with what we have. Blow some bubbles for the children to pop. Point out how many bubbles come from just a little bit of bubble solution.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **Was there enough bubble solution?**
- **Did it look like much at first?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** In our story today, Jesus had only a little of something, but when He blessed it and shared it with others, there was plenty for everyone.

Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He gives me all I need.**

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a piece of paper and a pair of scissors.

**Share:** This piece of paper represents the boy’s lunch in our story today. He had only five loaves and two small fish. That wasn’t enough to feed thousands of people!

- **How many corners does this piece of paper have?** (Count them: four.)
- **If I want to give each of you a corner of the paper, will there be enough?** (No. If you have less than four students, ask if there will be enough for everyone at church today.)

Tell the children that you are going to try anyway. Cut off one corner and give it to a child.

- **How many corners are left now?** (They may say “three.” Point out that there are now five corners.)

Cut off another corner and hand it to another child. Count the remaining corners on your piece of paper. Continue until each child has a corner. Point out that the more corners you cut off, the more corners you have left.

**Teaching Point:** Sometimes we might feel as though we don’t want to give what we have to others. We sometimes feel selfish. But God is the opposite of selfish. He always gives. We should remember that it’s God who gives us all that we have, and He also gives us all we need. He asks us to share with others, just as the little boy shared his lunch with Jesus. Do you need to share with others more?

Repeat the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. He gives me all I need.**

**CRAFT**

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Multiplying Loaves and Fish**. After completing the craft, the children can practice telling each other the story about the food multiplying as they show how more food appears in their craft basket. When all children have finished their crafts, invite them to hold them up and say together, “God gives me all I need!”

Alternatively, show children how to make bread dough, which they can take home, bake, and share with someone.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned that Jesus gives us everything we need and more! When we can share what we have with Jesus and with others, He will multiply it.
