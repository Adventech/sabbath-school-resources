Resting in the shade of a spreading oak tree, Abram gazed at the plain of Jordan far below. He wondered how Lot was getting on down there. He loved his nephew, and he missed him.

But Abram also knew he had done the right thing to let Lot choose where to go. The Bible says, “Do for other people the same things you want them to do for you” (Matthew 7:12, ICB). He might not be living on the best land, but living with peace and unselfishness in his heart was so much better.

As Abram looked down at the plains and thought of Lot, God looked down at the hills and thought of Abram. He saw that Abram’s kind and generous heart needed cheering up, so He spoke. What did God say to Abram? **Read Genesis 13:14, 15.**

What a beautiful reminder of God’s previous promise! When God first gave it, Abram had no idea what the land would be like, but now his eyes rested upon it. And it was wonderfully good.

Then God said, “Arise, walk in the land through its length and its width, for I give it to you” (Genesis 13:17). Imagine the comfort this brought to Abram. He likely jumped to his feet, grabbed his staff, and began exploring more of the beauty around his camp right away. It would take awhile to trek over the whole land, but he could begin today, as God had lovingly encouraged him to.
