The Egyptian soldiers rubbed their eyes, amazed. They stared at the path running through the Red Sea. They gasped at the towering walls of water. This was impossible! The Israelites had escaped—right through the middle of the Red Sea!

The Egyptians jumped on their chariots and started to chase the Israelites. Their whips cracked as their horses raced down the sandy beach and through the sea.

Who was watching, and what did He do? **Read Exodus 14:24.**

Suddenly their strong shiny chariots started to wobble and shake. Then . . . _creak_ and _crash_! God had stopped the wheels of the chariots from turning. God had stopped the chase!

Safely on the other side, the Israelites watched. Their enemy was stuck in the mud! Then God told Moses, “Stretch out your hand over the sea, that the waters may come back upon the Egyptians, on their chariots, and on their horseman” (Exodus 14:26).

Moses stretched out his hand, and the watery walls came crashing down. The Egyptians were never again to chase the Israelites.

The Israelites had seen their God do the impossible. “They trusted the Lord” (Exodus 14:31, ICB) and knew He was leading.

- How many miracles has God done in the Exodus story this week?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that He can do anything, because nothing is ever too hard for Him.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Collect things from around the house with different-sized wheels: toys, scooters, or bikes. Line them up for a race across a pretend Red Sea. Can you make their wheels all stop still at the same time? Only God can do the impossible.
