**Focus:** I can tell others that I am special because God cares for me, and they are special too.

**Location:** Help a child locate **the Arctic Circle** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Late one night 9-year-old Sasha slipped into her house in a city located right on the Arctic Circle. She had been playing outside in the cold snow, and now she wanted to warm up. It was also time for bed.

As Sasha entered the room, she heard someone speak to her. Sasha didn’t hear the voice with her ears; she heard it in her head, almost like a thought.

“You are special,” said a warm and kind voice.

How was Sasha special? Was it because she lived in a Far North region of Russia called Yamal, which means “end of the land”? Her home did not seem to be at the end of the land, perched at the top of the world.

Or was Sasha special because she was a member of an indigenous (people who lived on the land from earlier times) group of people called the Khanty?

Sasha wasn’t sure why she was special. But she liked being told that she was. Joy filled her heart.

But then Sasha began to have problems. At school other children were mean to her. At home her much older sister, Olga, started drinking. Father and Mother also began to drink. It wasn’t fun being at home or at school.

Sasha wondered if she really was special. She wondered where people came from and what is their purpose.

Several years passed, and Sasha kept wondering. Then she met a new friend named Alyona. She was happy and smiled often. Sasha wanted to be happy, as Alyona was. She was surprised to find out that Alyona was happy even though she hadn’t had a happy life. She didn’t know her parents. She had lived in an orphanage for many years. Then she had been adopted by a Seventh-day Adventist family.

Alyona introduced Sasha to her family. They welcomed her, loved her, and taught her about God. Through her new friend, Sasha learned that people live because God created them. She realized that she is special because God created her and has a purpose for her life..

Today Sasha is happier than ever before. She knows why she is special. God cares for her. She is a child of God.

“I live for God,” she said.

- **How could Alyona be happy even though she had an even harder life than Sasha?**
- **What did Alyona do, that you could do too, to show Sasha that God cared for her?**
