Adam e Eve were criou para each other as the very Primeiro male e female. Deus wanted eles a passar happy dias together, just as Deus, Jesus, e the Espírito Santo Faça. Nós call this togetherness unity, e isso’s a very grande blesCante.

Then Deus abençoou Adam e Eve em another way. Children would come de seu especial amor para each other. Nosso families todos come de this very Primeiro family. Nós’re todos Deus’s precious children. O que words Façaes Deus use a Conte nos como especial nós are? **Leia Isaías 43:4** (Primeiro half).

As Deus’s loved e precious children, Adam e Eve wore a covering de Deus’s warm light e glory that fitted eles perfectly. Imagine never having a change seu clothes e eles never getting old e ripped!

Then Deus deu eles a lindo home too. Ele knew Adam e Eve então well that Ele deu eles the perfeito home that was just right para eles. O que was this home called? **Leia Gênesis 2:15.**

Isso was a wonderful home com a blue sky roof that sparkled com stars em night. Soft, green grass carpeted the floor, e the walls were lush, green plants. Sweet-smelling flowers perfumed the air, e birds tweeted happily. There were no winws a wash ou floors a sweep. Instead, Adam e Eve enjoyed the beauty de seu garden. Eles talked com Deus there, feeling close a Ele em seu peaceful home.

Even though nós Façan’t live em a perfeito world as Adam e Eve did, Deus still wants nos a be close friends com Ele. Nós can feel close a Deus em nosso homes quando nós Leia the Bible, ou out em nature among the bom things Ele has made.
