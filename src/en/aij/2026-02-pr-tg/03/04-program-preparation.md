Opening Activities

Print and cut out **angel outlines** and hide around the Sabbath School area. Source **glitter glue or sparkly pens** for children to then decorate.

Source a **jar with lid**, **water**, **sand**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.

Bible Story and Unpack the Story

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source **a large stone**.

For the jumping competition, source **tape or string** to mark a line to jump from and to jump to (an unreachable distance away). Mark the jumps with tape, or simply have an adult stand adjacent to the farthest jump. A long piece of **fabric** (can be tied together), **string, or tape** to be the “ladder” bridging the gap.
