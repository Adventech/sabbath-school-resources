Lucifer watched Jesus speaking com Deus the Father. O que foi oy discusCante? Como Lucifer wished ele knew! Actually por que didn’t ele know? Wasn’t ele the Primeiro de the covering cherubs, the highest order de Anjos? Wasn’t ele the Anjo quem was closest a the throne de Deus? Por que was _Jesus_ invited a talk closely com Deus the Father, e ele wasn’t?

Lucifer felt jealous de Jesus. The more ele thought sobre Jesus, the more jealous e poisonous seu thoughts became.

Mas _he_ kept thinking eles anyway. Deus knew como poisonous jealous thoughts can be, então Ele warned nos sobre eles em the Ten Commandments. O que did Deus Diga? **Leia Êxodo 20:17.**

Deus knows that quando nós really, really want _anything_ someone else has, nosso hearts will fill up com a poison that will never Faça nos happy. Se only Lucifer would stop a Pense sobre this.

Mas ele didn’t. Ele didn’t Pense sobre como Deus criou todos things através Jesus. Por que would Lucifer, a criou Anjo, be jealous de the One quem criou ele? Whatever was ele thinking?

Thinking too much sobre himself was making seu thoughts ugly e crooked; então crooked that ele even said, “Eu will be like [Deus] Most High” (Isaías 14:14).

No one had ever been jealous em Céu antes. Cada Anjo was happy that seu Anjo friends had especial gifts. No one even knew o que jealousy was! Como sad that Lucifer was inventing stinking thinking.

pense . . . THEN TALK Sobre Isso

- _What would the world be like se there were no jealousy ou pride?_
- _How Faça você feel quando você really, really want o que someone else has? (Remind seu child that muitos people work hard para whatever eles have.)_

Deus loves a ajude nos guarde nosso hearts loving e free de the poison de jealousy.
