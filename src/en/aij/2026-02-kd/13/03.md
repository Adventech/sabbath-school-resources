Tall wheat and barley stalks swayed in the breeze in the fields around Bethlehem. Ruth watched as the men moved through the golden fields and cut the ripe stalks. It was harvesttime in Bethlehem.

As she watched, Ruth thought about her problem. She really needed to find food for Naomi and herself. She had a great idea! What would she do? **Read Ruth 2:2.**

The next day, Ruth got up early and went to the fields. She asked if she could gather the grain that fell to the ground behind the workers. She worked very hard! The worker’s noticed. The owner of the field noticed too. His name was Boaz. He was part of Naomi’s husband’s family.

Boaz called Ruth to him. He would make her feel welcome in this new place. He said, “Listen, my daughter. Stay here in my field to gather grain for yourself. Do not go to any other person’s field” (Ruth 2:8 ICB). Then he invited her to drink the servant’s water whenever she was thirsty. He even gave her grain to eat.

“Why have you been so kind?” Ruth asked him (Ruth 2:10, ICB). Boaz smiled. He knew that Ruth had helped Naomi. He knew she had chosen to follow Naomi and to follow God.

“The Lord will reward you for all you have done,” he said (Ruth 2:12, ICB). God would keep looking after Ruth, and so would he.

- Why do you think he did this?
