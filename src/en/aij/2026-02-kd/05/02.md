As Jacob came closer and closer to his home in Canaan, he felt a warm coziness inside. He noticed the familiar green hills in front of him and smiled. He was almost home!

But his smile soon disappeared. He began thinking about his brother Esau. He remembered his angry words and waving fist. What would Esau think when he found out Jacob was on his way home? What would he do?

God could see that Jacob was worried. He wanted to give Jacob peace instead of worry. Who did God send to meet him? **Read Genesis 32:1.**

Suddenly Jacob blinked. Wow! He rubbed his eyes and looked again. Up ahead and behind him were big, bright angels. It was like God had put special glasses on Jacob to let him see the invisible angels. A whole army of guardian angels were traveling with his family, keeping them safe!

Jacob’s heart instantly filled with peace. He had nothing to fear because God and His angels were near! God was stronger and bigger than any problem he could ever face.

The Bible says, “Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you” (John 14:27). The next time you feel scared or worried, remember you can have God’s peace because His angels are busy protecting you just like they were protecting Jacob. God “has put his angels in charge of you. They will watch over you wherever you go” (Psalm 91:11, ICB).
