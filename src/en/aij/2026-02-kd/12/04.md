For 10 years Naomi, Mahlon, Chilion, Ruth, and Orpah lived as a happy family. They worked, ate, laughed, and worshipped God together.

Back in Canaan, life was still very hard. For the whole 10 years that Naomi and her family had been gone, there had been no rain and no food. Can you imagine being hungry for 10 whole years?

But thankfully the land of Moab was very different. Naomi’s family had plenty to eat. There was always bread to bake and a big pot of yummy stew to eat.

But one day another very sad thing happened. What was it? **Read Ruth 1:5.**

Naomi had lost her two dear sons. Ruth and Orpah had lost their husbands. They hugged each other and cried. Although they couldn’t see Him, God was right there comforting them. He is always “close to the brokenhearted” (Psalm 34:18, ICB).

Good news soon came to sad Naomi. “She heard that the Lord had taken care of his people. He had given food to them in Judah” (Ruth 1:6, ICB). Bethlehem had a good harvest of food coming! In this very sad time God was helping Naomi. He promises, “I am the Lord your God . . . Who leads you by the way you should go” (Isaiah 48:17). It was time to go home.
