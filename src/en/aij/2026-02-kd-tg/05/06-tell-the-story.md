**Big Idea:** God is with me. He gives me peace.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Invite children to hug themselves each time you say “peace.”
- **Stick** for staff.
- **Peg people**: Jacob, Laban, Esau.
- **Glasses** (or sunglasses with no lenses), an angel on a craft stick to fly around the children.
- Use **toy animals** as gifts given to Esau.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 5 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- With a wave of his staff, happy Jacob chased animals. He and his family were going home to Canaan! ^[(Stick.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Laban found out they’d secretly left and was angry. He chased after them, but God told him to listen and be peaceful.
- Laban caught up with Jacob, and they talked. God helped them make peace. ^[(Children hug themselves each time you say “peace.”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Laban went home, Jacob traveled on.
- Jacob became worried about meeting Esau again. God knew. He would help him have peace!
- Suddenly God showed him an army of angels protecting his family. It was as if God gave him glasses to see them. ^[(Glasses; fly angels over children’s heads.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Jacob wanted to make peace with Esau. He sent a peaceful message with a servant. God helped him use polite words.
- His servant ran back panting ^[(run in place)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Oh, no! Esau was coming with an army of 400 men!
- Jacob sent peaceful gifts. Lots and lots of animals! ^[(Toy animals.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

- The Person touched Jacob’s hip, and he fell to the ground. It was God! God blessed him and called him Israel ^[(winner or overcomer)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He received God’s blessing directly, rather than through trickery.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a wave of his stick ^[(stick)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob chased the spotted goats up the rocky path. His smile was big and bright. His family, servants, and animals had left Laban’s camp for good. They were going to Jacob’s home in Canaan!

Back in Haran, Uncle Laban didn’t know that Jacob had left. When he found out, he was very angry! He jumped onto his camel and chased after them! But God spoke to Laban and told him to be quiet and listen. He needed to have peace ^[(children to hug themselves each time you say “peace”)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Laban caught up with his big family. He took a deep breath as he remembered God’s words ^[(take deep breath)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). He said to Jacob, “Why did you run away without telling me?” (Genesis 31:27, ICB).

“I left without telling you, because I was afraid!” Jacob answered (Genesis 31:31, ICB). He was honest with Laban.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After they talked together, they agreed to make peace—no more tricks, lying, or fighting. Then Laban went back home to Haran, and Jacob traveled on.

As Jacob came closer to his home in Canaan, He began thinking about Esau. He remembered his brother’s angry words and waving fist. He was worried!

God could see that Jacob was afraid. He showed him something amazing to give him peace!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Suddenly Jacob blinked. Wow! He rubbed his eyes and looked again! Up ahead and behind him were big, bright angels. It was as if God had put special glasses on Jacob—just for a moment—to let him see the invisible angels. A whole army of angels were traveling with his family, keeping them safe! ^[(Put glasses on each child, then fly angels over their head.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Jacob’s heart filled with peace. He had nothing to fear, because God and His angels were near!

Jacob thought hard. How could he meet Esau in a peaceful way? He asked one of his servants to run ahead with a peaceful message for Esau. God helped Jacob to be wise and polite with his words.

The messenger came back, puffing and panting. Esau was coming with 400 men!

Oh, no! Jacob began to tremble with fear! Esau was coming with an army of men, ready to fight him!

Then he remembered the angel army. God would help him! He prayed. “Please save me from my brother Esau” (Genesis 32:11, ICB).

As he prayed, he had a wonderful idea. He had sent a peaceful message; now he would send a peaceful gift. Not just one gift—lots and lots of gifts!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The next day Esau stared in surprise as a gift of goats trotted toward his camp, with a servant ^[(toy animals)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Then came goats . . . rams . . . camels . . . bulls . . . donkeys, and foals! The servants told Esau they were gifts from Jacob.

Jacob prayed that God would fill Esau’s heart with peace and forgiveness.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When it was time to sleep, Jacob kept praying. Suddenly strong arms grabbed him. ^[(Show peg Jacob wrestling with Someone invisible. Explain that it was dark so Jacob couldn’t see whom he was wrestling.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Jacob grabbed back. He _twisted_ and _turned_, _pushed_ and _pulled_. _Who was this enemy?_ he wondered. It was so dark that he couldn’t see His face.

On and on they wrestled. Then the Man reached out and touched Jacob’s hip. Instantly Jacob fell down to the ground—his hip was badly hurt.

That was no ordinary touch! Jacob realized he had been wrestling with God! Jacob asked God to bless him. He knew he needed God’s forgiveness, strength, and peace. God did! He gave him a new name too, Israel, which means winner (that is, struggle and prevail). He would be a special leader.

Jacob limped back to his family camp ^[(peg Jacob limping to camp)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Then came Esau and his 400 men. Smiling, Jacob hobbled toward his brother and bowed low. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Esau ran toward him and gave him a big hug! ^[(Peg Jacob and Esau embrace.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God had heard Jacob’s prayer. He had changed Esau’s anger and hatred into love and forgiveness. At last there was peace between the brothers! ^[(Put on glasses again.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) He now saw his brother with new eyes of love.
