Moses wiped the sweat from his face. It had been a long and tiring journey all the way from the palace in Egypt to the land of Midian. He “sat down by a well” (Exodus 2:15) and took a deep breath. Ah, he would have a drink of water.

Moses noticed others coming for water too. Who were they? **Read Exodus 2:16.**

As these women tried to pour water for the thirsty sheep, some unkind “shepherds came and chased the girls away” (Exodus 2:17, ICB). That was very mean! Big strong caring Moses stood up to those rude shepherds—he wouldn’t let them pick on others. But this time he didn’t let his anger blow up. Moses was already letting God change him.

Moses brought the women back to the well. He gave them a cool drink, and then he filled the trough with water so that all the thirsty sheep had enough to drink too. How kind!

When the women arrived home, they excitedly told their father, Priest Jethro, what had happened. He was so happy that someone had looked after his daughters.

“Invite him to eat with us” (Exodus 2:20, ICB), Jethro told his daughters. The family welcomed Moses into their home to eat and rest. He found out that this family worshipped the one true God, just as he did! He became part of their family when he married Zipporah, one of Jethro’s daughters. They had two sons named Gershom and Eliezer.

God can make us brave and kind too. Why not ask Jesus to give you a new heart of love right now?
