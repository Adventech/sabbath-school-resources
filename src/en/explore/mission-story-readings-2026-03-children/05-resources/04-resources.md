The Thirteenth Sabbath Offering is known as the Quarterly Mission Project Offering in some world regions, which may use an offering goal device to help focus attention on world missions and increase weekly mission giving: Determine a goal for your class’s weekly mission offering. Multiply it by 14, allowing a double goal for the Thirteenth Sabbath Offering. Remind the listeners that their regular weekly mission offerings help the missionary work of the world church and that one-quarter of the offering will go directly to the projects in the Inter-European Division. Report on mission giving during the quarter. Encourage the listeners to double or triple their normal mission giving on the upcoming Thirteenth Sabbath. Count the offering and record the amount given at the end of Sabbath School.
