As Jacob stood, looking at the reminder rock he had just set up,

he felt so free! He didn’t need to worry about his mistakes anymore. God had forgiven him! God’s love for him was bigger than he could ever imagine!

Jacob looked back at the dusty road. It would soon be time to leave this special place. Surely there was something else he could do to show God how thankful he felt? His big smile grew bigger. He had a great idea—a way to show God his thanks wherever he went! What was it? **Read Genesis 28:22.**

Jacob promised to give God tithe. Every time he counted ten gifts from God, he would give one as an offering back to God.

If Jacob counted out ten shiny coins, he would give one to God. If he counted out ten fluffy sheep in his flock, he would give one to God. And if he would bring in ten baskets of yummy food from the garden, he would give one to God. Jacob wanted to give the best of everything back to God.

What a beautiful thank You! Jacob had found out that “Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and comes down from the Father” (James 1:17).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- What might you give Jesus to show Him how important He is to you?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God for the gifts He has given you, including his love and forgiveness.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})\
Count out 10 different objects then take one away as tithe. Try it with lots of different things: buttons, toys, rice, or pencils. You might like to turn an old jar into your own “tithe” jar for coins, if you have them. Count out 10, then place one in your jar to give to God. Remember that all good gifts come from God.
