[[Revelation 15:1-8]]

- The fact that “no one was able to enter the temple” (verse 8) shows that with the seven last plagues Jesus’ work in the heavenly sanctuary as high priest will cease. There will be no more undecided people to plead for. All decisions for or against Christ will at that time become permanent. There will be no second chance.
