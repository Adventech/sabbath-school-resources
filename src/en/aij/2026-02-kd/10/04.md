Governor Joseph looked up from his work to see 10 shepherds walking toward him to buy grain. He bent his head and looked a little closer. Could it be . . . ?

Yes! It was his brothers! There was Reuben, Simeon, Levi, Judah, Dan, Naphtali, Gad, Asher, Issachar, and Zebulun.

Joseph quickly hid his surprise. His brothers didn’t know it was him! Quietly he watched as “they bowed facedown on the ground before him” (Genesis 42:6, ICB).

Suddenly Joseph remembered the dreams he’d had as a boy! In his first dream his brothers’ wheat had all bowed down to his. In the next dream the sun, moon, and 11 stars had bowed down to him. He remembered his brothers’ angry words: “Do you truly think you will rule over us?” (Genesis 37:8, ICB).

Governor Joseph looked at his brothers carefully. Were they still angry, jealous men? He would test them and see.

Joseph frowned. Then he pretended to get very, very upset. He told his brothers he thought they were enemy spies. Had they come to cause trouble for Egypt?

What did the brothers say? **Read Genesis 42:13.**

Were they telling the truth? Yes! Maybe they had changed. Joseph still wasn’t sure. He decided to give them another test to find out. He had them put in prison for three days. The brothers were so confused! But God was with them in prison, just like He had been with Joseph.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why do you think the brothers didn’t recognize Joseph?
- Will it be a good or bad thing for Joseph to be a ruler over his brothers, just as in his dreams? Why?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that even when we are confused, we can trust that He loves us and cares for us.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Can you find 12 things that are all alike? Perhaps 12 rocks, flowers, blocks, or . . . socks? God loved all the 12 sons of Jacob. He knew them by name. Do you? They could trust Him with all their needs.
