### I Can Ask God for Forgiveness

> <p></p>
> “Lord, listen to my prayer. Lord, forgive us. Lord, hear us and do what we ask.” Daniel 9:19 (ICB)

Have you ever made a mistake and felt sorry? Maybe you said something unkind, did something wrong, or forgot to obey. What can you do when that happens? Daniel teaches us something important: we can always talk to God.

Daniel loved God and trusted Him. When Daniel was worried about his people, he did not ignore the problem. He prayed.

First, Daniel praised God because he knew God was great, loving, and faithful. Then Daniel told God that he and his people had made mistakes. He asked God to forgive them and help them.

Daniel knew that God was fair, but he also knew that God was full of mercy and love. He trusted that God listens when His children come to Him with humble hearts.

Daniel’s prayer was not only about asking for something he wanted. Daniel wanted God’s name honored and people to follow God’s ways.

We can pray like Daniel. We can thank God, tell Him when we are sorry, ask Him to forgive us, and ask Him to help us do what is right.

God is always ready to listen because He loves us.

> <p>Let’s think</p>
> What is something you would like to thank God for today? Is there something you want to ask God to forgive?

> <p>Remember</p>
> God listens when I pray and ask for forgiveness!

> <p>Let’s pray</p>
> Dear God, thank You for loving me and for listening to my prayers. Please forgive me when I make mistakes. Help me follow Your ways and honor You every day. Amen.
