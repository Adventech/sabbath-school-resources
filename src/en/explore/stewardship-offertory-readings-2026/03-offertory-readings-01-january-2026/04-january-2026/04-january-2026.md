Have you heard about the rule of ten? It’s based on some ancient financial advice, and it is still practiced by many people around the world today. Using this rule of ten system has allowed some families to manage their money wisely and to find financial freedom.

It works like this. When God gives you income, the very first payments should go to the three categories of ten:

- 10% for God’s Tithe: This is an act of loyalty, faithfulness, and gratitude. It is a recognition that all you have comes from God and that the first 10% is actually already His, so you faithfully return it to Him.
- 10% as Offerings: This 10% is given as Promise offerings, donations, and gifts. It shows that you recognize God’s gifts and that it is more blessed to give than to receive.
- 10% for Savings: This is a gift to yourself and your family so that you can be better prepared for future needs that might arise. The Bible teaches us the importance of saving to better care for our family in hard times.

In Malachi 3, God challenges us to test Him by faithfully returning the 10% tithe and giving a percentage amount that we can choose as Promise offerings. True financial freedom comes through our returning tithe to God, giving to others, saving wisely, and spending carefully.
