**Focus:** It is wonderful to share the blessing of the Sabbath with others.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Israel** on your **globe** or world **map**.

Kristina and her mother are Seventh-day Adventists who moved to Israel from Ukraine. They didn’t know where to find an Adventist church in the city, so they worshipped every Sabbath in their apartment.

One Sabbath a new school friend, Alona [ah-LOH-nah], knocked on Kristina’s door and asked her to play. Because it was Sabbath, Kristina suggested that the girls play Sabbath School. “What’s Sabbath School?” Alona asked.

“Sabbath School is fun!” Kristina explained. “We sing songs about God and listen to Bible stories. Then we learn a Bible verse and play a game based on the Bible.” Alona thought Sabbath School sounded fun!

The girls gathered their dolls and stuffed animals and set them up in Kristina’s bedroom. Then Kristina began teaching Alona and the dolls songs about Jesus; they read a Bible verse and played a Bible game. The girls had fun playing Sabbath School and decided to play again the next Sabbath.

The girls told their friends about Kristina’s Sabbath School, and one week their friend Nadia [NAH-de-ah] came. She liked it so much that she continued to come whenever she could.

One day Kristina’s mother had good news. “I found an Adventist church today!” she said. “Now we can go to church on Sabbath!” Kristina was so happy. “May I invite Alona and Nadia to come with us?” she asked.

Kristina invited the girls, but their parents thought the church was too far away. Kristina continued to play Sabbath School with them on Sabbath afternoons. Kristina is happy to help others learn that Jesus loves them.
