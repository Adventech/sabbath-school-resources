{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8B3F37"}}}
Create

Choose an empty jar at home. Brainstorm some caring acts that you could do as a family for others. Write each idea on a separate piece of paper and put them into the jar. Pull out an idea every Sabbath afternoon to do together as a family.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8B3F37"}}}
Model

Give your child some scenarios in which they could choose to be caring. For example: “What would a caring heart look like if Mommy or Daddy were folding the washing/ making the beds/taking out the garbage? What would it look like to show care for your grandparents?” Ask God to help your child have a caring heart.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8B3F37"}}}
Build

Build a difficult obstacle course (inside or outside) that your child would need your help to complete. Do the course together, holding your child’s hand. Share how Ruth followed Naomi and helped her along the way as she traveled back to Bethlehem.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8B3F37"}}}
Create

Make a small “caring” heart out of red paper. Decorate it with your child using plastic gems or glitter. Put a safety pin on the back and attach it to your child’s clothing whenever you see them do something caring for someone else. Exclaim “I just love your caring heart!”

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#8B3F37"}}}
Pray

Each night before bed, affirm your child for a specific way they showed a caring heart that day. Share how it makes your heart and God’s heart so happy when they show a caring heart to others. Pray with your child and ask God to come into their heart.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “. . . love one another; as I have loved you” (John 13:34).
