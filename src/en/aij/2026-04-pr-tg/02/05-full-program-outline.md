**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Show the children a **heavy object** and ask a volunteer to lift it. When they struggle, ask if they would like some help. Help them lift and move it so that it’s now out of the way. Share how sometimes in life we have things that weigh us down and are too heavy to carry, such as worries, doubts, and problems. Explain that we face these challenges because we live in a world of sin, where there is a war between good and evil, Jesus and Satan.

- **What might be a problem or worry that makes someone feel “heavy” in their heart?**
- **Whom can we take our problems to?**
- **How does Jesus help us with our problems?**

Invite a few children to try to lift the heavy object together (with an adult too, if needed). Share that Jesus also wants us to help each other, so that no one is left carrying a big problem on their own. **Can you think of someone whom you could help lift his or her burden a bit?** Invite them to think, then say in their mind the name of a person that they might help with a “burden” this week.

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** This week’s story is about someone who had a big problem. He knew that **the only person who could help him was Jesus**, but a part of him doubted whether Jesus really could or not. The man realized he needed to give all his doubts and worries to Him.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together from the whiteboard or Program PowerPoint: **I can bring my worries and doubts to Jesus, for He cares for me.**

**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story to bring it to life for the children.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Worry and sadness made the nobleman go to Jesus for help. We sometimes carry worries and concerns too. Give each child a smooth stone to hold. Have them hold their arm out straight while holding their stone.

- **Think of something you’ve felt worried about lately** (don’t say it out loud). **It’s a little like this stone. How’s your arm feeling? Is it getting tired?** If you were to carry the stone around all day, with your arm held out, you would feel tired. Even little worries can be a heavy burden over time.
- **Do you ever doubt that Jesus can help you with your worries?**
- **What does Jesus want us to do when we are feeling this way?** (Pray, ask God for help to believe and to give our worries and problems to Him to work out for us.)

Spend a moment in silent prayer, inviting each child to give any worries to God. Then have children put their stones in a pile at the front of the room.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Jesus wants us to go to Him with all our doubts and worries. In the Bible He tells us not to worry, but to pray to Him and tell Him everything we need (Philippians 4:6).

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Card 41: Nobleman and Son** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture. Invite the children to say the **memory verse** together as a group: “. . . casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7). Then sing the **Scripture Song**.

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-04-pr-tg/assets/2-1-peter-5-7.mp3] “1 Peter 5:7” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singers","value":"Elle Stojanovic, TVAC Choir"},{"key":"Pianist","value":"Johanna McKay"},{"key":"Guitarist","value":"David Williams"},{"key":"Percussion","value":"David Williams"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation of Seventh-day Adventists®"}

- **What does it mean to “cast” our care on Him?** (To give, to throw off, as a fisherman throws off a net.)
- **What might “care” mean in this verse? How is this meaning different from “He cares for you”?** (Care is our worries and troubles; to have loving concern, help for, and kindness toward someone.)
- **How does this verse make you feel?**

Then if time allows, give each child a beanbag. Place a container at the front and invite the children to stand in a line. One at a time children say a word of the memory verse as they “cast” their beanbag into the bucket. Repeat, ensuring each child has a turn.

- **How does the memory verse describe God?**
- **How, in this week’s story, has God shown that He is like this?**

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Nature Lesson, **“The Fawn,”** and discuss the follow-up questions.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Display your **The Nobleman’s Son craft** and explain how to make it.

Play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** quietly in the background as children complete their craft. They can practice telling the story to a friend after they’ve finished the craft. Challenge the children to use their craft to share the story and its message with at least one other person beyond their Sabbath School class this week.
