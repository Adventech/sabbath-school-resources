“Happy birthday, Joe!” Joe’s eyes sparkled as he touched the shiny red pedal car his mom and dad had given him. His very own car! His parents laughed with joy to see how thrilled he was. Joe climbed in behind the steering wheel and pumped the pedals with his feet to make the car go.

Once Joe got the hang of it, he was allowed to pedal up and down the driveway and out on the sidewalk. One morning he decided he was a good enough driver to take his car onto the street. Mom was frightened when she saw what he was doing. They had a long talk about safety, and Joe promised not to drive on the street again.

Several days later Joe was back on the street again. He believed he was an excellent driver and should be allowed out with the real cars. This time Joe’s mom had to punish Joe, and she made him stay in his room to think about things for a while.

A few days later Joe was back out on the street with the big cars. This time Joe’s punishment was harder. He lost out on treats and couldn’t ride his car for two whole weeks. In the end Joe promised he would never go out into the street again. So at last his mom and dad allowed him to drive his red car again.

_Soon after_, Joe thought, _My parents don’t understand. I just have to drive on the street, as all men do._ This time it was Joe’s dad who saw him. He felt that Joe’s punishment should be something he would remember for a long, long time. He gave Joe’s car away. Joe was so upset, but his dad wouldn’t give in. “You broke your promise and disobeyed many times, and you didn’t listen to us. It would not be safe to let you have the car anymore.”

Joe was upset for days. He let himself think that his parents didn’t love him and that they were mean to give his car away.
