{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#76806D"}}}
Create

Write your child’s name on a piece of paper. Talk to them about the meaning of their name and why you chose it for them. Use leaves and flowers to stick over the letters. Share how Jesus knows your child’s name and everything about them. He loves to listen and talk to us. He loves us so much!

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#76806D"}}}
Play

Hide in the house and call your child’s name, asking them to come. When they find you, praise them for their listening ears and obedient heart.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#76806D"}}}
Sing

Sing or listen to a comforting song about God knowing you, before your child goes to sleep every night this week.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#76806D"}}}
Pray

Take turns praying together every morning and night. Thank God that He made you and knows everything about you.

**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Speak, for Your servant hears” (1 Samuel 3:10).
