Big brother Reuben looked down into the dark hole. He squinted and tried his hardest to see. He had left his brothers just after they had thrown Joseph into the deep pit. Now he’d come back to secretly free his little brother. But where was he? He was gone!

Reuben was so upset! What had his brothers done to poor Joseph?

With tears in his eyes, he raced to find them. The brothers held up their bag of money. Joseph wasn’t hurt. They had sold him as a slave!

The brothers had told Reuben the truth. But now they had another choice to make. What would they tell their father? Would they have the courage to tell the truth or not?

All 10 brothers chose to tell a very terrible lie. Picking up Joseph’s colorful coat, they dipped it in goat’s blood and carried it home. Finding their father, “They said, ‘We found this robe. Look it over carefully. See if it’s your son’s robe’ ” (Genesis 37:32, ICB).

The brothers let their father believe Joseph had been killed by a wild animal. Poor Father Jacob. What did he do? **Read Genesis 37:34.**

The brothers watched their father cry. He was so sad, no one could comfort him. They realized they had made a big, horrible mistake. But none of them, not even Reuben, had the courage to tell the truth.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- What should the brothers have done?
- Can you think of a time when you had the courage to tell the truth about something you did that was wrong?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you always choose to be honest about your mistakes.
