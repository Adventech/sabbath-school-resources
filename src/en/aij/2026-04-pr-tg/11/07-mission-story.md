**Focus:** People who know Jesus want to make Him known to others.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Mongolia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

Itgel is a 10-year-old boy in Mongolia. His name, Itgel, means “faith” in the Mongolian language. Itgel has a lot of faith.

When the school year began, Itgel was excited to return to the Seventh-day Adventist school. He was ready to start fourth grade. At the end of the first week of school all the boys and girls got together for a special sports day. Itgel ran and ran and ran as fast as he could. That night, however, he didn’t feel well. His legs hurt terribly. He could barely move.

His mom was worried, and she put a hand on his forehead. He was burning up with fever! Mom and Dad took the boy to the hospital. The doctor saw Itgel and was also worried. He pulled out a syringe and gave Itgel a shot. Itgel had to stay in the hospital.

That night Itgel tried to fall asleep, but it was hard to sleep when he felt so hot and his legs hurt so much. He prayed in his heart, “God, please heal me quickly.”

Itgel couldn’t walk. His legs hurt, and he felt so weak. The doctor told him not to try to stand up. A nurse helped him sit in a wheelchair.

Itgel was happy for the wheelchair, but he wanted to walk. He prayed, “God, please heal me quickly. I want to start walking on my own. I want to go back to school to see my friends.”

Itgel didn’t know when or how, but he believed that God would heal him. He decided to keep praying. He prayed first thing in the morning. He prayed when the doctor came to help him. He prayed when he went to sleep at night. For two weeks Itgel prayed.

One day the doctor said, “You’re well! You can go home.”

Itgel was so happy! God had answered his prayers.

When Itgel returned to school, his friends had many questions. Some friends didn’t come from Christian homes. Itgel was so happy to tell them what God had done for him. He hopes they will learn to trust and love Him too.

“I was really sick, but now I’m well because I prayed,” Itgel told his friends. “God healed me.”

**Discuss:**

- **What did Itgel’s faithful prayers say to God?** (He had faith in a big God who could do miracles.)
- **What did Itgel want to do when Jesus said “Yes” to his prayers?** (Share with other people what God had done for him.)
- **What words would you use to describe Itgel’s faith and trust?** (Big faith, faithful, believes.)
