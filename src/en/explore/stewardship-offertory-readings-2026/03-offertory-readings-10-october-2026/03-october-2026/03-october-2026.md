---
title: Carefully and Prayerfully, but Not Fearfully
date: 17/10/2026
---

In Matthew 14:27, Jesus said, “Be of good cheer! It is I; do not be afraid” (NKJV). Believing Jesus, Peter soon found himself in the middle of the stormy sea, standing on nothing but water!

But just moments into his faith journey, he took his eyes off Jesus, saw all the dangers surrounding him, and began to sink in fear. How easy it is to be like Peter—from faithful to fearful in a moment.

We often fear because we’re overfocusing on temporary securities, like finances, careers, investments, health, or even our lives. Yes, we must manage these things faithfully, but Jesus tells us not to worry or fear.

When fear starts rising, we are invited to dive into our Bibles; fix our minds on Jesus, His promises, and our eternity with Him; and let the fear melt away like frost in the morning sun. When we study God’s perfect love, it casts out all our fears.

The Bible clearly explains that calamities will happen on earth. Jesus foretold these terrible things before they happened so that our faith would be strengthened when we see them happening.

In times of crisis—with our health, finances, or other losses—let’s move forward carefully and prayerfully, but not fearfully.
