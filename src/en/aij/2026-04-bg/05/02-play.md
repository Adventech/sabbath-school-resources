{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Create

Use play-dough to make “grain” (barley stalks). Make a little basket for Ruth to collect it in. God cared for Ruth by giving her food to collect.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Explore

Go on a special hunt to find a bird (outside or in a book). Share how God looks after the little birds and makes sure they have food and a home. Read Luke 12:6 to your child. Explain that God loves to take care of us—just as He does the birds.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Connect

Ask your child what they would like for dinner one night. Allow them to help you make it. Together, share how God has provided food for your family. Say a special prayer thanking God for this food, and pray for other children who are hungry today.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Donate

Give food to a food pantry or cook something for someone you think might need it. Pray for the people around the world who do not have enough food. Consider giving money to a charity that helps feed the hungry.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#4A6782"}}}
Pray

Each night for family worship this week, thank God for one thing each that God has given you.

{"style": {"text": {"color": "#4A6782", "typeface": "Omnes-Regular"}}}
**Extension weekly memory verse**: “Those who seek the Lord shall not lack any good thing” (Psalm 34:10).
