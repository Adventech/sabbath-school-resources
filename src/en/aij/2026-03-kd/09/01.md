An Invitation

There would be a family wedding in the little town of Cana. How exciting! And Jesus was invited. He wasn’t the only one invited. Mary, His mother, would be there too, along with other family and friends.

Who else had been invited? **Read John 2:2.**

Weddings are such a happy time to celebrate the love between a man and a woman. Right from the creation of the world, God asked a man to “leave his father and mother and be united with his wife” (Genesis 2:24, ICB). Marriage is something God created. It is a special forever promise between a man and a woman who love each other.

Jesus and His disciples wanted to be at the wedding to celebrate with the happy couple too. Off they went, down the road toward Cana in Galilee.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#C393C2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been to a wedding? What did you see? What happened?
- Why do you think Jesus wanted to go to the wedding?
