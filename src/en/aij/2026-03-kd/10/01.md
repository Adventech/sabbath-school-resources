**Big Idea:**\
Jesus is my God. He gives me all I need.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Give us this day our daily bread”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ (Matthew 6:11).

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Give”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - When we give something, we take something we have and make it someone else’s. Jesus loves to give us the things we need, such as food, family, and a warm, safe place to sleep at night.

**This week’s lesson**\
is based on Matthew 14:13-21; Mark 6:30-44; Luke 9:10-17; John 6:1-14; and _The Desire of Ages_, pp. 364-371.

The Lake Race

The disciples were tired—really tired. It had been a big and busy day with lots of crowds. Their eyes were heavy, their legs ached, and their shoulders drooped.

Jesus looked at the disciples with kindness and care. He knew what they needed.

“Come with me,” Jesus said to them. “We will go to a quiet place to be alone. There we will get some rest” (Mark 6:31, ICB).

The disciples slowly followed Jesus down to a boat that swayed gently on the rippling waves. They pushed it out into the deep water and climbed in. Where were they going? **Read Mark 6:32.**

_Ahh, quiet rest._ The boat glided peacefully through the water toward the other side of the lake.

But back on the shore the people noticed Jesus leaving. They weren’t ready to say goodbye to Jesus yet! Quickly they made a decision.

The crowds began to run, run, run! They dashed over rocks and sprinted along the sand. With a huff and a puff they raced down the winding dirt path that led around the lake. The people ran so fast that “they got there before Jesus arrived” (Mark 6:33, ICB). Finally the boat came to land on the far side of the lake. But Jesus and the disciples realized they weren’t alone. The crowds had followed them.

Jesus’ eyes glowed with love and care. He could see how much the people wanted to be close to Him. Jesus knew they needed His help.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#8AC3A7", "size": "lg"}}})

- Would you have liked to have raced around the lake to surprise Jesus?
- How can you search for and follow Jesus each day?
