**Opening Activities**

Set out some puzzles for the children to do.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box:** Cut up some **fruit** or prepare bite-sized fruits, such as berries, for the children to share. Store in a container to place inside the box as Sabbath School is starting.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.

**Tell the Story**

Source **suggested Storytelling Props**: keys to shake, cup, grapes (toy or real), bread (toy or real) and basket, toy bird, fancy cloth as governor’s coat.

Source a **felt set** or the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source items for **Story Explorer Box**: Make a king’s **crown** out of cardstock, stapling or taping the ends together. Use the **paper chain** you made as a storytelling prop last week. Include a **small mirror**.
