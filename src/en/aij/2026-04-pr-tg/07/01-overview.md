**Lesson Summary**

Jairus, the ruler of the synagogue, asked Jesus in faith to heal his sick and dying daughter. Jesus went with the man to his home right away. But on the way a messenger arrived telling Jairus that his daughter had died. It looked hopeless. Jesus, however, encouraged Jairus to still believe that his daughter would be healed. Trusting Jesus, he continued with Jesus toward his home to witness the great miracle of his daughter being brought back to life.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that they can have faith in Jesus.
- **Feel** encouraged to keep believing and trusting in Jesus, even when things are hard or hopeless.
- **Respond** by encouraging others to trust Jesus too.
