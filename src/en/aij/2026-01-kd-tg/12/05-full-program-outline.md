**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a large T-shirt. Invite two friends to get into the T-shirt together. Instruct them to try to find the sock and shoe hidden in the room before you can count to 10. (Invite the other children to count slowly with you.)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ask the children in the T-shirt:

- **How well did you stick together to find the shoe?**
- **How well do a sock and shoe stick together when you wear them?**
- **How might sticking together in a T-shirt and a sock/shoe remind us of good friends?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Good friends stick together. We call this being loyal to each other. Loyal friends are kind, loving, and “there for each other” no matter what. They are “forever friends.”

Our loving God is our loyal friend too. He is the most loyal friend we can have.

Invite the children to stand up and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is loving. He can help me to be a loyal friend.**

Today’s Bible story is about how loyal Abram rescued his nephew and good friend Lot from trouble.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal a water bottle, two glass jars, and some food coloring. Pour water into the two glass jars. Add a drop of food coloring into one jar for each answer to the following question:

- **What bad choices did Lot make in our story today?** (He moved close to Sodom, visited Sodom, stayed the night in Sodom, moved into Sodom, made friends with the people in Sodom.)

Compare the water in the two glasses and highlight how the water got darker and darker with each tiny drop of color.

- **Which jar reminds you that one bad choice can lead to another and another?** (The jar of colored water.)
- **Which one reminds you to stay loyal to God and your friends who love Him?** (The clear water jar.)

**Teaching Point:** The dark jar reminds us of what happens when we choose friends that are making the wrong choices. Like the clear jar, our big loving God can help us to see clearly and choose loyal friends.

Repeat the **Big Idea: God is loving. He can help me to be a loyal friend.**
