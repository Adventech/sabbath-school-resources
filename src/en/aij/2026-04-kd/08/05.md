It was another delightful Sabbath day. The synagogue service had ended, and the people made their way home with their family and friends. A Pharisee leader had planned a Sabbath meal at his fancy house and asked Jesus to join him. Jesus happily accepted.

The Pharisee had invited his proud Pharisee friends too. They liked to show off to others. When it came time to eat, Jesus “noticed that some of the guests were choosing the best places to sit” (Luke 14:7, ICB). They wanted to show that they were more important than others.

Jesus humbly sat down on a seat where no one really wanted to sit. After prayer, they all began eating a yummy meal. All the proud Pharisees watched Jesus closely, as they munched and crunched their lunch.

Who was brought to Jesus? **Read Luke 14:2.**

This man had a sickness that made his arms and legs swell up.

The Pharisees didn’t show any care for this man. They were too busy trying to prove how important they were. But Jesus cared about him. Jesus cares about everyone! Jesus could see the man’s pain and wanted to help him.

Jesus reached out and touched the man. Instantly the man’s body was healed! The man was so happy and thankful.

On that special Sabbath, Jesus showed this man who had been sick that he mattered. He also showed the Pharisees that Sabbath was a good day to make someone feel better. Sabbath is a happy day, and a day when we should show others how much God loves them.
