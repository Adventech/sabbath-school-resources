The Thief

_Clink, clink._ Zacchaeus slid two coins into the money box with a smile.

Zacchaeus was a “chief tax collector, and he was rich” (Luke 19:2). He had the job of collecting tax money from everyone in Jericho and giving it to the Roman rulers.

Shopkeepers, farmers, and fishermen all lined up in front of Zacchaeus’ table. Quietly they waited their turn to pay. But Zacchaeus was sneaky and dishonest. He sat at that table and lied and cheated.

What is commandment number eight of the Ten Commandments? **Read Exodus 20:15.**

Zacchaeus didn’t follow God’s way of love. This hurt God. Instead, he chose to steal. If a farmer needed to pay one silver coin, Zacchaeus made him pay two. Zacchaeus would drop one coin into the money box. Then, shh . . . when no one was watching, the other coin would disappear into his pocket—all for himself.

The people knew Zacchaeus was a thief. But they couldn’t stop him. They would walk away from his table sad and angry. Now their families would go without things they really needed.

When we take things that aren’t ours, we hurt the people we steal from. Jesus wants us to always be giving and loving instead. He can change us so that we want to be like that too!

Jesus could even help greedy Zacchaeus.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})

- Why was it wrong for Zacchaeus to take extra coins for himself?
- Who was hurt by his cheating and stealing?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask Jesus to help you respect others and be honest.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#F79A83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Our memory verse says, “Therefore, if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation” (2 Corinthians 5:17). What can you find outside that is brand-new? Can you find a flower that’s just opened up, a new green shoot, new snow, or a new ants’ nest? Our Creator loves to make things new—even inside our hearts!
