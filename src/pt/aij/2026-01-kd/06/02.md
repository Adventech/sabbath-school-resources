Adam added a big stone to the pile. One, two, three, four . . . He counted all the stones. Yes, that looked perfect! This special pile of stones was called an altar.

God had asked Adam and Eve to build the altar. They were to place a pure lamb on top and say they were sorry to God for their sins. God would send down fire to burn the sacrifice, and He would forgive them.

Adam, Eve, Cain, and Abel obeyed God. They would come to the family altar together, bringing a lamb. This special offering reminded them of God’s promise. God’s Son would be just like that lamb—perfect and pure. Jesus would come and die on the cross so that sins could be forgiven.

With God’s help, Adam, Eve, and their boys would do their best to obey His ways. But they would still make mistakes—just like us. Would God’s love ever change? **Read Psalm 136:26.**

Little Cain and Abel followed their parents back home from the family altar. They skipped along, smiling. It was so good to be forgiven!

God loves to forgive anyone who says, “I’m sorry; please forgive me.” The Bible says that when people do this, “He will freely forgive them” (Isaiah 55:7, ICB).

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Tell your sins to Jesus and ask Him to forgive you. Thank Him for loving you forever.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#f79a83", "size": "lg"}}})\
Using either chalk on a chalkboard or a lead pencil on paper, do a giant scribble. Really make a mess! This is like our hearts when we don’t obey God’s way of love. Now ask God for His forgiveness and rub it all clean with a cloth/eraser.
