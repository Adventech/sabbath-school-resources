The dust that rested in the shape of a man lay on the ground right where God had formed him. Animals may have stood close by, watching. What did God do next? **Read Genesis 2:7.**

God bent down and breathed into the nose of the man, and all of a sudden life went into him. The man who had been just a shape on the ground started to breathe and move. His eyes blinked open, and he looked into God’s kind, beautiful face.

God gave this man a name. (Do you know what it was?) Adam! This word means “ground” or “man.” Adam was very, very tall—as tall as two men standing on top of each other! He was strong and handsome and very smart. God had made Adam in His likeness. He also gave Adam special clothes to wear, made out of light, like the angels wore.

The Bible says that God knew you even before you were born. It says, “Before I formed you in the womb I knew you” (Jeremiah 1:5). God knows everyone in the whole wide world. He knows every child, every mommy and daddy, and every grandma and grandpa. He knew them even before they were born.

The Bible tells us that God created everything right at the beginning, but more than that, it tells us how wonderful God is to have made it all. What a loving God He is!
