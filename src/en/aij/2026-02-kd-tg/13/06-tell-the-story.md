**Big Idea:** God is with me. He welcomes everyone into His family.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Lay some **stones** in a sandbox to make a path to Bethlehem. Invite the children to walk a **toy donkey** and **wooden peg person** for the journey.
- Give the children some **grass stalks** as wheat to throw into the sandbox as you talk about the barley fields. Invite them to collect it to illustrate what Ruth was able to do. Heavy/full bag to carry.
- Use a **blanket** and **small pillow** to illustrate what Naomi told Ruth to do.
- Invite children to take turns holding a **baby doll** wrapped in a **blanket**.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 13 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set** or **peg people**, if desired.
- Always use expressive tones and actions to engage them.

```

**Pray:** Before you begin, pray. Ask God to help everyone understand today’s Bible story. By doing this, you will model how important it is to pray to invite God to teach us from His living Word before we read the Bible.

**TELL THE STORY: QUICK CUES**

- The donkey swished its tail as Naomi walked home to Bethlehem.
- Ruth was coming too! She had promised, “Every place you go, I will go. . . . Your God will be my God” (Ruth 1:16, ICB). ^[(Walk toy donkey and two wooden people along the path in the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Ruth and Naomi arrived in Bethlehem. The excited women welcomed them.
- Ruth and Naomi unpacked and set up their home. But what to eat? Ruth had an idea.
- She went to the barley fields to pick up the leftover grain the workers left on the ground. ^[(Invite children to pick up pieces of grain from the sandbox.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- The workers saw she was a hard worker. The owner of the field, Boaz, noticed her too. He invited her to always work in his field, and gave her water and grain.
- Boaz wanted to be extra kind: _Shh!_ He told his servants to drop extra grain for her. Ruth carried home lots and lots of grain! They had all they needed ^[(drop some more grain into the sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).
- Naomi told Ruth it was time to get married. Boaz would be the perfect man! She told Ruth what to do.
- Ruth went to where Boaz was sleeping and pulled some of his blanket off his feet and put it over herself. ^[(Invite a child to lie down on the pillow. Place part of the blanket over her.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})
- Boaz was so happy. Ruth was asking him to marry her! Boaz and Ruth became husband and wife.
- Soon they had a baby, Obed ^[(pass doll around for children to hold)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). One day King David, and even Jesus, would be born into their family.
- God had truly welcomed Ruth into His family. He welcomes everyone with love.

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The donkey swished its tail as he plodded down the dusty road. Naomi patted his head softly as she walked beside him. She was going home. Home to Bethlehem ^[(walk toy donkey and wooden people in sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

Ruth was coming too! She had promised Naomi, “Every place you go, I will go. . . . Your God will be my God (Ruth 1:16, ICB). Naomi had lost her husband and her two sons, but Ruth had promised to stick with her like super glue! And Ruth had chosen to follow God too.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Finally they made it to Bethlehem. Everyone in Bethlehem was excited! The women of the town all rushed to see Naomi. They were sad to hear about her family, but happy to meet Ruth! When they heard how Ruth had chosen to follow Naomi and follow God, they welcomed her.

The two women unpacked and made Bethlehem their home. But they would need food to eat. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Ruth looked out at the fields full of ripe barley and wheat ^[(use grasses as wheat)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). She had a great idea. Naomi thought so too!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) The next day Ruth got up early and went to the fields. The farm workers were cutting the grain for harvest. She asked if she could gather the grain that fell to the ground behind the workers. She worked very hard! The workers noticed. Boaz, the owner of the field, noticed too ^[(children collect grain from sandbox)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Boaz called Ruth to him. He wanted to make her feel welcome! He invited her to stay in his field to work. Then he offered her water and grain to eat.

Boaz wanted to be even more helpful to kind Ruth. He knew that Ruth had helped Naomi. _Shh!_ Secretly he asked his workers to drop more grain for Ruth to pick up each day ^[(drop more grain)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}).

All day Ruth worked hard picking up the grain. Whew, it was hard work. There was lots and lots of grain! She bundled it all up and carried it home to Naomi. Naomi clapped her hands for joy! God had truly looked after Ruth!

Every working day Ruth went back to Boaz’s field. Then she carried home a big, big sack of grain! ^[(Carry heavy bag.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) When their pile of grain grew big enough, Ruth or Naomi could take it to the market and swap it for such things as oil, fruit, or wool. They had all they needed.

One day Naomi called Ruth to her side. She smiled as she told Ruth it was time for her to get married again. She knew the perfect man for her—kind Boaz! Boaz had shown them that He loved God.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) That evening Ruth made her way to the place where Boaz worked. He lay under a blanket on the work floor, fast asleep. Ruth tiptoed over to where he slept and gently pulled a little bit of his blanket off his feet and placed it over her. Boaz woke up. He was surprised to see her, but so happy. In those days this was a way for Ruth to ask Boaz to look after her—to marry her.

Kind Boaz and Ruth were married. Naomi couldn’t stop smiling. God had been good to her. She now had a daughter and a son. Soon she became a grandmother. ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Ruth and Boaz had a little baby boy, named Obed ^[(pass doll around for children to hold)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Happy tears slid down Naomi’s face as she cuddled her little grandson. One day this special little boy would grow up to become the grandfather of King David. And years later Jesus, the Promised One, would be born into this family too.

God had truly welcomed Ruth into His family. And we can be sure that He welcomes everyone with love.
