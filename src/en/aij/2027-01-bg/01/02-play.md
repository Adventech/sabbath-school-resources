Create

Place green-colored noodles/play-dough (grass); blue tissue paper (water); and plastic animals, fish, flowers, people, and trees in a container. Talk about the wonderful world God made as your child designs a world in the container. Tell them about how God made the world in seven days, each one with something special for them.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#2a459c"}}}
Explore

Take a nature walk through your neighborhood or a park or the grocery store. Look for different colors of flowers or various shapes of leaves, and count how many kinds of animals or types of fruit and vegetables you can find. Talk about what a beautiful world God made. Spend time thanking God for the things you find.

{"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Omnes-BlackItalic", "size": "lg", "color": "#2a459c"}}}
Learn

Play a game of hide-and-seek with your child using a toy or stuffed animal. This game can be adapted for the development level of your child. For the littlest ones, hide the object under a blanket while they are watching and ask, “Where did it go?” Wait for them to pull off the blanket and then say, “There it is!” The game can become more complex for older toddlers by hiding more than one object and asking them to remember where each one was hidden. Say, “Good thinking! God made you to remember so much! We can remember God made a wonderful world for you and me too!”

Thank

Each morning, repeat poem with motions to thank God for the wonderful world He made.

Thank You, God, for the whales in the sea, _(Point up, hands to swoosh through “water.”)_\
The marching elephants and tall, tall trees. _(Marching, then reach up on tiptoe.)_\
Thank You, God, for making me, _(Point up, then to self.)_\
My nose and mouth, toes and knees. _(Point to each.)_\
Thank You, God, for the wonderful world I see! _(Point up, then point to eyes.)_

Pray

Throughout the day, watch for moments to pause with your child and say a thank-You prayer to God: when they find a trail of ants or a pretty leaf; before eating or upon waking up; when they jump so high or draw a colorful picture.
