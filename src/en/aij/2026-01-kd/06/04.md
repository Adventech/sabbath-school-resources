Abel gently petted the little lamb that sat on his lap. He loved being a shepherd even if the sheep were sometimes a bit naughty!

In the distance he could see Cain, working hard digging the soil and planting a new field full of seeds. Cain was a really good farmer. He grew yummy fruit and vegetables for the whole family.

Cain and Abel were even taller now! They were big, strong men who did their jobs well. They were big enough to build their own altars and be sorry for their own sins. Would they still obey God when they offered their sacrifices?

One morning Abel took his lamb out to his altar, just as God had asked him to do. He asked God to forgive him for his bad words and sinful thoughts. Abel knew that God is loving and he wanted to obey Him.

God sent down a blazing fire from heaven to show Abel that He had heard and forgiven him. Abel was so thankful!

Then it was Cain’s turn. What did he put on the altar? Read Genesis 4:3.

Fruit? That wasn’t what God had asked Cain to put on the altar. Fruit showed only what Cain could grow. It was nothing like the lamb offering that showed what God’s Son would do one day.

Cain waited and waited for the fire from heaven . . . but there was nothing. He looked up. Nothing. He looked around. Nothing.

Cain noticed Abel’s altar burning brightly, and he “became very angry” (Genesis 4:5, ICB). He glared at his brother.

How unfair! he thought. Cain wasn’t sorry at all—he was just angry, proud, and stubborn.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever been angry instead of sorry because you had done wrong?
- What made Cain so angry?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you make good choices that will lead to happiness.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#c393c2", "size": "lg"}}})\
With help, write “I’m so sorry, Jesus” on a piece of paper. Pray these words quietly to God. Then ask an adult to safely burn up the paper. When Jesus forgives us, He takes our sins away completely.
