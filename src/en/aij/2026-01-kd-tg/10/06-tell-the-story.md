**Big Idea:** God is loving. I will follow Him.

As you prepare to share this story, please read the student guide for the full, detailed narrative. Include additional props as needed, depending on what you have available. To maximize engagement, avoid reading the story to the children. See the storytelling suggestions at the front of this publication.

```=Storytelling Suggestions

- Travel to one of your “destination” **^[signs]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** each time you share where Abram and his family went. **Colored blocks** and **world map**.
- Stop to point out hills, valleys, and wide-open spaces as you walk with the children. Pick **fruit** along the way. Use **plush animals** to “walk” with as a reminder of everything that went with them.
- Use a small **suitcase** or **bag** to “pack” items inside for each journey.
- Have **clothing, pillow, blanket, containers, etc.**, to place inside the suitcase.
- Use **green** and **blue fabric** to lay out to illustrate the land of Canaan.
- Use the **^[cardboard box altar]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** (week 6) or **stones** to build an altar with the children each time this arises in the story.
- Show the ^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) pictures from the student guide as you tell the story (**^[week 10 Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**).
- Use a **felt set**, if available.
- Use expressive tones and actions to engage the children’s interest.

```

**TELL THE STORY**

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) New homes, new farms, and even big cities popped up around the world. ^[(Place colored blocks onto a world map on the floor.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) God’s plan to fill the world with people was happening! But sadly, most people had forgotten about God again.

God loved all the people, so He looked for a helper to remind them of His great love. God began a kind of “treasure hunt”! He didn’t need a map or even clues. He didn’t look for shiny rubies or sparkly diamonds. He looked for friends! ^[(Children greet the person next to them as a friend.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Adam, Abel, Seth, Enoch, and Noah had been God’s true friends.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Now God was looking for a new, faithful friend. He found a friend named Abram who loved Him. Abram had grown up in the city of Ur. Nearly everyone in Ur worshipped idols. But not Abram! No, Abram refused to become like everyone else. Abram chose the one true God and talked to Him every day.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) One day God said, “Leave your country. . . . Go to the land I will show you. I will make you a great nation, and I will bless you. . . . And you will be a blessing to others” (Genesis 12:1, 2, ICB). Leave? Without knowing where he was going?

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Abram obeyed! Just like that. He knew that God is loving, so he could follow Him.

Busily Abram and his family packed up “everything they owned” (Genesis 12:5, ICB). ^[(Pack suitcase with the children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) They strapped their bags and baskets onto their camels and donkeys ^[(bring along plush animals)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) and set off. Abram smiled with excitement as he walked along the dusty road toward Haran ^[(point to Haran sign)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). God had promised He would bless him, lead him, and make him the father of many, many people.

Abram did not know it yet, but they were going to Canaan. God was leading him there ^[(head toward Shechem sign)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Finally they arrived in Canaan at a place called Shechem. It was beautiful! The grass was lush and green. ^[(Lay out green and blue fabric, unpack the suitcase, and place animals down.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Juicy grapes covered the vine trees, and fresh, clear water filled the rivers and streams.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) With a thankful heart “Abram built an altar there to God” (Genesis 12:7). ^[(Use cardboard-box altar or use stones to build one with the children.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) It was the very first altar to God in the land of Canaan. He gathered everyone close and offered a sacrifice to say, “Thank You, God.” Soon it was time to travel again. Abram and his family packed up again and went to Bethel. ^[(Take suitcase and animals, and go to Bethel sign.)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})

Everywhere they stopped, Abram and Sarai always built an altar to God ^[(use altar again)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Abram and Sarai invited others to join them. They wanted everyone to learn about their one true God and worship Him too. They were wonderful missionaries!

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) After some time there was no rain in Canaan. Abram’s traveling household and animals became thirsty and hungry. Abram led them to find food and water in Egypt ^[(take suitcase and go to Egypt sign)]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}). Abram was worried they wouldn’t be safe there, so instead of trusting God to protect them, he disobeyed God’s law of love and told the people and the king of Egypt a lie about his family.

^[►]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}}) Lying is never the right choice. But God didn’t forget about Abram or stop being His friend. God forgave Abram and helped him leave Egypt safely.

In good times and bad, Abram was learning to trust his Best Friend, God. He knew that God is loving. He would follow Him.
