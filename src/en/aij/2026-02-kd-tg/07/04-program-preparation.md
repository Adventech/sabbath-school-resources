**Opening Activities**

Source items to create a cubby house “dark cave”: **blankets or sheets** over **chairs/tables or a pop-up tent, pillows, and a small flashlight**.

Source items for **Secret Surprise Box: Stickers** to use as rewards, **blindfold**. **Tape** to mark out two jumping courses on the ground. Use the tape to mark a square that’s big enough for them to stand in. Make it a leap for the children to get to the next square, but keep it safe. Make about four squares for each course.

Source a birthday gift of your choice to give to a child with a birthday this past week.
