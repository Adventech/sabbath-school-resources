Day after day God provided new manna. Every morning the Israelites would look outside and smile. Their sweet miracle bread was dotted all over the camp. Grabbing their bowls, they would hurry to collect it. Everyone took just what they needed, and nobody ever went hungry.

They were to collect the manna fresh each morning. God had told them not to keep any for the next day. But some people didn’t listen. They didn’t trust God to be their provider. After they’d eaten their sweet, yummy bread, they kept a little extra aside. Then they stored it away, where no one could see.

When they woke up the next day, their tent was filled with a horrible stink. It was the manna! It was already rotting and filled with wriggling worms. Yuck! They should have listened to God.

When Friday morning came, the people woke up to collect their manna as usual. But this time they took an extra-big bowl with them. God had told them that there would be no manna the next day, on Sabbath, which was a day He had set aside for them to rest and worship. To get ready, they needed to collect extra on Friday.

But if they kept the manna overnight, wouldn’t it go stinky and wormy? What happened the next morning? **Read Exodus 16:24.**

What a beautiful Sabbath miracle. The Israelites didn’t need to collect or cook their manna. They could spend the whole Sabbath resting and worshipping God. Moses told the happy people, “Look, the Lord has made the Sabbath a day of rest for all of you” (Exodus 16:29, ICB).
