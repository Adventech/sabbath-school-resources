“Who
^[Touched]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#df75a2", "typeface": "Omnes-BoldItalic"}}}) Me?” 
^^^
^^^
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/12/01.png)

Jesus smiled as little hands reached up to Him. He gave their hands a gentle squeeze (squeeze hand). He walked slowly on, through the town by the lake. As He walked, He talked. He told the people about God’s big love. There were people up high (reach up). There were people down low (crouch down). There were people beside Him, row after row. There were so many people in the big, big crowd! Everyone wanted to be with Jesus. Jesus loved being with people. They pressed in close. Squish, squash. Elbows knocked elbows (gently knock elbows). Knees knocked knees (gently knock knees). Jesus smiled and walked as He talked. Then suddenly He stopped. Jesus looked up high (reach up). He looked down low (crouch down). He looked at the people, row after row. He said, “Who touched My clothes?” (Mark 5:30). What a funny question! Lots of people were touching Jesus. But Jesus knew this touch was different. “Who touched Me?”     
^^^
^^^
{"style":{"image":{"storyTextAlign": "top"}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2025-02-bg/12/02.png)
{"style":{"text":{"color": "#ffffff"}, "block":{"backgroundColor": "#181818ad"}}}
A woman crouched down low, near Jesus’ feet. She had been sick for a long, long time. She had seen lots of doctors, but no one could make her better. She knew that Jesus could help! She had squeezed past the people, row after row. Past the people up high (reach up) and down low (crouch down). She was the one who had touched His clothes. She said to herself, “If I can even touch His coat, that will be enough to heal me” (Mark 5:28, ICB). And it was! The woman now touched Jesus’ feet. He looked down at her with kind eyes and a big smile. Jesus knew she was the one! He had known it all along. She had touched His coat. The woman told Jesus that He had made her better right away—just as she knew He would. Oh, how Jesus loved her! Jesus gently pulled the woman to her feet. Then He said, “Daughter, your faith has made you well” (Mark 5:34).

She smiled back at Jesus (big smile). She held His hand tight (squeeze). Jesus called her “daughter!” She felt special and loved. And now she felt well! Jesus had made her all better. She ran home with a happy heart (hooray!). Jesus knew there was a sick woman in the big crowd that day. He made time for her. He made her feel important. Jesus sees and loves you too. He always has time for you.\
(Say together) Thank You, Jesus, for caring for me!
