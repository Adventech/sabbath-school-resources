**Word of the Week**

**All-powerful:** Being powerful means to have power, strength, or force to do something. Someone who is powerful can make challenging things happen. God is not just powerful—He is all-powerful. He is bigger and stronger than anything or anyone in the whole universe! Nothing is impossible for God.

**Lesson Summary**

Jesus’ friend Lazarus was sick. His sisters, Mary and Martha, sent a messenger to tell Jesus the news. Jesus stayed where He was for two days, not rushing to get to Lazarus. When He finally traveled to Bethany, Lazarus had been dead for four days. Mary and Martha were heartbroken, and Jesus shared their sorrow. Jesus encouraged His friends to believe that He has power to give life. Jesus went to the tomb and called Lazarus from the grave. Lazarus, covered in graveclothes, shuffled out of the tomb, showing that Jesus is more powerful than death.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus has power over death.
- **Feel** safe knowing they can trust Jesus even when they don’t understand everything.
- **Respond** by telling others that Jesus will take away death forever when He comes again.
