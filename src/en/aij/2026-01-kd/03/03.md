Sabbath was God’s idea. And what a great idea it was! God made it a gift for everyone—from Adam and Eve, all the way down through time, to you and your family. The Bible tells us that “the Sabbath was made for man” (Mark 2:27).

God has made it a happy time to talk to Him, read His words in the Bible, worship Him at church, and show others His love.

We can talk to God and read His words in the Bible every day. But on Sabbath we come together to thank God with happy hearts. We can praise Him with our songs, offerings, actions, and our words.

God doesn’t want our Sabbath joy just to be hidden inside church or kept to ourselves. Jesus showed us how to help others and share our happiness on Sabbath. We can tell people about Him, and help people who are sad, sick, hurt, or lonely—just as Jesus did.

God knew it would also make us happy to spend time in nature on Sabbath. He has given us tall forests and curious creatures to discover. He has given us parks and gardens and happy, singing birds. What a loving God!

The Sabbath is very important to God. What does the Bible say about some very special Sabbaths God is planning for us? **Read Isaiah 66:22, 23.**

Sabbath truly is a happy day—now and always!
