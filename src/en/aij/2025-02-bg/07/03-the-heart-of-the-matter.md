Jesus’ first miracle was all about joy and compassion for human need. He is, after all, the Giver and Creator of joy. He desires for us to be filled with joy, overflowing with His love and peace. For His first miracle He could have marched into the temple and healed everyone in sight, or freed His people from their Roman oppressors, but instead He chose a humble Cana wedding to show “His sympathy with men, and His desire to minister to their happiness” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 144).

Mary came to Jesus with a problem. This problem was stealing her joy and threatened to disrupt the joy of the wedding celebration. She came full of faith that Jesus could do the impossible. How amazing that Jesus honored Mary’s faith in such a public way! Whatever problems you face, nothing is too small to take to Jesus. He longs to give you, your family, and your friends the joy that only He can give. 

“If our eyes are fixed on Jesus, we shall see a compassionate Redeemer, and shall catch light from His countenance. Wherever His Spirit reigns, there peace abides. And there will be joy also, for there is a calm, holy trust in God” (_The Desire of Ages_, pp. 152, 153).

May God bless you this week as you catch light from His countenance. And may you experience His joy as you take your problems, both large and small, to Him, for “in Your presence is fullness of joy” (Psalm 16:11).
