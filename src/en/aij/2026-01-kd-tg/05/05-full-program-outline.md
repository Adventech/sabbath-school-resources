**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**. Invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal a sign that says: “You Choose.” Point to the words as you read them aloud.

In response to the questions below, invite the children to go to one side of the room if their answer is yes, and the other side if their answer is no.

- **Would you jump off a tall building? Why?**
- **Would you stay inside during a lightning storm? Why?**
- **Would you pray to God each day? Why?**
- **Would you push your little brother over? Why?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Every choice we make has a consequence—something that happens because of the choice we make. When we are tempted to choose something wrong, we can **stop, pray, think, and choose wisely.**

In today’s Bible story (hold up Bible), sad choices lead to very sad consequences. But we will learn that **God is loving. He took my consequences.** Invite the children to stand up and repeat this **Big Idea** after you.

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Adam and Eve Choose”** in an interactive and engaging way using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#4d4d4f", "size": "sm"}}}
**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal:

**Option 1:** The **Choice Scenarios** at the end of this lesson. Have children discuss and respond with their thoughts.

**Option 2:** A set of **dominos** and a **cross**. Stand several dominos in a row on a hard surface.

- **What were some of the sad consequences that happened because of Adam and Eve’s choices?** (Light disappeared, weeds, sickness, pain, sadness, getting old, flowers drooping, leaves falling, etc.) Add a domino after each answer is given.

Push over the first domino and watch them all fall. Explain that this reminds us of how Adam and Eve’s wrong choices led to sad consequences. The same can happen for us.

Show the **cross** and share how it reminds you of the **memory verse**.

- **How do you feel, knowing that Jesus died on the cross to take the consequences for your sins?** (Stand up and show each other with your words and actions.)

**Teaching Point:** Because of sin in our world, we sometimes make bad choices. God knew we would, and that is why Jesus came to take the consequences for our sins. He can help us to make good choices. What a good, loving God!

Repeat the **Big Idea** again together: **God is loving. He took my consequences.**

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up **Nature Card 5: Jackfruit Tree** and share the following facts:

- This beautiful tree is special because it is the largest one in the world that bears fruit.
- The delicious giant tropical fruit on this tree has a strong smell but tastes sweet.
- The tree has a very thick, strong trunk, and the fruit grows close to the ground.
- These amazing trees are found in Southeast Asia.
This amazing tree can remind us of how we will be able to eat fruit from the tree of life with our loving God in heaven
one day! Give each child Nature Card 5 to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Craft activity: Standing Trees. Using cardboard tubes and paper plates create two different scenes to illustrate before and after sin to remind the children of the consequences of Adam and Eve’s choice. Remind the children that when we are tempted to choose something wrong, we can **stop, pray, think, and choose wisely**.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})**

- **What did you learn in Sabbath School today?** (Wait for the children to respond.)

Point to your **“You Choose” sign** again. **Today we’ve learned that choices have consequences.** We’ve learned that:

- God lets us choose because that’s what love does.
- We can choose to go only one way at a time.
- We can choose to say, “Thank You.” We can choose to say, “I want to obey You, Jesus.”

Say the **Big Idea** together: **God is loving. He took my consequences. Ask Jesus to help you to choose to obey Him.**
