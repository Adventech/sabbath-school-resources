---
title: Ministry to People With Disabilities
---

This caring ministry “functions under the personal ministries council and develops programs for members and others with disabilities.” <sup>73</sup> The coordinator may utilize the services of organizations that provide assistance for the disabled, such as Christian Record. <sup>74</sup>
