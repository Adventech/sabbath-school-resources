**Word of the Week**

**Image:** a likeness, picture, reflection, or representation of something. You are made in God’s image, to be like Him.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores the sixth day of Creation, when God made animals, then Adam and Eve. God created the world and everything in it to be a special gift of love for His final, very special act of Creation: a man and a woman made in His image. Surrounded by beauty, they were to be loving caretakers of everything in God’s new world.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that we are God’s very special creation, made in His image.
- **Feel** glad that God created so many beautiful and intricately designed things because He loves us.
- **Respond** by thanking God for everything He created.
