Hot winds whirled across the dry land. The plants shriveled and the streams dried up. The land of Canaan was having a bad time of no rain. Abram’s traveling household and animals became thirsty and hungry.

It would have been easy to grumble, but Abram didn’t complain. He believed God’s promise, “I will make you a great nation; I will bless you . . . ; and you shall be a blessing” (Genesis 12:2).

Abram prayed more. God helped him to be brave in the middle of trouble. His servants noticed, and they felt braver too.

Together the family packed up and started walking again. Abram led them to a place they might find food and water. Where was it? **Read Genesis 12:10.**

Abram felt worried they wouldn’t be safe in this new land. Instead of trusting God to protect them, Abram came up with a bad plan. He told the people and the king of Egypt a lie about his family. Lying is never the right choice. It goes against what God asked us to do in His law of love.

Abram’s lie brought trouble for everyone. The king was angry! The people were angry!

But God didn’t forget about Abram or stop being his friend. God forgave Abram and helped him leave Egypt safely.

In good times and bad, Abram was learning to trust God.

^[Parent Faith Talk]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21a0a9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Do a “faith fall” challenge with your child. Have your child stand and fall back into your arms. Remind your child that when things are scary, God is always there.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})

- What should Abram have done when he felt worried? What can you do?
- Why did God still help Abram after he made a big mistake?
- Can you think of a time God has forgiven you for something big?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994cc", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank God that when we make mistakes, He is ready to forgive and help us.
