**Big Idea:**\
God is with me. He gives me peace.

**^[Memory Verse]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "size": "lg"}}})**\
_^[“Peace I leave with you, My peace I give to you”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}}) (John 14:27)._

**Word of the Week**\
_^[“Peace”]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#00929c"}}})_ - Peace is quiet and calm. God’s peace in our hearts gives us a safe and content feeling deep inside. Our peaceful words are soft, gentle, and forgiving. Peace means no fighting or war.

Going Home!

With a wave of his stick, Jacob chased the spotted goats up the rocky path. His sheep, camels, donkeys, servants, and family all followed behind. What a great big line of people and animals on their way to Canaan. Together, they were going home!

Back in Haran, Uncle Laban didn’t know that Jacob had left. How long did it take Laban to find out they were gone? **Read Genesis 31:22.**

Laban was very angry! He jumped on his camel and chased after them. But God spoke to Laban and said, “Be careful! Do not say anything to Jacob, good or bad” (Genesis 31:24, ICB). God was asking Uncle Laban to have peace. He needed to be quiet and listen.

Finally Laban caught up with his big family. With God’s words of peace in his mind, he went to talk with Jacob. “Why did you run away without telling me?” he asked (Genesis 31:27, ICB).

“I left without telling you, because I was afraid!” Jacob answered. “I thought you would take your daughters away from me” (Genesis 31:31, ICB). After they talked together, they agreed to make peace—no more tricks, lying, or fighting. To show that they really meant it, they made a pillar out of a big pile of rocks.

They called it Mizpah, meaning “A Pile to Remind Us.” Then Laban headed back to Haran and Jacob traveled on. As Jacob came closer to Canaan, he began to think about his angry brother Esau. Had God given him peace and forgiveness too?

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Do you think it was hard or easy for Jacob and Uncle Laban to chat peacefully? Why?
- How could you learn from them?
