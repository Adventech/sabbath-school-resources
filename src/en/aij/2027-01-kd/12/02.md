Kids raced each other back to their tents. Mothers and fathers came quickly behind them. Soon blankets were unrolled, bottles emptied, and baskets overturned. All throughout the camp people were looking . . . looking . . . looking. Everyone wanted to find a gift for God.

God had said that He would love to receive gifts from them. He would use these gifts to make His holy home.

Would they like to give to God and His home? Yes, they truly would! The Egyptians had given them many treasures when they had left Egypt. Now they could give them all to God, who had set them free!

God gave Moses a list of things He needed for His special home. He would like gold, silver, and bronze. Sparkling stones and precious jewels too. Beautiful cloths, wood, and animal skins would be needed. Finally, olive oil and spices.

People sang out and cheered as they found a perfect gift for God. _Yes, a sparkly ring! Hooray, a shiny silver bowl! Here’s a beautiful tablecloth!_

“Everyone who wanted to give came and brought a gift to the Lord” (Exodus 35:21, ICB). Each morning the piles of gifts grew bigger and bigger, higher and higher. Soon there was a problem. What was it? **Read Exodus 36:5.**

Moses told the Israelites to stop bringing their gifts. They had more than enough! Imagine how happy it must have made God to see such generous and loving hearts.

- Why did the Israelites keep giving more and more?
- How do you show your love to God?
