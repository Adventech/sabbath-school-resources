---
title: Blessings to Praise God For
---

> <callout></callout>
> Pray and praise your way through God’s Word! Claim each promise and trust Him to do what He says He will do.

### For Forgiveness

1 John 1:9 If we confess our sins, He will forgive and cleanse.

Matt. 11:28-30 Bring Jesus your burdens.

John 6:37 Whoever comes to Christ will not be cast out.

1 Tim. 1:15 Christ came to save sinners.

Luke 19:10 Christ came to seek and to save the lost.

Rom. 8:1 No condemnation for those who are in Christ.

Isa. 1:18 Sins are made white as snow.

Isa. 38:17 He casts our sins behind His back.

Isa. 44:22 God sweeps away your transgressions.

### For Christlikeness

2 Cor. 5:17 If anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation and the old is gone.

Ezek. 36:26, 27 God promises to give you a new heart and new spirit.

2 Cor. 6:16 God will dwell in you.

Eph. 3:16, 17 Christ will dwell in your heart through the Holy Spirit.

2 Pet. 1:4 Through Christ’s promises you can partake of the divine nature.

2 Cor. 10:4, 5 Every thought can be made captive to Christ.

2 Cor. 3:18 By beholding the Lord we are changed into His likeness.

Phil. 1:6 God will complete the work He began in you.

### For Everyday Life

Phil. 4:19 God will supply your every need.

Matt. 6:33 Seek God’s kingdom first, and He will care for your needs.

2 Cor. 9:6 He who sows bountifully will reap bountifully.

1 Cor. 10:13 God will not allow you to be tempted beyond your strength.

Rom. 8:32 We have “all things” in Christ.

Jude 24 He can keep you from falling.

2 Cor. 12:9 God’s grace is sufficient for you.

Phil. 4:7 The peace of God will keep your hearts and minds.

Isa. 26:3 God keeps in perfect peace those whose minds are stayed on Him.

### For Wisdom And Strength

James 1:5, 6 Wisdom will be given when you ask in faith.

Prov. 3:5, 6 Rely on God for guidance.

Ps. 32:8 God will teach you the way to go.

Ex. 33:14 God’s presence will go with you and give you rest.

Isa. 58:11 He will guide you continually.

Phil. 4:13 I can do all things in Christ.

Jer. 1:7 He will give you words to speak.

Prov. 14:26 In the fear of the Lord you can have strong confidence.

Isa. 41:10 God will strengthen, God will help, God will uphold.

Isa. 41:13 The Lord your God will hold your right hand.

Heb. 4:15, 16 Come with confidence to the throne of grace.

James 4:7, 8 Draw near to God and He will draw near to you.

James 5:14, 15 The prayer of faith will save the sick and sins will be forgiven.

James 5:16-18 Prayer has great power.

1 Pet. 5:7 Cast all your anxieties on Him.

1 John 5:14, 15 You can know He hears when you ask anything according to His will.

### For The Holy Spirit

Luke 11:13 We can receive the Holy Spirit.

John 14:26 The Holy Spirit teaches us and brings things to our remembrance.

John 16:8 He convicts of sin.

John 15:26 He testifies of Christ.

John 16:7 He comes as the Counselor.

Eph. 3:16 He strengthens you.

Rom. 8:26 Helps in our weakness and intercedes for us.

### For Others

Ezek. 11:19, 20 God promises to give people a new heart that they may keep His laws and be His people.

1 John 5:16 God promises to give spiritual life to those for whom you pray.

John 12:32 Christ uplifted on the cross draws people to Him.

Acts 5:31 Repentance and forgiveness are gifts from God.

John 16:8 The Holy Spirit convicts of sin.

Heb. 4:12 God’s Word is living and powerful and reaches the heart.

Jer. 32:40 I will put my fear in their hearts so they will not depart from me.

Jer. 33:3 If we call, God will answer with great things.

Isa. 49:24, 25 God promises to deliver those who are captives of Satan.

### All-inclusive

Ps. 34:10 If you seek the Lord, you will not lack any good thing.

Ps. 37:3-5 Delight in the Lord, commit your way to Him, and He will act.

Ps. 84:11 God will withhold no good thing from you if you walk uprightly.

Eph. 3:20 He can do more than we ask.

Luke 1:37 With God, nothing is impossible.

Mark 11:24 Believe and you will receive.

Rom. 8:28 In everything God works for the good of those who love Him.

John 16:24 Ask in His name and receive.

John 15:7 If you abide in Christ, ask what you will, and it shall be done.

John 15:16 Anything you ask in Christ’s name the Father will give to you.

Luke 11:9 Ask and it will be given.

Matt. 28:20 I am with you always.

> <callout></callout>
> “All His biddings are enablings” (Christ’s Object Lessons, p. 333). God never asks us to do anything without giving us the power to do it. Therefore, every command in the Bible is also a promise!
