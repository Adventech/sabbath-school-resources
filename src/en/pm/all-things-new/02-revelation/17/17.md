- Chapter 17 begins by going back in time and giving a more detailed view of the character and actions of end-time Babylon (“the great harlot” in verse 1).

[[Revelation 17:3-7]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- The woman Babylon is “drunk with the blood of the saints.” This clearly shows her history of persecuting the woman of Revelation 12, who symbolizes the true followers of Jesus, millions of whom were martyred during the 1260 years of persecution.
- Her riding on the beast with seven heads and 10 horns shows continuity with the sea beast of Revelation 13:1. This is an end-time manifestation of the same power, but on a global scale.
- The woman is called “the Mother of Harlots,” indicating that she gave birth to other false churches. In contrast, the woman of Revelation 12 gave birth to true followers of Christ, who show their faithfulness to Him by keeping God’s commandments (Revelation 12:17).
- John “marveled with great amazement,” but elsewhere in the book of Revelation only the deceived world marvels at the beast (Rev. 13:3). That’s why the angel asks John with surprise, “Why did you marvel?”

[[Revelation 17:8]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- Revelation 17 here introduces us to the most complex prophetic symbol found in either Daniel or Revelation.
- The woman and the beast the woman rides on are inseparable. Both represent the Papacy, the woman symbolizes its religious power, and the beast symbolizes its political power. For more insight on this interpretation, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r17a/)**.

[[Revelation 17:9-11]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- There are various interpretations of verses 9-11.
- The eighth king is most likely a revived Papacy that, though still future, will help lead the forces of Babylon in its final battle against God.
- To learn more about this interpretation, please  **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r17b/)**.
- Revelation 17 shows that the Papacy unites with secular powers of the earth to achieve its goals. The union of church and state historically has always led to oppression and bloodshed, and Revelation here predicts such disasters on a global scale.

[[Revelation 17:12-18]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "base", "start": "base", "end":"base", "bottom": "base"}, "rounded": true}}}
- We do not yet know the identities of these 10 kingdoms, because its fulfillment is still future.
- Verses 14-17 provide new information about the battle of Armageddon: The allies of end-time papal Rome will ultimately turn on her and participate directly in her destruction (verse 16).
