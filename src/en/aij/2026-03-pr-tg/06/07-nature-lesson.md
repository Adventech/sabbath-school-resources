**Focus:** God’s love can change our hearts.

![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/nature-lesson-a.png)

Mzee the tortoise walked slowly to the shade. There he sat down and pulled his legs and head inside his shell. Now the Aldabra tortoise didn’t look like a tortoise anymore, but rather a big rock!

Aldabra tortoises are one of the largest tortoises in the world. They also live a long, long time—to about 150 years of age. They are dark gray with a high, curved shell, a very long neck, and short, thick legs.

Mzee the tortoise had a very hard shell on the outside. But he also had a hard heart on the inside. He was 130 years old, and he was grumpy and liked to be on his own. But one day everything changed for Mzee. He met a bouncy, lovable baby hippo named Owen.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/06/nature-lesson-b.png)

Owen had lost his family in the terrible tsunami of 2004. An earthquake had caused huge waves to crash over many countries, hurting animals and people. The little hippo had been rescued and taken to Haller Nature Park, where Mzee lived.

The baby hippo was too small to be put with other hippos, so the keepers put him in Mzee’s part of the park. Little Owen played and ran and hid behind Mzee. And then something wonderful happened. Mzee’s lonely, hard heart started to soften. He accepted the playful little hippo, and they soon became best friends.

Owen, the young hippo, followed Mzee, the old tortoise, everywhere. They would eat, swim, and even sleep together. They spent so much time together that they also figured out a way to communicate with each other, nibbling and nudging each other.

How special that God could soften a hard-hearted tortoise. His love can change us too.

- **What would it look like at your school (or in your neighborhood) if everyone allowed God to soften their hearts?**
