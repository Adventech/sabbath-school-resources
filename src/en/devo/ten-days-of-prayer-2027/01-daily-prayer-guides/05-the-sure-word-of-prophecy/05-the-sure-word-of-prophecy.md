#### A Word More Sure Than Sight

Two disciples walking along the road to Emmaus were discouraged as they thought of the events of the crucifixion. As Jesus walked beside them, He learned of their discouragement, but instead of announcing His resurrection to them, “beginning at Moses and all the Prophets, He expounded to them in all the Scriptures the things concerning Himself” (Luke 24:27). The apostle Peter, describing Jesus’ Transfiguration, says they “were eyewitnesses of His majesty,” when “He received from God the Father honor and glory” and “a voice came to Him from the Excellent Glory” (2 Pet. 1:17). But even though he was an “eyewitness,” he indicates that “we have a more sure word of prophecy”—more sure than seeing Jesus in the flesh, and more sure than being eyewitnesses of His majesty.

We have the unique privilege of looking back across the sweep of history and seeing the fulfillment of prophecy. We saw how the prophecy of Daniel 9 predicted the precise time when Jesus would be baptized, be anointed by the Holy Spirit, and begin His messianic ministry—in AD 27. Daniel 2 covers an even broader scope, describing four world empires that have come and gone (Babylon, Medo-Persia, Greece, and Rome). It described the fourth empire, the iron kingdom of Rome, as being divided into the several nations of Europe. Despite many and varied efforts through the centuries to unite these nations, they remain divided because God said, “they will not hold together” (Dan. 2:43, ESV).

#### Light for a Dark World

God has revealed the future to us not merely to satisfy our curiosity about what is coming but to assure us that His word is true, that it can be trusted, and that He is in control: “Remember the former things of old [history], for I am God, and there is no other; I am God, and there is none like Me, declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all My pleasure” (Isa. 46:9, 10).

God not only knows what will happen; He knows how best to guide us. So, if events in the world trouble us, if things in your life seem out of control, remember that we can trust the One who has our future in His hands. The light of Bible prophecy shines into our dark world and into our hearts, enabling us to go forward in faith. Let’s pray together.

#### Praying God’s Word — 2 Peter 1:19, KJV

“We have also a more sure word of prophecy; whereunto ye do well that ye take heed, as unto a light that shineth in a dark place, until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts.”

**“A more sure word of prophecy”**

_Lord, thank You for the sure word of prophecy—surer even than what our eyes can see. Make us so confident in Your Word that we cannot keep silent._

**“As unto a light that shineth in a dark place”**

_Father, in a dark and confusing world, let Your prophetic Word be a light—not only to our own path but to the people around us. Through OneVoice27 this year, make us light-bearers who carry Your truth into homes and communities still living in darkness._

**“Until the day dawn, and the day star arise in your hearts”**

_Lord Jesus, Bright and Morning Star, arise in our hearts—and through us in the hearts of many. Fill us with the hope of Your soon return and send us out with one voice to proclaim it. Come quickly, Lord; until then, let our lives shine until the day dawns._
