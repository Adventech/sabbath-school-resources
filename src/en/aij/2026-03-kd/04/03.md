One night, as Mary, Joseph, and little Jesus fell fast asleep under their warm blankets, something happened. Joseph had a dream. In the dream an angel talked to him. The angel said, “Get up! Take the child and His mother and escape to Egypt. Herod will start looking for the child to kill Him” (Matthew 2:13, ICB).

Joseph sat up in bed. He quietly looked around the dark room where Mary and little Jesus lay sleeping. This dream was no ordinary dream. No, this was a message from God. God was trying to protect them. He wanted to keep them safe from wicked King Herod.

Joseph woke Mary up. With urgent whispers he explained the dream. As quiet as mice, they collected their things. Then they picked up sleepy Jesus and hurried down the road. They must get far away from evil King Herod.

How would they know when it was safe to return home? **Read the last part of Matthew 2:13.**

God would look after them! They didn’t need to worry, because God and His angels would protect them as they traveled. He would keep them safe in a new country. Even in the middle of the night, God was with them, because God “never rests or sleeps” (Psalm 121:4, ICB).
