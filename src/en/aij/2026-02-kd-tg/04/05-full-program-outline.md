**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Ring your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box**, revealing **masking tape**, a **spoon**, and a **ball**. Mark a line across the floor with the masking tape and invite the children to walk along the line. Balance a ball on a spoon and ask the children to walk along the line a second time, while holding the spoon, without allowing the ball to fall.

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})**

- **How easy was it to walk along the line the first time?**
- **What was it like when you had to walk along it while balancing the ball on the spoon?** (Harder! We had to concentrate on both walking straight and balancing the ball.)
- **List one chore you do at home.** (Prompt easy tasks: make bed, get dressed, brush teeth.) **What does this chore look like when you do your best? What does it look like when you don’t do your best?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Doing our best means doing something as well as we possibly can! It makes God and others happy when we choose to do things well. Today’s Bible story is about Jacob, who did his best even when it was hard!

Invite the children to stand and repeat the **Big Idea** after you: **God is with me. He helps me do my best.**

**TELL THE STORY** (10 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then share the story **“Jacob in Haran”** in an interactive and engaging way, using the **storytelling suggestions** and **Program PowerPoint**.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (6 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the Story Explorer Box, revealing some colored sticker dots.

- **What do these spots remind you of in our Bible story today?** (The spotted sheep and goats.)
- **Would it have been hard for Jacob to do his best for Uncle Laban? Why?** (Yes; Laban kept tricking him and cheating him.) **But what did God give Jacob?** (Lots of spotty sheep and goats.)

Give each child a set of colored **sticker dots**. After counting to three, ask them to place the spots on each other as fast as they can in just 60 seconds! Then say “Stop!” and ask them to sit down.

**Teaching Point:** God gave Jacob lots of spotty sheep and goats in a short amount of time! God blessed Jacob and was with Jacob. God helped Jacob do his best, even when his uncle tricked him.

**^[SAY and PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Explain that sometimes things seem unfair or hard. People might be mean or rude to us, or they might even cheat us and take what is not theirs. When this happens, we can talk to God about it. God always hears your prayers, and He loves to listen and help you. He is always with you, and He will help you do your best.

Pause for a short moment and invite the children to close their eyes. Invite them to say a short silent prayer now, just between them and God.

**NATURE/MISSION** (7 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold Up **Nature Card 17: Spotty Sheep** and share the following facts:

- Sheep are very curious. They are fun-loving, playful creatures.
- They love to eat seeds, grass, and plants.
- Sheep are covered in a type of hair called wool. People use wool to make clothes and blankets. Wool is warm. (Do you have any clothing made from wool?)
- Newborn sheep can walk within minutes of being born!
- A group of sheep is called a flock, herd, or mob.

Give each child **Nature Card 17: Spotty Sheep** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}}) Share the mission story**: “Airplanes for God.” After you’ve shared the story, give each child a small paper plane. Invite them to throw their colorful paper planes once while repeating the **Big Idea** together: **God is with me. He helps me do my best.** Ask:

- **Is there someone you could share this message with and give your paper plane to this week?**
