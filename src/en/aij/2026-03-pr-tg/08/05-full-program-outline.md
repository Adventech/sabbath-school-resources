**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them. (Don’t yet discuss the footprints they see.)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Set up a fishing game. Tell the children that you’re interested to see if they can **remember some facts about the people they have been learning about**. Write the names of characters or places from the past few lessons on small pieces of paper or fish shapes (e.g., King Herod, John the Baptist, Simeon, Anna, Wise Men, angel, Peter, Philip). Children take turns to fish. When they “catch” a name, they can **tell what they remember** about that person. When you come to the disciples’ names, ask what is known about them and put those names aside.

Then ask: **I wonder what these names have to do with fishing, and what fishing has to do with today’s lesson?**

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Send the children on a **Disciple Hunt** to search the room and find all the pieces of paper that you hid before Sabbath School began. They are to find the names of 12 disciples. Some pieces of paper will be blank to make it harder. Ask:

- **Who are these people?** (Jesus’ disciples)

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}}) “What is a disciple?”**

**Teaching Point:** A disciple is a follower of a leader. In Jesus’ day, many people wanted to be a disciple. They followed a wise, learned person called a rabbi. Only the best students who did very well at school could be a disciple. Everyone else left school and did other jobs. Disciples didn’t just want to know what their leader knew; they wanted to be just like their leader.

- **What sort of people did Jesus chose to be His disciples?** (Jesus chose ordinary men, fishermen, a tax collector.)

**Teaching Point:** Jesus looked at their hearts. He didn’t just choose the smartest or most clever people to be His disciples. He chose people who loved God and had a heart that was open to following Him.

- **I wonder: Were there disciples only in Bible times, or can Jesus have disciples now too?**

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Point to the **Big Idea** and read it together: **Jesus loves me so much He calls me to follow Him.**

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Collector Card 34: Jesus and His Disciples** to place in their storage container. Darken your space and shine a light on the card. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the memory verse.

**Memory Verse Challenge:** If time allows, engage the children in a memory verse challenge. Write each word of the memory verse on a separate piece of paper and then hide the papers under a heavy blanket. Have the children form a line and give the child at the front of the line a small flashlight. The group’s challenge is to take turns running to the blanket, where they will take turns going under the blanket with their flashlight and looking for the next word of the memory verse. They are to take this next word to the other side of the room to add to the correct order of the memory verse. For large classes, you can make two teams. Can they do it in the time it takes to play the song “All the Time”?

**^[NATURE LESSON:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Read the Nature Lesson, **“Making Waves.”**

**Teaching Point:** Being a disciple is all about making a difference for Jesus.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your **Fish Miracle craft** sample. Explain and demonstrate the instructions for how to make the craft. Sing **“I Will Make You Fishers of Men”** together, then play the quarterly **Scripture Song playlist** as the children make the craft.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Jesus first said **“Follow Me”** to His disciples (Matthew 4:19). Later, after they had learned from Him, He said, **“Go . . . and make disciples”** (Matthew 28:19). If you would like to “come” to Him this week and spend time with Him, and then “go” and connect with people around you, come forward and pick up the footprint with your name on it to show you want to follow Him in this way.

Allow time for this. Then challenge the children to share this week’s Bible story or Big Idea with someone at school or in their neighborhood who they don’t usually play with and invite them to play a game or enjoy an activity.

**^[REVIEW and COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus loves me so much He calls me to follow Him.** Display the following words as a call and response:

**Teacher:** Who loves you most of all?\
**Children:** Jesus loves me, and He calls!

**Teacher:** What does Jesus ask you to do?\
**Children:** Follow Him!

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing the chorus of **“All the Time,”** then conclude with a prayer, thanking God for **loving each one of us so much** that He calls us to “come” and follow Him, so we can then “go” to help others want to follow Jesus too.
