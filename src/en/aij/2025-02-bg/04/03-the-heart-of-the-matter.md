How blessed the man with paralysis was, to have four good friends. These friends were so invested in his life, health, and happiness that they personally carried him to Jesus. They didn’t just stop at the first roadblock. No! They thought outside the box and created a solution that would allow their friend to come close to the Savior. Then they watched with joy as Jesus healed him—wholly and fully. His life was changed forever. They too would never be the same. 

You have an opportunity to be one of these friends. Whom will you carry to Jesus? How will you go out of your way to help someone discover His love, forgiveness, and healing? What obstacles are you willing to overcome in order to get them there? 

The four friends didn’t judge their friend with paralysis. They didn’t allow polite society to keep him at the edge of the crowd. They found a way to bring him right to Jesus. “Now, in words that fell like music on the sufferer’s ear, the Savior said, ‘Son, be of good cheer; thy sins be forgiven thee.’ The burden of despair rolls from the sick man’s soul; the peace of forgiveness rests upon his spirit, and shines out upon his countenance. His physical pain is gone, and his whole being is transformed” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 268).

Imagine the joy, amazement, and wonder! 

As you go throughout this week, intentionally invest in friendship. Ask God to help you to find time and create opportunities for meaningful connections. That’s not always easy in this phase of life. We need His help! Saturate your friendship in prayer. You may even consider boldly having a short prayer to begin a conversation. Your children will watch how you do friendship and model your ways. Make Jesus your best Friend and ask Him to help you as you grow friendships for Him. 

“This is My commandment, that you love one another as I have loved you” (John 15:12).
