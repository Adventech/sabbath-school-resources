What a delightful Sabbath! The people of Capernaum were filled with joy. They had just heard Jesus speak wonderful and wise words in the synagogue.

It was true that “God anointed Jesus of Nazareth with the Holy Spirit and with power, who went about doing good” (Acts 10:38).

But now the morning service was finished. It was time to say goodbye. Young children skipped down the streets toward their homes. Their parents followed close behind.

Simon Peter’s family lived in Capernaum. So he invited Jesus over to his house for the afternoon.

After a short walk, Simon Peter and Jesus stepped inside the house. But something was terribly wrong. As they looked around, there were no happy hellos and no cheery smiles. Instead, Simon’s family stood around frowning and whispering words of worry to each other. What was wrong? **Read the first part of Luke 4:38.**

During a fever your body feels hot because it is trying to fight a sickness. The family had tried to cool down Simon Peter’s mother-in-law’s temperature with wet, cool cloths. They had given her water. But nothing seemed to help. The poor woman lay on her bed, very unwell.

Jesus looked at Simon Peter’s very sick mother-in-law. He cared about her, and He cared for her worried family too. He really wanted to help.

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever had a fever? What did it feel like?
- How do you know that Jesus cares about you?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus that He cares about you when you are sick.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Can you think of anyone who is sick at the moment? Plan to do something to show that you care for them, just as Jesus does. You might pick them flowers or send them a cheerful drawing and note.
