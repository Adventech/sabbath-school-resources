**Focus:** God had a great plan to save Romena and her family.

**Location:** Invite a child to help you find **Timor-Leste** on a **map** or **globe**.

Romena and her husband, Alfonso, had four children. They had never read the Bible before and didn’t know Jesus. Yet God used a difficult situation as part of His great plan to save them.

When Romena’s son was born, he had a very bad problem with his eyes. They spent lots of money hoping the local magicians could heal him, but nothing worked. Finally they decided to take him to the hospital. The doctor told them that their son would need to have an operation. Romena was afraid for her little boy. What if it made things worse? What if her son didn’t survive?

While they waited for the surgery to be done, Romena met an Adventist woman who was sick in the hospital. The woman said kind things to Romena and offered to pray with her. Romena suddenly felt hopeful that everything would be OK.

After the surgery was done, Romena stayed with her son at the hospital while he got better. Every day, the Adventist woman was able to share Jesus with Romena and encourage her to read the Bible. After a few days her son was better, and they were able to go home. But Romena wanted to keep learning about Jesus, so she asked two missionaries to study the Bible with her every day.

One day Romena’s son became very sick again. At the hospital Romena prayed, “Jesus, if You are really my God and my Creator, please heal my son!” To her amazement, her son was healed within a few days. Romena’s faith in God was stronger than ever!

Romena now sees how God led them to the hospital to meet the Adventist woman so she could learn about Jesus and be saved. God used a bad thing, such as sickness, to work for good!

- **How can God use us as part of His great plan to help others come to know Jesus as their Best Friend?** (He can give us opportunities to share Jesus with others.)

**Teaching Point:** Like Romena, we can trust that God will use everything for good.
