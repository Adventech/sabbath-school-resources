**What you need:**

Week 5 craft template on yellow paper (page 1) and white paper (page 2), blue paper, scissors, glue, ruler, pencil.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5a.jpg)

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut two large rectangles out of blue paper, measuring 2.6 x 5.7 inches (6.7 x 14.5 centimeters).
- Cut two small rectangles out of blue paper, measuring 1 x 2.6 inches (2.5 x 6.7 centimeters).

**[Download Craft Template](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/2027-01-pr-craft-05.pdf)**

**Create it together:**

1\. Cut out the yellow frame, including the inside rectangle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5b.jpg)

2\. Glue the corners of the frame onto the backing.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5c.jpg)

3\. Cut slits—about a finger width apart—on one side of each large blue rectangle.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5d.jpg)

4\. Wind each slit around a pencil to curl them up like waves. Slide the flat end of each rectangle into the short sides of the frame. Then glue the small blue rectangles over the short sides of the yellow frame.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5e.jpg)

5\. Cut out the picture on page 2 of the craft resource. Slide it into the long side of the frame.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5f.jpg)

6\. Slide the picture through the frame and the waves out and in as you retell the story!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2027-01-pr-tg/assets/5g.jpg)
