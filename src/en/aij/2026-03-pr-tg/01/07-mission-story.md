**Focus:** God used the gift of a little baby to reach a family.

**Location:** Help a child locate **Georgia** on your **globe** or **world map**.

When Lana was 2 years old, Father and Mother asked her what she wanted for New Year’s. In many countries parents ask children what they want for Christmas. But in Georgia, where Lana lived, children open wrapped gifts on New Year’s Day.

“What do you want for New Year’s?” Mother asked Lana.

“I want a little brother,” Lana said.

Mother was surprised. Lana was the only child in the family. Mother wouldn’t mind giving her a little brother. But she didn’t think that she was able to have any more children.

Lana didn’t get a little brother that New Year’s.

When Lana was 3 years old, Mother asked her again what she wanted for New Year’s.

“I want a little brother,” Lana said.

She asked again when she was 4, but still Lana didn’t get a little brother.

When Lana was 9, Mother became pregnant with a little boy. Mother couldn’t believe it—it seemed impossible! She hadn’t thought that she would be able to have any more children. Immediately she thought that the baby must be a gift from God. She hadn’t thought about God in a long time. But God had been thinking of her! He had a very special plan for this baby.

Lana was so excited! “Can we name him Alex?” she asked. Mother agreed.

Mother brought little Alex home from the hospital on Lana’s tenth birthday. This was even better than a New Year’s gift. She had a little brother!

Seeing the new baby, a neighbor told Mother about a Seventh-day Adventist children’s center where the boy could play and learn about God. Mother took him along. She and other mothers studied the Bible while their children played. Slowly Mother fell in love with Jesus and started taking her family to church on Sabbath.

Mother is so happy that Alex was born. Not only did the family gain a son, but it also gained a Father in heaven. Because of Alex, the whole family now knows and loves God.

“Nothing is impossible with God.”
