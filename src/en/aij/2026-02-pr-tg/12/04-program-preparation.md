Opening Activities

Prepare to play animal charades by writing the names of different animals on **small pieces of paper**. Include these animals (that are committed to each other): beaver, swan, penguin, monkey, seahorse, lion, ant, elephant, bee, wolf, dolphin.

Source a **first-aid kit** with a variety of items (**bandage, thermometer, sling, disinfecting solution**, etc.)

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard** or use the **Program PowerPoint**.
