**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and show **Secret Surprise Box**, but don’t open it yet. **Ask the children to jump to their feed and sit down three times** to help turn on their “memory minds.” When they are quiet and still, have a child open the box to reveal a **treasured item** given to you by family in your childhood, such as a teddy bear (or picture of it), **blank note cards** (palm cards), and a **marker**. Share about the treasured item and how it reminds you of who gave it to you and the special times you shared together.

Give each child a card and ask them to draw (very simply) a few responses on their card as you ask each question.

- **What is a special toy or present your family has given you?**
- **Where is a special place your family took you?**
- **What is a special food, perhaps a treat, your family has made you?**

Turn the cards over and shuffle them around. Turn over a card. Ask the child whose memory it is to not say a word, but for all the other children to try to guess whose it is. Repeat a few times.

- **How does it make you feel about your family to remember these fun times and special things?**

**Teaching Point:** It’s great to remember the good things our families have given us and done for us. It reminds us how much they love us. It’s similar with God. He gives us so many good things and helps us in amazing ways. When we remember, we’ll love and trust Him more!

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO and DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker** and invite someone to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal sticky notes and the five story cards (just used):

- **What can you remember from our story?**

Show the cards one by one, asking the following questions for each story.

- What did God show the people He was like in this story? Write what they say (e.g., kind) on a sticky note and invite children to stick them on a board (or similar) near you.
- **How does it make you feel to know that God is ______** (read the note/s)**?** Ask them to tell you with their words.

Repeat for all five stories.

**Teaching Point:** There are so many stories that show that God is so very good to His people. The more we think about God’s goodness, the more we want to smile. Thank You, God!

Repeat the **Big Idea** together: **God’s way is best. I will remember all His good works.** Ask the children to start thinking about things **God has given them or done for them** that show His “marvelous” goodness.

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Hold up a **Nature Card 65: Clark’s Nutcracker** and share the following facts:

- These birds have very sharp beaks that they use to crack open pinecones.
- They collect large seeds from inside the pinecones and carry them in a pouch under their tongue.
- Clark’s nutcrackers bury thousands and thousands of seeds to store for winter.
- They have an amazing memory and can remember where most of their seeds are buried!
- The seeds that are not eaten grow into new pine trees. Many pine forests exist today because of the Clark’s nutcracker.

These amazing birds use their memory to find food and stay alive. We can also use our memory to remember God’s goodness and stay alive in Jesus!

Give each child **Nature Card 65: Clark’s Nutcracker** to place in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})** Share the **Mission Story**: “Saying Thank You.”

**CRAFT** (15 MINUTES)

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Memory Box.** Ask those who finish first to draw a thing God has done for them or given them that shows His goodness. Share it with a friend, then place it inside their **Memory Box**.

**^[REVIEW:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Today we learned that it’s important to remember God’s goodness. Invite children who wrote or drew an **idea in their Memory Box** to share their ideas with everyone.

- **How else can we remember the good things God does for us this week?** (We can tell each other Bible stories, sing songs, look for things in nature to hold and keep, draw a picture, or ask an adult to help us write a note to put in our memory box. We can also talk to others about the good things God has done for us and share what He is like.)

**^[APPLY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}})** Invite the children to copy your actions if they can remember something wonderful God has done. (Adapt to suit the special needs of your group.)

- If you can remember something good God has done for your **family**. (Action: hug self.)
- If you can remember something good God has given your **body** so it can move and grow. (Action: crouch, then jump up high.)
- If you can remember the good **friends** God has given you. (Action: two-handed clap with a friend.)
- If you can remember a time God **answered your prayer**. (Action: prayer hands.)

**Teaching Point:** God is always working for our good. We can remember all the good things He has done so we can trust Him with whatever is to come.

Finish with a **callout: “God is good; God is great; thank You, God!”** (Clap.)
