**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Glorious songs of praise echoed through the courts of heaven, giving voice to the indescribable love that filled the angels’ hearts. Love for their Creator came as naturally to the angels as breathing, for they had only experienced harmony, peace, unity, and serenity in their heavenly home.

Then, in an inconceivable twist, Lucifer entertained the notion of self-importance. Pride crept in. Jealousy stalked close on its heels, followed by gossip, and veiled slander. A whisper here, a subtle untruth there. An angry glance, a puzzled frown, a surprised stare. As the seeds of discontent and confusion matured, full-blown rebellion was unleashed. God looked on in everlasting love as heaven’s harmony unraveled.

God or Lucifer? Every angel was free to choose. While Lucifer tried to coerce others, “a compassionate Creator, in yearning pity for Lucifer and His followers, was seeking to draw them back from the abyss of ruin into which they were about to plunge.”<sup>1</sup>

We know what happened, and we know the sad result. We experience it every day.\
Choices . . .\
God or Lucifer?\
God’s way or my way?\
God’s peace or my pleasure?\
In love, God will always let us choose.

As you explore this sad chapter in heaven’s history, may you experience the love of God anew. God, who IS love, doesn’t force or coerce, but rather seeks to draw with compassion and “yearning pity.” Love never gives up, never dies, never fails. God’s love drew two thirds of the angels to His side—may it draw the children too, today, and always.

---

<sup>1</sup> Ellen G. White, _Patriarchs and Prophets_ (Mountain View, Calif.: Pacific Press Publishing Association, 1890, 1908), p. 39.
