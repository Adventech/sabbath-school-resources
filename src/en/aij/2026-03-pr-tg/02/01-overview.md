**Lesson Summary**

This lesson explores God’s amazing love for us, a love so deep that He was willing to send heaven’s best—His Son—as a gift for each one of us. A census was announced, and a heavily pregnant Mary and Joseph had to travel to Bethlehem to be counted. There was no room for them to lodge, so they found a humble stable. That night God’s Son, Jesus, was born in the stable and laid in a manger. Hallelujah! The greatest gift had arrived!

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus is God’s most precious gift, given to us.
- **Feel** grateful that God gave Jesus to us.
- **Respond** by accepting God’s gift of Jesus.
