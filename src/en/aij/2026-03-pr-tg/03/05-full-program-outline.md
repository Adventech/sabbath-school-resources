**^[CONNECT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Before you begin Sabbath School, have an appropriate eating treat in view for the children, but don’t refer to it. If they make reference to it, simply say, “That’s not for now,” and redirect their attention.

Warmly acknowledge all children by name as they arrive. Share how happy you are to see them. Ask them about something good that happened for them this week.

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#4d4d4f"}}})** Invite the children to try playing the different instruments you have brought as you praise God together. You may like to invite the children to play along with songs from the Alive in Jesus website.

**OPENING ACTIVITIES** (10 MINUTES)

**^[WELCOME:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Bring the children together and welcome them as a group. Briefly share some ways God has been good to you this week. Talk about how you’re so looking forward to praising God today!

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Sing **“God Is So Wonderful.”** Between each verse, invite the children to share why God is so wonderful.

- **What words in this song do you love the most?”**

Remind the children that God loves to hear us praise and worship Him. Briefly explain what praise is: You praise something or someone when you share (with some excitement) a quality or something you admire or really love about them.

- **What do you really love about Jesus?**

Praying is another way we praise God. Before you sing your prayer song, share the first three phrases of the lyrics to highlight some other things we can be grateful to God for (He made us, He saved us, He’s coming back to take us home), then reverently sing this prayer song: **“Sabbath Song.”**

**^[PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** In your prayer, praise God for who He is, and thank Him for giving us the Sabbath, so we can come and learn about Him, and for being a God who loves us more than we can imagine.

**^[GIVE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#d34d5e"}}})** Explain that giving is another way we can say “Thank You” to God for all He does for us. Explain that we give our offerings to God for His work both in our area and around the world. We also give our tithe, 10 percent of anything we earn, which is collected by our wider church to pay for our pastors. Encourage the children to bring their own tithe to church from any money or gifts they receive.

Have a child collect the offering as you sing **“Thank You, Jesus.”** Display your **Offering Chart** and share how much money the class has given so far this quarter. Thank the children for their generous giving. Share this week’s **Mission Fast Fact** about this quarter’s mission project. Pray and ask God to bless and take the offering to where it’s most needed.

**FOCUS ACTIVITIES** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Make the room as dark as possible. Shine a flashlight around the room and challenge children to follow the light. Repeat this several times.

Ask: **Have you ever followed something to find out where it led? How did you feel?** (Curious, excited, adventurous.)

**^[EXPLAIN:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** In our story today, Wise Men were willing to follow what looked like a bright star wherever it led. **I wonder why they would do that?** The answer might be found in our Big Idea.

**^[READ:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#e6b349"}}})** Read the **Big Idea** together: **Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF0E8", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**BIBLE STORY** (15 MINUTES)

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649b7c"}}})** Pray, asking God to help the children understand today’s story. Then creatively share the Bible story. Use props, sing carols within the story.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#ECF6F2", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**UNPACK THE STORY** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DISCUSS:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})**

- **What do people do when their hearts are full of love?** (Hug, say nice words, smile, sing, help, give gifts.)
- **How do you like to show Jesus that you love Him as you worship Him? What might you do with your hands? Your body? Your face? Your mouth? Your eyes?** (One example is to give our offerings as an act of worship God, as the wise men gave gifts to Jesus.)

Invite children to show God their love as they quietly talk to Him in prayer, just as the Wise Men and shepherds quietly talked to God from their hearts. Play quiet instrumental music if desired.

**^[SING:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Read **Psalm 27:8, “Your face, Lord, I will seek.”** Invite the children to praise God with some instruments or percussion and sing a **praise song of choice**. As they sing, invite them to think about what it will be like when Jesus returns, and we can look into God’s face and praise Him in heaven.

**^[TEACHING POINT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#00b7aa"}}})** Remind them again of today’s **Big Idea: Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship.**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F2ECF0", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#6D6E6D", "size": "sm"}}}
**TIE IT ALL TOGETHER** (20 MINUTES)

**^[MEMORY VERSE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Give each child **Character Collector Card 29: Wise Men: Worshipping Jesus** to place in their storage container. Discuss the picture and ask the children to recite the memory verse. Then sing the **Scripture Song “Psalm 146:1, 2.”** Ask: **Why do you choose to sing praises to God?**

!a[https://sabbath-school-resources-assets.adventech.io/en/aij/2026-03-pr-tg/assets/3-psalm-146-1-2.mp3] “Psalm 146:1-2” {"credits":[{"key":"Writer","value":"Rosie Smith"},{"key":"Singer","value":"Elle Stojanovic"}],"title": "Credits","copyright":"Copyright © 2026 General Conference Corporation® of Seventh-day Adventists."}

**^[MISSION STORY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Share the Mission Story, **“Christmas Miracle,”** and discuss the follow-up question.

**^[MISSION CHALLENGE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** The mother in the story began her friendship with Jesus by beginning a friendship with Sumanta. When we encourage others, we are helping God.

Your Mission Challenge this week: With your family, think about a family member (grandparent, uncle, cousin) who may need some encouragement or help or peace. Think of a creative way to connect with them this week through a song, letter, or video message.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Show the children your completed **Flying Angel craft**. Demonstrate how to make it, then distribute a circle template to each child along with other supplies required to complete their crafts. You may like to play Christmas hymns in the background as they do this.

- **Whom could you share today’s story with?**

This week, use your craft to share the story and its message with at least one other person.

**^[COMMIT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Repeat the **Big Idea** together, **Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship.**

Show the storytelling star.

- **Was the star the shepherds and Wise Men saw actually a star? What was it really?**

**Teaching Point:** It was a whole choir of angels who were bursting to sing a praise song to God.

**^[REVIEW MEMORY VERSE AND BIG IDEA:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Say your **memory verse** one last time as a praise song to God: “Praise the Lord, O my soul! While I live, I will praise the Lord; I will sing praises to my God while I have my being” (Psalm 146:1, 2).

Say the **Big Idea** one final time: **Jesus is worthy of my praise and worship.**

Invite children who want to say these words to God in worship this week to come forward and take a small **star**. This can be a reminder at home to praise and worship God every day.

**^[SING and PRAY:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#a771b0"}}})** Sing **“God Bless You.”** Then, as you pray, praise God for being so amazing and always worthy of our praise and worship.
