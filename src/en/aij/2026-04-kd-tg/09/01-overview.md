**Word of the Week**

**Helpless:** Being helpless is when you can’t do anything to help yourself, or anyone else. A helpless person needs help. As sinners we are helpless to get out of sin. Jesus can help us and save us.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus visited the Pool of Bethesda on a Sabbath day and saw a man who had lived with a physical disability for 38 years. He asked the man if he wanted to get well. The man told Jesus that he was helpless to get into the healing waters to try to save himself. Jesus then told the man to pick up his bed and walk. The man obeyed and was healed. The Pharisees were angry that their Sabbath rules had been broken in both the healing and the carrying of the mat. The man went on to the temple to praise God, and there Jesus found Him and encouraged him to sin no more. By grace, he had been saved.

**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus is the only one who can save them.
- **Feel** a desire to trust Jesus when they feel helpless.
- **Respond** by asking Jesus to save them.
