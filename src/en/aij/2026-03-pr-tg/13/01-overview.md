**Word of the Week**

**Blessing:** A blessing is a special favor or gift that improves a person’s life, making them happy and content. You can also say a blessing: request God to care for someone.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Lesson Summary**

Jesus loved to bless others and teach them God’s wonderful truths. After teaching in the synagogue one Sabbath, Jesus went to Peter’s house, where Peter’s mother-in-law was sick. Jesus healed her from her sickness and thus helped and blessed her and her household. Once healed, she immediately got up to serve others. The whole town heard about the healing and came to Jesus to be healed too.

---

{"style": {"text": {"size": "lg"}}}
**Learning Goals**

The children will:

- **Know** that Jesus wants to bless their lives.
- **Feel** blessed knowing they are loved by God.
- **Respond** by being a blessing to others too.
