[[Revelation 13:1]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- At the end of time the dragon, Satan, recruits two other major powers to assist him, the first being this “beast rising up out of the sea” (or sea beast).
- Commentators have often called this evil trio “the unholy trinity,” for they are clearly attempting to impersonate the true holy Trinity in earth’s last days.
- The members of the Trinity are God the Father, God the Son (Jesus), and God the Holy Spirit, the three divine persons of the one true God. _God is one God and one God only!_ And He is _one_ God who reveals Himself through _three_ distinct persons. This is a holy mystery. But it is what the two witnesses of the Old and New Testaments clearly describe.
- The unholy trinity will become the organizers of what Revelation refers to as “Babylon,” an evil, global confederation of forces that persecute God’s people in the very last days of earth’s history.

[[Revelation 13:2-10]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- Notice that this sea beast has authority and persecutes God’s people (“the saints”) for “42 months” (verse 5). This is yet another appearance of the 1260-year prophecy.
- You’ll recall also that we stated that the sea beast is the same power as the little horn of Daniel 7. The little horn of Daniel 7 was clearly identified in our study on Revelation 11 as papal Rome.
- To review evidence that the little horn and the sea beast represent the same power, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r13a/)**.
- The sea beast has a deadly wound, but then the deadly wound is healed (verse 3). This wound is a reference to the pope being taken captive by the French in 1798 at the end of the 1260-year prophecy. The Papacy’s power had been in decline for years, and 1798 brought that decline to its low point.
- But the wound would be “healed,” a process we will talk about more later.
- Note also that this language of “deadly” wounding/healing parallels the crucifixion and resurrection of Jesus. In other words, the Papacy is portrayed in this prophecy as an imitation of Jesus, the second member of the holy Trinity.
- Consequently, the Papacy here can also be accurately described as the “antichrist” of the end-times.
- To learn more about this term, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r13b/)**.

[[Revelation 13:11-13]]

- {"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F1E5DA", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "base", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
- The beast coming up out of the earth (or, more simply, the earth beast) is the third member of the unholy trinity.
- Since the dragon was an imitation of God the Father, and the sea beast an imitation of God the Son, we can expect that the earth beast will be an imitation of God the Holy Spirit.
- The earth beast represents the United States of America.
- To see how this is so, **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r13c/)**.
- Note that according to the prophecy, the United States will attempt to force our planet to worship the Papacy, including by calling fire to “come down from heaven on the earth.” We will see what this symbolizes when we study Revelation 16.

- What is this image? While we can never know with certainty every detail of unfulfilled prophecy, we can know at least the following with reasonable accuracy and confidence:
	- It will be\
(1) a global system that\
(2) combines religious goals with political power so that\
(3) all the world will be forced to conform to the false worship of the Papacy.
- To learn more, please **[click here 🔗](https://thingsnew.org/en/r13d/)**.
- This obviously would require the United States to abandon the principles of its Constitution—in fact, the scene described in Revelation 13 means it will soon abandon support for the separation of church and state, individual freedoms, the rule of law, etc., in favor of a forced religious observance.

[[Revelation 13:16-18]]

- The mark of the beast is quite serious and has understandably been the focus of endless speculation by millions. It will be part of a global and deadly attempt to take worship away from the one true God and focus it instead on the dictates of the sea beast and the dragon.
- We therefore _must_ know what the mark of the beast is. It will be revealed in our study of chapter 14.
