When they get there, they place another heavy item into their bag and carry it back to the first pile. Have them continue collecting heavy items until it becomes too heavy. Encourage their efforts.

**SECRET SURPRISE BOX** (7 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Play your **bell** or **sound maker**, then invite a child to open the **Secret Surprise Box** to reveal some **colorful slime**, a small **Lego brick set**, a cute **teddy bear**, and/or other culturally appropriate items/food the children would be tempted to touch or eat.

For each item, ask:

- **Would you like to play with (or eat) this?**
- **What if I put it in your lap for two minutes but told you not to touch it? Would it be hard not to?**

If appropriate, place one or more items in a child’s lap and tell them not to play with or eat the item.

- **Were you tempted to play with/eat it?**
- **Who can help us when we are tempted to do the wrong thing?**

**^[SHARE:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#649B7C"}}})** Jesus was also tempted as we are. He knows how hard it is, and He wants to give us strength when Satan tempts us to do the wrong thing.

Share the **Big Idea: Jesus is my God. His words are powerful.**

Let’s find out how Jesus used the words of the Bible when Satan tempted Him.

**STORY EXPLORER BOX** (5 MINUTES)

**^[DO:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#A771B0"}}})** Play your bell or sound maker. Then invite a child to open the **Story Explorer Box** to reveal **a picture of two children arm wrestling**. (Ensure that this activity is done to focus on the meaning as an object lesson rather than a competition.)

Ask for two volunteers who would like to try arm wrestling. Choose a pair of children and seat them at a small table. Say, “Ready, set, go!” and see who is strongest. Encourage both of their efforts. After two pairs of children have had a turn, tell the children that now we will try one more time. Choose a larger child and a smaller child and have them come to the front to prepare to arm wrestle. First, ask:

- **Who do you think will win? Is this fair?**

Have the two children start arm wrestling but then help the smaller child to win.

- **Why did (smaller child’s name) win?** (Because you helped him/her.)

**Teaching Point:** This is like what Jesus does for us. Satan’s temptations are too strong for us, but Jesus helps us, just as I did for [smaller child’s name]. Whenever we are tempted to do wrong, and whenever it feels too hard, we can remember Jesus’ words and ask Him to help us. Jesus is stronger, and He will always help us when we ask Him!

**NATURE/MISSION** (3 MINUTES)

**^[NATURE COLLECTOR CARD:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#215E9E"}}})**

- **What can you think of in nature that is very strong?**

Hold up **Nature Card 33: Spiderweb** and share the following facts:

- Spiderwebs are lighter than cotton and up to 1,000 times thinner than a human hair.
- They are stronger than a piece of steel of the same size.
- Spiderwebs are made from silk that comes from silk glands on the spider’s body.
- The silk turns from a watery liquid to a solid as it leaves the spider’s silk gland.
- Spiders use silk to make webs of many different shapes and sizes.

Next time Satan tempts you to do the wrong thing, ask Jesus to help you to be as strong as a spiderweb!

Give each child **Nature Card 33: Spiderweb** to put in their Nature Collector Bag.

**^[CRAFT:]({"style": {"text": {"color":"#F4793B"}}}) Memory Verse Heart Pocket.** Thread ribbon through holes punched into two heart shapes to create a pocket where children can place their memory verses or nature cards (memory verses on the second side).
