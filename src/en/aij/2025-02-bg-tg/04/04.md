**WEEK 4: FOUR GOOD FRIENDS**

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#F8F7F6", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}, "text": {"color": "#418fcd", "size": "sm"}}}
**Story bag**: Cardboard box with cut hole in the roof, fabric, small doll, cardboard piece, string, wooden blocks

Ask: **Can you wriggle your toes? Can you move your legs up and down?** (Invite children to try each movement together with you.)

Open your Bible, point, and say: **Our Bible story today is about a man who couldn’t use his legs! He couldn’t jump, or hop, or skip.**

Invite a child to sit beside you. Wrap fabric around their legs as you share about the man who could not walk.

Say: **A man had four friends** (count to four using your fingers). **These friends knew that Jesus had made the man who was blind to see. Do you think Jesus can help their friend to walk?** (Encourage responses.) **How will the man see Jesus if he can’t walk?**

Place a small doll onto a piece of fabric that has a piece of string on each corner.

Say: **Jesus was in a house full of lots of people** (show cardboard box). **The friends had a good idea! “The men went up** (to the top of the house) **and made a hole in the** (roof) . . . (and then) **lowered the mat so the man was lying right before Jesus”** (Luke 5:19, ICB).

Lower the doll through the “roof” and make surprised expressions as you talk about the people who watched the man come through the roof.

Ask: **What do you think Jesus’ face looked like?** (Smile.) **What do you think Jesus said? He said, “Friend, your sins are forgiven . . . . Stand up, take your mat, and go home!”** (Luke 5:24, ICB). The man’s legs moved. He picked up his mat and began walking!

Make some fun movements with the children to show how happy the man was (jumping, hopping, running in place).

Say: **The man’s heart was so happy! He wanted to tell everyone about Jesus!**

Say together: **Thank You, Jesus, for showing me how much You care for me and others. Help me to tell others about Your love.**
