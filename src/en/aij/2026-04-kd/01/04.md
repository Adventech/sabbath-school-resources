The disciples’ eyes filled with fear. Hands held on tightly so they wouldn’t get tossed into the big waves. The water was all around their feet. The boat was filling up fast!

In all the cloudy darkness and the noisy wind, the disciples had forgotten something very important.

Jesus was with them in their boat. He wasn’t scared of the cloudy darkness or the noisy wind. In fact, Jesus was so peaceful, He was still asleep!

Jesus loves you so much, He wants you to always feel peaceful too. You can be calm when you trust in Him. **What does John 14:27 say?**

The disciples had forgotten to trust Jesus to give them peace. They had also forgotten that Jesus had said, “Let us cross over to the other side” (Mark 4:35). Jesus had said they would go there, and He would make sure they arrived.
