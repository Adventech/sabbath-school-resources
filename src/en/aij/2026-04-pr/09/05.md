The healed man had an amazing story to share, but it was about to get even better! Shaking off the grumpy reaction of the Pharisees, he headed joyously to the temple to thank God for His great mercy. He was sure the miracle came from God. He now knew that “the Lord is gracious and full of compassion, slow to anger and great in mercy” (Psalm 145:8).

It was here, in the temple, that Jesus found him. With a soft smile and a gentle voice, Jesus told the man, “See, you are well now. But stop sinning or something worse may happen to you!” (John 5:14, ICB).

Jesus had healed the man’s body at the pool of Bethesda. Now Jesus found him a second time, to heal him spiritually. Jesus’ lovingkindness shows us that physical healing isn’t enough—He wants to touch hearts and change lives, too.

The healed man instantly recognized Jesus as the One who had healed him, and he was excited to see Jesus again! What did he do next? **Read John 5:15.**

A miracle cure after 38 miserable years was the best thing that had ever happened to the man. There was no way he could keep such amazing news to himself! He wanted others to know that Jesus had healed him. First, he told the Pharisees who had questioned him. Then, as he walked through the streets of Jerusalem, we can be sure he told many others. That is what grateful people do when they have been changed by Jesus’ lovingkindness.

Jesus wants to heal our bodies, touch our hearts, and change our lives too.

THINK . . . THEN TALK ABOUT IT:

- Why did Jesus find and talk to the healed man again?
- The sick man couldn’t make his body well; he needed Jesus. Can you make your thoughts and actions all pure, perfect, and good? (No.) Who do you need to heal you from sin?
