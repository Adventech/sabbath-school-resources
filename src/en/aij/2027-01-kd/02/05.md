Moses listened carefully to God’s mighty voice coming from inside the flickering, burning bush.

“I have seen the troubles my people have suffered in Egypt,” God said, “And I have heard their cries when the Egyptian slave masters hurt them. . . . So now I am sending you to the king of Egypt. Go! Bring my people, the Israelites, out of Egypt” (Exodus 3:7-10, ICB).

God wanted to help His children who were being treated so unkindly. God didn’t want them to be slaves. He wanted them to be free, safe, and happy.

Before, Moses had wanted to save the people of Israel in his own angry way. Now, God had a better plan for Moses to do this.

But Moses was afraid of doing such a big job. He said, “I am not a great man! Why should I be the one to go to the king and lead the Israelites out of Egypt?” (Exodus 3:11, ICB).

What did God reply? **Read Exodus 3:12.**

What a beautiful promise. God would help Moses be the strong leader he needed to be. And God would do everything needed—even the impossible—to bring the Israelites out of Egypt to worship in this very spot! He said, “I will stretch out My hand and strike Egypt with all My wonders which I will do in its midst; and after that he will let you go” (Exodus 3:20).

God has called you too! He has important things for you to do. You don’t need to be bigger, stronger, or smarter. You just need to trust that God’s way is best, and do what He asks—follow Him and obey Him.
