Joseph thought about his dream as he rolled up his bedroll. He worried about it as he fed the lambs and when he mended the fence. His dream was on his mind all the time!

Then, under the light of another silvery moon, Joseph had another dream. In the morning he got up even more confused. He decided to tell his brothers about it again.

“Listen,” he told them, “I had another dream. I saw the sun, moon and 11 stars bowing down to me” (Genesis 37:9, ICB).

Joseph’s brothers glared at him. Did he say 11 stars? Joseph had 11 brothers! Their faces turned so red they looked like a volcano ready to explode!

Joseph’s brothers already thought that their father treated Joseph like he was the best. Now it sounded like Joseph thought he was the best too!

Feeling even more confused, Joseph also told his father about his dream. What did Jacob say to Joseph? **Read Genesis 37:10.**

But old Jacob couldn’t help but wonder about this dream. He scratched his head and thought long and hard. God had given him a very special dream once, long ago. Maybe God was trying to tell Joseph something too.

When you are puzzled about something, don’t let it upset you. Remember to give it to Jesus, “. . . casting all your care upon Him, for He cares for you” (1 Peter 5:7).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})

- Have you ever had an interesting or unusual dream?
- Did you tell someone about it? How did they respond?
- Why do you think God gave these dreams to Joseph?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Ask God to help you trust Him even when you don’t understand what is happening.

^[Do]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#7994CC", "size": "lg"}}})\
Put a puzzle together. Notice how one piece on its own doesn’t often make sense, but once together, all the pieces make a big, beautiful picture. God cares for you and sees the big picture all the time!
