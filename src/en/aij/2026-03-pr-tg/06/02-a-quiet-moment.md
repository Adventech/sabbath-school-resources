**Dear Sabbath School Teacher,**

Life is noisy, distracting, and sometimes deceiving. It’s easy to be swept up in its turmoil. But there are moments that arrest our attention and give us clarity about our true spiritual state. In such times we can feel remorse, regret, shame, or guilt. In essence, we see our need of Jesus. This is the work of the Holy Spirit in our hearts and lives.

Repentance is not a pleasant experience. To bring about repentance in a person’s life, we read that God “lays heavy burdens upon the conscience of the wrongdoer, and pierces the soul with arrows of conviction” (Ellen G. White, _The Desire of Ages_, p. 104). It is a painful and difficult experience. But it is an important part of the work that Jesus must do to prepare us for our heavenly home (see Matthew 1:21).

John the Baptist was used by the Holy Spirit to bring about such a moment. He was to prepare the people to receive Jesus. Boldly, he appealed, “Repent, for the kingdom of God is at hand” (Matthew 3:2). How do his words sit with you, today?

When our regret leads to repentance, our merciful God is ever ready to take away our sin, guilt and shame, and offer us forgiveness. He grants a clean heart in exchange for our heart of stone. And His Spirit to guide us to live out His laws of love. (Ezekiel 36:26, 27).

Our prayer is that as you prepare this lesson for the children, you ask God to prepare the way for your heart, and the little hearts in your care, to meet Jesus.
