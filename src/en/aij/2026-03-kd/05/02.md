Life wasn’t always easy for Jesus. Mary and Joseph didn’t have much money. They lived in a simple, small home. Every day they had to work hard to pay for their food and clothes.

Perhaps Jesus carried heavy water jars on His little shoulders, swept the floor, and wiped down the table. No job was too boring or hard for Jesus.

Jesus cheerfully helped others. For “the Son of Man did not come to be served. He came to serve” (Mark 10:45, ICB).

Even though Jesus always showed kindness, not everyone was kind to Him. Sometimes the children in Nazareth would do naughty or disobedient things. When they saw Jesus being obedient and honest, they would laugh and call Him names. It annoyed them that Jesus always did what was right.

Other times adults in Nazareth whispered lies about Jesus. They would point at Him and shake their heads. But Jesus loved these people too.

Why had Jesus come to live here on earth? **Read Luke 19:10.**

Jesus never stopped showing everyone God’s big love and perfect ways. When others were unkind, Jesus showed kindness. When people lied, Jesus told the truth. When people did the wrong thing, Jesus did what was right.

And so “Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men” (Luke 2:52).

^[I Wonder]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})

- Can you think of a time someone was unkind to you? What do you think Jesus would have done if the same thing had happened to Him?

^[Prayer Idea]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "DKWhaleSong-Regular", "color": "#21A0A9", "size": "lg"}}})\
Thank Jesus that He understands our problems and pain, because He experienced it too.
