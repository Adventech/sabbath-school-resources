Imagine receiving an amazing gift that is too big, too important, and too special to ever forget! That’s exactly what the Sabbath is! But sadly God knew that people would forget it. What did He tell us to do? **Read Exodus 20:8** (first word).

God wrote this command in stone with His own finger when He gave His people the Ten Commandments—that’s how important it is! It’s also the only commandment that starts with the word “remember.” In the same way that a memorial that your country might have to remind people of a national hero or a battle, God made the Sabbath to remind us of where we came from and who made us. Because it’s so important, God instructed that we don’t do any work on the Sabbath. So instead of doing all the things we think about during the week (work, shopping, and lots of other things), we do different things that focus on God. That’s how we keep the Sabbath holy. God should be part of our everyday lives, but Sabbath is extra-special!

God also sent prophets, Jesus Himself, and teachers in the Bible, such as Paul, to remind us to keep Sabbath as God’s special day. It’s so special that the Bible says we will be enjoying Sabbath with Him forever in the earth made new (Isaiah 66:23). Just as Adam and Eve did at the beginning, we will be able to spend Sabbath face-to-face with God!

Until then we can meet God every Sabbath in other ways. We can gather together with friends and family to sing to Him, pray, read the Bible, and tell Jesus how grateful we are that He died to save us.

God’s beautiful sunsets help us remember when the Sabbath day begins and ends. As the sun sinks in the sky on Friday evening, we know it’s time to stop and worship God, because Sabbath has begun. Then on Saturday night the setting sun tells us Sabbath is ending. We can pause again to worship God, remembering that He is our special Sabbath friend.

What a big gift and a blessing it is to remember God’s Sabbath!
