Sold Again!

When Joseph closed his eyes, he could still imagine the green hills of his home. But those hills were now far, far away. The brothers who had sold him as a slave, and his father and Benjamin, were far away too.

Joseph tried not to cry. Instead, he opened his eyes wide. There was so much to see! After walking down dusty roads and through sandy deserts, he had come to the big city. He was finally in Egypt.

The traders led the camels through noisy, busy streets. Joseph walked behind, with his hands tied together. He watched everything closely. The people were so different. They spoke words he didn’t understand, and they wore clothes he had never seen before. Joseph looked up at the tall palaces and great statues. It looked like a very rich city.

Finally the camels stopped. The traders untied Joseph and led him through the busy marketplace. He stood still as the traders began talking to the people nearby.

Joseph couldn’t believe it—he was being sold again! His heart beat fast. He was nervous but he knew God would be with him. The Bible tells us, “The Lord will give strength to his people; the Lord will bless His people with peace” (Psalm 29:11). That’s just what Joseph needed!

Money jingled, heads nodded, and Joseph was sold. Who had bought him? **Read Genesis 39:1.**
