The disciples were always watching and listening closely to Jesus so they could learn His way of love. One day they were quietly listening to their Teacher pray. Jesus prayed a lot, and His prayers were simple and beautiful. It was as though He was talking to a good friend whom He knew very well.

The disciples had heard people pray long, loud prayers to impress others, but they had never seen anyone talking alone with God in a quiet place. They wanted to be able to pray just as Jesus did, so one of them asked, “Lord, teach us to pray” (Luke 11:1).

Jesus was very happy to teach them to pray, because He knew that talking to God would help them get to know God as their best friend and allow God to change their hearts to be loving like His. This is one of the best and most important things a growing disciple can do.

Before Jesus taught them to pray by praying His own beautiful prayer, He reminded them that God loves to hear prayers that are honest and real. He didn’t want them to thoughtlessly say the same old lines, or to show off. Heartfelt prayers are the kind of prayers that nurture faith and help disciples trust God and feel close to Him.

How did Jesus pray? **Read Matthew 6:9-13** out loud, then silently as your own prayer. Jesus loves to hear us pray to Him anytime and anywhere. **Read Jeremiah 29:12.**
