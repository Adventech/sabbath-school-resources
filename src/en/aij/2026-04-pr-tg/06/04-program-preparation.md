**Opening and Focus Activities**

Print a memory verse **coloring page** for each child. Source **colored pencils** and **markers**.

Update **^[Offering Chart]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Prepare **^[Mission Fast Fact]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Write the **Big Idea** on your **whiteboard**, use the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**, or **print/write out** and display.

Source items for the Focus Activity: **One or two props you may use when sick**. This may include a tissue box, mask, hot-water bottle, pajamas.

{"style": {"block": {"backgroundColor": "#E4EEF5", "padding": {"top": "sm", "start": "sm", "end":"sm", "bottom": "sm"}}}}
**Bible Story and Unpack the Story**

Source the **^[Program PowerPoint]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})**.

Source suggested Storytelling Props: a **piece of cloth, peg people**, or **felt set**. Optional: **paper plates or round pieces of cardstock**.

Use **tape** to mark a wall (or tie a string) to indicate a level that the children can reach only by jumping.

**Tie It All Together**

Print **^[Character Card 45: Jesus and a Sick Woman]({"style": {"text": {"color": "#58B0E3"}}})** for each child.

Source a **sticky note** or small piece of paper and **pencil** for each child.
