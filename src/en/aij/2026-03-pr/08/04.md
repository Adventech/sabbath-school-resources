In the early-morning quiet, four disappointed disciples pulled their boats onto the sandy shore and began to wash their nets. Peter, Andrew, John, and his brother, James, had fished all night and caught nothing—not even one tiny fish. Peter was especially discouraged.

Time had passed since Jesus had called them to follow Him. Peter had seen Jesus perform miracles and great wonders. But jealous Jewish leaders had rejected Jesus and made life difficult; even John the Baptist had been thrown into prison. Things didn’t seem to be going to plan—and now he couldn’t even catch a fish!

Jesus came walking along the beach with a huge crowd of followers. Seeing Peter’s boat, Jesus stepped into it and began to teach the people about God’s love and how they could be saved from their sins. Old men with walking sticks, muscular farmers, fishermen, teachers, sick people, and children all crowded close to hear Jesus’ wonderful words of peace.

When Jesus had finished teaching, He spoke to Peter. What did He say? **Read Luke 5:4.**

Peter looked at Jesus in surprise. That sounded crazy! People fished on this lake only at night! But he replied, “Master, we worked hard all night trying to catch fish, but we caught nothing. But you say to put the nets in the water; so I will” (Luke 5:5, ICB). It made no sense, but Peter trusted anyway.

We will become disappointed or discouraged sometimes, just as the disciples did. We might be disappointed when we miss out on something fun, or when we get a bad grade in school. We might become discouraged when a friend doesn’t want to follow Jesus. But Jesus will always love us very much. When we choose to keep following and trusting Him, good things will happen. Just wait and see!

^[FAST FACTS:]({"style": {"text": {"typeface": "Trashbone-Regular"}}})\
• Fishing by boat on the Sea of Galilee was done only at night, as this is when the fish are close to the surface. During the day they swim deeper down, farther from the light.\
• There were about 230 fishing boats on the lake in Jesus’ day.
